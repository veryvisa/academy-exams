① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 机构职能与管辖权辨析题：给你一个具体监管动作（发牌、罚款、审批样本计划、管理补助或接收反洗钱报告），考你能否分清省证监会、加拿大证券管理局（Canadian Securities Administrators, CSA）、加拿大投资监管组织（Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization, CIRO）、加拿大税务局（Canada Revenue Agency, CRA）、加拿大就业与社会发展部（Employment and Social Development Canada, ESDC）及加拿大金融交易和报告分析中心（Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada, FINTRAC）各自的法定边界。Institutional roles and jurisdictional boundaries: Given a specific regulatory action (licensing, levying fines, approving specimen plans, administering grants, or receiving anti-money laundering reports), questions test your ability to distinguish the statutory boundaries of provincial and territorial securities commissions, the Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA), the Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization (CIRO), the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC), and the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada (FINTRAC).
  • 注册全周期与时限情景题：围绕客户居住地判据、授权公司代表（Authorized Firm Representative, AFR）通过全国注册数据库（National Registration Database, NRD）提交申请、注册获批前严禁抢跑交易，以及注册后 10 天与 30 天两档信息变更报备时限出应用题。Registration lifecycle and timeline scenarios: Scenario questions test client residency as the jurisdictional criterion, submission of applications by the Authorized Firm Representative (AFR) through the National Registration Database (NRD), the strict prohibition against trading before registration approval is confirmed, and the 10-day versus 30-day reporting deadlines for changes in registration information.
  • 招股书收据性质与撤回权起算题：考察招股书收据（Prospectus Receipt）仅代表形式与内容达标而非监管背书，以及全国政策第 15 号（National Policy 15, NP 15）赋予奖学金计划认购人的 60 天撤回权从何时起算、未交付招股书会导致什么法律后果。Prospectus receipt legal status and withdrawal right triggers: Questions test that a prospectus receipt indicates only compliance with form and content standards rather than regulatory endorsement of investment merit, when the 60-day withdrawal right granted to scholarship plan subscribers under National Policy 15 (NP 15) begins, and the legal consequences of failing to deliver a prospectus.
  • 跨法域叠加与优先级陷阱题：最典型的是反洗钱法豁免注册计划身份核实与证券法强制核实客户身份的叠加，以及反垃圾邮件法 2 年默示同意期与全国谢绝来电名单 18 个月豁免期的数字对比。Cross-jurisdictional overlap and priority traps: Questions highlight conflicts and dual layers of regulation, most notably the overlap where anti-money laundering legislation exempts registered plans from identity verification while securities legislation independently mandates client identity verification, as well as numerical contrasts between the 2-year implied consent window under anti-spam legislation (CASL) and the 18-month exemption window under the National Do Not Call List (DNCL).
  • 执法工具与民事救济边界题：区分证券监管机构可直接采取的行政处分（停牌、停止交易令、罚款）与必须由投资者通过法院行使的民事诉讼救济（180 天撤销之诉、3 年损害赔偿之诉），重点考监管机构通常无权直接命令赔偿投资者或强迫客户履约。Enforcement powers versus civil remedies: Questions test the distinction between administrative penalties that securities commissions can directly impose (suspensions, cease-trade orders, administrative penalties) and statutory civil remedies that investors must pursue through the courts (an action for rescission within 180 days or an action for damages within 3 years), emphasizing that regulators generally lack the authority to order direct restitution to investors or compel clients to honour subscription contracts.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 把协调协会或自律组织当成直管机关Mistaking Coordinating Associations or SROs for Direct Regulatory Authorities

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生容易用日常印象把 CSA 当成联邦证监会，或误以为 2023 年合并成立的 CIRO 统一接管了包括奖学金计划经销商（Scholarship Plan Dealer, SPD）在内的所有证券从业者。Candidates often assume from casual impressions that the CSA is a federal securities commission, or mistakenly believe that CIRO (formed by merger on January 1, 2023) assumed regulatory oversight of all securities registrants, including scholarship plan dealers.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 national regulator、federal securities commission、CSA fining a dealer 或 CIRO registering a scholarship plan dealing representative 等表述。Look for phrasing in question stems or options such as national regulator, federal securities commission, CSA fining a dealer, or CIRO registering a scholarship plan dealing representative.

⚠ 以办公地代替客户居住地判断注册辖区Using Representative Office Location Instead of Client Residency to Determine Registration Jurisdiction

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 从业者常以为只要在自己办公室所在省份持牌，就可以接听外省亲友电话或远程帮外省客户开户，忽略了证券法按客户居住地划分管辖权。Practitioners often mistakenly assume that holding a registration in the province where their office is located allows them to take calls from or remotely enrol out-of-province relatives or clients, overlooking that securities law allocates jurisdiction strictly by client residence.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述代表在 A 省注册，而潜在认购人居住在 B 省，或出现电话、视频、返乡探亲顺便介绍计划等跨省展业场景。The question stem describes a representative registered in Province A while a prospective subscriber resides in Province B, or depicts cross-provincial prospecting via phone, video call, or personal family visits.

⚠ 把招股书收据当成政府认可或批准投资价值Mistaking a Prospectus Receipt for Government Endorsement or Approval of Investment Merits

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 日常语言中收到官方回执常被理解成审批通过，容易顺水推舟认为证监会已经替投资者把过关、认可该计划是审慎安全的产品。In everyday language, receiving an official receipt is often misunderstood as regulatory approval, leading candidates to assume securities commissions have vetted the plan and endorsed it as a safe, prudent investment.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 选项中出现 approved the merits、endorsed as a prudent investment、guaranteed by the commission 等评价投资质量的词。Look for options containing phrases such as approved the merits, endorsed as a prudent investment, or guaranteed by the commission evaluating product quality.

⚠ 只记反洗钱豁免而漏掉证券法强制身份核实Relying on Anti-Money Laundering Exemptions While Overlooking Mandatory Securities Identity Verification

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到注册教育储蓄计划（Registered Education Savings Plan, RESP）在反洗钱法下豁免身份核实，就直接选无需核验客户身份，忘了证券法 NI 31-103 对所有注册人另有独立的身份确认要求。Candidates who learn that RESPs are exempt from identity verification under federal anti-money laundering legislation mistakenly conclude that no identity check is needed, forgetting that NI 31-103 s.13.2 independently mandates client identity verification for all registrants.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时提及 PCMLTFA 豁免与开立奖学金计划账户，问你是否仍需核实认购人身份以及法律依据是什么。The stem mentions the PCMLTFA exemption alongside opening a scholarship plan account and asks whether subscriber identity must still be verified and under what legal authority.

⚠ 混淆两套营销规则与两套报备时钟的数字Confusing Timelines Across Dual Marketing Rules and Registration Reporting Clocks

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 10 天与 30 天信息更新、2 个营业日与 60 天撤回权、180 天与 3 年诉讼时效、2 年与 18 个月既有业务关系豁免，数字密集且场景相似，极易张冠李戴。The dense overlap of numerical timelines—10 versus 30 days for registration updates, 2 business days versus 60 days for withdrawal rights, 180 days versus 3 years for civil lawsuits, and 2 years versus 18 months for existing business relationship marketing exemptions—frequently leads candidates to swap figures across similar contexts.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现天数、月数或年数，且同时涉及信息变更、撤回取消、起诉索赔或给老客户发邮件与打电话。The question stem cites numbers of days, months, or years while combining information updates, cancellation rights, civil lawsuits, or contacting past clients via email or telephone.

全国性文件与政策工具的层级Hierarchy of National Instruments and Policy InstrumentsNational Instrument (NI), Multilateral Instrument (MI) & National Policy (NP)NI 是经全部 13 个辖区各自采纳为本地规则而具有法律约束力的统一规则，MI 仅在部分辖区适用，NP 则是监管机构的解释与预期（但 NP 15 是奖学金计划取得招股书收据的前置标准）。A National Instrument (NI) is a legally binding uniform rule adopted in the same form by all 13 provincial and territorial jurisdictions as a local rule. A Multilateral Instrument (MI) applies in only some jurisdictions, while a National Policy (NP) reflects regulatory interpretation and expectations (though NP 15 serves as a prerequisite standard for scholarship plans to obtain a prospectus receipt).

Until written confirmation is received from the dealer that registration approval has been granted, an individual must not engage in any trading or solicitation activities.

Firms registered solely as scholarship plan dealers and their dealing representatives remain directly registered with and regulated by provincial and territorial securities commissions.
招股书披露标准、交付期限与 60 天撤回权Prospectus Disclosure Standards, Delivery Deadlines, and the 60-Day Withdrawal RightFull, True and Plain Disclosure, Lapse Date & 60-Day Withdrawal Right招股书须作完整、真实、清楚的重大事实披露并在签订购买协议后第 2 个营业日午夜前交付；NP 15 将奖学金计划认购人的无成本撤回权从收到招股书起 2 个营业日延长至 60 天，未交付则取消权持续存在。A prospectus must provide full, true, and plain disclosure of all material facts and be delivered no later than midnight on the second business day after entering into a purchase agreement. Under National Policy 15 (NP 15), the unconditional withdrawal right for scholarship plan subscribers is extended from the general two business days to 60 days following receipt of the prospectus; if never delivered, the right to cancel continues indefinitely.

Parties other than the issuer may raise a due diligence defence, but the issuer itself cannot use this defence.

Although federal AML legislation exempts registered plan accounts from identity verification, NI 31-103 s.13.2 independently requires registrants to take reasonable steps to verify the identity of every client.

第 1 题Q1CSA与NI的法律性质Legal nature of the CSA and National Instruments★陷阱：把协调协会或自律组织当成直管机关

Marcus is preparing for his registration exam and reads that his dealing representative proficiency requirement is set out in National Instrument 31-103, developed through the Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA). Which statement accurately describes the legal status of the CSA and National Instruments?

Marcus 正在备考注册考试，读到他的交易代表准入考试要求写在由加拿大证券管理局（CSA）制定的《全国性文件 31-103》（NI 31-103）中。下列哪项准确描述了 CSA 与全国性文件（NI）的法律地位？

  1. ✓ 正确。加拿大证券管理局（Canadian Securities Administrators, CSA）是 13 家省和地区证监会的非正式全国性协会（informal national association），没有独立执法权；全国性文件（National Instrument, NI）是由各省和地区监管机构各自在本辖区以统一方式采纳的省级规则。Correct. The Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA) is an informal national association of the 13 provincial and territorial securities commissions with no independent enforcement authority. A National Instrument (NI) is a uniform rule drafted through the CSA that becomes legally binding only when adopted by each individual province and territory as a local rule.

  2. ✗ 错误。落入「把协调协会当成联邦机关」陷阱：证券监管属省级管辖权，加拿大没有联邦证监会或联邦证券法，CSA 不是联邦皇冠公司，NI 也不是联邦法律。Incorrect authority trap: Falls into mistaking a coordinating association for a federal agency. Securities regulation falls under provincial and territorial jurisdiction; Canada has no federal securities commission or federal securities act. The CSA is not a federal Crown corporation, and an NI is not a federal statute.

  3. ✗ 错误。CSA 不是自律监管组织（Self-Regulatory Organization, SRO），也不能直接给个人注册；此外 NI 是有法律约束力的规则，解释性与预期性的文件叫做全国政策（National Policy, NP）。Incorrect regulatory category: The CSA is not a self-regulatory organization (SRO) and does not directly register individuals. Furthermore, an NI is a legally binding rule, whereas explanatory documents setting out regulatory expectations are designated as National Policies (NPs).

  4. ✗ 错误。CSA 与联邦税务局（CRA）毫无隶属关系；只在部分而非全部辖区适用的文件叫做多边文件（Multilateral Instrument, MI），而 NI 覆盖全部辖区。Incorrect institutional linkage and instrument scope: The CSA has no organizational relationship with the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA). Instruments that apply in only some jurisdictions are Multilateral Instruments (MIs), whereas an NI applies across all 13 jurisdictions.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

During a team study session, a trainee claims that the Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA) fined an unlicensed representative last month under a federal securities act. Why is the trainee's statement incorrect?

在一次小组学习中，一名学员声称加拿大证券管理局（CSA）上个月依据一部联邦证券法对一名无牌代表进行了罚款。为什么该学员的说法是错误的？

  1. ✗ 错误。CIRO 既不依据联邦证券法执法，也完全不管辖奖学金计划经销商（SPD）及其交易代表。Incorrect governing body and scope: CIRO does not enforce federal securities laws, nor does it regulate scholarship plan dealers (SPDs) or their dealing representatives.

  2. ✗ 错误。CSA 是非正式协调协会，没有任何起诉或行政处罚权，证券监管也不归市政当局管辖。Incorrect enforcement powers: The CSA is an informal coordinating forum without statutory power to prosecute offences or impose administrative fines, and securities regulation is never delegated to municipal authorities.

  3. ✓ 正确。加拿大宪法将证券监管归入省级管辖权，没有联邦证券法；CSA 只负责协调与起草统一规则，真正拥有注册、调查和罚款执法权的永远是 13 家省或地区证监会中的具体某一家。Correct. Under the Canadian constitution, securities regulation is a matter of exclusive provincial jurisdiction, and there is no federal securities act. The CSA serves solely as a coordinating body to develop uniform policies and rules; direct authority to register individuals, conduct investigations, and levy administrative fines resides exclusively with each provincial or territorial securities commission.

  4. ✗ 错误。三个地区（育空、西北地区、努纳武特）与十个省一样各自设有独立的证券监管机构，CSA 在任何省或地区都没有直接停牌权。Incorrect territorial distinction: The three territories (Yukon, Northwest Territories, and Nunavut), like the ten provinces, each maintain independent securities regulators. The CSA has no direct power to suspend registrations in any province or territory.

  4. ✗ 错误。发出董事或高管禁任令（prohibition from acting as a director or officer）以及处以行政罚款（部分辖区可达 100 万加元或以上）均属于证监会的法定行政处分权力。Statutory administrative penalties: Imposing director and officer bans and levying administrative penalties (which can reach $1,000,000 or more in many jurisdictions) are statutory administrative sanctions.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Which statement correctly distinguishes administrative enforcement by a provincial securities commission from criminal or quasi-criminal prosecution in court?

下列哪项正确区分了省级证监会的行政执法与通过法院进行的刑事或准刑事起诉？

  1. ✗ 错误。监禁（imprisonment）只能由法院在定罪后判处，行政机关无权判处监禁；而停止交易令（cease-trade order）是证监会的行政工具。Imprisonment authority error: Prison sentences can only be imposed by the courts following a criminal or quasi-criminal conviction; administrative regulators cannot impose imprisonment. Conversely, cease-trade orders are administrative remedies.

  2. ✗ 错误。CSA 没有执法或起诉权，省级证监会既可作严厉行政处分（最高百万加元级罚款、停牌、禁任），也可就无牌交易、虚假陈述等罪名提起诉讼。Misallocated prosecuting authority: The CSA has no prosecution or enforcement powers. Provincial commissions possess statutory authority to impose severe administrative sanctions (suspensions, bans, penalties up to $1,000,000) and to initiate court prosecutions for quasi-criminal offences.

  3. ✓ 正确。不经法院的行政处分包括暂停/撤销注册、停止交易令、剥夺豁免、禁任董事高管及行政罚款；而虚假陈述、无牌交易、无招股书分销、非法内幕交易和市场操纵则可走法院起诉（prosecution），定罪后可判超 100 万加元罚金及数年监禁。Correct. Direct administrative sanctions imposed by the commission following an opportunity for a hearing include registration suspension or revocation, cease-trade orders, denial of exemptions, director and officer prohibitions, and administrative penalties. By contrast, serious statutory offences such as unregistered trading, illegal distribution without a prospectus, or misrepresentation are prosecuted in court, where convictions can result in fines exceeding $1,000,000 and imprisonment of several years.

  4. ✗ 错误。法院定罪需要严格证明犯罪构成要件，且各省证监会的行政听证各自独立进行，无需 13 省一致同意。Procedural misconceptions: Court prosecutions require the prosecution to prove every element of an offence beyond a reasonable doubt regardless of investor loss, and administrative hearings are conducted independently by each provincial commission without requiring unanimous approval from all 13 jurisdictions.

答案Answer：c

第 7 题Q7双重身份核实与营销时限Dual identity verification framework and marketing timelines★★★陷阱：只记反洗钱豁免而漏掉证券法强制身份核实

Liam is opening a Registered Education Savings Plan (RESP) for a new client and also reviewing his list of past clients to send commercial electronic messages (emails) and make telemarketing calls to individuals registered on the National Do Not Call List (DNCL). Which combination of rules regarding client identity verification and existing-client marketing windows is accurate?

Liam 正在为一位新客户开立注册教育储蓄计划（RESP），同时梳理老客户名单，准备发送商业电子信息（电邮）并向已登记在全国谢绝来电名单（National DNCL）上的客户拨打营销电话。关于客户身份核实以及既有客户营销豁免期，下列哪项规则组合是准确的？

  1. ✗ 错误。同时掉进两个经典陷阱：第一，虽然反洗钱法豁免注册计划账户的身份核实，但证券法 NI 31-103 s.13.2 仍强制要求核实每位客户身份；第二，把 CASL 的 2 年和 DNCL 的 18 个月完全记反了。Dual trap on identity and marketing timelines: First, although federal AML legislation exempts registered plan accounts, NI 31-103 s.13.2 independently requires registrants to verify each client's identity. Second, the 2-year CASL email window and 18-month DNCL telemarketing window are reversed.

  2. ✗ 错误。核实 RESP 客户身份的强制依据是证券法（NI 31-103 s.13.2）而非反洗钱法（PCMLTFA 对注册计划有豁免），且两个营销豁免期分别是 2 年和 18 个月，不是 12 个月。Misattributed authority and incorrect duration: The mandatory basis for verifying RESP subscriber identity is provincial securities law (NI 31-103 s.13.2), not federal AML law (which exempts registered plans). Furthermore, the existing-client exemption windows are two years and 18 months, not 12 months.

  3. ✓ 正确。完美避开两个高频陷阱：① 身份核实的双层结构——《犯罪所得（洗钱）及恐怖主义融资法》（PCMLTFA）虽豁免注册计划账户，但证券法 NI 31-103 s.13.2 强制要求注册人采取合理步骤确认每位客户身份；② 两个既存关系时长不同——加拿大反垃圾邮件立法（CASL）下基于购买行为的默示同意（implied consent）为 2 年内，而加拿大广播电视和电信委员会（CRTC）全国谢绝来电名单（National DNCL）的既有业务往来豁免为 18 个月内。Correct. Successfully navigates two frequent exam traps: (1) The dual-layer identity verification framework—while the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act (PCMLTFA) exempts registered plan accounts, NI 31-103 s.13.2 independently mandates that registrants take reasonable steps to verify every client's identity; (2) Contrasting marketing exemption periods—implied consent arising from a product purchase under Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) lasts up to two years, whereas the existing business relationship exemption under CRTC National DNCL telemarketing rules lasts up to 18 months.

  4. ✗ 错误。NI 31-103 s.13.2 的身份核实要求适用于每一位客户，不设 $10,000 门槛；CASL 要求每一封商业电子信息（CEM）都必须包含免费、醒目且立即生效的退订机制；电话营销周末允许时段为 10:00–18:00（周一至周五为 9:00–21:30），周日晚上 23:00 严禁拨打。Fabricated threshold and prohibited marketing hours: Identity verification under NI 31-103 s.13.2 applies to every client regardless of contribution amount, with no $10,000 threshold. Under CASL, every commercial electronic message must include a free, prominent unsubscribe mechanism. Under telemarketing rules, calling hours are 9:00 to 21:30 on weekdays and 10:00 to 18:00 on weekends; calling at 23:00 on a Sunday is strictly prohibited.

Which statement accurately describes the statutory penalties and offences under federal marketing and criminal legislation applicable to securities sales practices?
