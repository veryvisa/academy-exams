本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 披露文件属性辨析题：给你四项关于招股书（Prospectus）、计划摘要（Plan Summary）或持续披露文件的描述，问哪一项成立或不成立，核心考更新频率（每年续期而非按月）、交付义务（两部分必须交付，引用并入文件不随附印刷）与长度语言限制（摘要不超过 4 页且用平实语言）。Distinguishing disclosure document attributes: You are given four statements concerning the Prospectus, Plan Summary, or continuous disclosure documents and asked which statement is true or false. Key tested concepts include renewal frequency (renewed annually, not monthly), delivery obligations (both parts must be delivered; documents incorporated by reference are not printed and enclosed), and length and language constraints (Plan Summary must not exceed four pages and must use plain language).
  • 监管批文与平台职能题：围绕收据（Receipt）、CRA 批准及 SEDAR+ 设问，测试你能否区分「准许销售的合规门槛／免费公开备案库」与「政府背书推荐／合规监管机构」，专抓将政府批准当作销售卖点的违规话术。Regulatory approvals and platform functions: Questions test your understanding of prospectus receipts, CRA approval, and SEDAR+. They assess whether you can distinguish compliance thresholds for permitted distribution and free public filing databases from government endorsements, recommendations, or regulatory overseers, specifically catching non-compliant sales pitches that misrepresent regulatory receipt as an endorsement.
  • 财务报表与资金流向归类题：给出四张法定财报或净资产变动表（Statement of Changes in Net Assets）的具体科目，考谁是流入（认购人存款、收到的 CESG、计划转入）、谁是流出（EAP、退还费用、退回补助），以及 General Fund 的收益来源与用途。Financial statements and cash flow classification: Given the four statutory financial statements or specific line items in the Statement of Changes in Net Assets, questions test whether you can identify inflows (subscriber deposits, CESG received, plan transfers-in) versus outflows (EAPs, refunded fees, returned grants), as well as the revenue sources and purposes of the General Fund.
  • 销售费扣款节奏与退出后果计算／情境题：给出一组月供金额与总销售费（Sales Charge），按「先 100% 扣至付清一半、后 50% 扣至付清」规则算清账时长或计入本金的金额；或给定签约后天数（60 天内 vs 60 天后），考退费范围与政府补助额度的去向。Sales charge deduction schedules and cancellation consequences calculation/scenario questions: Given monthly contribution amounts and total sales charges, you calculate the time required to pay off the charges or the amount credited to principal under the rule of deducting 100% of contributions until half the sales charge is paid, followed by 50% until fully paid. Alternatively, given the elapsed days since signing (within 60 days vs. after 60 days), questions test the refund scope and the fate of government grant room.
  • 费率区间与主体归属题：考各类管理费、投资管理费、保管费的 RDRE 通用百分比区间，以及供款完成保险（Completion Insurance）保障的对象（保认购人身故或失能，而非受益人学业或性命）与从业代表能否直接向客户收费。Fee ranges and responsible entities: Questions test standard RDRE percentage ranges for administration fees, investment management fees, and custodial fees, as well as the covered party under completion insurance (insuring the subscriber against death or disability, not the beneficiary's academic completion or life) and the strict rule that dealing representatives never charge fees directly to clients.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 政府背书陷阱Government Endorsement Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到证监会发出 receipt 或 CRA 批准注册，就顺着日常思维误以为代表监管机构认可该计划的投资价值或安全性。Candidates see that a securities commission issued a receipt or CRA approved registration and mistakenly assume this means regulators endorse the investment merit, safety, or quality of the plan.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干或选项出现 endorse、recommend、guarantee、prudent investment 或 government approved 等字眼。Look for words like endorse, recommend, guarantee, prudent investment, or government approved in the question stem or options.

⚠ EAP 资金流向反转EAP Cash Flow Direction Reversal

为什么会错：Why people miss it: EAP 对学生来说是「收到的钱」，考生容易站在学生视角把它当成进账，忘了财务报表是站在奖学金计划（基金）的视角记账。Candidates view EAP payments from the student perspective as money received and mistake them for inflows, forgetting that financial statements are prepared from the perspective of the scholarship plan itself.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题目问 Statement of Changes in Net Assets（净资产变动表）中的 inflows（流入）或 outflows（流出），且选项里有 EAP。Questions asking about inflows or outflows in the Statement of Changes in Net Assets where EAPs appear as an option.

⚠ 销售费平摊错觉Sales Charge Equal Deduction Illusion

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉以为销售费是在整个供款期内按月均匀扣除，忽略了集团计划「先 100% 扣满前一半，再按供款 50% 扣完后一半」的前置扣费规则。Candidates intuitively assume sales charges are deducted evenly across the entire contribution schedule, overlooking the front-end schedule of group plans where 100% of early contributions pay the first half, followed by 50% of contributions for the second half.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 计算题给出月供金额与总 sales charge，问前几个月有多少钱进入本金（principal）或需要多少个月付清销售费。Calculation questions providing monthly contributions and total sales charges, asking how much principal is credited in early months or how many months are needed to pay off sales charges.

⚠ 保险保障对象错位Completion Insurance Coverage Misconception

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到 Completion Insurance（供款完成保险）里的 completion，误以为是保「孩子完成大学学业」或保受益人的人身安全。Seeing completion in Completion Insurance, candidates wrongly assume it guarantees the child completes post-secondary education or covers the life and safety of the beneficiary.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 选项把保障触发条件写成 beneficiary dies、fails to complete post-secondary education 或 graduates。Options listing trigger conditions such as beneficiary dies, fails to complete post-secondary education, or graduates.

⚠ 从业代表私收费与财报月寄陷阱Direct Representative Fee and Monthly Delivery Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把共同基金或顾问收费模式套进奖学金计划，误以为代表直接向客户收年费，或把年度披露文件当成每月寄送的月结单。Applying mutual fund or financial advisor fee practices to scholarship plans, mistakenly believing dealing representatives bill clients directly or that annual disclosure documents are updated and mailed monthly.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 选项出现 dealing representative charges the client directly，或 prospectus / Statement of Net Assets is updated / mailed monthly。Options stating dealing representative charges the client directly or that the prospectus or Statement of Net Assets is updated or mailed monthly.

It is renewed annually, and both parts must be delivered to the subscriber.
招股书收据的法律性质Legal Nature of Prospectus ReceiptReceipt for Prospectus未取得某省证监会发出的 receipt 不得在该省向公众分销，但 receipt 与 CRA 批准仅代表满足特定监管要求，绝不构成对计划价值或管理能力的背书或推荐。Distribution to the public in a province is prohibited without a receipt issued by that provincial securities commission. 持续披露与以引用方式并入Continuous Disclosure and Incorporated by ReferenceContinuous Disclosure & Incorporated by Reference计划备案招股书后成为 reporting issuer，其经审计年度财报、MRFP 与 IRC 年报以引用方式并入招股书，放在 SEDAR+ 供免费查阅，不随招股书印刷交付。After filing a prospectus, a plan becomes a reporting issuer whose audited annual financial statements, MRFP, and IRC annual report are incorporated by reference into the prospectus and made available for free inspection on SEDAR+, rather than printed and delivered with the prospectus.
四张财务报表与净资产变动流向Four Statutory Financial Statements and Net Asset Cash FlowsStatement of Changes in Net Assets净资产表是时点资产负债表；净资产变动表中认购人存款、收到的 CESG 与转入款属于流入（inflows），而支付的 EAP、退还费用与退回政府的补助属于流出（outflows）。The Statement of Net Assets is a point-in-time balance sheet. In the Statement of Changes in Net Assets, subscriber deposits, CESG received, and plan transfers-in represent inflows, whereas EAPs paid, refunded fees, and grants returned to the government represent outflows.
一般基金的收益来源与用途Revenue Sources and Purposes of the General FundGeneral Fund某个 pool 到期日之后计划赚到的全部投资收益记入 General Fund，可用于抵销部分计划费用并补充 EAP 水平。All investment income earned by the plan after the maturity date of a specific beneficiary group pool is credited to the General Fund, which may be used to offset certain plan expenses and supplement EAP payout levels.
三大国家文书与独立审查委员会Three Core National Instruments and the Independent Review CommitteeNI 41-101, NI 81-106 & NI 81-107 (IRC)NI 41-101（含 Form 41-101F3）管招股书与 Plan Summary，NI 81-106 管持续披露，NI 81-107 要求设立至少 3 名独立成员的 IRC 审查利益冲突并至少每年向认购人报告。NI 41-101 (including Form 41-101F3) governs the prospectus and Plan Summary; NI 81-106 governs continuous disclosure; and NI 81-107 requires an Independent Review Committee (IRC) of at least three independent members to review conflict of interest matters and report at least annually to subscribers.
销售费前置扣除节奏Front-End Sales Charge Deduction ScheduleSales Charge Deduction Schedule按单位计价时，多数基金会先将认购人最初的供款 100% 用于支付销售费直至付清一半，其后每笔供款的 50% 继续用于支付销售费直至全部付清。When priced per unit, most scholarship plans deduct 100% of a subscriber's initial contributions toward the sales charge until half of it is paid, after which 50% of each subsequent contribution is deducted until the sales charge is fully paid.
管理与运营费用区间Administration and Operating Fee RangesAdministration, Investment Management & Custodial Fees考试口径下，管理费在集团计划通常约 0.5%／年、个人与家庭计划为 0.5%–1.5%／年，投资管理费通常为 0.10%–0.33%／年，保管／受托费不足资产的 1%／年，从业代表从不直接向客户收管理费。For exam purposes, the administration fee is typically around 0.5% per year in group plans and 0.5% to 1.5% per year in individual and family plans; the investment management fee is typically 0.10% to 0.33% per year; custodial and trustee fees are usually less than 1% of assets per year; and dealing representatives never collect fees directly from clients.
60 天无条件全额退出权60-Day Unconditional Full Withdrawal Right60-Day Withdrawal Right认购人在签约后 60 天内退出可全额拿回所有款项（含已付销售费与保险费）；60 天后取消仅退净供款，投资收益全部损失、政府补助退回且除 CLB 外的补助额度永久损失。A subscriber who withdraws within 60 days of signing receives a 100% refund of all funds paid, including sales charges and insurance premiums. Cancelling after 60 days refunds only net contributions; all investment earnings are forfeited, government grants are returned, and grant contribution room other than the CLB is permanently lost.
供款完成保险的保障范围Scope of Completion Insurance CoverageCompletion Insurance供款完成保险保障的是认购人（subscriber）身故或失能时代其完成计划下的剩余供款，60 天内退出保费全退，60 天后终止则已缴保费一并损失。Completion insurance covers the subscriber in the event of death or disability by making the remaining scheduled contributions on their behalf. If the plan is cancelled within 60 days, premiums are refunded in full; if terminated after 60 days, all paid premiums are forfeited.

第 1 题Q1两部分招股书的交付、更新与摘要规则Delivery, renewal, and summary rules for the two-part prospectus★陷阱：从业代表私收费与财报月寄陷阱

Which of the following statements regarding a scholarship plan prospectus and its delivery to subscribers is correct?

关于奖学金计划招股书及其向认购人的交付，以下哪项陈述是正确的？

  1. ✗ 更新频率与交付范围均错：招股书（prospectus）是每年续期（renewed annually），而非按月更新；且 Plan Summary 与 Detailed Plan Disclosure 两部分都必须交给认购人，不能只给摘要。Incorrect renewal frequency and delivery scope: The prospectus is renewed annually rather than updated monthly. Furthermore, both the Plan Summary and the Detailed Plan Disclosure must be delivered to the subscriber; handing out only the summary is not permitted.

  2. ✓ 正确。招股书强制分为两部分：不超过 4 页、用平实语言（plain language）编写的 Plan Summary（计划摘要），以及数十页法律语言的 Detailed Plan Disclosure（详细计划披露）；招股书每年续期，销售时两部分都必须交付给认购人。Correct. The prospectus is mandatory two-part disclosure: a plain-language Plan Summary of no more than four pages, and a Detailed Plan Disclosure spanning dozens of pages in legal language. The prospectus is renewed annually, and both parts must be delivered to every subscriber.

  3. ✗ 混淆「直接交付」与「引用并入」：年度审计财报、MRFP 与 IRC 年报是以引用方式并入招股书（incorporated by reference），放在 SEDAR+ 或官网供查阅，并不随招股书印刷交给认购人。Confuses direct delivery with incorporation by reference: Audited annual financial statements, Management Reports of Fund Performance (MRFPs), and IRC annual reports are incorporated by reference into the prospectus and made available on SEDAR+ or the official website; they are not printed and handed to subscribers.

  4. ✗ 张冠李戴陷阱：每个在售计划都有各自独立的 Plan Summary，代表必须交付认购人所投资的那一个具体计划的 Plan Summary，不能混用。Plan substitution trap: Each plan offered has its own distinct Plan Summary. The representative must deliver the specific Plan Summary corresponding to the exact plan the subscriber is purchasing; using a generic or mismatched version is not allowed.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A dealing representative is preparing the enrollment package for a client who is purchasing units in one of three scholarship plans managed by the same foundation. Which document delivery rule applies?

一位从业代表正在为客户准备开户文件包，该客户购买的是同一家基金会管理的三款奖学金计划之一。以下哪项文件交付规则适用？

  1. ✓ 正确。认购人必须同时收到 Detailed Plan Disclosure 和他所认购的那一个具体计划的 Plan Summary（不超过 4 页，平实语言），两份缺一不可且不能给错计划版本。Correct. The subscriber must receive both the Detailed Plan Disclosure and the specific Plan Summary (four pages or fewer, written in plain language) for the plan being purchased. Both parts are mandatory, and delivering the wrong plan version is a violation.

  2. ✗ 交付义务错误：招股书的两部分（Plan Summary 与 Detailed Plan Disclosure）必须实际交付给每一位认购人；只有财报、MRFP 和 IRC 年报才是以引用方式并入（incorporated by reference）。Incorrect delivery obligation: Both parts of the prospectus (the Plan Summary and the Detailed Plan Disclosure) must be physically delivered to every subscriber. Only financial statements, MRFPs, and IRC annual reports are incorporated by reference.

  3. ✗ 更新与交付时间错误：两部分招股书都在开户时交付，且招股书按年续期，不存在每 30 天按月更新寄送的规则。Incorrect update frequency and delivery timing: Both parts of the prospectus are delivered at the time of enrollment. The prospectus is renewed annually; there is no requirement or practice to update and mail the Detailed Plan Disclosure every 30 days.

  4. ✗ 强制披露不可豁免：NI 41-101 强制要求交付 Plan Summary 且代表应在签字前带客户走一遍摘要，客户签字也不能豁免交付义务。Mandatory disclosure cannot be waived: National Instrument 41-101 legally mandates delivery of the Plan Summary, and the representative must review it with the client prior to signing. A client's written waiver cannot exempt the representative from this obligation.

第 2 题Q2招股书收据与CRA批准的真实含义True meaning of prospectus receipts and CRA approval★陷阱：政府背书陷阱

During a sales meeting, a prospective subscriber asks what it means that the provincial securities commission has issued a receipt for the scholarship plan's prospectus and the CRA has approved the plan. How must the dealing representative respond?

在销售面谈中，一位准认购人询问：省证监会对奖学金计划招股书发出收据（receipt）且加拿大税务局（CRA）已批准该计划，这意味着什么？从业代表必须如何回答？

  1. ✗ 政府背书陷阱：证监会发出 receipt 和 CRA 批准绝不代表核实了披露内容的准确性，也不代表推荐该计划为审慎投资。声称监管机构对证券发表过意见属于违法行为。Government endorsement trap: A securities commission receipt and CRA approval do not verify the accuracy of disclosure contents or projections, nor do they recommend the plan as a prudent investment. Stating that regulators have passed judgment on the merits of a security is an offense.

  2. ✗ 政府担保与 CDIC 陷阱：奖学金计划不受任何政府担保，且奖学金计划机构不是 CDIC（加拿大存款保险公司）成员，不得让客户误以为受 CDIC 保护。Government guarantee and CDIC trap: Scholarship plans carry no government guarantee, and scholarship plan dealers are not members of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation (CDIC). A representative must never lead clients to believe their deposits are CDIC-insured.

  3. ✓ 正确。证监会发出 receipt（收据）是该省准许公开发售的前提，CRA 批准则涉及 RESP 注册资格；两者都只说明满足了特定监管要求，绝不构成对计划投资价值、披露准确性或管理层能力的背书、推荐或担保。Correct. A prospectus receipt issued by a provincial securities commission is a prerequisite for public distribution in that province, and CRA approval confirms registered status as an RESP. Both simply establish regulatory compliance and never constitute an endorsement, recommendation, or guarantee.

  4. ✗ 职能混淆陷阱：招股书由计划管理人编写而非证监会撰写，且 SEDAR+ 只是一个免费公开的备案文件检索系统，不是监管交易的机构。Role confusion trap: The prospectus is authored and filed by the plan manager, not drafted by the securities commission. Furthermore, SEDAR+ is merely a public electronic filing and retrieval system, not a regulatory body supervising day-to-day trades.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Which statement regarding SEDAR+ and regulatory oversight of scholarship plans is true?

关于 SEDAR+ 与奖学金计划的监管，以下哪项陈述是正确的？

  1. ✗ 平台属性与背书双重错误：SEDAR+ 是文件备案与检索系统而非监管机构；发出 receipt 的是各省证监会，且 receipt 也不是安全投资背书。Dual error regarding platform role and endorsement: SEDAR+ is an electronic filing and retrieval system, not a regulatory agency. Prospectus receipts are issued by provincial securities commissions, and receipts never serve as an endorsement of safety.

The refund of sales charges operates via two mutually exclusive pathways: Pathway 1 requires remaining in the group plan to maturity and collecting all four EAPs, refunding 50% in total (12.5% alongside each EAP; missed EAPs forfeit that portion, and refunds are non-taxable and exclude income earned on the sales charges). Pathway 2 applies upon transferring to an individual or family plan, refunding 0% to 25% based on years in the group plan (0% for 0-3 years, up to 25% for 15 or more years).

  3. ✗ 叠加陷阱：全程留在集团计划拿 50% 与中途转入个人／家庭计划按年限拿 0–25% 是两条互斥的路径，绝不能叠加成 75%。Double-counting trap: Receiving the 50% maturity refund by staying in the group plan and receiving 0% to 25% based on years upon transferring to an individual or family plan are mutually exclusive alternatives; they cannot be stacked to total 75%.

  4. ✗ EAP 挂钩规则错误：销售费退还笔数不能超过 EAP 笔数（每笔 EAP 退 12.5%）。如果孩子只读两年、四笔 EAP 只拿到两笔，则销售费退还也只能拿到两笔，即 25%（12.5% × 2），另外两笔永久失去。Incorrect EAP linkage rule: The number of sales charge refund installments cannot exceed the number of EAPs collected (12.5% per EAP). If the student completes only a two-year program and qualifies for two of the four EAPs, the subscriber receives only two refund installments totaling 25% (12.5% x 2), permanently forfeiting the remainder.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A subscriber stays in the group scholarship plan until maturity and makes all scheduled contributions, but her child enrolls in a two-year program and collects only two of the four available EAPs. How much of the sales charge is refunded, and how is the refund taxed?

一位认购人留在集团奖学金计划直至到期并交齐了全部应缴供款，但她的孩子就读于两年制项目，四笔可用 EAP 中只领到了两笔。销售费能退还多少？该笔退还款如何征税？

  1. ✗ 比例与税务均错：退还笔数不能超过实际领取的 EAP 笔数，只领两笔 EAP 只能退 25%（而非 50%）；且销售费退还对认购人与受益人都不构成应税收入。Incorrect percentage and tax treatment: The number of refund installments cannot exceed the number of EAPs actually collected, so collecting two EAPs yields only a 25% refund (not 50%). Furthermore, sales charge refunds do not constitute taxable income to either subscriber or beneficiary.

  2. ✗ 全有或全无错觉：并非少拿一笔 EAP 就连前两笔也归零——每领一笔 EAP 就退还该单位销售费的 12.5%，领两笔可退 25%；只有完全不符合 EAP 资格（0 笔 EAP）时才一分钱拿不到。All-or-nothing misconception: Failing to collect all four EAPs does not wipe out the refund for the installments already qualified. Each qualifying EAP triggers a 12.5% sales charge refund, so two EAPs qualify for 25%. A complete forfeiture occurs only if zero EAPs are collected.

  3. ✓ 正确。每笔 EAP 退还该单位已付销售费的 12.5%，退还笔数不能超过 EAP 笔数；四笔 EAP 只拿两笔时，销售费退还也只拿两笔，共 25%（12.5% × 2），且退还款对认购人与受益人都不构成应税收入（not taxable income）。Correct. Each EAP payout triggers a refund of 12.5% of the sales charges paid per unit, and the total refund installments cannot exceed the number of EAPs collected. Collecting two out of four EAPs yields two refund payments totaling 25% (12.5% x 2). In addition, the refund is not taxable income to either the subscriber or the beneficiary.

  4. ✗ 收益归属与税务错误：隔离账户（segregated account）每两年估值由基金会兜底缺口，但退还给客户的金额不含销售费所产生的收益（does not include income earned on the sales charges），且退还款本身不征税。Incorrect interest entitlement and taxation: Although the segregated account is actuarially valued biennially and backstopped by the foundation, the refund paid to the subscriber excludes any income earned on the sales charges. Moreover, the refund itself is non-taxable.
