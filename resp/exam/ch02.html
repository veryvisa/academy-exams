本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 概念辨析题：专门区分了解你的客户（Know Your Client, KYC）与适合性评估（Suitability Assessment），测试考生是否明白收集记录客户事实与用事实判断推荐是否合适是两项独立义务。Concept distinction questions: Specifically differentiating Know Your Client (KYC) from Suitability Assessment, testing whether candidates understand that collecting and recording client facts versus using those facts to judge whether a recommendation is suitable represent two distinct regulatory obligations.
  • 情境合规题：给出客户拒绝提供信息、要求返还部分佣金、或打算从其他机构转移计划等日常展业场景，测试考生能否按法定步骤拒绝违规要求并履行披露程序。Scenario-based compliance questions: Presenting day-to-day practice scenarios such as a client refusing to provide information, requesting a partial commission rebate, or intending to transfer a plan from another dealer, testing whether candidates can follow statutory procedures to reject non-compliant requests and execute required disclosures.
  • 时间与数字边界题：集中考查注册信息变更（10天）、招股书无成本退出期（60天）、投诉处理（5个营业日/90天/180天）、业绩数据有效期（180天）以及电话营销（18个月）与反垃圾邮件法（2年）的豁免窗口。Timeline and numerical boundary questions: Focusing on reporting registration information changes (10 days), the prospectus unconditional withdrawal period (60 days), complaint-handling timelines (5 business days / 90 days / 180 days), the maximum performance data lag (180 days), and the exemption windows under telemarketing rules (18 months) versus anti-spam legislation (2 years).
  • 定义排除与分类题：考查销售沟通（Sales Communication）的广义覆盖范围（含口头招揽）与法定除外文件（招股书、账户结单、交易确认），以及重大利益冲突的披露、管理与回避原则。Definition exclusion and classification questions: Testing the broad scope of sales communications (including oral solicitations) versus statutory exclusions (prospectus, account statement, trade confirmation), as well as the principles of disclosure, management, and avoidance for material conflicts of interest.
  • 监管归属与跨辖区题：测试从业代表在每个展业辖区注册的硬性要求、奖学金计划经销商受各省证券监管机构直管而非自律组织管辖的现行规则，以及魁北克省争议解决机构（AMF vs OBSI）的特殊性。Regulatory authority and jurisdictional questions: Testing the mandatory requirement for dealing representatives to be registered in every operating jurisdiction, the current rule that scholarship plan dealers are directly regulated by provincial securities commissions rather than self-regulatory organizations (CIRO), and the specific dispute resolution body for Quebec residents (AMF vs. OBSI).

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ KYC与适合性概念偷换KYC Versus Suitability Concept Substitution

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生容易把整个开户提问和推荐过程统称为KYC，忽略了收集事实与作出推荐判断在法规上分属两项不同义务。Candidates often loosely refer to the entire account opening and recommendation process as KYC, overlooking that collecting facts and judging recommendations represent two distinct legal obligations.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「只推荐符合客户需求、目标与风险承受度的投资」或「基于收集的信息评估投资行动是否合适」等描述判断动作的词句。Look for phrasing describing evaluative judgment, such as 'recommend only those investments that match client needs, objectives, and risk tolerance' or 'assessing whether an investment action is appropriate based on collected information.'

⚠ 客户自愿豁免与人情诱因Client Voluntary Waiver and Personal Enticements

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉认为客户签字声明自担风险即可跳过财务信息收集，或认为自己少赚一点佣金返还给客户属于让利而非违规。Candidates intuitively assume that a signed waiver accepting personal risk allows skipping financial information collection, or believe that rebating part of their commission to a client is merely a commercial concession rather than a regulatory violation.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现客户以隐私为由拒绝提供收入/SIN、要求签署免责声明，或代表提议代付定金、分担销售费、赠送礼品促成签约。The stem mentions a client refusing to provide income or SIN citing privacy, requesting a written waiver, or a representative offering to pay deposits, split sales charges, or provide gifts to close an enrollment.

⚠ 口头沟通不受广告规则约束Oral Communications Exempt from Advertising Rules

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 日常习惯把合规预批与禁令仅同印刷海报或群发广告挂钩，误以为面谈或电话里随口承诺回报率不算销售沟通。Candidates routinely associate compliance pre-approval and prohibitions only with printed flyers or mass advertising, mistakenly assuming that casual verbal promises of returns during meetings or phone calls do not count as sales communications.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述代表在与潜在客户面谈或电话交流时口头提及过往回报、未来收益保证或竞品对比。The stem describes a representative verbally quoting past returns, guaranteeing future earnings, or comparing competitor plans during an in-person meeting or phone call with a prospect.

⚠ DNCL与CASL时间窗口互换National DNCL and CASL Time Window Confusion

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 电话营销的既有业务关系豁免（18个月）与反垃圾邮件法的默示同意窗口（2年且限既有产品沟通）数字相近，极易张冠李戴或忽略产品范围限制。The telemarketing existing business relationship exemption (18 months) and the anti-spam legislation implied consent window (2 years and restricted to existing product communications) involve similar numbers, making them easy to reverse or miss the product-scope restriction.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现「老客户」「过去某段时间买过产品」以及「致电」或「发送商业电子讯息（CEM）推销新计划」。The stem mentions former clients who purchased a product at some point in the past alongside calling them or sending commercial electronic messages (CEMs) to solicit a new plan.

⚠ 集团计划跨机构转移的理由误判Misinterpreting Rationale Against Inter-Dealer Group Plan Transfers

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常以为道德守则不鼓励从其他会员转入业务是为了维护同业关系或减少行政文书工作。Candidates often assume the Code of Ethics discourages transferring business from other members merely to maintain industry relationships or reduce administrative paperwork.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干询问为何《道德守则》（Code of Ethics）不鼓励客户将奖学金计划从一个会员转移到另一个会员，或接收方代表应如何处理。The stem asks why the Code of Ethics discourages clients from transferring a scholarship plan from one member to another, or how a receiving representative must proceed.

Dealers must send an initial written response within 5 business days and provide a substantive response within 90 days. Dissatisfied clients may escalate their complaint within 180 days to the Ombudsman for Banking Services and Investments (OBSI, or the AMF for Quebec residents).

第 1 题Q1区分KYC与适合性评估两项独立义务Distinguishing KYC and suitability assessment as two separate legal obligations★陷阱：KYC与适合性概念偷换

Which regulatory obligation specifically requires a dealing representative to recommend only those investment actions that match a client's needs, objectives, risk profile, and ability to afford ongoing contributions?

哪一项监管义务专门要求从业代表只推荐符合客户需求、目标、风险画像以及持续供款负担能力的投资行动？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是「KYC与适合性概念偷换」陷阱。了解你的客户（Know Your Client, KYC）是收集与记录客户个人情况、财务情况、投资知识、风险画像和投资期限等事实，而用这些事实判断推荐是否合适属于另一项独立义务。Incorrect. This is the 'KYC Versus Suitability Concept Substitution' trap. Know Your Client (KYC) involves collecting and documenting facts regarding the client's personal circumstances, financial circumstances, investment knowledge, risk profile, and investment time horizon. Using those facts to judge whether a recommendation is appropriate is an entirely separate legal obligation.

  2. ✗ 错误。适任标准（Fit and Proper）是监管机构与保荐经销商在注册阶段评估代表本人是否具备专业能力（Proficiency）、诚信（Integrity）和偿付能力（Solvency）的标准，而非针对客户交易的推荐评估义务。Incorrect. The Fit and Proper standard is evaluated by regulators and sponsoring dealers during registration to assess whether the representative possesses proficiency, integrity, and solvency; it does not govern evaluating specific investment recommendations for clients.

  3. ✓ 正确。适合性评估（Suitability Assessment）要求代表在合理基础上判定推荐给客户的每一项投资行动对他都合适，评估必须考虑客户 KYC、经销商了解你的产品（KYP）、投资成本影响、合理范围的投资选项以及奖学金计划的可负担性（Affordability）证据。Correct. The Suitability Assessment obligation requires the representative to determine on a reasonable basis that every recommended investment action is suitable for the client. The evaluation must consider client KYC, dealer KYP, the impact of investment costs, a reasonable range of investment options, and documented evidence of affordability for scholarship plans.

  4. ✗ 错误。关系披露（Relationship Disclosure）是在关系之初向客户交付并解释关于其与经销商关系的重要信息（如自有产品限制、收费、争议解决等），属于信息披露义务而非推荐判断义务。Incorrect. Relationship Disclosure requires delivering and explaining important information regarding the client's relationship with the dealer at the outset (such as proprietary product restrictions, fees, dispute resolution, etc.); it is an informational disclosure duty rather than an obligation to evaluate recommendations.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

During a compliance audit, a supervisor notes that a dealing representative accurately recorded a subscriber's income, net worth, investment knowledge, risk profile, and time horizon on the application form, but recommended a contribution schedule that consumes most of the family's disposable income. Which specific obligation did the representative fail to fulfill properly?

在合规审计中，主管发现从业代表在申请表上准确记录了认购人的收入、净资产、投资知识、风险画像和投资期限，但却推荐了一个占用该家庭大部分可支配收入的供款计划。代表未能妥善履行哪一项具体义务？

  1. ✓ 正确。代表已完成了收集与记录事实的了解你的客户（KYC）步骤，但在用事实做判断的适合性评估（Suitability Assessment）及奖学金计划特有的可负担性（Affordability）评估上失职。Correct. The representative properly completed the Know Your Client (KYC) step of collecting and documenting facts, but failed in the Suitability Assessment step of using those facts to make an appropriate recommendation, specifically violating the duty to ensure and document client affordability.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是「KYC与适合性概念偷换」陷阱。KYC 负责收集与记录五维度事实，题干明确指出代表已准确记录这些事实，出问题的是后续用事实做判断的适合性评估环节。Incorrect. This is the 'KYC Versus Suitability Concept Substitution' trap. KYC is responsible for collecting and recording facts across five dimensions. The stem explicitly notes that the representative accurately recorded these facts; the violation occurred during the subsequent suitability assessment stage where those facts must be evaluated.

  3. ✗ 错误。可信联系人（Trusted Contact Person, TCP）仅在怀疑客户遭受财务剥削或心智能力下降等特定情形下依书面同意联系，不用于常规开户的可负担性审批。Incorrect. A Trusted Contact Person (TCP) may only be contacted with written consent under specific circumstances, such as suspected financial exploitation or diminished mental capacity; the TCP is never contacted for standard affordability reviews during account opening.

  4. ✗ 错误。了解你的产品（Know Your Product, KYP）要求理解产品结构与成本并只销售已批准产品；本题症结在于未结合客户财务状况评估可负担性，属于适合性评估（Suitability Assessment）缺失。Incorrect. Know Your Product (KYP) requires understanding product structures and costs and offering only dealer-approved products. The core failure here was not evaluating affordability in light of the client's financial circumstances, which constitutes a failure of Suitability Assessment.

第 2 题Q2KYC不可豁免与拒绝提供信息的处理Non-waivable nature of KYC and handling clients who refuse to disclose information★★陷阱：客户自愿豁免与人情诱因

A prospective subscriber wants to open a scholarship plan but refuses to disclose her household income and net worth, offering instead to sign a written waiver releasing the dealer from liability. How must the dealing representative respond?

一位潜在认购人想开立奖学金计划，但拒绝披露家庭收入和净资产，并提出签署一份书面豁免书以免除经销商的责任。从业代表必须如何回应？

  1. ✓ 正确。了解你的客户（KYC）是代表与经销商的法定义务，法律并未赋予客户放弃（Waive）这项义务的权利；代表须说明三点（法定义务、正确建议与确认供款资格的前提、证券法与隐私法下的保密义务），若客户仍拒绝，则不能为其开户。注：本题按考试口径作答；2025-12 发布的联合通报 31-368 进一步强化了 KYC 实务标准，但不改变此考试结论。Correct. Know Your Client (KYC) is a statutory obligation of both representative and dealer, and securities law gives clients no right to waive this requirement. The representative must explain three points (it is a statutory duty; it is a prerequisite for proper advice and verifying contribution eligibility; and information is kept strictly confidential under securities and privacy laws). If the client still refuses, the representative cannot open the account. Note: This question reflects exam specifications; Joint Notice 31-368 issued in December 2025 further reinforced KYC practice standards but does not alter this exam conclusion.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是「客户自愿豁免与人情诱因」陷阱。法律不赋予客户放弃 KYC 规则的权利，任何书面豁免书均无效，主管也不能批准缺失核心 KYC 与可负担性信息的开户申请。Incorrect. This is the 'Client Voluntary Waiver and Personal Enticements' trap. Furthermore, performance ratings or rankings cannot be quoted unless prepared by a third party, and credit ratings are prohibited unless current and issued by an authorized rating organization.

  4. ✗ 错误。业绩数据标准明确要求：与竞品比较必须使用相同时间段（Same Time Period）的数据并提供全部相同的比较信息，且必须事先取得对方公司许可才能在材料中列出其名称。Incorrect. Performance presentation standards explicitly require comparisons against competing plans to use the exact same time period and provide identical comparative data. Furthermore, written permission must be obtained from the competitor before citing its corporate name.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A dealing representative wants to order new business cards and stationery to promote his scholarship plan practice. Which of the following is required under the standards for business cards and stationery?

一位从业代表想印制新的名片和信笺来推广其奖学金计划业务。根据名片与信笺的合规标准，下列哪一项是必须遵守的要求？

  1. ✗ 错误。未经事先批准，代表不得在销售沟通、名片或信笺中使用任何其他商号（Trade Name）；60 天联系方式有效期是《加拿大反垃圾邮件法》（CASL）对商业电子讯息的要求。Incorrect. A representative may not use any other trade name in sales communications, business cards, or stationery without prior dealer approval. The 60-day contact info requirement is a rule under CASL for commercial electronic messages.

  2. ✗ 错误。名片与信笺六条标准明确规定，其使用仅限代表经销商的业务往来，不得混用于其他无关业务。Incorrect. The six standards for business cards and stationery clearly mandate that they be used solely for business conducted on behalf of the dealer, and never mingled with unrelated side businesses.

  3. ✗ 错误。代表不得就资质或头衔误导投资者，只能使用经经销商授权且公平反映其注册状态与职能的头衔。Incorrect. Representatives must not mislead investors regarding their qualifications or titles; they may use only titles authorized by the dealer that fairly reflect their registration category and function.

  4. ✓ 正确。名片与信笺六条标准要求：按经销商政策制作使用、仅限代表经销商的业务往来、不得就资质或头衔误导投资者、须披露经销商完整法定名称（Full Legal Name）及你的姓名、职衔、营业地址、只能用经销商授权且公平反映注册状态与职能的头衔、未经批准不得使用其他商号。Correct. The six standards for business cards and stationery require: producing and using them in accordance with dealer policies, using them solely for dealer business, never misleading investors regarding qualifications or titles, disclosing the dealer's full legal name along with the representative's name, authorized title, and business address, using only authorized titles fairly reflecting registration status, and refraining from using any other trade name without approval.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

第 7 题Q7DNCL电话规则与CASL反垃圾邮件法Telemarketing rules under the National DNCL versus electronic messaging under CASL★★★陷阱：DNCL与CASL时间窗口互换

How does the existing client exemption under the National Do Not Call List (National DNCL) telemarketing rules differ from implied consent for an existing business relationship under Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL)?

《全国拒接来电名单》（National DNCL）电话营销规则下的既有客户豁免，与《加拿大反垃圾邮件法》（CASL）下既有业务关系的默示同意有何不同？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是典型的「DNCL与CASL时间窗口互换」陷阱。把 National DNCL 的 18 个月与 CASL 的 2 年完全颠倒了，而且 CASL 下对现有客户的默示同意按考试口径仅限沟通既有产品或服务，不能用于招揽新产品。Incorrect. This is a classic 'National DNCL and CASL Time Window Confusion' trap. It reverses the 18-month DNCL exemption with the 2-year CASL window. Moreover, under CASL exam specifications, implied consent with existing clients is strictly limited to communicating about existing products or services and cannot be used to solicit new products.

  2. ✗ 错误。National DNCL 许可致电时段为周一至周五 9:00–21:30、周六与周日 10:00–18:00（按潜在客户当地时间）；CASL 罚则上限为个人最高 $1,000,000、企业最高 $10,000,000。Incorrect. Permitted calling hours under National DNCL rules are 9:00 to 21:30 Monday through Friday and 10:00 to 18:00 Saturday and Sunday (local prospect time). Maximum penalties under CASL are up to $1,000,000 for individuals and up to $10,000,000 for corporations.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据电话营销规则，哪怕号码名列《全国拒接来电名单》（National DNCL），若是过去 18 个月内有过业务往来的消费者（现有客户），仍可致电；而根据《加拿大反垃圾邮件法》（CASL），既有业务关系的默示同意（Implied Consent）要求客户在发出商业电子讯息（CEM）前 2 年内购买过产品或服务，且考试口径强调前提是你沟通的是既有产品或服务，而不是在招揽新的产品或服务。Correct. Under telemarketing rules, even if a telephone number is registered on the National DNCL, a representative may call a consumer with whom they had business dealings within the past 18 months (an existing client). Under CASL, implied consent based on an existing business relationship requires the recipient to have purchased a product or service within the past 2 years prior to sending the CEM, and exam specifications emphasize this consent applies only when communicating about existing products or services, not soliciting new ones.

  4. ✗ 错误。考试口径在 CASL 规则中明文规定：每封商业电子讯息都要有退订方法，必须立即执行每一项退订请求（请求一经发出即时生效），且不得再发一封邮件确认退订已处理。Incorrect. Exam specifications explicitly state that under CASL rules, every commercial electronic message must include an unsubscribe mechanism, all unsubscribe requests must be processed immediately upon receipt, and sending a follow-up email confirming that an unsubscribe request has been completed is strictly prohibited.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A dealing representative is planning a telemarketing campaign to contact prospective subscribers who have no prior relationship with the dealer, and also plans to send commercial electronic messages (CEMs) to prospects who gave express consent. Which set of rules must the representative follow?

一位从业代表计划开展电话营销活动，联系与经销商无既有关系的潜在认购人，同时计划向已给予明示同意的潜在客户发送商业电子讯息（CEM）。代表必须遵守哪一组规则？

  1. ✓ 正确。电话营销须注册订阅《全国拒接来电名单》（National DNCL）并自建内部名单、不得致电两份名单上的号码、表明身份，且只能在潜在客户当地时间周一至周五 9:00–21:30、周六与周日 10:00–18:00 致电；发送商业电子讯息（CEM）须包含姓名/企业名称/邮寄地址、自发出起至少有效 60 天的电话/电邮/网址，以及即时生效的免费退订链接。Correct. Telemarketing requires subscribing to the National DNCL, maintaining an internal do-not-call list, refraining from calling numbers on either list, displaying identification, and calling only between 9:00-21:30 weekdays and 10:00-18:00 weekends (local prospect time). Commercial electronic messages (CEMs) must disclose the sender's name/business name/mailing address, contact information (phone, email, or web) valid for at least 60 days, and a readily accessible, cost-free unsubscribe mechanism that takes effect immediately.

  2. ✗ 错误。把销售电话伪装成问卷调查属于对来电性质与目的作虚假陈述，是明确列出的四类冷呼（Cold Calling）滥用行为之一；且 CEM 中的联系方式必须至少有效 60 天而非 10 天。Incorrect. Disguising a sales call as a survey constitutes a misrepresentation of the call's nature and purpose, which is one of four explicitly prohibited forms of cold calling abuse. Furthermore, contact information in a CEM must remain valid for at least 60 days, not 10 days.

  3. ✗ 错误。周六与周日的许可致电时段是 10:00–18:00（9:00–21:30 仅限周一至周五）；且收到 CEM 退订请求后严禁再发一封邮件确认退订已处理。Incorrect. Permitted calling hours on Saturdays and Sundays are 10:00 to 18:00 (9:00 to 21:30 applies only Monday through Friday). Furthermore, sending a confirmation email after an unsubscribe request is received is strictly prohibited.

  4. ✗ 错误。根据《加拿大反垃圾邮件法》（CASL），明示同意（Express Consent）需主动 opt-in，但可口头或书面取得；此外 CRTC 罚则对个人同样适用（个人最高 $1,000,000，企业最高 $10,000,000）。Incorrect. Under CASL, express consent requires an active opt-in but may be obtained either orally or in writing. In addition, CRTC penalties apply directly to individuals as well as corporations (up to $1,000,000 for individuals and up to $10,000,000 for corporations).
