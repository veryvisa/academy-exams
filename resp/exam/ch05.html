① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 角色与资金流辨析题：给你非营利基金会、营利分销兼投资基金经理子公司、双注册姊妹交易商及独立受托保管人，考谁与认购人签约、谁收管理费、销售代表佣金从哪笔费用出。Role and cash-flow distinction questions: Presents the non-profit foundation, the for-profit distributor and investment fund manager subsidiary, the dual-registered sister dealer, and the independent trustee and custodian to test who signs contracts with subscribers, who collects administration fees, and which fee pays dealing representative commissions.
  • 集团计划核心机制与计算题：给出受益人年龄、月供金额与每单位供款价格，考如何计算认购单位数（Units）、总供款额，以及到期前脱落（Pre-maturity Attrition）与到期后脱落（Post-maturity Attrition）的不同去向。Group plan core mechanics and calculation questions: Provides the beneficiary's age, monthly contribution amount, and per-unit contribution price to test how to calculate the number of subscribed units, total contributions, and the different destinations of pre-maturity attrition versus post-maturity attrition.
  • 三类在售计划横向对比题：并列集团奖学金计划、个人自主型计划与家庭自主型计划，考最低认购门槛、受益人年龄与亲属关系要求、销售费标准及哪类计划独有账户管理费（Account Maintenance Fee）。Comparative analysis of the three active plans: Compares the group scholarship plan, individual self-determined plan, and family self-determined plan side by side to test minimum contribution thresholds, beneficiary age and relationship requirements, sales charge schedules, and which plan exclusively charges an account maintenance fee.
  • 计划转换与销售费退还场景题：给一个中途想从集团计划转入自主型计划或走完全程的家庭，考 21 岁转换关门时限、100% 转入范围、失去的脱落分享权（Attrition Sharing Right），以及 50% 全程退还与 0–25% 年限阶梯退还的区分。Plan transfer and sales charge refund scenario questions: Presents a family deciding whether to transfer mid-stream from a group plan to a self-determined plan or complete the plan to maturity, testing the age 21 transfer cut-off deadline, the scope of 100% transferability, the forfeiture of attrition sharing rights, and the distinction between the 50% full-maturity refund and the 0% to 25% tenure-based refund schedule.
  • 历史节点与考纲实务双轨题：考 1960、1967、1972、1974、1988、1996、2010 等关键年份事件，以及 2020 培训手册考试口径与 2026 现行招股书实务规则（如魁省 2027 停售新单位、旧数字计划并入共同基金产品线）的边界。Historical milestones and syllabus versus current practice dual-track questions: Tests key milestone years including 1960, 1967, 1972, 1974, 1988, 1996, and 2010, as well as the boundaries between the 2020 training manual exam syllabus and 2026 active prospectus rules (such as the cessation of new unit sales in Quebec by 2027 and the integration of discontinued numbered plans into mutual fund product lines).

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 佣金来源混淆陷阱Commission Source Confusion Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常以为计划收取的综合管理费或账户管理费会用来给销售代表发佣金，忽略了签约非营利基金会与营利分销子公司的收支隔离设计。Candidates often assume that the plan administration fee or account maintenance fee is used to pay dealing representative commissions, overlooking the ring-fenced financial separation between the contracting non-profit foundation and the for-profit distributor subsidiary.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 management fees、account maintenance fees 与 representative compensation 或 dealer commission 的资金来源关系。The question stem connects management fees or account maintenance fees to representative compensation or dealer commissions as funding sources.

⚠ 两类脱落去向颠倒陷阱Pre-maturity vs. Post-maturity Attrition Confusion Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把认购人中途取消计划（到期前脱落）与受益人到期后不入读合资格课程（到期后脱落）混为一谈，或误以为中途取消也能退还累积收益。Candidates confuse premature plan cancellation by a subscriber (pre-maturity attrition) with a beneficiary failing to enrol in eligible post-secondary studies after maturity (post-maturity attrition), or mistakenly assume that accumulated earnings are refunded upon premature cancellation.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述 subscriber cancels before maturity 或 beneficiary does not enrol in eligible post-secondary studies after maturity。The question stem describes subscriber cancels before maturity or beneficiary does not enrol in eligible post-secondary studies after maturity.

⚠ 计划费用张冠李戴陷阱Plan Fee Misattribution Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 记混三类计划的收费科目，误以为个人或家庭自主型计划也收账户管理费，或把 $200/单位与 $50/计划的销售费标准搞反。Candidates confuse the fee structures of the three plan types, mistakenly believing that individual or family self-determined plans charge account maintenance fees, or swapping the $200 per unit and $50 per plan sales charge schedules.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问 which fee applies exclusively to the group scholarship plan 或比较 group plan 与 self-determined plans 的收费差异。The question stem asks which fee applies exclusively to the group scholarship plan or compares fee differences between the group plan and self-determined plans.

⚠ 无损转换片面承诺陷阱Cost-Free Transfer Assumption Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到 100% 净供款、政府补助及累积收益免手续费转入自主型计划，就误判认购人没有任何权益损失，漏掉了放弃单位与脱落分享权这一核心代价。Seeing that 100% of net contributions, government grants, and accumulated earnings transfer into a self-determined plan without transfer fees, candidates mistakenly assume the subscriber incurs no loss, overlooking the crucial sacrifice of plan units and attrition sharing rights.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 transferring from the group plan to an individual or family self-determined plan prior to age 21。The question stem mentions transferring from the group plan to an individual or family self-determined plan prior to age 21.

⚠ 考纲口径与现行新规错位陷阱Exam Syllabus vs. Current Rules Misalignment Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把 2027 年魁北克停售集团计划新单位误当成全国停售，或在考试与实务交界处混淆 2020 手册旧口径与 2026 招股书新披露。Candidates mistakenly interpret the January 1, 2027 cessation of new group plan unit sales in Quebec as a nationwide discontinuation, or confuse 2020 training manual exam specifications with updated 2026 prospectus disclosures at the intersection of theory and practice.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及 2020 manual vs. current prospectus、Quebec 2027 unit sales cessation 或历史停售集团计划的销售费退还比例。The question stem involves 2020 manual vs. current prospectus, Quebec 2027 unit sales cessation, or sales charge refund percentages for historical discontinued plans.

两类交易商的监管归属差异Regulatory Jurisdiction Differences Between Sister DealersScholarship Plan Dealer (SPD) vs. Mutual Fund Dealer (MFD)专营集团与自主型奖学金计划的分销子公司注册为奖学金计划交易商（SPD），由各省证监会直管且不加入 CIRO；销售共同基金组合的姊妹公司具 SPD 与共同基金交易商双注册，为 CIRO 及 CIPF 会员。The distribution subsidiary dedicated to group and self-determined scholarship plans is registered as a scholarship plan dealer (SPD), directly regulated by provincial securities commissions and not a member of CIRO; the sister dealer distributing mutual fund portfolios holds dual registration as an SPD and a mutual fund dealer, and is a member of CIRO and CIPF.
单位、受益人组与两类脱落机制Units, Beneficiary Groups, and Two Attrition MechanismsUnit, Beneficiary Group & Pre-/Post-maturity Attrition单位数由供款额、供款频率与入组年龄决定（单位数 = 每期供款 ÷ 单位价格），同一受益人组（同一年入学资格）内每单位到期收益相同；到期前脱落在取消或转出时触发且被没收收益进入该组 EAP 账户，到期后脱落在不入读或未领满四笔 EAP 时触发。The number of units is determined by the contribution amount, contribution frequency, and entry age (units = periodic contribution divided by unit price), with all units in the same beneficiary group (sharing the same year of eligibility) receiving equal earnings per unit at maturity; pre-maturity attrition is triggered upon cancellation or transfer, forfeiting earnings to the group EAP account, while post-maturity attrition is triggered when a beneficiary does not enrol or fails to collect all four EAPs.

集团计划转入自主型计划的时限与得失Time Limits and Trade-offs of Transferring to Self-determined PlansTransfer to Self-determined Plan认购人可在受益人年满 20 岁当年年末前（21 岁当年 1 月 1 日失效）免手续费将 100% 净供款、政府补助及全部累积收益转入自主型计划（非亲属仅可转个人计划），但立即丧失全部单位及脱落分享权。Subscribers may transfer 100% of net contributions, government grants, and accumulated earnings into a self-determined plan without transfer fees before December 31 of the year the beneficiary reaches age 20 (closing on January 1 of the year turning 21; non-relatives may only transfer to an individual plan), but they immediately forfeit all plan units and attrition sharing rights.
销售费退还（忠诚奖金）双轨标准Dual Standards for Sales Charge Refunds and Loyalty BonusesRefund of Sales Charges (SCR) / Loyalty Bonus走完集团计划全程并领满四笔 EAP 可免税退还 50% 销售费（每笔 EAP 退 12.5%，2007-10 前老计划为 100%）；中途转入自主型计划按在组年限退还 0–25%（0–3 年 0%、3–6 年 5%、6–9 年 10%、9–12 年 15%、12–15 年 20%、15 年以上 25%）；主动减单位则被取消单位退费权作废。Completing the full group plan term and collecting all four EAPs entitles the subscriber to a tax-free refund of 50% of sales charges (12.5% per EAP, or 100% for plans entered prior to October 2007); transferring mid-stream to a self-determined plan provides a tenure-based refund of 0% to 25% (0% for 0–3 years, 5% for 3–6 years, 10% for 6–9 years, 15% for 9–12 years, 20% for 12–15 years, 25% for 15+ years); voluntarily reducing units forfeits refund eligibility on cancelled units.
1967、1972 与 1974 三大历史转折点Three Major Historical Turning Points: 1967, 1972, and 1974Provincial Securities Regulation, Sole National Provider & Tax Deferral1967 年因基金会总裁致函各省检察长提示同业准备金不足使奖学金计划纳入各省证券监管；1972 年该机构成为全国唯一提供奖学金协议的机构；1974 年从税务局争得计划收益递延至受益人领 EAP 时纳税并追溯至 1972 年。In 1967, a warning letter from the foundation's president to provincial attorneys general regarding inadequate competitor reserves led to scholarship plans being placed under provincial securities regulation; in 1972, the organization became the sole national provider of scholarship agreements; in 1974, it secured tax deferral on plan earnings until EAP distribution from the tax authority, retroactive to 1972.

第 1 题Q1基金会与分销子公司职责及佣金来源Responsibilities of the foundation and distributor subsidiary, and sources of representative commissions★陷阱：佣金来源混淆陷阱

A prospective subscriber reviewing the plan disclosure document asks how the non-profit foundation and its wholly owned for-profit distributor subsidiary divide their roles, and whether the annual management fees deducted from the plan are used to pay the dealing representative's commission. Which explanation is accurate?

一位潜在认购人在审阅计划披露文件时询问：非营利基金会与其全资营利分销子公司如何划分职责？从计划中扣除的年度管理费是否用于支付销售代表的佣金？下列哪项解释是准确的？

  1. ✓ 正确。非营利基金会作为计划发起人（Plan Sponsor）与认购人签订协议并收取综合管理费与账户管理费；全资营利子公司作为主分销商（Principal Distributor）所得分销报酬直接来自认购人支付的销售费（Sales Charges），销售代表佣金绝不来源于计划的管理费。Correct. The non-profit foundation acts as plan sponsor, signing agreements with subscribers and collecting administration fees and account maintenance fees; the wholly owned for-profit subsidiary acts as principal distributor, receiving distribution compensation directly from subscriber-paid sales charges, and dealing representative commissions never come from plan administration fees.

  2. ✗ 错误。与认购人签订教育储蓄计划协议（Education Savings Plan Agreement）的合同对手方是非营利基金会而非营利子公司，且代表佣金不从管理费中提取，属于佣金来源混淆陷阱。Incorrect. The legal counterparty signing the Education Savings Plan Agreement with the subscriber is the non-profit foundation, not the for-profit subsidiary, and representative commissions are never deducted from administration fees. Falls into the Commission Source Confusion Trap.

  3. ✗ 错误。专营奖学金计划的分销子公司注册为奖学金计划交易商（Scholarship Plan Dealer, SPD），由各省证监会直管，不加入加拿大投资监管组织（CIRO），仅销售共同基金的姊妹公司才是 CIRO 会员，属于监管归属混淆陷阱。Incorrect. The distribution subsidiary dedicated to scholarship plans is registered as a scholarship plan dealer (SPD), directly regulated by provincial securities commissions, and is not a member of the Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization (CIRO); only the sister company distributing mutual funds is a CIRO member. Falls into the regulatory jurisdiction confusion trap.

  4. ✗ 错误。$200/单位的销售费（Sales Charge）用于划入销售费退还隔离准备金及支付主分销商的分销费，并非由受托人平分给销售代表和组合经理（组合经理费用由基金会从综合管理费中支付），属于资金流向张冠李戴陷阱。Incorrect. The $200 per-unit sales charge is allocated to the segregated sales charge refund reserve and to pay distributor distribution fees; it is not split equally by the trustee between dealing representatives and portfolio managers (portfolio manager fees are paid by the foundation from the administration fee). Falls into the cash-flow misattribution trap.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Two sister dealers operate under the same non-profit foundation: Dealer X distributes only group and self-determined scholarship plans, while Dealer Y distributes mutual fund education portfolios alongside scholarship plans. How does regulatory oversight differ between Dealer X and Dealer Y?

同一非营利基金会旗下设有两家姊妹交易商：交易商 X 仅分销集团与自主型奖学金计划，而交易商 Y 同时注册分销共同基金教育组合与奖学金计划。交易商 X 与交易商 Y 的监管归属有何不同？

  1. ✗ 错误。加拿大证券管理局（CSA）的注册委托仅涵盖投资交易商与共同基金交易商，奖学金计划交易商（Scholarship Plan Dealer, SPD）仍由各省证监会直管，属于监管归属过度概括陷阱。Incorrect. The delegation of registration by the Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA) applies only to investment dealers and mutual fund dealers; scholarship plan dealers (SPDs) remain directly supervised by provincial securities commissions. Falls into the regulatory over-generalization trap.

  2. ✓ 正确。仅注册为奖学金计划交易商（SPD）的交易商 X 不入 CIRO，由各省证监会直管；兼具 SPD 与共同基金交易商（Mutual Fund Dealer）双注册的姊妹公司交易商 Y 是 CIRO 会员及加拿大投资者保护基金（CIPF）会员。Correct. Dealer X, registered solely as a scholarship plan dealer (SPD), is outside CIRO and directly regulated by provincial securities commissions; sister Dealer Y, holding dual registration as an SPD and a mutual fund dealer, is a CIRO Dealer Member and a Canadian Investor Protection Fund (CIPF) member.

  3. ✗ 错误。关系恰好颠倒：双注册并销售共同基金（Mutual Funds）的交易商 Y 才是 CIRO 和 CIPF 会员，线上渠道不豁免自律监管，属于姊妹公司属性颠倒陷阱。Incorrect. 第 7 题Q71967与1972及1974历史节点Key historical milestones in 1967, 1972, and 1974★★陷阱：考纲口径与现行新规错位陷阱

Three closely spaced historical milestones shaped the scholarship plan industry in Canada: 1967, 1972, and 1974. Which option correctly matches each year with the event tested on the exam?

1967 年、1972 年和 1974 年这三个年份相邻的历史里程碑深刻塑造了加拿大奖学金计划行业。下列哪一项正确对应了考试所考查的各年份事件？

  1. ✗ 错误。1967 年与 1972 年的事件被对调了，且首次发放教育援助金（Educational Assistance Payments, EAPs）是在 1971 年而非 1974 年，属于相邻年份事件错位陷阱。Incorrect. Securing tax deferral occurred in 1974 (retroactive to 1972), expanding eligible institutions to community colleges occurred on December 1, 1979, and incorporating the for-profit distributor occurred on September 1, 1988. Falls into the complete timeline confusion trap.

  3. ✗ 错误。基金会以专利特许状（Letters Patent）成立是在 1960-12-15，第三代计划将受益人入组年龄上限从 10 岁提高到 13 岁是在 1988 年，属于首尾年份混淆陷阱。Incorrect. The foundation was incorporated by Letters Patent on December 15, 1960, and the third-generation plan raising the maximum entry age from 10 to 13 was introduced in 1988. Falls into the first and last milestone confusion trap.

  4. ✓ 正确。这三个相邻年份是高频考点：1967 年基金会时任总裁致函各省检察长指出十余家同业可能未保有足够准备金，促成奖学金信托计划被纳入各省证券监管（Provincial Securities Regulation）；1972 年该机构成为全国唯一提供奖学金协议的机构；1974 年从税务部门争得计划收益递延纳税（Tax Deferral）待遇并追溯至 1972 年，使收益改在受益人领取 EAP 时计入受益人所得。Correct. These three closely spaced years are high-frequency exam points: in 1967, the foundation's then-president wrote to provincial attorneys general warning that more than a dozen competitors might hold inadequate reserves, leading to scholarship trust plans being brought under provincial securities regulation; in 1972, the organization became the sole national provider of scholarship agreements; in 1974, it secured tax deferral on plan earnings from tax authorities retroactive to 1972, allowing earnings to be taxed in the hands of beneficiaries upon receiving EAPs.
