① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 定义排除题：最常考 GDP（国内生产总值）不包含什么（转移支付、企业买原材料及家务劳动都不算），以及衰退（至少连续六个月经济收缩）与经济周期（4至7年）的固定定义。Definition and exclusion questions: Most frequently tests what Gross Domestic Product (GDP) excludes (transfer payments, raw materials purchased by businesses, and homemaker productivity are all excluded), as well as the standard definitions of a recession (at least six consecutive months of economic contraction) and the business cycle (typically fluctuating over 4 to 7 years).
  • 反向与方向传导题：考高通胀对消费行为的影响（促使当下消费而非储蓄）、利率升降对国内支出/储蓄与加元汇率/进出口的四个传导方向，以及市场利率与已发行固定收益证券价格的反向关系。Inverse and transmission direction questions: Tests the impact of high inflation on consumer behavior (encouraging spending now rather than saving), the four transmission directions of interest rate changes on domestic spending/saving and the Canadian dollar exchange rate/trade (exports vs. imports), and the inverse relationship between market interest rates and the market value of existing fixed-income securities.
  • 双栏对比归属题：把政府财政政策（税收与支出、时滞不确定）与加拿大央行货币政策（调节货币量与短期利率、1%至3%通胀控制目标、18至24个月时滞）混在同一道题的四个选项里考归属。Policy tool classification and comparison questions: Tests the distinction between government fiscal policy (taxation and government spending, with unpredictable time lags) and Bank of Canada monetary policy (regulating money supply and short-term interest rates, maintaining a 1% to 3% inflation-control target, with an 18 to 24 month time lag) by mixing their tools and characteristics across the four options.
  • 证券属性与风险谱系排序题：考流动性、预期回报、风险三属性的关系（上市不保证流动性、绝对化表述必错），八种风险成分（特别是奖学金计划特有的负担能力风险 Affordability Risk），以及从国库券到普通股/衍生品的风险档位与特征。Security attributes and risk spectrum ranking questions: Tests the relationship among the three core security attributes (liquidity, expected return, and risk—noting that exchange listing aids but never guarantees liquidity, and absolute wording is always incorrect), eight risk components (especially the affordability risk unique to scholarship plans), and the risk tiers and characteristics spanning from Treasury bills to common shares and derivatives.
  • 税务待遇与登记账户对比题：考利息、加拿大公司股息、资本利得三者的税负轻重与资本损失冲抵规则（只能冲抵资本利得），以及 RESP、RRSP、TFSA 在供款抵税、账内增长、取款课税与政府补助上的差异。Tax treatment and registered account comparison questions: Tests the relative tax burdens of interest, Canadian corporate dividends, and capital gains, along with capital loss offset rules (capital losses can only offset capital gains), as well as the key differences among RESPs, RRSPs, and TFSAs regarding contribution tax deductibility, sheltered growth, withdrawal taxation, and eligibility for government grants.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ GDP重复计算与转移支付陷阱GDP Double-Counting and Transfer Payment Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生直觉认为政府发养老金、失业保险或企业买生产原材料都属于经济活动，容易误选进 GDP。Candidates intuitively assume that government pension and employment insurance payments, or business purchases of raw materials, represent economic activity and mistakenly include them in GDP.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问 Which of the following is included in / excluded from GDP，选项出现 transfer payments、raw materials 或 homemaker productivity。The question stem asks which item is included in or excluded from GDP, with options featuring transfer payments, raw materials, or homemaker productivity.

⚠ 通胀与利率方向反推陷阱Inflation and Interest Rate Direction Reversal Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 按日常省钱直觉以为物价飞涨时大家会存钱，或把利率变动对加元汇率、进出口和旧债价格的方向推反。Candidates rely on everyday penny-pinching intuition and assume that soaring prices cause people to save more, or they reverse the directional impact of interest rate changes on the Canadian dollar, exports, imports, and existing bond prices.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 high inflation、interest rates rise/fall 或 long-term bond prices，选项描述 saving vs. spending、exports vs. imports 或 bond market value。The question stem contains high inflation, interest rates rise or fall, or long-term bond prices, with options describing saving vs. spending, exports vs. imports, or bond market value.

⚠ 财政与货币政策串位陷阱Fiscal vs. Monetary Policy Confusion Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把政府的税收/支出决策与加拿大央行的货币量/短期利率工具混为一谈，或误以为央行每月直接设定通胀率而非1%–3%目标区间。Candidates confuse government taxation and spending decisions with the Bank of Canada's money supply and short-term interest rate tools, or mistakenly believe the central bank directly sets the monthly inflation rate rather than a 1% to 3% target band.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问 Fiscal policy 或 Monetary policy / Bank of Canada 的职责、工具或时滞（18 to 24 months）。The question stem asks about fiscal policy, monetary policy, or the responsibilities, tools, or time lag (18 to 24 months) of the Bank of Canada.

⚠ 绝对化措辞与低风险保本错觉陷阱Absolute Wording and Capital Preservation Illusion Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为在交易所上市就保证流动性、国库券付高息，或误以为奖学金计划资本损失风险低就等于保证拿到投资回报。Candidates assume that a stock exchange listing guarantees liquidity, that Treasury bills pay high interest, or that the low risk of capital loss in scholarship plans equates to guaranteed investment returns.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 选项含 always、guarantees liquidity、pays high interest，或把 low risk of capital loss 等同于 guaranteed return。Options contain words like always, guarantees liquidity, pays high interest, or equate low risk of capital loss with guaranteed returns.

⚠ 资本损失乱冲抵与竞品贬低陷阱Improper Capital Loss Offset and Competitor Disparagement Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为资本损失可以冲抵工资或利息收入，或在比较 TFSA 与 RESP 时通过贬低 TFSA 来推销计划。Candidates mistakenly assume capital losses can offset employment or interest income, or attempt to sell scholarship plans by disparaging the TFSA during client comparisons.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及 capital losses 冲抵对象、three types of investment income 税负对比，或客户拿 TFSA / GIC 与奖学金计划做比较。The question stem involves the eligible offset targets for capital losses, the tax burden ranking among the three types of investment income, or client comparisons between TFSAs or GICs and scholarship plans.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
衰退与经济周期的界定Definition of Recession and Business CycleRecession and Business Cycle衰退（recession）定义为至少连续六个月的经济收缩；经济周期通常在四到七年之间起伏，难以预测且更难控制。A recession is defined as at least six consecutive months of economic contraction; the business cycle typically fluctuates over four to seven years and is difficult to predict and even harder to control.
GDP 的核算范围与排除项Calculation Scope and Exclusions of Gross Domestic ProductGross Domestic Product (GDP)GDP 由 Statistics Canada 计算，包含消费、政府支出、企业投资及净出口，但不包含转移支付（养老金、失业保险、福利金）、企业购买的原材料（避免重复计算）及家庭主妇生产力。GDP is calculated by Statistics Canada and includes consumer spending, government expenditures, business investment, and net exports, but excludes transfer payments (pensions, employment insurance, and welfare benefits), raw materials purchased by businesses to avoid double counting, and homemaker productivity.
CPI、高通胀行为后果与央行目标Consumer Price Index, Inflationary Spiral, and Central Bank TargetConsumer Price Index (CPI) and Inflationary SpiralCPI 按月衡量固定一篮子消费品成本；高通胀会促使消费者当下多消费而非储蓄从而引发通胀螺旋；加拿大央行将通胀控制目标设在 1% 到 3% 之间。The CPI measures the monthly cost of a fixed basket of consumer products; high inflation encourages consumers to spend more now rather than save, potentially triggering an inflationary spiral; the Bank of Canada sets its inflation-control target between 1% and 3%.
财政政策与货币政策对照Comparison Between Fiscal Policy and Monetary PolicyFiscal Policy vs. Monetary Policy政府通过增减支出与税收执行财政政策（时滞不确定）；加拿大央行通过调节货币量与影响短期利率执行货币政策，其时滞通常为 18 到 24 个月。The government conducts fiscal policy by adjusting government spending and taxation (with uncertain time lags); the Bank of Canada conducts monetary policy by regulating the money supply and influencing short-term interest rates, with a typical time lag of 18 to 24 months.
利率变动的双向传导机制Two-Way Transmission Mechanisms of Interest Rate ChangesInterest Rate Transmission利率下降会增加国内支出、减少储蓄并使加元走低（促进出口、抑制进口）；利率上升会抑制国内支出并使加元走高（抑制出口、鼓励进口）。Lower interest rates increase domestic spending, reduce saving, and weaken the Canadian dollar (stimulating exports and dampening imports); higher interest rates dampen domestic spending and strengthen the Canadian dollar (dampening exports and encouraging imports).
证券三大属性与流动性陷阱Three Security Attributes and Liquidity ConstraintsLiquidity, Expected Return, and Risk预期回报率越高风险越大；在交易所上市有助于流动性（liquidity / marketability），但绝不保证流动性。A higher expected return entails greater risk; listing securities on an exchange aids liquidity or marketability, but never guarantees liquidity.
负担能力风险与风险承受能力双维度Affordability Risk and Dual Dimensions of Risk ToleranceAffordability Risk and Risk Tolerance负担能力风险是奖学金计划特有的重大风险（无力持续供款被迫提前取消而蒙受损失）；风险承受能力由财务承受能力与心理接受意愿两个维度共同决定。Affordability risk is a major risk unique to scholarship plans (the inability to sustain contributions leading to premature cancellation and financial losses); risk tolerance is determined by both financial capacity to absorb losses and psychological willingness to accept risk.
利率与债券价格反向及 T-bill 特征Inverse Bond Pricing Dynamics and Treasury Bill CharacteristicsYield, Bond Prices, and Treasury Bills (T-bills)市场利率上升时已发行债券市值下跌（对长期债券影响尤大）；国库券（T-bills）风险极低、期限不足一年、不付利息而是以低于到期面值的折价出售。When market interest rates rise, the market value of existing bonds falls (having the greatest impact on long-term bonds); Treasury bills (T-bills) carry very low risk, mature in under a year, pay no interest, and are sold at a discount below their face value.
奖学金计划单位的属性与定位Attributes and Positioning of Scholarship Plan UnitsScholarship Plan Units and Pooled Investment奖学金计划属于集合投资，风险档位为可变（variable）：虽然资本损失风险低，但实际回报取决于组合收益率、受益人是否升学及认购人能否持续供款，且 60 天冷静期后取消不退入会费。Scholarship plans are pooled investments classified as having variable risk: while the risk of capital loss is low, actual returns depend on portfolio performance, whether beneficiaries qualify for post-secondary education, and whether subscribers sustain contributions, with enrolment fees non-refundable upon cancellation after the 60-day cooling-off period.
三种投资收入税负与三种登记账户Tax Treatment of Three Investment Incomes and Three Registered AccountsInterest, Dividends, Capital Gains, and Registered Accounts利息按普通收入全额课税（税负最重），加国股息享股息税收抵免，资本利得部分免税且资本损失只能冲抵资本利得；RESP 供款不可抵税但享政府补助且收益在受益人手中课税，须客观与客户讨论 TFSA。Interest is fully taxed as ordinary income (carrying the heaviest tax burden), Canadian dividends receive a dividend tax credit, and capital gains are partially exempt with capital losses only deductible against capital gains; RESP contributions are not tax-deductible but attract government grants with earnings taxed to the beneficiary, and the TFSA must be discussed objectively with clients.

第 1 题Q1GDP核算包含与排除项及衰退定义GDP calculation inclusions and exclusions, and the definition of a recession★陷阱：GDP重复计算与转移支付陷阱

Statistics Canada is compiling the national Gross Domestic Product (GDP) figures for the period. Which of the following items is included in the calculation of Canada's GDP?

加拿大统计局（Statistics Canada）正在编制该时期的全国国内生产总值（GDP）数据。下列哪一项包含在加拿大 GDP 的计算中？

  1. ✗ 错误。属于「GDP重复计算与转移支付陷阱」：国内生产总值（Gross Domestic Product, GDP）明确不包含养老金、失业保险和福利金等政府转移支付（transfer payments）。Incorrect. Falls into the GDP Double-Counting and Transfer Payment Trap: Gross Domestic Product (GDP) explicitly excludes government transfer payments such as old age pensions, employment insurance, and welfare benefits.

  2. ✗ 错误。属于「GDP重复计算与转移支付陷阱」：为避免重复计算（double counting），企业为生产最终产品而购买的原材料与服务不包含在 GDP 内。Incorrect. Falls into the GDP Double-Counting and Transfer Payment Trap: To avoid double counting, raw materials and services purchased by businesses to produce finished goods are excluded from GDP.

  3. ✓ 正确。国内生产总值（Gross Domestic Product, GDP）衡量给定时期内生产的商品与服务总价值，包含个人消费、政府支出、企业投资（包括企业投入新工厂建筑的资金）以及净出口。Correct. Gross Domestic Product (GDP) measures the total value of goods and services produced in a country over a given period, including personal consumption, government spending, business investment (such as money spent by businesses on new factory buildings), and net exports.

  4. ✗ 错误。属于「GDP重复计算与转移支付陷阱」：考试口径专门指出「GDP 衡量家庭主妇的生产力（homemaker productivity）」是常见错误选项，GDP 不衡量未经过市场交易的家务劳动。Incorrect. Falls into the GDP Double-Counting and Transfer Payment Trap: The exam curriculum specifically notes that GDP measuring homemaker productivity is a common incorrect distractor; GDP does not measure unpaid household work that does not pass through the marketplace.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An analyst reviewing Canadian economic indicators notes that the economy has been contracting for several months and checks the composition of GDP. Which statement is correct?

一位分析师在审阅加拿大经济指标时注意到经济已收缩数月，并核对了 GDP 的构成。下列哪项陈述是正确的？

  1. ✓ 正确。衰退（Recession）定义为至少连续六个月的经济收缩；国内生产总值（GDP）不包含转移支付以及企业为生产最终产品购买的原材料。Correct. A recession is defined as at least six consecutive months of economic contraction; Gross Domestic Product (GDP) excludes transfer payments as well as raw materials purchased by businesses to produce finished goods.

  2. ✗ 错误。衰退是至少连续六个月（而非两年）的经济收缩，且失业保险属于转移支付，不计入 GDP。Incorrect. A recession is defined as at least six consecutive months (not two consecutive years) of economic contraction, and employment insurance benefits are transfer payments that are excluded from GDP.

  3. ✗ 错误。18 到 24 个月是货币政策的时滞，经济周期（business cycle）常在四到七年之间起伏；且工厂购买的原材料不计入 GDP 以免重复计算。Incorrect. The 18 to 24 month period is the time lag for monetary policy, whereas the business cycle typically fluctuates over four to seven years. Furthermore, raw materials purchased by factories are excluded from GDP to prevent double counting.

  4. ✗ 错误。GDP 由加拿大统计局（Statistics Canada）计算，而按月用固定一篮子产品计算的是消费者价格指数（CPI）。Incorrect. GDP is calculated by Statistics Canada, whereas the Consumer Price Index (CPI) is calculated monthly based on the cost of a fixed basket of consumer products.

第 2 题Q2财政政策与货币政策的工具及时滞Tools and time lags of fiscal policy versus monetary policy★★陷阱：财政与货币政策串位陷阱

How does monetary policy differ from fiscal policy in managing the Canadian economy?

在管理加拿大经济方面，货币政策与财政政策有何不同？

  1. ✓ 正确。货币政策（Monetary Policy）由加拿大央行（Bank of Canada）执行，主要通过影响短期利率来调节流通中的货币量；其时滞通常为 18 到 24 个月，因此央行必须采取前瞻性行动。Correct. Monetary policy is conducted by the Bank of Canada, primarily by influencing short-term interest rates to adjust the money supply; its time lag is typically 18 to 24 months, requiring the central bank to act forward-looking.

  2. ✗ 错误。属于「财政与货币政策串位陷阱」：税收与基建支出属于政府的财政政策（Fiscal Policy），且财政政策的时滞既不即时也不确定。Incorrect. Falls into the Fiscal vs. Monetary Policy Confusion Trap: Taxation and infrastructure spending belong to government fiscal policy, and fiscal policy time lags are neither immediate nor predictable.

  3. ✗ 错误。属于「财政与货币政策串位陷阱」：央行设定的是 1% 到 3% 的通胀控制目标区间（而非每月直接设定 CPI 数值，4 到 7 年则是经济周期的长度）。Incorrect. Falls into the Fiscal vs. Monetary Policy Confusion Trap: The central bank sets an inflation-control target band between 1% and 3% (it does not directly set the monthly CPI rate, and four to seven years is the typical duration of a business cycle).

  4. ✗ 错误。属于「财政与货币政策串位陷阱」：加税（raising tax rates）从经济中抽走资金是政府财政政策的工具，不是加拿大央行的货币政策工具。Incorrect. Falls into the Fiscal vs. Monetary Policy Confusion Trap: Raising tax rates to withdraw money from the economy is a tool of government fiscal policy, not a tool of monetary policy conducted by the Bank of Canada.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

When inflation rises above the Bank of Canada's target range of 1% to 3% because strong demand is pushing industries near their capacity limits, what policy action and time lag apply?

当强劲需求推动行业逼近产能极限，导致通胀超出加拿大央行 1% 至 3% 的目标区间时，适用的政策行动和时滞是什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。减税是刺激经济的财政政策，会进一步推高需求；且六个月是衰退的定义时长，不是政策时滞。Incorrect. Cutting taxes is an expansionary fiscal policy that further drives up aggregate demand. Additionally, six months is the qualifying threshold for a recession, not a policy time lag.

  2. ✗ 错误。通胀低于 1% 时央行才会降息刺激支出；超出 1%–3% 目标时应加息压制需求。Incorrect. The Bank of Canada lowers interest rates to stimulate spending when inflation drops below 1%; when inflation exceeds the 1% to 3% target, it raises interest rates to dampen demand.

  3. ✗ 错误。供应纸币与执行货币政策是加拿大央行（Bank of Canada）的职责，而非加拿大统计局（Statistics Canada）。Incorrect. Supplying bank notes and conducting monetary policy is the responsibility of the Bank of Canada, not Statistics Canada.
