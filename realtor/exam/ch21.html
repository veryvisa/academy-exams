① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 概念分拣题：题干给一个价钱或一句客户的问话，要你判断它是底价、天花板价、交换价值、业主价值、市场价值还是投资价值。真正考的是这几个词不能互相代替。Concept-sorting questions: the stem gives a price, or one question from a client, and you must decide whether it is the floor price, the ceiling price, value in exchange, value to the owner, market value, or investment value. What is really tested is that these words cannot stand in for one another.
  • 方法适用题：住宅、出租型商业物业、缺少成交的特殊物业各用哪种方法；三个数字不一致时怎么收口。考的是适用场景，以及调和不是取平均，不考调整表和资本化率怎么算。Method-fit questions: which approach is used for a house, for rented commercial property, and for a special-purpose property with few sales, and how the opinion is closed when the three figures disagree. What is tested is the setting each approach fits, and that to reconcile is not to take an average. Adjustment grids, and how a capitalization rate is calculated, are not tested.
  • 文件定性题：几页定性比较、银行用的勾选表、征收听证用的长报告、一次法庭上的口述，要你说出这是比较市场分析还是哪一种评估报告。考的是工作范围和正式程度，不是谁更懂行。Document-type questions: a few pages of qualitative comparison, a checkbox form used by a bank, a long report for an expropriation hearing, or an oral statement in court. You must say whether it is a comparative market analysis or which kind of appraisal report. What is tested is scope of work and formality, not who knows the field better.
  • 责任标准题：后来的成交价对不上、借款人没有合同、报告写了免责、持牌人只出了一份简短比较。考的是合理评估师标准、没有合同也可以有注意义务、免责声明只能限范围。Standard-of-care questions: a later sale price does not match, the borrower has no contract, the report contains a disclaimer, or the licensee produced only a short comparison. What is tested is the reasonable appraiser test, that a duty of care can exist without a contract, and that a disclaimer can only limit the scope.
  • 口径错位题：题干写 according to the course 或 licensing exam 时，牌照、市场价值定义、贷款人为何要评估、评估日，都按课程原文作答。现行的持牌人不能私下单干、曝光期与营销期、不得单靠一种方法、每年 7 月 1 日估税基准日，写在解释里。Exam-wording mismatch questions: when the stem says according to the course or licensing exam, the licence, the definition of market value, why a lender wants an appraisal, and the effective date are all answered from the course text. The current rules belong in the explanation: a licensee cannot rely on the exemption to work privately, Exposure Time and Marketing Time, no reliance on any one method alone, and a property-tax valuation date of July 1 each year.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 一个价回答所有问题One price answers every question

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 底价、天花板价、成交价、业主价值、市场价值和投资价值都像在问值多少钱，于是用卖家底价充当市场价值，或用一份市场价值去回答「我买划不划算」。The floor price, the ceiling price, the sale price, value to the owner, market value, and investment value all sound like a question about what the property is worth. The seller's floor price is then used as market value, or one market-value opinion is used to answer whether the purchase is a good buy for me.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现最低愿卖价、最高愿买价和成交价，或客户拿着估价问值不值得买。The stem gives, together, the lowest price the seller will accept, the highest price the buyer will pay, and the sale price, or the client holds an appraisal and asks whether it is worth buying.

⚠ 调和就是平均Reconciliation is an average

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 三个数字不一样时，最省事的是加总除以三；成本法纸面上有土地、造价和折旧，又会被当成实务中最准确的那个。When the three figures differ, the easiest step is to add them and divide by three. The cost approach shows land, construction cost, and depreciation on paper, so it is also treated as the most accurate method in practice.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出两种或三种方法的不同结论，或强调成本法看起来最精确，问最终意见怎么定。The stem gives different conclusions from two or three approaches, or it stresses that the cost approach looks the most exact, and asks how the final opinion is set.

⚠ 合并后要牌照A licence is required after the merger

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 发牌和纪律收进同一家机构之后，容易以为评估也被收进强制持牌，再把协会资格当成政府牌照，以为开除会籍就不能接单。After licensing and discipline were brought into one body, it is easy to think appraisal was also brought into mandatory licensing, then to treat an association designation as a government licence, and to think that expulsion means the person can no longer take assignments.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现监管合并、谁可以自称评估师、被协会开除，或问 BC 做评估要不要牌照。The stem mentions the regulatory merger, who may call themselves an appraiser, expulsion by the association, or whether appraisal work in BC needs a licence.

⚠ 按现状把地看死The existing use locks the land

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 地上还有房子、也还在收租，就按现有用途定价，不再问这块地如果毁掉，市场会不会原样重建。The building is still there and rent is still being collected, so the price follows the existing use, and the question is never asked: if it were destroyed, would the market rebuild it the same way.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 分区已经改了、周围用途变了，或题干问最高最佳用途、潜在价值、剩余法。Zoning has changed, surrounding uses have changed, or the stem asks about highest and best use, latent value, or the residual method.

⚠ 责任看合同或看结果Duty follows the contract or the result

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 没有合同、写了免责，就被当成没有责任；数字和后来的售价不同，又被当成当然有过错。两头都和合理评估师标准相反。No contract, or a written disclaimer, is treated as no duty. A figure that differs from a later sale price is treated as fault for certain. Both ends are the opposite of the reasonable appraiser test.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干里有非委托人的买家或借款人、免责声明、页脚写着这不是评估，或者估价和后来的成交价对不上。The stem has a buyer or a borrower who is not the client, a disclaimer, a footer saying this is not an appraisal, or an appraisal figure that does not match a later sale price.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
估价是意见，签字才定稿An appraisal is an opinion, final only when signedappraisal估价是对不动产价值的专家意见，必须用具体金额、数值区间或不高于、不低于某种关系来表达；签字或作出最终陈述才是定稿，在此之前的沟通必须标明这不是最终结论。An appraisal is an expert opinion of the value of real property. It must be stated as a specific amount, a numerical range, or a relationship such as not above or not below a figure. Signing, or making a final statement, is what makes it final. Until then, every communication must say that this is not the final conclusion.
卷面一般不要求牌照The exam generally requires no licenceExemption for appraisers卷面答案是 BC 评估师一般无需牌照，依据是 Real Estate Services Regulation s.2.8，AACI 可做全品类、CRA 限于不超过四户的住宅，开除会籍仍可接单；现行另规定已经持牌的人不能援引该豁免私下单干。The exam answer is that appraisers in BC generally need no licence, under the exemption for appraisers in Real Estate Services Regulation s.2.8. AACI may cover every property type. CRA is limited to homes of not more than four units. Expulsion from membership still leaves the person free to take assignments. The current rule adds that a person who already holds a licence cannot rely on that exemption to work privately.
成交价必须落在两端之间The sale price must fall between the two endsvalue in exchange底价是卖家最低接受额，天花板价是买家最高支付额，交换价值必须落在二者之间交易才可能发生；二者合称业主价值，估价师负责的是客观的市场价值，不是投资价值。The floor price is the lowest amount the seller will accept. The ceiling price is the highest amount the buyer will pay. Value in exchange must fall between the two, or no transaction can occur. The two together are called value to the owner. The appraiser is responsible for objective market value, not for investment value.
卷面只考市场价值要件The exam tests only the market-value elementsmarket value卷面的市场价值是指定日期、充分曝光后，买卖双方审慎知情、无不当压力、公平交易下最可能的价格，估价师日常工作 90% 以上估的是这个；现行才把曝光拆成向后的曝光期和向前的营销期。On the exam, market value is the most probable price on a specified date, after adequate exposure, with both parties prudent and informed, under no undue pressure, in a fair transaction. More than 90% of an appraiser's day-to-day work estimates this. Only the current standard splits exposure into Exposure Time, looking back, and Marketing Time, looking forward.
工作范围匹配即可合格A matching scope of work can be competentscope of work流程按定义问题、工作范围、收集数据、分析数据、选择方法、调和、沟通结论来讨论；工作范围与用途匹配即可，课程中的 $100 路过式意见和 $5,000 叙述式报告只要如实交代都可以合格。The process is discussed as define the problem, scope of work, collect the data, analyze the data, select the approach, reconcile, and communicate the conclusion. Competence requires only that the scope of work match the use. In the course, a $100 drive-by opinion and a $5,000 narrative report can both be competent if each states honestly what was done.
最佳用途要过四项约束Highest and best use must pass four testsHighest and Best Use最高最佳用途是在合法、物理可行、财务可行、市场支撑下净回报最大的用途；若毁掉后市场不会原样重建，就有潜在价值，剩余法是用完工市场价值减去拆除成本和开发成本。Highest and Best Use is the use with the greatest net return that is legally permissible, physically possible, financially feasible, and supported by the market. If the market would not rebuild the same improvement after it was destroyed, the land has latent value. The residual method subtracts demolition cost and development cost from the market value on completion.
三种方法调和不是平均Reconcile the three approaches; do not averagereconcile直接比较法基于替代原则、多用于住宅，收益法把价值看成未来净收益的现值、多用于出租型商业物业，成本法常用于缺少成交的特殊物业且实务上往往最不精确；结论不一致时用专业判断调和，不取平均数。The Direct Comparison Approach rests on the principle of substitution and is used most often for houses. The Income Approach treats value as the present value of future net income and is used most often for rented commercial property. The Cost Approach is often used for special-purpose property with few sales, and in practice it is often the least precise. When the conclusions disagree, reconcile by professional judgment. Do not take an average.
CMA差在范围不是水平A CMA differs in scope, not in skillComparative Market Analysis正式报告可以是口头、表格式或叙述式；比较市场分析通常用 3 到 5 个可比做优于、劣于或相近的定性比较，篇幅可以短到一到三页，并且必须说明做了什么和没做什么。A formal report may be an oral report, a form report, or a narrative report. A Comparative Market Analysis usually uses 3 to 5 comparables and makes a qualitative comparison of better, worse, or similar. It can be as short as one to three pages, and it must state what was done and what was not done.
过错不看合同也不看对错Fault follows neither the contract nor the resultreasonable appraiser test过错问的是同等信息下一般同行会不会这样判断，单纯高估或低估本身不构成过错；没有合同的第三方仍可因可预见的依赖而主张注意义务，免责声明不能豁免这项义务，比较市场分析适用同一标准。Under the reasonable appraiser test, fault asks whether an ordinary peer, with the same information, would have judged the same way. A simple overestimate or underestimate is not fault by itself. A third party with no contract may still claim a duty of care where reliance was foreseeable. A disclaimer cannot waive that duty. A Comparative Market Analysis is held to the same standard.
评估日不必等于估税日The effective date need not equal the tax dateeffective date估价报告的评估日可以是委托人指定的任何日期；现行物业税评估值用每年 7 月 1 日作基准日，物理状况与用途冻结在 10 月 31 日，两套数字对不上并不说明哪一个错了。The effective date of an appraisal report may be any date the client specifies. Current property-tax assessed value uses July 1 each year as the valuation date, and physical condition and use are frozen on October 31. The two sets of figures need not match, and a difference does not show that either one is wrong.

第 1 题Q1交换价值必须居中Value in exchange must sit in the middle★★陷阱：一个价回答所有问题

A seller will not accept less than a private minimum. A buyer will not pay more than a private maximum. They later sign at a price between those two figures, and neither knew the other's limit. Which statement matches the course?

卖家有一个不愿再低的私下最低价，买家有一个不愿再高的私下最高价。后来的成交价落在这两个数之间，双方都不知道对方的底线。哪一项符合课程？

  1. ✗ 卖家最低接受额是底价（floor price），不是市场价值（market value）。市场价值来自市场证据和公认技术。把底价当成市场价值，就是一个价回答所有问题。The lowest amount the seller will accept is the floor price, not market value. Market value comes from market evidence and accepted technique. Treating the floor price as market value is using one price to answer every question.

  2. ✓ 成交价是交换价值（value in exchange），必须落在底价与天花板价（ceiling price）之间，交易才可能发生。双方不知道对方的底线，谈判才有必要。这个成交价不是业主价值，也不回答买家个人买得划不划算。The agreed price is value in exchange. It must fall between the floor price and the ceiling price, or no transaction can occur. Neither party knows the other's limit, which is why negotiation is needed. That sale price is not value to the owner, and it does not answer whether the purchase is a good buy for the buyer personally.

  3. ✗ 底价和天花板价合在一起才叫业主价值（value to the owner）。它高度主观，估价师通常不负责估算。只把买家上限叫作业主价值，又交给估价师去算，两层都错了。The floor price and the ceiling price together are value to the owner. It is highly subjective, and the appraiser is not usually responsible for estimating it. Calling only the buyer's maximum value to the owner, and then giving it to the appraiser to calculate, is wrong on both points.

  4. ✗ 「这个价格对我划不划算」是投资价值（investment value），要看买家自己的税务和融资。估价师负责的是客观的市场价值，不能用同一份结论回答投资问题。Whether this price is a good buy for me is investment value. It depends on the buyer's own taxes and financing. The appraiser is responsible for objective market value. The same conclusion cannot answer the investment question.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A buyer who already holds a market-value appraisal asks the listing licensee, "Given my taxes and the financing I can get, is this price a good buy for me?" What distinction does the course draw?

买家已经有一份市场价值估价，却问挂牌的持牌人：按我的税负和我能拿到的融资，这个价格对我算不算值得买？课程怎么区分？

  1. ✗ 市场价值（market value）不包含某个买家的个人税务和融资。用估价数字直接回答「对我值不值」，就是把市场价值和投资价值当成同一个问题。Market value does not include one buyer's personal tax position and financing. Answering whether it is worth it for me with the appraisal figure treats market value and investment value as the same question.

  2. ✗ 底价（floor price）是卖家愿意接受的最低额，通常对方并不知道。买家问的是自己买得划不划算，不是卖家的底线。The floor price is the lowest amount the seller is willing to accept, and the other party usually does not know it. The buyer is asking whether the purchase is a good buy personally, not for the seller's limit.

  3. ✗ 交换价值（value in exchange）是双方谈成的成交价，不是把估价和买家上限平均。课程也没有把平均规定成价值意见的做法。Value in exchange is the price the two parties agree. It is not the average of the appraisal and the buyer's maximum. The course also does not prescribe an average as the way to form a value opinion.

  4. ✓ 「对我值不值得买」是投资价值（investment value），要结合买家个人的税务状况和融资能力。市场上大概能卖多少才是市场价值（market value）。两个问题不能用同一份市场价值结论来回答。Whether it is worth buying for me is investment value. It has to be read with the buyer's own tax position and financing capacity. What the property would probably sell for in the market is market value. The two questions cannot be answered with the same market-value conclusion.

答案Answer：d

第 2 题Q2调和不是取平均Reconciliation is not an average★★陷阱：调和就是平均

For one house, the direct comparison approach, the income approach, and the cost approach produce three different figures. The cost figure is built from land value, replacement cost, and depreciation, and it looks the most exact on paper. How should the final opinion be reached?

同一栋住宅上，直接比较法、收益法和成本法得出三个不同数字。成本法那个数由土地价值、重置成本和折旧构成，纸面上看最精确。最终意见应当怎么形成？

  1. ✗ 调和（reconcile）不是把三个结论相加后取平均数。权重来自这类物业的市场参与者实际怎么定价，不是来自「有三种方法就各占一份」。这是调和就是平均的陷阱。To reconcile is not to add the three conclusions and divide by three. The weight comes from how market participants actually price this kind of property. It does not come from giving each approach an equal share because there are three of them. This is the trap that reconciliation is an average.

  2. ✗ 成本法（Cost Approach）理论上最精确，实际操作却常常是最不精确的一种，因为建造成本和市场认可的价值经常对不上，旧楼折旧也难估准。住宅最常用来定价的是直接比较。纸面精确不是采用它的理由。The Cost Approach is the most precise in theory, but in practice it is often the least precise, because construction cost and the value the market recognizes often do not match, and depreciation on an older building is hard to estimate accurately. Houses are most often priced by direct comparison. Looking exact on paper is not a reason to adopt the cost figure.

  3. ✗ 直接比较法（Direct Comparison Approach）基于替代原则，最常用于住宅。比租金和营运费用的是收益法，最常用于出租型商业物业。住宅改成只看收益，方法用反了。The Direct Comparison Approach rests on the principle of substitution and is used most often for houses. Comparing rent and operating expenses is the Income Approach, used most often for rented commercial property. Valuing a house only from income uses the approach the wrong way.

  4. ✓ 三种方法不一致时要调和（reconcile）：用专业判断说明哪个结论最贴近这类物业的市场参与者实际的定价方式，而不是取平均数。本篇只要求记住适用场景和这一步。When the three approaches disagree, reconcile: use professional judgment to say which conclusion is closest to how market participants actually price this kind of property, and do not take an average. This chapter only asks you to remember which setting each approach fits, and this reconciling step.

答案Answer：d

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A place of worship has almost no recent sales of similar properties. Which approach does the course say is often used, and what caution comes with it?

一处礼拜场所几乎没有相似物业的近期成交。课程说这时常用哪种方法，同时要记住什么限制？

  1. ✓ 缺少市场数据的特殊物业，例如教堂一类，常用成本法（Cost Approach）：空地价值加新建成本，再减去建筑物折旧。它理论上最精确，实操却常常最不精确，因为造价和市场认可的价值经常对不上。For a special-purpose property that lacks market data, such as a church, the Cost Approach is often used: vacant-land value, plus the cost to build new, minus depreciation of the building.   2. ✗ 课程的理由之一是每宗物业都独一无二，地段、格局、状况很难标准化。说同年龄就完全同质，和「需要专家」的方向相反。One reason in the course is that every property is unique. Location, layout, and condition are hard to standardize. Saying that the same age makes the houses fully the same runs the opposite way from the reason an expert is needed.

  3. ✓ 不动产钉在一个位置，会被周边的外部性（externality）影响。街对面新建公园是正外部性，隔壁有毒气味的工厂是负外部性。这是四条理由里「市场是地方性的」那一条。另外三条是成交频率低、每宗独一无二、耐久且反应慢。Real property is fixed in one place, so it is affected by externalities around it. A new park across the street is a positive externality. A factory next door that gives off a toxic smell is a negative externality. Among the four reasons, this is the one that the market is local. The other three are a low frequency of sales, each property being unique, and durability with a slow reaction.

  4. ✗ 房子耐久、反应慢，市场跌了也不能马上退货，需要跨周期的专业判断。能很快退出的是股票一类的资产，不是课程对不动产的描述。A house is durable and reacts slowly. When the market falls it cannot be handed back at once, so professional judgment across a cycle is needed. An asset that can be exited quickly, such as a share, is not the course's description of real property.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Which comparison does the course use to explain why real estate, unlike company shares, calls for an appraisal opinion?

课程用哪一种对比，说明不动产为什么不像公司股份那样、却需要一份估价意见？

  1. ✗ 方向反了。同一家公司的股票彼此同质，不动产才是每宗独一无二。同质加上成交频繁，才是股票几乎不需要估价师的原因。The direction is reversed. Shares in one company are homogeneous with one another. Real property is what is unique in every parcel. Homogeneity, together with frequent sales, is why shares almost never need an appraiser.

  2. ✗ 课程的对比是：单笔股票买卖的重要性通常低于一次地产交易。把重要性说反，就解释不了为什么不动产更需要一份专家意见。The course's comparison is that one share trade is usually less important than one real estate transaction. Reversing that importance does not explain why real property needs an expert opinion more.

  3. ✗ 成交频率低才是不动产需要专家的理由之一。房子往往几年才卖一次，能参考的数据点稀少；成交频繁的是股票那一边。A low frequency of sales is one reason real property needs an expert. A house often sells only once in several years, so the data points to refer to are few. Frequent sales are on the share side.

  4. ✓ 考试口径的四条理由是：成交频率低、每宗独一无二、耐久且反应慢、市场是地方性的并受外部性（externality）影响。股票则成交频繁、流通范围大、彼此同质，单笔决策的重要性通常也更低。The exam's four reasons are a low frequency of sales, each parcel being unique, durability with a slow reaction, and a market that is local and affected by externalities. Shares trade frequently, circulate widely, and are homogeneous with one another, and a single decision is usually less important.
