① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 定性对照题：问按揭究竟是一笔贷款、一次过户，还是普通法上的转让，考卷面按手册主流观点答仍保留转让性质，同时分得清现行法首先把它写成押记、贷款人不是注册所有人。Characterisation questions ask whether a mortgage is a loan, a conveyance of title, or a transfer at common law. On the paper, follow the manual's mainstream view and answer that it still retains the character of a transfer, while keeping clear that current law first writes it as a charge and that the lender is not the registered owner.
  • 条款效力题：同一题里放上贷款当时的买断期权、事后另签的独立期权、禁止提前还款或禁止承担，考哪一条卡住了最终赎回、哪一条仍然可以强制执行。Clause-effect questions place in one item an option to purchase taken when the loan was made, a separate option signed later, and a ban on prepayment or on assumption. They test which one clogs the final redemption and which one can still be enforced.
  • 责任主体题：买家承担之后原借款人还负不负责，催告有没有落在三个月里，物业是住宅还是生意，考默示个人契诺还在、三条解除路径只限住宅，商业按揭要另看更替契约成不成立。Liability questions ask whether the original borrower is still liable after the buyer assumes, whether a demand fell inside three months, and whether the property is a home or a business. They test that the implied personal covenant remains, that the three release paths are limited to a residential mortgage, and that a commercial mortgage depends on whether a novation is made out.
  • 顺位方向题：给出几份按揭的登记顺序，问某一顺位止赎会抹掉谁、借款人只够还一份时必须先还谁，考顺位看登记先后而不是签约日，方向是赎上、止下。Priority-direction questions give the registration order of several mortgages and ask whom a foreclosure at one rank wipes out, and which mortgage the borrower must pay first when the money is enough for only one. They test that priority follows the order of registration rather than the signing date, and that the direction is redeem up, foreclose down.
  • 程序与红线题：把外省不经法院的售卖、最终止赎令和法院批准的出售放在一题里，考BC必须以呈请提起、最终令消灭个人契诺、售价不足才继续追差额；利率题则要求按手册的60%有效年利率作答，不把现行35%年百分率填进卷面。Procedure and ceiling questions place an out-of-province sale without a court, a final order of foreclosure, and a court-approved sale in one item. They test that in BC the proceeding must be started by petition, that a final order extinguishes the personal covenant, and that the difference is pursued only when the sale price is not enough. A rate question must be answered with the manual's effective annual rate of 60%, and the current annual percentage rate of 35% must not be entered on the paper.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 止赎两条路的后果记反Swapping the results of the two foreclosure routes

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把安大略、阿尔伯塔那种不经法院的售卖套进BC，或者把最终止赎令与司法拍卖对个人契诺的效果记成同一套。最终令使贷款人成为产权人并消灭差额追索；法院批准的出售如果钱不够，被申请人仍要对差额负责。The candidate imports into BC the sale without a court used in Ontario and Alberta, or treats a final order of foreclosure and a judicial sale as having the same effect on the personal covenant. A final order makes the lender the owner and ends any claim for the difference. If a court-approved sale does not bring in enough money, the respondent remains liable for the shortfall.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现其他省份、power of sale、不经法院出售，或同时问成为产权人之后还能不能再追差额。The stem mentions another province, a power of sale, or a sale without a court, or it asks whether the difference can still be claimed after the lender becomes the owner.

⚠ 承担即脱身Treating assumption as a release

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 买家在文件上写了承担，卖方就以为自己的个人契诺已经换人。普通法上原借款人默示仍要负责，三条制定法解除路径只适用于住宅按揭，而且各有三个月的条件，承担本身不是解除。Once the buyer has written an assumption into the documents, the seller thinks the personal covenant has moved to someone else. At common law the original borrower remains impliedly liable. The three statutory release paths apply only to a residential mortgage, and each has a three-month condition. Assumption itself is not a release.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写买家已经承担、卖方没有申请书面批准，或催告发生在到期后的三个月之内，选项却说卖方在过户或到期当天自动解脱。The stem says the buyer has already assumed, the seller did not apply for written approval, or the demand came within three months after expiry, while an option says the seller was released automatically on completion or on the expiry date.

⚠ 顺位与赎回方向记反Reversing priority and the direction of redemption

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生按合同签订日排先后，或以为止赎会把排在前面的按揭一起清掉，又以为赎回要从第一按揭开始。顺位看谁先登记；止赎向下抹掉后面的人，赎回从最低顺位往上。The candidate ranks the mortgages by the date the contract was signed, or thinks foreclosure also clears the mortgages that rank ahead, or thinks redemption must start with the first mortgage. Priority is who registered first. Foreclosure wipes out the people behind. Redemption runs from the lowest rank upward.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出第一到第四的登记顺序，问某一顺位的最终令抹掉谁，或借款人只够还一份时必须先碰哪一份。The stem gives the registration order from first to fourth and asks whom a final order at one rank removes, or which mortgage the borrower must touch first when the money is enough for only one.

⚠ 设卡看错对象Aiming the clog rule at the wrong clause

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把限制提前还款、禁止承担也当成无效，或者把贷款谈成之后另签的独立买断期权也判无效。设卡只针对最终能不能无条件赎回，不针对还款的时点和方式。The candidate treats a restriction on prepayment, or a ban on assumption, as void, or also holds void an independent option to purchase signed after the loan was agreed. A clog concerns only whether the land can in the end be redeemed without conditions. It does not concern the time or the manner of payment.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干里同时出现买断期权、禁止提前还款、禁止承担，并问哪一份文件无效，还写明期权是同一次谈判还是事后独立交易。The stem contains an option to purchase, a ban on prepayment, and a ban on assumption together, asks which document is void, and states whether the option was in the same negotiation or a later independent transaction.

⚠ 用现行红线答卷面Answering the paper with the current rate ceiling

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 手册写的犯罪利率是有效年利率超过60%。现行法从2025-01-01起改成年百分率超过35%，商业借款另有两档例外。考生看见个人消费贷款，就用现行档次替换题目标明的课程手册。The criminal interest rate in the manual is an effective annual rate over 60%. From 2025-01-01, current law uses an annual percentage rate over 35%, with two further bands for a business loan. When the candidate sees a personal consumer loan, the current bands are substituted for the course manual named in the item.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写 course manual、课程手册或考试口径，选项里同时出现60%有效年利率、35%年百分率、48%商业档或更大本金不设上限。The stem says course manual or exam wording, and the options include an effective annual rate of 60%, an annual percentage rate of 35%, a business band of 48%, or no ceiling on a larger principal.

卷面说转让，现行法定性是押记The paper says transfer; current law characterises it as a chargemortgage / charge法律上按揭不是贷款本身，而是对土地权益的担保；考试口径的主流观点是按揭仍保留普通法上的转让性质，现行《土地产权法》第231(1)条则写明登记按揭首先是押记，第231(2)条只拟制视同已转让的权利义务，按揭权人不是注册所有人。In law a mortgage is not the loan itself. It is security over an interest in land. The mainstream view on the exam is that a mortgage still retains the common-law character of a transfer. Section 231(1) of the current Land Title Act states that a registered mortgage is first a charge. Section 231(2) only deems the rights and obligations to be those of a transfer. The mortgagee is not the registered owner.
卡住最终赎回的条款无效A clause that blocks final redemption is voidno clog on the equity of redemption借款人最终必须能无条件赎回自己的地，贷款谈成时捆绑的买断期权以及离谱地遥远的赎回期无效，贷款成立后作为独立交易另谈的期权有效，限制提前还款或禁止承担不算设卡。The borrower must in the end be able to redeem the land without conditions. An option to purchase tied in when the loan was negotiated, and a redemption date that is absurdly remote, are void. An option negotiated later as an independent transaction, after the loan exists, is valid. A limit on prepayment or a ban on assumption is not a clog on the equity of redemption.
漏交标准条款不使按揭作废Failing to deliver the standard terms does not void the mortgageForm B / Land Title Act s.229登记处只接受Form B，Part 2可以是局长标准条款、贷款人备案条款或明示条款；签约当时或之前没有把标准条款副本交给借款人的，Part 2视为局长核准的默认版本，债务、法院止赎权和普通法上的其他救济都不因此消失。The land title office accepts only Form B. Part 2 may be the registrar's standard terms, the lender's filed terms, or express terms. If a copy of the standard terms was not given to the borrower at or before signing, Part 2 is treated as the default version approved by the registrar. The debt, the court's power to foreclose, and the other common-law remedies do not disappear because of that. This is Land Title Act s.229.
加速是选择，承诺人可先被追Acceleration is a choice, and a covenantor can be pursued firstacceleration clause / covenantor / guarantor加速条款是贷款人在违约时可以选择立即要求偿还全部未到期余额，不是自动触发；共同承诺人是主债务人，贷款人可以在追究借款人之前直接追索，担保人是次债务人，必须先用尽对借款人的手段，法院看合同实质而不是签署栏头衔。An acceleration clause lets the lender, on default, choose to demand immediate repayment of the whole balance that is not yet due. It does not trigger by itself. A covenantor is a primary debtor, and the lender may claim against that person before pursuing the borrower. A guarantor is a secondary debtor, and the lender must first exhaust the remedies against the borrower. The court looks at the substance of the contract, not the title on the signature line.
利息法四条，考试口径仍然准确Four Interest Act rules, and the exam wording is still accurateInterest Act要求付息却没写利率的按年息5%计；本息混合却不载明年或半年、非预付利率的，一分利息都收不了，写低了只能按声明的低利率收；逾期不得造成高于未逾期本金利率的实际负担，违约就取消折扣同样违反，只因时间推移上调则不违反；只对个人且约定五年内不到期的按揭，满五年后可还清本息再加三个月利息。If interest is payable but no rate is stated, it is 5% per year. If principal and interest are blended and the rate is not stated as one calculated yearly or half-yearly (not in advance), no interest at all can be collected; if a lower rate is stated, only that stated lower rate may be collected. Arrears must not create a real burden higher than the rate on principal that is not in arrears. Cancelling a discount on default also offends that rule. A rate that rises only because time has passed does not. Only for an individual, and only for a mortgage agreed not to come due within five years, after five years the borrower may pay off the principal and interest plus three months' interest.
卷面犯罪利率仍是超过六十The criminal rate on the paper is still over sixtycriminal interest rate考试口径写有效年利率超过60%构成刑事犯罪；现行《刑法》第347(2)条自2025-01-01起改为年百分率超过35%，借款人不是自然人且用于商业目的时，本金超过 $10,000 且不超过 $500,000 放宽到48%，超过 $500,000 不设上限。The exam wording states that an effective annual rate over 60% is a criminal offence. Section 347(2) of the current Criminal Code, from 2025-01-01, changes this to an annual percentage rate over 35%. Where the borrower is not a natural person and the loan is for a business purpose, a principal over $10,000 and not over $500,000 is relaxed to 48%, and a principal over $500,000 has no ceiling.
承担之后原借款人默示仍负责After assumption the original borrower is still impliedly liableassumption / novation买家承担后，原借款人默示仍对个人契诺负责，住宅按揭才有三条解除路径：过户后三个月内申请书面批准且贷款人不得无理拒绝、到期后三个月内未被书面催告、或见索即付按揭在贷款人收到承担通知后三个月内未被催告；商业按揭要靠更替契约，条件是新借款人承担全部责任、被接受为主债务人、新约完全替代旧约。After the buyer assumes, the original borrower remains impliedly liable on the personal covenant. Only a residential mortgage has three release paths: apply for written approval within three months after completion, and the lender must not refuse unreasonably; no written demand within three months after expiry; or, on a demand mortgage, no demand within three months after the lender receives notice of the assumption. A commercial mortgage depends on a novation. The conditions are that the new borrower assumes the whole liability, is accepted as the primary debtor, and the new agreement fully replaces the old one.
顺位看登记，方向是赎上止下Priority follows registration, and the direction is redeem up, foreclose downpriority / redeem up, foreclose down按揭权人之间按登记先后定优先，不是按签约日或放款日；止赎只能抹掉排在自己后面的权益，借款人赎回必须从最低顺位往上；后续放款只要没收到后手书面登记通知、或后手尚未登记、或按揭约定必须放款、或已有书面优先协议之一，就保住先顺位，口头通知不算，物业税则不登记也优先。As between mortgagees, priority follows who registered first, not the signing date or the date of the advance. Foreclosure can wipe out only interests that rank behind. The borrower must redeem from the lowest rank upward. A later advance keeps the earlier priority if any one of these is true: no written notice of the later registration was received, the later interest is not yet registered, the mortgage requires that the advance be made, or a written priority agreement is already in place. Oral notice does not count. Property taxes have priority even without registration.

If a judicial sale price is not enough, the respondent remains liable. The proceeds pay property taxes and utilities, strata arrears, and commission first, then the lender's principal, interest, and legal costs, and the balance is paid into court.

Under the first, the seller keeps registered ownership, the buyer's right to purchase is registered as a charge on Form C, and title transfers only when payment is complete. Under the second, the buyer first receives the fee simple and the seller then registers a mortgage.

第 1 题Q1最终令不能再追差额A final order cannot claim the difference★★陷阱：止赎两条路的后果记反

A lender in British Columbia holds a registered mortgage that is in default. The home is worth less than the amount owing. The lender wants both to become the owner and to sue the borrower for the unpaid difference. An associate says a power of sale in the mortgage allows a sale without a court proceeding, as in some other provinces. Which statement is the exam answer?

一家贷款人在不列颠哥伦比亚持有已登记并且已经违约的按揭。房屋价值低于欠款。贷款人既想成为所有人，又想起诉借款人追讨不足的差额。同事说，按揭里的售卖权可以像某些其他省份那样，不经法院就把房子卖掉。按考试口径，哪一项正确？

  1. ✓ 正确。BC没有非诉讼的私力售卖（power of sale），止赎必须以呈请（petition）向最高法院提起。物业价值低于欠款时可以申请最终止赎令（final order of foreclosure，考试口径亦称 order absolute）。该令使贷款人成为产权人，衡平赎回权与个人契诺一并消灭，不能再追差额。两个名称指的是同一件事。Correct. BC has no non-court power of sale. Foreclosure must be started by petition in the Supreme Court. Where the property is worth less than the amount owing, a final order of foreclosure may be sought. The exam wording also calls that order an order absolute. The order makes the lender the owner and extinguishes both the equity of redemption and the personal covenant, so the unpaid difference cannot be claimed. The two names refer to the same thing.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是止赎两条路的后果记反，把外省程序套了进来。没有任何一条允许贷款人跳过法院、依约定条款自行出售。BC不能用 power of sale 代替向法院提出的呈请。Wrong. This swaps the results of the two foreclosure routes and imports an out-of-province procedure. Nothing allows the lender to skip the court and sell under a contractual clause. In BC a power of sale cannot replace a petition to the court.

  3. ✗ 错误。这是止赎两条路的后果记反。最终止赎令消灭个人契诺，差额不能再追。售价不足仍然要负责的，是司法拍卖（judicial sale），不是最终令。Wrong. This swaps the results of the two foreclosure routes. A final order of foreclosure extinguishes the personal covenant, so the difference cannot be claimed. The route on which a price that falls short still leaves liability is a judicial sale, not the final order.

  4. ✗ 错误。这是止赎两条路的后果记反，用价值高低决定要不要上法院。价值低于欠款只说明可以申请最终止赎令，不改变必须先由法院处理。法院命令作出之前，借款人仍保有所有权。Wrong. This swaps the results of the two foreclosure routes and uses the level of value to decide whether to go to court. A value below the debt only shows that a final order of foreclosure may be sought. It does not change the need for the court to deal with the matter first. Until the court order is made, the borrower still holds ownership.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是用现行红线答卷面，还把手册说成已经改写。考试口径仍是超过60%的有效年利率。商业档是现行条例，不是这道题的得分句。Wrong. This answers the paper with the current ceiling and also says the manual has already been rewritten. The exam wording is still an effective annual rate over 60%. The business bands are the current regulation. They are not the sentence that scores on this item.

  3. ✓ 正确。这名个人的家庭借款按课程手册已经超过60%有效年利率（effective annual rate），应当认定为犯罪利率，商业档不是手册的判断标准。现行法自2025-01-01起以年百分率超过35%为红线，自然人的消费借款也进不了商业例外，但不能用那一套改这道题的答案。Correct. On the course manual, this individual's household loan is already over an effective annual rate of 60% and is the criminal interest rate. The business bands are not the manual's test. From 2025-01-01 the current law uses an annual percentage rate over 35% as the ceiling, and a natural person's consumer loan also cannot enter the business exception. That current scheme cannot be used to change the answer on this item.

  4. ✗ 错误。这是用现行红线答卷面，把个人借款说成任何利率都犯罪。考试口径不是个人即犯罪，而是有效年利率有没有超过60%。没超过这条线的个人借款，手册并不把它写成犯罪利率。Wrong. This answers the paper with the current ceiling and says that a loan to an individual is criminal at any rate. The exam test is not that an individual borrower means an offence. It is whether the effective annual rate is over 60%. An individual loan that does not cross that line is not written in the manual as a criminal interest rate.

答案Answer：c

第 6 题Q6卷面仍答转让性质The paper still answers with the character of a transfer★★★陷阱：用现行定性答卷面

A course-manual item asks how the manual characterises a mortgage in British Columbia. The item says to use the manual's mainstream view. Which statement is the exam answer?

一道课程手册题目问：手册如何定性不列颠哥伦比亚的按揭。题目要求采用手册里的主流观点。按考试口径，应当选哪一项？

  1. ✗ 错误。手册写明按揭在法律上不是贷款（a mortgage is not a loan）。按揭不是那笔钱本身，而是用来给那笔钱做担保的土地权益。答成只是贷款、土地上没有权益，和手册相反。Wrong. The manual states that in law a mortgage is not a loan. A mortgage is not the money itself. It is an interest in land given as security for that money. Answering that it is only a loan, and that there is no interest in the land, is the opposite of the manual.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是把历史上的整体转让说成了登记簿改名。按揭权人从来不是注册所有人，产权登记簿上按揭人的名字一直在。现行《土地产权法》第231(1)条也是先把它写成押记，而不是把所有权过给贷款人。Wrong. This turns the historical transfer of the whole interest into a change of the name on the register. The mortgagee has never been the registered owner. The mortgagor's name stays on the title register. Section 231(1) of the current Land Title Act also writes it first as a charge, and does not pass ownership to the lender.

  3. ✓ 正确。考试口径的主流观点是：按揭仍保留普通法上转让（transfer）的性质，卷面按这一句作答。现行《土地产权法》第231(1)条把登记按揭首先定为押记（charge），第231(2)条只拟制视同已转让的权利义务，给客户说明时用押记这一套，不能拿去改卷面。Correct. The mainstream view on the exam is that a mortgage still retains the common-law character of a transfer, and the paper is answered with that sentence. Section 231(1) of the current Land Title Act first defines a registered mortgage as a charge. Section 231(2) only deems the rights and obligations to be those of a transfer that has been made. Use the charge account when explaining to a client. Do not use it to change the paper.

  4. ✗ 错误。这是用现行定性答卷面。押记加上视同已转让，是现行《土地产权法》第231条的精确表述，不是手册要你选的主流观点。这道题要答仍保留转让性质。Wrong. This answers the paper with the current characterisation. A charge, together with a deemed transfer, is the precise wording of section 231 of the current Land Title Act. It is not the mainstream view the manual asks you to select. This item is answered by saying that the character of a transfer is still retained.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A candidate is told to answer from the course manual. The candidate wants to select the statement that a registered mortgage is a charge whether or not the document uses words of transfer. The item asks for the manual's mainstream view. Which statement is the exam answer?

考生被要求按课程手册作答。考生想选这一句：已登记按揭是一项押记，不论文件里有没有使用转让字样。题目问的是手册的主流观点。按考试口径，哪一项正确？

  1. ✓ 正确。题目要手册的主流观点，就答按揭仍保留普通法上的转让性质。现行《土地产权法》第231(1)条才写明登记按揭首先是押记、不论有没有转让字样，这一句不能填进这道题。Correct. The item asks for the manual's mainstream view, so the answer is that a mortgage still retains the common-law character of a transfer. Only section 231(1) of the current Land Title Act states that a registered mortgage is first a charge, whether or not the document uses words of transfer. That sentence cannot be entered on this item.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是用现行定性答卷面。押记是现行法第231(1)条的定性，不是手册的主流观点。手册保留的是转让性质。Wrong. This answers the paper with the current characterisation. A charge is the characterisation in section 231(1) of the current Act. It is not the manual's mainstream view. What the manual retains is the character of a transfer.

  3. ✗ 错误。这是用现行定性答卷面，又加了一层登记簿改名。手册的主流观点不取决于文件里有没有转让字样，也没有把贷款人写成注册所有人。现行法第231(1)条同样说，不论有没有转让字样，效果首先是押记。Wrong. This answers the paper with the current characterisation and adds a change of the name on the register. The manual's mainstream view does not depend on whether the document uses the word transfer, and it does not make the lender the registered owner. Section 231(1) of the current Act likewise says that, whether or not there are words of transfer, the effect is first a charge.

  4. ✗ 错误。手册否认的是按揭等于贷款本身，不是否认土地上的权益。转让性质和押记都是在描述那份土地权益，不能答成土地上什么都没有。Wrong. What the manual denies is that a mortgage equals the loan itself. It does not deny an interest in the land. Both the character of a transfer and a charge describe that interest in land. The answer cannot be that there is nothing in the land.

第 7 题Q7分期合约卖方留产权Under an agreement for sale the seller keeps title★陷阱：两种卖方融资当成同一种

A seller will be paid in instalments. One structure transfers the fee simple to the buyer now and registers a mortgage back to the seller. The other leaves the seller as the registered owner, and the buyer's right to purchase is registered as a charge until the last payment. Which statement matches the course?

卖方的价款将分期收取。一种结构是现在就把完全所有权过给买家，再把按揭登记回卖方名下。另一种是卖方继续做登记所有人，买家的购买权先作为押记登记，直到付清最后一期。哪一项符合课程？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是两种卖方融资当成同一种。财务上两者等价，法律上不同。卖方分期融资里买家先拿到完全所有权；agreement for sale 里买家在付清之前不是登记所有人。Wrong. This treats the two kinds of seller financing as the same thing. They are equivalent in finance and different in law. Under a vendor take-back the buyer first receives the fee simple. Under an agreement for sale the buyer is not the registered owner until payment is complete.

  2. ✓ 正确。第一种是卖方分期融资（vendor take-back mortgage）：买家先登记为完全所有权人，卖方登记按揭作为回款担保。第二种是 agreement for sale：卖方保留登记所有权，买家的购买权（right to purchase）用 Form C 登记为押记，付清最后一期才过户。Correct. The first is a vendor take-back mortgage. The buyer is registered first as the owner of the fee simple, and the seller registers a mortgage as security for the balance. The second is an agreement for sale. The seller keeps registered ownership, the buyer's right to purchase is registered as a charge on Form C, and title transfers only when the last payment is made.

  3. ✗ 错误。这是两种卖方融资当成同一种，把名字对调了。先把完全所有权过给买家、再登记按揭，是卖方分期融资。按揭登记不会让卖方继续当注册所有人。Wrong. This treats the two kinds of seller financing as the same thing and swaps the names. Transferring the fee simple to the buyer first, and then registering a mortgage, is a vendor take-back. Registering that mortgage does not leave the seller as the registered owner.

  4. ✗ 错误。这是两种卖方融资当成同一种，换成了另一种特殊按揭。倒按揭（reverse annuity mortgage）是贷款人分期付钱给借款人，到期或身故再清偿。本题是买家分期付房价，不是贷款人分期付钱给业主。Wrong. This treats the two kinds of seller financing as the same thing and substitutes another special mortgage. A reverse annuity mortgage is the lender paying the borrower in instalments, repaid at maturity or on death. This question is the buyer paying the price in instalments. It is not the lender paying the owner in instalments.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A buyer under an agreement for sale wants proof of being the registered owner so a new mortgage can be granted before the last instalment is paid. The seller is still shown as the owner. The buyer's right to purchase is already on title as a charge. Which statement matches the course?

分期付款合约的买家想证明自己已经是登记所有人，以便在付清最后一期之前再办一份新按揭。卖方的名字仍在所有人栏。买家的购买权已经作为押记登在产权上。哪一项符合课程？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是两种卖方融资当成同一种。购买权（right to purchase）是买家权益的押记，不是完全所有权已经过户。登记所有人仍然是卖方。Wrong. This treats the two kinds of seller financing as the same thing. A right to purchase is a charge for the buyer's interest. It is not a completed transfer of the fee simple. The registered owner is still the seller.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是两种卖方融资当成同一种，把 Form C 当成了过户。这份登记是买家的购买权押记。完全所有权要等最后一期付清，才由卖方过户。Wrong. This treats the two kinds of seller financing as the same thing and treats Form C as the transfer. That filing is a charge for the buyer's right to purchase. The fee simple waits until the last instalment is paid, and only then does the seller transfer it.

  3. ✗ 错误。这是两种卖方融资当成同一种，把卖方分期融资的结果套了过来。买家先拿到完全所有权，是 take-back 的结构。agreement for sale 故意把卖方留在登记所有人的位置上。Wrong. This treats the two kinds of seller financing as the same thing and imports the result of a vendor take-back. The buyer receiving the fee simple first is the take-back structure. An agreement for sale deliberately leaves the seller in the place of registered owner.

  4. ✓ 正确。agreement for sale 里卖方全程是登记所有人，买家的权益是一份押记，付清最后一期才过户。卖方分期融资（vendor take-back mortgage）才是买家先拿完全所有权、卖方再登记按揭。Correct. Under an agreement for sale the seller is the registered owner throughout, the buyer's interest is a charge, and title transfers only when the last payment is made. A vendor take-back mortgage is the arrangement in which the buyer first receives the fee simple and the seller then registers a mortgage.
