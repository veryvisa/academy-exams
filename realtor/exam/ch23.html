本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 算式题：楼已经满租，合同租金却低于同区，问潜在毛收入用哪一个数、空置准备放在哪一行。真正考的是按当前市场租金假设满租，空置按同类长期经验从毛收入里折减，不是一项运营费用。Calculation questions: the building is already fully leased, but the contract rent is below the area, and the question asks which figure is potential gross revenue and which line the vacancy allowance sits on. What is tested is assuming full occupancy at current market rent, and deducting vacancy from gross revenue on the long-run experience of similar properties, not as an operating expense.
  • 排除题：折旧、资本成本抵扣、所得税、按揭本息和物业税写在同一张清单上，问净营业收入减哪些。真正考的是前四项因人而异必须排除，净营业收入是融资前对整栋楼的回报。Exclusion questions: depreciation, capital cost allowance, income tax, mortgage principal and interest, and property tax are written on the same list, and the question asks which of them net operating income subtracts. What is tested is that the first four vary by person and must be excluded, and that net operating income is the return on the whole building before financing.
  • 资本化题：给出成交价、有效毛收入和净营业收入，或把资本化率从 7.5% 改到 7.0%，问比率和价值。真正考的是用净营业收入除以成交价来反推，分母小改会把价格放大，可比要当成一个整体看。Capitalization questions: the stem gives the sale price, effective gross income, and net operating income, or moves the capitalization rate from 7.5% to 7.0%, and asks for the rate and the value. What is tested is deriving the rate by dividing net operating income by the sale price, that a small change in the denominator magnifies the price, and that a comparable has to be judged as a whole.
  • 快算对照题：总收入乘数和直接资本化给出两个价格，可比的乘数看起来很整齐，标的费用率却更高。真正考的是乘数只认有效毛收入，费用更高就会系统性偏高，差距用来回头检查假设。Quick-check comparison questions: the gross income multiplier and direct capitalization give two prices. The comparables' multiplier looks tidy, but the subject's expense ratio is higher. What is tested is that the multiplier recognizes only effective gross income, that a higher expense ratio makes the result systematically high, and that the gap is used to go back and check the assumptions.
  • 边界题：毛租还是净租、一笔屋顶大修进不进今年的费用、客户拿这个数字去贷款还是只用来挂牌。真正考的是费用归谁、一次性支出从价值里扣一次、持牌人的价格意见不是正式估价。Boundary questions: whether the lease is gross or net, whether a one-time roof repair enters this year's expenses, and whether the client takes the figure to a lender or uses it only to list. What is tested is who bears the expenses, that a one-time outlay is deducted once from value, and that a licensee's price opinion is not a formal appraisal.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 满租当最高租金Full occupancy as the top rent

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 100% 出租看起来已经收到了这栋楼能收的全部租金，于是用合同租金当潜在毛收入。老租客长期不涨，账面可以低于当期市场。100% occupancy looks as if the building has already collected every dollar of rent it can collect, so the contract rent is used as potential gross revenue. Long-term tenants whose rent was never raised can leave the books below the current market.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写明已经满租，或租客住了很多年，问还要不要改用当前市场租金。The stem states that the building is already fully leased, or that the tenants have lived there for many years, and asks whether current market rent must still be used.

⚠ 按揭塞进费用Mortgage stuffed into expenses

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 每年都要付的本息很像物业税和保险，就被当成运营费用扣掉。扣完的数只对这一笔贷款有意义，换一个买家就变了。Principal and interest paid every year look like property tax and insurance, so they are deducted as an operating expense. The figure that remains means something only for that one loan, and it changes with a different buyer.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 费用清单里出现按揭本息、折旧、所得税或资本成本抵扣，问净营业收入要不要减。The expense list includes mortgage principal and interest, depreciation, income tax, or capital cost allowance, and the question asks whether net operating income subtracts them.

⚠ 毛收入算资本化率Cap rate from gross income

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 有效毛收入好找，用它去除以成交价，公式外形和资本化率一样。费用还没扣，两栋费用结构不同的楼被当成同一个收益率。Effective gross income is easy to find, and dividing it by the sale price has the same shape as the capitalization-rate formula. Expenses have not been deducted, so two buildings with different expense structures are treated as the same yield.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出有效毛收入和净营业收入，问资本化率用哪一个去除以成交价。The stem gives both effective gross income and net operating income, and asks which one is divided by the sale price to get the capitalization rate.

⚠ 快算当终值Quick estimate as the final value

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 两个可比的总收入乘数是同一个数，快算又简单，就把较高的那个价格当成结论。标的费用率更高时，这个价格会系统性偏高。Two comparables share one gross income multiplier, and the quick estimate is simple, so the higher price is taken as the conclusion. When the subject's expense ratio is higher, that price is systematically high.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 直接资本化和总收入乘数差出一截，题干又给出标的的费用率高于可比。Direct capitalization and the gross income multiplier are a long way apart, and the stem also says the subject's expense ratio is higher than the comparables'.

⚠ 大修按年摊Major repair spread by the year

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 屋顶这类支出也叫维修，就被加进今年的年度维修费，再随净营业收入年年资本化。一次性的钱被当成每年都要再花一次。An outlay such as a roof is also called a repair, so it is added to this year's annual repair expense and then capitalized every year with net operating income. A one-time sum is treated as money that must be spent again every year.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出一个明确的一次性金额，并问它进年度运营费用，还是从价值里扣一次。The stem gives a stated one-time amount and asks whether it enters annual operating expenses or is deducted once from value.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
收益法估的是市场价值The income approach estimates market valueIncome (Investment) Approach收益法只用于能产生租金收入的物业，价格是买家为这份净营业收入愿意支付的资本额，不是某一个业主个人的价值。The income (investment) approach is used only for property that can produce rental income. The price is the capital a buyer will pay for that net operating income, not the personal value to one owner.
潜在毛收入用市场租金Potential gross revenue uses market rentGross Potential Revenue潜在毛收入按当前市场租金假设 100% 出租，再加上车位、储物或洗衣等其他收入，满租仍要和当期市场行情比较。Potential gross revenue assumes 100% occupancy at current market rent, plus other income such as parking, storage, or laundry. A full building is still compared with the current market.
空置准备不是运营费用The vacancy allowance is not an operating expenseVacancy and Bad Debt Allowance空置与坏账准备从潜在毛收入中折减，依据同类物业的长期空置经验，不用标的过去一年的实际数字，也不列入运营费用。The vacancy and bad debt allowance is deducted from potential gross revenue. It is based on the long-run vacancy experience of similar properties. It does not use the subject's actual figure for the past year, and it is not listed as an operating expense.
净营业收入只扣业主的费用Net operating income deducts only the owner's expensesNet Operating Income有效毛收入减去业主承担的运营费用得到净营业收入，物业税和保险是固定费用，管理、水电、维修等是可变费用，物业税通常是最大单项。Effective gross income minus the operating expenses the owner bears equals net operating income. Property tax and insurance are fixed expenses. Management, utilities, repairs, and similar items are variable expenses. Property tax is usually the largest single item.
四项支出排除在外Four outflows are excludedDebt Service折旧、资本成本抵扣、所得税和按揭还款都因业主而异，必须排除，净营业收入是融资前对全部物业价值的回报。Depreciation, capital cost allowance, income tax, and debt service all vary by owner and must be excluded. Net operating income is the return on the whole property value before financing.
三把尺子决定要求回报Three measures set the required returnCapitalization Rate风险、管理要求和流动性越差，要求的回报率越高，所以政府公债最低，出租公寓更高，工业厂房往往还要更高。The greater the risk, the heavier the management requirements, and the poorer the liquidity, the higher the required rate of return. A government bond is the lowest, a rental apartment is higher, and an industrial building often has to be higher still.
价值等于净收入除以率Value equals net income divided by the rateDirect Capitalization资本化率从近期类似风险的可比销售用净营业收入除以成交价反推，市场价值等于净营业收入除以资本化率，不能用有效毛收入去除以成交价，资本化率只降 0.5 个百分点，同一笔净营业收入 $97,050 就会从 $1,294,000 升到约 $1,386,000。The capitalization rate is derived from recent comparable sales of similar risk by dividing net operating income by the sale price. Market value equals net operating income divided by the capitalization rate. Effective gross income must not be divided by the sale price. If the capitalization rate falls by only 0.5 of a percentage point, the same net operating income of $97,050 rises from $1,294,000 to about $1,386,000.
总收入乘数忽略费用The gross income multiplier ignores expensesGross Income Multiplier总收入乘数等于成交价除以有效毛收入，快算价值等于标的有效毛收入乘以该乘数，费用率高于可比时这个快算会系统性偏高，所以它只用来和直接资本化的结果交叉核对。The gross income multiplier equals the sale price divided by effective gross income. The quick value equals the subject's effective gross income multiplied by that multiplier. When the expense ratio is higher than the comparables, this quick estimate is systematically high, so it is used only to cross-check the direct capitalization result.
杠杆方向看借款成本The direction of leverage follows borrowing costPositive Leverage借款成本低于资本化率是正杠杆，权益回报率高于资本化率，借款成本更高则是负杠杆，现金流折现超出课程范围。Borrowing cost below the capitalization rate is positive leverage, and the equity yield is above the capitalization rate. A higher borrowing cost is negative leverage. Discounted cash flow is beyond the scope of the course.
租约决定费用归谁The lease decides who bears the expensesTriple Net Lease毛租由业主承担运营费用，三重净租由租客承担全部运营费用、业主只管结构性维修，持牌人的价格意见不是正式估价报告。On a gross lease the owner bears the operating expenses. On a triple net lease the tenant bears all operating expenses, and the owner looks after structural repairs only. A licensee's price opinion is not a formal appraisal report.

第 1 题Q1潜在毛收入用市场租金Potential gross revenue uses market rent★★陷阱：满租当最高租金

A six-unit apartment building is fully leased. The leases were signed years ago, and the rents were never brought up to what similar units now rent for. Laundry income is collected separately. For the licensing exam, which figure is potential gross revenue?

一栋 6 个单元的公寓楼已经满租。租约是多年前签的，租金一直没有调到同类单元现在的市场水平。洗衣房收入另行收取。就执照考试而言，潜在毛收入是哪一个数？

  1. ✗ 这是「满租当最高租金」。考试口径明确说，即使满租，仍要拿租金和当期市场行情比；老租客长期不涨，账面可以低于市场。洗衣房等其他收入通常还要单独加上，不能丢掉。This treats full occupancy as the top rent. The exam wording says plainly that even a full building is still compared with the current market. Long-term tenants whose rent was never raised can leave the books below the market. Other income such as laundry is usually added on its own and cannot be dropped.

  2. ✓ 潜在毛收入（Gross Potential Revenue）假设 100% 出租，按当前市场租金而不是账面旧租金，再加上其他收入。考试要的就是这个数。现行《住宅租赁法》下，同一租客不换时不能按市场行情自由涨租，2026 年上限为 2.3%、2027 年为 2.2%，只有换新租客才能按市场租金重签。Potential gross revenue assumes 100% occupancy at current market rent rather than the old rent on the books, plus other income. That is the figure the exam wants. Under the current Residential Tenancy Act, rent cannot be freely raised to the market while the same tenant stays. The cap is 2.3% for 2026 and 2.2% for 2027. Only when a new tenant takes the unit can the lease be signed again at market rent.

  3. ✗ 2.3% 是现行年度涨租上限，不是考试用来计算潜在毛收入的规则。把现行上限写成卷面算法，就是用现行法代替考试口径。潜在毛收入仍按当前市场租金、并假设满租。2.3% is the current annual rent-increase cap. It is not the rule the exam uses to calculate potential gross revenue. Writing the current cap into the exam calculation substitutes the current statute for the exam wording. Potential gross revenue is still current market rent, assuming full occupancy.

  4. ✗ 潜在毛收入在四行算式的最上面，还没有扣空置，也没有扣运营费用。净营业收入（Net Operating Income）更在有效毛收入之后，按揭还款还不进这一行。这一项把第一行和最后一行并成了一行。Potential gross revenue is the top line of the four-line calculation. Vacancy has not been deducted, and operating expenses have not been deducted. Net operating income comes after effective gross income, and the mortgage payment does not enter this line either. This item folds the first line and the last line into one line.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A fully leased industrial building has one long-term tenant. The contract rent is below what similar industrial space now leases for. Reserved parking brings in separate monthly income. For the licensing exam, which amount is potential gross revenue?

一栋工业楼已经满租，只有一个长期租户。合同租金低于同类工业空间现在的租金。预留车位另有每月收入。就执照考试而言，潜在毛收入是哪一个数？

  1. ✗ 这是「满租当最高租金」。老租约可以低于当期市场，在工业、商业和投资类物业里，能识别账面租金低于市场本身就是一项专业能力。车位收入是其他收入，不该丢掉。This treats full occupancy as the top rent. An old lease can sit below the current market. On industrial, commercial, and investment property, recognizing that the rent on the books is below the market is itself a professional skill. Parking income is other income and should not be dropped.

  2. ✗ 空置与坏账准备（Vacancy and Bad Debt Allowance）在潜在毛收入之后才扣，而且看同类长期经验，不看这栋楼去年。车位是其他收入，要加进潜在毛收入，不是运营费用。The vacancy and bad debt allowance is deducted only after potential gross revenue, and it looks at the long-run experience of similar properties, not at this building last year. Parking is other income. It is added into potential gross revenue. It is not an operating expense.

  3. ✗ 净营业收入在业主承担的运营费用之后。按揭还款（Debt Service）不进净营业收入，更不能反过来定义潜在毛收入。潜在毛收入是算式的第一行。Net operating income comes after the operating expenses the owner bears. Debt service does not enter net operating income, and it cannot be turned around to define potential gross revenue. Potential gross revenue is the first line of the calculation.

  4. ✓ 潜在毛收入（Gross Potential Revenue）按当前市场租金假设 100% 出租，车位这类其他收入单独加上。楼已经满租、租约还在，都不等于租金已经达到市场水平。Potential gross revenue assumes 100% occupancy at current market rent, and other income such as parking is added on its own. A building that is already full, with the lease still in place, does not mean the rent has already reached the market.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

第 2 题Q2按揭不进净营业收入The mortgage stays out of net operating income★★陷阱：按揭塞进费用

An investor asks the appraiser to treat four outflows as operating expenses when calculating net operating income: depreciation, capital cost allowance, income tax, and this year's mortgage principal and interest. For the licensing exam, which treatment is right?

一位投资者要求估价师在计算净营业收入时，把四项流出都当成运营费用：折旧、资本成本抵扣、所得税，以及今年的按揭本金和利息。就执照考试而言，哪一种处理是对的？

  1. ✗ 这是「按揭塞进费用」，再把税和折旧一起扣掉。换一个全款买家或另一档税率，数字立刻不一样，资本化率就无法在不同物业、不同买家之间比较。This stuffs the mortgage into the expenses, and then deducts tax and depreciation along with it. Change to an all-cash buyer, or to another tax bracket, and the figure is immediately different. The capitalization rate then cannot be compared across different properties and different buyers.

  2. ✗ 每个买家的融资不同，现金购买、八成按揭、10 年期或 25 年期都会改变这笔还款。只扣按揭，算出来的数仍然只对这一笔贷款有意义。这仍是按揭塞进费用。Each buyer's financing is different. A cash purchase, an 80% mortgage, a 10-year term, or a 25-year term all change this payment. Deducting only the mortgage still leaves a figure that means something only for that one loan. This is still stuffing the mortgage into the expenses.

  3. ✗ 资本成本抵扣（Capital Cost Allowance）只在报税时有意义，所得税随边际税率变化，两者都和市场价值无关。折旧同样排除，不是因为另外两项是现金就可以留下。四项都因业主而异。Capital cost allowance matters only when a tax return is filed. Income tax changes with the marginal tax rate. Neither has anything to do with market value. Depreciation is excluded as well. The other two items are not kept just because they are cash. All four vary by owner.

  4. ✓ 折旧（Depreciation）、资本成本抵扣、所得税和按揭还款（Debt Service）都排除。净营业收入（Net Operating Income）是融资前对物业全部价值的回报，不是对某一个买家自有资金的回报，这样不同买家才能比较同一栋楼。Depreciation, capital cost allowance, income tax, and debt service are all excluded. Net operating income is the return on the whole property value before financing, not the return on one buyer's own money. That way different buyers can compare the same building.

答案Answer：d

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Two buyers want the same rental building. One will pay all cash. The other will use a mortgage. The file shows a different net operating income for each buyer, because the mortgage payment was deducted for the second buyer only. What should the appraiser do?

两位买家要买同一栋出租楼。一位全款，一位使用按揭。卷宗里因此出现两个净营业收入，因为只给第二位买家扣了按揭还款。估价师应该怎么做？

  1. ✓ 净营业收入（Net Operating Income）必须是融资前的数字，两位买家用同一个数。按揭还款（Debt Service）随贷款条件改变，不是运营费用。扣掉它，资本化率就只对这一笔贷款有意义。Net operating income must be the figure before financing, and both buyers use the same figure. Debt service changes with the loan terms. It is not an operating expense. Deduct it, and the capitalization rate means something only for that one loan.

  2. ✗ 按揭放款、遗产分割和诉讼都在课程点名的正式估价用途里。不是只有打官司才需要有专业估价资质的人。放贷要依靠的那份文件，不能由持牌人的价格意见来充当。A mortgage advance, a division of an estate, and a lawsuit are all among the formal-appraisal uses the course names. A court case is not the only time a person with professional appraisal qualifications is needed. The document a lender relies on cannot be filled by a licensee's price opinion.

  3. ✓ 持牌人做的是价格意见，不是正式估价报告（appraisal）。供按揭放款、遗产分割、诉讼使用的正式估价，通常要由具备专业估价资质的执业估价师出具。挂牌或出价参考可以停留在价格意见。What a licensee prepares is a price opinion, not a formal appraisal report. A formal appraisal for a mortgage advance, a division of an estate, or a lawsuit is usually issued by a practising appraiser who holds professional appraisal qualifications. A reference for a list price or an offer can remain a price opinion.

  4. ✗ 资本化率取整与否改变不了文件的性质。边界在于谁有资格出具正式估价，不在于数字圆不圆。把两者说成同一份报告，就是意见说成估价。Whether the capitalization rate was rounded does not change what the document is. The boundary is who is qualified to issue a formal appraisal, not whether the figure is a round number. Calling the two the same report is calling an opinion an appraisal.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A licensee tells a seller, "I have appraised your building at $1,294,000." The work was a price opinion to help set a list price, not a report prepared for a lender, an estate, or a lawsuit. What is the problem?

持牌人对卖方说：「我已经把你的楼估成 $1,294,000。」这份工作是用来帮助确定挂牌价的价格意见，不是为贷款机构、遗产或诉讼准备的报告。问题在哪里？

  1. ✓ 把自己给客户的价格意见说成评估或估价，模糊了持牌人和执业估价师（appraiser）的边界，也模糊了责任。$1,294,000 即使来自净营业收入除以资本化率，也不使这份挂牌意见变成正式估价报告（appraisal）。Calling the price opinion given to the client an appraisal blurs the line between the licensee and the practising appraiser, and it blurs the responsibility. Even if $1,294,000 comes from net operating income divided by the capitalization rate, that does not turn this list-price opinion into a formal appraisal report.

  2. ✗ 公式用得对，改变不了签字的人有没有正式估价的资质。挂牌用的价格意见可以讨论这个数字，但不能因此叫做评估。这是意见说成估价。Using the formula correctly does not change whether the person who signs holds the qualifications for a formal appraisal. A price opinion used for a listing can discuss this figure. It is not called an appraisal because of that. This calls an opinion an appraisal.

  3. ✗ 总收入乘数和直接资本化都是估价方法里的算法，选用哪一个并不决定持牌人的价格意见是不是正式估价。角色边界与用了哪条算式无关。The gross income multiplier and direct capitalization are both calculations inside the appraisal method. Which one is chosen does not decide whether a licensee's price opinion is a formal appraisal. The boundary of the role has nothing to do with which formula was used.

  4. ✗ 挂牌参考同样不能说成评估。贷款、遗产分割和诉讼需要正式估价，是用途上的区分；价格意见在任何一种用途里都不会因为换了一个听众就变成估价报告。A reference for a list price cannot be called an appraisal either. A loan, a division of an estate, and a lawsuit needing a formal appraisal is a distinction of use. A price opinion does not become an appraisal report in any of those uses just because the audience changed.
