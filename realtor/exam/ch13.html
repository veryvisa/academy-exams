① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 换算辨析题：给出带复利频率的名义利率，考你会不会把按半年复利的 j₂ 直接除以 12 当月利率，以及两个复利频率不同的报价必须先换成等价年利率再比谁贵。Conversion questions give a nominal rate together with its compounding frequency. They test whether you divide a semi-annually compounded j₂ by 12 and treat the result as the monthly rate, and whether two quotes with different compounding frequencies must first be converted to effective annual rates before you compare which one costs more.
  • 还款结构题：用一段现金流（期间不付、每期只付利息、等额本金加递减利息、或每期总额固定），考你归入哪一种还款计划，并判断摊还期与合同期是否同一天还清、到期有没有尾款。Repayment-structure questions give a cash flow (nothing paid during the term, interest only each period, equal principal plus declining interest, or a fixed total each period). They test which repayment plan you place it in, and whether the amortization period and the term pay the loan off on the same day or leave a balance owing at maturity.
  • 法条边界题：用个人或公司借款人、合同是否超过五年、利率有没有按年或半年复利写明，考《利息法》s.6 不能收息和 s.10 满五年后个人可加三个月利息提前还清，两条都问适用边界。Statutory-boundary questions use an individual or a corporate borrower, whether the contract runs longer than five years, and whether the rate is stated as compounded annually or semi-annually. They test Interest Act s.6 (no interest at all may be collected if the compounding disclosure is missing) and s.10 (after five years an individual may prepay in full by adding three months' interest), and in both cases where the section's boundary lies.
  • 市场机制题：用储蓄者、贷款机构或小投资人的处境，考一级市场与二级市场、按揭作为投资的五个特征，以及利率由机会成本、风险补偿和管理成本一起定出来。Market-mechanism questions put a saver, a lending institution, or a small investor in a situation. They test the primary market and the secondary market, the five features of a mortgage as an investment, and that the rate is set from opportunity cost, a risk premium, and administration cost together.
  • 口径与角色题：用旧资料上的公司名、客户追问精确月供、或开发商项目是否安全，考卷面要的是分类而不是现行招牌，以及持牌代表只解释概念、不替客户出具贷款计算结果。Exam-scope and role questions use a company name from old materials, a client who presses for an exact monthly payment, or whether a developer's project is safe. They test that the paper wants the category rather than the current brand, and that a licensee only explains the concept and does not issue a loan calculation for the client.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 年利率除以十二Dividing the annual rate by twelve

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 住宅按揭几乎都用按半年复利的名义年利率报价，还款却按月。考生看到按月还款，就把合同上的年利率直接除以 12，当成月供用的周期利率。Almost every residential mortgage is quoted as a nominal annual rate compounded semi-annually, while the payments are monthly. A candidate sees the monthly payments and divides the annual rate on the contract by 12, treating that as the periodic rate used for the payment.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写了 compounded semi-annually 或 j₂，同时又是 monthly payments，选项里出现 divide by 12 或 0.5%。The stem says compounded semi-annually or j₂, and also monthly payments, and an option contains divide by 12 or 0.5%.

⚠ 名义数字直接比Comparing the nominal figures directly

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 两家报价的复利频率不同时，名义利率更低的那家，等价年利率可能更高。考生只看合同上印的那个百分数，觉得数字小就更便宜。When two quotes compound at different frequencies, the one with the lower nominal rate can have the higher effective annual rate. The candidate looks only at the percentage printed on the contract and treats the smaller number as the cheaper loan.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 两个百分数很接近，一个写 semi-annually，另一个写 monthly，题目问哪一家真实年成本更低。Two percentages sit close together. One says semi-annually and the other says monthly, and the question asks which one has the lower true annual cost.

⚠ 摊还期当成还清日Treating the amortization period as the payoff date

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 常见住宅按揭按很长的摊还期计算月供，合同期却短得多。考生把「按 25 年的节奏在还」理解成合同期结束时本金已经为零。A typical residential mortgage calculates the monthly payment over a long amortization period, while the term is much shorter. The candidate reads paying on a 25-year schedule as meaning the principal is already zero when the term ends.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 同一题里同时出现 amortization period 和 term，并问合同到期还要不要付余额、续约或转贷。The same item contains both amortization period and term, and asks whether a balance must still be paid when the contract ends, or whether the loan must be renewed or moved to another lender.

⚠ 利息法对象看宽Applying the Interest Act too widely

为什么会错：Why people miss it: s.6 管的是不按年或半年复利披露就一分利息都不能收；s.10 只给个人、要从按揭日期满五年起、额外只付三个月利息。考生把保护套到公司、未满五年，或改成三个月本金。Section 6 says that if the rate is not disclosed as compounded annually or semi-annually, no interest at all may be collected. Section 10 is only for an individual, only from the fifth anniversary of the mortgage date, and the extra charge is only three months' interest. Candidates extend the protection to a company, to a mortgage that has not yet reached five years, or rewrite the extra charge as three months of principal.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 借款人是 corporation，或选项改写了已过年数、罚的是本金还是利息、以及缺的是复利披露还是提前还款权。The borrower is a corporation, or an option rewrites how many years have passed, whether what is charged is principal or interest, and whether what is missing is the compounding disclosure or the prepayment right.

⚠ 旧名字当现行分类Treating an old name as the current category

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考试口径点名的保险公司和专营按揭公司后来有的改了名、有的被收购。考生要么把改名当成这个类别已经消失，要么在卷面上用现行招牌替换分类本身。Some insurers and monoline mortgage lenders named in the exam materials were later renamed, and some were acquired. Candidates either treat the renaming as the end of that category, or replace the category itself with the current brand on the exam.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干说课程材料举的公司后来改名或被收购，或问卷面要选的是类别还是现在的招牌。The stem says a firm used as an example in the course materials was later renamed or acquired, or it asks whether the paper wants the category or the current brand.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
购房缺口靠负债补，按揭是产权上的担保Debt fills the purchase gap, and a mortgage is security on titleequity investor / debt investor / mortgage买家很少一次付清、卖家也很少愿意等，这个缺口靠负债补上；贷款人为收回本金而在借款人产权上取得权利，违约后可在清偿在先债权后接管，这项借款就叫按揭。Buyers rarely pay the whole price at once, and sellers rarely want to wait, so debt fills the gap. To recover the principal, the lender takes a right on the borrower's title and, after default, may take over the property once charges that rank ahead have been paid. That borrowing is called a mortgage.
利率是把储蓄导到借款人的价格The interest rate is the price that guides savings to borrowerscapital market / deposit intermediary储蓄者经直接借贷、经纪撮合或存款中介把钱交给借款人，存款中介是当代最主流的路径；按揭需求上升会推高利率，把资金从债券、股票等其他市场吸引过来。Savers pass money to borrowers by direct lending, by a broker match, or through a deposit intermediary, and the deposit intermediary is the dominant path today. Rising mortgage demand pushes the rate up and draws funds over from other markets, such as bonds and stocks.
定价看三样，多数机构不改单笔利率Pricing uses three items, and most institutions do not change the rate on a single loanopportunity cost / risk premium / administration cost贷款人综合资金的机会成本、这笔具体风险的补偿和管理成本来定价；同期限优质第一按揭利率通常高于政府公债，但多数机构维持统一市场利率，用期限、摊还年限和要不要保险来消化风险差异。A lender prices from the opportunity cost of the funds, the risk premium for this specific risk, and the administration cost. A high-quality first mortgage of the same term usually carries a higher rate than a government bond, but most institutions keep one market rate and absorb differences in risk through the term, the amortization period, and whether insurance is required.
一级市场创设新贷，二级市场买卖还款权The primary market creates new loans, and the secondary market trades the right to paymentsprimary mortgage market / secondary mortgage market / MBS / CMBs一级市场是贷款人直接把钱借给购房者、形成新按揭；二级市场买卖已经存在的按揭，买家买到的是收取未来还款的权利；MBS 与 CMBs 让小额投资人不必自己管一整笔按揭也能间接参与。In the primary mortgage market the lender lends directly to the home buyer and creates a new mortgage. The secondary mortgage market buys and sells mortgages that already exist, and the buyer acquires the right to collect future payments. MBS and CMBs let a small investor take part indirectly without servicing one whole mortgage.
MIC 必须把净收入全部分给投资人An MIC must distribute all of its net income to investorsMortgage Investment Corporation (MIC)MIC 依《所得税法》的资格规则运作，条件是把 100% 净收入分配给投资人；它服务被主流机构挡在门外的借款人，利率更高，因为违约风险更高、抵押物或借款人条件通常更边缘。A Mortgage Investment Corporation (MIC) operates under the qualification rules of the Income Tax Act, on the condition that it distributes 100% of net income to investors. It serves borrowers shut out by mainstream institutions, and its rate is higher, because default risk is higher and the security or the borrower is usually more marginal.
五个特征合在一起，市场由大机构主导Taken together, the five features leave large institutions dominating the marketunique / illiquid / reinvestment problem按揭独一无二、难以单独转手、小额还款有再投资难题、管理成本不随贷款变小而等比例缩小、资本门槛高，所以小投资人很难直接持有整笔按揭，要靠 MBS、CMB 或 MIC 间接进入。A mortgage is unique, illiquid if it must be sold on its own, subject to a reinvestment problem on small payments, carries administration costs that do not shrink in proportion as the loan gets smaller, and has a high capital threshold. A small investor therefore finds it hard to hold one whole mortgage directly, and enters indirectly through MBS, CMB, or an MIC.
四种还款加上全额摊还与部分摊还Four repayment plans, plus full amortization and partial amortizationinterest accruing / interest only / straight-line / constant payment / balloon payment只计息不还款风险最高、期限通常一年以内；纯利息常见于建设期；直线本金摊还早期压力最大、住宅里不流行；等额本息是住宅主流。摊还期长于合同期就是部分摊还，到期有尾款；摊还期越长，月供越轻，总利息越贵。Interest accruing carries the highest risk, and the term is usually one year or less. Interest only is common during construction. Straight-line principal reduction puts the heaviest pressure on the borrower first and is not popular for homes. Constant payment is the usual home loan. When the amortization period is longer than the term, the loan is partially amortized and a balloon payment is due at the end. The longer the amortization, the lighter the monthly payment and the more the total interest costs.
开放与封闭、固定与浮动是两组选择Open or closed, and fixed or floating, are two separate choicesopen mortgage / closed mortgage / variable rate / policy interest rate开放式通常可随时提前还款、利率更高；封闭式利率更低，提前还款要付罚金，除非落进合同额度或《利息法》的口子。浮动利率随 prime rate 变动，prime rate 跟随加拿大银行的政策利率，具体数字不以材料写死。An open mortgage can usually be prepaid at any time, and its rate is higher. A closed mortgage has a lower rate, and prepayment carries a penalty unless it falls within a contractual privilege or an opening in the Interest Act. A variable rate moves with the prime rate, and the prime rate follows the Bank of Canada's policy interest rate. The materials do not lock in a specific figure.
j₂ 不能除以 12，比价要先换算成 EARj₂ cannot be divided by 12, and a comparison must first be converted to an EARnominal rate (j_m) / periodic rate (i) / effective annual rate (EAR)周期利率 i 等于 j_m 除以 m，等价年利率 EAR 等于 (1+i) 的 m 次方减 1。讲义指出 j₂ 为 6.00% 不是月利率 0.5%；j₂ 为 5.90% 时直接除以 12 约得 0.4917%，等价月利率约 0.4857%。j₂ 为 5.90% 的 EAR 约 5.9870%，低于 j₁₂ 为 5.85% 的 EAR 约 6.0094%。The periodic rate i equals j_m divided by m, and the effective annual rate (EAR) equals (1 + i) raised to the power of m, minus 1. The materials state that a j₂ of 6.00% is not a monthly rate of 0.5%. When j₂ is 5.90%, dividing by 12 gives about 0.4917%, while the equivalent monthly rate is about 0.4857%. The EAR of a j₂ of 5.90% is about 5.9870%, which is lower than the EAR of a j₁₂ of 5.85%, about 6.0094%.
《利息法》s.6 管披露，s.10 管个人提前还清Interest Act s.6 governs disclosure, and s.10 governs an individual's prepayment in fullInterest Act s.6 / Interest Act s.10s.6 要求按揭利率按年或按半年复利披露，否则一分利息都不能收；以不动产抵押且五年以上才到期的，从满五年那天起，个人借款人可提前还清全部欠款，只需额外付三个月利息，法人不适用。考试口径与现行条文一致。Section 6 requires a mortgage rate to be disclosed as compounded annually or semi-annually. If it is not, no interest at all may be collected. Where the mortgage is on real property and is not payable for more than five years, from the day those five years are up an individual borrower may prepay the whole amount owing by paying only an extra three months' interest. A corporation does not qualify. The exam wording matches the current section.

第 1 题Q1j₂不能直接除以12j₂ cannot be divided directly by 12★★陷阱：年利率除以十二

Noah is offered a residential mortgage at a nominal rate of 6.00% per annum, compounded semi-annually. He will pay monthly. A friend says the monthly rate for the payment calculation is 6.00% divided by 12, which is 0.5%. Which statement is correct?

Noah 拿到一份住宅按揭报价：名义年利率 6.00%，按半年复利，按月还款。朋友说，月供计算用的月利率就是 6.00% 除以 12，即 0.5%。哪一项正确？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是「年利率除以十二」陷阱。名义年利率（nominal rate, j_m）的下标 m 才是一年复利几次。这里是 j₂，按半年复利，不能因为按月还款就改成除以 12。Wrong. This is the trap of dividing the annual rate by twelve. On a nominal rate (j_m), the subscript m is how many times a year the rate compounds. Here the rate is j₂, compounded semi-annually. Monthly payments do not change the division to 12.

  2. ✗ 错误。6.00% 是按年报出的名义年利率（nominal rate），不是每个月使用的周期利率（periodic rate）。周期利率 i 等于 j_m 除以 m，这里 m 是 2。Wrong. 6.00% is the nominal rate quoted per year, not the periodic rate applied each month. The periodic rate i equals j_m divided by m, and here m is 2.

  3. ✓ 正确。讲义写明 j₂ 等于 6.00% 并不是月利率 0.5%。要先求半年周期利率，即 j₂ 除以 2，再把这个半年利率换算成等价的月复利周期利率。半年复利和月复利是两种计息节奏。Correct. The materials state that a j₂ of 6.00% is not a monthly rate of 0.5%. First find the half-year periodic rate, which is j₂ divided by 2, then convert that half-year rate into an equivalent monthly periodic rate. Semi-annual compounding and monthly compounding are two different rhythms of interest.

  4. ✗ 错误。《利息法》（Interest Act）s.6 卡住的是利率没有按年或按半年复利披露，而不是按月还款这件事。本报价已经写明按半年复利，按月还款是住宅按揭的常态。Wrong. Interest Act s.6 catches a rate that is not disclosed as compounded annually or semi-annually. It does not catch the fact of monthly payments. This quote already states compounding semi-annually. Monthly payments are the usual pattern on a residential mortgage.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Mei enters j₂ = 6.00%, compounded semi-annually, into a spreadsheet and divides by 12. The sheet labels 0.5% as the monthly rate for her home-loan payments. Which description of that 0.5% figure is right?

Mei 把按半年复利的 j₂ = 6.00% 输入表格，再除以 12，表格把 0.5% 标成她住房贷款的月利率。对这个 0.5%，哪一种说法正确？

  1. ✓ 正确。同一考点：j₂ 为 6.00% 不能直接除以 12 得到 0.5% 当月利率。先算半年周期利率 i_sa = j₂ ÷ 2，再换算成等价的月周期利率（periodic rate）。Correct. Same point: a j₂ of 6.00% cannot be divided by 12 to get 0.5% as the monthly rate. First compute the half-year periodic rate i_sa = j₂ / 2, then convert it to the equivalent monthly periodic rate.

  2. ✗ 错误。j₂ 的下标 2 表示一年复利两次，即按半年复利的名义年利率（nominal rate），不是按月报价。Wrong. The subscript 2 on j₂ means the rate compounds twice a year. It is a nominal rate compounded semi-annually, not a rate quoted monthly.

  3. ✗ 错误。0.5% 不是等价年利率（effective annual rate, EAR）。EAR 等于 (1+i) 的 m 次方减 1，而且大于等于名义年利率；复利次数多于一次时，两者会拉开。Wrong. 0.5% is not the effective annual rate (EAR). The EAR equals (1 + i) raised to the power of m, minus 1, and it is greater than or equal to the nominal annual rate. When the rate compounds more than once, the two pull apart.

  4. ✗ 错误。《利息法》（Interest Act）s.6 要求以年或半年复利披露利率，并没有规定月利率必须等于报价除以 12。直接除以 12 正是讲义点名的最常见算错方式。Wrong. Interest Act s.6 requires the rate to be disclosed as compounded annually or semi-annually. It does not say the monthly rate must equal the quoted rate divided by 12. Dividing straight by 12 is the miscalculation the materials name as the most common one.

第 2 题Q2先换等价年利率再比Convert to the effective annual rate before comparing★★★陷阱：名义数字直接比

Omar has two quotes on the same residential loan. Quote P is 5.90% per annum compounded semi-annually. Quote Q is 5.85% per annum compounded monthly. He circles Q because 5.85 is less than 5.90. Which statement matches the course comparison?

Omar 就同一笔住宅贷款拿到两份报价。报价 P 是年利率 5.90%、按半年复利。报价 Q 是年利率 5.85%、按月复利。他因为 5.85 小于 5.90 而圈了 Q。哪一项符合课程里的比较？

The promise is less reliable, liquidity is worse, and administration cost is higher, so a high-quality first mortgage of the same term usually carries a higher rate than the yield on a government bond. The two rates do not have to be equal.

  2. ✗ 错误。管理成本不会因为贷款规模小而等比例缩小。大机构靠规模摊薄单笔成本，小投资人摊不了，这是五个特征里的另一条，不是本题这条的反面。Wrong. Administration cost does not shrink in exact proportion as the loan gets smaller. A large institution spreads the cost of each loan across its volume, and a small investor cannot. That is a different one of the five features. It is not the reverse of the point in this question.

  3. ✗ 错误。资本门槛高是五个特征之一：放出一笔完整按揭往往要凑出很大一笔钱，很多有意愿的储蓄者凑不出来。本题的不便还叠着还款太碎、暂时贷不出去。Wrong. A high capital threshold is one of the five features. Advancing one complete mortgage often takes a very large sum of money, and many willing savers cannot raise it. The awkwardness in this question is also that the repayments are too small to be lent out again for the time being.

  4. ✓ 正确。这就是再投资难题（reinvestment problem）。小投资人每月只有一小笔本金加利息，先落进利息更低的储蓄账户，等攒够才能放出下一笔，等待期拉低实际到手的收益率。大机构因为笔数多，可以立刻再贷出去。Correct. This is the reinvestment problem. Each month a small investor receives only a small blend of principal and interest. It first sits in a savings account that pays less until there is enough to advance the next loan, and the waiting period pulls down the yield actually received. A large institution has many loans, so it can lend the money out again at once.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

第 7 题Q7卷面考分类不考旧名The exam tests the category, not the old name★★陷阱：旧名字当现行分类

An exam item says the course materials give two examples of private mortgage insurers: one has since been renamed, and the other still trades under its original name. Which answer does the exam expect?

一道考试题说：课程材料举了两家私营按揭保险公司为例，其中一家后来改了名，另一家名称未变、仍在运营。按考试口径应选哪一项？

  1. ✓ 正确。卷面考的是分类：CMHC 之外有私营按揭保险人参与，而不是举例的公司名字对不对。改名的那家公司还在，只是换了牌子；实务上对客户要用现行名称，但不要拿现行名称去替换卷面上的分类答案。Correct. The exam tests the category: private mortgage insurers take part alongside CMHC. It does not test whether the name of each example is still right. The renamed firm is still there. It has only changed its brand. In practice, use the current name with a client, but do not replace the category answer on the paper with that current name.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是「旧名字当现行分类」陷阱。改名不等于公司消失，更不能把「私营保险人」这个类别涂掉。讲义写明改名的那家品牌与业务延续。Wrong. This is the trap of treating an old name as the current category. A new name does not mean the firm has disappeared, and it cannot be used to erase the category of private insurer. The materials state that the renamed firm's brand and business continue.

  3. ✗ 错误。改名不会改变类别。专营按揭公司（monoline lender）是放贷方，私营按揭保险公司是保险人，两类在考试口径的分类框架里各自独立。Wrong. A new name does not change the category. A monoline lender is a lender. A private mortgage insurer is an insurer. In the classification used on the exam, the two classes stay separate.

  4. ✗ 错误。题干已说明另一家名称未变、仍在运营；一家改名也推不出材料点名的私营保险人都已停业。Wrong. The stem already says the other firm is unchanged in name and still operating. One renaming also does not show that every private insurer named in the materials has stopped operating.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A classmate says a firm the course materials name as a private mortgage insurer is "a monoline lender that no longer exists," because it later changed its name. What is the correction?

课程材料把一家公司当作私营按揭保险公司的举例，这家公司后来改了名。同学说它是「一家已经不存在的专营按揭公司」。应当怎样改正？

  1. ✗ 错误。这家公司在考试口径里不是银行，也不是要用现行招牌替换类别。银行是机构贷款人里的一类，而且仍是按揭资金最大的单一来源。Wrong. In the exam wording this firm is not a bank, and the current brand is not to be substituted for the category. A bank is one class of institutional lender, and banks are still the largest single source of mortgage funds.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是「旧名字当现行分类」陷阱。卷面要的不是招牌还在不在。改名之后公司仍在，类别并没有从市场上消失。Wrong. This is the trap of treating an old name as the current category. The paper is not asking whether the brand is still in use. After the renaming the firm is still there, and the category has not left the market.

  3. ✓ 正确。同一考点：考试口径把它当作 CMHC 之外的私营按揭保险公司举例，卷面答分类。公司未消失，只是换了牌子；实务上对客户要用现在的名字。Correct. Same point. The exam wording uses the firm as an example of a private mortgage insurer beside CMHC, and the paper is answered with the category. The firm has not disappeared. It has only changed its brand. In practice, use the current name with a client.

  4. ✗ 错误。政府放贷是另一类（联邦与省级政府直接放贷项目）。改名不等于被政府接管，它在考试口径里仍是私营按揭保险公司的举例。Wrong. Government lending is a separate class: direct lending programs of the federal and provincial governments. A new name does not mean the firm was taken over by a government. In the exam wording it is still an example of a private mortgage insurer.

答案Answer：c
