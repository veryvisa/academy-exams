本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 情景定性题：给出业主切分地块、出租整栋建筑的一部分或出租土地一部分的案例，考你能否区分什么行为构成《土地产权法》（Land Title Act）第 73 条下的法定分割（subdivision），以及分区（zoning）允许的户数与分割审批之间的界限。Scenario classification questions: Presenting cases where an owner divides a parcel, leases part of an entire building, or leases a portion of land, testing whether you can distinguish what constitutes a statutory subdivision under section 73 of the Land Title Act, as well as the boundary between permitted zoning density and subdivision approval.
  • 四种分割对比题：把传统平面分割、建筑共管、裸地共管（bare land strata）和空间地块（air space parcel）混在同一题里，重点考内部道路谁来修、空间地块是否默示地役权（easement）以及上下边界外的空间归属。Four-type subdivision comparison questions: Combining conventional subdivision, building strata, bare land strata, and air space parcels in a single question, focusing on who maintains internal roads, whether air space parcels imply easements, and who owns the space beyond upper and lower boundaries.
  • Torrens 四大支柱与例外辨析题：给你伪造契据、无偿赠与、未登记租约或登记了伪造按揭的连环交易，考不可推翻性（indefeasibility）的十项例外、善意有价对价（valuable consideration）的门槛、押记（charge）不享不可推翻性，以及通知原则（doctrine of notice）被废除后的欺诈认定。Torrens four pillars and exceptions questions: Presenting forged deeds, voluntary transfers by gift, unregistered leases, or chains of transactions with registered forged mortgages, testing the ten exceptions to indefeasibility, the threshold of bona fide valuable consideration, the lack of indefeasibility for charges, and the definition of fraud following the abolition of the doctrine of notice.
  • 产权负担时限与保留金计算题：围绕重复产权证（duplicate indefeasible title）、异议登记（caveat）、待决诉讼证明书（certificate of pending litigation, CPL）、判决（judgment）及建筑留置权（builders lien）的失效期与扣留比例设题，专门测试 2 个月、21 天、45 天、55 天、1 年、2 年及 10% 与 7% 的精准对应。Encumbrance time limits and holdback calculation questions: Framing questions around lapse periods and holdback percentages for duplicate indefeasible titles, caveats, certificates of pending litigation (CPL), judgments, and builders liens, specifically testing the exact correspondence of 2 months, 21 days, 45 days, 55 days, 1 year, 2 years, as well as 10% and 7%.
  • 跨登记系统与口径对照题：把制造房屋（manufactured home）、污染场地登记（Site Registry）、原住民所有权确立路径及《土地所有人透明法》（Land Owner Transparency Act, LOTA）放在一起，考考试口径（©2020）与现行规则的差异及不同登记簿的功能边界。Cross-registry systems and syllabus comparison questions: Comparing manufactured homes, the Site Registry, pathways to establish Aboriginal title, and the Land Owner Transparency Act (LOTA), testing differences between the exam syllabus (course manual) and current rules, as well as functional boundaries across distinct registries.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 三年与十年租约线混用Confusing the Three-Year and Ten-Year Lease Thresholds

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生只记住了「三年短租约」这个高频数字，忽略了切分土地出租、免登记对抗第三人以及 LOTA 申报各自适用不同的条件与年限。Candidates memorize the high-frequency three-year short lease threshold while overlooking that leasing subdivided land, exemption from registration against third parties, and LOTA transparency filings each apply distinct conditions and statutory terms.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 lease、actual occupation、part of a parcel、part of a building 或 transparency declaration 时，先核对是《土地产权法》的三年线还是 LOTA 的十年线。When the question stem mentions a lease, actual occupation, part of a parcel, part of a building, or a transparency declaration, first verify whether the three-year Land Title Act line or the ten-year LOTA line applies.

⚠ 把押记当成不可推翻产权Treating Charges as Indefeasible Titles

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生以为只要在产权登记处办妥登记，所有权益都受 Torrens 不可推翻性保护，忘了押记（charge）如按揭只产生可反驳的推定。Candidates assume that any interest registered in the land title office enjoys Torrens indefeasibility, forgetting that registered charges such as mortgages create only a rebuttable presumption rather than guaranteed validity.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 forged transfer 之后紧跟着 registered mortgage 或 charge，问无辜贷款人能否保住按揭或向保证基金索赔。The question stem features a forged transfer immediately followed by a registered mortgage or charge, asking whether an innocent lender can uphold the mortgage or claim against the assurance fund.

⚠ 裸地共管道路当市政路Treating Bare Land Strata Roads as Municipal Roads

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 裸地共管社区外观与传统独立屋分割一模一样，考生容易套用传统分割「道路献给公众由政府维护」的规则。Bare land strata developments appear identical to conventional single-family subdivisions from the street, leading candidates to incorrectly apply the conventional rule where roads are dedicated to the public and maintained by the municipality.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 bare land strata plan、internal roads、common property 或审批官签名旁的裸地共管注记。The question stem mentions a bare land strata plan, internal roads, common property, or the statutory bare land strata notation beside the approving officer signature.

⚠ 留置权 10% 与共管买家 7% 混淆Confusing the 10% Builders Lien and 7% Strata Buyer Holdbacks

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到 builders lien holdback 就条件反射选 10%，没看清主体是工程业主扣施工款，还是买家从开发商手里买共管单位。Candidates reflexively select 10% upon seeing builders lien holdback, failing to distinguish between a project owner retaining funds under a construction contract and a purchaser buying a strata lot from a developer.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 purchasing a strata lot from a developer 对比 owner retaining holdback under a construction contract。The question stem presents purchasing a strata lot from a developer versus an owner retaining a holdback under a construction contract.

⚠ 2020 考试口径与 2026 现行接口错位Misalignment Between the 2020 Exam Syllabus and Current Rules

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 实务中天天用的 BC Registry Services、LOTA 强制申报和 2024 年海达族立法确认，在 2020 版考试口径里要么写的是 BC OnLine，要么整块缺失。Day-to-day practical systems such as BC Registry Services, mandatory LOTA filings, and the 2024 statutory recognition of Haida title are either referenced under obsolete channels such as BC OnLine or omitted entirely from the 2020 exam syllabus.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问 according to the course manual 的线上登记入口（BC OnLine）或原住民所有权确立途径（两条 vs 三条）。The question stem asks, according to the course manual, about online registry access portals (BC OnLine) or pathways for establishing Aboriginal title (two routes versus three routes).

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
法定分割禁令与建筑例外Statutory Subdivision Prohibition and Building Exceptionsubdivision / section 73 of the Land Title Act未经依《土地产权法》第 7 部审批，任何人不得为转让或签订超过三年（含终身）的租约而切分土地，但为出租建筑物或建筑物的一部分而做的划分不算分割。Under section 73 of the Land Title Act, no person may subdivide land to transfer or lease it for a term exceeding three years (including a lease for life) without Part 7 approval; however, a subdivision does not include a division of land to lease a building or part of a building.
分割审批与存放的三个时限Three Statutory Timelines for Subdivision Approval and Depositapproving officer timelines审批官须在提交送审后 2 个月内批或驳；被驳或逾期可在 1 个月内上诉高等法院（法院可再延最多 1 个月）；获批后须在 2 个月内将分割图送交登记处存放。The approving officer must approve or refuse a subdivision plan within 2 months of submission; an applicant may appeal a refusal or failure to approve to the Supreme Court within 1 month (which the court may extend by up to 1 additional month); upon approval, the plan must be tendered for deposit at the land title office within 2 months.
裸地共管与传统分割的道路差异Road Ownership and Maintenance Differences in Bare Land Stratabare land strata plan vs. conventional subdivision传统分割的道路献给公众由公共部门维护，而裸地共管的内部道路属于共有财产，维修费由全体业主分摊，且图上审批官签名旁须明写裸地共管图。Roads in a conventional subdivision are dedicated to the public and maintained by public authorities, whereas internal roads in a bare land strata plan are common property whose maintenance costs are shared by all owners, and the plan must be explicitly designated as a bare land strata plan beside the approving officer signature.
空间地块的边界与地役权规则Boundaries and Easement Rules for Air Space Parcelsair space parcel空气本身构成土地并可切出独立不可推翻产权证，但授予空间地块不转让任何默示地役权，且上界之上与下界之下的空间除非明文授予仍归出让人所有。Air space constitutes land and can be granted as a separate parcel with an indefeasible title, but a grant of an air space parcel does not transfer any implied easements, and air space above the upper boundary or below the lower boundary remains with the grantor unless expressly granted.
不可推翻性与善意有价对价Indefeasibility and Bona Fide Valuable Considerationindefeasible title / valuable consideration不可推翻产权证受第 23(2) 条十项例外约束；参与欺诈者本人不受保护，但向其支付按当时市价属合理对价的善意受让人登记后即取得受保护的完全所有权。An indefeasible title is subject to ten statutory exceptions under section 23(2); a perpetrator participating in fraud receives no statutory protection, but an innocent transferee who pays fair market valuable consideration acquires protected fee simple title upon registration.
押记不享有不可推翻性Lack of Indefeasibility for Registered Chargesregistration of a charge (section 26)押记的登记只产生可反驳的推定而不构成有效性保证，伪造转让后登记的无辜按揭在原所有人恢复产权时一并涂销且不能向保证基金索赔。Under section 26, registration of a charge creates only a rebuttable presumption rather than guaranteed validity; a mortgage registered after a forged transfer is cancelled when the true owner recovers title, and the innocent mortgagee cannot claim against the assurance fund.
重复产权证与异议登记的效力Legal Effects of Duplicate Titles and Caveatsduplicate indefeasible title / caveat重复产权证在外未交回时登记处停办转让、按揭或长租登记；异议登记自登记起 2 个月失效，若被登记人提前送达通知则在送达后 21 天失效。When a duplicate indefeasible title is issued and outstanding, the land title office will not register a transfer, mortgage, or long-term lease; a caveat lapses 2 months after registration, or 21 days after formal notice is served by the owner if served at least 21 days before the two-month expiry.
建筑留置权时限与保留金比例Builders Lien Statutory Windows and Holdback Percentagesbuilders lien / holdback留置权须在 45 天内提交、登记后 1 年内（或收到通知后 21 天内）起诉并登 CPL；工程保留金为 10% 扣留 55 天，而向开发商买共管单位的买家须按总购价扣留 7%。A claim of lien must be filed within 45 days, and an action with a CPL must be commenced within 1 year of filing (or within 21 days after receiving a notice to commence action); the construction contract holdback is 10% retained for 55 days, while a buyer purchasing a strata lot from a developer must retain 7% of the gross purchase price.
LOTA 强制申报与两个核心门槛Mandatory LOTA Filings and Core ThresholdsLand Owner Transparency Act (LOTA)申请登记土地权益（含超 10 年租约）时每位受让人必交透明申报表否则拒收，相关公司（持股或表决权达 10% 为权益持有人）、相关信托与合伙还须交透明报告。Any transferee applying to register an interest in land (including a lease exceeding 10 years) must submit a transparency declaration or the registrar will refuse registration, and reporting bodies (including relevant corporations with corporate interest holders holding at least 10% of shares or voting rights, relevant trusts, and relevant partnerships) must also file a transparency report.
制造房屋登记与移动规则Manufactured Home Registration and Moving RulesManufactured Home Act / transport permit制造房屋不进产权登记处且无不可推翻性与保证基金，未登记不得出售或移动，运输许可 30 天失效，诉讼警示 3 个月失效，违规最高罚 $2,000。Manufactured homes are recorded in a separate registry outside the Land Title Act without indefeasible title or an assurance fund; an unregistered home cannot be sold, offered, or moved; a transport permit expires in 30 days; a caution expires in 3 months; and contraventions carry a maximum fine of $2,000.

第 1 题Q1第73条分割禁令与租约例外Section 73 subdivision prohibition and lease exceptions★★陷阱：三年与十年租约线混用

An owner of a commercial property in British Columbia plans to enter into two new leases without applying to the approving officer for subdivision approval: Lease X is an eight-year lease for a single retail unit inside the existing building, and Lease Y is an eight-year lease for a fenced portion of the unimproved parking lot land. Under section 73 of the Land Title Act, which statement is correct?

BC 省一处商业物业的业主计划在不向审批官申请分割批准的情况下签订两份新租约：租约 X 是出租现有建筑物内一个零售铺面的八年期租约，租约 Y 是出租未开发停车场土地一角围栏区域的八年期租约。根据《土地产权法》第 73 条，下列哪项陈述是正确的？

  1. ✓ 正确。依《土地产权法》（Land Title Act）第 73 条，任何人不得在未经第 7 部审批的情况下为签订超过三年（含终身）的租约而把土地分成更小地块（因此租土地一角的八年租约 Y 必须按分割办）；但第 73(3) 条规定，为出租建筑物或建筑物的一部分（part of a building）而做的划分不算分割，故八年商铺租约 X 豁免分割审批。Correct. Under section 73 of the Land Title Act, no person may subdivide land into smaller parcels to grant a lease exceeding three years (including a lease for life) without Part 7 approval (therefore Lease Y for a portion of the parking lot land requires subdivision approval); however, section 73(3) provides that a subdivision does not include a division of land to lease a building or part of a building, so the eight-year retail shop Lease X is exempt from subdivision approval.

  2. ✗ 错误。掉进了「三年与十年租约线混用」陷阱：十年线是《土地所有人透明法》（LOTA）触发透明申报的门槛，而《土地产权法》第 73 条禁止未批分割的租约年限线是三年。Incorrect. Falls into the 'confusing the three-year and ten-year lease thresholds' trap: the ten-year threshold is the trigger for transparency filings under the Land Owner Transparency Act (LOTA), whereas the statutory threshold prohibiting unapproved subdivision of land for leases under section 73 of the Land Title Act is three years.

  3. ✗ 错误。忽略了第 73(3) 条的关键例外：出租建筑物或建筑物的一部分不算分割，所以一栋楼可以租给许多长租租户而不必先做土地分割。Incorrect. Overlooks the critical exception in section 73(3): leasing a building or part of a building does not constitute a subdivision, meaning a building can be leased to multiple long-term tenants without first obtaining subdivision approval.

  4. ✗ 错误。混淆了分区（zoning）与分割（subdivision）：地方政府分区允许的用途或户数走的是《地方政府法》，并没有取消《土地产权法》第 73 条的分割禁令或审批官审批。Incorrect. Confuses zoning with subdivision: permitted uses or density under local government zoning bylaws operate under the Local Government Act and do not override the subdivision prohibition or the requirement for approving officer approval under section 73 of the Land Title Act.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A rural landowner wishes to lease an orchard portion of her land to a neighbouring farmer for a term of life, while also leasing the upper floor of her farmhouse to a tenant for eight years. Under section 73 of the Land Title Act, how are these two arrangements treated?

一名农村土地所有人希望把她土地上的一片果园区域租给邻近农夫，租期为终身；同时把她农舍的楼上一层租给一名租客，租期为八年。根据《土地产权法》第 73 条，这两项安排如何处理？

  1. ✗ 错误。农舍楼上一层属于建筑物的一部分（part of a building），依第 73(3) 条豁免分割审批。Incorrect. The upper floor of the farmhouse constitutes part of a building and is exempt from subdivision approval under section 73(3).

  2. ✓ 正确。依《土地产权法》第 73 条，为签订超过三年（含终身，for life）的租约而切分土地必须按第 7 部办分割审批；但依第 73(3) 条，出租建筑物的一部分（农舍楼上一层）不算分割。Correct. Under section 73 of the Land Title Act, dividing land to grant a lease exceeding three years (including a lease for life) requires Part 7 subdivision approval; however, under section 73(3), leasing part of a building (the upper floor of the farmhouse) does not constitute a subdivision.

  3. ✗ 错误。第 73 条明文将「超过三年（含终身）」的土地部分租约一并纳入分割禁令范围。Incorrect. Section 73 explicitly includes leases of part of a parcel exceeding three years (including a lease for life) within the statutory subdivision prohibition.

  4. ✗ 错误。正好颠倒了规则：私有农业地块切分租超三年受第 73 条约束，而建筑物内部划分依第 73(3) 条豁免。Incorrect. Inverts the rules: leasing a subdivided portion of private agricultural land for more than three years is governed by section 73, whereas dividing space inside a building is exempt under section 73(3).

第 2 题Q2裸地共管与传统分割差异Differences between bare land strata and conventional subdivision★陷阱：裸地共管道路当市政路

A buyer is comparing two detached houses that look identical from the street: Property M was created by a conventional subdivision plan, while Property N was created by a bare land strata plan. Which statement accurately describes the legal difference between the two properties?

一名买家正在比较两栋从街上看一模一样的独立屋：物业 M 由传统分割图创设，而物业 N 由裸地共管图创设。下列哪项准确描述了这两处物业之间的法律差异？

  1. ✗ 错误。掉进了「裸地共管道路当市政路」陷阱：只有传统分割图上标为道路的部分依第 107 条献给公众由公共一方维护，裸地共管（bare land strata）的内部道路是共有财产。Incorrect. Falls into the 'treating bare land strata roads as municipal roads' trap: only roads shown on a conventional subdivision plan vest in the Crown under section 107 and are maintained by public authorities, whereas internal roads in a bare land strata plan are common property.

  2. ✓ 正确。传统分割的道路献给公众并由公共一方维护，没有共管治理；裸地共管切的是土地，内部道路属于共有财产（common property），维修铲雪等费用由全体业主按单位权益分摊，且法规要求必须在审批官签名旁明写自己是裸地共管图（bare land strata plan）。Correct. Roads in a conventional subdivision are dedicated to the public and maintained by public authorities with no strata governance; bare land strata subdivides land where internal roads are common property, with maintenance and snow removal costs shared among all owners based on unit entitlement, and regulations require the plan to be explicitly designated as a bare land strata plan beside the approving officer signature.

  3. ✗ 错误。裸地共管虽然切的是土地，但同样设有共管法团（strata corporation）、章程（bylaws）与专项储备金（contingency reserve fund）。Incorrect. Even though a bare land strata subdivides land rather than building space, it is still governed by a strata corporation, bylaws, and a contingency reserve fund.

Under sections 29(5) and 29(6) of the Land Title Act, a person dealing with a registered owner is not affected by any filed transparency declaration, transparency report, or publicly searchable information, and knowledge does not constitute fraud.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

After a relevant corporation properly files a transparency report upon buying land in BC, a share transfer occurs and a new individual acquires 10% of the corporation's shares, while the land title itself remains unchanged. Under section 16(1) of the Land Owner Transparency Act, what must the corporation do?

一家相关公司在 BC 省买地并妥善提交透明报告后，发生了股权转让，一名新个人取得了该公司 10% 的股份，而土地产权本身没有任何变动。根据《土地所有人透明法》第 16(1) 条，该公司必须怎么做？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是实务中最常漏掉的合规点：即使土地产权一动没动，公司股权或信托受益人变动照样触发 LOTA 补报义务。Incorrect. This is one of the most commonly overlooked compliance requirements in practice: even if the land title itself remains unchanged, a change in corporate shareholding or trust beneficiaries triggers an obligation to file an updated transparency report under LOTA.

  2. ✗ 错误。股权变动走的是 LOTA 透明报告更新，与产权登记处的异议登记（caveat）或 45 天建筑留置权期无关。Incorrect. A shareholding change requires updating the LOTA transparency report and has nothing to do with registering a caveat or the 45-day builders lien window at the land title office.

  3. ✗ 错误。PID 与地块终身绑定，只有土地分割或合并时才会改变，股权变动不改变 PID。Incorrect. A PID is permanently tied to a parcel and changes only upon subdivision or consolidation of land; a corporate share transfer does not alter the PID.

  4. ✓ 正确。依 LOTA 第 16(1) 条，申报主体（reporting body）知道或理应知道其已交报告不再反映现时权益持有人（interest holder，新股东持股达 10% 门槛）之后，须在 2 个月内另交一份新的透明报告。Correct. Under section 16(1) of LOTA, a reporting body must file a new transparency report within 2 months after it becomes aware, or reasonably ought to have become aware, that its previously filed report no longer discloses the current interest holders (where the new shareholder meets the 10% threshold).

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

第 7 题Q7制造房屋登记与移动规则Manufactured home registration and moving rules★★陷阱：2020 考试口径与 2026 现行接口错位

An owner of an unregistered manufactured home wants to list the home for sale and move it to a new pad across town next month. Under the Manufactured Home Act and the course manual, which statement correctly describes the legal requirements and registry system?

一名未登记制造房屋的业主想要把该房屋挂牌出售，并在下个月把它移到城另一头的新地台上。根据《制造房屋法》及课程考试资料，下列哪项正确描述了相关法律要求和登记系统？

  1. ✗ 错误。混淆了多处规则：依第 18 条，未登记的制造房屋不得出售、要约出售或移动；安全认证是 CSA 标签而非 MHR 标签（MHR 只是登记编号身份标识）；且制造房屋登记没有产权证、没有不可推翻性。Incorrect. Confuses several distinct rules: under section 18, an unregistered manufactured home cannot be sold, offered for sale, or moved; safety certification is a CSA mark rather than an MHR decal (which merely identifies registry registration); and manufactured home registration confers no certificate of title or indefeasibility.

  2. ✗ 错误。把产权登记处 caveat 的时限套到了制造房屋上：制造房屋的运输许可（transport permit）自签发日起 30 天失效（第 17(1) 条），而诉讼警示（caution）是 3 个月后失效（第 22(3) 条）。Incorrect. Applies the land title caveat timeline to manufactured homes: a transport permit for a manufactured home expires 30 days after issuance (section 17(1)), whereas a caution in the registry expires after 3 months (section 22(3)).

  3. ✓ 正确。依《制造房屋法》（Manufactured Home Act）第 15(1)、17(1) 及 18 条，未登记的制造房屋不得出售、要约出售或移动，移动前必须先登记并取得有效期 30 天的运输许可（transport permit），违规最高罚 $2,000；考试口径说登记通常只接受通过 BC OnLine 电子提交，而现行入口已改为 BC Registry Services。Correct. Under sections 15(1), 17(1), and 18 of the Manufactured Home Act, an unregistered manufactured home must not be sold, offered for sale, or moved, and before moving it must be registered and obtain a transport permit valid for 30 days, with contraventions subject to a maximum fine of $2,000; the exam syllabus states that filings are generally accepted electronically only through BC OnLine, whereas the current portal is BC Registry Services.

  4. ✗ 错误。有没有轮子不影响制造房屋的法定定性；且制造房屋停在出租地台（pad）上时，业主与地台出租人之间适用的是《制造房屋园区租赁法》（Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act），不是《住宅租赁法》。Incorrect. The presence or absence of wheels does not alter the statutory classification of a manufactured home; furthermore, when a manufactured home sits on a rented pad, the tenancy relationship between the home owner and the pad landlord is governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act rather than the Residential Tenancy Act.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A claimant who has commenced a court action over ownership of a manufactured home registers a caution in the Manufactured Home Registry. How long does that caution remain effective unless a court orders otherwise, and what is the maximum fine for moving a manufactured home without a transport permit?

一名已就制造房屋所有权提起诉讼的索赔人在制造房屋登记处登记了一条警示（caution）。除非法院另有命令，该警示的有效期是多久？在没有运输许可的情况下移动制造房屋的最高罚金又是多少？

  1. ✓ 正确。依《制造房屋法》第 22(3) 条，已提起诉讼的人提交的警示（caution）在 3 个月后失效，除非法院在此期间下令注销或延续；违反移动、运输许可（30 天有效）与销售规定的，罚金上限为 $2,000。Correct. Under section 22(3) of the Manufactured Home Act, a caution registered by a person who has commenced legal proceedings expires after 3 months unless a court orders it cancelled or continued; contraventions of moving, transport permit (valid for 30 days), and sales provisions carry a maximum fine of $2,000.

  2. ✗ 错误。2 个月是《土地产权法》下异议登记（caveat）的失效期，$25,000 是 LOTA 个人罚款的数字，均不适用于《制造房屋法》。Incorrect. Two months is the lapse period for a caveat under the Land Title Act, and $25,000 is the individual penalty under LOTA; neither applies under the Manufactured Home Act.

  3. ✗ 错误。30 天是运输许可（transport permit）的有效期（第 17(1) 条），不是警示（caution）的 3 个月有效期。Incorrect. Thirty days is the validity period for a transport permit under section 17(1), not the 3-month lifespan of a caution.

  4. ✗ 错误。1 年是建筑留置权起诉期，且制造房屋登记系统完全没有保证基金（assurance fund）或不可推翻性。Incorrect. One year is the builders lien lawsuit limitation period, and the Manufactured Home Registry has no assurance fund or indefeasible title whatsoever.
