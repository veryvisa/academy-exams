① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 概念归属题：给出某项具体规定（如谁需要持牌、信托账户管理细则、楼花披露声明），考它属于 RESA、Real Estate Services Rules 还是 REDMAStatutory source questions: Given a specific provision (such as licensing requirements, trust account operating rules, or pre-sale disclosure statements), testing whether it falls under RESA, the Real Estate Services Rules, or REDMA.
  • 机构职能题：考单一监管机构 BCFSA 的管辖范围，并用已解散的 RECBC、已并入的 OSRE 或其他保护机构（E&O、赔偿基金、地产基金会）做干扰项Regulatory body questions: Testing the jurisdiction of the single regulator BCFSA, using the dissolved RECBC, the merged OSRE, or other protective bodies (E&O, Compensation Fund, Real Estate Foundation) as distractors.
  • 牌照类别与级别题：给出具体执业场景，考属于三大牌照类别（Trading、Rental、Strata）中的哪一类，以及三个牌照级别与挂靠经纪行（Brokerage）的强制关系Licence category and level questions: Given a specific practice scenario, testing which of the three licence categories (Trading, Rental, Strata) applies, and the mandatory requirement that all three licence levels must be engaged by a brokerage.
  • 考牌与发牌时限数字题：密集考查 CELPIP 7 级、课程 1 年、考完 2 年内参考、重考间隔 90 天、连挂两次重修、通过考试后 1 年内申牌及 19 岁年龄门槛Licensing timeline and number questions: Intensively testing CELPIP Level 7, completing the course within 1 year, writing the exam within 2 years, the 90-day rewrite waiting period, retaking the course after two consecutive failures, applying for a licence within 1 year of passing, and the age 19 threshold.
  • 罚款上限与双撤销权对比题：考查纪律罚款（个人 $250,000 / 经纪行 $500,000）与行政罚款（$100,000）的区别，以及 REDMA 7 天撤销期与住宅买家 3 个工作日冷静期的区分Penalty maximums and dual rescission rights comparison questions: Testing the difference between disciplinary penalties ($250,000 individual / $500,000 brokerage) and administrative penalties ($100,000), as well as distinguishing REDMA 7-day rescission rights from the residential buyer 3-business-day cooling-off period.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 旧机构名残留陷阱Outdated entity name trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 2021 年前的旧版考试资料与网帖到处写着向 RECBC 申请牌照或由 OSRE 查处无牌执业，考生刷旧题容易顺手选 RECBC 或 OSREPre-2021 study materials and online posts frequently refer to applying for a licence through RECBC or having OSRE investigate unlicensed practice, leading candidates doing older practice questions to instinctively select RECBC or OSRE.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问谁负责发牌、制定规则、调查投诉或执行纪律处分，选项同时出现 BCFSA、RECBC、OSREThe question stem asks who is responsible for licensing, rulemaking, investigating complaints, or imposing discipline, and the options list BCFSA alongside RECBC and OSRE.

⚠ 四大保护机构张冠李戴Mismatched public protection entities trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: E&O 保险、赔偿基金（Compensation Fund）、地产基金会（Real Estate Foundation）名字相似，考生常把疏忽大意赔付和挪用信托资金赔付搞混The E&O Insurance Corporation, Compensation Fund, and Real Estate Foundation have similar names; candidates often confuse compensation for negligence with compensation for misappropriation of trust funds.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「挪用信托资金（misappropriation of trust funds）」或「执业疏忽（errors and omissions）」或「公益与研究资助」The question stem mentions misappropriation of trust funds, professional negligence (errors and omissions), or public interest and research funding.

⚠ 考牌与申牌时间窗混淆Exam and licensing timeline confusion trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 1 年、2 年、6 个月、90 天多个期限并存，且容易误以为参加补考（supplemental exam）能顺延申牌的 1 年期限Multiple time limits coexist (1 year, 2 years, 6 months, 90 days), and candidates frequently mistakenly assume that writing a supplemental exam extends the 1-year window for applying for a licence.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现完成课程作业后多久内考试、挂科后隔多久重考、连续几次不及格重修、或通过考试后多久内申请牌照The question stem mentions how long after finishing course assignments one must write the exam, the waiting period to rewrite after failing, how many consecutive failures require repeating the course, or the deadline to apply for a licence after passing.

⚠ 纪律罚款与行政罚款混同Disciplinary penalty and administrative penalty conflation trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 个人纪律罚款上限 $250,000、经纪行纪律罚款上限 $500,000、行政罚款上限 $100,000 三个数字容易串位，或漏看主体是个人还是经纪行The three figures—individual disciplinary maximum of $250,000, brokerage disciplinary maximum of $500,000, and administrative penalty maximum of $100,000—are easily mixed up, or candidates overlook whether the subject is an individual licensee or a brokerage.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问 maximum disciplinary penalty 或 administrative penalty，且明确指出受罚对象是 individual licensee 还是 brokerageThe question stem asks for the maximum disciplinary penalty or administrative penalty, and specifies whether the penalized party is an individual licensee or a brokerage.

⚠ REDMA 与 HBRP 撤销权混淆REDMA and HBRP rescission rights confusion trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把开发商楼花的 REDMA 7 天无条件撤销权，与转售住宅的 3 个工作日冷静期（HBRP，需付 0.25% 撤销费）混为一谈Conflating the 7-day unconditional rescission right for developer pre-sales under REDMA with the 3-business-day cooling-off period (HBRP, requiring a 0.25% fee) for residential resale properties.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 developer、disclosure statement 或 pre-sale strata lot，选项里却混入 3 business days 或 0.25% feeThe question stem mentions developer, disclosure statement, or pre-sale strata lot, but options include 3 business days or a 0.25% fee.

第 1 题Q1三层法规职责划分Three-tier regulatory division of responsibilities★陷阱：法规层级混淆

A real estate team is reviewing British Columbia's regulatory framework to determine which legislation establishes who is required to hold a licence, how licences are issued, and the statutory penalties for contraventions, versus which instrument sets out the specific rules on trust accounts and agency disclosures. Which pairing is accurate?

一个地产团队正在梳理 BC 省的监管框架，以确定哪部法律规定了谁必须持牌、牌照如何发放以及违规的法定处罚，而哪部文件规定了信托账户与代理披露的具体细则。下列哪项配对是准确的？

  1. ✓ 正确。《地产服务法》（Real Estate Services Act, RESA）负责规定谁需要牌照、牌照怎么发、违规怎么罚；而依 RESA s. 89.2 制定的《地产服务规则》（Real Estate Services Rules, B.C. Reg. 209/2021）负责规定披露、信托账户、广告、代理关系等执业行为细则。Correct. The Real Estate Services Act (RESA) establishes who must be licensed, how licences are issued, and penalties for contraventions; the Real Estate Services Rules (B.C. Reg. 209/2021), enacted under RESA s. 89.2, govern practice conduct including disclosures, trust accounts, advertising, and agency relationships.

  2. ✗ 属于法规层级混淆陷阱。REDMA 管的是开发商卖楼花/共管单位的披露声明（disclosure statement）与撤销权，不管持牌要求；管持牌要求的是 RESA。Incorrect. This is a regulatory hierarchy confusion trap. REDMA governs developers' disclosure statements and buyer rescission rights for pre-sale development units; it does not govern licensing requirements, which are governed by RESA.

  3. ✗ 属于旧规则残留陷阱。自 2021-08-01 起，旧的「地产议会规则（Council Rules）」已被整体重编号的《地产服务规则》（B.C. Reg. 209/2021）取代，且发牌依据是 RESA、信托账户归 Rules 管而非 REDMA。Incorrect. This is an outdated rules trap. Effective August 1, 2021, the former Council Rules were replaced by the renumbered Real Estate Services Rules (B.C. Reg. 209/2021); furthermore, licensing authority derives from RESA, and trust accounts are governed by the Rules rather than REDMA.

  4. ✗ 属于法规名称拼凑陷阱。《2021 年金融成文法修正案》是合并监管机构的立法依据，旧《地产法》（Real Estate Act）早于 2005 年初被 RESA 取代。Incorrect. This is a fabricated statute pairing trap. The Finance Statutes Amendment Act, 2021 was the statute consolidating the regulators, while the repealed Real Estate Act was replaced by RESA in early 2005.

A member of the public wishes to file a formal complaint regarding a licensee's conduct, and an applicant who recently passed the licensing exam needs to submit a licence application through a brokerage. Under British Columbia's regulatory framework in effect since August 1, 2021, how are these matters handled?

一名公众人士希望对某持牌人的执业行为提出正式投诉，同时一名刚通过牌照考试的申请人需要经由经纪行递交牌照申请。在 2021 年 8 月 1 日生效的 BC 省现行监管框架下，这些事务如何处理？

RECBC was dissolved and OSRE was merged into BCFSA; although pre-2021 exam materials described them as separate bodies, neither exists as an independent entity since August 1, 2021.

  2. ✓ 正确。依《Finance Statutes Amendment Act, 2021》，自 2021-08-01 起 RECBC 解散、OSRE 并入 BC 金融服务管理局（BC Financial Services Authority, BCFSA）；尽管旧版考试资料仍提及 RECBC/OSRE，现行规则下从考牌、发牌、执业规则、投诉调查到纪律处分全部只面对 BCFSA 一个机构。Correct. Under the Finance Statutes Amendment Act, 2021, RECBC was dissolved and OSRE merged into BCFSA effective August 1, 2021.   3. ✗ 属于四大保护机构张冠李戴陷阱。地产基金会负责研究与公益资助，E&O 保险公司负责职业责任保险，均无发牌或纪律处分权。Incorrect. This is a mismatched public protection entities trap. The Real Estate Foundation funds research and public projects, and the E&O Insurance Corporation provides liability insurance; neither possesses licensing or disciplinary authority.

  4. ✗ 属于四大保护机构张冠李戴陷阱。赔偿基金公司（Real Estate Compensation Fund Corporation）仅负责赔付持牌人挪用信托资金造成的损失，不负责发牌和日常投诉调查。Incorrect. This is a mismatched public protection entities trap. The Real Estate Compensation Fund Corporation only compensates losses resulting from a licensee's misappropriation of trust funds; it does not issue licences or investigate general complaints.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Why did the British Columbia legislature enact the Finance Statutes Amendment Act, 2021 to dissolve the Real Estate Council of BC (RECBC) and merge the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate (OSRE) into the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA)?

BC 省立法机关为何通过《2021 年金融成文法修正案》，解散 BC 地产议会（RECBC）并将地产总监办公室（OSRE）并入 BC 金融服务管理局（BCFSA）？

  2. ✗ 属于 REDMA 与 HBRP 撤销权混淆陷阱。3 个工作日及 0.25% 撤销费是 2023-01-03 生效的住宅买家冷静期（Home Buyer Rescission Period, HBRP），适用于转售交易，不适用于 REDMA 下的开发商楼花销售。Incorrect. This is a REDMA and HBRP rescission rights confusion trap. The 3-business-day period and 0.25% fee belong to the Home Buyer Rescission Period (HBRP) effective January 3, 2023, which applies to residential resales, not to developer pre-sales under REDMA.

  3. ✗ 属于时间窗与通知对象混淆陷阱。REDMA 撤销期为 7 天（90 天是考牌挂科重考间隔），且撤销通知须书面发给开发商（developer）而非 BCFSA。Incorrect. This is a timeline and notice recipient confusion trap. The REDMA rescission window is 7 days (90 days is the exam rewrite waiting period), and written notice must be served on the developer rather than BCFSA.

  4. ✓ 正确。根据 REDMA s. 21(2)，买家享有法定撤销权（rescission rights）：从签订购买协议（第 3 天）与开发商取得买家「已有机会阅读披露声明（disclosure statement）」书面确认（第 1 天）这两天中较晚的一天（第 3 天）起 7 天内，可书面通知开发商撤销。Correct. Under section 21(2) of REDMA, a buyer has a statutory right of rescission: within 7 days counted from the later of the date the purchase agreement was entered into (Day 3) and the date the developer received written acknowledgment of having had an opportunity to read the disclosure statement (Day 1)—namely Day 3—by serving written notice on the developer.
