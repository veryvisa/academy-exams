① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 工具时态题：题干同时给出官方社区规划的地图指定和仍在生效的分区，问今天能不能按规划上的用途施工。真正考的是规划管未来的政策、分区管现在的规则，不符规划要先改规划。Tool-tense questions give both the official community plan map designation and the zoning that is still in force, and ask whether construction can proceed today for the use on the plan. What they test is that the plan is policy for the future and zoning is the rule for the present, and that a use which does not match the plan must amend the plan first.
  • 数字门槛题：合法非合规使用空置了一段时间，或建筑物损毁到某个比例，问旧用途还在不在。真正考的是全省连续停用六个月、地基以上价值百分之七十五；把温哥华的九十天和百分之六十套到全省就会选错。Number-threshold questions set a legal non-conforming use vacant for a period, or a building damaged to some percentage, and ask whether the old use is still there. What they test is six continuous months of discontinuance province-wide and 75 percent of the value above the foundation. Applying Vancouver's 90 days and 60 percent to the whole province picks the wrong answer.
  • 权限边界题：申请人想改用途或提高楼面积比，却去找开发变通许可或变通委员会。真正考的是这两条路都不得变更用途或密度，临时使用许可也不能把用途做成永久。Authority-boundary questions have an applicant who wants to change the use or raise the floor space ratio, but who goes to a development variance permit or the board of variance. What they test is that neither route may change use or density, and that a temporary use permit cannot make the use permanent.
  • 顺序与赔偿题：问听证排在几读之间，或地价因规划、分区下跌后业主能不能索赔。真正考的是听证在一读之后、三读之前，三读和采纳至少隔一天，以及跌价原则上不赔。Sequence-and-compensation questions ask between which readings the hearing sits, or whether the owner can claim after land value falls because of a plan or zoning. What they test is that the hearing is after first reading and before third reading, that third reading and adoption are at least one day apart, and that a drop in value is in principle not compensated.
  • 口径对照题：题干写明按课程手册作答，选项把听证一般要开、旧契约多半仍有效、加户由市里定、便利设施捐助只能谈、住宅需求至少五年，和二零二三年之后的现行规则并排。真正考的是卷面选考试口径，解释里用一句话写出现行规则。Standard-comparison questions say to answer from the course manual, and the options place a hearing generally being required, most old contracts still being in effect, adding a dwelling being decided by the municipality, an amenity contribution only being negotiated, and housing need of at least five years, beside the current rules after 2023. What they test is to choose the exam standard on the answer sheet, and to state the current rule in one sentence in the explanation.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ OCP写成现在能建Treating the OCP as what can be built now

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看见规划图上写着住宅或高密度，就认为今天可以按那个用途报建。规划指定的是打算怎么用，分区才规定现在的用途、密度、退线、高度和停车。The candidate sees residential or high-density written on the plan map and thinks a building application can be made today for that use. The plan designation is the intended use. Zoning is what sets the present use, density, setbacks, height, and parking.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现官方社区规划的指定和仍在生效的分区，两者不一致，选项却说按规划就能施工。The stem gives both an official community plan designation and zoning that is still in force, the two do not match, and an option says construction can proceed under the plan.

⚠ 变通改得了用途Thinking a variance can change the use

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把开发变通许可和变通委员会当成避开改分区的捷径。两条路都可以处理退线、体量这类规定，但都不得变更用途或密度。The candidate treats a development variance permit and the board of variance as a shortcut around a zoning amendment. Both routes can deal with rules such as setbacks and massing, but neither may change use or density.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 申请人想增加户数、改成公寓或提高楼面积比，选项却写成变通许可或委员会命令可以批。The applicant wants to add dwellings, change to an apartment, or raise the floor space ratio, and an option says a variance permit or a board order can approve it.

⚠ 用现行法答考卷Using current law as the exam answer

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把二零二三年之后的强制条款写进考试答案，例如某些住宅改分区禁止听证、小型多户必须允许、住宅需求至少二十年、便利设施费可以单方征收、土地使用契约已全部终止。考试手册没有这些现行规则。The candidate writes mandatory rules from after 2023 into the exam answer, for example that some residential rezonings forbid a hearing, that small-scale multi-unit housing must be allowed, that housing need is at least 20 years, that an amenity cost charge may be imposed unilaterally, and that land use contracts have all ended. The exam manual does not contain these current rules.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写明按课程手册或按执照考试作答，选项里出现禁止听证、省定户数，或二零二四年六月三十日已经全部终止。The stem says to answer from the course manual or for the licensing exam, and an option shows a ban on a hearing, a provincially set unit count, or that all of them had already ended on 30 June 2024.

⚠ 两套数字记串了Mixing up the two sets of numbers

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 全省的连续停用六个月、损毁百分之七十五，和温哥华的停用九十天、火损百分之六十，被记成一套。建筑内部可以把非合规使用扩到整栋，土地范围不能扩大，这两句也常被说反。The province-wide six continuous months of discontinuance and 75 percent damage, and Vancouver's 90 days of discontinuance and 60 percent fire damage, get memorized as one set. A non-conforming use inside a building may extend through the whole building, and the area of land may not be enlarged. Those two sentences are often reversed.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写明物业在不在温哥华市界内，或同时问空置时间和损毁比例，选项用了另一套数字。The stem states whether the property is inside the City of Vancouver, or asks about the vacancy period and the damage percentage together, and an option uses the other set of numbers.

⚠ 分区通过就当能开业Treating zoning approval as a licence to open

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 查完分区允许零售或服务，就认为某一种具体生意也能开。营业执照 bylaw 是另一份文件，可以禁止分区条文没有点名的那一类经营。After checking that zoning allows retail or service, the candidate thinks one particular business can open. The business licensing bylaw is a separate document, and it can prohibit a class of business that the zoning text does not name.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写分区允许大类用途，后面又出现执照 bylaw 禁止这种具体生意，选项说分区已经够了。The stem says zoning allows a broad class of use, and then a licence bylaw prohibits this particular business, and an option says zoning is already enough.

市的权力是省借来的A municipality's power is borrowed from the provinceenabling legislation市政府不是宪法主体，权力来自省的授权立法，省改法就可以收回或扩大。A municipality is not a constitutional body. Its powers come from provincial enabling legislation, and the province can take them back or enlarge them by amending the statute.
改分区跌价原则上不赔A drop from rezoning is in principle not compensatedno compensation采纳官方社区规划、通过分区 bylaw、发出土地利用许可或终止土地使用契约造成的跌价不予补偿，唯一例外是把土地限定为公共用途的 bylaw。No compensation is payable for a drop in value caused by adopting an official community plan, passing a zoning bylaw, issuing a land use permit, or terminating a land use contract. The only exception is a bylaw that limits the land to a public use.
规划是政策，分区是规则The plan is policy and zoning is the ruleofficial community plan官方社区规划管长期方向，分区 bylaw 管现在的用途、密度、退线、高度、停车和招牌；与规划不符的用途必须先改规划。The official community plan governs long-term direction. The zoning bylaw governs the present use, density, setbacks, height, parking, and signs. A use that does not conform to the plan must amend the plan first.
楼面积比与层数无关Floor space ratio does not depend on the number of storeysfloor space ratio楼面积比等于总楼面积除以地块面积，0.5用在1000平方米地块上就是总楼面积500平方米，加层不增加这个总量。Floor space ratio equals total floor area divided by lot area. A ratio of 0.5 on a 1,000 square metre lot is 500 square metres of total floor area. Adding a storey does not increase that total.
考试里加户仍由市定On the exam, adding a dwelling is still decided by the municipalityzoning bylaw考试口径认为独立屋区能不能加户或设独立套间，由地方政府用分区权决定；现行省法已对许多单户和双拼分区规定最低户数。The exam standard is that whether a detached-house zone may add a dwelling or have a secondary suite is decided by the local government through its zoning power. Current provincial law already sets a minimum number of dwellings for many single-detached and duplex zones.
考试住宅需求写五年The exam writes housing need as five yearsofficial community plan考试口径要求官方社区规划写出至少未来五年的住宅需求；现行法改为至少20年，并且必须覆盖住房需求报告中的20年总数。The exam standard requires an official community plan to state housing need for at least the next five years. Current law changes that to at least 20 years, and the plan must cover the 20-year total in the housing needs report.
全省停用六月、损毁七五Province-wide, discontinuance is six months and damage is 75 percentlegal non-conforming use合法非合规使用连续停用满6个月即丧失，损毁达地基以上价值的75%或以上不得按原用途重建；温哥华现行是停用90天、火损60%。A legal non-conforming use is lost after six continuous months of discontinuance. If damage reaches 75 percent or more of the value above the foundation, it may not be rebuilt for the former use. The current Vancouver figures are 90 days of discontinuance and 60 percent fire damage.
两条路都不改用途密度Neither route changes use or densitydevelopment variance permit开发变通许可和变通委员会命令都不得变更用途或密度；临时使用许可最长3年，只能续期一次。A development variance permit and a board of variance order must not change use or density. A temporary use permit runs for at most three years and may be renewed only once.
考试口径旧契约多半还在On the exam standard, most old contracts are still in effectland use contract考试口径说1971至1978年的土地使用契约多半仍然有效，除非双方同意终止，并提到2014年修法要求2024年前终止；现行法是全部已于2024-06-30终止且不补偿。The exam standard says land use contracts from 1971 to 1978 are mostly still in effect unless the parties agree to end them, and it notes that a 2014 amendment required termination before 2024. Current law is that all of them ended on 2024-06-30 with no compensation.
分区通过不等于执照通过Zoning approval is not licence approvalbusiness licensing bylaw分区允许某一类用途，营业执照 bylaw 仍可禁止其中一种具体生意；执照申请不得被不合理拒绝，拒绝时须应要求书面说明理由。Zoning may allow a class of use, and the business licensing bylaw may still prohibit one particular business within that class. A licence application must not be refused unreasonably, and a refusal must give written reasons on request.

第 1 题Q1OCP不是现在能建The OCP is not what can be built now★陷阱：OCP写成现在能建

A buyer is considering a corner lot. The official community plan designates that block for future high-density housing. The municipality's certified zoning map still zones the lot for one detached house, with limits on height, site coverage and the number of dwellings. The buyer asks what the course treats as buildable today.

买方在看一块街角地。官方社区规划把这个街区指定为未来的高密度住宅。市里认证的分区图仍把该地划为只允许一户独立屋，并限制高度、覆盖率和住宅数量。买方问：按课程，今天能盖什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把官方社区规划写成现在能建。规划图指定的是打算怎么用，不是今天的建造许可。Wrong. The trap is treating the official community plan as what can be built now. The plan map designates the intended use. It is not a building permission for today.

  2. ✓ 正确。官方社区规划（official community plan）是政策，管长期方向；分区 bylaw（zoning bylaw）才规定现在能建什么。今天的限度是现行分区允许的内容，不是规划图上的未来高密度。与规划不符的用途，必须先改规划。Correct. The official community plan is policy and governs long-term direction. The zoning bylaw is what states what may be built now. Today's limit is what the current zoning allows, not the future high density on the plan map. A use that does not conform to the plan must amend the plan first.

  3. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把两份文件当成可以二选一。规划采纳之后，此后的 bylaw 和许可必须与它一致，但眼下能盖什么仍以分区规则为准。Wrong. The trap is treating the two documents as a choice of either one. After the plan is adopted, later bylaws and permits must be consistent with it, but what can be built right now is still governed by the zoning rules.

  4. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把两件工具的时态对调。分区 bylaw 管现在，官方社区规划管未来。规划写了高密度，不等于可以跳过改分区直接施工。Wrong. The trap is swapping the time of the two tools. The zoning bylaw governs the present. The official community plan governs the future. High density written in the plan does not mean construction can skip a zoning amendment.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An owner of a highway parcel wants a building permit this year for a small warehouse. The official community plan designates the corridor for future light industry. The certified zoning bylaw still allows only a detached house and a home occupation. The owner says adoption of the plan is enough.

公路边一块地的业主今年就想领取小型仓库的建筑许可。官方社区规划把这条走廊指定为未来的轻工业。经认证的分区 bylaw 仍只允许独立屋和家庭办公。业主说规划已经采纳，这就够了。

  1. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把官方社区规划写成现在能建。规划采纳不取代分区 bylaw，仓库今天仍不被现行分区允许。Wrong. The trap is treating the official community plan as what can be built now. Adoption of the plan does not replace the zoning bylaw. The warehouse is still not allowed by the current zoning today.

  2. ✗ 错误。陷阱是用层数改工具的分工。层数不决定哪一份文件生效。现在能不能建，看分区 bylaw，不看建筑物高矮。Wrong. The trap is using the number of storeys to change which tool does what. The number of storeys does not decide which document is in force. Whether something can be built now is read from the zoning bylaw, not from how tall the building is.

  3. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把家庭办公扩大成仓库。家庭办公是分区里常见的附属规定，课程没有把它写成可以代替工业仓储用途。Wrong. The trap is stretching a home occupation into a warehouse. A home occupation is a common accessory rule in zoning. The course does not write it as a substitute for an industrial warehouse use.

  4. ✓ 正确。官方社区规划（official community plan）只表明未来方向。分区 bylaw（zoning bylaw）没有允许仓库之前，现在的规则仍是独立屋和家庭办公。规划图不能单独支撑建筑许可。Correct. The official community plan shows future direction only. Until the zoning bylaw allows the warehouse, the present rules remain the detached-house and home-occupation rules. The plan map cannot support a building permit on its own.

第 2 题Q2全省停用要满六月Province-wide discontinuance must reach six months★★陷阱：两套数字记串了

A lawful warehouse became a legal non-conforming use when a new zoning bylaw banned warehousing. The building is in a city other than Vancouver. The operator left it empty for five continuous months, then leased it again for warehousing. Nothing was damaged. Which statement matches the course?

一处合法仓库在新的分区 bylaw 禁止仓储之后，成为合法非合规使用。房子在温哥华以外的城市。经营者让它连续空置了五个月，然后又租出去做仓储。建筑物没有损坏。哪一项符合课程？

  1. ✓ 正确。合法非合规使用（legal non-conforming use）可以延续，不因换了占用者而丧失；连续停用满6个月才丧失。五个月未到。现行法只在温哥华市界内把停用门槛改为90天，本题不在温哥华。Correct. A legal non-conforming use may continue, and it is not lost because the occupier changes. It is lost only after six continuous months of discontinuance. Five months has not reached that line. Current law changes the discontinuance threshold to 90 days only inside the City of Vancouver, and this question is not in Vancouver.

  2. ✗ 错误。陷阱是两套数字记串了。90天是《温哥华宪章》的停用门槛，不是全省规则。全省除温哥华外是连续6个月。Wrong. The trap is mixing up the two sets of numbers. Ninety days is the discontinuance threshold in the Vancouver Charter, not the province-wide rule. Outside Vancouver, the province-wide rule is six continuous months.

  3. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把身份绑在原来的占用者身上。非合规使用随所有权更迭不受影响，用途不变而换租户，并不使其丧失。Wrong. The trap is tying the status to the original occupier. A non-conforming use is unaffected by a change of ownership. Changing the tenant while the use stays the same does not end it.

  4. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把空置当成损毁。75%是地基以上价值的重建门槛，由建筑检查员认定。本题没有损毁，空置要单独按6个月计算。Wrong. The trap is treating vacancy as damage. Seventy-five percent is the rebuilding threshold on the value above the foundation, determined by the building inspector. This question has no damage. Vacancy is counted on its own against the six months.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A non-conforming repair shop outside Vancouver closed and stayed empty for eight continuous months. The owner now wants to reopen the same repair use. The building was not damaged. Which statement matches the course?

温哥华以外的一处非合规修理店关门后，连续空置了八个月。业主现在想按原来的修理用途重新开业。建筑物没有损坏。哪一项符合课程？

  1. ✗ 错误。陷阱是自造了一个十二个月的门槛。课程的停用线是连续6个月。出售本身也不终止非合规使用。Wrong. The trap is inventing a twelve-month threshold. The course line for discontinuance is six continuous months. A sale by itself does not end a non-conforming use either.

  2. ✗ 错误。陷阱是两套数字记串了。把90天延长到最多180天，只是温哥华变通委员会的权力。八个月也已经超过180天。Wrong. The trap is mixing up the two sets of numbers. Extending 90 days to 180 days at most is a power of the Vancouver board of variance only. Eight months is also already past 180 days.

  3. ✓ 正确。连续停用满6个月，合法非合规使用（legal non-conforming use）即丧失，此后的使用必须合规。八个月已过这条线。所有权是否更换，不改变这6个月的计算。Correct. After six continuous months of discontinuance, a legal non-conforming use is lost, and later use must conform. Eight months is past that line.   3. ✗ 错误。陷阱是用规划采纳取消契约，并加上赔偿。考试口径不把官方社区规划当成自动终止土地使用契约的事件。终止土地使用契约造成的损失也不予补偿。Wrong. The trap is using adoption of the plan to cancel the contract, and adding compensation. The exam standard does not treat adoption of an official community plan as an event that automatically ends a land use contract. Loss caused by terminating a land use contract is not compensated either.

  4. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把契约当成建筑许可上的备注。它写的是这块地能怎么用，登记之后优先于分区 bylaw，不是施工许可的附注。Wrong. The trap is treating the contract as a note on a building permit. What it states is how this land may be used. Once registered, it ranks ahead of the zoning bylaw. It is not an annotation on a construction permit.
