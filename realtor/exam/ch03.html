① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 概念辨析与归类题：给出一段权利安排（如终身居住、按他人寿命计时、四类损耗、地役权与许可、收益权），要求识别准确的法律术语及权利义务归属（谁付地税和按揭利息、谁付按揭本金和保险）。Concept distinction and classification questions: Given a specific legal arrangement (such as a life estate, estate pur autre vie, four categories of waste, easements versus licences, or profits a prendre), candidates must identify the precise legal terms and allocate rights and duties (who pays property taxes and mortgage interest versus who pays mortgage principal and insurance).
  • 要件判断与随地转移题：设定买卖过户场景，测试地役权三要件、法定路权例外、限制性契约五要件（尤其是消极性质）、建筑方案或未登记三年内短租约是否对新买家有约束力（runs with the land）。Essential elements and running with the land: Setting a purchase and sale scenario to test the three requirements for an easement, the statutory right of way exception, the five requirements for a restrictive covenant (especially its negative nature), building schemes, and whether an unregistered lease of three years or less binds a new purchaser.
  • 附着物与动产两步测试题：给出交易中引发争议的物品（仅凭自身重量放置、轻微固定为了欣赏物品本身或稳定设备、或为了改良土地），考核推定起点与固定意图（更佳用途测试）的判断。Two-step test for fixtures and chattels: Presenting disputed items in a transaction (resting solely by own weight, slightly affixed to enjoy the article itself or steady equipment, or affixed to improve the land), testing the initial presumption and the objective intention of affixation (the better use test).
  • 共有方式与分割后果题：围绕多人买房、转让文件未写明共有方式、一名联权共有人私自出售或立遗嘱处分份额、或共有人申请法院强制出售，考核联权共有四个一致、生存者取得权及分权共有推定。Forms of co-ownership and severance consequences: Focusing on multiple purchasers, transfer documents silent on the form of co-ownership, one joint tenant secretly selling or attempting to devise their interest by will, or applying for a court-ordered sale, testing the four unities of joint tenancy, the right of survivorship, and the statutory presumption of tenancy in common.
  • 考试口径与现行判例边界题：考核普通法地权原则（归复与征收）、Torrens 登记的无可争辩效力及原住民地权在考试口径下的定性，同时在实务情景中区分传统考试口径与最新判例及家事法规则。Course manual standard versus current legal developments: Testing the common law doctrine of estates (escheat and expropriation), the conclusive effect of Torrens indefeasibility, and the status of Aboriginal title under the exam syllabus, while distinguishing traditional course answers from current court decisions and family law rules in practical contexts.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 混淆按揭本息与运营费分担Confusing mortgage principal and interest with operating expense allocations

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉认为住在房子里的终身权人既然享有全部收益，就应当承担全部按揭供款与房屋保险，或误以为剩余权人什么都不用付。Candidates intuitively assume that because the life tenant occupies the property and collects all revenues, they must pay all mortgage instalments and insurance, or mistakenly believe the remainderman pays nothing at all.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 life tenant（终身权人）与 remainderman/reversioner（剩余权人/归复权人）争议谁付地税、按揭利息、按揭本金或保险费。The question stem features a dispute between a life tenant and a remainderman or reversioner over who pays property taxes, mortgage interest, mortgage principal, or insurance premiums.

⚠ 误把积极义务当成随地转移的限制性契约Mistaking a positive obligation for a restrictive covenant that runs with the land

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到契约登记在产权上且为了周边土地受益，就以为任何承诺都能约束下一任买家，忽略了限制性契约必须是消极性质（不做某事）。Seeing a covenant registered on title for the benefit of neighbouring land leads candidates to assume any promise binds subsequent buyers, overlooking the strict requirement that a restrictive covenant must be negative in nature (refraining from doing something).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现契约要求业主必须出钱维修、必须建造或必须完成某项积极行为（positive covenant），并问新买家是否受约束。The question stem mentions a covenant requiring an owner to expend money on maintenance, construct something, or perform an affirmative act (positive covenant), asking if a subsequent purchaser is bound.

⚠ 以为没有受益地就绝无地役权，或有便利就算地役权Assuming an easement can never exist without a dominant tenement, or treating personal convenience as an easement

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 记住了普通法地役权必须有受益地和负担地，却忘了公用事业法定路权（statutory right of way）无需受益地；或者把仅方便业主个人的许可（licence）当成地役权。Candidates remember that common law easements require both a dominant and servient tenement but forget the statutory right of way exception for utilities, or confuse a personal licence that merely accommodates an individual with an easement.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现市政府、王室或公用事业铺设管线（无受益地），或者出现口头允许邻居借用土地、仅方便个人而非改良受益地本身。The question stem involves a municipality, the Crown, or a utility laying pipes or lines with no dominant tenement, or an oral permission allowing a neighbour to use land that accommodates only the person rather than benefiting the land itself.

⚠ 默认多人买房就是联权共有，或以为分割需全员同意Defaulting to joint tenancy when multiple buyers purchase, or assuming severance requires unanimous consent

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 日常习惯把夫妻或合伙买房默认为联权共有，且以为联权共有人是一体，卖掉自己那份必须通知其他共有人或可以用遗嘱留给子女。Candidates routinely assume that spouses or co-purchasers automatically take as joint tenants, or assume joint tenants are inseparable so that one cannot sell without notifying others or can leave their share to children by will.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写转让给两人但文件未写明共有方式，或写某联权共有人立遗嘱处分份额、或瞒着其他共有人单方出售/抵押自己那份权益。The question stem states a transfer to two persons without specifying the co-ownership form, or describes a joint tenant executing a will or unilaterally selling or mortgaging their interest without notifying the others.

⚠ 看到固定就判附着物，忽略固定目的Classifying an item as a fixture solely because it is attached while ignoring the object of annexation

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 死记“固定在墙上或地上就是附着物（fixture）”，没看第二步的“更佳用途测试”——固定是为了更好地使用物品本身还是为了改良土地。Rote memorization that physical attachment creates a fixture overlooks the second-step better use test: whether the purpose of attachment was for the better use and enjoyment of the chattel itself or to improve the land.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述画作挂在钉子上、或设备焊接在地上只是为了稳定设备本身而非改良土地，且买卖合同未列明去留。The question stem describes a picture hung on a nail, or equipment welded to the ground solely to steady the equipment itself rather than improve the land, where the contract of purchase and sale is silent.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
地产权原则与归复、征收Doctrine of estates, escheat, and expropriationdoctrine of estates / escheat / expropriation王室（Crown）是土地的终极所有者，私人持有的是不同存续期的地产权（estate）；业主无遗嘱且无法定继承人时土地归复（escheat）王室，政府亦可依法征收（expropriation）。The Crown is the ultimate owner of all land, and private individuals hold estates of varying duration; when an owner dies intestate without legal heirs, the land escheats to the Crown, and the government may also expropriate land under statutory authority.
终身产权的费用分担与按他人寿命产权Life estate expense allocation and estate pur autre vielife estate / estate pur autre vie / reversioner / remainderman终身权人承担日常运营费（水电、地税）及按揭利息，归复权人或剩余权人承担按揭本金与保险费；终身权人转让其权利后，受让人持有按原终身权人寿命计时的产权（estate pur autre vie）。The life tenant pays current operating expenses (utilities, property taxes) and mortgage interest, while the reversioner or remainderman pays mortgage principal and insurance premiums; when a life tenant transfers their interest, the transferee holds an estate pur autre vie measured by the original life tenant's lifetime.
终身权人的四类损耗责任Four categories of waste and life tenant liabilityvoluntary / permissive / ameliorating / equitable waste主动损耗（拆毁设施）须赔偿；放任损耗（任由自然老化）一般无须赔偿；改良性损耗提升物业价值；衡平法损耗（恶意毁损）即使文件免责也不可豁免。Voluntary waste (demolishing improvements) creates liability for damages; permissive waste (allowing deterioration through passive neglect) generally carries no liability; ameliorating waste improves the property value; equitable waste (malicious destruction) cannot be excused even where the instrument grants exemption from waste.
三年内未登记短租约的约束力Binding effect of unregistered leases of three years or lessleasehold estate / Land Title Act s.23(2)(d)产权证的无可争辩效力不排除期限 3 年以内且承租人已实际占用的未登记租约，新买家仍受其约束。The indefeasibility of a certificate of title does not override an unregistered lease for a term not exceeding three years where the tenant is in actual occupation, and a subsequent purchaser remains bound by it under section 23(2)(d) of the Land Title Act.
地役权三要件与法定路权例外Three essential requirements for an easement and statutory right of way exceptioneasement / dominant and servient tenement / statutory right of way普通法地役权必须有受益地和负担地、真正让受益地本身受益、且范围明确；但依 Land Title Act s.218 设立的法定路权无需受益地即可成立。A common law easement requires both a dominant and servient tenement, must accommodate the dominant tenement itself, and must be capable of forming the subject matter of a grant; however, a statutory right of way created under section 218 of the Land Title Act requires no dominant tenement.
限制性契约五要件与建筑方案Five requirements for a restrictive covenant and building schemesrestrictive covenant / positive covenant / building scheme随地转移的限制性契约内容必须是消极性质（不做某事），积极契约不随地转移；建筑方案（Land Title Act s.220）一经登记即统一约束细分地块内所有现在及未来的业主与租户。A restrictive covenant that runs with the land must be negative in nature (refraining from an act), whereas positive covenants do not run with the land; a statutory building scheme registered under section 220 of the Land Title Act uniformly binds all current and future owners and tenants within a subdivision.
收益权与许可的区别Distinction between profit a prendre and licenceprofit à prendre / licence收益权（取走土地产出物）无需受益地即可独立存在且须明示设立；许可（licence）只是合同权利，不能登记在产权上，也从不随地转移。A profit a prendre (the right to enter land and sever and take products of the soil) can exist in gross without a dominant tenement and must be expressly granted; a licence is merely a personal contractual right that cannot be registered on title and never runs with the land.
自然支撑权与空间、水权边界Natural right of support and boundaries of air, water, and subsurface rightssupport / air space subdivision / water licence相邻土地之间的自然支撑权只保护土地本身而不延伸至建筑物（保护建筑物需另设支撑地役权）；业主对有效利用的地上空间享权，水权与地下矿产通常归省政府。The natural right of support between adjoining parcels protects the land in its natural state only and does not extend to buildings (protecting structures requires an easement for support); an owner owns air space only to the extent of ordinary use and enjoyment, while water rights and subsurface minerals are generally reserved to the Crown.
附着物与动产的两步测试Two-step test for fixtures and chattelsfixture / chattel只凭自身重量放置、没有其他固定的物品先推定为动产（有证据表明有意让它成为土地一部分时除外），轻微固定物品先推定为附着物；再按更佳用途测试看固定意图是为了更好地使用物品本身（动产）还是改良土地（附着物）。Articles resting solely by their own weight with no other affixation are presumed to be chattels (unless circumstances show they were intended to form part of the land), while even slightly affixed articles are presumed to be fixtures; the better use test then examines whether the affixation was intended for the better use of the article itself (chattel) or for the permanent improvement of the land (fixture).
联权共有、分权共有推定与强制出售Joint tenancy, statutory tenancy in common presumption, and partition or salejoint tenancy / tenancy in common / four unities / right of survivorship / severance联权共有须具备四个一致且有生存者取得权（不可遗嘱处分，但可不通知共有人单方处分而分割为分权共有）；未写明时依 Property Law Act s.11 默认推定为相等份额的分权共有；合计持有 1/2 或以上权益可依 Partition of Property Act s.6 请求法院强制出售。Joint tenancy requires the four unities and carries the right of survivorship (cannot be defeated by will, but can be severed unilaterally without notice to create a tenancy in common); an instrument silent on co-ownership is presumed under section 11 of the Property Law Act to create a tenancy in common in equal shares; co-owners holding 1/2 or more of the interest may request a court order for sale under section 6 of the Partition of Property Act.

第 1 题Q1终身产权转让与本息费用分担Life estate transfer and allocation of mortgage principal and interest★★陷阱：混淆按揭本息与运营费分担

Evelyn holds a life estate in a residential property, with her nephew Marcus named as the remainderman in fee simple. Evelyn later transfers her entire interest in the property to her friend Diana. While Evelyn is still alive, whose life measures the duration of Diana's estate, and how are the property expenses allocated between the holder of the life estate and Marcus?

Evelyn 持有一处住宅物业的终身产权（life estate），其侄子 Marcus 被指定为完全保有产权的剩余权人（remainderman）。随后 Evelyn 将她在该物业中的全部权益转让给朋友 Diana。在 Evelyn 在世期间，Diana 的产权存续期按谁的寿命计时？终身产权持有人与 Marcus 之间的物业费用应如何分担？

  1. ✓ 正确。终身权人（life tenant）将权利转让给他人后，受让人拿到的是按原终身权人（Evelyn）寿命计时的产权（estate pur autre vie），一旦 Evelyn 身故，Diana 的权利即告终止；费用分担上，终身权人承担年度运营费用（水电、地税）与按揭利息（不含本金），剩余权人（remainderman）为保护未来权益负责偿还按揭本金与保险费。Correct. When a life tenant transfers their interest to another person, the transferee receives an estate pur autre vie measured by the lifetime of the original life tenant (Evelyn); upon Evelyn's death, Diana's estate automatically terminates. Regarding expense allocation, the life tenant is responsible for annual operating expenses (utilities, property taxes) and mortgage interest (excluding principal), while the remainderman (Marcus) is responsible for paying mortgage principal and insurance premiums to protect their future estate.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是“转让即改按受让人寿命计时”及“住户全包按揭本息”的双重陷阱：终身权人不能转让出超出自己寿命的权利，故仍按 Evelyn 的寿命计时（estate pur autre vie）；且按揭本金由剩余权人承担，地税由终身权人承担。Incorrect. This falls into the double trap of assuming a transfer resets the duration to the transferee's life and that the resident must cover all mortgage payments: a life tenant cannot transfer a greater interest than they possess, so duration remains measured by Evelyn's life (estate pur autre vie); furthermore, mortgage principal is paid by the remainderman, while property taxes are paid by the life tenant.

  3. ✗ 错误。虽然正确识别了按他人寿命计时的产权（estate pur autre vie），但把终身权人与剩余权人的费用义务完全颠倒了：日常运营费与按揭利息归终身权人，按揭本金与保险费归剩余权人。Incorrect. Although it correctly identifies an estate pur autre vie measured by Evelyn's life, it reverses the expense obligations between the life tenant and the remainderman: current operating expenses and mortgage interest belong to the life tenant, whereas mortgage principal and insurance belong to the remainderman.

  4. ✗ 错误。终身产权属于自由保有产权（freehold），可以在终身权人在世时转让给他人成为 estate pur autre vie，转让行为本身不会导致终身产权自动消灭。Incorrect. A life estate is a freehold estate that can be transferred during the life tenant's lifetime to become an estate pur autre vie; the transfer itself does not automatically extinguish the life estate.

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Arthur grants a life estate in a rental cottage to his sister Clara, while retaining the future fee simple interest for himself as the reversioner. Clara collects all the rental income from the cottage. Which of the following statements accurately describes the legal obligations of Clara and Arthur during Clara's lifetime?

Arthur 将一栋出租度假屋的终身产权（life estate）授予妹妹 Clara，同时为自己保留将来的完全保有产权，作为归复权人（reversioner）。Clara 收取该度假屋的全部租金收益。下列哪项准确描述了在 Clara 在世期间她与 Arthur 的法律义务？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入了“谁收租谁就付清全部本息和保险”的直觉陷阱：终身权人（life tenant）虽然享有全部租金收益，但只承担年度运营费用（水电、地税）和按揭利息，按揭本金与保险费由归复权人（reversioner）承担。Incorrect. This falls into the intuitive trap of assuming that whoever collects rent must pay all principal, interest, and insurance: although the life tenant collects all rental income, they are only responsible for annual operating expenses (utilities, property taxes) and mortgage interest, while mortgage principal and insurance premiums are paid by the reversioner.

  2. ✓ 正确。终身权人（life tenant）在有生之年享有占有、使用和收取全部收益的权利，对应的义务是承担年度运营费用（水电、地税）与按揭利息（不含本金）；归复权人（reversioner）Arthur 为保护自己未来的完整产权，负责偿还按揭本金与保险费。Correct. A life tenant enjoys the right to possess, use, and collect all revenues during their lifetime, with the corresponding obligation to pay annual operating expenses (utilities, property taxes) and mortgage interest (excluding principal); the reversioner (Arthur) is responsible for paying mortgage principal and insurance premiums to protect their future complete fee simple estate.

  3. ✗ 错误。终身产权虽然作为一项负担（charge）登记在完全保有产权名下，但占有和收益归终身权人 Clara，因此日常水电和地税由终身权人承担，而非归复权人。Incorrect. Although the life estate is registered as a charge against the fee simple title, possession and revenues belong to the life tenant Clara; therefore, routine utilities and property taxes are paid by the life tenant, not the reversioner.

  4. ✗ 错误。终身权人负有不得造成损耗（waste）的义务；主动拆毁建筑属于主动损耗（voluntary waste），终身权人须对归复权人承担赔偿责任。Incorrect. A life tenant owes an obligation not to commit waste; intentionally demolishing a building constitutes voluntary waste, for which the life tenant is liable in damages to the reversioner.

第 2 题Q2限制性契约的消极性质要件Negative nature requirement of a restrictive covenant★★陷阱：误把积极义务当成随地转移的限制性契约

A developer sells Lot 1 to a buyer and registers a covenant against Lot 1 for the benefit of the neighbouring Lot 2, which the developer retains. The registered document contains two clauses: Clause I prohibits the owner of Lot 1 from operating any commercial business on the land, and Clause II requires the owner of Lot 1 to contribute money each year to repaint a fence on Lot 2. When Lot 1 is later resold to a new purchaser, which clause runs with the land to bind the new purchaser?

  2. ✓ 正确。两步测试规则：（1）只凭自身重量放置、没有其他固定的物品推定为动产（有证据表明有意让它成为土地一部分时除外）；（2）哪怕只是轻微固定，也先推定为附着物（fixture），除非能证明固定的意图（更佳用途测试）是为了让该物品本身作为动产（chattel）更好用，而不是为了改良土地本身。Correct. The rules of the two-step test state: (1) articles resting solely by their own weight with no other affixation are presumed to be chattels (unless evidence shows they were intended to form part of the land); (2) articles even slightly affixed are initially presumed to be fixtures, unless it can be shown that the object of annexation (better use test) was for the better use and enjoyment of the article itself as a chattel rather than to improve the land.

  3. ✗ 错误。轻微固定或焊接产生的附着物推定是可推翻的，法院会进一步通过“更佳用途测试”考察固定的客观意图。Incorrect. The presumption of a fixture arising from slight affixation or welding is rebuttable; courts examine the objective intention of affixation through the better use test.

  4. ✗ 错误。法院通过固定程度和客观可见的“更佳用途”来判断固定意图，而不是听凭卖方挂牌时未写入合同的私下想法。Incorrect. Courts determine the intention of affixation by examining the degree of attachment and the objectively visible better use test, rather than relying on secret subjective intentions of the seller unstated in the contract.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

第 6 题Q6共有默认推定与联权共有单方分割Co-ownership statutory presumption and unilateral severance of joint tenancy★★★陷阱：默认多人买房就是联权共有，或以为分割需全员同意

Consider two co-ownership situations in British Columbia: In Situation 1, a transfer deed conveys a cottage to 'Liam and Noah' without stating whether they take as joint tenants or tenants in common, and without stating their share proportions. In Situation 2, three sisters—Ava, Mia, and Zoe—properly hold a house as registered joint tenants, and Ava secretly sells her share to a stranger, Leo, without telling Mia or Zoe, and then Ava dies leaving a will. What is the legal outcome in both situations?

考虑不列颠哥伦比亚省的两个共有情形：情形一，一份转让契据将一栋度假屋转让给“Liam 和 Noah”，但未写明他们是联权共有还是分权共有，也未写明各自的份额比例；情形二，三姐妹 Ava、Mia 和 Zoe 合法登记为一套房屋的联权共有人（joint tenants），随后 Ava 瞒着 Mia 和 Zoe 私下将自己那份卖给了陌生人 Leo，不久后 Ava 去世并留有遗嘱。这两种情形的法律结果是什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。两处均踩中核心陷阱：第一，Property Law Act s.11 规定未写明时默认推定为分权共有（tenancy in common），而非联权共有；第二，联权共有人单方出售自己的权益不需要征得其余共有人的同意或知情。Incorrect. Both parts hit core traps: first, section 11 of the Property Law Act provides that when an instrument is silent, the statutory presumption is a tenancy in common, not a joint tenancy; second, a joint tenant can unilaterally transfer their interest without the consent or knowledge of the other joint tenants.

  2. ✗ 错误。虽然情形一正确指出了分权共有（tenancy in common）默认推定，但在情形二中犯了“一人分割导致全员解除联权共有”的错误：三名联权共有人中有一人单方转让份额时，仅就转让出去的那一份转化为分权共有，其余共有人（Mia 和 Zoe）之间仍维持联权共有（joint tenancy）关系。Incorrect. While Situation 1 correctly identifies the statutory presumption of tenancy in common, Situation 2 makes the error of assuming severance by one joint tenant severs the entire co-ownership: when one of three joint tenants unilaterally transfers their share, severance occurs only as to that transferred share, while the remaining co-owners (Mia and Zoe) continue as joint tenants between themselves.

  3. ✓ 正确。情形一：依 Property Law Act s.11，转让文件将土地转让给两人或以上（个人代表或受托人除外）且未明确表达相反意图时，一律推定为分权共有（tenancy in common），且份额推定相等。情形二：任何一位联权共有人（joint tenant）都可以单方出售、抵押或处分自己的权益，无需其余共有人同意或知情；一旦处分即发生分割（severance），受让人 Leo 就该份权益成为分权共有人，而其余共有人 Mia 与 Zoe 之间仍维持联权共有关系。Correct. Situation 1: Under section 11 of the Property Law Act, a transfer to two or more persons (other than personal representatives or trustees) without expressing a contrary intention is presumed to create a tenancy in common in equal shares. Situation 2: Any joint tenant can unilaterally sell, mortgage, or dispose of their interest during their lifetime without the consent or knowledge of the other joint tenants; such a disposition operates as a severance, so the transferee Leo holds that share as a tenant in common, while the remaining joint tenants Mia and Zoe remain joint tenants between themselves.

  4. ✗ 错误。联权共有（joint tenancy）的核心是生存者取得权（right of survivorship），联权共有人不能通过遗嘱（will）处分或分割联权共有份额；Ava 是在生前通过出售完成了分割（severance），故其份额已归买受人 Leo，同样不会进入她的遗产。Incorrect. The defining feature of a joint tenancy is the right of survivorship; a joint tenant cannot dispose of or sever a joint tenancy interest by will. Ava completed a severance during her lifetime by selling her interest, so that share passed to the buyer Leo and did not form part of her estate under the will.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Two business partners acquire a warehouse under a transfer instrument that names both of them as transferees but says nothing about the form of co-ownership. Meanwhile, on a separate residential property held by two brothers in a valid joint tenancy, one brother executes a last will and testament leaving 'all my share in the house to my daughter' without taking any inter vivos step to sever the joint tenancy before he dies. What is the legal result for the warehouse and the residential property?

两名商业合伙人通过一份将两人列为受让人但只字未提共有方式的转让文件购入一间仓库。与此同时，在两兄弟以有效联权共有（joint tenancy）方式持有的另一处住宅物业上，其中一名兄弟立下一份遗嘱，写明“将我在该房屋中的全部份额留给我的女儿”，但在去世前并未采取任何生前法律行为来分割该联权共有。该仓库与该住宅物业的法律结果分别是什么？

  1. ✓ 正确。依 Property Law Act s.11，转让文件未明确表达相反意图时，默认推定为份额相等的分权共有（tenancy in common）；而在联权共有（joint tenancy）中，由于生存者取得权（right of survivorship），一名共有人身故时其份额自动归于在世的共有人，不能通过遗嘱处分。Correct. Under section 11 of the Property Law Act, where an instrument does not express a contrary intention, the statutory presumption is a tenancy in common in equal shares; in contrast, in a joint tenancy, the right of survivorship dictates that upon the death of one joint tenant, their interest vests automatically in the surviving joint tenant and cannot be disposed of by will.

  2. ✗ 错误。两个结论全反了：法律默认推定是分权共有而非联权共有；且联权共有份额不能用遗嘱处分。Incorrect. Both conclusions are reversed: the statutory presumption is tenancy in common rather than joint tenancy; furthermore, a joint tenancy interest cannot be disposed of by will.

  3. ✗ 错误。立遗嘱不会导致联权共有分割（severance），联权共有人必须在生前通过出售、抵押、协议或法院申请等方式分割，否则死后由生存者取得权自动转移给在世共有人。Incorrect. Executing a will does not sever a joint tenancy; a joint tenant must sever the joint tenancy during their lifetime through sale, mortgage, agreement, or court application, otherwise the right of survivorship automatically vests the interest in the surviving joint tenant upon death.

  4. ✗ 错误。这两处物业均有合法共有人，根本不适用绝嗣无继承人的归复（escheat）制度。Incorrect. Both properties have lawful co-owners, so the doctrine of escheat (applicable only where an owner dies intestate without legal heirs) does not apply.

答案Answer：a

第 7 题Q7原住民地权考试口径与现行判例Aboriginal title under the exam syllabus versus current case law★★陷阱：混淆考试资料考试口径与最新法院判例

A prospective buyer is considering a $450,000 waterfront parcel registered in fee simple near a historic Indigenous settlement. First, how does the licensing course manual (the exam standard) classify Aboriginal title in relation to estates in land? Second, how should the licensee handle the transaction in practice in light of the 2025 BC Supreme Court ruling in Cowichan Tribes v. Canada?

一名潜在买家正在考虑购买一块估值 $450,000、位于历史原住民聚落遗址附近、登记为完全保有产权（fee simple）的滨水地块。第一，牌照课程考试资料（考试口径）如何界定原住民地权（Aboriginal title）与地产权（estates）的关系？第二，鉴于 2025 年 BC 最高法院在 Cowichan Tribes v. Canada 案中的裁决，持牌人在实务中应如何处理这笔交易？

  1. ✗ 错误。考试口径明确指出原住民地权（Aboriginal title）不是一种 estate，不能像 fee simple 一样自由转让；且实务中遇到敏感地块绝不能替客户打包票说“产权证没问题”。Incorrect. The exam syllabus explicitly states that Aboriginal title is not an estate in land and cannot be freely alienated like fee simple; furthermore, in practice a licensee must never give absolute assurances to a buyer that a title certificate removes all risk when dealing with sensitive lands.

  2. ✗ 错误。考试口径下，Torrens 产权证具有无可争辩（indefeasible）效力，是对王室及所有其他人证明业主享有 fee simple 的决定性证据；且持牌人不应越界自行起草复杂的产权法律意见，应转介产权律师。Incorrect. Under the exam standard, a Torrens certificate of title is indefeasible and serves as conclusive evidence of fee simple ownership against the Crown and all others; furthermore, a licensee must not draft complex title indemnity clauses and should refer the client to a property lawyer.

  3. ✗ 错误。原住民地权不是租赁产权（leasehold estate）；18 个月是 Cowichan 案对部分政府所持 fee simple 无效宣告的延后生效期，且该案目前正在上诉中。Incorrect. Aboriginal title is not a leasehold estate; 18 months was the suspension period granted by the court in the Cowichan decision regarding declarations of invalidity for certain Crown-held fee simple interests, and that decision is currently under appeal.

  4. ✓ 正确。按考试口径，原住民地权（Aboriginal title）不是一种地产权（estate），不能像 fee simple 那样自由转让，且 Land Title Act 下的产权证具有无可争辩（indefeasible）效力；而在现行实务中，2025 BCSC 1490（正在上诉）认定原住民地权与 fee simple 可并存且两者均非绝对，因此针对这宗估值 $450,000 的敏感滨水地块，正确动作是提醒买方在尽调阶段咨询产权律师。Correct. Under the exam standard, Aboriginal title is not an estate in land, cannot be freely transferred like fee simple, and a certificate of title under the Land Title Act is indefeasible; in current practice, 2025 BCSC 1490 (under appeal) held that Aboriginal title and fee simple can coexist and neither is absolute, so for this sensitive $450,000 waterfront parcel, the proper action is to advise the buyer to retain a property lawyer during due diligence.

答案Answer：d

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

When answering an examination question based on the licensing course manual regarding the Torrens registration system and Aboriginal title, which statement represents the exam answer, and what is the current legal context noted for practice?

在回答基于牌照课程考试资料的关于 Torrens 登记制度与原住民地权的考试题时，哪项陈述代表考试标准答案？为实务指出的现行法律背景又是什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。无论考试口径还是现行判例都不支持这两句话：考试口径认为产权证具有无可争辩效力，而 2025 年判决明确拒绝了“批出 fee simple 就自动消灭原住民地权”的主张。Incorrect. Neither the exam syllabus nor current case law supports these statements: the exam syllabus treats a certificate of title as indefeasible, while the 2025 decision explicitly rejected the argument that granting fee simple automatically extinguished Aboriginal title.

  2. ✓ 正确。考试口径下，产权证具有无可争辩（indefeasible）效力，且原住民地权（Aboriginal title）不是一种 estate；现行实务中，Cowichan Tribes v. Canada (2025 BCSC 1490，上诉中) 认定原住民地权与 fee simple 可以并存、两者都不是绝对产权，重叠时须逐案调和。Correct. Under the exam standard, a certificate of title is conclusive and Aboriginal title is not an estate in land; in current practice, Cowichan Tribes v. Canada (2025 BCSC 1490, under appeal) held that Aboriginal title and fee simple can coexist and neither is absolute, requiring reconciliation on a case-by-case basis when they overlap.

  3. ✗ 错误。收益权（profit à prendre）是进入他人土地取走产出物的权利，s.218 规定的是无需受益地的法定路权，两者都不是原住民地权；考试口径只说原住民地权不是一种 estate。Incorrect. A profit a prendre is a right to enter land and take products of the soil, and section 218 governs statutory rights of way without a dominant tenement, neither of which describes Aboriginal title; the exam syllabus states only that Aboriginal title is not an estate.

  4. ✗ 错误。DRIPA 要求 BC 政府逐步让省法与联合国原住民权利宣言保持一致，本身不直接规范某一笔交易的产权登记程序，更没有给住宅或共管转让设部长审批。Incorrect. The Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples Act (DRIPA) mandates that BC progressively align provincial laws with the UN Declaration, but it does not directly regulate individual title transfer registrations, nor does it impose ministerial approval on residential or strata sales.
