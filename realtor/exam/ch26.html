第 26 章 · 科技与持牌人Chapter 26 · Technology and the Real Estate LicenseeTechnology Licensee

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 四层归属题：同一部手机上的行为，问它归执业、隐私、营销触达还是电子形式。真正考的是四层各管一段、同时适用，违反另外三层时仍可按诚实与合理谨慎追究。Layer questions take one act on the same phone and ask whether it belongs to practice regulation, privacy, marketing outreach, or electronic form. What they test is that the four layers each govern one stretch and apply at the same time, and that a breach of any of the other three can still be pursued under honesty and reasonable care and skill.
  • 署名与修饰题：中文广告、资料页、联合挂牌，或一张改过的照片。真正考的是经纪行持牌名必须按登记原样出现，以及修掉的东西是杂物还是这处物业的现实条件。Name and edit questions use a Chinese advertisement, a profile page, a joint listing, or an altered photograph. What they test is that the brokerage's licensee name must appear exactly as registered, and whether what was removed is clutter or a real condition of the property.
  • 电子形式题：买卖合同、挂牌、披露表，对比须登记的产权转让。真正考的是电子签名一般算数，创设或转让土地权益且须登记的文件被排除，卖方不能只在屏幕上完成转让。Electronic-form questions set a contract of purchase and sale, a listing, or a disclosure form against a transfer of title that must be registered. What they test is that an electronic signature generally counts, that a document creating or transferring an interest in land which must be registered is excluded, and that the seller cannot complete the transfer on a screen alone.
  • 同意窗口题：开放日或申请之后还能否群发。题干写课程原文时，真正考的是考试口径把既有关系写成两年；现行才把购买和询价拆开。Consent-window questions ask whether a bulk message is still allowed after an open house or an application. When the stem follows the course manual, what they test is that the exam standard writes an existing relationship as two years, and that only the current law splits a purchase from an inquiry.
  • 版本题：题干写执照考试或课程原文，问电子记录的旧条号、向谁要打印件、代表关系披露表谁发布。真正考的是按课程原文作答，不把现行条号或现行发布机构写进卷面。Version questions state that the stem is the licensing exam or the course manual, and ask for the old rule numbers on electronic records, who may demand a printout, or who publishes the Disclosure of Representation form. What they test is answering from the course manual, and not writing the current rule numbers or the current publishing body on the answer sheet.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 译名当持牌名Treating a translation as the licensee name

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 中文译名、缩写或集团标志看起来像已经署名，于是觉得资料页和广告都过关了。规矩要的是登记簿上的经纪行持牌名原样，译名等于用了一个没有登记的行名。A translation into Chinese, an abbreviation, or a group emblem looks as if the name is already there, so the profile page and the advertisement both seem to pass. The rule wants the brokerage's licensee name exactly as it stands on the register. A translation is the same as using a brokerage name that is not registered.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 广告、名片、电台口播或社交资料页上的行名，是译名、简称或只有标志，而不是执照上的全称。The brokerage name on an advertisement, a business card, a radio read, or a social profile page is a translation, a short form, or only an emblem, and not the full name on the licence.

⚠ 电子签套到转让Extending an e-signature to a transfer

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 买卖合同可以在屏幕上签，于是把同一套做法套到产权转让表。须登记才能对抗第三人的土地权益文件不走电子交易法，卖方仍要在纸上签并经见证。A contract of purchase and sale can be signed on a screen, so the same method gets applied to a transfer of title. A document of an interest in land that must be registered before it binds a third party does not go through the Electronic Transactions Act. The seller must still sign on paper, and the signature must be witnessed.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 文件是产权转让、创设或转让土地权益，并且要拿去登记；或者有人说卖方自己在屏幕上签名就能过户。The document is a transfer of title, or it creates or transfers an interest in land, and it is to be registered. Or someone says the seller's own signature on a screen is enough to transfer title.

⚠ 用现行口径答卷Answering with the current standard

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 现行询价窗口是六个月，电子记录是第91条和第92条、向总监要打印件，披露表由监管机构发布。这些是现行法。题干如果写课程原文，卷面要的仍是两年、旧条号和议会。The current inquiry window is six months. Electronic records are rules 91 and 92, a printout is demanded from the superintendent, and the disclosure form is published by the regulator. Those are the current law. If the stem follows the course manual, the answer sheet still wants two years, the old section numbers, and the Council.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 licensing exam、course manual，或问考试要的是哪一套、课程原文怎么写。The stem says licensing exam or course manual, or it asks which version the exam wants, or how the course manual puts it.

⚠ 私密群当例外Treating a closed group as an exception

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 封闭群组、私聊和个人账号看起来没有外人，于是把客户底价、收入或出售动机发出去。保密义务没有私下场合除外，个人账号上的言行也可以构成有失体面的行为。A closed group, a private chat, and a personal account look as if no outsider is there, so a client's bottom price, income, or reason for selling gets posted. The duty of confidentiality has no exception for a private setting. Conduct on a personal account can also be conduct unbecoming.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 客户保密信息出现在非公开群、私聊或个人账号，说话的人强调群很小、已设为私密或是在下班后。Confidential client information appears in a non-public group, a private chat, or a personal account, and the speaker stresses that the group is small, that it is set to private, or that it is after hours.

⚠ 通报义务套错法Applying the notice duty under the wrong statute

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 公共机构那部法有强制泄露通报，联邦私营部门法也常被中文资料写成主法，于是给经纪行套上一条并不存在的通报期限。省内日常业务的主法没有强制通报条款。The public-sector statute has mandatory breach notification, and Chinese-language materials often treat the federal private-sector statute as the main statute as well, so a brokerage is given a notification deadline that does not exist for it. The main statute for ordinary business inside the province has no mandatory notification provision.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 出事的是经纪行在省内收集的客户资料，题干问必须通知谁，或把公共机构、联邦法当成主法。What was lost is client information a brokerage collected inside the province. The stem asks who must be notified, or it treats the public-sector statute or the federal statute as the main statute.

第 1 题Q1行名须按登记原样The brokerage name must appear as registered★★陷阱：译名当持牌名

Priya posts listing commentary in Chinese on a business social-media account. The profile page shows a Chinese translation of the brokerage's name and a group emblem. It does not show the brokerage's licensed name in the form on the licence. The posts themselves do not repeat a brokerage name. Priya says the guidance does not require every post to carry the brokerage name, so the account complies. Which statement is correct?

普丽娅在一个业务社交账号上用中文发布房源评论。个人资料页上是经纪行名称的中文翻译和一个集团标志，没有执照上那种形态的经纪行持牌名。帖子本身也没有再写一遍行名。普丽娅说，指引并不要求每一条帖子都写行名，所以这个账号是合规的。哪一句话是对的？

  1. ✗ 不要求每一条帖子都写行名，前提是资料页已经写了登记原样的经纪行名称。这些帖子没重复行名并不是合规的理由。这是把“帖子可以不重复”读成了“资料页也可以不写登记名”，属于译名当持牌名。Posts do not each have to carry the brokerage name, but only if the profile page already shows the brokerage name in its registered form. That these posts do not repeat the name is not a reason the account complies. This reads 'posts need not repeat the name' as 'the profile page need not show the registered name.' It treats a translation as the licensee name.

  2. ✓ 规则第40条第2款要求，任何情况下经纪行的持牌名（licensee name）都必须显著且易读。出于业务目的使用社交媒体时，个人资料页必须写上所属经纪行名称，不要求每一条帖子重复。外语广告不得把经纪行名称翻译成其他语言，行名必须按登记形态原样出现。资料页只有译名和标志，第40条第2款没有满足，每一条帖子都处在违规状态。Rule 40(2) requires that in every case the brokerage's licensee name be prominent and easy to read. When social media is used for a business purpose, the profile page must show the name of the brokerage. Not every post has to repeat it. An advertisement in another language must not translate the brokerage name into that language. The name must appear exactly in its registered form. A profile page that shows only a translation and an emblem does not satisfy rule 40(2). Every post is then in breach.

  3. ✗ 集团标志不是持牌名。持牌名是登记上的名称，不是标志、缩写或译名。规则第39条还要求在提供地产服务时标明自己的持牌名，代表、副经纪和管理经纪必须同时标明经纪行的持牌名。这是用标志代替行名。A group emblem is not the licensee name. The licensee name is the name on the register, not an emblem, an abbreviation, or a translation. Rule 39 also requires a person to state their own licensee name when providing real estate services. A representative, an associate broker, and a managing broker must also state the brokerage's licensee name. This uses an emblem in place of the brokerage name.

  4. ✗ 原因说反了。资料页已经按登记原样写了行名之后，帖子可以不重复。不能过关的是资料页上的译名，翻译不能代替登记名称。这仍是译名当持牌名。The reason is reversed. Once the profile page shows the brokerage name exactly as registered, the posts need not repeat it. What fails is the translation on the profile page. A translation cannot stand in for the registered name. This still treats a translation as the licensee name.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A licensee prints a Chinese flyer for a listing. The licensee's own name appears. The brokerage appears only under a translated name the licensee invented, because the translation sounds friendlier. Which statement is correct?

一名持牌人印了一张中文房源传单。持牌人自己的姓名印在上面。经纪行只以持牌人自拟的一个译名出现，理由是译名听起来更亲切。哪一句话是对的？

  1. ✓ 中文传单可以加中文说明，但经纪行的持牌名（licensee name）必须按BCFSA登记的形态原样出现，不得翻译成其他语言。自拟的亲切译名等于发布了一个没有登记的行名，规则第40条第2款没有满足。本人的姓名同样要按登记形态呈现。A flyer in Chinese may add an explanation in Chinese, but the brokerage's licensee name must appear exactly in the form registered with BCFSA. It must not be translated into another language. A friendlier translation the licensee invented is the same as publishing a brokerage name that is not registered. Rule 40(2) is not satisfied. The licensee's own name must also appear in its registered form.

  2. ✗ 不得翻译的正是非英文广告里的行名。语种不能成为例外。这是译名当持牌名，而且把例外用在了最容易出错的外语广告上。What must not be translated is the brokerage name in an advertisement that is not in English. The language cannot become an exception. This treats a translation as the licensee name, and it places the supposed exception on the foreign-language advertisement, which is where the mistake is easiest.

  3. ✗ 资料页规则是社交媒体的落地口径，不是说纸质广告可以不写行名。规则第39条和第40条适用于提供地产服务过程中的表明身份，地产广告包括传单、招牌和告示。这是把渠道收成了只有网页。The profile-page rule is how the requirement lands on social media. It does not say a paper advertisement may omit the brokerage name. Rules 39 and 40 apply to identifying oneself while providing real estate services. Real estate advertising includes flyers, signs, and notices. This reduces the channels to a web page alone.

  4. ✗ 标志不是登记上的持牌名，加上标志也不能治愈译名。行名必须显著、易读，并且是登记原样。这是用标志代替行名。An emblem is not the licensee name on the register, and adding an emblem does not cure a translation. The brokerage name must be prominent, easy to read, and exactly as registered. This uses an emblem in place of the brokerage name.

第 2 题Q2另层违规仍可追究A breach of another layer can still be pursued★★陷阱：四层互相替代

Owen keeps copies of a buyer's government identification on a phone, and the copies are later exposed. Owen says this is only a privacy-statute problem, so the real estate regulator cannot treat it as a licensing breach. Which statement matches this lesson?

欧文把一份买方政府身份证件的复印件存在手机里，后来这些复印件外泄了。欧文说，这只是隐私法的问题，所以地产监管机构不能把它当成牌照违规。哪一句话符合这一课？

  1. ✓ 四层各管一段，互不替代。四层里只有执业监管由BCFSA主管，但隐私、营销触达或电子形式出了问题，仍可用规则第33条的诚实义务（duty of honesty）和第34条的合理谨慎与技能（reasonable care and skill）追究。说“违反的是隐私法，不关地产监管的事”不成立。使用人工智能的指引也是同一句：使用工具不豁免任何法律与道德义务。The four layers each govern one stretch. They do not replace one another. Of the four, only practice regulation is administered by BCFSA. If privacy, marketing outreach, or electronic form goes wrong, it can still be pursued under the duty of honesty in rule 33 and reasonable care and skill in rule 34. Saying that a breach of the privacy statute is no concern of real estate regulation does not hold. The guidance on using artificial intelligence says the same thing: using a tool does not exempt any legal or ethical duty.

  2. ✗ 隐私专员可以处理隐私这一层，包括命令和事后的损害赔偿之诉，但并不独占后果。牌照规则不止广告署名，保密、诚实和合理谨慎都可以用上。这是把四层读成只有一层能动手。The privacy commissioner can deal with the privacy layer, including orders and a later action for damages, but the commissioner does not hold the only consequence. The licensing rules reach past advertising identification. Confidentiality, honesty, and reasonable care and skill can all be used. This reads the four layers as if only one of them can act.

  3. ✗ 四层是同时压在同一部手机上，不是四选一。隐私法适用，不能把执业监管、营销触达和电子形式取消。这是四层互相替代。The four layers press on the same phone at the same time. They are not a choice of one. The privacy statute applying does not cancel practice regulation, marketing outreach, or electronic form. This treats the four layers as substitutes for one another.

If the licensee believes that writing it out would benefit the client, written permission must be obtained first. The exam standard does not contain this code of conduct. The current guidance was published on 2025-11-13.

  2. ✗ 封闭或私密的论坛同样不能发客户保密信息。截图没有成本，条文也没有“私下除外”。这是私密群当例外。A closed or private forum is equally a place where confidential client information must not be posted. A screenshot costs nothing, and the text has no exception for a private setting. This treats a closed group as an exception.

  3. ✗ 把个人账号的可见性调得更私密，是指引要求的做法，但不能用来发布保密信息，也不能把个人账号用于业务。私密设置不是豁免。这是私密群当例外。Setting a personal account to a more private visibility is something the guidance asks for. That setting cannot be used to publish confidential information, and a personal account cannot be used for business. A private setting is not an exemption. This treats a closed group as an exception.

  4. ✗ 写上经纪行持牌名满足的是广告署名，不是保密。底价和出售原因不会因为署名完整就变成可以公开。两套规则不能互相抵销。这是用署名代替保密。Writing the brokerage's licensee name satisfies the advertising identification rule. It does not satisfy confidentiality. A bottom price and a reason for selling do not become fit to publish because the identification is complete. The two rules do not cancel each other. This uses identification in place of confidentiality.

In a private chat visible only to three colleagues, and from a personal account, a licensee types a buyer's income and employer, taken from an application. The licensee says three people in a private chat are not the public, so confidentiality does not apply. Which statement matches this lesson?

一名持牌人用个人账号，在只有三名同事能看见的私聊里，打出了申请表上的买方收入和雇主。持牌人说，私聊里只有三个人，不是公众，所以保密不适用。哪一句话符合这一课？

  1. ✗ 保密义务不看来的人算不算公众，也不看能不能被搜索到。封闭私聊同样不能发客户保密信息。这是私密群当例外。The duty of confidentiality does not turn on whether the people who see the message count as the public, and it does not turn on whether the message can be found by a search. A closed private chat is equally a place where confidential client information must not be sent. This treats a closed group as an exception.

  2. ✗ 其他持牌人不是这个买方的同一方，规则也没有“同行之间可以传客户收入”的例外。团队内部的指定代理人规则解决的是代理关系，不是把申请表发给三位同事。这是用同行身份取消保密。Other licensees are not the same party as this buyer. The rules also have no exception that lets colleagues pass around a client's income. The designated-agent rule inside a team deals with the agency relationship. It does not authorize sending an application form to three colleagues. This uses the status of a fellow licensee to cancel confidentiality.

  3. ✓ 规则第30条第e项的保密没有私下场合除外。指引禁止在封闭或私密的群组里发布客户保密信息，也禁止把个人账号用于业务。人数少到三个人，不能改变这个结论。有失体面的行为还可以包括个人账号上的言行。考试口径没有这套守则；现行指引把个人账号和私密场合都算进去。Confidentiality under rule 30(e) has no exception for a private setting. The guidance forbids posting confidential client information in a closed or private group, and it forbids using a personal account for business. A count as small as three people does not change that conclusion. Conduct unbecoming can also include words and acts on a personal account. The exam standard does not contain this code. The current guidance counts both personal accounts and private settings.

  4. ✗ 资料页上的持牌名解决的是广告署名，不能授权转发买方的收入和雇主。署名合规和保密合规是两件事。这是用行名代替保密。A licensee name on a profile page deals with advertising identification. It cannot authorize passing on a buyer's income and employer. Complying with the identification rule and complying with confidentiality are two different things. This uses the brokerage name in place of confidentiality.

The licensing exam follows the course manual on how electronic brokerage records are kept and who can demand a printout. Which statement does the exam want?

执照考试按课程原文，问经纪行的电子记录如何保存、谁可以要求打印件。考试要的是哪一句话？

  1. ✗ 这是现行规则第91条和第92条：须能随时转成纸质，有权的人可以要求立即打印，至少保存七年，除非总监书面批准更短。索要打印件的是总监及其授权人。它不是课程原文的条号和索要主体。这是用现行口径答卷。This is the current rule 91 and rule 92. The records must be convertible to paper at any time, a person with authority may demand an immediate printout, and they are kept for at least seven years unless the superintendent approves a shorter period in writing. The person who may demand the printout is the superintendent and a person the superintendent authorizes. Those are not the rule numbers, or the body that may demand the printout, in the course manual. This answers with the current standard.

  2. ✗ 云端备份不是判据，要的时候拿不出打印件就不算满足。一年是PIPA对直接影响个人的决定之后的最短保存，不是课程原文的经纪记录年限，也不是现行的七年。这是把两个期限拧在一起，又把备份当成了可打印。A cloud copy is not the test. If a printout cannot be produced when it is demanded, the requirement is not met. One year is the minimum retention under PIPA after a decision that directly affects an individual. It is not the brokerage-record period in the course manual, and it is not the current seven years. This twists the two periods together, and it treats a backup as if it were a printout on demand.

  3. ✗ 课程原文允许电子记录，条件是应议会的要求能够打印。现行第91条同样允许电子形式，前提是能立即转成打印件，第92条还允许保存副本而非原件。这一项把两套口径都读成了禁止电子保存。The course manual allows electronic records, on the condition that they can be printed when the Council asks. Current rule 91 likewise allows electronic form, provided the records can be turned into a printout at once. Rule 92 also allows a copy to be kept rather than the original. This item reads both standards as a ban on keeping records electronically.

  4. ✓ 考试口径把电子记录写成规则第8-9.1条、把保存年限写成规则第8-10条，并说应议会（Council）要求打印。现行是规则第91条须能随时转成纸质、向有权的人立即提供打印件，以及规则第92条至少保存七年；索要打印件的是总监（superintendent）及其授权人。The exam standard writes electronic records as rule 8-9.1 and the retention period as rule 8-10, and it says a printout is produced when the Council asks. The current law is rule 91, which requires that the records be convertible to paper at any time and that a printout be given at once to a person with authority, and rule 92, which requires keeping them for at least seven years. The person who may demand the printout is the superintendent and a person the superintendent authorizes.

A course-manual question asks who may require the brokerage to produce its electronic records in printed form. Which answer does the exam want?

课程原文问：谁可以要求经纪行把电子记录做成打印件交出来？考试要的是哪一个？

  1. ✗ 总监及其授权人是现行第91条的索要主体。课程原文写的是议会。题干按课程原文提问。这是用现行口径答卷。The superintendent, and a person the superintendent authorizes, are who may demand the printout under current rule 91. The course manual names the Council. The stem follows the course manual. This answers with the current standard.

  2. ✓ 考试口径写应议会（Council）要求打印，条号是旧的第8-9.1条和第8-10条。现行有权要求立即打印的是总监及其授权人，条号是第91条，保存至少七年则在第92条。The exam standard says a printout is produced when the Council asks. The rule numbers are the old rule 8-9.1 and rule 8-10.   3. ✗ 打印件是经纪记录规则里的要求，不是隐私专员独占的救济。隐私专员可以下命令，但不能代替课程原文里的议会，也不能代替现行的总监。这是把记录规则换成了隐私专员。A printout is a requirement of the brokerage-record rules. This swaps the record rules for the privacy commissioner.

  4. ✗ 电子记录不是一对公众公开。现行是有权检查、审阅或接收记录的人可以要求打印，经纪行必须立即转成打印件，并在总部备查。课程原文也只写议会可以要求，不是任何公众。这是把备查读成了公开。An electronic record is not opened to the public. Under the current law, a person with authority to inspect, review, or receive the records may demand a printout. The brokerage must turn the records into a printout at once and keep them available for inspection at the head office. The course manual also says only that the Council may require the printout, not any member of the public. This reads availability for inspection as publication to the public.
