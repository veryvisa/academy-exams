① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 年限对照题：问材料人工、建筑围护、结构缺陷各保几年。真正考的是 2、5、10 有没有对错位，而不是把「新房保修」当成一整段十年。Duration-matching questions ask how many years cover materials and labour, the building envelope, and structural defects. What is really tested is whether 2, 5, and 10 are put in the wrong places, rather than treating new-home warranty as one unbroken ten-year block.
  • 保额计算题：给购房价、户数或原购房总价，要你在「较低者」或「三者中最低」里取出最低保额。考的是独立产权、共管单元、共有部分三套公式不能混用，而且取低不取高。Coverage-amount questions give a purchase price, a unit count, or the total original purchase price, and ask you to take the minimum warranty coverage from the lower amount or from the lowest of three. What is tested is that the three formulas, for fee simple, a strata unit, and common property, must not be mixed, and that the lower figure is taken rather than the higher one.
  • 原理反直觉题：用沿海老公寓、面密封、打胶、没有空腔这类描述，考你还站不站在「密封越严越防水」上。配套会问窗台该看泛水还是只看胶。Counter-intuitive principle questions use details such as an older coastal apartment, face sealing, caulking, and no cavity, to test whether you still hold that a tighter seal is more waterproof. A paired question asks whether the sill is read by the flashing or only by the caulking.
  • 资格与比例题：大幅重建的 75% 与 50%、业主自建建成后 12 个月、保修起算日、1950 年前配线和 1955 年前管材。考的是用比例和日期判断，不用外观或转手次数判断。Qualification and percentage questions cover the 75% and 50% tests for substantial reconstruction, the 12 months after an owner-builder completes the home, warranty commencement, wiring from before 1950, and pipe material from before 1955. What is tested is judgment by percentage and date, not by appearance or by how many times the home has changed hands.
  • 卷面与行为边界题：题干写课程手册或考试口径时，规范版本、温哥华 bylaw 和绿色评级要按课程原文作答；验房题考的是至少三人、不代付、披露介绍费，以及代理人不替客户判断电线安不安全。Exam-text and conduct-boundary questions: when the stem says course manual or exam wording, the code edition, the Vancouver bylaw, and the green ratings must be answered from the course text. Inspection questions test at least three names, not advancing the fee, disclosing the referral fee, and that the licensee does not decide for the client whether the wiring is safe.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 年限对调Years swapped

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 2、5、10 三个数字连在一起背，材料人工、围护、结构容易整段记住但位置对调，五年常被说成结构或材料。The figures 2, 5, and 10 are memorized as one string, so materials and labour, the envelope, and structure are easy to remember as a block with the positions swapped. Five years is often called structure or materials.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问哪一项是五年、哪一项是十年，或只说「新房还有没有保修」却不说是哪一类。The stem asks which item is five years and which is ten, or it only asks whether the new home still has a warranty without saying which kind.

⚠ 保额取高Taking the higher amount

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 「不得低于」听上去像保得越多越对，考生会选购房总价或单价较高的那个数，忘记条文取的是较低者或三者中的最低数。"Must not be less than" sounds as if more coverage is the right answer. Candidates then pick the total purchase price or the higher unit figure and forget that the provision takes the lower of two amounts, or the lowest of three.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 出现购房价、户数、原购房总价、$200,000、$100,000、$2,500,000 里的两个以上，并且问的是 minimum。Two or more of these appear: purchase price, unit count, total original purchase price, $200,000, $100,000, and $2,500,000, and the question asks for the minimum.

⚠ 密封越严越防水A tighter seal is more waterproof

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 日常经验是缝越小越不漏，和 BC 沿海排水通风墙的原理相反；漏水公寓的教训恰恰是面密封把水锁在墙里。Everyday experience says a smaller gap leaks less. That is the opposite of the rainscreen principle on the BC coast. The leaky-condo lesson is that face sealing locks water inside the wall.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 沿海、低层共管、面密封、取消空气层、只靠打胶，或窗台只提到 caulking。The stem mentions the coast, a low-rise strata, face sealing, removing the air space, relying only on caulking, or a sill that mentions only caulking.

⚠ 看起来旧就不是新房Old-looking means not a new home

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把「新房」理解成建成年份近、第一手、或者看起来新。保修看起算日，大幅重建看拆除或新增比例，跟门牌新旧和转手几次无关。New home is taken to mean a recent year built, a first sale, or a house that looks new. Warranty is read from the commencement date, and substantial reconstruction is read from the percentage removed or added. How old the place looks, and how many times it has changed hands, do not decide it.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 保留旧地基或旧外皮、拆掉大部分上部结构、已经转手，或者卖方说这是 character home、renovation。The old foundation or the old cladding is kept, most of the superstructure is removed, the home has already changed hands, or the seller calls it a character home or a renovation.

⚠ 用今天的规范答卷Answering with today's code

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 现行已经是 2024 版建筑规范和与许可挂钩的能效阶梯，考生以为写更新的法条才能得分，于是把课程没写的内容填进卷面。The current rules are already the 2024 building code and the energy-efficiency steps tied to the permit. Candidates think that writing the newer provision is what scores, so they put onto the exam content the course never wrote.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 course manual、licensing exam、according to the course。看到这些就停在课程原文，不要用 2024 版或 Step Code 作答。The stem contains course manual, licensing exam, or according to the course. When those words appear, stop at the course text. Do not answer with the 2024 edition or the Step Code.

第 1 题Q12-5-10保修年限2-5-10 warranty years★★陷阱：年限对调

A buyer of a new detached house asks which part of the mandatory new-home warranty is the five-year part. Which coverage runs for five years?

买家购买一套新建独立屋，问强制新房保修里哪一部分是五年。五年保的是哪一项？

  1. ✗ 材料与人工（materials and labour）是 2 年那一档。把它说成五年，就是年限对调，短档和中档换了位置。Materials and labour are the 2-year tier. Calling that tier five years swaps the durations and exchanges the short tier with the middle tier.

  2. ✓ 俗称 2-5-10（2-5-10 warranty）：材料与人工 2 年，建筑围护（building envelope）含渗水 5 年，结构性缺陷 10 年。五年对应的是围护，而且包含渗水。这组年限考试口径与现行规例一致。The usual name is the 2-5-10 warranty: materials and labour for 2 years, the building envelope including water penetration for 5 years, and structural defects for 10 years. Five years matches the envelope, and water penetration is included. These durations in the exam wording match the current regulation.

  3. ✗ 结构性缺陷是 10 年，不是 5 年。年限对调时，最容易把最长的一档误说成围护。Structural defects are 10 years, not 5. When the years are swapped, the longest tier is the one most easily misstated as the envelope.

  4. ✗ 强制保修不是把所有潜在缺陷收成统一的五年。三类年限必须分开，不能用一个中间数代替整张表。The mandatory warranty does not gather every latent defect into one flat five-year period. The three durations must stay separate. A middle number cannot stand in for the whole table.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A buyer of a new strata townhouse asks which item in the mandatory new-home warranty lasts ten years. Which coverage is the ten-year item?

买家购买一套新建共管联排，问强制新房保修里哪一项保十年。十年保的是什么？

  1. ✗ 材料与人工是 2 年。选它是把最短的一档说成了最长的一档，仍是年限对调。Materials and labour are 2 years. Choosing this item calls the shortest tier the longest tier. That is still a swap of the years.

  2. ✗ 建筑围护含渗水是 5 年。五年和十年相邻，题干问十年时最容易停在围护上。The building envelope, including water penetration, is 5 years. Five and ten sit next to each other, so a stem that asks for ten years is most easily stopped at the envelope.

  3. ✓ 2-5-10 里，10 年对应结构性缺陷（structural defects）。围护是 5 年，材料与人工是 2 年。联排是共管单元，不改变这三档年限的对应关系。In the 2-5-10 warranty, 10 years matches structural defects. The envelope is 5 years, and materials and labour are 2 years. A townhouse is a strata unit, and that does not change which duration matches which tier.

  4. ✗ 共有部分的 $2,500,000 等数字是最低保额，不是第四种年限。保额公式和 2、5、10 年是两套表，不能把金额说成十年期的一个险种。Figures such as $2,500,000 for common property are minimum coverage amounts, not a fourth duration. The coverage formula and the periods of 2, 5, and 10 years are two separate tables. An amount cannot be described as a ten-year class of cover.

答案Answer：c

第 2 题Q2共有部分最低保额Common-property minimum coverage★★★陷阱：保额取高

A new 40-unit strata building carries the mandatory new-home warranty. The original purchase prices of all units add up to $18,000,000. What is the minimum warranty amount for the common property?

一栋 40 户的新建共管楼投保了强制新房保修，全部单元原购房价合计 $18,000,000。共有部分的最低保额是多少？

  1. ✗ 全部单元原购房总价是三个候选数之一，不是自动采用的那个。本题里它最高。保额取高就会选它，而条文要的是三者中的最低数。The combined original purchase price of all units is one of the three candidate figures, not the figure adopted automatically. On this question it is the highest. Taking the higher amount selects it, while the provision wants the lowest of the three.

  2. ✗ 每户 $100,000 乘以户数也只是候选数。40 户等于 $4,000,000，仍高于 $2,500,000。另外不要跟共管单元本身的公式混淆：单个单元的最低保额是该单元购房价与 $100,000 的较低者。$100,000 per unit times the number of units is also only a candidate figure. Forty units equal $4,000,000, which is still above $2,500,000. Do not mix this with the formula for the strata unit itself: the minimum coverage for one unit is the lower of that unit's purchase price and $100,000.

  3. ✗ $200,000 属于独立产权：最低保额不得低于购房价与 $200,000 的较低者。它不是共有部分三选一里的数，也不能把整栋楼收成只保这一档。$200,000 belongs to fee simple: the minimum coverage must not be less than the lower of the purchase price and $200,000. It is not one of the three common-property figures, and the whole building cannot be reduced to that one tier of cover.

  4. ✓ 共有部分（common property）最低保额不得低于三者中的最低数：全部原购房总价 $18,000,000、每户 $100,000 乘以 40 户等于 $4,000,000、以及 $2,500,000。最低是 $2,500,000。户数若少到乘积低于 $2,500,000，最低数就会改成那个乘积；本题不是那种情况。这些数字考试口径与现行规例一致。The minimum coverage for common property must not be less than the lowest of three figures: the total original purchase price of $18,000,000, $100,000 per unit times 40 units, which equals $4,000,000, and $2,500,000. The lowest is $2,500,000. If the unit count were small enough that the product fell below $2,500,000, the lowest figure would become that product. This question is not that case. These figures in the exam wording match the current regulation.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A buyer is purchasing a new fee-simple house for $640,000. What minimum warranty coverage does the course require for that home?

买家以 $640,000 购买一套新建独立产权房屋。课程要求的最低保额是多少？

  1. ✓ 独立产权（fee simple）的最低保额不得低于购房价与 $200,000 两者中的较低者。$640,000 更高，所以较低者是 $200,000。这是保额下限，不是把共有部分的公式拿来用。The minimum coverage for fee simple must not be less than the lower of the purchase price and $200,000. $640,000 is the higher figure, so the lower figure is $200,000. This is a floor on coverage. It is not the common-property formula applied to this home.

  2. ✗ 购房价高于 $200,000 时，较低者不是总价。选全价就是保额取高，把「不得低于较低者」听成「必须覆盖全部房价」。When the purchase price is above $200,000, the lower figure is not the full price. Choosing the full price is taking the higher amount. It hears "must not be less than the lower figure" as "must cover the entire purchase price."

  3. ✗ 购房价与 $100,000 取较低者，是共管单元的公式。这套是独立产权，不能改用单元那一档。The lower of the purchase price and $100,000 is the strata-unit formula. This home is fee simple, so the unit tier cannot be substituted.

  4. ✗ $2,500,000 只出现在共有部分「三者取最低」的公式里，不是每套新房的统一最低保额，更不是独立屋的答案。$2,500,000 appears only in the common-property formula that takes the lowest of three figures. It is not a uniform minimum for every new home, and it is not the answer for a detached house.

答案Answer：a

第 3 题Q3沿海围护靠排水通风A coastal envelope drains and dries★★陷阱：密封越严越防水

A licensee views a 1990s low-rise wood-frame strata near the coast. The wall is face-sealed, with sealant at the joints and no cavity behind the cladding. Which statement matches the course?

持牌人在看一栋 1990 年代的沿海低层木框架共管。外墙是面密封，接缝打了胶，外覆层后面没有空腔。哪一句符合课程？

  1. ✓ 排水通风墙（rainscreen）是在外覆材料后面留一道空气层，让偶然渗入的水靠重力排出并风干。漏水公寓危机的教训就是外墙不能只靠面密封（face-sealing）。课程写明沿海地区采用这套原理，而且这一点没有过时。A rainscreen leaves an air space behind the cladding so water that happens to enter can drain by gravity and dry. The lesson of the leaky condo crisis is that an exterior wall cannot rely only on face sealing. The course states that coastal areas use this principle, and this point has not gone out of date.

  2. ✗ 因果关系反了。面密封在实践中做不到滴水不漏，水一旦进入又被锁住，比有排水路径的墙更难干。这就是密封越严越防水的陷阱。The cause and effect are reversed. Face sealing does not, in practice, keep every drop out. Once water enters it is locked in, and the wall is harder to dry than a wall with a drainage path. That is the trap that a tighter seal is more waterproof.

  3. ✗ 课程写的是多数受影响的是低层多户共管产权物业，损失估计达数十亿元。低层木框架正是该看围护的类型，不能排除在外。The course states that most of what was affected was low-rise multi-unit strata property, with losses estimated at several billion dollars. Low-rise wood frame is exactly the type whose envelope should be examined. It cannot be left out.
