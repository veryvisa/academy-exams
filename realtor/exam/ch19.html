本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 义务人对调题：同一次成交里同时出现物业转让税和商品服务税，问谁交、谁代收、什么事件触发。真正考的是转让税在土地产权登记处登记时由买方直接交给政府，商品服务税一般由卖方代收，只有买方已注册或者卖方是非居民才改由买方处理。Payer-swap questions put property transfer tax and GST in the same completion and ask who pays, who collects, and what event triggers the tax. What they test is that the transfer tax is paid by the buyer directly to the government when an interest is registered at the land title office, that GST is generally collected by the seller, and that the buyer handles GST only when the buyer is already registered or the seller is a non-resident.
  • 分段计税题：给出公平市值，问某一档落在哪一段，尤其是住宅超过 $3,000,000 的再加 2%，以及楼花按实付总额而不是登记日市值。真正考的是只对超出的那一段计征，不是整笔跳档，商业部分不加那一档。Bracket questions give a fair market value and ask which slice a bracket falls into, especially the further 2% on a residence above $3,000,000, and that a pre-sold strata unit is taxed on the total actually paid rather than on fair market value on the registration day. What they test is that only the amount above the line is charged, that the whole price does not jump into a higher bracket, and that the commercial portion does not bear that further rate.
  • 口径对照题：题干写明按课程手册作答，把首购 $500,000 全免至 $525,000 部分、新建住房 $750,000 与 $800,000、空屋税 1%、投机与空置税 0.5% 与 2%、延税只写供养子女加 15% 权益，和 2024 年之后的现行数字并排。真正考的是卷面选考试口径，解释里用一句话写出现行规则。手册里没有外国买家禁令、联邦未充分利用住房税、首次购房者商品服务税返还和炒房税。Standard-comparison questions say to answer from the course manual, and they set the first-time full exemption at $500,000 with a partial exemption to $525,000, the new-housing lines of $750,000 and $800,000, the empty homes tax at 1%, the speculation and vacancy tax at 0.5% and 2%, and deferment written only as supporting a child plus 15% equity, beside the current figures after 2024. What they test is to choose the exam standard on the answer sheet and to state the current rule in one sentence in the explanation. The manual does not contain the foreign buyer ban, the federal underused housing tax, the first-time buyer GST rebate, or the home flipping tax.
  • 三税拆开题：一处房子同时可能碰到省投机与空置税和温哥华空屋税，问不申报会怎样、一个税的申报或抵免能不能覆盖另一个，以及 Lions Bay 在手册里算不算应税地区。真正考的是各报各的，不交物业状态申报即视同空置。Three-tax questions put one house where it may face both the provincial speculation and vacancy tax and Vancouver's empty homes tax, and ask what happens if nothing is filed, whether a filing or a credit under one tax covers the other, and whether the manual treats Lions Bay as a taxable area. What they test is that each tax is filed on its own, and that failing to file a property status declaration means the property is deemed vacant.
  • 卖出定性题：问利得按资本利得还是营业收入、主居所豁免盖住哪些年份和面积、买方要不要从总价款预扣 25%。真正考的是豁免不自动覆盖全部持有期，预扣义务在买方，持有不足 730 天的炒房税不在考试口径里。Sale-character questions ask whether a gain is a capital gain or business income, which years and what area the principal residence exemption covers, and whether the buyer must withhold 25% of the gross price. What they test is that the exemption does not automatically cover the whole holding period, that the withholding duty sits with the buyer, and that the home flipping tax on a hold of fewer than 730 days is not in the exam standard.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 把GST代收套到PTTApplying GST collection to the transfer tax

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 商品服务税一般由卖方代收上缴，物业转让税却由买方在登记前自己交给政府。两个税的义务人相反，题干一写卖方一并代收，就容易选成同一个人。GST is generally collected and remitted by the seller, while property transfer tax is paid by the buyer to the government before registration. The person who owes each tax is the opposite of the other. Once the stem says the seller collects both, it is easy to choose the same person.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干把物业转让税和商品服务税放在同一次成交里，或者写卖方答应代收转让税、签合同就触发。The stem puts property transfer tax and GST in the same completion, or it says the seller agrees to collect the transfer tax, or that signing the contract is what triggers it.

⚠ 三百万整笔加2%Adding 2% to the whole amount over three million

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 住宅超过 $3,000,000 再加 2% 听起来像整套房改按更高税率，其实只对住宅部分超出的那一段计征，下面各段照旧，商业部分不加这一档。A further 2% on a residence above $3,000,000 sounds as if the whole house is moved to a higher rate. Only the residential slice above that line is charged. The lower slices keep their own rates, and the commercial portion does not bear this further rate.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 公平市值高于 $3,000,000，选项写成整笔都再加 2%，或者把商业部分也算进这一档。Fair market value is above $3,000,000, and an option says the whole amount bears a further 2%, or it puts the commercial portion into that bracket too.

⚠ 用现行门槛答考卷Using a current threshold as the exam answer

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 首购、新建住房、空置税税率、空屋税、延税和应税市镇，2021 年之后都改过。考试手册仍是旧线和旧机制，把 $835,000、空屋税 3% 或延税 25% 填进卷面会错。First-time buyer relief, new housing, the vacancy-tax rates, the empty homes tax, deferment, and the taxable municipalities have all changed since 2021. The exam manual still uses the old lines and the old mechanism. Writing $835,000, an empty homes tax of 3%, or deferment at 25% equity on the answer sheet is wrong.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写明按课程手册或按执照考试作答，选项里出现 $835,000、$1,100,000、空屋税 3%、外国业主投机与空置税 3% 或常规延税 25% 权益。The stem says to answer from the course manual or for the licensing exam, and an option shows $835,000, $1,100,000, an empty homes tax of 3%, a 3% speculation and vacancy tax for a foreign owner, or regular deferment at 25% equity.

⚠ 免了一个就都免One exemption covers every tax

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 省投机与空置税、温哥华空屋税、联邦未充分利用住房税各有申报和豁免。一个税报过或免了，另一个仍可能要报要交；不申报本身会被当成应税或视同空置。The provincial speculation and vacancy tax, Vancouver's empty homes tax, and the federal underused housing tax each have their own filing and their own exemptions. A filing or an exemption under one tax can still leave another tax to be filed and paid. Failing to file can itself be treated as taxable, or as deemed vacant.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 业主说省税已经申报或已经抵免，但市的物业状态申报没交，或者物业同时在温哥华市内和省的应税地区里。The owner says the provincial tax has already been filed or a credit has already been claimed, but the City's property status declaration was not filed, or the property sits both inside the City of Vancouver and inside a provincial taxable area.

⚠ 预扣当成卖方的事Treating withholding as the seller's job

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 卖方不是加拿大税务居民时，25% 由买方从总价款里预扣。人容易把它当成卖方自己的所得税，或者换成资本利得 50%、附加转让税 20%、炒房税的 730 天。When the seller is not a Canadian tax resident, the buyer withholds 25% from the gross price. It is easy to treat that as the seller's own income tax, or to swap in the 50% capital-gain inclusion, the 20% additional transfer tax, or the 730 days of the home flipping tax.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 卖方的税务居民身份没查清，或者已经全额付款，选项却说买方没有义务，或把 25% 换成别的比例。The seller's status as a tax resident has not been checked, or the price has already been paid in full, and an option says the buyer has no duty, or it replaces 25% with some other percentage.

第 1 题Q1转让税登记买方交The buyer pays the transfer tax on registration★★陷阱：把GST代收套到PTT

Nadia is buying a resale townhouse in Kamloops. The seller's representative says the seller will collect the property transfer tax on completion and remit it, just as a seller collects GST on a new home. Registration at the land title office is set for next week. According to the course, who pays the property transfer tax, and what triggers it?

Nadia 在坎卢普斯购买一套二手联排。卖方代表说，卖方会在成交时收取物业转让税并上缴，就像卖方代收新房的 GST 一样。土地产权登记处的登记安排在下周。按课程，物业转让税由谁交，什么事情触发它？

  1. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把GST代收套到PTT。商品服务税一般才由卖方代收。物业转让税的触发点不是买卖合同，卖方也没有代收义务。Wrong. The trap is applying the GST collection rule to property transfer tax. GST is the tax the seller generally collects. The trigger for property transfer tax is not the contract of purchase and sale, and the seller has no duty to collect it.

  2. ✓ 正确。物业转让税（Property Transfer Tax）在土地产权登记处（Land Title Office）登记不动产权益时触发。买方须先交清，连同申报表交给政府，才能完成登记。卖方没有代收义务，这一点与商品服务税相反。考试口径与现行这一格一致。Correct. Property transfer tax is triggered when an interest in land is registered at the land title office. The buyer must pay it in full, together with the return, to the government before registration can be completed. The seller has no duty to collect it, which is the opposite of GST. The exam standard and the current rule agree on this point.

  3. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把外国实体附加转让税的五个指定区域套到一般转让税上。一般物业转让税不限那五个区域，签合同本身也不触发，触发的是登记。Wrong. The trap is applying the five specified areas of the additional property transfer tax to the general transfer tax. The general property transfer tax is not limited to those five areas. Signing the contract does not trigger it. Registration does.

  4. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把商品服务税的豁免供应（exempt supply）套到物业转让税上。二手自用住宅通常免的是商品服务税，登记时买方通常仍要交物业转让税。Wrong. The trap is applying the GST exempt supply to property transfer tax. What previously occupied housing used as a residence is usually exempt from is GST. On registration the buyer usually still pays property transfer tax.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Omar is given a life estate in a house by his aunt. No price is paid, and they never sign a purchase contract. His lawyer asks whether property transfer tax can arise when that life estate is registered. What does the course say?

Omar 的姑姑把一处房屋的终身权益给了他。没有支付价款，也没有签买卖合同。他的律师问，这项终身权益办理登记时会不会产生物业转让税。课程怎么说？

  1. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把能登记的权益收成只有付价的完全所有权。终身权益（life estate）在登记射程内，没有买卖合同也不能把它排除。Wrong. The trap is shrinking the interests that can be registered down to a fee simple sold for a price. A life estate is within the reach of registration. The absence of a purchase contract does not take it out.

  2. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把GST代收套到PTT。出让权益的人不是物业转让税的代收人，税在买方或受让人一侧，于登记前交给政府。Wrong. The trap is applying the GST collection rule to property transfer tax. The person who grants the interest is not the collector of property transfer tax. The tax sits with the buyer or the transferee and is paid to the government before registration.

  3. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把 $2,000,000 的税率分档当成了起征点。超过这一档只是改用 3% 的那一段，不是低于它就不征税。Wrong. The trap is treating the $2,000,000 rate bracket as the point where tax begins. Crossing that line only moves the excess into the 3% slice. Value below it is still taxed.

  4. ✓ 正确。终身权益属于能在土地产权登记处登记的权益，登记可以触发物业转让税（Property Transfer Tax）。受让人须在登记完成前交税。触发点是登记，不是买卖合同，赠与人没有代收义务。Correct. A life estate is an interest that can be registered at the land title office, and registration can trigger property transfer tax. The transferee must pay the tax before registration is completed. The trigger is registration, not a contract of purchase and sale, and the donor has no duty to collect it.

第 2 题Q2超额部分才再加Only the excess bears the further rate★★陷阱：三百万整笔加2%

Priya, a Canadian citizen, buys a fully residential house in Prince George. The fair market value registered the same week is $3,400,000. The sale is at arm's length. She is not a first-time buyer, and no exemption applies. A friend says the whole price bears an extra 2% because it is over $3,000,000. Which statement matches the course?

Priya 是加拿大公民，在乔治王子城购买一处全部为住宅的房屋。同一周登记的公平市值是 $3,400,000。买卖双方没有关联。她不是首次购房者，也没有豁免。朋友说，因为超过 $3,000,000，整笔价格都要再加 2%。哪一项符合课程？

  1. ✗ 错误。陷阱是三百万整笔加2%。超过 $3,000,000 不会把整笔公平市值改挂到最高档，再加的 2% 也不覆盖头 $3,000,000。Wrong. The trap is adding 2% to the whole amount once the value passes three million. Going over $3,000,000 does not move the entire fair market value into the top bracket, and the further 2% does not cover the first $3,000,000.

  2. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把五个指定区域套到一般转让税的高档上。再加 2% 是住宅高档，不限外国实体附加税的那五个区域。乔治王子城的住宅超过 $3,000,000 的部分仍然要加。Wrong. The trap is applying the five specified areas to the upper bracket of the general transfer tax. The further 2% is the high residential bracket. It is not limited to the five areas of the additional tax on a foreign entity. The portion of a Prince George residence above $3,000,000 still bears it.

  3. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把GST代收套到PTT，同时又整笔加税。物业转让税由买方直接交给政府。再加的 2% 只落在超过 $3,000,000 的住宅部分，而且 $2,000,000 以上本来还有 3% 那一档。Wrong. The trap is applying the GST collection rule to property transfer tax and also charging the further rate on the whole amount. Property transfer tax is paid by the buyer directly to the government.   3. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把外国实体附加转让税的 20% 套到预扣上。20% 是指定区域住宅的附加转让税。居民卖方的成交没有 25% 预扣，更不是改扣 20%。Wrong. The trap is applying the 20% additional property transfer tax to the withholding. Twenty percent is the additional transfer tax on residential property in the specified areas. A completion with a resident seller has no 25% withholding, and it is not changed to a 20% withholding.

  4. ✗ 错误。陷阱是预扣当成卖方的事。预扣义务在买方，而且只在卖方不是加拿大税务居民时才发生。居民卖方不必从自己的售房款里扣 25%。Wrong. The trap is treating withholding as the seller's job. The withholding duty sits with the buyer, and it arises only when the seller is not a Canadian tax resident. A resident seller does not withhold 25% from her own sale proceeds.

答案Answer：b

第 7 题Q7不申报视同空置No declaration means the home is deemed vacant★★★陷阱：免了一个就都免

The licensing exam follows the course manual. A Canadian citizen who is not a satellite family owns a house in the City of Vancouver. The house was empty the whole year. He filed the provincial speculation and vacancy tax return. He did not file the City's property status declaration. Which result does the manual give?

执照考试按课程手册作答。一名加拿大公民不是卫星家庭，在温哥华市内有一处房屋。这套房全年空置。他提交了省的投机与空置税申报，但没有提交市的物业状态申报。手册给出的结果是哪一个？

  1. ✓ 正确。温哥华空屋税（Empty Homes Tax）要提交物业状态申报（Property Status Declaration），不申报即视同空置。考试口径的税率是 1%。省投机与空置税的申报不能决定市税。现行规则不同：2025 参照年空置的物业按评估应税值的 3% 征收空屋税。Correct. Vancouver's empty homes tax requires a property status declaration, and failing to file means the property is deemed vacant. The exam-standard rate is 1%. A speculation and vacancy tax return does not decide the City tax. The current rule is different: a property that was vacant in the 2025 reference year is charged empty homes tax at 3% of taxable assessed value.

  2. ✗ 错误。陷阱是免了一个就都免。省税申报不能代替市的物业状态申报，一个税报过不等于另一个税也过了。Wrong. The trap is treating one filing as covering every tax. A provincial return cannot stand in for the City's property status declaration. Filing one tax does not mean the other tax is done.

  3. ✗ 错误。陷阱是用现行门槛答考卷。不申报视同空置这一点是对的，但 3% 是 2025 参照年的现行税率。手册里的空屋税是 1%。Wrong. The trap is using a current threshold as the exam answer. Treating the property as vacant when no declaration is filed is right, but 3% is the current rate for the 2025 reference year. The empty homes tax in the manual is 1%.

  4. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把三个税的数字捏成一个。730 天是炒房税的持有期，不在手册里。0.5% 是手册里加拿大公民的投机与空置税税率，不是空屋税。空屋税看的是不申报视同空置，以及一年中空置满六个月。Wrong. The trap is folding the figures from three taxes into one. 730 days is the holding period of the home flipping tax, and that tax is not in the manual. 0.5% is the manual's speculation and vacancy tax rate for a Canadian citizen, not the empty homes tax. The empty homes tax looks at a failure to file, which deems the property vacant, and at vacancy for six months in the year.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

The licensing exam follows the course manual. A foreign owner leaves a condo in Kelowna empty for the year. Kelowna is inside the speculation and vacancy tax area in the manual. Vancouver's empty homes tax does not apply. Which rate does the manual use for this foreign owner?

执照考试按课程手册作答。一名外国业主把基洛纳的一套公寓全年空置。手册里的投机与空置税地区包括基洛纳。温哥华空屋税不适用。手册对这名外国业主用哪一档税率？

  1. ✗ 错误。陷阱是把公民税率套到外国业主身上。手册里 0.5% 是加拿大公民或永久居民那一档，外国业主不是这一档。Wrong. The trap is applying the citizen's rate to a foreign owner. In the manual, 0.5% is the rate for a Canadian citizen or a permanent resident. A foreign owner is not in that bracket.

  2. ✗ 错误。陷阱是用现行门槛答考卷。外国业主 3% 是 2026 税年的现行税率，2027 年起是 4%。手册记的不是这一档。Wrong. The trap is using a current threshold as the exam answer. 3% for a foreign owner is the current rate for the 2026 tax year, and from 2027 the rate is 4%. That is not the bracket the manual records.

  3. ✓ 正确。考试口径自 2019 年起，外国业主的投机与空置税（Speculation and Vacancy Tax）是 2%，加拿大公民或永久居民是 0.5%。基洛纳在手册的应税地区里，温哥华空屋税不到基洛纳。现行规则不同：2026 税年外国业主是 3%，2027 年起是 4%。Correct. Under the exam standard, from 2019 the speculation and vacancy tax is 2% for a foreign owner and 0.5% for a Canadian citizen or a permanent resident. Kelowna is inside a taxable area in the manual, and Vancouver's empty homes tax does not reach Kelowna. The current rule is different: for the 2026 tax year a foreign owner pays 3%, and from 2027 the rate is 4%.

  4. ✗ 错误。陷阱是免了一个就都免，把市税税率套到省税上。手册里 1% 是温哥华空屋税，不是投机与空置税。基洛纳这套公寓不适用空屋税。Wrong. The trap is treating one tax as covering the others and applying the City rate to the provincial tax. In the manual, 1% is Vancouver's empty homes tax, not the speculation and vacancy tax. The empty homes tax does not apply to this Kelowna condo.
