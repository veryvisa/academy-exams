① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 方向判断题：给出合同利率低于、高于或等于当下市场利率，问现金等价价格相对表面成交价偏低、偏高还是相等。真正考的是利率和现值反向：低于市场是折价，高于市场才是溢价。Direction questions give a contract rate below, above, or equal to the current market rate, and ask whether the cash-equivalent price is below, above, or equal to the stated price. What they test is that the rate and the present value move in opposite directions: below the market is a discount, and only above the market is a bonus.
  • 期数陷阱题：同一题放下合同期、摊还期和已经过去的时间，问折现的N，有时还问期末还欠什么。真正考的是剩余合同期的月数，以及部分摊还的到期余额要一起折现。Period-count traps put the contractual term, the amortization, and the time already passed in the same item, ask for the N used in discounting, and sometimes also ask what is still owing at the end. What they test is the number of months left in the contractual term, and that the balance due at the end of a partial amortization is discounted together with the payments.
  • 融资拆开题：全价报价里夹着卖方回按或承接旧按揭，问第二段只改哪一个数、未偿面值算不算现金、低利率能不能直接接手。真正考的是只换折现利率、承接照抄原还款，以及核贷和价格优势是两件事。Financing-split questions place a vendor take-back mortgage or an assumable mortgage inside a full-price offer, and ask which single figure the second step changes, whether the outstanding face amount counts as cash, and whether a low rate can be taken over directly. What they test is that only the discount rate is changed, that an assumption copies the original payments, and that qualification and a price advantage are two separate things.
  • 成本披露题：私人贷款扣点，或把费用加进面值，问真实利率叫什么、法律要写进披露的是哪一个、没有额外费用时声明还要不要提前给出。真正考的是资金实际成本只是比较工具，年利率百分比才要披露，而且至少提前两个营业日。Cost-and-disclosure questions deduct points on a private loan, or add a charge to the face amount, and ask what the true rate is called, which figure the law requires to be written into the disclosure, and whether the statement must still be given in advance when there is no extra fee. What they test is that the cost of funds advanced is only a comparison tool, that the Annual Percentage Rate is what must be disclosed, and that it is given at least two business days ahead.
  • 罚金与脚注题：封闭式合同尚未到期，市场利率已经低于或高于合同利率，问三个月利息和利率差额罚金取哪一档；另一类问手册脚注让读者去查哪一家。真正考的是取较大者，以及卷面脚注写旧机构名、现行对客户要说BCFSA。Penalty and footnote questions set a closed contract that has not yet ended, with the market rate already below or above the contract rate, and ask which of three months' interest and the Interest Rate Differential is taken. Another kind asks which body the manual footnote tells the reader to consult. What they test is that the greater of the two is taken, and that the footnote on the paper names the old body while the current name to use with a client is BCFSA.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 折价溢价方向记反Reversing discount and bonus

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把“卖方肯借钱”一律当成让利，或把“合同利率比较高”当成成交价更不值钱。方向只看合同利率落在市场利率的哪一侧：低于市场，现值低于面值，报价是折价；高于市场，现值高于面值，报价是溢价。The candidate treats every case of the seller being willing to lend as a concession, or treats a higher contract rate as making the sale worth less. Direction depends only on which side of the market rate the contract rate falls on. Below the market, present value is below face, and the offer is a discount. Above the market, present value is above face, and the offer is a bonus.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时写出合同利率和市场利率谁高谁低，选项却说两种偏离都让现金等价价格上升，或都让它下降。The stem states which of the contract rate and the market rate is higher, but an option says that both kinds of departure raise the cash-equivalent price, or that both lower it.

⚠ 把摊还年限当成NUsing the amortization as N

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 计算器上最容易把“摊还期还剩多少年”填进折现期数。要折现的是合同期还没走完的月供，加上到期余额。用整段剩余摊还年限，会把合同期之外的年份也算进去。On the calculator it is easiest to enter how many years of amortization are left as the number of discount periods. What must be discounted is the monthly payments not yet run in the contractual term, plus the balance due at the end. Using the whole remaining amortization also counts the years beyond the contractual term.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时写出合同期、摊还期和已经过去的时间，并问折现的N是多少。The stem states the contractual term, the amortization, and the time already passed, and asks what N is for the discounting.

⚠ 面值当成现金等价Treating face amount as the cash equivalent

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看见未偿余额或“全价”两个字，就认为卖方实收、买方承担的经济代价已经等于这个面值。利率一偏离市场，面值就不是按揭的市场价值。When the candidate sees the outstanding balance, or the words full price, the candidate treats the economic amount the seller actually receives, and the buyer actually bears, as already equal to that face amount. Once the rate departs from the market, face amount is not the market value of the mortgage.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写承接未偿余额、全价成交，或月供已经在合同里写死，选项却说余额本身就是这笔融资的现金价值。The stem describes assuming an outstanding balance, a sale at the full price, or a monthly payment already fixed in the contract, but an option says the balance itself is the cash value of that financing.

⚠ 把COFA当成法定披露Treating the cost of funds advanced as the required disclosure

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生算出扣点之后更高的真实利率，就说贷款人必须把资金实际成本写进披露。法定项目是年利率百分比。也有人因为费用是从本金里扣除、而不是加在面值上，就说这份贷款不用书面披露。After working out the higher true rate once points are deducted, the candidate says the lender must write the cost of funds advanced into the disclosure. The required item is the Annual Percentage Rate. Others say this loan needs no written disclosure because the fee is deducted from principal rather than added to the face amount.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现点数、经纪费或贷款人加价，并问法律要求披露的是哪一个利率，或有没有书面披露。The stem mentions points, a brokerage fee, or a lender's bonus, and asks which rate the law requires to be disclosed, or whether there is a written disclosure.

⚠ 罚金取错那一档Picking the wrong penalty

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把两个罚金记成永远相加，或记成市场利率跌了就改收三个月利息。规则是取较大者：市场利率低于合同利率且剩余期数长，通常是利率差额罚金更大；市场利率高于合同利率，通常是三个月利息更大。The candidate remembers the two penalties as always added together, or as switching to three months' interest once the market rate has fallen. The rule is to take the greater. When the market rate is below the contract rate and many periods remain, the Interest Rate Differential is usually larger. When the market rate is above the contract rate, three months' interest is usually larger.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写封闭式、合同期未满，并比较合同利率和当前市场利率，问实际收取的是哪一种罚金。The stem says the mortgage is closed and the term has not ended, compares the contract rate with the current market rate, and asks which penalty is actually charged.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
报价市值是现金加按揭市值The cash-equivalent price is cash plus the market value of the mortgagecash-equivalent price报价的市场价值等于现金首付加上按揭的市场价值，按揭的市场价值则是未来还款流和到期余额按市场利率折现后的现值。The market value of the offer equals the cash down payment plus the market value of the mortgage. The market value of the mortgage is the present value of the future payment stream and the balance due at the end, discounted at the market rate.
低息折价，高息溢价A lower rate is a discount and a higher rate is a bonusdiscount / bonus合同利率等于市场利率时，市场价值等于表面成交价；合同利率更低则市场价值更低，称为折价；合同利率更高则市场价值更高，称为溢价。When the contract rate equals the market rate, market value equals the stated price. When the contract rate is lower, market value is lower, and that is a discount. When the contract rate is higher, market value is higher, and that is a bonus.
折现期数用剩余合同月Discount over the contract months still left, including the balloon paymentballoon payment部分摊还在合同期末交付的是未偿余额而不是零，讲座示例中一笔 $500,000 的贷款五年末余额为 $442,537.54、已还本金 $57,462.46，折现时要把剩余月供和这笔余额按剩余合同月数一起折现。A partial amortization delivers the outstanding balance at the end of the term, the balloon payment, and not zero. In the lecture example, a loan of $500,000 has a balance of $442,537.54 at the end of five years, and principal repaid of $57,462.46. When discounting, the remaining monthly payments and that balloon payment are discounted together over the number of contract months remaining.
回按两段计算只换利率A vendor take-back mortgage changes only the rate between the two stepsvendor take-back mortgage卖方回按先按合同利率得出月供和到期余额，再把同一串现金流按市场利率折现，两段之间只换利率，折价或溢价本身都不表示这份报价无效。A vendor take-back mortgage first uses the contract rate to obtain the monthly payment and the balance due at the end, then discounts that same stream of cash flows at the market rate. Only the rate changes between the two steps. Neither a discount nor a bonus, by itself, means the offer is invalid.
承接折现且不能跳过核贷An assumable mortgage is discounted, and qualification cannot be skippedassumable mortgage承接按揭用同一套现值，但期数是合同期剩余期数，月供和到期余额通常照原合同抄下；低于市场时市场价值低于未偿面值，差额只是谈判起点，买方仍须向原贷款人重新核贷。An assumable mortgage uses the same present value, but the number of periods is the periods remaining in the contractual term, and the monthly payment and the balance due are usually copied from the original contract. When the rate is below the market, market value is below the outstanding face amount. The difference is only a starting point for negotiation. The buyer must still requalify with the original lender.
可携仍看当下压力测试A portable mortgage is still tested under today's stress testportable mortgage可携式按揭把现有利率和条件搬到下一套房，从而削弱用承接当卖点的吸引力，但即便签约次日也要按现行压力测试重测，额度不够只能搬走一部分，本章不给解约罚金的单一比例。A portable mortgage moves the existing rate and terms to the next home, and that weakens the appeal of using an assumption as a selling point. Even on the day after signing, it must be retested under the current stress test. If the qualified amount is not enough, only part can be moved. This chapter does not give a single percentage for a breakage penalty.
扣点推高资金实际成本Points raise the cost of funds advancedcost of funds advanced经纪费或贷款人加价无论从本金扣除还是加进面值，都使借款人到手变少而还款义务不变，资金实际成本高于合同利率，且期限越短放大越明显；讲座中 $300,000 扣2点后到手 $294,000，合同半年复利5.50%，反推的等价半年复利约5.7130%。Whether a brokerage fee or a lender's bonus is deducted from principal or added to the face amount, the borrower receives less cash while the repayment obligation stays the same. The cost of funds advanced is above the contract rate, and the shorter the term, the more the effect is magnified. In the lecture, $300,000 with 2 points deducted leaves $294,000 advanced. The contract rate is 5.50% compounded semi-annually, and the equivalent semi-annual rate worked back is about 5.7130%.
法定披露是APR不是COFAThe required disclosure is the Annual Percentage Rate, not the cost of funds advancedAnnual Percentage Rate个人、家庭或生活用途的借款必须书面披露年利率百分比，按揭披露声明至少在承担合同义务或付款前两个营业日给出，借款人可按规例放弃这段期间，资金实际成本不是法定披露项目。A loan for personal, family, or household use must disclose the Annual Percentage Rate in writing. The mortgage disclosure statement must be given at least two business days before the borrower becomes bound or makes a payment. The borrower may waive that period on the terms the regulation allows. The cost of funds advanced is not a required disclosure item.
罚金取三个月与差额较大者The penalty is the greater of three months' interest and the Interest Rate DifferentialInterest Rate Differential封闭式且合同期不满五年时，贷款人没有义务允许提前全部还清；若允许，罚金取三个月利息和利率差额罚金中较大者，市场利率低于合同利率且剩余期数长时，通常是利率差额罚金更大。When the mortgage is closed and the term is less than five years, the lender has no duty to allow the whole loan to be paid off early. If the lender allows it, the penalty is the greater of three months' interest and the Interest Rate Differential. When the market rate is below the contract rate and many periods remain, the Interest Rate Differential is usually the larger one.
脚注写FICOM，清单并未过时The footnote names the Financial Institutions Commission of BC, and the list is not out of dateFinancial Institutions Commission of BC考试口径脚注让读者查阅 Financial Institutions Commission of BC，现行机构则是 BCFSA，于2019-11-01接替金融机构、养老金与按揭经纪监管，并于2021-08-01整合地产监管；同表中的初始披露清单以及《利息法》按年或半年复利披露、满五年后个人借款人可提前还清并加三个月利息，均与现行文本没有实质冲突。The exam footnote tells the reader to consult the Financial Institutions Commission of BC. The current body is BCFSA, which on 2019-11-01 took over regulation of financial institutions, pensions, and mortgage brokers, and on 2021-08-01 integrated real estate regulation. In the same table, the initial disclosure list and the Interest Act points (mortgage interest is disclosed as compounded annually or semi-annually, and after five years an individual borrower may prepay by adding three months' interest) do not conflict in substance with the current text.

第 1 题Q1低于市场即为折价Below the market is a discount★★陷阱：折价溢价方向记反

A buyer will pay part of the price in cash. The seller will lend the rest at a contract rate below the rate the buyer would pay to borrow the same amount in the market today. The loan will be repaid in full under its terms. Which statement describes the cash-equivalent price of this offer?

买方一部分房价付现金，其余由卖方借出。这笔贷款的合同利率，低于买方今天在市场上借入同样金额要付的利率。贷款会按合同条款全部还清。这份报价的现金等价价格应当如何描述？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是把面值当成现金等价价格。本金将来会还清，并不取消利息优惠。合同利率低于市场利率时，这串还款的现值（present value）低于面值。Wrong. This treats face amount as the cash-equivalent price. Repaying the principal later does not cancel the interest saving. When the contract rate is below the market rate, the present value of that payment stream is below face.

  2. ✓ 正确。现金等价价格（cash-equivalent price）等于现金首付加上按揭的市场价值。合同利率低于市场利率时，贷款人在放弃一部分利息，按市场利率折现后的现值低于面值，报价的市场价值低于表面成交价，这是折价（discount）。Correct. The cash-equivalent price equals the cash down payment plus the market value of the mortgage. When the contract rate is below the market rate, the lender is giving up part of the interest. The present value discounted at the market rate is below face, so the market value of the offer is below the stated price. That is a discount.

  3. ✗ 错误。这是折价溢价方向记反。溢价（bonus）只出现在合同利率高于市场利率时。低于市场的卖方融资是买方占便宜，不是卖方在房价之外又多收一笔。Wrong. This reverses discount and bonus. A bonus appears only when the contract rate is above the market rate. Below-market seller financing is an advantage to the buyer. It is not an extra amount the seller collects on top of the price.

  4. ✗ 错误。这是把公式拆掉了一半。报价的市场价值是现金首付加上按揭的市场价值，不是丢掉现金，也不等于回按的面值。Wrong. This drops half of the formula. The market value of the offer is the cash down payment plus the market value of the mortgage. It does not drop the cash, and it does not equal the face amount of the take-back.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A seller will carry part of the price because the buyer is a weaker credit. The take-back rate is higher than the current market rate. The rest of the price is cash. Which statement describes the cash-equivalent price of this offer?

因为买方信用较弱，卖方愿意借出一部分房价，而且要求的利率高于当前市场利率。其余房价以现金支付。这份报价的现金等价价格应当如何描述？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是折价溢价方向记反。合同利率高于市场利率时，买方在多付利息，不是在拿优惠。折价只在合同利率低于市场利率时出现。Wrong. This reverses discount and bonus. When the contract rate is above the market rate, the buyer is paying extra interest, not receiving a concession. A discount appears only when the contract rate is below the market rate.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是把将来收回的面值当成现在的经济价值。利率更高时，这串还款按市场利率折现的现值高于面值，表面成交价没有把这份风险补偿算进去。Wrong. This treats the face amount to be collected later as today's economic value. When the rate is higher, the present value of that payment stream, discounted at the market rate, is above face. The stated price does not include this compensation for risk.

  3. ✗ 错误。高于市场的贷款仍有市场价值，而且高于其面值。现金等价价格要把现金首付和按揭的市场价值加在一起，不能把回按当成零。Wrong. An above-market loan still has a market value, and that value is above its face amount. The cash-equivalent price adds the cash down payment and the market value of the mortgage. The take-back cannot be treated as zero.

  4. ✓ 正确。合同利率高于市场利率时，借款人多付一部分本不必付的利息，按揭的市场价值高于面值。报价的市场价值因此高于表面成交价，这是溢价（bonus），卖方多收的是风险补偿。Correct. When the contract rate is above the market rate, the borrower pays some interest that would not otherwise be paid, and the market value of the mortgage is above face. The market value of the offer is therefore above the stated price. That is a bonus. What the seller collects extra is compensation for risk.

答案Answer：d

第 2 题Q2折现期数取剩余月The discount count uses the months remaining★★★陷阱：把摊还年限当成N

A mortgage has a 5-year term and a 25-year amortization, with monthly payments. Two years have passed. A buyer will assume the payments still due and the balance due when the term ends. Those cash flows are discounted at today's market rate. Which figure is N?

一笔按揭的合同期是5年，摊还期是25年，按月还款。现在已经过去2年。买方将承接尚未支付的各期月供，以及合同期满时的余额。这些现金流按今天的市场利率折现。N应当取多少？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是把摊还年限当成N。还剩23年是剩余摊还年限，不是这次要折现的期数。用它当N，会把合同期结束之后的年份也折进去。Wrong. This uses the amortization as N. The 23 years left are the remaining amortization, not the number of periods to discount this time. Using that as N also discounts the years after the contractual term ends.

  4. ✓ 正确。市场利率高于合同利率时，利率差额罚金（Interest Rate Differential, IRD）通常小于三个月利息。贷款人按“取较大者”收取三个月利息。这一档算法也更直接。Correct. When the market rate is above the contract rate, the Interest Rate Differential (IRD) is usually smaller than three months' interest. Taking the greater of the two, the lender charges three months' interest. That calculation is also the more direct one.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

第 7 题Q7脚注答案仍是旧机构The footnote answer is still the old body★陷阱：用现行机构答脚注

A course-manual footnote tells the reader where to look for more information about the annual percentage rate. The question says to answer from that footnote. Which body does the footnote name?

课程手册的一条脚注告诉读者，想进一步了解年利率百分比可以查阅哪里。题目要求按这条脚注作答。脚注写的是哪一家？

  1. ✓ 正确。考试口径脚注建议查阅的是 Financial Institutions Commission of BC（FICOM）。现行监管机构是 BCFSA：2019-11-01起接替金融机构、养老金与按揭经纪监管，2021-08-01完成地产监管整合。Correct. The exam footnote says to consult the Financial Institutions Commission of BC (FICOM). The current regulator is BCFSA: from 2019-11-01 it took over regulation of financial institutions, pensions, and mortgage brokers, and on 2021-08-01 it completed the integration of real estate regulation.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是用现行机构回答卷面脚注。BCFSA是现在对客户应当使用的名字，不是这条考试口径脚注里写的名字。Wrong. This answers the footnote on the paper with the current body. BCFSA is the name to use with a client now. It is not the name written in this exam footnote.

  3. ✗ 错误。年利率百分比（Annual Percentage Rate, APR）是披露项目，不是在土地登记处另行登记的一笔收费。脚注指向的是当时的金融机构监管机构，不是登记处。Wrong. The Annual Percentage Rate (APR) is a disclosure item. It is not a separate charge registered at the land title office. The footnote points to the financial-institutions regulator of that time, not to the land title office.

  4. ✗ 错误。年利率百分比的依据是《商业行为与消费者保护法》，不是交易服务的行为守则。脚注写的也不是地产委员会。地产监管后来并入 BCFSA，不能拿来替换脚注里的名字。Wrong. The Annual Percentage Rate rests on the Business Practices and Consumer Protection Act, not on a trading-services conduct rule. The footnote does not name the former real estate council either. Real estate regulation was later brought into BCFSA, and that cannot be used to replace the name in the footnote.
