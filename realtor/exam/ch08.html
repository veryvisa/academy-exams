本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 场景判断：给出卖生意的不同交易结构（资产打包出售 vs. 买部分股份 vs. 买全部股份），考哪一种属于地产交易、需要持有地产牌照。Scenario judgment: Given different transaction structures for selling a business (asset sale package vs. purchasing partial shares vs. purchasing 100% of the shares), testing which one constitutes a real estate trade requiring a real estate licence.
  • 组织形式辨析：给出一宗投资或执业场景（如有限合伙人参与日常管理、持牌人设立个人地产公司 PREC、一般合伙人不知情的合伙债务），考谁承担无限责任、谁享有限制责任保护以及如何纳税。Business organization analysis: Given an investment or practice scenario (such as a limited partner participating in daily management, a licensee establishing a Personal Real Estate Corporation (PREC), or a general partner facing unknown partnership debt), testing who bears unlimited liability, who enjoys limited liability protection, and how profits are taxed.
  • 会计准则配对：描述一笔具体业务（如佣金在解除条件还是过户时入账、全年保费按月分摊、低值文具直接列支、物业按实付价而非评估价入账），考它对应哪一条公认会计准则（GAAP）。Accounting principles matching: Describing a specific business transaction (such as recognizing commission at subject removal vs. completion, amortizing annual insurance premiums monthly, immediately expensing low-value supplies, or recording real property at purchase price rather than appraised value), testing which Generally Accepted Accounting Principle (GAAP) applies.
  • 报表咬合与折旧辨析：考资产负债表、损益表、现金流量表三者如何勾稽，特别是土地不可折旧、折旧属于非现金支出（在现金流量表加回）、按揭本金偿还不进损益表，以及会计折旧与报税资本成本折让（CCA）的区别。Statement linkage and depreciation analysis: Testing how the balance sheet, income statement, and statement of cash flows interconnect, specifically that land cannot be depreciated, depreciation is a non-cash expense added back on the statement of cash flows, mortgage principal repayments do not appear on the income statement, and accounting depreciation differs from tax Capital Cost Allowance (CCA).
  • 实务尽调与监管合规：考协助客户购买带地产的生意时应索取哪三样财务文件（含 12 个月且距签约不超过 120 天的损益表），以及经纪行信托账户的会计归属与年度结束后 120 天内的报表申报义务。Practical due diligence and regulatory compliance: Testing the three minimum financial documents to obtain when assisting a client purchasing a business involving real estate (including an income statement for the last 12 months dated within 120 days of the contract), as well as the accounting ownership of brokerage trust accounts and the annual 120-day financial filing requirements.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 股份交易一刀切免牌照Assuming all share transactions are exempt from licensing

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生只记住「买卖股份属于证券交易」，却忽略了如果目标公司持有地产且买方收购其全部（100%）股份，考试口径仍要求地产牌照。Candidates remember only that trading shares falls under securities regulation, overlooking that if the target corporation holds real estate and the buyer acquires 100% of its shares, the exam benchmark still requires a real estate licence.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现持有地产的公司（company holding real estate），且出现收购全部股份（100% of the shares）或部分股份（partial shares）的对比。The question stem mentions a corporation holding real estate and contrasts acquiring 100% of the shares against acquiring partial shares.

⚠ 公司或有限合伙挡住一切个人责任Believing a corporation or limited partnership shields all personal liability

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到「有限合伙人（LP）」「有限责任合伙（LLP）」或「个人地产公司（PREC）」就以为绝对没有个人无限责任，忘了有限合伙人参与日常经营会丧失保护，而 LLP 合伙人和 PREC 持牌人对自身执业过失始终负个人无限责任。Seeing a limited partner (LP), limited liability partnership (LLP), or Personal Real Estate Corporation (PREC), candidates assume unlimited personal liability is eliminated, forgetting that an LP loses protection upon participating in daily management, while LLP partners and PREC licensees remain personally liable for their own professional negligence.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描写有限合伙人插手日常管理（active management），或持牌人以为成立 PREC 后连提供地产服务的执业过失也由公司兜底。The question stem describes a limited partner engaging in active management, or a licensee assuming that a PREC shields personal liability for negligence in providing real estate services.

⚠ 把收现日当成收入或费用确认日Treating cash receipt or payment dates as revenue or expense recognition dates

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 用日常生活里的流水账思维（收付实现制）代替权责发生制与配比原则，误以为收到现金才算收入、付了按揭本金就算损益表费用。Using cash-basis thinking instead of the accrual basis and matching principle, mistakenly assuming revenue is earned only when cash is received or that mortgage principal payments are expenses on the income statement.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问佣金何时确认、跨期保险费如何入账、年末未收到的应收租金算不算当年收入，或按揭本金偿还进哪张表。The question asks when commission is recognized, how prepaid annual insurance is recorded, whether uncollected rent receivable counts as revenue, or where mortgage principal repayments appear.

⚠ 把折旧当成真金白银的流出或报税扣除额Treating depreciation as a cash outflow or direct tax deduction

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为账面计提折旧会导致现金减少，或者以为财务报表上的直线法折旧可以直接填进报税表抵税，甚至把土地也一起计提折旧。Mistakenly believing that booking depreciation reduces cash, that straight-line accounting depreciation can be claimed directly on a tax return, or that land can be depreciated alongside buildings.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出土地与建筑价值要求算折旧、问为什么净利润与现金净增加不一致，或将会计折旧（depreciation）与资本成本折让（CCA）混谈。The question stem gives both land and building values to calculate depreciation, asks why net profit differs from net cash change, or confuses accounting depreciation with Capital Cost Allowance (CCA).

⚠ 把账面净值当市场价或把信托款当经纪行资产Equating net book value to market value or treating trust funds as brokerage assets

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到资产负债表上的建筑净值就以为代表当前市场售价，或看到信托账户在经纪行名下开设就以为里面代管的客户资金是经纪行自己的资产。Assuming that net book value of a building on the balance sheet reflects current market price, or believing that client trust funds deposited in the brokerage name belong to the brokerage as corporate assets.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干用历史成本减累计折旧去推算物业当前市值，或问经纪行资产负债表里该不该把尚未赚得的客户信托存款列为经纪行资产。The question stem estimates current property market value using historical cost less accumulated depreciation, or asks whether unearned client trust deposits should appear as brokerage balance sheet assets.

第 1 题Q1出售生意的持牌要求Licensing requirements for business sales★★陷阱：股份交易一刀切免牌照

Clara wants to acquire a dry-cleaning business that owns the commercial building in which it operates. In which of the following scenarios must the intermediary representing Clara hold a real estate licence?

Clara 想收购一家拥有自用商业楼宇产权的干洗店生意。在以下哪种情形中，代理 Clara 的中间人必须持有地产牌照？

  1. ✓ 根据考试口径，如果持有地产的是一家公司，且本次交易是买下该公司的全部股份（100% of the shares），则视为涉及地产的交易，需要持有地产牌照。Correct. Under the exam benchmark, if real estate is owned by a corporation and the transaction involves purchasing 100% of the shares of that corporation, it is treated as a trade in real estate requiring a real estate licence.

  2. ✗ 如果只买下持有地产公司的部分股份（partial shares），这属于证券交易，受《证券法》（Securities Act）管辖，不需要地产牌照（股份交易一刀切免牌照陷阱）。Purchasing only a partial shareholding in a corporation that owns real estate constitutes a securities transaction governed by the Securities Act and does not require a real estate licence (the trap is assuming that every share transaction is exempt from licensing).

  3. ✗ 只购买机器、客户名单与化学品存货而不包含任何物业产权或租约转让，不涉及地产成分，不需要地产牌照。Purchasing only equipment, customer lists, and chemical inventory without any real property title or lease assignment involves no real estate component and does not require a real estate licence.

  4. ✗ 如果目标生意完全不持有任何地产资产，即使买下全部股份也不需要地产牌照。If the target business owns no real property interests or leases, acquiring all of its shares does not require a real estate licence.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Marcus is negotiating to buy a neighbourhood restaurant. Under which of the following transaction structures is a real estate licence NOT required for the representative handling the sale?

Marcus 正在洽谈收购一家社区餐馆。在以下哪种交易结构下，经办该笔交易的代表不需要持有地产牌照？

  1. ✗ 按资产打包出售（asset sale）且包含转让租约（assignment of lease）等地产成分时，必须持有地产牌照。An asset sale that includes an assignment of lease involves a real estate component and requires the representative to hold a real estate licence.

  2. ✗ 包含出售物业产权的资产打包出售涉及地产成分，必须持有地产牌照。An asset package selling physical land and building directly involves real property and legally requires a real estate licence.

  3. ✓ 当持有地产的公司仅出售部分股份（而非 100% 全部股份）时，该交易属于证券交易（可能需按《证券法》Securities Act 注册），不算地产交易，不需要地产牌照。Correct. When an incorporated company owning real estate sells only a partial block of shares rather than 100% of its shares, the trade is a securities transaction governed by the Securities Act and is not a real estate trade, so no real estate licence is required.

  4. ✗ 买下持有地产公司的全部（100%）股份属于需要地产牌照的情形（股份交易一刀切免牌照陷阱）。Purchasing 100% of the issued shares of a corporation holding title to real estate is treated as a real estate trade requiring a licence, falling into the trap of assuming all share sales are exempt.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

第 2 题Q2企业组织形式的责任与纳税Liability and taxation across business organizations★★陷阱：公司或有限合伙挡住一切个人责任

Elena is a limited partner in a limited partnership (LP) formed to acquire a commercial plaza. Worried about rising operating costs, Elena begins negotiating directly with maintenance contractors and managing the plaza's daily operations. What is the legal consequence of Elena's actions?

Elena 是一家为收购商业广场而成立的有限合伙企业（LP）中的有限合伙人。由于担心运营成本上升，Elena 开始直接与维修承包商谈判并管理广场的日常经营。Elena 这一行为的法律后果是什么？

  1. ✗ 凭设立证书成立的独立法律实体是公司（corporation），而非有限合伙；且有限合伙人一旦参与日常经营就会丧失有限责任保护（公司或有限合伙挡住一切个人责任陷阱）。An incorporated company created by certificate of incorporation is a distinct legal entity, not a limited partnership; furthermore, a limited partner loses liability protection upon participating in daily management, falling into the trap of believing an LP shields all personal liability.

  2. ✓ 在有限合伙（limited partnership, LP）中，有限合伙人的责任原本以出资额为限，但一旦参与日常经营（participates in daily management），即丧失该有限责任保护。Correct. In a limited partnership (LP), a limited partner's liability is normally capped at their capital contribution, but a limited partner who participates in daily management loses that limited liability protection and becomes exposed to unlimited liability.

  3. ✗ 有限合伙人参与经营不会把合伙企业变成个人地产公司（PREC），PREC 是持牌人个人开设的公司结构。A limited partner participating in operations does not convert the partnership into a Personal Real Estate Corporation (PREC), which is a corporate vehicle used exclusively by licensed real estate representatives.

  4. ✗ 有限合伙与一般合伙相同，在税务上属于「导管」（conduit）实体，利润按约定份额并入合伙人个人报税表，不会因合伙人参与经营而自动变成按公司税率纳税的股份有限公司。A limited partnership is a tax conduit whose profits flow through directly to partners' personal returns, and an LP does not convert into a corporation paying corporate taxes simply because a partner participates in management.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Two accountants form a general partnership to invest in rental properties. Without consulting his partner, Partner A signs a supply contract on behalf of the partnership that results in a large unpaid debt. How is Partner B affected?

两名会计师成立了一家一般合伙企业（general partnership）来投资出租物业。在未征求合伙人意见的情况下，合伙人 A 代表合伙企业签下一份采购合同并导致大额欠款。合伙人 B 会受到什么影响？

  1. ✗ 在一般合伙（general partnership）中，各合伙人互为代理人，不论本人是否知情或同意，都对全部合伙债务负连带无限责任。In a general partnership, each partner acts as an agent for the partnership and bears joint and several unlimited liability for all partnership debts, regardless of whether they knew of or consented to the contract.

  2. ✗ 以出资额为限是有限合伙人（未参与日常经营时）或公司股东的待遇，一般合伙人承担的是无限责任（公司或有限合伙挡住一切个人责任陷阱）。Limiting liability to initial capital contribution applies only to inactive limited partners or corporate shareholders; general partners bear unlimited personal liability, falling into the liability shield trap.

  3. ✗ 一般合伙不是独立纳税的公司实体，而是税务导管，且合伙人个人资产可被追偿。A general partnership is not a separate incorporated entity that pays corporate tax or absorbs debts independently; creditors can pursue the personal assets of the partners.
