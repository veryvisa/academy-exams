第 17 章 · 按揭核贷与借款人资格Chapter 17 · Mortgage Underwriting and Borrower QualificationUnderwriting Qualification

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 概念配对题：给就业、居住、收入比率或征信里的一段材料，问它落在五个C的哪一个。真正考的是品格问会不会还、还款能力问还得起，两件事不能互相代替。Concept-matching questions give a passage on employment, residence, an income ratio, or the credit report, and ask which of the Five Cs it falls under. What they test is that character asks whether the person will pay and capacity asks whether the person can pay, and that the two cannot stand in for each other.
  • 词语辨析题：同一套房同时写出成交价、购置成本、市场价值和贷款价值，问贷款价值比乘的是哪一个。真正考的是贷款人取市场价值与贷款价值中较低者，买贵的那一段不认。Term-distinction questions state the purchase price, the purchase cost, the market value, and the lending value of the same house, and ask which figure the loan-to-value ratio multiplies. What they test is that the lender takes the lower of market value and lending value, and does not recognize the part paid above that figure.
  • 两尺取小题：题干让收入能支撑的额度高于或低于八成上限，问这笔贷款卡在哪里、该动首付还是该动其他债务。真正考的是先看哪一把尺卡住，再选对应的解法。Two-ruler questions set the amount income can support above or below the 80% ceiling, and ask where the loan is stuck and whether to move the down payment or the other debt. What they test is to see which ruler binds first, then choose the matching adjustment.
  • 保费三规则题：保费已经加进贷款面值，问贷款价值比变不变、偿债比变不变、续期还收不收。真正考的是不进成数、要进偿债比、保险覆盖整个摊还期。Three-rule premium questions add the premium to the face amount of the loan, and ask whether the loan-to-value ratio changes, whether the debt-service ratios change, and whether a premium is charged again at renewal. What they test is that the premium stays out of the ratio, goes into the debt-service ratios, and that the insurance covers the whole amortization.
  • 口径对照题：题干写明按课程手册作答，选项把考试数字和2026年数字并排。真正考的是考试口径仍用加拿大银行五年公布利率、保险按揭毛偿债比35%与总偿债比42%、房价上限$1,000,000，解释里用一句话点出现行数字。Standard-comparison questions say to answer from the course manual, and the options place the exam figures beside the 2026 figures. What they test is that the exam standard still uses the Bank of Canada five-year posted rate, a gross debt service ratio of 35% and a total debt service ratio of 42% on an insured mortgage, and a price ceiling of $1,000,000, with one sentence in the explanation naming the current figures.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 品格与能力记混Mixing up character and capacity

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看见工作年限长，就认为偿债比也过了；或看见比率够了，就认为品格没有问题。品格问这个人会不会还，是行为预测；还款能力问还得起吗，是用收入和偿债比做的财务测算。The candidate sees a long work history and treats the debt-service ratios as already passed, or sees ratios that fit and treats character as clear. Character asks whether this person will pay, and that is a behavioural forecast. Capacity asks whether the person can pay, and that is a financial calculation from income and the debt-service ratios.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干一边给就业或居住稳不稳定，另一边给收入比率够不够，选项把两件事写成同一个C。The stem gives employment or residence stability on one side and whether the income ratios fit on the other, and an option writes the two as the same C.

⚠ 出价当成贷款价值Treating the offer price as lending value

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生认为银行按成交价乘以成数放款。贷款价值是为放贷做的长期保守估值，贷款人取它与市场价值中较低的那个；买贵的那一段银行不认。购置成本还高于成交价，更不能拿来当基数。The candidate thinks the bank advances a percentage of the purchase price. Lending value is a long-term conservative figure prepared for lending, and the lender takes the lower of that figure and market value. The bank does not recognize the part paid above that figure. Purchase cost is higher still than the purchase price, so it cannot be used as the base either.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写买方出价高于估价，或同时出现成交价、市场价值和贷款价值，选项仍按成交价或购置成本乘贷款价值比。The stem says the buyer's offer is above the appraisal, or states the purchase price, market value, and lending value together, and an option still multiplies the loan-to-value ratio by the purchase price or the purchase cost.

⚠ 解法用错那把尺Using the fix for the wrong ruler

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生不管卡住的是成数还是收入，都去延长摊还或先还掉车贷。抵押物那把尺卡住时，收入尺上多出来的额度用不上，要加首付或换更便宜的房。Whatever binds, the ratio or the income, the candidate lengthens the amortization or pays off the car loan first. When the collateral ruler binds, extra room on the income ruler cannot be drawn. The adjustment is a larger down payment or a less expensive house.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干已经说明八成上限低于收入能支撑的贷款额，选项却说还掉其他债务或拉长摊还能提高这笔放款。The stem already says the 80% ceiling is below the loan the income can support, and an option says paying off other debt or lengthening the amortization will raise this advance.

⚠ 保费计入贷款价值比Putting the premium into the loan-to-value ratio

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到保费加进了贷款面值，就把它同时算进贷款价值比，或认为续期还要再收一次。保费不进贷款价值比，含保费的还款要进偿债比，保险覆盖整个摊还期。The candidate sees the premium added to the face amount and counts it in the loan-to-value ratio as well, or expects a premium to be collected again at renewal. The premium stays out of the loan-to-value ratio. Payments that include the premium go into the debt-service ratios. The insurance covers the whole amortization.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写保费加进贷款、以后按含保费的面值还款，选项在讨论成数、偿债比或续期是否再收费。The stem says the premium is added to the loan and that later payments are on the face amount including the premium, and an option discusses the ratio, the debt-service ratios, or whether a premium is charged again at renewal.

⚠ 用现行数字作考试答案Using current figures as the exam answer

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把2026年仍在用的5.25%下限、毛偿债比39%与总偿债比44%、房价上限$1,500,000写进考试答案。考试口径是加拿大银行五年公布利率与合同利率加2%取高者、保险按揭毛偿债比35%与总偿债比42%、房价上限$1,000,000。The candidate writes into the exam answer the floor of 5.25% still in use in 2026, a gross debt service ratio of 39% and a total debt service ratio of 44%, and a price ceiling of $1,500,000. The exam standard is the higher of the Bank of Canada five-year posted rate and the contract rate plus 2%, a gross debt service ratio of 35% and a total debt service ratio of 42% on an insured mortgage, and a price ceiling of $1,000,000.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写明按课程手册或按考试口径作答，选项里同时出现5.25%、39%、44%或$1,500,000。The stem says to answer from the course manual or on the exam standard, and an option shows 5.25%, 39%, 44%, or $1,500,000.

贷款价值取市场价值中的低者The lender takes the lower of market value and lending valuelending value贷款人按市场价值与贷款价值中较低者审贷，两份估价相差3%到5%时取低的那份；购置成本是成交价加上费用和奖金，一定高于成交价。The lender underwrites on the lower of market value and lending value. When two appraisals differ by 3% to 5%, the lower one is taken. Purchase cost is the purchase price plus fees and any bonus, and it is always higher than the purchase price.
无保险按揭法定上限八成The statutory ceiling on an uninsured mortgage is 80%conventional mortgage联邦特许金融机构做无保险按揭，贷款价值比上限是80%，这种只靠个人承诺加房产作保的贷款叫常规按揭；超过80%必须投保，可做到95%，私人贷款人不受这条80%约束。A federally chartered financial institution making an uninsured mortgage is capped at a loan-to-value ratio of 80%. A loan secured only by the personal promise plus the property is a conventional mortgage. Above 80% it must be insured, and it can go to 95%. A private lender is not bound by that 80% cap.
两把尺子取较小的那个The two rulers take the smaller oneloan-to-value ratio抵押物那把尺卡住时加首付或换更便宜的房，收入那把尺卡住时延长摊还、降低利率、还掉其他债务或找担保人，先确认哪一把尺卡住再动变量。When the collateral ruler binds, increase the down payment or choose a less expensive house. When the income ruler binds, lengthen the amortization, lower the rate, pay off other debt, or add a guarantor. Confirm which ruler binds before moving a variable.

The current minimum qualifying rate is the higher of the contract rate plus 2% and 5.25%.
保费不进成数但进偿债比The premium stays out of the ratio but goes into the debt-service ratiosmortgage loan insurance保费可以加进贷款额，加进后不计入贷款价值比，但含保费的还款计入偿债比，保险覆盖整个摊还期，续期或期末不再另收保费。The premium may be added to the loan amount. Once added, it is not included in the loan-to-value ratio, but payments that include the premium are included in the debt-service ratios. The insurance covers the whole amortization. No further premium is charged at renewal or at the end of the term.

The lecture marks that statement as wrong, and it is not to be used on the exam or in practice. Current self-employed products can go as high as a 95% loan-to-value ratio with no extra charge, and sole-proprietorship or partnership income may be grossed up by 15%.
安全边际与偿债覆盖率互换The safety margin and the debt coverage ratio convert into each otherdebt coverage ratio收益性物业以净营运收入为基准，偿债覆盖率等于1除以（1减安全边际），安全边际20%等价于偿债覆盖率1.25，偿债覆盖率1.30等价于安全边际约23.08%。An income property is measured from net operating income. The debt coverage ratio equals 1 divided by (1 minus the safety margin). A safety margin of 20% equals a debt coverage ratio of 1.25. A debt coverage ratio of 1.30 equals a safety margin of about 23.08%.
财产险按全部可保价值投保Property insurance is placed for the full insurable valuefull insurable value借款人须按全部可保价值投保财产险，受益指向按揭权人；只按贷款额投保、其余另保给自己，损失会由两家保险人分摊，贷款人可能只拿到一部分赔款。The borrower must place property insurance for the full insurable value, with the proceeds payable to the mortgagee. If only the loan amount is insured and the rest is insured for the borrower alone, a loss is shared by the two insurers, and the lender may receive only part of the proceeds.

第 1 题Q1品格与能力不是一回事Character and capacity are not the same thing★陷阱：品格与能力记混

A lender's notes show nine years in the same trade and six years at the same address. The debt-service ratios calculated from gross income leave no room for the proposed payment. The credit report is otherwise orderly. Which of the Five Cs is the weak point?

贷款人的记录显示，申请人在同一行业做了九年，在同一地址住了六年。按年总收入算出的偿债比，容不下这笔拟定还款。征信报告除此以外是整齐的。五个C里，弱项是哪一个？

  1. ✗ 错误。陷阱是品格与能力记混。就业稳定和居住时长是品格（Character）的材料，问的是会不会还。比率算不下来，弱的是还款能力，不是品格。Wrong. The trap is mixing up character and capacity. Employment stability and length of residence are the materials of character. They ask whether the person will pay. Ratios that do not work mean capacity is the weak point, not character.

  2. ✓ 正确。还款能力（Capacity）问还得起吗，材料是年总收入和偿债比。品格问会不会还。核贷同时是财务预测和行为预测，这一笔卡在财务预测上。Correct. Capacity asks whether the person can pay. Its materials are gross annual income and the debt-service ratios. Character asks whether the person will pay. Underwriting is a financial forecast and a behavioural forecast at the same time. This file is stuck on the financial forecast.

  3. ✗ 错误。信用记录（Credit）看的是过去的还款习惯和征信报告。题干写报告是整齐的，卡住的是用收入算出来的偿债比，那是还款能力。Wrong. Credit looks at past payment habits and the credit report. The stem says the report is orderly. What binds is the debt-service ratio calculated from income, and that is capacity.

  4. ✗ 错误。抵押物（Collateral）问的是万一还不上拿什么补，材料是房产本身。收入比率不够，还没有进入抵押物这第二道防线。Wrong. Collateral asks what makes up the shortfall if the borrower cannot pay, and its material is the property itself. Income ratios that do not fit have not yet reached collateral, which is the second line of defence.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

The debt-service ratios fit comfortably. The same file shows four jobs in three years and three home addresses in that time. The credit report is clean. Which of the Five Cs is the weak point?

偿债比算下来宽裕。同一份档案显示，三年里换了四份工作、三个住址。征信报告是干净的。五个C里，弱项是哪一个？

  1. ✗ 错误。陷阱是品格与能力记混。比率已经宽裕，还款能力（Capacity）不是弱项。课程没有把搬家次数写成自动扣减年总收入。Wrong. The trap is mixing up character and capacity. The ratios already fit, so capacity is not the weak point. The course does not treat the number of moves as an automatic reduction of gross annual income.

  2. ✗ 错误。抵押物看的是房产本身。住址更换是品格的材料，用来判断这个人会不会还，不是房子能不能作保。Wrong. Collateral looks at the property itself. A change of address is material for character, used to judge whether this person will pay. It is not whether the house can stand as security.

  3. ✗ 错误。资本（Capital）看的是自己投入多少首付、会不会攒钱，材料在申请表的资产负债栏。换工作本身不能证明首付是借来的。Wrong. Capital looks at how much of the down payment is the buyer's own money and whether the buyer saves. Its material is the assets-and-liabilities section of the application. A job change by itself cannot prove that the down payment was borrowed.

  4. ✓ 正确。品格（Character）问会不会还，看就业稳定性、居住时长和行业经验，属于行为预测。比率宽裕只说明还款能力过了，不能代替品格。Correct. Character asks whether the person will pay. It looks at employment stability, length of residence, and industry experience, and it is a behavioural forecast. Ratios that fit only show that capacity has passed. They cannot stand in for character.

第 2 题Q2贷款价值取较低的估值Lending value takes the lower valuation★★陷阱：出价当成贷款价值

Two appraisals of the same house differ by an amount inside the 3% to 5% range described in the course. One estimates the price informed buyers and sellers would agree in a reasonable listing period. The other is a long-term conservative figure prepared for lending, and it is the lower of the two. The contract price is higher than both. Which amount does the lender use for loan-to-value?

同一套房的两份估价，差距落在课程所说的3%到5%之内。一份估计的是信息充分的买卖双方在合理挂牌期内可能成交的价格。另一份是为放贷准备的长期保守数字，而且更低。合同价比两份都高。贷款人用哪一个数计算贷款价值比？

  1. ✗ 错误。陷阱是出价当成贷款价值。成交价（purchase price）是双方谈出来的数。买贵的那一段，贷款人不认，贷款价值比不乘成交价。Wrong. The trap is treating the offer price as the lending value. The purchase price is the figure the two parties negotiated. The lender does not recognize the part paid above value, and the loan-to-value ratio does not multiply the purchase price.

  2. ✗ 错误。购置成本（purchase cost）是成交价加上律师费、估价费、征信费等费用和奖金，一定高于成交价。它比成交价更大，更不是贷款价值。Wrong. Purchase cost is the purchase price plus fees such as legal fees, the appraisal fee, and the credit-report fee, plus any bonus. It is always higher than the purchase price. It is larger than the purchase price, so it is even less the lending value.

  3. ✗ 错误。同一处住宅两份估价相差3%到5%时，取低的那份。贷款人也是按市场价值与贷款价值中较低的那个来审，不是取更新或更高的一份。Wrong. When two appraisals of the same home differ by 3% to 5%, the lower one is taken. The lender also underwrites on the lower of market value and lending value, not on the more recent or the higher one.

  4. ✓ 正确。市场价值（market value）是合理挂牌期内可能成交的估计值。贷款价值（lending value）是长期保守估值，通常更低。贷款人取两者中较低者；两份估价相差3%到5%时也取低的那份。成交价更高并不能抬高这个基数。Correct. Market value is the estimated price that could be agreed in a reasonable listing period. Lending value is a long-term conservative valuation, and it is usually lower. The lender takes the lower of the two. When two appraisals differ by 3% to 5%, the lower one is also taken. A higher purchase price does not raise that base.

  4. ✗ 错误。偿债覆盖率1.30对应的安全边际约是23.08%，是另一对换算，不是20%。不能把20%的边际和公寓楼的习惯倍数加在一起。Wrong. A debt coverage ratio of 1.30 corresponds to a safety margin of about 23.08%. That is a different pair, not 20%. The 20% margin and the customary apartment multiple cannot be added together.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A term sheet for a small warehouse states a debt coverage ratio of 1.30 and does not state a safety margin. The buyer asks what share of net operating income must be kept back from the annual payment. Which figure matches the course?

一份小型仓库的条件清单写偿债覆盖率1.30，没有写安全边际。买方问：年还款之外，净营运收入要留出多大份额？哪一个数字符合课程？

  1. ✗ 错误。陷阱是覆盖率当成付款比例。1.30不是30%的安全边际。它是净营运收入必须达到年还款的1.30倍。Wrong. The trap is treating the coverage ratio as the share that may be paid. 1.30 is not a safety margin of 30%. It means net operating income must reach 1.30 times the annual payment.

  2. ✗ 错误。安全边际20%对应的偿债覆盖率是1.25，不是1.30。公寓楼常见1.2、市况波动时1.3到1.35、仓库工业等非公寓类1.4到1.5，都是市场惯例不是法规。题干已经给了1.30，就要按1.30换算。Wrong. A safety margin of 20% corresponds to a debt coverage ratio of 1.25, not 1.30. Apartment buildings are often at 1.2, at 1.3 to 1.35 when the market is volatile, and non-apartment property such as warehouse and industrial at 1.4 to 1.5. All of those are market custom, not statute. The stem already gives 1.30, so the conversion uses 1.30.

  3. ✓ 正确。偿债覆盖率（debt coverage ratio）1.30等价于安全边际约23.08%。换算关系是覆盖率等于1除以（1减安全边际）。年还款不得超过净营运收入里扣掉这个边际之后的部分。Correct. A debt coverage ratio of 1.30 equals a safety margin of about 23.08%. The conversion is that the coverage ratio equals 1 divided by (1 minus the safety margin). The annual payment may not exceed the part of net operating income left after that margin is kept back.

  4. ✗ 错误。安全边际是相对净营运收入的留存比例，不是贷款额的1.30%。1.30这个倍数换成的边际约是23.08%。Wrong. The safety margin is the share kept back from net operating income. It is not 1.30% of the loan amount. The margin that the multiple 1.30 converts into is about 23.08%.
