① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 情景定性题：给出一宗口头协议、一方未签字或租期长短不同的租约，考《Law and Equity Act》第59条下合同是「不可执行（not enforceable）」而非「无效（void）」，以及支付定金或合理信赖能否触发例外。Scenario characterization questions: Presenting an oral agreement, an agreement unsigned by one party, or leases of varying lengths, testing whether the contract under section 59 of the Law and Equity Act is 'not enforceable' rather than 'void', and whether paying a deposit or reasonable reliance can trigger statutory exceptions.
  • 持牌人合规动作题：给出买方止付定金支票、卖方要求隐瞒重大隐蔽缺陷（material latent defect）、或买方指示删去标准转让条款（Standard Assignment Terms），考持牌人按《Real Estate Services Act》及规则必须采取的唯一合规步骤。Licensee compliance action questions: Presenting scenarios where a buyer stops payment on a deposit cheque, a seller demands concealment of a material latent defect, or a buyer instructs the omission of Standard Assignment Terms, testing the single mandatory compliant step the licensee must take under the Real Estate Services Act and Rules.
  • 条款分类与救济匹配题：给出合同中的条件条款（subject clause）、要害条款（essential term / condition）或次要条款（minor term / warranty），考合同是否已经成立、卖方能否撤回要约、以及违约时无过错方能否终止合同还是只能索赔。Term classification and remedy matching questions: Presenting contract subject clauses, essential terms (conditions), or minor terms (warranties), testing whether a binding contract has formed, whether a seller can revoke the offer, and whether an innocent party upon breach may terminate the contract or is limited to claiming damages.
  • 时限与金额计算题：结合住宅买家冷静期（Home Buyer Rescission Period）的3个工作日（排除周六、周日及法定假日）与0.25%撤销费、共管物业开发商7%留置权扣款或一般改建10%扣款，考起算点、截止日与信托账户划付。Time limit and calculation questions: Combining the 3 business days (excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and statutory holidays) and 0.25% rescission fee under the Home Buyer Rescission Period, with the developer's 7% strata builders lien holdback or the general 10% renovation holdback, testing start dates, deadlines, and trust account disbursements.
  • 考试口径与现行法对照题：在《竞争法》（Competition Act）第90.1条民事措施、信托账户定金出账路径、《Homeowner Protection Act》新房保证等考点上，考考试资料考试口径的传统规则，并辨析现行法新增的行政罚款、冷静期出账通道与2-5-10年质保结构。Course manual versus current law comparison questions: Testing traditional course manual rules against current BC and federal law regarding Competition Act section 90.1 civil remedies, trust account deposit release paths, and Homeowner Protection Act statutory warranties, while distinguishing newly enacted administrative monetary penalties, the statutory rescission release channel, and the 2-5-10 year warranty structure.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 无效与不可执行混淆Confusing void with unenforceable

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常把不满足《Law and Equity Act》第59条书面要求的土地合同误认为自始无效（void），忽略了合同依然存在、且可通过定金收付等行为例外或信赖例外变成可执行。Candidates often mistakenly believe that a land contract failing to satisfy the writing requirement of section 59 of the Law and Equity Act is void from the beginning, overlooking that the contract still exists and can become enforceable through statutory exceptions such as deposit payment or reasonable reliance.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现口头土地买卖、一方未签字或租期三年及以下的租约，选项同时出现 void、illegal 与 unenforceable。The question stem features an oral land sale, an agreement unsigned by one party, or a lease for three years or less, with options simultaneously presenting void, illegal, and unenforceable.

⚠ 中立保管人擅自退定金Brokerage releasing deposit without authority as stakeholder

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生凭直觉认为买方止付支票、交易告吹或买方违约时，经纪行可以直接把支票退给买方或按没收条款把钱付给卖方，忘了经纪行是中立保管人（stakeholder）。Candidates intuitively assume that when a buyer stops payment on a cheque, a transaction collapses, or a buyer defaults, the brokerage may directly return the cheque to the buyer or pay the funds to the seller under a forfeiture clause, forgetting that the brokerage holds the deposit as a stakeholder.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现买方要求索回尚未存入的定金支票、通知止付（stop payment）、或一方拒签 release 时另一方索要定金。The question stem describes a buyer demanding the return of an undeposited deposit cheque, notifying a stop payment, or one party demanding deposit funds while the other refuses to sign a mutual release.

⚠ 主观条件当成已成立合同Treating a subjective condition as a binding contract

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生以为只要双方在买卖合同上签了字卖方就不能撤回，没看出完全主观的条件（whim and fancy clause）使合同停留在要约阶段，且定金不能充当选择权（option）的对价。Candidates assume that once both parties sign a Contract of Purchase and Sale the seller cannot revoke, failing to recognize that a wholly subjective condition (whim and fancy clause) keeps the agreement at the offer stage, and that a deposit cannot serve as consideration for an option.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干的 subject to 条款完全取决于买方个人喜好或公司总裁批准，且卖方在条件移除前收到更高报价想撤回。The subject clause in the question stem depends entirely on the buyer's personal taste or corporate president approval, and the seller receives a higher offer and wishes to revoke before subject removal.

⚠ 条款性质与违约救济错配Mismatching term classification with breach remedies

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到一方违约就选终止合同，没有先区分被违反的是要害条款（condition）还是次要条款（warranty），或误以为看房时的口头夸赞在成交后自动成为合同保证。Candidates immediately select contract termination upon any breach without first distinguishing whether the breached provision is an essential term (condition) or a minor term (warranty), or mistakenly assume that verbal praise during a viewing automatically becomes an enforceable contractual warranty after completion.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述次要承诺违约、或交割后发现卖方口头陈述不实，问无过错方能否拒绝交割或直接解除履行义务。The question stem describes a breach of a minor promise, or an inaccurate verbal statement discovered after completion, and asks whether the innocent party can refuse to complete or terminate their own obligations.

⚠ 考试口径与现行新规或楼花混同Confusing course manual rules with current regulations or pre-sales

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生容易把二手住宅冷静期（3个工作日、0.25%撤销费、过户即消灭）与REDMA楼花撤销权（7天、无撤销费、过户仍可撤）混为一谈，或在《竞争法》与新房保证上答错考试口径。Candidates easily conflate the resale Home Buyer Rescission Period (3 business days, 0.25% fee, extinguished upon transfer) with the REDMA development unit rescission right (7 calendar days, no fee, survives transfer), or answer incorrectly regarding Competition Act penalties and new home warranties by mixing manual and current positions.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现撤销期含周末节假日、转售住宅与开发单位对比、信托账户出账路径、或《竞争法》第90.1条处罚措施。The question stem includes weekends or holidays in rescission calculations, compares resale homes to development units, asks about trust account release mechanisms, or tests penalties under Competition Act section 90.1.

第 1 题Q1《法律与衡平法》第59条书面要求与例外Writing requirements and exceptions under section 59 of the Law and Equity Act★★陷阱：无效与不可执行混淆

Clara and Marcus reach a detailed verbal agreement for Marcus to purchase Clara's residential lot. No written document is signed by Clara, but Marcus immediately hands Clara a deposit cheque, which Clara accepts and cashes. A week later, Clara receives a higher offer and refuses to complete, claiming the oral contract is void under section 59 of the Law and Equity Act. Which statement best describes the legal status of their contract?

Clara 与 Marcus 就 Marcus 购买 Clara 的住宅地块达成了详尽的口头协议。Clara 没有签署任何书面文件，但 Marcus 立即交给 Clara 一张定金支票，Clara 收受并兑现了支票。一周后，Clara 收到更高的报价并拒绝交割，声称口头合同依《Law and Equity Act》第59条无效。下列哪项最准确地描述了该合同的法律状态？

  1. ✗ 错误。违反《Law and Equity Act》第59条书面要求的土地合同是「不可执行（not enforceable）」，绝不是无效（void）、可撤销（voidable）或违法（illegal），属于「无效与不可执行混淆」陷阱。Incorrect. A contract respecting land that fails to satisfy the writing requirement of section 59 of the Law and Equity Act is not enforceable; it is never void, voidable, or illegal. This illustrates the trap of confusing void with unenforceable.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据《Law and Equity Act》第59条，缺乏书面的土地合同并非无效（void），而是原则上不可执行（not enforceable）；但第59(3)(b)及第59(4)条明确规定了「行为例外」——买方支付定金或卖方收受定金/部分价款，即属于表明合同存在的行为，使 Marcus 可以对未签字的 Clara 强制执行该合同。Correct. Under section 59 of the Law and Equity Act, an unwritten land contract is not void, but generally unenforceable. However, sections 59(3)(b) and 59(4) establish an act exception: a buyer paying a deposit or a seller accepting a deposit or part payment constitutes an act establishing that a contract was made, allowing Marcus to enforce the contract against Clara despite her lack of signature.

  3. ✗ 错误。第59(2)条的「三年或以下」豁免仅适用于租期为三年或以下的租约（lease for a term of three years or less），不适用于土地买卖合同。Incorrect. The exemption under section 59(2) applies strictly to a lease for a term of three years or less and does not exempt contracts for the sale of land.

  4. ✗ 错误。首先收受定金已触发第59(3)(b)及(4)条的行为例外使合同可执行；其次即便在判定某合同不可执行时，第59(5)条也明确授权法院命令返还所受利益（restitution）并补偿已支出的费用。Incorrect. First, accepting the deposit triggers the statutory act exception under sections 59(3)(b) and 59(4), rendering the contract enforceable. Second, even when declaring a contract unenforceable, section 59(5) grants the court express authority to order restitution of benefits received and compensation for expenses incurred.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Elena verbally agrees to grant Victor a two-year lease on a small house, and in a separate deal, verbally agrees to sell her vacant acreage to Nadia without any writing, deposit, or change of position by either party. Under section 59 of the Law and Equity Act, how does the law treat these two oral agreements?

Elena 口头同意授予 Victor 一份为期两年的小屋租约；而在另一笔独立交易中，她口头同意将一块空地卖给 Nadia，双方没有任何书面文件、未收付定金、也没有任何一方因信赖而改变处境。根据《Law and Equity Act》第59条，法律如何对待这两份口头协议？

  1. ✗ 错误。第59条的后果是「不可执行（not enforceable）」而非「无效（void）」，且三年或以下的租约根本不受第59条限制，属于「无效与不可执行混淆」陷阱。Incorrect. The statutory consequence under section 59 is that the contract is not enforceable rather than void, and leases for three years or less are completely exempt from section 59. This falls into the trap of confusing void with unenforceable.

  2. ✗ 错误。把两者的适用规则完全颠倒了：两年租约在三年或以下豁免范围内，无需书面即可执行；而空地买卖既无书面又无行为或信赖例外，属于不可执行。Incorrect. This completely reverses the applicable rules: the two-year lease falls within the statutory exemption for leases of three years or less and is enforceable without writing, whereas the oral acreage sale lacks writing, acts, or reliance exceptions and is therefore unenforceable.

  3. ✗ 错误。口头土地买卖协议并不违法（not illegal），也不受针对持牌人的《Real Estate Services Rules》处罚，只是在法院拿不到强制执行救济。Incorrect. An oral contract to sell land is not illegal and is not subject to administrative penalties under the Real Estate Services Rules; it simply cannot be enforced in a court of law.

  4. ✓ 正确。《Law and Equity Act》第59(2)(a)(b)条将三年或以下租期的租约（leases of three years or less）排除在书面要求之外，因此两年口头租约完全可执行；而出售空地的口头协议因无书面且未触发第59(3)条的行为或信赖例外，虽然合同存在（非无效），但在法院不可执行（not enforceable）。Correct. Sections 59(2)(a) and (b) of the Law and Equity Act exclude leases for a term of three years or less from the writing requirement, so the oral two-year lease is fully enforceable. In contrast, the oral agreement to sell vacant acreage lacks writing and does not trigger any act or reliance exceptions under section 59(3); although the contract exists (it is not void), it cannot be enforced in court.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

第 2 题Q2重大隐蔽缺陷的披露与拒绝服务义务Disclosure of material latent defects and duty to refuse service★★陷阱：中立保管人擅自退定金

While listing a house, the seller informs his listing licensee that the municipal government recently issued a formal notice requiring expensive structural remediation of the foundation — a defect not visible on a reasonable inspection — and instructs the licensee not to tell any prospective buyers. The buyer has not received any written disclosure of this issue from the seller. Under section 59 of the Real Estate Services Rules, what must the listing licensee do?

在挂牌一套房屋时，卖方告知其挂牌持牌人：市政府近期发出了一份正式通知，要求对地基进行代价高昂的结构整改——这一缺陷在合理查看物业时无法发现——并且卖方指示持牌人不得告诉任何潜在买方。买方并未从卖方处收到关于该问题的任何书面披露。根据《Real Estate Services Rules》第59条，挂牌持牌人必须怎么做？

  2. ✗ 错误。只有当对方违反双方视为要害的条款（essential term，普通法称 condition）时，无过错方才能把合同当作终止；因违反次要条款而拒不交割，买方自己反而会构成毁约（repudiation），属于「条款性质与违约救济错配」陷阱。Incorrect. Only a breach of an essential term (condition) entitles the innocent party to treat the contract as terminated. Refusing to complete due to a breach of a minor warranty would make the buyer guilty of wrongful repudiation. This reflects the trap of mismatching term classification with breach remedies.

  3. ✗ 错误。写进买卖合同的次要条款（warranty）具有合同约束力，无过错方无需在土地登记处登记即可就违约请求金钱损害赔偿。Incorrect. A minor term (warranty) written into a Contract of Purchase and Sale is legally binding, and the innocent party can sue for monetary damages upon breach without needing to register a charge in the land title office.

第 5 题Q5条件条款的主客观分类与合同成立Subjective versus objective conditions precedent and contract formation★★★陷阱：主观条件当成已成立合同

A corporate buyer and a seller sign an unsealed document for the purchase of a commercial building, containing the clause: "Subject to the approval of the price and terms by the president of the buyer corporation on or before May 15." No separate consideration is given to keep the offer open. On May 10, before the buyer's president has approved the deal, the seller receives a much higher offer from another party and notifies the corporate buyer that she is revoking and cancelling the deal. Based on BC Court of Appeal principles, what is the legal result?

一家公司买方与卖方签署了一份未盖印（unsealed）的商业楼宇购买文件，其中包含条款：「以买方公司总裁在5月15日当日或之前批准价格与条款为条件。」买方未支付任何独立对价来维持要约不撤回。5月10日，在买方总裁批准交易之前，卖方收到另一方高得多的报价，遂通知公司买方撤回并取消交易。根据 BC 上诉法院确立的原则，法律结果如何？

Peace Holdings Ltd., the BC Court of Appeal firmly established that a deposit cannot serve as separate consideration for an irrevocable option (since a deposit is credited toward the purchase price upon completion or forfeited upon breach, it cannot support an irrevocable promise to keep an offer open). This illustrates the trap of treating a subjective condition as a binding contract.

Absent a seal or separate consideration for an option, no binding contract is formed in law; the arrangement remains merely an offer (only the buyer removing the condition would amount to acceptance), so the seller is entitled to withdraw it before the president approves.

A time clause is an instrument used when a binding contract has formed or where the seller is bound by an option to compel early subject removal. Here, because the document was unsealed and the condition was wholly subjective, no contract existed and the seller could revoke immediately.

The 0.25% rescission fee applies to a buyer exercising rescission rights under the residential Home Buyer Rescission Period and has no relevance to commercial transactions, subjective clauses, or seller revocation rights.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A buyer and seller enter into a properly executed Contract of Purchase and Sale subject to the buyer obtaining a conventional first mortgage from a local credit union on specified objective terms by Friday. Two days after signing, the buyer finds another house he likes better, makes no application for a mortgage at all, and tells the seller on Friday that the contract is at an end because financing was not obtained.   1. ✓ 正确。当先决条件（condition precedent）具备客观标准时，买卖合同已经成立，只是主要履行义务被悬置；此时法律会默示一项条款（implied term），要求享有条件利益的买方必须公平、诚实、善意地采取合理步骤去促成条件成就（act honestly and in good faith to fulfill the condition）。买方故意不去申请按揭构成对该默示条款的违约，卖方有权把定金作为损害赔偿留下。Correct. The law implies a contractual term requiring the party for whose benefit the condition is inserted to take reasonable, honest, and good faith steps to satisfy it. By deliberately failing to apply for financing, the buyer breached this implied obligation, entitling the seller to retain the deposit as damages.

  2. ✗ 错误。附有明确客观标准的融资条件不是完全主观（whim and fancy）条款，买方没有故意不申请按揭而无责退出的自由裁量权，属于「主观条件当成已成立合同」的反向误区。Incorrect. A financing clause containing clear objective terms is not a whim and fancy clause; the buyer has no arbitrary discretion to refrain from applying for a mortgage and walk away without liability. This represents the converse misconception of treating subjective conditions as contracts.

  3. ✗ 错误。故意令先决条件落空违反的是关于条件成就的默示义务，卖方有权将定金作为损害赔偿主张，并非次要条款违约。Incorrect. Intentionally sabotaging a condition precedent breaches the implied obligation to take reasonable steps to fulfill it, allowing the seller to retain the deposit as damages; it is not treated as a minor warranty breach requiring cash completion.

  4. ✗ 错误。移除条件必须采用书面通知（written notice），且买方在这里是想退出合同而非放弃条件继续买房。Incorrect. Removing or waiving a condition precedent requires written notice, and here the buyer was attempting to terminate the contract rather than waive the condition to proceed with the purchase.

答案Answer：a

第 6 题Q6标准转让条款与缺省时的双向通知义务Standard Assignment Terms and dual notification duties upon omission★★陷阱：考试口径与现行新规或楼花混同

A buyer instructs his licensee in writing to prepare an offer for a resale residential house that omits the two Standard Assignment Terms prescribed by section 8.2(3) of the Real Estate Services Regulation. What must the buyer's licensee and the seller's licensee do when this offer is presented to the seller?

一位买方书面指示其持牌人起草一份购买二手住宅的要约，并在要约中删去《Real Estate Services Regulation》第8.2(3)条规定的两条标准转让条款（Standard Assignment Terms）。当这份要约呈交给卖方时，买方持牌人与卖方持牌人必须分别做什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。标准转让条款（Standard Assignment Terms）并不是法律禁止转让，客户有权书面指示不写入（unless instructed otherwise in writing by the person to whom the licensee is providing services），只要走完法定通知流程即可。Incorrect. Standard Assignment Terms do not prohibit assignments by law; clients have the statutory right to instruct their licensee in writing to omit them, provided the mandatory disclosure and notice procedures are completed.

  2. ✗ 错误。恰恰相反。根据《Real Estate Services Regulation》第8.2(2)条，开发商依 REDMA 销售开发单位的合同豁免适用第8.2条，而二手住宅买卖合同正属于第8.2条的核心适用对象，属于「考试口径与现行新规或楼花混同」陷阱。Incorrect. Quite the opposite: under section 8.2(2) of the Real Estate Services Regulation, developer contracts marketing pre-sale development units under REDMA are exempt from section 8.2, making resale residential contracts the primary target of these rules. This falls into the trap of confusing course manual rules with pre-sale regulations.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据《Real Estate Services Regulation》第8.2(4)–(7)条，当呈交给卖方的合同缺少任一标准转让条款（Standard Assignment Terms：未经卖方书面同意不得转让、转让利润归卖方）时，买方持牌人必须与合同同时送达一份独立于合同之外、采用总监（superintendent）批准表格的通知（建议卖方在签署前取得独立专业意见）；代表卖方的持牌人则必须将该通知交给卖方，并告知卖方合同缺少该条款、合同能否被转让及转让条件、以及卖方是否有权取得转让利润。（注：考试口径称表格由 RECBC 批准，现行归 BCFSA 总监批准。）Correct. Under sections 8.2(4) through (7) of the Real Estate Services Regulation, if a proposed contract omits either Standard Assignment Term (no assignment without the seller's written consent, and assignment profits go to the seller), the buyer's licensee must deliver, together with the offer, a notice separate from the contract on a form approved by the superintendent, advising the seller to obtain independent professional advice before signing. The seller's licensee must then give this notice to the seller and explain that the terms are omitted, whether the contract can be assigned and on what conditions, and whether the seller is entitled to any assignment profit. (Note: The course manual says the form is approved by RECBC, whereas under current law it is approved by the BCFSA Superintendent.)

  4. ✗ 错误。0.25% 是住宅冷静期撤销费，CSAIR 是开发商报送住宅 strata 楼花转让信息的登记册，均与二手房删去标准转让条款的通知流程无关。Incorrect. The 0.25% fee applies to the Home Buyer Rescission Period, and CSAIR is a reporting registry for pre-sale residential strata assignments; neither has any connection to the statutory notice procedures for omitting Standard Assignment Terms in resale transactions.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Unless the client instructs otherwise in writing, what are the two Standard Assignment Terms that a licensee must include in a proposed Contract of Purchase and Sale for real estate (other than a development unit marketed by a developer under REDMA) under section 8.2(3) of the Real Estate Services Regulation?

除非客户书面另行指示，根据《Real Estate Services Regulation》第8.2(3)条，持牌人在拟备不动产买卖合同（开发商依 REDMA 销售的开发单位除外）时，必须写入的两条标准转让条款（Standard Assignment Terms）是什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。3个工作日是住宅冷静期，7% 是向开发商买共管物业时的建筑留置权扣款，都不是标准转让条款的内容。Incorrect. Three business days pertains to the Home Buyer Rescission Period and 7% applies to developer strata builders lien holdbacks; neither represents the content of the Standard Assignment Terms.

  2. ✗ 错误。标准转让条款要求任何转让均须取得卖方书面同意，且全部转让利润归卖方所有；若买方需要以公司名义接手或家庭成员加名而保留转让权，必须书面指示修改并走通知流程。Incorrect. The Standard Assignment Terms require written seller consent for any assignment and entitle the seller to all assignment profits. If a buyer wishes to retain assignment rights to an affiliate or family member, they must provide written instructions to amend the terms and follow the statutory notice procedure.

  3. ✗ 错误。60天是 Form F 有效期，10年是新房结构质保期，与标准转让条款无关。Incorrect. Sixty days is the validity period for a Form F Certificate of Payment and 10 years is the new home structural warranty duration; neither relates to Standard Assignment Terms.

  4. ✓ 正确。根据《Real Estate Services Regulation》第8.2(3)条，标准转让条款（Standard Assignment Terms）包含两项核心内容：(1) 未经卖方书面同意，本合同不得转让（must not be assigned without the written consent of the seller）；(2) 买方或任何后手转让人转让本合同所产生的任何利润，归卖方所有（the seller is entitled to any profit resulting from an assignment）。Correct. Under section 8.2(3) of the Real Estate Services Regulation, the Standard Assignment Terms require that: (1) the contract must not be assigned without the written consent of the seller; and (2) the seller is entitled to any profit resulting from an assignment of the contract by the buyer or any subsequent assignee.

答案Answer：d

第 7 题Q7《竞争法》刑事合谋与民事条款对比Competition Act criminal conspiracy versus civil provisions★★★陷阱：考试口径与现行新规或楼花混同

According to the course manual, how does the civil provision in section 90.1 of the federal Competition Act differ from the criminal conspiracy provision in section 45 with respect to penalties and who may bring an application?

根据课程考试资料（考试口径），联邦《Competition Act》第90.1条的民事条款与第45条的刑事合谋条款相比，在处罚措施与谁有权提起申请方面有何不同？

  1. ✓ 正确。按考试资料考试口径，《Competition Act》第45条是刑事合谋罪（criminal conspiracy），最高可判14年监禁或由法院裁量的罚金；而第90.1条是民事条款，仅由竞争事务专员（Commissioner of Competition）针对竞争者之间的协议提起，且没有罚金或监禁（仅能作出禁止令等补救命令）。现行法经2022–2024年三轮修法已大幅改写第90.1条：现行第90.1(1.3)条允许处以行政罚款（AMP，上限为 $10,000,000［再犯 $15,000,000］与协议获利3倍或全球年总收入3%中的较大者），第90.1(1)条允许依第103.1条获准的私人申请，且第90.1(1.01)条不再要求当事人都是竞争者。Correct. Under the course manual position, section 45 of the Competition Act is a criminal conspiracy offence punishable by up to 14 years imprisonment or a discretionary fine, whereas section 90.1 is a civil provision that can be brought only by the Commissioner of Competition against agreements between competitors, carrying no fines or imprisonment (only remedial orders such as prohibition orders). Under current law, following three rounds of amendments between 2022 and 2024, section 90.1 has been substantially rewritten: section 90.1(1.3) permits administrative monetary penalties (up to the greater of $10,000,000, or $15,000,000 for subsequent orders, and either three times the benefit derived from the agreement or 3% of annual gross global revenue), section 90.1(1) permits private applications with leave under section 103.1, and section 90.1(1.01) no longer requires all parties to be competitors.

  2. ✗ 错误。把刑事与民事条款完全颠倒了：最高14年监禁属于第45条刑事合谋罪，而 $2,500 是 $1,000,000 房价的冷静期撤销费，属于「考试口径与现行新规或楼花混同」陷阱。Incorrect. This completely reverses the criminal and civil provisions: imprisonment up to 14 years applies to criminal conspiracy under section 45, whereas $2,500 represents the rescission fee on a $1,000,000 property under the Home Buyer Rescission Period. This illustrates the trap of confusing course manual rules with modern regulations.

  3. ✗ 错误。《Competition Act》第45(3)条明确允许法院从间接证据（circumstantial evidence）推断合谋存在，只要整体达到排除合理怀疑（beyond a reasonable doubt）的证明标准即可。Incorrect. Section 45(3) of the Competition Act explicitly allows a court to infer the existence of a conspiracy from circumstantial evidence, provided the overall evidence satisfies the criminal standard of proof beyond a reasonable doubt.

  4. ✗ 错误。同业之间约定固定或维持佣金率、或者划分销售地域/客户，正是第45(1)条刑事合谋罪在本行业最典型的违法形态。Incorrect. Agreements between competing brokerages to fix or maintain commission rates or allocate sales territories and clients represent classic criminal conspiracy offences under section 45(1).

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Three competing real estate brokerages in a BC municipality quietly reach an informal agreement that none of them will charge less than a specified commission rate on residential listings and each brokerage will stay out of the others' designated neighbourhoods. Under section 45 of the federal Competition Act, what is the legal consequence?

BC 省某市的三家竞争性地产经纪行暗中达成一项非正式默契：任何一家在住宅挂牌上收取的佣金率均不低于某一特定比例，且各家互不进入对方划定的街区。根据联邦《Competition Act》第45条，法律后果是什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。第45(3)条明确允许法院凭间接证据（circumstantial evidence）推断合谋存在，非正式默契或君子协定同样构成刑事合谋罪。Incorrect. Section 45(3) expressly permits a court to infer the existence of a conspiracy from circumstantial evidence; an informal understanding or gentlemen's agreement is fully sufficient to constitute criminal conspiracy.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据《Competition Act》第45(1)及(2)条，与竞争者约定固定/维持价格（含佣金率）或分配销售、地域、客户或市场（allocating sales, territories, customers or markets），均构成可公诉刑事合谋罪，最高可判处14年监禁、或由法院裁量的罚金、或两者并处。（注：2022年修法新增的第45(1.1)条还将非关联雇主之间固定薪酬条件、互不招揽或互不挖角的协议一并纳入该刑事罪名。）Correct. Under sections 45(1) and (2) of the Competition Act, entering into an agreement with competitors to fix or maintain prices (including commission rates) or to allocate sales, territories, customers, or markets constitutes an indictable criminal conspiracy punishable by up to 14 years imprisonment, a discretionary fine, or both. (Note: The 2022 amendments added section 45(1.1), which also brings wage-fixing and no-poaching agreements between unaffiliated employers within this criminal offence.)

  3. ✗ 错误。向客户披露佣金是《Real Estate Services Rules》第43条对单家经纪行服务协议的要求，绝不能使竞争者之间合谋固定价格或瓜分市场的刑事犯罪合法化。Incorrect. Disclosing commission terms to a client is a regulatory requirement for individual service agreements under section 43 of the Real Estate Services Rules; it cannot legalize a criminal conspiracy between competitors to fix prices or divide markets.

  4. ✗ 错误。第45条是联邦刑法级别的重罪（最高14年监禁与不封顶裁量罚金），与共管物业 Form F 的60天有效期毫无关系。Incorrect. Section 45 is a serious federal criminal offence carrying up to 14 years imprisonment and uncapped fines; it has nothing to do with the 60-day validity period of a strata Form F.
