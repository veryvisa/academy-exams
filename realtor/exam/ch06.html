本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 定性辨析题：给出一段占用处所的事实（如合同抬头写许可但实际授予排他占有、或租客与业主共用厨房卫生间、或成交前买方提前入住），考你识别属于租赁（lease）还是许可（licence）、属于意愿租赁（tenancy at will）还是周期性租赁（periodic tenancy），以及是否受《住宅租赁法》（Residential Tenancy Act, RTA）保护。Classification and distinction questions: presenting facts of premises occupancy (such as agreements titled licence but actually granting exclusive possession, tenants sharing kitchen or bathroom facilities with the owner, or buyers occupying early before completion), testing your ability to identify whether an arrangement constitutes a lease or a licence, a tenancy at will or a periodic tenancy, and whether it falls under the protection of the Residential Tenancy Act (RTA).
  • 商业与住宅双轨对比题：故意把商业租赁（commercial tenancy）的普通法默认规则（如租客当心 caveat lessee、扣押财物变卖 distress、无减损损失义务、未明文禁止即可自由转租）套进住宅场景，或反过来把住宅的法定保护套进商业场景，测试你能否先分清物业性质再选法律工具。Commercial vs. residential dual-track comparison questions: intentionally applying commercial tenancy common law default rules (such as caveat lessee, distress by seizing and selling goods, absence of a duty to mitigate, or free subletting unless expressly prohibited) to residential scenarios, or vice versa by applying residential statutory protections to commercial scenarios, testing your ability to distinguish property character before selecting legal remedies.
  • 数字与时限精准记忆题：围绕住宅涨租间隔（12 个月）与提前通知期（3 个月）、保证金与宠物押金上限（各半个月租金）及退还/索赔期限（15 天）、房东进入处所的书面通知窗口（提前至少 24 小时且不超过 30 天、早 8 点至晚 9 点）、以及各类终止通知的通知期与异议期设问。Precise numbers and statutory deadlines questions: testing residential rent increase intervals (12 months) and advance notice periods (3 months), security deposit and pet damage deposit limits (half a month's rent each) and return or claim deadlines (15 days), written notice windows for landlord entry (at least 24 hours and not more than 30 days ahead, between 8:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m.), and notice and dispute periods for various notices to end tenancy.
  • 考试口径与现行新法分流题：在自住收房通知期、装修收房（renoviction）流程、超额涨租理由等修法重灾区，考题按课程手册（考试口径）设问（如自住收房为两个月通知、15 天异议，装修收房为四个月通知），测试你能否准确答出考试口径答案，同时在实务中分清 2026 年现行规则。Exam manual vs. current law divergence questions: testing major legislative reform areas such as landlord's use notice periods, renoviction procedures, and additional rent increase grounds, where exam questions follow the course manual (such as 2 months' notice and 15 days to dispute for landlord's use, and 4 months' notice for renovations), testing whether you can accurately provide the course manual answer while distinguishing current 2026 rules for practice.
  • 违约救济与带租客售房实务题：考商业租客弃租时房东依据 Highway Properties 案兼顾终止租约与索赔未来租金差额的做法，以及持牌人代理出售带租客住宅时核实租赁协议、处理买家自住收房通知、交割押金与支付一个月租金补偿的操作规范。Default remedies and tenanted property sales questions: testing commercial landlords' remedies under Highway Properties to terminate the lease while claiming damages for lost future rent upon tenant abandonment, and licensee standards of practice when selling tenanted residential properties, including verifying tenancy agreements, processing buyer's use notices, transferring deposits, and handling the one-month rent compensation.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 商业与住宅规则串台Confusing commercial and residential tenancy rules

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生容易把住宅租赁里耳熟能详的保护（如禁止扣押财物、房东负责维修、转租须经房东同意、双方必须减损损失）误当成所有租赁通用的规则，忽略了商业租赁主要由合同与普通法支配且规则往往相反。Candidates often assume familiar residential protections (such as the ban on distress, landlord repair obligations, mandatory consent for subletting, and mutual duties to mitigate) apply universally to all tenancies, overlooking that commercial leases are governed primarily by contract and common law with often opposite default rules.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 读题干第一句先圈出物业用途是商业店铺/写字楼/仓库（commercial）还是居住单元（residential），以及租期是否超过 20 年。In the first sentence of the stem, immediately identify whether the premises are used as a commercial shop, office, or warehouse versus a residential living accommodation, and verify whether the term exceeds 20 years.

⚠ 考试旧口径与现行新法混淆Conflating course manual exam rules with current statutory amendments

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 2021 至 2025 年间住宅收房与涨租规则被大幅改写，平时做实务或看政府网页记住的 3 个月通知、21 天异议期、RTB 门户生成通知等现行规定，会干扰对课程手册历史考点的作答。Substantial amendments from 2021 to 2025 reshaped residential end-of-tenancy and rent increase rules; recalling current requirements from practice or government websites—such as 3 months' notice, 21 days to dispute, and web portal-generated notices—can disrupt answering course manual exam questions.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 看到住宅自住收房、装修收房、超额涨租或强制执行占有令等题目时，先按考试口径选标准答案，同时在脑中对照现行法差异。When encountering questions on landlord's use, renovictions, additional rent increases, or enforcing an Order of Possession, answer according to the course manual standard while keeping current statutory differences clear in mind.

⚠ 抬头名称与实质排他占有倒置Prioritizing document title over substantive exclusive possession

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到协议标题写着「许可协议（Licence Agreement）」或「特许持有人（licensee）」，就顺着字面选它只是许可而不创设土地权益。Seeing an agreement titled 'Licence Agreement' or referring to the occupant as a 'licensee' leads candidates to rely on literal labels and conclude that it is merely a licence without creating an interest in land.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干一面强调合同抬头写许可，一面描述占用人拥有独立门锁、连业主本人未经允许也不能入内，这就是在考排他占有（exclusive possession）决定实质为租赁。The question emphasizes a licence label in the document title while describing that the occupant holds keys to lock out even the owner without permission, which tests whether exclusive possession substantively creates a lease.

⚠ 禁止转让与禁止转租混为一谈Conflating prohibitions on assignment with prohibitions on subletting

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 商业租约中法院对限制条款作严格解释，考生常以为写了「不得转让（shall not assign）」就当然禁止了转租，或写了「不得转租该处所」就禁止了部分转租。Courts strictly construe restrictive covenants in commercial leases; candidates often assume that a covenant stating the tenant 'shall not assign' automatically prohibits subletting, or that a covenant prohibiting subletting the premises prevents subletting a portion thereof.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现商业租约引述的具体禁止措辞，且租客采取了未被字面点名的另一种方式（如只禁转让时去转租，或只禁整体时去分租一部分）。The stem quotes specific restrictive wording from a commercial lease, and the tenant adopts an unmentioned method (such as subletting when only assignment is prohibited, or subletting part of the premises when only the whole is specified).

⚠ 商业重租构成交回租赁陷阱Commercial re-letting constituting an unintended surrender

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 商业租客中途弃租时，考生凭直觉认为房东立即把铺位重新租出去既能止损又能继续向原租客追讨差额，却忘了普通法下未事先发通知声明保留权利就重租会被视为接受交回租赁（surrender）。When a commercial tenant abandons premises, candidates intuitively assume that immediately re-letting mitigates losses while allowing the landlord to recover rent deficits, overlooking that re-letting without prior clear notice of reserving rights constitutes an acceptance of surrender at common law.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述商业租客在固定期未满时搬空违约，询问房东如何操作才能既收回处所又保留对剩余租期租金损失的索赔权。The stem describes a commercial tenant abandoning during a fixed term, asking how the landlord can re-enter premises while preserving the right to claim damages for future rent losses over the unexpired term.

第 1 题Q1租赁与许可的排他占有判准Exclusive Possession Test for Lease versus Licence★★陷阱：抬头名称与实质排他占有倒置

A property owner signs a document titled 'Personal Licence Agreement' with a florist for a street-level shop. Although the document calls the florist a 'licensee,' it grants the florist the sole keys and the right to exclude everyone, including the owner, from the premises for a two-year period at a monthly fee. If the owner sells the building six months later, how will a court most likely classify the florist's interest?

一名业主与花店店主就一间临街商铺签署了一份题为「个人许可协议」的文件。尽管文件称花店店主为「被许可人（licensee）」，但协议授予店主唯一的钥匙，并允许其在两年期内按月付费、排除包括业主在内的任何人进入该处所。如果业主在六个月后卖掉该栋楼，法院最可能如何认定花店店主的权益？

  1. ✗ 掉入「抬头名称与实质排他占有倒置」陷阱。合同抬头写「licensor / licensee」并不决定法律性质，法院看重的是当事人的真实意图与是否授予排他占有。This falls into the trap of prioritizing the document's title over substantive exclusive possession. Labelling the parties 'licensor' and 'licensee' does not determine legal classification; courts look to the true intention of the parties and whether exclusive possession was granted.

  2. ✓ 正确。判断协议是租赁（lease）还是许可（licence）看当事人的意图，而最有分量的证据是是否授予排他占有（exclusive possession）。给了排他占有，法院通常认定它是租赁，从而创设随地而走（runs with the land）的土地权益（interest in land），约束新业主。Correct. Determining whether an agreement creates a lease or a licence depends on the parties' intention, with the most critical evidence being whether exclusive possession was conferred. Where exclusive possession is granted, courts generally classify the agreement as a lease, creating an interest in land that runs with the land and binds the new building owner.

  3. ✗ 掉入「商业与住宅规则串台」陷阱。花店商铺属于商业用途，创设的是商业租赁（commercial tenancy），受普通法与《商业租赁法》（CTA）调整，而非《住宅租赁法》（RTA）。This falls into the trap of confusing commercial and residential rules. A street-level florist shop is a commercial premises, creating a commercial tenancy governed by common law and the Commercial Tenancy Act (CTA), not the Residential Tenancy Act (RTA).

  4. ✗ 混淆了固定期商业租赁与意愿租赁（tenancy at will）。该协议有明确的两年期限与按月租金，且赋予排他占有，属于固定期商业租赁而非随时终止的意愿租赁。This confuses a fixed-term commercial lease with a tenancy at will. Because the agreement specifies a definite two-year term, monthly fees, and exclusive possession, it constitutes a commercial fixed-term lease rather than a tenancy at will terminable at any time.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An artist signs a contract with a studio owner to use one open workbench inside a shared hall. The owner retains full access to the hall at all hours and can move the artist to any available bench each morning. When the studio owner sells the hall to a developer, the artist claims to hold a lease that binds the developer. Is the artist's claim valid?

一名画师与画室业主签了一份合同，使用共享大厅内的一张开放式工作台。业主保留全天候进出大厅的完整权限，且每天早上可以把画师调换到任何空置的工作台。当画室业主把大厅卖给开发商时，画师声称自己持有约束开发商的租约。画师的主张成立吗？

  1. ✗ 混淆了合同特权与土地权益。付费使用空间若没有排他占有，只构成许可（licence），不创设土地权益（interest in land）。This confuses a contractual privilege with an interest in land. Paying a fee to use space without exclusive possession constitutes only a licence and creates no interest in land.

  2. ✗ 捏造了法律规则。物业出售不会把许可变成周期性租赁；相反，正因为许可不随地而走，物业易主时新业主不受其约束。This fabricates a legal rule. Selling a property does not convert a licence into a periodic tenancy; rather, because a licence does not run with the land, the new owner is not bound by it.

  3. ✗ 掉入「商业与住宅规则串台」陷阱。工作台既非住宅，也无排他占有，完全不属 RTB 管辖。This falls into the trap of confusing commercial and residential rules. A studio workbench is neither residential accommodation nor held under exclusive possession, and is entirely outside the jurisdiction of the Residential Tenancy Branch.

  4. ✓ 正确。画师没有固定空间的排他占有（exclusive possession），因此只持有许可（licence）；许可只是一份合同特权，不创设土地权益（interest in land），也不随地而走（does not run with the land），不能约束买下物业的开发商。Correct. Because the artist lacks exclusive possession over a defined space, the artist holds only a licence. A licence is merely a personal contractual privilege that creates no interest in land and does not run with the land, meaning it does not bind the purchasing developer.

  3. ✗ 掉入「商业与住宅规则串台」陷阱。商业租约可以通过合同条款保留房东随时进入权；但住宅租赁的安宁享用与进入权被 RTA ss.28–29 钉死，合同条款不得规避法定限制。This falls into the trap of confusing commercial and residential rules. A commercial lease can reserve unrestricted landlord entry by express contract, but quiet enjoyment and entry restrictions under RTA ss.28-29 are non-waivable statutory protections that cannot be avoided by lease terms.

  4. ✗ 混淆了例行检查进入权与驱逐拒不搬离租客的强制执行令状（Writ of Possession）。This confuses routine landlord entry rights with a BC Supreme Court Writ of Possession, which is used to enforce an eviction against a tenant refusing to vacate.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A seller's licensee is hired to sell a tenanted residential condo. On Tuesday afternoon, the licensee mails a written notice of entry to the tenant's mailbox address for a buyer showing scheduled for Thursday morning. Based on the deemed service rules in section 90 of the Residential Tenancy Act and best practices for showing tenanted properties, why is this approach problematic?

一名卖方持牌人受聘出售一套有租客的住宅共管公寓。周二下午，持牌人向租客邮寄了一份书面进入通知，安排在周四上午带买家看房。根据《住宅租赁法》第 90 条的视为收到规则以及带看有租客物业的实务规范，为什么这种做法有问题？

  1. ✗ 记错了视为收到天数与进入时段。邮寄是寄出后第 5 天视为收到（而非第 10 天），且法定进入时段是早 8 点至晚 9 点之间。This misstates the deemed service timeline and lawful entry hours. A mailed document is deemed received on the 5th day after mailing (not the 10th day), and statutory entry hours are between 8:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m.

  2. ✗ 过于绝对。只要取得租客同意（最好先商定书面看房日程）或合规送达至少 24 小时书面通知，持牌人可以在早 8 点至晚 9 点之间带看。This is overly absolute. Provided tenant consent is obtained (preferably through an agreed written showing schedule) or valid 24 hours' written notice is served, a licensee may enter between 8:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m.

  3. ✗ 张冠李戴了看房进入通知与终止租赁占有令。带看房屋不终止租赁，不需要占有令。This conflates a notice of entry for showings with an Order of Possession to terminate a tenancy. Showing a rental unit does not end the tenancy and requires no RTB order.

  4. ✓ 正确。根据 RTA s.90，邮寄送达在寄出后第 5 天才视为收到（贴门上、传真或投信箱为第 3 天），而 24 小时通知期从「视为收到日」起算，周二邮寄到周四根本未满期；且挂牌持牌人第一步应先联系租客谈成双方接受的书面看房日程，谈不成才退守 24 小时书面通知。Correct. Under RTA s.90, mail is deemed received on the 5th day after mailing (whereas attaching to a door or leaving in a mailbox is deemed received on the 3rd day), and the 24-hour notice window only begins upon deemed receipt, meaning Thursday morning is far too early; moreover, best practice requires the listing licensee to first attempt to agree on a mutually acceptable written showing schedule before resorting to unilateral 24-hour written notices.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

第 7 题Q7商业与住宅的转租转让及减损义务Assignment, Subletting, and Mitigation in Commercial and Residential Leases★★★陷阱：商业重租构成交回租赁陷阱

A commercial tenant abandons a retail store with three years left on a five-year fixed-term lease. Under common law rules, including the Supreme Court of Canada decision in Highway Properties, which statement accurately describes the commercial landlord's options and duty to mitigate?

一名商业租客在五年固定期租约尚余三年时弃租了一家零售铺位。根据包括加拿大最高法院 Highway Properties 案在内的普通法规则，下列哪项准确描述了商业房东的选择权与减损损失义务？

  1. ✗ 掉入「商业与住宅规则串台」陷阱。RTA s.7(2) 对住宅租赁双方课以法定减损义务（mitigation）；但普通法上商业房东没有重新出租以减损损失的义务，甚至可以放空铺位逐月起诉追租。This falls into the trap of confusing commercial and residential rules. Section 7(2) of the RTA imposes a statutory duty to mitigate on residential parties; at common law, a commercial landlord owes no general duty to re-let to mitigate losses and may leave the premises vacant while suing for rent as it falls due.

  2. ✗ 掉入「商业重租构成交回租赁陷阱」。在普通法下，如果商业房东未事先明确通知租客就擅自把处所重新出租，可能被法院认定为接受交回租赁（surrender），从而丧失对原租客其后租金的请求权。This falls into the commercial re-letting surrender trap. At common law, if a commercial landlord re-lets premises without prior clear notice to the defaulting tenant, courts may interpret this as an acceptance of surrender by operation of law, forfeiting the right to recover future rent from the original tenant.

  3. ✓ 正确。普通法上商业房东没有重新出租以减损损失（mitigation）的义务，且直接重租有被认定为交回租赁（surrender）而丧失后续追租权的风险；但依据最高法院 Highway Properties 案，房东只要向租客发出明确通知（clear notice），表明其意图终止租约并就剩余租期的利益损失索赔损害赔偿，即可既收回处所又保住对租金差额的请求权。Correct. At common law, a commercial landlord owes no general duty to re-let to mitigate losses, and re-letting directly risks being deemed an acceptance of surrender; however, under the Supreme Court of Canada ruling in Highway Properties, if the landlord gives clear notice to the tenant terminating the lease and claiming damages for the lost prospective benefit over the unexpired term, the landlord can retake possession while preserving the claim for rent shortfalls.

  4. ✗ 把住宅 RTB 15 天押金索赔期限与商业维修原则（caveat lessee 租客当心）混作一团。商业租约违约归法院管辖，且 caveat lessee 指的是商业房东无维修义务而非违约追租。This jumbles the RTB 15-day deposit claim deadline with caveat lessee. Commercial lease breaches are litigated in court, and caveat lessee denotes that commercial landlords have no implied repair obligations, having nothing to do with breach remedies.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A residential tenant holds a one-year fixed-term tenancy agreement with eight months remaining. The tenant gets a job in another province and asks the landlord for written consent to assign the tenancy to a qualified replacement tenant. Under section 34 of the Residential Tenancy Act, what are the landlord's rights and obligations?

一名住宅租客持有一份尚余八个月的一年期固定期租赁协议。租客在外省找到工作，请求房东书面同意将租赁转让给一名合格的接替租客。根据《住宅租赁法》第 34 条，房东有哪些权利和义务？

  1. ✗ 双重违反 RTA s.34。固定期尚余 6 个月以上的，房东不得无理拒绝同意；且房东在任何时候都不得为同意转让或转租收费（收费构成 s.95(1)(i) 的罪行）。This commits a double violation of RTA s.34. Where 6 months or more remain on a fixed-term tenancy, the landlord must not unreasonably withhold consent, and the landlord may never charge a fee for granting consent to an assignment or sublet (doing so constitutes an offence under s.95(1)(i)).

  2. ✓ 正确。根据 RTA s.34，住宅租客未经房东书面同意不得转让（assign）或转租（sublet）；但如果固定期租赁尚余 6 个月或以上，房东不得无理拒绝同意（must not unreasonably withhold consent），且任何时候都不得为同意收取费用。Correct. Under section 34 of the RTA, a residential tenant cannot assign or sublet without the landlord's written consent; however, if the fixed-term tenancy has 6 months or more remaining, the landlord must not unreasonably withhold consent, and the landlord cannot charge a fee for providing consent.

  3. ✗ 掉入「商业与住宅规则串台」陷阱。未明文禁止即无须房东同意是商业租赁的普通法规则；住宅租赁由 RTA s.34 明文规定一律须经房东书面同意。This falls into the trap of confusing commercial and residential rules. The rule that assignment or subletting is permitted without consent unless expressly prohibited applies to commercial tenancies at common law; residential tenancies strictly require written consent under RTA s.34.

  4. ✗ 再次把商业普通法规则套到了住宅上。住宅租赁下 RTA s.7(2) 明文课以双方减损损失义务（duty to mitigate），租客提前弃租的，房东仍须尝试以合理经济租金重新出租。This again misapplies commercial common law rules to a residential tenancy. Section 7(2) of the RTA imposes a statutory duty to mitigate losses on both parties; if a tenant abandons early, the landlord must make reasonable efforts to re-rent the unit at a fair economic rent.

答案Answer：b
