① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 情境分流题（去哪个门）：给出纠纷金额门槛（如 $5,000、$35,000、$50,000 或无上限）与案由（如一般小额债务、共管物业费、买卖违约、车祸或租客歧视），直接考该去 CRT、小额法庭、BC 最高法院还是 BC 人权裁判庭。Scenario-based forum routing questions: Given monetary dispute thresholds (such as $5,000, $35,000, $50,000, or unlimited) and causes of action (such as general small debts, strata fees, purchase contract breaches, motor vehicle accidents, or tenant discrimination), directly testing whether the dispute belongs in the CRT, Small Claims Court, the Supreme Court of British Columbia, or the BC Human Rights Tribunal.
  • 救济方式与优先顺位辨析题：用卖家毁约拒不过户或侵权场景，考普通法损害赔偿（damages）与衡平法强制履行（specific performance）/禁制令（injunction）的区别、冲突时谁优先，以及原告拖延起诉对衡平救济的影响。Remedies and priority analysis questions: Using scenarios such as seller breach of contract refusing title transfer or tort claims, testing the distinctions between common law damages and equitable remedies (specific performance and injunctions), which body of law prevails in a conflict, and the effect of unreasonable plaintiff delay on eligibility for equitable relief.
  • 先例与上诉机制概念题：考 stare decisis（遵循先例）的核心目的是可预测性、外省或外国判例仅有说服力（persuasive）而无约束力（binding）、法官如何通过区分事实（distinguish）避开旧先例，以及上诉法院只审法律问题不重听证据。Precedent and appellate mechanism concept questions: Testing that the core purpose of stare decisis is predictability, that out-of-province or foreign judicial decisions are merely persuasive rather than binding, how judges distinguish cases based on material factual differences to depart from prior precedents, and that appellate courts review only legal errors without re-hearing witness evidence.
  • 时效期计算与重置陷阱题：给出行为发生日、发现日与被告中途承认责任（部分还款或书面确认）的情形，考基本时效期（2 年，发现起算）、最长时效期（15 年，行为发生起算）以及承认责任后两项时效重新起算。Limitation period calculation and restart trap questions: Presenting scenarios with the date the act occurred, the date of discovery, and an intervening acknowledgment of liability by the defendant (partial payment or written confirmation), testing the 2-year basic limitation period (measured from discovery), the 15-year ultimate limitation period (measured from the act or omission), and how acknowledgment resets both limitation periods.
  • 立法权限与人权合规题：考地产法规（RESA、Property Law Act）归属省级「财产与公民权利」立法权、市级 bylaw 权力源于省授权而非宪法直授，以及《人权法》不以主观歧视意图为要件、租赁场景特有的「合法收入来源」受保护理由。Legislative authority and human rights compliance questions: Testing that real estate legislation (such as RESA and the Property Law Act) falls under provincial jurisdiction over property and civil rights, that municipal bylaw powers derive from provincial delegation rather than direct constitutional grants, that the Human Rights Code does not require subjective intent to discriminate, and that residential tenancy specifically protects lawful source of income as a prohibited ground of discrimination.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 先例必死套与跨省强制陷阱Rigid precedent application and out-of-province binding authority trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生误以为只要存在上级法院判例就必须机械照搬，或误以为外省上诉法院判例对 BC 下级法院有强制约束力，忽略了区分事实（distinguish）和跨省判例仅具说服力的规则。Candidates mistakenly assume that any prior appellate decision must be mechanically followed, or that an out-of-province Court of Appeal decision binds BC trial courts, ignoring the power to distinguish material facts and the rule that extra-provincial rulings are merely persuasive.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「must follow a decision from another province」或「cannot depart from an earlier precedent even when material facts differ」。The question stem states that a judge must follow a decision from another province or cannot depart from an earlier precedent even when material facts differ.

⚠ 衡平救济当成法定必给权利Treating equitable remedies as an automatic statutory entitlement

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生知道房产买卖可以申请强制履行（specific performance），就以为只要卖家违约、买家起诉就一定能拿到过户令，忽略了衡平救济属于法院自由裁量且拖延起诉会丧失资格。Candidates know specific performance can be sought in real estate purchases and assume an order transferring title is guaranteed upon seller breach, overlooking that equitable remedies are discretionary and unreasonable delay forfeits eligibility.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「automatic right to specific performance」或描述原告「waited an unreasonable length of time before seeking equitable relief」。The question stem mentions an automatic right to specific performance or describes a plaintiff who waited an unreasonable length of time before seeking equitable relief.

⚠ 上诉等于重打官司重新举证Assuming an appeal means a brand-new trial with fresh evidence

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 日常思维把上诉当成「不服就去上级法院把证人叫来再审一遍」，忘了除小额法庭获准在 BC 最高法院进行全新审理（trial de novo）外，上诉法院只审法律适用错误，不重听证人证据。Candidates confuse an appeal with retrying a case by calling witnesses before a higher court, forgetting that except for rare Small Claims appeals granted a trial de novo in the Supreme Court of British Columbia, appellate courts only review errors of law and do not re-hear witness testimony.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「Court of Appeal」「Supreme Court of Canada」并搭配「call new witnesses」「re-hear all evidence」。The question stem mentions Court of Appeal or Supreme Court of Canada paired with calling new witnesses or re-hearing all evidence.

⚠ 时效起算点混淆与承认责任重置陷阱Confusing limitation start dates and missing the acknowledgment restart trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把 2 年基本时效期错从「行为发生日」起算，或把 15 年最长时效期错从「发现日」起算，更容易漏看被告中途写信认账或部分付款会导致两项时效全部从承认日重新起算。Candidates incorrectly measure the 2-year basic limitation period from the date the act occurred, or measure the 15-year ultimate limitation period from discovery, and often miss that a defendant's written acknowledgment or partial payment restarts both periods from the acknowledgment date.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出「date the act occurred」「date of discovery」以及「written confirmation / partial payment」。The question stem provides the date the act occurred alongside the date of discovery and mentions written confirmation or partial payment.

⚠ 考试口径与现行规则错位陷阱Course manual exam standard versus current law misalignment trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: ©2020 考试资料写小额 CRT 不服提 Notice of Objection 到小额庭 trial de novo、共管物业裁决上诉到 BC 最高法院，且人权法受保护理由未列 Indigenous identity；实务已改为 60 天内司法审查且新增原住民身份，混用会选错考试标准答案。The (c)2020 course manual states that dissatisfied parties in CRT small claims file a Notice of Objection for a trial de novo in Small Claims Court and strata decisions are appealed to the Supreme Court of British Columbia, omitting Indigenous identity from the Human Rights Code; current practice requires judicial review within 60 days and includes Indigenous identity, so confusing the two leads to the wrong exam answer.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问「According to the course manual」涉及 CRT 不服救济路径或《Human Rights Code》受保护理由清单。The question stem asks According to the course manual regarding CRT appeal routes or the list of protected grounds under the Human Rights Code.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
遵循先例与区分事实Doctrine of Stare Decisis and Distinguishing Casesstare decisis / distinguish普通法靠遵循先例保障法律结果的可预测性；本省上诉法院与加拿大最高法院判例对下级法院有约束力，外省或外国判例仅有说服力，且若案件关键事实存在实质差异，法官可区分（distinguish）旧先例而不受其约束。Common law relies on stare decisis to ensure the predictability of legal outcomes. Decisions of the provincial Court of Appeal and the Supreme Court of Canada are binding on lower courts, whereas out-of-province or foreign decisions are merely persuasive. Furthermore, if the material facts of a case differ substantially, a judge may distinguish the prior precedent and not be bound by it.
衡平法优先与自由裁量救济Supremacy of Equity and Discretionary Remediesequity / specific performance / injunction普通法与衡平法冲突时衡平法优先；同一个法官既可判普通法损害赔偿（damages），也可自由裁量授予强制履行（specific performance）或禁制令（injunction），但原告若无理拖延起诉可能丧失衡平救济资格。Where common law and equity conflict, equitable principles prevail. Under the merged court system, the same judge may award either common law damages or discretionary equitable remedies such as specific performance or an injunction; however, a plaintiff who delays unreasonably in seeking relief may lose eligibility for equitable remedies.
三级政府立法权与成文法优先Three Levels of Legislative Authority and Primacy of Statute Lawstatute law / property and civil rights / residual power《宪法》（Constitution Act, 1867）将财产与公民权利划归省级立法权（地产法如 RESA 均属省法），剩余权力归联邦，市级 bylaw 权力源于省的授权；法官判案先看成文法，成文法未规定才看普通法。The Constitution Act, 1867 assigns property and civil rights to provincial jurisdiction (meaning real estate laws like RESA are provincial statutes), while residual power belongs to the federal government, and municipal bylaw authority derives entirely from provincial delegation. In adjudicating disputes, judges consult applicable statute law first and turn to common law only where statutes do not govern.
公法与私法的界限Distinction Between Public Law and Private Lawpublic law / private law私法处理个人间纠纷（合同、侵权、代理关系、不动产交易），是持牌人日常业务重心；公法涉及国家利益与政府监管（宪法、税法、刑法，以及 RESA 中关于牌照准入与违规处分的监管框架）。Private law resolves disputes between private individuals (such as contracts, torts, agency relationships, and real property transactions) and forms the core of a licensee's daily business. Public law involves the public interest and governmental administration (such as constitutional law, tax law, criminal law, and the regulatory framework under RESA governing licensing qualification and discipline).
初审法院门槛与上诉审理原则Trial Court Thresholds and Appellate Review PrinciplesSmall Claims Court / Supreme Court of BC / Court of Appeal小额法庭管辖 $35,000 或以下民事索赔且有地域限制，BC 最高法院无金额与省内地域限制；除小额法庭获准在 BC 最高法院全新审理（trial de novo）外，上诉法院只审法律问题、不重新听取证据。Small Claims Court has jurisdiction over civil claims of $35,000 or less subject to territorial limits, whereas the Supreme Court of British Columbia has no monetary cap and province-wide territorial jurisdiction. Except for rare Small Claims appeals granted a trial de novo in the BC Supreme Court, appellate courts review questions of law only and do not re-hear evidence.
CRT 管辖范围与金额门槛CRT Jurisdiction and Monetary ThresholdsCivil Resolution Tribunal (CRT)CRT 是线上行政裁判机构，管辖共管物业纠纷（无金额上限）、$5,000 或以下一般小额纠纷（强制管辖）、车祸伤害纠纷（责任与损害赔偿上限 $50,000）及现行新增的亲密影像纠纷，不受理诽谤、恶意起诉、涉政府/宪法及《人权法》独立投诉。The Civil Resolution Tribunal (CRT) is an online administrative tribunal with jurisdiction over strata property disputes (no monetary cap), tribunal small claims of $5,000 or less (mandatory jurisdiction), motor vehicle accident claims (capped at $50,000 for liability and damages), and intimate image disputes, but it has no jurisdiction over defamation, malicious prosecution, claims involving government or constitutional questions, or independent Human Rights Code complaints.
诉讼时效双轨制与承认责任重置Two-Tier Limitation Periods and Reset Upon Acknowledgmentbasic limitation period / ultimate limitation period / discoverability《Limitation Act》规定基本时效期为发现（discovery）之日起 2 年，最长时效期为行为发生之日起 15 年；一旦被告通过部分付款或书面确认承认责任，两项时效均从承认之日重新起算。Under the Limitation Act, the basic limitation period is 2 years from the date of discovery, and the ultimate limitation period is 15 years from the date the act or omission occurred. If the defendant acknowledges liability through partial payment or written confirmation, both the basic and ultimate limitation periods restart from the date of acknowledgment.
人权法无过错歧视与租赁专属理由Discrimination Without Proof of Intent and the Tenancy-Specific GroundHuman Rights Code / protected grounds / lawful source of income违反《人权法》构成歧视不以具备主观歧视意图为要件；租赁场景除种族、宗教、家庭状况、婚姻状况、身心障碍、年龄等理由外，额外保护「合法收入来源」（lawful source of income），独立人权投诉由 BC 人权裁判庭管辖。A violation of the Human Rights Code does not require proof of subjective intent to discriminate. In residential tenancy, in addition to grounds such as race, religion, family status, marital status, physical or mental disability, and age, lawful source of income is specifically protected, and independent discrimination complaints fall within the jurisdiction of the BC Human Rights Tribunal.
民事诉讼五步流程与举证标准Civil Litigation Stages and Standard of Proofpleadings / discovery / balance of probabilities / court costs民事诉讼依次经历书面诉状、证据开示、审理、判决与执行五个阶段，审理采用「盖然性权衡」（balance of probabilities）举证标准，胜诉方获得的诉讼费（court costs）仅覆盖部分而非全部律师费。Civil litigation proceeds through five successive stages: pleadings, discovery, trial, judgment, and enforcement. The trial applies the balance of probabilities standard of proof, and court costs awarded to a successful litigant cover only a portion, not the entirety, of actual legal expenses.
调解与仲裁及执行工具Mediation, Arbitration, and Judgment Enforcement Toolsmediation / arbitration / writ of execution / garnishing order当事人可依规例单方面发起强制对方到场的调解（mediation），或通过仲裁（arbitration）取得有约束力的裁决；判决后若败诉方拒不履行，可通过扣押拍卖动产（writ of execution）、登记不动产负担或申请扣薪令（garnishing order）强制执行。A party may initiate mandatory mediation under procedural rules to compel the opposing party to attend, or resolve disputes through binding arbitration. If a judgment debtor fails to pay, the judgment creditor can enforce payment through a writ of execution to seize and sell personal property, registering a charge against real property, or obtaining a garnishing order against wages or bank accounts.

第 1 题Q1遵循先例与区分事实Doctrine of Stare Decisis and Distinguishing Cases★★陷阱：先例必死套与跨省强制陷阱

A dispute over a rural property boundary is being heard in the Supreme Court of British Columbia. The defendant's lawyer relies on an older British Columbia Court of Appeal decision, while the plaintiff's lawyer points out that the older case involved a commercial port lease with materially different facts and also cites a recent Alberta Court of Appeal ruling. How does the doctrine of stare decisis apply here?

BC 最高法院正在审理一起乡村土地边界纠纷。被告律师依赖 BC 上诉法院的一项旧判例，而原告律师指出该旧案涉及商业港口租约、关键事实存在实质差异，并同时援引了阿尔伯塔省上诉法院近期的一项裁决。遵循先例（stare decisis）原则在此处应如何适用？

  1. ✓ 正确：遵循先例（stare decisis）旨在保证法律结果的可预测性，但适用先例的关键动作是区分（distinguish）——若旧案关键事实与本案存在实质差异，法官可以将其区分掉而不受约束；同时，外省上诉法院（Court of Appeal）的判决对本省下级法院仅有说服力（persuasive），没有强制约束力（binding）。Correct. The doctrine of stare decisis is designed to ensure predictability in legal outcomes, but its application requires comparing key facts: if the material facts of an earlier case differ from the current case, the judge may distinguish the precedent and not be bound by it. Furthermore, appellate decisions from another province are merely persuasive to a British Columbia trial court and carry no binding authority.

  2. ✗ 错在「先例必死套与跨省强制陷阱」：各省上诉法院（Court of Appeal）的判决只对本省下级法院有约束力，对其他省份的下级法院只有说服力但不约束，唯有加拿大最高法院（Supreme Court of Canada）的判决才对全国法院具有最高约束力。Incorrect, falling into the rigid precedent and out-of-province binding authority trap. Provincial Court of Appeal decisions are binding only on lower courts within their own province and are merely persuasive to courts in other provinces. Only decisions of the Supreme Court of Canada bind courts nationwide.

  3. ✗ 错在「先例必死套与跨省强制陷阱」：把「先例存在」直接等同于「先例必然适用」，忽略了法官在发现案件关键事实存在实质差异时，有权通过区分（distinguish）不受旧先例约束。Incorrect, falling into the rigid precedent trap by equating the mere existence of a precedent with its mandatory application, ignoring the judicial power to distinguish an earlier decision when material facts differ.

  4. ✗ 错在曲解普通法核心机制：遵循先例（stare decisis）是整个普通法（common law）体系保持可预测性的根基，广泛适用于民事与商业纠纷，绝非仅限于联邦刑事制定法。Incorrect, misconstruing the core mechanism of the common law. The doctrine of stare decisis is the foundation of predictability across the entire common law system, applying broadly to civil and commercial disputes rather than being restricted to federal criminal statutes.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Why does the common law system rely on the principle of stare decisis, and how should a British Columbia trial judge treat a judicial precedent from the United Kingdom when no Canadian precedent exists on the issue?

普通法体系为什么要依赖遵循先例（stare decisis）原则？当某一问题没有加拿大先例可循时，BC 初审法官应如何对待来自英国的司法判例？

  1. ✗ 错在颠倒遵循先例的目的及外国判例效力：遵循先例（stare decisis）是为了可预测性而非随意造法，且英国或其他普通法国家的判例本身对加拿大法院没有法律约束力。Incorrect, reversing the purpose of stare decisis and misstating the effect of foreign decisions. Stare decisis promotes certainty and predictability rather than arbitrary law-making, and judicial decisions from the United Kingdom or other common law jurisdictions carry no binding authority in Canadian courts.

  2. ✗ 错在混淆成文法与普通法的顺位：法官判案时先看有没有成文法（statute law），普通法先例不能凌驾于有效制定的省法之上。Incorrect, confusing the hierarchy of statute law and common law. Judges determine first whether applicable statute law exists, and common law precedents cannot override valid provincial legislation.

  3. ✓ 正确：遵循先例（stare decisis）的核心价值在于让人能够预判自己行为的法律后果（确定性与可预测性）；找不到加拿大先例时，法官可以参考英国或其他普通法联邦国家的判例，但这些判例本身没有法律约束力，除非被加拿大法院采纳。Correct. The central purpose of stare decisis is to enable parties to predict the legal consequences of their actions through certainty and predictability. In the absence of Canadian precedent, a judge may consult decisions from the United Kingdom or other common law jurisdictions for persuasive guidance, but such decisions carry no binding effect unless adopted by a Canadian court.

  4. ✗ 错在「先例必死套与跨省强制陷阱」：法官在适用先例时必须对比关键事实并区分（distinguish）实质差异，且无加拿大先例时可参考其他普通法国家判例而非直接驳回起诉。Incorrect, falling into the rigid precedent trap. Judges must compare material facts and distinguish substantial differences rather than ignoring them, and the absence of Canadian precedent permits consulting persuasive foreign common law decisions rather than dismissing the action.

第 2 题Q2衡平法救济与强制履行Equitable Remedies and Specific Performance★★陷阱：衡平救济当成法定必给权利

Marcus signed a binding contract to purchase a unique waterfront cottage from Elena. Elena changed her mind and refused to complete the transfer. Instead of suing promptly, Marcus waited a long and unreasonable time while watching the market before asking the court for an order of specific performance to force Elena to transfer the title. How is the court most likely to view Marcus's request for specific performance?

Marcus 签了一份有约束力的合同，向 Elena 购买一套独特的滨水度假屋。Elena 反悔并拒绝完成过户。Marcus 没有及时起诉，而是在观望市场、无理拖延了很长时间之后，才向法院申请强制履行令（specific performance），要求强迫 Elena 过户产权。法院最可能如何看待 Marcus 的强制履行请求？

  4. ✗ 错在编造前置程序：《Limitation Act》是全省民事索赔的默认时效法，统一适用 2 年基本时效与 15 年最长时效，60 天是 CRT 裁决申请司法审查的期限。Incorrect, fabricating a procedural prerequisite. The Limitation Act is the standard statute of limitations across the province applying the 2-year and 15-year rules; the 60-day deadline applies to filing a judicial review of a CRT decision rather than civil construction claims.

Under the British Columbia Limitation Act (in force since June 1, 2013), what is the length of the ultimate limitation period, and from what point in time is it measured if the defendant never acknowledges liability?

第 7 题Q7人权法受保护理由与租赁红线Human Rights Protected Grounds and Tenancy Compliance★★陷阱：考试口径与现行规则错位陷阱

A landlord instructs a licensed rental agent to turn away any prospective tenant whose income comes from government social assistance, explaining that the landlord has no prejudice against anyone and only wants tenants with traditional employment salaries. Which statement accurately describes the legal position under the British Columbia Human Rights Code?

一名房东指示持牌租赁经纪拒绝任何收入来自政府社会救济金的潜在租客，并解释说自己对任何人没有偏见，只是想要有传统工薪收入的租客。下列哪项陈述准确描述了 BC 省《人权法》（Human Rights Code）下的法律定性？

  1. ✓ 正确：《人权法》（Human Rights Code）在租赁场景（s.10）中明确将「合法收入来源」（lawful source of income）列为受保护理由（protected grounds），因申请人靠社会救济金或伤残津贴付房租而拒租即构成歧视；且第 2 条明定歧视不要求主观意图，独立投诉由 BC 人权裁判庭（BC Human Rights Tribunal）受理。注意 2021-11-25 生效的修法还把原住民身份（Indigenous identity）加入了现行受保护理由清单（©2020 考试资料尚未收录该新增项）。Correct. The Human Rights Code expressly includes lawful source of income as a protected ground in tenancy under section 10, meaning refusing an applicant whose rent is paid through social or disability assistance constitutes discrimination. Furthermore, section 2 provides that proof of subjective intent is not required, and independent complaints are heard by the BC Human Rights Tribunal. Note that amendments effective November 25, 2021, added Indigenous identity to the current list of protected grounds, which was not yet included in the (c)2020 course manual.

  2. ✗ 错在误以为需要主观恶意：《人权法》（Human Rights Code）第 2 条明文规定，构成违法歧视并不要求行为人具有歧视的主观意图（intent）。Incorrect, assuming subjective malice is required. Section 2 of the Human Rights Code explicitly states that establishing discrimination does not require proof of discriminatory intent.

  3. ✗ 错在记反适用条款：「合法收入来源」（lawful source of income）恰恰是租赁场景（s.10）比一般住宿/服务（s.8）和购房场景额外多出的受保护理由。Incorrect, reversing statutory provisions. Lawful source of income is specifically added as a protected ground in residential tenancy under section 10, distinguishing it from general accommodation or real estate purchase provisions.

  4. ✗ 错在把人权投诉塞进 CRT：CRT 一般不受理《人权法》（Human Rights Code）本身的独立投诉，这类投诉走的是独立于 CRT 与法院之外的 BC 人权裁判庭（BC Human Rights Tribunal）。Incorrect, directing human rights complaints to the CRT. The CRT does not handle independent complaints under the Human Rights Code; such matters must be filed with the BC Human Rights Tribunal.
