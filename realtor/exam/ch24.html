本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 入表位置题：定金写明付进买方经纪行的信托账户，问它在哪一张调整表、记借还是记贷。真正考的是卖方没收到的钱不进卖方表，买方已经付出的定金是贷方。Statement-placement questions: the deposit is stated as paid into the buyer's brokerage trust account, and the question asks which statement of adjustments it enters and whether it is a debit or a credit. What is tested is that money the seller has not received does not enter the seller's statement, and that a deposit the buyer has already paid is a credit.
  • 融资落表题：新银行按揭、卖方清偿旧贷、卖方自贷、买方承接四种事实放在一起。真正考的是只有同一笔钱同时改变双方现金，才会同时出现在两张表上。Financing-entry questions: a new bank mortgage, the seller paying off an old loan, a loan made by the seller, and the buyer assuming a loan are set out together. What is tested is that the same money appears on both statements only when it changes both parties' cash at the same time.
  • 地税方向题：全年已缴、尚未到期由买方缴、已到期未缴三种事实换着出。真正考的是谁垫了钱，买方应摊的那段记在谁的借方或贷方，而且买方承担调整日当天。Property-tax direction questions: tax paid for the whole year, tax not yet due that the buyer will pay, and tax already due but unpaid are rotated. What is tested is who advanced the money, whose debit or credit carries the buyer's share, and that the buyer bears the adjustment date itself.
  • 版本题：题干写执照考试或课程原文，问文件包、时间线、佣金如何用定金抵。真正考的是按考试口径作答，不把透明度申报、撤销期或法定赚取日写进卷面。Version questions: the stem says licensing exam or course manual, and asks about the document package, the timeline, or how commission is offset from the deposit. What is tested is answering on the exam wording, and not writing the transparency declaration, the rescission period, or the statutory date commission is earned onto the answer.
  • 程序题：物业转让税豁免了还要不要申报、住过的房子有没有消费税、钥匙能不能提前交。真正考的是免税不等于免申报，二手住宅转售一般没有消费税，占有日严格照合同。Procedure questions: whether a property transfer tax exemption still requires a return, whether a lived-in house has GST, and whether the keys may be handed over early. What is tested is that an exemption is not an exemption from filing, that a resale of a lived-in home generally has no GST, and that the possession date follows the contract strictly.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 定金当成卖方已收Deposit treated as received by the seller

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 定金最终构成价款，卖方调整表上又有佣金从所得里扣，于是把定金写进卖方表。钱还在买方经纪行时，卖方从来没有收到。The deposit ultimately forms part of the price, and the seller's statement of adjustments also deducts commission from the proceeds, so the deposit is written onto the seller's statement. While the money is still at the buyer's brokerage, the seller has never received it.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写明定金付给买方经纪行、存在信托账户，问卖方调整表上有没有这一行。The stem states that the deposit was paid to the buyer's brokerage and is held in the trust account, and asks whether that line appears on the seller's statement of adjustments.

⚠ 会计借贷套调整表Accounting debit applied to the statement

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 会计课里借方像是资产增加，看到 Debit 就当成这一方收到钱。调整表上借方是这一方欠出去的钱，方向正好相反。In an accounting class a debit looks like an increase in assets, so Debit is read as money this party received. On the statement of adjustments a debit is money this party owes out, and the direction is the opposite.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问某一笔是增加还是减少买方要带去的现金，或增加还是减少卖方能收到的现金。The stem asks whether an item increases or decreases the cash the buyer must bring, or increases or decreases the cash the seller can receive.

⚠ 两边都要入表Both statements must show it

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 同一笔交易里的钱，直觉上买卖双方各记一笔。买方新借的按揭只改变买方的钱从哪来，卖方不关心，所以不进卖方表。Money in one transaction feels as if each of the buyer and the seller records an entry. A mortgage the buyer newly borrows only changes where the buyer's money comes from. The seller does not care, so it does not enter the seller's statement.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写的是买方向银行新借按揭，或卖方用自己的所得清偿自己的旧按揭，问另一张表要不要出现。The stem is a new mortgage the buyer borrows from a bank, or the seller paying off the seller's own old mortgage from the seller's own proceeds, and asks whether the other statement must show it.

⚠ 用现行法答考试题Current law used on an exam question

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 透明度申报、三个营业日撤销期、佣金在提交登记之日赚到，柜台上都是真的。课程原文的清单和时点仍是考试要选的那一版。The transparency declaration, the three-business-day rescission period, and commission earned on the day the transfer is submitted for registration are all true at the counter. The list and the timing in the course text are still the version the exam wants.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写 licensing exam 或 course manual，问文件包、时间线，或佣金按什么授权从定金里抵。The stem says licensing exam or course manual, and asks about the document package, the timeline, or what authority lets commission be offset from the deposit.

⚠ 免税当成免申报Exemption treated as no filing

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 首次购房或新房豁免把税额减到零，看起来就不用交表。申请登记时申报表照报，只是在表上选豁免。A first-time buyer exemption or a new-home exemption reduces the tax to zero, so it looks as if no return is filed. When registration is applied for, the return is still filed. The exemption is only selected on the return.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写买方符合某项物业转让税豁免，问提交转让时还要不要报送申报表。The stem states that the buyer qualifies for a property transfer tax exemption, and asks whether a return must still be filed when the transfer is submitted.

A buyer's debit increases the cash due on completion, and a seller's debit reduces the net proceeds. The direction is the opposite of an accounting class.

When the tax is not yet due and the buyer will pay it, the direction is reversed. The contract makes the buyer responsible for the adjustment date itself.

On the exam wording, the deposit may be paid out only in statutory cases or under both parties' written agreement.
定金和佣金是两笔钱The deposit and the commission are two sumsCommission定金是买方按买卖合同付的价款，佣金是卖方按上市合同欠的报酬；考试口径按上市合同授权用定金抵佣金，多出的转回交割，不足从卖方所得补。The deposit is price the buyer pays under the contract of purchase and sale. Commission is remuneration the seller owes under the listing contract. On the exam wording, the listing contract authorizes offsetting commission from the deposit. Any excess is carried back into the completion figures, and any shortfall is made up from the seller's proceeds.
考试文件包没有透明度申报The exam package has no transparency declarationForm A Freehold Transfer考试口径的交割文件包是卖方签署的 Form A、双方签署的两张调整表、买方的物业转让税申报表、必要时的 Form F 和 Form B，以及买方补足款。On the exam wording, the completion package is the Form A signed by the seller, both statements of adjustments signed by the parties, the buyer's property transfer tax return, Form F and Form B where needed, and the buyer's funds to complete.
免税仍然要报送申报表An exemption still requires the returnProperty Transfer Tax申请登记应税交易时必须报送物业转让税申报表，无论是否免税；电子提交的申请必须电子缴税、电子申报。When registration of a taxable transaction is applied for, a property transfer tax return must be filed whether or not the transaction is exempt. An application submitted electronically must pay the tax electronically and file the return electronically.

The keys may be delivered only on the possession date in the contract. Handing them over one day early can be a discipline matter.

第 1 题Q1定金不进卖方表The deposit stays off the seller's statement★★陷阱：定金当成卖方已收

Nora is buying a house from Sam. She paid the deposit to her own brokerage, and the money is still in that brokerage's trust account. Sam has never received it. For the licensing exam, how does this deposit appear on the statements of adjustments?

Nora 向 Sam 买房。定金付给了她自己的经纪行，现在仍在该经纪行的信托账户里。Sam 从来没有收到这笔钱。就执照考试而言，这笔定金在调整表上怎么出现？

  1. ✗ 这是定金当成卖方已收。钱还在买方经纪行的信托账户里，卖方没有收过。卖方调整表算的是交割那天能拿到的现金，拿不到的钱不进卖方表，也不能只记在卖方的贷方。This treats the deposit as already received by the seller. The money is still in the buyer's brokerage trust account, and the seller has not received it. The seller's statement of adjustments calculates the cash the seller can receive on the completion day. Money the seller cannot receive does not enter the seller's statement, and it cannot be entered only as a credit to the seller.

  2. ✓ 定金（deposit）付给买方经纪行并存在信托账户时，只作为买方调整表的贷方（credit），减少买方交割还要拿出的现金。卖方从来没有收到，所以卖方表上没有这一行。When the deposit is paid to the buyer's brokerage and held in the trust account, it is a credit on the buyer's statement of adjustments only, and it reduces the cash the buyer must still bring to completion. The seller has never received it, so the seller's statement has no such line.

  3. ✗ 定金直接付给卖方时，才是买方贷方、卖方借方。这里钱在买方经纪行，不是在卖方手里。两边各记一笔，是两边都要入表，用错了定金的存放位置。A credit to the buyer and a debit to the seller apply only when the deposit is paid directly to the seller. Here the money is at the buyer's brokerage, not in the seller's hands. Entering it once on each side puts it on both statements, and it uses the wrong place where the deposit is held.

  4. ✗ 这是会计借贷套调整表。买方已经付过的定金是贷方，减少她交割还要拿出的现金。借方才是这一方欠出去的钱，不会因为付了定金而增加买方的补足款。This applies an accounting debit to the statement of adjustments. A deposit the buyer has already paid is a credit, and it reduces the cash she must still bring to completion. A debit is money this party owes out. Paying the deposit does not increase the buyer's funds to complete.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Victor paid the deposit directly to the seller, Helen, under the contract. The money is not in any brokerage trust account, and Helen has already received it. For the licensing exam, how does that deposit appear on the statements of adjustments?

Victor 按合同把定金直接付给了卖方 Helen。这笔钱不在任何经纪行的信托账户里，Helen 已经收到了。就执照考试而言，这笔定金在调整表上怎么出现？

  1. ✗ 这是把经纪行托管的规则套到了已经直接付给卖方的定金上。卖方已经收到价款的一部分，交割当天要少收这一截，卖方表必须记借方，不能整行消失。This applies the brokerage-stakeholder rule to a deposit already paid directly to the seller. The seller has already received part of the price and will receive that much less on the completion day. The seller's statement must show a debit. The whole line cannot disappear.

  2. ✗ 这是会计借贷套调整表。买方已经付出的定金是贷方，减少他还要带去的现金，不是借方。只记买方借方，也漏掉了卖方已经收到这一事实。This applies an accounting debit to the statement of adjustments. A deposit the buyer has already paid is a credit. It reduces the cash he must still bring. It is not a debit. Entering only a debit to the buyer also misses the fact that the seller has already received the money.

  3. ✗ 卖方已经收到的定金不会增加她交割当天还能拿到的现金，所以不是卖方贷方。她少收这一截，应记借方。这是把已收价款的方向记反了。A deposit the seller has already received does not increase the cash she can still receive on the completion day, so it is not a credit to the seller. She receives that much less, so it is a debit. This records price already received in the wrong direction.

  4. ✓ 定金直接付给卖方时，买方调整表记贷方（credit），卖方调整表记借方（debit）。这和存在买方经纪行、卖方从未收到的情形正好相反。When the deposit is paid directly to the seller, the buyer's statement of adjustments shows a credit and the seller's statement of adjustments shows a debit. That is the opposite of a deposit held at the buyer's brokerage that the seller has never received.

答案Answer：d

第 2 题Q2新按揭只进买方表A new mortgage enters the buyer's statement only★★★陷阱：两边都要入表

Priya is buying from Owen. Her bank will advance a new mortgage to Priya alone and will pay that advance to her conveyancer. Owen is not lending any of the price, and Priya is not taking over Owen's loan. For the licensing exam, where does Priya's new mortgage appear?

Priya 向 Owen 买房。她的银行只向 Priya 发放一笔新按揭，并把放款付给她的过户人。Owen 没有出借价款，Priya 也没有承接 Owen 的贷款。就执照考试而言，Priya 的新按揭记在哪里？

  1. ✗ 这是两边都要入表。两张调整表各自配平，不是用同一笔贷款彼此对冲。买方新借的第三方按揭只改变买方的现金来源，卖方不关心，卖方表不出现。This puts the item on both statements. Each statement of adjustments balances on its own. The two statements do not offset each other with the same loan. A new third-party mortgage the buyer borrows only changes the source of the buyer's cash. The seller does not care, and the seller's statement does not show it.

  2. ✗ 卖方表上的按揭借方，是卖方自己要在交割时清偿的旧按揭。买方新借的贷款不会减少卖方能收到的现金，也不记在卖方贷方。这是把清偿旧贷和新借款混成了一笔。A mortgage debit on the seller's statement is the seller's own old mortgage that the seller must pay off on completion. A new loan the buyer borrows does not reduce the cash the seller can receive, and it is not a credit to the seller. This mixes paying off an old loan with a new borrowing.

  3. ✗ 方向和位置都错了。新按揭对买方是贷方，减少她还要拿出的现金，不是借方；卖方表不出现这一行。这是会计借贷套调整表，再加上两边都要入表。Both the direction and the place are wrong. A new mortgage is a credit to the buyer. It reduces the cash she must still bring. It is not a debit. The seller's statement does not show this line. This applies an accounting debit to the statement and also puts the item on both statements.

  4. ✓ 买方向银行等第三方新借按揭，只在买方调整表记贷方（credit），金额是贷款额。卖方不关心买方的钱从哪来，所以卖方表不入这一笔。只有同一笔钱同时改变双方现金时，才会出现在两张表上。A new mortgage the buyer borrows from a bank or another third party is a credit on the buyer's statement of adjustments only, in the amount of the loan. The seller does not care where the buyer's money comes from, so the seller's statement does not include this item. An item appears on both statements only when the same money changes both parties' cash at once.

答案Answer：d

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Owen still owes his own lender. On completion that loan will be paid from Owen's sale proceeds, together with the lender's discharge fee. Priya's new loan is a separate advance from her own bank. For the licensing exam, where does the payout of Owen's old loan appear?

Owen 自己的贷款还没还清。交割时要从他的售房所得里清偿，并付给放款人解除费。Priya 的新贷款是她自己的银行另外发放的。就执照考试而言，清偿 Owen 的旧贷款记在哪里？

  1. ✓ 卖方现有按揭在交割时清偿，只在卖方调整表记借方（debit），包括未偿余额和解除费等。买方不关心卖方在拿走所得之前先还掉自己的贷款，买方表不入这一笔。The seller's existing mortgage, paid off on completion, is a debit on the seller's statement of adjustments only, including the outstanding balance, the discharge fee, and similar amounts. The buyer does not care that the seller pays off the seller's own loan before taking the proceeds. The buyer's statement does not include this item.

  2. ✗ 买方新借的按揭才记买方贷方。卖方清偿自己的旧贷，不减少买方要带去的现金，也不进买方表。这是把两笔贷款换成了一笔。Only a new mortgage the buyer borrows is a credit to the buyer. The seller paying off the seller's own old loan does not reduce the cash the buyer must bring, and it does not enter the buyer's statement. This turns two loans into one.

  4. ✗ 考试口径的告诫正好相反：即使写在同一天也不该用中午，两个日期不同时更不能在交割日中午交钥匙。占有只能发生在合同约定的占有日。这是提前交钥匙。The exam warning says the opposite. Noon should not be used even when the two dates are written as the same day, and the keys still cannot be handed over at noon on the completion date when the dates differ. Possession can occur only on the possession date set in the contract. This is handing over the keys early.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

One client wants to sell a home in the morning and buy the next home that same afternoon. The draft sets possession at noon on the completion date for both contracts. For the licensing exam, which drafting choice fits the course warning?

同一个客户想在上午卖掉一套房子，并在同一天下午买下一套。两份草稿都把占有写成交割日的中午。就执照考试而言，哪一种写法符合课程的告诫？

  1. ✗ 交割程序本身要跑好几个小时，同一天占有已经太紧，写成中午更不符合考试口径。中午不是安全时点。这是把占有和交割叠在一起。The completion process itself takes several hours. Possession on the same day is already too tight, and writing noon fits the exam wording even less. Noon is not a safe time. This stacks possession on completion.

  2. ✓ 交割日、调整日、占有日要分开想，占有日不早于交割日，也不该与交割日同一天，更不该在占有条款里写中午。同一个客户上午卖、下午买排在同一天，是给交割挖坑。占有日仍然必须严格照合同执行。The completion date, the adjustment date, and the possession date are kept separate. The possession date is not earlier than the completion date, it should not be the same day as completion, and the possession clause should not say noon. Scheduling one client's morning sale and afternoon purchase on the same day digs a hole under completion. The possession date must still be followed strictly as the contract writes it.

  3. ✗ 上午交钥匙，等于占有早于交割完成。定金付清也不改变占有日。这是提前交钥匙。Handing over the keys in the morning means possession before completion is finished. Paying the deposit in full does not change the possession date. This is handing over the keys early.

  4. ✗ 三个日期不能合成一个，买方也不能靠一句放弃来换取提前搬入。占有日必须严格照合同执行，提前交钥匙可以被纪律追究。The three dates cannot be collapsed into one, and the buyer cannot trade one waiver sentence for an early move-in. The possession date must be followed strictly as the contract writes it. Handing over the keys early can be a discipline matter.
