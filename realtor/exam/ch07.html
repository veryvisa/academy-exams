本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 表决门槛辨析题：给出预算批准、修订章程、指定限定共用财产（LCP）、动用应急储备金（CRF）或清盘等具体议案，考过半数、¾ 票、80% 票与全体一致的对应关系及分母差异。Voting threshold questions: testing majority, 3/4 vote, 80% vote, and unanimous vote requirements and denominator differences for specific resolutions such as budget approval, bylaw amendments, designating limited common property (LCP), contingency reserve fund (CRF) expenditures, and winding up.
  • 单位权益（unit entitlement）与特别摊派计算题：给全楼总单位权益、某单元权益及特别摊派总额求该户分摊额，或结合成交日切分卖方与买方的分期付款责任。Unit entitlement and special levy calculation questions: calculating an owner's share given total unit entitlement, the lot's entitlement, and the total special levy, or allocating installment liability between seller and buyer based on the completion date.
  • 考试口径与现行法对照题：围绕 CRF 年度强制缴款（旧规 25% 门槛 vs 现行每年至少 10%）、折旧报告（旧规 3 年可豁免 vs 现行 5 年不可豁免）及出租限制章程考考试口径答案。Exam manual vs. current law comparison questions: asking for the exam answer while noting current rules regarding mandatory annual CRF contributions (traditional 25% threshold vs. current minimum 10% annually), depreciation reports (former 3-year waivable cycle vs. current 5-year non-waivable cycle), and rental restriction bylaws.
  • 交割文件与欠款追偿题：区分信息证明书（Form B）的披露事项与付清证明书（Form F）的过户闸门作用，常考未付章程罚款不能登记留置权但可扣发 Form F。Closing documents and debt recovery questions: distinguishing between disclosures in the Information Certificate (Form B) and the closing gatekeeper role of the Certificate of Payment (Form F), frequently testing that unpaid bylaw fines cannot be secured by a lien but permit withholding Form F.
  • 产权形态与合作社（cooperative）对比题：考车位四种法律形态的转让后果，以及房屋合作社买股份加长期租约、董事会审批买家、不适用《住宅租赁法》的核心差异。Property ownership types and cooperative comparison questions: testing the transfer consequences of the four legal forms of parking stalls, and key legal distinctions of housing cooperatives including shares plus a long-term lease, board approval of buyers, and exemption from the Residential Tenancy Act.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 80% 票与 ¾ 票的分母混淆Confusing denominators of 80% vote and 3/4 vote

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为所有高门槛决议都只按到场（含委托）投票的业主计算，忽略了清盘的 80% 票以全体有投票权者为分母，缺席即等于反对。Mistakenly assuming all high-threshold resolutions are calculated based solely on voters present in person or by proxy, overlooking that an 80% vote for winding up uses all eligible voters as the denominator, so an absence acts as a vote against.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出全体有资格投票的业主总数与实际出席大会的人数，或议案涉及法团清盘（winding up）与注销图则。The question stem provides both the total number of eligible voters and the number of voters attending the meeting, or involves winding up the strata corporation and cancelling the strata plan.

⚠ 考试口径与 2023–2024 新规错位Conflating exam manual rules with 2023-2024 amendments

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把现行法（CRF 每年至少缴 10% 无上限、折旧报告 5 年一次不可豁免、长租限制废止）直接套进考考试资料旧规的题干，或反之。Applying current rules (mandatory CRF contribution of at least 10% with no cap, 5-year non-waivable depreciation report, abolishment of long-term rental limits) to questions testing course manual rules, or vice versa.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 course manual、traditional 25% threshold、3-year depreciation report cycle 等字样，或明确指明按现行 SPA 作答。The stem mentions course manual, traditional 25% threshold, 3-year depreciation report cycle, or specifies answering under the current Strata Property Act.

⚠ 罚款登记留置权（Certificate of Lien）陷阱Filing a Certificate of Lien for fines

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为业主欠法团的任何款项都能登记优先留置权，不知道章程与规则罚款及整改费用不在 SPA s.116 留置权范围内。Assuming any debt owed to the strata corporation allows filing a priority lien, not realizing that bylaw or rule fines and remediation costs fall outside the scope of a Section 116 lien.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问法团对拒不缴纳章程罚款（unpaid bylaw fines）的业主能否登记留置权，或问如何在产权过户环节拦截该业主。The stem asks whether the strata corporation can register a lien against an owner who refuses to pay bylaw fines, or how to intercept that owner during title transfer.

⚠ 车位四种法律形态混同Confusing the four legal forms of parking stalls

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把共用财产上的短期专用许可（short-term exclusive use）或限定共用财产（LCP）当成可单独出售的独立产权单元。Treating short-term exclusive use permission on common property or limited common property (LCP) as an individually sellable, separate title strata lot.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 parking stall 或 storage locker，问能否单独卖给外人、最长许可期（1 年或 EV 车位 5 年）或是否随单元转让。The stem features a parking stall or storage locker, asking if it can be sold separately to an outsider, its maximum permission term (1 year or 5 years for EV), or whether it transfers with title.

⚠ 合作社（Co-op）套用 Strata 或 RTA 规则Applying Strata or RTA rules to a housing cooperative

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到多户公寓外观就按 strata 的完全保有地产（fee simple）作答，或把合作社与会员的租约纠纷交给住宅租赁处与 CRT 共管物业部处理。Assuming a multi-family building is a fee simple strata, or referring co-op lease disputes to the Residential Tenancy Branch or CRT strata division.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 cooperative association、shares、long-term lease、board approval of buyer 或会员因欠费被终止会籍的上诉权。The stem mentions cooperative association, shares, long-term lease, board approval of buyer, or a member's right of appeal upon termination of membership for non-payment.

第 1 题Q1共用财产专用与车位四种形态Exclusive Use of Common Property and Four Legal Forms of Parking Stalls★★陷阱：车位四种法律形态混同

A strata corporation wishes to grant an owner the exclusive right to use a standard surface parking stall located on common property. If the strata corporation does NOT amend the strata plan and does NOT pass a 3/4 vote to designate the stall as limited common property (LCP), what is the maximum duration for which short-term exclusive use of this non-EV stall may be granted under Section 76 of the Strata Property Act?

某共管物业法团希望授予一名业主排他使用位于共用财产上的一个普通地面车位的权利。如果法团既不修改共管物业图则，也不通过 ¾ 票将该车位指定为限定共用财产（LCP），根据《共管物业法》第 76 条，该非电动车充电车位的短期专用许可最长期限是多久？

  1. ✓ 根据 SPA s.76，共用财产（common property）上非 EV 充电车位的短期专用许可（short-term exclusive use）最长期限为 1 年，期满可续、可附条件，且法团可在给予合理通知后撤销。Under Section 76 of the Strata Property Act, short-term exclusive use permission for non-EV parking stalls on common property may be granted for a maximum term of up to 1 year, subject to renewal and conditions, and may be cancelled by the strata corporation on reasonable notice.

  2. ✗ 最长 5 年仅适用于业主依 SPA s.90.1 申请安装电动车充电设施获批后的 EV 充电车位专用（条例 s.5.101），普通车位短期专用最长只有 1 年，这是车位形态与期限混淆陷阱。The maximum term of up to 5 years applies only to exclusive use granted for EV charging stalls after an owner's request under Section 90.1 is approved (Strata Property Regulation s.5.101); short-term exclusive use for standard parking stalls is capped at 1 year.

  3. ✗ 永久且随单元走（runs with the land）的是限定共用财产（limited common property, LCP），短期专用许可随时可在合理通知后收回，绝不随产权转让。A designation that is permanent and runs with the land applies to limited common property (LCP); short-term exclusive use permission can be revoked on reasonable notice at any time and does not run with title transfer.

  4. ✗ 60 天是付清证明书（Form F）的有效期及理赔款决定不修复的期限，与共用财产短期专用许可无关。The 60-day period is the statutory validity period of a Certificate of Payment (Form F) and the deadline to decide not to repair damaged property after an insurance payout; it has nothing to do with short-term exclusive use of common property.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A seller tells his listing licensee that his apartment comes with two parking stalls that 'belong to the unit forever.' Upon checking the strata plan and Land Title Office filings, the licensee discovers that Stall 12 is designated as limited common property (LCP) for the seller's strata lot, while Stall 45 is common property used under a permission from the strata council. Which statement accurately describes how these stalls can be dealt with in a sale?

一位卖家告诉他的挂牌持牌人，他的公寓带有两个“永久属于该单元”的车位。经核查共管物业图则与土地产权登记处备案文件，持牌人发现 12 号车位被指定为卖方单元的限定共用财产（LCP），而 45 号车位是凭议会许可使用的共用财产。下列哪项陈述准确描述了这两个车位在买卖中的处理方式？

  1. ✗ 限定共用财产（LCP）和短期专用（short-term exclusive use）都不是独立的共管物业单元（strata lot），不能单独转让完全保有地产产权。Neither limited common property (LCP) nor short-term exclusive use constitutes an independent strata lot; neither can be separately transferred by conveying a fee simple title.

  2. ✓ 限定共用财产（limited common property, LCP）随单元走（runs with the land）但不能单独拆卖；而议会许可的短期专用（short-term exclusive use）最长 1 年（EV 车位 5 年），法团可合理通知后收回，若在挂盘文案中直接写成含两个永久车位将构成失实陈述。Limited common property (LCP) runs with the strata lot to the buyer but cannot be sold separately; short-term exclusive use granted by council lasts up to 1 year (5 years for EV stalls) and may be cancelled by the strata corporation on reasonable notice. Advertising both as permanently belonging to the unit constitutes misrepresentation.

  3. ✗ 已备案的限定共用财产（LCP）自动随单元转让给买方使用，无需在年度大会上重新进行全体一致投票（unanimous vote）。Registered limited common property (LCP) automatically transfers with the strata lot for the buyer's use; no new unanimous vote at an annual general meeting is needed to confirm the transfer.

  4. ✗ 这把限定共用财产（LCP，永久随单元走）与短期专用许可（最长 1 年、可撤销）的法律后果完全颠倒了。This completely reverses the legal consequences of limited common property (LCP, which permanently runs with the unit) and short-term exclusive use permission (which lasts up to 1 year and is revocable).

答案Answer：b

第 2 题Q2清盘80%票与¾票的分母差异Denominator Differences Between 80% Winding-up Vote and 3/4 Vote★★★陷阱：80% 票与 ¾ 票的分母混淆

An aging strata building has 100 strata lots, each entitled to one vote. At a duly called Special General Meeting (with 4 weeks' written notice), 80 eligible voters attend in person or by proxy and all 80 cast valid votes—none abstain. If 70 of those voters vote in favour, what is the outcome for (1) a resolution to amend the bylaws, and (2) a resolution to wind up the strata corporation and cancel the strata plan?

一栋老旧共管物业共有 100 个单元，每个单元享有一票投票权。在一次合法召开（已给足 4 周书面通知）的特别业主大会上，有 80 名有资格投票者亲自或委托出席，且 80 人全部投出有效票（无人弃权）。如果有 70 票赞成，对于（1）修订章程的决议，以及（2）清盘法团并注销图则的决议，结果分别是什么？

  1. ✗ 这是最典型的分母混淆陷阱：清盘的 80% 票分母是全体 100 名有投票权者（70 ÷ 100 = 70% < 80%），而不是到场投票的 80 人。This is a classic denominator confusion trap: the denominator for an 80% winding-up vote is all 100 eligible voters (70 / 100 = 70% < 80%), not the 80 voters present at the meeting.

  2. ✓ 虽然章程罚款（bylaw fines）不能登记留置权（Certificate of Lien），但法团可以拒绝出具付清证明书（Form F, Certificate of Payment，收费 $15、有效期 60 天）直至罚款付清或达成满意安排；而除法院命令出售外，没有现行 Form F 土地产权登记处不予办理过户登记（SPA s.256）。Although bylaw fines cannot be secured by a Certificate of Lien, the strata corporation may withhold a Certificate of Payment (Form F, fee $15, valid for 60 days) until fines are paid or satisfactory arrangements made; without a valid Form F, the registrar of the Land Title Office will refuse to register the transfer (SPA s.256).

  3. ✗ 《时效法》（Limitation Act）的一般债务时效是 2 年而非 60 天，且 BC 法院与 CRT 裁决表明两年时效并不适用于罚款的催收；法团有权因欠罚款扣发 Form F。The general limitation period under the Limitation Act is 2 years rather than 60 days, and BC court and CRT decisions indicate that the two-year period does not apply to collecting fines; the strata corporation may withhold Form F for unpaid fines.

  4. ✗ 法团无权因业主欠缴罚款而注销其单位权益或没收其独立单元。A strata corporation has no authority to cancel an owner's unit entitlement or forfeit a strata lot due to unpaid bylaw fines.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A buyer's licensee orders a Form B (Information Certificate) during the subject-removal period. Which statement accurately describes the statutory rules governing Form B under the Strata Property Act?

买方持牌人在条件解除期内申请了一份 Form B（信息证明书）。下列哪项陈述准确描述了《共管物业法》关于 Form B 的法定规则？

  1. ✗ 收费上限 $15、有效期 60 天、过户登记必备文件是付清证明书（Form F, Certificate of Payment），而非信息证明书（Form B）。The maximum fee of $15, 60-day validity period, and mandatory status for title registration apply to a Certificate of Payment (Form F), not an Information Certificate (Form B).

  2. ✗ 虽然 Form B 上的信息原则上对法团有约束力（SPA s.59(5)），但 SPA s.59(5.1) 明文规定法团保险摘要（summary of insurance coverage）若来自保险人或保险代理人，则不对法团产生约束力。While information in Form B is generally binding on the strata corporation under Section 59(5), Section 59(5.1) explicitly states that the summary of insurance coverage provided by an insurer or insurance agent is not binding on the strata corporation.

  3. ✗ Form B 不仅披露已批准的特别摊派，还必须披露「已发出通知但尚未表决的 ¾ 票、80% 票、全体一致决议或章程修订议案」，这是尽职调查中最关键的预警格。Form B discloses not only approved special levies, but also any resolutions requiring a 3/4 vote, 80% vote, or unanimous vote that have been noticed but not yet voted on, serving as a critical early warning during buyer due diligence.

  4. ✓ 信息证明书（Form B, Information Certificate）须在 1 周内出具（考试口径旧值为 8 天），收费上限 $35 加每页复印费不超过 25 分；必须披露月物业费、欠款、已发通知尚未表决的高门槛议案及保险摘要，并附上规则、当期预算、最近一份折旧报告及已取得的电力规划报告。An Information Certificate (Form B) must be issued within 1 week (formerly 8 days under exam rules) for a fee of up to $35 plus up to 25 cents per page; it must disclose strata fees, arrears, unvoted noticed high-threshold resolutions, and insurance summaries, with attached rules, current budget, the latest depreciation report, and any electrical planning report.

答案Answer：d

第 7 题Q7房屋合作社与Strata的法律差异Legal Differences Between Housing Cooperatives and Strata Corporations★★陷阱：合作社（Co-op）套用 Strata 或 RTA 规则

A buyer is comparing a fee simple strata apartment with a unit in a residential housing cooperative incorporated under the Cooperative Association Act. How does purchasing and financing the cooperative unit differ legally from purchasing the strata lot?

一位买家正在比较一套完全保有地产的共管物业公寓与一套依《合作社协会法》设立的住宅合作社单元。购买和融资合作社单元在法律上与购买共管物业单元有何不同？

  1. ✓ 合作社（cooperative）持有整块土地的产权或长租权，买家买到的是协会股份（shares）外加长期承租某单元的租约，而非单元的完全保有地产（fee simple）；转让通常须经合作社董事会（board of directors）批准买家（理由未必限于财务能力），且融资需走《动产担保法》（PPSA）股份质押或租约抵押，比常规按揭更难、利率常更高。In a housing cooperative, the association holds the fee simple or a long-term ground lease on the whole property; the buyer acquires shares in the association plus a long-term lease to occupy a unit, rather than fee simple title to the unit. The board of directors usually must approve the buyer (and need not limit its reasons to financial ability), and financing requires a pledge of the shares or a charge over the lease under the Personal Property Security Act (PPSA), which is harder to obtain than a conventional mortgage and often carries higher interest rates.

  2. ✗ 合作社不存入共管物业图则，买家拿不到单元的独立完全保有地产（fee simple），且不受《共管物业法》（SPA）管辖。A cooperative does not deposit a strata plan; buyers do not receive separate fee simple title to their units and are not governed by the Strata Property Act.

  3. ✗ 合作社董事会对买家人选有很大裁量权，拒批理由未必与财务能力有关；且《地产开发营销法》（REDMA）的披露义务同样适用于销售两个以上合作社权益。Cooperative boards have broad discretion over prospective buyers, and the grounds for refusal need not relate to financial ability; moreover, the disclosure obligations under the Real Estate Development Marketing Act (REDMA) apply equally to sales of two or more cooperative interests.

  4. ✗ 协会以整块地产做抵押贷款时，若部分会员断供，其余会员必须凑出这笔钱，否则整个合作社协会可能面临止赎（foreclosure）。When an association finances the building under a blanket mortgage, if some members default on their housing charges, remaining members must cover the shortfall to avoid foreclosure against the entire cooperative.

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A seller lists a residential unit in a 12-unit housing cooperative. Before accepting an offer, what key risks and legal rules about housing cooperatives should the licensee explain to the parties?

一位卖家挂牌出售一个 12 户住宅合作社中的单元。在接受要约前，持牌人应当向交易双方解释关于住宅合作社的哪些关键风险与法律规则？

  1. ✗ Form F 和单元完全保有地产过户属于《共管物业法》（SPA）下的 strata 制度，合作社转让的是协会股份与长期租约。Form F and fee simple lot transfers apply strictly to strata properties under the Strata Property Act; a cooperative transfers association shares and a long-term lease.

  2. ✗ 合作社董事会（board of directors）对买家人选拥有实质批准权，即使买方财务信用良好仍可能被拒，因此合同与周期存在较大不确定性。The cooperative board of directors has substantive power to approve buyers; even a financially qualified buyer may be rejected, so there is considerable uncertainty about the contract and its timeline.

  3. ✗ 《住宅租赁法》（RTA s.4(a)）明文排除非营利房屋合作社租给其会员的居住单位。Section 4(a) of the Residential Tenancy Act explicitly excludes residential premises rented by a non-profit housing cooperative to its members.

  4. ✓ 合作社（cooperative）转让通常须经董事会批准买家且裁量空间很大；同时合作社内部融资以整块地产做抵押，小型合作社若有几户断供，其余会员必须补足差额，否则整个协会面临止赎风险。Cooperative transfers typically require board approval of the purchaser with wide discretion; additionally, under blanket mortgage financing, if several members in a small co-op default, remaining members must make up the deficit or risk foreclosure against the entire property.
