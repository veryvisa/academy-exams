本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 情景定性题：给出一段持牌人报错面积、漏查产权或私下返佣的案例，考查该行为触发过失（negligence）、过失性虚假陈述（negligent misrepresentation）、欺诈（deceit）还是违反受托义务（fiduciary duty），核心在于辨别心理状态、关系前提与举证责任归属。Scenario characterization questions: Presenting cases where a licensee misstates floor area, fails to verify title, or provides undisclosed referral fees or rebates, testing whether the conduct triggers negligence, negligent misrepresentation, deceit, or breach of fiduciary duty, with the core focus on identifying state of mind, relationship prerequisites, and the allocation of the burden of proof.
  • 侵权要件与抗辩甄别题：围绕侵入（trespass）与私人妨害（private nuisance）设问，考查「是否须证明损害（actionable per se）」「行为发生在谁的土地上」「物理损害与享用干扰的抗辩差异」以及哪些常见借口（如搬来妨害旁、已尽最大注意）被法院明确否定。Tort elements and defence screening questions: Focusing on trespass and private nuisance, testing whether an action is actionable per se without proof of damage, on whose land the conduct occurs, differences in defences between physical damage and interference with enjoyment, and which common excuses (such as coming to the nuisance or exercising reasonable care) the courts have explicitly rejected.
  • 责任归属与连带边界题：在开放日访客摔伤或代表（representative）在外闯祸的情景中，考查《占用人责任法》（Occupiers Liability Act）下多重占用人的认定，以及经纪行替代责任（vicarious liability）中「通常雇佣过程」与「独立承包商合同标签」的判断。Liability allocation and boundary questions: In scenarios involving open house visitor slip-and-fall injuries or misconduct committed by representatives, testing the identification of multiple occupiers under the Occupiers Liability Act, as well as the determination of the ordinary course of employment versus the independent contractor contractual label in brokerage vicarious liability.
  • 双钱袋与三条线分流题：给出客户受损或持牌人受罚的场景，考查该笔账单归错误与遗漏保险（E&O insurance）、特别赔偿基金（special compensation fund）还是持牌人自掏腰包，核心抓手是区分「不小心（过失）」与「不老实（侵吞或挪用托付资金）」以及保险不保任何罚款。Fund allocation and avenue triage questions: Presenting scenarios where a client suffers financial loss or a licensee is sanctioned, testing whether liability falls on Errors and Omissions (E&O) insurance, the Real Estate Compensation Fund, or the licensee personally; the key distinction is between carelessness (negligence) and dishonesty (misappropriation or wrongful conversion of trust funds), along with the rule that insurance never covers regulatory fines.
  • 考试口径与现行法规对照题：针对纪律处分机关、受托义务成文化（Rules s.30）及条文重编号设问，卷面按课程手册（©2020）口径考共同法原则或旧称谓时，要求选出考试口径答案并清楚 2026 年现行《地产服务法》（RESA）与规则中的真实制度。Exam standard versus current statutory comparison questions: Asking about disciplinary authorities, the codification of fiduciary duties (Rules s.30), and renumbered rules; when the exam tests common law principles or former terminology under the 2020 course manual, candidates must select the exam-standard answer while understanding the actual framework under the current Real Estate Services Act (RESA) and Rules.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 通用免责声明护身陷阱General Disclaimer Shield Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常以为在挂牌信息上写一句「信息据信正确但不作保证」就能免除报错数据的责任，忽略了法院认定泛泛的免责声明不足以推翻注意义务，且任何免责声明都救不了明知不准确仍发布的人。Candidates often assume that adding a clause like 'Information believed to be correct but not guaranteed' to listing information exempts them from liability for misstated figures, overlooking that the courts treat blanket disclaimers as insufficient to negate a duty of care, and that no disclaimer can protect someone who publishes information knowing it to be inaccurate.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 general disclaimer、believed to be correct but not guaranteed 或持牌人明知数据有误仍照发并援引免责条款。The question stem mentions a general disclaimer, phrases such as 'believed to be correct but not guaranteed', or a licensee publishing data knowing it is inaccurate and relying on an exculpatory clause.

⚠ 受托义务举证与后果错位陷阱Fiduciary Duty Burden and Consequence Confusion Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 普通民事侵权由原告举证且后果是赔偿损失，考生容易把这套逻辑套到受托义务（fiduciary duty）上，忘记受托义务是全课唯一举证责任倒置（由持牌人自证清白）且第一顺位经济后果是丧失佣金（forfeiture of commission）的一格。In ordinary civil torts the plaintiff bears the burden of proof and the remedy is damages; candidates easily apply this logic to breach of fiduciary duty, forgetting that fiduciary duty is the only area in the course where the burden of proof is reversed (requiring the licensee to prove full compliance) and the primary financial consequence is forfeiture of commission.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问 burden of proof（举证责任）在谁身上、未获利/未影响房价的事项要不要披露，或问违反受托义务后关于佣金的判法。The question asks where the burden of proof lies, whether matters must be disclosed even if they did not yield profit or affect sale price, or how commission is handled upon a breach of fiduciary duty.

⚠ 独立承包商免除替代责任陷阱Independent Contractor Vicarious Liability Exemption Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 实务中代表（representative）多与经纪行签独立承包商协议，考生误以为经纪行借此可免除替代责任（vicarious liability），或反过来以为只要开了公司的车就一定算职务行为。In practice representatives often sign independent contractor agreements with brokerages, leading candidates to mistakenly believe this exempts the brokerage from vicarious liability, or conversely assuming that simply driving a company car automatically makes any private trip a course-of-employment act.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调 independent contractor agreement，或出现下班后办私事（如看电影）途中闯祸 vs 在履行地产职务过程中实施不当行为。The question stem emphasizes an independent contractor agreement, or contrasts an accident while running personal errands after hours (such as going to a movie) with wrongful conduct committed in the course of real estate duties.

⚠ 侵入与妨害要件互换陷阱Trespass and Nuisance Element Inversion Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 侵入（trespass）与私人妨害（private nuisance）都与土地相关，考生极易把「无须证明损害即可起诉（actionable per se）」错安在妨害上，或误以为「买房前妨害就存在」「已尽最大注意」可以作为妨害的抗辩。Because both trespass and private nuisance relate to land, candidates often mistakenly attribute actionable per se (actionable without proof of damage) to nuisance, or wrongly assume that 'the nuisance existed before purchase' or 'exercising utmost care' serves as a valid defence to private nuisance.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干对比直接踏入/越界置物与噪音气味逸出、问无实际损失能否胜诉，或被告辩称原告是自己搬到妨害源旁边的。The question stem contrasts direct entry or placing objects across a boundary with escaping noise or fumes, asks if an action succeeds without actual damage, or has the defendant argue that the plaintiff came to the nuisance.

⚠ 保险与赔偿基金混袋陷阱Insurance and Compensation Fund Commingling Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常把错误与遗漏保险（E&O insurance）和特别赔偿基金（special compensation fund）混为一谈，误以为赔偿基金能赔持牌人粗心算错面积的损失，或误以为 E&O 能报销 BCFSA 的行政罚款与执法开支。Candidates frequently confuse Errors and Omissions (E&O) insurance with the Real Estate Compensation Fund, mistakenly assuming that the compensation fund reimburses losses from a licensee's careless floor area mismeasurement, or that E&O covers BCFSA administrative penalties and enforcement expenses.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问过失报错 vs 侵吞定金找哪个钱袋赔、纪律罚款和执法开支谁付，或问未按期缴 E&O 分摊费次日牌照状态如何。The question stem asks which fund covers negligent misstatements versus misappropriated deposits, who pays disciplinary fines and enforcement expenses, or what licence status results the day after failing to pay an E&O assessment.

民事、纪律与赔偿基金三条线并行Three Parallel Avenues of Liability: Civil, Disciplinary, and Compensation Fundparallel avenues of liability同一不当行为可同时触发民事诉讼、纪律处分与特别赔偿基金申请，三条线互不替代，且纪律线旨在维护行业声誉与公众信心，不以有人实际受损为前提。The same misconduct can simultaneously trigger civil litigation, disciplinary proceedings, and a claim against the Real Estate Compensation Fund; the three avenues are parallel and mutually non-exclusive, and disciplinary action aims to uphold industry reputation and public confidence rather than requiring actual financial injury to any person.
过失三要件与免责声明效力Three Elements of Negligence and Validity of Disclaimersnegligence / duty of care / standard of care / remoteness过失之诉须同时证明注意义务、违反注意标准及损害类型不太遥远；泛泛的通用免责声明不能免除持牌人责任，具体免责声明在明知信息不准确时也一律无效。An action in negligence requires proof of a duty of care, a breach of the standard of care, and damage of a kind that is not too remote; a general disclaimer does not relieve a licensee of liability, and even a specific disclaimer is completely ineffective if the licensee publishes information knowing it to be inaccurate.
过失性虚假陈述与欺诈的分野Distinction Between Negligent Misrepresentation and Deceitnegligent misrepresentation vs. deceit过失性虚假陈述要求陈述人是专家、与合理依赖者存在特殊关系且心理状态为粗心，而欺诈基于明知不实或鲁莽不顾真假的道德欺骗，不要求陈述人具备专家身份。Negligent misrepresentation requires the representor to be an expert possessing special skill, a special relationship with a party who reasonably relies on the advice, and a careless state of mind, whereas deceit is grounded in moral fraud (knowing the statement is false or recklessly unconcerned with its truth) and does not require the representor to hold expert status.
受托义务四原则与举证责任倒置Four Fiduciary Principles and Reverse Burden of Prooffiduciary duty / utmost good faith / burden of proof持牌人对委托人负有保密、充分披露、争取不低于第三方可得最佳价格的义务，且由持牌人承担已完全履责的举证责任，违反时首先面临丧失佣金。A licensee owes the client duties of confidentiality, full disclosure, and securing a price no lower than the best available from third parties, with the burden of proof placed entirely on the licensee to prove full compliance; the primary remedy for a breach is forfeiture of commission.
替代责任与代表身份认定Vicarious Liability and Representative Employment Statusvicarious liability / ordinary course of employment经纪行须为持牌人在通常雇佣过程中实施的不当行为承担替代责任，即使签有独立承包商协议，法院仍将代表（representative）视为雇员对待。A brokerage is vicariously liable for wrongful acts committed by its licensees within the ordinary course of employment; even where an independent contractor agreement is signed, the courts treat representatives as employees for the purposes of vicarious liability.
民事诉讼与赔偿基金的法定期间Statutory Limitation Periods for Civil Actions and Compensation Fund Claimslimitation period / basic and ultimate limitation《时效法》规定民事诉讼基本时效为索赔被发现之日起 2 年、最长时效为自行为发生之日起 15 年；特别赔偿基金申请期为自最早起算点起 2 年，而纪律程序无一般性法定上限。Under the Limitation Act, the basic limitation period for civil actions is 2 years from the date the claim is discovered, with an ultimate limitation period of 15 years from the date the act or omission occurred; claims against the Real Estate Compensation Fund must be filed within 2 years of the earliest triggering event, while disciplinary proceedings have no general statutory limitation period.
纪律处分决定人与罚款上限Disciplinary Decision-Maker and Statutory Penalty LimitsSuperintendent / professional misconduct / administrative and discipline penalties考试口径（©2020）称纪律机关为 Real Estate Council，2026 年现行法由 BCFSA 任命的 Superintendent 决定：行政罚款分 A–F 六档（上限 $100,000），纪律罚款每次违规个人最高 $250,000、经纪行最高 $500,000。Under the 2020 exam standard, the disciplinary body is identified as the Real Estate Council; under current 2026 law, all disciplinary orders are made by the Superintendent appointed by BCFSA: administrative penalties range across Categories A–F (up to $100,000), while statutory discipline penalties reach up to $250,000 for an individual licensee and $500,000 for a brokerage per contravention.
占用人责任与开放日风险防范Occupiers Liability and Open House Risk ManagementOccupiers Liability Act (OLA) / occupier对场所状况、活动及获准进入者有控制权的开放日主持持牌人可与业主同为占用人，须尽统一合理注意使访客及其财物合理安全，但不排除《住宅租赁法》等其他法律的更高标准。A hosting licensee who exercises control over premises conditions, activities, and permitted entrants during an open house can be deemed an occupier alongside the owner under the Occupiers Liability Act, owing a common duty to take reasonable care to see that visitors and their property are reasonably safe, without displacing higher standards under statutes like the Residential Tenancy Act.
侵入与私人妨害的核心对比Core Comparison Between Trespass and Private Nuisancetrespass vs. private nuisance / actionable per se / abatement侵入是故意、直接针对他人土地的行为且无须证明损害即可起诉；私人妨害是在自己土地上实施并实质干扰邻地使用或造成损害的行为，必须证明损害且造成物理损害时不得以行为合理抗辩。Trespass is an intentional, direct physical interference with land in another's possession and is actionable per se without proof of damage; private nuisance originates on one's own land and substantially interferes with the use, enjoyment, or physical integrity of neighbouring land, requiring proof of damage, and reasonableness of conduct is never a defence where physical damage occurs.
错误与遗漏保险与特别赔偿基金Errors and Omissions Insurance Versus the Real Estate Compensation Funderrors and omissions (E&O) insurance vs. special compensation fundE&O 保险保过失性错误或遗漏（逾期未缴分摊费或免赔额次日自动停牌，且不保任何罚款或执法开支）；特别赔偿基金赔托付资金被侵吞、私自转用或欺诈取得（单一申请人上限 $200,000，同一责任经纪行合计上限 $1,000,000）。Errors and Omissions (E&O) insurance covers negligent errors or omissions (unpaid assessments or deductibles result in automatic licence suspension the next day, and it never covers regulatory penalties or enforcement expenses); the Real Estate Compensation Fund reimburses trust funds that are misappropriated, wrongfully converted, intentionally not paid over, or fraudulently obtained (capped at $200,000 per claimant and $1,000,000 in aggregate per liable brokerage).

第 1 题Q1过失三要件与免责声明的效力Three elements of negligence and the legal effect of disclaimers★★陷阱：通用免责声明护身陷阱

Before publishing a residential listing, a listing licensee notices that the homeowner's sketch shows a finished basement area that clearly includes an unheated detached garage. The licensee still advertises the total figure as finished living area, adding the standard phrase 'Information believed to be correct but not guaranteed.' After purchasing the property, the buyer sues for loss caused by the overstated living area. Which statement best describes the licensee's liability?

在发布住宅挂牌信息前，挂牌持牌人注意到业主草图中的已装修地下室面积明显把一间无暖气的独立车库算了进去。持牌人仍将该总数字作为已装修居住面积发布，并附上一句标准用语「此信息据信正确但不作保证」。买方购入物业后，就居住面积虚高造成的损失提起诉讼。下列哪项最准确地描述了持牌人的责任？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是「通用免责声明护身陷阱」；法院认定泛泛的通用免责声明（general disclaimer）不足以推翻卖方持牌人对买方负有的注意义务（duty of care），更何况持牌人已看出数据有误。Incorrect. This falls into the general disclaimer shield trap. The courts have held that a vague, blanket general disclaimer is insufficient to override the duty of care owed by a listing licensee to a buyer, especially where the licensee has already noticed that the data is inaccurate.

  2. ✓ 正确。在过失（negligence）与虚假陈述原则下，泛泛的免责声明本就难以免责，而最重要的一条底线是：没有任何形式的免责声明（哪怕是具体免责声明）能够保护一个明知信息不准确却仍照常发布的人。此外在现行《地产服务规则》s.34 下，未尽合理注意与技能还可直接触发 C 档行政罚款。Correct. Under negligence and misrepresentation principles, blanket disclaimers rarely shield a licensee, and the fundamental rule is that no form of disclaimer (even a specific disclaimer) can protect a person who publishes information while knowing it to be inaccurate. In addition, under current Real Estate Services Rules s.34, failing to exercise reasonable care and skill can directly trigger a Category C administrative penalty.

  3. ✗ 错误。混淆了受托义务与过失注意义务的边界；共同法上的注意义务（duty of care）及于一切可合理预见会受其行为影响的人（包括买方），现行 Rules s.34 的合理注意与技能义务同样对所有人成立。Incorrect. This confuses the boundary between fiduciary duties and the common law duty of care in tort. The common law duty of care extends to anyone reasonably foreseeable as being affected by the licensee's statements (including buyers), and current Rules s.34 similarly requires reasonable care and skill to be provided to all parties.

  4. ✗ 错误。损害是否过于遥远（remoteness）看的是损害类型在被告行为时是否可合理预见；报错居住面积导致买方遭受经济损失完全属于可预见的损害类型。Incorrect. Remoteness of damage depends on whether the type of harm was reasonably foreseeable at the time of the defendant's conduct; financial loss resulting from misstated living area is entirely foreseeable.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A listing licensee copies septic system capacity figures from a decade-old brochure without checking municipal records or advising the buyer to verify them, relying solely on a general footnote stating 'All information is believed accurate but not warranted.' When the septic system proves undersized for the listed bedroom count, why does the licensee's defence fail in a negligence action?

某挂牌持牌人未核查市政记录，也未建议买方核实，便直接照抄十年前宣传册上的化粪池容量数据，仅依赖页脚一句泛泛的「所有信息据信准确但不作保证」。当化粪池被证实无法满足挂牌所列卧室数量时，为什么持牌人在过失之诉中的抗辩不能成立？

  1. ✓ 正确。法院判例明确指出，「此信息据信正确但不作保证」这类泛泛的通用免责声明（general disclaimer）不足以推翻卖方持牌人对买方负有的注意义务（duty of care）；可能有效的是要求独立核实的具体（specific）免责声明。Correct. Case law clearly establishes that a vague general disclaimer such as 'Information believed to be correct but not guaranteed' is insufficient to override the duty of care owed by a listing licensee to a buyer. What may be effective is a specific disclaimer advising independent verification.

  2. ✗ 错误。特别赔偿基金（Real Estate Compensation Fund）只管托付资金被侵吞或欺诈取得，化粪池信息失实属于民事过失（negligence）与过失性虚假陈述范畴。Incorrect. The Real Estate Compensation Fund only compensates for trust funds that have been misappropriated or fraudulently obtained; inaccurate septic system information falls under civil negligence and negligent misrepresentation.

  3. ✗ 错误。风险越大，注意标准（standard of care）越高；鉴于房产交易金额巨大且公众依赖持牌人的特殊技能，BC 法院对持牌人施加的是「非常接近完美」的高标准。Incorrect. The greater the risk, the higher the standard of care required. Because real estate transactions involve substantial financial commitments and the public relies on the licensee's specialized skill, BC courts hold licensees to a very high standard approaching perfection.

  4. ✗ 错误。免责声明的有效性取决于其是否具体明确（specific disclaimer）以及陈述人是否明知不实，法律并无由 Superintendent 预先联署免责声明的规定。Incorrect. The validity of a disclaimer depends on whether it is clear and specific and whether the representor knew the information was false; there is no statutory requirement or procedure for the Superintendent to countersign disclaimers prior to publication.

  2. ✗ 错误。烟气、异味、噪音从自己土地逸出影响到邻地是私人妨害（private nuisance）的典型形态，而非直接针对他人土地的侵入（trespass）。Incorrect. Smoke, odours, and noise escaping from one's own property and interfering with neighbouring land constitute a classic form of private nuisance, rather than a direct trespass to land.

  3. ✓ 正确。在私人妨害（private nuisance）中，①「你自己搬到妨害旁」与「已尽最大注意和技能」均被法院明确判定为无效抗辩；② 更重要的是，当妨害造成实际物理损害（actual physical damage，如外墙油漆腐蚀剥落）而非仅干扰享用时，行为本身是否合理不再是抗辩理由。Correct. In private nuisance: (1) both 'coming to the nuisance' and 'exercising utmost care and skill' have been rejected by the courts as valid defences; and (2) where a nuisance causes actual physical damage to property (such as corroding exterior paint) rather than merely interfering with enjoyment, the reasonableness of the defendant's conduct is no defence.

  4. ✗ 错误。「不是我一个人造成的，是几家凑起来才有的」同样被法院明确否定——每一家制造妨害者都必须为自己造成的那一份负责。Incorrect. The argument that 'it was not caused by me alone but resulted from the combined emissions of several operations' has been explicitly rejected by the courts; every party contributing to a nuisance is liable for their contribution.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Which statement correctly contrasts the tort of trespass to land with the tort of private nuisance?

下列哪项陈述正确对比了土地侵入侵权与私人妨害侵权？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是「侵入与妨害要件互换陷阱」；无须证明任何损害即可起诉（actionable per se）的是侵入（trespass），而私人妨害（private nuisance）必须证明对使用与享用的实质干扰或实际物理损害。Incorrect. This falls into the trespass and nuisance element inversion trap. Trespass is actionable per se without proof of damage, whereas private nuisance requires proof of substantial interference with use and enjoyment or actual physical damage.

  2. ✓ 正确。侵入（trespass）是故意、直接针对他人土地实施的行为，且本身即可诉（actionable per se，无须证明损害）；私人妨害（private nuisance）则通常是在自己土地上实施、反复或持续影响到邻地，且必须证明实质干扰或物理损害。Correct. Trespass is an intentional, direct physical interference with land in another's possession and is actionable per se (without proof of damage). Private nuisance typically originates on the defendant's own land as a continuing or recurring state of affairs affecting neighbouring property, requiring proof of substantial interference or physical damage.

  3. ✗ 错误。在侵入之诉中，「不知情」或「放错地方」均非有效抗辩（判例中快递公司误送到错误地址仍被判构成不法置物侵入）。Incorrect. In trespass, honest mistake or lack of knowledge is not a valid defence (for instance, a courier mistakenly delivering goods to the wrong driveway is still liable for trespass by placing objects on another's land).

  4. ✗ 错误。除害（abatement）是私人妨害的救济（如修剪越界树枝，移除他人土地上妨害前一般须先通知）；驱逐侵入者叫自助（self-help），且必须先要求对方离开、不得使用超过必要限度的武力。Incorrect. Abatement is a self-help remedy for private nuisance (such as trimming overhanging branches, which generally requires notice before entering another's land); ejecting a trespasser is self-help that requires first requesting them to leave and using no more force than reasonably necessary.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

第 7 题Q7错误遗漏保险、赔偿基金与纪律处分口径Errors and omissions insurance, the compensation fund, and disciplinary frameworks★★陷阱：保险与赔偿基金混袋陷阱

A managing broker misappropriates trust deposits belonging to three separate households and flees. Meanwhile, an associate broker at the same brokerage faces a $5,000 Category C administrative penalty for failing to exercise reasonable care and skill on an unrelated file. How do the Real Estate Compensation Fund and the Errors and Omissions (E&O) Insurance plan respond?

某管理经纪从经纪行信托账户中侵吞了属于三户不同家庭的定金后潜逃。与此同时，同一经纪行的一名副经纪因在另一宗无关交易中未尽合理注意与技能而面临 C 档 $5,000 行政罚款。特别赔偿基金与错误与遗漏（E&O）保险计划将如何应对？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是「保险与赔偿基金混袋陷阱」；E&O 保的是「不小心（过失）」，不保侵吞定金；特别赔偿基金保的是「不老实（侵吞托付资金）」，而行政罚款无论哪个钱袋都不替付。Incorrect. This falls into the insurance and compensation fund commingling trap. E&O insurance covers carelessness (negligence), not misappropriation of deposits; the Real Estate Compensation Fund covers dishonesty (misappropriation of trust funds), and neither fund will ever pay an administrative penalty.

  2. ✗ 错误。特别赔偿基金有明确法定上限（单一申请人 $200,000、同一责任经纪行全部申请合计 $1,000,000，而非每户 $1,000,000），且 E&O 保险只赔民事过失责任，绝不报销 BCFSA 的行政罚款、纪律罚款或执法开支。Incorrect. The Real Estate Compensation Fund has strict statutory caps ($200,000 per claimant and $1,000,000 in aggregate for all claims against a single brokerage, not $1,000,000 per household), and E&O insurance only pays civil negligence liability, never reimbursing BCFSA administrative penalties, disciplinary fines, or enforcement costs.

  3. ✓ 正确。托付资金被侵吞属特别赔偿基金（Real Estate Compensation Fund，RESA s.60）范围，受单一申请人上限 $200,000 及同一责任经纪行全部申请合计上限 $1,000,000 限制（超出时按比例分摊 prorated）；而 Rules s.34 触发的 C 档 $5,000 行政罚款不在 E&O 保障范围内，须由持牌人自己掏钱。Correct. Misappropriated trust deposits fall under the Real Estate Compensation Fund (RESA s.60), subject to a statutory cap of $200,000 per claimant and a maximum aggregate limit of $1,000,000 for all claims against a single brokerage (prorated if claims exceed the limit). The $5,000 Category C administrative penalty under Rules s.34 is excluded from E&O coverage and must be paid personally by the licensee.

  4. ✗ 错误。行政罚款是交给 BCFSA 的，BCFSA 不会替违规持牌人交罚款，被侵吞定金则由全体持牌人缴费设立的特别赔偿基金在上限内补偿。Incorrect. Administrative penalties are payable to BCFSA; BCFSA never pays fines on behalf of a licensee, and misappropriated deposits are compensated up to statutory limits by the Real Estate Compensation Fund funded by licensees.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

According to the 2020 course manual wording, which body was described as the industry disciplinary body and what three traditional sanctions were listed — even though under current BC law (RESA Part 4) the Superintendent at BCFSA exercises all disciplinary powers with expanded statutory penalties?

按照 2020 版课程手册的考试口径表述，哪个机构曾被称为行业纪律机关、列出了哪三项传统处分工具——尽管在现行 BC 法律（RESA 第 4 部分）下，所有纪律职权已由 BCFSA 的地产总监（Superintendent）行使并配备了大幅扩充的法定罚款工具？

  1. ✗ 错误。金融服务仲裁庭（FST）是受理上诉的机构（30 天内、$850 上诉费），无论旧版还是现行法都不是一审纪律机关。Incorrect. The Financial Services Tribunal (FST) is the appellate body that hears appeals (filed within 30 days with an $850 filing fee); under neither the 2020 manual nor current law is it the first-instance disciplinary body.

  2. ✗ 错误。E&O 保险公司自 1988 年设立以来一直是非营利自保机构，从来不是监管或纪律处分机关。Incorrect. The Real Estate Errors and Omissions Insurance Corporation has operated since 1988 as a non-profit captive insurance entity and has never served as a licensing regulator or disciplinary authority.

  3. ✗ 错误。赔偿基金法团只负责管理托付资金被挪用时的补偿基金，不负责发牌或纪律处分。Incorrect. The Real Estate Compensation Fund Corporation is responsible solely for administering the compensation fund for misappropriated trust monies; it does not license professionals or conduct disciplinary proceedings.

  4. ✓ 正确。考试口径（©2020）将 Real Estate Council of BC 表述为纪律机关，且处分工具只列了训诫（reprimand）、停牌（suspension）与吊牌（cancellation）三项；而 2026 年现行 RESA 下 Real Estate Council 已解散，由 BCFSA 任命的 Superintendent 决定全部处分，拥有 s.43(2) 十项纪律命令工具（含个人最高 $250,000、经纪行最高 $500,000 纪律罚款）及最高 $100,000 的六档行政罚款。Correct. The 2020 exam standard described the Real Estate Council of BC as the disciplinary body, listing only three traditional disciplinary tools: reprimand, licence suspension, and licence cancellation. Under current RESA, the Council has been dissolved and all disciplinary powers are exercised by the Superintendent appointed by BCFSA, with ten statutory order powers under s.43(2) (including disciplinary penalties up to $250,000 per licensee and $500,000 per brokerage) plus six categories of administrative penalties up to $100,000.
