本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 状态辨析题：给出一段有瑕疵的交易（如缺少书面记录、一方未成年、受欺诈或标的物灭失），要求判断合同属于无效（void）、可撤销（voidable）、非法（illegal）还是不可执行（unenforceable）。Contract status classification questions: Presenting a transaction with a legal defect (such as lack of writing, an infant party, fraudulent inducement, or destruction of the subject matter), testing whether the contract is void, voidable, illegal, or unenforceable.
  • 时间线与生效节点题：围绕要约（offer）、反要约（counter-offer）、撤回（revocation）与邮寄承诺规则（postal acceptance rule）排列先后顺序，考合同究竟在哪个时间点成立或消灭。Timeline and effective moment questions: Ordering events involving offers, counter-offers, revocations, and the postal acceptance rule to test the exact point in time when a contract is formed or extinguished.
  • 虚假陈述与缺陷分类题：给出卖方或持牌人的一句话、或者房屋的某项物理状况与周边事件，判断属于无心虚假陈述（innocent misrepresentation）、故意虚假陈述（fraudulent misrepresentation）、潜在缺陷（latent defect）还是明显缺陷（patent defect），并追问对应的救济手段。Misrepresentation and defect classification questions: Given statements by a seller or licensee, or physical property conditions and external events, classifying them as innocent misrepresentation, fraudulent misrepresentation, latent defects, or patent defects, and testing the applicable remedies.
  • 终止条件与违约救济匹配题：区分条件（condition）与保证（warranty）、条件先决条款（condition precedent）的单方放弃规则，以及损害赔偿（damages）与特定履行（specific performance）的适用边界。Discharge and breach remedy matching questions: Distinguishing conditions from warranties, applying the rules for unilateral waiver of a condition precedent, and determining the boundaries between damages and specific performance.
  • 成文法要件核对题：考查《衡平法》（Law and Equity Act）第 59、54、36 条，《未成年人法》（Infants Act）第 19(1) 条，以及《成年人监护法》（Adult Guardianship Act）第 60.2 条的具体构成要件与例外。Statutory requirements check questions: Testing specific statutory elements and exceptions under Sections 59, 54, and 36 of the Law and Equity Act, Section 19(1) of the Infants Act, and Section 60.2 of the Adult Guardianship Act.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 把不可执行当成无效Confusing unenforceable with void

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常把「口头协议不算数」直接等同于合同自始无效（void），忽略了缺少书面形式或未成年人签约在法律上属于不可执行（unenforceable），且现行《衡平法》第 59(3) 条还有行为确认与不利变更依赖路径。Candidates often equate an oral agreement respecting land with being void from the outset, overlooking that lacking written form or contracting with an infant makes an agreement unenforceable, and that Section 59(3) of the Law and Equity Act provides pathways to enforceability through acts of confirmation or detrimental reliance.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 oral agreement、not in writing、Law and Equity Act s.59 或 19 岁以下当事人（infant）。The question stem mentions an oral agreement, not in writing, Law and Equity Act s. 59, or a party under 19 years of age (infant).

⚠ 把邮寄规则套用到撤回上Applying the postal acceptance rule to revocations

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生记住了承诺「投邮即生效」，就误以为撤回要约（revocation）也是寄出信件即生效，或者误以为写明「要约保留至某日」但未收独立对价就不能提前撤回。Candidates recall that acceptance takes effect upon posting and mistakenly assume that revocation of an offer also takes effect when mailed, or assume that specifying an offer will remain open until a certain date prevents early revocation even when no separate consideration was paid.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现 mailed revocation 与 mailed acceptance，或出现要约写明有效期但未签订选择权合同（option agreement）。The question stem mentions both a mailed revocation and a mailed acceptance, or states an offer expiration date without an option agreement.

⚠ 反要约后试图回头接受原要约Attempting to accept the original offer after making a counter-offer

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把修改价格或交割日的回复当成普通讨价还价，不知道反要约（counter-offer）提出的瞬间就已经在法律上消灭了原要约，原受要约人无法再单方面回头接受最初的开价。Candidates treat a price or completion date amendment as ordinary bargaining, failing to recognize that making a counter-offer immediately terminates the original offer as a matter of law, preventing the offeree from unilaterally turning back to accept the original proposal.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干中受要约人改动了任一条款后遭拒，随后声明「那我接受你原来的价格」。The question stem shows the offeree modifying any term, being rejected, and then stating that they accept the original terms.

⚠ 混淆无心与故意虚假陈述的过户后救济Confusing post-completion remedies for innocent and fraudulent misrepresentation

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生知道虚假陈述可以撤销合同，却忽略了无心虚假陈述（innocent misrepresentation）在土地交易完成过户后不能再撤销、也不能索赔损害赔偿，且标准排除条款能挡住无心却挡不住故意虚假陈述。Candidates know that misrepresentation can allow rescission, but overlook that innocent misrepresentation cannot be rescinded once a land transaction completes and cannot support damages, and that standard exclusion clauses protect against innocent but never fraudulent misrepresentation.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调陈述人「真诚相信（honestly believed）」或「完成过户后（after completion）」才发现陈述不实，或提及合同中的排除条款（exclusion clause）。The question stem emphasizes that the speaker honestly believed the statement, that the misrepresentation was discovered after completion, or refers to an exclusion clause.

⚠ 以为任何条件先决都能单方放弃、任何义务都能转让Assuming any condition precedent can be unilaterally waived or any contractual obligation can be assigned

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生忽略了《衡平法》第 54 条单方放弃条件先决（waiver of condition precedent）必须「仅为放弃方一方利益而设」，以及合同只能转让权利（assignment of rights）、不能借转让甩掉义务。Candidates overlook that under Section 54 of the Law and Equity Act, a unilateral waiver of a condition precedent requires that the condition was inserted solely for the benefit of the waiving party, and that under contract law, only rights can be assigned, not liabilities or obligations.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现条件对双方均有利却由一方单独声明 waive，或债务人声称已将还债义务 assign 给第三方。The question stem shows one party unilaterally attempting to waive a condition benefiting both parties, or a debtor purporting to assign performance obligations to a third party.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
合同四大瑕疵状态区分Distinction among the four contract defect statusesvoid / illegal / voidable / unenforceable无效（void）自始不存在须退钱；非法（illegal）法院可拒绝帮知情方追回已付款；可撤销（voidable）在被撤销前照常有效；不可执行（unenforceable）要素齐全但因程序理由无法起诉强制履行。A void contract never existed in law and money paid must be returned; an illegal contract allows the court to refuse assistance to a knowing party seeking to recover money; a voidable contract remains valid and enforceable until rescinded; an unenforceable contract contains all essential elements but cannot be enforced through court action due to a procedural defect.
土地合同书面要求与三路径Writing requirement for land contracts and three enforcement pathwaysLaw and Equity Act s.59考试口径强调土地合同须以书面作出否则不可执行，现行《衡平法》第 59(3) 条则并列规定了书面签署、行为确认（如收付定金）与不利变更依赖三条可执行路径。The exam rule emphasizes that contracts respecting land must be in writing to be enforceable; current Section 59(3) of the Law and Equity Act sets out three concurrent pathways to enforceability: a signed writing, an act of confirmation such as paying or accepting a deposit, and reasonable detrimental reliance.
要约撤回与选择权合同Revocation of offers and option agreementsrevocation / option agreement要约在被接受前随时可撤回（即使写了保留期限），但撤回必须实际传达给受要约人才生效；唯有另付独立对价订立选择权合同才能锁住要约。An offer can be revoked at any time before acceptance even if a deadline is stated, but revocation is effective only when actually communicated to the offeree; an offer is bindingly held open only where separate consideration is paid to form an option agreement.
邮寄承诺规则与反要约Postal acceptance rule and counter-offerspostal acceptance rule / counter-offer即时通讯的承诺须实际收到才生效，邮寄承诺在投邮那一刻即生效；反要约一旦提出即终止原要约，但单纯询问（inquiry）不会终止原要约。Acceptance via instantaneous communication takes effect only when received, whereas acceptance by mail takes effect the moment it is posted; a counter-offer terminates the original offer upon being communicated, but a mere inquiry does not terminate the original offer.
对价规则、印鉴与合理补偿Rules of consideration, seals, and quantum meruitconsideration / seal / quantum meruit法院不审查对价是否划算（$1 即可），但过去的对价（past consideration）无效；无对价合同加盖个人法律印鉴（非公司印章）仍有约束力；未约定金额时按 quantum meruit 支付合理数额。Courts do not assess the adequacy of consideration ($1 suffices), but past consideration is no consideration; a contract lacking consideration remains binding if executed under a personal legal seal (not a corporate seal); where no price is agreed upon, a reasonable amount is payable under quantum meruit.
未成年人与心智失能者缔约能力Capacity of infants and mentally incapacitated adultsInfants Act s.19(1) / Adult Guardianship Act s.60.2BC 省未满 19 岁者订立的合同对其本人不可执行（除法定、成年后追认、成年后一年内履行或一年内未反悔四例外），但未成年人可要求对方履行；心智失能成年人的财产转让依《成年人监护法》第 60.2 条可撤销，除非对价充分且已付/有担保，或理性人不知其失能。In BC, a contract entered into by an infant under age 19 is unenforceable against the infant (subject to four statutory exceptions: authorized by statute, ratified after turning 19, performed within one year after turning 19, or not repudiated within one year of turning 19), though enforceable by the infant; a property disposition by an incapacitated adult is voidable under Section 60.2 of the Adult Guardianship Act unless full consideration was paid or secured, or a reasonable person would not have known of the incapacity.
无心与故意虚假陈述及缺陷披露Innocent versus fraudulent misrepresentation and defect disclosureinnocent vs. fraudulent misrepresentation / latent vs. patent defect无心虚假陈述只能在合同完成前撤销且无损害赔偿，故意虚假陈述任何时候都可撤销并索赔且不受排除条款保护；卖方与持牌人须主动披露重大潜在缺陷，明显缺陷适用买者自负（caveat emptor）。Innocent misrepresentation allows rescission only before completion and never damages; fraudulent misrepresentation permits rescission and damages at any time and cannot be excluded by an exclusion clause; sellers and licensees must disclose known material latent defects, while patent defects remain subject to caveat emptor.
三种错误与三类同意瑕疵Three types of mistake and three categories of impaired consentcommon, mutual, unilateral mistake / duress / undue influence / unconscionable transaction共同错误与相互错误使合同无效（void）；胁迫、不当影响与显失公平交易则使合同可撤销（voidable），由无辜方决定是否撤销。Common mistake and mutual mistake render a contract void; duress, undue influence, and unconscionable transactions render a contract voidable at the option of the innocent party.
条件先决放弃三要件与违约轻重Three requirements to waive a condition precedent and severity of breachLaw and Equity Act s.54 / condition vs. warranty依《衡平法》第 54 条单方放弃条件先决须满足仅为放弃方利益、无该条件合同仍可履行、且在期限前放弃；违反条件（含「时间是要素」）可终止合同并索赔，违反保证只能索赔不能终止合同。Under Section 54 of the Law and Equity Act, unilateral waiver of a condition precedent requires that it was inserted solely for the benefit of the waiving party, the contract can be performed without it, and waiver occurs before the deadline; breach of a condition (including time of the essence) allows termination and damages, while breach of a warranty supports damages only.
四大违约救济与法定转让三要件Four breach remedies and three statutory assignment requirementsdamages / specific performance / Law and Equity Act s.36普通法常规救济是损害赔偿（须减损且符合可预见性），特定履行与禁令属衡平法酌情救济；依《衡平法》第 36 条法定转让须书面作出、绝对无条件且书面通知原债务人，且只能转让权利不能转让义务。The standard common law remedy is damages (subject to mitigation and foreseeability), whereas specific performance and injunctions are discretionary equitable remedies; under Section 36 of the Law and Equity Act, a statutory assignment must be in writing, absolute and unconditional, and notified in writing to the original debtor, and can assign only rights, never liabilities.

第 1 题Q1LEA s.59 书面要求与行为确认路径Writing requirement and act of confirmation pathways under LEA s. 59★★陷阱：把不可执行当成无效

A buyer and a seller reach an oral agreement for the sale of a residential lot, and the seller accepts a partial payment of the purchase price from the buyer without signing any written document. The seller later refuses to complete the transfer, claiming that any oral land deal is completely void. Which statement best describes the legal position?

买方与卖方就一处住宅用地的买卖达成口头协议，卖方收下了买方支付的部分购房款，但双方未签署任何书面文件。卖方事后拒绝完成过户，声称任何口头土地交易都完全无效。下列哪项最准确地描述了其法律地位？

  1. ✓ 根据考试口径，涉及土地的合同未以书面作出时属于不可执行（unenforceable）而非无效（void）；且现行《衡平法》（Law and Equity Act）第 59(3)(b) 条规定，支付或接受定金/部分价款属于行为确认路径，法院仍可据此认定合同可执行。Under the exam rule, a contract respecting land that is not in writing is unenforceable rather than void; furthermore, Section 59(3)(b) of the current Law and Equity Act provides that paying or accepting a deposit or part payment constitutes an act of confirmation, allowing the court to enforce the contract.

  2. ✗ 合同成立只有七大要素，书面形式不是成立要素而是《衡平法》第 59 条的程序性证明要求；欠缺书面形式不会令合同自始无效（void），这是「把不可执行当成无效」的陷阱。Contract formation requires only seven essential elements, and a writing is not an essential element of formation but a procedural requirement of proof under Section 59 of the Law and Equity Act; lacking a writing does not render the contract void from the outset, reflecting the trap of confusing unenforceable with void.

  3. ✗ 口头达成土地协议并不违反公共政策或刑法，不属于非法（illegal）合同，更不会触发非法合同下拒绝协助追回款项的惩罚。Reaching an oral agreement respecting land does not violate public policy or criminal law, does not constitute an illegal contract, and does not trigger the penalty under illegal contracts where courts refuse to assist in recovering money paid.

  4. ✗ 《土地产权法》第 56 条的三年期限针对的是授权书（power of attorney）登记效力，与《衡平法》第 59 条口头土地合同的执行路径毫无关系。The three-year period under Section 56 of the Land Title Act applies to the validity of registering a power of attorney and has no connection to the enforcement pathways for oral land contracts under Section 59 of the Law and Equity Act.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A landowner orally agrees to sell a parcel of land to a developer. Relying reasonably on this oral agreement, the developer alters its position to its detriment before any written contract is signed, such that refusing to enforce the deal would produce an inequitable result. If the landowner claims the deal is void for lack of writing, what is the legal position?

一位业主口头同意将一块土地卖给开发商。在签署任何书面合同之前，开发商因合理信赖该口头约定而改变了自身处境并承受不利，以至于不强制执行该约定将造成不公平的结果。如果业主声称交易因缺少书面文件而无效，法律立场是什么？

  1. ✗ 缺少书面形式不会令合同无效（void），且现行成文法已将部分履行与信赖原则写入条文。Lacking a written form does not make the contract void, and the current statute has codified the principles of part performance and detrimental reliance.

  2. ✗ 口头土地协议不属于非法（illegal）合同，也不需要通过补签选择权合同（option agreement）来洗白。An oral land agreement does not constitute an illegal contract, nor does it require retroactively executing an option agreement to become valid.

  3. ✓ 考试口径强调土地合同一般须书面作出否则不可执行（unenforceable）；同时现行《衡平法》（Law and Equity Act）第 59(3)(c) 条明文规定了不利变更依赖路径，当一方合理信赖合同改变处境、不执行会造成不公时，合同仍可执行。The exam rule emphasizes that contracts respecting land must generally be in writing to be enforceable; however, Section 59(3)(c) of the current Law and Equity Act expressly codifies the detrimental reliance pathway, making the contract enforceable where a party has reasonably altered their position in reliance on the contract such that non-enforcement would produce an inequitable result.

  4. ✗ 罚金条款（penalty clause）在合同法下不可执行，且 BPCPA 一般不适用于土地买卖。A penalty clause is unenforceable under contract law, and the BPCPA generally does not apply to contracts for the sale of land.

第 2 题Q2要约撤回与邮寄承诺规则Revocation of offers and the postal acceptance rule★★★陷阱：把邮寄规则套用到撤回上

By mail, a seller sends a written offer to sell a property, stating that the offer will remain open until Friday at noon, without receiving any consideration to keep it open. On Wednesday morning, the seller mails a letter revoking the offer. On Thursday morning, before the revocation letter arrives, the buyer mails a letter accepting the offer. The revocation letter reaches the buyer on Thursday afternoon. Which statement is correct?

卖方通过邮寄发出一份出售物业的书面要约，写明要约保留至周五中午，但并未收取任何对价来锁住要约。周三上午，卖方寄出一封撤回要约的信件。周四上午，在撤回信送达之前，买方寄出了接受要约的信件。撤回信于周四下午到达买方手中。下列哪项表述正确？

  1. ✗ 这是「把邮寄规则套用到撤回上」的典型陷阱：邮寄承诺规则（postal acceptance rule）只适用于承诺，撤回（revocation）不适用投邮生效，必须实际传达给对方才有效。This is a classic trap of applying the postal rule to revocations: the postal acceptance rule applies only to acceptances, whereas revocation of an offer does not take effect upon posting and is effective only when actually communicated to the offeree.

  2. ✓ 由于要约以邮寄发出，买方的邮寄承诺（acceptance）适用邮寄承诺规则（postal acceptance rule），在周四上午投入邮筒那一刻即生效；而卖方的撤回（revocation）必须在周四下午实际送达买方时才生效，此时合同早已成立。Because the offer was made by mail, the buyer's mailed acceptance is governed by the postal acceptance rule and took effect the moment it was posted on Thursday morning; the seller's revocation took effect only when actually delivered to the buyer on Thursday afternoon, after a binding contract had already been formed.

  3. ✗ 只要没有另付独立对价订立选择权合同（option agreement），即使要约里写明了「保留到某个时间」，要约人在被接受前随时有权撤回（只要在承诺前传达给对方）。So long as no separate consideration was paid to create an option agreement, an offeror retains the right to revoke the offer at any time before acceptance, even if the offer specifies that it will remain open until a particular time, provided the revocation is communicated before acceptance.

第 7 题Q7LEA s.36 法定转让与特定履行救济Statutory assignment under LEA s. 36 and the remedy of specific performance★★★陷阱：以为任何条件先决都能单方放弃、任何义务都能转让

Under Section 36 of the Law and Equity Act, a creditor transfers the full benefit of a contract debt to an assignee in an absolute, unconditional written document, but neither party gives written notice of the transfer to the original debtor. If the debtor defaults and the assignee wishes to sue, what is the legal position?

根据《衡平法》第 36 条，债权人通过一份绝对、无条件的书面文件将合同债权的全部利益转让给受让人，但双方均未向原债务人发出书面转让通知。如果债务人违约且受让人希望起诉，法律地位如何？

  1. ✗ 转让债权并不需要原债务人「事先同意」，法定转让只需要「书面通知」原债务人；未通知也不会使转让无效或非法。Assigning a debt does not require the debtor's prior consent, and a statutory assignment requires only written notice to the debtor; lacking notice does not make the assignment void or illegal.

  2. ✗ 法定转让（statutory assignment）必须同时满足三点：书面作出、绝对且无条件、且已书面通知原债务人；缺少书面通知就不能构成法定转让。A statutory assignment must satisfy three requirements concurrently: in writing, absolute and unconditional, and given by written notice to the debtor; absent written notice, it cannot take effect as a statutory assignment.

  3. ✓ 依《衡平法》（Law and Equity Act）第 36 条，法定转让（statutory assignment）须同时满足书面作出、绝对无条件、以及书面通知原债务人三点；三点不全时仍可作为衡平法转让（equitable assignment）成立，但在起诉时原债权人、受让人与原债务人三方都要被列为诉讼当事人。Under Section 36 of the Law and Equity Act, a statutory assignment must be in writing, absolute and unconditional, and notified in writing to the debtor; if any element is missing, it may still take effect as an equitable assignment, but the original creditor, assignee, and debtor must all be joined as parties to the action.

  4. ✗ 这是「混淆权利转让与义务代履行」的陷阱：本题是债权人转让收款权利（assignment），而代为履行（vicarious performance）是债务人找别人帮忙干活。This illustrates the trap of confusing assignment of rights with vicarious performance: here the creditor assigns a right to receive money, whereas vicarious performance involves a debtor engaging another party to perform contractual duties.

A buyer signs a contract to purchase a mass-produced investment condominium unit in a large suburban development. When the seller breaches the contract and refuses to complete, the buyer sues for specific performance rather than damages. Based on recent judicial trends discussed in the course, how is a court likely to approach the buyer's claim for specific performance?

买方签约购买一处大型郊区开发项目中批量建造的投资型公寓单元。当卖方违约拒绝完成过户时，买方起诉要求特定履行而非损害赔偿。根据课程讨论的近年判例趋势，法院很可能会如何处理买方的特定履行诉求？

  1. ✗ 特定履行（specific performance）是衡平法（equitable）上的酌情救济而非普通法常规救济，且近年判例已不再默认每一处批量开发的房产都是独一无二的。Specific performance is a discretionary equitable remedy rather than a standard common law remedy, and modern case law no longer presumes that every mass-produced development unit is unique.

  2. ✓ 特定履行（specific performance）是衡平法酌情救济，只在损害赔偿（damages）不足以弥补、标的物独一无二时才适用；近年判例趋势不再默认大规模开发的住宅独一无二，除非买方能证明该物业对其确有不可替代性，否则只能拿到损害赔偿。Specific performance is a discretionary equitable remedy available only where damages are inadequate and the subject property is truly unique; recent judicial trends no longer presume that mass-produced residential properties are unique, limiting the buyer to damages unless the property is shown to be irreplaceable to them.

  3. ✗ 合同法下不执行惩罚性的罚金条款（penalty clause），且强制性禁令（mandatory injunction）用于命令当事人为特定行为，并非直接判处监禁。Contract law does not enforce punitive penalty clauses, and a mandatory injunction directs a party to perform a positive act rather than imposing imprisonment.

  4. ✗ Hadley v. Baxendale 原则是用来界定损害赔偿（damages）的可预见性范围，而不是在买方请求特定履行时直接驳回全部诉讼。The rule in Hadley v. Baxendale governs the foreseeability of damages, rather than serving as grounds to dismiss an action entirely when specific performance is sought.
