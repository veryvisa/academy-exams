第 9 章 · 职业伦理Chapter 9 · Professional EthicsProfessional Ethics

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 机构权限辨析题：给你地产局纪律委员会与省监管方，考谁能对会籍停权或开除、谁能暂停或吊销牌照（考场上按考试口径认准地产局不能吊牌，同时留意现行监管方已并入 BCFSA）。Institutional authority analysis: Given the discipline committee of a real estate board versus the provincial regulator, testing who can suspend or expel from membership and who can suspend or cancel a licence (answering under the exam benchmark that a real estate board cannot suspend or cancel a licence, while noting that current regulators have merged into BCFSA).
  • 利益冲突与不具名买家情景题：描述持牌人或其关系人参与买卖，或买家要求对卖方隐瞒身份（如隔壁地块整合），考你是否履行完整书面披露（full disclosure）及向客户披露重大信息。Conflict of interest and undisclosed buyer scenarios: Describing situations where a licensee or associate participates in a trade, or where a buyer requests that their identity be concealed from the seller (such as in an adjacent lot assembly), testing whether you give full written disclosure and disclose material information to your client.
  • 双重代理与指定代理边界题：给出同一家经纪行两名指定代理分别代表买卖双方，或同一名持牌人手握两位竞争同一套房的买家，考你区分合法的指定代理（designated agency）与被禁的双重代理（dual agency）。Dual agency versus designated agency boundaries: Describing two designated agents from the same brokerage representing the seller and buyer separately, or a single licensee representing two competing buyers for the same property, testing your ability to distinguish lawful designated agency from prohibited dual agency.
  • 服从指示与法定底线冲突题：设置卖方客户明确要求隐瞒市政整改通知或无许可改建等重大隐蔽缺陷（material latent defect），考你能否识破「服从合法指示」的边界并果断选择拒绝继续提供交易服务。Conflict between client instructions and statutory duties: Describing a seller client who explicitly instructs the licensee to conceal a material latent defect such as a municipal notice to correct or unpermitted renovation, testing whether you recognize the limit of following lawful instructions and decisively refuse to continue providing trading services.
  • 广告、人权与同业规范判断题：结合《人权法》受保护理由清单、家庭办公室限制、未经业主同意刊登广告或提前接触他人在约客户，考你判断具体执业动作是否违规。Advertising, human rights, and industry conduct assessment: Combining protected grounds under the Human Rights Code, home office restrictions, advertising without owner consent, or soliciting clients under existing representation agreements, testing whether specific practice actions constitute contraventions.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 自律与法定权限混同Confusing self-regulatory authority with statutory authority

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常把地产局（Real Estate Board）当成发牌机关，误以为违反 REALTOR® Code 会被地产局直接吊销牌照，或忘了考试口径里的 Real Estate Council 在现行法下已并入 BCFSA。Candidates often treat a real estate board as the licensing authority, mistakenly believing that violating the REALTOR® Code allows a board to suspend or cancel a licence, or forgetting that the Real Estate Council under the exam benchmark merged into BCFSA under current law.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 discipline committee of a real estate board、suspend or cancel a licence 或 comparing the REALTOR® Code with RESA/Rules。The question stem mentions the discipline committee of a real estate board, suspending or cancelling a licence, or comparing the REALTOR® Code with RESA/Rules.

⚠ 盲从客户非法指示Blindly following unlawful client instructions

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 背熟了「按客户指示行事（follow lawful instructions）」，一看到客户要求保密就顺从，忽略了重大隐蔽缺陷（material latent defect）必须向交易其他方披露、客户若坚持隐瞒须拒绝继续服务。Having memorized the duty to follow lawful client instructions, candidates immediately comply when a client demands secrecy, overlooking that a material latent defect must be disclosed to all other parties and that if the client insists on concealment, the licensee must refuse to continue providing trading services.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 seller instructs the licensee not to mention、unpermitted renovation、municipal notice to correct 或 latent defect。The question stem mentions the seller instructing the licensee not to disclose, an unpermitted renovation, a municipal notice to correct, or a latent defect.

⚠ 指定代理误判为双重代理Misidentifying designated agency as dual agency

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 一看到买卖双方都在同一家经纪行（same brokerage）就以为是双重代理，或者反过来以为同一持牌人带两个买家抢同一套房只需口头说一声。Seeing buyer and seller represented within the same brokerage, candidates mistakenly assume dual agency exists, or conversely assume that a single licensee representing two buyers competing for the exact same property only needs verbal notice.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 two designated agents at the same brokerage 或 one licensee representing two competing buyers for the same property。The question stem mentions two designated agents at the same brokerage or one licensee representing two competing buyers for the same property.

⚠ 关系人或夹带披露豁免幻觉Illusion of exemption for associates or embedded disclosure

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为帮配偶等关系人（associate）买房不算自己的利益冲突，或者以为把冲突披露写进买卖合同条款里就算完成了书面披露。Assuming that helping a spouse or associate buy property is not a conflict of interest, or believing that inserting a disclosure clause into the contract of purchase and sale fulfills the statutory written disclosure requirement.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 buying for a spouse/associate、disclosing in a clause inside the contract of purchase and sale 或 separate written disclosure。The question stem mentions buying for a spouse or associate, disclosing in a clause inside the contract of purchase and sale, or a separate written disclosure.

⚠ 人权保护理由与适用条文错位Mismatched protected human rights grounds and sections

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 分不清守则旧版清单与现行《Human Rights Code》新增的性别认同（gender identity）及原住民身份（Indigenous identity），或混淆 s. 8（服务）与 s. 9（购房）在年龄与家庭状况上的差异。Failing to distinguish between the older Code list and current Human Rights Code protected grounds (such as gender identity and Indigenous identity), or confusing the difference between section 8 (services) and section 9 (property purchase) regarding age and family status.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 protected grounds of discrimination、Human Rights Code s. 8 vs s. 9、family status 或 age。The question stem mentions protected grounds of discrimination, Human Rights Code s. 8 versus s. 9, family status, or age.

第 1 题Q1吊销牌照权限与考试现行机构错位Licence suspension and cancellation authority and regulatory body transitions★陷阱：自律与法定权限混同

After a formal hearing, the discipline committee of a local real estate board finds that a REALTOR® violated the REALTOR® Code. According to the course manual, which penalty is BEYOND the authority of the local real estate board's discipline committee and can only be imposed by the statutory regulator?

在正式听证会后，本地地产局的纪律委员会认定某名 REALTOR® 违反了《地产经纪守则》（REALTOR® Code）。根据课程考试口径，下列哪一项处罚超出了本地地产局纪律委员会的权限，只能由法定监管机构作出？

  1. ✓ 正确。无论是考试口径还是 2026 现行制度，本地地产局纪律委员会（discipline committee of a local real estate board）都属于行业自律线，只能对会籍处以谴责、罚款、停权或开除，无权暂停或吊销牌照（suspending or cancelling a licence）。注意：本卷按考试口径回答由 Real Estate Council 或 Superintendent 吊牌；而在 2026 现行 BC 法规下，这两个机构已于 2021-08-01 并入 BCFSA，只有 BCFSA 的 superintendent 能暂停或吊销牌照（RESA s. 43(2)）。Correct. Under both the exam benchmark and current rules, the discipline committee of a local real estate board operates strictly on the self-regulatory track and can only issue reprimands, fines, suspensions, or expulsions regarding board membership; it has no legal authority to suspend or cancel a real estate licence. Note: The exam benchmark identifies the Real Estate Council or the Superintendent as the licensing authority, whereas under current BC regulations, both merged into BCFSA on August 1, 2021, and only the superintendent of BCFSA has statutory authority to suspend or cancel a licence (RESA s. 43(2)).

  2. ✗ 落入「自律与法定权限混同」陷阱。开除会籍（expulsion from membership）并使其失去 MLS® 使用权正是本地地产局在自律线上拥有的最高处分权。Falls into the trap of confusing self-regulatory with statutory authority. Expulsion from membership and terminating access to the MLS® system is precisely the highest disciplinary penalty within the authority of a local real estate board.

  3. ✗ 落入「自律与法定权限混同」陷阱。谴责（reprimand）和命其补课都属于考试资料列出的地产局自律处分清单。Falls into the trap of confusing self-regulatory with statutory authority. Issuing a formal reprimand and ordering remedial ethics education are standard disciplinary penalties available to a local real estate board under the course manual.

  4. ✗ 落入「自律与法定权限混同」陷阱。会籍罚款与承担听证费用均在地产局纪律委员会的自律权限之内。Falls into the trap of confusing self-regulatory with statutory authority. Levying a membership fine and ordering the payment of hearing costs fall squarely within the jurisdiction of a local real estate board discipline committee.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A REALTOR® is found guilty of breaching both the REALTOR® Code and the Real Estate Services Rules. Under the course manual's framework, how do the enforcement powers of a local real estate board differ from those of the statutory regulator?

一名 REALTOR® 被认定同时违反了《地产经纪守则》与《地产服务规则》。在课程考试资料的框架下，本地地产局的执法权限与法定监管机构的权限有何不同？

  1. ✗ 落入「自律与法定权限混同」陷阱。本地地产局在任何情况下都不能暂停或吊销牌照（cannot suspend or cancel a licence），哪怕一天也不行。Falls into the trap of confusing self-regulatory with statutory authority. A local real estate board cannot suspend or cancel a real estate licence under any circumstances, even for a single day.

  2. ✗ 落入「自律与法定权限混同」陷阱。地产局只能管会籍与 MLS® 使用权，不能暂停牌照。Falls into the trap of confusing self-regulatory with statutory authority. A real estate board controls only board membership and MLS® system access, and lacks statutory authority to suspend a licence.

  3. ✓ 正确。地产局只能停权或开除会籍（suspend or expel from membership），只有法定监管机构能暂停或吊销牌照（suspend or cancel a licence）。注意：考试口径将法定吊牌机构表述为 Real Estate Council 或 Superintendent，而 2026 现行法下该权限专属于 BCFSA 的 superintendent（且纪律罚款上限已达经纪行 $500,000、个人 $250,000）。Correct. A local real estate board can only suspend or expel a member from the board, whereas only the statutory regulator can suspend or cancel a real estate licence. Note: The exam benchmark attributes licensing authority to the Real Estate Council or Superintendent, while under current law that power belongs exclusively to the superintendent of BCFSA (with maximum discipline fines reaching $500,000 for brokerages and $250,000 for individuals).

  4. ✗ 落入「自律与法定权限混同」陷阱。管辖对象与文件完全颠倒：法定监管机构依 RESA 和 Rules 管所有持牌人，地产局依 REALTOR® Code 只管会员。Falls into the trap of confusing self-regulatory with statutory authority. This completely reverses the jurisdiction and regulatory instruments: the statutory regulator enforces RESA and the Rules over all licensees, whereas the real estate board enforces the REALTOR® Code exclusively over its members.

第 2 题Q2客户十项义务与冲突独立书面披露Ten duties to clients and separate written conflict disclosure★★陷阱：关系人或夹带披露豁免幻觉

Under s. 30 of the Real Estate Services Rules, when a licensee encounters an unavoidable conflict of interest while representing a client, how must the licensee fulfill the duty of disclosure under s. 30(j)?

根据《地产服务规则》第 30 条，当持牌人在代表客户过程中遇到无法回避的利益冲突时，必须如何履行第 30(j) 条下的披露义务？

  1. ✗ 违反书面披露形式要求。Rules s. 30(j) 明确要求完整地书面披露（full and timely written disclosure），口头告知加内部日记不合法。Violates the mandatory written form requirement. Rules s. 30(j) explicitly requires prompt, full written disclosure to the client; verbal notice paired with an internal personal diary note does not satisfy the statutory requirement.

  2. ✗ 落入「关系人或夹带披露豁免幻觉」陷阱。Rules s. 30(j) 特别规定冲突披露必须与服务协议、与交易文件分开（separate from the service agreement and any agreement for the trade in real estate），不能夹在买卖合同里。Falls into the trap of assuming an embedded disclosure is valid. Rules s. 30(j) specifically dictates that conflict disclosure must be separate from the service agreement and separate from any agreement for the trade in real estate, rather than embedded as a clause inside a purchase agreement.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据 Rules s. 30(i)(j)，持牌人须先采取合理步骤回避任何利益冲突（avoid any conflict of interest）；当冲突确实存在时，必须立即完整地向客户作书面披露，且该披露必须与服务协议和交易协议分开（separate document），以确保客户真正知情。Correct. Under Rules s. 30(i) and (j), a licensee must take reasonable steps to avoid any conflict of interest; when an unavoidable conflict arises, the licensee must promptly make full written disclosure to the client, and that disclosure must be in a separate document apart from any service agreement or trade agreement to ensure informed client decision-making.

  4. ✗ 违反即时性要求。s. 30(j) 要求冲突一存在就必须「立即（promptly）」披露，拖延至解除条件日违反法定义务。Violates the statutory timing requirement. Section 30(j) requires prompt disclosure as soon as the conflict arises, and delaying written disclosure until subject removal violates the licensee's statutory duty.

  1. ✗ 推卸责任陷阱。虽然 s. 28 规定了管理经纪的监督责任，但 s. 29(5) 明确赋予代表与副经纪立即报告的独立法定义务。Falls into the buck-passing trap. While s. 28 establishes supervisory duties for managing brokers, s. 29(5) places an independent statutory duty on representatives and associate brokers to promptly report misconduct.

  2. ✗ 定性夸大陷阱。讲义明确指出，专业不当行为不能直接称为刑事「重罪（felony）」，且金融服务仲裁庭（Financial Services Tribunal）是处理上诉的机构而非刑事公诉机关。Exaggerates regulatory violations into criminal offenses. Professional misconduct is an administrative licensing breach rather than a criminal felony, and the Financial Services Tribunal hears administrative appeals rather than prosecuting crimes.

  3. ✗ 落入「自律与法定权限混同」陷阱。行政罚款（administrative penalty）由 BCFSA 依 RESA 开出而非本地地产局，且 A 档是记录类违规。Falls into the administrative penalty confusion trap. Administrative penalties are issued by BCFSA under RESA rather than by local real estate boards, and Category A penalties govern recordkeeping contraventions.

  4. ✓ 正确。BCFSA 的举报义务指引明确指出，持牌人明知不当行为而不报，其不作为本身可能被认定为有失体面的行为（conduct unbecoming a licensee）并受到纪律处分。Correct. BCFSA regulatory guidance on the duty to report explicitly warns that a licensee who knowingly turns a blind eye to misconduct may have that failure deemed conduct unbecoming a licensee, subjecting them to disciplinary action.

第 7 题Q7纪律处分罚款上限、开单清单与上诉路径Maximum discipline fines, designated ticket lists, and appeal routes★★★陷阱：自律与法定权限混同

Following a discipline hearing (or consent order) under the Real Estate Services Act (RESA), what are the maximum discipline fines that BCFSA's superintendent can impose per contravention, and to which body can a licensee appeal a discipline order?

根据《地产服务法》（RESA），在纪律听证会（或同意命令）之后，BCFSA 的 superintendent 针对每一项违规可处以的最高纪律罚款是多少？持牌人如不服纪律处分令，可以向哪个机构提出上诉？

  1. ✗ 落入「自律与法定权限混同」及金额混淆陷阱。$10,000 是 C 档再次违规的行政罚款（administrative penalty），且纪律处分令的上诉机构不是 CREA 仲裁组。Falls into the penalty confusion trap. A fine of $10,000 is the Category C administrative penalty for a subsequent contravention, and discipline orders are never appealed to CREA arbitration panels.

  2. ✗ 金额与机构双重错位陷阱。$100,000 是 RESA s. 56(2) 下行政罚款（administrative penalty）的法定上限，不是 s. 43 纪律罚款上限，且地产局无权审理法定纪律上诉。Confuses monetary ceilings and appellate bodies. The figure of $100,000 is the statutory ceiling for administrative penalties under RESA s. 56(2), not the maximum fine for discipline orders, and real estate boards cannot hear statutory discipline appeals.

  3. ✓ 正确。考试旧考试资料仅泛泛提及罚款而无金额，但现行 RESA s. 43(2)(i)(j) 及 s. 43(2.1) 明确规定纪律处分令（discipline order）的罚款上限为：经纪行不超过 $500,000，其他持牌人不超过 $250,000（按每一项违规分别计算），另可追缴不超过该笔业务所收报酬的附加罚款；不服纪律处分令的，可依 RESA s. 54(1)(e) 上诉至金融服务仲裁庭（Financial Services Tribunal）。Correct. While the older course manual mentioned fines generally without specifying dollar amounts, current RESA s. 43(2)(i), (j), and s. 43(2.1) set maximum discipline penalties at up to $500,000 for a brokerage and up to $250,000 for any other licensee per contravention, plus remuneration disgorgement; appeals of discipline orders lie to the Financial Services Tribunal under RESA s. 54(1)(e).

  4. ✗ 混淆 C 档行政罚单与纪律处分令陷阱。$5,000 / $10,000 是 Rules s. 27 下 C 档行政罚单的固定金额，而纪律处分依 RESA s. 54 享有向金融服务仲裁庭（Financial Services Tribunal）上诉的法定权利。Confuses fixed ticket amounts with discipline orders. Amounts of $5,000 and $10,000 are fixed tariffs for Category C administrative penalties under Rules s. 27, whereas discipline orders carry statutory appeal rights to the Financial Services Tribunal under RESA s. 54.

Why can BCFSA NOT issue a direct fixed-tariff administrative penalty ticket solely for a breach of s. 33 (duty to act honestly), s. 59 (disclosure of material latent defects), or s. 63 (prohibition against dual agency) of the Real Estate Services Rules?

为什么 BCFSA 不能仅以违反《地产服务规则》第 33 条（诚实行事义务）、第 59 条（重大隐蔽缺陷披露）或第 63 条（禁止双重代理）本身为由，直接开出定额行政罚单（administrative penalty）？

  1. ✓ 正确。判断能否开行政罚单（administrative penalty）必须先查条款是否在 Rules s. 26 的 A–F 六档指定清单内：s. 33（诚实）、s. 59（重大隐蔽缺陷）与 s. 63（禁止双重代理）均不在 s. 26 清单内，因此不能仅凭违反这三条本身开行政罚单，而需按证据、情节与公众风险通过纪律程序（如 RESA s. 41 同意命令或 s. 42 正式听证等）处理。Correct. Being excluded from the administrative ticket list means matters proceed through discipline where statutory limits ($250,000 or $500,000) are maximums rather than mandatory fines, and licensees retain hearing or consent order rights.
