① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 区分题：同一则对外信息，问它是广告、宣传、公共关系、人员推销还是销售推广。真正考的是有没有付钱、内容能不能由持牌人控制，不是稿子是谁起的草、发在什么渠道。Distinction questions give one message sent outward and ask whether it is advertising, publicity, public relations, personal selling, or sales promotion. What they test is whether anyone paid, and whether the licensee can control the content, not who drafted the copy or which channel carried it.
  • 归类题：把地产服务放进购物行为四类，或把某种收费方法放进营销组合。真正考的是选购品的三个特征，以及价格指的是服务收费。Classification questions place the real estate service in one of the four shopping-behaviour classes, or place a method of charging a fee inside the marketing mix. What they test is the three features of a shopping good, and that Price means the fee charged for the service.
  • 时点题：签约之后卖方或买方又开始怀疑决定。真正考的是认知失调会在多个节点反复出现，签完一次并没有结束。Timing questions begin after signing, when the seller or the buyer starts to doubt the decision. What they test is that cognitive dissonance returns at several points, and that one signing does not end it.
  • 标准题：给一句目标，问它算不算 SMART。真正考的是五个条件要同时具备，空泛的愿望句不算。Standard questions give one goal and ask whether it counts as SMART. What they test is that all five conditions must be present at the same time, and that a vague wish does not count.
  • 版本题：题干写明执照考试或课程原文，问广告罚款怎么分级、团队名向谁申请。真正考的是按考试口径作答，不把现行档次或现行批准机构写进卷面。Version questions state that the stem is the licensing exam or the course text, and ask how advertising penalties are stepped, or which body a team name is applied to. What they test is answering on the exam standard, and not writing the current schedule or the current approving body on the answer sheet.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 广告宣传不分Mixing advertising and publicity

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 两者都是对外说话，于是按谁起稿、发在报纸还是网上来分。课程的分界是付不付钱，以及内容能不能由你控制。Both speak to people outside the firm, so they get split by who drafted the copy, or by whether it ran in a newspaper or online. The course line is whether anyone paid, and whether you can control the content.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写谁付钱、谁改定稿、报道的媒体是不是受雇于持牌人，以及角度对持牌人有利还是不利。The stem says who pays, who approves the final copy, whether the reporting medium is employed by the licensee, and whether the angle favours the licensee or works against the licensee.

⚠ 价格当成房价Treating Price as the home price

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 这一章讲的是卖房子，看到价格就想到成交价或挂牌价。营销组合里的价格是服务怎么收费。This chapter is about selling homes, so Price is read as the sale price or the asking price. In the marketing mix, Price is how the service is charged.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现声望定价、渗透定价、成本加成这类词，或直接问营销组合里的价格指什么。The stem uses words such as prestige pricing, penetration pricing, or cost-plus, or it asks directly what Price means in the marketing mix.

⚠ 便利品误套Forcing it into convenience goods

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 住房是日用必需，看起来像天天要买的便利品；又因为客户指名一位持牌人，看起来像特殊品。课程把地产服务的一般情形归为选购品。Housing is an everyday necessity, so it looks like a convenience good bought every day. Because a client names one licensee, it also looks like a specialty good. The course classes the usual real estate service as a shopping good.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写购买前花时间比较、买得少、单价高，问购物行为模型里属于哪一类。The stem says the buyer spends time comparing before the purchase, buys rarely, and the unit price is high, and asks which class in the Shopping Behaviour Model it belongs to.

⚠ 失调只算一次Counting dissonance only once

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 签完委托协议像是已经拿下客户，于是把买家的忧郁当成签约那一刻的一次性风险。它在后面几个节点还会再来。Signing the listing agreement feels as if the client is already secured, so the buyer's gloom is treated as a one-time risk at the moment of signing. It comes back at later points.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写签约之后持牌人停止联系，或客户在接受要约、解除条件、搬家前又开始自我怀疑。The stem says the licensee stops contact after signing, or the client starts to doubt again when accepting an offer, when conditions are removed, or before the move.

⚠ 用现行档次答卷Answering with the current schedule

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 柜台上的首次一千、再次两千、按天加罚是真的，团队那一档更高。卷面要的仍是课程里那套一次性的固定金额，批准机构也仍写议会。The figures used at the counter are real: $1,000 the first time, $2,000 the next time, plus a daily add-on, and the team tier is higher. The answer sheet still wants the course's one-time fixed amounts, and the approving body is still written as the Council.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写 licensing exam、course manual 或课程原文，问罚款金额、旧条号，或团队名向谁申请。The stem says licensing exam, course manual, or the course text, and asks for a penalty amount, an old section number, or which body a team name is applied to.

第 1 题Q1广告与宣传的分界The line between advertising and publicity★★陷阱：广告宣传不分

Mei, a licensee, is interviewed by a community newspaper about why homes in her town are taking longer to sell. She pays nothing, and she does not see the article before it runs. The paper then questions her reading of the market. In the course's promotional mix, what is this article?

持牌人 Mei 接受社区报纸采访，谈为什么镇上的房子卖得更慢。她没有付钱，发表前也没有看到稿子。报纸随后质疑她对市场的判断。按课程的促销组合，这篇文章是什么？

  1. ✗ 广告（advertising）是付费的非人员销售陈述。这篇稿子她没有付钱，也不是她买下的版面。把所有公开露面都叫广告，是广告宣传不分。Advertising is a paid, non-personal sales presentation. She did not pay for this article, and she did not buy the space. Calling every public appearance advertising is mixing advertising with publicity.

  2. ✓ 宣传（publicity）是非受雇于你的媒体主动报道。它不花钱，但内容不受你控制，可以是正面，也可以像这里一样是负面。可信度往往更高，代价是角度不能由你保证。Publicity is a report started by a medium that is not employed by you. It costs nothing, but you do not control the content. It can be favourable, or negative as it is here. Credibility is often higher. The cost is that you cannot guarantee the angle.

  3. ✗ 公共关系（public relations）是持牌人自己与员工、客户、公众做的整体沟通，用来塑造形象，由你发起，目标更长期。这里是报纸自己决定质疑她。把一切对外露面都叫公关，是广告宣传不分之外又混进了公关。Public relations is the licensee's own overall communication with staff, clients, and the public, used to shape an image. You start it, and the aim is longer term. Here the newspaper itself decided to question her. Calling every public appearance public relations mixes public relations in on top of mixing advertising with publicity.

  4. ✗ 销售推广（sales promotion）是赠品、竞赛这类短期刺激，效果比较容易衡量。一篇市场采访不是赠品，也不是竞赛。看见对外传播就选销售推广，是把五个元素收成了一个。Sales promotion is a short-term incentive such as a gift or a contest, and its effect is relatively easy to measure. A market interview is not a gift and is not a contest. Choosing sales promotion whenever something is sent outward collapses the five elements into one.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Omar pays a community newspaper to print a full page he wrote, praising his own negotiation record. He approves every sentence before it is printed. In the course's promotional mix, what is that page?

Omar 付钱给社区报纸，刊登他自己写的一整版，夸奖自己的谈判业绩。付印前每一句都由他改定。按课程的促销组合，这一版是什么？

  1. ✗ 读者更信任报纸，不能把付费版面变成宣传。宣传的前提是媒体不受雇于你、内容不由你控制。这一版是他买的、他改的。这是广告宣传不分。Readers trusting a newspaper more does not turn paid space into publicity. Publicity requires that the medium is not employed by you and that you do not control the content. He bought this page and he edited it. This mixes advertising with publicity.

  2. ✗ 人员推销（personal selling）是面对面沟通，能即时应变。一整版印刷文字没有当面交谈。指名道姓也不能把广告变成人员推销。Personal selling is face-to-face communication that can respond on the spot. A full printed page has no in-person conversation. Naming him does not turn advertising into personal selling.

  3. ✗ 目的是塑造形象，还不足以叫公共关系。他为这版付了钱，又控制了全部文字，课程把它归在付费的非人员陈述里。这是用目的代替了付不付钱。An aim of shaping an image is not enough to call it public relations. He paid for the page and controlled every word. The course places it with paid, non-personal presentations. This uses the purpose in place of whether anyone paid.

  4. ✓ 广告是付费的非人员销售陈述，触达可以很广，但要花钱，内容由出资的一方控制。他自己撰写并买下版面，符合这个定义，不是免费也不可控的宣传。Advertising is a paid, non-personal sales presentation. Its reach can be wide, but it costs money, and the party who pays controls the content. He wrote it himself and bought the space, which fits that definition. It is not publicity, which is free and not under his control.

第 2 题Q2价格指的是服务费Price means the service fee★★陷阱：价格当成房价

A licensee prices one service package by prestige pricing and another by penetration pricing. A colleague says both methods belong under Price in the marketing mix, and that Price here means the sale price of the homes she lists. Which statement matches the course?

持牌人用声望定价给一套服务定价，用渗透定价给另一套服务定价。同事说，两种方法都属于营销组合里的价格，而且这里的价格就是她所挂房子的成交价。哪一句符合课程？

  1. ✗ 声望定价和渗透定价确实列在价格（Price）下面，但这里的价格不是卖方希望拿到的房屋成交价。把服务收费读成房价，是价格当成房价。Prestige pricing and penetration pricing do sit under Price, but Price here is not the sale price the seller hopes to receive for the home. Reading the service fee as the home's price is treating Price as the sale price.

  2. ✗ 产品（Product）卖的是持牌人的经验、知识、谈判和沟通能力，服务本身无法试用、无法拿样品比对。收费方法并不因此变成产品。这是把价格这一项塞进了产品。Product is the licensee's experience, knowledge, and skill in negotiation and communication. The service itself cannot be tried, and it cannot be compared with a sample. A method of charging a fee does not become the product for that reason. This pushes the Price item into Product.

  3. ✗ 促销（Promotion）是人员推销、广告、销售推广、公共关系和宣传。定价方法不是这五种活动。这是把价格工具误放进促销。Promotion is personal selling, advertising, sales promotion, public relations, and publicity. A pricing method is not one of those five activities. This places a Price tool inside Promotion by mistake.

  4. ✓ 营销组合（marketing mix）的价格指服务收费，不是房屋成交价。课程列出的声望定价、渗透定价、成本加成等方法，都是在给服务怎么收费，不是在给房子定挂牌价。Price in the marketing mix means the fee charged for the service, not the sale price of the home. Prestige pricing, penetration pricing, and cost-plus, as the course lists them, are all ways of charging for the service. They are not ways of setting the asking price of a home.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Nora uses cost-plus pricing for her buyer-agency fee and follow-the-competition pricing for her listing fee. She tells a new licensee that these are Price decisions about the asking price of a home. What does the course say?

Nora 用成本加成给定买方代理的收费，用跟随竞争给定上市服务的收费。她告诉新持牌人，这是在对房子的挂牌价做价格决策。课程怎么说？

  1. ✓ 成本加成和跟随竞争都是课程列在价格项下的收费方法。营销组合里的价格是服务收费，不是房屋挂牌价或成交价。Cost-plus and follow-the-competition are both fee methods the course lists under Price. Price in the marketing mix is the fee for the service, not the asking price or the sale price of a home.

  2. ✗ 地点（Place）讲的是在哪里接触客户，包括办公室、网站、邮箱和愿意上门的能力，不是收费公式。这是把价格决策错放到地点。Place is where the client is reached, including the office, the website, email, and the ability to go to the client. It is not a fee formula. This places a Price decision under Place by mistake.

  3. ✗ 地产服务是无形的，不能拿出样品来试。收费高或低并不把服务变成有形样品。这是把价格当成了产品。A real estate service is intangible. A sample cannot be produced for the client to try. A higher or lower fee does not turn the service into a physical sample. This treats Price as Product.

  4. ✗ 跟随竞争是一种定价法，不是赠品或竞赛那种销售推广，也不是广告。短时让利的错觉不能把价格项改成促销。这是价格当成了促销。Follow-the-competition is a pricing method. It is not a sales promotion such as a gift or a contest, and it is not advertising. The impression of a short-term concession cannot turn the Price item into Promotion. This treats Price as Promotion.

答案Answer：a

第 3 题Q3地产服务是选购品The real estate service is a shopping good★陷阱：便利品误套

Before hiring a licensee, the Chen family compares several licensees' experience, reads how their recent listings sold, and visits open houses. They hire a licensee rarely, and the fee is large beside an everyday purchase. In the Shopping Behaviour Model, how does the course class the real estate service?

聘请持牌人之前，陈一家比较了几位持牌人的经验，读了他们近期房源的成交情况，还去看了开放日。他们很少聘请持牌人，费用相对日常采购也高。按购物行为模型，课程把地产服务归入哪一类？

  1. ✓ 选购品（shopping goods）的特征就是购买前比价比质、购买频率低、单价高。课程明确把地产服务和住房本身都归在这一类，不归在另外三类的一般情形里。A shopping good is marked by comparing price and quality before the purchase, a low frequency of purchase, and a high unit price. The course expressly places both the real estate service and the housing itself in this class, and not in the usual case of the other three classes.

  2. ✗ 便利品（convenience goods）是高频、即时使用的日用品，例如日常杂货。陈一家正在认真比较，而且很少购买。把地产服务放进便利品，是便利品误套。A convenience good is an everyday item bought often and used at once, such as ordinary groceries. The Chen family is comparing carefully, and they buy rarely. Placing the real estate service among convenience goods forces it into that class.

  3. ✗ 特殊品（specialty goods）是只认一个牌子、不做替代比较。课程说这只出现在极少数极端案例，例如指名某一位专家，不是地产服务的一般归类。陈一家正在比较好几位，更不是这一类。A specialty good means accepting only one brand and making no comparison of substitutes. The course says this appears only in a very few extreme cases, such as naming one particular expert. It is not the usual class for a real estate service. The Chen family is comparing several people, so it is even less this class.

  4. ✗ 非渴求品（unsought goods）是消费者本来不主动想要、靠恐惧或预防推动的东西，课程举的例子是人寿保险。陈一家在主动比较持牌人。地产服务不属于这一类。An unsought good is something the consumer does not actively want, pushed by fear or by prevention. The course's example is life insurance. The Chen family is actively comparing licensees. A real estate service does not belong in this class.

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A first-time buyer compares several licensees at length, then hires one. A friend says that much comparison means the service is a convenience good, like an everyday product. What does the course say?

一位首次购房的人仔细比较了好几位持牌人，然后选定一位。朋友说，比得这么仔细，说明这项服务是便利品，和日常商品一样。课程怎么说？

  1. ✗ 这位买方是在主动比较，不是被恐惧或预防推着买一项本来不想要的服务。非渴求品不是地产服务的归类。这是把保险那一类套到了选人上。This buyer is comparing on purpose, and is not being pushed by fear or prevention into a service the buyer did not want.   3. ✓ 购买前比价比质、频率低、单价高，正是选购品。地产服务属于这一类。花时间比较，不能把它变成便利品。Comparing price and quality before the purchase, a low frequency, and a high unit price are exactly what a shopping good is. The real estate service belongs in this class. Spending time comparing does not turn it into a convenience good.

  4. ✗ 家庭会用到，不等于高频、即时、很少比较。便利品是日用品那一类，课程明确说地产服务不属于它。这是便利品误套。A household using something does not make it frequent, immediate, and rarely compared. Convenience goods are the everyday-product class. The course says expressly that the real estate service does not belong there. This forces it into convenience goods.

第 4 题Q4认知失调会反复出现Cognitive dissonance can return★★陷阱：失调只算一次

After the seller signs the listing agreement, the licensee stops calling, saying cognitive dissonance can arise only at the moment of signing. Later the seller accepts a conditional offer and asks whether a higher asking price would have been wiser. What does the course say?

卖方签完上市协议后，持牌人就不再联系，说认知失调只可能在签字那一刻出现。后来卖方接受了一份附条件要约，又问当初把挂牌价定高一点是不是更明智。课程怎么说？

  1. ✗ 签完委托协议并不等于拿下了客户。认知失调不是签约那一次就结束。持牌人因此停止联系，接不住后面的节点。这是失调只算一次。Signing the listing agreement does not mean the client has been secured. Cognitive dissonance does not end with that one signing. Stopping contact for that reason misses the later points. This counts dissonance only once.

  2. ✗ 搬家前确实可能再出现，但不是唯一的节点，也不是第一次觉得决策真实的时刻。接受附条件要约时就可以再犯。这是把多个节点收成了搬家那一次。It can appear again before the move, but that is not the only point, and it is not the first moment the decision feels real. It can return when a conditional offer is accepted. This collapses several points into the move alone.

  3. ✓ 认知失调（cognitive dissonance），俗称买家的忧郁，在签约、接受附条件要约、解除条件、实际搬家等多个节点都可能反复出现。卖方问挂牌价是不是该定得更高，正是这种自我怀疑。持续沟通比签约时说服一次更重要。Cognitive dissonance, commonly called the buyer's gloom, can return at several points: signing, accepting a conditional offer, removing conditions, and the move itself. The seller asking whether the asking price should have been set higher is exactly this self-doubt. Ongoing contact matters more than persuading the client once at signing.

  4. ✗ 倾听和同理心重要，是因为失调会反复，需要持续沟通，不是因为第一次会面听得够仔细，后面就不会再出现。这是失调只算一次，又把原因收成了第一次会面。Listening and empathy matter because dissonance returns and needs ongoing contact, not because careful listening at the first meeting means it will not appear later. This counts dissonance only once, and it also reduces the cause to the first meeting.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A buyer removes the last subject on a purchase and then says, 'Maybe I should have kept looking.' The licensee replies that this kind of doubt cannot occur once subjects are removed, and that it occurs only on moving day. What does the course say?

买方解除了购买合同上的最后一项条件，然后说：也许我该继续看下去。持牌人回答，条件一解除就不会再有这种怀疑，怀疑只发生在搬家当天。课程怎么说？

  1. ✗ 签约只是可能出现的节点之一。条件解除之后买方仍然可以怀疑自己是不是该继续看。这是失调只算一次。Signing is only one of the points where it can appear. After the conditions are removed, the buyer can still doubt whether to keep looking. This counts dissonance only once.

The time before the move is another, but it is not the only time. Saying there will be no further doubt once the conditions come off is the opposite of the course.

This buyer has already signed a purchase contract and removed the conditions, which is long past that stage. This places post-purchase doubt in the first stage.

It is not the same thing as anxiety after the purchase. This uses a promotion category in place of the buying process.

第 5 题Q5SMART五个条件都要All five SMART conditions are required★★陷阱：空泛也算SMART

Luis writes the goal 'Improve my service.' A colleague writes 'By a stated deadline, contact every client whose file closed in that period, and record how many agreed to a referral conversation.' Which comparison matches the course?

Luis 写下的目标是：把我的服务做得更好。同事写下的是：在写明的期限之前，联系该期间内结案的每一位客户，并记下有多少人同意谈一次转介。哪一种比较符合课程？

  1. ✗ 有改进客户工作的愿望，还不等于具体、可衡量。只写把服务做得更好，没有可计算的结果，也没有完成标准。这是空泛也算 SMART。A wish to improve work with clients is not yet specific or measurable. Writing only that the service will be made better gives no result that can be counted and no standard of completion. This treats a vague wish as SMART.

  2. ✓ SMART 要同时满足具体（Specific）、可衡量（Measurable）、可达成（Achievable）、相关（Relevant）、有时限（Timely）。同事那句有对象、有计数、做得到、和转介这项工作相关、又有期限。只写把服务做得更好，和课程里「多拿些新盘」一样，不是 SMART 目标。SMART must be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and timely, all at the same time. The colleague's sentence has a target, a count, can be carried out, relates to the referral work, and has a deadline. Writing only that the service will be made better is the same as the course example of taking on more new listings. It is not a SMART goal.

  3. ✗ 短，不等于可达成，更不能代替具体和可衡量。写出可以计数的结果，正是可衡量这一项要的东西，不是扣分点。这是把 SMART 读成了越短越好。Being short does not mean achievable, and it cannot stand in for specific and measurable. A result that can be counted is what the measurable condition asks for. It is not a mark against the goal. This reads SMART as the shorter the better.

  4. ✗ 客户、计数和期限正是具体、可衡量、有时限的写法。课程对照的合格例子就是带着对象、数量和期限的那一种。禁止这些要素，是把标准颠倒了。Clients, a count, and a deadline are exactly how a goal is written so that it is specific, measurable, and timely. The passing example the course sets beside the weak one is the kind that carries a target, a quantity, and a deadline. Banning those elements turns the standard upside down.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A team leader praises the goal 'Get busier.' She says it is achievable and relevant, so the other three SMART tests can be skipped. What does the course say?

团队负责人夸奖「变得更忙」这个目标。她说它可达成，也相关，所以 SMART 的另外三项可以跳过。课程怎么说？

  1. ✗ 五个条件都要有，不能只留两项。可达成、相关，补不回具体、可衡量和有时限。这是空泛也算 SMART。All five conditions are required. Two of them cannot be kept while the rest are dropped. Achievable and relevant do not make up for the lack of specific, measurable, and timely. This treats a vague wish as SMART.

  2. ✗ 只有一个愿望，不等于具体。具体要说清对谁、做什么、做到什么程度。变得更忙没有这些内容。这是把简短当成了具体。Having only one wish does not make it specific. Specific means saying to whom, what is to be done, and to what extent. Get busier has none of that content. This treats brevity as being specific.

  3. ✗ 有时限不区分目标写的是个人努力还是客户档案。没有期限，就缺了 SMART 的一项。这是给五条件开口子。Timely does not change according to whether the goal is about personal effort or a client file. With no time limit, one of the SMART conditions is missing. This opens a gap in the five conditions.

  4. ✓ SMART 五项缺一不可。变得更忙没有具体对象，没有可计算的结果，也没有期限，即使有人觉得它可达成、也和业务相关，仍然不是 SMART 目标。None of the five SMART conditions can be left out. Get busier has no specific target, no result that can be counted, and no time limit. Even if someone finds it achievable and related to the business, it is still not a SMART goal.

第 6 题Q6考试口径的广告罚款The exam-standard advertising penalty★★★陷阱：用现行档次答卷

On the licensing exam, an item asks for the advertising-penalty schedule set out in the course manual. A licensee's advertisement breaches the rules the manual is describing. Which schedule does the exam want?

执照考试有一题，问课程原文里的广告罚款是怎么排的。持牌人的广告违反了原文正在讲的那些规则。考试要的是哪一套？

  1. ✓ 考试口径引旧规则通报：第一次 $250、第二次 $500、第三次或以上 $1,000，按单一固定金额算。现行广告条款落在 D 档，首次基础罚款 $1,000、再次 $2,000，并按持续天数每天另加 $250。The exam standard cites the old rules bulletin: $250 the first time, $500 the second time, and $1,000 for a third time or later, each counted as a single fixed amount. Current advertising provisions fall in Category D, with a base penalty of $1,000 the first time and $2,000 the next time, plus $250 for each day the breach continues.

  2. ✗ 这是现行规则第 26、27 条下，广告条款第 39、40、41、42 条所在的 D 档，不是课程原文那套一次性固定金额。这是用现行档次答卷。This is Category D under current sections 26 and 27, where advertising sections 39, 40, 41, and 42 sit. It is not the course text's set of one-time fixed amounts. This answers the exam with the current schedule.

  3. ✗ 首次 $5,000、再次 $10,000 是团队实体性约束所在的 C 档，不是普通广告条款，也不是考试口径的三级固定金额。这是把团队那一档套到了广告罚款上。A first offence of $5,000 and a later offence of $10,000 is Category C, where the substantive constraints on a team sit. It is not an ordinary advertising provision, and it is not the exam standard's three fixed amounts. This applies the team tier to an advertising penalty.

  4. ✗ 课程原文写了三级递增的固定罚款，不是只有提醒、没有金额。把纪律读成没有罚款，和原文相反。The course text states three rising fixed penalties. It is not a warning only, with no dollar amount. Reading the discipline as having no penalty is the opposite of the text.

A course-manual question asks for the penalty on a third advertising breach, after two earlier breaches under that same old schedule. Which figure does the exam want?

  4. ✗ $250 只是课程原文的第一次。第三次或以上升到 $1,000，不是每次都停在第一次。这是把三级阶梯读成了只罚一次的金额。$250 is only the first offence in the course text. A third time or later rises to $1,000. It does not stay at the first-offence amount every time. This reads the three-step ladder as the amount charged for a single offence.

第 7 题Q7跨界可能违反胜任力Crossing over may breach competence★★陷阱：跨界不算胜任力

A licensee whose work has been residential listings agrees to market a small warehouse the licensee has never handled, and sets aside no time to learn that work, because the month has been slow. What does the course say?

一直做住宅上市的持牌人答应去营销一处自己从未经手的小仓库，也没有留出时间学习这项工作，因为这个月生意清淡。课程怎么说？

  1. ✗ 利基营销（niche marketing）是挑一个能发挥专长、竞争可控、有利润的细分市场深耕。临时接一项自己没有专长的业务，不是利基，课程还提醒个人持牌人贪多嚼不烂。这是把跨界误当成利基。Niche marketing means choosing a segment where expertise can be used, competition can be managed, and there is a profit, and then working that segment in depth. Taking on, for the moment, work in which you have no expertise is not a niche. The course also warns an individual licensee against taking on more than can be done well. This mistakes crossing over for a niche.

  2. ✗ 无差异营销（undifferentiated marketing）是一套方案打全市场，它是三种差异化策略之一，不是要求每人来者不拒。这里的问题是没有时间和专长。这是用策略名称盖过了胜任力。Undifferentiated marketing is one plan aimed at the whole market. It is one of three differentiation strategies. It does not require every person to accept whoever comes. The problem here is the lack of time and expertise. This covers the competence issue with the name of a strategy.

  3. ✓ 课程原话是：没有投入足够的时间或专长（failure to devote adequate time/expertise），可能构成违反职业伦理（a breach of professional ethics）。住宅持牌人临时接手自己不会的商业物业，就是这个例子所指的跨界。The course's own words are that a failure to devote adequate time or expertise may be a breach of professional ethics. A residential licensee temporarily taking on a commercial property the licensee does not know how to handle is the crossing over this example points to.

  4. ✗ 产品定位（product positioning）回答的是消费者心里怎么看你，不是要求接下每一种物业。定位做不好，也不能成为越过胜任力的理由。这是把定位读成了来者不拒。Product positioning answers how the consumer sees you. It does not require accepting every type of property. Positioning done poorly is also not a reason to step past competence. This reads positioning as accepting whoever comes.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A residential licensee declines a request to list a commercial site the licensee does not understand, and keeps working the neighbourhood of family homes where the licensee already has results. A colleague calls that a wasted fee. What does the course support?

一位做住宅的持牌人谢绝了自己并不懂的商业项目上市邀请，继续做已经有成绩的家庭住宅社区。同事说这是放掉了一笔收费。课程支持什么？

  1. ✓ 个人持牌人资源有限，应留在能发挥专长的细分市场。没有足够时间或专长仍把单接下来，可能构成违反职业伦理。谢绝自己不懂的商业项目，符合这个提醒。An individual licensee has limited resources and should stay in a segment where expertise can be used. Taking the file anyway, without enough time or expertise, may be a breach of professional ethics. Declining a commercial project the licensee does not understand fits this warning.

  2. ✗ 无差异营销不是强制接单。一套方案打全市场，仍不能代替对这项业务的时间和专长。这是用策略名称要求人跨界。Undifferentiated marketing is not a duty to accept the file. One plan aimed at the whole market still cannot replace time and expertise for this work. This uses the name of a strategy to require crossing over.

  3. ✗ 选购品描述的是消费者怎么买，不是持牌人必须接受每一单。买方会比较，不产生持牌人的接单义务。这是把购买分类当成了胜任力豁免。A shopping good describes how the consumer buys. It does not mean the licensee must accept every file. That the buyer will compare creates no duty on the licensee to take the work. This treats a purchase class as an exemption from competence.

  4. ✗ 地点是接触客户的渠道，包括办公室、网站、邮箱和愿意上门，不是每一种物业类型都要接。上门的能力也不能填补商业项目上的专长空白。这是把地点读成了全域执业。Place is the channel for reaching the client, including the office, the website, email, and a willingness to go to the client. It does not mean every property type must be accepted. A willingness to go to the client also cannot fill a gap in expertise on a commercial project. This reads Place as authority to practise in every field.
