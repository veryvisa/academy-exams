本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 五变量关系题：给出贷款金额、利率、期数、还款额、到期余额里的四个，考第五个是不是唯一确定，以及利率升高时同样月供能撑起的贷款变小、摊还期拉长则月供变轻但总利息更贵。Five-variable questions give four of the loan amount, the interest rate, the number of payments, the payment, and the balance at maturity. They test whether the fifth is uniquely determined, and that when the rate rises the loan the same payment can support becomes smaller, while a longer amortization makes the payment lighter but total interest more expensive.
  • 结欠与还款结构题：用卖房核对还欠多少、还了三年本金少了多少、或最后一期略小，考结欠余额是剩余各期还款按合同利率折成的现值，早期利息占大头，完全摊还的尾款不是一大笔到期余额，多付的加速还款是缩短摊还、少付利息。Outstanding-balance and repayment-structure questions use a sale to check how much is still owed, how much principal is gone after three years, or a last instalment that is slightly smaller. They test that the outstanding balance is the present value of the remaining payments discounted at the contract rate, that interest is the larger share early on, that the final payment on a fully amortized loan is not a large balance due at maturity, and that paying extra as an accelerated payment shortens the amortization and saves interest.
  • 法定提前还款题：用个人或法人、住宅或非住宅、合同期是否超过五年、已过年数、多付的是利息还是本金，考《利息法》s.10 的适用边界。这一层考试口径与现行条文一致。Statutory prepayment questions use an individual or a corporation, a house or a non-residential property, whether the term is longer than five years, how many years have passed, and whether the extra amount is interest or principal. They test the boundary of Interest Act s.10. On this layer the exam wording matches the current section.
  • 摊还期与合同期题：同一题里同时出现按多少年算月供、合同哪一年重谈，考摊还期只决定还款节奏，合同期才决定何时重谈，到期时余额通常还在。Amortization-and-term questions put both in the same item: how many years the payment is calculated over, and in which year the contract is renegotiated. They test that the amortization period only sets the payment rhythm, that the term is what decides when the contract is renegotiated, and that a balance usually remains when the term ends.
  • 口径对照题：题干写明按课程手册或考试口径作答时，考你复述保险按揭最长25年、首付达到或超过20%被写成政府规则允许30到35年；用现行的机构自定上限或首购新建30年去替换，是这道题的失分点。Wording-comparison questions state that the answer must follow the course manual or the exam wording. They test whether you restate that an insured mortgage has a maximum of 25 years, and that a down payment of 20 percent or more is written as a government allowance of 30 to 35 years. Replacing that with the current lender-set maximum, or with 30 years for a first-time buyer or a new build, is where this item is lost.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 加减法算结欠Calculating the balance by subtraction

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把已经付出的月供当成已经还掉的本金，用原始贷款减去三年还款来估算还欠多少。等额本息早期利息占大头，结欠余额是剩下各期还款按合同利率折成的现值。The candidate treats the payments already made as principal already retired, and estimates what is still owed by subtracting three years of payments from the original loan. On a constant payment mortgage, interest is the larger share early on. The outstanding balance is the present value of the remaining payments, discounted at the contract rate.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问卖房时还欠贷款机构多少，或出现“已经还了三年，本金应该少了一大截”，选项里用贷款金额减去已付月供。The stem asks how much is still owed to the lender when the property is sold, or it says that after three years of payments a large share of principal must already be gone, and an option subtracts the payments already made from the loan amount.

⚠ 摊还节奏当成重谈日Treating the amortization rhythm as the renegotiation date

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 摊还期只决定月供按多长的节奏来算，合同期才决定何时重谈利率。考生把“按25年还”理解成25年内不用重谈，或者把五年合同到期理解成已经还清。The amortization period only sets how long a rhythm the payment is calculated on. The term is what decides when the rate is renegotiated. The candidate reads paying over 25 years as meaning no renegotiation for 25 years, or reads the end of a five-year contract as meaning the loan is already paid off.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 同一题里同时出现 amortization 和 term，并问合同到期还欠不欠、利率要不要重谈、月供是按哪一个年限算的。The same item contains both amortization and term, and asks whether anything is still owed when the contract ends, whether the rate must be renegotiated, and which of the two periods the payment is calculated on.

⚠ 利息法对象看宽Reading the Interest Act's protected class too widely

为什么会错：Why people miss it: s.10 只给个人借款人，只适用于约定五年以上才到期的按揭，要从按揭日期满五年起，代价是额外三个月利息，而且不限住宅。考生把它套到法人、合同期五年及以内、未满五年，或改成三个月本金，或收窄成只保住宅。Section 10 is only for an individual borrower, only for a mortgage that is not payable until more than five years have passed, and only from the fifth anniversary of the mortgage date. The extra charge is three months' interest, and the right is not limited to houses. Candidates apply it to a corporation, to a term of five years or less, or before five years have passed, rewrite the extra charge as three months of principal, or narrow it so that it protects houses only.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 借款人是公司，合同期写五年整或更短，已过年数不足五年，物业不是住宅，或选项把代价写成三个月本金。The borrower is a company, the term is written as five years exactly or shorter, fewer than five years have passed, the property is not a house, or an option writes the extra charge as three months of principal.

⚠ 旧上限当现行法Treating the old cap as current law

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考试口径写保险按揭最长25年，首付达到或超过20%时政府规则允许30到35年。现行是低首付一般仍25年，但首次购房和/或新建可到30年；高首付没有联邦法定上限，由贷款机构自定。考生要么用现行规则答历史题，要么对客户把“最长35年”说成法律写死。The exam wording states that an insured mortgage has a maximum of 25 years, and that when the down payment reaches or exceeds 20 percent, government rules allow 30 to 35 years. Currently a low down payment is still generally 25 years, but a first-time buyer and/or a new build can go to 30 years. A high down payment has no federal statutory maximum, and the lender sets its own. Candidates either answer a historical item with the current rule, or tell a client that a maximum of 35 years is fixed in the statute.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写 course manual、考试口径或 ©2020，选项里出现25年、30到35年、政府规定，或反过来用“贷款机构自定”去答卷面。The stem says course manual, exam wording, or ©2020, and an option contains 25 years, 30 to 35 years, or a government rule, or answers the paper by saying the lender sets its own cap.

⚠ 尾款当成气球款Treating the final payment as a balloon

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 完全摊还走到结束时余额是零，最后一期只是略小于常规还款的尾款。考生把它当成部分摊还才有的那一大笔到期未还本金，看到略小的最后一期就以为还欠着一大截。When full amortization reaches the end, the balance is zero, and the last instalment is only a final payment slightly smaller than a regular payment. The candidate treats it as the large unpaid principal due at maturity that only partial amortization has, and reads a slightly smaller last instalment as a large amount still owing.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写 fully amortized 或到期余额为零，最后一期略小于常规还款，选项却把这一期叫成一大笔 balloon，或说完全摊还期末必须剩下一笔大余额。The stem says fully amortized, or that the balance at maturity is zero, and the last instalment is slightly smaller than a regular payment, but an option calls that instalment a large balloon, or says a fully amortized loan must leave a large balance at the end.

A closed mortgage first uses the contractual penalty-free amount, and what is unused usually cannot be carried forward. The excess usually takes the higher of three months' interest, which is the outstanding balance times the contract annual rate divided by four, and the Interest Rate Differential (IRD). There is no single federal formula for the interest rate differential.

Above that point there is negative amortization. The exam wording has no such concept. Federally regulated institutions must now disclose it, and the point rises slightly as the outstanding balance falls.

They require a federal institution to disclose in an information box the principal, the term, the amortization, the penalty calculation, and similar items. A written notice is required within 30 days after a mid-term change, and both a renewal and a non-renewal require at least 21 days' notice. The exam wording states only Interest Act s.6. Those information-box and notice rules do not cover a credit union local to British Columbia, a private lender, or an MIC.

Currently, a down payment below 20 percent is still generally 25 years, a qualifying first-time buyer and/or a new build can go to 30 years, and a down payment of 20 percent or more has no federal statutory maximum. The lender sets it.

To change the rate before the term ends, blend-and-extend blends the rates by time and lengthens the term. It does not trigger a penalty and charges only an administration fee. To keep the old terms when changing homes, porting moves the mortgage to the new property, if the institution agrees and the valuation lines up with the loan amount.

第 1 题Q1利率升则能借更少A higher rate means less can be borrowed★陷阱：第五个被估掉

A buyer can carry a payment of $2,000 a month on a constant-payment mortgage. The interest rate rises. The payment the buyer can carry, the payment frequency, and the amortization do not change. What happens to the loan that payment can support?

买家在一笔等额本息按揭上每月能承担的还款是 $2,000。利率上升。买家能承担的还款额、还款频率和摊还期都不变。这笔月供能支撑的贷款会怎样？

  1. ✓ 正确。等额本息按揭（constant payment mortgage）的贷款金额、周期利率、期数、每期还款额和到期余额互相绑定，已知四个，第五个唯一确定。月供不变而利率升高，每期流向本金的部分变少，能支撑的贷款变小。这是同一套数学关系，不是机构故意压贷款。Correct. On a constant payment mortgage, the loan amount, the periodic rate, the number of payments, the payment each period, and the balance at maturity are locked together. Given four, the fifth is uniquely determined. If the payment stays the same and the rate rises, less of each payment goes to principal, so the loan that payment can support becomes smaller. This is the same mathematical relationship. It is not the institution deliberately pressing the loan down.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是“第五个被估掉”陷阱：以为只有摊还期能动贷款额。利率本身就是五个绑定变量之一，它升高会单独把可贷金额压小。Wrong. This is the trap of estimating the fifth variable: it assumes only the amortization can move the loan amount. The rate itself is one of the five locked variables. A rise in the rate alone makes the amount that can be borrowed smaller.

  3. ✗ 错误。这是“第五个被估掉”里把方向看反。利率越高，同样的月供能撑起的贷款越小，不是越大。更高的利率不会为了填满同一笔月供而多放本金。Wrong. This reverses the direction inside the trap of estimating the fifth variable. The higher the rate, the smaller the loan the same payment can support. A higher rate does not advance more principal in order to fill the same payment.

  4. ✗ 错误。这是“第五个被估掉”陷阱。已知其中四个，第五个是唯一确定的。“利率一变，最高能借多少”有唯一答案，不是估出来的。Wrong. This is the trap of estimating the fifth variable. Given four of them, the fifth is uniquely determined. When the rate changes, the most that can be borrowed has one answer. It is not an estimate.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A constant-payment mortgage is approved but not yet funded. The lender then raises only the periodic rate. The borrowers' payment, the monthly frequency, and the 25-year amortization stay as approved. Which result follows?

一笔等额本息按揭已获批准，但尚未放款。贷款机构随后只提高周期利率。借款人的还款额、按月还款和25年摊还期都保持原批准数字。哪一项随之发生？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是“第五个被估掉”里换错了变量。利率升高仍然可以留在等额本息（constant payment mortgage）里。变的是同样月供能够支撑的贷款金额，不是必须改成只付利息。Wrong. This changes the wrong variable inside the trap of estimating the fifth variable. A higher rate can still sit inside a constant payment mortgage. What changes is the loan amount the same payment can support. The mortgage does not have to become interest only.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是“第五个被估掉”陷阱。利率就是五个变量之一。月供、频率和摊还期不变时，贷款金额必须跟着变，不能改了一个、其余却全部原样。Wrong. This is the trap of estimating the fifth variable. The rate is one of the five variables. When the payment, the frequency, and the amortization stay the same, the loan amount must change with the rate. One of them cannot be changed while every other one stays exactly as it was.

  3. ✓ 正确。已知月供、期数和升高后的周期利率，能支撑的贷款金额（PV）唯一确定，而且变小。每期还款里利息占得更多，剩下能还本金的部分更少。Correct.   2. ✓ 正确。这就是考试口径那句话：保险按揭最长摊还25年，首付达到或超过20%的非保险按揭，政府规则允许30到35年。现行规则不同：首付低于20%一般仍最长25年，符合首次购房和/或新建的可以到30年；首付达到或超过20%没有联邦法定上限，由贷款机构自定。Correct. This is the exam sentence: an insured mortgage has a maximum amortization of 25 years, and an uninsured mortgage with a down payment of 20 percent or more is allowed 30 to 35 years by government rules. The current rule is different. A down payment below 20 percent is still generally a maximum of 25 years, a qualifying first-time buyer and/or a new build can go to 30 years, and a down payment of 20 percent or more has no federal statutory maximum. The lender sets it.

  3. ✗ 错误。这是“旧上限当现行法”陷阱。首付达到或超过20%没有联邦法定上限、由贷款机构自定，是现行规则，不是课程手册的写法。历史题要复述“政府规则允许30到35年”。Wrong. This is the trap of treating the old cap as current law. No federal statutory maximum, with the lender setting its own, is the current rule for a down payment of 20 percent or more. It is not what the course manual states. A historical item must restate that government rules allow 30 to 35 years.

  4. ✗ 错误。这是“旧上限当现行法”里把两段年限对调了。30年不是考试口径给全部保险按揭的上限，35年也不是它写给非保险按揭的单一法定年数。手册写的是保险按揭25年，以及30到35年这一段。Wrong. This swaps the two bands inside the trap of treating the old cap as current law. Thirty years is not the exam maximum for every insured mortgage, and 35 years is not a single statutory number the exam writes for an uninsured mortgage. The manual states 25 years for an insured mortgage, and the band of 30 to 35 years.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A practice item is expressly limited to the course manual. A buyer puts more than 20 percent down, the mortgage is not insured, and the item asks what the manual says government rules allow for the amortization. Which selection is the exam answer?

一道练习题明确限定按课程手册作答。买方首付高于20%，按揭不需要保险。题目问手册说政府规则允许的摊还期是什么。按考试口径，应当选哪一项？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是“旧上限当现行法”里拿错了那一段。25年是考试口径写给保险按揭的上限。本题首付高于20%、不需要保险，手册没有把这一段也写成25年封顶。Wrong. This picks the wrong band inside the trap of treating the old cap as current law. Twenty-five years is the maximum the exam wording gives an insured mortgage. On this item the down payment is above 20 percent and insurance is not required. The manual does not also cap this band at 25 years.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是“旧上限当现行法”陷阱，用现行定性去改手册。现行确实没有联邦法定上限，但手册把这一段写成了政府规则，而且写了30到35年。题目要的是手册怎么说。Wrong. This is the trap of treating the old cap as current law, and it rewrites the manual with the current characterization. There is indeed no federal statutory maximum now, but the manual writes this band as a government rule, and it writes 30 to 35 years. The item asks what the manual says.

  3. ✗ 错误。这是“旧上限当现行法”里把数字搬错了位。保险按揭在手册里是25年，不是30年。首付高于20%这一段，手册写的是政府规则允许30到35年，不是把保险按揭的年限再搬过来。Wrong. This moves the figures into the wrong place inside the trap of treating the old cap as current law. An insured mortgage in the manual is 25 years, not 30 years. For a down payment above 20 percent, the manual says government rules allow 30 to 35 years. It does not carry the insured-mortgage figure over to this band.

  4. ✓ 正确。题目限定课程手册，就要复述考试口径：首付达到或超过20%的非保险按揭，政府规则允许30到35年。现行规则不同，这一段没有联邦法定上限，年限由贷款机构自定，不能把现行说法填进这道历史题。Correct. The item is limited to the course manual, so the exam wording must be restated: an uninsured mortgage with a down payment of 20 percent or more is allowed an amortization of 30 to 35 years by government rules. The current rule is different. That band has no federal statutory maximum, and the lender sets the period. The current statement cannot be written into this historical item.

第 7 题Q7多还缩短摊还省利息Paying more shortens the amortization and saves interest★陷阱：加速还款方向反了

On a constant-payment mortgage, a borrower pays more than the minimum payment required by the schedule. The borrower asks what the extra amount does to the life of the loan and to total interest. Which statement matches the course?

在一笔等额本息按揭上，借款人付的比计划要求的最低还款额更多。借款人问：多出来的这部分对贷款还要还多久、以及对总利息有什么影响。哪一项符合课程？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是“加速还款方向反了”之外又把名字叫错。尾款（final payment）才是完全摊还最后那笔略小于常规还款的一期。本题是在还款过程中比最低额多付，不是最后一期的名称。Wrong. This uses the wrong name, on top of reversing the direction of an accelerated payment. The final payment is the last instalment of full amortization, slightly smaller than a regular payment. This item is paying more than the minimum during the life of the loan. It is not the name of the last instalment.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是“加速还款方向反了”陷阱。比最低额多付是缩短摊还期、少付总利息，不是把更大的还款摊到更多期上、让总利息变贵。Wrong. This is the trap of reversing the direction of an accelerated payment. Paying more than the minimum shortens the amortization and reduces total interest. It does not spread a larger payment over more periods and make total interest more expensive.

  3. ✓ 正确。加速还款（accelerated payment）就是比最低还款额多付，从而缩短摊还期、少付利息。它改变的是还款节奏和总利息，不是把期末余额改成一大笔未还本金。Correct. An accelerated payment is paying more than the minimum payment, and that shortens the amortization and saves interest. What it changes is the payment rhythm and the total interest. It does not turn the balance at the end into a large unpaid principal.

  4. ✗ 错误。这是“加速还款方向反了”陷阱。多付最低额以上的钱，不是把未付利息加进本金。结欠余额（outstanding balance）会因为多还本金而降得更快。余额不减反增是负摊还，方向相反。Wrong. This is the trap of reversing the direction of an accelerated payment. Money paid above the minimum is not unpaid interest added to principal. The outstanding balance falls faster because more principal is repaid. A balance that rises instead of falling is negative amortization, and that runs in the opposite direction.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A borrower adds an amount above the minimum on each payment and worries that the loan will last longer and cost more interest because the payment got larger. Which statement matches the course?

借款人在每期最低还款之外又多付一笔，并担心：因为还款变大，贷款会拖得更久、总利息也会更多。哪一项符合课程？

  1. ✓ 正确。比最低还款额多付，称为加速还款（accelerated payment）。效果是缩短摊还期、少付利息，不是把贷款拖长、把总利息抬高。Correct. Paying more than the minimum payment is called an accelerated payment. The effect is to shorten the amortization and pay less interest. It does not stretch the loan or raise total interest.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是“加速还款方向反了”陷阱。更大的还款如果是在最低额之上多付，摊还期变短，总利息变少。Wrong. This is the trap of reversing the direction of an accelerated payment. If the larger payment is an amount paid above the minimum, the amortization becomes shorter and total interest becomes smaller.

  3. ✗ 错误。这是“加速还款方向反了”里把名字换成了尾款。尾款（final payment）是完全摊还结束时略小的最后一期，不是还款中途在最低额之上多付的那一笔。Wrong. This replaces the name with the final payment inside the trap of reversing the direction of an accelerated payment. The final payment is the slightly smaller last instalment when full amortization ends. It is not an amount paid above the minimum partway through the loan.

  4. ✗ 错误。这是“加速还款方向反了”陷阱。多付出去的钱不会加进结欠。负摊还是月供不够付利息时余额不减反增，和主动在最低额之上多付不是一回事。Wrong. This is the trap of reversing the direction of an accelerated payment. Money paid extra is not added to the balance. Negative amortization is a balance that rises instead of falling when the payment is not enough to cover the interest. It is a different thing from choosing to pay more than the minimum.
