本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 方向题：题干说明待估物业和可比案例谁多了车库、卫浴或地，要你决定加减、以及改谁的价格。真正考的是待估物业不动，只把可比案例调向它。Direction questions: the stem says which of the subject property and the comparable has the extra garage, bathroom, or lot, and you decide whether to add or subtract, and whose price changes. What is tested is that the subject property stays fixed, and only the comparable is adjusted toward it.
  • 顺序题：同一笔成交里既有低于市场利率的卖方融资，又有房间或地块差异，问先处理哪一步，或先算物理百分比会把基数怎样带偏。考的是先把成交价里的交易噪音洗掉。Sequence questions: the same sale has both below-market vendor financing and a room or lot difference. The question asks which step comes first, or how working the physical percentage first pulls the base off. What is tested is washing the transaction noise out of the sale price first.
  • 可靠性题：给出净调整和总调整，或给出几个调整后的指示价值。考的是净额对抵不等于可比、总调整一般不超过成交价的两成，以及收口时不把几个数字平均。Reliability questions: the stem gives the net adjustment and the gross adjustment, or several indicated values after adjustment. What is tested is that amounts which cancel to a net do not make the sale comparable, that the gross adjustment generally does not exceed 20% of the sale price, and that the close does not average the figures.
  • 成本概念题：在重置和重建、现行和历史之间选一个，或把折旧放进四个格子并用直线法。考的是用评估日的功能等价成本，可治愈只看修复划不划算。Cost-concept questions: choose between replacement and reproduction, or between current and historic, or place depreciation in the four cells and apply the straight-line method. What is tested is the functionally equivalent cost on the effective date, and that curable looks only at whether the repair is worth the cost.
  • 适用题：独立屋、收益型大楼、教堂、保险要拆开土地和建筑物、单个共管单元，各走哪条路。共管还要加问共同区域和费用怎么分。考的是方法的边界。Fit questions: a detached house, an income building, a church, an insurance assignment that must split land and building, and a single strata unit, and which path each one takes. For a strata unit, two further questions are added: common property, and how the fees are shared. What is tested is the boundary of each method.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 方向写反The direction is reversed

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到待估物业更好，就去改待估物业，或从可比案例的价格里把这项优势减掉。方向被记成谁好谁加价，基准也被记反。Seeing that the subject is better, the subject itself is changed, or that advantage is subtracted from the comparable's price. The direction is remembered as adding to whoever is better, and the benchmark is remembered the wrong way around.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写明待估物业多了某项，或可比案例多了某项，问这笔钱加到谁的价格上、是加还是减。The stem states that the subject has an extra item, or that the comparable has an extra item, and asks whose price the money is added to, and whether it is an addition or a subtraction.

⚠ 顺序颠倒The sequence is reversed

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 房间和地块看得见，卖方融资藏在合同里，于是先算物理差异。百分比就落在被低息融资抬高的成交价上，被这个基数拉低估。Rooms and lots are visible, while vendor financing is hidden in the contract, so the physical differences are calculated first. The percentage then falls on a sale price inflated by below-market financing, and that base pulls the percentage too low.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 同一可比案例既有低于市场利率的卖方融资，又有卫浴、地块或装修差异，问先做哪一步。The same comparable has both below-market vendor financing and a bathroom, lot, or finish difference, and the question asks which step comes first.

⚠ 净额为零就可比A zero net means comparable

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 加减抵消以后净调整是零，看起来像一对完美的可比案例。两笔很大的正负调整可以刚好抵消，总调整仍然说明两处不是同一类物业。After the additions and subtractions cancel, the net adjustment is zero, and the pair looks like a perfect comparable. Two large positive and negative adjustments can cancel exactly, and the gross adjustment still shows that the two properties are not the same kind.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出净调整和总调整，净额很小或为零，总调整占成交价的比例却很高。The stem gives both the net adjustment and the gross adjustment. The net is small or zero, while the gross adjustment is a high share of the sale price.

⚠ 发票当成市场The invoice is the market

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 业主拿得出装修发票或当年造价，数字具体，就被当成调整额或成本法的起点。市场未必愿意为这项功能付到成本那么多，过去的花费也不是评估日的现行重置成本。The owner can produce a renovation invoice or the construction cost from that year. The figure is specific, so it is taken as the adjustment or as the starting point of the cost approach. The market may not be willing to pay as much as that cost for the feature, and past spending is not current replacement cost on the effective date.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现装修花了多少、当年建造或复刻要花多少，并要求按发票或按一模一样的复制品计价。The stem states what a renovation cost, or what it cost to build or to reproduce in that year, and asks for a value based on the invoice or on an exact replica.

⚠ 三个数取平均Average the three figures

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 几个指示价值不一样，最省事的是加总再除。调整很粗糙的案例和有配对销售支持的案例就被赋了同样的权重。When several indicated values differ, the easiest step is to add them and divide. A sale with very rough adjustments and a sale supported by paired sales are then given the same weight.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出两个以上调整后的指示价值，问最终意见是平均、取最低，还是选最可靠的那个。The stem gives two or more indicated values after adjustment, and asks whether the final opinion is the average, the lowest, or the most reliable one.

正式估价和CMA不能混A formal appraisal and a Comparative Market Analysis are not the sameComparative Market Analysis执业估价师受 CUSPAP 约束并出具正式估价报告，持牌人的比较市场分析不受该准则约束，不能当成正式估价；转让登记上的申报值也不是可比交易的证据。A practising appraiser is bound by CUSPAP and issues a formal appraisal report. A licensee's Comparative Market Analysis is not bound by that standard and cannot be treated as a formal appraisal. The declared value on a transfer registration is not evidence of a comparable sale.
近期和相似都有定义Recent and similar both have definitionscomparables近期不是固定月数，而是评估日前这一市场供需仍相对稳定的时段，不能用通用价格指数调整某一套房子；相似只要求影响出价的因素相近，临街宽度通常比进深更重要，三点检验都过才进入调整，考试口径建议至少四个可比案例。Recent is not a fixed number of months. It is the period before the effective date when supply and demand in that market stayed relatively stable. A general price index cannot be used to adjust one house. Similar requires only that the factors which affect the offer be close. Frontage is usually more important than depth. All three tests must be passed before a sale enters adjustment. The exam wording suggests at least four comparables.
只调可比案例的价格Adjust only the comparable's pricesubject property待估物业是基准，只调整可比案例的成交价：待估物业某项更好就把市场贡献加到可比案例上，可比案例某项更好就从它的成交价里减去，调整额用市场证据而不是装修成本。The subject property is the benchmark. Only the comparable's sale price is adjusted. If the subject is better on an item, add that market contribution to the comparable. If the comparable is better on an item, subtract it from the comparable's sale price. The adjustment uses market evidence, not renovation cost.
先去噪音再比物理Remove the noise before comparing physical featurescash equivalency调整顺序是融资的现金等价、交易条件、市场条件、最后才是物理特征；总调整一般不应超过成交价的 20%，卖方融资类调整可以例外，净调整为零仍可能只是巨大正负项互相抵消。The adjustment order is cash equivalency of the financing, conditions of sale, market conditions, and physical features last. The gross adjustment generally should not exceed 20% of the sale price. Vendor-financing adjustments can be an exception. A net adjustment of zero can still be only large positive and negative items cancelling each other.
指示价值不取平均Do not average the indicated valuesindicated value案例一的三个指示价值是 $852,000、$877,600 和 $837,100，收口时主要采用总调整比例最低、且关键物理调整有配对销售支持的那个，取整为 $878,000，而不是三个数字的平均数。In the first worked example the three indicated values are $852,000, $877,600, and $837,100. The close relies mainly on the one with the lowest gross-adjustment ratio whose key physical adjustment is supported by paired sales, and that figure is rounded to $878,000. It is not the average of the three figures.
共管要多核对两组A strata comparison needs two extra checksdirect comparison approach直接比较法最适合成交多的独立屋和公寓，较弱于罕见物业、剧烈波动的市场和收益型物业；共管单元还要共同区域权益相似，并且共同费用的分摊方式与项目相似，两问都答是才能互相当可比案例。The direct comparison approach fits best for detached houses and apartments with many sales. It is weaker for rare property, a sharply moving market, and income property. Strata units also need similar rights in the common property, and common expenses that are shared in a similar way and cover similar items. Both questions must be answered yes before the units can be comparables for each other.
成本法用重置和现行The cost approach uses current replacement costreplacement cost只有现状代表最高最佳用途时，土地价值加建筑物重置成本减折旧才约等于市场价值；成本法用评估日的现行重置成本，不用重建成本和历史成本，土地价值通常仍靠直接比较法。Only when the existing use is the highest and best use does site value plus building replacement cost minus depreciation approximate market value. The cost approach uses current replacement cost on the effective date. It does not use reproduction cost or historic cost. Site value is usually still found by the direct comparison approach.
可治愈只看划不划算Curable depreciation looks only at whether the repair payscurable depreciation修复成本不高于因此增加的价值才是可治愈，扣的是修复成本；高于则不可治愈，扣估价师的专业判断，物理不可治愈常用重置成本除以经济寿命再乘有效年龄，有效年龄可以不等于实际年龄。An item is curable depreciation only when the repair cost is not higher than the value the repair adds, and the deduction is the repair cost. If the repair cost is higher, the item is incurable, and the deduction is the appraiser's professional judgment. Incurable physical depreciation is commonly replacement cost divided by economic life, then multiplied by effective age. Effective age need not equal actual age.
配对销售孤立一项差异Paired sales analysis isolates one differencepaired sales analysis两笔其余条件相同、且不需要时间调整的近期成交，价差就是那一项特征的市场贡献；课程示例中 $718,000 与 $700,000 的差额 $18,000 被用作车库的调整额。Two recent sales that match in every other respect, and that need no time adjustment, have a price gap equal to the market contribution of that one feature. In the course example the difference between $718,000 and $700,000, which is $18,000, is used as the garage adjustment.
老旧住宅成本法要降权Give the cost approach less weight on an older housecost approach成本法最适合缺少可比交易的特殊用途建筑，以及保险理赔和物业税评估这类必须把土地与建筑物分开的场合；住宅市场价值里通常只作最后手段或交叉验证，物业越老越不可靠，单个共管单元几乎不用。The cost approach fits best for special-purpose buildings that lack comparable sales, and for assignments that must split land and building, such as an insurance claim or a property-tax assessment. For the market value of a house it is usually only a last resort or a cross-check. The older the property, the less reliable it is. It is almost never used for a single strata unit.

第 1 题Q1只把可比案例调向待估Adjust the comparable toward the subject only★陷阱：方向写反

Mei is adjusting one sale. The subject lot is larger. The comparable's lot is 500 square feet smaller, and market evidence supports $6,000 for that difference. No other item is being adjusted. What should Mei do?

Mei 正在调整一笔成交。待估物业的地块更大。可比案例的地块小 500 平方英尺，市场证据支持这项差异值 $6,000。没有其他项目要调。Mei 应当怎么做？

  1. ✗ 可比案例地块更小，是它劣于待估物业（subject property），不是它优于待估物业。劣的那一项要把缺失的市场贡献加回可比案例（comparable）的成交价。减 $6,000 是方向写反。The comparable's lot is smaller, so the comparable is inferior to the subject property, not superior to it. For the inferior item, the missing market contribution is added back to the comparable's sale price. Subtracting $6,000 reverses the direction.

  2. ✓ 待估物业（subject property）是基准，物理属性不能改，只能把可比案例（comparable）的成交价调向它。可比案例少 500 平方英尺，所以加上市场证据支持的 $6,000。The subject property is the benchmark. Its physical attributes cannot be changed. Only the comparable's sale price is adjusted toward it. The comparable is short 500 square feet, so the $6,000 supported by market evidence is added.

  3. ✗ 调整不加在待估物业（subject property）上。待估物业是比较基准，发生变化的是可比案例（comparable）的成交价。给待估物业加 $6,000，等于改了基准本身。这是方向写反。The adjustment is not placed on the subject property. The subject property is the benchmark of the comparison, and what changes is the comparable's sale price. Adding $6,000 to the subject changes the benchmark itself. This reverses the direction.

  4. ✗ 先限定在同一街区，是为了让周边设施这类差异可以先放下，从而少比几组因素。可比案例的地块已经小了 500 平方英尺，而且市场证据支持 $6,000，这项差异仍然要调。同一街区不是跳过已识别差异的理由。Limiting the search to the same block first lets differences such as nearby amenities be set aside, so fewer groups of factors have to be compared. The comparable's lot is already 500 square feet smaller, and market evidence supports $6,000, so that difference is still adjusted. The same block is not a reason to skip a difference that has already been identified.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Harpreet is adjusting a different sale. The comparable has one more half-bathroom than the subject. Market evidence supports $9,000 for that half-bathroom. What should Harpreet do?

Harpreet 正在调整另一笔成交。可比案例比待估物业多一个半卫浴。市场证据支持这个半卫浴值 $9,000。Harpreet 应当怎么做？

  1. ✗ 多出来的半卫浴在可比案例（comparable）身上，这笔成交价里已经包含了这项优势。应当把 $9,000 减掉，才能和没有这个半卫浴的待估物业相比。加回去是方向写反。The extra half-bathroom is on the comparable, so this sale price already includes that advantage. The $9,000 should be subtracted before the sale is compared with a subject property that does not have that half-bathroom. Adding it back reverses the direction.

  2. ✗ 待估物业（subject property）的卫浴数量不能在调整表里被改掉。$9,000 作用在可比案例的成交价上。去改待估物业，是把基准调向了可比案例。The subject property's bathroom count cannot be changed on the adjustment grid. The $9,000 acts on the comparable's sale price. Changing the subject property moves the benchmark toward the comparable.

  3. ✗ 基准的意思是待估物业（subject property）不动，可比案例向它靠拢。从待估物业减去 $9,000，方向和对象都错了。多出的半卫浴属于可比案例（comparable）。Benchmark means the subject property stays fixed and the comparable moves toward it. Subtracting $9,000 from the subject gets both the direction and the object wrong. The extra half-bathroom belongs to the comparable.

  4. ✓ 可比案例（comparable）多一个半卫浴，优于待估物业（subject property），成交价里已经含有这项溢价。把市场证据支持的 $9,000 从可比案例的价格里减去，它才和待估物业可比。The comparable has one more half-bathroom, so it is superior to the subject property, and the sale price already contains that premium. Subtract the $9,000 supported by market evidence from the comparable's price, and the comparable can then be compared with the subject.

第 2 题Q2调整顺序不能颠倒The adjustment sequence cannot be reversed★★★陷阱：顺序颠倒

Luis has one comparable. Its contract price includes a below-market vendor take-back mortgage, and the cash-equivalency adjustment is a deduction of $12,000. The house also differs physically, and Luis wants those differences as a percentage of price. He applies the physical percentage first and removes the financing premium afterward. What is wrong?

Luis 有一个可比案例。合同价里包含低于市场利率的卖方回按，现金等价调整是减去 $12,000。房子在物理特征上也不相同，Luis 想把这些差异做成价格的百分比。他先套用物理百分比，之后才去掉融资溢价。问题在哪里？

  1. ✓ 调整顺序的第一步是融资的现金等价（cash equivalency）。低于市场利率的卖方融资把成交价抬高了 $12,000，要先扣掉。先在这个虚高价格上计算物理特征的百分比，百分比会被虚高的基数拉低估。物理比较要建立在干净价格上。The first step in the adjustment order is cash equivalency of the financing. Below-market vendor financing inflated the sale price by $12,000, and that amount is deducted first. Working the physical-feature percentage on that inflated price pulls the percentage too low because of the inflated base. The physical comparison has to rest on a clean price.

  2. ✗ 卖方融资（vendor take-back mortgage, VTB）是融资条件，不是房间或地块。它排在物理特征之前。先做物理、后做融资，就是顺序颠倒，百分比的基数还是那份虚高的合同价。A vendor take-back mortgage (VTB) is a financing term, not a room or a lot. It comes before physical features. Doing the physical items first and the financing afterward reverses the sequence, and the base of the percentage is still that inflated contract price.

  3. ✗ 低于市场利率的卖方融资是买家额外拿到的好处，成交价偏高，所以从可比案例里减去 $12,000。把它理解成可比案例更差、还要再加 $12,000，是方向写反，融资溢价会被加两次。Below-market vendor financing is an extra benefit the buyer received, so the sale price is high, and $12,000 is subtracted from the comparable. Reading the comparable as inferior, and then adding another $12,000, reverses the direction, and the financing premium would be added twice.

  4. ✗ 百分比的分母就是当时采用的价格。分母里还留着 $12,000 的融资溢价，算出来的物理百分比会被这个虚高基数拉低估。顺序会改变百分比，不是可有可无的记账习惯。The denominator of the percentage is the price being used at that moment. If the $12,000 financing premium is still in the denominator, the physical percentage that comes out is pulled too low by that inflated base. The order changes the percentage. It is not an optional bookkeeping habit.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A comparable has below-market vendor financing, the seller is the buyer's employer, the sale closed well before the effective date, and the house has one less bathroom than the subject. In which order does the course process these four differences?

一个可比案例有低于市场利率的卖方融资，卖方是买方的雇主，成交日远早于评估日，而且这栋房子比待估物业少一个卫浴。课程按什么顺序处理这四项差异？

  3. ✓ 保养特别好的老房子，有效年龄（effective age）可以小于实际年龄。物理不可治愈的直线法（straight-line）用有效年龄去乘每年的折旧额，不自动用日历上的建成年数。课程那栋房子是因为保养正常，才把两个年龄看成一致。On an older house with especially good upkeep, effective age can be below actual age. The straight-line method for incurable physical depreciation multiplies the depreciation per year by effective age. It does not automatically use the years since construction on the calendar. The course treats the two ages as the same on its house only because upkeep was ordinary.

  4. ✗ 维修完成以后，应当归零的是可治愈那两格。不可治愈折旧不会因为做了油漆就消失，而要重新评估有效年龄。这一项把归零的格子说反了。After the repairs are finished, the two cells that go to zero are the curable ones. Incurable depreciation does not disappear because the paint was done. Effective age has to be estimated again. This item names the wrong cells as the ones that go to zero.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

第 7 题Q7共管比较要多答两问A strata comparison must answer two further questions★★陷阱：户型一样就能比

Priya is comparing two strata units. Layout, floor area, and building age are close. One building has a gym and a pool. The other has neither. In one building, heat is inside the common fee and the strata owns a vacant parcel of common property. In the other, heat is billed separately and there is no such parcel. Which statement matches the course?

Priya 在比较两个共管单元。户型、面积和楼龄都接近。一栋楼有健身房和泳池，另一栋没有。其中一栋的共同费用包含供暖，而且名下有一块尚未开发的公共土地；另一栋供暖单独计费，也没有这块地。哪一项符合课程？

  1. ✗ 共管单元只看户型、面积和楼龄还不够。还要核对共同区域的权益是否相似，以及共同费用的分摊方式和项目是否相似。健身房、泳池、供暖由谁承担、名下有没有公共土地，都在这两组里。这是户型一样就能比。Layout, floor area, and building age are not enough for a strata unit. The rights in the common property also have to be checked for similarity, and so do the way common expenses are shared and whether the items are similar. A gym, a pool, who bears the heat, and whether common land is held in the building's name all sit in those two groups. This treats a matching layout as enough to compare.

  2. ✗ 单个共管单元几乎不用成本法（cost approach）。整栋楼的地价、成本、软成本和利润要全部算清再分摊，而且单元估价师通常看不到屋顶、机房和地下车库。有了成交，应当走直接比较，并先做那两组额外核对。A single strata unit almost never uses the cost approach. The land price, the costs, the soft costs, and the profit of the whole building all have to be worked out and then allocated, and the appraiser of the unit usually cannot see the roof, the mechanical room, and the underground garage. Once there is a sale, the path is direct comparison, and those two extra checks come first.

  3. ✓ 共管公寓做直接比较（direct comparison），两组问题都要回答是：共同区域的权益是否相似，共同费用的分摊方式与项目是否相似。一边有健身房、泳池和未开发的公共土地，供暖还打进共同费用，另一边都没有，现在还不能把它们当成彼此的可比案例。Direct comparison of a strata apartment requires both groups of questions to be answered yes: whether the rights in the common property are similar, and whether the way common expenses are shared and the items in them are similar. One side has a gym, a pool, and undeveloped common land, and heat is inside the common fee. The other side has none of these. They cannot yet be treated as comparables for each other.

  4. ✗ 共同费用怎么分、共同区域名下有没有资产或负债，正是共管比较必须额外核对的内容，不是直接比较法（direct comparison approach）不管的事。只按面积调整，等于没有回答那两问。How common expenses are shared, and whether the common property holds assets or liabilities, are exactly the extra checks a strata comparison must make. They are not matters outside the direct comparison approach. Adjusting only for floor area leaves those two questions unanswered.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Two strata units match in layout and outlook. Both buildings have similar common facilities. Both share common expenses on the same basis. Neither building holds extra land or a mortgage against the common property. What does the course say about the two extra tests?

两个共管单元的户型和朝向都接近。两栋楼的公共设施相似，共同费用也按同样的方式分摊。两栋楼的共同区域都没有额外土地，也没有背着按揭。课程怎么看这两组额外检验？

  1. ✓ 共同区域权益相似，共同费用的分摊方式和项目也相似，两组额外问题都答了是，才可以在这个层面上把两个共管单元当可比案例（comparables）。只靠户型和朝向接近，课程认为还不够。The rights in the common property are similar, and the way common expenses are shared and the items in them are also similar, so both extra questions are answered yes. Only then may the two strata units be treated as comparables on this point. The course holds that a close layout and outlook alone are not enough.

  2. ✗ 两组额外检验已经都答是，直接比较可以继续往下做。单个共管单元几乎不靠把整栋楼的成本分摊来估值。再要求一份整栋楼的成本法，是换了一条课程不用的路径。Both extra tests have already been answered yes, so direct comparison can continue. A single strata unit is almost never valued by allocating the cost of the whole building. Requiring a cost approach on the whole building switches to a path the course does not use.

  3. ✗ 直接比较法最适合同类成交多的公寓单位。共管单元可以互相当可比案例，条件是近期、相似，再加上共同区域和共同费用这两组都相似。已经满足的配对，不能因为物业类型是共管就被整类排除。The direct comparison approach fits best for apartment units that have many similar sales. Strata units can be comparables for each other when they are recent and similar, and when both the common-property group and the common-expense group are similar. A pair that already meets those conditions cannot be excluded as a class because the property type is strata.

  4. ✗ 课程要求两个问题都回答是，不是答对其中一个就够。户型接近，不能代替共同区域权益，也不能代替共同费用怎么分。跳过任何一组，就是户型一样就能比。The course requires both questions to be answered yes. One yes is not enough. A close layout cannot stand in for rights in the common property, and it cannot stand in for how common expenses are shared. Skipping either group treats a matching layout as enough to compare.
