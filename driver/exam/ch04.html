① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 情景判断型：给出饮酒或服药后的日常驾驶场景（如喝咖啡醒酒、次日清晨通勤、朋友聚会喝半杯啤酒），考查唯一能降低血液酒精浓度的因素（时间）及新手零容忍规则。Scenario judgement questions: Present everyday driving scenarios after drinking or consuming drugs (such as drinking coffee to sober up, commuting the next morning, or having half a glass of beer at a gathering), testing the only factor that lowers blood alcohol concentration (time) and the novice zero-tolerance rule.
  • 阈值与阶梯型：对比 0.05（WARN）与 0.08（FAIL）的血液酒精浓度门槛，考查即时路边禁驾（IRP）对应的禁驾天数、扣车天数及拒测同档原则。Threshold and escalation questions: Compare the blood alcohol concentration thresholds of 0.05 (WARN) and 0.08 (FAIL), testing the number of driving prohibition days and vehicle impoundment days under an Immediate Roadside Prohibition (IRP), as well as the principle that refusing a breath test equals a FAIL.
  • 新手限制与重置型：针对 L 牌、N 牌或带限制 55 的驾驶者，考查检出任何酒精或药物时的当场 12 小时停牌（不可复核）以及后续禁驾导致 N 阶段计时清零的机制。Novice restrictions and reset questions: Target Learner (L), Novice (N), or Class 5 drivers with Restriction 55, testing the immediate 12-hour roadside suspension (non-reviewable) upon detecting any alcohol or drugs and the mechanism where a subsequent driving prohibition resets the N-stage timeline to zero.
  • 违规成本与连带责任型：结合分心驾驶、超速超过 40 km/h（excessive speed）等场景，考查记分点（4 分即触发 DPP 保费）、无车照常收账单、以及非车主驾驶超速时由车主承担扣车拖管费的规则。Violation costs and joint liability questions: Combine scenarios such as distracted driving and excessive speeding (more than 40 km/h over the limit), testing penalty points (4 points triggering a DPP premium), billing drivers even without vehicle ownership, and the rule that registered owners must pay vehicle towing and impound fees when a non-owner speeds.
  • 制度逻辑辨析型：考查省级行政处罚（IRP/ADP，警察路边当场作出、无需法院定罪）与联邦刑事追诉（需法院定罪）两套系统并行且互不豁免的关系。System framework distinction questions: Test the relationship between provincial administrative penalties (IRP/ADP, issued immediately roadside by police without requiring a court conviction) and federal criminal prosecution (requiring a court conviction) as two parallel systems that do not exempt each other.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 不到0.08就没事陷阱Assuming under 0.08 means no penalty

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常把联邦刑法的 0.08 犯罪门槛当成唯一红线，忽略了 BC 省从 0.05（WARN）就开始执行路边当场禁驾、扣车与罚款，而 L/N 新手更是零容忍。Candidates often treat the federal Criminal Code 0.08 criminal threshold as the only red line, overlooking that BC enforces immediate roadside driving prohibitions, vehicle impoundments, and penalties starting at 0.05 (WARN), while L and N novice drivers face zero tolerance.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「只喝了一两杯」「肯定不到 0.08」或 L/N 牌驾驶者体内检出微量酒精、THC。The question stem mentions having only one or two drinks, being certainly under 0.08, or detecting a trace of alcohol or THC in an L or N driver.

⚠ 醒酒偏方有效陷阱Believing sobering remedies work

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉认为喝浓咖啡、冲冷水澡、呼吸新鲜空气或运动能加快醒酒，实际上这些做法只提升主观警觉度，完全不会加快肝脏代谢酒精的固定速率。Intuitively assuming that drinking strong coffee, taking a cold shower, breathing fresh air, or exercising will speed up sobering, whereas these practices only increase subjective alertness and do not accelerate the liver's fixed rate of metabolizing alcohol.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干询问饮酒后如何「更快安全开车」或选项出现 coffee、cold shower、fresh air、exercise。The question stem asks how to drive safely sooner after drinking, or options include coffee, cold shower, fresh air, or exercise.

⚠ 拒测逃避重罚陷阱Refusing a test to avoid severe penalties

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为不吹气警察就没有读数证据，殊不知拒绝提供呼气样本在行政与记分上直接等同于最高档 FAIL（禁驾 90 天、扣车 30 天）。Mistakenly believing that refusing to blow leaves police without breath reading evidence, unaware that refusing to provide a breath sample legally equals the highest FAIL tier in administrative penalties and penalty points (90-day prohibition, 30-day impoundment).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述驾驶者在路边被要求使用筛查仪（ASD）时拒绝配合或借故拖延。The question stem describes a driver refusing to cooperate or stalling when asked to provide a sample into an Approved Screening Device (ASD) at the roadside.

⚠ 禁驾与扣车天数同步陷阱Assuming prohibition and impoundment days are identical

为什么会错：Why people miss it: WARN 档的禁驾与扣车天数一致（3/7/30 天），导致考生误以为 FAIL 档也是禁驾 90 天配扣车 90 天，或混淆超速扣车（7/30/60 天）与酒驾扣车阶梯。The prohibition and impoundment days match for WARN tiers (3, 7, 30 days), leading candidates to assume FAIL also pairs a 90-day prohibition with a 90-day impoundment, or confusing excessive speeding impoundment ladders (7, 30, 60 days) with alcohol impoundment tiers.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干询问吹出 FAIL（0.08 及以上）、拒测或超速 40 km/h 以上的具体禁驾与车辆扣押天数。The question stem asks for specific driving prohibition and vehicle impoundment days for blowing FAIL (0.08 or higher), refusing a test, or speeding over 40 km/h.

⚠ 罚单即全部成本陷阱Believing the ticket is the only cost

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为交完罚单金额就了事，忽略了 12 个月超 3 分即触发 DPP 保费（分心驾驶一张 4 分罚单即超线，无车也照收）以及借车给他人超速由车主付拖管费。Mistakenly believing paying the ticket amount settles everything, ignoring that accumulating more than 3 penalty points in 12 months triggers DPP premiums (a single 4-point distracted driving ticket crosses the line, billed even without a car), and lending a vehicle to someone who speeds leaves the owner paying towing and storage fees.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「名下没车」「第一次使用手机被抓」或「借车给朋友开出超速 41 km/h 以上」。The question stem mentions owning no vehicle, being caught using a phone for the first time, or lending a car to a friend who drives 41 km/h or more over the limit.

第 1 题Q1GLP新手驾驶者酒精与药物零容忍Zero Tolerance for GLP Novice Drivers Regarding Alcohol and Drugs★★陷阱：不到0.08就没事陷阱

Kevin holds a Class 7 Novice (N) licence and has driven safely for 23 months without incident. At a dinner party, he drinks half a glass of beer and is stopped at a police checkstop where a tiny amount of alcohol (below 0.05) is detected. What happens to Kevin?

Kevin 持有 7 级新驾驶者（N）驾照，已安全无事故驾驶了 23 个月。他在晚宴上喝了半杯啤酒，随后在警方路检点被拦下，检出微量酒精（低于 0.05）。Kevin 会面临什么后果？

  1. ✗ 落入「不到0.08就没事陷阱」。0.05 是普通全牌照驾驶者的警告区间（Warn range）门槛，而分级驾照（GLP）的 L、N 阶段驾驶者实行零容忍（zero tolerance），体内不能有任何可检出的酒精或药物。Falls into the trap of assuming under 0.08 means no penalty. While 0.05 is the Warn range threshold for fully licensed drivers, Graduated Licensing Program (GLP) drivers in the L and N stages are subject to zero tolerance, prohibiting any detectable alcohol or drugs.

  2. ✓ 正确。GLP 的 L、N 驾驶者检出任何量的酒精、THC 或可卡因，当场处以 12 小时停牌（12-hour suspension，不可申请复核）；随后 RoadSafetyBC 审查记录若加处禁驾（driving prohibition），之前攒下的 23 个月无禁驾期全部归零，N 阶段从头再算。Correct. GLP drivers in the L and N stages who have any detectable alcohol, THC, or cocaine receive an immediate 12-hour suspension that cannot be appealed; if RoadSafetyBC subsequently reviews their record and imposes a driving prohibition, the 23 accumulated prohibition-free months are reset to zero, restarting the N stage from the beginning.

  3. ✗ 混淆了 WARN 首次禁驾与 GLP 零容忍规则。低于 0.05 时当场是 12 小时停牌而非 3 天禁驾；且一旦后续被加处禁驾，N 阶段是整个计时从头重算，而不是只扣一个月。Confuses a first-time WARN prohibition with the GLP zero-tolerance rule. When below 0.05, the immediate roadside penalty is a 12-hour suspension rather than a 3-day prohibition; furthermore, if a prohibition is subsequently added, the entire N-stage timeline is restarted rather than deducting only one month.

  4. ✗ 记错复核规则。讲义明确指出当场的 12 小时停牌（12-hour suspension）不可申请复核，没有申辩渠道。Mistakes the review rules. An immediate 12-hour suspension cannot be appealed or reviewed, leaving no formal dispute avenue.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Sonia is a Learner (L) driver who smoked cannabis earlier in the evening. At a roadside check, police detect a trace of THC in her system. Which consequence applies to Sonia?

Sonia 是一名学习驾照（L 牌）驾驶者，傍晚早些时候吸食了大麻。在路边检查中，警方在她体内检出微量 THC。Sonia 会受到什么处罚？

  1. ✗ 落入「不到0.08就没事陷阱」的药物版。5 ng/mL 是一般驾驶者触发省级行政禁驾（ADP）的门槛，但 L 牌和 N 牌驾驶者对酒精、THC 和可卡因实行零容忍（任何可检出的量均违规）。Falls into the drug version of assuming that under 0.08 means no penalty. While 5 ng/mL is the threshold for triggering a provincial Administrative Driving Prohibition (ADP) for experienced drivers, L and N drivers face zero tolerance for alcohol, THC, and cocaine, where any detectable amount is a violation.

  2. ✗ 混淆处罚措施。L 牌驾驶者违反零容忍规定时必须当场交出驾照并接受 12 小时停牌，而不是只交罚款继续开车。Confuses penalties. An L driver who violates zero-tolerance rules must immediately surrender her driver's licence and serve a 12-hour suspension, rather than simply paying a fine and driving away.

  3. ✗ 混淆扣车阶梯。60 天扣车适用于两年内第三次及以上的严重超速（excessive speed）等行为类违规，不是 L 牌首次检出微量物质的当场处罚。Confuses impoundment ladders. A 60-day vehicle impoundment applies to a third or subsequent behavioural offence such as excessive speeding within two years, not an immediate roadside penalty for an L driver with trace substances.

  4. ✓ 正确。L 阶段驾驶者检出任何量的酒精、THC 或可卡因，当场 12 小时停牌（12-hour suspension）并交出驾照；若 RoadSafetyBC 随后加处禁驾，L 阶段驾驶者须重新参加全部测试。Correct. L-stage drivers with any detectable alcohol, THC, or cocaine must immediately surrender their licence for a 12-hour suspension; if RoadSafetyBC subsequently adds a driving prohibition, the learner must retake all L-stage tests.

第 2 题Q2酒精代谢唯一有效方式与次日残留Only Method to Metabolize Alcohol and Next-Morning Impairment★陷阱：醒酒偏方有效陷阱

After drinking several alcoholic beverages at an evening gathering, Marcus drinks two cups of strong black coffee and takes a cold shower before driving home. How do these actions affect his ability to drive safely?

在晚间聚会喝了几杯酒后，Marcus 喝了两杯浓黑咖啡并冲了个冷水澡，准备开车回家。这些做法对他安全驾驶的能力有什么影响？

  1. ✗ 落入「醒酒偏方有效陷阱」。酒精绝大部分由肝脏以近似固定的速率代谢，浓咖啡和冷水澡完全不能加快代谢或降低血液酒精浓度（Blood Alcohol Content, BAC）。Falls into the trap of believing sobering remedies work. The liver metabolizes the vast majority of alcohol at a relatively fixed rate, and strong coffee or cold showers cannot accelerate metabolism or lower blood alcohol content (BAC).

  2. ✗ 落入「醒酒偏方有效陷阱」。没有任何外部偏方能降低血液酒精浓度读数，只有时间（time）有效。Falls into the trap of believing sobering remedies work. No external remedy can reduce blood alcohol content readings; only time works.

  3. ✓ 正确。只有时间能降低血液酒精浓度（Blood Alcohol Content, BAC）。浓咖啡与冷水澡只提升主观警觉度而不降低酒精浓度，容易让人产生「感觉清醒、敢开车」的危险错觉。Correct. Only time can lower blood alcohol content (BAC). Strong coffee and cold showers only increase subjective alertness without reducing alcohol concentration, creating the dangerous illusion of feeling sober enough to drive.

  4. ✗ 错误。警方路边筛查仪（ASD）测定的是客观血液酒精浓度，喝咖啡或洗冷水澡不会带来任何法律豁免。Incorrect. Police Approved Screening Devices (ASDs) measure objective blood alcohol concentration, and drinking coffee or showering provides no legal exemption.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Elena drank heavily until midnight, slept for five hours, and now plans to drive to work at 6:00 a.m. after jogging in the fresh morning air. What should she know before driving?

Elena 深夜饮酒到午夜，睡了五个小时，早上 6 点在清新空气中慢跑后准备开车去上班。她在开车前应当知道什么？

  4. ✗ 错误。单独检出任何量的可卡因、冰毒、LSD 等即可直接触发省级 ADP，不需要同时饮酒。Incorrect. Detecting any amount of cocaine, methamphetamine, or LSD alone directly triggers a provincial ADP, with no requirement for alcohol consumption.
