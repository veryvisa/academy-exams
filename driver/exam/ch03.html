① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 场景识别题：描述雪天牌面被遮挡或远距离逆光场景，考察能否仅凭独一无二的几何轮廓（八角形、倒等边三角形、荧光黄绿五角形）直接判断最高优先级的驾驶指令。Scenario identification questions: Describe scenes where signs are obscured by snow or backlit by glare, testing whether you can identify the highest-priority driving instruction solely by its unique geometric outline (octagon, inverted equilateral triangle, fluorescent yellow-green pentagon).
  • 时段与条件对比题：把学校区（School zone）和游乐场区（Playground zone）的生效天数、起止时段及附牌（tab）规则混合设问，测试是否掌握「学校区看日历和钟、游乐场区看天光」。Timeframe and condition comparison questions: Mix the effective days, operating hours, and bottom tab rules for school zones and playground zones, testing whether you understand that school zones follow the calendar and clock while playground zones follow daylight.
  • BC 特有信号辨析题：针对闪动绿圆灯（Flashing green light）与闪动绿箭头（Advance green arrow）设置跨省认知干扰，考察在 BC 遇到不同绿灯形态时的正确应对。BC-specific signal distinction questions: Use interprovincial differences regarding flashing circular green lights and advance green arrows as distractors, testing your correct response when encountering different green signal types in BC.
  • 路口信号与优先权实操题：结合稳定黄灯、信号灯全灭故障、铁路道口红灯刚停闪或施工区有交通指挥员（Traffic control person）在场等情境，测试法定停车条件与指挥优先顺序。Intersection signals and right-of-way practical questions: Combine situations such as steady yellow lights, blacked-out traffic light failures, railway crossing red lights that have just stopped flashing, or having a traffic control person present in a construction zone to test statutory stopping conditions and command priority.
  • 标线跨越规则题：给出单黄实线、双黄实线或黄实线配虚线的路面组合，结合超车、左转进出私家车道或超越骑自行车的人等具体动作，考察标线颜色与虚实的精确法律边界。Pavement marking crossing rules: Present roadway combinations of single solid yellow lines, double solid yellow lines, or solid yellow lines paired with broken lines, combined with specific actions such as passing, turning left into or out of a private driveway, or passing a cyclist, to test the precise legal boundaries of marking colors and solid versus broken lines.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 学校区与游乐场区时段对调Swapping school zone and playground zone hours

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 两者限速都是 30 km/h 且都保护儿童，考生容易把「仅上课日 8:00–17:00」和「每天黎明到黄昏」混为一谈，或在周日忽略游乐场区限速。Both have a 30 km/h speed limit and protect children, making it easy to confuse school days only from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. with dawn to dusk every day, or to overlook playground zone limits on a Sunday.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 School zone 或 Playground zone，且提及周末（如周日下午）、非上课日或具体时段。The question stem mentions School zone or Playground zone along with weekends (such as Sunday afternoon), non-school days, or specific timeframes.

⚠ 把闪动绿圆灯当成提前左转或即将转红Mistaking a flashing circular green light for an advance left turn or an impending red light

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 外省（如安大略）用闪动绿圆灯表示允许提前左转，部分二手资料又误传它代表绿灯快结束，导致考生选减速停车或直接左转。Other provinces (such as Ontario) use a flashing circular green light to indicate an advance left turn, and some second-hand materials incorrectly claim it means the green light is about to end, leading candidates to choose slowing to a stop or making an immediate left turn.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 flashing green light（无 arrow 字样），询问该信号的含义或驶近路口时的做法。The question stem mentions a flashing green light (without the word arrow) and asks about the signal meaning or the proper action when approaching the intersection.

⚠ 认为所有实线都绝对不可跨越Assuming all solid lines can never be crossed under any circumstances

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把双黄实线禁止超车的规则泛化到一切实线，忽略了单黄实线允许格外谨慎地安全超车（含超越骑车人），以及双黄实线允许安全左转进出道路或私家车道。Generalizing the rule that double solid yellow lines prohibit passing to all solid lines, while overlooking that a single solid yellow line allows passing with extra caution (including passing a cyclist), and double solid yellow lines allow turning left into or out of a road or private driveway when safe.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 single solid yellow line、double solid yellow line，并搭配 passing a cyclist 或 turning left into a private driveway。The question stem mentions single solid yellow line or double solid yellow line paired with passing a cyclist or turning left into a private driveway.

⚠ 无人施工区不减速或纠结罚款精确倍数Failing to slow down in an empty construction zone or getting hung up on the exact fine multiplier

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为深夜没有工人在场时施工区限速不生效，或在考试作答时没有顺应考试资料「罚款加倍」与警示牌「最低罚款 $196」的标准口径。Mistakenly believing that construction speed limits do not apply late at night when no workers are present, or failing to align test answers with standard exam material stating 'fines are double' and warning signs showing a '$196 minimum fine.'

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 construction zone、no workers present（无人作业）或询问施工区超速罚款规则。The question stem mentions a construction zone, no workers present, or asks about construction zone speeding fine rules.

⚠ 红圈无斜杠误判为禁止、黄灯忽略安全条件Misinterpreting a red circle without a slash as a prohibition or ignoring the safety condition for a yellow light

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到红圈就条件反射选「禁止」，没先看是否有红斜杠开关；看到稳定黄灯只记「必须停车」而漏掉「除非已无法安全停下」的关键前提。Reflexively choosing 'prohibited' upon seeing a red circle without checking for a diagonal slash; or remembering only 'must stop' at a steady yellow light while omitting the critical condition 'unless unsafe to do so.'

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述 red circle without a slash（红圈无斜杠）或 steady yellow light（稳定黄灯）。The question stem describes a red circle without a slash or a steady yellow light.

BC 闪动绿色圆灯的专属含义Exclusive Meaning of a Flashing Circular Green Light in BCFlashing green light在 BC 闪动绿圆灯代表行人控制的路口（侧街面对停车标志），不是快转红灯也不是提前左转（提前左转是闪动绿箭头）。In BC, a flashing circular green light indicates a pedestrian-controlled intersection (side street traffic faces a stop sign); it does not mean the green light is about to turn red, nor does it indicate an advance left turn (advance left turns are shown by a flashing green arrow).

第 1 题Q1标志专属几何形状识别Identifying Unique Geometric Sign Shapes★陷阱：混淆八角形与倒三角形的专属指令

Heavy wet snow has completely covered the face of a sign at an intersection, but you can clearly see its eight-sided (octagon) outline. What must you do?

大雪完全糊住了路口一块标志的牌面，但你能清楚认出它的八角形轮廓。你必须怎么做？

  1. ✓ 正确。整套交通标志体系里八角形（octagon）只有一个含义，即停车标志（Stop sign）；哪怕牌面完全被雪盖住，指令依然成立。Correct. In the entire traffic sign system, an octagon has only one meaning: a Stop sign. Even if the sign face is completely covered by snow, the command remains fully in effect.

  2. ✗ 混淆了形状含义。让行标志（Yield sign）使用的是独一无二的倒等边三角形，而不是八角形。Confuses the meaning of shapes. A Yield sign uses a unique inverted equilateral triangle, not an octagon.

  3. ✗ 混淆了形状含义。学校区（School zone）使用的是专属的荧光黄绿五角形，而非八角形。Confuses the meaning of shapes. A School zone uses a distinctive fluorescent yellow-green pentagon, not an octagon.

  4. ✗ 混淆了形状含义。施工区警告标志（Construction sign）使用的是橙底菱形，八角形必为强制停车。Confuses the meaning of shapes. A Construction sign uses an orange diamond, whereas an octagon is always a mandatory stop.

第 2 题Q2学校区与游乐场区时段区分Distinguishing School Zone and Playground Zone Hours★★陷阱：学校区与游乐场区时段对调

On a sunny Sunday afternoon, Maya drives past a playground zone sign with a 30 km/h tab, and later passes a standard school zone sign with no extra time tab. Which 30 km/h speed limit applies?

在一个晴朗的周日下午，Maya 驾车经过一块附有 30 km/h 标牌的游乐场区标志，随后又经过一块没有额外延长时段附牌的标准学校区标志。哪一处的 30 km/h 限速正在生效？

  1. ✗ 落入「学校区与游乐场区时段对调」陷阱。学校区（School zone）仅在上课日（school days）8:00–17:00 有效，周日不上课故不生效。Falls into the trap of swapping school zone and playground zone hours. A School zone is only in effect on school days from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.; because there is no school on Sunday, it is not in effect.

  2. ✓ 正确。游乐场区（Playground zone）的 30 km/h 限速在每天（every day）黎明到黄昏（from dawn to dusk）均有效，周日下午照常生效；而标准学校区仅在上课日有效。Correct. A Playground zone 30 km/h speed limit is in effect every day from dawn to dusk, so it applies normally on a Sunday afternoon, whereas a standard school zone is in effect only on school days.

  3. ✗ 把学校区的「仅上课日」规则错误套用到游乐场区。游乐场在周末人反而更多，每天黎明到黄昏都限速 30 km/h。Mistakenly applies the school-days-only rule to a playground zone. Playgrounds are typically busier on weekends, and the 30 km/h limit applies every day from dawn to dusk.

  4. ✗ 混淆了施工区与儿童限速区的时效。施工区（Construction zone）有牌时一天 24 小时有效，而学校区和游乐场区都有各自的法定生效时段。Confuses the effective hours of a construction zone with child safety zones. A Construction zone is in effect 24 hours a day whenever signs are posted, whereas school zones and playground zones have their own statutory operating hours.

第 3 题Q3BC 闪动绿圆灯的含义Meaning of a Flashing Circular Green Light in BC★★陷阱：把闪动绿圆灯当成提前左转或即将转红

You are driving along a main street in BC and see a flashing green circular traffic light at an intersection ahead. What does this signal indicate?

你在 BC 省的一条主干道上行驶，看到前方路口有一个闪动的绿色圆形信号灯。这个信号表示什么？

  1. ✗ 落入「把闪动绿圆灯当成提前左转」的跨省陷阱。在安大略省闪动绿圆灯表提前左转，但在 BC 提前左转必须由闪动绿箭头（Advance green arrow）表示。Falls into the interprovincial trap of mistaking a flashing circular green light for an advance left turn. In Ontario, a flashing circular green indicates an advance left turn, but in BC an advance left turn must be indicated by an advance green arrow.

  2. ✗ 落入「二手资料误传即将转红」陷阱。除非行人按下按钮，否则主路平时一直保持闪绿；无故急刹会导致后车追尾。Falls into the trap of second-hand materials claiming the signal is about to turn red. Unless a pedestrian pushes the button, the main street stays flashing green; braking suddenly without reason risks a rear-end collision.

  3. ✓ 正确。在 BC，闪动绿圆灯（Flashing green light）专指行人控制的路口，主路长绿、行人按按钮才转黄变红，且侧向街道面对的是停车标志。Correct. In BC, a flashing circular green light specifically indicates a pedestrian-controlled intersection: the main street remains green until a pedestrian pushes the button to trigger yellow and red, and side-street traffic faces a stop sign.

  4. ✗ 混淆了正常闪绿灯与信号灯全灭。只有当信号灯全部熄灭（blacked-out）故障时，才按四向停车处理。Confuses a functioning flashing green light with a total signal outage. Only when traffic signals are completely blacked out due to a malfunction is the intersection treated as a four-way stop.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

At an intersection in BC, the traffic signal displays a flashing green arrow together with a steady red light. What are drivers facing this signal permitted to do?

在 BC 省的一个路口，交通信号灯同时显示闪动绿箭头和稳定红灯。面对该信号的驾驶者允许做什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。配合稳定红灯亮起的是闪动绿箭头而非全向绿灯，直行车辆必须等待红灯。Incorrect. What illuminates alongside the steady red light is a flashing green arrow, not a general green light; through traffic must wait for the red light.

  2. ✓ 正确。在 BC，闪动绿箭头加稳定红灯（Advance green arrow + steady red）表示允许按箭头方向提前左转，但直行车必须等红灯。Correct. In BC, a flashing green arrow with a steady red light indicates that you may make an advance left turn in the direction of the arrow, while through traffic must wait for the red light.

  3. ✗ 错误。闪动绿箭头专门赋予该转向车流优先通行许可，左转车辆无需像普通红灯那样先停车等待。Incorrect. An advance green arrow specifically grants protected right-of-way to turning traffic; left-turning vehicles do not need to come to a stop first as they would at a normal red light.

  4. ✗ 混淆了闪动绿箭头与闪动绿圆灯。行人控制路口使用的是闪动绿色圆灯（Flashing green light），而非绿箭头。Confuses a flashing green arrow with a flashing circular green light. Pedestrian-controlled intersections use a flashing circular green light, not an arrow.

第 4 题Q4稳定黄灯与红灯转弯规则Steady Yellow Light and Turning on Red Rules★陷阱：红圈无斜杠误判为禁止、黄灯忽略安全条件

As you approach an intersection at normal speed, the traffic light changes to a steady yellow. What does BC law require you to do?

当你以正常速度驶近路口时，交通信号灯变成了稳定黄灯。BC 省法律要求你怎么做？

  1. ✗ 错误。稳定黄灯预告红灯即将亮起，指令是停车而非加速抢行。Incorrect. A steady yellow light warns that a red light is about to appear; the command is to stop, not to speed up and beat the light.

  2. ✗ 落入「黄灯忽略安全条件」陷阱。若离停车线太近还无条件急刹，极易制造后车追尾事故。Falls into the trap of ignoring the safety qualification for a yellow light. Braking hard unconditionally when already too close to the stop line creates a high risk of being rear-ended.

  3. ✗ 混淆了稳定黄灯与闪动黄灯。「减速并小心通过」是闪动黄灯（flashing yellow light）的含义。Confuses a steady yellow light with a flashing yellow light. Slowing down and proceeding with caution is the instruction for a flashing yellow light.

  4. ✓ 正确。稳定黄灯（steady yellow light）的完整规则是必须在进入路口前停车，除非因距离太近、车速太快已无法安全停下（unless unsafe to do so）。Correct. The complete rule for a steady yellow light is that you must stop before entering the intersection, unless it is unsafe to do so due to close distance or vehicle speed.

You are waiting at a steady red light and want to turn left onto an intersecting street. Under what condition is a left turn on a steady red light permitted in BC?

你在稳定红灯前等待，想要左转进入横向街道。在 BC 省，什么条件下允许在稳定红灯时左转？

  1. ✓ 正确。稳定红灯（steady red light）下，先完全停车、没有禁止相应转向的标志，并向车辆、骑车人及行人让行确认安全后，可右转，也可左转进入单行道（one-way street）。Correct. At a steady red light, after coming to a complete stop, with no sign prohibiting the turn, and yielding to vehicles, cyclists, and pedestrians to ensure safety, you may turn right or turn left onto a one-way street.

  2. ✗ 漏记了左转进入单行道的例外。只要目标道路是单行道且满足停车、无禁牌、让清三类交通的条件，红灯也允许左转。Misses the exception for turning left onto a one-way street. As long as the intersecting street is a one-way street and the conditions of a complete stop, no prohibitory sign, and yielding to all traffic are met, a left turn on red is permitted.

  3. ✗ 错误。红灯转弯必须先完全停车（complete stop），且左转仅限转入单行道，不能转入双向道。Incorrect. Turning on red requires coming to a complete stop first, and left turns on red are strictly limited to entering a one-way street, not a two-way street.

  4. ✗ 无关干扰项。红灯左转进入单行道的规则与学校区时段或侧街标志无关。Irrelevant distractor. The rule permitting a left turn on red onto a one-way street has nothing to do with school zone hours or side-street signs.

第 5 题Q5铁路道口安全通行规则Railway Crossing Safety Rules★★陷阱：火车刚过就抢行忽略红灯与第二列火车

You are stopped at a railway crossing with flashing red lights and gates. A long freight train finishes passing in front of you. When may you drive across the tracks?

你在红灯闪动且放下栏杆的铁路道口前停车等待。一列长货运火车刚从你面前驶过。你什么时候可以驾车穿过铁轨？

  1. ✗ 落入「火车刚过就抢行」陷阱。此时红灯可能仍在闪动，且第一列火车会遮挡视线，反方向可能正驶来第二列火车。Falls into the trap of rushing across as soon as a train passes. The red lights may still be flashing, and the first train can block your view of a second train approaching from the opposite direction.

  2. ✓ 正确。铁路道口（Railway crossing）正常通行必须等红灯停止闪动、栏杆完全升起，再观察所有轨道确认没有第二列火车（second train）且出口有足够空间后方可通过。Correct. To cross a railway crossing safely, you must wait until the lights stop flashing and the gates are fully raised, then check all tracks to confirm no second train is approaching and ensure there is enough room on the other side.

  3. ✗ 错误。必须等栏杆完全升起（fully raised）且红灯停闪，同时还要确认对岸出口有空间容纳你的车，不能盲目跟车停在铁轨上。Incorrect. You must wait until gates are fully raised and lights stop flashing, while also confirming there is room on the other side for your vehicle so you do not stop on the tracks.

  4. ✗ 违反法定停车义务。铁路道口红灯闪动时必须完全停车并保持等待，绝不能因暂时看不到或听不到火车就自行通过。Violates the statutory stopping duty. When railway crossing red lights are flashing, you must come to a complete stop and remain waiting; you must never cross simply because you cannot see or hear a train.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Why does BC traffic law place the entire obligation to stop and yield at a railway crossing on the motor vehicle driver rather than on the train?

为什么 BC 交通法规把铁路道口的全部停车和避让义务放在机动车驾驶者一侧，而不是放在火车一侧？

  1. ✗ 错误。虽然多轨道需防范反方向第二列火车，但并非所有火车都成对行驶。Incorrect. Although multiple tracks require watching for a second train from the opposite direction, trains do not always travel in pairs.

  2. ✗ 错误。铁路道口规则全天适用，与游乐场区的黎明至黄昏时段无关。Incorrect. Railway crossing rules apply 24 hours a day and have nothing to do with playground zone dawn-to-dusk hours.

  3. ✓ 正确。铁路道口规则的底层逻辑是物理不对等：一列货运列车在紧急制动下要滑行超过一公里（more than one kilometre），根本无法为汽车减速。Correct. The underlying logic of railway crossing rules is physical disparity: a freight train under emergency braking takes more than one kilometre to stop and cannot slow down for a car.

  4. ✗ 错误。原因在于列车巨大的制动距离这一物理客观规律，而非标志性质。Incorrect. The reason is the physical reality of a train's massive stopping distance, not the advisory nature of warning signs.

Late at night, Kevin drives into an orange-signed construction zone where no workers or machinery are active. According to the knowledge test standard, how do construction zone rules and speeding fines apply?

深夜，Kevin 驶入一个立有橙色标志的施工区，现场没有工人或机械在作业。根据笔试口径，施工区的规则和超速罚款如何适用？

  1. ✓ 正确。施工区（Construction zone）有牌时一天 24 小时有效，与当下有无工人在场无关；考试口径写的是「施工区罚款加倍」（按现行罚款附表，超速 20 km/h 以内罚 $170、21–40 km/h 罚 $220，高于普通路段的 $120／$170 但非整整一倍，卷面须照「加倍」作答）。Correct. A Construction zone is in effect 24 hours a day whenever signs are posted, regardless of whether workers are currently present; the exam standard states that fines for speeding are double (under current fine schedules, speeding up to 20 km/h carries a $170 fine and 21–40 km/h carries a $220 fine, higher than the $120/$170 for regular roads though not exactly double, but the test requires answering 'double').

  2. ✗ 落入「无人施工区不减速」陷阱。施工区限速的判据是牌子在不在，不是人在不在，深夜无人照样开重罚单。Falls into the trap of not slowing down in an empty construction zone. The criterion for a construction speed limit is whether signs are posted, not whether workers are present; heavy tickets are issued late at night even with no workers around.

  3. ✗ 错误。施工区罚款比普通路段加重，考试口径明确为「罚款加倍」（且警示牌印有含附加费的最低罚款 $196）。Incorrect. Construction zone fines are increased compared to regular roads; exam materials clearly specify 'fines are double' (and warning signs display a $196 minimum fine including surcharges).

第 7 题Q7路面单双黄实线跨越规则Rules for Crossing Single and Double Solid Yellow Lines★★★陷阱：认为所有实线都绝对不可跨越

You are driving behind a slow-moving cyclist on a two-way road marked with a single solid yellow centre line. Are you allowed to cross the single solid yellow line to pass the cyclist?

你在一条画有单条黄实线中心线的双向道路上，跟在一名慢速骑自行车的人后面行驶。你是否允许跨越这条单黄实线来超越骑车人？

  1. ✗ 落入「认为所有实线都绝对不可跨越」陷阱。不能把白实线禁变道、双黄实线禁超车概括成所有实线都不可跨。Falls into the trap of assuming all solid lines can never be crossed. You cannot generalize solid white lines prohibiting lane changes and double solid yellow lines prohibiting passing into a rule that no solid line may ever be crossed.

  2. ✗ 混淆了黄白标线的功能。白线分隔的是同向车道（same direction），双向道路分隔对向车流使用的是黄线。Confuses the functions of yellow and white pavement markings. White lines separate lanes travelling in the same direction; yellow lines are used to separate opposing traffic on two-way roads.

  3. ✓ 正确。单黄实线（Single solid yellow line）允许格外谨慎地安全超车；法规专门明确在安全前提下，允许跨越单条黄实线以超越骑自行车的人（cyclist）。Correct. A single solid yellow line permits passing with extra caution; regulations specifically clarify that when safe, you are permitted to cross a single solid yellow line to pass a cyclist.

  4. ✗ 选对了动作但理由完全错误。黄线分隔的是对向车流（opposing traffic），白线才分隔同向车道。Selects the right action but for the entirely wrong reason. Yellow lines separate opposing traffic, while white lines separate lanes moving in the same direction.
