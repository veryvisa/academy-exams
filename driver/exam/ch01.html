第 1 章 · 路权、共享道路与驾驶态度Chapter 1 · Right of Way & Sharing the RoadRules of the Road

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 路口先后顺序判断：给出四向停车、无控制路口或左转交汇场景，测试考生是否按「先完全停住者先走→同时到达让右→对向左转让直行」的两级顺序作答，而非盲目套用「让右」或主张自身优先。Intersection priority judgment: Given a four-way stop, uncontrolled intersection, or left-turn crossing scenario, tests whether the candidate applies the two-stage hierarchy of 'first to complete stop goes first -> yield to the right if arriving simultaneously -> left turns yield to oncoming straight traffic', rather than blindly applying 'yield to the right' or asserting personal priority.
  • 弱势道路使用者保护：通过无斑马线路口行人、举起白手杖的视障行人、右转遇自行车或左转遇对向摩托车等情境，测试驾驶者是否主动承担更高的观察与让行义务。Protection of vulnerable road users: Uses scenarios such as pedestrians at intersections without painted crosswalks, visually impaired pedestrians raising a white cane, right turns encountering bicycles, or left turns facing oncoming motorcycles to test whether drivers proactively take on heightened duty to scan and yield.
  • 特种车辆法定动作对比：针对闪灯鸣笛紧急车辆、闪红灯停靠校车、打灯驶离站台公交车及执行任务消防车，测试不同情形下的停车范围、中央分隔带例外归属及限速与距离条件。Statutory actions for special vehicles: Tests required stopping areas, the applicability of median exceptions, and speed or distance conditions across different scenarios involving flashing-light and siren emergency vehicles, school buses stopped with flashing red lights, transit buses signaling to leave a stop, and fire trucks on duty.
  • 盲区观察与安全超车判据：考察变道转向「查镜→打灯→肩查」三步分工、倒车360度环视加回头、坡顶弯道禁超车原因，以及超车后「后视镜能完整看到被超车辆前保险杠」才可并回的二值判据。Blind spot observation and safe passing criteria: Assesses the three-step sequence of 'mirror -> signal -> shoulder check' for lane changes and turns, 360-degree vision plus turning around while reversing, reasons for no-passing zones on crests and curves, and the binary criterion of only merging back when the passed vehicle's front bumper is fully visible in the rearview mirror.
  • 防御性沟通与紧急失控处置：在被紧贴尾随、后部侧滑、水面滑行或爆胎场景中，测试考生能否压制「踩刹车或反向打轮」的本能冲动，选择松油门、顺向修正或拉开前方空间等可控动作。Defensive communication and emergency recovery: Tests whether candidates can suppress the instinctive impulse to slam on the brakes or counter-steer sharply during tailgating, rear-wheel skids, hydroplaning, or tire blowouts, choosing controlled actions such as easing off the accelerator, steering in the intended direction, or expanding forward following distance.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 只记让右忘先后Remembering yielding to the right while forgetting arrival order

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生死背「无控制路口和四向停车要让右方」，忽略了第一层判据永远是「谁先完全停住谁先走」，且绿灯只代表许可进路口而不代表左转优先。Candidates memorize 'yield to the right at uncontrolled intersections and four-way stops' while overlooking that the primary criterion is always 'whoever comes to a complete stop first goes first', and that a green light only grants permission to enter the intersection, not priority to turn left.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 four-way stop、uncontrolled intersection 或绿灯左转，且明确交代了到达先后或对向直行车辆。Question stem mentions a four-way stop, uncontrolled intersection, or turning left on green, and clearly describes arrival order or oncoming straight traffic.

⚠ 分隔带例外套校车Applying the median divider exception to school buses

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 紧急车辆在有中央分隔带的对向车道可不必停，考生常把这条例外错误套用到闪红灯的校车上，忽略了孩童可能横穿马路的风险。Drivers facing an emergency vehicle on the opposite side of a physical median do not need to stop, and candidates often mistakenly apply this exception to school buses with flashing red lights, overlooking the risk of children crossing the roadway.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现 school bus with alternating flashing red lights 与对向车道或中央分隔带（median）。Question stem mentions both a school bus with alternating flashing red lights and oncoming lanes or a central median.

⚠ 无斑马线不算横道Assuming no painted lines means no crosswalk

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为只有画了白线的斑马线才受保护，或把视障行人举起白手杖当成挥手礼让，导致在普通路口、转弯及出私家车道时漏让行人。Candidates mistakenly believe only painted crosswalks are legally protected, or misinterpret a visually impaired pedestrian raising a white cane as a polite waving gesture, failing to yield to pedestrians at standard intersections, turns, or driveways.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述路口没有画线（no painted lines）、车辆正在右转或驶出私家车道，或行人手持 white cane。Question stem describes an intersection with no painted lines, a vehicle turning or exiting a driveway, or a pedestrian carrying a white cane.

⚠ 盲信目测与后视镜Blindly relying on visual estimation and mirrors

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 认为后视镜或倒车影像能替代转头肩查，或凭肉眼觉得对向摩托车「还远还慢」、大卡车右转前左摆是留出右侧超车空隙。Assuming mirrors or backup cameras can replace shoulder checks, or relying on naked eye judgment that an oncoming motorcycle is 'far away and slow', or that a large truck swinging left before turning right is opening a passing lane on the right.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及变道、右转横切自行车道、倒车、对向驶来 motorcycle，或前方 large truck 向左摆头准备右转。Question stem involves changing lanes, turning right across a bike lane, reversing, an oncoming motorcycle, or a large truck swinging wide before a right turn.

⚠ 遇险本能急踩刹车Instinctively slamming on the brakes during emergencies

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 在车辆侧滑、水面滑行、爆胎或被后车紧贴挑衅时，本能地踩刹车或点刹「提醒」后车，结果彻底耗尽轮胎抓地力或诱发追尾。Instinctively braking or brake-tapping to 'warn' following vehicles during a skid, hydroplaning, tire blowout, or aggressive tailgating, which exhausts remaining tire traction or triggers a rear-end collision.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 rear wheels skidding、hydroplaning、tire blowout 或被 tailgater 紧贴尾随。Question stem mentions rear wheels skidding, hydroplaning, a tire blowout, or being closely followed by a tailgater.

第 1 题Q1四向停车先后与让右顺序Four-way stop arrival order and yielding to the right★陷阱：只记让右忘先后

You arrive at a four-way stop intersection and come to a complete stop just before another car stops at the cross street to your right. Who should proceed first?

你到达一个四向停车路口并完全停稳，紧接着你右侧横向道路上也有一辆车停下。谁应该先走？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是典型的「只记让右忘先后」陷阱。四向停车（four-way stop）的判断是两级的，只有两车真正同时到达时才用「让右」，分得出先后时一律按先完全停住者先行。Incorrect. This is a classic 'remembering yielding to the right while forgetting arrival order' trap. Judgment at a four-way stop is two-staged: yielding to the right only applies when two vehicles arrive at the exact same moment. If arrivals are distinct, the vehicle that stopped completely first always goes first.

  2. ✓ 正确。在四向停车路口（four-way stop），第一层判据是「先到先走」（谁先完全停住谁先走）；同时路权是被让出来的，起步前仍须确认对方确实让行。Correct. At a four-way stop, the primary rule is first-come, first-served (whoever comes to a complete stop first goes first). Furthermore, right of way is given, not taken, so you must confirm the other driver is actually yielding before moving.

  3. ✗ 错误。路权（right of way）是规则指定的等待义务，不能靠闪远光灯去抢夺或主张，且闪灯容易造成对方误解。Incorrect. Right of way is a designated legal duty to wait; it cannot be claimed or seized by flashing high beams, which can also easily confuse other road users.

  4. ✗ 错误。明明自己先停稳却犹豫等待对方手势，正是四向停车路口两车同时起步发生刮蹭的常见原因，属于「只记让右忘先后」陷阱。Incorrect. Hesitating and waiting for gestures when you stopped first is a frequent cause of simultaneous starts and collisions at four-way stops. This falls into the 'remembering yielding to the right while forgetting arrival order' trap.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

You and an oncoming vehicle arrive at an uncontrolled intersection at the exact same moment. You want to turn left, and the oncoming vehicle is going straight. What should you do?

你与一辆对向来车在同一时刻到达一个无控制路口。你准备左转，而对向车辆准备直行。你应该怎么做？

  1. ✗ 错误。左转需要横切对向车道，暴露时间最长，抢在直行车前左转极易引发侧面碰撞，违背了左转让直行的基本原则。Incorrect. Left turns cross oncoming lanes and have the longest exposure time; accelerating across ahead of straight traffic creates extreme risk of a T-bone collision and violates the fundamental rule that left turns yield to straight traffic.

  2. ✗ 错误。车头先探入路口并不赋予优先权，对向同时到达时左转车辆必须让直行车辆，属于「只记让右忘先后」陷阱。Incorrect. Nosing into an intersection first does not grant priority. When arriving simultaneously from opposite directions, left-turning vehicles must yield to oncoming traffic going straight. This falls into the 'remembering yielding to the right while forgetting arrival order' trap.

  3. ✗ 错误。喇叭只能提醒「我在这里」，不能改变路权规则要求对向直行车为你让路。Incorrect. A horn only alerts others to your presence; it cannot alter right-of-way rules or demand that oncoming straight-through traffic yield to you.

  4. ✓ 正确。在无控制路口（uncontrolled intersection）两车对向且同时到达时，左转车辆侵入对向路径，必须让直行车辆先行。Correct. At an uncontrolled intersection when two oncoming vehicles arrive at the exact same moment, the left-turning vehicle crosses the other's travel path and must yield to oncoming traffic going straight.

第 2 题Q2校车闪红灯双向停车Stopping in both directions for flashing red school bus lights★★陷阱：分隔带例外套校车

You are driving on a multi-lane road with a raised central median when an oncoming school bus stops and activates its alternating flashing red lights. What are you required to do?

你正行驶在设有凸起中央分隔带的多车道道路上，此时对向一辆校车停下并开启交替闪烁的红灯。你必须怎么做？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是高频的「分隔带例外套校车」陷阱。中央分隔带（median）的免停例外只适用于闪灯鸣笛的紧急车辆，校车闪红灯没有任何分隔带例外。Incorrect. This is a frequent 'applying the median divider exception to school buses' trap. The physical median exception applies only to emergency vehicles with sirens and flashing lights; there is no median exception for a school bus flashing red lights.

  2. ✗ 错误。面对闪红灯停靠的校车（school bus with flashing red lights），仅减速通过依然违法，因为下车孩童随时可能横穿整条马路。Incorrect. Simply slowing down when passing a school bus with flashing red lights is illegal because exiting children may cross the entire width of the street at any moment.

  3. ✓ 正确。当校车闪红灯（alternating flashing red lights）停靠时，同向与对向所有车道都必须完全停下，直到校车起步或司机关灯收牌示意可以通行，没有中央分隔带例外。Correct. When a school bus stops with alternating flashing red lights, traffic traveling in all lanes in both directions must come to a complete stop until the bus moves or the driver turns off the lights and retracts the stop sign. There is no central median exception.

  4. ✗ 错误。变道继续行驶同样违法，对向所有车道都必须停稳等待，属于「分隔带例外套校车」陷阱。Incorrect. Changing lanes and continuing is equally illegal; all oncoming lanes must stop completely and wait. This falls into the 'applying the median divider exception to school buses' trap.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

While driving on a two-way street, you hear a siren and see an ambulance with flashing lights approaching from behind. What is the correct action?

在双向街道上行驶时，你听到警笛声并看到一辆闪灯的救护车从后方驶近。正确的做法是什么？

  1. ✓ 正确。遇到闪灯鸣笛接近的紧急车辆（emergency vehicle），双向车流都必须靠边并完全停下（pull over and stop），让出中间通道。Correct. When an emergency vehicle approaches with flashing lights and siren, traffic in both directions must pull over to the edge of the roadway and stop completely to clear a passage.

  2. ✗ 错误。加速在紧急车辆前方抢行会阻碍救援并带来极高碰撞风险，必须立即靠右减速并停下。Incorrect. Speeding up ahead of an emergency vehicle impedes rescue operations and creates severe crash risks; you must immediately pull to the right, slow down, and stop.

  3. ✗ 错误。原地停在车道中央会堵死紧急车辆的通行路径，规则要求先靠边（pull over）再停下。Incorrect. Stopping dead in the middle of your lane blocks the path of emergency vehicles; the rule requires you to pull over to the side before stopping.

  4. ✗ 错误。严禁尾随紧急车辆抢道，例如跟在执行任务的消防车后方至少要保持 150 公尺距离。Incorrect. Following an emergency vehicle to bypass traffic is strictly prohibited; for example, you must maintain at least 150 metres of distance behind a fire truck responding to an alarm.

第 3 题Q3未标示横道与视障行人Unmarked crosswalks and visually impaired pedestrians★★陷阱：无斑马线不算横道

As you approach a residential intersection with no painted crosswalk lines, a pedestrian with a white cane raises the cane toward the street. What should you do?

当你接近一个没有画斑马线的住宅区路口时，一名持白手杖的行人向街道方向举起手杖。你应该怎么做？

  1. ✓ 正确。每个路口延长线都构成受保护的未标示人行横道（unmarked crosswalk），且持白手杖（white cane）行人举起手杖代表「我要过马路但不确定是否安全」，驾驶者必须完全停稳并保持停住，让对方凭声音确认安全。Correct. The sidewalk extension at every intersection forms a legally protected unmarked crosswalk. When a pedestrian raises a white cane, it signals that they intend to cross but are unsure if it is safe; drivers must stop completely and remain stopped so the pedestrian can hear that the car is stationary.

  2. ✗ 错误。持白手杖的视障行人看不见你的挥手手势，正确做法是完整停住并保持静止，不作手势或鸣笛干扰。Incorrect. A visually impaired pedestrian with a white cane cannot see hand waves; the proper action is to stop completely and remain motionless without distracting gestures or honking.

  3. ✗ 错误。这是「无斑马线不算横道」陷阱。BC 法律承认未标示人行横道（unmarked crosswalk），画不画线只影响显眼程度，不影响行人受保护的权利。Incorrect. This is the 'assuming no painted lines means no crosswalk' trap. BC law recognizes unmarked crosswalks; painted lines only improve visibility and do not alter a pedestrian's legal protection.

  4. ✗ 错误。把举起白手杖误解为「让你先走」是极其危险的认知错误，举杖恰恰是「我要过马路」的求助信号。Incorrect. Misinterpreting a raised white cane as an invitation to proceed is a dangerous error; raising the cane is a signal requesting safety to cross.

You have a green light and are about to turn right at an intersection where the side street has no painted lines on the pavement. A pedestrian steps off the curb to cross that side street. What must you do?

你在绿灯路口准备右转，所转入的支路地面没有画斑马线，此时一名行人迈下路缘准备横穿该支路。你必须怎么做？

  1. ✗ 错误。绿灯只是进入路口的许可（permission），不是凌驾于行人之上的路权，转弯车辆必须让正在过马路的行人，属于「只记让右忘先后」陷阱。Incorrect. A green light is only permission to enter the intersection, not a right of way over pedestrians; turning vehicles must yield to pedestrians crossing the street. This falls into the 'remembering yielding to the right while forgetting arrival order' trap.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是「无斑马线不算横道」陷阱。每个路口的延长线上都存在法定的未标示人行横道（unmarked crosswalk），且转弯时必须让行人。Incorrect. This is the 'assuming no painted lines means no crosswalk' trap. Every intersection contains a statutory unmarked crosswalk across its extensions, and turning drivers must yield to pedestrians.

  3. ✓ 正确。路口即使没有画线也构成受保护的未标示人行横道（unmarked crosswalk），且转弯车辆横切人行路径时必须让行人先行。Correct. Even without painted lines, intersection corners form legally protected unmarked crosswalks, and turning vehicles crossing pedestrian paths must yield to pedestrians.

  4. ✗ 错误。喇叭不能用来驱赶拥有合法路权的行人，转弯驾驶者承担等待义务。Incorrect. A horn must never be used to intimidate pedestrians who hold legal right of way; turning drivers bear the duty to wait.

第 4 题Q4超越骑车人法定侧向距离Statutory lateral passing distance for cyclists★★陷阱：盲信目测与后视镜

You are driving on a two-lane rural highway with a posted speed limit over 50 km/h and need to pass a cyclist riding along the right edge of the road. What minimum lateral distance must you leave?

你行驶在限速超过 50 km/h 的双车道乡村公路上，需要超越一名沿道路右缘骑行的骑车人。你至少必须留出多少侧向距离？

  1. ✗ 错误。0.5 公尺仅适用于对方在人行道或有实体隔离的自行车道（separated bike lane）里的情况；在同一车道内硬挤过去极易刮擦骑车人。Incorrect. The 0.5-metre clearance applies only when the cyclist is on a sidewalk or in a physically separated bike lane; crowding past within the same lane creates a serious risk of striking the cyclist.

  2. ✗ 错误。1 公尺是一般低速道路的下限，但题干明确指出限速超过 50 km/h（over 50 km/h），此时法定最小侧向距离提高到了 1.5 公尺。Incorrect. While 1 metre is the baseline for lower-speed roads, the question explicitly states a speed limit over 50 km/h, which increases the statutory minimum passing distance to 1.5 metres.

  3. ✗ 错误。在确认安全的前提下，驾驶者可以跨越单条黄实线（single solid yellow line）去超越骑车人，决不能为了不压线而在同一车道内贴身挤过去。Incorrect. When safe, drivers are permitted to cross a single solid yellow line to pass a cyclist; you must never squeeze dangerously close within the same lane just to avoid touching the line.

  4. ✓ 正确。超越骑车人（cyclist）的法定最小侧向距离（minimum passing distance）在一般道路为 1 公尺，而在限速超过 50 km/h 的道路上必须留出至少 1.5 公尺。Correct. The statutory minimum passing distance when overtaking a cyclist is at least 1 metre on general roads, and at least 1.5 metres on roads with posted speed limits over 50 km/h.

You are preparing to make a right turn at an intersection on a street that has a painted bicycle lane along the curb. How should you handle the turn safely?

你准备在一个路边设有画线自行车道的路口右转。你应该如何安全地完成这次转弯？

  1. ✗ 错误。只看后视镜就直接从主车道横切自行车道，极易撞上盲区里直行的骑车人，造成典型的「右钩」事故（right hook），属于「盲信目测与后视镜」陷阱。Incorrect. Turning sharply across a bike lane relying solely on mirrors risks striking a cyclist continuing straight in your blind spot, causing a classic 'right hook' collision. This falls into the 'blindly relying on visual estimation and mirrors' trap.

  2. ✓ 正确。右转前先做右肩查（right shoulder check）确认盲区无骑车人，再短距借入自行车道，可把「横切冲突」变为「同向排队」，有效预防右钩事故（right hook）。Correct. Performing a right shoulder check before merging briefly into the bike lane turns a perpendicular crossing into same-direction queuing, effectively preventing a right-hook collision.

  3. ✗ 错误。加速抢在直行骑车人前方右转正是引发「右钩」碰撞的直接原因，转弯车必须让直行的骑车人。Incorrect. Speeding up to cut in front of a straight-riding cyclist directly triggers right-hook crashes; turning vehicles must yield to through cyclists.

  4. ✗ 错误。倒车影像只覆盖车尾固定视角，根本无法替代转弯前查探侧后方盲区的转头肩查（shoulder check），属于「盲信目测与后视镜」陷阱。Incorrect. Backup cameras cover only a fixed view behind the vehicle and cannot replace a shoulder check into the blind spot beside and behind you. This falls into the 'blindly relying on visual estimation and mirrors' trap.

第 5 题Q5对向摩托车左转与超车并回Turning left facing oncoming motorcycles and returning after passing★★陷阱：盲信目测与后视镜

You are waiting at a green light to turn left, and an oncoming motorcycle appears to be far away and moving slowly. What should you do?

你在绿灯路口等待左转，对面驶来一辆摩托车，看起来距离还远、速度也慢。你应该怎么做？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是「盲信目测与后视镜」陷阱。摩托车正面投影小，在视网膜上增大速率慢，人脑极易把实际上又近又快的摩托车误判为「还远、还慢」，抢行左转是摩托车致死事故最典型的原因。Incorrect. This is the 'blindly relying on visual estimation and mirrors' trap. Because of a motorcycle's narrow front profile, it expands slowly on the retina, causing the brain to misjudge a close, fast motorcycle as far and slow. Turning left across its path is the most common cause of fatal motorcycle crashes.

  2. ✓ 正确。人眼视觉系统会天然低估摩托车（motorcycle）的接近速度与距离，正确对策是不做速度估算，直接等它安全通过后再左转。Correct. Human vision naturally underestimates the approach speed and distance of oncoming motorcycles; the proper strategy is not to guess speed, but simply to wait until the motorcycle has cleared before turning left.

  3. ✗ 错误。绿灯只是进入路口的许可，左转车辆必须让对向直行车辆，侵入对向车道逼停摩托车既违法又极其危险。Incorrect. A green light is only permission to enter the intersection; left-turning vehicles must yield to oncoming through traffic, and edging into oncoming lanes to force a motorcycle to yield is illegal and dangerous.

  4. ✗ 错误。闪远光灯不能把让行义务转移给直行的摩托车，且强光会干扰骑手视线，属于危险抢行。Incorrect. Flashing high beams does not transfer the duty to yield to oncoming traffic, and the glare can blind the motorcyclist, constituting dangerous aggressive driving.

After overtaking a slower vehicle on a highway, how do you know it is safe to pull back into the right lane?

在公路上超越一辆慢车后，你如何判断何时可以安全并回右侧原车道？

  1. ✗ 错误。车尾刚越过对方前门时两车距离极近，此时并回会直接别车或刮蹭，属于「盲信目测与后视镜」陷阱。Incorrect. When your rear bumper barely passes the front door of the other vehicle, the gap is dangerously narrow; merging then cuts off or clips the vehicle. This falls into the 'blindly relying on visual estimation and mirrors' trap.

  2. ✗ 错误。高速行驶时单凭肉眼估计几公尺或几个车身长度极易失准，考试标准采用更可靠的后视镜二值判据。Incorrect. Visually estimating metres or car lengths at highway speeds is notoriously inaccurate; the exam standard relies on a clear, binary rearview mirror criterion.

  3. ✓ 正确。超车（overtaking）后并回原车道的标准判据是：在车内后视镜（rearview mirror）里能完整看到被超越的那辆车（包括前保险杠），并在并回后保持正常速度。Correct. The standard criterion for returning to your lane after overtaking is seeing the entire front of the passed vehicle, including its front bumper, in your inside rearview mirror, and then maintaining normal cruising speed.

  4. ✗ 错误。过早并回且马上踩刹车减速，会迫使被超车辆急刹甚至引发追尾，并回后必须恢复并保持正常速度。Incorrect. Cutting in early and immediately braking forces the passed driver to brake abruptly and risks a rear-end collision; you must resume and maintain normal speed after merging.

第 6 题Q6被紧贴尾随与侵略性驾驶应对Responding to close tailgating and aggressive driving★陷阱：遇险本能急踩刹车

Another driver has been tailgating you closely for two kilometres and honking aggressively. What is the safest way to respond?

另一名驾驶者已经紧贴你车尾行驶了两公里，并不断挑衅鸣笛。最安全的应对方式是什么？

  1. ✓ 正确。遇到侵略性驾驶者（aggressive driving / tailgating），你无法控制后方空间，但可以加大自己车前的空间（increase space in front），这样需要减速时可以平缓过渡，避免急刹被追尾，同时不作眼神与手势交流以降级冲突。Correct. Faced with an aggressive tailgater, you cannot control the space behind you, but you can increase the following distance in front of your vehicle. This allows gradual braking if needed to prevent a rear-end collision, while avoiding eye contact or gestures helps de-escalate tension.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是典型的「遇险本能急踩刹车」陷阱。点刹「教训」或提醒对方，极易把一次尾随骚扰直接升级成追尾事故。Incorrect. This is a classic 'instinctively slamming on the brakes during emergencies' trap. Brake-tapping to 'teach a lesson' or warn a tailgater escalates harassment directly into a rear-end crash.

  3. ✗ 错误。超速并不能摆脱侵略性驾驶者，反而缩短了你自己与前车的反应距离，增加了失控和追尾前车的风险。Incorrect. Speeding does not shake off an aggressive driver; instead, it reduces your own reaction distance to vehicles ahead, increasing risks of losing control or rear-ending someone.

  4. ✗ 错误。若对方持续跟随，绝对不要开回家（暴露住址且孤立无援），应当驶向人多的地方或警察局，必要时报警。Incorrect. If pursued persistently, never drive home (which reveals your residence and leaves you isolated); drive toward a populated area or a police station, calling police if necessary.

You are about to pull away from a curbside parking space on a quiet residential street where no other cars are visible. Do you need to use your turn signal?

你准备在一条安静的住宅区街道上驶离路边停车位，周围看不到其他车辆。你需要打转向灯吗？

  3. ✗ 错误。无论在主干道还是住宅区小街，驶离路边车位都属于改变方向并汇入车道，规则没有道路等级例外。Incorrect. Whether on an arterial road or a quiet residential street, pulling away from a curb changes direction and merges into traffic; there is no road classification exemption.

  4. ✓ 正确。转向灯（turn signal）是车上唯一能表达「我下一步打算做什么」的装置，只要改变行进方向（包括驶离路边车位 pulling away from the curb），就必须打灯。Correct. Turn signals are the only device on a vehicle that communicates intended actions; whenever changing direction, including pulling away from a curb, signaling is mandatory.

第 7 题Q7后轮侧滑与爆胎水面滑行处置Handling rear-wheel skids, tire blowouts, and hydroplaning★★★陷阱：遇险本能急踩刹车

On a slippery road, the rear of your vehicle suddenly starts skidding to the right. What should you do to regain control?

在湿滑路面上，你的车尾突然开始向右发生侧滑。你应该怎么做才能重新控制车辆？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是「遇险本能急踩刹车」陷阱。侧滑（skidding）的本质是轮胎抓地力已被用光，此时踩刹车会继续索取抓地力，导致车辆彻底失控打转。Incorrect. This is the 'instinctively slamming on the brakes during emergencies' trap. A skid occurs when tire traction is exhausted; braking demands even more grip, throwing the vehicle into an uncontrollable spin.

  2. ✗ 错误。反向急打方向盘并踩油门会加剧车尾摆动，违背了「松开油门、看哪儿转哪儿」的侧滑修正原则。Incorrect. Overcorrecting in the opposite direction while accelerating violently exaggerates fishtailing, violating the core skid-recovery rule of easing off the gas and steering where you want to go.

  3. ✓ 正确。发生后部侧滑（rear-wheel skid）时，应先松开油门（ease off the accelerator）把抓地力还给转向，同时眼睛看向目标方向并将方向盘转向你想去的方向（steer in the direction you want to go），不要踩刹车。Correct. During a rear-wheel skid, ease off the accelerator to return traction to steering, look toward your target path, and steer smoothly in the direction you want the vehicle to go without touching the brakes.

  4. ✗ 错误。视觉引导效应会导致「盯着什么看，手就往哪儿转」，盯着障碍物看反而会让车直接撞上去，必须盯着想去的安全路径看。Incorrect. The visual steering phenomenon causes your hands to steer where your eyes are focused; staring at an obstacle guides the vehicle straight into it. You must look toward the clear escape route.

While driving at highway speed, one of your tires suddenly blows out and the car pulls strongly to one side. What is the correct immediate response?

在公路高速行驶时，你的一个轮胎突然爆胎，车辆被猛烈拽向一侧。此时正确的第一时间动作是什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。属于「遇险本能急踩刹车」陷阱。爆胎（tire blowout）瞬间车辆已被拽向一侧，急踩刹车会在同一方向上叠加剧烈偏航力，极易引发翻车或失控。Incorrect. This falls into the 'instinctively slamming on the brakes during emergencies' trap. At the moment of a tire blowout, the vehicle pulls hard to one side; hard braking adds violent yaw forces in that direction, risking a rollover or total loss of control.

  2. ✓ 正确。爆胎（tire blowout）处置的关键是第一步先握稳方向盘（grip the steering wheel firmly）、松开油门让车自然减速，待车速降下来后再缓慢驶向路肩并开启双闪。Correct. The key response to a tire blowout is to grip the steering wheel firmly and ease off the gas to slow down gradually; once speed has dropped, steer smoothly to the shoulder and turn on hazard flashers.

  3. ✗ 错误。高速爆胎瞬间猛打方向盘会与爆胎拉扯力叠加导致车辆失控，必须等自然减速后再平缓靠边。Incorrect. Jerking the steering wheel during a high-speed blowout combines with drag forces to cause a loss of control; you must allow the car to slow down naturally before steering gently to the roadside.

  4. ✗ 错误。行驶中挂倒挡或急促踩刹会彻底破坏传动系统与车身稳定，爆胎时严禁急刹与突然输入。Incorrect. Shifting into reverse or pumping brakes erratically while moving destroys the transmission and destabilizes the vehicle; sudden steering or braking inputs are strictly prohibited during a blowout.
