第 2 章 · 速度、跟车与停车泊车Chapter 2 · Speed, Following Distance & ParkingSpeed & Parking

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 场景判断限速题：给出雨夜、浓雾、结冰或车流超速等路况，考你是否理解限速牌只是理想条件下的上限（Speed limit），恶劣条件下必须减速且绝不能随波逐流超速。Speed limit scenario judgment: Given conditions such as rainy nights, dense fog, icy roads, or speeding traffic flow, tests whether you understand that posted speed limit signs represent the maximum ceiling under ideal conditions only, requiring you to slow down in adverse conditions and never speed just to go with the flow.
  • 跟车秒数与特殊距离题：给出良好天气、跟在大货车或摩托车后方、暴雨湿滑路面，或跟随亮警灯执勤消防车等情境，考 2 秒、3 秒、4 秒三档规则与 150 公尺绝对距离。Following distance seconds and special distance rules: Given scenarios of good weather, following a large truck or motorcycle, heavy rain with slick pavement, or following a fire truck responding with flashing lights, tests the 2-second, 3-second, and 4-second tier rules along with the 150-metre absolute distance requirement.
  • 减速让行（Slow Down, Move Over）情境题：给出路边停着亮闪灯的拖车、养护车或警车，结合路段原有限速（80 km/h 及以上或低于 80 km/h），考通过时的最高限速（70 km/h 或 40 km/h）与变道义务。Slow Down, Move Over situational questions: Given a roadside-stopped tow truck, maintenance vehicle, or police car displaying flashing lights, combined with the posted speed limit (80 km/h and above, or below 80 km/h), tests the maximum passing speed (70 km/h or 40 km/h) and the duty to change lanes when safe.
  • 泊车距离与禁停识别题：直接考消防栓（5 公尺）、人行横道或信号灯（6 公尺）、铁轨（15 公尺）与路缘间距（30 公分），或用「蓝色路缘」等伪造选项测试禁停规则。Parking distance and no-parking zone identification: Tests statutory distances from a fire hydrant (5 metres), crosswalk or traffic light (6 metres), railway tracks (15 metres), and curb clearance (30 centimetres), or uses fabricated options such as 'blue curb' to test prohibited parking rules.
  • 坡道停车与失控应对题：给出上坡或下坡、有路缘或无路缘的组合考前轮朝向，以及桥面黑冰（Black ice）、水面滑行（Hydroplaning）和长下坡发动机制动（Engine braking）的正确操作。Hill parking and vehicle control response: Given combinations of uphill or downhill with or without a curb, tests front wheel direction, as well as correct actions for black ice on bridge decks, hydroplaning, and using engine braking on long downgrades.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 把限速牌当成必达目标Treating the posted speed limit as a target to maintain

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生误以为只要不超过牌面数值就不算违法，或者认为跟着周围超速车流开更安全，忽略了「速度相对于条件过快」可独立开罚单。Candidates mistakenly believe that driving at or below the posted speed limit cannot be unlawful, or assume that keeping up with speeding traffic flow is safer, overlooking that driving too fast for conditions can result in an independent traffic ticket.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 heavy rain、icy road、poor visibility 或 keeping up with traffic faster than the posted limit。Question stem mentions heavy rain, icy road, poor visibility, or keeping up with traffic faster than the posted limit.

⚠ 坡道打轮方向记反Reversing the direction to turn front wheels when hill parking

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 死记硬背左右容易在考场上混淆上坡与下坡、有路缘与无路缘的四种组合，忘记用「溜车时让轮胎卡住路缘或滑离车道」来推导。Rote memorization of left and right leads candidates to confuse the four combinations of uphill vs downhill and with a curb vs without a curb, forgetting the underlying reasoning of ensuring tires catch the curb or roll off the roadway if the vehicle slips.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现 parking uphill 或 parking downhill，并明确说明 with a curb 或 without a curb。Question stem mentions parking uphill or parking downhill and explicitly specifies with a curb or without a curb.

⚠ 泊车最小距离张冠李戴Mixing up minimum statutory parking distances

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把消防栓 5 公尺、路口与横道及信号灯 6 公尺、铁路道口 15 公尺和离路缘 30 公分互相套错，或误信卷面编造的「蓝色路缘」选项。Candidates interchange the 5 metres for a fire hydrant, 6 metres for an intersection, crosswalk, or traffic light, 15 metres for a railway crossing, and 30 centimetres from the curb, or fall for fabricated exam options such as 'blue curb'.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干询问 how close may you park to a fire hydrant / crosswalk / stop sign / railway crossing，或选项出现 blue curb。Question stem asks how close you may park to a fire hydrant, crosswalk, stop sign, or railway crossing, or options mention a blue curb.

⚠ 减速让行门槛与对象缩水Narrowing the protected vehicles and thresholds under Slow Down, Move Over

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为只有警车、救护车才适用减速让行，忽略了闪黄灯的拖车和道路施工养护车；或把 80 km/h 分界线对应的 70 km/h 与 40 km/h 记混。Candidates mistakenly assume Slow Down, Move Over applies only to police cars and ambulances, ignoring flashing amber lights on tow trucks and road maintenance vehicles, or confuse the speed ceilings of 70 km/h and 40 km/h corresponding to the 80 km/h threshold.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 official vehicle with flashing lights、tow truck、maintenance worker，并给出该路段的 posted speed limit。Question stem mentions an official vehicle with flashing lights, tow truck, or maintenance worker, and provides the posted speed limit for that road.

⚠ 跟车秒数与特殊距离混淆Confusing following distance seconds with special vehicle distances

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为摩托车体积小就可以跟近一点（2 秒），没想到摩托车刹车更快反而要留 3 秒；或把跟执勤消防车的 150 公尺当成普通秒数题。Candidates mistakenly assume that because a motorcycle is small they can follow closer at 2 seconds, failing to recognize that motorcycles stop faster and require at least 3 seconds, or treat the 150 metres behind a fire truck as a standard seconds question.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 following a motorcycle、large commercial vehicle、wet or icy pavement，或 following a fire truck responding to an emergency。Question stem mentions following a motorcycle, large commercial vehicle, wet or icy pavement, or following a fire truck responding to an emergency.

限速牌的法律性质Legal nature of posted speed limitsSpeed limit / Speed relative to conditions标志上的限速只是理想条件下的最高允许速度；恶劣天气下按牌面速度行驶仍可能因「速度相对于条件过快」被单独开罚单，且绝不可为跟上车流而超速。The posted speed limit is the maximum permitted speed under ideal conditions only; driving at the posted speed in adverse conditions can still result in a separate ticket for driving too fast for conditions, and you must never speed to keep up with traffic.
法定默认限速数值Statutory default speed limitsDefault speed limits无标志时，市区内默认 50 km/h，市区外默认 80 km/h；已立附例城市的宽度不超过 8 公尺小巷为 20 km/h（不必另立牌），有牌学校区与游乐场区为 30 km/h。When no signs are posted, the default speed limit is 50 km/h inside a municipality and 80 km/h outside a municipality; narrow alleys up to 8 metres wide in municipalities with bylaws are 20 km/h without requiring signs, and signed school and playground zones are 30 km/h.
三档安全跟车距离Three-tier safe following distancesFollowing distance / Two-second rule良好天气与路面至少留 2 秒；高速路段或跟随大型车辆、摩托车至少留 3 秒；恶劣天气、路面不平或湿滑至少留 4 秒。Leave at least 2 seconds in good weather and road conditions; leave at least 3 seconds on highways or when following large vehicles or motorcycles; leave at least 4 seconds in adverse weather, uneven roads, or slippery pavement.
闪灯作业车减速让行Slow Down, Move Over for flashing vehiclesSlow down, move over遇路边停靠亮闪灯的警车、救护车、消防车、拖车、养护车等，限速 80 km/h 及以上路段须减至 70 km/h，低于 80 km/h 路段须减至 40 km/h，并在多车道安全时变道远离（违者罚 $173 加 3 分）。When approaching stopped police, ambulance, fire, tow, or road maintenance vehicles displaying flashing lights, slow to 70 km/h on roadways posted at 80 km/h or higher, slow to 40 km/h on roadways posted below 80 km/h, and change lanes away when safe on multi-lane roads (violators face a $173 fine and 3 penalty points).
法定最小泊车距离Statutory minimum parking distancesParking distances距消防栓至少 5 公尺（从路缘最近点量起），距人行横道、路口、停车标志或信号灯至少 6 公尺，距最近一条铁轨至少 15 公尺，车身与路缘平行且不超过 30 公分。Park at least 5 metres from a fire hydrant (measured from the point on the curb closest to it), at least 6 metres from a crosswalk, intersection, stop sign, or traffic light, at least 15 metres from the nearest rail at a railway crossing, and parallel within 30 centimetres of the curb.
完全禁止停车区域Strictly prohibited parking zonesNo-parking zones人行道、车道进出口前、路口内、自行车道、桥面与隧道内、遮挡标志处，以及涂黄色或红色路缘处严禁泊车（蓝色路缘常为考题干扰项）。Parking is strictly prohibited on sidewalks, in front of driveways, inside intersections, in bicycle lanes, on bridge decks, inside tunnels, where signs are obstructed, and along yellow or red painted curbs (blue curbs are frequently used as exam distractors).
坡道停车前轮朝向与挂挡Front wheel angles and gears for hill parkingHill parking只有「上坡且有路缘」时前轮向左打（转离路缘），上坡无路缘及所有下坡情形前轮一律向右打；拉手刹，自动挡挂 P，手动挡上坡挂一挡、下坡挂倒挡。Turn front wheels to the left (away from the curb) only when parking uphill with a curb; turn front wheels to the right for uphill without a curb and in all downhill situations. Set the parking brake, shift an automatic to Park, and shift a manual to first gear uphill or reverse downhill.
掉头禁区与视距判据U-turn prohibited zones and visibility criteriaU-turn restrictions弯道上、坡顶附近（其他车在 150 公尺内看不到你）、设有交通信号灯的路口、有禁掉头标志处，以及商业区非无信号灯路口均禁止掉头。U-turns are prohibited on curves, near the crest of a hill where other drivers cannot see you within 150 metres, at intersections with traffic lights, where a no U-turn sign is posted, and in business districts except at intersections without traffic lights.

When following a fire truck responding to an emergency, you must maintain a distance of at least 150 metres.

第 1 题Q1限速是理想条件上限Speed limit as ideal condition ceiling★陷阱：把限速牌当成必达目标

You are driving at night in heavy rain on a highway with a posted speed limit of 80 km/h. Most vehicles around you are still travelling at 80 km/h. What should you do?

你在大雨夜晚驶在限速牌标示为 80 km/h 的公路上，周围大多数车辆仍以 80 km/h 行驶。你应该怎么做？

  1. ✗ 错误，掉入「把限速牌当成必达目标」陷阱。限速（Speed limit）只是理想条件下的上限，「大家都开这么快」不构成违法抗辩，雨夜视线与附着力下降时仍开 80 km/h 会因速度相对于条件过快（Speed relative to conditions）违规。Incorrect. This falls into the 'treating the posted speed limit as a target to maintain' trap. A speed limit is only a ceiling under ideal conditions. 'Everyone else was driving that fast' is not a legal defence; driving 80 km/h when visibility and traction are reduced on a rainy night constitutes a violation of driving too fast for conditions.

  2. ✓ 正确。牌面限速仅适用于理想条件；当降雨或夜间导致路面附着力与能见度变差时，低于限速、按当时条件安全行驶（Speed relative to conditions）才是守法做法。Correct. Posted speed limits apply only under ideal conditions. When rain or nighttime reduces road traction and visibility, driving below the speed limit at a speed that is safe for the actual conditions is the lawful action.

  3. ✗ 错误，掉入「把限速牌当成必达目标」陷阱。任何情况下都绝不允许超过最高限速，恶劣天气超速更易诱发水面滑行（Hydroplaning）与追尾。Incorrect. This falls into the 'treating the posted speed limit as a target to maintain' trap. Exceeding the maximum speed limit is never permitted under any circumstances, and speeding during adverse weather greatly increases the risk of hydroplaning and rear-end collisions.

  4. ✗ 错误。行驶中挂空挡会失去发动机制动（Engine braking）与驱动控制，在湿滑路面上极其危险且不合法。Incorrect. Shifting into neutral while driving causes you to lose engine braking and throttle control, which is extremely dangerous on wet pavement and unlawful.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A street has a posted speed limit of 50 km/h, but dense fog has reduced visibility and the pavement is slick with frost. Which statement is true?

某条街道张贴的限速为 50 km/h，但浓雾降低了能见度且路面结霜湿滑。下列哪项说法正确？

  1. ✗ 错误，掉入「把限速牌当成必达目标」陷阱。依 Motor Vehicle Act s.144(1)(c)，即使没超过牌面数值 50 km/h，速度相对于条件过快（Speed relative to conditions）也是可单独开罚单的独立违规。Incorrect. This falls into the 'treating the posted speed limit as a target to maintain' trap. Under Motor Vehicle Act s.144(1)(c), driving too fast for conditions is an independent violation that can result in a ticket even if you do not exceed the posted 50 km/h speed limit.

  2. ✗ 错误。只有低风险良好条件下无必要地开得过慢才会被评估扣分；在浓雾结霜的高风险条件下，减速慢行是法定义务。Incorrect. Driving too slowly is only penalized when driving unnecessarily slow under low-risk, ideal conditions; in high-risk conditions with dense fog and frost, driving at a reduced speed is a statutory duty.

  3. ✗ 错误，掉入「把限速牌当成必达目标」陷阱。永远不允许为了跟上车流而超速。Incorrect. This falls into the 'treating the posted speed limit as a target to maintain' trap. You are never permitted to exceed the speed limit to keep up with surrounding traffic.

  4. ✓ 正确。标志上的 50 km/h 只是理想条件下的上限（Speed limit）；在浓雾与结霜路面上按牌面速度行驶，仍可因速度相对于条件过快（Speed relative to conditions）被单独开具罚单。Correct. The posted 50 km/h is only a ceiling under ideal conditions; driving at the posted speed on frosted pavement in dense fog can still result in a separate ticket for driving too fast for conditions.

第 2 题Q2跟车距离三档秒数Three-tier following distance seconds★★陷阱：跟车秒数与特殊距离混淆

On a dry day with good visibility, you are driving behind a motorcycle on a city road. What minimum following distance should you leave, and why?

在天气晴朗、视线良好的干爽路面上，你在市区道路上跟在一辆摩托车后面行驶。你至少应留出多少跟车距离？为什么？

  1. ✗ 错误，掉入「跟车秒数与特殊距离混淆」陷阱。2 秒规则（Two-second rule）仅适用于良好条件下跟随普通乘用车；跟摩托车必须增加到 3 秒。Incorrect. This falls into the 'confusing following distance seconds with special vehicle distances' trap. The 2-second rule applies only when following standard passenger cars under good conditions; when following a motorcycle, you must increase your following distance to at least 3 seconds.

  2. ✗ 错误，把物理事实记反了。摩托车车身轻，能停得比普通轿车更快（而非更慢），因此留 2 秒容易追尾。Incorrect. This reverses the physical reality. Motorcycles are lightweight and can stop significantly faster than passenger cars, not slower, making a 2-second gap dangerous for rear-ending them.

  3. ✓ 正确。跟车距离（Following distance）分三档：跟随大型车辆或摩托车时须至少留 3 秒，其中跟摩托车留 3 秒是因为它能比你的车停得更快。Correct. Safe following distances fall into three tiers: you must leave at least 3 seconds when following a large vehicle or a motorcycle, specifically because a motorcycle can stop much more quickly than your car.

  4. ✗ 错误，掉入「跟车秒数与特殊距离混淆」陷阱。150 公尺是跟随正在执行任务的消防车（Fire truck）的专用规定，不是跟摩托车的距离。Incorrect. This falls into the 'confusing following distance seconds with special vehicle distances' trap. The 150-metre rule is a specific requirement for following a fire truck responding to an emergency, not for following a motorcycle.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

You are driving in pouring rain on wet, uneven pavement. What is the minimum following distance you should keep behind the vehicle ahead?

你在倾盆大雨中行驶在湿滑且不平的路面上。你与前车之间至少应保持多少跟车距离？

  1. ✓ 正确。在恶劣天气、路面不平或湿滑条件下，跟车距离（Following distance）必须从良好条件的 2 秒增加到至少 4 秒。Correct. In adverse weather, on uneven pavement, or in slippery conditions, your following distance must be increased from the baseline 2 seconds in good conditions to at least 4 seconds.

  2. ✗ 错误，掉入「跟车秒数与特殊距离混淆」陷阱。2 秒规则（Two-second rule）只适用于天气与路面均良好的基准条件，湿滑路面制动距离加长，2 秒不够安全。Incorrect. This falls into the 'confusing following distance seconds with special vehicle distances' trap. The 2-second rule applies only under baseline conditions with good weather and dry roads; stopping distances increase on wet pavement, making 2 seconds unsafe.

  3. ✗ 错误，掉入「泊车最小距离张冠李戴」陷阱。6 公尺是路口、人行横道与信号灯的静态泊车距离，动态跟车用秒数且在 50 km/h 时 2 秒就已约 28 公尺。Incorrect. This falls into the 'mixing up minimum statutory parking distances' trap. Six metres is the minimum parking distance from a crosswalk, intersection, or traffic light. Moving following distance is measured in seconds, and 2 seconds at 50 km/h is already about 28 metres.

  4. ✗ 错误，掉入「泊车最小距离张冠李戴」陷阱。15 公尺是铁路道口泊车最小距离，暴雨中动态跟车应保持至少 4 秒距离。Incorrect. This falls into the 'mixing up minimum statutory parking distances' trap. Fifteen metres is the minimum parking distance from a railway crossing; in pouring rain, the moving following distance should be at least 4 seconds.
