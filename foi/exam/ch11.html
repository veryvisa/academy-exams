① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 直接考清单排除（例如问哪一项不是旅行险的定价因素、哪一项不属于个人 A&S 合同特征），实质检验对高频要素清单与反例（如交通方式）的精确记忆。Direct list-elimination questions (such as asking which factor is not used in pricing travel insurance, or which item is not a characteristic of an individual A&S contract), essentially testing precise memorization of high-frequency element lists and typical non-factors (such as mode of transportation).
  • 用简短伤病场景考工伤计划与个人或团体 A&S 的赔付边界（工作操作受伤 vs 下班途中意外、职业病 vs 自然发生的疾病），实质检验工伤计划只保工作相关伤病的原则及考试口径数据。Short injury or sickness scenarios testing the coverage boundaries between workers' compensation plans and individual or group A&S (e.g., injury while performing work duties vs. accident while commuting from work, industrial disease vs. naturally occurring illness), essentially testing the principle that workers' compensation covers only work-related injuries and diseases along with exam-standard benchmark figures.
  • 考保费与保障宽窄的反向推导（比较不同失能定义、等待期长短、给付期长短及可注销与不可注销保单），实质检验理赔成本如何决定失能收入保单的保费高低。Inverse relationship deductions between premium rates and scope of coverage (comparing different disability definitions, lengths of elimination periods, lengths of benefit periods, and cancellable versus non-cancellable policies), essentially testing how the cost of claims determines premium levels for disability income policies.
  • 考两年不可抗辩期的两个法定例外（欺诈陈述 vs 年龄误报）以及可保利益、欠缴保费的处理规则，实质检验对违法欺诈与一般年龄报错不同法律后果的区分能力。Questions on the two statutory exceptions to the two-year incontestability period (fraudulent misrepresentation vs. misstatement of age) as well as rules governing insurable interest and unpaid premiums, essentially testing the ability to distinguish between the different legal consequences of fraudulent deception and simple age misstatements.
  • 用家庭出游或半途生病场景综合考旅行险条款与常见除外（受供养子女年龄与在读条件、既往症稳定期、行程取消与行程中断的出发前后分界、妊娠与职业体育除外），实质检验对多条件并列规则的实战判别。Comprehensive family travel or mid-trip illness scenarios testing travel insurance provisions and common exclusions (age and student status conditions for dependent children, pre-existing condition stability periods, pre-departure vs. post-departure dividing line between trip cancellation and trip interruption, pregnancy and professional sports exclusions), essentially testing practical application of multi-condition criteria.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 欺诈与年龄误报后果对调Confusing the consequences of fraud and age misstatement

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生只记住两年不可抗辩有两个例外，却把『欺诈导致合同通常无效』和『年龄误报仅按真实年龄调整保费或给付』的法律后果张冠李戴。Candidates remember that the two-year incontestability period has two exceptions, but mix up the legal consequences: fraudulent misrepresentation typically renders the contract void, whereas misstatement of age merely adjusts the premium or benefits to reflect the true age.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现两年后发现投保书陈述不实（incontestability）、隐瞒病史（fraudulent misrepresentation）或报错年龄（misstatement of age）。The question stem mentions an application misstatement discovered after two years (incontestability), concealment of medical history (fraudulent misrepresentation), or an incorrect age (misstatement of age).

⚠ 等待期与失能定义对保费方向反记Reversing the impact of elimination periods and disability definitions on premiums

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉误以为等待期数字越大保障越多，或分不清本职业（own occupation）与任何职业（any occupation）哪一个对被保险人更宽、理赔成本更高。Candidates intuitively assume that a longer elimination period provides more coverage, or fail to recognize whether own occupation or any occupation offers broader protection to the insured and results in higher claims costs.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干要求为客户降低保费（reduce the premium）、挑选最宽保障，或比较转行从事其他工作时哪类保单仍继续全额给付。The question stem asks how to reduce the premium for a client, select the broadest coverage, or compare which policy continues full benefits if the insured switches to another occupation.

⚠ 工伤与非工伤及团体周给付起付日混淆Confusing work-related vs. non-work-related injuries and group weekly indemnity start dates

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 容易把通勤途中受伤或自然疾病算进工伤，或把团体周给付（weekly indemnity）中事故第 1 天起付、疾病第 4／8／15 天起付的规则混为一谈。Candidates tend to misclassify commuting injuries or natural illnesses as workers' compensation claims, or confuse the rules under group weekly indemnity where benefits start on day 1 for accidents but on day 4, 8, or 15 for sickness.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述雇员非工作时间受伤、患普通感冒流感的短期请假，或询问工伤计划保费由谁承担、按什么收入基数赔付。The question stem describes an employee injured off the job, taking short-term sick leave for a common cold or flu, or asks who pays workers' compensation premiums and what earnings base is used for benefits.

⚠ 旅行险定价因素混入交通方式与受供养子女误判Injecting mode of transportation into travel insurance pricing or misjudging dependent children

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 常识觉得乘飞机比自驾风险不同会影响旅行险定价，或误以为只要未婚无业跟父母同住无论多大年龄都算受供养子女（dependant child）。Candidates intuitively assume that flying versus driving changes the risk and affects travel insurance pricing, or mistakenly believe that an unmarried, unemployed child living with parents qualifies as a dependent child regardless of age.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问哪一项不是旅行险核保定价因素，或出现超过 20 岁未在读、超过 26 岁成年子女随家庭旅行保单出险的场景。The question stem asks which factor is not used in travel insurance underwriting and pricing, or features a claim involving an adult child over age 20 not enrolled in school, or over age 26, under a family policy.

⚠ 行程取消与行程中断及除外边界颠倒Reversing trip cancellation vs. interruption and misapplying exclusion boundaries

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把出发前无法成行（trip cancellation）与出发后半途返程（trip interruption）混用，或把业余休闲运动当作职业体育（professional sports）除外。Candidates confuse inability to depart before travel begins (trip cancellation) with cutting a trip short after departure (trip interruption), or treat amateur recreational sports as excluded professional sports.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干有明确的时间节点（出发前 vs 旅途中）、预产期前 8 周内分娩、高风险妊娠或急性食物中毒等旅行索赔事件。The question stem features clear timing benchmarks (prior to departure vs. during travel), childbirth within 8 weeks of the expected delivery date, high-risk pregnancy, or travel claims such as acute food poisoning.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
失能原因与非工伤事故占比Causes of disability and proportion of non-work-related accidentsdisability / Accident and Sickness Insurance (A&S)65 岁前有 1/3 的人会遭遇超过 90 天的失能（平均超 2 年），疾病是失能的首要原因，且 90% 以上的事故与工作无关。Before age 65, one out of three people will suffer a disability lasting longer than 90 days (averaging more than 2 years); sickness is the primary cause of disability, and more than 90% of accidents are not work-related.
工伤计划核心原则与四项给付Core principles of workers' compensation plans and four categories of benefitsworkers' compensation / no fault benefits / net earnings工伤计划只保工作相关事故与职业病，保费只由雇主付，雇员获无过错给付且不起诉雇主（无痛苦赔偿），临时失能按净收入的 90% 从第 1 天起付。Workers' compensation plans cover only work-related accidents and industrial diseases; premiums are paid entirely by employers, employees receive no-fault benefits and waive the right to sue employers (no pain and suffering compensation), and temporary disability benefits pay 90% of net earnings starting from day 1.
失能定义三档宽窄对比Comparison of three tiers of disability definitionsown occupation / regular occupation / any occupation本职业（own occupation）定义最宽，无法从事本职即使转做其他工作也全额给付；任何职业（any occupation）最窄，能从事任何与教育培训经验或收入相称的工作就不赔。Own occupation is the broadest definition, paying full benefits if the insured cannot perform the duties of their own occupation even if they engage in another occupation; any occupation is the narrowest definition, paying no benefits if the insured can engage in any occupation commensurate with their education, training, experience, or earnings.
等待期与给付期规则Rules governing elimination periods and benefit periodselimination period / benefit period等待期是失能开始到给付开始前的自保期，等待期越长保费越低；给付期（1、2、5、10 年或至 65 岁）止于康复、身故或给付期结束三者中的先到者。The elimination period is the self-insured waiting period between the onset of disability and the start of benefit payments, where a longer elimination period lowers the premium; the benefit period (1, 2, 5, 10 years, or to age 65) terminates upon the earliest of recovery, death, or the expiration of the benefit period.
个人失能险定价三因素与保单类型Three pricing factors and policy types of individual disability insurancenon-cancellable / exclusion rider保费由理赔成本（含保单类型、年龄性别职业个人史、所选给付）、营运费用和投资收益决定；可注销保单只能按整类被保险人加费而不能单挑个人，不可注销保单保费保证到 65 岁不变。Premiums are determined by cost of claims (including policy type, age, sex, occupation, personal history, and selected benefits), operating expenses, and investment income; under cancellable policies, the insurer can raise rates only for an entire class of insureds rather than singling out an individual, whereas under non-cancellable policies, premiums are guaranteed to age 65.
个人 A&S 合同四特征与不可抗辩例外Four characteristics of individual A&S contracts and incontestability exceptionsincontestability / misstatement of age / insurable interest投保书是合同一部分；生效时无可保利益则合同无效（被保险人书面同意可满足）；欠费可从赔款扣除；满 2 年后不可抗辩，但欺诈陈述随时可挑战（通常无效），年龄误报则按真实年龄调整给付或保费。The application forms part of the contract; lack of insurable interest at policy inception renders the contract void (written consent of the insured satisfies the requirement); unpaid premiums may be deducted from claim payments; after two years, statements become incontestable, but fraudulent misrepresentation can be challenged at any time (typically voiding the contract), while misstatement of age adjusts benefits or premiums to reflect the true age.
团体失能保障结构与起付期Group disability coverage structure and elimination periodsgroup insurance / weekly indemnity / Long Term Disability (LTD)团体险由雇主购买且雇员离职即自动终止；周给付（13、26 或 52 周）对事故从第 1 天起付、对疾病从第 4、8 或 15 天起付，长期失能（LTD）在周给付结束时接续并常付至 65 岁。Group insurance is purchased by employers and automatically terminates upon an employee's cessation of employment; weekly indemnity (13, 26, or 52 weeks) begins on day 1 for accidents and on day 4, 8, or 15 for sickness, while Long Term Disability (LTD) attaches upon expiry of weekly indemnity and commonly pays to age 65.
旅行险保期与定价因素Travel insurance policy terms and pricing factorssingle trip / annual / coverage territory旅行险无标准保单但受法定条件约束，单次保单最长 180 天，年度保单每次可选 30、60、90 或 180 天；定价看个人或家庭、单次或年度、次数、时长、旅行地区、限额、年龄与健康，不看交通方式。Travel insurance has no standard policy forms but is governed by statutory conditions, with single-trip policies available up to 180 days and annual policies offering options of 30, 60, 90, or 180 days per trip; pricing is based on individual or family plan, single trip or annual, number of trips, duration, travel destination, policy limits, and age and health, but does not consider the mode of transportation.
受供养子女与既往症稳定期Dependent children criteria and pre-existing condition stability periodsdependant child / pre-existing condition / stability period受供养子女须未婚、同住且全额供养，年龄未满 20 岁（就读认证学校未满 26 岁，身心障碍不限年龄）；既往症不自动承保，部分保单要求出发前病情稳定 3 或 6 个月。Dependent children must be unmarried, residing in the household, and dependent on the insured for full support, under age 20 (under age 26 if attending an accredited educational institution, with no age limit for mental or physical disability); pre-existing conditions are not automatically covered, and some policies require a stability period of 3 or 6 months prior to departure.
行程取消与中断及旅行险常见除外Trip cancellation, trip interruption, and common travel insurance exclusionstrip cancellation / trip interruption / emergency行程取消保出发前因承保紧急情况或指定亲属去世无法成行，行程中断保出发后半途中断；常见除外包括预产期前 8 周内或高风险妊娠、战争、自杀、犯罪、酒精药物滥用、职业体育、危险活动、艾滋病及工伤已保费用。Trip cancellation covers inability to travel prior to departure due to a covered emergency or death of a specified family member, whereas trip interruption covers disruptions occurring after departure; common exclusions include pregnancy, miscarriage, or childbirth within 8 weeks of the expected delivery date or high-risk pregnancy, war, suicide, criminal acts, alcohol or drug abuse, professional sports, hazardous activities, AIDS, and expenses covered by workers' compensation.

第 1 题Q1个人A&S四特征与不可抗辩期例外Four characteristics of individual A&S contracts and incontestability exceptions★★陷阱：欺诈与年龄误报后果对调

An individual disability income policy has been in force for more than two years when the insurer discovers while reviewing a claim that the insured accidentally misstated their age on the original application, and also still owes an overdue renewal premium. How should the insurer handle this claim?

一份个人失能收入保单生效已超过两年，保险人在审核理赔时发现被保险人在原投保书上无意报错了年龄，且仍欠缴一笔逾期保费。保险人应如何处理该理赔？

  1. ✗ 掉入『欺诈与年龄误报后果对调』陷阱。只有欺诈陈述（fraudulent misrepresentation）或合同生效时缺乏可保利益（insurable interest）才会导致合同无效，年龄误报（misstatement of age）不会使合同无效。Falls into the trap of confusing the consequences of fraud and age misstatement. Only fraudulent misrepresentation or lack of insurable interest at policy inception renders a contract void; a misstatement of age does not void the contract.

  2. ✗ 错误理解不可抗辩期（incontestability）的范围。两年不可抗辩期明确将欺诈陈述和年龄误报列为例外，且保险人依法有权从赔款中扣除欠缴保费（unpaid premium）。Misunderstands the scope of the incontestability period. The two-year incontestability clause explicitly excludes fraudulent misrepresentation and misstatement of age as exceptions, and the insurer is legally entitled to deduct unpaid premiums from the claim payment.

  3. ✓ 符合个人意外与疾病保险（Accident and Sickness Insurance, A&S）合同特征：年龄误报（misstatement of age）是两年不可抗辩期（incontestability）的法定例外，处理方式是按真实年龄调整给付或保费而非宣告合同无效；同时欠缴保费可从理赔款中直接扣除。Correct. Matches the characteristics of individual Accident and Sickness Insurance (A&S) contracts: misstatement of age is a statutory exception to the two-year incontestability clause, handled by adjusting benefits or premiums to reflect the insured's true age rather than declaring the contract void; additionally, unpaid premiums may be directly deducted from the claim payment.

  4. ✗ 混淆了可保利益（insurable interest）中的书面同意规则。个人对自己本就有可保利益，无需亲属书面同意，合同中也无行政罚金规定。Confuses the written consent rule under insurable interest. An individual inherently has an insurable interest in their own life and health, requiring no written consent from family members, and the policy contains no provision for administrative penalties.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

More than two years after issuing an individual accident and sickness policy, an insurer proves that the applicant intentionally concealed a serious heart condition on the application. Which statement correctly describes the insurer's legal position?

一份个人意外与疾病保单出单超过两年后，保险人证实投保人在投保书上故意隐瞒了严重的心脏病史。下列哪项准确描述了保险人的法律地位？

  1. ✓ 两年不可抗辩期（incontestability）有两个例外：欺诈陈述（fraudulent misrepresentation）与年龄误报（misstatement of age）。故意隐瞒重大既往病史属于欺诈陈述，保险人任何时候都可以挑战并通常宣告合同无效。Correct. The two-year incontestability clause has two exceptions: fraudulent misrepresentation and misstatement of age. Intentionally concealing a serious pre-existing medical condition constitutes fraudulent misrepresentation, which the insurer may challenge at any time and typically declare the contract void.

  2. ✗ 忽略了不可抗辩期（incontestability）的欺诈例外。两年后不可抗辩只保护非欺诈性的陈述，欺诈陈述随时可被挑战并拒赔。Overlooks the fraud exception to incontestability. After two years, incontestability protects only non-fraudulent statements; fraudulent misrepresentation can be challenged and claims denied at any time.

  3. ✗ 掉入『欺诈与年龄误报后果对调』陷阱。按真实情况调整给付或保费是年龄误报（misstatement of age）的后果，而不是欺诈陈述的后果。Falls into the trap of confusing the consequences of fraud and age misstatement. Adjusting benefits or premiums to reflect true circumstances is the remedy for a misstatement of age, not for fraudulent misrepresentation.

  4. ✗ 违背个人 A&S 合同的第一项特征：投保书（application）本身就是保险合同的组成部分，而非仅允许扣除欠缴保费。Contradicts the first characteristic of an individual A&S contract: the application itself forms part of the insurance contract, rather than merely allowing unpaid premiums to be deducted.

第 2 题Q2个人失能险定价因素与保单类型Pricing factors and policy types of individual disability insurance★★陷阱：等待期与失能定义对保费方向反记

A self-employed graphic designer wants to lower the premium on a new individual disability income policy while also understanding how insurers adjust rates on cancellable versus non-cancellable contracts. Which combination of policy choices and contract rules is accurate?

一位自雇平面设计师希望降低新购个人失能收入保单的保费，同时想了解保险人在可注销与不可注销合同上如何调整费率。下列哪项关于投保选择与合同规则的组合是正确的？

  1. ✗ 掉入『等待期与失能定义对保费方向反记』陷阱。等待期（elimination period）越短、选择本职业（own occupation）定义，保费都会越贵；且可注销保单加费只能针对整类（class）被保险人，不能单挑个人。Falls into the trap of reversing the impact of elimination periods and disability definitions on premiums. A shorter elimination period and choosing an own-occupation definition both increase the premium; furthermore, rate increases under cancellable policies can apply only to an entire class of insureds, never singling out an individual.

  2. ✓ 等待期（elimination period）越长、给付期（benefit period）越短、失能定义越窄如任何职业（any occupation），理赔成本越低，保费就越便宜；同时不可注销（non-cancellable）保单的续保保费保证到 65 岁，保险人不能更改。Correct.   3. ✗ 混淆了受供养子女 20 岁年龄界限与既往症稳定期规则，且个人旅行险同样受法定条件（statutory conditions）约束。Confuses the age 20 threshold for dependent children with pre-existing condition stability period rules; furthermore, individual travel insurance policies are governed by statutory conditions.

  4. ✓ 既往症（pre-existing condition）不自动承保，有的保单在病情受控稳定一段时间即稳定期（stability period，三或六个月，视病情而定）后会赔，且旅行险受省法定条件（statutory conditions）约束。考试口径答稳定期三或六个月且受法定条件约束，而实务现行没有全国统一稳定期天数且 BC 省《Insurance Act》对未点名排除的既往症及团体险法定条件另有具体条文规定。Correct. Pre-existing conditions are not automatically covered, although some policies provide coverage after a stability period (3 or 6 months depending on the medical condition) where the condition has remained controlled and unchanged, and travel insurance is governed by provincial statutory conditions. For exam purposes, state that stability periods are 3 or 6 months and statutory conditions apply; current practice has no nationally standardized stability duration, and the BC Insurance Act contains specific provisions regarding pre-existing conditions not explicitly excluded and group policy statutory conditions.

答案Answer：d

第 7 题Q7旅行险行程取消中断与常见除外Travel insurance trip cancellation, trip interruption, and common exclusions★★陷阱：行程取消与行程中断及除外边界颠倒

Four travellers file claims under their travel insurance policies for events occurring during a trip outside the province. Based on common travel insurance exclusions and ancillary coverages, which claim is covered?

四名旅客因出省旅途中发生的事件向各自的旅行保险保单索赔。根据旅行险常见除外责任与附带保障，哪一项索赔会获得赔付？

  1. ✗ 踩中常见除外：预产期前 8 周内（within 8 weeks of the expected delivery date）的妊娠、流产或分娩，以及任何高风险妊娠，均属旅行险除外责任。Triggers a common exclusion: pregnancy, miscarriage, or childbirth occurring within 8 weeks of the expected delivery date, as well as any high-risk pregnancy, is explicitly excluded under travel insurance.

  2. ✗ 掉入『行程取消与行程中断及除外边界颠倒』陷阱。参加职业体育（professional sports）或危险活动（hazardous activities）属于旅行险常见除外。Falls into the trap of misapplying exclusion boundaries. Participating in professional sports or hazardous activities is a common exclusion under travel insurance.

  3. ✓ 旅途中突然发生的严重食物中毒符合紧急情况（emergency：保险期内突然发生、不可预见、需要立即治疗的健康问题）定义，且因为已经出发，半途住院导致的不可退预付旅费、酒店康复及返程费用属于行程中断（trip interruption）在限额内承保的范围。Correct. Sudden severe food poisoning during travel meets the definition of an emergency (an unforeseen, unexpected medical condition occurring during the coverage period requiring immediate treatment), and because the trip had already commenced, mid-trip hospitalization resulting in non-refundable prepaid travel expenses, hotel convalescence, and return transportation falls within the coverage limits of trip interruption.

  4. ✗ 掉入『行程取消与行程中断及除外边界颠倒』陷阱。行程取消险（trip cancellation）只在出发日之前（prior to departure）起作用；出发后在航程途中受伤中断行程属于行程中断（trip interruption），不是行程取消。Falls into the trap of confusing trip cancellation with trip interruption. Trip cancellation operates only prior to departure; an injury occurring mid-voyage during the trip that forces travel to end falls under trip interruption, not trip cancellation.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Prior to departing on a prepaid international tour, an insured's immediate family member covered by the policy terms passes away unexpectedly, making it impossible for the insured to travel. Which travel insurance coverage responds to this loss, and how does it differ from coverage that applies after departure?

在预付国际旅行团出发前，保单条款所列的一名指定亲属突发去世，导致被保险人无法成行。哪项旅行险保障对此损失作出回应？它与出发后适用的保障有何区别？

  1. ✓ 行程取消险（trip cancellation）在出发日之前起作用：当出发前发生承保的医疗紧急情况或指定亲属去世导致被保险人不可能成行时予以补偿；而出发后发生变故则由行程中断（trip interruption）承保。Correct. Trip cancellation coverage operates prior to departure: it reimburses the insured when a covered medical emergency or the death of a specified family member occurs before the departure date, making it impossible to travel; disruptions occurring after departure are covered under trip interruption.

  2. ✗ 掉入『行程取消与行程中断及除外边界颠倒』陷阱。行程中断（trip interruption）只管出发之后的损失，出发前无法成行导致预付旅费损失归行程取消（trip cancellation）管。Falls into the trap of reversing trip cancellation and trip interruption. Trip interruption covers only losses occurring after departure; prepaid travel losses caused by inability to depart before the trip begins are covered by trip cancellation.

  3. ✗ 混淆了可选非医疗保障（行李、机票、护照遗失与法律咨询）与行程取消险（trip cancellation）。指定亲属去世正是行程取消险的标准承保触发事件之一。Confuses optional non-medical coverages (baggage, ticket, passport loss, and legal counsel) with trip cancellation. The death of an immediate family member is one of the standard covered triggers under trip cancellation.

  4. ✗ 把承保地域（coverage territory：离家 500 公里以上或出省生效）的旅途医疗生效条件错误套用到出发前的行程取消险（trip cancellation）上。Erroneously applies coverage territory requirements (effective more than 500 kilometres from home or out-of-province) for travel medical coverage to pre-departure trip cancellation coverage.
