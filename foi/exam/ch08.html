第 8 章 · 法律责任原理Chapter 8 · Principles of Legal LiabilityPrinciples of Legal Liability

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 限定词偷换型（「找不对的那一句」）：在四句判例规则中把 legal duty 换成 moral duty、ordinary care 换成 highest care、generally 换成 always，专测考生是否背准每条规则的精确边界。Qualifier substitution questions (identifying the false statement): Replacing legal duty with moral duty, ordinary care with highest care, or generally with always within four common law rule statements, specifically testing whether candidates have memorized the precise boundaries of each legal rule.
  • 成文法 vs 普通法特征辨析：把「以先例为基础（based on precedent）」张冠李戴到成文法上，或考成文法通过后与旧先例冲突时谁优先。Statute law versus common law distinction questions: Misattributing based on precedent to statute law, or testing which source of law takes priority when an enacted statute conflicts with an earlier common law precedent.
  • 进入者身份与注意义务分级配对：给出进入场所的具体场景（顾客、抄表员、侵入者、受诱惑物吸引的儿童，或四省合并后的访客），要求选出占用人对应的注意义务档次。Entrant category and duty of care matching questions: Providing specific scenarios of persons entering premises (such as retail customers, meter readers, trespassers, children attracted by allurements, or visitors under four provincial statutes), requiring candidates to identify the corresponding standard of care owed by the occupier.
  • 雇主替代责任与独立承包商的边界判断：通过送货员顺路办私事、私自找人顶替、或业主插手指挥承包商施工等具体故事，测试「是否在职务过程中」及三项共担例外。Vicarious liability and independent contractor boundary questions: Using factual scenarios such as delivery workers running personal errands, unauthorized job delegation, or owners interfering with and directing contractor work, testing whether an act occurred in the course of employment and the three exceptions triggering shared liability.
  • 特殊主体（房东、保管人、连带侵权人、动物主人、父母）的默认规则与例外：先考默认不负责或按同行标准负责，再考触发责任的法定例外条件（如收到合理通知仍不修、家犬第一口就赔、父母四项例外）。Special legal parties (landlords, bailees, joint tortfeasors, animal owners, and parents) default rules and exception questions: Testing default positions of non-liability or customary trade standards, followed by statutory exception conditions triggering legal liability (such as failure to repair after reasonable notice, liability for a domestic dog from the first bite, and four parental liability exceptions).

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 限定词偷换陷阱Qualifier substitution trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 日常口语里觉得有道义就该赔、保管别人财物该尽最高注意、父母当然替孩子赔钱，容易顺着直觉放过被篡改的限定词。In everyday speech, people feel moral obligations warrant payment, holding others' goods requires the highest degree of care, or parents should naturally pay for their children's damage, leading candidates to intuitively overlook altered qualifying terms.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问 Which statement is FALSE / NOT true，且选项中出现 moral duty、highest degree of care、all sources of loss、always responsible 等绝对化或拔高词。The stem asks Which statement is FALSE or NOT true, and options contain absolutist or exaggerated terms such as moral duty, highest degree of care, all sources of loss, or always responsible.

⚠ 法源特征张冠李戴Misattributing legal source characteristics

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 普通法（common law）和成文法（statute law）同属法律渊源，考生容易混淆「靠先例（precedent）」与「书面成文且优先于先例」的归属。Common law and statute law are both primary sources of Canadian law, making it easy for candidates to confuse which one is based on precedent versus which one is written and takes priority over precedent.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干聚焦 statute law 或 common law 的定义、成年年龄变更（21 岁降为 18 或 19 岁），或问哪类法律可以在没有先例的领域立规。The stem focuses on the definition of statute law or common law, the reduction of the age of majority from 21 to 18 or 19, or asks which body of law can establish rules in areas with no prior court decisions.

⚠ 进入者义务档次错位Entrant duty of care mismatch

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 普通法下侵入者、被许可人、受邀人、儿童四档义务层层递增，加上 BC 等四省把被许可人与受邀人合并为访客（visitor），极易把「免于任何危险」错套给受邀人。Under common law, duties owed to trespassers, licensees, invitees, and children increase progressively, and with four provinces merging licensees and invitees into visitors, candidates easily misapply keeping premises free from any danger to invitees.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干点明进入者的身份（trespasser / licensee / invitee / visitor / children）或出现 attractive nuisance / allurement。The stem specifies the entrant's status (trespasser, licensee, invitee, visitor, or children) or mentions an attractive nuisance or allurement.

⚠ 职务过程与承包控制混淆Confusing course of employment with contractor control

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到穿着公司制服、开着公司车或受雇于业主就以为雇主/占用人一定赔，忽略了「办私事（frolic of their own）」免责和业主「插手控制施工方式」要共担。Seeing an employee in company uniform, driving a company van, or hired by an owner leads candidates to assume the employer or occupier always pays, overlooking non-liability for a frolic of their own and shared liability when an owner directs contractor methods.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描写雇员下班私用设备、私自找外人代班、午休接私活，或业主向独立承包商提供缺陷工具、亲自指挥具体施工做法。The stem describes an employee using equipment after hours, delegating duties to an outsider, taking private jobs at lunch, or an owner providing defective tools and directing specific construction methods.

⚠ 严格责任与拒赔划等号Equating strict liability with claim denial

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把「严格责任（strict liability，不问过错）」误当成「故意侵权（intentional tort）」，误以为责任保单像拒赔犯罪或故意伤害那样拒赔严格责任，或以为家养狗适用「第一口免费」。Candidates mistake strict liability (liability without fault) for intentional torts, assuming liability policies exclude strict liability just as they exclude crimes or intentional harm, or mistakenly believing domestic dogs have a free first bite.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及 Rylands v. Fletcher、危险活动/危险物逃逸、野生动物 vs 家养狗，或问责任保单是否承保严格责任。The stem mentions Rylands v. Fletcher, dangerous activities, escape of dangerous substances, wild animals versus domestic dogs, or asks whether liability policies cover strict liability.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
刑法与民法的分工及可保性Criminal Law versus Civil Law division and insurabilityCriminal Law vs. Civil Law (Private Law)刑法处理对社会的错行并以罚款、缓刑、监禁定罪处罚，保险不保犯罪行为的财务后果；民法解决私人纠纷，目标是让受损方获得经济赔偿（damages）。Criminal law addresses wrongs against society and punishes offenders through fines, probation, and imprisonment, with insurance never covering the financial consequences of criminal acts; civil law resolves private disputes with the goal of awarding financial damages to compensate the injured party.
普通法与成文法的优先关系Priority relationship between Common Law and Statute LawCommon Law (Precedent) and Statute Law魁北克以外各省适用基于先例（precedent）的普通法；成文法是议会通过的书面法，一旦通过即优先于针对同一事项的任何普通法先例。Provinces outside Quebec operate under common law based on the rule of precedent; statute law is written law enacted by parliament or provincial legislatures that, once passed, takes priority over any conflicting common law precedent on the same subject.
侵权成立的三个要素Three essential elements of a tortThree Elements of a Tort任何侵权诉讼必须同时具备三要素：被告对原告负有法律义务（duty owed，非道德义务）、违反了该义务（duty breached）、损害是违约行为的直接后果（proximate cause）。Every successful tort action must satisfy three concurrent elements: the defendant owed a legal duty to the plaintiff (a legal duty, not merely a moral duty), that duty was breached by an act or omission, and the damage suffered was the direct result and proximate cause of the breach.
过失的标准与两项额外条件Standard of negligence and two additional requirementsNegligence, Reasonable Person, and Foreseeability过失是没做合理人会做的事或做了合理谨慎人不会做的事；除三要素外，原告还必须举证证明被告过失，且后果必须是可合理预见的（foreseeable）。Negligence is doing what a reasonable person would not do or failing to do what a reasonable and prudent person would do; beyond the three basic tort elements, the plaintiff must prove the defendant was negligent, and the consequences must have been reasonably foreseeable.
严格责任的三类活动与保单承保Three categories of strict liability activities and insurance coverageStrict Liability (Rylands v. Fletcher)合理活动放在不合理地点、本身危险的活动、让故意点燃的火蔓延出去（以及场所危险物逃逸、饲养野生动物）适用不问过错的严格责任，除非保单另有除外，责任险一般照赔。Conducting a reasonable activity in an unreasonable location, engaging in inherently dangerous activities, and allowing a deliberately lit fire to spread (along with the escape of dangerous substances and keeping wild animals) trigger strict liability without proof of fault; standard liability insurance covers these claims unless specifically excluded.
占用人对不同进入者的注意义务Occupier duty of care owed to different classes of entrantsOccupier's Liability (Trespasser, Licensee, Invitee, Visitor, Children)普通法对侵入者仅限不设陷阱不故意伤害，对被许可人防已知隐蔽危险，对受邀人防已知及合理注意能发现的危险，对儿童须使场所免于任何危险；BC、AB、ON、NS 将被许可人与受邀人合并为访客（visitor），统一要求尽一切合理注意确保场所合理安全。Under traditional common law, occupiers owe trespassers only a duty not to set deliberate traps or intentionally cause harm, owe licensees protection against known concealed dangers, owe invitees protection against both known dangers and those discoverable with reasonable care, and must keep premises free from any danger for children; under statutes in BC, Alberta, Ontario, and Nova Scotia, licensees and invitees are merged into visitors, requiring reasonable care to keep them reasonably safe.
房东、租客与有偿保管人的责任标准Liability standards for landlords, tenants, and bailees for hireLandlord, Tenant, and Bailee for Hire (Ordinary Care)房东默认不保证房屋适合用途（明示保证、隐瞒缺陷、带家具出租、约定维修且收到合理通知后仍不修、多租户公共区域除外）；租客可依侵权或合同对房东负责；有偿保管人须尽同行水平的通常注意（ordinary care）。Landlords do not warrant that premises are fit for purpose by default (except for express warranty, concealed defects, furnished rentals, failure to repair after reasonable notice under contract, and multi-tenant common areas); tenants are liable to landlords in tort or contract; bailees for hire must exercise ordinary care matching the standards of others in the same trade.
雇主替代责任的三项例外与工伤保险Three exceptions to vicarious liability and workers compensationVicarious Liability and Workers' Compensation雇主对雇员职务过程中的侵权负替代责任，但雇员擅自转托他人、办私事（frolic of their own）、未经许可私用雇主财产三者除外；受工伤保险保障的雇员领无过错给付并放弃起诉雇主，范围外行业需买 Employer's Liability Rider 或自愿参保。Employers are vicariously liable for torts committed by employees in the course of employment, but are excused when an employee delegates work without authority, embarks on a frolic of their own, or uses employer property without permission; workers covered by workers compensation receive no-fault benefits and surrender the right to sue the employer, while exempt industries require an Employer's Liability Rider or voluntary enrollment.
独立承包商三项共担例外与连带责任Independent contractor shared liability exceptions and joint tortfeasorsIndependent Contractor and Joint Tortfeasors占用人选任独立承包商尽了合理注意且工作本应进行则一般不负责，但工作本身危险、占用人提供缺陷设备、占用人控制施工方式三种情形须共担；共同侵权人对份额有争议时各自对全额负责（实践中先各付一半）。An occupier who exercises reasonable care in selecting an independent contractor for necessary work is generally not liable, but must share liability if the work is inherently dangerous, if the occupier supplies defective equipment, or if the occupier controls how the work is performed; when joint tortfeasors dispute fault proportions, each remains liable to the plaintiff for the full award (normally paying half initially in practice).
动物责任与儿童及父母的四项例外Animal liability and four exceptions for child and parent liabilityDomestic/Wild Animals and Liability of Children/Parents家养狗没有「第一口免费」（出自安大略省法律），野生动物负严格责任；儿童能理解后果则自己负责，父母一般不代赔，仅在监管失职、交给危险物或动物、按父母指示行事、受雇于父母生意四种情形下负责。Under exam rules, domestic dogs have no free first bite (derived from Ontario law) and wild animals carry strict liability; children who appreciate the consequences of their actions are personally liable, and parents are generally not liable unless they fail to supervise, entrust a dangerous object or animal to the child, direct the act, or employ the child in the family business.

第 1 题Q1普通法与成文法的特征及优先关系Characteristics and priority relationship of common law and statute law★陷阱：法源特征张冠李戴

A trainee broker is reviewing the sources of law used in Canadian provinces outside Quebec. Which of the following statements regarding common law and statute law is INCORRECT?

一名见习经纪人正在复习魁北克以外加拿大各省使用的法律渊源。下列关于普通法与成文法的陈述中，哪一项是不正确的？

  1. ✗ 该项本身正确，故不选。普通法（common law）以先例原则（rule of precedent）为基础，法院遇到相似案情一般按以往判决审理。Incorrect because the statement itself is true. Common law is based on the rule of precedent, meaning courts generally follow decisions rendered in earlier similar cases.

  2. ✓ 正确选项（陈述错误）。成文法（statute law）是联邦议会或省议会通过的书面法，可以在完全没有先例的领域立规，并非以先例（precedent）为基础；把「基于先例」套在成文法上属于典型的【法源特征张冠李戴】。Correct as the false statement. Statute law is written law enacted by parliament or provincial legislatures that can establish rules in areas with no prior court decisions; it is not derived from court precedents, representing a classic trap of misattributing legal source characteristics.

  3. ✗ 该项本身正确，故不选。成文法一旦通过，就优先于针对同一事项的任何普通法先例。Incorrect because the statement itself is true. Once enacted, statute law takes priority over and supersedes any conflicting common law precedent on the same subject.

  4. ✗ 该项本身正确，故不选。普通法下成年年龄为 21 岁，多数省份通过成文法将其降到了 18 或 19 岁（考试按此通说作答；BC《Age of Majority Act》现行规定为 19 岁成年）。Incorrect because the statement itself is true. Under common law the age of majority was 21, but most provinces enacted statutes lowering it to 18 or 19 (exam candidates follow this general rule; BC Age of Majority Act sets the current age of majority at 19).

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

During a licensing study session, four candidates compare how common law and statute law operate in British Columbia. Which candidate's statement is ACCURATE?

在一次考牌学习讨论中，四名考生比较了普通法与成文法在不列颠哥伦比亚省的运作方式。哪位考生的陈述是准确的？

  1. ✗ 颠倒了优先顺序。省议会通过成文法（statute law）后，优先于同一事项上的任何普通法先例（common law precedent），属于【法源特征张冠李戴】。Incorrect because it reverses the order of priority. When a provincial legislature passes a statute, statute law takes precedence over any conflicting common law precedent on the same subject, falling into the trap of misattributing legal source characteristics.

  2. ✗ 混淆了成文法的立法条件。议会可以在完全没有先例的领域直接通过成文法制定规则，不需要先有法院判例，属于【法源特征张冠李戴】。Incorrect because it misstates the prerequisites for legislation. Legislatures have the authority to enact statutes and create rules in areas where no prior judicial precedents exist, falling into the trap of misattributing legal source characteristics.

  3. ✗ 违背普通法演进规则。当社会时代条件变化时，旧的普通法先例可能被法院推翻，属于【限定词偷换陷阱】中的绝对化表述。Incorrect because it violates the evolutionary nature of common law. As social values and conditions evolve over time, courts have the power to overturn earlier common law precedents, representing an absolutist qualifier substitution trap.

  4. ✓ 正确。议会或省议会通过的书面成文法（statute law）优先于针对同一事项的任何冲突普通法先例。Correct. Written statute law enacted by parliament or a provincial legislature supersedes and takes priority over any conflicting common law precedent on the same subject.

答案Answer：d

第 2 题Q2侵权三要素与过失的可预见性Three elements of a tort and foreseeability in negligence★★陷阱：限定词偷换陷阱

To succeed in a civil action for a tort, the plaintiff must establish three essential elements. Which of the following is NOT one of those required elements?

要在侵权民事诉讼中胜诉，原告必须证明三个基本要素。下列哪一项不属于这些必备要素？

  1. ✓ 正确选项（不属于侵权三要素）。侵权法要求的第一要素是被告对原告负有法律上的义务（legal duty owed），把 legal 偷换成 moral duty（道德义务）是考卷最经典的【限定词偷换陷阱】。Correct as the false element. The first essential element of a tort is that the defendant owed a legal duty to the plaintiff; substituting a moral duty for a legal duty is a classic qualifier substitution trap on licensing exams.

  2. ✗ 该项属于侵权第二要素（duty breached），故不选。被告必须违反了对原告负有的法律义务。Incorrect because this is the second required element of a tort (duty breached). The defendant must have failed to fulfill the legal duty owed to the plaintiff through an affirmative act or omission.

  3. ✗ 该项属于侵权第三要素，故不选。必须真有伤害或损失，且是被告违反义务行为的直接后果。Incorrect because this is the third required element of a tort. The plaintiff must have sustained actual bodily injury or property damage as a direct consequence of the defendant's breach.

  4. ✗ 该项是第三要素中近因（proximate cause）的正确表述，故不选。事件须从最初行为到最终结果不间断展开，中间没有插入另一个主因。Incorrect because this is an accurate statement of proximate cause within the third element. An unbroken chain of events must link the initial breach directly to the resulting loss without an independent intervening cause.

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

After slipping on icy steps outside a bakery, a courier breaks an ankle. On the way to the hospital, the ambulance is struck by a driver who runs a red light, giving the courier a concussion. Under the elements of a tort and negligence, what is the bakery occupier's legal position?

一名快递员在面包店外的结冰台阶上滑倒摔断脚踝。送医途中，救护车被一名闯红灯的司机撞上，导致快递员脑震荡。根据侵权三要素与过失规则，面包店占用人的法律责任是什么？

  1. ✗ 忽略了近因（proximate cause）中断规则。虽然最初在结冰台阶滑倒开启了事件链，但闯红灯司机是插进来的另一个主因，脑震荡并非台阶结冰的直接后果，属于【限定词偷换陷阱】。Incorrect because it overlooks the break in proximate cause. Although slipping on the icy steps initiated the sequence, the red-light-running driver acted as an independent intervening cause, meaning the concussion was not a direct result of the icy steps, falling into the qualifier substitution trap.

  2. ✗ 颠倒了普通法过失的举证责任。过失必须由原告证明（negligence must be proved），被告在被证明前视为无辜，并非预设对两项伤害都有过失、须自证已清理撒盐才能免责，只有严格责任才倒置举证，属于【严格责任与拒赔划等号】。Incorrect because it reverses the burden of proof in negligence.   4. ✓ 正确。按考试口径，占用人选任独立承包商尽了合理注意且工作本应进行时一般不负责，但在三种情形下须与承包商共担责任：工作本身危险、伤害由占用人提供的有缺陷设备造成、或占用人控制了施工方式。（注：本题按考试口径作答；BC 现行《Occupiers Liability Act》s.5(1)(a) 还要求占用人在选任之外对监督 supervision 也尽到合理注意才能免责。）Correct. Under exam rules, an occupier who exercises reasonable care in selecting an independent contractor is normally not liable, but must share liability if the work is inherently dangerous, if the occupier supplies defective equipment, or if the occupier controls how the work is executed (note: this reflects exam standard rules; current BC Occupiers Liability Act s.5(1)(a) additionally requires reasonable care in supervising contractor work to avoid liability).

答案Answer：d

第 7 题Q7连带责任、家养动物与父母对子女的责任Joint liability, domestic animals, and parental liability for children★★★陷阱：限定词偷换陷阱

Which of the following statements concerning joint tortfeasors, animals, and children is CORRECT under the rules tested on the exam?

根据考试所考的规则，下列关于共同侵权人、动物和儿童的陈述中，哪一项是正确的？

  1. ✗ 错误。父母一般不必为未成年子女的侵权付钱（仅在监管失职、交给危险物或动物、按父母指示行事、受雇于父母生意四种例外情形下负责），使用 always 属于官方练习卷第 73 题的【限定词偷换陷阱】。Incorrect because parents are generally not liable in tort for the actions of their minor children, becoming liable only under four specific exceptions (failure to supervise, entrusting dangerous things or animals, acting on the parents' instructions, or employment in the family business); using always represents a qualifier substitution trap from question 73 of the official practice exam.

  2. ✗ 错误。按考试口径（源自安大略省法律），家养狗不存在「第一口免费（no free first bite）」，即使此前从未表现出攻击性，主人也从第一口起就负责，属于【严格责任与拒赔划等号】。（注：本题按考试口径作答；BC 现行法律并无安省同类狗主责任专法，《Community Charter》s.49 仅管危险犬扣押与处死申请。）Incorrect because under the national exam standard (derived from Ontario statute law), there is no free first bite for domestic dogs; owners are legally liable from the very first bite even without prior signs of viciousness, falling into the trap of equating strict liability with claim denial (note: exam answers follow this textbook rule; BC law does not have a comparable dog owner liability statute, and Community Charter s.49 addresses court seizure and destruction of dangerous dogs).

  3. ✓ 正确。按考试口径，共同侵权人（joint tortfeasors）对各自过错份额有争议时，为了让原告尽快拿到赔偿，法律规定每个侵权人都要对原告的全部赔偿额负责（实践中通常先各付一半，再内部结算）。（注：本题按考试口径作答；BC 现行《Negligence Act》s.4(2) 与 s.1(2) 规定对无过错受损人连带、内部按过错比例分担，无法确定比例时才均分。）Correct. Under exam rules, when joint tortfeasors dispute their respective shares of fault, each is held legally liable to the plaintiff for the entire damage award to ensure prompt compensation (in practice, each commonly pays half initially pending internal settlement; note: under current BC Negligence Act s.4(2) and s.1(2), tortfeasors are jointly and severally liable to innocent plaintiffs with fault apportioned proportionately, and damages are split equally only when fault degrees cannot be determined).

  4. ✗ 错误。未成年儿童对自己造成的伤害或损害负责，除非能证明他不能理解自己行为的后果（unable to appreciate the consequences of their actions），属于【限定词偷换陷阱】。Incorrect because minor children are personally liable for their torts unless it is proven that they were unable to appreciate the consequences of their actions; claiming children can never be held liable represents a qualifier substitution trap.

A 14-year-old is given a high-powered snowmobile by their parents and rides it unsupervised across a neighbour's landscaped yard, destroying rare shrubs. Under what rule can the neighbour hold the parents legally liable?

  1. ✓ 正确。父母默认不代赔子女的侵权，但有四项例外：明显未尽监管控制、损害由父母交给孩子控制的危险物或动物（如枪、刀、全地形车、雪地摩托、鞭炮、猛兽）造成、按父母指示授权行事、或受雇于父母的生意。本题触发了「交给危险物并疏于监管」例外。Correct.   2. ✗ 错误。父母一般不替同住的未成年子女赔钱，并非自动且无条件对一切财产损害负责，属于【限定词偷换陷阱】。Incorrect because parents are not automatically and unconditionally liable for all property damage committed by minor children residing in their home, falling into the qualifier substitution trap.

  4. ✗ 错误。无论是14岁能理解后果的少年本人，还是把危险物交给孩子的父母，都不存在「此前未造成类似邻里财产损害就免责」的说法，属于【严格责任与拒赔划等号】。Incorrect because neither a 14-year-old capable of appreciating consequences nor parents entrusting dangerous equipment are shielded by a lack of prior property damage, falling into the trap of equating strict liability with claim denial.
