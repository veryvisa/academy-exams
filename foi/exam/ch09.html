① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 反向否定题（下面哪一句不适用）：官方练习卷在本章连出 4 道此类题，分别盯住承保协议五要点、事故发生制与限额、被保险人出险后五项义务、附加给付四项，专查考生能不能一眼揪出混入其中的伪规则。Negative elimination questions (Which statement does NOT apply): The official practice exam features 4 consecutive questions of this type for this chapter, targeting the five points of the insuring agreement, occurrence basis and limits, the insured's five post-loss duties, and the four supplementary payments, testing whether candidates can immediately identify spurious rules mixed into the options.
  • 财产损害与人身伤害边界判定题：给你一个生活或经营场景（如未直接受损但无法使用的邻屋开支、因伤引发的后续疾病或数月后死亡），考你它算不算保单定义的 bodily injury 或 property damage。Boundary determination questions for property damage and bodily injury: Presenting a real-life or business scenario (such as extra living expenses for an undamaged but unusable neighboring building, or subsequent illness or death occurring months after an injury), testing whether it falls under the policy definitions of bodily injury or property damage.
  • 三种限额两步计算题：给出多次索赔或单次事故多名伤者的金额，考 per occurrence limit、aggregate limit 或 split limits 的赔付计算，检验「先压每次/每人、再卡累计/单次合计」的顺序。Two-step limit calculation questions: Providing amounts for multiple claims or multiple injured claimants from a single accident, testing claim payout calculations under a per occurrence limit, aggregate limit, or split limits to verify the sequence of 'capping per occurrence or per person first, then checking the aggregate or single-accident total'.
  • 出险后义务与违约后果情景题：描述被保险人觉得对方没事而未报案、当场私了给钱或试图阻止对方索赔，考你哪项行为违反保单条件、为什么唯有急救费用（first aid）属于例外。Post-loss duties and breach scenario questions: Describing situations where an insured fails to report an incident assuming the other party was unharmed, pays cash on the spot to settle, or attempts to prevent the claimant from filing, testing which actions breach policy conditions and why first aid expenses are the sole exception.
  • 附加给付（supplementary payments）归属判断：给出抗辩费、诉讼费、判决后利息及超限额判决本金，考哪些在保单限额之外另付、哪些必须由被保险人自掏腰包。Supplementary payments classification questions: Listing defence costs, court costs, post-judgment interest, and judgment awards exceeding policy limits, testing which items are paid over and above the policy limit and which must be absorbed out-of-pocket by the insured.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 把使用损失排除在财产损害外Excluding Loss of Use from Property Damage

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉认为只有东西被砸坏、烧毁的修复或更换费才叫财产损害，忽略了有形财产的使用损失（含未受物理损坏财产的使用损失）也属于财产损害。Intuitively assuming that property damage refers only to the repair or replacement costs of physically damaged or destroyed property, overlooking that loss of use of tangible property (including tangible property that has not suffered physical damage) also qualifies as property damage.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干或选项出现 only covers physical repair or replacement、excludes loss of use，或问邻居因火灾临时租房与宠物寄养费归入哪一类。The question stem or options contain phrases like 'only covers physical repair or replacement' or 'excludes loss of use', or ask how a neighbor's temporary rent and pet boarding costs caused by a fire are classified.

⚠ 道义责任与私自认责陷阱Moral Obligation and Voluntary Admission of Liability Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为被保险人自己承认错误、觉得内疚，或保险理算员、律师口头判断有错，就能触发责任保单赔付，甚至自己先掏钱安抚伤者。Mistakenly assuming that an insured feeling guilty, admitting fault, or an insurance adjuster or lawyer verbally suggesting fault can trigger coverage under a liability policy, leading the insured to pay cash on the spot to placate the injured party.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 出现 morally responsible、insured admits fault、pays cash on the spot to settle，或问谁有权最终决定被保险人在法律上是否有责任。The question stem mentions 'morally responsible', 'insured admits fault', 'pays cash on the spot to settle', or asks who ultimately determines whether the insured is legally liable.

⚠ 每次事故限额当成全年总额Mistaking Per Occurrence Limit for Policy Period Total

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把 per occurrence limit 误解为保单期内所有索赔加起来的最高赔付上限，或者在算 aggregate limit、split limits 时忘了先用每次/每人限额压第一道。Misinterpreting the per occurrence limit as the maximum aggregate payout for all claims during the policy period, or failing to first apply the per occurrence or per person limit when calculating payouts under aggregate limits or split limits.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 选项描述 per occurrence limit 为 maximum paid for all occurrences during the policy period，或题干给出三次事故、三位伤者的一串美元数字。Options describe the per occurrence limit as the 'maximum paid for all occurrences during the policy period', or the stem presents a series of dollar amounts for three occurrences or three injured parties.

⚠ 惩罚性赔偿与超限额本金混入保单Confusing Punitive Damages and Excess Judgments with Covered Policy Benefits

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到法院判决金额就以为保险公司全包，没区分补偿性赔偿（compensatory damages）与惩罚性赔偿（punitive damages），或以为附加给付能兜底超限额的判决本金。Assuming the insurer pays the entire court award upon seeing a judgment amount, failing to distinguish covered compensatory damages from uncovered punitive damages, or incorrectly believing that supplementary payments cover judgment principal exceeding policy limits.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 选项同时列出 compensatory, punitive, and exemplary damages，或声称 supplementary payments pay judgments in excess of the policy limit。Options list compensatory, punitive, and exemplary damages together, or claim that supplementary payments cover judgments in excess of the policy limit.

⚠ 等对方索赔才报案或强行阻拦索赔Waiting for a Claim Before Reporting or Forcibly Preventing Claims

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把报告义务的对象记成 claim 而不是 occurrence，以为伤者口头说没事就不用报；或被「被保险人必须尽力阻止第三方索赔」这种看似替保险公司省钱的假义务骗倒。Mistaking the object of the reporting duty as a formal claim rather than any occurrence that may give rise to a claim, believing no report is needed if the injured party claims to be uninjured, or falling for the distractor that the insured must try to prevent the third party from making a claim.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干说伤者当场表示没事所以没报案、几周后才起诉，或选项出现 prevent the third party from making a claim。The question stem states that the injured party claimed to be unhurt so no report was filed until a lawsuit was served weeks later, or an option includes 'prevent the third party from making a claim'.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
承保协议核心承诺Core Promise of the Insuring Agreementinsuring agreement保险人承诺支付被保险人在法律上应付给第三方的补偿性赔偿，且损害须由保单期内、承保地域内的一次 occurrence 造成。The insurer agrees to pay compensatory damages that the insured becomes legally obligated to pay to a third party, provided the injury or damage is caused by an occurrence within the policy period and coverage territory.
人身伤害与财产损害定义Definitions of Bodily Injury and Property Damagebodily injury (BI) / property damage (PD)人身伤害含身体伤害、疾病及由此日后导致的死亡；财产损害含他人有形财产的物理损坏与使用损失，以及未受物理损坏的有形财产的使用损失。Bodily injury includes physical bodily harm, sickness, or disease, and resulting death; property damage includes physical injury to tangible property including resulting loss of use, as well as the loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically injured.
只保第三方Coverage Limited to Third Partiesthird party第三方是指保单上没有列名的任何人或实体，被保险人自己受伤或自有财产受损不属于责任险保障范围。A third party refers to any person or entity not named in the policy; injury suffered by the insured or damage to the insured's own property is excluded.
法律责任由法院认定Legal Liability Determined by a Courtlegally liable / out-of-court settlement道义责任不触发保单，被保险人是否在法律上有责任最终由法院认定，但证据明显支持原告时多数案件以庭外和解结束。Moral obligations do not trigger the policy; whether the insured is legally liable is ultimately decided by a court of law, though the majority of cases are resolved through out-of-court settlements when evidence clearly favours the plaintiff.
事故发生制与事故定义Occurrence Basis and the Definition of Occurrenceoccurrence / accident / occurrence basisoccurrence 比突发且时间确定的 accident 更宽，还包含长期持续或反复暴露于大体相同的有害条件；发生制只看伤害或损害是否发生在保单期内。An occurrence is broader than a sudden and time-definite accident, also encompassing continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same harmful conditions; coverage on an occurrence basis requires only that the bodily injury or property damage occurs during the policy period.
每次事故限额与累计限额Per Occurrence Limit vs. Aggregate Limitlimit per occurrence / aggregate limit每次事故限额对每次事故都重新可用；设有累计限额时，先用每次事故限额压算单次赔付，再受保单期内累计限额剩余额度的封顶约束。The per occurrence limit resets and is fully available for each separate occurrence; when an aggregate limit applies, claims are first capped by the per occurrence limit and then subject to the remaining cumulative aggregate limit for the policy period.
分项限额与综合限额Split Limits vs. Inclusive Limitsplit limits / inclusive limit综合限额由人身伤害与财产损害合用一个数；分项限额拆为每人人身伤害、每次事故人身伤害合计、每次事故财产损害三个数，计算时先压每人限额再看单次合计限额。An inclusive limit applies a single dollar amount combining bodily injury and property damage; split limits specify separate amounts for bodily injury per person, total bodily injury per occurrence, and property damage per occurrence, calculated by capping each person's claim first before applying the single-occurrence bodily injury total.
只赔补偿性赔偿Compensatory Damages Covered Exclusivelycompensatory damages / punitive or exemplary damages责任保单只赔旨在弥补第三方损失的补偿性赔偿，法院为惩罚和威慑而判处的惩罚性或惩戒性赔偿完全由被保险人自付。Liability policies pay only compensatory damages intended to indemnify third parties for actual loss; punitive or exemplary damages awarded by a court to punish or deter wrongful conduct must be borne entirely out of pocket by the insured.
出险后五项义务与急救例外Five Post-Loss Duties and the First Aid Exceptionnotice of a claim / first aid / admission of liability被保险人必须及时报告可能引起索赔的 occurrence、立即转交法律文件、授权取证、协助调查和解与抗辩，且除事故当时的急救费用外不得未经同意自行付款或承担义务。The insured must promptly report any occurrence that may give rise to a claim, immediately forward legal documents, authorize access to records, and assist in investigation, settlement, or defence; furthermore, the insured must not voluntarily make payments or assume obligations without consent, with emergency first aid at the time of the accident being the sole exception.
限额之外的四项附加给付Four Supplementary Payments Beyond Policy Limitssupplementary payments / costs of defence / court costs抗辩费用、应保险人要求发生的合理协助费用、判由被保险人承担的诉讼费、以及自判决日起就保单限额以内部分计算的利息，均在保单限额之外另付且通常无金额上限。Costs of defence, reasonable expenses incurred at the insurer's request, court costs taxed against the insured, and interest accruing from the date of judgment calculated on the portion of the judgment within the policy limit are paid over and above the policy limit, typically without a specific monetary cap.

第 1 题Q1承保协议与赔偿性质Insuring agreement and nature of damages★陷阱：惩罚性赔偿与超限额本金混入保单

Which statement accurately describes the coverage provided under the insuring agreement of a standard liability insurance policy?

下列哪项陈述准确描述了标准责任保险单承保协议所提供的保障？

  1. ✗ 掉入「惩罚性赔偿与超限额本金混入保单」陷阱。责任保单只赔补偿性赔偿（compensatory damages），法院用于惩罚和威慑的惩罚性或惩戒性赔偿（punitive or exemplary damages）必须由被保险人自己承担。Falls into the 'confusing punitive damages with covered benefits' trap. Liability policies cover only compensatory damages; punitive or exemplary damages awarded by a court to punish and deter wrongful conduct must be borne entirely by the insured.

  2. ✓ 正确。财产损害（property damage）不仅包括有形财产（tangible property）的物理损坏及由此带来的使用损失（loss of use），还包括没有受到物理损坏的有形财产的使用损失。Correct. Property damage includes not only physical damage to tangible property and resulting loss of use, but also the loss of use of tangible property that has not suffered physical damage.

  3. ✗ 掉入「道义责任与私自认责陷阱」。道义上的责任不会触发责任保单，只有被保险人在法律上有责任（legally liable）且过失成立时保单才响应。Falls into the 'moral obligation and voluntary admission' trap. A moral obligation does not trigger a liability policy; the policy responds only when the insured is legally liable and negligence is established.

  4. ✗ 违反「只保第三方」原则。责任险只保障保单上未列名的第三方（third party）遭受的人身伤害或财产损害，被保险人自己受伤或自有财产受损需靠第一方保障。Violates the principle of covering third parties only. Liability insurance covers only bodily injury or property damage suffered by third parties not named in the policy; injury to the insured or damage to the insured's own property requires first-party insurance.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A client asks her broker what types of damages and losses are covered by the insuring agreement of a liability policy. Which explanation is correct?

一位客户向经纪人询问责任保单的承保协议保哪些类型的赔偿和损失。下列哪项解释是正确的？

  1. ✗ 掉入「把使用损失排除在财产损害外」陷阱。财产损害（property damage）明确包含使用损失（loss of use），并非只保修复或更换费用。Falls into the 'excluding loss of use from property damage' trap. Property damage expressly includes loss of use and is not restricted to repair or replacement costs.

  2. ✗ 概念缩水错误。人身伤害（bodily injury）不仅包括身体受到的实际伤害，还包括疾病以及由该伤害或疾病日后导致的死亡。Incorrectly narrows the definition. Bodily injury encompasses not only physical bodily trauma, but also sickness or disease and resulting death.

  3. ✗ 掉入「惩罚性赔偿与超限额本金混入保单」陷阱。惩戒性或惩罚性赔偿（exemplary or punitive damages）不在责任保单保障范围内，由被保险人自付。Falls into the 'confusing punitive damages with covered benefits' trap. Exemplary or punitive damages are outside the coverage of a liability policy and must be paid by the insured.

  4. ✓ 正确。承保协议（insuring agreement）的核心就是支付被保险人因一次事故（occurrence）造成第三方人身伤害或财产损害而在法律上应付的补偿性赔偿（compensatory damages）。Correct. The core of the insuring agreement is to pay compensatory damages that the insured becomes legally obligated to pay to a third party for bodily injury or property damage caused by an occurrence.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

第 2 题Q2法律责任的认定主体Entity determining legal liability★陷阱：道义责任与私自认责陷阱

A delivery cart operated by a warehouse owner rolls down a ramp and injures a passerby. The owner immediately tells the passerby that he is completely at fault. Under a liability policy, who determines whether the owner is legally liable?

一位仓库业主推的送货车滑下斜坡撞伤了一名路人。业主当场对路人表示完全是自己的错。在责任保单下，由谁来认定业主是否在法律上有责任？

  1. ✗ 掉入「道义责任与私自认责陷阱」。被保险人往往缺乏足够的法律知识来判断自己是否负有法律责任，他自己的认定不能决定法律责任。Falls into the 'moral obligation and voluntary admission' trap. The insured generally lacks the legal expertise to determine legal liability, and an admission at the scene does not establish legal liability.

  2. ✗ 混淆调查角色与法定裁判权。独立理算师（independent adjuster）受聘负责调查和谈判，但无权在法律上裁定被保险人是否有责任。Confuses an investigative role with judicial authority. An independent adjuster is retained to investigate and negotiate, but possesses no authority to determine legal liability.

  3. ✓ 正确。真正有权认定被保险人是否在法律上有责任（legally liable）的是法院（a court of law）；不过当证据明显支持原告时，多数案件会通过庭外和解（out-of-court settlement）解决。Correct. A court of law is the authority that ultimately determines whether the insured is legally liable; however, when evidence clearly favours the plaintiff, most cases are resolved through an out-of-court settlement.

  4. ✗ 掉入「道义责任与私自认责陷阱」。无论是原告律师还是保险公司律师，都不能决定法律责任是否成立，只有法院说了算。Falls into the 'moral obligation and voluntary admission' trap. Neither the plaintiff's counsel nor the insurer's counsel can determine legal liability; only a court of law has that authority.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

After a visitor slips on wet tiles in a bakery, the bakery owner feels guilty and asks her broker to make sure the liability insurer pays the visitor right away. What should the broker explain about how liability coverage works?

一名访客在面包店湿滑的地砖上滑倒后，店主感到内疚，要求经纪人确保责任保险公司立即赔钱给访客。关于责任险如何运作，经纪人应当怎样解释？

  1. ✓ 正确。被保险人觉得有道义责任不触发保单，责任险只在被保险人法律上有责任（legally liable）时才响应，而法律责任由法院认定。Correct. An insured's sense of moral obligation does not trigger coverage; a liability policy responds only when the insured is legally liable, which is determined by a court of law.

  2. ✗ 把「由法院认定」误解为「每案必开庭」。虽然法律责任由法院认定，但多数情况下证据明显支持原告时，保险人会同意庭外和解（out-of-court settlement）。Misinterprets judicial determination as requiring a full trial for every claim. Although courts determine liability, insurers routinely settle out of court when the evidence clearly supports the claimant.

  3. ✗ 掉入「道义责任与私自认责陷阱」。经纪人和核保人都无权对被保险人在法律上是否有责任下结论。Falls into the 'moral obligation and voluntary admission' trap. Neither the insurance broker nor the underwriter has legal authority to determine whether negligence has been established.

  4. ✗ 掉入「道义责任与私自认责陷阱」。仅有道义责任绝不触发责任保单，过失和法律责任必须成立。Falls into the 'moral obligation and voluntary admission' trap. A moral obligation never triggers a liability policy; negligence and legal liability must be established.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

第 3 题Q3事故发生制与occurrence定义Occurrence basis and occurrence definition★★陷阱：每次事故限额当成全年总额

From October to March, exhaust fumes from a workshop's newly installed vent continuously drift onto a neighbouring greenhouse, gradually ruining its exterior panels. How does an occurrence-basis liability policy treat this situation?

从十月到次年三月，一家工坊新装排风口排出的废气持续飘向隔壁温室，逐渐毁坏了温室的外墙板。按事故发生制承保的责任保单如何处理这种情况？
