第 14 章 · 车险可选保障与保障条件Chapter 14 · Optional Auto Coverages and Coverage ConditionsOptional Coverage & Conditions

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 套餐组成与子项边界题：直接考四件套与七件套附加包各含哪几项、哪些客户或费用被除外（如非居民不享租车与出行被盗、目的地协助 12 小时内且不含租车、路边应急每期最多 2 次且不含油电胎零件）。Package composition and sub-coverage boundary questions: Directly testing which sub-coverages are included in the four-item package and the seven-item roadside package, and which customers or expenses are excluded (such as non-residents being ineligible for rental-vehicle and vehicle travel protection, destination assistance being limited to 12 hours and excluding rental cars, and roadside emergency assistance being limited to twice per policy term and excluding gas, batteries, tires, and replacement parts).
  • 重叠与前置条件判断题：给一个生活场景问“需不需要另买某险”或“买某险必须先有什么险”（如已有碰撞险就不必另买 Hit and Run；买七件套须有综合险；买新车保障须同时有碰撞险和综合险，且房车不能买）。Coverage overlap and prerequisite condition questions: Presenting a real-life scenario to ask whether an additional coverage must be purchased or which prerequisite coverage is required (such as not needing to purchase Hit and Run if Collision is already carried, requiring Comprehensive to purchase the seven-item roadside package, and requiring both Collision and Comprehensive to purchase new vehicle coverage, with motorhomes being ineligible).
  • 未列名驾驶人（UDP）除外情形判别题：给一位亲友或员工开投保人的车出了有责事故，考是否属于 UDP 的四种除外（同住家人或雇员、12 个月内开超 12 天、无照、2019-09-01 起 5 年内在投保人车上出过事），从而判断车主是否要付 UDAP。Unlisted Driver Protection (UDP) exclusion identification questions: Describing an at-fault crash caused by a relative, friend, or employee driving the insured vehicle, testing whether the driver falls under the four UDP exclusions (household family members or employees, driving more than 12 days in 12 months, unlicensed drivers, or having caused an at-fault crash in the insured vehicle within five years since September 1, 2019) to determine if the owner must pay the Unlisted Driver Accident Premium (UDAP).
  • 自动与可选使用损失（Loss of Use）对比题：考触发原因、起算时间与理赔前提的差异——特别是“只偷零件不触发自动使用损失”“整车被盗报警后 72 小时起算”以及“可选使用损失事故后立即起算但须有自车险理赔”。Automatic versus Optional Loss of Use comparison questions: Testing differences in triggering perils, commencement times, and claim prerequisites—specifically that theft of parts does not trigger Automatic Loss of Use, that theft of the entire vehicle begins 72 hours after reporting to police, and that Optional Loss of Use begins immediately after the incident but requires an own-damage claim.
  • 违反保障条件（Breach of Conditions）后果与判据题：给无有效驾照、酒驾、超期外省行驶或比赛等场景，考三步标准后果（照赔受害第三方、拒赔违约者自车、向违约者追偿），或区分考试清单口径（酒测超过 0.05）与情景题口径（醉到无法正常控制车辆、无需被起诉）。Breach of coverage conditions consequence and criterion questions: Presenting scenarios such as driving without a valid licence, impaired driving, unauthorized out-of-province operation, or racing, testing the standard three-step consequence (paying innocent third parties, denying the breaching driver's own-damage claim, and seeking recovery from the breaching driver), or distinguishing the exam list criterion (blood alcohol level over 0.05) from the scenario criterion (intoxicated to the point of being incapable of proper vehicle control, without requiring criminal charges).

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 同住亲友当成偶尔借车Treating co-residing relatives as occasional borrowers

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到“一年只开几天”就直接套用 UDP 的 12 天免费额度，忽略了家庭成员（同住家人）和雇员本来就在 UDP 的四种除外名单里。Seeing that someone drove only a few days a year leads candidates to apply UDP's 12-day allowance, overlooking that household family members and employees are already on UDP's list of four exclusions.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 household member、family member、employee，或提示借车人与车主同住、受雇于车主。The question stem mentions household member, family member, employee, or indicates the driver lives with or is employed by the vehicle owner.

⚠ 偷零件误当整车被盗Mistaking theft of vehicle parts for theft of the entire vehicle

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到“买全了综合险、车因被盗没法开”就以为自动使用损失（Automatic Loss of Use）会赔租车费，忘了它只保整车被盗，单偷轮胎或发动机不赔。Seeing that the vehicle has full Comprehensive coverage and cannot be driven due to theft leads candidates to assume Automatic Loss of Use pays rental costs, forgetting that it covers only theft of the entire vehicle and pays nothing if only tires, wheels, or the engine are stolen.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写 wheels、tires、engine、catalytic converter 等部件被偷，而车辆本身还在原地。The question stem mentions stolen parts such as wheels, tires, engine, or catalytic converter while the vehicle itself remains behind.

⚠ 混淆套餐内含与除外Confusing package inclusions and exclusions

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把七件套新增的三项（目的地协助、盗窃免赔豁免、路边应急）塞进四件套，或者以为目的地协助可以报销租替代车、路边应急可以报销新电瓶和汽油。Candidates often place the three items added by the seven-item package (destination assistance, theft deductible waiver, and roadside emergency) into the four-item package, or mistakenly believe destination assistance reimburses substitute vehicle rentals and roadside emergency covers new batteries or fuel.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问四件套含不含某项，或问 Destination Assistance / roadside emergency 能报销哪笔具体费用。The question stem asks whether the four-item package covers a specific item, or asks which specific expenses Destination Assistance or roadside emergency will reimburse.

⚠ 新车三险的年款与第3年火盗陷阱Model year and third-year fire and theft trap in new vehicle coverages

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把三种新车保障的适用年款（1–2 年 vs 1–3 年）和全损门槛（50% vs 可高至 90%）记串，或漏掉限折旧险（Limited-Depreciation）在第 3 个年款生效时不保火、盗、破坏、恶意毁损全损的特殊除外。Candidates confuse the applicable model years (1–2 years vs. 1–3 years) and total-loss thresholds (50% vs. up to 90%) across the three new vehicle coverages, or overlook the special exclusion under Limited-Depreciation Coverage denying total-loss coverage for fire, theft, vandalism, or malicious mischief if effective in the third model year.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干标明车辆是第几个 model year、修理费占市值百分比，或提到在第 3 个年款当天买限折旧险后发生火灾/被盗全损。The question stem specifies the vehicle model year, repair cost as a percentage of market value, or mentions purchasing Limited-Depreciation on the start of the third model year followed by a total loss from fire or theft.

⚠ 违约后果颠倒或酒驾等起诉Reversing breach consequences or expecting criminal charges for impaired driving

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为驾驶人违反保障条件（如驾照过期、酒驾）后保险公司对受害第三方也一概拒赔，或者以为警察没起诉酒驾就不能拒赔自车损失。Candidates mistakenly assume that an insurer denies third-party claims when a driver breaches coverage conditions (such as an expired licence or impaired driving), or believe that own-damage claims cannot be denied unless police lay criminal charges for impaired driving.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 expired licence、alcohol、not charged、breach of condition，并问对第三方和车主自身分别怎么处理。The question stem mentions expired licence, alcohol, not charged, or breach of condition, and asks how third-party and own-damage claims are handled respectively.

第 1 题Q1UDP四种除外情形Four exclusions under Unlisted Driver Protection★★陷阱：同住亲友当成偶尔借车

A car owner in Kelowna has a new basic auto policy with automatic Unlisted Driver Protection (UDP). Her adult brother, who lives in the same house, borrows her sedan for only the second time this year and causes an at-fault collision. How does UDP respond to this accident?

基隆拿一位车主有一份自动附带未列名驾驶人保障（UDP）的新基本车险保单。与她同住一栋房子的成年弟弟今年第二次借开她的轿车，并造成了一起有责碰撞事故。UDP 对这起事故如何响应？

  1. ✗ 掉入“同住亲友当成偶尔借车”陷阱：虽然一年最多 12 天是未列名驾驶人保障（Unlisted Driver Protection, UDP）的一般限额，但同住的家庭成员本来就在四种除外之内，一天也不适用。Incorrect. Falls into the 'treating co-residing relatives as occasional borrowers' trap: Although up to 12 days per year is the general limit for Unlisted Driver Protection (UDP), household family members are already among the four exclusions and do not qualify for even a single day.

  2. ✓ 正确：主驾、登记车主或承租人的家庭成员（household/family members）或雇员属于未列名驾驶人保障（Unlisted Driver Protection, UDP）明确除外的四种人之一；他们未列名而在投保人车上造成有责事故，车主须付未列名驾驶人事故保费（UDAP）。Correct. Household or family members and employees of the principal operator, registered owner, or lessee are among the four categories explicitly excluded from Unlisted Driver Protection (UDP); if they cause an at-fault crash in the insured vehicle while unlisted, the owner must pay the Unlisted Driver Accident Premium (UDAP).

  3. ✗ 把“符合 UDP 条件的未列名者出事后续保要付费”误套在同住家人身上：同住家人从一开始就不受 UDP 保护，第一次出事就要算 UDAP。Incorrect. Misapplies the rule where eligible unlisted drivers require a fee upon renewal after an accident to household members: Household members are not protected by UDP from the outset, so the UDAP applies to the very first crash.

  4. ✗ 以偏概全：有有效驾照且自 2019-09-01 起 5 年内未在投保人车上出过事故只是排除另外两项除外，并不能绕过“家庭成员除外”这一条。Incorrect. Generalizes from a single condition: Holding a valid driver's licence and having no at-fault crashes in the insured vehicle within five years since September 1, 2019 only satisfies the other two exclusion criteria, but cannot override the household member exclusion.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A bakery owner in Victoria lends her personal SUV to one of her full-time bakery employees for a single afternoon errand. The employee is not listed on the policy and causes an at-fault crash. Why will Unlisted Driver Protection (UDP) NOT protect the owner from a penalty?

维多利亚一位面包店老板把自己的私人 SUV 借给店里一名全职雇员跑一下午腿。该雇员未在保单上列名，并造成了一起有责车祸。为什么未列名驾驶人保障（UDP）不能帮车主免受罚金？

  1. ✗ 编造规则：未列名驾驶人保障（Unlisted Driver Protection, UDP）并没有“工作日白天不保”的规定，拒保原因在于驾驶人是车主的雇员。Incorrect. Fabricates a rule: Unlisted Driver Protection (UDP) has no restriction excluding daytime weekday driving; protection is denied because the driver is the owner's employee.

  2. ✗ 与规则相反：新的基本车险保单会自动免费附带 UDP，不需要事先加费购买。Incorrect. Contradicts the rule: New basic auto policies automatically include UDP at no additional premium, without requiring an extra purchase in advance.

  3. ✗ 把上限当成下限：12 天是一年最多可开的天数上限（超过 12 天即除外），而不是最低天数要求。Incorrect. Confuses an upper limit with a minimum requirement: 12 days per year is the maximum allowable driving limit (driving more than 12 days results in exclusion), not a minimum threshold.

  4. ✓ 正确：登记车主、承租人或主驾的家庭成员与雇员（employees）均在 UDP 的四种除外之列，即使一年只开一天也不受 UDP 保护。Correct. Household family members and employees of the registered owner, lessee, or principal operator are among the four exclusions under UDP, receiving no protection under UDP even if driving only one day a year.

答案Answer：d

第 2 题Q2自动与可选使用损失区别Distinguishing Automatic and Optional Loss of Use★★陷阱：偷零件误当整车被盗

A commuter in Burnaby carries Comprehensive coverage on his hatchback but has not purchased Optional Loss of Use. 阿伯茨福德一位送货司机的驾照两个月前过期未续。在送货途中，他闯了停牌，撞伤一名骑车人并撞坏了他自己的货车和路边一辆停着的车。按考试口径关于违反保障条件的规则，结果是什么？

  1. ✗ 掉入“违约后果颠倒或酒驾等起诉”陷阱：违反保障条件（breach of coverage conditions）时，保险公司仍然会赔付受害第三方，不会让无辜第三方落空。Incorrect. Falls into the 'reversing breach consequences or expecting criminal charges for impaired driving' trap: Upon a breach of coverage conditions, the insurer still pays innocent third parties and will not abandon third-party victims.

  2. ✗ 编造双倍免赔规则：违反保障条件的结果是拒赔违约者自己的车损，而不是加倍免赔额。Incorrect. Fabricates a doubled deductible rule: The consequence of a breach of coverage conditions is denial of the breaching driver's own-damage claim, not a doubled deductible.

  3. ✗ 违背考试口径：没有有效驾照（包括驾照过期未续）在考试口径中明确属于违反保障条件（注：尽管法条字面读法与 2026 年驾照限制条款新规有细节差异，本讲考试口径一律按“没有有效驾照即违反条件”作答）。Incorrect. Contradicts the exam standard: Operating without a valid driver's licence (including an expired licence) is explicitly tested as a breach of coverage conditions on the exam (Note: Regardless of subtle statutory distinctions or 2026 licence restriction amendments, candidates must answer that driving without a valid licence constitutes a breach).

  4. ✓ 正确：按考试口径，没有有效驾照属于违反保障条件（breach of coverage conditions）（注：法条细节与考试口径的对照以考试口径“无有效驾照＝违反”为准）；违反条件的标准三步后果是：照赔受害第三方、不赔违约者自己的车、之后向违约者追偿付给第三方的钱。Correct. Under the exam standard, driving without a valid driver's licence is a breach of coverage conditions (Note: Tested strictly as no valid licence equals breach); the standard three-step consequence of a breach is paying innocent third parties, denying the breaching driver's own vehicle claim, and seeking reimbursement from the driver for payments made to third parties.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

After drinking heavily at a party, a motorist loses control on a curve and rolls her sports car into a ditch, damaging only her own vehicle. Attending police officers note that she is intoxicated to the point of being incapable of proper control of the vehicle, but they do not lay charges. How does the insurer handle her own-damage claim?

一位驾驶人在聚会上大量饮酒后，在弯道失控将跑车翻进沟里，只撞坏了她自己的车。到场警察记录她已醉到无法正常控制车辆的程度，但没有提起起诉。保险公司如何处理她的自车损失理赔？

  1. ✗ 掉入“违约后果颠倒或酒驾等起诉”陷阱：只要驾驶人醉到无法正常控制车辆，即使警察没有起诉（not charged），保险公司也照样拒赔她的车。Incorrect. Falls into the 'reversing breach consequences or expecting criminal charges for impaired driving' trap: As long as the driver is intoxicated to the point of being incapable of proper vehicle control, the insurer denies her own-damage claim even if police lay no criminal charges.

  2. ✓ 正确：酒驾导致醉到无法正常控制车辆（incapable of proper control of the vehicle）即构成违反保障条件（注：考试口径的清单题写酒测超 0.05，而现行 BC 法规 s.55(8)(a) 与考试案例题均用“无法正常控制车辆”判据），不需要被起诉或定罪即可拒赔自车损失。Correct. Impaired driving resulting in being incapable of proper control of the vehicle constitutes a breach of coverage conditions (Note: Exam list questions reference a blood alcohol level over 0.05, whereas current BC Regulation s.55(8)(a) and exam scenario questions use the incapable of proper control standard), allowing the insurer to deny own-damage claims without requiring criminal charges or a conviction.

  3. ✗ 编造折半赔付规则：违反保障条件时，违约者自己的车损是一律拒赔，不会因为没撞到别人就赔一半。Incorrect. Fabricates a 50% payout rule: Upon a breach of coverage conditions, the breaching driver's own-damage claim is denied entirely, and no partial payment is made simply because third-party property was unharmed.

  4. ✗ 把两项独立的违反条件绑在一起：参加比赛或测速、酒后驾驶各自单独构成违反条件，不需要同时发生。Incorrect. Combines two independent breaches: Participating in a race or speed test and impaired driving each independently constitute a breach of coverage conditions without needing to occur concurrently.
