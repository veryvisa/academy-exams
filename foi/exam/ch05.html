① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 赔款计算综合题：给出重置成本（replacement cost）、折旧（depreciation）、被保险人权益比例、保单保额及免赔额（deductible），要求先按实际现金价值（actual cash value, ACV ＝ 重置成本 − 折旧）与权益、保额“三者取最小”，再扣除免赔额算出最终赔付。Comprehensive loss settlement calculations: given replacement cost, depreciation, the insured's insurable interest, policy limit, and deductible, testing whether you can determine actual cash value (ACV = replacement cost minus depreciation), apply the least-of-three-amounts rule (ACV, insurable interest, policy limit), and subtract the deductible to calculate the final claim payout.
  • 按揭权人（mortgagee）叠加理赔题：把“三者取最小”与标准按揭条款（Standard Mortgage Clause）串在一道题里，先确定保单应付总赔款，再把按揭余额优先赔给按揭权人，最后问被保险人本人（insured）能拿到多少，专门抓跳步或看错受款人的考生。Mortgagee loss settlement priority scenarios: combining the least-of-three-amounts rule with the Standard Mortgage Clause in a single question, first determining total policy payable loss, paying the outstanding mortgage balance to the mortgagee first, and asking how much the insured personally receives, targeting candidates who skip steps or mistake the payee.
  • 条件（condition）与保证（warranty）辨析题：通过正误判断或场景案例，考察违反保证“不论与损失有无因果关系均可拒赔”的考试口径，以及法定条件（statutory conditions）与保单条件（policy conditions）的来源与适用保单。Distinguishing conditions from warranties: using true/false or scenario-based questions to test the official exam standard that breaching a warranty allows claim denial regardless of whether the breach had any connection to the loss, as well as the origins and policy applications of statutory conditions versus policy conditions.
  • 除外财产与除外风险找例外题：用“下列哪项不属于除外财产/除外风险”反向设问，常把连续空置超过 30 天（vacant property）篡改为 15 天，或把火险法定必保的雷电（lightning）混入除外风险列表。Identifying exceptions to excluded property and excluded perils: using reverse question stems asking which item is NOT excluded, frequently altering the threshold for vacant property from more than 30 consecutive days to 15 days, or inserting statutorily covered lightning into the excluded perils list.
  • 直接损失（direct loss）、重置成本三条件与成对成套（pair and set）场景题：给定火灾施救损坏、停电致存货变质、异地重建、未实际修复或成套藏品单件遗失等具体情境，测试考生能否按保单规则判断赔与不赔、按 ACV 还是按重置成本赔。Direct loss, replacement cost conditions, and pair and set scenarios: given situations involving firefighting damage, power interruption leading to spoiled stock, rebuilding on another site, failure to repair, or loss of one item from a set, testing whether candidates can apply policy rules to determine coverage and whether settlement is on an ACV or replacement cost basis.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 三者取最小漏算或错选受款人Omitting the Least-of-Three Rule or Selecting the Wrong Payee

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生算出实际现金价值（ACV）后忘记与保额、被保险人权益比大小，或者在有按揭贷款时直接答总赔款而忘了先扣除按揭权人的份额。Candidates calculate actual cash value (ACV) but forget to compare it against the policy limit and insurable interest, or when a mortgage exists, answer with the total claim payout without first deducting the mortgagee's share.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现多个金额（重置成本、折旧、保额、按揭余额）并问“how much is the insured entitled to receive”时。Questions presenting multiple dollar figures (replacement cost, depreciation, policy limit, mortgage balance) and asking how much the insured is entitled to receive.

⚠ 把保证当条件看因果关系Treating Warranties as Conditions Regarding Causation

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 受第 3 讲重大变更、不实陈述需“与损失直接相关”影响，误以为违反保证（warranty）也只有在直接引起损失时才会拒赔。Influenced by material change and misrepresentation requiring a direct connection to loss, candidates mistakenly assume that breaching a warranty only permits claim denial when the breach directly caused the loss.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 warranty、strict compliance 或描述被保险人违规行为与起火原因毫无关系时。Question stems mentioning warranty, strict compliance, or describing an insured's non-compliance that has no connection to the cause of fire.

⚠ 重置成本三条件缺一仍按新价算Applying Replacement Cost When Missing One of the Three Conditions

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到保单附加了重置成本（replacement cost）保障，就忽略了“尽职迅速、同址或邻址、实际完成重置后才付差额”三个硬门槛，或把存货（stock）也算进 IBC 4005 批单。Seeing that replacement cost coverage is attached, candidates overlook the three strict requirements (due diligence and dispatch, same or adjacent site, and difference paid only after actual repair or replacement), or erroneously apply the IBC 4005 endorsement to commercial stock.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提到搬迁到其他城市重建、决定拿钱不修、已买新机但旧机未扔，或标的是商业存货时。Question stems mentioning rebuilding in another city, taking a cash settlement instead of repairing, keeping old equipment after buying new, or covering commercial stock.

⚠ 空置天数缩水与雷电误列除外Truncated Vacancy Days and Misclassifying Lightning as Excluded

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 死记“空置不保”却没盯住“连续超过 30 天”的数字，被 15 天骗过；或看到雷击损坏电器除外，就误以为整个雷电（lightning）都是除外风险。Candidates memorize that vacant property is excluded but overlook the required more than 30 consecutive days threshold, falling for 15 days, or notice that lightning damage to electrical devices is excluded and assume lightning as a whole is an excluded peril.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问 EXCEPT / NOT excluded，选项中出现 vacant for 15 consecutive days 或 lightning 时。Question stems asking EXCEPT or NOT excluded, where options list vacant for 15 consecutive days or lightning.

⚠ 灭火破坏当间接、断电坏货当直接Mistaking Firefighting Damage for Indirect and Power Loss Spoilage for Direct

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 凭直觉认为没被火烧到的水渍、斧劈天窗不算火损，却把外线起火断电后冷库货品腐烂或火灾停业期间的利润与租金损失当成直接损失（direct loss）。Candidates intuitively assume water damage and axe holes not touched by flames are not fire losses, while treating freezer spoilage from off-premises power loss or lost business profit and rent during closure as direct loss.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干区分消防施救造成的损坏（水、烟、高温、斧劈）与后续营业收入、租金或断电导致的存货损失时。Questions requiring candidates to distinguish firefighting damage (water, smoke, heat, axe cuts) from subsequent loss of income, rent, or freezer inventory spoilage caused by power disruption.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
列明风险与一切险Named Perils versus All RisksNamed perils vs. All risks财产险没有统一标准保单（多基于 IBC 示范表单修改）；列明风险保单只保列出的风险，一切险承保一切直接实质性损毁但仍受保单明文除外的限制。There is no standardized property insurance policy, as most forms are adapted from IBC advisory models; named perils policies cover only specified perils, whereas all risks policies cover all direct physical damage subject to explicit policy exclusions.
承保财产与地点限制Property and Location InsuredProperty and location insured保障只限保单列明的地点；房主表自动承保住宅建筑（dwelling）、独立附属建筑（detached private structures）和个人财物（personal property）三项，商业险则只有写了保额的项目（如建筑、存货、设备）才有保障。Coverage is strictly restricted to locations specified in the policy; homeowners forms automatically cover dwelling buildings, detached private structures, and personal property, whereas commercial policies cover only items with a specific limit of insurance shown, such as building, stock, or equipment.
直接损失与间接损失Direct Loss versus Indirect LossDirect loss vs. Indirect loss财产险只保直接损失（含灭火水渍、消防斧劈屋顶、烟熏和高温损坏）；租金损失、利润损失或因外线起火断电导致的冷库货物变质属于间接损失，需另加营业中断（Business Interruption）或额外费用（Extra Expense）表单。Property insurance covers direct loss only, including firefighting water damage, roof axe cuts, smoke, and heat damage; loss of rent, loss of profit, or refrigerated stock spoilage from off-premises power disruption are indirect losses requiring Business Interruption or Extra Expense endorsements.
条件与保证的区别Distinction Between Conditions and WarrantiesConditions and Warranties条件（法定条件与保单条件）要求做或不做某事；保证（warranty）担保某事实如此并持续如此，要求严格遵守，违反保证时无论该违反与损失原因有无关系，保险公司均可拒赔。Conditions, both statutory and policy conditions, require the insured to do or refrain from doing something; warranties certify that certain facts are and will continue to be true and require strict compliance, such that breaching a warranty allows the insurer to deny a claim regardless of whether the breach had any connection to the loss.
九项除外财产与九项除外风险Nine Excluded Properties and Nine Excluded PerilsProperty excluded and Perils excluded通常除外的九项财产含债权凭证、证券、汽车、船、飞机、现金、其他机动车、账簿及连续空置超过 30 天的财产；共同除外风险含地震、洪水、农工业烟、磨损变质、锈蚀、战争、核事故、建筑法规增加费用及下水道倒灌（地震和洪水易加买）。The nine standard excluded properties include evidences of debt or title, securities, automobiles, watercraft, aircraft, money, other motorized vehicles, books of account, and vacant property vacant for more than 30 consecutive days; common excluded perils include earthquake, flood, agricultural or industrial smoke, wear and tear, rust, war, nuclear incident, increased cost of compliance with building by-laws, and sewer back-up (earthquake and flood are readily buyable).
免赔额与部分自留Deductibles and Partial Risk RetentionDeductible and Partial retention免赔额是每次损失由被保险人先自留承担的金额（partial retention），免赔额越高保费越低；个人险常见区间为 $200–$1,000 且全单共用一个免赔额，商业险以最低 $500 为标准。A deductible is the amount of each loss retained by the insured representing partial retention, where a higher deductible yields a lower premium; personal lines policies typically range from $200 to $1,000 with a single deductible applying to the entire policy, while commercial policies establish a $500 minimum standard.
成对成套与部件规则Pair and Set and Parts RulesPair and set / Parts成对成套财物遗失或损坏其中一件时，赔款须扣除剩余完好物件的价值；由多个部件组成的财产仅局部损坏时，只赔受损部件的价值加安装费，不赔整件。When one item of a matched pair or set is lost or damaged, the claim settlement must deduct the value retained by the remaining item; when property consisting of several parts suffers partial damage, payment covers only the value of the damaged part plus installation costs, not the entire item.
三者取最小与实际现金价值Least-of-Three Amounts and Actual Cash ValueActual Cash Value (ACV) and Indemnity Agreement赔款不超过出险时实际现金价值（ACV ＝ 重置成本 − 折旧，折旧看状况、过时、转售价值、正常寿命四因素）、被保险人权益、声明页保额三者中的最小数，再扣免赔额。Claim payment does not exceed the least of actual cash value at the time of loss, where ACV equals replacement cost minus depreciation based on condition, obsolescence, resale value, and normal life expectancy, the insured's insurable interest, and the policy limit on the declarations page, minus the deductible.
建筑重置成本三条件与定值保单Three Replacement Cost Conditions and Valued PoliciesReplacement Cost Basis and Valued Policy建筑按重置成本（同类同质新财产、不扣折旧）赔付须同时满足尽职迅速重置、同址或邻址重建、实际完成重置后才付差额三项条件（缺一按 ACV 赔，IBC 4005 不保商业存货）；无法重置的古董艺术品等可用定值保单按评估师约定的价值赔付。Building settlement on a replacement cost basis, providing new property of like kind and quality without deduction for depreciation, requires fulfilling three conditions: replacement with due diligence and dispatch, repair or reconstruction on the same or an adjacent site, and payment of the difference only after work is actually completed; failing any condition restricts settlement to ACV, and IBC 4005 excludes commercial stock; irreplaceable antiques and fine arts may be insured under a valued policy paying the value agreed with an appraiser.
标准按揭条款两项保证Two Protections of the Standard Mortgage ClauseStandard Mortgage Clause被保险人须负责将保单核证副本（certified copy）交予按揭权人；标准按揭条款保证被保险人违反条件或做不实陈述时按揭权人仍照常受偿，且保险公司不给同样法定通知不得终止保单或作不利改动。The insured is responsible for delivering a certified copy of the policy to the mortgagee; the Standard Mortgage Clause guarantees that the mortgagee's protection remains valid even if the insured breaches policy conditions or makes a misrepresentation, and the insurer cannot cancel or amend the policy to the mortgagee's prejudice without providing the same statutory notice.

第 1 题Q1三者取最小与ACV计算Least-of-Three Rule and ACV Calculation★★陷阱：三者取最小漏算或错选受款人

Dr. Lin is the sole owner of a dental clinic whose specialized sterilizing autoclave is destroyed by a covered peril. On the policy declarations page, dental equipment is insured for a limit of $5,000 with a $500 deductible. Supplier quotes supplied by Dr. Lin show that a new autoclave of like kind and quality costs $9,000, and the adjuster calculates $3,000 in depreciation based on its normal life expectancy and condition. Under an actual cash value (ACV) settlement basis, how much will the insurer pay?

林医生独资拥有一家牙科诊所，诊所内一台专用高压灭菌器因承保风险被毁。在保单声明页上，牙科设备的保额为 $5,000，免赔额为 $500。林医生提供的供应商报价显示同类同质的新灭菌器价格为 $9,000，理赔人根据其正常寿命和状况核定折旧为 $3,000。在实际现金价值（ACV）赔付基础下，保险公司将赔付多少？

  1. ✗ 错在只算出了实际现金价值（actual cash value, ACV ＝ 重置成本 $9,000 − 折旧 $3,000 ＝ $6,000），掉进了“三者取最小漏算”陷阱，既未受 $5,000 保额上限约束，也未扣除 $500 免赔额（deductible）。Incorrect. Only calculates actual cash value (ACV = replacement cost $9,000 minus depreciation $3,000 = $6,000), falling into the trap of omitting the least-of-three rule; it is neither capped by the $5,000 policy limit nor reduced by the $500 deductible.

  2. ✓ 正确。赔偿协议（Indemnity Agreement）实行“三者取最小”：第一项实际现金价值（ACV ＝ 重置成本 $9,000 − 折旧 $3,000 ＝ $6,000）；第二项被保险人权益（独资 100% 即 $6,000）；第三项声明页保额（$5,000）。三者中最小的是 $5,000，再扣除 $500 免赔额，最终赔付 $4,500。Correct. The Indemnity Agreement applies the least-of-three-amounts rule: first, actual cash value (ACV = replacement cost $9,000 minus depreciation $3,000 = $6,000); second, the insured's interest (100% sole ownership, or $6,000); third, the limit on the declarations page ($5,000). The least of these three is $5,000, and deducting the $500 deductible yields a final claim payout of $4,500.

  3. ✗ 错在虽然按“三者取最小”找到了保额上限 $5,000，但忘记扣除每次损失由被保险人自留承担的 $500 免赔额。Incorrect. Although correctly identifying the $5,000 policy limit under the least-of-three rule, it forgets to deduct the $500 deductible retained by the insured on each loss.

  4. ✗ 错在直接拿重置成本（replacement cost）$9,000 作为赔付额，既未扣折旧算 ACV，也忽略了保额上限和免赔额。Incorrect. Directly uses the replacement cost of $9,000 as the payout amount, failing to deduct depreciation to determine ACV while also ignoring both the policy limit and the deductible.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Marcus owns a custom glassblowing furnace that is destroyed when the studio roof collapses under snow load. When the adjuster determines the actual cash value (ACV) of the furnace under the policy's indemnity agreement, which rule applies to the valuation?

马库斯拥有一座定制玻璃吹制窑炉，在工作室屋顶被积雪压塌时全毁。当理赔人根据保单赔偿协议核定该窑炉的实际现金价值（ACV）时，适用下列哪项定损规则？

  1. ✗ 错在把未来投资价值和感情价值算入重置成本与赔款；保险遵循完全补偿（complete indemnity）原则，不赔错过的未来收益或感情价值。Incorrect. Erroneously includes future investment value and sentimental value in replacement cost and loss settlement; insurance adheres to the principle of complete indemnity and does not pay for missed future earnings or sentimental value.

  2. ✗ 错在颠倒了举证责任；通过供应商报价证明重置成本（replacement cost）的举证责任在被保险人，且折旧是从重置成本而非原买价中扣除。Incorrect. Reverses the burden of proof; the burden of proving replacement cost through supplier quotes rests with the insured, and depreciation is deducted from replacement cost rather than original purchase price.

  3. ✗ 错在无视“三者取最小”原则（ACV、被保险人权益、保额三者取最小），并非不论被保险人实际财务利益一律按声明页保额赔付。Incorrect. Ignores the least-of-three-amounts rule (least of ACV, insured's interest, and policy limit); claims are not settled for the full declarations page limit regardless of the insured's actual financial interest.

  4. ✓ 正确。计算实际现金价值（ACV ＝ 重置成本 − 折旧）时，重置成本的举证责任在被保险人（需提供供应商或承包商报价），理赔人再综合状况（condition）、过时（obsolescence）、转售价值（resale value）和正常寿命（normal life expectancy）四个因素核定折旧。Correct. In calculating actual cash value (ACV = replacement cost minus depreciation), the burden of proving replacement cost rests with the insured (by providing supplier or contractor quotes), and the adjuster evaluates depreciation across four factors: condition, obsolescence, resale value, and normal life expectancy.

答案Answer：d

第 2 题Q2建筑重置成本三条件Three Building Replacement Cost Conditions★★陷阱：重置成本三条件缺一仍按新价算

Clara's bicycle repair shop building is damaged by a covered peril. Her commercial property policy includes an IBC 4005 replacement cost endorsement. It would cost $10,000 to rebuild the damaged structure with new materials of like kind and quality, whereas accumulated depreciation is $4,000. In which of the following situations will the insurer settle Clara's loss for the full $10,000 replacement cost (less the deductible) rather than the $6,000 actual cash value (ACV)?

克拉拉的自行车修理店建筑因承保风险受损，其商业财产险保单附有 IBC 4005 重置成本批单。用同类同质新材料重建受损结构需花费 $10,000，累计折旧为 $4,000。在下列哪种情形下，保险公司会按全额 $10,000 重置成本（扣减免赔额后）而非 $6,000 实际现金价值（ACV）向克拉拉理赔？

  1. ✗ 错在掉进“重置成本三条件缺一仍按新价算”陷阱。不实际完成重置就违反了第三项条件，只能拿实际现金价值（ACV ＝ $10,000 − $4,000 ＝ $6,000）。Incorrect. Falls into the trap of applying replacement cost when missing one of the three conditions. Failing to actually repair or rebuild breaches the third condition, restricting settlement to actual cash value (ACV = $10,000 minus $4,000 = $6,000).

  1. ✓ 正确。根据成对成套（pair and set）规则，一对或一套财物中丢了一件不等于整对全损，留存的那件仍有价值，必须从赔款中扣掉其价值，以符合完全补偿（complete indemnity）原则。Correct. Under the pair and set rule, losing one item of a pair or set does not constitute a total loss of the pair; the remaining item retains value, which must be deducted from the claim settlement in accordance with the principle of complete indemnity.

  2. ✗ 错在按被保险人“剩一件没用”的主观想法全额赔付，违反了成对成套（pair and set）须扣除剩余单件价值的规则。Incorrect. Settles in full based on the insured's subjective belief that a single item lacks utility, violating the pair and set rule requiring deduction of the value retained by the remaining item.

  3. ✗ 错在把成对成套索赔自动当作欺诈拒赔；此题考察的是成对成套（pair and set）扣减规则而非拒赔。Incorrect. Automatically treats a pair and set claim as fraud; this question tests the deduction mechanism of the pair and set rule rather than claim denial.

  4. ✗ 错在超额赔偿并计入感情价值，违背了完全补偿（complete indemnity）原则。Incorrect. Pays an excessive amount and factors in sentimental value, violating the principle of complete indemnity.
