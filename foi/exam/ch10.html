① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 情景定性四选一（占本讲考题大半）：给出四个日常生活或出游导致他人受伤、财物受损的小故事，要求按『是否普通居民行为或承保场所→受害人是否第三方→是否触发除外或例外→归入 E/F/G/H 哪一项』四步筛出唯一获赔或拒赔的一项。Scenario-based four-choice questions (making up the majority of exam questions for this chapter): Four scenarios of daily living or travel resulting in third-party bodily injury or property damage are presented, requiring candidates to filter out the single covered or excluded item through a four-step analysis: whether it involves ordinary personal activities or an insured premises; whether the victim is an eligible third party; whether any exclusions or exceptions are triggered; and which coverage among E, F, G, or H applies.
  • 承保场所（premises）真假辨析：围绕五类免列明自动承保地点设题，专门把空置农地、连续租期超过 90 天的度假屋、未在摘要页列明的自有季节性住所混入选项，测试考生能否抓出地点限定词。Premises definition and qualifying location identification: Questions center on the five categories of locations automatically included as premises without being listed on the Coverage Summary, deliberately mixing in excluded locations such as vacant farmland, temporary rentals exceeding 90 continuous days, or unlisted owned seasonal residences to test whether candidates catch qualifying location conditions.
  • 自愿给付（Coverage F / G / H）条件核对：直接考 F（1 年内、不问过错、不含被保险人及同住家属）与 G（12 岁及以下故意损物可赔、不含自有或租客财产）的硬性边界。Voluntary payment conditions check (Coverages F, G, and H): Questions directly test strict boundaries, including Coverage F (expenses incurred within 1 year, no-fault, excluding the insured and household residents) and Coverage G (intentional damage covered only if caused by an insured aged 12 or under, excluding property owned or rented by the insured or tenant).
  • 特殊标的与经营活动例外数字题：考查自有与非自有船艇的功率及长度红线、挂接状态的拖车、以及六项免加批的经营活动例外（未满 21 岁兼职、寄宿者人数、出租车位数）。Numerical limits on special property and business pursuits exceptions: Testing power and length thresholds for owned versus non-owned watercraft, the attachment status of trailers, and the six automatic business pursuits exceptions that require no endorsement (part-time pursuits of insureds under age 21, maximum number of boarders, and maximum rented parking spaces or stalls).
  • 商业责任险种与风险对号入座：给出企业经营事故（如售出商品缺陷、完工交付后管线故障、房东非法驱逐租客）或专业人士失误，要求在 CGL 七大风险、Malpractice、E&O、S.P.F. No. 4 与伞式保单之间准确匹配。Commercial liability coverage matching and hazard classification: Presenting business occurrences (such as defective products sold, plumbing failures after completed operations, or wrongful tenant eviction) or professional mistakes, requiring precise matching among the seven CGL hazards, Malpractice Insurance, Errors and Omissions (E&O), S.P.F. No. 4 (Standard Garage Policy), and Umbrella Policies.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 第一方与第三方混淆Confusing first-party with third-party coverage

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到有人在家里摔伤或被保险人在外旅行受伤就想当然选赔付，忽略了责任险与自愿医疗给付（Coverage F）只保第三方，被保险人本人及同住亲属均在除外之列（住家雇员除外）。Candidates see someone injured at home or an insured injured while traveling and intuitively assume coverage applies, overlooking that liability insurance and Voluntary Medical Payments (Coverage F) cover only third parties, strictly excluding the insured and household family members (with residence employees as the only exception).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干主语或受伤者出现 the insured、the insured's spouse、children residing in the household 或 boarders。The question stem or injury scenario mentions the insured, the insured's spouse, children residing in the household, or boarders.

⚠ 场所自动承保边界Premises automatic coverage boundaries

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 背会了『空地、临时租住处、新购主屋自动算场所』，却漏掉『加拿大境内』『农地除外』『租约不超过连续 90 天』『新主屋最长 30 天』及『自有季节性住所必须列明』的前置条件。Candidates memorize that vacant land, temporary rentals, and newly acquired principal residences automatically qualify as premises, but miss the prerequisites: within Canada, farmland excluded, continuous rental not exceeding 90 days, newly acquired principal residence covered for a maximum of 30 days, and owned seasonal residences must be specifically listed.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 vacant land for grazing/farming、seasonal cottage not listed、rented for more than 90 days 或 vacant lot outside Canada。The question stem mentions vacant land for grazing or farming, seasonal cottage not listed, rented for more than 90 days, or vacant lot outside Canada.

⚠ 照管保管控制四因例外Four-peril exceptions to care, custody or control exclusion

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 知道借用或租用他人财物受损属于照管、保管或控制（care, custody or control）除外，但分不清其例外仅针对『他人场所及室内财物』且仅限『火灾、爆炸、水损、取暖或烹饪设备故障烟熏』四种原因，误以为借来的工具摔坏或壁炉冒烟也能在 E 下获赔。Candidates know that damage to borrowed or rented property falls under the care, custody or control exclusion, but fail to realize that the exceptions apply only to premises and contents owned by others, and are strictly limited to fire, explosion, water damage, or smoke from faulty heating or cooking equipment, mistakenly believing that dropping borrowed tools or smoke from a fireplace is covered under Coverage E.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 borrowed tool/equipment、rented premises/lodge 搭配 fire、explosion、water damage 或 smoke from a fireplace。The question stem mentions borrowed tools or equipment, or rented premises or lodges paired with fire, explosion, water damage, or smoke from a fireplace.

⚠ 自愿给付年龄与对象限制Age and eligibility restrictions on voluntary coverages

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 知道自愿财产损害给付（Coverage G）为未成年人故意损物开了绿灯，但把年龄上限 12 岁记错为其他年龄；或者把自愿医疗给付（Coverage F）1 年的费用期限及住家雇员（residence employee）的例外漏看。Candidates know that Voluntary Payment for Damage to Property (Coverage G) covers intentional property damage by minors, but misremember the age ceiling of 12 years of age or under as another number; or they overlook the 1-year expense window for Coverage F or the special exception for residence employees.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干明确写出肇事儿童的具体年龄（如 10-year-old、11-year-old、over 12）或雇佣关系（residence employee）。The question stem explicitly states the child's exact age (such as 10-year-old, 11-year-old, or over 12) or an employment relationship involving a residence employee.

⚠ 商业与专业责任错位Mismatches between commercial hazards and professional liabilities

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 将施工过程中伤人（经营作业）、完工交付后出事（完工作业）与售出商品致害（产品责任）混为一谈；或按字面把人身权益侵害（personal injury，如诽谤、非法驱逐）当成身体受伤（bodily injury），把医疗过失（Malpractice）与咨询错漏（E&O）混用。Candidates confuse injury occurring during ongoing operations (premises and operations), accidents occurring after handover (completed operations), and damage caused by goods sold (products liability); or they literally mistake personal injury (such as slander, libel, or wrongful eviction) for bodily injury, or conflate healthcare negligence (Malpractice) with advisory mistakes (Errors and Omissions).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现商业场景中的 after the work was completed、defective product sold、wrongful eviction/slander 或专业身份 physician/broker。The question stem presents commercial scenarios with phrases like after the work was completed, defective product sold, wrongful eviction or slander, or professional titles like physician or broker.

第 1 题Q1承保场所的定义与自动承保地点Definition of premises and automatically included locations★★陷阱：场所自动承保边界

Under Section II of a Homeowners Policy, which of the following locations is automatically included within the definition of 'premises' without being specifically listed on the Coverage Summary page?

在房主保单第二部分（Section II）下，下列哪个地点无须在保障摘要页（Coverage Summary）上专门列明，即可自动包含在“场所（premises）”的定义之内？

  1. ✗ 属于“场所自动承保边界”陷阱。列名被保险人或其配偶拥有的季节性住所（seasonal residence）必须在保障摘要页（Coverage Summary）上列明才属于承保场所，未列明不会自动承保。Falls into the trap of premises automatic coverage boundaries. Seasonal residences owned by the named insured or their spouse must be specifically listed on the Coverage Summary page to be covered as premises; unlisted seasonal properties are not covered automatically.

  2. ✓ 正确。被保险人拥有的个人或家庭墓地、墓穴（individual or family cemetery plot or burial vault）属于五类免列明即可自动纳入场所（premises）定义的地点之一。Correct. An individual or family cemetery plot or burial vault owned by an insured is one of the five categories of locations automatically included within the definition of premises without being listed.

  3. ✗ 属于“场所自动承保边界”陷阱。虽然被保险人在加拿大境内拥有或租用的空地（vacant land）自动承保，但条款明确把空置的农地（farmland）除外。Falls into the trap of premises automatic coverage boundaries. Although vacant land owned or rented by the insured in Canada is automatically included, the policy wording explicitly excludes vacant farmland.

  4. ✗ 属于“场所自动承保边界”陷阱。临时居住或使用的非自有场所（premises where the insured is temporarily residing）要求连续租期不超过 90 天，租约超过连续 90 天则不在自动承保范围内。Falls into the trap of premises automatic coverage boundaries. Non-owned premises where the insured is temporarily residing qualify only if the continuous occupancy does not exceed 90 days; a rental exceeding 90 continuous days is not automatically covered.

When renting part of an insured two- or three-family dwelling, the exception allows not more than 2 roomers or boarders per family unit; accommodating more than 2 is excluded.

  3. ✗ 属于“商业与专业责任错位”陷阱。提供或未能提供任何专业服务（rendering or failure to render any professional service）属于第二部分的一般除外，必须购买错漏责任险（E&O）。Falls into the trap of confusing commercial and professional liabilities. The rendering of or failure to render professional services is an absolute general exclusion under Section II, requiring Errors and Omissions (E&O) coverage.

  4. ✓ 正确。偶尔把自己平时自住的那部分住宅出租（occasional rental of the portion of the dwelling usually occupied by you）属于经营活动除外的六项自动承保例外之一。Correct. The occasional rental of the portion of the dwelling usually occupied by the insured is one of the six automatic exceptions to the business pursuits exclusion.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

第 7 题Q7企业 CGL 七大风险与专业责任区分Seven commercial CGL hazards and professional liability distinctions★★陷阱：商业与专业责任错位

A plumbing contractor finishes installing a new water heater in a customer's home and leaves after the job is accepted. Later, a poorly soldered pipe joint bursts and floods the customer's basement. Under a Commercial General Liability (CGL) policy, which hazard covers this claim?

一名水管承包商在客户家中安装完新热水器并验收离场。事后，一处焊接不良的管接头爆裂，淹没了客户的地下室。在商业综合责任险（CGL）保单下，这一索赔属于哪种风险保障？

  1. ✗ 属于“商业与专业责任错位”陷阱。经营作业责任（premises and operations）保的是活正在干的时候（while work is in progress）发生的伤害或损害；本案在完工交付以后才出事，不属于经营作业。Falls into the commercial hazard classification trap. Premises and operations liability covers bodily injury or property damage occurring while work is in progress; because this loss occurred after work was completed and handed over, it is not an ongoing operations loss.

  2. ✗ 属于“商业与专业责任错位”陷阱。产品责任（products liability）针对的是被保险人卖出去的物品（things sold by the insured）本身缺陷致害，而承包商做完的安装活交付后因施工缺陷出事属于完工作业。Falls into the commercial hazard classification trap. Products liability applies to defects in goods or items sold by the insured, whereas property damage resulting from faulty installation after completion falls under completed operations.

  3. ✓ 正确。完工作业责任（completed operations liability）专门承保被保险人完成的工作在交付使用后（after the work has been completed and handed over）因施工质量问题造成的第三方人身伤害或财产损害。Correct. Completed operations liability covers third-party bodily injury or property damage arising out of work completed and handed over by the contractor caused by faulty workmanship.

  4. ✗ 属于“商业与专业责任错位”陷阱。错漏责任险（E&O）承保会计师、律师、建筑师、保险经纪人等提供咨询和意见的专业人士，水管承包商完工后的物理水损归 CGL 的完工作业责任保障。Falls into the commercial hazard classification trap. Errors and omissions covers professionals providing advice or consultancy (such as accountants, lawyers, or brokers), while physical water damage resulting from plumbing work is covered under CGL completed operations.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Under a Commercial General Liability (CGL) policy, which of the following claims falls under the definition of 'personal injury' rather than 'bodily injury' or professional liability?

在商业综合责任险（CGL）保单下，下列哪项索赔属于“人身权益侵害（personal injury）”而非“身体伤害（bodily injury）”或职业责任？

  1. ✗ 属于“商业与专业责任错位”陷阱。超市通道内撞上理货车摔断脚踝属于场所状况与作业引起的身体伤害（bodily injury），不属于人身伤害以外的权益侵害（personal injury）。Falls into the commercial hazard classification trap. Tripping over a stock cart and breaking an ankle is physical harm classified as bodily injury arising from premises operations, not personal injury.

  2. ✓ 正确。CGL 中的人身权益侵害（personal injury）是指经营活动造成的、身体伤害以外的侵害（injury other than bodily injury），包括书面诽谤（libel）、口头诽谤（slander）、恶意控告、非法进入或驱逐以及歧视。Correct. In a CGL policy, personal injury refers to injury other than bodily injury arising out of business operations, including libel, slander, malicious prosecution, wrongful entry or eviction, and discrimination.

  3. ✗ 属于“商业与专业责任错位”陷阱。售出商品缺陷破裂致人烫伤属于产品责任（products liability）导致的身体伤害（bodily injury），而非 personal injury。Falls into the commercial hazard classification trap. Scalding from a defective coffee carafe sold by the business is bodily injury covered under products liability, not personal injury.

  4. ✗ 属于“商业与专业责任错位”陷阱。医生、牙医、药剂师等医疗保健人员的专业失误不在 CGL 承保范围内，须由医疗过失责任险（Malpractice Insurance）承保。Falls into the commercial hazard classification trap. Professional errors by healthcare practitioners such as dentists or physicians are excluded under CGL and must be covered by Malpractice Insurance.

答案Answer：b
