本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 概念配对与辨析题：直接考 rate class（按车辆用途调基本险）与 rate group（按车型特征调自车损失险）、base premium 与 driver factor、纯无过失省（MB、QC）与 BC Enhanced Care 的区别，检验是否真正分清相近术语。Concept pairing and distinction questions: directly testing distinctions between rate class (adjusting Basic premium by vehicle use) and rate group (adjusting own damage coverage by vehicle make and model characteristics), base premium and driver factor, and pure no-fault provinces (MB, QC) versus BC Enhanced Care, verifying whether candidates truly distinguish closely related terms.
  • “找不是的”（EXCEPT / NOT）反向排除题：给出四项关于 ICBC 制度好处、私营保险公司可售超额保障范围、或拒绝出单令（refuse-to-issue order）触发原因的描述，要求揪出塞入的年龄性别定价、基本险销售权或理赔次数过多等干扰项。Negative elimination questions (EXCEPT / NOT): presenting four statements regarding the benefits of the ICBC system, the permitted scope of excess coverage sold by private insurers, or the trigger reasons for refuse-to-issue orders, requiring candidates to spot distractors such as age and gender rating, selling Basic insurance, or having too many insurance claims.
  • 规则与比例计算题：给出家庭或企业多驾驶人场景计算合并驾驶因子（75% 主驾 + 25% 最高风险列名者），或给出未列名驾驶人出事后的保费差额按 15 倍及封顶规则计算 UDAP。Rule and ratio calculation questions: presenting household or business multiple-driver scenarios to calculate the combined driver factor (75% principal driver + 25% highest-risk listed driver), or calculating the Unlisted Driver Accident Premium (UDAP) based on 15 times the premium difference and applicable cap rules following an unlisted driver crash.
  • 条件与门槛判定题：在具体客户情境中测试事故宽免（20 年驾龄 + 近 10 年无责）、新居民头 3 年加高与省外事故豁免、低里程（<5,000 km）与按距离折扣（<10,000 km）以及长者折扣（65 岁、每月最多 6 天通勤）的资格边界。Condition and threshold determination questions: testing eligibility boundaries in specific client scenarios for crash forgiveness (20 years of driving experience plus no chargeable claims in the last 10 years), new resident higher factors for the first 3 years and pre-BC crash exclusions, Low-Kilometre (<5,000 km) and Distance-Based (<10,000 km) discounts, and senior discounts (age 65, up to 6 days of commuting per month).
  • 考试口径优先题：针对新居民 30 天登记期、BC 境内 20 年驾龄宽免、按距离折扣 10,000 km 门槛及 UDAP 可选险应付保费 2 倍上限等考试口径与现行法规微调处，专门按考试口径设问。Exam-standard priority questions: specifically framing questions around exam-standard benchmarks where they differ from subtle current regulatory adjustments, including the 30-day registration period for new residents, the 20-year BC driving experience requirement for crash forgiveness, the 10,000 km threshold for the Distance-Based discount, and the cap of 2 times the payable Optional premium for UDAP.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ rate class 与 rate group 混为一谈Confusing rate class with rate group

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 两个词都带 rate，考生容易把“按车型赔付率与维修成本分组”当成基本险的调整因子。Both terms contain 'rate', so candidates easily mistake grouping by vehicle claim loss frequency and repair costs as an adjustment factor for Basic insurance.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 vehicle use（车辆用途）或 Basic premium adjustment 时选 rate class；出现 vehicle make/model 或 own damage 时才是 rate group。When the question stem mentions vehicle use or Basic premium adjustment, select rate class; only when it mentions vehicle make/model or own damage does it refer to rate group.

⚠ 合并因子 25% 误取最优或乱拉低Mistakenly selecting the lowest-risk driver for the 25% combined factor or improperly lowering it

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉以为多列一个老司机能稀释风险，或以为任何低风险朋友都能把主驾的合并因子往下拉。Candidates intuitively assume that listing an experienced driver will dilute risk, or believe that any low-risk friend can lower the principal driver's combined factor.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干列有多名额外驾驶人时，25% 必取风险最高者（highest-risk）；若次驾因子优于主驾，紧盯其身份是否为家庭成员（family member）或雇员（employee）。When multiple secondary drivers are listed, the 25% weight must take the highest-risk driver; if the secondary driver's factor is better than the principal driver's, verify whether that person is a family member or employee.

⚠ 未列名驾驶人出事怪错人或漏算上限Misattributing fault to the owner for an unlisted driver crash or missing the UDAP caps

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 容易按传统“保险跟车走”思维认为车主驾驶因子会涨，或者算 UDAP 时忘了“ICBC 实际付款”前提及基本险 $5,000、可选险 2 倍应付保费的封顶。Candidates often assume under traditional 'insurance follows the car' thinking that the owner's driver factor will rise, or forget the prerequisite of ICBC actually paying a claim and the caps of $5,000 on Basic and 2 times payable premium on Optional when calculating UDAP.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 unlisted driver 发生 at-fault claim 时，先拆开看“谁的记录受影响”（开车的人）与“谁交一次性 UDAP 罚金”（车主，且须三条件齐备）。When an unlisted driver causes an at-fault claim, separate who has their record affected (the driver) from who pays the one-time UDAP surcharge (the owner, provided all three conditions are met).

⚠ 两档里程折扣的险种与公里数张冠李戴Mismatching coverage types and mileage thresholds between the two mileage discounts

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 5,000 km 与 10,000 km、10% 与 10–15%、基本险与可选险容易交叉记混，或用现行 15,000 km 新规去答考题。Candidates easily cross 5,000 km with 10,000 km, 10% with 10–15%, and Basic with Optional coverage, or attempt to answer exam questions using the current 15,000 km practice rule.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 看到 5,000 km 对应 Low-Kilometre（基本险 10%）；看到 5,000–10,000 km 区间（如 7,500 km）只够 Distance-Based（可选险 10–15%）。Associate 5,000 km with Low-Kilometre (10% on Basic); associate the 5,000–10,000 km range (e.g. 7,500 km) with Distance-Based (10–15% on Optional).

⚠ 省别制度与私营可售范围越界Confusing provincial insurance systems and exceeding permitted private insurer coverages

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把安省（Ontario）误当成政府强制省，把魁省（Quebec）政府管人伤记反成管车损，或以为 BC 私营公司能卖基本险。Candidates mistakenly treat Ontario as a compulsory government province, reverse Quebec's government bodily injury and private property damage roles, or assume private insurers in BC can sell Basic insurance.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问 government compulsory provinces 只认 BC、SK、MB；问 BC private insurers 只认三样超额保障（excess coverage）及更低车损免赔额。For government compulsory provinces, recognize only BC, SK, and MB; for BC private insurers, recognize only the three excess coverages and lower own-damage deductibles.

第 1 题Q175/25合并驾驶因子与亲属雇员限制75/25 combined driver factor and family member or employee restrictions★★陷阱：合并因子 25% 误取最优或乱拉低

Nadia is the principal driver on an Autoplan policy with a driver factor of 1.000. Which of the following listed-driver changes would result in a combined driver factor that is LOWER than Nadia's individual factor of 1.000?

Nadia 是一份 Autoplan 保单的主驾，其个人驾驶因子为 1.000。下列哪项列名驾驶人变动会使保单的合并驾驶因子低于 Nadia 个人的 1.000？

  1. ✗ 掉入“合并因子 25% 误取最优”陷阱：列有多名额外驾驶人时，合并驾驶因子（[combined driver factor]）的 25% 取的是风险最高（[highest-risk]）的那位列名者（儿子 1.250），而不是因子最好的哥哥（0.700），结果会高于 1.000。Falls into the trap of mistakenly selecting the lowest-risk driver for the 25% combined factor: when multiple secondary drivers are listed, the 25% weight of the combined driver factor uses the highest-risk listed driver (the son at 1.250), not the brother with the best factor (0.700), resulting in a combined factor higher than 1.000.

  2. ✗ 掉入“合并因子乱拉低”陷阱：虽然邻居的驾驶因子（[driver factor]）比主驾（[principal driver]）好，但规则明确规定只有投保人的家庭成员（[family member]）或雇员（[employee]）才能把合并因子拉低，邻居不能用来压低保费。Falls into the trap of improperly lowering the combined factor: although the neighbour's driver factor is better than the principal driver's, the rules explicitly state that only a family member or employee of the policyholder can lower the combined factor; a neighbour cannot be used to reduce the premium.

  3. ✓ 正确：当保单除主驾（[principal driver]）外只列配偶一人时，合并驾驶因子（[combined driver factor]）按 75% 主驾（1.000）加 25% 配偶（0.720）计算；因为配偶属于家庭成员（[family member]），其更低的个人因子可以把合并因子拉低至 1.000 以下。Correct: when only a spouse is listed in addition to the principal driver, the combined driver factor is calculated as 75% principal driver (1.000) plus 25% spouse (0.720); because a spouse qualifies as a family member, their lower individual driver factor can reduce the combined driver factor below 1.000.

  4. ✗ 掉入“合并因子乱拉低”陷阱：无亲属或雇佣关系的室友既非家庭成员（[family member]）也非雇员（[employee]），即使其个人因子低于 1.000，系统也不会允许用他的名字把合并因子拉低。Falls into the trap of improperly lowering the combined factor: an unrelated household roommate is neither a family member nor an employee, so even though their individual driver factor is below 1.000, the system does not permit listing them to lower the combined factor.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Marcus is listed as the principal driver of his personal car with a driver factor of 0.900. He also lists his sister (driver factor 0.650) and his nephew (driver factor 1.150) on the policy. How will ICBC calculate the combined driver factor for this policy?

Marcus 是其私家车保单上的主驾，驾驶因子为 0.900。他在保单上还列了妹妹（驾驶因子 0.650）和外甥（驾驶因子 1.150）。ICBC 将如何计算该保单的合并驾驶因子？

  1. ✓ 正确：当列有两人以上时，合并驾驶因子（[combined driver factor]）由 75% 主驾（[principal driver]）因子加上 25% 风险最高（[highest-risk]）的那位额外列名驾驶人（外甥 1.150）决定。Correct: when two or more drivers are listed, the combined driver factor is determined by 75% of the principal driver's factor plus 25% of the highest-risk secondary listed driver (the nephew at 1.150).

  3. ✓ 正确：同时满足三个条件——保单没有未列名驾驶人保障（[Unlisted Driver Protection (UDP)]）、未列名驾驶人（[unlisted driver]）造成有责理赔、且 ICBC 实际付了款，同时高风险表亲列名后的应付保费高于实付保费，车主须交一次性 UDAP。Correct: satisfies all three conditions simultaneously: the policy lacks Unlisted Driver Protection (UDP), an unlisted driver causes an at-fault claim, and ICBC actually makes a claim payment; since the higher-risk cousin's payable premium if listed exceeds the paid premium, the owner must pay a one-time UDAP.

  4. ✗ 掉入“DRP 与 UDAP 混淆”陷阱：已列名的主驾开车使用电子设备被定罪两次以上触发的是向司机本人收取的驾驶风险保费（[Driver Risk Premium (DRP)]），而不是针对未列名者出事的 UDAP。Falls into the trap of confusing DRP with UDAP: a listed principal driver receiving two or more convictions for using an electronic device while driving triggers a Driver Risk Premium (DRP) billed directly to the driver, not a UDAP for an unlisted driver crash.

第 6 题Q6三类省别制度与私营超额保障范围Three provincial insurance systems and private excess coverage scope★陷阱：省别制度与私营可售范围越界

A resident of Kamloops wants $2,000,000 in third-party liability coverage and a lower collision deductible than the minimum offered by ICBC. How do British Columbia's auto insurance rules govern where the resident can buy these coverages?

一位住在 Kamloops 的居民想要 $2,000,000 的第三方责任险，并且想要比 ICBC 最低档还低的碰撞险免赔额。BC 的车险规则如何规定这位居民可以在哪里购买这些保障？

  1. ✗ 掉入“私营公司能打包卖基本险”陷阱：在 BC 这一政府强制车险省，基本险（[Autoplan Basic]）由 ICBC 独家垄断销售，私营保险公司只能卖超额保障（[excess coverage]），不能连基本险一起打包出售。Falls into the trap of assuming private insurers can package Basic coverage: in BC, a compulsory government auto insurance province, Autoplan Basic is sold exclusively by ICBC; private insurers may sell only excess coverage and cannot package or sell Basic insurance.

  2. ✓ 正确：基本险（[Autoplan Basic]）只能向 ICBC 买；私营保险公司只能卖三样超额保障（[excess coverage]）（加高第三方责任、加强无保险/保额不足驾驶人保障、自车损失险），其中加高责任险在 ICBC 或私营公司都能买，但比 ICBC 更低的车损免赔额只有私营公司提供。Correct: Autoplan Basic must be purchased from ICBC; private insurers may sell only three excess coverages (extended third-party liability, excess underinsured motorist protection, and own damage coverage); extended liability can be bought from either ICBC or a private insurer, but deductibles lower than ICBC's minimum are offered only by private insurers.

  3. ✗ 掉入“把自车损失险排除在私营可售范围外”陷阱：自车损失险（含碰撞险）属于 BC 私营公司获准销售的三样超额保障（[excess coverage]）之一，并非只能向 ICBC 购买。Falls into the trap of excluding own damage from private insurer authority: own damage coverage (including collision) is one of the three excess coverages BC private insurers are permitted to sell; it does not have to be purchased exclusively from ICBC.

  4. ✗ 掉入“BCUC 职能乱套”陷阱：BC 公用事业委员会（[BCUC]）负责监管 ICBC 的基本险费率并禁止交叉补贴（[cross-subsidization]），并不为个别投保人审批更低的可选险免赔额；更低免赔额只能去私营公司买。Falls into the trap of misattributing BCUC functions: the BC Utilities Commission (BCUC) regulates ICBC's Basic insurance rates and enforces the prohibition on cross-subsidization; it does not approve lower optional deductibles for individual policyholders; lower deductibles must be purchased from private insurers.

Which statement correctly compares the provincial automobile insurance systems across Canada?

第 7 题Q7保费调整因子与新居民30天登记Basic premium adjustment factors and 30-day new resident registration★陷阱：rate class 与 rate group 混为一谈

Which combination accurately pairs an Autoplan Basic premium adjustment factor with the way ICBC applies it?

下列哪项组合准确配对了 Autoplan 基本险保费的调整因子及其适用方式？

  1. ✗ 掉入“rate class 与 rate group 混为一谈”陷阱：按车辆用途（休闲或商业送货）调整基本险保费的是费率类别（[rate class]）；rate group 是按车型赔付率、维修成本等分组来调自车损失险保费的。Falls into the trap of confusing rate class with rate group: the factor that adjusts Basic premium according to vehicle use (pleasure or commercial delivery) is the rate class; rate group adjusts own damage coverage premiums based on vehicle make/model claim frequency and repair costs.

  2. ✗ 掉入“地区归属规则颠倒”陷阱：在 14 个省内地区加省外 Z 区的地区（[territory]）划分中，私家车和摩托车按停放地归区，出租车和巴士才按通常行驶的地区归区。Falls into the trap of reversing territory assignment rules: across the 14 provincial territories and out-of-province Zone Z, passenger cars and motorcycles are assigned based on where they are garaged, whereas taxis and buses are assigned based on the territory in which they are normally operated.

  3. ✓ 正确：基本险保费在统一的基础保费（[base premium]）之上，受地区（[territory]）、费率类别（[rate class]）和驾驶人记录三个调整因子影响；其中 rate class 反映车辆用途，共 150 多种，商用一般比纯休闲用途贵。Correct: Basic premium starts from a uniform base premium and is adjusted by three factors: territory, rate class, and driver record; rate class reflects vehicle use across more than 150 categories, with commercial use generally costing more than pleasure use.

  4. ✗ 掉入“虚构人口统计定价因子”陷阱：ICBC 制度的一大好处正是费率不按年龄、性别、婚姻状况定，只按驾驶经验与事故记录定价。Falls into the trap of inventing demographic rating factors: a primary benefit of the ICBC system is that premiums do not use age, gender, or marital status, pricing coverage strictly on driving experience and crash history.
