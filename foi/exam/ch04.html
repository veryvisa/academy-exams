第 4 章 · 保险流程：投保到理赔Chapter 4 · The Insurance Process: From Application to ClaimsThe Insurance Process

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 定性判断经纪人（broker）的双重义务：题干给出客户利益与保费高低、风险瑕疵冲突的场景，考你能否选出「对客户尽合理技能、谨慎与勤勉」及「对保险公司如实披露全部重要事实」，剔除「只看最低价」或「只替客户说话」的绝对化表述。Qualitatively evaluate the dual duties of a broker: given scenarios where client interests conflict with premium levels or risk deficiencies, test whether you can identify reasonable skill, care and diligence owed to the client and full disclosure of all material facts to the insurer, while eliminating absolutist statements such as seeking only the lowest premium or acting solely for the client.
  • 区分错误与遗漏（E&O）的头号原因与其他成因：直接问哪一项最常引发 E&O 索赔，考你能否锁定占近 50% 的「保障不足（inadequate coverage）」及其四种具体表现，不把「违反经纪协议」或「续保出错」当成主因。Distinguish the primary cause of errors and omissions (E&O) from secondary sources: directly asking which factor most commonly triggers E&O claims, testing whether you can pinpoint inadequate coverage (accounting for nearly 50%) along with its four specific forms, without mistaking brokerage agreement violations or renewal errors for the primary cause.
  • 通过核保调查动作反推危险类别：给出一项核保人（underwriter）的核查行为（如查阅财务状况、过往理赔历史或实地看建筑与杂物堆放），考你判断核保人真正担心的是物质危险（physical hazard）还是道德危险（moral hazard）。Infer the hazard category from an underwriter's investigation actions: given a verification action by an underwriter (such as examining financial condition, prior claims history, or inspecting building construction and clutter), test whether you can determine if the underwriter is concerned with a physical hazard or a moral hazard.
  • 考查核保人的身份、首要职能、四类信息源与五种核保决定：常把「绑定保障、签发保单、计算个人险费率」塞进干扰项，考你能否认准核保人是无须领牌的保险公司雇员，首要职能是挑出最可能盈利的风险（risk selection）。Examine the underwriter's role, primary function, four information sources, and five underwriting decisions: commonly placing binding coverage, issuing policies, or calculating personal lines rates into distractors, testing whether you recognize that an underwriter is an unlicensed employee of the insurance company whose primary function is risk selection (selecting risks most likely to be profitable).
  • 考查出险后的两步报案链路与两类理赔人（claims adjuster）分工：问客户报小案时经纪行怎么应对，或什么时候动用独立理赔人（independent adjuster），考你区分公司理赔人（处理多数案件）与独立理赔人（缺人手或需特殊专长时聘用）。Examine the two-step loss reporting path and division of labor between two types of claims adjusters: asking how a brokerage handles minor loss reports, or when an independent adjuster is engaged, testing whether you distinguish company adjusters (handling most claims) from independent adjusters (hired during staff shortages or when special expertise is required).

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 单边义务陷阱One-Sided Duty Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生记住了 Broker 首要对客户负责，看到「仅为客户最大利益行事」或「必须帮客户找最低保费」就以为是尽责，忘了经纪人同时是「首线核保人」，对保险公司负有说真话、不隐瞒重要信息的义务。Candidates remember that a broker is primarily responsible to the client and assume that acting solely in the client's best interest or finding the lowest premium represents complete diligence, forgetting that the broker is also a first line underwriter who owes the insurer a duty to tell the truth and not withhold material facts.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 选项里出现 only（只）、lowest premium（最低保费）、no obligation to the insurer（对保险公司无义务）等极端词。Options containing extreme words such as only, lowest premium, or no obligation to the insurer.

⚠ E&O主因与次因混淆Confusing Primary vs Secondary Causes of E&O

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 违反经纪协议、处理拖延、续保或改单出错确实都会引发 E&O 索赔，在选项里看起来很专业，容易把它们误当成占近一半比例的头号原因（保障不足）。Violating brokerage agreements, processing delays, and renewal or endorsement errors do trigger E&O claims and sound plausible in options, leading candidates to mistake them for the leading cause (inadequate coverage), which accounts for nearly half of all claims.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 primary cause、most common source、nearly half of all E&O claims 等问「主要原因」的字眼。Question stems containing phrases asking for the leading cause, such as primary cause, most common source, or nearly half of all E&O claims.

⚠ 物质与道德危险混淆Confusing Physical and Moral Hazards

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生容易把凡是能看到的行为或结果都归为物质危险，或者被复习资料里的 morale hazard 干扰，忘了考试口径把「财务困难、品行不端、觉得有保险就对损失漠不关心」全部归入道德危险。Candidates tend to categorize any visible condition or behavior as a physical hazard, or become distracted by the term morale hazard from other study guides, forgetting that the exam standard classifies financial difficulty, character flaws, and indifference to loss because of having insurance under moral hazard.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述核保人查的是「申请人的人品、财务、过往理赔水分或粗心态度」还是「建筑结构、占用性质、场所整洁度」。Notice whether the stem describes investigating the applicant's character, finances, inflated past claims, or careless attitude versus building construction, occupancy type, or housekeeping neatness.

⚠ 核保首要职能与次要工作混淆Confusing Primary Underwriting Function with Routine Tasks

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 核保人平时确实会参与复杂商业险定价、改单、准备取消通知和续保证明，考生容易把这些具体日常事务当成核保人的首要职能（风险选择）。Underwriters do participate in complex commercial rating, endorsements, and preparing cancellation notices and renewal certificates, so candidates often mistake these routine daily tasks for the underwriter's primary function (risk selection).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问 primary function 或 main role of an underwriter，干扰项列出 rating、issuing policies、policyholder service。Question stems asking for the primary function or main role of an underwriter while distractors list rating, issuing policies, or policyholder service.

⚠ 考试口径与BC现行法混淆Confusing Exam Syllabus with Current BC Practice

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 已做过功课的考生知道 BC 现行规则要求每位持牌人都被 E&O 覆盖、理赔人必须持牌，但在 FOI 考卷上答题必须按考试资料考试口径（如经纪行 E&O 保单覆盖全员故个人不必另买、只区分公司与独立理赔人而不考理赔牌照）。Well-prepared candidates know that current BC rules require every licensee to be covered by E&O and adjusters to be licensed, but FOI exam questions must be answered strictly according to the textbook syllabus (e.g., brokerage E&O covers all staff so individuals need not buy separate coverage, and distinguishing company vs independent adjusters without testing adjuster licensing).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干围绕 E&O 个人是否需单独投保、核保人是否需要领牌、Agent 与 Broker 的定义区别提问。Question stems inquiring whether brokerage employees need individual E&O, whether underwriters must be licensed, or the legal distinctions between agents and brokers.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
保险不是普通商品的两点原因Two Reasons Why Insurance Is Not an Ordinary Commoditycommodity / application保险必须先提交投保申请（不合标准通常拒保），且保费因被保风险的不同因素而异，不是人人同价。An application must be submitted before insurance is issued (substandard risks are generally declined), and premiums vary based on specific risk factors rather than being the same for everyone.
合同当事人与中介角色Parties to the Contract and Intermediary Rolesintermediary / parties to the contract保险合同只有被保险人和保险公司两个当事人；经纪人和代理人都只是中介，不是合同当事人。An insurance contract has only two parties: the insured and the insurer; both brokers and agents are merely intermediaries and are not parties to the contract.
Agent 与 Broker 的两点区别Two Distinctions Between an Agent and a Brokeragent / brokerAgent 首要对保险公司负责且通常只代表一家公司；Broker 首要对客户负责且通常可在多家公司间比较。An agent owes primary duty to the insurer and usually represents a single company; a broker owes primary duty to the client and typically compares options across multiple insurers.
对客户的法律标准与 E&O 索赔Legal Standard Owed to Clients and E&O Claimsreasonable skill, care and diligence / errors and omissions (E&O)经纪人须以合理的技能、谨慎与勤勉确保客户获得恰当建议与保障，未达标致客户受损会引发针对经纪人和经纪行的 E&O 索赔。Brokers must exercise reasonable skill, care and diligence to ensure clients receive proper advice and coverage; failure to meet this standard resulting in client financial loss can trigger E&O claims against both the broker and the brokerage.
E&O 头号原因：保障不足Primary Cause of E&O Claims: Inadequate Coverageinadequate coverage保障不足占 E&O 索赔近 50%，包括未告知除外责任、未安排或安排太晚、安排了错误保障、对能否买到某保障给了错误说法。Inadequate coverage accounts for nearly 50% of all E&O claims, arising from failure to advise on exclusions, failing to place coverage or placing it too late, arranging incorrect coverage, or misrepresenting the availability of coverage.
对保险公司的义务与首线核保人Duty to the Insurer and First Line Underwriterfirst line underwriter经纪人最先接触风险，须对保险公司说真话且不隐瞒重要信息，绝不能因怕客户流失而隐瞒不利事实。As the first to assess a risk, the broker must tell the insurer the truth and not withhold material information, never concealing unfavorable facts out of fear of losing the client to a competitor.
核保人身份、首要职能与信息源Underwriter Role, Primary Function and Information Sourcesunderwriter / risk selection / loss experience data核保人是无须领牌的保险公司雇员，首要职能是挑出最可能盈利的风险，依据投保单、经纪人、过去 3 到 5 年损失经验数据及检查报告做判断。An underwriter is an unlicensed employee of the insurance company whose primary function is risk selection (selecting risks most likely to be profitable), relying on the application, the broker, 3 to 5 years of loss experience data, and inspection reports.
物质危险与道德危险的区分Distinction Between Physical Hazard and Moral Hazardphysical hazard / moral hazard / indifference to loss物质危险关乎场所（建筑结构、占用性质、内务管理），看得见且常能降低；道德危险关乎人（财务状况、品行、对损失漠不关心），极难发现。Physical hazards relate to the premises (building construction, occupancy, housekeeping), are observable, and can often be reduced; moral hazards relate to the applicant (finances, character, indifference to loss) and are extremely difficult to detect.
五种核保决定与三类定价分工Five Underwriting Decisions and Three Rating Approachesrating / rate manual / rating sheet核保决定有拒保、原样接受、加费接受、提高免赔额接受、附条件接受五种；个人险由经纪人查费率手册算，小企业用费率表，复杂商业险全由核保人定价。Underwriters have five decisions: reject, accept as submitted, accept with a rate increase, accept with a higher deductible, or accept subject to conditions; rating is done by brokers using rate manuals for personal lines, rating sheets for small businesses, and entirely by underwriters for complex commercial risks.
理赔两步流程与两类理赔人Two-Step Claims Process and Two Types of Claims Adjustersclaims adjuster / company adjuster / independent adjuster出险后先由被保险人报经纪行、再由经纪行报保险公司；公司理赔人处理大部分案件，独立理赔人在内部缺人手或需特殊专长时使用。Following a loss, the insured reports first to the brokerage, which then notifies the insurer; company adjusters handle the majority of claims, while independent adjusters are used when internal staff is unavailable or specialized expertise is required.

第 1 题Q1E&O头号原因与成因Primary Cause and Sources of E&O Claims★★陷阱：E&O主因与次因混淆

Nearly half of all errors and omissions (E&O) claims brought against insurance brokers arise from the same primary cause. Which of the following situations is an example of that primary cause?

针对保险经纪人提出的错误与遗漏（E&O）索赔中，有近一半源于同一个主要原因。下列哪种情况属于这一主要原因的例子？

  1. ✓ 正确。在错误与遗漏（errors and omissions, E&O）索赔中，占近 50% 的头号原因是保障不足（inadequate coverage），其四种成因之一就是没告诉客户有哪些除外责任，导致客户未能获得所需的保障。Correct. In errors and omissions (E&O) claims, the leading cause accounting for nearly 50% is inadequate coverage. One of its four specific causes is failing to inform the client about key policy exclusions, leaving the client without necessary coverage when a loss occurs.

  2. ✗ 错误。违反经纪协议（brokerage agreement）属于「其他五种 E&O 来源」之一，不是占近一半比例的头号原因，属于「E&O主因与次因混淆」陷阱。Incorrect. Breaching the terms of the brokerage agreement is one of the other five sources of E&O claims rather than the primary cause that accounts for nearly half of all claims; this falls into the trap of confusing primary and secondary causes of E&O.

  3. ✗ 错误。取消或续保出错属于「其他五种 E&O 来源」之一，而非头号原因「保障不足」，属于「E&O主因与次因混淆」陷阱。Incorrect. Clerical errors in cancellation or renewal procedures belong to the other five sources of E&O claims rather than the primary cause of inadequate coverage; this falls into the trap of confusing primary and secondary causes of E&O.

  4. ✗ 错误。对被保风险的不实陈述或描述错误属于「其他五种 E&O 来源」之一，不是占近 50% 的头号原因，属于「E&O主因与次因混淆」陷阱。Incorrect. Misrepresentation or misdescription of the insured risk is one of the other five sources of E&O claims and not the leading cause accounting for nearly 50% of claims; this falls into the trap of confusing primary and secondary causes of E&O.

第 2 题Q2对客户的法律标准与E&O保险Legal Standard Owed to Clients and E&O Insurance★★陷阱：考试口径与BC现行法混淆

Under the rules tested on the exam regarding errors and omissions (E&O) liability and insurance, which statement is correct?

按照考试所依据的错误与遗漏（E&O）责任及保险标准，下列哪项陈述是正确的？

  1. ✗ 错误。按考试口径，经纪行的错误与遗漏（errors and omissions, E&O）保单覆盖行里全体工作人员，个人不必另买来防范客户索赔；注意考试口径与 BC 现行法（Rule 7(11) 要求每位持牌人须持有或被覆盖于最低每宗 $1,000,000、累计 $2,000,000 的 E&O 保险）表述侧重不同，答题须按考试口径，这属于「考试口径与BC现行法混淆」陷阱。Incorrect. Under the exam standard, the brokerage's errors and omissions (E&O) policy covers all staff members, so individuals do not need to purchase separate coverage to protect against client claims. Note the difference in focus from current BC law (Council Rule 7(11) requires every licensee to hold or be covered by E&O insurance with minimum limits of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate); exam questions must be answered according to the textbook syllabus, avoiding the trap of confusing exam standard with current BC practice.

  2. ✓ 正确。法律要求经纪人达到合理的技能、谨慎与勤勉（reasonable skill, care and diligence）标准；客户因经纪人未达标而受损，可向经纪人和经纪行提出 E&O 索赔。注：关于 E&O 保险，考试口径称有些省立法要求经纪行投保且保单覆盖全体员工故个人不必另买，而 BC 现行规则要求每位持牌人必须持有或被 E&O 覆盖。Correct. The law requires brokers to exercise reasonable skill, care and diligence; if a client suffers a loss because the broker failed to meet this standard, an E&O claim may be brought against both the broker and the brokerage. Note on E&O insurance: the exam standard states that some provinces legally require brokerages to purchase E&O coverage which protects all staff so individuals need not buy their own, whereas current BC rules require each licensee to hold or be covered by E&O insurance.

  3. ✗ 错误。经纪人只是中介（intermediary），不是保险合同的法律当事人（parties to the contract 只有被保险人和保险公司），E&O 索赔不会在客户的财产险保单下直接提出，而是针对经纪人和经纪行的职业过失。Incorrect. The broker is merely an intermediary and not a legal party to the insurance contract (the parties to the contract are strictly the insured and the insurer); an E&O claim is never filed directly under the client's property insurance policy, but is brought against the broker and brokerage for professional negligence.

  4. ✗ 错误。经纪人对客户的首要义务是确保客户得到对的建议和对的保障，法律标准是合理的技能、谨慎与勤勉，而不是单纯找最低价格，属于「单边义务陷阱」。Incorrect. The broker's primary duty to the client is to ensure the client receives proper advice and appropriate coverage; the legal standard is reasonable skill, care and diligence, not simply finding the lowest price; this falls into the one-sided duty trap.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Under the rules tested on the exam, why do individual employees working inside an insurance brokerage not need to purchase their own separate errors and omissions (E&O) insurance policy?

根据考试考试口径，为什么在保险经纪行内部工作的个人员工不需要自己单独购买一份错误与遗漏（E&O）保险？

The two distinctions are: (1) To whom primary duty is owed: an agent to the insurer, a broker to the client; (2) Number of companies represented: an agent usually represents one company, while a broker may represent one but typically compares across multiple insurers on behalf of the client. Note: current BC Financial Institutions Act defines only insurance agent, insurance salesperson, and insurance adjuster, with broker being an industry term rather than a licence class, but exam questions must be answered according to these two distinctions.

  3. ✗ 错误。经纪人两头都有义务：对客户尽合理技能、谨慎与勤勉，对保险公司作为首线核保人必须说真话、不隐瞒重要信息，并非只对保单持有人客户诚实，属于「单边义务陷阱」。Incorrect. The broker owes duties to both parties: reasonable skill, care and diligence to the client, and telling the truth without withholding material information to the insurer as a first line underwriter; duty is not owed solely to the client, falling into the one-sided duty trap.

  4. ✗ 错误。复杂商业险保单定价是核保人（underwriter）的工作，出险后的损失理算是理赔人（claims adjuster）的工作，都不是区分 Agent 与 Broker 的标准。Incorrect. Rating complex commercial policies is the role of underwriters, and adjusting losses after a claim is submitted is the role of claims adjusters; neither distinguishes agents from brokers.
