第 2 章 · 保险合同Chapter 2 · Insurance ContractsThe Insurance Contract

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 情景判断缺要素：给出投保、报价修改或出险的日常场景，判断合同缺少八大要素中的哪一项（最常考合意 Agreement、缔约能力 Legal Capacity、可保利益 Insurable Interest 与标的合法 Legality of Object 的区分）。Missing contract element scenario questions: Given an everyday scenario of insurance application, quote amendment, or loss occurrence, identifying which of the eight contract elements is missing (most frequently testing the distinctions among Agreement, Legal Capacity, Insurable Interest, and Legality of Object).
  • 风险处理四招对号入座：用家庭或小微商户的防损、留存免赔额、放弃经营或买保险的做法，考查回避、控制、自留、转移四者的定义及「最不实用 vs 最常用实用」的定性判断。Four risk handling methods matching questions: Using loss prevention, retaining a deductible, abandoning operations, or purchasing insurance by families or small businesses to test the definitions of avoidance, control, retention, and transfer, along with the qualitative judgment of 'least practical versus most common and practical'.
  • 纯风险与投机风险辨析：直接考定义或给创业、赌博、自然灾害等场景，考「只有纯风险可保」以及用投机风险的「可赚可亏」作为头号干扰项。Pure risk versus speculative risk distinction questions: Directly testing definitions or presenting scenarios such as starting a business, gambling, or natural disasters to test that 'only pure risk is insurable', using the 'chance of gain or loss' of speculative risk as the primary distractor.
  • 主体缔约资格筛查：列出四个商户名称（纯商号、带 t/a 或 d/b/a 的个体户、带 Ltd./Inc. 的公司）或未成年人、醉酒者签单场景，判断谁具备缔约能力。Party contracting capacity screening questions: Listing four business names (pure trade names, sole proprietorships with t/a or d/b/a, corporations with Ltd./Inc.) or scenarios of policy signing by minors or intoxicated persons to determine who possesses legal capacity to contract.
  • 临时保单与改单三文件匹配：考查口头与书面临时保单（Binder）的同等法律效力、承保权限的两个出处与超权限 E&O 责任，以及批单（Endorsement）、浮动保单（Floater）、另立保单（Separate Policy）的适用边界。Binder and three policy change documents matching questions: Testing the equal legal effect of oral and written binders, the two sources of binding authority and E&O liability for exceeding authority, and the application boundaries of endorsements, floaters, and separate policies.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 附条件接受当合意Treating Conditional Acceptance as Agreement

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到客户说「接受报价」就以为合同成立，忽略了后面跟着修改免赔额或保额的条件，本质上仍在谈判，没有无条件承诺（Unconditional Acceptance）。Assuming a contract is formed upon seeing a client say 'accepts the quote', overlooking subsequent conditions altering deductibles or limits, which essentially remains ongoing negotiation without unconditional acceptance.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「accepts the quote, provided that / as long as / but changes…」或保单下发条款与投保单不一致。The question stem contains 'accepts the quote, provided that / as long as / but changes...' or the issued policy terms conflict with the application.

⚠ 商号直接当签约主体Treating a Trade Name Directly as a Contracting Party

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把响亮的店铺招牌当成独立法人，忘了纯商号（Trade Name）没有法律地位，必须连上业主法定姓名（用 t/a、d/b/a、o/a）或本身是 Ltd./Inc. 公司才能缔约。Mistaking a prominent storefront trade name for an independent legal entity, forgetting that a pure trade name has no legal standing and must be linked to the owner's legal name (using t/a, d/b/a, o/a) or be an incorporated company (Ltd./Inc.) to contract.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 选项并列出现四个商户名称，其中有的只写店名，有的带 Ltd./Inc. 或 d/b/a。Options list four business names in parallel, where some show only a trade name while others include Ltd./Inc. or d/b/a.

⚠ 赃物与别人财产混为一谈Confusing Stolen Goods with Property of Others

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到「不是自己的东西」就条件反射选无可保利益，忘了偷来的赃物违反的是标的合法（Legality of Object），而给邻居财物或期待继承物投保才是缺乏可保利益（Insurable Interest）。Reflexively choosing lack of insurable interest upon seeing 'property not owned by oneself', forgetting that insuring stolen goods violates legality of object, whereas insuring a neighbour's property or an expected inheritance lacks insurable interest.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干明确提到「stolen goods（赃物）」vs「neighbour's property（邻居财产）/ expected inheritance（期待继承物）」。The question stem explicitly mentions stolen goods versus a neighbour's property or an expected inheritance.

⚠ 免赔额当风险转移Mistaking Deductibles for Risk Transfer

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 因为免赔额写在保单里，就误以为选高免赔额属于风险转移（Transfer）或风险控制（Controlling），其实免赔额是自己扛下小额损失，属于风险自留（Retention）。Assuming that selecting a higher deductible represents risk transfer or risk control simply because deductibles are written into a policy, whereas absorbing minor losses yourself is actually risk retention.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「deductible（免赔额）」或「self-insure（自保）」并问属于四种风险处理方式中的哪一种。The question stem mentions 'deductible' or 'self-insure' and asks which of the four risk handling methods it represents.

⚠ 改条款乱选 Rider 或 BinderIndiscriminately Choosing Rider or Binder for Policy Amendments

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把临时保单（Binder）、浮动保单（Floater）、批单（Endorsement）和 Rider 混用；考试标准框架下，双方同意修改现有保单条款一律选批单（Endorsement）。Confusing binders, floaters, endorsements, and riders; under the standard exam framework, mutually agreed changes to existing policy terms must always be acknowledged by an endorsement.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问「acknowledge a change in the terms of an existing contract」且选项同时出现 endorsement、rider、binder、floater。The question stem asks to 'acknowledge a change in the terms of an existing contract' and options simultaneously include endorsement, rider, binder, and floater.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
风险的四类处理方式Four Methods of Handling RiskAvoidance / Controlling / Retention / Transfer面对人身、财产、责任三类风险有四招：回避最不实用，控制只能降频率和严重度，自留含免赔额（越高自留越多），转移（买保险）最常用最实用。There are four methods to deal with personal, property, and liability risks: avoidance is the least practical; control only reduces frequency and severity; retention includes deductibles (higher deductibles mean more retained risk); and transfer (buying insurance) is the most common and practical.
纯风险与投机风险Pure Risk vs. Speculative RiskPure risk vs. Speculative risk纯风险只有亏损可能、没有获利机会，只有纯风险可保；投机风险既可能亏也可能赚，不可保。Pure risk involves only the chance of loss and no opportunity for gain, and only pure risk is insurable; speculative risk involves the possibility of either loss or gain and is uninsurable.
合意与对价Agreement and ConsiderationAgreement and Consideration填好的投保单是要约（Offer），保险公司无条件按要约出单是承诺（Acceptance），口头与书面合意同样有约束力；投保人的对价是已付保费或日后付保费的承诺。A completed application is an offer, and the insurer unconditionally issuing a policy in accordance with the offer is acceptance, with oral and written agreements being equally binding; the applicant's consideration is the premium paid or the promise to pay premium in the future.
缔约能力与商号规则Legal Capacity and Trade Name RulesLegal capacity and Trade names纯商号无缔约地位，须带业主法定姓名与 t/a、d/b/a 或 o/a，而 Ltd./Inc. 公司可独立缔约；考试口径下成年年龄为普通法 21 岁（多数省降到 19 岁或更低），未成年人除生活必需品外的合同可在合理期限内撤销。A pure trade name has no legal status to contract and must be accompanied by the owner's legal name and t/a, d/b/a, or o/a, whereas an incorporated company (Ltd./Inc.) can contract independently; under the exam standard, the common law age of majority is 21 (reduced to 19 or lower in most provinces), and contracts entered into by minors for items other than necessities of life are voidable within a reasonable time.
标的合法与真实意思Legality of Object and Genuine IntentionLegality of object and Genuine intention违背公共利益（如给赃物投保）的合同因标的不合法而不可执行；真实意思要求合意未受欺诈（Fraud）、胁迫（Duress）、隐瞒（Concealment）或错误（Mistake）污染。A contract that violates public policy (such as insuring stolen goods) is unenforceable due to illegality of object; genuine intention requires that consent is not tainted by fraud, duress, concealment, or mistake.
保险合同专有三要素Three Elements Unique to Insurance ContractsInsurable interest / Utmost good faith / Indemnity保险合同除一般五要素外还须具备可保利益（出险会受财务损失，含所有人、合伙人、抵押权人、受托保管人等）、最大诚信与补偿（其中补偿常被单列为保险合同专有）。In addition to the five general elements, insurance contracts require insurable interest (suffering financial loss upon occurrence of a loss, including owners, partners, mortgagees, and bailees), utmost good faith, and indemnity (with indemnity often specifically highlighted as unique to insurance).
无效与可撤销的区别Distinction Between Void and VoidableVoid vs. Voidable八要素缺一会导致合同无效（Void，法律上视为从未存在）或可撤销（Voidable，对过错方无效，受害方可选择是否将其当作无效）。The absence of any essential element causes a contract to be void (treated in law as though it never existed) or voidable (unenforceable by the wrongdoer, with the aggrieved party holding the option to treat it as void).
临时保单与承保权限Binders and Binding AuthorityBinder / Binding authority / E&O临时保单（Binder / Cover note）本身就是保险合同，口头与书面同样有效但口头承保应立刻书面确认；经纪人承保权限来自代理协议与费率手册，越权承保出险由经纪行承担错误与遗漏（E&O）赔偿责任。A binder (or cover note) is itself an insurance contract, equally binding whether oral or written, though oral binders should be confirmed in writing immediately; a broker's binding authority stems from the agency agreement and the rate manual, and if a loss occurs on coverage bound in excess of authority, the brokerage bears liability under errors and omissions (E&O).
合同变更的三种书面文件Three Written Documents for Policy ChangesEndorsement / Floater / Separate policy保单变更须双方同意且一律书面：修改现有条款用批单（Endorsement），承保高流动性财产用浮动保单（Floater），原单不动新增独立保障用另立保单（Separate policy）。Policy changes require mutual consent and must always be in writing: modifying existing terms uses an endorsement, insuring highly mobile property uses a floater, and adding independent coverage while leaving the original policy intact uses a separate policy.

第 1 题Q1合意要素：要约与无条件承诺Agreement element: Offer and unconditional acceptance★★陷阱：附条件接受当合意

A bakery owner submits a signed application requesting a deductible of $100. The insurer issues the policy with a $500 deductible instead. Before the owner reads the policy, a fire damages the bakery's ovens. Why is there no enforceable contract on the $500 terms?

一位烘焙店店主交了签字的投保单，要求 100 加元免赔额。保险公司下发的保单却写着 500 加元免赔额。店主还没看保单，一场大火烧坏了店里的烤箱。为什么按 500 加元条款没有可强制执行的合同？

  1. ✓ 正确。填好的投保单是向保险公司发出的要约（Offer），保险公司必须作出无条件承诺（Unconditional Acceptance）才有合意（Agreement）；把 100 加元免赔额改成 500 加元属于未按要约出单，双方没有达成意思一致。Correct. A completed application constitutes an offer to the insurer, and the insurer must make an unconditional acceptance for agreement to exist; issuing a policy with a $500 deductible instead of the requested $100 deductible means the policy was not issued in accordance with the offer, so the parties reached no meeting of the minds.

  2. ✗ 错误（概念混淆陷阱）。对价（Consideration）是投保人支付保费或日后支付保费的承诺，本题问题出在双方对免赔额条款没有达成一致，而非缺乏对价。Incorrect (concept confusion trap). Consideration is the applicant's payment of premium or promise to pay premium in the future; the issue here is that the parties failed to agree on the deductible term, not that consideration was lacking.

  3. ✗ 错误（要素张冠李戴陷阱）。隐瞒（Concealment）通常指投保人不实陈述或不披露重要事实；保险公司改写免赔额下发保单属于未按要约承诺，缺的是合意（Agreement）而非最大诚信（Utmost Good Faith）。Incorrect (misattributed element trap). Concealment generally refers to an applicant misrepresenting or failing to disclose material facts; the insurer altering the deductible when issuing the policy constitutes a failure to accept the offer, meaning agreement is missing rather than utmost good faith.

  4. ✗ 错误（无中生有陷阱）。合意本身甚至不必是书面的，本题不成立的原因是出单内容与投保单要约不一致导致无合意，而不是缺乏批单（Endorsement）或真实意思（Genuine Intention）。Incorrect (invented requirement trap). Agreement itself does not even need to be in writing; the contract fails because the issued policy terms differed from the application's offer resulting in a lack of agreement, not due to the absence of an endorsement or genuine intention.

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An insurer sends a property insurance quote with a $500 deductible to a warehouse owner. The owner replies, 'I accept your quote, provided you lower the deductible to $100.' That night, before the insurer responds, wind damages the roof. What is the status of the coverage?

保险公司向仓库业主发出一份免赔额为 500 加元的财产险报价。业主回复：「我接受报价，前提是你们把免赔额降到 100 加元。」当晚在保险公司回复前，大风吹坏了屋顶。保障处于什么状态？

  1. ✗ 错误（附条件接受当合意陷阱）。虽然业主口头说了接受，但附带了把免赔额从 500 加元降到 100 加元的条件，不属于无条件承诺（Unconditional Acceptance），合同并未成立。Incorrect (treating conditional acceptance as agreement trap). Although the owner used the word 'accept', introducing a condition to lower the deductible from $500 to $100 is not an unconditional acceptance, so no contract was formed.

  2. ✗ 错误（无效与未成立混淆陷阱）。可撤销（Voidable）发生在合同形式上已存在但某要素有瑕疵时；这里双方仍在讨价还价，根本没有达成合意（Agreement）。Incorrect (confusing voidable status with lack of contract formation trap). A contract is voidable when it exists in form but possesses a defect in one of its elements; here, the parties were still bargaining and never reached agreement.

  3. ✓ 正确。合意（Agreement）要求一方提出要约、另一方作出无条件承诺；附条件的回应（要求把 500 加元免赔额改成 100 加元）说明双方仍在谈判，没有合意，因此合同尚未成立。Correct. Agreement requires an offer by one party and an unconditional acceptance by the other; a conditional response (demanding that the deductible be lowered from $500 to $100) constitutes ongoing negotiation rather than agreement, meaning no contract was formed.

  4. ✗ 错误（原则滥用陷阱）。最大诚信（Utmost Good Faith）要求双方诚实，但不能代替合意（Agreement）自动替未回复的保险公司答应新条款。Incorrect (doctrine misapplication trap). Utmost good faith requires both parties to act honestly, but it cannot substitute for agreement by automatically binding the insurer to new terms without its response.

答案Answer：c

第 2 题Q2缔约能力：商号与未成年人规则Legal capacity: Trade names and minors' contracts★★陷阱：商号直接当签约主体

A broker is reviewing four commercial applications to verify the legal capacity of each applicant. Under the rules tested on the exam, which of the following applicants has the full legal capacity to enter into an enforceable commercial insurance contract as written?

一位经纪人正在审核四份商业投保单，以核实每个投保人的缔约能力。按照考试所考的考试口径，下列哪个投保人按所写名称具备完整的缔约能力，可以订立可强制执行的商业保险合同？

  1. ✗ 错误（商号直接当签约主体陷阱）。只写商号（Trade Name）的生意在法律上没有地位，不能单独缔约，必须带上业主的法定姓名和 t/a、d/b/a 或 o/a。Incorrect (treating a trade name directly as a contracting party trap). A business identified solely by a trade name has no legal standing and cannot contract on its own; it must include the owner's legal name along with t/a, d/b/a, or o/a.

  2. ✓ 正确。带有 Ltd. 或 Inc. 的公司（Corporation）是独立于股东的法律实体（Legal Entity），和自然人一样享有缔约能力（Legal Capacity），保单上不必写股东名字。Correct. A corporation designated with Ltd. or Inc. is an independent legal entity separate from its shareholders, possessing full legal capacity to contract just like a natural person, without needing shareholders' names on the policy.

  3. ✗ 错误（未成年人效力记错陷阱）。按考试考试口径，普通法成年年龄为 21 岁（多数省已降到 19 岁或更低），未成年人可为生活必需品（Necessities of life）有效订约，其他非必需品合同保留在合理期限内撤销（Voidable）的权利而非一律绝对无效。（注：此题考考试口径；BC 现行《Age of Majority Act》成年年龄为 19 岁，且《Infants Act》规定未成年人合同对未成年人不可执行、以成年后一年为期，不靠合理期限。）Incorrect (minors' contractual effect trap). Under the exam standard, the common law age of majority is 21 (reduced to 19 or lower in most provinces); minors can validly contract for necessities of life, while contracts for non-necessities remain voidable within a reasonable time rather than automatically void. (Note: This question tests the exam standard; in current BC practice, the Age of Majority Act sets the age at 19, and the Infants Act makes minors' contracts unenforceable against the minor within one year of reaching majority rather than relying on a reasonable time.)

  4. ✗ 错误（商号直接当签约主体陷阱）。合伙企业虽有缔约权，但如果保单上只写店名（Trade Name）而不写合伙人法定姓名及连接词（t/a、d/b/a、o/a），在法律上没有缔约地位。Incorrect (treating a trade name directly as a contracting party trap). Although a partnership has the capacity to contract, writing only a trade name on the policy without the partners' legal names and connecting abbreviations (t/a, d/b/a, o/a) leaves it without legal standing to contract.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Under the rules tested on the exam on legal capacity, which statement accurately describes a contract entered into by a minor for an item that is not a necessity of life?

按照考试考试资料关于缔约能力的规则，下列哪项准确描述了未成年人就非生活必需品所订立的合同？

  1. ✓ 正确。按考试考试口径，成年年龄在普通法下是 21 岁（多数省已降到 19 岁或更低），未成年人可以为生活必需品（食物、衣服、住所）订立有效合同，其他合同则保留在合理期限内撤销（Voidable）的权利。（注：考试按考试口径作答；BC 现行《Infants Act》规定未成年人合同对未成年人不可执行，除非成年即 19 岁后一年内追认或未否认，例外清单中无生活必需品一项。）Correct. Under the exam standard, the common law age of majority is 21 (lowered to 19 or less in most provinces); a minor can enter into a binding contract for necessities of life (food, clothing, lodging), while other contracts remain voidable at the minor's option within a reasonable time.   1. ✗ 错误（文件用途混淆陷阱）。常离开原地点的摄影器材用浮动保单（Floater），办公楼保单不保的职业过失需要另立保单（Separate Policy），而不是临时保单（Binder）。Incorrect (document purpose confusion trap). Mobile photography equipment requires a floater, and professional liability excluded under an office policy requires a separate policy, not a binder.

  2. ✗ 错误（顺序与用途混淆陷阱）。流动摄影器材用浮动保单（Floater），而新增完全独立的职业责任险才用另立保单（Separate Policy）。Incorrect (inverted order and document purpose trap). Mobile photography equipment uses a floater, while adding an entirely independent line of professional liability insurance requires a separate policy.

  3. ✗ 错误（文件用途混淆陷阱）。临时保单（Binder）只用于正式出单前的临时保障，不能解决长期流动器材保障或新增独立险种的问题。Incorrect (document purpose confusion trap). A binder is used solely for temporary coverage prior to formal policy issuance, and cannot provide ongoing mobile equipment coverage or establish an independent coverage line.

  4. ✓ 正确。承保流动性大、常离开原地点的财产（如承包商工具、摄影师器材、珠宝）用浮动保单（Floater）；原合同一字不动、另买一份独立险种（如在办公楼保单外另买职业责任险，且不必是同一家保险公司）用另立保单（Separate Policy）。Correct. A floater is used to cover property that frequently moves from location to location (such as contractor tools, photography gear, and jewellery); a separate policy is used to purchase an independent line of coverage while leaving the original contract unaltered (such as buying professional liability in addition to an office building policy, without needing to use the same insurer).
