第 3 章 · 政府与监管Chapter 3 · Government and Regulation of InsuranceThe Role of Government

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 「找例外（EXCEPT / NOT / FALSE）」题型（占比最高）：题干列出省政府职能、重大变更规则、终止退费或出险后义务的四个陈述，要求揪出唯一夹带私货的错误项（例如把「省政府定保费费率」塞进监理官职责，或声称「主动退保无权拿退费」）。Negative elimination questions (EXCEPT / NOT / FALSE, highest proportion): The stem presents four statements regarding provincial government functions, material change rules, termination refunds, or post-loss obligations, requiring identification of the single incorrect statement (such as slipping premium rate-setting into the Superintendent's duties, or claiming an insured has no right to a refund upon voluntary cancellation).
  • 数字与起算点精准匹配题：直接考《Insurance Act》与法定条件（Statutory Conditions）里的硬数字（12:01 a.m.、7 天、15 天与 5 天、60 天、30 天与 45 天、1 年），专门用相近数字或调换起算事件（自收到损失证明 vs 自损失发生）做干扰。Precise statutory number and triggering event matching questions: Directly testing exact statutory numbers in the Insurance Act and Statutory Conditions (12:01 a.m., 7 days, 15 days and 5 days, 60 days, 30 days and 45 days, 1 year), using adjacent numbers or swapped starting events (from receipt of proof of loss vs. after the loss occurs) as distractors.
  • 受托责任（Fiduciary Duty）范围辨析题：考保险人与经纪人代管资金的边界，核心测试「未赚（Unearned）」与「已赚（Earned）」的区分，以及安大略省独立信托账户（Trust Account）的省际例外。Scope of fiduciary duty distinction questions: Testing boundaries of funds held in trust by insurers and brokers, focusing on distinguishing unearned from earned funds, along with Ontario's provincial exception requiring a separate trust account.
  • 火险承保与法定除外（Statutory Exclusions）情景题：给一个家庭或商铺出险的小场景（搬离避险、雷击或电流损坏电器后起火、友火变敌火），测试「只除那件东西本身、引发的火灾照赔」和搬移条款（Removal Clause）的触发前提。Fire coverage and statutory exclusion scenario questions: Presenting residential or commercial loss scenarios (evacuation to avoid peril, electrical breakdown followed by fire, friendly fire versus hostile fire) to test that exclusions apply only to the damaged item itself with ensuing fire covered, and the triggering conditions of the Removal Clause.
  • 法定条件（Statutory Conditions 1–15）实务判定题：围绕不实陈述（Misrepresentation）、重大变更（Material Change，考是否既实质又持续）、索赔欺诈（Fraud，考整单拒赔）及委付或评估规则，测试各方权利与举证责任归属。Statutory Conditions (1 to 15) practical determination questions: Focusing on misrepresentation, material change (testing whether a change is both substantial and continuing), claims fraud (testing that fraud invalidates the entire claim), and rules governing abandonment or appraisal, examining the allocation of rights and burdens of proof.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 省政府职能混入「定价」Confusing Provincial Regulation with Premium Rate Setting

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生知道省保险监理官（Superintendent of Insurance）审批保单条款、发牌并监管省注册公司财务，容易顺手把「制定或核准保费水平」也当成省级法定职能。Candidates know that the provincial Superintendent of Insurance approves policy forms, licenses insurers, and monitors provincial solvency, and easily assume that establishing or approving premium rate levels is also a provincial statutory duty.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问省级政府或 Superintendent 的职责（functions / duties），选项中出现 setting premium levels 或 fixing rates。The question stem asks for the functions or duties of the provincial government or Superintendent, and options mention setting premium levels or fixing rates.

⚠ 把「未赚」偷换成「全部」Replacing 'Unearned' with 'All' Commissions or Premiums

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到受托责任（Fiduciary）和信托持有（held in trust）觉得越严格越对，忽略了经纪人可以用已赚佣金支付日常运营开支。Seeing fiduciary duty and held in trust makes candidates assume the strictest rule is correct, overlooking that brokers may use earned commissions to pay ongoing operating overhead.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 选项出现 all commissions 或 entire premium must be held in trust until policy expiry 等绝对化词。Options contain absolute language such as all commissions or entire premium must be held in trust until policy expiry.

⚠ 起保时间与地址张冠李戴Swapping Inception Time and Location Variables

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 只记住了 12:01 这个数字，没盯住「a.m. 还是 p.m.」以及「被保险人地址、风险所在地还是保险公司总部地址」。Candidates remember the figure 12:01 but fail to distinguish a.m. from p.m., or confuse the named insured's address with the location of the risk or insurer's head office.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问保单何时生效（time a policy takes effect），四个选项排列组合 a.m./p.m. 与不同地点。The question stem asks when a policy takes effect, and options combine variations of a.m./p.m. with different addresses.

⚠ 火险法定除外「连坐」后续火灾Extending Statutory Exclusions to Ensuing Fires

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 记住加热、雷击电器、电流异常是法定除外，却忘了除外只排除「被加热物或受损电器本身」，若随后引燃房屋或周边财物，后续火灾损失依然受保。Candidates remember that application of heat, lightning damage to electrical devices, and electrical surges are excluded, but forget that exclusions apply only to the heated item or damaged device itself; an ensuing fire that spreads to the building or contents remains fully covered.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现电器遭雷击或电流烧毁、熨斗烫坏衣物，紧接着出现 ensuing fire 或 fire spreads to nearby property。The question stem describes appliances damaged by lightning or electrical surge, or clothing scorched by an iron, followed by phrases like ensuing fire or fire spreads to nearby property.

⚠ 法定条件「临时活动算重大变更」与「欺诈只扣虚报项」Treating Temporary Events as Material Change and Deducting Only Fraudulent Items

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉认为只要风险变大就是重大变更（忽略了必须既实质又持续），或以为索赔时多报一件东西只会拒赔多报的那件（实际上故意欺诈会导致整笔索赔作废）。Candidates intuitively assume any increase in hazard constitutes a material change (overlooking that it must be both substantial and continuing), or believe that fraudulently claiming an extra item only forfeits that specific item (whereas deliberate fraud invalidates the entire claim).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述一天或短期的临时活动（one-day event），或索赔清单中故意虚列财物（fraudulently includes an item）。The question stem describes a one-day event or short temporary activity, or an insured who fraudulently includes a fictitious item in a proof of loss.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
联邦与省级监管分工及偿付能力Division of Federal and Provincial Regulation and SolvencySolvency & Superintendent of Insurance联邦（OSFI）监管联邦注册保险公司的财务稳健；省监理官负责审批合同条款、在本省发牌（有联邦牌照仍须领省牌）及监管省注册公司的偿付能力（Solvency，即付清全部承保损失的能力），但省政府不负责制定保费水平。The federal government (OSFI) monitors the financial stability of federally registered insurers; the provincial Superintendent of Insurance supervises policy terms, licenses insurers (a provincial licence is required even if holding a federal licence), and monitors the solvency of provincially chartered insurers (solvency being the ability to pay all insured losses), but the provincial government does not establish premium rates.
PACICC 破产兜底与同业摊派PACICC Insolvency Protection and Industry AssessmentProperty and Casualty Insurance Compensation Corporation (PACICC)保险公司破产时由 PACICC 先行赔付有效索赔，资金按各参与保险公司的直接承保保费总额向同业摊派（Assessment）；考试口径限额为单一事故最高 250,000 加元，未赚保费退还 70%（每单最高 700 加元）。When an insurer becomes insolvent, PACICC pays valid claims first, funded through assessments based on total direct written premiums of participating insurers; under the exam standard, the coverage limit is a maximum of $250,000 per occurrence, with unearned premiums refunded at 70% (up to $700 per policy).
保险人与经纪人的受托责任及安大略例外Fiduciary Duty of Insurers and Brokers and the Ontario ExceptionFiduciary Duty & Unearned Commission保险人须将未赚保费、经纪人须将未赚佣金（Unearned Commission）视为信托持有以备退保返还，经纪人仅可动用已赚佣金经营；多数省不要求单设信托账户（30 至 60 天内汇交保费），唯独安大略省要求收款后 3 个银行工作日内存入独立信托账户。Insurers must hold unearned premiums and brokers must hold unearned commissions in trust to fund refunds if policies are cancelled early, while brokers may only use earned commissions for operating expenses; most provinces do not require a separate trust account (allowing premium remittance within 30 to 60 days), whereas Ontario specifically requires premiums to be deposited into a separate trust account within three banking days of receipt.
保单必载六项与起保时刻Six Mandatory Policy Contents and Inception TimeContents of Insurance Policies & Policy Period保单须载明当事人（第三方不是合同当事人）、保期、受款人、险别与金额、费率与保费、标的；法定起保时刻为被保险人（Named Insured）地址的 12:01 a.m. 标准时间（经纪人有承保权限时自绑定那一刻起保）。Insurance policies must specify the parties to the contract (a third party is not a contracting party), policy period, loss payee, perils insured and amounts, rate and premium, and subject matter; the statutory inception time is 12:01 a.m. Standard Time at the address of the named insured (or the moment coverage is bound when the broker has binding authority).
搬移条款的三个条件Three Conditions of the Removal ClauseRemoval Clause财产因遭受承保危险威胁、为防止损失或进一步损失而必须搬往未列明地点时，自动受保 7 天或保单剩余期（取短者），可用保额须先扣减原地点已发生的损失。When property is necessarily removed to an unnamed location to prevent loss or further damage threatened by an insured peril, coverage is automatically extended for 7 days or the unexpired policy term (whichever is shorter), with the available limit reduced by any loss already incurred at the original named location.
限额警示、代位求偿、弃权与保单交付Limitation Clause Warning, Subrogation, Waiver, and DeliveryLimitation Clause, Subrogation, Waiver & Delivery含免赔额或共保等限额条款的保单须在首页印红墨水警示否则无约束力（考试口径）；保险人赔付后以被保险人名义行使代位求偿（Subrogation）；弃权（Waiver）须由保险人授权之人书面签字；保单一旦交付即使未付保费也约束保险人。A policy containing a limitation clause such as a deductible or coinsurance must have a red-ink warning on its first page or the clause is not binding on the insured (exam standard); after paying a claim, the insurer exercises subrogation rights in the name of the insured; a waiver must be in writing and signed by a person authorized by the insurer; once delivered, a policy binds the insurer even if the premium has not been paid.
火险三项必保、敌意之火与四项法定除外Three Mandatory Fire Perils, Hostile Fire, and Four Statutory ExclusionsFire, Lightning, Explosion & Statutory Exclusions火险保单必保火（仅限跑出容器的敌意之火 Hostile Fire）、雷电及天然气／煤气／人造气爆炸；四项法定除外为直接加热、雷击电器、其他电流异常及放射性污染，但前三项只除外受损物品本身，由此引发的火灾照赔。Policies covering fire must insure against fire (limited to hostile fire that has escaped its proper container), lightning, and explosion of natural, coal, or manufactured gas; four statutory exclusions are application of heat, lightning damage to electrical devices, other electrical surges, and radioactive contamination, though the first three exclude only the damaged article itself with any ensuing fire loss covered.
法定条件 1 与 4：不实陈述与重大变更Statutory Conditions 1 and 4: Misrepresentation and Material ChangeMisrepresentation & Material Change对重要事实的不实陈述或欺诈性遗漏使相关财产保障无效（举证责任在保险人）；重大变更须在被保险人控制与知情下发生且既实质又持续（一天的临时活动不算），告知后若保险人要求加费，被保险人须在 15 天内补缴。Misrepresentation or fraudulent omission of a material fact renders coverage on the affected property void (burden of proof is on the insurer); a material change must be within the control and knowledge of the insured and be both substantial and continuing (a one-day temporary event does not qualify), and if the insurer requests an additional premium upon notification, the insured must pay within 15 days.
法定条件 5 与 7：合同终止退费与索赔欺诈Statutory Conditions 5 and 7: Policy Termination and Claims FraudTermination & Fraud保险人终止须提前挂号信 15 天（自到达收件邮局次日起算）或当面书面通知 5 天并按比例（Pro Rata）退费；被保险人可随时终止并按短期费率（Short Rate）退费；索赔中若证明故意欺诈（Fraud），整笔索赔作废而非仅拒赔虚报部分。An insurer may terminate by giving 15 days' notice by registered mail (commencing the day following arrival at the post office of destination) or 5 days' written notice delivered personally, with a pro rata refund; the insured may terminate at any time with refund calculated at short rate; if deliberate fraud is proven in a proof of loss, the entire claim is invalidated rather than merely the falsely claimed portion.
法定条件 9 至 14：施救、委付、评估与理赔时限Statutory Conditions 9 to 14: Salvage, Abandonment, Appraisal, and Claim TimelinesSalvage, Abandonment, Appraisal & Action保险人按比例分担合理施救（Salvage）费用；未经保险人同意不得委付（Abandonment）财产；评估（Appraisal）只定价值不定责任；自收到损失证明起 60 天内付款（选择修复重置须 30 天内书面通知、45 天内开工）；诉讼须在损失发生后 1 年内提起（考试口径）。The insurer contributes pro rata to reasonable salvage expenses; property cannot be abandoned to the insurer without its consent; appraisal determines only valuation and not policy liability; claim payment is due within 60 days after completion of proof of loss (written notice of intention to repair or replace within 30 days and work commenced within 45 days of receipt of proof of loss); lawsuits must be commenced within 1 year after the loss occurs (exam standard).

第 1 题Q1省级监管职能与偿付能力Provincial regulatory functions and insurer solvency★陷阱：省政府职能混入「定价」

Which of the following activities is NOT a responsibility of the provincial Superintendent of Insurance when regulating general insurers?

在监管普通保险公司时，下列哪一项活动不是省保险监理官（Superintendent of Insurance）的职责？

  1. ✗ 属于省级职责。省保险监理官（Superintendent of Insurance）负责执行《Insurance Act》，其中第一项就是监督并批准私营保险公司在本省销售的保险合同条款（Supervising terms and conditions of insurance contracts）。Incorrect. This is a provincial responsibility. The provincial Superintendent of Insurance enforces the Insurance Act, which includes supervising and approving the terms and conditions of insurance contracts sold by private insurers in the province.

  2. ✓ 正确（即不属于省级职责）。省政府的监管职能包括监督合同条款、发放本省营业牌照、监控省注册保险公司的财务稳健与偿付能力（Solvency），但不为私营保险合同制定或统一保费水平。注：考试口径称省级监管者为 Superintendent of Insurance，BC 现行监管机构为 BCFSA。Correct (not a provincial duty). Provincial regulatory responsibilities include supervising policy terms, licensing insurers to transact business, and monitoring the financial stability and solvency of provincially chartered companies, but the province does not set or standardize premium rate levels for private insurance policies. (Note: The exam standard designates the provincial regulator as the Superintendent of Insurance; the current regulator in BC is BCFSA.)

  3. ✗ 属于省级职责。保险公司在哪一个省开展业务，就必须取得该省的牌照（Licensing），即使它已经持有联邦牌照也不例外。Incorrect. This is a provincial responsibility. An insurer must obtain a provincial licence in each province where it transacts business, even if it already holds a federal licence.

  4. ✗ 属于省级职责。联邦注册公司的财务归联邦金融机构监理署（OSFI）监控，而按省章程成立的保险公司，其财务稳健和偿付能力由省监理官负责监控。Incorrect. This is a provincial responsibility. While federally registered insurers are monitored financially by the federal regulator (OSFI), the financial stability and solvency of provincially chartered insurers are monitored by the provincial Superintendent of Insurance.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A newly incorporated property insurer has obtained its federal licence after meeting the strict financial standards of OSFI. Before this insurer can legally sell property policies to residents in a specific province, what else is required and how is its solvency regulated?

一家新成立的财产保险公司达到了联邦金融机构监理署（OSFI）的严格财务标准并取得了联邦牌照。在该保险公司合法向某省居民销售财产保单之前，还需要满足什么要求？其偿付能力如何受监管？

  1. ✗ 错在「无需省牌照」。讲义明确指出，在哪个省做业务就要在哪个省领牌，即使已经有联邦牌照（Federal Licence）也必须取得该省牌照。Incorrect (unlicensed transacting trap). An insurer must obtain a provincial licence in every province where it conducts business, even if it already holds a federal licence.

  2. ✗ 掉入「省政府定价」陷阱。省保险监理官负责监督合同条款和发牌，不为私营财产险设定保费水平。Incorrect (government rate-setting trap). The provincial Superintendent of Insurance supervises policy forms and issues licences, but does not establish premium rates for private property insurance.

  3. ✓ 正确。联邦注册的保险公司由联邦金融机构监理署（OSFI）持续监控其财务稳健与偿付能力（Solvency），但进入任何一个省营业时，仍必须向该省保险监理官申领省级牌照。Correct. Federally registered insurers have their financial stability and solvency monitored continuously by the federal regulator (OSFI), but must still apply for and obtain a provincial licence from the Superintendent of Insurance in each province where they operate.

  4. ✗ 混淆了偿付能力监管与破产兜底机制。财产与意外险赔偿公司（PACICC）是在同业破产时先垫付有效索赔、再按直接承保保费向同业摊派（Assessment），不需要事前把全部保费存入 PACICC。Incorrect (confusing solvency monitoring with compensation fund mechanism). The Property and Casualty Insurance Compensation Corporation (PACICC) advances payments for valid claims when an insurer becomes insolvent and recovers funds through post-loss assessments on participating insurers based on direct written premiums; insurers do not deposit entire premium revenues with PACICC in advance.

第 2 题Q2经纪人与保险人的受托责任Fiduciary duty of brokers and insurers★★陷阱：把「未赚」偷换成「全部」

How does the fiduciary obligation apply to the commission retained by an insurance brokerage when a client pays an annual premium?

当客户支付一年期保费时，受托责任（Fiduciary Obligation）如何适用于保险经纪行留存的佣金？

  1. ✓ 正确。经纪人作为受托人（Fiduciary），佣金要到保单期满才算完全赚到；只能用已赚佣金（Earned Commission）支付日常经营开支，未赚佣金（Unearned Commission）必须视为信托持有，以备保单提前取消时退还。Correct. As fiduciaries, brokers do not fully earn commissions until the policy period has expired; they may only use earned commissions to pay ongoing operating expenses, while unearned commissions must be held in trust to remain available for refund if the policy is cancelled early.

  2. ✗ 掉入「把未赚偷换成全部」陷阱。随着保单时间推移，按月或按日已经赚到的那部分佣金（Earned Commission）经纪行可以用于发工资和运营，只有未赚部分才视为信托持有。Incorrect (replacing unearned with all trap). As policy time elapses, the portion of commission that has become earned may be used by the brokerage for payroll and operating overhead; only the unearned portion must be held in trust.

  3. ✗ 错在「必须按原费率维持到续保」。重大变更大幅增加了损失可能性，保险人有权取消或要求在 15 天内补交加费。Incorrect. A material change substantially increases the risk of loss, and the insurer is not obligated to maintain coverage at original residential rates; it may cancel or require an additional premium within 15 days.

  4. ✗ 违反法定条件不可修改原则。法定条件（Statutory Conditions）对合同双方均有约束力，任何改动、删减或增加对被保险人都无效，即使得到被保险人同意也不能改。Incorrect (violating non-alteration of statutory conditions). Statutory Conditions are binding on both parties by law, and any alteration, omission, or addition is void against the insured, even if agreed to in writing by the policyholder.

第 7 题Q7法定条件6至15：理赔、欺诈与时效Statutory Conditions 6 to 15: Claims handling, fraud, and limitation periods★★★陷阱：法定条件「临时活动算重大变更」与「欺诈只扣虚报项」

Following an insured fire loss, which statement is TRUE under Statutory Conditions 6 through 15?

在发生受保火灾损失后，根据法定条件（Statutory Conditions）第 6 至 15 条，下列哪项陈述是正确的？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据法定条件 7（欺诈 Fraud），欺诈是指为谋取利益而故意欺骗；一旦保险人证明被保险人在索赔中有故意欺诈行为，可以拒赔整个索赔（Invalidate the entire claim），而不只是扣掉虚报的那一台笔记本电脑。Correct. Under Statutory Condition 7 (Fraud), fraud involves intentional deception for gain; once the insurer proves that the insured acted fraudulently in making a claim, it may invalidate the entire claim rather than simply deducting the fictitious laptop.

  2. ✗ 违反法定条件 10（进入、控制、委付 Entry, Control, Abandonment）。未经保险人同意，被保险人不能把受损财产委付（Abandon）给保险人。Incorrect. Under Statutory Condition 10 (Entry, Control, Abandonment), the insured cannot abandon damaged property to the insurer without the insurer's consent.

  3. ✗ 混淆了天数与起算点。根据法定条件 13（修复重置 Replacement），保险人选择修复或重建的，必须在「收到损失证明（Proof of Loss）后 30 天内」发出书面通知，并在收到损失证明后 45 天内开工；60 天是法定条件 12 的付款期限。Incorrect (confusing days and triggers). Under the exam standard (Statutory Condition 14, Action), any legal action against the insurer must be commenced within 1 year after the loss occurs (2 years in Manitoba and Saskatchewan). (Note: Current BC legislation relocated limitation of actions to s.23 with a 2-year period from discovery, but exam answers must use 1 year after the loss occurs.)

After a warehouse fire, the insured submits a verified proof of loss on May 1. Under the exam rules for the Statutory Conditions, what are the statutory deadlines for payment, election to repair, and starting legal action?

仓库失火后，被保险人于 5 月 1 日提交了经核实的损失证明（Proof of Loss）。按法定条件（Statutory Conditions）的考试口径，付款、选择修复重置以及提起诉讼的法定期限分别是什么？

  1. ✗ 把所有天数和起算点都搞混了。付款是损失证明完成后 60 天内，通知修复是收到损失证明后 30 天内（45 天内开工），诉讼是自损失发生后 1 年内（考试口径）。Incorrect. All days and starting points are confused. Claim payment is due within 60 days after completion of proof of loss; notice of intent to repair is within 30 days of receipt of proof of loss (work commenced within 45 days); and lawsuits must be commenced within 1 year after the loss occurs (exam standard).

  2. ✗ 把 45 天（开工期限）和 60 天（付款期限）调换了位置，且修复通知、开工期限与 6 个月诉讼时效均错。Incorrect. This transposes the 45-day work commencement and 60-day payment deadlines, while distorting notice of repair and inventing a 6-month lawsuit limitation.

  3. ✗ 错在「15 天通知修复」和「自损失证明起算 1 年」。通知修复是收到损失证明后 30 天内，而 1 年诉讼时效是从「损失发生之日（after the loss occurs）」起算，不是从交损失证明起算。Incorrect. Notice of intention to repair is required within 30 days (not 15 days) of receipt of proof of loss, and the 1-year lawsuit limitation runs from the date the loss occurs rather than delivery of proof of loss.

  4. ✓ 正确。把条件 12、13、14 的四个核心数字和两个起算点全部对齐：① 付款：损失证明（Proof of Loss）完成后 60 天内；② 修复重置（Replacement）：收到损失证明后 30 天内书面通知意图、45 天内开工；③ 诉讼（Action）：考试口径下须在损失发生后 1 年内提起（MB、SK 为 2 年）。注：BC 现行《Insurance Act》将财产险诉讼时效改为自知道或应当知道损失发生起 2 年，且法定条件现行只剩 14 条，但考场答题一律按 15 条及 1 年时效作答。Correct. This accurately aligns the four statutory numbers and triggering events in Conditions 12, 13, and 14: 1) payment is due within 60 days after completion of proof of loss; 2) replacement requires written notice of intention within 30 days and work commenced within 45 days of receiving proof of loss; 3) action against the insurer must be commenced within 1 year after the loss occurs (2 years in MB and SK). (Note: BC current practice sets a 2-year limitation from discovery across 14 statutory conditions, but exam questions must be answered using 15 conditions and 1 year after the loss.)
