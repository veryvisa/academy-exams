第 6 章 · 住宅保险Chapter 6 · Habitational Insurance (Homeowners, Condominium, Tenants, and Related Forms)Habitational Insurance

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 情景理赔判断（赔不赔）：给出具体出险经过（如壁炉冒烟、拔掉冰柜插头、分居配偶失火、带营业电脑去外地出差被盗），测试对承保危险附加条件与除外条款的精准识别。Scenario-based claim coverage determinations (covered vs. not covered): presenting specific loss scenarios (such as fireplace smoke, unplugging a freezer, fire damage involving a separated spouse, or theft of a business laptop while traveling), testing precise identification of insured perils, qualifying conditions, and policy exclusions.
  • 找例外题（EXCEPT / NOT covered）：列出四个看似合理的保障陈述（如钥匙丢失或被盗换锁、房客计入被保险人、共管单元表承保单元购买价），专考条文里的一字之差。Identifying exceptions (EXCEPT / NOT covered questions): presenting four plausible coverage statements (such as lock replacement for lost or stolen keys, counting boarders as insureds, or condominium unit forms covering the unit purchase price), specifically testing subtle wording distinctions within policy provisions.
  • 特别限额与免赔额计算：区分「不论原因（all losses）」与「仅限被盗（theft only）」两类特别限额，以及「先扣免赔额、再套特别限额」和五项不扣免赔额扩展的算账规则。Special limits and deductible calculations: distinguishing between the two categories of special limits—those applying to all losses versus those applying to theft only—and applying the accounting rules where the deductible is deducted before the special limit is applied, as well as identifying the five extensions of coverage subject to no deductible.
  • 时钟与数字边界题：围绕空置两只时钟（当刻停 3 险 vs 超过 30 天全停）、冬季离家超过 4 个连续日的冻结条件、180 天重置补差期、民政当局禁入 2 周等硬数字设题。Timelines and numerical thresholds: testing exact numerical limits surrounding the two vacancy clocks (immediate suspension of three perils versus total suspension after 30 consecutive days), the freezing condition requiring daily checks when away for more than 4 consecutive days in winter, the 180-day replacement cost settlement window, and the two-week civil authority prohibited access extension.
  • 表型横向对比（1151 vs 1153 vs 1155 及共管表）：考查 1153 只把建筑升级为一切险而个人财产仍为列名险、1155 建筑与财产双升级，以及共管业主损失分摊与单元改良的触发前提。Cross-comparison of policy forms (Form 1151 vs. Form 1153 vs. Form 1155 and condominium forms): testing how Form 1153 upgrades only the dwelling to all risks while personal property remains named perils, how Form 1155 upgrades both building and personal property to all risks, and the specific triggers for loss assessment and unit improvements under condominium unit owner forms.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 特别限额错把「仅被盗」当「不论原因」Mistaking Theft-Only Special Limits for All-Loss Limits

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生死记了珠宝皮草 $2,000、自行车 $500 等金额，却忽略了右栏五项限额只在被盗（theft）时触发，火灾等其他承保危险全额赔付；反之营业财产只在处所上保且不论原因封顶 $2,000。Candidates memorize limit dollar amounts such as $2,000 for jewellery and furs or $500 for bicycles, but overlook that these five limits apply only when the loss is caused by theft, whereas losses from fire and other insured perils are paid in full; conversely, business property is covered only on the premises and is capped at $2,000 for all insured perils.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现皮草、珠宝、钱币、邮票、收藏卡、自行车受损时，先看受损原因是「火灾/风暴」还是「被盗」；出现营业工具或电脑时，先看是在「处所内」还是「带出门」。When question stems mention damaged furs, jewellery, numismatic property, stamps, collector cards, or bicycles, first check whether the peril is fire/windstorm or theft; when business tools or laptops appear, first check whether they were on the premises or taken off the premises.

⚠ 扩展保障的条件刺与免赔额混淆Confusing Extension Conditions and Deductible Exceptions

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 容易把「钥匙丢失」当成换锁扩展承保范围，把「宠物碰掉插头或跳闸」当成冰柜食品承保原因，或把温差扩展当成炉子自然故障也赔，且常搞错哪五项扩展不扣免赔额。Candidates easily mistake lost keys for covered lock replacement, treat a tripped breaker or a pet unplugging a cord as covered freezer food spoilage, or assume change of temperature covers ordinary furnace breakdowns, while also confusing which five extensions are exempt from the policy deductible.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现钥匙（看是 lost 还是 stolen）、冰柜化冻（看是机械故障/意外停电还是拔插头/跳闸）、室内植物冻死（看有无承保危险先造成建筑或设备实体损坏）。In question stems mentioning keys, check whether they were lost or stolen; for freezer thawing, check whether caused by off-premises power interruption/mechanical breakdown or local unplugging/tripped breakers; for frozen indoor plants, check whether an insured peril first caused physical damage to the building or heating equipment.

⚠ 1151 十三项列名险的隐含前提缺失Overlooking Prerequisites and Internal Exclusions in Form 1151 Named Perils

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 只背了十三项危险的名字（烟、坠落物、撞击、风暴、水损、玻璃），没记清每一项成立的前置动作或内部除外。Candidates memorize the names of the thirteen named perils (smoke, falling object, vehicle impact, windstorm, water damage, glass breakage) but forget the strict qualifying conditions or internal exclusions embedded in each peril.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 看到 fireplace（壁炉烟不保）、tree branch hitting patio furniture（没先砸建筑外部）、insured driving own car（自家车撞不保）、rain through open window（风暴未先造成建筑开口）。Watch for fireplace smoke (excluded under smoke), a tree branch hitting patio furniture (falling object must first strike the exterior of the building), an insured driving their own vehicle (impact excludes vehicles owned or driven by an insured or employee), or rain entering through an open window (windstorm requires storm damage to first create an opening in the roof or walls).

⚠ 空置（vacant）两只时钟与冬季离家 4 天混用Confusing the Two Vacancy Clocks with the Four-Day Winter Absence Rule

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为留有家具就不算空置，或以为所有危险都是空置满 30 天才停保；同时常把冬季冻结的「超过 4 个连续日」记成必须事先通知保险公司。Candidates mistakenly assume leaving furniture behind prevents a dwelling from being vacant, or assume that all perils continue until vacancy exceeds 30 consecutive days; candidates also frequently assume that winter unoccupancy of more than 4 consecutive days requires prior notification to the insurer.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写明住户迁出无意返回（哪怕留着家具）即触发空置：破坏、水损、玻璃破碎当刻停保，超过 30 天全部停保；题干写冬季度假离家看是否超过 4 个连续日及有无每日查看或排空水管。When occupants move out with no intent to return (even if furnishings remain), vacancy is triggered: vandalism, water damage, and glass breakage cease immediately, while all coverage ceases once vacant for more than 30 consecutive days; for winter vacations, check whether the absence exceeds 4 consecutive days and whether daily heating checks or draining of water supplies were performed.

⚠ 1153 阔表承保范围与共管摊派条件张冠李戴Misapplying Form 1153 Broad Form Coverage and Condominium Assessment Triggers

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为 1153 Broad Form 把个人财产也升级成一切险，或误以为共管业主自己的免赔额过高可以触发损失分摊（loss assessment）保障。Candidates mistakenly believe Form 1153 Broad Form upgrades personal property to all risks, or assume that a unit owner's own high deductible can trigger Loss Assessment coverage under a condominium policy.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 看到 1153 Broad Form 先区分受损的是建筑（A/B，一切险）还是个人财产（C，仍是列名险）；看到共管法团摊派，核对摊派原因是否属于法团保单不足且损失原因受业主自己保单承保。Under Form 1153 Broad Form, distinguish whether damaged property is building (Coverages A and B, all risks) or personal property (Coverage C, remains named perils); for condominium corporation assessments, check whether the assessment results from a shortfall in the corporation's master policy and whether the underlying peril is covered by the unit owner's own policy.

第 1 题Q1被保险人定义与同住条件Definition of the Insured and Cohabitation Requirements★★陷阱：忽略非具名被保险人出险时必须同住的硬条件

Marcus is the sole named insured on an IBC Homeowners Form. Which of the following individuals would have their personal property covered under Marcus's policy at the time of a fire loss?

Marcus 是 IBC 家主表上唯一的具名被保险人。在发生火灾损失时，下列哪一位的个人财产会受到 Marcus 保单的承保？

  1. ✗ 非具名被保险人（the Insured）必须在出险时与具名人同住（living in the same household）。分居并已搬出五个月的妻子在出险时不同住，若未列为具名被保险人，保单不承保其财产。Incorrect. An unnamed insured must be living in the same household with the named insured at the time of loss. A spouse who separated and moved out five months earlier is not living in the household at the time of loss, so unless specifically added as a named insured, her personal property is not covered.

  2. ✓ 在读学生（students）即使超过 21 岁且暂住校外，只要是全日制（full-time）并受被保险人供养（dependent on the Named Insured for support），仍属于被保险人，其个人财产受保。Correct. While backing out of his driveway, an insured accidentally hits his attached garage door and also crushes his own bicycle that was leaning against the inside wall of the garage. Under an IBC 1153 Broad Form, which part of this loss is covered?

被保险人在倒出自家车道时，意外撞坏了附着车库的门，同时压扁了靠在车库内墙上的自家自行车。在 IBC 1153 阔表（Broad Form）下，这次损失的哪一部分受保？

  1. ✗ 落入 1153 范围陷阱：1153 Broad Form 只把住宅与附属建筑（Coverage A & B）升级为一切险（All Risks），个人财产（Coverage C）仍然是十三项列名险；把两者都升为一切险的是 1155 Comprehensive Form。Incorrect. Falls into the Form 1153 scope trap: Form 1153 Broad Form upgrades only the dwelling building and detached private structures (Coverages A and B) to all risks, while personal property (Coverage C) remains named perils; it is Form 1155 Comprehensive Form that upgrades both to all risks.

  2. ✗ 「被保险人拥有或驾驶的车辆撞击除外」是十三项列名险（Named Perils）里的限制；在 1153 下，建筑（A、B）已升级为一切险，不再受该列名险条件限制，因此车库门获赔。Incorrect. The exclusion of impact by a vehicle owned or operated by an insured applies to named perils; under Form 1153, the building is upgraded to all risks and is no longer subject to that named peril restriction, so the garage door is covered.

  3. ✓ 1153 阔表（Broad Form）对住宅建筑（Coverage A）按一切险（All Risks）承保，故自家车撞坏车库门可赔；但对个人财产（Coverage C）仍按十三项列名险承保，而车辆撞击险（impact by land vehicle）明确除外被保险人或其雇员拥有或驾驶的车辆，故自行车不赔。Correct. Form 1153 Broad Form covers the dwelling building (Coverage A) on an all risks basis, so damage to the garage door from the insured's own vehicle is covered; however, personal property (Coverage C) remains on a named perils basis, where vehicle impact explicitly excludes vehicles owned or operated by an insured or employee, so the bicycle is not covered.

  4. ✗ 自行车 $500 的特别限额仅适用于被盗（theft），且 1153 下个人财产因自家车撞击根本不属于承保危险；反而是建筑车库门在一切险下受保。Incorrect. The $500 special limit on bicycles applies only to theft, and under Form 1153 personal property damaged by an insured's own vehicle is not an insured peril; conversely, the garage door is covered under all risks.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Smoke puffs back from a wood-burning living room fireplace, staining the living room sofa and curtains. Under which IBC Homeowners form(s) would the damage to the sofa and curtains be covered?

客厅烧木柴的壁炉倒灌冒烟，熏黑了客厅沙发和窗帘。在哪一张（或哪几张）IBC 家主表下，沙发和窗帘的损坏会得到赔付？

  1. ✓ 沙发和窗帘属于个人财产（Coverage C）。在列名险中，烟害（smoke）明确除外壁炉冒烟（smoke from a fireplace）；1151 与 1153 的个人财产均按十三项列名险承保，只有 1155 Comprehensive Form 将个人财产升级为一切险（All Risks），从而承保壁炉烟害。Correct. Sofas and curtains are personal property (Coverage C). Under named perils, smoke explicitly excludes smoke from a fireplace; because personal property under both Form 1151 and Form 1153 is insured on a named perils basis, only Form 1155 Comprehensive Form upgrades personal property to all risks, covering fireplace smoke.

  2. ✗ 1153 Broad Form 仅把建筑（A、B）升级为一切险，室内沙发和窗帘属于个人财产（C），在 1153 下仍适用十三项列名险，壁炉烟不赔。Incorrect. Form 1153 Broad Form upgrades only buildings (Coverages A and B) to all risks; indoor furniture and curtains are personal property (Coverage C), which remains on a named perils basis under Form 1153 where fireplace smoke is excluded.

  3. ✗ 虽然烟（smoke）是十三项列名险之一，但条文限定为供暖或炊事设备突发、异常、故障运行冒烟，并明确除外壁炉（fireplace）冒烟。Incorrect. Although smoke is one of the thirteen named perils, the wording requires sudden, unusual, and faulty operation of a heating or cooking unit, and explicitly excludes smoke from a fireplace.

  4. ✗ 1155 综合表（Comprehensive Form）下的一切险并未将壁炉冒烟列为除外责任，这正是讲义指出的 1155 典型「多赔」好处之一。Incorrect. All risks under Form 1155 Comprehensive Form does not exclude smoke from a fireplace, which is one of the primary coverage advantages of Form 1155.

答案Answer：a

第 7 题Q7共管单元业主表的损失分摊与改良Condominium Unit Owners Form Loss Assessment and Unit Improvements★★陷阱：1153 阔表承保范围与共管摊派条件张冠李戴

Under the Condominium Unit Owners Form, Loss Assessment coverage responds when the Condominium Corporation assesses unit owners for a shortfall in the corporation's insurance on collectively owned property. Which of the following is NOT a valid trigger for a Loss Assessment claim under the unit owner's policy?

在共管单元业主表（Condominium Unit Owners Form）下，当共管法团因共有财产保单赔付不足而向单元业主摊派差额时，损失分摊（Loss Assessment）保障会作出赔付。下列哪一项不是单元业主保单下损失分摊索赔的有效触发原因？

  1. ✗ 法团保单保额不足（inadequate insurance limits）是触发损失分摊（loss assessment）的五种标准原因之一，且火灾属于业主保单承保危险。Incorrect. Inadequate insurance limits on the condominium corporation's master policy is one of five standard triggers for Loss Assessment, and fire is a peril insured under the unit owner's policy.

  2. ✗ 法团保单因共保罚则（coinsurance penalty）出现赔付缺口向业主摊派，且风暴属业主保单承保危险，属于损失分摊保障范围。Incorrect. An assessment resulting from a coinsurance penalty shortfall on the corporation's master policy where the windstorm loss is covered under the unit owner's policy is a valid trigger for Loss Assessment coverage.

  3. ✓ 触发损失分摊的五种原因之一是「共管法团保单的大额免赔额（large deductible on the corporation's policy）」，而不是单元业主自己保单的免赔额。（注：本题按考试口径作答；在 BC 现行《Strata Property Act》实务中称为 strata corporation，法团免赔额原则上由全体分摊但可向造成损失的业主追偿。）Correct. One of the five valid triggers for Loss Assessment is a large deductible on the condominium corporation's policy, not a deductible on the unit owner's personal policy. (Note: Answered according to exam standard; in current BC Strata Property Act practice, the entity is a strata corporation and deductibles may be charged back to an owner responsible for the loss.)

  4. ✗ 法团因违反保单条件（breach of policy conditions）导致保单拒赔而向业主摊派，只要该爆炸损失本身属于业主保单承保的危险，损失分摊保障即予赔付。Incorrect. An assessment resulting from claim denial due to a breach of policy conditions by the corporation is covered under Loss Assessment, provided the explosion itself is a peril insured under the unit owner's policy.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A condominium unit owner spent $15,000 upgrading the original builder-grade carpet in his unit to hardwood flooring. A burst water pipe destroys the flooring. How is coverage arranged between the Condominium Corporation's policy and the unit owner's Condominium Unit Owners Form?

一位共管单元业主花费 $15,000 将单元内开发商原装的地毯升级为硬木地板。一根爆裂的水管毁坏了地板。共管法团保单与业主的共管单元业主表之间如何划分保障？

  1. ✗ 共管法团（Condominium Corporation）的保单只保开发商原装标准配置（original installations），不赔业主后来自己做的改良所增加的价值。Incorrect. The Condominium Corporation's master policy covers only the developer's original standard installations and does not cover the value added by subsequent owner improvements.

  2. ✓ 原装地毯在共管法团保单里，而业主后来改装硬木地板增加的价值由业主自己的共管单元业主表中的「单元改良（Unit Improvements and Betterments）」按重置成本（replacement cost）承保（基本限额 $1,000，常见为 C 的 25%）。（注：本题按考试口径作答；BC 现行《Strata Property Act》s.149/s.161 规定分契法团必须保原装固定装置，业主自保后期改良。）Correct. The original builder-grade carpet is covered by the Condominium Corporation's policy, while the value added by the hardwood flooring upgrade is covered under Unit Improvements and Betterments in the unit owner's policy on a replacement cost basis (basic limit $1,000, commonly increased to 25% of Coverage C). (Note: Answered according to exam standard; BC Strata Property Act ss. 149/161 requires strata corporations to insure original fixtures, leaving subsequent improvements to owners.)

  3. ✗ 共管单元业主表（Condominium Unit Owners Form）不设住宅建筑 Coverage A，也不承保单元的购买价（does not cover the purchase price of the unit），楼体结构由法团保单承保。Incorrect. The Condominium Unit Owners Form does not provide standard Coverage A for the building and does not cover the purchase price of the unit; building structure is insured under the corporation's master policy.

  4. ✗ 损失分摊（loss assessment）保的是共有财产损失向全体业主的摊派，而非业主自己单元内的装修改良；且单元改良是按重置成本（replacement cost）而非 ACV 赔付。Incorrect. Loss Assessment covers assessments against all unit owners for collective property shortfalls, not private renovations inside an owner's unit; furthermore, unit improvements are settled on a replacement cost basis rather than ACV.
