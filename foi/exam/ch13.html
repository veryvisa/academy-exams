第 13 章 · Autoplan 基本保障与自车损失Chapter 13 · Autoplan Basic Coverage and Own-Damage CoverageBasic Autoplan & Own Damage

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 险种配对情景题：给出一起具体的交通事故或财产受损经过（如撞上已倒在路面的树、撞到野生动物、围栏被逃逸车辆撞坏、部分有责的两车碰撞），直接考哪一项基本险或可选自车损失险负责赔付。Coverage matching scenario questions: Describing a specific motor vehicle accident or property damage scenario (such as striking a tree already fallen on the road, hitting wildlife, a fence damaged by a hit-and-run driver, or a two-vehicle collision with shared fault), testing directly which Basic coverage or optional own-damage coverage pays.
  • EAB 给付规则与计算题：围绕加强意外给付（Enhanced Accident Benefits）的九项内容出题，高频考查收入替代（Income Replacement）的 90% 净收入比例、第 8 天起付、排在其他工资损失计划之后，以及照护者给付、永久伤残和死亡给付的适用条件。EAB benefit rules and calculation questions: Formulating questions around the nine categories of Enhanced Accident Benefits, frequently testing Income Replacement rules (90% of net income, starting on the 8th day, paying after other wage-loss plans) and qualifying conditions for Caregiver Benefits, Permanent Impairment, and Death Benefits.
  • 诉讼限制例外与责任限额题：考查 BC 禁止车祸人伤与车损诉讼的大原则下，哪六类情形仍可提起诉讼并触发第三方责任（Third-Party Liability），以及一般车 $200,000、重型商用车等 $1 million、危险品运输车 $2 million 的法定限额区分。Lawsuit limitation exceptions and liability limit questions: Testing which six situations allow lawsuits triggering Third-Party Liability under BC's general prohibition against motor vehicle injury and vehicle damage lawsuits, distinguishing the statutory limits of $200,000 for standard vehicles, $1,000,000 for heavy commercial vehicles, and $2,000,000 for dangerous goods carriers.
  • 双条件与正误辨析题：针对保额不足驾驶人保障（UMP）的两个同时成立条件、反向责任（Inverse Liability）的两种触发情形、基本车损（BVD）非公路事故的“两辆持牌标准车”要求，用“only”或偷换条件的方式设问。Dual-condition and true-or-false analysis questions: Testing the two concurrent conditions for Underinsured Motorist Protection (UMP), the two trigger scenarios for Inverse Liability, and the requirement of two licensed standard vehicles for Basic Vehicle Damage (BVD) off-highway crashes, using 'only' or swapped conditions to create distractors.
  • 承保范围与除外责任排查题：考查修车行与泊车人员即使获车主许可也不受车主基本险保障（需另购 garage policy）、加美及往返港口船只的地域边界（不含墨西哥），以及自车损失险对车内物品、轮胎、家人或雇员偷车的除外规定。Coverage scope and exclusion screening questions: Testing that auto repair and valet personnel are excluded from the owner's Basic coverage even with permission (requiring a garage policy), territorial limits covering Canada, the United States, and vessels between their ports (excluding Mexico), and own-damage exclusions for contents, tires, and theft by family or employees.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 碰撞险与综合险边界混淆Confusing boundaries between Collision and Comprehensive

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生容易把凡是和天气、树木、动物有关的损失都归入综合险（Comprehensive），忽略了“撞上已经倒在路面上的树或石头、压过坑洞、被逃逸车辆撞坏”属于碰撞险（Collision），而“撞动物、风挡玻璃受损”才归综合险。Candidates tend to categorize any loss related to weather, trees, or animals under Comprehensive, overlooking that striking a tree or rock already lying on the roadway, driving into a pothole, or being damaged by a hit-and-run vehicle fall under Collision, whereas hitting an animal or windshield damage falls under Comprehensive.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 fallen tree on the road、pothole、guardrail、hit-and-run damaging the insured vehicle，或 animal、windshield、hail、vandalism 等具体致损物。The question stem mentions fallen tree on the road, pothole, guardrail, hit-and-run damaging the insured vehicle, or specific damage perils like animal, windshield, hail, or vandalism.

⚠ BVD全额赔付或全有全无误区All-or-nothing or full-payout misconception for Basic Vehicle Damage

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 部分考生受改制前旧观念影响认为基本险不保自车，或误以为只要自己有一点责任基本车损（BVD）就完全不赔；实际上 BVD 按投保人“不负责任的比例”赔付，自己负责的比例及对应比例的免赔额由可选碰撞险承担。Some candidates hold outdated pre-reform beliefs that Basic insurance never covers the insured vehicle, or mistakenly believe that carrying any fault completely forfeits Basic Vehicle Damage (BVD); in reality, BVD pays according to the percentage the insured is not responsible for, while the at-fault share and proportional deductible are covered by optional Collision.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出车损总额及责任划分百分比（如 20% at fault / 80% not at fault），或出现 off-highway 非公路碰撞中涉及山地车、拖拉机等非标准车辆。The question stem states total vehicle damage alongside fault percentages (such as 20% at fault / 80% not at fault), or describes an off-highway collision involving non-standard vehicles such as mountain bikes or tractors.

⚠ UMP与法定无保险条款张冠李戴Conflating UMP with the Statutory Uninsured Motorist Provision

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常把 UMP（最高 $1 million/人，管无 EAB 资格且对方责任险不足的人伤）与法定无保险/身份不明驾驶人条款（最高 $200,000，管 BC 公路上非车辆财产受损，逃逸免赔额 $750）混为一谈，甚至误以为法定条款会赔被逃逸司机撞坏的自车。Candidates frequently confuse UMP (up to $1,000,000 per person, covering bodily injury for those ineligible for EAB when the at-fault driver has inadequate liability limits) with the Statutory Uninsured/Unidentified Motorist Provision (up to $200,000, covering non-vehicle property damaged on BC highways, with a $750 hit-and-run deductible), or incorrectly assume the statutory provision repairs the insured's own hit-and-run vehicle.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干区分 injured person without EAB 还是 non-vehicle property（如 fence、building、bicycle）damaged by an unidentified or uninsured driver。The question stem distinguishes between an injured person without EAB versus non-vehicle property (such as a fence, building, or bicycle) damaged by an unidentified or uninsured driver.

⚠ EAB收入替代基数与顺位陷阱EAB Income Replacement calculation base and payment order trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 题目常把收入替代（Income Replacement）的 90% 净收入（net income）偷换成毛收入（gross income），或声称从事故第 1 天起付、优先于雇主伤残计划和 EI 赔付，还会编造考试根本不考的具体津贴金额作为干扰项。Questions often substitute gross income for the 90% net income calculation base under Income Replacement, falsely claim payments start on day 1 or pay prior to employer disability plans and EI, or fabricate specific dollar allowances not tested on the exam as distractors.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 gross income vs. net income、first day vs. eighth day、primary payer vs. after other wage-loss plans，或 Catastrophic Injuries 标题下的给付分类。The question stem presents contrasts such as gross income vs. net income, first day vs. eighth day, primary payer vs. after other wage-loss plans, or benefit categories under Catastrophic Injuries.

⚠ 反向责任与车库人员绝对化表述Absolutist statements regarding Inverse Liability and garage personnel

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 判断型单选题喜欢说反向责任（Inverse Liability）“只在事故发生于禁止索偿辖区时适用”（漏掉肇事者居住在该类辖区的第二种情形），或以为只要车主开口许可，修车师傅试车出事也能走车主的基本险。Multiple-choice questions often assert that Inverse Liability applies only when the accident occurs in a jurisdiction prohibiting claims (omitting the second scenario where the at-fault driver resides in such a jurisdiction), or assume that an auto mechanic road-testing a car with owner permission is covered under the owner's Basic policy.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 with the owner's permission 搭配 auto repair / valet / dealership，或 Inverse Liability 搭配 only when the accident occurs in a jurisdiction。The question stem combines 'with the owner permission' with auto repair, valet, or dealership, or pairs Inverse Liability with 'only when the accident occurs in a jurisdiction'.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
受保人范围、汽车行业排除与承保地域Insured persons, garage policy exclusions, and territorial limitsInsured, Garage Policy, and Territorial Limits基本险保障车主、承租人、列名驾驶人及持有效驾照获许可驾驶者，但从事汽车买卖、修理、保养、存放或代客泊车者即使获许可也不受保（须另购 garage policy）；地域限于加拿大、美国及往返加美港口的船只，不含墨西哥。Basic coverage protects the vehicle owner, lessee, named drivers, and any licensed driver operating the vehicle with consent; however, persons engaged in buying, selling, repairing, maintaining, storing, servicing, or valeting vehicles are excluded even with permission (requiring a garage policy); territorial limits are confined to Canada, the United States, and vessels travelling between their ports, excluding Mexico.
基本险五项组成与有责车损分工Five Basic coverages and division of at-fault vehicle damageFive Basic Coverages基本险包含第三方责任（TPL）、加强意外给付（EAB）、基本车损（BVD）、保额不足驾驶人保障（UMP）和反向责任（Inverse Liability）五项；投保人不负责任的车损由 BVD 赔，自己负责的部分须靠可选的碰撞险（Collision）。Basic coverage consists of five parts: Third-Party Liability (TPL), Enhanced Accident Benefits (EAB), Basic Vehicle Damage (BVD), Underinsured Motorist Protection (UMP), and Inverse Liability; damage to the insured vehicle for which the insured is not responsible is paid by BVD, while the portion for which the insured is at fault must be covered by optional Collision.
第三方责任限额与诉讼限制的六个例外Third-Party Liability (TPL) limits and six lawsuit limitation exceptionsThird-Party Liability (TPL) and Lawsuits Limitation基本险 TPL 一般限额为 $200,000（重型商用车、商用挂车、巴士、出租车、豪华轿车自动 $1 million，运载危险品自动 $2 million）；BC 虽禁止大部分车祸人伤与车损诉讼，但非车辆财产损失、第三方车内物品或货物、营业中断、环境损害清理、允许诉讼的省外事故及涉及非标准车辆的非公路事故仍可起诉。The standard Basic TPL limit is $200,000 (automatically $1,000,000 for heavy commercial vehicles, commercial trailers, buses, taxis, and limousines carrying passengers or cargo for compensation, and $2,000,000 for vehicles transporting dangerous goods); although BC prohibits most lawsuits for bodily injury and vehicle damage resulting from motor vehicle crashes, lawsuits remain permitted for damage to property other than a vehicle, cargo or contents of third parties, business interruption losses, environmental damage cleanup, out-of-province crashes in jurisdictions permitting tort actions, and off-highway crashes involving non-standard vehicles.
加强意外给付（EAB）原则与非居民规则Enhanced Accident Benefits (EAB) core principles and non-resident rulesEnhanced Accident Benefits (EAB)EAB 对所有 BC 居民和客户不问过错、无总上限，涵盖司机、乘客、骑车人和行人；非居民在 BC 受伤可得医疗康复但金额可能随过错调整，若其另有其他车险意外给付则以该保险为主要来源。EAB provides benefits to all BC residents on a no-fault basis without an overall limit, covering drivers, passengers, cyclists, and pedestrians; non-residents injured in BC receive medical and rehabilitation benefits that may be reduced according to their degree of fault, and if they carry other auto accident benefits coverage, that other policy acts as the primary payer.
收入替代与其他 EAB 核心给付细则Income Replacement and other core EAB benefit provisionsIncome Replacement, Caregiver, Permanent Impairment, and Catastrophic Injuries考试口径下收入替代按税后净收入的 90% 计算、从事故后第 8 天起付且排在雇主伤残计划、WorkSafeBC 和 EI 之后；照护者给付针对无薪照护 16 岁以下儿童或无工作能力者，永久伤残按附表一次性给付，哀伤辅导单程超 50 公里的交通住宿可预先批准。Under the exam standard, Income Replacement pays 90% of net income starting on the 8th day after the crash, paying after employer disability plans, WorkSafeBC, and EI; Caregiver Benefits apply to unpaid caregivers caring for children under age 16 or persons unable to work; Permanent Impairment provides a lump-sum payment according to a statutory schedule; and travel and lodging for grief counselling exceeding 50 kilometres one-way can be pre-approved.
基本车损（BVD）限额、按比例赔付及非公路条件Basic Vehicle Damage (BVD) limit, proportional settlement, and off-highway rulesBasic Vehicle Damage (BVD)BVD 最高 $200,000，仅适用于 BC 公共道路（省外不适用），按投保人不负责任的比例赔付车辆修理、永久附属设备、使用损失及拖车存放；非公路事故须至少有两辆持牌标准车（或持牌挂车）相撞才适用，不赔车内物品、货物和营业中断。BVD provides coverage up to $200,000 solely on BC public roads (not out-of-province), paying in proportion to the percentage the insured is not responsible for to cover vehicle repairs, permanently attached equipment, loss of use, towing, and storage; off-highway accidents require a collision between at least two licensed standard vehicles (or licensed trailers) to qualify, and BVD excludes contents, cargo, and business interruption.
保额不足驾驶人保障（UMP）双条件Underinsured Motorist Protection (UMP) dual qualification conditionsUnderinsured Motorist Protection (UMP)UMP 每人最高 $1 million，仅在投保人或同住家人同时满足“不享有 EAB 资格”且“肇事司机第三方责任险不足以支付判定损害”两项条件时补足差额，在不允许车祸诉讼的地区（含 BC 大部分道路事故）不适用。UMP provides up to $1,000,000 per person to cover bodily injury shortfall only when the insured or household family member concurrently satisfies two conditions: not being entitled to Enhanced Accident Benefits (EAB), and the at-fault driver's third-party liability insurance being insufficient to satisfy the adjudicated damages; it does not apply in jurisdictions where motor vehicle tort lawsuits are barred (including most roadway crashes in BC).
法定无保险或身份不明驾驶人条款Statutory Uninsured/Unidentified Motorist ProvisionStatutory Uninsured/Unidentified Motorist Provision该条款独立于基本险，自动保障全体 BC 居民（无论是否有车或驾照）在 BC 公路上被无保险或逃逸司机撞坏的非车辆财产（如建筑、围栏、自行车），最高 $200,000，其中逃逸事故须由索赔人先承担 $750 免赔额。Operating independently of Basic coverage, this statutory provision protects all BC residents (regardless of vehicle ownership or driver's licence) for damage to non-vehicle property (such as buildings, fences, or bicycles) caused by uninsured or hit-and-run drivers on BC highways up to $200,000, subject to a $750 deductible borne by the claimant in hit-and-run cases.
反向责任（Inverse Liability）的两种触发情形Two triggering scenarios of Inverse LiabilityInverse Liability当事故发生在加美某个法律不允许就车损索偿的辖区，或者肇事者居住在该类辖区时，反向责任按对方负责的比例赔付投保车辆损失，并由基本险替投保人承担对方责任比例那部分的碰撞险免赔额。When an accident occurs in a Canadian or U.S. jurisdiction whose laws bar claims for such vehicle damage, or when the at-fault driver resides in such a jurisdiction, Inverse Liability covers the insured vehicle's damage in proportion to the other driver's percentage of fault, and Basic coverage pays that same percentage of the insured's Collision deductible.
三种自车损失险、定价类别与除外责任Three own-damage coverages, rating categories, and exclusionsCollision, Specified Perils, Comprehensive, Rate Group, and Declared Value碰撞险保碰撞翻车、坑洞及逃逸撞车；综合险保碰撞以外一切（含撞动物、风挡玻璃）且理赔不影响驾驶因子；列明风险险不含故意破坏、恶意毁损、撞动物和风挡；多数私家车按车型分组（rate group）定价，房车、摩托、重型商用车等按申报价值（declared value）定价，轮胎、车内物品及家人雇员偷车均除外。Collision covers impact, rollover, potholes, and hit-and-run vehicle damage; Comprehensive covers all losses except collision (including animal impact and windshield damage) without affecting the driver factor; Specified Perils excludes vandalism, malicious mischief, animal impact, and windshields; most private passenger vehicles are rated using rate groups, whereas motorhomes, motorcycles, and heavy commercial vehicles use declared value; tires, vehicle contents, and theft by family or employees are excluded.

第 1 题Q1EAB收入替代计算与给付顺位EAB Income Replacement calculation and payment order★★陷阱：EAB收入替代基数与顺位陷阱

Elena, a BC resident employed full-time as a lab technologist, earns an annual gross salary of $80,000 and takes home $60,000 in net income after taxes. She is injured in a highway collision in BC and is unable to work for several months. She also has a short-term disability plan through her employer. How does the Income Replacement benefit under Enhanced Accident Benefits (EAB) respond?

Elena 是一名全职担任实验室技术员的 BC 居民，税前毛年薪为 $80,000，税后净收入为 $60,000。她在 BC 公路的一起碰撞事故中受伤，数月无法工作。她同时享有雇主提供的短期伤残计划。加强意外给付（EAB）下的收入替代给付将如何响应？

  1. ✗ 错误。掉入了“EAB收入替代基数与顺位陷阱”：收入替代（Income Replacement）按税后净收入（net income）而非毛收入（gross income）的 90% 计算，从事故后第 8 天而非第 1 天起付，且排在雇主伤残计划之后。Incorrect. Falls into the 'EAB Income Replacement calculation base and payment order trap': Income Replacement is calculated on 90% of after-tax net income rather than gross income, begins on the 8th day after the crash rather than the 1st day, and pays after employer disability plans.

  2. ✓ 正确。在加强意外给付（Enhanced Accident Benefits, EAB）下，收入替代（Income Replacement）按税后净收入的 90% 计算（90% × $60,000 = $54,000/年），从事故后第 8 天起付，并排在雇主伤残计划、WorkSafeBC 和 EI 之后赔付。（注：考试口径只考“90% × 净收入”这一条算法，而 BC 现行规定为 90% 净收入与平均毛周薪 75% 两条取大。）Correct. Under Enhanced Accident Benefits (EAB), Income Replacement is calculated as 90% of after-tax net income (90% × $60,000 = $54,000/year), begins on the 8th day after the crash, and pays after employer disability plans, WorkSafeBC, and EI. (Note: The exam standard tests only the single formula of '90% × net income', whereas current BC regulations take the greater of 90% net income and 75% of average gross weekly earnings.)

  3. ✗ 错误。比例和起付日均错误，且 EAB 对 BC 居民实行不问过错（no-fault）原则，不会因投保人在事故中的过错比例而削减收入替代金额。Incorrect. Both the percentage and the start date are incorrect, and EAB operates on a no-fault basis for BC residents, meaning Income Replacement is not reduced by the insured's percentage of fault in the accident.

  4. ✗ 错误。混淆了永久伤残（Permanent Impairment）与收入替代：按附表一次性给付（lump-sum payment）是永久伤残的规则，而收入替代是针对因伤无法工作期间的持续收入补偿，且排在雇主计划之后。Incorrect. Basic Vehicle Damage (BVD) applies only to crashes involving specific vehicles; a single-vehicle impact with a fallen tree, pothole, or guardrail on the road does not constitute a collision with another vehicle and must be claimed under optional Collision.

  4. ✓ 正确。碰撞险（Collision）承保与另一车、人或物体的碰撞或翻车，明确包含撞上路面、坑洞或裂缝、掉在路上的物体（如石头、已经倒在路上的树）以及护栏、桥墩等。Correct. Collision covers impact with another vehicle, person, or object, or rollover, explicitly including impacts with the road surface, potholes, cracks, objects lying on the road (such as rocks or already fallen trees), guardrails, and bridge piers.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Hannah wants to know how different own-damage claims affect her insurance and which perils are covered under each option. Which of the following comparisons among Collision, Comprehensive, and Specified Perils is correct?

Hannah 想了解不同的自车损失索赔如何影响她的保险，以及每种选项各自承保哪些风险。下列关于碰撞险、综合险和列明风险险的比较哪一项是正确的？

  1. ✗ 错误。掉入了“碰撞险与综合险边界混淆”陷阱：把两者的保障范围完全颠倒了——撞动物（活的或死的）和风挡玻璃归综合险（Comprehensive），压过坑洞（pothole）归碰撞险（Collision）。Incorrect. Falls into the 'confusing boundaries between Collision and Comprehensive' trap: Completely reverses the two coverage scopes; hitting an animal (living or dead) and windshield damage fall under Comprehensive, whereas driving into a pothole falls under Collision.

  2. ✓ 正确。列明风险险（Specified Perils）承保火灾、盗窃、雷电风暴冰雹涨水、地震、爆炸等列明风险，但明确不保故意破坏（vandalism）、恶意毁损（malicious mischief）、撞动物和风挡玻璃；这些需购买综合险（Comprehensive），且综合险理赔不影响驾驶因子。Correct. Specified Perils covers listed perils like fire, theft, lightning, windstorm, hail, rising water, earthquake, and explosion, but explicitly excludes vandalism, malicious mischief, animal impact, and windshields; these require Comprehensive coverage, and claims under Comprehensive do not affect the driver factor.

  3. ✗ 错误。虽然综合险理赔确实不影响驾驶因子（driver factor），但“车内物品（personal property inside the vehicle）”和“家人或雇员偷车”均属于碰撞险与综合险的共同除外责任。Incorrect. While Comprehensive claims indeed do not affect the driver factor, personal property inside the vehicle and vehicle theft by family members or employees are common exclusions under both Collision and Comprehensive.

  4. ✗ 错误。将两种定价类别记反了：大多数私家车和轻型商用车使用车型分组（rate group），而挂车、房车、摩托车、总重 5,000 kg 以上商用车、雪地车、高尔夫球车和定制车才使用申报价值（declared value）。Incorrect. Reverses the two rating categories: Most private passenger and light commercial vehicles use rate groups, whereas trailers, motorhomes, motorcycles, commercial vehicles over 5,000 kg, snowmobiles, golf carts, and custom vehicles use declared value.

答案Answer：b

第 6 题Q6受保人范围、车库保单排除与承保地域Insured definition, garage policy exclusion, and territorial limits★陷阱：反向责任与车库人员绝对化表述

Owen leaves his BC-plated sedan at a local auto repair shop for a brake inspection and gives the mechanic explicit permission to road-test the car. While road-testing Owen's car, the mechanic negligently causes a collision. Later that month, Owen plans a road trip driving from BC through Washington and California into Mexico. Which statement is correct regarding Owen's Basic Autoplan policy?

Owen 把他在 BC 上牌的轿车留在当地一家汽车修理厂检查刹车，并明确许可修车师傅上路试车。修车师傅在试车时因疏忽引发碰撞事故。当月晚些时候，Owen 计划从 BC 自驾途经华盛顿州和加州进入墨西哥旅行。关于 Owen 的基本险保单，下列哪项陈述是正确的？

  1. ✓ 正确。从事汽车买卖、修理、保养、存放、服务或代客停放业务的人员（auto repair / garage personnel），即使得到车主许可（even with the owner's permission），也不在车主的基本险保障内，须另买车库保单（garage policy）；同时基本险的承保地域（territorial limits）仅限加拿大、美国及往返加美港口的船只，进入墨西哥后不赔。Correct. Persons engaged in buying, selling, repairing, maintaining, storing, servicing, or valeting motor vehicles (auto repair / garage personnel) are excluded from the owner's Basic coverage even with the owner's permission, and must purchase a separate garage policy; additionally, Basic territorial limits are restricted to Canada, the United States, and vessels between their ports, providing no coverage once in Mexico.

  2. ✗ 错误。掉入了“反向责任与车库人员绝对化表述”陷阱：汽车修理等行业人员是“持照且获车主许可即受保”这一规则的法定排除对象；且承保地域不含墨西哥。Incorrect. Falls into the 'absolutist statements regarding Inverse Liability and garage personnel' trap: Auto repair personnel are a statutory exception to the rule that licensed drivers operating with owner permission are covered; furthermore, territorial limits do not include Mexico.

  3. ✗ 错误。修车师傅不受车主保单保障；而美国全境（含往返加美港口的船只）本来就在基本险自动承保地域内，无需额外批单。Incorrect. Auto mechanics are not covered under the vehicle owner's policy; meanwhile, the entire United States (including vessels between Canada and US ports) is automatically within Basic territorial limits without requiring an additional endorsement.

  4. ✗ 错误。基本险的承保地域明确包含加拿大、美国以及往返加美两国港口的船只（vessels travelling between ports in Canada and the United States）。Incorrect. Basic territorial limits explicitly include Canada, the United States, and vessels travelling between ports in Canada and the United States.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Which of the following individuals is covered as an 'insured' under a vehicle owner's Basic Autoplan policy when an accident occurs?

当发生事故时，下列哪一位人员作为“被保险人（insured）”受到车主基本险保单的保障？

  1. ✗ 错误。掉入了“反向责任与车库人员绝对化表述”陷阱：代客泊车（valet parking）属于汽车存放/服务行业，即使持有有效驾照并获车主许可，也被排除在车主的基本险之外。Incorrect. Falls into the 'absolutist statements regarding Inverse Liability and garage personnel' trap: Valet parking belongs to the vehicle storage and servicing industry; even with a valid driver's licence and owner permission, valet drivers are excluded from the owner's Basic coverage.

  2. ✗ 错误。基本险承保地域（territorial limits）仅限加拿大、美国及往返加美港口的船只，在墨西哥境内驾车不受保障。Incorrect. Basic territorial limits are restricted to Canada, the United States, and vessels between Canada and US ports; driving within Mexico is not covered.

  3. ✗ 错误。从事汽车买卖（buying or selling motor vehicles）业务的个人或公司，即使获得车主同意，也不在车主那份基本险内，必须依赖自己的 garage policy。Incorrect. Individuals or businesses engaged in buying or selling motor vehicles are excluded from the owner's Basic policy even with the owner's consent, and must rely on their own garage policy.

  4. ✓ 正确。基本险的被保险人（insured）包括车主或承租人（lessee）、保单列名驾驶人，以及任何持有有效驾照并经车主或承租人许可驾驶的非汽修/泊车行业人员；往返加美港口的船只也在承保地域内。Correct. Insured persons under Basic coverage include the owner or lessee, drivers named on the policy, and any person holding a valid licence who drives with the owner's or lessee's consent (except auto repair and valet personnel); vessels travelling between Canadian and U.S. ports are also within the coverage territory.

第 7 题Q7反向责任与法定无保险条款的适用Applicability of Inverse Liability and Statutory Uninsured Motorist Provision★★陷阱：UMP与法定无保险条款张冠李戴

Priya, a BC resident who does not own a car and does not hold a driver's licence, wakes up to find that an unidentified hit-and-run driver veered off a BC public road overnight and smashed her front wooden fence and her bicycle parked by the porch, causing $3,500 in damage. She has no other insurance covering this loss. What coverage, if any, is available to Priya?

Priya 是一位既没有车也没有驾照的 BC 居民。她早上醒来发现，夜间有一名身份不明的肇事逃逸司机驶出 BC 公共道路，撞毁了她家门前的木围栏和停在门廊旁的自行车，造成 $3,500 损失。她没有其他保险承保这笔损失。Priya 可以获得什么保障（如有）？

  1. ✗ 错误。法定无保险/身份不明驾驶人条款（Statutory Uninsured/Unidentified Motorist Provision）来自《Insurance (Vehicle) Act》，对全体 BC 居民开放，即使没有车、没有保单、没有 BC 驾照也同样受保。Incorrect. The Statutory Uninsured/Unidentified Motorist Provision from the Insurance (Vehicle) Act is open to all BC residents, providing coverage even without owning a car, holding a policy, or having a BC driver's licence.

  2. ✗ 错误。掉入了“UMP与法定无保险条款张冠李戴”陷阱：UMP 保的是无 EAB 资格且对方保额不足时的人伤判赔差额，不管非车辆财产损失。Incorrect. Falls into the 'conflating UMP with the Statutory Uninsured Motorist Provision' trap: UMP covers bodily injury judgment shortfalls when ineligible for EAB and the at-fault driver has inadequate insurance, and does not cover non-vehicle property damage.

  3. ✓ 正确。独立于基本险的法定无保险/身份不明驾驶人条款（Statutory Uninsured/Unidentified Motorist Provision）自动赔付全体 BC 居民在 BC 公路上被无保险或逃逸司机损坏的非车辆财产（建筑、围栏、自行车），最高限额 $200,000；因系逃逸司机所致，索赔人须先承担 $750 免赔额。Correct. The Statutory Uninsured/Unidentified Motorist Provision, operating independently of Basic coverage, automatically compensates all BC residents for non-vehicle property (buildings, fences, bicycles) damaged by uninsured or hit-and-run drivers on BC highways up to $200,000; because it was caused by a hit-and-run driver, the claimant must first bear a $750 deductible.

  4. ✗ 错误。反向责任（Inverse Liability）只赔投保机动车的车损，且仅适用于事故发生地或肇事者居住地为加美不允许索偿辖区的情形，不保围栏和自行车。Incorrect. Inverse Liability covers only damage to the insured motor vehicle itself, and applies strictly when the crash occurs in or the at-fault driver resides in a Canadian or U.S. jurisdiction prohibiting claims, offering no coverage for fences and bicycles.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Kevin's BC-plated car is damaged in an accident in which the other driver is 75% at fault and Kevin is 25% at fault. The accident triggers Kevin's Basic Inverse Liability coverage, and Kevin also carries optional Collision coverage. How do Inverse Liability and Collision work together to settle Kevin's vehicle damage and Collision deductible?

Kevin 在 BC 上牌的轿车在一起事故中受损，对方司机负 75% 责任，Kevin 负 25% 责任。该事故触发了 Kevin 基本险中的反向责任保障，且 Kevin 同时购买了可选碰撞险。反向责任与碰撞险将如何配合处理 Kevin 的车损及碰撞险免赔额？

  1. ✗ 错误。掉入了“反向责任与车库人员绝对化表述”陷阱：反向责任在“事故发生地”或“肇事者居住地（even if the crash happened elsewhere）”是不允许就此类车损索偿的加美辖区时均适用；且基本险会替投保人支付对方责任比例的碰撞险免赔额。Incorrect. Falls into the 'absolutist statements regarding Inverse Liability and garage personnel' trap: Inverse Liability applies whether the crash occurred in or the at-fault driver resides in a Canadian or U.S. jurisdiction that bars such vehicle damage claims (even if the crash happened elsewhere); furthermore, Basic coverage pays the at-fault driver's proportion of the Collision deductible.

  2. ✓ 正确。当事故发生在加美某个法律不允许就车损索偿的辖区、或肇事者居住在该类辖区时，反向责任（Inverse Liability）按对方负责的比例（75%）赔付投保车辆损失；投保人自己负责的 25% 走可选碰撞险（Collision），且基本险会替投保人付对方责任比例（75%）的那部分碰撞险免赔额。Correct. When an accident occurs in or the at-fault driver resides in a Canadian or U.S. jurisdiction where laws prohibit claims for such vehicle damage, Inverse Liability pays the percentage the other driver is responsible for (75%); the insured's own 25% share is covered by optional Collision, and Basic coverage pays the other driver's fault percentage (75%) of the Collision deductible.

  3. ✗ 错误。把反向责任的赔付比例记反了（应按对方有责的 75% 赔），且胡乱套用了只保非车辆财产的法定无保险条款和 $750 免赔额。Incorrect. Reverses the Inverse Liability payout percentage (it pays the other driver's 75% fault share), and incorrectly applies the statutory uninsured motorist provision and $750 deductible, which apply exclusively to non-vehicle property.

  4. ✗ 错误。混淆了保额不足驾驶人保障（UMP，$1 million、无 EAB 条件）与反向责任（Inverse Liability，按对方责任比例赔车损）。Incorrect. Confuses Underinsured Motorist Protection (UMP, $1,000,000 limit, requires no EAB) with Inverse Liability (which pays vehicle damage according to the other driver's fault percentage).

答案Answer：b
