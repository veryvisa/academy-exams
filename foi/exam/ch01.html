① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 主次甄别题：四个选项全是真实的保险社会功能，题干用 main 提问，专考能否锁定唯一的「主要功能」（分散风险）。Primary vs. secondary function questions: All four options describe genuine social functions of insurance, but the stem asks for the main purpose, specifically testing whether you can identify the single primary function (spread of risk).
  • 否定排错题（EXCEPT / NOT true）：围绕保险法定定义的五个要点或三种分销方式的特征，把某一条篡改成绝对化或张冠李戴的说法让你挑错。Negative elimination questions (EXCEPT / NOT true): Focusing on the five key points of the legal definition of insurance or the characteristics of the three distribution channels, testing whether you can spot an absolute or misattributed statement.
  • 术语法定含义辨析题：在具体损失场景中区分 risk（亏钱的可能性）、peril（造成损失的起因）以及口语里常被误称为 risk 的保险标的。Statutory term distinction questions: Distinguishing in concrete loss scenarios among risk (the chance of financial loss), peril (the cause of loss), and the object of insurance, which is often loosely called a "risk" in everyday speech.
  • 补偿金额计算与选择权判定题：给出房屋当初买价与出事前一刻的市值，考 indemnity（补偿）按哪个时间点赔付，以及修、换或付现由谁决定。Indemnity calculation and settlement option questions: Providing an original purchase price and the market value immediately before a loss, testing at which point in time indemnity is measured, and who decides whether to repair, replace, or pay cash.
  • 承保与分销结构对比题：横向对比股份公司与相互公司、直营与独立经纪及代理在「身份、代表几家、业务归属、报酬方式」四格上的差异，以及伦敦劳合社的市场属性。Underwriting and distribution structure comparison questions: Comparing stock versus mutual companies, and contrasting direct writing, independent brokerages, and agencies across legal status, representation, ownership of records, and compensation, along with the marketplace nature of Lloyd's of London.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 真话非主True Statement but Not the Primary Function

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 选项里写的「充当信贷基础」「防损减损」「消除忧虑」全都是考试资料里对的保险功能，考生看到熟悉且正确的表述就顺手选了，忘了题干问的是唯一的主要功能。Options such as serving as a basis of credit, loss prevention, and eliminating worry are all accurate functions of insurance found in study materials. Candidates recognize these true statements and select them, forgetting that the question specifically asks for the single main purpose.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 main purpose 或 primary function of insurance。The question stem contains terms like 'main purpose' or 'primary function of insurance'.

⚠ 口语偷换法定含义Colloquial Usage Confused with Statutory Meaning

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 日常业务中大家常把被保的房屋或车辆直接叫作 risk，考生容易把这种口语习惯带进考场，或者把 risk（可能性）和 peril（火灾、冰雹等起因）搞反。In daily business, people often refer to an insured building or vehicle directly as a risk. Candidates bring this colloquial habit into the exam, or confuse risk (the chance of loss) with peril (the cause of loss such as fire or hail).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干要求按法定定义（legal definition）区分 risk、peril 或 object of insurance。The question stem requires distinguishing risk, peril, or object of insurance under the legal definition.

⚠ 买价误当补偿额与赔付方式绝对化Mistaking Purchase Price for Indemnity and Absolutizing Settlement Method

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为补偿是退还当初买房的钱，或者因为日常理赔常见给支票，就误以为法律规定必须赔付金钱、或被保险人有权指定要现金。Mistakenly believing that indemnity refunds the original purchase price of the property, or assuming that because claim settlements are often paid by cheque, the law requires payment in money or gives the insured the right to demand cash.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出历史购买价与出事前一刻价值，或出现 must pay in money、at the insured's option 等字眼。The question stem provides both historical purchase price and pre-loss value, or contains phrasing such as 'must pay in money' or 'at the insured's option'.

⚠ 代表一家等于雇员Equating Single-Company Representation with Employee Status

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到「只代表一家保险公司」就直接归为直营雇员，忽略了代理人（agent）虽然只代表一家公司（外加剩余市场协会），但身份是小业主且自己拥有业务簿。Assuming that representing only one insurer automatically makes an intermediary a direct writing employee, overlooking that an agent represents only one company (plus the Facility Association) but is an independent small business owner who owns the book of business.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述中介只代表一家保险公司，同时询问其雇佣身份或业务簿（book of business）归属。The question stem describes an intermediary representing only one insurer while asking about employment status or ownership of the book of business.

⚠ 把劳合社当保险公司Treating Lloyd's as an Insurance Company

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 伦敦劳合社名气很大，考生直觉把它当成一家跨国巨型保险公司，或误以为承保条（slip）只要有人牵头就能出单，忽略了必须认满 100% 才送签章处。Because Lloyd's of London is widely known, candidates intuitively assume it is a giant multinational insurance company, or mistakenly think a policy can be issued once a lead underwriter signs, overlooking that a slip must be 100% subscribed before being sent to the Policy Signing Office.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 Lloyd's of London、syndicate、underwriter 或 slip 等词。The question stem contains terms such as Lloyd's of London, syndicate, underwriter, or slip.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
保险的唯一主要功能The Sole Primary Function of Insurancespread of risk保险有五项社会功能，但只有分散风险（spread of risk，把少数人的损失分摊给多数人）是公认的主要功能。Insurance has five social functions, but only the spread of risk (sharing the losses of the few among the many who contribute to a pool) is recognized as the primary function.
法定定义的五个要点Five Key Points of the Legal Definitionlegal definition of insurance法定定义包含五点：转移财务责任、特定风险或危险发生才赔、以补偿为限、只保将来的意外损失、赔付可以是钱或等值物。The legal definition contains five points: shifting financial responsibility, paying only upon occurrence of a specific risk or peril, limiting payment to indemnity, covering only future accidental losses, and allowing settlement in money or other thing of value.
风险与危险的法定区分Statutory Distinction Between Risk and Perilrisk vs. peril法定定义中 risk 是亏钱的可能性，peril 是造成损失的起因（如火、风、冰雹），而被保的那栋楼是保险标的（object of insurance）。In the legal definition, risk is the chance of financial loss, peril is the cause of loss (such as fire, wind, or hail), and the building being insured is the object of insurance.
补偿原则与价值时点Principle of Indemnity and Pre-Loss Valuationindemnity补偿的目标是恢复实际损失、不多不少，赔付金额按损失发生前一刻的价值计算，而非当初买价。The objective of indemnity is to restore the actual loss, neither more nor less, with the settlement amount measured by the value of the property immediately before the loss occurred rather than the original purchase price.
赔付方式的选择权Option in the Method of Settlementmoney or other thing of value保险人有权选择以修理、更换或支付现金的方式履行赔付，选择权在保险人而不在被保险人。The insurer has the right to settle a claim by repairing, replacing, or paying in money; the option belongs to the insurer, not the insured.
财产与意外险三大块排位Ranking of Three Main Property and Casualty Classesproperty and casualty (P&C) / general insurance寿险与健康险以外的保险统称财产与意外险（一般保险），按保费规模排序依次为汽车险（超总保费一半）、财产险、责任险。Insurance other than life and health is collectively termed property and casualty (P&C) or general insurance; ranked by premium volume, automobile insurance ranks first (over half of total premiums), followed by property insurance, and then liability insurance.
股份公司与相互公司对比Stock Companies vs. Mutual Companiesstock company vs. mutual company股份公司归股东所有、以赚取投资回报为首要目标；相互公司归保单持有人所有、以低成本提供保险为目标，盈余通过红利或费率调整返还保单持有人。Stock companies are owned by shareholders with the primary goal of earning an investment return; mutual companies are owned by policyholders with the goal of providing insurance at low cost, returning surplus through dividends or rate adjustments.
政府保险人与强制车险省份Government Insurers and Compulsory Auto Provincesgovernment insurerBC、SK、MB、QC 四省由政府向居民提供强制汽车险，其中 BC、SK、MB 三省的政府强制车险通过独立经纪行分销（QC 不在其内）。The governments of BC, SK, MB, and QC provide compulsory automobile insurance to residents; in BC, SK, and MB, government compulsory auto insurance is distributed through independent brokerages (QC is not included in this brokerage arrangement).
直营、独立经纪与代理四格对比Four-Attribute Comparison of Direct Writing, Brokerage, and Agencydirect writing / independent brokerage / agency直营销售是公司雇员、只卖一家、业务归公司；独立经纪非雇员、代表多家及剩余市场协会、业务归经纪行、占八成以上业务；代理人是小业主、只代表一家及剩余市场协会、业务簿归自己。Direct writers are salaried employees representing one insurer with business owned by the insurer; independent brokers are not employees, represent multiple insurers and the Facility Association, have business owned by the brokerage, and handle over 80% of business; agents are small business owners representing one insurer and the Facility Association, and own their book of business.
伦敦劳合社的运作机制Operating Mechanism of Lloyd's of LondonLloyd's of London / syndicate / slip劳合社是承保人市场而非保险公司，承保人组成辛迪加并以自己名义认风险，经纪人递交的承保条须认满 100% 后送保单签章处盖钢印。Lloyd's is a market of underwriters rather than an insurance company; underwriters form syndicates to accept risks in their own names, and a slip submitted by a broker must be 100% subscribed before being stamped by the Policy Signing Office.

第 1 题Q1保险的主要社会功能Primary social function of insurance★陷阱：真话非主

A small business owner in Victoria is reviewing why insurance exists in society. While insurance contributes to the economy in several ways, which of the following is recognized as the main purpose of insurance?

维多利亚的一位小企业主正在了解保险为何在社会中存在。虽然保险在多个方面对经济有所贡献，但下列哪一项被公认为保险的主要功能？

  1. ✗ 充当信贷体系的基础（basis of credit）确实是保险的五项社会功能之一，但并非公认的「主要」功能，属于「真话非主」陷阱。Serving as a basis of credit is indeed one of the five social functions of insurance, but it is not the recognized 'main' purpose, falling into the 'true statement but not the primary function' trap.

  2. ✓ 分散风险（spread of risk）即把少数出险者的损失分摊给多数缴纳保费的人，是五项功能中唯一被公认为「主要（main）」的功能。Spread of risk, which means sharing the losses of the few who suffer losses among the many who contribute premiums, is the only one among the five functions recognized as the 'main' purpose.

  3. ✗ 防损与减损（loss prevention）是保险的真实社会功能之一，但题干问的是 main purpose，不能把附带社会功能当成主要功能。Loss prevention is a genuine social function of insurance, but the question stem asks for the main purpose; incidental social functions must not be mistaken for the primary function.

  4. ✗ 提供就业与投资资本（employment and capital）属于五项功能之一，但不是保险存在的首要或主要功能，属于「真话非主」陷阱。Providing employment and investment capital is one of the five functions, but it is not the primary or main purpose for which insurance exists, falling into the 'true statement but not the primary function' trap.

答案Answer：b

  1. ✗ 消除忧虑、鼓励创业（eliminates worry）是五项社会功能之一，但不是唯一的主要功能，掉入了「真话非主」陷阱。Eliminating worry and encouraging enterprise is one of the five social functions, but it is not the sole primary function, falling into the 'true statement but not the primary function' trap.

  2. ✗ 这是信贷体系的基础（basis of credit）功能，虽然表述本身正确，但并非主要功能。This describes the basis of credit function; although the statement itself is correct, it is not the primary function.

  3. ✗ 这是就业与投资资本（employment and capital）功能，并非题干所问的主要功能。This describes the employment and investment capital function, not the primary function requested in the question stem.

第 2 题Q2法定定义五要点与赔付方式选择权Five legal definition points and settlement option★★陷阱：买价误当补偿额与赔付方式绝对化

After a windstorm tears the shingles off a warehouse roof, the policyholder demands a cash settlement rather than having the insurer arrange repairs. Under the legal definition of insurance, which statement correctly describes how the claim may be settled?

一场暴风刮掉了仓库屋顶的瓦片后，保单持有人要求直接拿现金赔款，而不是由保险人安排修理。根据保险的法定定义，下列哪项正确描述了该理赔可以如何结案？

  1. ✓ 根据保险法定定义（legal definition of insurance）第五个要点，赔付可以是钱也可以是等值物（money or other thing of value），且选择修理、更换还是付现金的权利在保险人手中。Under the fifth key point of the legal definition of insurance, settlement may be made in money or other thing of value, and the right to choose between repairing, replacing, or paying cash rests with the insurer.

  2. ✗ 选择修理、更换还是付现金的权利在保险人而不在被保险人（insured），误以为被保险人在修房前能指定要现金是常见陷阱。The right to choose between repair, replacement, or cash rests with the insurer, not the insured; assuming the insured can demand cash before repairs begin is a common trap.

  3. ✗ 把「通常给钱」偷换成「法律只许给钱以确保精确补偿」属于赔付方式绝对化陷阱；法定定义明确允许赔付等值物。Distorting 'usually paid in money' into 'the law strictly requires cash to guarantee exact indemnity' is an absolutist settlement trap; the legal definition explicitly allows settlement in other things of value.

  4. ✗ 赔付方式的选择权依法归属保险人（insurer），不由独立经纪行在仓库保单出单时选定。The right to select the settlement method legally belongs to the insurer, and is not determined by an independent brokerage when the warehouse policy is bound.

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Which of the following statements is NOT part of the five key points in the legal definition of insurance?

下列哪项表述不属于保险法定定义五个要点的内容？

  1. ✗ 把财务责任转移给保险人并支付保费（premium）是法定定义的第一个要点，表述正确。Shifting financial responsibility to an insurer in exchange for a premium is the first key point of the legal definition, making this statement correct.

  2. ✗ 只保将来可能遇到的意外损失、不保故意或已发生财产损失是法定定义的第四个要点，表述正确。Covering only future accidental losses while excluding deliberate or already-occurred property losses is the fourth key point of the legal definition, making this statement correct.

  3. ✓ 法定定义第五点明确规定赔付可以是钱也可以是等值的东西（如修理或更换受损财产），说「必须只赔钱」是错误的绝对化陷阱，因此是本题要选的 NOT 项。The fifth point of the legal definition explicitly states that payment may be in money or other thing of value (such as repairing or replacing damaged property); stating that payment must always be in cash is a false absolutist trap, making this the correct NOT choice to select.

  4. ✗ 赔付以补偿（indemnity）为限、出险后不多不少是法定定义的第三个要点，表述正确。Restricting payment to indemnity so that the insured neither profits nor falls short after a loss is the third key point of the legal definition, making this statement correct.

第 3 题Q3risk与peril的法定含义区分Legal distinction between risk and peril★★陷阱：口语偷换法定含义

A hailstorm shatters the skylights of a commercial greenhouse in Kelowna. In the context of the legal definition of insurance, how are the terms 'risk' and 'peril' properly applied to this situation?

一场冰雹砸碎了基洛纳一座商业温室的天窗。在保险法定定义的语境下，risk 和 peril 这两个术语应如何正确应用于该情境？

  1. ✗ 把被保温室建筑叫作 risk 是日常口语用法，在法定定义中建筑是保险标的（object of insurance），而碎天窗玻璃是损失结果而非起因，掉入了「口语偷换法定含义」陷阱。Calling the insured greenhouse building a risk is everyday colloquial usage; in the legal definition, the building is the object of insurance, and shattered skylight glass is the resulting damage rather than the cause, falling into the 'colloquial usage confused with statutory meaning' trap.

  2. ✗ 把风险（risk，温室业主亏钱的可能性）与危险（peril，冰雹这一造成损失的起因）两个法定定义正好颠倒了。This completely reverses the statutory definitions of risk (the chance of financial loss to the greenhouse owner) and peril (the hailstorm as the cause of loss).

  3. ✓ 在法定定义中，风险（risk）指亏钱的可能性，危险（peril）指造成损失的起因（如火、风、冰雹），两者对应完全准确。In the legal definition, risk refers to the chance of financial loss, and peril refers to the cause of loss (such as fire, wind, or hail), matching both terms completely accurately.

  4. ✗ 温室是保险标的（object of insurance），业主支付的保费（premium）是转移风险的对价，都不是危险或风险。The greenhouse is the object of insurance, and the premium paid by the owner is the consideration for transferring risk; neither is a peril or risk.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

During an office discussion, an underwriter refers to an insured bakery building as 'a well-maintained risk.' How does this everyday phrasing compare with the strict legal definition of insurance?

在一次办公室讨论中，承保人把一栋受保的面包房建筑称为「一个维护良好的 risk」。这种日常说法与严格的保险法定定义相比有何不同？

  1. ✓ 法定定义中风险（risk）指亏钱的可能性，而被保的那栋面包房建筑是保险标的（object of insurance），口语里常把标的叫成 risk，考场必须按法定含义区分。In the legal definition, risk refers to the chance of financial loss, while the insured bakery building is the object of insurance; colloquial speech often calls the object a risk, but the exam requires strict adherence to statutory meanings.

  2. ✗ 火灾等损失起因是危险（peril），不是 risk，面包房建筑本身也不是 peril。The cause of loss such as fire is a peril, not a risk, and the bakery building itself is not a peril.

  3. ✗ 《Insurance Act》法定定义里的 risk 是亏钱的可能性，把受保的面包房实物财产当成法定定义的 risk 属于口语偷换陷阱。Under the legal definition in the Insurance Act, risk is the chance of financial loss; mistaking the insured physical bakery property for the statutory definition of risk is a colloquial confusion trap.

  4. ✗ 收取的保单保费（premium）和补偿（indemnity）与风险及面包房保险标的的定义完全不同。Charged policy premium and indemnity have entirely different meanings from risk and the bakery as the object of insurance.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

第 4 题Q4补偿原则与损失发生前一刻价值Principle of indemnity and pre-loss valuation★★陷阱：买价误当补偿额与赔付方式绝对化

Ten years ago, a homeowner purchased a house for $75,000. Immediately before a covered fire completely destroyed the building today, its value was $140,000. Under the principle of indemnity in the legal definition of insurance, what amount is the homeowner entitled to receive?

十年前，一位房主花 75,000 加元买下一栋房子。在今天一场受保火灾将该建筑彻底烧毁的前一刻，它的价值为 140,000 加元。根据保险法定定义中的补偿原则，房主有权获得多少赔付金额？

  1. ✗ 若只退还房主十年前支付的买价 75,000 加元，房主就没有得到完整补偿，掉入了「买价误当补偿额」陷阱。Refunding only the $75,000 purchase price paid ten years ago leaves the homeowner under-compensated, falling into the 'mistaking purchase price for indemnity' trap.

  2. ✗ 只赔当前价值与原买价的净差额远低于实际损失，违反了补偿（indemnity）让人拿回实际损失、不少赔的原则。Paying only the net difference between the current value and the original purchase price falls far short of the actual loss, violating the principle of indemnity that restores the actual loss without under-compensating.

  3. ✗ 将原买价与火灾前一刻价值叠加赔付会让被保险人靠出事赚钱，违反了补偿原则「不多赔」的上限要求。Combining the original purchase price and the pre-fire value would allow the insured to profit from a loss, violating the indemnity principle, which prohibits profiting from a loss.

  4. ✓ 补偿（indemnity）的目标是不多也不少地恢复实际损失，金额按损失发生前一刻的价值（140,000 加元）计算，而非当年的买价。The objective of indemnity is to restore the actual loss neither more nor less, with the settlement amount measured by the value of the property immediately before the loss occurred ($140,000) rather than the original purchase price.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Why does the legal definition of insurance restrict claim payments to indemnity based on the value immediately before the loss, while excluding deliberate losses?

为什么保险的法定定义将理赔金额限制在按损失发生前一刻价值计算的补偿范围内，同时排除故意制造的损失？

  1. ✓ 补偿（indemnity）按出事前一刻价值算，是为了让人拿回实际损失、既不靠出事赚钱也不少赔；不保故意损失是因为那会浪费社会资源。Indemnity is measured immediately before the loss so the insured recovers the actual loss without profiting or falling short; deliberate losses are excluded because they waste economic and social resources.

  2. ✗ 已经发生的损失绝对不保（即便同意付更高保费），否则人人都等出事再买，保险公司无法生存。Losses that have already occurred are strictly excluded (even if an applicant offers a higher premium); otherwise individuals would only purchase coverage after suffering damage, collapsing the insurance pool.

  3. ✗ 补偿按受损房屋在损失发生前一刻的价值计算，不是按历史购买价计算。Indemnity is calculated based on the value of the damaged home immediately before the loss occurred, not its historical purchase price.

  4. ✗ 限制在补偿范围内是为了防止从损失中获利或补偿不足，与私营股份公司留存保费或拒付现金理赔无关。Restricting payment to indemnity prevents profiting from loss or under-compensation; it has nothing to do with private stock companies retaining premiums or refusing cash claim settlements.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

第 5 题Q5股份公司、相互公司与政府保险人Stock companies, mutual companies, and government insurers★★陷阱：口语偷换法定含义

A client asks how a mutual insurance company differs from a stock insurance company in Canada. Which statement accurately contrasts these two types of private insurers?

一位客户询问在加拿大相互保险公司与股份保险公司有何不同。下列哪项表述准确对比了这两类私营保险人？

  1. ✓ 相互公司（mutual company）归保单持有人所有，首要目标是尽量低成本地提供保险，盈余通过红利（dividend）或后续费率调整返还给保单持有人；而股份公司（stock company）归股东所有并追求投资回报。A mutual company is owned by its policyholders with the primary objective of providing insurance at the lowest possible cost, returning surplus through dividends or subsequent rate adjustments, whereas a stock company is owned by shareholders seeking investment returns.

  2. ✗ 由股东所有、追求投资回报的是股份公司（stock company），省政府经营的是政府保险人（government insurer），把三者 ownership 搞混了。An insurer owned by shareholders seeking investment returns is a stock company, while one operated by a provincial government is a government insurer, confusing the ownership structures of the three insurer types.

  3. ✗ 加拿大大部分一般保险业务由股份公司承保而非相互公司，且相互公司的盈余归保单持有人而非外部股东。The vast majority of general insurance in Canada is underwritten by stock companies rather than mutuals, and surplus in a mutual company belongs to policyholders, not external shareholders.

  4. ✗ 在 BC、SK、MB、QC 四省向居民提供强制汽车险的是政府保险人（government insurer），相互公司属于私营保险人。In BC, SK, MB, and QC, compulsory automobile insurance is provided to residents by government insurers; mutual companies are private insurers.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

下列哪项正确描述了政府保险人在加拿大财产与意外险市场中的角色？

  1. ✗ 虽然 BC、SK、MB、QC 四省政府都办强制车险，但只有 BC、SK、MB 三省通过独立经纪行（independent brokerage）分销，QC 不在其内。Although governments in BC, SK, MB, and QC all operate compulsory auto insurance, only BC, SK, and MB distribute it through independent brokerages; QC is not included in this arrangement.

  4. ✗ 在财产与意外险（property and casualty, P&C）三大险种中，汽车险排第一且超过全部 P&C 保费的一半，财产险排第二，责任险排第三。Among the three main lines of property and casualty (P&C) insurance, automobile ranks first and accounts for over half of all P&C premiums, property ranks second, and liability ranks third.

第 6 题Q6直营、独立经纪与代理的特征对比Characteristics of direct writing, independent brokerages, and agencies★★★陷阱：代表一家等于雇员

Marcus sells general insurance in British Columbia. He represents only one insurance company (plus the Facility Association), operates as a small business owner, is paid commission and bonuses, and owns his book of business. Under which distribution system does Marcus operate?

Marcus 在 BC 省销售一般保险。他只代表一家保险公司（外加剩余市场协会），以小业主身份经营，拿佣金和奖金，并且拥有自己的业务簿。Marcus 属于哪种分销方式？

  1. ✗ 掉入了「代表一家等于雇员」陷阱：直营（direct writing）销售确实只卖一家，但他们是公司受薪雇员且业务归保险公司；Marcus 是小业主且拥有业务簿，不是直营。This falls into the 'equating single-company representation with employee status' trap: direct writers do sell for only one insurer, but they are salaried employees whose business belongs to the company; Marcus is a small business proprietor who owns his book of business, so he is not a direct writer.

  2. ✗ 独立经纪（independent brokerage）通常代表多家保险公司，并非只有多公司经纪才能拥有业务簿；Marcus 只代表一家公司（外加剩余市场协会）。Independent brokerages normally represent multiple insurers, but owning a book of business is not exclusive to multi-insurer brokers; Marcus represents only one company (plus the Facility Association).

  3. ✓ 代理制（agency）的特征正是：像直营一样只代表一家保险公司（外加剩余市场协会 Facility Association），但身份是小业主，拿佣金和奖金，且客户业务簿（book of business）归自己所有。The hallmarks of the agency system are precisely that the intermediary represents only one insurance company (plus the Facility Association), operates as a small business proprietor, is paid commissions and bonuses, and personally owns the client book of business.

  4. ✗ 独立经纪和代理人都能把次标准汽车保单放到剩余市场协会（Facility Association），这与伦敦劳合社辛迪加无关。Both independent brokers and agents can place substandard automobile policies with the Facility Association; this has nothing to do with Lloyd's of London syndicates.

答案Answer：c

Which of the following statements does NOT apply to the independent brokerage distribution system?

  4. ✗ 独立经纪是最主流的分销渠道（占八成以上 P&C 业务），且在 BC、SK、MB 三省经办政府强制车险，表述正确。Independent brokerage is the predominant distribution channel (handling over 80% of P&C business) and distributes government compulsory auto insurance in BC, SK, and MB, making this statement accurate.

第 7 题Q7伦敦劳合社的性质与承保条流程Nature of Lloyd's of London and the slip process★★★陷阱：把劳合社当保险公司

An entertainment organizer needs coverage for an outdoor concert in case severe weather forces a cancellation, and standard insurers decline the application. When a broker places this business through Lloyd's of London, how does the placement process work?

一位演出主办方需要为一场户外音乐会投保因恶劣天气被迫取消的保险，常规保险公司拒绝承保。当经纪人通过伦敦劳合社安排这项业务时，其承保流程是如何运作的？

  1. ✗ 掉入了「把劳合社当保险公司」陷阱：伦敦劳合社（Lloyd's of London）不是一家股份保险公司，自己不从总部直接出单做保险业务，而是由承保人组成的市场。This falls into the 'treating Lloyd's as an insurance company' trap: Lloyd's of London is not a corporate stock insurer and does not issue insurance policies directly from a head office; it is a marketplace of underwriting members.

  2. ✓ 在劳合社市场中，承保人（underwriter）或辛迪加（syndicate）以自己的名义在承保条（slip）上认领一定风险比例，必须认满 100% 后才送到保单签章处（Policy Signing Office）盖钢印。In the Lloyd's market, individual underwriters or syndicates subscribe to portions of risk on a slip in their own names, and the slip must be 100% subscribed before it is sent to the Policy Signing Office to be officially stamped.

  3. ✗ 承保条（slip）必须认满 100% 才能送保单签章处盖章，不存在认满 50% 就由承保代理人出单并由英国政府兜底的规则。A slip must be 100% subscribed before it can be sent to the Policy Signing Office; there is no rule allowing an underwriting agent to bind at 50% subscription with the British government guaranteeing the remainder.

  4. ✗ 给劳合社保单盖钢印的是劳合社的保单签章处（Policy Signing Office），而不是剩余市场协会（Facility Association）。Lloyd's policies are stamped by the Lloyd's Policy Signing Office, not by the Canadian Facility Association.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

Which statement accurately describes the structure of Lloyd's of London?

下列哪项表述准确描述了伦敦劳合社的结构？

  1. ✗ 伦敦劳合社（Lloyd's of London）既不是由投保通信卫星和海上钻井平台的商业保单持有人拥有的加拿大相互保险公司，也不是保险公司，而是一个承保人市场。Lloyd's of London is neither a Canadian mutual company owned by policyholders insuring satellites and oil rigs nor an insurance company at all; it is an underwriters' marketplace.

  2. ✗ 劳合社不是省政府保险人，不与私营公司争抢常规住宅财产和汽车险业务，而是主要承保常规保险公司接不了的特殊或复杂风险。Lloyd's is not a provincial government insurer and does not compete with private companies for standard residential property and auto business; it primarily underwrites unusual or complex risks that standard insurers decline.

  3. ✓ 劳合社本身不是保险公司，而是由承保人（underwriter）组成的市场，每个承保人以自己名义和资产认风险，复杂风险由多个承保会员组成辛迪加（syndicate）并由承保代理人（underwriting agent）管理。Lloyd's itself is not an insurance company, but a marketplace of underwriters where members assume risks in their own names, and complex risks are underwritten by syndicates of members managed by underwriting agents.

  4. ✗ 劳合社不是由受薪雇员直接为特殊商业风险出单的直营保险公司，业务需由经纪人将风险资料写在承保条（slip）上交由承保人认领比例。Lloyd's is not a direct writing company where salaried employees issue policies directly; business must be brought by brokers who write risk details on slips for underwriters to subscribe proportions.
