① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 情景归类题：给出具体物品（如租户出资的装修、大堂活棕榈树、待售盆栽、日常维护油漆或大修建材），要求判断应归入建筑（building）、存货（stock）还是设备（equipment），重点考查「可保利益」与「用途边界」。Scenario classification questions: Given specific items (such as tenant-funded improvements, live lobby palm trees, potted plants for sale, routine maintenance paint, or major construction lumber), determine whether they belong to building, stock, or equipment, testing insurable interest and usage boundaries.
  • 共保算术与豁免题：给出建筑或存货的实际现金价值（ACV）、80%共保比例、投保额和部分损失金额，考查「Did ÷ Should × Loss」公式计算，以及「适用保额2%与$5,000取较小者」的共保豁免门槛，最常设坑在问「被保险人自担多少」而非「保险人赔多少」。Coinsurance arithmetic and waiver questions: Given the actual cash value (ACV) of a building or stock, an 80% coinsurance requirement, the insurance amount carried, and a partial loss amount, test the Did divided by Should times Loss formula and the coinsurance waiver threshold (the lesser of 2% of the applicable limit or $5,000), frequently trapping candidates by asking how much the insured must bear rather than what the insurer pays.
  • 表单与除外对比题：对比列名险表（IBC 4036）与广式表（IBC 4037）的承保范围，考查广式表多出的五类保障（如雇员驾车撞击建筑、水管冻裂、积雪压塌）以及两张表均除外的风险（如地震、洪水、下水道倒灌、农业熏烟与工业冒烟）。Form and exclusion comparison questions: Compare coverage between the Named Perils Form (IBC 4036) and the Broad Form (IBC 4037), testing the five additional coverages provided by the Broad Form (such as vehicle impact by an employee, frozen ruptured plumbing pipes, and collapse from snow load) as well as perils excluded under both forms (such as earthquake, flood, sewer backup, agricultural smudging, and industrial smoke).
  • 犯罪险与处所外财产证据门槛题：通过夜间被盗、店员送款途中昏迷被拿走钱袋、无人看管车辆失窃等场景，考查入室盗窃（burglary）、抢劫（robbery）、盗窃（theft）及上锁车辆保证（Locked Vehicle Warranty）各自的刚性取证条件。Crime coverage and off-premises property evidentiary standard questions: Using scenarios such as nighttime break-ins, a clerk fainting on the way to make a bank deposit while the money bag is taken, and unattended vehicle thefts, test the strict evidentiary conditions required for burglary, robbery, theft, and the Locked Vehicle Warranty.
  • 营业中断与专业附加险匹配题：给出停业损失、邻近火灾导致警方封街、超过15 psi压力容器爆炸或在建工程停工超30天等情形，考查盈利式（Earnings Form）、利润式（Profits Form）、额外费用险（Extra Expense）、锅炉机器险与建工险的触发边界。Business interruption and specialty endorsement matching questions: Presenting business closure losses, police street closures due to neighboring fires, explosions of pressure vessels exceeding 15 psi, or construction site cessations exceeding 30 consecutive days, test the trigger boundaries for the Earnings Form, Profits Form, Extra Expense insurance, Boiler and Machinery insurance, and Builder's Risk coverage.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 偷换所问（赔款 vs 自担额）Switching the question (insurer payout vs insured share)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生背熟了 Did ÷ Should × Loss 公式，算出结果后直接在选项里勾选该数字，忽略了题干最后问的是被保险人自己要承担多少损失。Candidates memorize the Did divided by Should times Loss formula, calculate the payout, and immediately select that number, overlooking that the question ultimately asks how much loss the insured must bear personally.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干末尾出现 how much must the insured bear / absorb / pay out of pocket 或 what is the insured's share of the loss。The stem ends with phrases such as how much must the insured bear, absorb, pay out of pocket, or what is the insured's share of the loss.

⚠ 租户改良与植物错格Misclassifying tenant's improvements and plants

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉认为嵌进墙里的装修、固定隔断或办公室所有植物都属于建筑（building），忘了租户对建筑本体没有可保利益，以及非业主建筑或非活体植物不归建筑。Candidates intuitively assume that wall-embedded fixtures, permanent partitions, or all office plants belong to building, forgetting that a tenant has no insurable interest in the building structure, and that plants in non-owner buildings or artificial plants are not covered under building.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 tenant's improvements（租户改良）、previous tenant（前任租户）、silk plants（丝绸花）、outdoor trees（户外树木）或 major renovation materials（大修翻新材料）。The stem mentions tenant's improvements, previous tenant, silk plants, outdoor trees, or major renovation materials.

⚠ 小额损失误套共保罚则Incorrectly applying the coinsurance penalty to small losses

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 一看到投保额低于80%要求就开始按比例打折，忘了先核对损失是否未超过「保额的2%」与「$5,000」中的较小者（共保豁免）。Seeing an insurance amount below the 80% requirement, candidates immediately reduce the claim proportionally, forgetting to first verify whether the loss does not exceed the lesser of 2% of the policy limit or $5,000 (waiver of coinsurance).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干是部分损失且金额较小（在 $5,000 以内），同时给出了不足额投保的保额与资产价值。The stem describes a small partial loss (under $5,000) while also providing an underinsured policy limit and asset value.

⚠ 广式表当成「包赔一切」Assuming the Broad Form covers everything

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到 Broad Form（all risks）就以为所有水损、烟熏和现金被盗都能赔，忽略了洪水、下水道倒灌、工业作业冒烟、缺陷工艺以及现金证券仍在除外清单中。Seeing Broad Form (all risks), candidates assume all water damage, smoke, and stolen cash are covered, overlooking that flood, sewer backup, industrial smoke, defective workmanship, and money and securities remain excluded.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调投保了 IBC 4037 Broad Form，随后发生 sewer backup（下水道倒灌）、industrial smoke（工业烟熏）、cash stolen（现金被盗）或员工人数影响火险费率等干扰项。The stem highlights an IBC 4037 Broad Form policy, followed by distractors such as sewer backup, industrial smoke, cash stolen, or employee count affecting fire rates.

⚠ 犯罪险与上锁车辆缺证据Missing required evidence for crime and locked vehicle claims

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 只要看到东西被偷就选赔付，没核对入室盗窃（burglary）和上锁车辆保证（Locked Vehicle Warranty）必须同时具备「门窗锁闭」加「可见强行进入痕迹」的硬条件。Candidates choose coverage whenever property is stolen, without checking whether burglary and the Locked Vehicle Warranty strictly require both locked doors and windows plus visible marks of forcible entry.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 no visible marks of forcible entry（无可见强力痕迹）、unlocked window（车窗未关）、open flatbed truck（敞篷卡车）或 custodian unconscious（保管人昏迷）。The stem mentions no visible marks of forcible entry, unlocked window, open flatbed truck, or custodian unconscious.

商业财产三格与租户改良Three commercial property classes and tenant's improvementsbuilding / stock / equipment / tenant's improvements存货加设备合称内容物（contents），三格合称全部财产（all property）；租户自费或买下前任租户的装修因非建筑业主，一律归入设备（equipment）。Stock plus equipment is collectively termed contents, and all three classes combined are termed all property; improvements made at the tenant's expense or purchased from a prior tenant are classified under equipment because the tenant is not the building owner.
列名险表与广式表的差异Differences between Named Perils Form and Broad FormNamed Perils Form (IBC 4036) / Broad Form (IBC 4037)IBC 4036只保八项列名危险（无盗窃、无一般水损）；IBC 4037比4036多保自家或雇员车辆撞击、非农工烟熏、水管冻裂、盗窃和塌陷，但地震、洪水、渗水倒灌及缺陷工艺仍除外。IBC 4036 covers only eight named perils (no theft, no general water damage); IBC 4037 covers five additional areas over IBC 4036, including vehicle impact by owned or employee vehicles, non-agricultural and non-industrial smoke, frozen ruptured water pipes, theft, and collapse, but earthquake, flood, seepage, sewer backup, and defective workmanship remain excluded.
基本火险费率的五个因素Five underwriting factors of the basic fire ratebasic fire rate / loading基本火险费率按每 $100 保额计算（广式表另加附加费 loading），核保只看结构、公共与私人保护、占用、地点、过去五年理赔记录五项，不看员工人数或营业额。The basic fire rate is calculated per $100 of insurance (with an additional loading added for Broad Form coverage), and underwriting considers only five factors: construction, public and private protection, occupancy, location, and loss experience over the past five years, excluding staff headcount and business revenue.
共保条款计算与豁免门槛Coinsurance clause calculation and waiver thresholdco-insurance / waiver of co-insurance共保只管部分损失，赔付按「实际保额 Did ÷ 应保额 Should × 损失」计算（与保额、实际损失三者取最小）；若损失不超过适用保额的 2% 与 $5,000 中较小者，则豁免共保按实赔付。Coinsurance applies only to partial losses, with payment calculated as insurance carried (Did) divided by insurance required (Should) multiplied by loss (capped at the lesser of this calculation, the policy limit, or the actual loss); if the loss does not exceed the lesser of 2% of the applicable limit or $5,000, coinsurance is waived and paid in full.
残骸清理、恢复与代位求偿Debris removal, reinstatement, and subrogation waiverdebris removal / reinstatement / subrogation残骸清理含风暴吹来的外物但不增加保额（保额赔光则无清理费）；赔付后保额自动恢复且不加收保费；代位求偿对保单涉及其利益的收款人、合伙人、子公司放弃。Debris removal covers windblown debris from external property but does not increase the policy limit (no debris removal is paid if the limit is exhausted); policy limits are automatically reinstated after a loss without additional premium; subrogation is waived against payees, partners, and subsidiary companies having an interest in the policy.
防护系统通知义务与上锁车辆保证Protective safeguards notification duty and Locked Vehicle Warrantyprotective safeguards / Locked Vehicle Warranty被保险人仅对「已知」的防护系统缺陷、维护合同取消或警方因误报暂停出警负有立即通知义务；运输途中无人看管车辆被盗须满足全封闭车身、门锁、窗关及可见强行进入痕迹四要件。The insured has a duty of immediate notification only for known protective safeguard defects, maintenance contract cancellations, or police suspension of response due to false alarms; theft from an unattended vehicle in transit strictly requires four conditions: fully enclosed body, locked doors, closed windows, and visible marks of forcible entry.
三类犯罪险与现金证券保障Three crime coverage types and money and securities coverageburglary / robbery / theft / Money and Securities (Broad Form)入室盗窃（burglary）须证明进出点有可见强力痕迹；抢劫（robbery）须证明对保管人施暴威胁、保管人昏迷死亡或亲眼目睹被拿走；现金与证券在任何财产表下均除外，须另买专门保险。Burglary requires proof of visible marks of forcible entry or exit; robbery requires proof of actual or threatened violence against a custodian, taking while the custodian is killed or unconscious, or taking witnessed by the custodian; money and securities are excluded under all standard property forms and require separate coverage under Money and Securities (Broad Form).
锅炉机器险与建工险边界Boundaries of Boiler and Machinery and Builder's RiskBoiler and Machinery / Builder's Risk (IBC 4042)超过 15 psi 的压力容器爆炸及机械电气故障归锅炉机器险（检验员遇险可立即中止保障无需通知按揭权人）；建工险按完工价值投保，不保承包商工具，停工超 30 个连续日或被占用即终止。Explosion of pressure vessels exceeding 15 psi and mechanical or electrical breakdown fall under Boiler and Machinery insurance (inspectors discovering imminent danger may suspend coverage immediately without notifying mortgagees); Builder's Risk is insured on a completed value basis, excludes contractor tools, and terminates if work ceases for more than 30 consecutive days or upon occupancy.
营业中断与额外费用险对比Comparison of Business Interruption and Extra Expense insuranceEarnings Form / Profits Form / Extra Expense (IBC 4027)营业中断须由承保危险引发实际财产损失（民政当局禁入除外），通常赔付12个月且不受保单到期日限制；盈利式赔到修好，利润式赔到收入恢复至出险前水平，额外费用险不要求证明减少了损失。Business Interruption requires actual property damage by an insured peril (with an exception for civil authority access denial), generally provides 12 months of indemnity, and is not limited by policy expiration; the Earnings Form pays until property is repaired, the Profits Form pays until income is restored to pre-loss levels, and Extra Expense does not require proof that expenses reduced the loss.
共保警示与诉讼时效的考试口径Exam rules on coinsurance warning stamping and limitation periodlimitation period / co-insurance stamping考试口径下，限额类条款须在首页用红色墨水印警示（BC现行法为醒目粗体字），商业财产保单条件14的诉讼时效为损失发生后1年（BC现行《保险法》为自知道或应当知道起2年）。Under the exam standard, limitation clauses must bear a warning stamped in red ink on the first page (while current BC law requires conspicuous bold lettering), and the limitation period under Commercial Property Policy Condition 14 is one year from the date of loss (while the current BC Insurance Act allows two years from when the insured knew or ought to have known).

第 1 题Q1建筑、存货与设备的归类边界Classification boundaries among building, stock, and equipment★★陷阱：租户改良与植物错格

A commercial tenant operates a retail clothing boutique in a leased unit and purchases custom wall panelling and recessed lighting installed by the previous tenant. Which category of a commercial property policy covers these items?

一名商业租户在租来的铺位经营服装精品店，并买下了前一任租户安装的定制护墙板和嵌入式灯具。商业财产保单中的哪一格承保这些物品？

  1. ✗ 错在忽视了可保利益（insurable interest）。只有建筑业主自己做的永久固着物才归入建筑（building）；租户不是建筑业主，对建筑没有可保利益，这属于典型的「租户改良与植物错格」陷阱。Incorrect because it ignores insurable interest. Only permanent fixtures installed by the building owner belong to building; a tenant is not the building owner and holds no insurable interest in the building structure, falling into the trap of misclassifying tenant's improvements and plants.

  2. ✓ 正确。租户改良（tenant's improvements）由于租户不是建筑业主、不能投保建筑，一律归入设备（equipment）；租户买下前一任租户所做的改良，其使用权益视同自己出资所做，同样归入设备。Correct. Because a tenant is not the building owner and cannot insure the building structure, tenant's improvements are classified under equipment; purchasing improvements made by a prior tenant confers a use interest that is treated the same as if funded by the current tenant, falling under equipment.

  3. ✗ 错在混淆了存货（stock）与装修陈设。存货仅包括该业务惯常经营的待售货物、包装与广告材料，店内展示用的固定装修和设备不属于待售商品。Incorrect because it confuses stock with decorative fixtures. Stock includes only merchandise usual to the business held for sale, packing materials, and advertising supplies; permanent store fixtures and display equipment are not goods held for sale.

  4. ✗ 错在认为前任租户做的装修不可保。只要现租户花钱买下了前一任租户的改良，就拥有使用权益，视同自己出资所做，可归入设备（equipment）即内容物（contents）的一部分投保。Incorrect because it assumes improvements made by a prior tenant are uninsurable. As long as the current tenant purchases improvements made by a previous tenant, the tenant holds a use interest treated as own-funded improvements, insurable under equipment as part of contents.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A company owns its office building and keeps several items on the premises: live palm trees in the indoor lobby, silk floral arrangements in the reception area, and lumber stored for a major structural addition. Which of these is insured under the building category?

一家公司拥有自己的办公楼，并在处所内放有几样物品：室内大堂里的活棕榈树、前台区域的丝绸插花，以及为大型结构加建而存放的木材。其中哪一项由建筑（building）这一格承保？

  1. ✗ 错在把丝绸花也算作建筑（building）。只有业主自有建筑内用于装饰的活植物（live decorative plants）才属于建筑，办公室里的丝绸花属于设备，落入了「租户改良与植物错格」陷阱。Incorrect because it includes silk flowers under building. Only live decorative plants inside an owner-occupied building qualify as building; artificial silk plants belong to equipment, falling into the trap of misclassifying tenant's improvements and plants.

  2. ✗ 错在混淆了日常维护材料与大修翻新材料。只有为维护、日常修理和小改动放在处所上的材料才归建筑（building），大修或新建用的材料不在此列，需要另买建工险。Incorrect because it confuses routine maintenance materials with major renovation materials. Only materials kept on premises for maintenance, normal repairs, and minor alterations qualify as building; materials for major additions or new construction are excluded and require Builder's Risk insurance.

  3. ✗ 错在颠倒了建筑（building）的承保边界。丝绸花和大修木材都不算建筑，反而是业主自有建筑内用于装饰的活棕榈树明确归入建筑。Incorrect because it reverses the coverage boundaries of building. Neither silk flowers nor lumber for a major addition qualify as building, whereas live palm trees kept for decorative purposes inside an owner-occupied building are explicitly covered under building.

  4. ✓ 正确。在业主自有建筑（owner-occupied building）内用于装饰的活植物属于建筑（building）范畴；丝绸花、门前户外种的树、大修翻新材料都不属于建筑。Correct. Live plants kept for decorative purposes inside an owner-occupied building fall under building; silk flowers, outdoor trees, and major renovation materials are not covered under building.

第 2 题Q2共保公式计算与被保险人自担额Coinsurance formula calculation and insured share of loss★★★陷阱：偷换所问（赔款 vs 自担额）

A commercial building has an actual cash value of $400,000 and is insured for $240,000 under a policy with an 80% co-insurance clause. If a covered fire causes a $60,000 partial loss, how much of the loss must the insured bear personally?

一栋商业建筑的实际现金价值（ACV）为 $400,000，在带有 80% 共保条款的保单下投保了 $240,000。如果承保火灾造成了 $60,000 的部分损失，被保险人自己必须承担多少损失？

  1. ✗ 错在掉进了「偷换所问（赔款 vs 自担额）」陷阱。按共保公式（co-insurance formula）Did $240,000 ÷ Should $320,000 × $60,000 算出的 $45,000 是保险人赔付的金额，而题干问的是被保险人自己承担多少。Incorrect because it falls into the trap of switching the question (insurer payout vs insured share). Applying the coinsurance formula of Did ($240,000) divided by Should ($320,000) times $60,000 yields $45,000, which is the insurer's payout, whereas the question asks how much the insured must bear personally.

  2. ✗ 错在误以为未达到 80% 共保要求（$320,000）会导致部分损失完全拒赔。不足额投保只是按比例分摊损失，不会使保单失效。Incorrect because it assumes failing to meet the 80% coinsurance requirement ($320,000) results in total denial of a partial loss. Underinsuring merely reduces partial loss payouts proportionally; it does not void coverage.

第 7 题Q7营业中断与额外费用险的触发与期限Trigger and indemnity period for Business Interruption and Extra Expense★★陷阱：广式表当成「包赔一切」

Shortly before its commercial property policy expires, a bakery suffers a covered fire that heavily damages its ovens. The bakery has a Business Interruption (Profits Form) endorsement with a 12-month indemnity period, and after reopening it experiences a drop in sales because regular customers switched to competitors during the closure. How will the Profits Form respond?

在商业财产保单即将到期前，一家烘焙店遭遇承保火灾，烤箱严重受损。该店附有 12 个月赔付期的营业中断（利润式 Profits Form）批单，且在重新开业后，由于关门期间老顾客流失到竞争对手那里，营业额出现下滑。利润式批单将如何赔付？

  1. ✗ 错在误以为营业中断受保单到期日限制。营业中断（Business Interruption）从损失日起通常赔 12 个月，期间就算保单到期也照赔不误。Incorrect because it assumes business interruption is cut off by policy expiration. Business Interruption coverage generally provides an indemnity period of 12 months from the date of loss and continues even if the policy expires during that period.

  2. ✗ 错在把利润式（Profits Form）答成了盈利式（Earnings Form）。赔到财产修复、重建或替换完成即止的是盈利式，而非利润式。Incorrect because it confuses the Profits Form with the Earnings Form. Coverage that ends as soon as the damaged property is repaired, rebuilt, or replaced is the Earnings Form, not the Profits Form.

  3. ✓ 正确。营业中断险不受保单到期日（policy expiration date）限制（从损失日起通常赔 12 个月），且利润式（Profits Form）会一直赔到营业收入恢复到没有损失时的应有水平（包含关门期间流失客户造成的收入下降）。Correct. Business Interruption coverage is not terminated by policy expiration (ordinarily providing 12 months of indemnity from the date of loss), and the Profits Form pays until business income is restored to normal pre-loss levels, including ongoing revenue declines caused by customer turnover during closure.

  4. ✗ 错在把营业中断当成了仅报销减损费用。营业中断的核心是赔付持续的费用（continuing expenses）加失去的利润（lost profits）。Incorrect because it treats business interruption as merely reimbursing loss mitigation expenses. The core purpose of business interruption is to indemnify for continuing fixed expenses plus net profits lost due to the interruption.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An insurance brokerage wants to ensure that if a fire destroys its office, it can immediately rent temporary quarters and lease computers at higher-than-normal cost so that operations never stop. Which coverage is specifically designed for this need?

一家保险经纪行希望确保万一办公室被大火烧毁，能够立即以高于平时的成本租用临时场所和电脑，使业务不中断。哪一种保障是专门为此需求设计的？

  1. ✗ 错在险种定位不符。盈利式（Earnings Form）保的是停业期间失去的利润和持续费用，赔到财产修好为止，而经纪行需要的是不停业继续运转的多花费用。Incorrect because the coverage objective does not match. The Earnings Form covers lost profits and continuing expenses during a business shutdown until property is repaired, whereas the brokerage needs reimbursement for excess costs incurred to prevent any shutdown.

  2. ✓ 正确。额外费用险（Extra Expense Insurance, IBC 4027）专保损失后继续正常营业所需的一切比平时多出来的额外费用，不要求证明这些费用减少了索赔，最适合保险经纪、旅行社、医生、报社、银行这类停几天都受不了的业务。Correct. Extra Expense Insurance (IBC 4027) is specifically designed to pay all necessary extra costs above normal operating expenses incurred to maintain continuous business operations following a loss, without requiring proof that these expenditures reduced the insurer's loss, making it ideal for insurance brokers, travel agencies, medical clinics, newspapers, and banks.

  3. ✗ 错在混淆险种。建工险（Builder's Risk）保的是在建工程的材料（按完工价值买，停工超 30 天终止），不保办公室灾后临时租房和租电脑的额外营业费用。Incorrect because it confuses policy forms. Builder's Risk covers property in the course of construction on a completed value basis (terminating if work ceases for more than 30 consecutive days); it does not cover extra operating costs for temporary premises and equipment rental.

  4. ✗ 错在混淆了安装流动险（Installation Floater）。安装流动险保的是专做机器设备安装的承包商从运输、等待安装到安装验收期间的物损，不保灾后维持营业的额外费用。Incorrect because it confuses the form with an Installation Floater. An Installation Floater covers machinery and equipment installed by specialized contractors during transit, staging, and testing until accepted; it does not cover extra operating expenses to sustain business continuity.

答案Answer：b
