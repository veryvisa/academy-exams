本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 三账户额度计算与结转题：按上年赚取收入算 RRSP 额度（18% 与 2026 年上限 33,810 元取小）、TFSA 年度额度 7,000 元的累积，以及 FHSA 参与额度（每年 8,000 元）只在开户后累积、结转封顶 8,000 元。Room calculation and carryforward for the three accounts: RRSP room from prior-year earned income (the lesser of 18% and the 2026 cap of $33,810), the accumulation of the annual TFSA room of $7,000, and FHSA participation room ($8,000 a year) that builds up only after the account is opened, with the carryforward capped at $8,000.
  • 取款性质判定与报表行号归属题：混合考查RRSP普通提取（line 12900全额计税加预扣）、HBP/LLP合格提取（免税进Schedule 7）、RRIF最低提取额（line 11500不预扣税）以及TFSA/FHSA免税出口，核心测试考生对不同账户出口课税待遇的区分。Character of withdrawals and which return line they belong on: mixes an ordinary RRSP withdrawal (fully taxed on line 12900 with withholding), a qualifying HBP/LLP withdrawal (tax-free, reported on Schedule 7), the RRIF minimum amount (line 11500, no withholding) and the tax-free TFSA/FHSA exits. The core skill is distinguishing the tax treatment of each exit.
  • 超额供款与罚税题：RRSP 的 2,000 元免罚缓冲（不能扣除）、同月取出免税、T1-OVP 期限（历年结束后 90 天），以及 TFSA 超额与非居民供款两条独立的 1%/月税（RC243）。Over-contributions and penalty taxes: the RRSP $2,000 buffer (no penalty but not deductible), withdrawal in the same month to avoid tax, the T1-OVP deadline (90 days after the end of the calendar year), and the two separate 1% per month taxes on TFSA excess and non-resident contributions (RC243).
  • 配偶计划与归属规则日历窗口辨析题：针对配偶RRSP提取场景，考查ITA s.146(8.3)提取当年及前两个纳税年度的归属窗口期计算，核心测试考生识别「12月存锁25个月、1月存锁36个月」的日历规划节点与分居/RRIF最低额豁免。Spousal plan and the attribution rule calendar window: for a spousal RRSP withdrawal, tests the ITA s.146(8.3) window of the year of withdrawal and the two preceding taxation years. The core skill is recognizing the calendar planning points "a December deposit locks 25 months, a January deposit locks 36 months" and the separation and RRIF minimum exceptions.
  • 生命周期终点与制度转换实务题：考查71岁年底RRSP必须转为RRIF或年金（否则视同全额提取入税）、RRIF次年起按年初市值乘法定因子提取最低额（可选更年轻配偶年龄），以及FHSA最长参与期（15年、71岁、首提次年底）到期未关户全额并入收入的严厉后果。Life-cycle end points and plan conversion in practice: the RRSP must be converted to a RRIF or annuity by the end of the year the holder turns 71 (otherwise it is deemed fully withdrawn and taxed), the RRIF minimum is the opening market value times a statutory factor starting the following year (a younger spouse's age may be chosen), and the severe result when an FHSA is not closed at the end of the maximum participation period (15 years, age 71, or the end of the year after the first withdrawal): the full balance is added to income.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ TFSA同年取存额度恢复陷阱TFSA withdraw-and-redeposit in the same year trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 纳税人常误以为从TFSA取出的资金会立即可供再次存入，实际上当年提取的资金必须等到次年1月1日才会全额恢复为供款额度，同年存回直接造成超额供款并触发1%/月的Part XI.01罚税。Taxpayers often assume funds withdrawn from a TFSA can immediately be contributed again. In fact, the amount withdrawn in a year is added back to contribution room only on January 1 of the following year. Putting it back in the same year creates an excess contribution and triggers the Part XI.01 tax of 1% per month.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「withdrew funds from TFSA and re-contributed in the same calendar year」「available room exceeded」「1% per month tax」等字眼。The question mentions "withdrew funds from TFSA and re-contributed in the same calendar year", "available room exceeded" or "1% per month tax".

⚠ FHSA跨年60天规则类比陷阱FHSA "60-day rule" analogy trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生容易将RRSP独特的次年前60天供款窗口（次年1月至2月）错误套用到FHSA上，忽略了FHSA当年的供款截止日严格锁定在当年的12月31日，次年年初供款无法倒算回上一税年扣除。Candidates easily apply the RRSP's special window for contributions in the first 60 days of the following year (January to February) to the FHSA. They overlook that the FHSA contribution deadline for a year is strictly December 31 of that year, and a contribution early in the next year cannot be applied back to the prior tax year's deduction.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「contributed to FHSA in January or February」「claim deduction for previous year」「first 60 days rule」「March 1 deadline」。The question mentions "contributed to FHSA in January or February", "claim deduction for previous year", "first 60 days rule" or "March 1 deadline".

⚠ RRSP两千元缓冲误作扣除额陷阱Treating the RRSP $2,000 buffer as a deduction trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为年满 18 岁者的 2,000 元超额缓冲是额外的可扣除额度。它只是免于 1%/月 Part X.1 税的安全区，不能在 line 20800 扣除。Thinking the $2,000 over-contribution buffer for people aged 18 or over is extra deductible room. It is only a safe zone that avoids the Part X.1 tax of 1% per month, and it cannot be deducted on line 20800.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「2,000 over-contribution buffer」「claim line 20800 deduction」「Part X.1 tax relief」。The question mentions "$2,000 over-contribution buffer", "claim line 20800 deduction" or "Part X.1 tax relief".

⚠ 配偶RRSP归属日历年计算陷阱Spousal RRSP attribution calendar-year counting trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 纳税人常将配偶RRSP归属规则（ITA s.146(8.3)）的「提取当年加前两个纳税年度」按365天满打满算，忽略了税法是以税收日历年度为准。12月供款仅锁25个月，次年1月供款则需锁定36个月。Taxpayers often count the spousal RRSP attribution rule (ITA s.146(8.3)), "the year of withdrawal plus the two preceding taxation years", as a full 365-day count. The law works on taxation calendar years. A December contribution is locked for only 25 months, while a January contribution of the next year is locked for 36 months.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「spousal RRSP withdrawal」「contributed in December vs January」「attribution to contributor」「two preceding taxation years」。The question mentions "spousal RRSP withdrawal", "contributed in December vs January", "attribution to contributor" or "two preceding taxation years".

⚠ 非居民TFSA供款独立罚税陷阱Separate penalty on non-resident TFSA contributions trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 纳税人误以为只要自己TFSA累计未用额度充足，移居海外成为非税务居民后依然可以继续存钱，忽略了ITA s.207.03明确规定非居民期间的任何供款按月全额征收1%罚税，无论额度是否用满。Taxpayers wrongly assume that as long as their cumulative unused TFSA room is sufficient, they can keep contributing after moving abroad and becoming a non-resident for tax purposes. They overlook that ITA s.207.03 expressly imposes the 1% monthly tax on any contribution made while non-resident, whether or not the room is used up.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「non-resident of Canada」「had unused TFSA contribution room」「contributed while living abroad」「Form RC243」。The question mentions "non-resident of Canada", "had unused TFSA contribution room", "contributed while living abroad" or "Form RC243".

第 1 题Q1RRSP额度计算与年限封顶RRSP room calculation and the annual cap★★陷阱：RRSP额度公式计算陷阱

In 2025, Liam had an earned income of $110,000 from employment and net rental income. His 2025 T4 slip reported a Pension Adjustment (PA) of $4,500. Liam's Notice of Assessment for the 2025 tax year indicated an unused RRSP contribution room carried forward of $8,000. In the 2026 tax year, the statutory annual RRSP limit is $33,810. What is Liam's RRSP deduction limit for the 2026 tax year?

2025年，Liam 的就业与租金净收入等赚取收入（earned income）共计$110,000。他2025年的 T4 凭单上显示养老金调整额（PA）为$4,500。Liam 的2025税年评税通知书（NOA）显示结转的未用 RRSP 额度为$8,000。2026税年法定 RRSP 年度上限为$33,810。Liam 在2026税年的 RRSP 扣除限额（RRSP deduction limit）是多少？

  1. ✓ 正确。额度＝结转 8,000 元＋min（110,000 × 18% ＝ 19,800 元，上限 33,810 元）− PA 4,500 元＝ 23,300 元。Correct. Room = carryforward $8,000 + min($110,000 x 18% = $19,800, cap $33,810) - PA $4,500 = $23,300.

  2. ✗ 错误。漏扣 PA，会得到 27,800 元。雇主养老金已享受的递延必须从次年 RRSP 额度里扣掉。Incorrect. Forgetting to subtract the PA gives $27,800. The deferral already enjoyed through the employer pension must be deducted from next year's RRSP room.

  3. ✗ 错误。上限只是封顶值；18% × 110,000 ＝ 19,800 元低于 33,810 元，应按 19,800 元算。Incorrect. The cap is only a ceiling. 18% x $110,000 = $19,800 is below $33,810, so $19,800 is used.

  4. ✗ 错误。往年未用额度无限期结转，要并入当年额度；漏掉会少算成 15,300 元。Incorrect. Unused room from prior years carries forward indefinitely and must be added to this year's room. Leaving it out understates the figure at $15,300.

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

In 2025, Evelyn had an earned income of $210,000 as a corporate executive. Her 2025 T4 slip reported a Pension Adjustment (PA) of $12,000. Her Notice of Assessment for 2025 showed zero unused RRSP contribution room carried forward. The 2026 statutory annual RRSP dollar limit is $33,810. What is Evelyn's RRSP deduction limit for the 2026 tax year?

2025年，Evelyn 作为企业高管的赚取收入为$210,000。她2025年的 T4 凭单上显示养老金调整额（PA）为$12,000。她2025年的评税通知书显示结转的未用 RRSP 额度为$0。2026税年法定 RRSP 年度法定上限为$33,810。Evelyn 在2026税年的 RRSP 扣除限额是多少？

  1. ✗ 属于「忽略年度上限封顶陷阱」。$210,000 × 18% ＝ $37,800，已经超过了2026年法定上限$33,810，必须被强行封顶在$33,810。This is the "ignoring the annual cap trap". $210,000 x 18% = $37,800 already exceeds the 2026 statutory cap of $33,810 and must be capped at $33,810.

  2. ✗ 属于「遗漏扣减PA陷阱」。即使触及最高限额，在雇主年金计划中积累的PA（$12,000）仍须从当年可用额度中全额扣除。This is the "forgetting to subtract the PA trap". Even when the maximum is reached, the PA ($12,000) accumulated in an employer pension plan must still be deducted in full from the room available for the year.

  3. ✓ 正确。Evelyn 的 210,000 × 18% ＝ 37,800 元超过 2026 年上限 $33,810，按上限封顶；再减 2025 年 PA $12,000，额度为 $21,810。Correct. Evelyn's $210,000 x 18% = $37,800 exceeds the 2026 cap of $33,810, so the cap applies; subtracting the 2025 PA of $12,000 leaves room of $21,810.

  4. ✗ 属于「纯比例裸算错误」。既未考虑法定封顶上限$33,810，也未扣除企业年金PA调整额。This is the "bare percentage error". It considers neither the statutory cap of $33,810 nor the pension adjustment for the employer plan.

答案Answer：c

第 2 题Q2TFSA取存时点与超额罚税TFSA withdrawal and redeposit timing and the excess tax★★陷阱：TFSA同年取存额度恢复陷阱

On January 1, 2026, Brandon had a TFSA contribution room of exactly $7,000 (having used all prior contribution room). In February 2026, Brandon contributed $7,000 into his TFSA. In June 2026, needing cash, he withdrew $5,000 from his TFSA. In September 2026, having surplus funds, he re-deposited $5,000 back into his TFSA and kept it there for the rest of 2026. What is the tax consequence of Brandon's actions for the 2026 tax year?

2026年1月1日，Brandon 的 TFSA 供款额度恰好为$7,000（此前历年额度已全部用满）。2026年2月，Brandon 向其 TFSA 存入$7,000。2026年6月因需要资金，他从 TFSA 提取了$5,000。2026年9月资金宽裕后，他又将$5,000重新存入该 TFSA 并保留至年底。Brandon 的上述操作在2026税年将产生何种税务后果？

  1. ✗ 属于「TFSA同年取存额度恢复陷阱」。纳税人误以为年内取款即时恢复额度，税法规定提取额必须等到次年1月1日才变回可用额度。This is the "TFSA same-year withdraw-and-redeposit trap". The taxpayer wrongly assumes room is restored immediately by a withdrawal in the year; the law restores it only on January 1 of the next year.

  2. ✓ 正确。当年提取的 5,000 元要到次年 1 月 1 日才恢复额度；2 月已用尽当年 7,000 元，9 月存回的 5,000 元全额超额，9 至 12 月共 4 个月，税额 5,000 × 1% × 4 ＝ 200 元，用 RC243 申报。Correct. The $5,000 withdrawn in the year does not restore room until January 1 of the next year. The year's $7,000 was already used up in February, so the $5,000 put back in September is entirely excess. September to December is 4 months, and the tax is $5,000 x 1% x 4 = $200, reported on RC243.

  3. ✗ 属于「TFSA套用RRSP缓冲额陷阱」。$2,000免罚缓冲额是RRSP特有的规定，TFSA根本没有任何免罚缓冲，超出1元即全额按1%/月罚税。This is the "applying the RRSP buffer to the TFSA trap". The $2,000 penalty-free buffer is specific to the RRSP; the TFSA has no buffer at all, and every dollar over is taxed at 1% per month.

  4. ✗ 属于「夸大惩罚后果陷阱」。超额供款仅按月计征1%特别税，不会剥夺账户资格或变更为劳动报酬课税。This is the "exaggerated penalty trap". An excess contribution is subject only to a special tax of 1% per month. It does not remove the account's eligibility or turn the earnings into taxable remuneration.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

On January 1, 2026, Grace had an unused TFSA contribution room of $4,000 carried forward, plus her new 2026 annual room of $7,000 (total available room: $11,000).   1. ✗ 属于「非居民TFSA账户内收益误征税陷阱」。加拿大税法明确规定TFSA账户内的增值与分红即使持有人为非居民，在加拿大一侧依然享受免税待遇。This is the "taxing earnings inside a non-resident's TFSA trap". Canadian tax law states that growth and dividends inside a TFSA remain tax-exempt on the Canadian side even if the holder is a non-resident.

  2. ✗ 属于「持有即受罚误读陷阱」。非居民完全可以合法保留离境前开立的TFSA，只要不进行新供款，绝不会产生每月1%罚税。This is the "holding alone is penalized misreading trap". A non-resident can lawfully keep a TFSA opened before leaving, and as long as no new contribution is made, no 1% monthly penalty arises.

  3. ✗ 属于「身份变更强制除名错误」。税法允许非居民继续持有TFSA外壳，无需强制转换为常规应税账户。This is the "forced removal on change of status error". The law allows a non-resident to keep the TFSA wrapper, and no forced conversion to an ordinary taxable account is required.

  4. ✓ 正确。根据讲义第二节2.3「离境后TFSA的完整待遇」，成为非税务居民后，纳税人已有的TFSA账户可以继续保留，账户内的股息、利息与资本利得在加拿大一侧完全免税（居住国如英国/美国可能课税）。非居民受限的仅是「不得存入新资金」和「全年在册非居民不产生新额度」。Correct. Under "the full treatment of a TFSA after leaving Canada" in section 2.3 of the lecture, after becoming a non-resident for tax purposes the taxpayer's existing TFSA can be kept, and dividends, interest and capital gains inside it remain fully exempt on the Canadian side (the country of residence, such as the UK or the US, may tax them). What a non-resident cannot do is contribute new money, and no new room accrues for the years the holder is a non-resident.

答案Answer：d

第 7 题Q771岁RRIF最低额与因子选择RRIF minimum at 71 and factor choice★★★陷阱：RRIF最低提取额预扣税误解陷阱

In December 2026, Edward turns 71 and converts his entire $600,000 RRSP into a RRIF. His spouse turns 65 in 2026. The RRIF is worth $600,000 on January 1, 2027. Which statement about the RRIF minimum amounts for 2026 and 2027, the spouse's-age election and withholding tax is correct?

Edward 2026 年 12 月满 71 岁，把全部 $600,000 RRSP 转成 RRIF；他的配偶 2026 年满 65 岁。2027 年 1 月 1 日 RRIF 市值 $600,000。关于 2026、2027 年的最低提取额、按配偶年龄计算的选择以及预扣税，下列哪项正确？

  1. ✗ 属于「成立当年有最低额与强制预扣误解」。RRIF在成立当年（2026）法定最低提取额为零；且法定最低提取额依法豁免预扣税。This is the "minimum in the year of opening and mandatory withholding misunderstanding". In the year a RRIF is opened (2026), the statutory minimum is zero, and the statutory minimum is by law exempt from withholding tax.

  2. ✗ 属于「剥夺配偶年龄选择权陷阱」。税法明确允许持有人在首次受领支付前一次性选定按更年轻配偶的年龄计算最低额。This is the "denying the choice of spouse's age trap". The law expressly allows the holder to elect once, before the first payment, to base the minimum on a younger spouse's age.

  3. ✓ 正确。RRIF 成立当年最低额为零，次年起才有。首次付款前可一次性选择按配偶年龄计算：配偶 2027 年初 65 岁，因子 1 ÷（90 − 65）＝ 4.00%，低于 Edward 的 5.28%，最低额由 31,680 元降到 24,000 元。最低额本身不预扣税。Correct. The minimum is zero in the year a RRIF is established and applies only from the next year. Before the first payment the holder may elect once to use the spouse's age: the spouse is 65 at the start of 2027, factor 1 / (90 - 65) = 4.00%, which is lower than Edward's 5.28%, so the minimum falls from $31,680 to $24,000. The minimum itself is not subject to withholding.

  4. ✗ 属于「最低额与超额预扣混淆陷阱」。只有超过最低提取额的部分才按10/20/30%分档预扣税，最低额本身完全不预扣。This is the "confusing the minimum with the excess withholding trap". Only the part above the minimum is withheld at the 10/20/30% tiers; the minimum itself is not withheld at all.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Leonard turned 71 in 2025 and established his RRIF. On January 1, 2026, Leonard is 71 years old (turning 72 later in the year), and the fair market value of his RRIF is $400,000. Under Income Tax Regulations s.7308(4), the prescribed factor for age 71 is 5.28%. Leonard withdraws a total of $35,000 from his RRIF in 2026. What is the statutory minimum withdrawal amount, and how is withholding tax applied by the financial institution on this $35,000 withdrawal?

Leonard 于2025年年满71岁并设立了 RRIF。2026年1月1日，Leonard 年初年龄为71岁（年内晚些时候满72岁），其 RRIF 的公允市值为$400,000。根据《所得税条例》s.7308(4)，71岁对应的法定因子为5.28%。Leonard 于2026年共从该 RRIF 提取了$35,000现金。该 RRIF 当年的法定最低提取额是多少？金融机构对这笔$35,000提取如何代扣预扣税？

  1. ✓ 正确。年初 71 岁因子 5.28%，最低额 400,000 × 5.28% ＝ 21,120 元，不预扣。超出部分 35,000 − 21,120 ＝ 13,880 元落在 5,000 至 15,000 元档，预扣 20%，即 2,776 元。Correct. At the start of the year the factor for age 71 is 5.28%, so the minimum is $400,000 x 5.28% = $21,120, with no withholding. The excess, $35,000 - $21,120 = $13,880, falls in the $5,000 to $15,000 tier, where 20% is withheld, which is $2,776.

  2. ✗ 属于「全额按最高档预扣错误」。最低提取额依法不预扣，且超额部分$13,880在$15,000以下，预扣税率为20%而非30%。This is the "withholding the whole amount at the top tier error". The minimum is by law not withheld, and the $13,880 excess is below $15,000, so the withholding rate is 20%, not 30%.

  3. ✗ 属于「超额提取免预扣误解」。超出法定最低额的提取款项必须依法按10/20/30%分档代扣代缴预扣税。This is the "no withholding on an excess withdrawal misunderstanding". A withdrawal above the statutory minimum must by law be withheld at the 10/20/30% tiers.

  4. ✗ 属于「虚构延期至75岁陷阱」。71岁是硬性分水岭，从满71岁的次年（72岁那年年初）起每年必须强制提取最低额。This is the "invented deferral to 75 trap". Age 71 is a hard dividing line: from the year after the holder turns 71 (the start of the year in which the holder turns 72), the minimum must be withdrawn every year.
