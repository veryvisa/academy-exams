① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • Slip与行号对应及防重报题：给出 T4A(OAS)、T4A(P)、T4A、T4RIF 等税单与具体框号，考你区分 Line 11300、Line 11400、Line 11500、Line 13000 的归属，特别是识别 T4A(P) box 16 伤残仅为 Line 11410 备注不可重复计入，以及受益人直领死亡给付走 Line 13000。Slip-to-line mapping and avoiding double reporting: gives T4A(OAS), T4A(P), T4A and T4RIF slips with specific box numbers and tests which of Line 11300, Line 11400, Line 11500 and Line 13000 each belongs on, especially recognizing that T4A(P) box 16 disability is only a memo on Line 11410 and must not be added again, and that a death benefit received directly by a beneficiary goes on Line 13000.
  • 养老金收入额与65岁门槛题：给出未满 65 岁 vs 满 65 岁退休人士的收入明细（RPP 定期年金、RRIF 提取、RRSP 提款、OAS/CPP），考你判定哪些收入属于 Line 31400 的合格养老金收入，牢记 OAS/CPP 无论何时均不合格、RRIF 需满 65 岁或因配偶去世承继。Pension income amount and the age 65 threshold: gives income details for retirees under 65 and at 65 or over (RPP periodic annuity, RRIF withdrawals, RRSP withdrawals, OAS/CPP) and tests which income is qualifying pension income for Line 31400, remembering that OAS/CPP never qualify at any age and that RRIF needs age 65 or inheritance on a spouse's death.
  • 退休收入分割（T1032）资格与计算题：给出夫妻双方年龄与各类养老金构成，考你判定哪些收入可做最高 50% 的联合选择分割（RPP vs RRIF 的年龄限制），排除 OAS 与 CPP，并掌握省表自动沿用联邦分割结果的规则。Retirement income splitting (T1032) eligibility and calculation: gives the spouses' ages and the make-up of their pensions and tests which income can be split under the joint election up to 50% (age limits for RPP versus RRIF), excluding OAS and CPP, and the rule that the provincial form automatically follows the federal split.
  • 股息毛额化与抵免双轨计算题：给出合资格（138% 毛额化 / 15.0198% DTC）或非合资格（115% 毛额化 / 9.0301% DTC）股息，考你计算 Line 12000 应税金额、Line 12010 复核项以及 Line 40425 联邦抵免，并区分有 slip 直接抄框号与无 slip 自算的实务操作。Dividend gross-up and the two-track credit calculation: gives eligible (138% gross-up / 15.0198% DTC) or non-eligible (115% gross-up / 9.0301% DTC) dividends and tests calculating the taxable amount on Line 12000, the Line 12010 check item and the federal credit on Line 40425, and distinguishing copying slip box numbers directly from self-calculating without a slip.
  • 注册账户提款与防避税规则题：考核 RRSP 阶梯预扣税率（10%/20%/30%）、拆单不降税率原则、配偶供款三年追溯归属（s.146(8.3)），以及 RRIF 首年最低提取额为零和借用年轻配偶年龄拉长递延期的规划要点。Registered account withdrawals and anti-avoidance rules: tests the RRSP tiered withholding rates (10%/20%/30%), the principle that splitting a withdrawal does not lower the rate, the three-year spousal contribution attribution (s.146(8.3)), and planning points that the first-year RRIF minimum is zero and a younger spouse's age may be used to lengthen deferral.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ OAS回收直接报净额Reporting the OAS recovery as a net amount

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为 OAS 既然被按超额 15% 扣回了（clawback），报税时就应该像预扣税一样直接从 Line 11300 减去净额申报。Believing that because OAS has been clawed back at 15% of the excess, the return should subtract it directly from Line 11300 like withholding tax and report the net amount.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出 T4A(OAS) box 18 总额以及纳税人当年净收入超过 95,323 元，问 Line 11300 应该填入多少金额。The stem gives the T4A(OAS) box 18 total and net income above $95,323 for the year, and asks what amount to enter on Line 11300.

⚠ 未满65岁借RRIF抵免Borrowing the RRIF credit before age 65

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为只要把 RRSP 转成了 RRIF，拿到了 T4RIF 提款，就能在任何年龄享受 Line 31400 的 $2,000 养老金收入额抵免。Thinking that once an RRSP has been converted to a RRIF and T4RIF withdrawals are received, the $2,000 pension income amount credit on Line 31400 is available at any age.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调纳税人年龄未满 65 岁（如 62 岁），主要收入来源为 RRIF 提取或个人 RRSP 年金，问是否有资格申报 pension income amount。The stem stresses that the taxpayer is under 65 (for example 62), with RRIF withdrawals or a personal RRSP annuity as the main income, and asks whether the pension income amount can be claimed.

⚠ 收到Slip才申报利息Reporting interest only if a slip is received

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误将金融机构的 $50 发单门槛当成法定义务门槛，以为银行没发 T5 表就代表不用报税。Mistaking the financial institution's $50 slip-issuing threshold for a legal reporting threshold, and thinking that if the bank issued no T5 nothing needs to be reported.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干中纳税人存款账户全年利息收入为 35 元或 42 元，银行未出具 T5 slip，问纳税人在 T1 表上是否需要申报该笔利息。The stem says the taxpayer's savings account earned $35 or $42 of interest for the year, the bank issued no T5 slip, and asks whether the interest must be reported on the T1.

⚠ 直接拿实收股息报收入Reporting the cash dividend received

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 习惯按实际到账现金报税，忘记加拿大公司股息实行「整合制」，必须按 138% 或 115% 毛额化后申报应税金额。Habitually reporting the actual cash received, forgetting that Canadian corporate dividends follow an integration system and must be reported as the taxable amount after a 138% or 115% gross-up.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出实际收到的合资格或非合资格股息金额（如 10,000 元），问应计入 Line 12000 的金额或应计算的股息税收抵免。The stem gives the actual eligible or non-eligible dividend received (for example $10,000) and asks for the amount to include on Line 12000 or the dividend tax credit to calculate.

⚠ OAS与CPP做T1032分割Splitting OAS and CPP on T1032

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 听到「退休收入可以夫妻五五分」，就顺理成章把政府发放的 OAS 和 CPP 给付也一起纳入 T1032 分割。Hearing that retirement income can be split 50/50 between spouses, candidates assume that government OAS and CPP benefits can also be split.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干中退休夫妻一方主要收入是 OAS 和 CPP，另一方收入低，问能否通过提交 Form T1032 将其 50% 转给配偶节税。The stem has one retired spouse whose main income is OAS and CPP and the other with low income, and asks whether filing Form T1032 can transfer 50% to the spouse to save tax.

第 1 题Q1养老金收入额合格范围判定Which income qualifies for the pension income amount★★陷阱：未满65岁借RRIF抵免

Martin, aged 62, retired during the current tax year. He received $14,000 from an employer Registered Pension Plan (RPP) periodic annuity, $6,000 from a Registered Retirement Income Fund (RRIF) withdrawal, and $4,500 in Canada Pension Plan (CPP) retirement benefits. None of these amounts were received as a consequence of a spouse's death. What is Martin's qualifying pension income for calculating the federal pension income amount on line 31400?

马丁（Martin）现年 62 岁，于当前税年退休。他收到了 $14,000 的雇主注册养老金计划（RPP）定期年金、$6,000 的注册退休收入基金（RRIF）提款，以及 $4,500 的加拿大退休金计划（CPP）退休给付。这些款项均非因配偶去世而承继。在计算 Line 31400 的联邦养老金收入额时，马丁的合格养老金收入是多少？

  1. ✗ 错误。此选项将 RPP、RRIF 和 CPP 全部加总（$14,000 + $6,000 + $4,500）。落入了「将所有退休性质的收入一概当作合格养老金」的陷阱。CPP 属于第二层强制社会保险，在任何年龄都不得计入合格养老金收入（qualified pension income）；且纳税人未满 65 岁，RRIF 提款同样不合格。Incorrect. This adds up the RPP, RRIF and CPP ($14,000 + $6,000 + $4,500) and falls into the trap of treating all retirement-type income as qualifying pension income. CPP is a second-pillar mandatory social insurance and can never be included in qualifying pension income at any age; and since the taxpayer is under 65, the RRIF withdrawals do not qualify either.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据税法规定，联邦养老金收入额（pension income amount, line 31400）的合格范围按 65 岁划分。纳税人未满 65 岁且非因配偶身故承继时，合格养老金收入仅限于雇主注册养老金计划（Registered Pension Plan, RPP）的定期给付。RRIF 提取须年满 65 岁才合格，而 CPP 任何年龄均不属于合格养老金收入，因此仅有 $14,000 RPP 符合资格。Correct. Under the tax rules, the qualifying scope of the federal pension income amount (Line 31400) is divided at age 65. When the taxpayer is under 65 and has not inherited because of a spouse's death, qualifying pension income is limited to periodic payments from an employer Registered Pension Plan (RPP). RRIF withdrawals qualify only from age 65, and CPP never qualifies at any age, so only the $14,000 RPP qualifies.

  3. ✗ 错误。此选项包含了 $14,000 的 RPP 和 $6,000 的 RRIF，排除了 CPP。这落入了「以为转入 RRIF 就能在任何年龄享受养老金抵免」的陷阱。在纳税人未满 65 岁且未发生配偶身故承继的情况下，RRIF 提款并不属于合格养老金收入。Incorrect. This includes the $14,000 RPP and the $6,000 RRIF but excludes CPP. It falls into the trap of thinking that converting to a RRIF gives the pension credit at any age. When the taxpayer is under 65 and no inheritance on a spouse's death has occurred, RRIF withdrawals are not qualifying pension income.

  4. ✗ 错误。此选项混淆了「合格养老金收入总额」（qualifying pension income）与「联邦养老金收入额的最高法定抵免基数」（maximum pension income amount of $2,000）。题干询问的是计算抵免前的合格收入金额，而非最终抵免限额。Incorrect. This confuses the 'total qualifying pension income' with the 'maximum pension income amount base of $2,000'. The stem asks for the qualifying income before the credit is calculated, not the final credit limit.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Evelyn, aged 67, received $8,000 from an Old Age Security (OAS) pension, $5,000 in Canada Pension Plan (CPP) retirement benefits, and $12,000 from Registered Retirement Income Fund (RRIF) withdrawals during the current tax year. How much of these receipts qualifies as eligible pension income for the federal pension income amount on line 31400?

艾芙琳（Evelyn）现年 67 岁，在当前税年收到了 $8,000 的老年保障金（OAS）、$5,000 的加拿大退休金计划（CPP）退休给付，以及 $12,000 的注册退休收入基金（RRIF）提款。其中有多少金额符合资格作为 Line 31400 联邦养老金收入额的合格养老金收入？

  1. ✗ 错误。此选项将 OAS、CPP 和 RRIF 三项全部简单加总（$8,000 + $5,000 + $12,000）。落入了「把所有政府福利和养老金都视为合格抵免收入」的陷阱。OAS 和 CPP 属于第一层和第二层社会保障，无论纳税人多大年龄均天然排除在合格养老金收入之外。Incorrect. This simply adds OAS, CPP and RRIF ($8,000 + $5,000 + $12,000) and falls into the trap of treating all government benefits and pensions as qualifying credit income. OAS and CPP are first- and second-pillar social security and are inherently excluded from qualifying pension income at any age.

  2. ✗ 错误。此选项包含了 $5,000 的 CPP 和 $12,000 的 RRIF。CPP 是基于供款的社会保险，在任何年龄都不得作为 Line 31400 的合格养老金收入（qualified pension income）。Incorrect. This includes the $5,000 CPP and the $12,000 RRIF. CPP is contribution-based social insurance and can never be qualifying pension income for Line 31400 at any age.

  3. ✗ 错误。此选项混淆了合格收入金额与法定抵免基数上限 $2,000。题干问的是有多少收入符合资格（qualifying income），而非最终可申报的最高抵免额。Incorrect. This confuses the qualifying income amount with the statutory credit base cap of $2,000. The stem asks how much income qualifies, not the maximum credit that can finally be claimed.

  4. ✓ 正确。纳税人年满 65 岁时，注册退休收入基金（Registered Retirement Income Fund, RRIF）的提款即进入合格养老金收入范围；而老年保障金（Old Age Security, OAS）与加拿大退休金计划（Canada Pension Plan, CPP）在任何年龄均不属于合格养老金收入。因此仅有 $12,000 的 RRIF 提款合格。Correct. When the taxpayer reaches age 65, Registered Retirement Income Fund (RRIF) withdrawals come within qualifying pension income, while Old Age Security (OAS) and Canada Pension Plan (CPP) never qualify at any age. So only the $12,000 RRIF withdrawal qualifies.

第 2 题Q22026年OAS回收门槛与列报2026 OAS recovery threshold and reporting★★陷阱：OAS回收直接报净额

For the 2026 tax year, Arthur received a T4A(OAS) slip showing total Old Age Security payments of $9,000 in box 18. His net income before adjustments on line 23400 is $105,323, which exceeds the 2026 OAS recovery threshold of $95,323 by exactly $10,000.   4. ✗ 错误。普通 RRSP 提款走 Line 12900，而非 Line 13000；且塞缪尔在提款时不能申报任何 RRSP 供款扣除。Incorrect. An ordinary RRSP withdrawal goes on Line 12900, not Line 13000; and Samuel cannot claim any RRSP contribution deduction at the time of the withdrawal.

第 6 题Q6RRSP提款阶梯预扣与拆单规则Tiered RRSP withholding and splitting of withdrawals★★陷阱：拆小额提款少预扣税

Frank, a resident of British Columbia, wishes to withdraw a total of $12,000 from his regular RRSP. To reduce the immediate withholding tax, Frank requests the financial institution to process this as three separate withdrawals of $4,000 on three consecutive days. Under CRA administrative rules and standard withholding rates outside Quebec, how should the financial institution apply the withholding tax to Frank's request?

定居卑诗省（BC）的弗兰克（Frank）打算从其普通 RRSP 中提取总额为 $12,000 的资金。为了降低当期的预扣税率，弗兰克要求金融机构在连续三天内将其拆分成三笔每次 $4,000 的独立提款分别执行。根据 CRA 行政规则及魁省以外的标准预扣税率，金融机构应如何对弗兰克的提款请求计征预扣税？

  1. ✓ 正确。魁省以外：不超过 5,000 元预扣 10%，超过 5,000 元至 15,000 元预扣 20%，超过 15,000 元预扣 30%。同一份提款指令拆成几次执行，预扣率按请求总额（$12,000，落在 20% 档）确定，不能靠拆单适用 10%。Correct. Outside Quebec: up to $5,000 has 10% withheld, over $5,000 up to $15,000 has 20%, and over $15,000 has 30%. When one withdrawal instruction is carried out in several payments, the withholding rate is set by the total amount requested ($12,000, in the 20% tier), and splitting the payments cannot get the 10% rate.

  2. ✗ 错误。这落入了「以为拆成多次小额提款就能适用较低预扣税率」的常见误解。CRA 明确规定连续或分批执行的同一提款指令按累计总额确定预扣税档次。Incorrect. This falls into the common misunderstanding that splitting into several small withdrawals gets a lower withholding rate. The CRA states clearly that the same withdrawal instruction carried out in a series or in batches has its withholding tier set by the cumulative total.

  3. ✗ 错误。拆单提款并不存在惩罚性预扣税率，金融机构只是按照实际总请求额（$12,000）对应的正常法定税档（20%）执行预扣。Incorrect. There is no penalty withholding rate for splitting withdrawals; the financial institution simply withholds at the normal statutory tier (20%) for the total amount actually requested ($12,000).

  4. ✗ 错误。普通 RRSP 提取属于应税付款，金融机构依法负有强制代扣代缴预扣税（withholding tax）的法定义务，不可免除预扣。Incorrect. An ordinary RRSP withdrawal is a taxable payment, and the financial institution has a mandatory statutory duty to withhold tax; withholding cannot be waived.

答案Answer：a

George, residing in BC, needs to make an unscheduled withdrawal of $18,000 from his Registered Retirement Savings Plan (RRSP). What is the mandatory federal/provincial lump-sum withholding tax rate that his financial institution must deduct from this payment?

  2. ✗ 错误。魁省以外的标准 RRSP 预扣税档位为 10%、20%、30%，不存在 15% 的档位。Incorrect. The standard RRSP withholding tiers outside Quebec are 10%, 20% and 30%; there is no 15% tier.

  3. ✗ 错误。20% 适用于超过 5,000 元至 15,000 元的提款，$18,000 已超过 15,000 元。Incorrect. 20% applies to withdrawals over $5,000 up to $15,000, and $18,000 is over $15,000.

  4. ✓ 正确。魁省以外 RRSP 一次性提款预扣分三档：不超过 5,000 元 10%，超过 5,000 元至 15,000 元 20%，超过 15,000 元 30%；$18,000 适用 30%。Correct. For a one-time RRSP withdrawal outside Quebec there are three withholding tiers: 10% up to $5,000, 20% over $5,000 up to $15,000, and 30% over $15,000; $18,000 gets 30%.

第 7 题Q7T4A(P)各框归属与死亡给付T4A(P) box mapping and the death benefit★★陷阱：伤残重复加进汇总

Irene received a T4A(P) slip from Service Canada for the current tax year. Box 20 (Total CPP benefits) shows $11,000, which includes $3,000 of disability benefit shown in box 16. In addition, as a named individual beneficiary of her deceased father, Irene received a $2,500 CPP death benefit reported in box 18 of a separate T4A(P) slip issued in her name. How should Irene report these amounts on her T1 tax return?

艾琳（Irene）在当前税年收到了加拿大服务局（Service Canada）寄发的一张 T4A(P) 凭单。Box 20（CPP 给付总额）显示为 $11,000，其中已包含 box 16 所列的 $3,000 伤残给付。此外，作为其已故父亲指定的个人受益人，艾琳收到了以其个人名义出具的另一张 T4A(P) 凭单，box 18 载明 $2,500 的 CPP 死亡给付。艾琳应如何在 T1 报税表上申报这些金额？

  1. ✗ 错误。此选项把 box 16 的伤残给付与 box 20 重复相加（$11,000 + $3,000 = $14,000）。落入了「将 T4A(P) box 16 再次加进 Line 11400」的常见错报陷阱；且错误地漏报了死亡给付。Incorrect. This adds the box 16 disability benefit to box 20 again ($11,000 + $3,000 = $14,000). It falls into the common misreport of adding T4A(P) box 16 to Line 11400 again, and wrongly omits the death benefit.

  2. ✗ 错误。此选项将本人领取的死亡给付 $2,500 也并入了 Line 11400。本人作为受益人直接收到的 CPP 死亡给付是唯一走出 Line 11400 汇总框的款项，必须改走 Line 13000。Incorrect. This also adds the $2,500 death benefit she received to Line 11400. A CPP death benefit received directly as beneficiary is the only amount that leaves the Line 11400 total box and must go on Line 13000.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据 T4A(P) 填报规则，box 20 已经将 box 14 至 19 的所有 CPP/QPP 给付汇总，因此 Line 11400 原样填入 $11,000；box 16 的伤残给付另抄入 Line 11410 仅作为信息性备注，绝不能与 Line 11400 重复相加；而个人以受益人身份直接收到的 CPP 死亡给付（death benefit）不进 Line 11400，须独立计入 Line 13000（其他收入）。Correct. Under the T4A(P) reporting rules, box 20 already totals all CPP/QPP benefits in boxes 14 to 19, so Line 11400 takes $11,000 as is; the box 16 disability benefit is also copied to Line 11410 only as an information memo and must never be added to Line 11400 again; and a CPP death benefit received personally as beneficiary does not go on Line 11400 but is included separately on Line 13000 (other income).

  4. ✗ 错误。此选项从 box 20 中扣减了 box 16 的伤残金（$11,000 - $3,000 = $8,000）。Line 11400 要求原样填入 box 20 的汇总全额，不需要扣除伤残部分。Incorrect. This subtracts the box 16 disability amount from box 20 ($11,000 - $3,000 = $8,000). Line 11400 requires the full box 20 total as is, with no deduction of the disability portion.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Kevin received a T4A(P) slip showing $9,500 in box 20 (Total CPP benefits), which includes $4,000 of disability benefits noted in box 16. What are the correct entries on lines 11400 and 11410 of Kevin's T1 return?

凯文（Kevin）收到一张 T4A(P) 凭单，其中 box 20（CPP 给付总额）显示为 $9,500，其中包括了 box 16 载明的 $4,000 伤残给付。凯文在 T1 税表的 Line 11400 和 Line 11410 上的正确填报方式是什么？

  2. ✓ 正确。T4A(P) 的 box 20 是各项给付的汇总数，直接原样抄入 Line 11400（计入总收入）；box 16 的伤残给付（disability benefit）金额抄入 Line 11410 仅作为税务局核对资格的信息性备忘，不影响收入总额，绝不能再加一次。Correct.
