本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 收入定性与计税口径判定题：给出利息、加国股息、外国股息及资本利得等混合凭据，考查各类别应税比例与抵免资格，核心测试考生能否识别外国股息无毛额化且无股息抵免、必须100%全额并入第12100行。Characterizing income and the basis of taxation: gives mixed slips of interest, Canadian dividends, foreign dividends and capital gains and tests each category's inclusion ratio and credit eligibility; the core test is whether the candidate recognizes that foreign dividends have no gross-up and no dividend tax credit and must be included 100% in full on Line 12100.
  • 归属规则对象与范围辨析题：给出向配偶或未满18岁未成年人转移生息资产或股票的场景，考查利息股息与资本利得的归属差异，核心测试未成年人资本利得不归属（s.74.2）而由其自行申报的关键例外。Who and what the attribution rules cover: gives scenarios of transferring interest-bearing assets or shares to a spouse or a minor under 18 and tests the difference in attribution of interest and dividends versus capital gains; the core test is the key exception that a minor's capital gains are not attributed (s.74.2) and are reported by the minor.
  • 规定利率贷款豁免持续性测试题：结合家庭内部借贷投资，考查s.74.5(2)豁免要件，重点测试每年1月30日前实际付清利息的持续性要求，识别逾期一年导致后续所有年份免税架构失效的考点。Continuity test for the prescribed rate loan exemption: combines a loan within the family to invest with the requirements of the s.74.5(2) exemption, mainly testing the continuing requirement to actually pay the interest by January 30 each year, and recognizing the point that being one year late voids the tax-exempt structure for all later years.
  • 承担费用（Line 22100）扣除边界题：列出投资顾问费、借款利息、交易佣金与账户类型，考查注册账户（RRSP/TFSA/FHSA）顾问费与买卖佣金严禁在第22100行扣除的界限。Boundaries of carrying charges (Line 22100): lists investment advisory fees, loan interest, trading commissions and account types and tests the line that advisory fees and trading commissions of registered accounts (RRSP/TFSA/FHSA) must not be deducted on Line 22100.
  • 特殊投资凭证与流程填报题：针对T3信托单各框号分流（box 21资本利得、box 25外国收益、box 26其他收入入第13000行）及剥离债券按年应计计税与ACB调整，考查实务填报规则。Special investment slips and reporting process: for the T3 trust slip, the routing of box numbers (box 21 capital gains, box 25 foreign income, box 26 other income on Line 13000) and the annual accrual taxation of strip bonds with ACB adjustments, tests practical reporting rules.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 外国股息整合陷阱Foreign dividend integration

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常把外国公司派发的股息误当成加拿大公司股息做138%或115%毛额化并申报联邦股息税收抵免（DTC），忽略了外国公司未缴纳加拿大企业所得税，没有整合对象，外国股息必须100%全额并入第12100行，已扣税款只能通过外国税收抵免（FTC）抵扣。Candidates often treat dividends paid by a foreign company as Canadian company dividends, grossing them up 138% or 115% and claiming the federal dividend tax credit (DTC), ignoring that a foreign company has not paid Canadian corporate tax, so there is nothing to integrate; a foreign dividend must be included 100% in full on Line 12100, and tax withheld can only be offset through the foreign tax credit (FTC).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「US shares」「foreign dividend」「T5 slip foreign income」「gross-up / DTC」等词。The stem contains words such as 'US shares', 'foreign dividend', 'T5 slip foreign income' and 'gross-up / DTC'.

⚠ 未成年人资本利得归属陷阱Attribution of a minor's capital gains

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生以为转移给未成年人的财产所有收益都会归属回父母，忽略了ITA s.74.2仅适用于配偶，对未满18岁的未成年人只归属利息与股息等经常性收入（s.74.1(2)），处置财产产生的资本利得或亏损不归属，始终由未成年人自己申报。Candidates think that all gains on property transferred to a minor are attributed back to the parents, ignoring that ITA s.74.2 applies only to spouses; for a minor under 18 only recurring income such as interest and dividends is attributed (s.74.1(2)), and capital gains or losses from disposing of the property are not attributed and are always reported by the minor.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述父母向未满18岁子女转移股票/基金，且题目针对处置时的资本利得（capital gain upon sale）提问归属。The stem describes parents transferring shares or funds to a child under 18 and asks about attribution of the capital gain upon sale.

⚠ 规定利率贷款付息逾期陷阱Late interest payment on a prescribed rate loan

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生误以为规定利率贷款只要首年按时付息便能终身豁免归属，忽略了每年1月30日前实际付清利息是持续性条件，一旦某年逾期，当年及往后所有利息股息收入将永久重新触发归属规则。Candidates believe that a prescribed rate loan is exempt from attribution for life if the interest is paid on time in the first year, ignoring that actually paying the interest by January 30 each year is a continuing condition; once a year is late, all interest and dividend income from that year onward permanently triggers the attribution rules again.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「prescribed rate loan」「paid interest on February 5 / missed January 30」「subsequent year」。The stem contains 'prescribed rate loan', 'paid interest on February 5 / missed January 30' and 'subsequent year'.

⚠ 注册账户投资顾问费扣除陷阱Advisory fees on registered accounts

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生以为持牌机构收取的顾问费均可在第22100行扣除，忽略了RRSP、TFSA、FHSA等注册账户已享受免税或递延优惠，其名下顾问费禁止双重扣除，仅非注册账户顾问费可扣。Candidates think all advisory fees charged by licensed firms can be deducted on Line 22100, ignoring that registered accounts such as RRSP, TFSA and FHSA already enjoy tax-free or deferred treatment, so fees in their name cannot be deducted twice; only advisory fees of non-registered accounts are deductible.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「TFSA / RRSP management fee」「portfolio fee for registered account」「line 22100」。The stem contains 'TFSA / RRSP management fee', 'portfolio fee for registered account' and 'line 22100'.

⚠ 交易佣金与承担费用混淆陷阱Confusing trading commissions with carrying charges

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生容易将买卖证券时支付的经纪佣金与日常投资顾问费混淆并塞入第22100行扣除，税法规定买入佣金必须计入调整后成本基数（ACB）、卖出佣金计入处置成本，只能在资本利得/亏损中体现。Candidates easily confuse brokerage commissions paid on buying and selling securities with ordinary investment advisory fees and put them on Line 22100; tax law requires purchase commissions to be added to the adjusted cost base (ACB) and selling commissions to be included in the costs of disposition, reflected only in capital gains or losses.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「trading commissions paid on stock purchases」「brokerage fee per trade」「line 22100 carrying charges」。The stem contains 'trading commissions paid on stock purchases', 'brokerage fee per trade' and 'line 22100 carrying charges'.

第 1 题Q1外国股息定性与全额计入Characterizing foreign dividends and including them in full★陷阱：外国股息整合陷阱

David, a Canadian resident taxpayer, received $3,000 CAD equivalent in dividends from shares of a US corporation held in his non-registered trading account during the 2026 tax year. US non-resident tax of 15% ($450 CAD) was withheld at source. Which of the following correctly describes how David must report this dividend on his Canadian T1 return?

David 是一名加拿大税务居民，在2026税年从其非注册交易账户持有的美国公司股票中收到了折合$3,000加元的股息。美国在来源国预扣了15%的非居民税（折合$450加元）。下列哪项正确描述了 David 必须如何在加拿大T1税表上申报该笔股息？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据讲义第2.1节与第2.3节，外国公司未在加拿大缴纳公司税，没有整合对象，因此外国股息不享有毛额化与股息税收抵免（Dividend Tax Credit, DTC），必须100%全额作为利息与其他投资收入并入第12100行；已在境外缴纳的预扣税走第40500行申报外国税收抵免（Foreign Tax Credit, FTC）。Correct. Under Sections 2.1 and 2.3 of the lecture, a foreign company has not paid corporate tax in Canada, so there is nothing to integrate; a foreign dividend therefore gets no gross-up and no dividend tax credit (DTC), and must be included 100% in full as interest and other investment income on Line 12100; withholding tax already paid abroad is claimed as a foreign tax credit (FTC) on Line 40500.

  2. ✗ 属于「外国股息整合陷阱」。将外国股息按加拿大合资格股息进行38%毛额化并申报DTC是重大定性错误，评税时虚报的抵免额与毛额化部分会被剔除，且外国税款应通过FTC抵税而非在第22100行扣除。This is the 'foreign dividend integration' trap. Grossing up a foreign dividend 38% as a Canadian eligible dividend and claiming the DTC is a major mischaracterization; on assessment the overstated credit and the gross-up part are removed, and foreign tax should be offset through the FTC, not deducted on Line 22100.

  3. ✗ 属于「收入分类混淆错误」。外国股息属于经常性财产收入而非处置资本财产实现的资本利得（capital gains），不能享受50%的纳入率。This is the 'income classification mix-up' error. A foreign dividend is recurring property income, not a capital gain from disposing of capital property, and cannot get the 50% inclusion rate.

  4. ✗ 属于「净额申报陷阱」。加拿大个人税申报必须申报毛额（gross foreign income），不得自行扣减外国预扣税后按净额申报，否则无法准确核算外国税收抵免。This is the 'net reporting' trap. A Canadian personal return must report gross income (gross foreign income); the foreign withholding tax cannot be subtracted by oneself to report a net amount, otherwise the foreign tax credit cannot be calculated accurately.

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Sarah, a Canadian resident, holds non-registered shares in a publicly traded UK corporation and received $2,000 in dividends during 2026, from which $300 in foreign tax was withheld abroad. Her previous tax preparer mistakenly applied a 15% gross-up and claimed a non-eligible dividend tax credit. How should Sarah properly report this income?

Sarah 是一名加拿大税务居民，持有非注册账户中一家英国上市公司的股票，在2026年收到$2,000股息，境外预扣了$300税款。她之前的报税员错误地按非合资格股息适用了15%毛额化并申报了股息抵免。Sarah 应当如何正确申报该笔收入？

  1. ✗ 属于「外国股息整合陷阱」。外国公司无论规模大小均未向加拿大缴纳企业所得税，根本不能套用非合资格股息的15%毛额化与9.0301% DTC待遇。This is the 'foreign dividend integration' trap. A foreign company, whatever its size, has not paid Canadian corporate tax at all, so the 15% gross-up and 9.0301% DTC treatment of non-eligible dividends cannot be applied.

  2. ✗ 属于「净额申报陷阱」。纳税人必须申报$2,000的税前毛额收入，直接按净额$1,700申报违反税法规定且浪费了境外已纳税额的抵免机会。This is the 'net reporting' trap. The taxpayer must report the pre-tax gross income of $2,000; reporting the net $1,700 directly breaks the tax rules and wastes the chance to credit the tax already paid abroad.

  3. ✓ 正确。外国股息定性上直接归入利息类收入（第12100行），不适用任何毛额化或加拿大股息税收抵免，已缴的$300外国税款通过Form T2209计算并在第40500行申报外国税收抵免（FTC）。Correct. A foreign dividend is classified directly as interest-type income (Line 12100), with no gross-up and no Canadian dividend tax credit; the $300 of foreign tax paid is calculated on Form T2209 and claimed as a foreign tax credit (FTC) on Line 40500.

  4. ✗ 属于「收入分类混淆错误」。常规外国股息是投资所得收益，不是资本本金返还或资本处置利得，不得按50%纳入第12700行。This is the 'income classification mix-up' error. A regular foreign dividend is investment income, not a return of capital or a capital gain on disposition, and cannot be included at 50% on Line 12700.

第 2 题Q2未成年人股息归属与利得豁免Attribution of a minor's dividends and the capital gain exception★★陷阱：未成年人资本利得归属陷阱

In May 2026, Arthur gifted $40,000 worth of shares in a Canadian public corporation to his 14-year-old daughter, Chloe. Later that year, Chloe received $1,200 in eligible dividends from these shares. In November 2026, Chloe sold all the shares for $50,000, realizing a $10,000 capital gain. Under ITA attribution rules (s.74.1 and s.74.2), how must the dividend and capital gain be reported?

2026年5月，Arthur 将其持有的价值$40,000的加拿大上市公司股票赠与了他14岁的女儿 Chloe。同年晚些时候，Chloe 从这些股票中收到了$1,200合资格股息。2026年11月，Chloe 以$50,000的价格卖出全部股票，实现了$10,000的资本利得。根据所得税法归属规则（s.74.1与s.74.2），该笔股息和资本利得必须如何申报？

  1. ✗ 属于「未成年人资本利得归属陷阱」。考生常误以为未成年人名下的全部收益都会归属回父母，忽略了ITA s.74.2的资本利得归属仅适用于配偶，从未延伸至未成年人。This is the 'minor's capital gains attribution' trap. Candidates often wrongly think all of a minor's gains are attributed back to the parents, ignoring that the capital gains attribution of ITA s.74.2 applies only to spouses and never extends to minors.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据讲义第2.4节，向未满18岁非公平交易关系亲属转移财产时，经常性收入（利息、股息）依s.74.1(2)归属回转移人Arthur；但处置该财产产生的资本利得或亏损不受s.74.2归属约束（该条文仅限配偶），因此$10,000资本利得始终由Chloe自己在个人税表上按50%纳入申报。Correct.   4. ✗ 属于「荒谬全盘错报陷阱」。外国收入不可享受38%毛额化，信托其他收入不可作为费用扣除，资本利得不可100%计入利息行。This is the 'absurd total misreport' trap. Foreign income cannot get the 38% gross-up, trust other income cannot be deducted as an expense, and a capital gain cannot be included 100% on the interest line.

答案Answer：b

A tax preparer is completing a return for a client who received a T3 slip displaying $4,000 in Box 21 (Capital gains) and $800 in Box 26 (Other income). What is the correct reporting line and treatment on the T1 return for these two boxes?

一名报税员正在为一位客户报税，其收到的 T3 凭单上显示 Box 21（资本利得）$4,000以及 Box 26（其他收入）$800。这两个框号在 T1 税表上的正确申报行号与处理方式是什么？

  2. ✗ 属于「T3整单混填陷阱」。将资本利得与其他收入整单混填入第12100行，不仅剥夺了资本利得50%纳入率的优惠，还会引起CRA系统不匹配核查。This is the 'T3 slip mixed in one entry' trap. Mixing the capital gain and other income into one entry on Line 12100 not only loses the 50% inclusion benefit for capital gains but also causes a CRA system mismatch review.

  4. ✗ 属于「框号完全倒置陷阱」。将资本利得与信托其他收入的行号张冠李戴，完全颠倒了税表流向。This is the 'box numbers completely reversed' trap. Putting the capital gain and the trust other income on each other's lines completely reverses the flow of the return.

第 7 题Q7归属规则与TOSI分工协调How attribution rules and TOSI divide the work★★★陷阱：反避税机制混淆陷阱

Under the Canadian tax framework explained in the lecture, which statement correctly explains the relationship and statutory coordination between the attribution rules (ITA s.74.1 to s.74.5) and the Tax on Split Income (TOSI)?

根据讲义中解释的加拿大税法框架，下列哪项陈述正确解释了归属规则（ITA s.74.1至s.74.5）与分割收入税（TOSI）之间的关系及法定协调机制？

  1. ✗ 属于「重复课税误解」。税法明确规定反避税机制不会对同一笔收入重复征税，两者有明确的协调条款。This is the 'double taxation misunderstanding'. The tax rules expressly provide that the anti-avoidance mechanisms do not tax the same income twice, and there is a clear coordination provision between the two.

  2. ✗ 属于「新法废除旧法误解」。2018年只是扩展了TOSI的适用人群，归属规则依然完全有效，两者并行。This is the 'new law repealed the old law' misunderstanding. In 2018 TOSI was only extended to more people; the attribution rules remain fully in force, and the two operate in parallel.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据讲义第2.4节与第五节，归属规则管直接财产转移，TOSI管通过家族企业或信托间接分红；根据ITA s.74.5(13)，已经按TOSI课税的部分不再重复触发归属规则，两者各司其职且互不重复叠加课税。Correct. Under Section 2.4 and Section 5 of the lecture, the attribution rules govern direct transfers of property, while TOSI governs indirect dividends through family businesses or trusts; under ITA s.74.5(13), an amount already taxed under TOSI no longer triggers the attribution rules, so each has its own role without stacking tax twice.

  4. ✗ 属于「年龄适用范围反转陷阱」。归属规则一直包含未成年人条款（s.74.1(2)），而TOSI自2018年起已扩展至18岁以上的成年家庭成员。This is the 'age scope reversed' trap. The attribution rules have always included a minors provision (s.74.1(2)), while TOSI has been extended since 2018 to adult family members over 18.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

When analyzing an anti-avoidance situation involving a private corporation distributing dividends to family members, how does ITA s.74.5(13) prevent double taxation between attribution rules and the Tax on Split Income (TOSI)?

在分析涉及私有企业向家庭成员分配股息的反避税情景时，ITA s.74.5(13) 如何防止归属规则与分割收入税（TOSI）之间的重复课税？

  1. ✗ 属于「重复课税误解」。税法s.74.5(13)正是为了排除这种重复征税后果而设立的法定协调收口。This is the 'double taxation misunderstanding'. Section 74.5(13) exists precisely as the statutory coordination provision to exclude this result of double taxation.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据讲义第2.4节，ITA s.74.5(13)明确规定：已经按分割收入税（TOSI）课过税的部分，归属规则不再重复起作用，从而在立法上排除了对同一笔分红重复适用两套反避税规则。Correct. Under Section 2.4 of the lecture, ITA s.74.5(13) expressly provides that an amount already taxed under the tax on split income (TOSI) is not caught again by the attribution rules, which excludes in legislation the repeated application of two anti-avoidance rules to the same dividend.

  3. ✗ 属于「适用对象完全倒置陷阱」。TOSI考察的是企业真实经营参与度（如排除股份/排除营业），归属规则关注的是无偿或低息财产转移。This is the 'objects completely reversed' trap. TOSI looks at the real degree of involvement in the business (such as the excluded shares or excluded business tests), while the attribution rules concern gratuitous or low-interest transfers of property.

  4. ✗ 属于「税率机制颠倒错误」。TOSI强制按最高联邦与省边际税率征税，而归属规则是将收入拉回转移人名下按转移人的整体边际税率计税。This is the 'tax rate mechanism reversed' error. TOSI compels tax at the top federal and provincial marginal rates, while the attribution rules pull income back to the transferor and tax it at the transferor's overall marginal rate.
