第 3 章 · 雇佣收入与 T4Chapter 3 · Employment Income and the T4 Slip: Mapping Boxes to Income, Deductions, and Tax Credits (2026 Tax Year)Employment income and the T4

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • T4 框号与申报表行号精准映射题：考查 Box 14（Line 10100 雇佣收入）、Box 22（Line 43700 预缴税款而非抵免）、Box 40（备忘框不要重复相加）以及 Box 52（PA 仅压缩次年 RRSP 额度不进 T1）的去向分类。Mapping T4 boxes to return lines: tests where each item goes: Box 14 (Line 10100 employment income), Box 22 (Line 43700 tax already withheld, not a credit), Box 40 (a memo box, do not add it again) and Box 52 (the pension adjustment only reduces next year's RRSP room and does not go on the T1).
  • CPP 与 EI 供款分流及 2026 现行计算题：测试 Box 16 基础段（4.95% 走 Line 30800 抵免，上限 3,519.45 元）与第一层增强段（1% 走 Line 22215 扣除，上限 711 元），以及 Box 16A（CPP2 4% 走 Line 22215 扣除，上限 416 元）的分流与税收待遇差异。Splitting CPP and EI contributions, with 2026 calculations: tests the split of Box 16 base portion (4.95% claimed as a credit on Line 30800, maximum $3,519.45) and first enhancement (1% claimed as a deduction on Line 22215, maximum $711), and Box 16A (CPP2 at 4% claimed as a deduction on Line 22215, maximum $416), and their different tax treatment.
  • 雇主非现金福利应税性判定题：考查根据「主要个人受益还是雇主业务需要」判断停车位、公司手机等福利，以及根据「谁付保费」判定团体工资损失替代保险给付是否应税（雇主全额付保费则给付全额应税；团体人寿保险无免税门槛）。Taxability of employer non-cash benefits: tests judging parking and a company phone by whether the main benefit is personal or the employer's business need, and judging whether group wage-loss replacement benefits are taxable by who paid the premiums (employer paid in full means the benefit is fully taxable; group life insurance has no tax-free threshold).
  • 证券期权应税福利与扣除比例题：考查合格证券期权行权福利在 Line 24900 享受 50% 扣除，识别 2024 年预算案提议降至 33.33% 的未遂改革已被全面撤销、现行仍维持 50% 的真实税法规则。Security option taxable benefit and deduction rate: tests the 50% deduction on Line 24900 for the exercise benefit of qualifying security options, and recognizing that the proposal in the 2024 budget to reduce it to 33.33% was fully withdrawn so the current rule is still 50%.
  • 多雇主多张 T4 汇总与实务避坑题：测试年中换工作或兼职时汇总所有 Box 14、核对 CPP 与 EI 超额扣缴并在 Line 44800 和 Line 45000 申报退还，以及识别因多个 TD1 均申报全额基本个人金额导致的源头预扣不足。Several T4s from several employers and practical pitfalls: tests adding up all Box 14 amounts when changing jobs mid-year or working part time, checking CPP and EI over-deductions and claiming the refund on Line 44800 and Line 45000, and recognizing under-withholding at source when several TD1 forms each claim the full basic personal amount.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ Box 40 重复相加陷阱Adding Box 40 again

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到 T4 上单独列出 Box 40 金额，直觉以为是额外收入而将其加到 Box 14 上，导致雇佣收入重复计入并虚增应税收入。Candidates see Box 40 listed separately on the T4, assume it is extra income and add it to Box 14, so employment income is counted twice and taxable income is overstated.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出 T4 的 Box 14 雇佣收入总额和 Box 40 其他应税福利金额，询问应填入 Line 10100 的最终申报数字。The stem gives both the T4 Box 14 employment income and the Box 40 other taxable benefits amount, and asks for the final figure to enter on Line 10100.

⚠ CPP 增强段与基础段混淆走错行陷阱Mixing CPP enhanced and base portions and using the wrong line

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 混淆了 CPP 两层扩容改革，误以为 Box 16 和 Box 16A 的全部供款都只能走 Line 30800 享受 14% 的固定抵免，忽略了增强段（1% 与 CPP2）走 Line 22215 扣除。Confusing the two CPP expansion layers and believing that all of Box 16 and Box 16A can only go on Line 30800 for the fixed 14% credit, ignoring that the enhanced portions (the 1% and CPP2) go on Line 22215 as a deduction.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出员工扣缴的 CPP（Box 16）或 CPP2（Box 16A）金额，询问分别计入 Line 30800 抵免与 Line 22215 扣除的准确数额。The stem gives the CPP withheld (Box 16) or CPP2 (Box 16A) and asks for the exact amounts for the Line 30800 credit and the Line 22215 deduction.

⚠ 股票期权扣除率未遂改革错误引用陷阱Quoting the failed stock option rate reform

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 受 2024 年联邦预算未遂提案影响，误以为股票期权扣除率已被降至 33.33%，忽略了该提议已随资本利得改革一并撤销、2026 税年仍维持 50%。Influenced by the failed 2024 federal budget proposal, candidates believe the stock option deduction has been cut to 33.33%, ignoring that the proposal was withdrawn along with the capital gains reform and the rate is still 50% for the 2026 tax year.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及雇员行权合格股票期权，给出代码 38 应税福利金额，询问在 Line 24900 申报的代码 39 扣除额或适用百分比。The stem involves an employee exercising qualifying stock options, gives the code 38 taxable benefit amount, and asks for the code 39 deduction or percentage claimed on Line 24900.

⚠ 多雇主 CPP 与 EI 超缴自动退税误区陷阱Believing CPP and EI overpayments from several employers are refunded automatically

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为由两个或更多雇主扣缴导致的 CPP 或 EI 超出年度法定上限的部分，CRA 会在系统里自动直接退回给纳税人，无需在申报表上主动填报。Believing that CPP or EI withheld above the annual limit because of two or more employers is automatically refunded by the CRA in its system, with nothing to claim on the return.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述纳税人年内换工作或有两份工作，多张 T4 汇总的 CPP 或 EI 扣缴总额超过年度上限，询问超额部分的申报方式与行号。The stem describes a taxpayer who changed jobs or held two jobs during the year, where the total CPP or EI withheld across several T4s exceeds the annual limit, and asks how and on which line the excess is reported.

⚠ Box 52 养老金调整额当年抵税误区陷阱Treating the Box 52 pension adjustment as a deduction this year

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误将 Box 52 养老金调整额（PA）当成当年的税前扣除项或可抵免项目填入 T1 表，忽略了该格纯属备忘信息、仅用于压缩次年 RRSP 额度。Mistakenly entering the Box 52 pension adjustment (PA) on the T1 as a deduction or credit for the current year, ignoring that this box is only a memo item used to reduce next year's RRSP room.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出纳税人 T4 上的 Box 20（RPP 供款）和 Box 52（PA 养老金调整额），询问两者在当年 T1 申报表上的具体填报去向。The stem gives Box 20 (RPP contributions) and Box 52 (PA) from the T4 and asks where each goes on this year's T1 return.

第 1 题Q1Box 14 与 Box 40 关系Relationship between Box 14 and Box 40★陷阱：Box 40 重复相加陷阱

Sarah received a 2026 T4 slip from her employer showing $65,000 in Box 14 (Employment income) and $3,200 in Box 40 (Other taxable allowances and benefits). What amount should Sarah enter on Line 10100 of her 2026 T1 personal income tax return?

Sarah 收到雇主寄来的 2026 年 T4 报表，其中 Box 14（雇佣收入）显示为 $65,000，Box 40（其他应税补贴与福利）显示为 $3,200。Sarah 在其 2026 年 T1 个人所得税申报表的第 10100 行应填报多少金额？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据税法与 T4 报表设计规则，Box 40（其他应税补贴与福利，other taxable allowances and benefits）是一个备忘栏，其金额本来就已经被计算包含在 Box 14（雇佣收入，employment income）的总数中；Line 10100 直接填报 Box 14 的 $65,000，不能重复相加。Correct. Under the tax rules and the T4 design, Box 40 (other taxable allowances and benefits) is a memo box whose amount is already included in the Box 14 total (employment income); Line 10100 takes Box 14 directly, $65,000, and the amounts must not be added again.

  2. ✗ 错误。落入「Box 40 重复相加陷阱」；误以为 Box 40 是额外收入，将其与 Box 14 重复相加（$65,000 + $3,200 = $68,200），导致虚增应税收入。Incorrect. This falls into the 'adding Box 40 again' trap: wrongly treating Box 40 as extra income and adding it to Box 14 ($65,000 + $3,200 = $68,200), which overstates taxable income.

  3. ✗ 错误。Box 40 属于应税福利，绝不能从工资总额中扣除。Incorrect. Box 40 consists of taxable benefits and must never be deducted from gross pay.

  4. ✗ 错误。Line 10100 是雇佣收入总额（包含工资与福利）的法定报告行，绝非仅填报福利部分。Incorrect. Line 10100 is the statutory line for total employment income (including salary and benefits), not just the benefits portion.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

David received a 2026 T4 slip from an engineering firm showing $82,000 in Box 14 and $4,500 in Box 40 for employer-paid parking and group term life insurance. When completing his tax return, how should David report these figures on Line 10100?

David 收到工程设计公司开具的 2026 年 T4 报表，其中 Box 14 显示为 $82,000，Box 40 显示为 $4,500（包含雇主支付的停车位及团体定期人寿保费）。在编制报税表时，David 应如何在第 10100 行申报这些数字？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入「Box 40 重复相加陷阱」；Box 40 的 $4,500 已经内含于 Box 14 中，相加会导致重复计税。Incorrect. This falls into the 'adding Box 40 again' trap; the $4,500 in Box 40 is already inside Box 14, so adding it would tax it twice.

  2. ✗ 错误。应税福利无需从工资总额中扣减。Incorrect. Taxable benefits are not subtracted from gross pay.

  3. ✓ 正确。Box 40 只是一面透视镜，告知纳税人 Box 14 内部有多少来自非现金福利，Line 10100 严格填报 Box 14 的总数 $82,000。Correct. Box 40 is just a window showing how much of Box 14 comes from non-cash benefits; Line 10100 takes the Box 14 total, $82,000.

  4. ✗ 错误。Line 10400 适用于无 T4 的其他雇佣收入（如境外雇佣），已有 T4 的应税福利不能填进 Line 10400。Incorrect. Line 10400 is for other employment income without a T4 (such as foreign employment); taxable benefits already on a T4 cannot go on Line 10400.

第 2 题Q2CPP 基础与增强供款分流Split of CPP base and enhanced contributions★★陷阱：CPP 增强段与基础段混淆走错行陷阱

In the 2026 tax year, Kevin earned $90,000 from his sole employer. His T4 slip shows the maximum employee contributions: $4,230.45 in Box 16 and $416.00 in Box 16A. How should Kevin allocate these CPP contributions between Line 30800 (tax credit) and Line 22215 (deduction) on his 2026 T1 return?

在 2026 纳税年度，Kevin 从其唯一雇主处获得 $90,000 的收入。其 T4 报表显示了雇员法定最高供款：Box 16 显示 $4,230.45，Box 16A 显示 $416.00。Kevin 在其 2026 年 T1 申报表中应如何将这些 CPP 供款分配到第 30800 行（抵免）与第 22215 行（扣除）？

  1. ✗ 错误。把 Box 16 与 Box 16A 全部当抵免：$4,230.45 + $416.00 = $4,646.45 全进 Line 30800，忽略了 1% 增强段与 CPP2 走 Line 22215 扣除。Incorrect. This treats all of Box 16 and Box 16A as a credit: $4,230.45 + $416.00 = $4,646.45 all on Line 30800, ignoring that the 1% enhanced portion and CPP2 go on Line 22215 as a deduction.

  2. ✓ 正确。2026 年 ($74,600 − $3,500) = $71,100；基础段 $71,100 × 4.95% = $3,519.45 报 Line 30800；第一层增强 $71,100 × 1% = $711.00，加 Box 16A 的 $416.00，合计 $1,127.00 报 Line 22215。Correct. For 2026, ($74,600 - $3,500) = $71,100; the base portion $71,100 x 4.95% = $3,519.45 goes on Line 30800; the first enhancement $71,100 x 1% = $711.00, plus the $416.00 in Box 16A, totals $1,127.00 on Line 22215.

  3. ✗ 错误。整个 Box 16 当抵免、只把 Box 16A 当扣除，漏了 Box 16 里的 1% 第一层增强（$711.00）也要分到 Line 22215。Incorrect. This treats all of Box 16 as a credit and only Box 16A as a deduction, missing that the 1% first-layer enhancement in Box 16 ($711.00) also belongs on Line 22215.

  4. ✗ 错误。基础段算对了，但 Line 22215 只填了 CPP2，漏了第一层增强 $711.00（$4,230.45 − $3,519.45）；应为 $1,127.00。Incorrect. The base portion is right, but Line 22215 includes only CPP2 and misses the $711.00 first enhancement ($4,230.45 - $3,519.45); it should be $1,127.00.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Rachel earned $74,600 in 2026, reaching exactly the YMPE cap. Her T4 Box 16 shows $4,230.45 and Box 16A shows $0. What exact amount should Rachel claim on Line 22215 as an enhanced CPP deduction on her 2026 T1 return?

Rachel 2026 年受雇收入为 $74,600，恰好达到 YMPE 上限。其 T4 Box 16 显示为 $4,230.45，Box 16A 显示为 $0。Rachel 在其 2026 年 T1 申报表的第 22215 行应申报多少金额的增强 CPP 扣除？

  1. ✗ 错误。以为没有 CPP2 就没有增强扣除；Box 16 本身含 1% 第一层增强（$711.00），照样走 Line 22215。Incorrect. This assumes no CPP2 means no enhanced deduction; Box 16 itself contains the 1% first-layer enhancement ($711.00), which still goes on Line 22215.

  2. ✗ 错误。整个 Box 16 当扣除；其中 4.95% 基础段 $3,519.45 只能走 Line 30800 抵免。Incorrect. This treats all of Box 16 as a deduction; the 4.95% base portion of $3,519.45 can only be claimed as a credit on Line 30800.

  3. ✗ 错误。把方向弄反：$3,519.45 是基础段，属于 Line 30800 抵免，不是 Line 22215 扣除。Incorrect. This reverses the direction: $3,519.45 is the base portion, which is a Line 30800 credit, not a Line 22215 deduction.

  4. ✓ 正确。($74,600 − $3,500) × 1% = $711.00；也就是 $4,230.45 − $3,519.45。这一截走 Line 22215 扣除。Correct. ($74,600 - $3,500) x 1% = $711.00, which is $4,230.45 - $3,519.45. This portion goes on Line 22215 as a deduction.
