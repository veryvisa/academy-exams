第 2 章 · 身份信息与配偶Chapter 2 · Identification and Marital Status: Spouse Details, Province of Residence, and Key Information Checkboxes (2026 Tax Year)Identification and marital status

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 婚姻状况法定分类与同居时间计算题：考查普通法伴侣12个月连续同居认定的具体时点，特别是同居期间不满90天的关系短暂破裂不中断计时的特殊规则。Marital status and cohabitation timing: tests when the 12 months of continuous common-law cohabitation are counted, especially the special rule that a break of fewer than 90 days in the relationship does not interrupt the count.
  • 分居成立条件与追溯生效日题：测试因感情破裂分居满90天的法定成立门槛，重点考查生效日期向前追溯至开始分开居住之日，而非满90天当天的时点规则。Separation and retroactive effective date: tests the legal threshold that a separation due to relationship breakdown must last 90 days, with the effective date reaching back to the day the couple began living apart, not the 90th day.
  • 配偶净收入填报与自雇连带申报期限题：考查12月31日有配偶时第23600行配偶净收入的填报义务（零收入亦须填报），以及配偶自雇对本人申报截止日（延至6月15日）的顺延效应。Spouse net income and self-employed spouse deadline: tests the duty to report the spouse's net income on Line 23600 when there is a spouse on December 31 (even if it is zero), and how a self-employed spouse extends the taxpayer's own filing due date to June 15.
  • 居住省判定与跨省搬迁省表适用题：考查以12月31日居住省单一事实基准确定适用哪张省表（Form 428）的规则，识别不按全年居住天数分摊省税的考点。Province of residence and move between provinces: tests the rule that the province of residence on December 31 is a single-fact test that decides which provincial form (Form 428) applies, so provincial tax is not prorated by days of residence.
  • 海外财产（T1135）申报门槛与违规法律后果题：测试特定海外财产成本额（ACB）超过100,000加元门槛的判定（按个人合并而非夫妻合并、排除自用财产与注册账户），以及漏报导致的复评期延长3年与罚款规则。Foreign property (T1135) threshold and consequences: tests the specified foreign property cost threshold of more than $100,000 (cost amount, tested per individual not per couple, excluding personal-use property and registered accounts), and the 3-year extension of the reassessment period and penalties for non-compliance.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 分开即计时归零陷阱Any break resets the clock

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为情侣同居期间一旦有短暂分开（即使不满90天又复合），12个月连续同居的计时器就会归零并重新开始计算。Mistakenly believing that any short separation during cohabitation, even under 90 days followed by reconciliation, resets the 12-month continuous cohabitation count to zero.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提及同居伴侣因争吵或关系短暂破裂分开居住数周或几十天（少于90天）后重新复合，询问同居满12个月的时点或婚姻状况认定。The stem says the partners lived apart for a few weeks or a few dozen days (under 90) after a quarrel or short breakdown and then reconciled, and asks when 12 months of cohabitation is reached or what the marital status is.

⚠ 分居生效日第90天陷阱Separation effective on day 90

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生误以为因感情破裂分居满90天后，分居状态的法定生效日期是第90天当天，而忽略了法律规定回溯至「开始分开居住的那一天」Candidates assume the legal effective date of separation is the 90th day after the breakdown, forgetting that the law reaches back to the day the couple began living apart.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干明确夫妻于某月某日因感情破裂开始分开居住且已持续满90天，询问分居正式生效的起始日期或年内申报状态。The stem says a couple began living apart on a given date because of relationship breakdown and 90 days have passed, and asks for the date separation takes effect or the filing status for the year.

⚠ 配偶零收入不报陷阱Zero-income spouse need not be reported

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生直觉认为配偶无任何收入或未报税就无需在自己申报表上填写配偶净收入，忽略了该字段是计算配偶额抵免和家庭福利核发的必需参数。Candidates feel that if the spouse has no income or does not file, nothing needs to be entered for spouse net income, ignoring that this field is a required input for the spouse amount and family benefits.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调纳税人已婚但配偶全年在海外或无任何收入且未报税，询问申报表第一步配偶信息及第23600行的填写规则。The stem stresses that the taxpayer is married but the spouse was abroad or had no income and did not file, and asks about the spouse information in Step 1 and Line 23600.

⚠ 居住省全年最长居住地陷阱Longest residence during the year

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 纳税人习惯于按全年实际居住天数最多的省份来选择省表，混淆了税法以「12月31日当天」单一事实基准定省表的法律拟制规则。Taxpayers habitually choose the provincial form by the province where they lived the most days, confusing this with the legal rule that the province is fixed by the single fact of December 31.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述纳税人在某省居住了大部分月份，但在年底（如12月20日）搬迁至另一省并定居，询问应适用哪个省份的税表 Form 428。The stem says the taxpayer lived in one province for most of the year but moved and settled in another late in the year (for example on December 20), and asks which province's Form 428 applies.

⚠ T1135市值与夫妻合并陷阱T1135: market value and couple combined

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生极易混淆两点：一是把成本额门槛（ACB）误当成公允市场价值（FMV）；二是把个人门槛（各人名下合计超 100,000 元）误当成夫妻家庭合并总额。Candidates mix up two points: treating the cost amount (ACB) threshold as fair market value (FMV), and treating the individual threshold (more than $100,000 held by each person) as a combined couple total.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出资产原始买入成本与当前大幅增值后的市场价值，或者给出夫妻双方各自名下持有的境外资产金额，询问是否触发 Form T1135 申报义务。The stem gives the original purchase cost and a much higher current market value, or gives foreign assets held separately by each spouse, and asks whether Form T1135 must be filed.

第 1 题Q1普通法同居判定与短暂分离Common-law cohabitation and a short separation★★陷阱：分开即计时归零陷阱

Liam and Sophia began living together in a conjugal relationship on March 1, 2025. In October 2025, due to a relationship dispute, Sophia moved out for 45 days, after which they reconciled and resumed living together. As of March 15, 2026, they had no children. When filing his 2026 personal income tax return, how should Liam determine his marital status as of December 31, 2026?

Liam 与 Sophia 于 2025 年 3 月 1 日开始保持同居伴侣关系。2025 年 10 月，因感情争执，Sophia 搬出居住了 45 天，之后两人复合并继续同居。截至 2026 年 3 月 15 日，两人未育有子女。在申报 2026 纳税年度个人所得税时，Liam 在确定其截至 2026 年 12 月 31 日的法定婚姻状况时应如何认定？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入「分开即计时归零陷阱」；根据税法，同居期间因感情破裂分开只要不满 90 天，仍算作连续同居，不会导致计时器归零。Incorrect. This falls into the 'any break resets the clock' trap: under the tax rules, a separation during cohabitation caused by relationship breakdown that lasts fewer than 90 days still counts as continuous cohabitation and does not reset the count.

  2. ✓ 正确。普通法同居（Living common-law）要求连续同居满 12 个月；税法明确规定，这 12 个月内因感情破裂分开但不满 90 天又复合的，该间断期仍计入连续同居时间。他们在 2026 年 3 月即满 12 个月，截至 2026 年 12 月 31 日应确认为普通法同居。Correct. Living common-law requires 12 months of continuous cohabitation; the tax rules state that a break of fewer than 90 days due to relationship breakdown, followed by reconciliation, still counts toward continuous cohabitation. They reached 12 months in March 2026, so on December 31, 2026 the status is living common-law.

  3. ✗ 错误。分居（Separated）要求因感情破裂分开居住满 90 天，且两人已复合，年底处于共同居住状态，不能填分居。Incorrect. Separated requires living apart for 90 days because of relationship breakdown, and the two have reconciled and were living together at year end, so Separated cannot be chosen.

  4. ✗ 错误。联邦《所得税法》规定的普通法伴侣同居基础门槛是 12 个月，而非 24 个月。Incorrect. The federal Income Tax Act sets the basic common-law cohabitation threshold at 12 months, not 24 months.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Ethan and Olivia began cohabiting in an intimate relationship on February 1, 2026. On August 15, 2026, a child was born to them. As of December 31, 2026, they had cohabited for only 11 months. What is Olivia's marital status for her 2026 T1 return?

Ethan 与 Olivia 于 2026 年 2 月 1 日开始保持亲密同居关系。2026 年 8 月 15 日，两人的亲生子女出生。截至 2026 年 12 月 31 日，两人同居仅满 11 个月。在填报 2026 年 T1 申报表时，Olivia 的法定婚姻状况应填报为什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。忽视了普通法关系的替代条件；只要对方是纳税人子女的生父/生母，无需等待满 12 个月即可成立普通法同居。Incorrect. This ignores the alternative condition for common-law status: if the partner is the parent of the taxpayer's child, there is no need to wait 12 months.

  2. ✗ 错误。已婚（Married）严格限于具有合法登记婚姻关系的人士，有共同子女不会自动变成已婚。Incorrect. Married is strictly limited to persons in a legally registered marriage; having a common child does not automatically make them married.

  3. ✗ 错误。普通法地位是法定事实判断，不以向政府提交公证声明为成立前提。Incorrect. Common-law status is a legal question of fact and does not depend on submitting a notarized declaration to the government.

  4. ✓ 正确。普通法同居三项判定条件满足其一即可：① 连续同居满 12 个月；② 对方是子女的生父/生母（含收养）；③ 拥有监护权且该子女完全依赖扶养。因两人有共同亲生子女，即使同居未满 12 个月亦依法成立普通法伴侣关系。Correct. Meeting any one of the three conditions is enough for common-law status: (1) 12 months of continuous cohabitation; (2) the partner is the child's biological or adoptive parent; (3) the partner has custody and control of the child, who is wholly dependent on the partner for support. Because they have a common biological child, common-law status exists even though cohabitation is under 12 months.

答案Answer：d

第 2 题Q2分居成立条件与追溯生效日Separation requirements and retroactive effective date★★陷阱：分居生效日第90天陷阱

Marcus and Elena, who are legally married, experienced a breakdown in their marriage and began living separate and apart on November 15, 2026. They continued to live apart continuously, reaching 90 days of separation on February 13, 2027. When Marcus files his 2026 T1 personal tax return in April 2027, what was his marital status as of December 31, 2026, and what was the effective date of that status?

Marcus 与 Elena 为法定夫妻。因感情破裂，两人于 2026 年 11 月 15 日开始分开居住。两人持续分居，至 2027 年 2 月 13 日分居满 90 天。当 Marcus 于 2027 年 4 月提交其 2026 年 T1 个人税表时，其截至 2026 年 12 月 31 日的法定婚姻状况是什么，且该状况的生效日期是哪一天？

  1. ✗ 错误。虽然 12 月 31 日时未满 90 天，但在 2027 年 4 月申报时 90 天已届满，分居成立后其生效日追溯至开始分开住的那一天（2026 年 11 月 15 日），因此 12 月 31 日当天的状态已被追溯确认为分居。Incorrect. Although fewer than 90 days had passed on December 31, the 90 days had been completed by the time of filing in April 2027; once separation is established its effective date reaches back to the day they began living apart (November 15, 2026), so the status on December 31 is confirmed as Separated.

  2. ✗ 错误。落入「分居生效日第90天陷阱」；分居生效日不是第 90 天那一天，而是开始分开居住的第一天。Incorrect. This falls into the 'separation effective on day 90' trap; the effective date is not the 90th day but the first day of living apart.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据 CRA 规则，分居（Separated）必须因感情破裂分开住满 90 天才成立，但一旦满 90 天，其生效日期不是第 90 天，而是回溯到开始分开住的那一天（2026 年 11 月 15 日）。到 2027 年 4 月报税时，90 天条件已满足，故 2026 年 12 月 31 日应填报为分居。Correct. Under CRA rules, Separated requires living apart for 90 days because of relationship breakdown, but once 90 days are met the effective date is not the 90th day but reaches back to the day they began living apart (November 15, 2026). By the April 2027 filing the 90-day condition is met, so the status on December 31, 2026 is Separated.

  4. ✗ 错误。分居满 90 天不等于离婚（Divorced），离婚必须具有法定的法院离婚判决文件。Incorrect. Living apart for 90 days is not a divorce; Divorced requires a court divorce judgment.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

David and Grace are legally married. In September 2026, Grace was transferred to another city for a temporary 1-year work assignment and lived in a rented apartment, while David remained in their family home. Their marital relationship remained intact throughout 2026. How should David report his marital status on his 2026 T1 return?

David 与 Grace 是合法夫妻。2026 年 9 月，Grace 因工作被临时外派至另一城市工作 1 年并租房居住，而 David 留在原家庭住宅，两人的婚姻感情在 2026 年始终保持良好。在申报 2026 年 T1 税表时，David 应如何填报其婚姻状况？

  1. ✓ 正确。因工作、求学、健康原因或服刑而分开居住属于非自愿分居（involuntary separation），并非感情破裂分居，双方在税法上依然按已婚（Married）申报。Correct. Living apart for work, school, health reasons or imprisonment is an involuntary separation, not a separation due to relationship breakdown, so the couple still file as Married for tax purposes.
