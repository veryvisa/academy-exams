本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 退还性抵免资格与排除条款辨析题：重点考查 CWB（附表 6）对全日制学生就读超 13 周的排除限制与例外（有合格受抚养人），以及 CTC（附表 11）在 26 至 65 岁年龄窗口与 CTCL 累积机制。Refundable tax credit eligibility and statutory exclusion questions: Testing CWB (Schedule 6) full-time student exclusion rules for enrollment exceeding 13 weeks and its exception (having an eligible dependant), as well as CTC (Schedule 11) age 26 to 65 eligibility window and CTCL accumulation mechanisms.
  • 家庭福利核算基准与共同监护法定各半实务题：重点考查 CCB 以夫妻双方合并的「调整后家庭净收入」（AFNI）为唯一定向递减尺度，以及离异父母共同监护下各拿 50% 的不可协商性与父母私下协议无效原则。Family benefit calculation baseline and statutory shared custody split questions: Testing CCB reliance on combined adjusted family net income (AFNI) as the sole income-testing benchmark, alongside the non-negotiable 50/50 statutory split for separated parents with shared custody and the strict invalidity of private parent agreements.
  • 预付款多领免追回与时间轴错位综合应用题：考查 ACWB 按上年 50% 自动预发后，即便当年收入超标也不予追回的法定保护，以及春季报税（基年）与 7 月起福利年（发放期）跨年错位运作逻辑。Advance payment overpayment no-clawback and benefit year timeline shift questions: Testing statutory protection where ACWB payments (automatically issued at 50% of the prior year's entitlement) are never clawed back even if current-year income exceeds thresholds, as well as the calendar timeline shift between spring tax filing (base year) and July benefit year payment cycles.
  • 牙科计划与残障福利双轨申领机制辨析题：重点考查 CDCP 对自愿退出私人牙科保险者的排除限制与每年 6 月 1 日续期要求，以及成人残障福利（服务部管、需主动申请）与儿童残障给付（CRA管、随CCB自动发）的管理双轨。Dental care plan and dual-stream disability benefit administration questions: Testing CDCP exclusion rules for individuals who voluntarily opt out of employer dental plans and mandatory June 1 annual renewal requirements, alongside the distinct administrative streams separating adult disability benefits (administered by Service Canada, requiring formal application) from child disability benefits (administered by CRA, automatically added to CCB).
  • 2026 税年新规改革与相邻省级差异辨析题：考查 2026 年 7 月起 GST/HST 抵免更名为 CGEB 并增额 25%（算法不变）的最新立法变动，CWB 在艾伯塔、魁北克、努纳武特三省重配协议，以及 BC 省气候行动抵免随碳税终止的失效背景。2026 tax year legislative reforms and provincial variation distinction questions: Testing legislative changes taking effect July 2026 renaming the GST/HST credit to CGEB with a 25% increase (retaining the same formula), CWB reconfiguration agreements in Alberta, Quebec, and Nunavut, and the termination of the BC Climate Action Tax Credit following the end of the consumer carbon tax.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 福利年与税年混淆陷阱Benefit Year vs. Tax Year Confusion Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误把福利年（7 月至次年 6 月）当成自然日历税年（1 月至 12 月），用当年尚未评税的即时收入倒推正在领取的福利，或以为春季报税调整会立即在当月生效改变发放额。Mistakenly confusing the benefit year (July 1 to June 30) with the calendar tax year (January 1 to December 31), attempting to calculate currently received benefits using unassessed real-time current-year income, or assuming that spring tax filing immediately adjusts monthly benefit payments in the filing month.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干中出现上半年（如 1 至 6 月）收入突变、失业或调薪，却询问当年 7 月前正按上一税年发放的福利金额变化。The question stem presents an income change, job loss, or salary adjustment occurring in the first half of the year (January to June) and asks how it impacts benefit payments issued before July based on the prior tax year.

⚠ 共同监护协议切分陷阱Shared Custody Agreement Allocation Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为分居父母签署的有法律效力的书面协议或法院调解书可以自由指定由某一方全额领取 CCB 或按 60/40 等比例分配，忽略了 CRA 只要判定共同监护即严格执行每人 50% 的刚性规则。Mistakenly assuming that a legally binding written agreement or court mediation accord between separated parents can allocate 100% of the CCB to one parent or split it along arbitrary ratios (such as 60/40), overlooking that CRA strictly enforces a mandatory 50% split per parent whenever shared custody is established.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出分居协议中明确约定「由某一方全额领取儿童福利金」或约定非 50/50 比例，询问家长实得 CCB 金额。The question stem presents a separation agreement stipulating that one parent claims 100% of the Canada Child Benefit or agreeing on a non-50/50 split, and asks for the actual CCB amount received by a parent.

⚠ 残障福利申领主体混淆陷阱Disability Benefit Administration Stream Confusion Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 混淆了 18 岁以下随 CCB 自动发放的儿童残障给付（CDB，CRA 管理）与 18 至 64 岁成人必须主动向加拿大服务部申报的加拿大残障福利（Canada Disability Benefit），误以为大人批了 T2201 也会自动按月收到现金。Confusing the Child Disability Benefit (CDB, administered by the CRA and paid automatically with the CCB for children under 18) with the Canada Disability Benefit for adults aged 18 to 64 (administered by Service Canada, requiring a separate application), mistakenly assuming that adults approved for Form T2201 automatically receive monthly cash payments.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 18 岁以上持 T2201 证明的纳税人，询问其如何获得新设立的加拿大残障福利月度给付。The question stem presents a taxpayer aged 18 or older holding an approved Form T2201 and asks what steps are required to receive the newly established monthly Canada Disability Benefit.

⚠ 工作福利全日制学生排除陷阱Full-Time Student Exclusion for CWB Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为只要年满 19 岁且有打工收入就能申领 CWB，忽略了全年在指定教育机构全日制就读超 13 周的学生被法定排除，除非年末有合格受抚养人。Mistakenly assuming that any working individual aged 19 or older with employment income qualifies for the Canada Workers Benefit (CWB), overlooking the statutory exclusion that completely bars students enrolled full-time for more than 13 weeks in the year unless they have an eligible dependant at the end of the year.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干中出现打工赚取数千元工作收入的无子女全日制在校大学生，询问其附表 6 可申报的 line 45300 抵免额。The question stem presents a full-time post-secondary student with no dependants earning several thousand dollars from a job and asks to calculate their claimable line 45300 CWB amount on Schedule 6.

⚠ CGEB更名与基年倒推陷阱CGEB Renaming and Base Year Calculation Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 机械死记历史考试资料中的「GST/HST Credit」旧名与旧成人上限 349 元，忽略了自 2026 年 7 月起该项目已正式更名为 CGEB 且增额 25%（成人上限升至 445 元），且其测算基年为 2025 税年。Mechanically memorizing the outdated name 'GST/HST Credit' and the historical adult maximum of $349 from obsolete study materials, failing to recognize that effective July 2026 the program was renamed CGEB with amounts increased by 25% (raising the adult maximum to $445), calculated using the 2025 base tax year.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干明确提及 2026 年 7 月起的商品与服务销售税退税/必需品福利，并要求计算单身或家庭适用的给付上限。The question stem references the sales tax credit or essentials benefit effective July 2026 and asks to determine the maximum benefit payable to a single individual or family.

第 1 题Q1工作福利全日制学生排除Full-time student exclusion for Canada Workers Benefit★★陷阱：工作福利全日制学生排除陷阱

A 21-year-old unmarried individual with no dependants lived in Ontario all year. During the tax year, they earned $7,500 from a summer job and were enrolled as a full-time student at a designated post-secondary institution for 20 weeks. Assuming standard federal rules apply, what is their eligible Canada Workers Benefit (line 45300)?

一位 21 岁、无受抚养人的未婚纳税人全年居住在安大略省。在税年内，该纳税人通过暑期工作赚取了 $7,500 的工作收入，并在指定高等教育机构全日制就读了 20 周。假定适用联邦通则，该纳税人可在 line 45300 申报的加拿大工作福利（CWB）金额是多少？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据《所得税法》第 122.7 条，在税年内于指定教育机构全日制就读超过 13 周的纳税人将被法定排除在 CWB 资格之外，除非在年末拥有合格受抚养人。该纳税人全日制就读 20 周且无受抚养人，福利金为 $0。Correct. Under Income Tax Act s. 122.7, an individual enrolled as a full-time student at a designated educational institution for more than 13 weeks in the tax year is statutorily excluded from CWB eligibility, unless they have an eligible dependant at the end of the year. Because this taxpayer was enrolled full-time for 20 weeks and has no dependants, their benefit entitlement is $0.

  2. ✗ 错误。此选项仅套用了基本额相位增计算公式，忽略了全日制就读超过 13 周且无受抚养人的法定排除条款（full-time student exclusion）。Incorrect. This option merely applies the basic phase-in formula, overlooking the statutory full-time student exclusion for individuals enrolled full-time for more than 13 weeks without an eligible dependant.

  3. ✗ 错误。即便收入低于递减起点，该纳税人因全日制就读超 13 周直接丧失资格，不能申领 $1,665 的上限。Incorrect. Even though net income is below the phase-out threshold, the taxpayer is disqualified due to full-time enrollment exceeding 13 weeks and cannot claim the single maximum entitlement of $1,665.

  4. ✗ 错误。税法不存在全日制学生减半发放 CWB 的折算规定，超 13 周即完全排除。Incorrect. The tax law contains no prorated 50% benefit provision for full-time students; enrollment exceeding 13 weeks results in complete disqualification.

A 23-year-old single parent lives with their 3-year-old child in British Columbia. During the tax year, the parent was enrolled full-time in university for 28 weeks and earned $14,000 from part-time work. Does the full-time student restriction disqualify this parent from claiming the Canada Workers Benefit (Schedule 6)?

一位 23 岁的单亲家长与 3 岁的孩子居住在 BC 省。在税年内，该家长在大学全日制就读了 28 周，并通过兼职赚取了 $14,000 工作收入。全日制学生限制条款是否会剥夺该家长申领加拿大工作福利（附表 6）的资格？

  1. ✗ 错误。全日制就读超 13 周并非绝对排除，税法对年末有合格受抚养人的纳税人明确设有豁免例外。Incorrect. Full-time enrollment exceeding 13 weeks is not an absolute bar; the tax law explicitly provides an exception for taxpayers who have an eligible dependant at the end of the year.

  2. ✗ 错误。助学金扣除与工作福利并不相互排斥，且工作福利是法定退还性抵免。Incorrect. Student bursary deductions and the Canada Workers Benefit are not mutually exclusive, and the CWB is a statutory refundable tax credit.

  3. ✓ 正确。法条对全日制在读学生的排除条款明确规定了例外情形：若纳税人在年末拥有合格受抚养人（如本案中 3 岁的孩子），则不受 13 周限制，依然可以按家庭口径申领 CWB。Correct. The statutory exclusion for full-time students explicitly provides an exception: if a taxpayer has an eligible dependant at the end of the year (such as the 3-year-old child in this scenario), the 13-week restriction does not apply, and they remain eligible to claim the CWB under the family entitlement rules.

  4. ✗ 错误。税法不存在因全日制就读而对有子女家庭罚扣 50% 福利的规定。Incorrect. The tax law contains no statutory penalty reducing benefits by 50% for student parents with eligible children.

第 2 题Q2共同监护儿童福利金各半分摊Shared custody 50/50 statutory split for Canada Child Benefit★★陷阱：共同监护协议切分陷阱

Divorced parents share custody of their 4-year-old child, with the child living roughly equal amounts of time with each parent. Their signed legal separation agreement specifies that the father shall receive 100% of the Canada Child Benefit (CCB). How will the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) issue the CCB for this child?

离异父母共同监护他们 4 岁的孩子，孩子在两位家长处同住的时间大致相等。两人签署的具有法律约束力的分居协议明确约定：加拿大儿童福利金（CCB）全额由父亲一人申领。加拿大税务局（CRA）将如何发放该孩子的 CCB？

  1. ✗ 错误。此选项掉入了协议切分陷阱。CRA 明确规定父母私下签署的协议不能改变法定共同监护各半规则。Incorrect. Falls into the agreement allocation trap. CRA explicitly rules that private agreements between parents cannot alter the statutory 50/50 shared custody split.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据共同监护（shared custody）的法定硬性规则，只要孩子在双方处同住时间大致相当且各负主要照料责任，CRA 不接受任何自行约定比例，一律按各自全权监护应得额的 50% 分别发放，且各自依据自身调整后家庭净收入（AFNI）独立计算。Correct. Under mandatory shared custody rules, when a child lives roughly equal time with each parent and both share primary care, the CRA rejects any privately agreed allocation ratio and strictly issues 50% of the sole-custody entitlement to each parent, calculated independently based on each parent's own adjusted family net income (AFNI).

  3. ✗ 错误。税局不接受「让收入低的一方全领」的私下避税安排，强制执行双方各半。Incorrect. The CRA does not accept private tax-planning arrangements assigning 100% of the benefit to the lower-income parent; the statutory 50/50 split is mandatory.

  4. ✗ 错误。税局无需等待法院重新修改裁决，而是会依职权直接按法定的各 50% 比例分开发放。Incorrect. The CRA does not need to wait for a family court amendment; it directly enforces the statutory 50% split per parent under its administrative authority.

  4. ✗ 错误。联邦卫生部不会将脱保人员自动转移至商业保险池。Incorrect. Health Canada does not automatically transfer individuals who lapse coverage into private insurance pools.
