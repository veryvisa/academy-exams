第 8 章 · 不可退还抵免Chapter 8 · Non-Refundable Tax Credits Schedule: Rules and Calculations After the Federal Rate Reduction to 14% (2026 Tax Year)Non-refundable tax credits

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 抵免机制与非退还性质测试题：考查扣除项（边际税率累进折算）与非退还抵免项（适用百分比拉平折算）的底层政策逻辑差异，测试纳税人对应缴税额作为抵免天花板的理解，以及纳税人在当期零税额情形下非退还抵免不可产生现金退税的法律后果。Credit mechanism and non-refundable nature questions: Testing the underlying policy logic difference between deductions (progressive conversion at marginal tax rates) and non-refundable tax credits (flat conversion at the appropriate percentage), assessing the candidate's understanding of tax payable as the credit ceiling, and the legal consequence that non-refundable credits cannot generate a cash refund when tax payable is zero.
  • 2026 税年联邦抵免率下降与 Top-Up 补丁测算题：考查联邦抵免率从 15% 降至 14%（第一档税阶门槛 $58,523）后，当纳税人各项非退还抵免额总和超出门槛时，ITA s.118(11) 顶额补充抵免（Top-Up Tax Credit）的自动触发条件、保 15% 还原乘数机制（7.14%）及慈善捐赠 $200 封顶计入规则。2026 tax year federal credit rate reduction and Top-Up credit calculation questions: Testing the scenario where the federal credit rate drops from 15% to 14% (first bracket threshold $58,523) and the sum of the taxpayer's non-refundable tax credit amounts exceeds the threshold, evaluating the automatic trigger condition for the Top-Up Tax Credit under ITA s. 118(11), the 15% rate restoration multiplier mechanism (7.14%), and the $200 cap inclusion rule for charitable donations.
  • 家庭类抵免额度核定与逐元递减题：考查基本个人金额（BPA 基础段 14,829 元 + 增强段 1,623 元，高收入线性递减）、配偶额（Line 30300 依配偶净收入逐元冲减至零）与合格受抚养人额（Line 30400 单亲额，与配偶额严格互斥且同一子女不可重复申报）的资格判定与额度测算。Family tax credit entitlement and dollar-for-dollar phase-out questions: Testing the eligibility determination and amount calculation for the basic personal amount (BPA base amount of $14,829 plus enhanced amount of $1,623, with linear phase-out for high earners), the spouse or common-law partner amount (Line 30300 reduced dollar-for-dollar by the spouse's net income to zero), and the amount for an eligible dependant (Line 30400 single-parent amount, strictly mutually exclusive with the spouse amount and prohibiting duplicate claims for the same child).
  • 学费额抵减顺序与结转不可逆单向门判断题：考查当年实付学费必须优先强制用于抵减学生本人税额至零、结余部分转让（联邦上限 $5,000 且须在学费证明背面签署指定给合格亲属）或结转，以及结转至未来年度的学费额永久锁定自用、终身不可再转让的单向门规则。Tuition amount claiming order and irreversible carry-forward one-way door questions: Testing the mandatory rule that current-year eligible tuition must first be used to reduce the student's own tax payable to zero, with remaining amounts transferred (federal ceiling of $5,000, requiring signed designation on the back of the tuition certificate to an eligible relative) or carried forward, and the one-way door rule whereby tuition carried forward to future years is permanently locked for the student's own use and can never be transferred in any future year.
  • 特殊专项抵免资格与 2026 新规防重复题：考查 2026 税年起无障碍改造抵免（HATC 20,000 元 上限）严禁与医疗费抵免（METC）双报的防重复新规、残障额（Line 31600 10,341 元 事前须 T2201 核准及未成年补充额受托儿费冲减）、以及 CPP 基础段（抵免）与增强段（扣除）的分流定性。Special targeted credit eligibility and 2026 anti-duplication rule questions: Testing the new anti-duplication rule effective in the 2026 tax year strictly prohibiting double-claiming between the Home Accessibility Tax Credit (HATC, $20,000 expense cap) and the Medical Expense Tax Credit (METC), the disability amount (Line 31600, $10,341, requiring prior T2201 approval and clawback of the supplement for persons under 18 by child care expenses), and the dual-track characterization of base CPP contributions (credit) versus enhanced CPP contributions (deduction).

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 抵免额无税照样能退钱Believing non-refundable credits yield cash refunds without tax payable

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 受可退还补助或商业退税广告的直觉误导，误以为只要满足非退还抵免资格就能从 CRA 获得真金白银的退税款，忽略了非退还抵免的法定职能只能将应缴税额压降至零，应缴税额为零时任何抵免额的实际节税价值均为零且多余部分作废。Misled by intuitions from refundable benefits or commercial tax refund advertisements, candidates mistakenly assume that qualifying for a non-refundable tax credit yields a cash refund directly from CRA, overlooking that the statutory function of a non-refundable credit is solely to reduce tax payable to zero; when tax payable is zero, the practical tax savings of any additional credit is zero and unused amounts expire without refund.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干中纳税人当年收入极低或已被基本个人金额将应缴税款抵到零，却申报大额医疗费、首次购房额或无障碍改造等非退还抵免，询问可获得的税收好处或退税款。The question stem features a taxpayer with very low income or whose tax payable is already reduced to zero by the basic personal amount, who then claims large medical expenses, the home buyers' amount, or the home accessibility tax credit, asking for the resulting tax savings or cash refund.

⚠ 配偶没工作即报满配偶额Claiming the maximum spouse amount simply because the spouse is not employed

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 将配偶「没有受雇工作（无 T4 薪资）」盲目等同于「净收入为零」，忽略了配偶额的递减基准是配偶的全年净收入（Line 23600），包含投资分红、利息、应税退休金或应税政府福利，按对方净收入逐元冲减。Blindly equating a spouse having no job (no T4 employment earnings) with having zero net income, overlooking that the reduction base for the spouse amount is the spouse's full-year net income (Line 23600), which includes taxable investment dividends, interest, taxable pensions, or taxable government benefits, reducing the credit dollar-for-dollar.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干说明配偶当年虽未外出工作或无受雇薪资，但名下有大额投资收益、利息或应税退休金给付，询问申报人可扣减的配偶额金额。The question stem notes that while the spouse did not work or had no employment earnings during the year, they received substantial investment returns, interest, or taxable pension payments, asking for the allowable spouse amount the claimant can deduct.

⚠ 学费当年不转留待以后转Carrying forward tuition amounts with the intention of transferring them later

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为学费额度和储蓄资金一样可以随时自由支配，忽视了税法规定学费转移决定必须在支出发生当年做出，一旦结转到未来年度，额度将永久锁定在学生本人名下自用，终身不可再转让给父母或配偶。Mistakenly assuming tuition credits can be freely allocated at any time like savings, ignoring the statutory rule that a tuition transfer designation must be executed in the year the expense was incurred; once carried forward to a future year, the balance is permanently locked in the student's name for personal use and can never be transferred to parents or a spouse in any future year.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干中学生当年有结转自以前年度的未用学费额度，或者当年未在学费证明上签署转移，询问在后续高收入年份能否将结转学费转让给父母抵税。The question stem mentions a student with unused tuition carried forward from prior years, or who did not sign the transfer designation on the certificate in the study year, asking whether the carried-forward tuition can be transferred to parents for tax savings in a subsequent high-income year.

⚠ 新规下改造与医疗费双报Double-claiming home accessibility renovations and medical expenses under the new rules

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 许多人仍沿用 2025 税年及以前的旧规惯性，加上 CRA 官网部分行末说明页更新滞后，误以为 2026 年发生的无障碍改造支出依然能同时申报 HATC 和医疗费抵免。Candidates rely on the inertia of pre-2026 rules and outdated guidance on certain CRA web pages, mistakenly assuming that home accessibility expenditures incurred in 2026 can still be claimed simultaneously under both the HATC and the medical expense tax credit.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及 2026 税年发生的无障碍居家改造支出（如安装轮椅坡道或升降梯），询问能否同时申报 Line 31285（HATC）与 Line 33099（医疗费抵免）。The question stem involves accessibility home renovations (such as installing wheelchair ramps or elevators) incurred in the 2026 tax year, asking whether the costs can be claimed simultaneously on Line 31285 (HATC) and Line 33099 (METC).

⚠ 高收入者套用固定满额BPAApplying the maximum fixed basic personal amount to high-income earners

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 习惯将基本个人金额视为全员适用的固定金额（如按上限 $16,452 申报），忽略了自 2020 年起高收入者（2026 税年净收入在 181,440 元 至 258,482 元 之间）其 1,623 元 的增强段将线性递减至零，最低仅保留 14,829 元。Habitually treating the basic personal amount as a universally fixed sum for all taxpayers (e.g. claiming the maximum $16,452), overlooking that since 2020, high-income earners (2026 net income between $181,440 and $258,482) face a linear phase-out of the $1,623 enhanced portion down to zero, retaining only the base amount of $14,829 at or above the top threshold.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干中纳税人净收入处于高税阶（如 $200,000 或更高），询问其 2026 税年可申报的基本个人金额（BPA）或折算税额。The question stem features a taxpayer with net income in the high brackets (such as $200,000 or above), asking for their allowable basic personal amount (BPA) or credit value for the 2026 tax year.

第 1 题Q1非退还抵免性质与零税额限制Non-refundable credit nature and zero tax payable limitation★陷阱：抵免额无税照样能退钱

Ethan graduated from university in late 2026 and worked for only one month, earning $4,500 of employment income. His federal tax payable before credits was $630 (14% × $4,500). His basic personal amount of $16,452 generated a tax credit of $2,303.28, which reduced his federal tax payable to zero. In December 2026, Ethan purchased his first home and qualified for the $10,000 home buyers' amount (Line 31270). What is the total federal tax benefit (reduction of tax or cash refund) Ethan receives from claiming the home buyers' amount on his 2026 return?

伊森（Ethan）于 2026 年底大学毕业并仅工作了一个月，获得受雇收入 $4,500。在计入抵免前其联邦应缴税额为 $630（14% × $4,500）。其基本个人金额 $16,452 产生了 $2,303.28 的税收抵免，已将他的联邦应缴税额抵减至零。2026 年 12 月，伊森购买了首套合资格自住房，符合申报 $10,000 首次购房者额（Line 31270）的条件。伊森在其 2026 年报税表上申报该首次购房者额可获得的联邦税收好处（减税或现金退税）是多少？

  1. ✗ 错误。此选项误将首次购房者额当成了可退还税收抵免（refundable tax credit）。首次购房者额是非退还抵免，绝不会直接派发现金退税。这落入了「抵免额无税照样能退钱」的典型陷阱。Incorrect. This option mistakenly treats the home buyers' amount as a refundable tax credit. The home buyers' amount is a non-refundable tax credit and never generates a direct cash refund. This falls into the classic trap of assuming non-refundable credits yield cash refunds without tax payable.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据第一节制度设计底层逻辑，非退还税收抵免（non-refundable tax credit）的法律效力仅限于将纳税人的当期应缴税额压降至零，不能变为负数，多余的抵免额既不退还现金，也不能结转。由于基本个人金额（basic personal amount, BPA）在法定扣减顺序中排在首位，且已完全将伊森的 $630 联邦税额冲抵至零，因此排在后面的首次购房者额（Line 31270）无法产生任何实际税收减免效果，最终税收价值为 $0。Correct. Under the institutional design principles in Section 1, the legal effect of a non-refundable tax credit is strictly limited to reducing the taxpayer's current-year tax payable to zero; it cannot create a negative balance, and unused credits can neither be refunded in cash nor carried forward. Because the basic personal amount (BPA) ranks first in the statutory claiming order and has already completely eliminated Ethan's $630 federal tax payable, the home buyers' amount (Line 31270) ranked behind it provides zero actual tax savings, resulting in a tax benefit of $0.

  3. ✗ 错误。不仅 2026 年联邦抵免率已调整为 14%（而非 15%），而且首次购房者额依法不可向未来年度结转（carry-forward）。Incorrect. In addition to the federal credit rate being reduced to 14% (rather than 15%) for 2026, the home buyers' amount cannot legally be carried forward to future years.

  4. ✗ 错误。伊森的联邦税已经被基本个人金额抵为零，首次购房者额无法再抵减任何联邦税；联邦与省税各自按独立表单结算，不存在平分 $630 的规则。Incorrect. Ethan's federal tax liability has already been reduced to zero by the basic personal amount, leaving no tax for the home buyers' amount to offset; federal and provincial taxes are calculated on separate forms, and there is no rule dividing $630 equally between them.

Chloe's father had substantial taxable income and asked Chloe to transfer her $6,000 carried-forward tuition balance to him on her 2026 return to save family tax. 2025 年，克洛伊（Chloe）大学毕业并拥有 $6,000 未使用的学费额度，她在附表 11（Schedule 11）上将其结转至 2026 税年。2026 年中，克洛伊应税收入为零。克洛伊的父亲应税收入很高，他要求克洛伊在 2026 年税表上将该笔 $6,000 的结转学费额度转让给他以抵减家庭税款。根据联邦税法，这项转让请求应如何处理？

  1. ✗ 错误。$5,000 转移上限仅适用于学费发生的当年，结转额度一旦形成，绝不能在以后年度转让。Incorrect. The $5,000 transfer limit applies only in the year the tuition was incurred; once an unused balance is carried forward, it can never be transferred in a later year.

  2. ✗ 错误。结转学费完全丧失了转让资格，既不能转 $5,000，更不能全额转让 $6,000。Incorrect. Carried-forward tuition permanently loses transfer eligibility; it cannot be transferred up to $5,000, nor can the full $6,000 be transferred.

  3. ✓ 正确。第二节第 2.9 小节明确指明了这道「不可逆单向门」：学费转让决定必须在产生学费的当年（2025 年）完成；学生当年未转出的部分一旦自动结转到自己名下的未来年度，该笔额度终身只能由学生本人在未来有应缴税款的年份抵减自身税额，法律严禁将结转额转让给父母或配偶。Correct. Section 2.9 highlights the irreversible one-way door rule: the decision to transfer tuition must be executed in the year the expense was incurred (2025); once untransferred amounts are carried forward to future years under the student's name, they can only be used by the student to reduce their own tax in future years, and the law strictly prohibits transferring carried-forward tuition to parents or a spouse.

第 6 题Q6残障额核准与未成年补充冲减Disability amount pre-approval and child supplement clawback★★陷阱：残障额需事前核准与冲减

In 2026, George and Martha's 12-year-old son, Peter, had a valid Form T2201 (Disability Tax Credit Certificate) on file and approved by CRA. In addition, George claimed $6,000 of qualifying child care expenses paid for Peter as a child care deduction on Line 21400. After the $6,000 child care deduction, what is Peter's supplemental disability amount for children (2026 maximum $6,032) on Line 31600?

2026 年，乔治与玛莎（George and Martha）12 岁的儿子彼得（Peter）已在 CRA 备有经官方正式核准有效的 Form T2201（残障税收抵免证明）。在其 2026 年税表上，他们申报了彼得的基础残障额 $10,341。此外，乔治在 Line 21400 申报了为彼得支付的 $6,000 合资格托儿费扣除。申报这 $6,000 托儿费后，彼得在 Line 31600 的未成年残障补充额（2026 年最高 $6,032）还剩多少？

  1. ✓ 正确。补充额被超过门槛的托儿费逐元冲减：$6,000 − $3,533 = $2,467；$6,032 − $2,467 = $3,565。Correct. The supplement for persons under 18 is reduced dollar-for-dollar by child care expenses exceeding the threshold: $6,000 − $3,533 = $2,467 reduction; allowable supplement is $6,032 − $2,467 = $3,565.

  2. ✗ 错误。两格各算各的，漏了冲减。Incorrect. This option calculates the amounts independently, omitting the mandatory child care expense clawback.

  3. ✗ 错误。把冲减额当成了剩余额：$6,000 − $3,533 = $2,467 是要减掉的部分，补充额还剩 $3,565。Incorrect. This option mistakes the clawback amount for the remaining credit: $6,000 − $3,533 = $2,467 is the reduction amount, leaving an allowable supplement of $3,565.

  4. ✗ 错误。按抵免率 14% 比例折减（$6,000 的 14% 从 $6,032 里扣掉）会得到 $5,192；冲减是逐元扣额度，不按 14%。Incorrect. Reducing the credit base by 14% of the expenses ($6,032 − 14% × $6,000 = $5,192) misapplies the rule; the clawback operates dollar-for-dollar on the credit base, not at the 14% credit rate.
