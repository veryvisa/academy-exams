① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 两层扣除行号归类与福利联动测试题：给出具体扣除项目（如托儿费 Line 21400、伤残支持 Line 21500、搬家费 Line 21900 与北方居民扣除 Line 25500），考查纳税人能否迅速识别行号分水岭（20700–23500 vs 24400–25600），并准确判定该扣除是否能降低 Line 23600 进而增加儿童福利（CCB）或 BC 家庭福利。Two-tier deduction line-number classification and benefit linkage questions: Given specific deduction items (such as child care expenses on line 21400, disability supports deduction on line 21500, moving expenses on line 21900, and northern residents deductions on line 25500), testing whether the candidate can promptly identify the line-number watershed (lines 20700–23500 vs lines 24400–25600) and accurately determine whether the deduction reduces net income on line 23600 to increase the Canada Child Benefit (CCB) or BC family benefit.
  • 托儿费三道天花板与申报资格计算题：给出纳税人实付托儿费、子女年龄（未满 7 岁 8,000 元 / 7-15 岁 5,000 元 / DTC 11,000 元）、夫妻双方就业与自雇收入，考查三道天花板取最低者的计算、挣得收入范围排除（不含投资收入与 EI）、强制低收入方申报规则及未扣额度当年作废不可结转。Child care expense three-ceiling and claimant eligibility calculation questions: Given actual child care expenses paid, children's ages ($8,000 for children under age 7 / $5,000 for children aged 7 to 15 / $11,000 for children eligible for the DTC), and both spouses' employment and self-employment earnings, testing the calculation of the lowest of the three ceilings, the exclusions from earned income (excluding investment income and Employment Insurance), the mandatory lower-income spouse claiming rule, and the restriction that undeducted balances expire in the current year and cannot be carried forward.
  • 搬家费资格测算与合格支出识别题：给出旧居、新居、新工作地三个地点距离，测试 40 公里量向的准确性；同时给出各类支出明细（搬运费、15 天内临时住宿、空置房持有成本封顶 5,000 元、看房差旅、卖房损失），考查合格费用的精准筛选以及仅限从新地点赚取的就业/自雇收入中扣除并仅可向后结转的规则。Moving expense eligibility measurement and qualifying expense identification questions: Given the distances between the old residence, new residence, and new work location, testing the accurate directional application of the 40-kilometre distance requirement; simultaneously providing expense breakdowns (moving haulage, temporary living expenses up to 15 days, maintaining the vacant former residence capped at $5,000, house-hunting trips, loss on sale), testing the precise screening of eligible expenses and the rule that deductions are strictly limited to employment or self-employment income earned at the new work location and can only be carried forward.
  • 赡养费双轨认定与双行申报题：根据书面协议或法院命令订立时间（1997 年 4 月分水岭），考查子女赡养费（不可扣/不计税）与配偶赡养费（可扣/计税）的税务定性，测试 Line 21999（全额）与 Line 22000（可扣额）的申报区分，以及子女赡养费必须优先清偿的法定扣除顺序。Support payment dual-track classification and two-line reporting questions: Based on the execution date of the written agreement or court order (the April 1997 watershed), testing the tax characterization of child support (non-deductible to payer / non-taxable to recipient) versus spousal support (deductible to payer / taxable to recipient), testing the reporting distinction between line 21999 (total support paid) and line 22000 (deductible support), and testing the statutory priority of payment rule requiring child support to be fully satisfied first.
  • 投资持有成本与伤残支持路径选择题：给出非注册账户管理费、借款利息、注册账户借款利息、保险箱租金等清单，考查 Line 22100 合格范围排除；或针对工作残障纳税人，考查 Line 21500 净收入层扣除与 Line 33099 医疗费抵免的区别与最优路径选择。Carrying charges and disability supports pathway selection questions: Given itemized expenses such as non-registered portfolio management fees, loan interest, borrowing interest for registered contributions, and safety deposit box rentals, testing statutory exclusions from line 22100; or for disabled working taxpayers, testing the distinction and optimal filing choice between the net income deduction on line 21500 and the medical expense tax credit on line 33099.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 扣除额一概降低净收入Assuming all deductions reduce net income

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常以为所有报税表上的扣除项都能降低净收入（Line 23600），忽略了第 4 步扣除（如 Line 25000 其他给付扣除、Line 25500 北方居民扣除）只减少应税收入，对净收入分毫不动，因而无法增加按净收入核定的各项政府福利。Candidates often assume that every deduction on the return reduces net income on line 23600, overlooking that Step 4 deductions (such as line 25000 other payments deduction and line 25500 northern residents deductions) only reduce taxable income while leaving net income untouched, thereby failing to increase income-tested government benefits.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干询问某项位于 Line 24400 至 Line 25600 的扣除对加拿大儿童福利（CCB）或 BC 家庭福利的影响。The question stem asks about the effect of a deduction located between lines 24400 and 25600 on the Canada Child Benefit (CCB) or BC family benefit.

⚠ 搬家距离只量两处住所Measuring moving distance only between the two residences

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 许多人凭直觉直接拿新居与旧居之间的直线或公路距离来判定是否满足 40 公里要求，忽视了税法法定参照点是「新工作地点」，必须比较新居到新工作地是否比旧居到新工作地近至少 40 公里。Candidates intuitively use the straight-line or road distance between the old home and new home to test the 40-kilometre requirement, failing to recognize that the statutory reference point is the new work location, requiring that the new residence be at least 40 kilometres closer to the new workplace than the old residence was.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干分别列出旧居、新居与新工作地点三个地址之间的距离，询问纳税人是否满足搬家费扣除的距离门槛。The question stem provides separate distances between the old residence, new residence, and new work location, asking whether the taxpayer satisfies the distance threshold for deducting moving expenses.

⚠ 高收入方扣托儿费更省税Assuming the higher-income spouse saves more by claiming child care

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 受家庭总税负最优化直觉驱使，认为托儿费填在高边际税率配偶名下能退更多税，忽视了税法强制规定必须由低收入方申报，除非满足 5 种法定例外。Driven by the intuitive desire to minimize total household tax, candidates believe claiming child care expenses under the spouse with the higher marginal rate yields a larger refund, overlooking that tax law mandatorily requires the lower-income spouse to claim the deduction unless one of five statutory exceptions is met.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出夫妻双方收入差距悬殊（如高收入方年薪 10 万、低收入方 3 万），且未提及任何在校就读、伤残或监禁等法定例外情形。The question stem presents a significant income disparity between spouses (such as $100,000 vs $30,000) without mentioning any statutory exception such as attending school, disability, or imprisonment.

⚠ 托儿费未扣余额跨年结转Carrying forward undeducted child care expense balances

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生受搬家费、学费或资本亏损可结转的惯性思维影响，误以为当年因三分之二挣得收入天花板未能扣除的托儿费差额可以在以后年度继续扣除。Influenced by carry-forward provisions for moving expenses, tuition, or capital losses, candidates mistakenly assume that any child care expense balance restricted by the two-thirds earned income ceiling can be carried forward to future tax years.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干中纳税人当年支付的托儿费超过了挣得收入的三分之二上限，询问未扣除部分的税务处理方式（是否结转）。The question stem describes child care expenses exceeding the two-thirds earned income ceiling, asking how the undeducted balance is treated for tax purposes.

⚠ 注册账户借款利息进Line 22100Claiming interest on loans for registered accounts on line 22100

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为只要是借钱做投资产生的利息就能抵税，混淆了非注册应税投资与注册延税/免税账户（如 RRSP、TFSA、FHSA），忽视了为注册计划借款的利息被税法明确排除在可扣范围之外。Candidates assume that any interest paid on money borrowed to invest is tax-deductible, conflating non-registered taxable investments with registered tax-deferred or tax-free accounts (such as RRSPs, TFSAs, or FHSAs), and failing to realize that interest on loans borrowed to fund registered plans is explicitly disallowed by tax law.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干列出纳税人为购买非注册股票和为向 RRSP 或 TFSA 供款而借入贷款所支付的利息，询问 Line 22100 可申报的投资持有成本金额。The question stem lists interest paid on loans borrowed both to buy non-registered shares and to contribute to an RRSP or TFSA, asking for the deductible carrying charges amount on line 22100.

第 1 题Q1两层扣除与福利联动效应Two tiers of deductions and benefit linkage effects★★陷阱：扣除额一概降低净收入

Liam relocated to Fort Nelson, British Columbia, and meets the qualifying residency requirements for the northern residents deduction. On his 2026 tax return, Liam claims a $4,000 northern residents deduction on line 25500. Liam and his spouse have two young children and receive the Canada Child Benefit (CCB). What is the direct effect of this $4,000 deduction on Liam's net income (line 23600) and his family's CCB entitlement?

莱姆（Liam）迁居至卑诗省纳尔逊堡（Fort Nelson），并符合北方居民扣除的法定居住要求。在其 2026 年报税表上，他在 Line 25500 申报了 $4,000 的北方居民扣除。莱姆与其配偶育有两名幼童且正在领取加拿大儿童福利（CCB）。这笔 $4,000 的扣除对莱姆的净收入（Line 23600）及家庭 CCB 待遇有何直接影响？

  1. ✗ 错误。此选项误将第 4 步的 Line 25500 扣除当成了第 3 步扣除。落入了「扣除额一概降低净收入」的典型陷阱。Incorrect. This option mistakes a Step 4 deduction on line 25500 for a Step 3 deduction, falling into the classic trap of assuming that all deductions reduce net income.

  2. ✗ 错误。北方居民扣除不存在联邦与省各分摊一半净收入扣减的规则。Incorrect. There is no rule in the northern residents deduction allocating half of the net income reduction to the federal government and half to the province.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据第一节制度设计原理，加税表将扣除明确划分为两层：Line 20700 至 Line 23500 属于第 3 步，既减税又压低净收入；而 Line 24400 至 Line 25600 属于第 4 步，仅减少应税收入（taxable income, line 26000），对净收入（net income, line 23600）分毫不动。由于加拿大儿童福利（Canada Child Benefit, CCB）完全依据父母双方的调整后家庭净收入核定，落在 Line 25500 的北方居民扣除（northern residents deductions）不会降低净收入，因此对家庭儿童福利待遇毫无增加效果。Correct. Under the institutional design principles in Section 1, the Canadian return strictly divides deductions into two tiers: lines 20700 through 23500 belong to Step 3, which reduce tax and lower net income; lines 24400 through 25600 belong to Step 4, which only reduce taxable income on line 26000 while leaving net income on line 23600 completely unchanged. Because the Canada Child Benefit (CCB) is calculated based on the parents' combined adjusted family net income, the northern residents deduction on line 25500 does not reduce net income and therefore provides zero increase to the family's CCB entitlement.

  4. ✗ 错误。北方居民扣除是《所得税法》规定的法定联邦应税收入扣除项目，直接在联邦 Line 25500 生效。Incorrect. The northern residents deduction is a statutory federal taxable income deduction under the Income Tax Act, taking effect directly on federal line 25500.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Chloe is completing her 2026 T1 personal return. She has $6,000 in allowable employment expenses on line 22900 and a $5,000 northern residents deduction on line 25500. Which of these deductions will reduce her net income on line 23600 for the purpose of calculating her eligibility for the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit?

克洛伊（Chloe）正在编制其 2026 年 T1 个人报税表。她在 Line 22900 申报了 $6,000 的合资格雇佣支出，并在 Line 25500 申报了 $5,000 的北方居民扣除。在核定她领取加拿大民生补贴（CGEB）的资格时，哪一项扣除会降低她在 Line 23600 的净收入？

  1. ✓ 正确。Line 22900（其他雇佣支出，other employment expenses）位于 Line 20700–23500 区间内，属于第 3 步净收入层扣除，能够有效降低 Line 23600 净收入，从而提升按净收入核定的杂货与基本生活物资福利；而 Line 25500（北方居民扣除）位于 Line 24400–25600 区间，属于第 4 步扣除，只扣减应税收入，对 Line 23600 净收入没有影响。Correct.   1. ✓ 正确。根据第二节赡养费规则，1997 年 4 月之后订立的协议中，子女赡养费不可扣除，配偶赡养费可全额扣除。当付款人未足额支付协议约定的款项时，税法规定已付款项优先冲抵子女赡养费。在实际支付的 $15,000 中，前 $12,000 优先清偿子女赡养费，剩余的 $3,000 才归属配偶赡养费。申报时必须走双行结构：Line 21999 申报实际支付的赡养费全额（$15,000），Line 22000 申报合资格可扣除金额（$3,000）。Correct. Under the support payment rules in Section 2, in agreements executed after April 1997, child support is non-deductible and spousal support is fully deductible. When the payer makes a partial payment that falls short of the total obligation, tax law mandatorily allocates payments to child support first. Of the $15,000 paid, the first $12,000 fully satisfies child support, leaving the remaining $3,000 allocated to spousal support. The payer must use the two-line reporting structure: report the total support paid ($15,000) on line 21999, and report the allowable deductible amount ($3,000) on line 22000.

  2. ✗ 错误。落入了「只填 Line 22000 漏填 Line 21999」的高频错报陷阱。CRA 必须靠 Line 21999 的全额与收款方的 Line 12799 进行交叉比对，漏填会导致核对失败并触发税局问询。Incorrect. This falls into the high-frequency reporting trap of entering line 22000 while leaving line 21999 blank. CRA relies on the total on line 21999 to cross-match with the recipient's line 12799; omitting line 21999 triggers matching failures and CRA inquiries.

  3. ✗ 错误。税法明确禁止按比例分摊欠付金额，必须强制优先足额偿付子女赡养费。Incorrect. Tax law explicitly prohibits prorating partial payments between child support and spousal support, requiring child support to be fully satisfied first.

  4. ✗ 错误。税法保护未成年子女权益，偿付顺序永远是子女赡养费优先，配偶赡养费在后。Incorrect. Tax law protects the welfare of minor children; the order of payment always prioritizes child support before spousal support.
