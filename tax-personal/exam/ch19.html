本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 居住联系三层判定阶梯与事实居民优先定性题：考查重大居住联系（住所、配偶、受养人）的排他性决定力，测试“先查事实联系定性、未定性才数 183 天逗留视同居民”的法定阶梯顺序，以及工签与留学生在境内租房生活被误判为视同居民的常见错误。The three-tier ladder of residential ties and the priority of factual residents: tests the decisive, exclusive power of significant residential ties (a home, spouse, dependants), the statutory ladder of "settle factual ties first, count the 183-day sojourn for a deemed resident only if that fails", and the common error of treating a work permit holder or student who rents and lives in Canada as a deemed resident.
  • 次要联系清单考量与考试口径“六项门槛”辨析题：考查旧版考试口径“凑满至少六项次要联系”与 CRA 现行官方口径“合起来看、权重不等”的口径差异，要求考生在答卷时准确识别考试口径判定表，同时掌握实务中强弱联系的权重分析。Weighing the secondary-ties list and the "six-item threshold" in exam wording: tests the difference between the old exam wording of "at least six secondary ties" and CRA's current official position of "looked at collectively, with unequal weights". Candidates must identify the exam-wording decision table when answering and also understand the weighting analysis of strong and weak ties in practice.
  • 183 天逗留规则要件与跨境通勤例外计算题：考查 ITA s.250(1)(a) 的三个并列要件（无重大联系、满 183 天、无协定判回），重点测试“一天的任何部分算一天”与“美加跨境每日通勤不构成逗留（零天）”的核心区分标准。Elements of the 183-day sojourn rule and the cross-border commuter exception: tests the three concurrent elements of ITA s.250(1)(a) (no significant ties, 183 days, not decided against by a treaty), in particular the core distinction between "any part of a day counts as a day" and "daily Canada-US cross-border commuting is not a sojourn (zero days)".
  • 视同非居民条约仲裁与全部条文适用法律后果题：考查 ITA s.250(5) 的反向阀门机制，测试当协定第四条仲裁判给缔约国另一方时，当事人立即视同非居民，且“for all purposes of the Act”全套触发离境税视同处置与 Part XIII 预扣税。Treaty tie-breaker deemed non-residence and the legal consequences of applying all provisions: tests the reverse-valve mechanism in ITA s.250(5). When the treaty Article IV tie-breaker awards residence to the other contracting state, the person is immediately deemed non-resident, and "for all purposes of the Act" the whole set of departure tax deemed disposition and Part XIII withholding is triggered.
  • 省税归属（12月31日规则）与 48% 联邦附加税税负对比题：考查 T1 表第 1 页四行居住信息的填报，以及视同居民适用 48% 联邦附加税顶替省税的比例公式，对比卑诗省事实居民（5.60% 最低税率加 $13,216 BPA 抵免）与视同居民（6.72% 附加税且零省抵免）的税负差距。Provincial attribution (the December 31 rule) and the 48% federal surtax compared: tests completing the four lines of residence information on page 1 of the T1, and the proportional formula in which the 48% federal surtax for a deemed resident replaces provincial tax. Compares the burden of a BC factual resident (5.60% lowest rate plus a $13,216 BPA credit) with a deemed resident (6.72% surtax and zero provincial credits).

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 颠倒判定顺序先数天数Reversing the order and counting days first

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 死记“住满 183 天即为居民”，第一反应是打开日历数天数，忽视了“先看事实联系，不满足才看视同居民”的法定阶梯，把有租房和医保的事实居民误判为视同居民。Memorizing "staying 183 days makes you a resident" and reacting by opening the calendar to count days, ignoring the statutory ladder of "look at factual ties first, and consider deemed residence only if they are not met", and so misjudging a factual resident with a rented home and health coverage as a deemed resident.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现留学生或工签持有人“在加拿大租房居住超过 183 天”，选项诱导选择适用 s.250(1) 视同居民并加征 48% 联邦附加税。The question has a student or work permit holder who "rents and lives in Canada for more than 183 days", and the options lure you to apply s.250(1) deemed residence and add the 48% federal surtax.

⚠ 混淆移民身份与税务居民身份Confusing immigration status with tax residency

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 受日常概念影响，误以为持有枫叶卡或公民身份才算税务居民，或认为持有工签、学签等临时签证就只能算非居民，混淆了法律权利与税务事实。Influenced by everyday concepts, assuming that only holders of a maple leaf card or citizenship can be tax residents, or that holders of temporary visas such as a work or study permit can only be non-residents, confusing a legal right with a tax fact.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调当事人“仅持有临时工作许可尚未取得永居”或“持有枫叶卡但已离开加拿大安家”，询问纳税义务范围。The question stresses that the person "holds only a temporary work permit and has not yet obtained permanent residence" or "holds a maple leaf card but has left Canada and settled elsewhere", and asks about the scope of tax obligations.

⚠ 机械套用六项次要联系数量门槛Mechanically applying the six secondary ties count threshold

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误把旧版培训判定表的“凑满至少六项”当成现行税法硬性规定，未区分答卷考试口径与 CRA 现行“合起来看、权重不等”的实务口径。Mistaking the old training decision table's "at least six" for a hard rule of current tax law, and failing to distinguish the exam wording for answering from CRA's current practice of "looked at collectively, with unequal weights".

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干罗列了多项弱联系（如名片、邮箱、电话登记）或五项核心强联系（如工作、医保、驾照），考查次要联系判定标准或口径差异。The question lists several weak ties (such as a business card, an email address, a phone registration) or five core strong ties (such as work, health coverage, a driver's licence), and tests the standard for judging secondary ties or the difference in wording.

⚠ 误把视同居民当成省钱通道Mistaking deemed residence for a way to save tax

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 望文生义以为视同居民“不用向任何省交税”即可少缴税款，忽视了顶替省税的是 48% 联邦附加税，且完全丧失省级基本个人额与抵免福利。Taking the words at face value and assuming that a deemed resident "pays no tax to any province" and so pays less, overlooking that what replaces provincial tax is the 48% federal surtax, and that the person entirely loses the provincial basic personal amount and credits.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干询问视同居民与卑诗省事实居民在省税与附加税上的税负差异，或计算边际税率与抵免额度。The question asks about the difference in the burden of provincial tax and surtax between a deemed resident and a BC factual resident, or asks to compute the marginal rate and credit amount.

⚠ 把 Part XIII 预扣税当作普通可退税款Treating Part XIII withholding tax as an ordinary refundable amount

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 将非居民被动收入 25% 的预扣税混同于居民的 T4 工资预扣税，误以为次年通过普通 T1 报税即可申请退回多扣税款。Confusing the 25% withholding tax on a non-resident's passive income with the withholding on a resident's T4 wages, and assuming that excess tax withheld can be refunded by filing an ordinary T1 next year.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现非居民收到加拿大股息或退休金被代扣 25%，询问能否通过常规 T1 申报全部退回。The question has a non-resident who receives a Canadian dividend or pension with 25% withheld, and asks whether it can all be refunded through an ordinary T1 filing.

第 1 题Q1事实居民与视同居民判定顺序Order of determining factual and deemed residents★★陷阱：颠倒判定顺序先数天数

Jin, an international graduate student from South Korea, moved to Vancouver in January 2026 to begin a full-time university program. Jin signed a one-year apartment lease, obtained BC Medical Services Plan (MSP) coverage, opened local bank accounts, and was physically present in BC for 330 days in 2026. A tax software prompts Jin to declare as a 'deemed resident under the 183-day rule' and pay the 48% federal surtax. How should Jin's residency status and provincial tax obligations be correctly reported under the Income Tax Act?

来自韩国的国际留学生 Jin 于 2026 年 1 月迁至温哥华攻读全日制大学课程。Jin 签署了一年期公寓租约，办理了 BC 省医疗保险 (MSP)，开设了本地银行账户，并在 2026 年内在 BC 省实际居留了 330 天。某报税软件提示 Jin 申报为“183 天规则下的视同居民”并缴纳 48% 联邦附加税。根据《所得税法》，Jin 的税务居民身份及省税纳税义务应如何正确申报？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是典型的“颠倒判定顺序先数天数”陷阱。根据 CRA 解释件，只有确认纳税人不是事实居民的前提下，s.250(1)(a) 的视同居民条款才适用。Incorrect. This is the classic "reverse the order and count days first" trap. Under CRA's folio, the deemed resident provision of s.250(1)(a) applies only once it is confirmed that the taxpayer is not a factual resident.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据最高法院判例及 CRA Folio S5-F1-C1，判定必须严格遵循阶梯顺序：第一步考察居住联系。Jin 租住公寓（重大居住联系）并办了 BC 医保和银行账户（次要联系），属于事实居民（factual resident）。事实居民按 12 月 31 日居住省（BC 省）缴纳省税并享受省级抵免，绝不适用视同居民，也不缴纳 48% 联邦附加税。Correct. Under Supreme Court case law and CRA Folio S5-F1-C1, the test must strictly follow the ladder: step one is to examine residential ties. Jin rents an apartment (a significant residential tie) and obtained BC health coverage and bank accounts (secondary ties), so Jin is a factual resident. A factual resident pays provincial tax to the province of residence on December 31 (BC) and has provincial credits, the deemed resident rule never applies, and the 48% federal surtax is not paid.

  3. ✗ 错误。混淆移民签证身份与税务居民身份。学签持有人只要建立居住联系，第一天起即可成为事实税务居民。Incorrect. This confuses immigration visa status with tax residency. A study permit holder who establishes residential ties can be a factual tax resident from the first day.

  4. ✗ 错误。视同居民在法律上不属于任何省份，不能申报省抵免，且附加税是应纳税额而非可退税额。Incorrect. A deemed resident belongs to no province by law and cannot claim provincial credits, and the surtax is an amount payable, not a refundable amount.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Carlos, a renewable energy researcher from Spain, arrived in Victoria, BC on February 1, 2026 on a temporary work permit. He rented a residential townhouse for his exclusive use, registered for BC healthcare, and lived there for 315 days during 2026. A tax preparer calculated Carlos's taxes using the federal package for deemed residents, claiming that 'anyone staying over 183 days is deemed a resident and pays the 48% surtax.' What is the correct classification for Carlos?

来自西班牙的可再生能源研究员 Carlos 于 2026 年 2 月 1 日持临时工作许可抵达 BC 省维多利亚。他租了一套供自己专属使用的联排住宅，办理了 BC 省医保，并在 2026 年内居住了 315 天。一名报税员使用视同居民专用税包为 Carlos 算税，声称“任何在加逗留超过 183 天的人均视同居民并交 48% 附加税”。Carlos 的正确定性是什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。成文法 183 天视同居民规则不能凌驾于事实居住联系之上，必须先看事实联系。Incorrect. The statutory 183-day deemed resident rule cannot override factual residential ties, and the factual ties must be looked at first.

  2. ✗ 错误。税务居民身份是事实判断，与是否取得永久居民身份无关。Incorrect. Tax residency is a question of fact and has nothing to do with whether permanent residence has been obtained.

  3. ✗ 错误。视同居民需就全年全球收入纳税，且 Carlos 具备事实联系，定性本身错误。Incorrect. A deemed resident is taxed on worldwide income for the whole year, and Carlos has factual ties, so the characterization itself is wrong.

  4. ✓ 正确。根据 CRA 判定阶梯，Carlos 租住联排住宅并办理省医保，建立了不可动摇的重大与次要居住联系，属于事实居民。他应按 12 月 31 日居住省使用 BC 省税包申报，缴纳 BC 省税并享受省级抵免，绝不能被当作视同居民缴纳 48% 联邦附加税。Correct. Under CRA's ladder of tests, Carlos rents a townhouse and has provincial health coverage, which establishes firm significant and secondary residential ties, so he is a factual resident. He should file using the BC provincial forms for the province of residence on December 31, pay BC tax with provincial credits, and can never be treated as a deemed resident paying the 48% federal surtax.

第 2 题Q2次要联系门槛考试与实务口径差异Secondary ties threshold: exam wording versus practice wording★★陷阱：机械套用六项次要联系数量门槛

For an individual with no significant residential ties in Canada (no home, spouse or dependants), how many secondary ties did the 2024 training-material checklist require to establish residency, and how does that contrast with current CRA practice in Folio S5-F1-C1?

  4. ✓ 正确。根据讲义第四部分，把“视同居民”当省钱通道完全算反了方向。在 2026 税年：视同居民在最低税阶的附加税边际税负为 14% × 48% = 6.72%，本身就高于卑诗省 5.60% 的最低税阶省税率；更重要的是，事实居民可享受卑诗省基本个人额 $13,216 抵税额及省级可退还抵免，而视同居民省抵免全无。因此视同居民在卑诗省几乎总是税负更贵的那一档。Correct. Under part four of the lecture, treating "deemed resident" as a way to save tax gets the direction completely backwards. In the 2026 tax year, the marginal burden of the surtax for a deemed resident in the lowest bracket is 14% x 48% = 6.72%, already above BC's lowest provincial rate of 5.60%. More important, a factual resident enjoys the BC basic personal amount credit of $13,216 and provincial refundable credits, while a deemed resident has none of the provincial credits. A deemed resident in BC therefore almost always falls in the more expensive tier.

An unadvised taxpayer in Vancouver assumes that filing as a deemed resident under the 183-day rule is preferable to filing as a BC factual resident because 'deemed residents avoid provincial tax entirely.' Based on the lecture's analysis for a taxpayer with modest taxable income in the lowest bracket, what is the comparative tax burden in 2026?

温哥华一位未咨询专业人士的纳税人认为，按 183 天规则申报为视同居民优于申报为 BC 省事实居民，因为“视同居民完全避免了省税”。根据讲义对最低税阶低收入纳税人的分析，2026 年两者的相对税负表现如何？

  2. ✓ 正确。讲义第四部分明确指出：视同居民并非省钱路径。最低税阶联邦税率 14%，附加税边际效果为 14% × 48% = 6.72%，已超过卑诗省最低税阶 5.60%；加上视同居民无法使用卑诗省 $13,216 的基本个人额抵税，亦无法获得任何卑诗省可退还抵免，其实际税负几乎必然高于事实居民。Correct. Part four of the lecture states clearly that deemed residence is not a way to save tax. The lowest federal bracket rate is 14%, so the marginal effect of the surtax is 14% x 48% = 6.72%, which exceeds BC's lowest bracket of 5.60%. In addition, a deemed resident cannot use the BC basic personal amount credit of $13,216 or any BC refundable credits, so the actual burden is almost certainly higher than for a factual resident.

  3. ✗ 错误。事实居民按居住省交省税，绝不交 48% 联邦附加税。Incorrect. A factual resident pays provincial tax to the province of residence and never pays the 48% federal surtax.

  4. ✗ 错误。省税与视同居民附加税是完全平行的计算，税负差异显著，CRA 不做拉平。Incorrect. Provincial tax and the deemed resident surtax are completely parallel calculations with markedly different burdens, and CRA does not level them out.

Daniel, who originally moved to Canada from New Zealand in 2018, decided to permanently return to New Zealand to re-establish his home. Daniel departed Canada on May 1, 2026 and began employment in Auckland. His spouse remained in Vancouver until August 31, 2026 to oversee the sale of their Canadian home and allow their child to finish the school term. Under CRA Folio S5-F1-C1 and the departure date exception explained in the lecture, what is Daniel's official departure date for Canadian tax purposes?

Daniel 最初于 2018 年从新西兰移居加拿大，后决定永久返回新西兰重建原居生活。Daniel 于 2026 年 5 月 1 日离开加拿大，并在奥克兰开始工作。其配偶在温哥华停留至 2026 年 8 月 31 日，以便处理出售加拿大房产事宜并让孩子完成本学年学业。根据 CRA Folio S5-F1-C1 及讲义中解释的离境日期例外规则，Daniel 在加拿大税务上的法定离境日期是哪一天？

This is common misreporting trap number 7 listed in part seven of the lecture: mechanically applying the general rule to the special case of "returning to re-establish a home in the country of origin" and wrongly postponing the departure date to the day family members leave.

  2. ✗ 错误。部分年度居民离境日在年内离境当天定格，绝非固定在年末。Incorrect. For a part-year resident the departure date is fixed on the day of leaving in the year, and is by no means fixed at year end.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据 CRA Folio S5-F1-C1 ¶1.24 及讲义第 7 部分，常规离境日判定虽取“三日期取最晚”（本人离境、家属离境、取得新国税籍），但存在一条关键例外：纳税人离开加拿大返回原居国重建原居（re-establishing residence in country of origin）的，以本人离境当日为准（即 2026 年 5 月 1 日）。配偶与孩子留在加拿大处理售房或完成学业，不会推迟当事人的法定离境日。若误按 8 月 31 日计算，会连带把视同处置日和全球收入截止日一起算错。Correct. Under CRA Folio S5-F1-C1 para 1.24 and part seven of the lecture, although the ordinary departure date takes "the latest of three dates" (the person leaves, family leaves, new country tax residency is acquired), there is a key exception: a taxpayer who leaves Canada to return to the country of origin and re-establish residence there uses the day the person leaves (May 1, 2026). A spouse and children staying in Canada to sell the home or finish school do not postpone the taxpayer's statutory departure date. If it were wrongly computed as August 31, the deemed disposition date and the cut-off for worldwide income would also be wrong.
