① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 居民身份三步判定与事实联系定性题：考查重大居住联系（住所、配偶、受养人）与次要联系的权重，严格测试“先判事实居民、不成立才看 183 天逗留视同居民”的法定顺序，以及两类居民在纳税范围（居民期 vs 全年全球收入）与 48% 联邦附加税上的重大差异。The three-step residency determination and characterizing factual ties: tests the weight of significant residential ties (a home, spouse, dependants) against secondary ties, and strictly tests the statutory order of "decide factual residence first, and look at the 183-day sojourn rule for a deemed resident only if that fails". Also tests the major differences between the two kinds of resident in the scope of tax (resident period versus worldwide income for the whole year) and the 48% federal surtax.
  • 首年非退还抵免分层与按天折算计算题：考查 ITA s.118.91 下第一层实际发生额（CPP/EI/学费）、第二层额度型抵免（基本个人额按居民天数 ÷ 365 折算）及第三层非居民期 90% 规则的适用边界，结合 2026 税年 $16,452 额度及 14% 抵免率进行精准算术测试。First-year non-refundable credit tiers and daily proration: tests the first tier of actual amounts (CPP/EI/tuition) under ITA s.118.91, the second tier of amount-based credits (the basic personal amount prorated by resident days / 365) and the limits of the third tier, the 90% rule for the non-resident period, using the 2026 tax year amount of $16,452 and the 14% credit rate for precise arithmetic.
  • 视同取得与 T1135 境外资产申报门槛题：考查 ITA s.128.1(1)(c) 成本垫高规则对日后资本利得和 T1135 申报门槛（成本额超 $100,000 加元）的传导影响，首年免报（s.233.7）仅限首次成为居民的特殊资格，以及漏交表每天 $25、封顶 $2,500 的信息申报罚款。Deemed acquisition and the T1135 foreign property threshold: tests how the cost step-up rule in ITA s.128.1(1)(c) carries through to later capital gains and to the T1135 filing threshold (cost amount over $100,000), the fact that the first-year filing exemption (s.233.7) is available only to a person who first becomes resident, and the information return penalty of $25 per day, capped at $2,500, for a missed filing.
  • 新移民首年福利申请与 SOI 流程实务题：考查 CGEB（原 GST/HST 补贴）须主动提交 RC151 表且不自动追补、工签学签满 18 个月且第 19 个月持有效许可方可领 CCB、以及登陆前两年全球收入（SOI）仅算福利门槛不纳税的实务规则。First-year benefit applications and the SOI process in practice: tests that the CGEB (formerly the GST/HST credit) requires an RC151 filed by the newcomer and is not paid retroactively on its own, that a work or study permit holder can receive the CCB only after 18 months and with a valid permit in the 19th month, and that the statement of world income (SOI) for the two years before landing counts only toward benefit thresholds and is not taxed.
  • 离境居民镜像规则与申报清单题：考查离境日期判定（最晚日期原则）、离境税视同处置、T1161 财产清单申报门槛（公允市值超 $25,000 加元）、以及非居民代扣（s.212 预扣 25% 与 s.116 卖房预扣 25% 毛售价）的法律责任划分。Mirror rules on emigrating and the filing checklist: tests determining the departure date (the latest-date principle), the deemed disposition on leaving, the T1161 list of properties threshold (fair market value over $25,000), and the division of legal liability for non-resident withholding (25% withholding under s.212 and 25% of gross proceeds withheld on a sale of a home under s.116).

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 混淆移民身份与税务居民身份Confusing immigration status with tax residency

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 受日常用语影响，误以为拥有枫叶卡或工签直接决定税务身份，忽略了加拿大按日常生活重心与居住联系征税，移民身份仅决定居留权而非税务义务。Influenced by everyday language, assuming that having a maple leaf card (PR card) or a work permit directly determines tax status. This overlooks that Canada taxes on where the center of daily life is and on residential ties, and that immigration status determines only the right to stay, not tax obligations.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现“持有枫叶卡但短登即离开”、“持工签或学签尚未取得永居身份”或考查开始申报全球收入的法定起算点。The question has "holds a PR card but leaves right after a brief landing", "holds a work or study permit but is not yet a permanent resident", or tests the statutory starting point for reporting worldwide income.

⚠ 视同居民与事实居民顺序倒置Reversing the order of deemed resident and factual resident

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 死记“住满 183 天即为居民”，未遵循先判事实联系、不满足才看视同逗留的法定顺序，误将事实居民判为视同居民而漏享省税抵免并多算 48% 附加税。Memorizing "staying 183 days makes you a resident" without following the statutory order of looking at factual ties first and the deemed sojourn rule only if they are not met, and so wrongly treating a factual resident as a deemed resident, missing provincial credits and over-counting the 48% surtax.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现“在加拿大租房或买房安家且居住超过 183 天”、“询问是否适用 s.250(1) 视同居民条款或加征 48% 联邦附加税”。The question has "rented or bought a home in Canada and settled for more than 183 days", or asks whether the s.250(1) deemed resident provision or the 48% federal surtax applies.

⚠ 首年基本个人额按全额申报Claiming the full basic personal amount in the first year

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 习惯了全年居民申报模式，未注意到首年作为部分年度居民，额度型非退还抵免依法必须按居民天数占全年比例（天数 ÷ 365）打折折算。Being used to full-year resident filing and overlooking that in the first year, as a part-year resident, amount-based non-refundable credits must by law be prorated by resident days as a share of the year (days / 365).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现“年中某月某日登陆成为居民”、“计算 T1 line 30000 可申报的基本个人额”或“计算该抵免实际可抵减的税额”。The question has "landed on a certain date mid-year and became a resident", "compute the basic personal amount claimable on T1 line 30000", or "compute the tax the credit actually reduces".

⚠ 用原始购入价判定 T1135 门槛与利得Using the original purchase price for the T1135 threshold and the gain

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为境外资产的历史购入成本一直沿用，忽略了 ITA s.128.1 视同取得规则已将资产成本基础垫高至登陆日市值，导致第二年漏报 T1135 或日后多报增值。Assuming the historical purchase cost of a foreign asset continues to apply and overlooking that the deemed acquisition rule in ITA s.128.1 has stepped the cost base up to the market value on the landing date, leading to a missed T1135 in the second year or to overreporting the gain later.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出“境外资产原始买入成本”与“登陆日公允市值”，并询问“第二年是否需要提交 T1135”或“出售时计算资本利得的 ACB”。The question gives both the original purchase cost of foreign assets and their fair market value on the landing date, and asks "whether a T1135 must be filed in the second year" or "the ACB for computing the capital gain on a sale".

⚠ 误以为报税会自动追补 CGEB 且拒报 SOIAssuming filing a return automatically catches up the CGEB and refusing to file the SOI

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为新居民消费税补贴（CGEB）与儿童福利无需单独申请会在报税后自动发放，且误将登陆前两年收入（SOI）当作征税对象而拒绝申报导致福利被锁。Assuming the CGEB (the former GST/HST credit) and child benefit need no separate application and will be paid automatically after filing, and mistaking the income for the two years before landing (SOI) for something that will be taxed, and refusing to file it so that the benefits are blocked.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现“新移民未填 RC151 希望报税补发补贴”、“询问登陆前两年全球收入是否会在加拿大课税”或福利审批受阻的情形。The question has "a newcomer did not file an RC151 and hopes filing a return will bring back payments", "asks whether income for the two years before landing will be taxed in Canada", or a case where the benefit approval is stuck.

第 1 题Q1事实居民与视同居民判定顺序Order of determining factual and deemed resident★★陷阱：视同居民与事实居民顺序倒置

Chloe, an international IT specialist from France, arrived in Vancouver on March 1, 2026 on a two-year work permit. She immediately signed a 12-month residential apartment lease, enrolled in the BC Medical Services Plan (MSP), and opened Canadian bank accounts. During the 2026 calendar year, Chloe stayed physically present in Canada for 306 days. How should Chloe's residency status and Canadian tax liability be determined for the 2026 tax year?

来自法国的国际 IT 专家 Chloe 于 2026 年 3 月 1 日持两年工签抵达温哥华。她随即签署了为期 12 个月的住宅公寓租约，办理了 BC 省医疗保险 (MSP)，并开设了加拿大银行账户。在 2026 日历年中，Chloe 在加拿大境内实际居留了 306 天。对于 2026 税年，Chloe 的税务居民身份及加拿大纳税义务应如何认定？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是典型的“倒置判定顺序”陷阱。根据 CRA Income Tax Folio S5-F1-C1 ¶1.30，ITA s.250(1)(a) 的 183 天逗留视同居民规则仅在确认个人不是事实居民（factual resident）的前提下才适用。Chloe 拥有住所和省医保，已构成事实居民，绝不能适用视同居民。Incorrect. This is the classic "reversed order of determination" trap. Under CRA Income Tax Folio S5-F1-C1 para 1.30, the 183-day sojourn deemed resident rule of ITA s.250(1)(a) applies only after it is established that the individual is not a factual resident. Chloe has a home and provincial health coverage, so she is already a factual resident and the deemed resident rule can never be applied.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据最高法院 Thomson 案判例及 ITA s.250(3)，判定居民身份必须先考察事实居住联系（residential ties）。Chloe 租住公寓（重大联系）、办理 BC 省医保及开设银行账户（次要联系），自 2026 年 3 月 1 日入境起即建立起通常居住关系，属于部分年度事实居民 (factual resident/part-year resident)。她只需就 3 月 1 日至年末期间的全球收入向加拿大纳税，按 12 月 31 日居住省缴纳 BC 省税，并享有省级抵免。Correct. Under the Supreme Court's Thomson decision and ITA s.250(3), residency must first be tested on factual residential ties. Chloe rents an apartment (a significant tie), obtained BC provincial health coverage and opened a bank account (secondary ties), and from entering on March 1, 2026 she established an ordinary residence, so she is a part-year factual resident. She pays Canadian tax only on worldwide income for the period from March 1 to year end, pays BC tax as the province of residence on December 31, and has provincial credits.

  3. ✗ 错误。这是“混淆移民身份与税务居民身份”陷阱。加拿大税法实行事实居住原则而非国籍或移民签证原则（ITA s.2(1)），工签持有人只要建立居住联系，第一天起即可成为事实税务居民。Incorrect. This is the "confusing immigration status with tax residency" trap. Canadian tax law follows the principle of factual residence and not nationality or immigration visas (ITA s.2(1)). A work permit holder who establishes residential ties can be a factual tax resident from the first day.

  4. ✗ 错误。视同居民不仅适用前提错误，而且法律后果也完全相反：视同居民按全年的全球收入纳税，且因不属于任何省份而无法享受省税抵免，须加征 48% 联邦附加税。Incorrect. The deemed resident rule not only has the wrong precondition, its legal consequences are the opposite. A deemed resident is taxed on worldwide income for the whole year, belongs to no province and so cannot get provincial credits, and must pay the 48% federal surtax.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

David, an engineering consultant from Australia, moved to Calgary on April 15, 2026 under a temporary employment contract. He rented a townhouse, moved his personal furniture, registered with provincial healthcare, and lived there with his spouse for 261 days in 2026. A tax software automatically flagged him as a 'deemed resident under the 183-day rule.' How should David's tax return be correctly filed under the Income Tax Act?

来自澳大利亚的工程顾问 David 于 2026 年 4 月 15 日持临时雇佣合同搬至卡尔加里。他在当地租了一套联排别墅，运来了个人家具，办理了省医疗保险，并与配偶在加拿大共同居住了 261 天。某报税软件自动将他标记为“依据 183 天规则的视同居民”。根据《所得税法》，David 的税表应如何正确填报？

  1. ✗ 错误。死记 183 天规则而忽略了法定判定阶梯。ITA s.250(1)(a) 是兜底条款，仅适用于不具备事实居住联系的个人。Incorrect. This memorizes the 183-day rule and ignores the statutory test sequence. ITA s.250(1)(a) is a backstop clause that applies only to individuals without factual residential ties.

  2. ✗ 错误。受雇收入属于 Part I 积极经营与受雇收入，非居民亦不适用 Part XIII 预扣；更重要的是 David 拥有重大居住联系，是事实居民而非非居民。Incorrect. Employment income is Part I employment income, to which Part XIII withholding does not apply even for a non-resident.   4. ✗ 错误。90% 规则的分母明确限定为“非居民期那一段时间内的全球净收入”（net worldwide income for that non-resident period），绝非全年收入。Incorrect. The denominator of the 90% rule is expressly limited to "net worldwide income for that non-resident period" and by no means the income for the whole year.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Brenda landed in Canada as a permanent resident on August 1, 2026. During the non-resident period (January 1 to July 31, 2026), Brenda earned $8,000 from Canadian freelance editing and $72,000 from local employment in her former home country. Can Brenda claim non-refundable personal tax credits for that pre-landing period under ITA section 118.94?

Brenda 于 2026 年 8 月 1 日作为永久居民登陆加拿大。在非居民期间（2026 年 1 月 1 日至 7 月 31 日），Brenda 从加拿大自由职业文字编辑中赚取了 $8,000 加元，在原国当地受雇赚取了等值 $72,000 加元。Brenda 能否根据《所得税法》第 118.94 条申报该登陆前期间的非退还个人税收抵免？

  1. ✗ 错误。税法不存在任何“加拿大收入满 $5,000 即可全额抵免”的绝对门槛，判定标准是相对比例（90%）。Incorrect. The law has no absolute threshold under which Canadian income of $5,000 allows full credits. The test is a relative proportion (90%).

  2. ✗ 错误。移民身份与抵免资格脱钩，部分年度居民默认仅享受居民期折算抵免，非居民期抵免严格受 s.118.94 的比例约束。Incorrect. Immigration status is decoupled from credit eligibility. A part-year resident by default gets only the prorated credits for the resident period, and credits for the non-resident period are strictly limited by the proportion in s.118.94.

  3. ✓ 正确。在非居民期间，Brenda 的加拿大来源收入为 $8,000，全球净收入为 $8,000 + $72,000 = $80,000，加拿大来源收入占比仅为 10%（远低于 CRA 依据 ITA s.118.94 设定的 90%“全部或几乎全部”门槛）。因此，她无法就非居民期申报额度型个人抵免，只能就 8 月 1 日至 12 月 31 日的居民期按天数比例申报基本个人额。Correct. During the non-resident period, Brenda's Canadian-source income was $8,000 and her net world income was $8,000 + $72,000 = $80,000, so Canadian-source income is only 10% (far below the "all or substantially all" threshold of 90% that CRA sets under ITA s.118.94). She therefore cannot claim amount-based personal credits for the non-resident period, and can only claim the basic personal amount prorated by days for the resident period from August 1 to December 31.

  4. ✗ 错误。个人本身不具备该期间抵免资格，根本不存在可以转移给配偶的有效额度。Incorrect. The individual has no credit eligibility for that period, so there is no valid amount that can be transferred to a spouse.

第 6 题Q6临时居民申领CCB十八月规则CCB 18-month rule for temporary residents★★陷阱：混淆移民身份与税务居民身份

In April 2026, two families arrived in British Columbia: the Miller family (permanent residents) and the Gupta family (holding temporary closed work permits). Both families have 4-year-old children, established factual residency on arrival, and filed all required applications. Under CRA rules governing the Canada Child Benefit (CCB), when are these two families eligible to begin receiving monthly CCB payments?

2026 年 4 月，两个家庭抵达 BC 省：Miller 一家（永久居民身份）与 Gupta 一家（持有临时封闭式工签）。两个家庭均育有一名 4 岁儿童，抵达时均建立了事实居住联系，并提交了所有必要申请表。根据 CRA 关于加拿大儿童福利 (CCB) 的规定，这两个家庭分别于何时有资格开始领取每月 CCB 款项？

  1. ✗ 错误。永久居民与受保护人从建立事实居住联系起即可享受福利，完全没有 18 个月的等待期限制。Incorrect. Permanent residents and protected persons can receive benefits from the time they establish factual residential ties, with no 18-month waiting period at all.

  2. ✗ 错误。持有临时工作许可或学习许可的临时居民受《所得税法》严格限制，不能落地即领，必须满足 18 个月的居住期门槛。Incorrect. Temporary residents holding a temporary work permit or study permit are strictly restricted by the Income Tax Act and cannot be paid on landing. They must meet the 18-month residence threshold.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据 CRA 官方指引，永久居民（PR）与受保护人只要成为税务居民并符合监护条件，抵加后即可立即申领 CCB，无任何等待期。而持学签、工签等临时居留许可的个人，必须在加拿大连续居住满 18 个月，且在第 19 个月必须持有有效的居留许可，方可开始领取 CCB 付款。Correct. Under CRA's official guidance, a permanent resident (PR) or protected person can apply for the CCB immediately after arriving in Canada as long as they are a tax resident and meet the custody conditions, with no waiting period. A person holding a temporary residence permit such as a study or work permit must live in Canada continuously for 18 months and must hold a valid permit in the 19th month before CCB payments can start.

  4. ✗ 错误。CCB 资格取决于居住身份和事实监护，绝不需要等待连续三年报税后才发放。Incorrect. CCB eligibility depends on residency status and actual custody, and there is never a need to wait until three consecutive years of returns have been filed before payments are made.

Raj, a foreign software engineer on a work permit, moved to Canada with his young daughter in January 2025 and received CCB payments starting in his 19th month (July 2026). His initial work permit was set to expire on November 30, 2026. Raj properly submitted an extension application on November 10, 2026, and is waiting for Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) to process it. What happens to his family's monthly CCB payments in December 2026?

Raj 是一名持工签的外籍软件工程师，于 2025 年 1 月携幼女移居加拿大，并在居住满 19 个月（2026 年 7 月）起开始领取 CCB。他的初始工签原定于 2026 年 11 月 30 日到期。Raj 于 2026 年 11 月 10 日合法递交了工签续签申请，目前正在等待移民局 (IRCC) 审理。在 2026 年 12 月，他家庭的每月 CCB 款项将如何处理？

  1. ✗ 错误。在原许可到期前递交续签享有“维持身份”，且此前合法领取的福利完全合法，绝无退还之说。Incorrect. Filing an extension before the original permit expires gives "maintained status", and benefits received earlier were lawfully received and are never refunded.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据讲义与 CRA 实务指引，临时居民只要在旧居留许可到期前递交了续签申请，在审理期间即享有法定“维持身份”（maintained status），其居留许可被视为依然有效，因此每月 CCB 款项不会被中断，可继续按期发放。Correct. Under the lecture and CRA practice guidance, a temporary resident who files an extension application before the old permit expires enjoys statutory "maintained status" while it is processed. The permit is treated as still valid, so the monthly CCB is not interrupted and continues to be paid on schedule.

  3. ✗ 错误。维持身份期间无需暂停支付，更不需要等到永居审批通过。Incorrect. During maintained status there is no need to suspend payments, much less to wait until a permanent residence approval.

  4. ✗ 错误。不存在向 CRA 缴纳所谓维持身份保证金的要求。Incorrect. There is no requirement to pay CRA a status-maintenance guarantee deposit.

第 7 题Q7离境税务规则与T1161门槛Departure tax rules and the T1161 threshold★★★陷阱：离境日期判断错误与忽视离境财产清单

Ethan and his family emigrated from British Columbia to Singapore in 2027. Ethan physically departed Canada on August 10, 2027; his spouse and children joined him on September 1, 2027; and Ethan officially established tax residency in Singapore on September 15, 2027. On that date, Ethan held Canadian bank cash of $40,000 CAD, an RRSP worth $60,000 CAD, and non-registered Canadian stock shares with a fair market value (FMV) of $55,000 CAD (ACB $30,000 CAD). Under the Income Tax Act rules stated in the lecture, what is Ethan's official departure date and his reporting obligation under Form T1161?

Ethan 一家于 2027 年从 BC 省移民至新加坡。Ethan 本人于 2027 年 8 月 10 日离开加拿大；其配偶与子女于 2027 年 9 月 1 日前往汇合；Ethan 于 2027 年 9 月 15 日正式取得新加坡税务居民身份。在离境日，Ethan 名下持有加元银行现金存款 $40,000 加元、价值 $60,000 加元的 RRSP，以及公允市值 (FMV) 为 $55,000 加元（ACB 为 $30,000 加元）的加拿大非注册股票。根据讲义中明确的《所得税法》规则，Ethan 的法定离境日期是哪一天，且他对 Form T1161 的申报义务是什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。离境日期不能简单取本人离开之日，必须取三者中最晚的日期（2027 年 9 月 15 日）；此外，T1161 的 $25,000 门槛考查的是财产公允市值总额而非资本利得。Incorrect. The departure date cannot simply be the day the person leaves. It must be the latest of the three dates (September 15, 2027). Also, the $25,000 threshold for the T1161 tests the total fair market value of property, not capital gains.

  2. ✗ 错误。离境日应为取得新国税籍的 9 月 15 日；且填报 T1161 判定 $25,000 门槛时，加元现金存款和注册退休储蓄计划 (RRSP) 均属于法定先予排除的资产。Incorrect. The departure date should be September 15, when new country tax residency was acquired. And when testing the $25,000 threshold for the T1161, Canadian-dollar cash deposits and RRSPs are assets that are by law excluded first.

  3. ✗ 错误。日期判定错误，且只要符合条件的财产总市值超过 $25,000，T1161 就是法定强制申报表。Incorrect. The date is wrong, and as long as the total market value of qualifying property exceeds $25,000, the T1161 is a statutory mandatory form.

  4. ✓ 正确。根据 CRA 离境税务规则，官方离境日取以下三个日期中最晚者：本人离开日（8月10日）、配偶及受养人离开日（9月1日）、取得新国税务居民日（9月15日），因此离境日为 2027 年 9 月 15 日。根据 Form T1161 申报指引，加元现金存款和规定注册权益（如 RRSP）属于法定排除项；排除后剩余财产（非注册股票市值 $55,000）的公允市值合计超过了 $25,000 门槛，因此 Ethan 必须依法提交 Form T1161。Correct. Under CRA's departure tax rules, the official departure date is the latest of three dates: the day the person leaves (August 10), the day the spouse and dependants leave (September 1) and the day the new country's tax residency is acquired (September 15), so the departure date is September 15, 2027. Under the Form T1161 guidance, Canadian-dollar cash deposits and prescribed registered interests (such as an RRSP) are excluded by law. After exclusion, the fair market value of the remaining property (unregistered shares worth $55,000) exceeds the $25,000 threshold, so Ethan must file Form T1161 by law.

After emigrating from Canada to the United Kingdom and establishing non-resident status, George continues to receive monthly rental income from a residential investment condo in Vancouver and quarterly dividend income from a Canadian corporation.   1. ✓ 正确。根据 ITA s.212(1) 及 CRA 非居民纳税人指引，离境成为非居民后，个人必须主动通知所有加拿大资金支付方（包括银行、租客、物业管理公司及派发股息的公司）其非居民身份，付款方依法负有代扣代缴 25% Part XIII 预扣税（或适用税收协定优惠税率）的法定扣缴义务。若未通知导致预扣不足，纳税人将面临追缴与利息处罚。Correct. By law the payer has a duty to withhold 25% Part XIII tax (or the concessionary rate under a tax treaty). If the failure to notify causes under-withholding, the taxpayer faces reassessment and interest penalties.

  3. ✗ 错误。加拿大来源被动收入默认走 Part XIII 预扣税体系，不由纳税人自行在次年 6 月 15 日按 48% 附加税申报。Incorrect. Canadian-source passive income by default goes through the Part XIII withholding system, and the taxpayer does not file by June 15 of the next year with a 48% surtax.

  4. ✗ 错误。税法完全允许非居民继续持有加拿大房产和投资，绝无强制出售的规定。Incorrect. The law fully allows a non-resident to keep Canadian properties and investments, and there is no forced sale rule.
