本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 纳入率判定与应税利得计算题：给出股票、基金或房地产处置情景，考查2026税年现行½纳入率与已撤销2/3拟议提案的界限，核心测试考生能否排除过期考试资料干扰，准确计算进入line 12700的应税资本利得。Inclusion-rate and taxable-gain calculation: given a disposition of shares, funds or real estate, tests the line between the current 1/2 inclusion rate for the 2026 tax year and the cancelled 2/3 proposal. The core skill is ignoring outdated exam material and computing the taxable capital gain that goes on line 12700.
  • 同种财产加权平均ACB测算题：结合跨券商多账户买入同一只股票的场景，考查ITA s.47强制合并加权平均规则，核心测试考生识别T5008单据成本栏缺陷、独立维护加权成本台账的能力。Weighted-average ACB for identical properties: the same stock bought through several brokerage accounts. Tests the mandatory pooled weighted-average rule in ITA s.47. The core skill is spotting the flaw in the T5008 cost box and keeping your own weighted cost ledger.
  • 房屋用途改变视同处置与主居所指定申报题：针对自住转出租或换房情景，考查视同处置公允市价重估、T2091(IND)强制申报义务及s.220(3.5)迟报罚款，核心测试「即使利得为零也必须申报以锁定免税基础」的规则。Change of use, deemed disposition and principal residence designation: a home converted to a rental, or a move to a new home. Tests the fair market value deemed disposition, the mandatory T2091(IND) filing and the s.220(3.5) late-filing penalty. The core rule is that you must report even when the gain is zero in order to lock in the exemption.
  • 资本亏损通道定性与限制规则辨析题：混合一般资本亏损、表面亏损（前后30天）、个人用途财产（PUP）及上市个人财产（LPP）亏损，考查结转期限、冲抵范围（能否抵经常收入或其它利得）以及被否亏损并入新资产ACB的机制。Capital loss channels and their limits: mixes general capital losses, superficial losses (30 days before and after), personal-use property (PUP) losses and listed personal property (LPP) losses. Tests carryover periods, what each loss may offset (ordinary income or other gains), and how a denied loss is added to the ACB of the new property.
  • 终身资本利得豁免（LCGE）与替代性最低税（AMT）联动题：合格小企业股份处置，考 2026 税年 line 25400 扣除上限（637,500 元），以及 AMT 下利得 100% 计入、LCGE 扣除只放大 7/5 的失衡，和 7 年回抵。Lifetime capital gains exemption (LCGE) and alternative minimum tax (AMT) together: disposition of qualified small business shares. Tests the 2026 line 25400 deduction limit ($637,500), the AMT rule that includes 100% of the gain while the LCGE deduction is only grossed up by 7/5, and the 7-year carryforward of AMT paid.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 纳入率过期拟议陷阱Outdated proposed inclusion rate trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常受2024至2025年培训考试资料与媒体宣传影响，误以为2024年6月25日起资本利得超过25万的部分要按66.67%纳入率计税。事实上该提案在2025年3月已被正式取消且从未获御准生效，2026税年一律按现行法定的50%（½）计算。Candidates are often influenced by 2024-2025 study and exam material and by media coverage, and wrongly believe that gains above $250,000 are taxed at a 66.67% inclusion rate from June 25, 2024. In fact the proposal was formally cancelled in March 2025 and never received Royal Assent. For the 2026 tax year the enacted rate of 50% (1/2) applies to every gain.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「超过 25 万的部分」「66.67% inclusion rate」「2/3 inclusion rate」「June 25, 2024 proposed changes」等措辞。The question mentions "the part above $250,000", "66.67% inclusion rate", "2/3 inclusion rate" or "June 25, 2024 proposed changes".

⚠ T5008成本栏直抄陷阱Copying the T5008 cost box trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 自主投资者常直接照抄券商出具的T5008单据上的成本栏作为ACB，忽略了税法ITA s.47强制要求纳税人名下所有同种财产必须跨账户加权平均，且单家券商往往信息不全或无法体现全局ACB。Do-it-yourself investors often copy the cost box on the brokerage's T5008 slip as the ACB. They overlook that ITA s.47 requires all identical properties owned by the taxpayer to be averaged across accounts, and a single brokerage often lacks the full picture or cannot show the overall ACB.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「T5008 cost box」「shares held in multiple brokerage accounts」「identical properties」「average cost per share」。The question mentions "T5008 cost box", "shares held in multiple brokerage accounts", "identical properties" or "average cost per share".

⚠ 主居所免税不报没事陷阱"The principal residence exemption means no reporting" trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为自住房出售或自住转出租利得全免就不用申报，忽略了 2016 年起不申报并做指定就不给免税；事后补报要按每满一个月 100 加元、封顶 8,000 加元罚款。Thinking that the sale of a home, or a conversion of the home to a rental, is fully exempt and so needs no reporting. Since 2016 the exemption is not granted unless the disposition is reported and the property designated. A late request is penalized at $100 for each complete month, capped at $8,000.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「converted principal residence to rental」「received no cash proceeds」「no need to report」「late designation penalty」。The question mentions "converted principal residence to rental", "received no cash proceeds", "no need to report" or "late designation penalty".

⚠ 个人用途财产亏损扣除陷阱Personal-use property loss deduction trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生误以为只要是财产处置亏损就能在附表三申报，忽略了个人自用的汽车、游艇、家具等个人用途财产（PUP）亏损在税法上属于消费损失，严禁扣除，Schedule 3第9行只填利得。Candidates assume any loss on the disposition of property can be reported on Schedule 3. They overlook that a loss on personal-use property (PUP) such as a private car, boat or furniture is a consumption loss and is not deductible. Schedule 3 line 9 takes gains only.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「sold personal boat at a loss」「furniture / private automobile」「Schedule 3 line 15800」「personal-use property」。The question mentions "sold personal boat at a loss", "furniture / private automobile", "Schedule 3 line 15800" or "personal-use property".

⚠ 表面亏损30天窗口陷阱Superficial loss 30-day window trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 投资者在年底为了抵税卖出亏损股票，但在卖出前后30天内自己或配偶、受控公司又买回相同股票，误以为仍能扣除亏损。税法规定该亏损属于表面亏损被当年否决，但被否金额必须加进替代股票的ACB中。An investor sells a losing stock near year-end to save tax, but within 30 days before or after the sale the investor, a spouse or a controlled corporation buys identical shares, and wrongly believes the loss is still deductible. The law treats it as a superficial loss and denies it for the year, but the denied amount must be added to the ACB of the replacement shares.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「sold at a loss and repurchased within 30 days」「spouse purchased identical shares」「affiliated person」「superficial loss」。The question mentions "sold at a loss and repurchased within 30 days", "spouse purchased identical shares", "affiliated person" or "superficial loss".

第 1 题Q1现行½纳入率与应税利得计算Current 1/2 inclusion rate and taxable gain calculation★陷阱：纳入率过期拟议陷阱

In September 2026, Brian sold shares in a publicly traded Canadian corporation held in his personal non-registered brokerage account, realizing a capital gain of $300,000. Brian had no other capital transactions in 2026. A friend told Brian that capital gains over $250,000 are subject to a 66.67% inclusion rate. According to the 2026 tax-year rules in the lecture, what is Brian's taxable capital gain to be entered on line 12700 of his federal return?

2026年9月，Brian 卖出了个人非注册经纪账户中持有的加拿大上市公司股票，实现资本利得$300,000。Brian 在2026年没有其他资本交易。一位朋友告诉 Brian，超过$250,000的资本利得按66.67%纳入率计税。根据讲义中2026税年规则，Brian 应在联邦税表 line 12700 填报的应税资本利得是多少？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据讲义第2.1节与第5节，2024年提出的提高纳入率至2/3（及个人前25万按½）的提案在2025年3月被正式取消，该提案一天都未曾生效；现行ITA s.38(a)与Schedule 3第23行白纸黑字印着的纳入率均为50%。因此全部$300,000利得统一乘以½，应税资本利得为$150,000。Correct. Under sections 2.1 and 5 of the lecture, the 2024 proposal to raise the inclusion rate to 2/3 (and to keep 1/2 on the first $250,000 for individuals) was formally cancelled in March 2025 and was never in effect for a single day. ITA s.38(a) and Schedule 3 line 23 both state a 50% inclusion rate. So the entire $300,000 gain is multiplied by 1/2, and the taxable capital gain is $150,000.

  2. ✗ 属于「纳入率过期拟议陷阱」。分段按25万元门槛（前25万按50%、超出按66.67%）计算是2024年已撤销提案的内容，该提案从未通过立法御准，在2026年照抄属于重大错报。This is the "outdated proposed inclusion rate trap". Splitting the gain at a $250,000 threshold (50% on the first $250,000, 66.67% above) comes from the 2024 proposal that was cancelled. It never received Royal Assent, and using it in 2026 is a serious misreporting.

  3. ✗ 属于「企业拟议税率误套陷阱」。2/3纳入率未在任何税年成为法律，个人与公司均不适用该税率。This is the "misapplied corporate proposed rate trap". The 2/3 inclusion rate never became law in any tax year, and it applies neither to individuals nor to corporations.

  4. ✗ 属于「纳入率与利息全额混淆错误」。资本财产处置利得享受ITA s.38(a)的½纳入率优惠，只有利息收入才100%计入。This is the "confusing the inclusion rate with full inclusion of interest" error. Gains on the disposition of capital property get the 1/2 inclusion rate under ITA s.38(a); only interest income is included at 100%.

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

In November 2026, Chloe realized a capital gain of $400,000 from disposing of investment property held in Canada. Her tax preparer relied on an outdated 2025 study manual that instructed preparers to apply a 2/3 inclusion rate to individual gains exceeding $250,000. Under the enacted 2026 federal rules, what is Chloe's correct taxable capital gain for line 12700?

2026年11月，Chloe 处置加拿大境内的投资物业实现了$400,000资本利得。她的报税员依据一本过期的2025年培训考试资料，指示其对个人超过$250,000的利得部分适用2/3纳入率。根据2026年已生效的联邦规则，Chloe 在 line 12700 上正确的应税资本利得是多少？

  1. ✗ 属于「纳入率过期拟议陷阱」。以25万为分界线套用66.67%的算法从未生效，讲义第2.1节明确指出该提议已在2025年被彻底取消。This is the "outdated proposed inclusion rate trap". Applying 66.67% above a $250,000 line never took effect, and section 2.1 of the lecture states that the proposal was fully cancelled in 2025.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据讲义第2.1节，2026税年的资本利得纳入率依然是全额按½（50%）计算，不存在$250,000的分段门槛。$400,000 × ½ ＝ $200,000进入第12700行。Correct. Under section 2.1 of the lecture, the inclusion rate for the 2026 tax year is still 1/2 (50%) on the entire gain, with no $250,000 threshold. $400,000 x 1/2 = $200,000, which goes on line 12700.

  3. ✗ 属于「提案生效误判陷阱」。2024年6月25日生效的2/3纳入率提案未获御准，且已被正式废止，Schedule 3官方表格明确印制50%。This is the "proposal in effect misjudgment trap". The 2/3 inclusion rate proposed for June 25, 2024 never received Royal Assent and has been formally withdrawn. The official Schedule 3 form prints 50%.

  4. ✗ 属于「捏造二次减免错误」。省税以联邦应税收入为税基，没有任何省份对个人投资物业额外减半课税。This is the "invented second reduction error". Provincial tax uses federal taxable income as its base, and no province halves the tax again on personal investment property.

第 2 题Q2同种财产加权ACB与T5008Weighted-average ACB for identical properties and the T5008★★陷阱：T5008成本栏直抄陷阱

Daniel holds shares of XYZ Ltd. in two non-registered accounts. In Account 1 he bought 600 shares in 2021 at $30 per share plus a $10 commission; in Account 2 he bought 400 shares in 2024 at $50 per share plus a $10 commission. In August 2026 he sold 300 shares from Account 2 at $70 per share, paying a $20 commission. Account 2's T5008 shows a cost based only on its own $50 shares. Which calculation of the capital gain follows ITA s.47?

Daniel 在两个非注册账户里持有 XYZ Ltd. 的股票：账户 1 于 2021 年以每股 $30 买入 600 股，佣金 $10；账户 2 于 2024 年以每股 $50 买入 400 股，佣金 $10。2026 年 8 月他从账户 2 以每股 $70 卖出 300 股，佣金 $20。账户 2 的 T5008 只按本账户每股 $50 的成本填。按 ITA s.47，哪种资本利得算法正确？

  1. ✗ 错误。照抄 T5008 成本栏：21,000 − 15,008 − 20 ≈ 5,972 元。券商只看得到本账户持仓，不符合跨账户加权平均的要求。Incorrect. This copies the T5008 cost box: $21,000 - $15,008 - $20 = about $5,972. The brokerage sees only the holdings in its own account, which does not meet the requirement to average across accounts.

  2. ✗ 错误。先进先出：21,000 − 300 × 30.0167 − 20 ≈ 11,975 元。加拿大税法对同种财产不用先进先出，必须按全部持仓加权平均。Incorrect. This is first-in, first-out: $21,000 - 300 x $30.0167 - $20 = about $11,975. Canadian tax law does not use first-in, first-out for identical properties; they must be averaged across all holdings.

  3. ✓ 正确。ITA s.47 要求名下全部同种财产加权平均：总成本 600 × 30 ＋ 10 ＋ 400 × 50 ＋ 10 ＝ 38,020 元，1,000 股，每股 38.02 元。卖出 300 股：处置所得 21,000 元 − ACB 11,406 元 − 佣金 20 元 ＝ 资本利得 9,574 元。Correct. ITA s.47 requires a weighted average across all identical properties held. Total cost = 600 x $30 + $10 + 400 x $50 + $10 = $38,020 for 1,000 shares, or $38.02 per share. Selling 300 shares: proceeds $21,000 - ACB $11,406 - commission $20 = capital gain $9,574.

  4. ✗ 错误。减半得到的是应税资本利得（约 4,787 元），不是题目问的资本利得本身。Incorrect. Halving gives the taxable capital gain (about $4,787), not the capital gain that the question asks for.

  2. ✗ 属于「LPP与PUP混同错误」。珠宝属于税法明确列举的五类LPP之一，享有向后回抵3年、向前结转7年的待遇，不是普通PUP那样永久作废。This is the "LPP and PUP mix-up error". Jewelry is one of the five categories expressly listed as LPP, with a 3-year carryback and 7-year carryforward, and is not simply forfeited like ordinary PUP.

  3. ✗ 属于「跨类别乱冲陷阱」。资本属性的亏损严禁冲抵受雇收入。This is the "offsetting across categories trap". A loss of a capital nature cannot be deducted against employment income.

  4. ✓ 正确。根据讲义第三节3.4与第七节自查清单第9条，上市个人财产（LPP）亏损自成一池，只能冲抵LPP利得，不能冲抵普通股票利得；未抵扣的LPP亏损可向后回抵3年、向前结转7年（ITA s.41(2)）。Correct. Under section 3.4 and item 9 of the section 7 checklist, listed personal property (LPP) losses form their own pool and can offset only LPP gains, not gains on ordinary stocks. An unused LPP loss can be carried back 3 years and forward 7 years (ITA s.41(2)).

第 7 题Q7LCGE与最低税AMT互动LCGE and AMT interaction★★★陷阱：大额资本利得漏跑AMT陷阱

In 2026, Martin sold shares of a qualified small business corporation (QSBCS), realizing a capital gain of $1,200,000. He had never used his Lifetime Capital Gains Exemption (LCGE) previously and has zero other income in 2026. Under regular tax rules, his taxable capital gain of $600,000 is completely offset by the $600,000 capital gains deduction on line 25400, leaving regular federal tax at $0. How does the Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT) apply to Martin's transaction in 2026?

2026年，Martin 卖掉了合格小型企业公司股份（QSBCS），实现资本利得$1,200,000。他此前从未用过终身资本利得豁免（LCGE），且2026年无其他收入。在常规计税下，其$600,000应税资本利得被 line 25400 上的$600,000资本利得扣除完全抵消，常规联邦税为$0。在2026年，替代性最低税（AMT）如何适用于 Martin 的这笔交易？

  1. ✗ 属于「LCGE免税即AMT豁免陷阱」。讲义第二节2.3与第六节6.3明确指出，常规税算到零的纳税人往往正是AMT的主要纳税人，LCGE不会豁免最低税。This is the "LCGE exemption means AMT exemption trap". Sections 2.3 and 6.3 of the lecture state that taxpayers whose regular tax is reduced to zero are often the main AMT payers; the LCGE does not exempt anyone from minimum tax.

  2. ✗ 属于「完全剥夺扣除误解」。AMT下LCGE扣除允许按7/5（1.4倍）放大扣除，并不是完全不给扣。This is the "deduction fully denied misunderstanding". Under AMT the LCGE deduction is allowed, grossed up by 7/5 (1.4 times); it is not denied entirely.

  3. ✓ 正确。算 AMT 时利得按 100% 计入（1,200,000 元），LCGE 扣除只放大 7/5（600,000 × 1.4 ＝ 840,000 元），调整后应税收入仍留 360,000 元；减去 2026 年免税额 181,440 元后按 20.5% 计算最低税，与常规税比较取大。多缴的 AMT 7 年内可回抵（ITA s.120.2）。Correct. For AMT, the gain is included at 100% ($1,200,000), while the LCGE deduction is only grossed up by 7/5 ($600,000 x 1.4 = $840,000), leaving adjusted taxable income of $360,000. After subtracting the 2026 exemption of $181,440, minimum tax is computed at 20.5% and compared with regular tax, and the larger applies. Excess AMT can be recovered over 7 years (ITA s.120.2).

  4. ✗ 属于「省税AMT捏造错误」。AMT是联邦所得税法设立的制度，且多缴部分在未来7年内可结转回抵常规税。This is the "provincial AMT invention error". AMT is a federal Income Tax Act regime, and any excess paid can be carried forward against regular tax for the next 7 years.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Nancy claimed the full Lifetime Capital Gains Exemption (LCGE) on the sale of her farm property in 2026, which eliminated her regular income tax. However, due to the 100% inclusion rate and 7/5 deduction scaling under AMT, Nancy was required to pay $35,000 in Alternative Minimum Tax for 2026. Nancy plans to retire immediately and anticipates having very modest pension income in future years. What is the tax recovery mechanism and risk regarding Nancy's $35,000 AMT payment?

Nancy 在2026年出售农场财产时全额申请了终身资本利得豁免（LCGE），从而免除了其常规所得税。然而，由于AMT下100%的纳入率和7/5的扣除放大比例，Nancy 在2026年仍需缴纳$35,000的替代性最低税（AMT）。Nancy 计划立即退休，预计未来年份的养老金收入非常微薄。关于 Nancy 这笔$35,000的 AMT 税款，其回抵机制与潜在风险是什么？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据讲义第二节2.3与第六节6.3，多缴的AMT可在其后7个税年内，在常规税超过最低税的限度内申请回抵（ITA s.120.2）；但如果纳税人卖掉资产后随即退休、未来7年收入过低无法产生足够的常规税，多缴的AMT将面临到期作废无法抵回的风险。Correct. Under sections 2.3 and 6.3 of the lecture, excess AMT paid can be recovered in the following 7 tax years, to the extent regular tax exceeds minimum tax (ITA s.120.2). But if the taxpayer retires right after selling the asset and income stays too low over the next 7 years to generate enough regular tax, the excess AMT may expire and never be recovered.

  2. ✗ 属于「AMT现金返还妄想陷阱」。AMT不是多退少补的预扣款，不能通过T1A直接申请现金退款，只能逐年冲抵未来的常规应税税额。This is the "AMT cash refund fantasy trap". AMT is not a withholding that is trued up by a refund, and a cash refund cannot be requested through T1A; it can only be applied against future regular tax year by year.

  3. ✗ 属于「AMT罚款误解」。讲义明确说明AMT不是罚款，而是一套平行的税收计算制度，具有法定7年回抵期。This is the "AMT as a fine misunderstanding". The lecture states that AMT is not a penalty but a parallel tax calculation with a statutory 7-year recovery period.

  4. ✗ 属于「向后回抵十年捏造错误」。AMT没有向后回抵（carry back）的机制，只能向后结转7年（carry forward 7 years）。This is the "10-year carryback invention error". AMT has no carryback mechanism; it can only be carried forward for 7 years.
