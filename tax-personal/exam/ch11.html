第 11 章 · 学生Chapter 11 · Students and Income Tax: Tuition Transfer and Carryforward, Scholarship Exemption, and Education SavingsStudents: tuition and related rules

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 学费抵免自用优先与转让上限核算题：重点考查附表 11 中学生必须先将自身联邦税抵减至零，当年转让给父母或配偶的上限为法定 $5,000 减去学生已自用额度的扣减计算。Tuition credit self-use first and the transfer cap: tests on Schedule 11 that the student must first reduce his or her own federal tax to zero, and that the amount transferable to a parent or spouse for the year is capped at the statutory $5,000 minus the amount the student has already used.
  • 学费结转不可逆与单向门实务题：重点考查未用完学费额一旦选择结转到未来年度，即永久失去向父母或配偶转让的资格，只能留给学生本人未来自用的法律硬性约束。Irreversible tuition carryforward (one-way gate): tests the hard legal rule that unused tuition amounts, once carried forward to a future year, permanently lose eligibility to be transferred to a parent or spouse and can only be used by the student in a future year.
  • 奖学金助学金三档免税待遇分类题：重点考查全日制合格学生全额免税、非全日制资格学生（学费加材料费加 $500 基础免税额）以及非合格教育项目（仅 $500 免税额）的应税金额计算。Three tiers of scholarship and bursary exemption: tests the taxable amount calculation for full-time qualifying students (fully exempt), part-time qualifying students (tuition + materials + $500 basic exemption) and non-qualifying programs (only the $500 exemption).
  • 注册教育储蓄计划（RESP）提款分流题：重点考查 EAP（学生名下应税收入，全日制首 13 周法定上限 $8,000，非全日制每 13 周 $4,000）与 AIP（供款人应税加征 20% 附加税）的税收待遇差异。Splitting RESP withdrawals: tests the different tax treatment of EAP (taxable income in the student's name, $8,000 limit for the first 13 weeks of full-time study and $4,000 per 13 weeks for part-time) and AIP (taxable to the subscriber with a 20% additional tax).
  • 学生专属项目与省税差异综合题：考查加拿大培训抵免（CTC）按 CTCL 与学费 50% 取小的退还计算、政府学生贷款利息（line 31900）仅限借款人本人申报，以及 BC 省取消省级教育额但保留学费额的省税规则。Student-specific items and provincial tax differences: tests the Canada Training Credit (CTC) refund calculated as the lesser of the CTCL and 50% of tuition, government student loan interest (Line 31900) claimable only by the borrower, and the BC provincial rule of eliminating the education amount while keeping the tuition amount.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 学费转让上限未扣自用陷阱Transfer cap without deducting self-use

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为当年未用完的学费额只要超过 $5,000 就能直接转满 $5,000 给父母，忽略了法定上限必须先扣减学生本人当年已经用掉的学费额（即 $5,000 - 本人自用额）。Believing that as long as unused tuition for the year exceeds $5,000, the full $5,000 can be transferred to a parent, ignoring that the statutory cap must first subtract the tuition amount the student used personally that year ($5,000 minus own use).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出学费金额、学生自用学费额及剩余未用完学费，询问学生最多可向父母或配偶转让的学费抵免额。The stem gives the tuition paid, the amount the student used personally and the remaining unused tuition, and asks for the maximum tuition credit the student can transfer to a parent or spouse.

⚠ 结转额度反悔转让陷阱Changing your mind and transferring a carryforward

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为往年结转下来的累积学费额只要没用完，在父母需要抵税的年份仍然可以拿出来转让给父母，混淆了当年额度转让与往年结转额度的法律边界。Believing that cumulative tuition amounts carried forward from earlier years can still be taken out and transferred to a parent in a year when the parent needs the tax relief, confusing the legal boundary between the current-year amount that can be transferred and prior-year carryforwards.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干中出现学生在以前年度已结转的学费额（Unused tuition credits carried forward），询问本年度能否转让给父母或祖父母。The stem shows tuition amounts the student carried forward in earlier years (unused tuition credits carried forward) and asks whether they can be transferred to a parent or grandparent this year.

⚠ 非全日制奖学金单纯五百陷阱Treating part-time as just $500

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把非合格学生的纯 $500 基础免税额机械套用在非全日制资格学生身上，忽略了非全日制资格学生享有该项目实际学费与材料费之和再加上 $500 的扩充免税额。Mechanically applying the plain $500 basic exemption of non-qualifying students to part-time qualifying students, ignoring that part-time qualifying students enjoy the expanded exemption of actual tuition plus materials plus $500 for the program.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干明确提及纳税人为非全日制资格学生（part-time qualifying student），给出学费、考试资料费支出及收到的 T4A 奖学金金额，询问应计入 line 13010 的应税奖学金金额。The stem states that the taxpayer is a part-time qualifying student, gives tuition, materials costs and the T4A scholarship received, and asks for the taxable scholarship amount to include on Line 13010.

⚠ 代付贷款利息父母申报陷阱Parents claiming interest they paid for the loan

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 认为谁实际掏钱还贷谁就能享受税收抵免，误让代为偿还学生贷款利息的父母在其自身 T1 税表 line 31900 申报抵免。Thinking that whoever actually pays the loan gets the tax credit, wrongly letting parents who repaid their child's student loan interest claim the credit on Line 31900 of their own T1.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干说明父母为了减轻刚毕业子女的负担代其全额偿还了政府学生贷款的利息，询问父母能否在其税表上申报该笔利息抵免。The stem says parents repaid in full the interest on a government student loan to ease the burden of a recent graduate child, and asks whether the parents can claim that interest credit on their return.

⚠ 零收入无需报税福利丧失陷阱Nil income means no filing and lost benefits

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为个人没有应缴税款就没有申报价值，忽略了报税是官方登记结转学费额（NOA）、累积次年 RRSP 额度以及按期获取 CGEB 等退税福利的法定前置程序。Mistakenly thinking that having no tax payable means filing has no value, ignoring that filing is the statutory prerequisite for officially registering carried-forward tuition (the NOA), building next year's RRSP room, and receiving CGEB benefits on time.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述年轻在读学生全年只有少量兼职收入或零收入、税负为零，询问是否应当申报当年度 T1 税表及不申报的后果。The stem describes a young student with little part-time income or zero income and no tax payable, and asks whether the T1 for the year should be filed and the consequences of not filing.

第 1 题Q1学费当年转让上限扣除自用Current-year tuition transfer cap after deducting self-use★★陷阱：学费转让上限未扣自用陷阱

A full-time university student paid $9,000 in eligible tuition in 2026. The student worked part-time and utilized $1,800 of the tuition tax credit to reduce their federal income tax payable to zero, leaving $7,200 in unused tuition fees. What is the maximum tuition amount the student can transfer to their father on their 2026 Schedule 11?

一名全日制大学生在 2026 年支付了 $9,000 合格学费。该学生兼职打工，当年使用了 $1,800 学费税收抵免将自身联邦应缴税额抵减至零，剩余 $7,200 未用完学费。该学生在 2026 年附表 11 上最多可向父亲转让多少学费额？

  1. ✗ 错误。$1,800 是学生本人当年自用额度，不能转让给父母。Incorrect. $1,800 is the amount the student used personally that year and cannot be transferred to a parent.

  2. ✗ 错误。此选项掉入了学费转让上限未扣自用陷阱。忽略了法定 $5,000 上限必须扣除学生本人已自用的学费额。Incorrect. This option falls into the trap of not deducting self-use from the transfer cap. It ignores that the statutory $5,000 cap must be reduced by the tuition amount the student has already used.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据附表 11（Schedule 11）规则，学费额转让上限（transfer of tuition amount）为法定上限 $5,000 扣除学生本人当年已用额度（即 $5,000 - $1,800 = $3,200），且不得超过当年未用完额度（$7,200）。因此最多可转让 $3,200 给父亲，剩余 $4,000 留作结转。Correct. Under the Schedule 11 rules, the cap on transfer of the tuition amount is the statutory $5,000 minus the amount the student used that year ($5,000 - $1,800 = $3,200), and cannot exceed the unused amount for the year ($7,200). So at most $3,200 can be transferred to the father, and the remaining $4,000 is left to be carried forward.

  4. ✗ 错误。转让额度受到法定最高限额的刚性约束，未用完的 $7,200 无法全部转让给父亲。Incorrect. The transferable amount is rigidly limited by the statutory maximum, so the whole unused $7,200 cannot be transferred to the father.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

In 2026, a post-secondary college student paid $7,500 in eligible tuition. The student had modest employment income and applied $2,200 of current-year tuition credits to reduce federal tax payable to zero on Schedule 11, leaving $5,300 unused. What is the maximum tuition amount the student can transfer to their supporting mother for 2026?

2026 年，一名大专学生支付了 $7,500 合格学费。该学生有少量兼职收入，在附表 11 上使用了 $2,200 当年学费额将联邦税额降至零，剩余 $5,300 未用完。该学生在 2026 年最多可向资助其读书的母亲转让多少学费额？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据学费抵免转让规则，转让上限为法定上限 $5,000 减去学生本人当年已用额度（$5,000 - $2,200 = $2,800），且不能超过未用完部分（$5,300）。因此最多可向母亲转让 $2,800。Correct. Under the tuition credit transfer rules, the cap is the statutory $5,000 minus the amount the student used that year ($5,000 - $2,200 = $2,800), and cannot exceed the unused part ($5,300). So at most $2,800 can be transferred to the mother.

  2. ✗ 错误。掉入了未扣自用陷阱。法定上限必须先减去学生自用的 $2,200。Incorrect. This falls into the self-use trap. The statutory cap must first be reduced by the student's own $2,200.

  4. ✗ 错误。$2,200 是学生本人抵税用掉的额度，绝非转让给母亲的额度。Incorrect. $2,200 is the amount the student used against his or her own tax, not the amount transferred to the mother.

第 2 题Q2学费结转单向门不可逆原则Tuition carryforward as an irreversible one-way gate★★陷阱：结转额度反悔转让陷阱

A university graduate accumulated $6,000 in unused tuition credits from 2024 and 2025, which were officially carried forward on their CRA Notice of Assessment. In 2026, the graduate's mother entered a higher marginal tax bracket and asked to claim $5,000 of the graduate's carried-forward tuition credits. How must these carried-forward tuition credits be treated on the 2026 return?

一名大学毕业生在 2024 年和 2025 年累积了 $6,000 未用完学费抵免，并已正式结转在 CRA 评税通知书上。2026 年，该毕业生的母亲边际税率上升，希望在自身税表上申报该毕业生 $5,000 的学费结转额度。在 2026 年税表上该笔学费结转额度必须如何处理？

  1. ✗ 错误。修改以往税表也无法将已选择结转的往年额度在随后年度重新转让给父母。Incorrect. Even amending earlier returns cannot make an amount already elected to be carried forward available for transfer to a parent in later years.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据学费抵免结转的单向门规则（single-direction gate），一旦纳税人在某年选择将未用完的学费额结转（carryforward），该笔额度就永久锁死在本人名下，只能由学生本人在未来有应税额的年份抵扣自用，再也无法转让给父母或配偶。Correct. Under the one-way gate rule for tuition carryforward, once a taxpayer elects in a year to carry unused tuition forward, the amount is locked permanently in the taxpayer's own name and can only be used by the student in a future year with tax payable; it can never again be transferred to a parent or spouse.

  3. ✗ 错误。税法不存在往年结转额对半分摊转让的规定。Incorrect. The tax rules have no provision for transferring half of a prior-year carryforward.

  4. ✗ 错误。结转学费额为非退还性质，不能向父母退发现金。Incorrect. Carried-forward tuition is non-refundable and cannot be paid out in cash to a parent.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A taxpayer completed an undergraduate program with $8,000 in unused tuition credits carried forward from prior tax years. In 2026, the taxpayer married a high-earning spouse. Can the taxpayer transfer any portion of these carried-forward tuition credits to the new spouse on their 2026 tax return?
