① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 医疗费用门槛计算与取小规则辨析题：给出纳税人净收入与合格医疗支出总额，考查按净收入 3% 还是按法定封顶值（2026 年为 2,890 元）扣除，重点测试高收入者锁定封顶值而非 3% 的「取小」机制。Medical expense threshold calculation and lesser-of rule distinction questions: Given a taxpayer's net income and total eligible medical expenses, testing whether the deduction threshold is based on 3% of net income or the statutory dollar cap ($2,890 for 2026), focusing on the lesser-of mechanism that locks high earners into the dollar cap rather than 3%.
  • 慈善捐赠分段税率与降税联动计算题：给出当年捐赠金额与收入档次，考查前 $200 适用当年法定「适当百分比」（2026 税年因最低税阶降税自动为 14%）、超过 $200 部分适用 29%/33% 的分段计算结构。Charitable donation tiered tax credit calculation and tax cut linkage questions: Given donation amounts and income brackets for the year, testing the tiered structure where the first $200 applies the statutory appropriate percentage (automatically 14% in the 2026 tax year due to the lowest federal tax bracket rate cut) and amounts exceeding $200 apply 29% or 33%.
  • 申报窗口选择与家庭成员分流行归集题：给出跨越两个自然年的多笔医疗开支及家庭成员，考查「任意连续 12 个月期」窗口选择、低收入配偶申报优先原则，以及 line 33099 与 line 33199（逐人独立算门槛）的分流归集。Claim window selection and family member routing questions: Given multiple medical expenses spanning two calendar years and family members, testing the choice of any 12-month period ending in the tax year, the rule prioritizing the lower-income spouse to make the claim, and routing between line 33099 and line 33199 (calculating a separate threshold per dependant).
  • 特殊资产捐赠与对价扣减实务场景题：考查直接捐赠已升值上市证券享受资本利得零纳入率（对比先卖后捐），以及筹款晚宴拍卖中的对价扣减（Split Receipting）与微量豁免（$75 或 10% 取小）规则。Special asset donations and split-receipting scenario questions: Testing direct in-kind donations of appreciated publicly listed securities qualifying for a zero capital gains inclusion rate (compared to selling first then donating cash), as well as split receipting (advantage deduction) and the de minimis rule (lesser of $75 and 10% of the gift) in fundraising dinners and auctions.
  • 2026 税年新规与相邻税目边界辨析题：考查 2026 税年起家居无障碍改造支出不得同时申报 HATC 与 METC 的排他性新规，以及慈善捐赠（line 34900）与联邦政治捐款（line 41000）独立公式、互不冲抵的界限。2026 tax year new rules and boundary distinction questions: Testing the mutually exclusive rule effective starting in the 2026 tax year that prohibits double-claiming qualifying home accessibility renovation expenses under both HATC and METC, and the clear boundary between charitable donations (line 34900) and federal political contributions (line 41000), which follow separate formulas and cannot be combined or offset.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 取小变取大陷阱Mistaking Lesser Of for Greater Of Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 习惯性认为门槛越高越严格，误以为高收入者按净收入 3% 扣除，将公式中的「取小（lesser of）」记成「取大（greater of）」，导致高收入者的扣减门槛被巨额虚增、抵免额凭空消失。Habitually assuming that higher thresholds represent stricter rules, mistakenly applying 3% of net income for high earners and confusing 'lesser of' with 'greater of' in the formula, which massively inflates the threshold and wipes out the credit.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出净收入超过约 $96,333 的纳税人并要求计算医疗费用税收抵免（METC）的可申报金额。The question stem provides a taxpayer whose net income exceeds approximately $96,333 and asks to calculate the claimable medical expense tax credit (METC) amount.

⚠ 捐赠首段固定 15% 惯性陷阱Assuming a Fixed 15% First-Tier Donation Rate Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 机械死记历史考试资料中的 15% 抵免率，忽略了首段税率法定锚定联邦最低税阶率（适当百分比），2026 税年已随联邦最低税阶降税自动降至 14%。Mechanically memorizing the historical 15% credit rate from outdated study materials while overlooking that the first-tier rate is statutorily tied to the lowest federal tax bracket rate (appropriate percentage), which automatically dropped to 14% in the 2026 tax year following the tax cut.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干明确标注为 2026 税年，且要求计算前 $200 额度或整体慈善捐赠税收抵免金额。The question stem explicitly specifies the 2026 tax year and asks to calculate the credit on the first $200 or the total charitable donation tax credit.

⚠ 自然年机械割裂陷阱Rigid Calendar-Year Boundary Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为医疗费用只能按自然日历年（1 月 1 日至 12 月 31 日）归集，忽略了税法允许自选任何在报税年度内结束的连续 12 个月窗口来合并跨年大额自费医疗支出。Mistakenly assuming that medical expenses can only be claimed on a strict calendar-year basis (January 1 to December 31), ignoring that tax law allows choosing any continuous 12-month period ending in the tax year to combine major out-of-pocket medical expenses spanning two years.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现上一年年末（如 11 月、12 月）与本年连续发生的牙科、手术或正畸等自费大额支出。The question stem presents major out-of-pocket dental, surgery, or orthodontic expenses incurred in late prior year (such as November or December) and the current year.

⚠ 受抚养人合并门槛陷阱Pooling Dependant Thresholds Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为全家所有人的医疗费共用一个单一门槛，将 line 33199 下父母等其他成年受抚养人的医疗费直接与申报人合并扣除单一门槛。Mistakenly assuming that medical expenses for all family members share a single combined threshold, pooling medical expenses for adult dependants like parents under line 33199 with the filer's expenses under a single threshold.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现为 18 岁以上成年子女或年迈父母申报医疗费，且要求计算 line 33199 的申报金额。The question stem involves claiming medical expenses for adult children aged 18 and older or elderly parents, and asks to calculate the claimable amount on line 33199.

⚠ 无障碍双报旧口径惯性陷阱Outdated Accessibility Double-Claiming Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 沿用 2026 税年以前的旧规定，误以为同一笔合资格家居无障碍改造支出在 2026 税年依然能同时申报 HATC（line 31285）和 METC（line 33099）。Relying on pre-2026 tax rules and incorrectly assuming that the same eligible home accessibility renovation expense can still be claimed under both HATC (line 31285) and METC (line 33099) in the 2026 tax year.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 2026 税年发生的轮椅坡道或无障碍卫浴改造支出，询问能否同时申领两项税收抵免。The question stem involves wheelchair ramp or accessible bathroom renovation expenses incurred in the 2026 tax year and asks whether both tax credits can be claimed simultaneously.

第 1 题Q1医疗费门槛取小与高收入封顶Lesser-of medical expense threshold rule and statutory cap for high earners★★陷阱：取小变取大陷阱

In the 2026 tax year, Liam reported a net income of $110,000 on line 23600 of his T1 return. During the year, he paid $6,500 in eligible out-of-pocket medical expenses for himself. Given the 2026 statutory threshold cap of $2,890, what is the eligible medical expense amount Liam can claim on line 33099 before calculating the non-refundable tax credit?

2026 税年，Liam 在 T1 申报表 line 23600 报告净收入为 $110,000。年内他为自己支付了 $6,500 的合资格自费医疗费用。已知 2026 年法定门槛封顶值为 $2,890，在计算非退还税收抵免前，Liam 可以在 line 33099 申报的合资格医疗费用金额是多少？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据医疗费用税收抵免（METC）规则，门槛取申报人净收入的 3%（$110,000 × 3% = $3,300）与法定封顶值（2026 年为 $2,890）中较小者（lesser of）。由于净收入超过分界点（约 $96,333），门槛锁定在 $2,890。可申报金额为 $6,500 − $2,890 = $3,610。Correct. Under the Medical Expense Tax Credit (METC) rules, the threshold is the lesser of 3% of the filer's net income ($110,000 × 3% = $3,300) and the statutory dollar cap ($2,890 for 2026). Because net income exceeds the crossover threshold (approximately $96,333), the threshold is capped at $2,890. The claimable amount is $6,500 − $2,890 = $3,610.

  2. ✗ 错误。此选项误按净收入 3%（$3,300）扣除门槛（$6,500 − $3,300 = $3,200），掉入了「取大（greater of）」而非「取小（lesser of）」的经典计算陷阱，忽略了高收入者门槛受法定封顶值 $2,890 保护。Incorrect. This option mistakenly deducts 3% of net income ($3,300) as the threshold ($6,500 − $3,300 = $3,200), falling into the classic trap of taking the 'greater of' instead of the 'lesser of' and overlooking that high earners are protected by the statutory cap of $2,890.

  3. ✗ 错误。此选项误使用了 2024 税年的历史封顶值 $2,759 进行扣除（$6,500 − $2,759 = $3,741），未采用 2026 税年经指数化调整后的最新现行封顶值 $2,890。Incorrect. This option mistakenly uses the historical 2024 tax year cap of $2,759 ($6,500 − $2,759 = $3,741), failing to apply the current indexed statutory cap of $2,890 for the 2026 tax year.

  4. ✗ 错误。此选项误以为高收入者不受任何门槛限制，忽略了法定必须先扣除「3% 与封顶值取小」的门槛扣减规则。Incorrect. This option mistakenly assumes that high earners are exempt from any threshold deduction, ignoring the statutory requirement to deduct the lesser of 3% of net income and the dollar cap.

第 2 题Q22026 捐赠首段 14% 费率Charitable donation first-tier 14% rate in the 2026 tax year★★陷阱：捐赠首段固定 15% 惯性陷阱

In the 2026 tax year, Ethan has a taxable income of $75,000 (falling in the second federal tax bracket) and makes an eligible cash donation of $1,000 to a registered Canadian charity. How is Ethan's federal charitable donation tax credit (line 34900) calculated?

2026 税年，Ethan 的应税收入为 $75,000（落在联邦第二税阶），向一家加拿大注册慈善机构作出 $1,000 合资格现金捐赠。Ethan 的联邦慈善捐赠税收抵免（line 34900）怎么计算？

  1. ✗ 错误。前 $200 用了 2024 税年（旧材料口径）的 15%；2026 税年「适当百分比」随最低税阶降为 14%。Incorrect. The first $200 incorrectly uses the 15% rate from the 2024 tax year (outdated study materials); in the 2026 tax year, the 'appropriate percentage' dropped to 14% alongside the lowest federal tax bracket.
