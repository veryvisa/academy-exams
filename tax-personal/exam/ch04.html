第 4 章 · CPP、EI 与雇佣扣除Chapter 4 · CPP, EI, and Employment Expenses: Deductions, Overpayments, and Form T777 ClaimsCPP, EI and employment deductions

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 多雇主扣缴汇总结算题：测试考生能否准确识别多张 T4 造成的超缴，分清实扣与应缴，在 Line 30800 和 Line 22215 填应缴额、在 Line 44800 和 Line 45000 申请超缴退税，而非直接把 T4 各框数字加总照抄。Settling overpayments from several employers: tests whether candidates can spot the overpayment created by several T4s, separate what was withheld from what was payable, enter the payable amounts on Line 30800 and Line 22215 and claim the overpayment refund on Line 44800 and Line 45000, instead of simply adding up the T4 boxes and copying them.
  • CPP 按月比例与 EI 对照题：测试考生对供款期折算规则的掌握，能否抓住生日当月之后数月份的规则折算 YMPE 和免税额，同时牢记 EI 绝不按月折算、没有年龄门槛与免税额。CPP monthly proration and EI comparison: tests the proration rule for the contributory period, whether candidates count only the months after the birthday month when prorating the YMPE and basic exemption, and remember that EI is never prorated by month and has no age threshold or exemption.
  • EI 低收入退还边界题：测试考生对 $2,000 可保收入开关的理解，能否准确判断低于或等于 $2,000 时保费全额退回 Line 45000 且 Line 31200 为零，而不是误当作普通免税额处理。EI low-income refund boundary: tests understanding of the $2,000 insurable earnings switch, that at $2,000 or less all premiums are refunded on Line 45000 and Line 31200 is zero, rather than treating it as an ordinary exemption.
  • 雇佣支出资格与受薪/佣金分类题：测试考生能否根据 T4 的 Box 42 判断雇员身份，熟记 T2200 必备要求、封闭清单范围（如按揭利息一律不可扣、房屋保险地税仅佣金雇员可扣），以及佣金支出受收入封顶的二选一规则。Employment expense eligibility and salaried versus commission classification: tests judging employee status from T4 Box 42, remembering the T2200 requirements and the closed list (for example mortgage interest is never deductible, and home insurance and property tax are deductible only for commission employees), and the either-or rule that commission expenses are capped by commission income.
  • GST370 雇员消费税退税核算题：测试考生对退税资格（雇主须为登记人、金融机构雇员排除）、BC 省仅退 5% GST（PST 7% 不退）、以及退款次年必须计入 Line 10400 应税收入的完整联动逻辑。GST370 employee GST/HST rebate calculation: tests rebate eligibility (the employer must be a registrant, and employees of financial institutions are excluded), that BC refunds only the 5% GST (not the 7% PST), and the full link that the refund is taxable income on Line 10400 in the following year.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 照抄实扣金额Copying the amounts withheld

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生习惯于将多张 T4 的 Box 16 或 Box 18 直接加总填入抵免行，忽略了超缴时报表抵免行必须填法定应缴上限，否则导致同一笔钱既抵免又退税。Candidates habitually add Box 16 or Box 18 across several T4s and enter the total on the credit line, ignoring that when there is an overpayment the credit line must show the statutory payable amount, otherwise the same money is both credited and refunded.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干中出现换工作、拥有两个及以上雇主、多张 T4 且合计收入超过 YMPE 或 MIE。The stem mentions a job change, two or more employers, or several T4s whose combined income exceeds the YMPE or MIE.

⚠ 将EI二千元开关误作免税额Treating the EI $2,000 switch as an exemption

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误将 EI 的 $2,000 规则等同于 CPP 的 $3,500 免税额，以为只是前 $2,000 免征，导致在收入低于 $2,000 时仍去计算 Line 31200 抵免。Mistakenly equating the EI $2,000 rule with the CPP $3,500 basic exemption, thinking only the first $2,000 is exempt, and so still calculating a Line 31200 credit when income is under $2,000.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干中受雇收入较低（尤其是打零工或暑期工），Box 24 可保收入不超过 $2,000。The stem gives low employment income (especially casual or summer work) with Box 24 insurable earnings of $2,000 or less.

⚠ 将CPP按月折算套用于EIApplying CPP monthly proration to EI

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到纳税人年内满 18 岁或满 70 岁，习惯性把折算比例同时乘到 EI 的最高可保收入或保费上，混淆了两套制度的底层逻辑。Seeing that the taxpayer turns 18 or 70 during the year, candidates habitually apply the proration ratio to the EI maximum insurable earnings or premiums too, confusing the underlying logic of the two systems.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调雇员在税年内某个月份年满 18 岁（或 70 岁），同时询问 CPP 和 EI 的申报金额。The stem stresses that the employee turns 18 (or 70) in some month of the tax year and asks for both the CPP and EI amounts.

⚠ 房主在家庭办公室扣除按揭利息Homeowner deducting mortgage interest for a home office

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉认为租客能扣房租，房主支付巨额按揭利息更应扣除，混淆了税法只认第三方实际营运支出、不认资本成本与机会成本的原则。Candidates assume that if a tenant can deduct rent, a homeowner paying large mortgage interest should deduct even more, confusing the principle that tax law recognizes only actual operating costs paid to third parties, not capital costs or opportunity costs.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述自住房屋的雇员申请家庭办公室扣除，列出了按揭利息、按揭本金、水电费等支出清单。The stem describes an employee homeowner claiming a home office deduction and lists mortgage interest, mortgage principal, utilities and other expenses.

⚠ 遗漏次年申报GST370退税收入Forgetting the GST370 rebate as next year's income

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误认为消费税退税如同普通退税一样免税，忘记税法规定该退税冲抵了往年已扣除的雇佣支出，依法须在收到当年作为收入纳税。Wrongly treating the GST rebate as a tax-free refund like an ordinary one, forgetting that it offsets employment expenses deducted in an earlier year and so must by law be included as income in the year it is received.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干询问申请 GST370 后的后续税务处理，或考察跨年度税务申报的联动义务。The stem asks about the follow-up tax treatment after filing GST370, or tests the linked obligation across tax years.

第 1 题Q1多雇主CPP超缴分流计算Splitting a multi-employer CPP overpayment★★陷阱：照抄实扣金额

In 2026, David worked for two different employers in Vancouver. Employer 1 paid him $50,000 and deducted $2,766.75 in Box 16. Employer 2 paid him $40,000 and deducted $2,171.75 in Box 16. Neither employer deducted CPP2 (Box 16A is blank) because neither salary alone reached the 2026 YMPE ($74,600). His total pensionable earnings of $90,000 exceed both the YMPE and the YAMPE ($85,000). Total CPP deducted in Box 16 was $4,938.50. How should David complete Line 44800 and the related CPP lines on his 2026 return?

2026 年，David 在温哥华先后为两家雇主工作。雇主 1 支付 $50,000，Box 16 代扣 $2,766.75；雇主 2 支付 $40,000，Box 16 代扣 $2,171.75。单家工资都没到 2026 年 YMPE（$74,600），两家都没扣 CPP2（Box 16A 为空）。他全年可供款收入 $90,000，超过 YMPE 与 YAMPE（$85,000）。两张 T4 的 Box 16 合计 $4,938.50。David 在 2026 年税表上应如何填 Line 44800 及相关 CPP 各行？

  1. ✓ 正确。Schedule 8 五段：基础段 (74,600 元 − 3,500 元) × 4.95% = 3,519.45 元报 Line 30800；第一层增强 71,100 元 × 1% = 711 元，加 CPP2 (85,000 元 − 74,600 元) × 4% = 416 元，合计 1,127 元报 Line 22215；应缴合计 $4,646.45，实扣 $4,938.50，超缴 $292.05 报 Line 44800。有超缴时前两行填应缴额，不填实扣额。Correct. Schedule 8, five steps: base portion ($74,600 - $3,500) x 4.95% = $3,519.45 goes on Line 30800; first enhancement $71,100 x 1% = $711, plus CPP2 ($85,000 - $74,600) x 4% = $416, total $1,127 on Line 22215; total payable $4,646.45, withheld $4,938.50, overpayment $292.05 on Line 44800. When there is an overpayment, the first two lines show the payable amounts, not the amounts withheld.

  2. ✗ 错误。照抄实扣金额陷阱：把两张 T4 的 Box 16 加总填 Line 30800，又在 Line 44800 要回超缴，同一笔钱既抵免又退款，还漏了 Line 22215 的增强扣除。Incorrect. This is the 'copying the amounts withheld' trap: adding up Box 16 on the two T4s for Line 30800 and also claiming the overpayment on Line 44800 credits and refunds the same money, and also misses the enhanced deduction on Line 22215.

  3. ✗ 错误。Schedule 8 把基础段、第一层增强与 CPP2 合并成一个净额比较；账面欠着 CPP2，但整体仍是超缴，CPP2 已在净额里抵掉，不用另行补缴。Incorrect. Schedule 8 combines the base portion, first enhancement and CPP2 into one net comparison; CPP2 may not have been withheld, but overall there is still an overpayment, and the CPP2 is already netted off, so no separate payment is needed.

  4. ✗ 错误。只减了第一层的 Box 16 上限，漏掉收入超过 YMPE 后依法应缴的 CPP2（2026 年 416 元），超缴额被算大。Incorrect. This subtracts only the first-layer Box 16 maximum and misses the CPP2 payable by law once income exceeds the YMPE ($416 for 2026), so the overpayment is overstated.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

In 2026, Brenda held two concurrent jobs in Surrey. Employer A deducted $2,500.00 in Box 16, and Employer B deducted $2,400.00 in Box 16. Neither employer deducted CPP2 (Box 16A is blank). Her total pensionable earnings from both T4 slips were $88,000, exceeding both the YMPE ($74,600) and YAMPE ($85,000). Total CPP deducted was $4,900.00. What is Brenda's correct CPP overpayment amount to be refunded on Line 44800?

2026 年，Brenda 在素里同时兼做两份工作。雇主 A 代扣了 $2,500.00 的 CPP（Box 16），雇主 B 代扣了 $2,400.00 的 CPP（Box 16），两家雇主均未扣缴 CPP2（Box 16A 为空）。两张 T4 的可供款收入合计为 $88,000，超过了 YMPE（$74,600）与 YAMPE（$85,000）。实扣 CPP 合计为 $4,900.00。Brenda 在 Line 44800 上应申报退回的 CPP 超缴金额是多少？

  1. ✗ 错误。只减了第一层上限 $4,230.45，漏掉收入已超 YMPE 依法应缴的 CPP2（416 元），超缴额被算大。Incorrect. This subtracts only the first-layer maximum of $4,230.45 and misses the CPP2 ($416) payable by law once income exceeds the YMPE, so the overpayment is overstated.

  2. ✗ 错判净额正负。实扣 $4,900.00 高于全部应缴法定总额 $4,646.45，冲抵欠缴的 CPP2 后仍有净超缴，不会归零。Incorrect. Wrong sign for the net: the $4,900.00 withheld exceeds the total statutory payable of $4,646.45, so even after offsetting the unpaid CPP2 there is still a net overpayment; it does not become zero.

  3. ✓ 正确。应缴合计＝基础段 3,519.45 元＋第一层增强 711 元＋CPP2 416 元＝$4,646.45；实扣 $4,900.00 − $4,646.45 = $253.55，报 Line 44800。Correct. Total payable = base $3,519.45 + first enhancement $711 + CPP2 $416 = $4,646.45; withheld $4,900.00 - $4,646.45 = $253.55, reported on Line 44800.

  4. ✗ 张冠李戴。$416.00 是 2026 年 CPP2 的应缴法定上限，并非超缴退税金额。Incorrect. Wrong item: $416.00 is the 2026 statutory maximum of CPP2 payable, not the overpayment refund amount.

第 2 题Q2EI二千元低收入退还开关EI $2,000 low-income refund switch★陷阱：将EI二千元开关误作免税额

In 2026, Kevin, an 18-year-old student, earned $1,800 from a short-term part-time job. His T4 slip showed Box 14 and Box 24 insurable earnings of $1,800, and his employer withheld $29.34 of EI premiums in Box 18 (1.63% of $1,800). Kevin had no other employment or insurable income during the year. How should Kevin report this EI premium on his 2026 personal income tax return?

2026 年，18 岁的学生 Kevin 从一份短期兼职工作中赚取了 $1,800。他的 T4 表显示 Box 14 和 Box 24 可保收入均为 $1,800，雇主在 Box 18 中代扣了 $29.34 的 EI 保费（$1,800 的 1.63%）。Kevin 在该年度内没有其他受雇或可保收入。Kevin 在 2026 年个人所得税申报表上应如何申报这笔 EI 保费？

  1. ✗ 掉入 EI 低收入处理误区。当全年可保收入不超过 $2,000 时，纳税人不应在 Line 31200 申报非退还抵免，而应全额申请退款。Incorrect. This falls into the EI low-income mistake: when insurable earnings for the year do not exceed $2,000, the taxpayer should not claim a non-refundable credit on Line 31200 but should claim the full refund.

  2. ✗ 将开关误作免税额陷阱。$2,000 是一道全额退还的判定开关，并非免税额；代扣的保费不会被没收，而是退还给雇员。Incorrect. This is the trap of treating the switch as an exemption: $2,000 is a switch that triggers a full refund, not a basic exemption; the premiums withheld are not forfeited but refunded to the employee.

  3. ✓ 准确应用 EI 低收入全额退款规则。根据税法，全年受保收入不超过 $2,000 的雇员几乎无法满足申领失业金的工时条件，因此在 T2204 表上将其全年已扣缴的 EI 保费全额（$29.34）填入 Line 45000 退回，Line 31200 填零。Correct. This applies the full EI low-income refund rule: an employee with insurable earnings of $2,000 or less for the year can hardly meet the hours needed to claim benefits, so on Form T2204 the full EI premiums withheld ($29.34) are entered on Line 45000 and refunded, and Line 31200 is zero.

  4. ✗ 性质混淆。EI 保费不是 Line 23200 的其他扣除项（Line 23200 用于退还多领的社会福利金），该保费属于可退还抵免。Incorrect. This mixes up the nature of the item: EI premiums are not an other deduction on Line 23200 (Line 23200 is for repayment of social benefits); the premium is a refundable credit.

Trevor 是卑诗省一名持证木工，2026 年因一次合格的临时建筑外派搬迁而发生了 $6,500 的合格差旅及临时住宿支出。其常住居所与工地相距 180 公里，临时居所离工地近了 130 公里。Trevor 在 2026 年从该临时项目中赚取了 $11,000。他没有拿到承包商签署的 T2200 表。Trevor 在 2026 年税表上可申报多少劳动力流动扣除？

  1. ✗ 错误。劳动力流动扣除不需要 T2200。Incorrect. The labour mobility deduction does not require a T2200.

  2. ✗ 错误。漏了 50% 收入限制：$6,500 虽低于 2026 年上限 $10,000，但超过 $11,000 × 50% = $5,500。Incorrect. This misses the 50% income limit: $6,500 is under the 2026 cap of $10,000 but exceeds $11,000 x 50% = $5,500.

  3. ✓ 正确。上限 $10,000、距离差 130 公里满足、不需要 T2200；扣除不得超过该次外派收入的 50%：$11,000 × 50% = $5,500。Correct. The cap is $10,000, the distance difference of 130 km qualifies, and no T2200 is needed; the deduction cannot exceed 50% of the income from that assignment: $11,000 x 50% = $5,500.

  4. ✗ 错误。用了 2025 年以前的旧上限；这里起作用的是 50% 收入限制。Incorrect. This uses the old cap that applied up to 2025; the 50% income limit is what applies here.

第 7 题Q7GST370退税比例与次年收入GST370 rebate rate and next year's income★★★陷阱：遗漏次年申报GST370退税收入

In 2026, Gary, an IT consultant employed by a registered commercial firm in BC, properly claimed $4,200 of eligible employment expenses (all subject to sales tax, excluding any motor vehicle CCA) on Line 22900 of his return. In early 2027, he filed Form GST370 to claim an employee GST/HST rebate. In July 2027, CRA approved the rebate and issued a refund payment of $200 ($4,200 × 5/105). Which of the following correctly describes the provincial sales tax rule and the subsequent tax reporting requirement for this rebate?

2026 年，Gary 作为卑诗省一家已登记商业公司的 IT 顾问，在报税表 Line 22900 上合规申报了 $4,200 的合格雇佣支出（全部含消费税，不含任何车辆 CCA）。2027 年初，他提交了 GST370 表申请雇员消费税退税。2027 年 7 月，CRA 批准并核发了 $200 的退税款（$4,200 × 5/105）。关于省销售税规则以及该笔退税款后续的税务申报要求，下列哪项说明完全正确？

  1. ✗ BC 省销售税误区与免税误区。BC 省为非参与省，PST（7%）不在联邦雇员消费税退还范围内；且该退税并非永久免税。Incorrect. This is the BC sales tax misconception and the tax-free misconception: BC is a non-participating province, the PST (7%) is not covered by the federal employee GST rebate, and the rebate is not permanently tax-free.

  2. ✗ 冲减 UCC 对象混淆。只有针对车辆资本折让（CCA）所退回的消费税才用于冲减次年年初 UCC，针对常规雇佣支出的退款不冲减 UCC。Incorrect. This mixes up what reduces the UCC: only GST refunded in respect of capital cost allowance on a vehicle is used to reduce next year's opening UCC; a refund relating to ordinary employment expenses does not reduce UCC.

  3. ✓ 准确阐释 BC 特殊规则与次年收入联动规则。BC 省未加入合并销售税（HST），7% 的 PST 绝不可退，退税率严格按 5/105 折算（$4,200 × 5/105 ＝ $200）；且税法规定，针对雇佣支出退回的消费税冲抵了往年已扣除的费用，必须在收到退款的纳税年度（2027 税年）计入 Line 10400（其他就业收入）缴税。Correct. This explains the BC special rule and the link to next year's income. BC did not join the harmonized sales tax (HST), the 7% PST is never refundable, and the rebate is calculated strictly at 5/105 ($4,200 x 5/105 = $200); and by law, GST refunded on employment expenses offsets expenses deducted in earlier years and must be included on Line 10400 (other employment income) in the tax year the refund is received (the 2027 tax year).

  4. ✗ 错判排除行业。不可申请 GST370 的是银行、信托、保险等'列名金融机构'（listed financial institutions），普通科技商业公司雇员完全具备申请资格。Incorrect. This wrongly excludes an industry: those who cannot claim GST370 are employees of banks, trusts, insurance and other 'listed financial institutions'; employees of ordinary technology businesses are fully eligible.

Alice, a structural engineer in Vancouver, incurred $6,300 in eligible travel and supplies expenses while working for a GST-registered consulting firm in 2026. Her receipts showed 5% GST and 7% BC PST. She filed Form GST370 within the statutory 4-year deadline and received a rebate payment of $300 ($6,300 × 5/105) in October 2027.   2. ✗ 追溯调整错误。收到退款无需追溯修改支出所属年度（2026年）的申报表，而是直接计入收到年度（2027年）收入；BC 省财政厅亦不退还该雇员 PST。Incorrect.   3. ✗ 捏造报表抵减行。消费税退款与 Line 31260 加拿大就业额没有任何扣减关联。Incorrect. This invents a deduction line: a GST refund has no linkage to reducing the Canada employment amount on Line 31260.

  4. ✓ 精准掌握 GST370 规则与报表对应。BC 省实行 GST 与 PST 双轨制，PST 非退税合格税种，退税仅限 5% GST（按 5/105 折算得 $300）；纳税人须在收到退税款的税年（2027 年）将该 $300 全额填入 Line 10400 申报应税收入。Correct. This grasps the GST370 rules and the return lines: BC runs separate GST and PST systems, PST is not a refundable tax, and the rebate is limited to the 5% GST (5/105 gives $300); the taxpayer must report the full $300 as taxable income on Line 10400 in the tax year the refund is received (2027).
