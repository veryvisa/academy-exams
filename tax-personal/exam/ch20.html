本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 个人异议期限取较晚者计算题：考查《所得税法》第 165(1)(a) 条的法定公式，测试个人与 graduated rate estate 适用「申报截止日后一年」与「NOA 发出后 90 天」取较晚者的特殊规则，辨析企业纯 90 天与个人的区别。Computing the individual's objection deadline as the later of two dates: tests the statutory formula in section 165(1)(a) of the Income Tax Act. The special rule for individuals and a graduated rate estate is the later of "one year after the filing due date" and "90 days after the NOA is mailed", which distinguishes the plain 90 days for corporations from that for individuals.
  • 正常复评期起算与法定例外判定题：考查个人与 CCPC 的 3 年正常复评期以原始 NOA 发出日为起算点，测试疏忽、粗心、故意隐瞒或欺诈导致无限期复评的法定条件，以及漏报 T1135 且漏报境外收入延长 3 年（合计 6 年）的组合要件。Start of the normal reassessment period and the statutory exceptions: tests that the 3-year normal reassessment period for an individual and a CCPC starts from the date the original NOA is issued, the statutory conditions of neglect, carelessness, wilful default or fraud that lead to an unlimited reassessment, and the combined elements (failing to file the T1135 and also failing to report foreign income) that add 3 years for a total of 6.
  • CRA 核对审查信类型与稽查边界辨析题：考查 Pre-assessment Review（NOA 前）、Processing Review（NOA 后）与 Matching Program（第三方匹配）的时点与对象差异，测试核对审查与全面稽查在程序性质、材料准备与应对策略上的核心界限。Types of CRA review letters and the limits of an audit: tests the differences in timing and subject among the Pre-assessment Review (before the NOA), the Processing Review (after the NOA) and the Matching Program (third-party matching), and the core line between a review and a full audit in procedural nature, preparation of materials and response strategy.
  • 改表十年退税上限与纳税人减免十年锚点对比题：考查 Change my return / T1-ADJ 退税受制于税年起算 10 个日历年的硬上限，对比纳税人减免（RC4288）按申请年回溯 10 年罚款（所属税年）与利息（累积日历年）的双锚点差异。The 10-year refund cap for changing a return versus the 10-year anchor for taxpayer relief: tests the hard cap by which Change my return / T1-ADJ refunds are limited to 10 calendar years counted from the tax year, and compares it with the dual anchors of taxpayer relief (RC4288), which looks back 10 years from the year of application for penalties (by the tax year they relate to) and for interest (by the calendar year it accumulated).
  • 税务法院非正式程序门槛与诉讼效力判断题：考查《税务法院法》第 18 条的 $25,000 争议税额与罚款门槛（三年未变、不随通胀指数化）、超额主动限额规则、免申请费与放宽证据规则，以及非正式判决无拘束力判例效力与上诉联邦限四项理由。The Tax Court informal procedure threshold and the effect of litigation: tests the $25,000 threshold of disputed tax and penalties under section 18 of the Tax Court of Canada Act (unchanged for three years and not indexed to inflation), the rule on voluntarily limiting an excess amount, the waiver of the filing fee and relaxed rules of evidence, and that an informal judgment has no binding precedential effect and may be appealed to the Federal Court on only four grounds.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 机械套用90天异议期限Mechanically applying the 90-day objection deadline

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 习惯于商业或常规行政程序的 90 天规定，混淆了企业与个人的异议规则，忽略了个人与 graduated rate estate 享有「申报日后一年」与「NOA日后90天」取较晚者的法定保护。Being used to the 90-day rule in commercial or ordinary administrative procedures, confusing the objection rules for corporations with those for individuals, and overlooking that an individual and a graduated rate estate have the statutory protection of the later of "one year after the filing date" and "90 days after the NOA date".

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出个人（非公司）纳税人的申报截止日（4月30日或6月15日）与评税通知发信日期，询问提交异议通知书（Notice of Objection）的法定期限。The question gives an individual (not a corporation) taxpayer's filing due date (April 30 or June 15) and the mailing date of the notice of assessment, and asks for the statutory deadline to file a Notice of Objection.

⚠ 把正常复评期当无条件保护伞Treating the normal reassessment period as an unconditional umbrella

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为个人的 3 年正常复评期是一道不可逾越的绝对防火墙，忽略了该保护以如实申报为前提；一旦存在可归因于疏忽、粗心、故意隐瞒的失实陈述或欺诈，复评期即变为无限期。Believing the individual's 3-year normal reassessment period is an absolute firewall that cannot be crossed, and overlooking that the protection assumes honest reporting. Once there is a misrepresentation attributable to neglect, carelessness or wilful default, or fraud, the reassessment period becomes unlimited.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提及距离评税已过 3 年，但纳税人在申报时存在粗心疏漏、未申报事实或同时漏报 T1135 与境外收入，询问 CRA 是否仍有权重新评税。The question mentions that 3 years have passed since the assessment, but that the taxpayer was careless or omitted facts in the filing, or failed both to file a T1135 and to report the foreign income, and asks whether CRA can still reassess.

⚠ 混淆核对审查与全面稽查Confusing a review with a full audit

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 收到 CRA 来信要求补交某项扣除凭证或解释差异时，恐慌地以为自己面临全面稽查（audit），过度反应或错过了按信中 claim code 补件的 30 天简易窗口。On receiving a CRA letter asking for a missing deduction receipt or an explanation of a difference, panicking that it is a full audit, overreacting, or missing the simple 30-day window to supply documents using the claim code in the letter.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 Pre-assessment Review、Processing Review 或 Matching Program，询问纳税人所面临程序的法律性质与第一步应对措施。The question has a Pre-assessment Review, a Processing Review or a Matching Program, and asks about the legal nature of the procedure the taxpayer faces and the first response.

⚠ 混淆改表十年与减免十年Confusing the 10 years for changing a return with the 10 years for relief

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 两个机制都包含「10 年」概念，容易记混起算锚点：Change my return 的 10 年锚在要改的税年（决定退税能否发放），而纳税人减免 RC4288 的 10 年锚在提出申请的年份（决定罚息减免范围）。Both mechanisms involve a "10 years" concept, and it is easy to mix up the starting anchors. The 10 years for Change my return is anchored to the tax year being changed (deciding whether a refund can be paid), while the 10 years for taxpayer relief RC4288 is anchored to the year the application is made (deciding the scope of penalty and interest relief).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及多年以前旧税年的退税调整申请，或多年前欠税累积利息的减免申请，询问是否受 10 年法定窗口限制。The question involves an application to adjust a refund for an old tax year many years back, or an application for relief of interest accumulated on a balance owing from many years ago, and asks whether the 10-year statutory window limits it.

⚠ 混淆已评定减免与未申报自愿披露Confusing relief on an assessed amount with voluntary disclosure of undeclared tax

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 望文生义地把罚款利息减免当成同一件事，未能分清自愿披露（VDP）仅适用于在 CRA 发现前主动补报未申报税项，而纳税人减免（RC4288）仅适用于已被正确评定但因不可抗力情有可原的罚款与利息。Taking the words at face value and treating relief of penalties and interest as the same thing, failing to distinguish that voluntary disclosure (VDP) applies only to proactively reporting undeclared tax before CRA finds it, while taxpayer relief (RC4288) applies only to penalties and interest that were correctly assessed but excusable because of circumstances beyond the taxpayer's control.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述纳税人多年漏报收入未被税务局联系，或者已被 CRA 正确下达了罚息账单但因重病等不可抗力逾期，询问应走 VDP 还是 RC4288 表格。The question describes a taxpayer who failed to report income for many years and has not been contacted by CRA, or one who was correctly billed penalties and interest by CRA but was late because of circumstances beyond control such as serious illness, and asks whether to use VDP or Form RC4288.

第 1 题Q1个人异议法定期限取较晚者计算Computing the individual objection deadline as the later of two dates★★陷阱：机械套用90天异议期限

Lucas is an employed individual taxpayer who timely filed his 2025 personal income tax return on April 25, 2026. The CRA assessed the return and mailed an initial Notice of Assessment on May 20, 2026, disallowing his moving expense deduction. Lucas intends to dispute the adjustment by serving a formal Notice of Objection (Form T400A). Under subsection 165(1) of the Income Tax Act, what is the statutory deadline for Lucas to serve his Notice of Objection on the CRA?

Lucas 是一名受雇个人纳税人，于 2026 年 4 月 25 日按时申报了其 2025 税年个人税表。CRA 完成评税并于 2026 年 5 月 20 日寄出了原始评税通知（NOA），剔除了他的搬迁费扣除。Lucas 拟通过递交正式异议通知书（Form T400A）进行争议。根据《所得税法》第 165(1) 条，Lucas 向 CRA 提出异议的法定期限是哪一天？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是落入“机械套用 90 天”陷阱；个人与 graduated rate estate 并非单纯适用 90 天规则，还要与申报截止日后一年比对并取较晚者。Incorrect. This falls into the "mechanically applying 90 days" trap. An individual and a graduated rate estate are not subject to the plain 90-day rule: it must be compared with one year after the filing due date and the later taken.

  2. ✗ 错误。税法从未规定以评税发出的日历年底作为异议截止期。Incorrect. The law has never set the end of the calendar year in which the assessment was issued as the objection deadline.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据 ITA s.165(1)(a) 及讲义第 2.5 节，个人（非信托）与 graduated rate estate 的异议期限为「该年申报截止日后一年」与「评税通知发出后 90 天」两者取较晚者。2025 税年普通雇员申报截止日为 2026 年 4 月 30 日，其后一年为 2027 年 4 月 30 日；NOA 发出日后 90 天约为 2026 年 8 月 18 日。取两者较晚者，法定异议截止日为 2027 年 4 月 30 日。Correct. Under ITA s.165(1)(a) and section 2.5 of the lecture, the objection deadline for an individual (not a trust) and a graduated rate estate is the later of "one year after the filing due date for the year" and "90 days after the notice of assessment is issued". The filing due date for an ordinary employee for the 2025 tax year is April 30, 2026, and one year after it is April 30, 2027. 90 days after the NOA date is about August 18, 2026. Taking the later of the two, the statutory objection deadline is April 30, 2027.

  4. ✗ 错误。混淆了异议时效与正常复评期（3 年）；正常复评期是 CRA 重新评税的期限，绝非纳税人提异议的法定期限。Incorrect. This confuses the objection time limit with the normal reassessment period (3 years). The normal reassessment period is the period within which CRA may reassess and is never the statutory period for the taxpayer to object.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Evelyn operates an unincorporated sole proprietorship whose 2025 personal tax return was subject to the June 15, 2026 filing deadline. Evelyn filed her return on June 10, 2026. Due to internal administrative backlogs, the CRA did not mail her initial Notice of Assessment until May 5, 2027. Evelyn disagrees with an expense disallowance. Under ITA s.165(1)(a), what is Evelyn's statutory deadline to file a Notice of Objection?

Evelyn 经营一家未成立公司的独资企业，其 2025 税年税表适用 2026 年 6 月 15 日的申报截止日。Evelyn 于 2026 年 6 月 10 日报税。由于内部积压，CRA 迟至 2027 年 5 月 5 日才发出原始评税通知（NOA）。Evelyn 对费用剔除有异议。根据 ITA s.165(1)(a)，Evelyn 提交异议通知书的法定期限为何？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据 ITA s.165(1)(a)，自雇人士 2025 税年申报截止日为 2026 年 6 月 15 日，其后一年为 2027 年 6 月 15 日；而 NOA 发出日（2027 年 5 月 5 日）后 90 天为 2027 年 8 月 3 日。两者取较晚者，8 月 3 日晚于 6 月 15 日，因此截止日为 NOA 后 90 天的 2027 年 8 月 3 日。Correct. Under ITA s.165(1)(a), the filing due date for a self-employed person for the 2025 tax year is June 15, 2026, and one year after it is June 15, 2027. 90 days after the NOA date (May 5, 2027) is August 3, 2027. Taking the later of the two, August 3 is later than June 15, so the deadline is August 3, 2027, 90 days after the NOA.

  2. ✗ 错误。忽略了“取较晚者”规则；当 CRA 严重迟发 NOA 时，NOA 发出后 90 天晚于申报截止日后一年，以较晚的 90 天为准。Incorrect. This ignores the "later of" rule. When CRA issues the NOA very late, 90 days after the NOA is later than one year after the filing due date, and the later 90 days governs.

  3. ✗ 错误。起算法定一年的基准是法定的“申报截止日”（filing-due date, 6月15日），而不是纳税人实际交表的日期（6月10日）。Incorrect. The base for the statutory one year is the statutory "filing due date" (June 15), not the date the taxpayer actually filed (June 10).

  4. ✗ 错误。从 NOA 发出日起算的时间长度是 90 天，并非整整一年；只有从申报截止日起算才是整整一年。Incorrect. The length counted from the NOA date is 90 days, not a full year. Only counting from the filing due date gives a full year.

第 2 题Q2正常复评期起算与疏忽超期复评Start of the normal reassessment period and reassessment beyond it for neglect★★陷阱：把正常复评期当无条件保护伞

Marcus, an individual taxpayer, filed his 2021 personal return on April 28, 2022, and the CRA issued his initial Notice of Assessment on June 15, 2022. In October 2026, during an audit of third-party corporate records, the CRA discovered that Marcus failed to report a $25,000 taxable dividend in 2021 as a result of careless bookkeeping. Marcus argues the CRA is legally prohibited from reassessing his 2021 return because more than three years have passed since his initial assessment. How does the Income Tax Act apply?

Marcus 是一名个人纳税人，于 2022 年 4 月 28 日申报了其 2021 税年税表，CRA 于 2022 年 6 月 15 日发出了原始评税通知（NOA）。2026 年 10 月，CRA 在审查第三方公司记录时发现 Marcus 因记账粗心遗漏了 2021 年一笔 $25,000 的应税股息。Marcus 辩称距其原始评税已过三年以上，CRA 在法律上已被禁止重新评税。根据《所得税法》，应如何处理？

2026 年，两名纳税人向 CRA 寻求行政救济。Sandra 在过去四个税年漏报了海外一套公寓的租金收入，CRA 从未就该收入联系过她或启动稽查。Gregory 则因 2024 年遭遇严重车祸长期住院，错过了报税和缴税截止日；CRA 随后下达评税，正确计征了迟报罚款与欠税利息。Sandra 与 Gregory 分别应走哪条救济渠道？

  1. ✗ 错误。完全搞反了两个制度的适用对象；漏报未评定收入不能走 RC4288，而已被正确评定罚息不能走 VDP。Incorrect. This reverses the persons to whom the two regimes apply. Unassessed income omitted cannot go through RC4288, and penalties and interest already correctly assessed cannot go through the VDP.

  2. ✗ 错误。异议针对的是评税本身的定性或计算错误；本案中 Gregory 的罚息是正确计算的（只是情有可原），而 Sandra 尚未被评税，均不可提起异议。Incorrect. An objection concerns an error in the characterization or computation of the assessment itself. In this case Gregory's penalties and interest were correctly computed (only excusable), and Sandra has not yet been assessed, so neither can file an objection.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据讲义第 2.7、2.8 节及第七节错报清单第 6 项，区分两者的核心判断标准是“CRA 到底有没有评定过这笔钱”。Sandra 的海外租金收入属于从未申报过的税项且 CRA 尚未发现，应走自愿披露计划（VDP，符合 Unprompted 条件）；而 Gregory 的罚款与利息已经被 CRA 正确下达，但因严重车祸等超出纳税人控制的不可抗力情有可原，应通过 RC4288 申请纳税人减免（Taxpayer Relief）。Correct. Under sections 2.7 and 2.8 of the lecture and item 6 of the section seven list of misreports, the core test that separates them is "has CRA ever assessed this money?" Sandra's foreign rental income is tax that was never declared and has not been found by CRA, so she should use the Voluntary Disclosures Program (VDP, meeting the Unprompted condition). Gregory's penalties and interest were already correctly assessed by CRA, but they are excusable because of circumstances beyond the taxpayer's control such as a serious car accident, so he should apply for taxpayer relief through RC4288.

  4. ✗ 错误。税务法院没有管辖权去减免或撤销被正确评定的罚款与利息，这项酌情权专属于 CRA 纳税人减免程序。Incorrect. The Tax Court has no jurisdiction to cancel or reduce correctly assessed penalties and interest, and that discretion belongs exclusively to CRA's taxpayer relief procedure.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

In May 2026, Kevin submits an application on Form RC4288 requesting: (1) the cancellation of a late-filing penalty assessed on his 2014 personal tax return, and (2) the cancellation of arrears interest that accumulated between 2018 and 2023 on an unpaid tax assessment originating from his 2012 tax year. How does the 10-year limitation rule apply to Kevin's requested relief under CRA guidelines (IC07-1R1)?

2026 年 5 月，Kevin 递交了 Form RC4288 申请，请求：(1) 减免其 2014 税年个人税表被征收的迟报罚款；(2) 减免其 2012 税年未缴税款在 2018 至 2023 年期间累积的滞纳利息。根据 CRA 指南（IC07-1R1），10 年法定申请时效如何适用于 Kevin 的减免请求？

  1. ✗ 错误。税法中的纳税人减免严格受 10 年时限约束，不存在所谓的 15 年伞形窗口。Incorrect. Taxpayer relief in the law is strictly limited by a 10-year time limit, and there is no such thing as a 15-year umbrella window.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据讲义第 2.7 节，纳税人减免（RC4288）对罚款与利息适用不同的十年锚点：罚款锚在“所属税年或申报期”，2026 年申请只能减免 2016 及以后税年的罚款，2014 税年罚款已过期；利息锚在“实际累积的日历年”，2026 年申请可审查 2016 年 1 月 1 日及以后累积的利息，因此即便原始欠税源于 2012 年，其在 2018 至 2023 年累积的利息均在 10 年窗口内，依法有资格获减免考虑。Correct. Under section 2.7 of the lecture, taxpayer relief (RC4288) uses different 10-year anchors for penalties and interest. Penalties are anchored to the "tax year or filing period they relate to", so an application in 2026 can relieve only penalties for the 2016 and later tax years, and the 2014 tax year penalty has expired. Interest is anchored to the "calendar year in which it actually accumulated", so an application in 2026 can review interest accumulated on or after January 1, 2016. So even if the original tax owing arose in 2012, the interest accumulated in 2018 to 2023 is inside the 10-year window and is by law eligible to be considered for relief.

  3. ✗ 错误。罚款受所属税年 10 年限制；而利息减免并不因原始欠税产生于 10 年前而被排除，只要利息是在近 10 年内滚存累加的即可申请。Incorrect. Penalties are subject to the 10-year limit by the tax year they relate to, while interest relief is not excluded just because the original tax owing arose more than 10 years ago, as long as the interest accumulated within the last 10 years.

  4. ✗ 错误。这忽略了利息减免锚在“累积年份”的特殊规则，误将利息与原始欠税年份进行绑定。Incorrect. This ignores the special rule that interest relief is anchored to the year of accumulation, and wrongly ties interest to the year of the original tax owing.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

第 7 题Q7税务法院非正式程序门槛与规则Tax Court informal procedure threshold and rules★★★陷阱：误认小额诉讼判决具判例效力

Nathan received a decision from the CRA Appeals Branch confirming an unfavorable reassessment involving $22,000 in disputed federal income tax and $2,500 in disputed penalties (totaling $24,500, excluding interest). Nathan files an appeal to the Tax Court of Canada (TCC) electing the Informal Procedure. Which of the following correctly describes the procedural rules and legal characteristics of Nathan's appeal under the Tax Court of Canada Act (s.18)?

Nathan 收到 CRA 上诉处的决定书，确认了一份涉及 $22,000 争议联邦所得税及 $2,500 争议罚款（合计 $24,500，不含利息）的不利重新评税。Nathan 向加拿大税务法院（TCC）提起上诉并选择走非正式程序（Informal Procedure）。根据《加拿大税务法院法》第 18 条，关于 Nathan 上诉的程序规则与法律特征，下列哪项表述是正确的？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是“一般程序（General Procedure）”的要求；非正式程序免申请费、不受严格证据规则约束，且允许非律师代理人出庭。Incorrect. These are the requirements of the "General Procedure". The informal procedure has no filing fee, is not bound by strict rules of evidence, and allows a non-lawyer representative to appear.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据《加拿大税务法院法》第 18 条及讲义第 2.9 节，非正式程序的法定门槛为争议税额加罚款不超过 $25,000（2024 至 2026 年未变且不随通胀指数化）。该程序免收法院申请费、不受严格证据规则约束、允许非律师代理人代理出庭；同时根据第 18.28 条，非正式程序的判决不得被援引作为约束其他案件的司法先例（no precedent value）。Correct. Under section 18 of the Tax Court of Canada Act and section 2.9 of the lecture, the statutory threshold for the informal procedure is that the tax in dispute plus penalties does not exceed $25,000 (unchanged from 2024 to 2026 and not indexed to inflation). The procedure has no court filing fee, is not bound by strict rules of evidence and allows a non-lawyer representative to appear. Also, under section 18.28, a judgment in the informal procedure may not be cited as binding judicial precedent in other cases.

  3. ✗ 错误。两万五门槛计算公式明确包含争议的联邦税与罚款（federal tax and penalties），仅不包含利息；$22,000 + $2,500 = $24,500，未超过 $25,000 门槛。Incorrect. The formula for the $25,000 threshold expressly includes the federal tax and penalties in dispute and excludes only interest. $22,000 + $2,500 = $24,500, which does not exceed the $25,000 threshold.

  4. ✗ 错误。落入“误认小额诉讼判决具判例效力”陷阱；《税务法院法》第 18.28 条明文规定非正式程序判决不具备正式判例拘束力。Incorrect. This falls into the trap of mistaking a small-claims judgment for a precedent. Section 18.28 of the Tax Court of Canada Act expressly provides that a judgment in the informal procedure does not have formal binding precedent.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Samantha received an unfavorable decision from the CRA Appeals Branch regarding a disallowed business expense deduction, involving $31,000 in disputed federal income tax. Samantha wishes to avoid expensive legal representation and formal evidentiary procedures. She asks her tax preparer whether she can access the Informal Procedure of the Tax Court of Canada. How should the preparer advise Samantha under the Tax Court of Canada Act?

Samantha 收到 CRA 上诉处的不利决定，其自雇业务费用扣除被剔除，涉及 $31,000 的争议联邦所得税。Samantha 希望避免高昂的律师代理费用与繁琐的正式证据规则，她询问报税员自己能否选择走加拿大税务法院的非正式程序。根据《加拿大税务法院法》，报税员应如何向 Samantha 提供建议？

  1. ✗ 错误。税法赋予了纳税人主动限额选择权，超过门槛并非绝对被排斥。Incorrect. The law gives the taxpayer a voluntary election to limit the amount, so exceeding the threshold does not mean absolute exclusion.

  2. ✗ 错误。税务法院没有所谓缴纳 10% 超额豁免费即可免除门槛限制的规定。Incorrect. The Tax Court has no rule under which paying a 10% excess waiver fee removes the threshold limit.

  3. ✗ 错误。是否走非正式程序取决于法定门槛或纳税人单方选择，无需司法部批准。Incorrect. Whether to use the informal procedure depends on the statutory threshold or the taxpayer's own election, and no approval of the Department of Justice is needed.

  4. ✓ 正确。根据《加拿大税务法院法》第 18 条及讲义第 2.9 节，争议金额超过 $25,000 门槛的纳税人，可以依法主动选择将争议金额上限限制在 $25,000 以内（放弃对超出部分的主张），从而换取走成本更低、流程更简便的非正式程序（Informal Procedure）。Correct. Under section 18 of the Tax Court of Canada Act and section 2.9 of the lecture, a taxpayer whose dispute exceeds the $25,000 threshold may by law elect to limit the amount in dispute to $25,000 or less (giving up the claim to the excess), in exchange for the lower-cost and simpler informal procedure.
