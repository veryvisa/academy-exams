第 12 章 · 老人与养老金分拆Chapter 12 · Seniors and Pension Splitting: The Three Age-65 Triggers, Optimal Pension Splitting, and OAS Clawback AvoidanceSeniors and pension income splitting

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 老年额递减计算题：2026 税年老年额上限 9,208 元、递减起点 46,432 元，超出部分按 15% 线性递减至归零。Age amount phase-out calculation: for the 2026 tax year the age amount maximum is $9,208, the phase-out starts at $46,432, and the excess is reduced at 15% in a straight line until it reaches zero.
  • 养老金收入额 65 岁合格分水岭判断题：重点考查 RRIF 提款在未满 65 岁（仅限配偶身故继承）与年满 65 岁后的资格转换，以及 CPP/OAS 永远不合格的法律边界。Judging the age 65 dividing line for the pension income amount: tests how RRIF withdrawals qualify before age 65 (only through inheritance on a spouse's death) and after age 65, and the legal boundary that CPP/OAS never qualify.
  • T1032 退休收入分割与边际税率及福利递减联动题：考查转满 50% 并非最优解的深层机制，重点测试转入方净收入增加对自身老年额削减与 OAS 回收税触发的逆向冲击。T1032 retirement income splitting linked to marginal rates and benefit reductions: tests the deeper mechanism that transferring the full 50% is not optimal, especially the reverse impact of the receiving spouse's higher net income on their own age amount reduction and on triggering the OAS recovery tax.
  • CPP 给付分享与 T1032 退休分割双轨辨析题：重点考查 Service Canada 与 CRA 两套独立行政体系、实际月给付划转与税表纸面分割、以及比例法定与自选比例的制度差异。Distinguishing CPP pension sharing from T1032 splitting: tests the two independent administrative systems of Service Canada and the CRA, the difference between actual monthly payment transfers and paper splitting on the tax return, and the difference between a statutory ratio and a freely chosen ratio.
  • 无障碍改造与长期照护组合申报题：2026 税年起 HATC 与医疗费互斥新规，以及护理院全额医疗费与「看护费限 10,000 元 ＋ 残障额」两条路的二选一。Combined claims for accessibility renovations and long-term care: tests the new rule from the 2026 tax year that HATC and medical expenses are mutually exclusive, and the either-or between claiming full nursing home fees as medical expenses and the 'attendant care limited to $10,000 + disability amount' path.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 老年额超额全扣陷阱Age amount fully lost above the threshold

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为净收入一旦超过 46,432 元的递减起点，老年额就立刻全额取消，忽略了超出部分只按 15% 线性递减，直到约 $107,819 才归零。Believing that once net income exceeds the $46,432 phase-out start the age amount is cancelled in full at once, ignoring that only the excess is reduced at 15% in a straight line and the amount reaches zero only at about $107,819.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出 65 岁以上纳税人净收入在 5 万至 8 万元之间，问可申报的老年额，选项里有直接写 0 的干扰项。The stem gives a taxpayer over 65 with net income between $50,000 and $80,000 and asks for the age amount claimable, with a distractor option that simply says 0.

⚠ 未满65岁RRIF抵免陷阱The RRIF credit under age 65

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为只要纳税人有 RRIF 提款就能无条件享受 $2,000 的养老金收入额抵免，忽略了未满 65 岁时 RRIF 提款除配偶身故接手外绝对不合格。Believing that any taxpayer with RRIF withdrawals unconditionally gets the $2,000 pension income amount credit, ignoring that before age 65 RRIF withdrawals never qualify except when taken over on a spouse's death.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干明确指出纳税人年龄在 60 至 64 岁之间，主要收入为自身 RRSP 转成的 RRIF 提款，询问 line 31400 抵免额。The stem states that the taxpayer is between 60 and 64, with income mainly from RRIF withdrawals from his or her own converted RRSP, and asks for the Line 31400 credit.

⚠ 退休收入分割无脑转满半数陷阱Blindly transferring half in income splitting

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为 T1032 分割总是转满 50% 最省税，忽略了个人净收入上升会反向削减受让方的老年额或推高受让方的 OAS 回收税，导致家庭总税负不降反升。Believing that a T1032 split of the full 50% always saves the most tax, ignoring that a rise in the receiving spouse's net income can reduce their age amount or raise their OAS recovery tax, so the family's total tax goes up instead of down.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述一方高收入另一方已有适度收入且领取 OAS，询问最优 T1032 申报策略及为什么不直接转让 50%。The stem describes one high-income spouse and another who already has moderate income and receives OAS, and asks for the best T1032 filing strategy and why not simply transfer 50%.

⚠ 办了T1032误当分了CPP陷阱Filing T1032 mistaken for splitting CPP

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为在报税表上勾选了 T1032 联合选择就等于把家庭的所有养老金（含 CPP）都平分了，忽略了 CPP 给付分享必须向 Service Canada 单独提交 ISP1002 申请。Believing that ticking the T1032 joint election on the return splits all of the family's pensions (CPP included) equally, ignoring that CPP sharing must be applied for separately with Service Canada on form ISP1002.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提及低收入配偶未缴纳过 CPP，双方已完成 T1032 申报但发现配偶仍未分得 CPP，询问如何实现 CPP 重新分配。The stem mentions that the low-income spouse never paid into CPP, both spouses completed T1032 but the spouse still did not receive any CPP, and asks how to redistribute CPP.

⚠ 无障碍改造跨年双报旧规陷阱The old rule of double-claiming accessibility work

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为 2026 税年仍能像往年一样将同一笔适老化无障碍改造支出同时申报联邦 HATC 和医疗费抵免（METC），忽略了 2026 年起两者已被立法强制改为二选一互斥。Believing that for the 2026 tax year the same accessibility renovation expense can still be claimed under both the federal HATC and the medical expense credit (METC) as in prior years, ignoring that from 2026 the two are legislated to be mutually exclusive.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出 2026 年发生的轮椅坡道或无障碍卫浴改造支出，询问能否同时在 line 31285 与 line 33099 申报抵免。The stem gives wheelchair ramp or accessible bathroom renovation costs incurred in 2026 and asks whether the credits can be claimed at the same time on Line 31285 and Line 33099.

第 1 题Q1老年额递减阶梯与门槛精算Age amount phase-out tiers and threshold calculation★★陷阱：老年额超额全扣陷阱

In the 2026 tax year, a 67-year-old Canadian resident reports net income (line 23600) of $56,432. The federal age amount maximum is $9,208, reduced by 15% of net income above the $46,432 threshold. What federal age amount can the taxpayer claim on line 30100?

2026 税年，一名 67 岁的加拿大居民净收入（line 23600）为 $56,432。联邦老年额上限 $9,208，净收入超过 $46,432 的部分按 15% 递减。该纳税人在 line 30100 可申报多少联邦老年额？

  1. ✗ 错误。掉入「超门槛就全扣」陷阱。老年额只按超出部分的 15% 递减，净收入约 $107,819 时才归零。Incorrect. This falls into the trap of everything being lost once over the threshold. The age amount is reduced only by 15% of the excess; it reaches zero only at net income of about $107,819.

  2. ✗ 错误。忽略了收入递减规则。纳税人净收入超出 $46,432 的部分必须按 15% 扣减老年额，不能直接申报全额。Incorrect. This ignores the income phase-out rule. Net income above $46,432 must reduce the age amount at 15%, so the full amount cannot be claimed directly.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据 2026 税年老年额（age amount, line 30100）计算公式，纳税人年满 65 岁，净收入超出起点部分为 $56,432 - $46,432 = $10,000；递减金额为 $10,000 × 15% = $1,500；因此实际可申报的老年额为 $9,208 - $1,500 = $7,708。Correct. Under the 2026 tax year formula for the age amount (Line 30100), the taxpayer is 65 or over; the net income above the start is $56,432 - $46,432 = $10,000; the reduction is $10,000 x 15% = $1,500; so the age amount actually claimable is $9,208 - $1,500 = $7,708.

  4. ✗ 错误。$1,500 是要从上限里扣掉的递减额，不是可申报的老年额；可申报额是 $9,208 − $1,500。Incorrect. $1,500 is the reduction to be deducted from the maximum, not the age amount that can be claimed; the claimable amount is $9,208 - $1,500.

In the 2026 tax year, a 71-year-old taxpayer has net income of $62,432 on line 23600. The federal age amount maximum is $9,208, reduced by 15% of net income above the $46,432 threshold. What federal age amount can the taxpayer claim on line 30100?

2026 税年，一名 71 岁的纳税人 T1 税表 line 23600 净收入为 $62,432。联邦老年额上限 $9,208，净收入超过 $46,432 的部分按 15% 递减。该纳税人在 line 30100 可申报的老年额是多少？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据 2026 税年老年额（age amount）规则，净收入超出门槛金额为 $62,432 - $46,432 = $16,000；递减额为 $16,000 × 15% = $2,400；可申报金额为 $9,208 - $2,400 = $6,808。Correct. Under the 2026 age amount rules, net income above the threshold is $62,432 - $46,432 = $16,000; the reduction is $16,000 x 15% = $2,400; the claimable amount is $9,208 - $2,400 = $6,808.

  2. ✗ 错误。掉入了全额申报陷阱。超过 $46,432 门槛的净收入必须按 15% 递减老年额。Incorrect. This falls into the trap of claiming in full. Net income above the $46,432 threshold must reduce the age amount at 15%.

  3. ✗ 错误。掉入了老年额超额全扣陷阱。净收入未达到归零门槛（约 $107,819），仍可申报部分抵免。Incorrect. This falls into the trap of losing everything over the threshold. Net income has not reached the zero point (about $107,819), so a partial credit can still be claimed.

  4. ✗ 错误。$2,400 是递减额本身，要从 $9,208 里扣掉，可申报额是 $9,208 − $2,400 = $6,808。Incorrect. $2,400 is the reduction itself, to be deducted from $9,208; the claimable amount is $9,208 - $2,400 = $6,808.

第 2 题Q2养老金收入额65岁合格界限Age 65 limit for the pension income amount★★陷阱：未满65岁RRIF抵免陷阱

In 2026, a 62-year-old taxpayer received $12,000 in life annuity payments from a former employer's registered pension plan (RPP) and withdrew $5,000 from a personal registered retirement income fund (RRIF) converted from their own savings. What is the maximum federal pension income amount the taxpayer can claim on line 31400?

2026 年，一名 62 岁的纳税人从前雇主的注册养老金计划（RPP）中领取了 $12,000 的人寿年金定期给付，并从自身积蓄转换的个人注册退休收入基金（RRIF）中提取了 $5,000。该纳税人在 line 31400 最多可申报多少联邦养老金收入额？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据养老金收入额（pension income amount, line 31400）规则，纳税人未满 65 岁时，合格养老金收入（eligible pension income）仅限于雇主 RPP 的终身年金定期给付（以及因配偶身故继承的养老金）；纳税人本人的 RRIF 提款在未满 65 岁时严格不合格。由于其合格 RPP 收入为 $12,000，已超过法定 $2,000 上限，因此可申报满额 $2,000。Correct. Under the pension income amount (Line 31400) rules, when the taxpayer is under 65, eligible pension income is limited to periodic life annuity payments from an employer RPP (and pensions inherited on a spouse's death); the taxpayer's own RRIF withdrawals are strictly not eligible before 65. Since the qualifying RPP income is $12,000, which exceeds the statutory $2,000 cap, the full $2,000 can be claimed.

  2. ✗ 错误。纳税人未满 65 岁并非绝对不能申报，只要收到了雇主 RPP 的定期年金给付，依然可以申报 line 31400。Incorrect. A taxpayer under 65 is not absolutely barred; as long as periodic annuity payments from an employer RPP are received, Line 31400 can still be claimed.

  3. ✗ 错误。掉入了未满65岁RRIF抵免陷阱。未满 65 岁时自身的 RRIF 提款绝不属于合格养老金收入。Incorrect. This falls into the trap of the RRIF credit under 65. Before 65, one's own RRIF withdrawals are never qualifying pension income.

  4. ✗ 错误。养老金收入额的法定上限长青不变为 $2,000，且未满 65 岁的 RRIF 根本不合格，绝无累加额度的可能。Incorrect. The statutory cap of the pension income amount is a fixed $2,000, and RRIF income under 65 does not qualify at all, so the amounts cannot be added up.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A 61-year-old retiree withdrew $8,000 from a personal RRIF (not inherited) and received $6,000 in Canada Pension Plan (CPP) retirement benefits during 2026, with no employer pension income. What is the allowable federal pension income amount on line 31400?

  2. ✓ 正确。全额报护理院费会排除残障额；只把看护部分在 10,000 元（去世当年 20,000 元）以内报医疗费，就能同时申报残障额（2026 税年 10,341 元），本人用不完的可转给支持他的家人（line 31800）。至于哪条路更省税，要按实数比较后再定。Correct. Claiming full nursing home fees excludes the disability amount; claiming as medical expenses only the attendant care part within $10,000 ($20,000 in the year of death) allows the disability amount ($10,341 for the 2026 tax year) to be claimed at the same time, and whatever the person cannot use can be transferred to a supporting family member (Line 31800). Which path saves more tax must be decided by comparing the actual figures.

  3. ✗ 错误。全额申报护理院医疗费的排斥效应是绝对的，不仅本人不能报残障额，任何家庭成员也均不得就同一人申报转移残障额。Incorrect. The exclusion effect of claiming full nursing home medical expenses is absolute: not only the person but no family member can claim a transfer of the disability amount for the same person.

  4. ✗ 错误。护理院日常照护费用不属于家居无障碍改造支出，无法申报 HATC。Incorrect. Routine care costs in a nursing home are not home accessibility expenses and cannot be claimed for HATC.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A taxpayer fully supports a frail parent residing in a licensed nursing home. The parent possesses a valid CRA-approved Form T2201. For 2026, the total nursing home bill was $42,000 with no breakdown for attendant care. The taxpayer claims the entire $42,000 as medical expenses for dependants on line 33199. Can the taxpayer also claim a transfer of the parent's unused disability amount on line 31800?

一名纳税人全额资助其住在持牌养老护理院的虚弱母亲。母亲持有有效的 CRA 核准表格 T2201。2026 年，护理院账单总额为 $42,000，且未提供看护费拆分明细。该纳税人在 line 33199 将全部 $42,000 作为受抚养人医疗费申报。该纳税人能否在 line 31800 同时申报母亲未用完的残障额转移？

  1. ✗ 错误。互斥规则对纳税人本人和任何代为申报的家属一视同仁，并无受抚养人家属豁免。Incorrect. The mutual exclusion rule treats the taxpayer and any family member claiming on their behalf equally; there is no exemption for supporting dependants.

  2. ✗ 错误。无论母亲收入多少，全额申报护理院医疗费用都会在法律上彻底排斥残障额及其转移。Incorrect. Whatever the mother's income, claiming full nursing home medical expenses completely excludes the disability amount and its transfer by law.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据《所得税法》第 118.2 条与第 118.3 条，一旦将护理院全额账单（full nursing home fees）作为医疗费申报，本人及任何所有人（包括出资资助的子女）在当年都绝对不能再为该人申报残障额（disability amount）或残障额转移（line 31800）。Correct. Under sections 118.2 and 118.3 of the Income Tax Act, once the full nursing home bill (full nursing home fees) is claimed as medical expenses, neither the person nor anyone else (including children who provide funding) can claim the disability amount or the transfer of the disability amount (Line 31800) for that person for the year.

  4. ✗ 错误。此场景属于法律完全互斥，不存在 $5,000 限额折中转让的可能。Incorrect. This scenario is one of complete legal exclusion; there is no compromise of a $5,000 transfer cap.
