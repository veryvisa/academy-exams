① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 当期费用与资本支出辨析题：考查房屋修缮维护支出的定性，重点测试区分单纯恢复原状的当期费用还是带来实质改良或延长寿命的资本支出，以及大额装修分年计提折旧的要求。Current expense versus capital expenditure: tests how to characterize spending on repairs and maintenance of the property. The key skill is telling a current expense that merely restores the original condition apart from a capital expenditure that brings a substantial improvement or extends the life of the property, and knowing that a large renovation must be depreciated over several years.
  • 折旧造亏限制与净收入封顶计算题：考查个人出租物业 CCA 上限规则，重点测试在合并本人全部出租物业净收入后，CCA 绝不能制造或扩大亏损的硬性红线，以及未用额度留存 UCC 的结转逻辑。The CCA limit and the net-income cap: tests the CCA limit for an individual's rental properties. The key skill is the hard rule that, after combining the net income of all of the taxpayer's rental properties, CCA can never create or increase a loss, and the logic that the unclaimed amount stays in UCC.
  • 房屋处置折旧收回与资本利得双轨计算题：考查出租物业出售时的两笔账，重点测试处置所得与年初 UCC 比较产生的 100% 计入收入的折旧收回（或终止亏损），以及超出原成本部分按现行 1/2 纳入率计入的资本利得。Recapture and capital gain on the sale of a rental property: tests the two separate calculations when a rental property is sold. The key skill is comparing proceeds of disposition with the opening UCC, where the excess is recaptured CCA included in income at 100% (or a terminal loss), and the part above original cost is a capital gain included at the current 1/2 inclusion rate.
  • 短租不合规费用剔除与持有期翻炒定性题：考查连续 90 天短租门槛及 2026 年无宽限期的按比例剔除规则，以及持有不足 365 连续天出租住宅处置利得视同经营收入的翻炒规则。Disallowed expenses for non-compliant short-term rentals and the holding-period flipping rule: tests the 90 consecutive day short-term rental threshold and the 2026 proportional denial rule with no grace period, and the flipping rule under which the gain on a rental residence held fewer than 365 consecutive days is deemed business income.
  • BC 投机空置税与联邦所得税双轨对比题：考查 2026 年公民 1% 与外国业主 3% 的最新税率、6 个月自然年出租豁免条件，以及省级豁免绝不替代联邦 T776 申报义务的并行机制。BC speculation and vacancy tax versus federal income tax: tests the latest 2026 rates of 1% for citizens and 3% for foreign owners, the exemption for renting for 6 months in the calendar year, and the parallel regime in which a provincial exemption never replaces the federal T776 filing duty.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 误用 CCA 制造或扩大出租亏损抵扣其他收入Using CCA to create or increase a rental loss to offset other income

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 混淆营业所得与财产所得的折旧规则，误以为只要资产有可提折旧空间就可以做成账面亏损去冲减工资或自雇收入，忽略了个人出租物业折旧受申报前净收入封顶的刚性限制。Confusing the depreciation rules for business income with those for property income, and assuming that as long as the asset has depreciable room you can book a paper loss against salary or self-employment income. This overlooks the rigid limit that an individual's rental CCA is capped at net income before the claim.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出净出租收入低于可用 CCA 最大额度，问当年最多能申报多少 CCA，或者询问总收入计算。The question gives net rental income lower than the maximum CCA available and asks for the most CCA that can be claimed for the year, or asks how total income is computed.

⚠ 将卖房收益全额按资本利得五成纳入率纳税Taxing the whole gain on a sale at the 50% capital gain inclusion rate

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为卖房赚的钱全部享受资本利得 1/2 纳入率优惠，忽略了过去计提过的折旧在卖房时会触发折旧收回 (Recaptured CCA)，必须按 100% 全额计入应税收入。Assuming all the profit on selling a property gets the 1/2 capital gain inclusion rate. This overlooks that CCA previously claimed triggers recaptured CCA on the sale, which must be included in taxable income at 100%.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出房屋原始资本成本、年初 UCC 以及高于原成本的售价，询问处置当年应计入收入的税法总额或各项明细。The question gives the original capital cost of the building, the opening UCC and a sale price above original cost, and asks for the total or the components included in income for the year of disposition.

⚠ 将短租法定门槛误记为 30 天或以为 2026 仍有宽限期Remembering the short-term rental threshold as 30 days or assuming a 2026 grace period

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 受日常习惯影响将短租误记为不足 30 天，或误以为 2024 年底补办登记即算全年合规的宽限期仍然有效，忽略了 ITA s.67.7 法定门槛为不足 90 连续天且 2026 年没有任何宽限。Out of everyday habit, remembering a short-term rental as under 30 days, or assuming the 2024 grace period, under which registering by the end of 2024 counted as compliant for the whole year, still applies. This overlooks that the ITA s.67.7 threshold is fewer than 90 consecutive days and that 2026 has no grace at all.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现租约天数（如 45 天或 60 天）、当地政府短租登记牌照缺失，问相关费用能否抵扣或剔除比例。The question gives the number of days of a tenancy (such as 45 or 60 days), a missing local government short-term rental registration or licence, and asks whether the expenses are deductible or what proportion is denied.

⚠ 将共有产权与合伙企业的折旧申报规则混淆Confusing the CCA reporting rules for co-ownership and for a partnership

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为多人合资购房出租必须步调一致申报折旧，忽略了共有产权 (Co-ownership) 下各共有人对各自份额独立记账、可自主选择是否提折旧，只有合伙企业才强制由合伙统一申报。Assuming people who jointly buy a property to rent out must report depreciation in lockstep. This overlooks that under co-ownership each co-owner keeps separate books on his or her own share and may decide whether to claim CCA, while only a partnership forces a single claim at the partnership level.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现两位共有人（非合伙人）持有出租房，一人处于高税阶想提折旧，另一人处于零税阶不想提折旧。The question has two co-owners (not partners) holding a rental home, one in a high tax bracket who wants to claim CCA and the other in a zero bracket who does not.

⚠ 误以为获得 BC 投机空置税出租豁免就无需报联邦税Assuming a BC speculation and vacancy tax rental exemption removes the federal filing

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 将省级空置税的豁免决定当成个人所得税的免税凭证，误以为只要拿到省府出租豁免确认书，这套房的租金收入就不用向 CRA 申报。Treating the provincial vacancy tax exemption decision as proof of exemption from personal income tax, and assuming that once the provincial rental exemption confirmation is obtained, the rent on the home need not be reported to CRA.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提到业主在 BC 省出租满 6 个月成功取得 SVT 出租豁免，问其联邦个人所得税表 T1 或 T776 的申报要求。The question says an owner in BC rented for 6 months and obtained the SVT rental exemption, and asks about the federal T1 or T776 filing requirement.

第 1 题Q1出租折旧造亏限制Limit on using CCA to create a rental loss★★陷阱：误用 CCA 制造或扩大出租亏损抵扣其他收入

In the 2026 tax year, Marcus owns a residential rental property. After deducting all eligible current operating expenses (property taxes, mortgage interest, insurance, and utilities), his net rental income before claiming Capital Cost Allowance (CCA) is $3,500. Marcus's maximum allowable CCA based on the building's UCC and class rate is $9,200. Marcus also earned $75,000 in employment salary. What is the maximum CCA Marcus can claim on Form T776 for 2026, and what happens to the unclaimed portion?

在 2026 税年，Marcus 拥有一处住宅出租物业。在扣除所有合规的当期运营费用（地税、房贷利息、保险和水电）后，他在计提折旧 (CCA) 前的净出租收入为 $3,500。根据建筑物的 UCC 和类别税率计算，Marcus 的最大可用 CCA 为 $9,200。Marcus 当年还取得了 $75,000 的受雇工资收入。Marcus 在 2026 年 T776 表上最多可申报多少 CCA？未申报的部分如何处理？

  1. ✗ 错误。掉入了典型的“用折旧制造亏损避税”陷阱。税法硬性规定，个人出租物业的 CCA 不能用来制造或扩大出租亏损（cannot create or increase a rental loss），严禁利用出租折旧冲减工资收入。Incorrect. This falls into the classic "using depreciation to create a loss" trap. The law firmly provides that an individual's rental CCA cannot create or increase a rental loss, and rental depreciation must not be used to reduce salary income.

  2. ✗ 错误。税法并没有剥夺纳税人扣除部分折旧的权利。纳税人可以在净出租收入允许的额度内申报，将净出租收入正好降为零。Incorrect. The law does not take away the right to deduct part of the depreciation. The taxpayer may claim within the amount that net rental income allows, bringing net rental income exactly to zero.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据个人出租物业折旧限制规则，当年可申报的资本成本折旧额 (Capital Cost Allowance, CCA) 以扣除其他费用后的净出租收入为上限（本案中为 $3,500），以防止制造出账面亏损去抵减其他收入。未申报的差额（$9,200 − $3,500 = $5,700）不会消失，而是继续保留在未折旧资本成本 (Undepreciated Capital Cost, UCC) 余额中，供未来年度重新计算计提。Correct. Under the rule limiting CCA on rental property, the capital cost allowance (CCA) claimable for the year is capped at net rental income after other expenses ($3,500 here), to prevent a paper loss from being created to offset other income. The unclaimed difference ($9,200 - $3,500 = $5,700) does not disappear but stays in the undepreciated capital cost (UCC) balance for recalculation in future years.

  4. ✗ 错误。未申报的 $5,700 并不构成独立的非资本亏损 (non-capital loss) 结转额度，而是体现在资产类别的 UCC 基础中，未来年度仍需按当年 UCC 余额与收入限制重新计算。Incorrect. The unclaimed $5,700 does not become a separate non-capital loss carryforward. It is reflected in the UCC base of the asset class and must be recalculated in future years against the then UCC balance and the income limit.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

In the 2026 tax year, Diana owns two separate residential rental condominiums. Property 1 generated net rental income before CCA of $8,000. Property 2 incurred an operating rental loss before CCA of $3,000. Across both properties, the total maximum calculated CCA allowable under the class rates is $11,000. Diana has $90,000 in professional employment income. What is the maximum aggregate CCA deduction Diana can claim across both properties on Form T776 for 2026?

在 2026 税年，Diana 拥有两处独立的住宅出租公寓。物业 1 在计提折旧前产生净租金收入 $8,000；物业 2 在计提折旧前发生运营租金亏损 $3,000。按资产类别税率计算，两处物业的理论最大可用 CCA 合计为 $11,000。Diana 当年另有 $90,000 的专业受雇收入。Diana 在 2026 年 T776 表上最多可就两处物业合计申报多少 CCA 折旧？

  1. ✗ 错误。误以为可以按单一盈利物业单独算上限。税法规定必须合并计算本人适用的全部出租物业损益，物业 2 的 $3,000 亏损必须与物业 1 的 $8,000 收益合并抵消，合计净出租收入仅为 $5,000。Incorrect. This wrongly assumes the limit can be worked out separately for a single profitable property. The law requires combining the results of all the taxpayer's rental properties: Property 2's $3,000 loss must be netted against Property 1's $8,000 income, so combined net rental income is only $5,000.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据出租物业 CCA 限制规则，纳税人必须合并计算名下所有出租物业扣除其他费用后的净损益。物业 1 的净收入 $8,000 扣除物业 2 的净亏损 $3,000 后，合计净出租收入为 $5,000。因此，Diana 合计最多只能申报 $5,000 的折旧扣除额 (CCA)，将整体净出租收入降为零，不得利用折旧造亏抵扣其 $90,000 工资。Correct. Under the CCA limit rule for rental property, the taxpayer must combine the net results, after other expenses, of all rental properties owned. Property 1's net income of $8,000 less Property 2's net loss of $3,000 gives combined net rental income of $5,000. So Diana can claim at most $5,000 of CCA in total, bringing overall net rental income to zero, and may not use depreciation to create a loss against her $90,000 salary.

  3. ✗ 错误。这是典型的违规造亏申报，违反了 CCA 不能制造或扩大出租亏损的核心法定红线。Incorrect. This is a typical non-compliant loss-creating claim, in breach of the core statutory red line that CCA cannot create or increase a rental loss.

  4. ✗ 错误。拥有一处亏损物业不会剥夺所有物业提折旧的权利，只要合并后的合计出租净收入大于零，即可在合计净收入范围内申报 CCA。Incorrect. Owning one loss-making property does not remove the right to claim depreciation on all of them. As long as combined net rental income is positive, CCA can be claimed up to the combined net income.

答案Answer：b

第 2 题Q2当期费用与资本支出辨析Current expense versus capital expenditure★★陷阱：把资本改良当维修全额扣除

In 2026, landlords Kevin and Rachel undertook renovations on their rental townhouse in Vancouver. They spent $18,000 completely replacing the 25-year-old asphalt shingle roof with an upgraded standing-seam architectural metal roof that substantially improves durability and carries a 50-year warranty. During the same renovation, they spent $1,200 patching drywall cracks and repainting the interior living room walls to repair normal tenant wear and tear. The net income after depreciation is then allocated to the partners according to the partnership agreement, and an individual partner cannot independently claim depreciation on his or her personal return.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是共有产权 (co-ownership) 的处理方式，不能套用到合伙企业上。合伙财产的 UCC 属于合伙整体，不在个人层面上独立分割记账。Incorrect. This is the treatment for co-ownership and cannot be applied to a partnership. The UCC of partnership property belongs to the partnership as a whole and is not divided and recorded separately at the personal level.

  3. ✗ 错误。合伙企业完全有权对其持有的合资格资本财产计提 CCA 折旧，只是折旧决定必须在合伙层面统一做出。Incorrect. A partnership is fully entitled to claim CCA on the qualifying capital property it holds; the depreciation decision just has to be made uniformly at the partnership level.

  4. ✗ 错误。税法中没有按个人最高边际税率决定合伙报税的规定，合伙报税决策由合伙协议及合伙整体管理机制决定。Incorrect. The law has no rule that the partner with the highest personal marginal rate decides the partnership's filing. The partnership's reporting decisions are governed by the partnership agreement and the management mechanism of the partnership as a whole.

第 6 题Q6出租物业持有未满一年翻炒Flipping a rental property held under one year★★陷阱：忘记住宅翻炒规则也管出租物业

In March 2026, Samantha purchased a residential rental condominium in Victoria as an investment. She immediately placed it on the long-term rental market and leased it to an arm's length tenant. In November 2026, after holding the property for 240 continuous days, Samantha received an unsolicited high offer from an eager buyer and sold the condo at a net profit of $45,000. Samantha did not experience any of the nine statutory life event exceptions. How is the $45,000 profit treated under the federal residential property flipping rule (ITA s.12(12)–(14))?

2026 年 3 月，Samantha 在维多利亚购入了一套住宅出租公寓作为投资。她立即将其投入长期租赁市场，租给了一名非关联租客。2026 年 11 月，在持有该房产连续满 240 天后，Samantha 收到了一位买家主动提出的一份高价要约，并以 $45,000 的净利润将该公寓售出。Samantha 并未遭遇九大免责法定生活事件中的任何一项。根据联邦住宅翻炒规则（ITA s.12(12)–(14)），这笔 $45,000 的利润应如何处理？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据《所得税法》第 12(12)–(14) 条的住宅物业翻炒规则 (flipped property rule)，持有不足 365 连续天的住宅处置利得视同经营收入 (business income)，100% 全额计税，不能享受资本利得 1/2（50%）的纳入率。极其重要的考点是：翻炒规则不仅适用于自住房，纯出租用途的住宅物业同样受其约束；由于 Samantha 持有仅 240 天且无生活事件例外，利润全额视同经营收入。Correct. Under the flipped property rule in ITA s.12(12)-(14), the gain on a residential property held fewer than 365 consecutive days is deemed business income, taxed in full at 100%, and does not get the 1/2 (50%) capital gain inclusion rate. A key exam point: the flipping rule applies not only to a home lived in but equally to a residential property used purely as a rental. Samantha held the condo only 240 days with no life-event exception, so the whole profit is deemed business income.

  2. ✗ 错误。掉入了“翻炒规则只管自住房”的典型认知陷阱。税法明确将住宅性质的出租物业同样纳入翻炒规则约束范围。Incorrect. This falls into the classic misconception that the flipping rule governs only a home lived in. The law expressly brings rental properties of a residential nature within the flipping rule.

  3. ✗ 错误。真实出租并不能豁免联邦 365 天翻炒规则，只有税法列明的九大生活事件（如死亡、残疾、工作调动等）才能豁免。Incorrect. Actually renting the home does not exempt it from the federal 365-day flipping rule. Only the nine life events listed in the law (such as death, disability or a job transfer) provide an exemption.

  4. ✗ 错误。翻炒规则没有减半或对半分摊的规定，一旦触发，整笔收益全额视同经营收入。Incorrect. The flipping rule has no halving or even split. Once triggered, the whole gain is deemed business income.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

In January 2026, Lucas bought a residential rental property in Surrey. Due to a sudden market downturn, he sold the property in August 2026 after owning it for 210 continuous days, realizing a net loss of $30,000. Lucas did not experience any of the nine statutory life event exceptions. How does ITA section 12(12)–(14) treat this $30,000 loss on Lucas's 2026 tax return?

2026 年 1 月，Lucas 在素里买入了一处住宅出租物业。由于市场突发下行，他在 2026 年 8 月（持有连续 210 天后）将该物业出售，录得净亏损 $30,000。Lucas 并未经历九大免责法定生活事件。根据 ITA 第 12(12)–(14) 条，这笔 $30,000 的亏损在 Lucas 2026 年税表上应如何处理？

  1. ✗ 错误。误以为利得被定性为经营收入则亏损也可作为经营亏损扣除。翻炒规则设置了单边惩罚机制：利得视同经营收入全额纳税，但亏损严禁扣除。Incorrect. This wrongly assumes that if the gain is characterized as business income, the loss can be deducted as a business loss. The flipping rule sets a one-sided penalty: a gain is deemed business income and taxed in full, but a loss is not deductible.

  2. ✗ 错误。ABIL 仅适用于合资格小企业股份或债务损失，住宅翻炒绝不符合 ABIL 资格。Incorrect. An ABIL applies only to losses on qualifying small business shares or debts, and a residential flip never qualifies for an ABIL.

  3. ✗ 错误。翻炒物业亏损不被允许作为常规资本亏损处理，税法明确规定其亏损在法律上单边归零。Incorrect. A loss on a flipped property is not allowed to be treated as an ordinary capital loss; the law expressly reduces it unilaterally to nil.

  4. ✓ 正确。根据住宅物业翻炒规则 (flipped property rule)，持有不足 365 连续天处置住宅且无生活事件豁免的，税法单边规定处置亏损视同为零（deemed to be nil）。Lucas 的 $30,000 亏损既不能抵扣其他收入，也不能作为资本亏损结转，彻底归零不可扣除。Correct. Under the flipped property rule, when a residence held fewer than 365 consecutive days is disposed of with no life-event exemption, the law unilaterally deems the loss on disposition to be nil. Lucas's $30,000 loss can neither be deducted against other income nor carried forward as a capital loss; it is reduced entirely to nothing and is not deductible.

第 7 题Q7BC投机空置税与联邦双轨BC speculation and vacancy tax alongside federal tax★陷阱：误以为获得 BC 投机空置税出租豁免就无需报联邦税

Victor, a Canadian citizen residing in Vancouver, owns a secondary residential condominium in Richmond. In the 2026 calendar year, he leased the condo to an arm's length tenant under a formal written residential tenancy agreement for 8 continuous months, collecting $18,000 in gross rental income. Victor applied for and successfully obtained the provincial tenancy exemption from the BC Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT). What are Victor's tax reporting obligations for the 2026 tax year?

Victor 是一名居住在温哥华的加拿大公民，他在列治文拥有第二套住宅公寓。在 2026 自然年内，他依据正式书面租约将该公寓出租给一名非关联租客长达连续 8 个月，收取了 $18,000 的总租金收入。Victor 申请并成功获得了 BC 省投机与空置税 (SVT) 的省级出租豁免。Victor 在 2026 税年还有哪些报税义务？

  1. ✗ 错误。掉入了将省级空置税豁免与联邦所得税混同的严重误区。省级财产税的免税不代表联邦所得免税。Incorrect. This falls into the serious misconception of confusing a provincial vacancy tax exemption with federal income tax. A provincial property tax exemption does not mean an exemption from federal income tax.

  2. ✓ 正确。BC 省投机与空置税 (Speculation and Vacancy Tax, SVT) 是省级财产税，与联邦个人所得税是完全独立并行的两套制度。Victor 满足了自然年出租满 6 个月的条件，成功豁免了 2026 年针对加拿大公民的 1% SVT；但只要取得了租金收入，联邦层面的 T776 出租收入申报义务（总收入 Line 12599、净收入 Line 12600）完全不受影响，必须如实申报。Correct. The BC speculation and vacancy tax (SVT) is a provincial property tax, a completely separate and parallel regime from federal personal income tax. Victor met the condition of renting for 6 months in the calendar year and so is exempt from the 1% SVT that applies to Canadian citizens for 2026. But as long as he received rental income, the federal T776 rental income filing duty (gross income Line 12599, net income Line 12600) is not affected at all and must be reported honestly.

  3. ✗ 错误。Victor 是加拿大公民，且列治文完全适用 SVT 出租豁免；同时联邦申报绝不能免除。Incorrect. Victor is a Canadian citizen, and the SVT rental exemption fully applies in Richmond. Also, the federal filing can never be waived.

  4. ✗ 错误。税法不存在以缴纳省级财产税来替代联邦所得税申报的选举权利，两者法律依据不同，必须依法独立履行。Incorrect. The law gives no right to elect to pay a provincial property tax in place of the federal income tax filing. The two have different legal bases and must each be met independently.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A foreign national investor who is not a Canadian resident owns a vacant residential condominium in Kelowna. Throughout the entire 2026 calendar year, the property was left completely unoccupied with zero rental activity. What are the owner's BC Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) and federal Canadian income tax obligations for the 2026 tax year?

一位非加拿大税务居民的外国投资者在基隆拿拥有一套住宅公寓。在整个 2026 自然年内，该物业完全空置，没有任何出租活动。该业主在 2026 税年的 BC 省投机与空置税 (SVT) 及联邦加拿大个人所得税申报义务是什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。外国业主在 2026 年适用上调后的 3% SVT 税率，而非 1% 居民税率；且未发生任何出租活动无需申报 T776。Incorrect. A foreign owner is subject to the raised 3% SVT rate in 2026, not the 1% resident rate. And since no rental activity occurred, no T776 needs to be filed.

  2. ✗ 错误。2% 是 2025 年的旧税率，2026 年外国业主税率已升至 3%；且联邦税法对完全空置房屋不征收视同租金收入。Incorrect. 2% is the old 2025 rate. For 2026 the foreign owner rate has risen to 3%. And federal tax law does not impose deemed rental income on a fully vacant home.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据 2026 税年现行规则，BC 省投机与空置税 (SVT) 对外国业主的税率上调至 3%（2025 年为 2%），由于房屋全年空置且无豁免，必须全额缴纳 3% 的 SVT；在联邦所得税层面，由于没有产生任何租金收入，纳税人无需填报 Form T776。Correct. Under the current rules for the 2026 tax year, the BC speculation and vacancy tax (SVT) rate for foreign owners is raised to 3% (it was 2% in 2025). Because the home was vacant all year with no exemption, the full 3% SVT must be paid. At the federal income tax level, no rental income arose, so the taxpayer does not need to file Form T776.

  4. ✗ 错误。空置住宅公寓不符合旅游住宿豁免，且不存在所谓的联邦非居民空置附加税替代机制。Incorrect. A vacant residential condominium does not qualify for the tourist accommodation exemption, and there is no such thing as a federal non-resident vacancy surtax that replaces it.
