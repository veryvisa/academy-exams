本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 申报义务判断题：给出纳税人具体经济行为（无欠税、处置资本财产、自雇净利润超额或申领家庭福利），测试是否准确掌握法定强制申报门槛与主动申报价值。Filing obligation questions: Present specific taxpayer economic situations (e.g., zero tax payable, disposition of capital property, self-employment net earnings exceeding threshold, or claiming family benefits) to test mastery of mandatory statutory filing thresholds versus voluntary filing benefits.
  • 申报与付款双轨截止日题：针对自雇人士及其配偶的延期情形，重点测试 6 月 15 日申报截止日与 4 月 30 日欠税付款截止日的区分，以及逾期计息起算点。Dual-track filing and payment deadline questions: For self-employed individuals and their spouses with extended deadlines, test the distinction between the June 15 filing due date and the April 30 balance owing payment deadline, as well as the starting date for prescribed interest.
  • 迟报罚款计算题：测试首次迟报罚款（5% + 1% × 月数，封顶 17%）的计算公式，核心考查计算基数必须是截止日未缴欠税余额而非应纳税总额。Late-filing penalty calculation questions: Test the first-time late-filing penalty formula (5% plus 1% per complete month, capped at 17%), specifically testing that the calculation base is the unpaid tax balance owing at the filing due date rather than total tax payable.
  • 评税异议期限取较晚规则题：考查个人纳税人对评税通知（NOA）不服时，提出异议的最晚法定截止日（申报截止日后 1 年与评税通知后 90 天两者取较晚）。Notice of Objection deadline 'later-of' rule questions: Test the statutory deadline for individual taxpayers disputing a Notice of Assessment (NOA) to file an objection, which is the later of one year after the filing due date or 90 days after the date of the Notice of Assessment.
  • 申报表历史结构演变与现行税阶题：测试 2021 年起省税并入第六步形成的六步申报结构，以及 2026 税年联邦最低税阶 14%（门槛 58,523 元）的现行规则。Historical return structure evolution and current tax bracket questions: Test the six-step return structure formed by integrating provincial tax into Step 6 starting in 2021, and the current 2026 tax-year rules for the lowest federal tax bracket of 14% (threshold of $58,523).

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 没欠税即免报陷阱Zero-tax-means-no-filing trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 纳税人直觉以为只有欠税才需要和税局打交道，忽视了处置资本财产、自雇 CPP 供款或申领家庭福利等法定强制与前置申报要求。Taxpayers intuitively assume they only need to interact with the CRA when tax is owed, overlooking mandatory statutory triggers or prerequisite filing conditions such as capital property dispositions, self-employment CPP contributions, or claiming family benefits.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调纳税人全年计算出的应纳税额为零（tax payable is nil），但同时提及出售资产、自雇利润超额或家有受抚养人领福利。The stem highlights that the taxpayer calculated net tax payable as $0, but also mentions selling capital property, exceeding self-employment net earnings thresholds, or having dependents eligible for benefits.

⚠ 自雇延期双日混淆陷阱Self-employed dual-deadline confusion trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生将自雇人士的申报截止日（6 月 15 日）误当成税款缴纳截止日，以为 6 月 15 日前缴税也没有任何财务代价。Candidates mistake the self-employed filing due date (June 15) for the balance owing payment deadline, incorrectly assuming that paying taxes by June 15 incurs no financial penalty or cost.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提及自雇经营者在 5 月或 6 月 15 日前申报并补缴欠税，询问利息起算日或迟报罚款是否存在。The stem describes a self-employed individual filing and paying taxes in May or by June 15, asking whether interest starts accruing or whether late-filing penalties apply.

⚠ 迟报罚款全额基数陷阱Late-filing penalty total tax base trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生误用整年应纳税总额（total tax payable）来乘以罚款比例，忽视了公式基数仅为申报截止日当天未缴清的差额（unpaid balance）。Candidates mistakenly multiply total annual tax payable by the penalty percentage, ignoring that the statutory base is strictly the unpaid balance owing on the filing deadline.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出全年应纳税款和雇主已代扣税款（或预缴税款），询问迟报罚款的具体金额。The stem provides both the total tax payable for the year and taxes withheld at source by an employer (or instalments), asking for the exact late-filing penalty amount.

⚠ 个人异议期限一律 90 天陷阱Individual objection 90-day universal deadline trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 许多人将公司或常规行政异议的 90 天规则机械套用到个人身上，忽略了个人拥有「申报截止日后一年」的更宽限保护期。Many candidates mechanically apply the corporate or standard 90-day administrative objection rule to individuals, ignoring the more generous protection granted to individuals of one year after the filing due date.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出个人在 3 月或 4 月收到评税通知 (NOA)，询问提出异议通知 (Notice of Objection) 的最晚法定截止日。The stem states an individual received a Notice of Assessment (NOA) in March or April, asking for the latest statutory deadline to file a Notice of Objection.

⚠ 旧版七步流程残留陷阱Legacy seven-step structure trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生受旧考试资料或历史资料影响，误以为省/属地税仍然在主表中单独列为独立计算步骤。Candidates influenced by legacy exam prep or outdated training materials assume provincial and territorial tax is still listed as an independent calculation step on the main return.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干询问现行 T1 表格结构步骤数量，或省税 (Form 428) 在哪一步骤参与汇总结算。The stem asks for the total number of steps in the current T1 return structure or at which step provincial tax (Form 428) is integrated into the final calculation.

第 1 题Q1零税额法定强制申报情形Mandatory filing triggers with zero tax payable★★陷阱：没欠税即免报陷阱

During the 2026 taxation year, Liam disposed of mutual fund units held in a non-registered account, realizing a taxable capital gain. After applying his allowable deductions and non-refundable tax credits, Liam's calculated net federal and provincial tax payable is $0. He did not receive a formal demand to file from the CRA. Is Liam legally required to file a 2026 T1 personal tax return?

在 2026 纳税年度中，Liam 处置了非注册账户中的互惠基金份额，实现了应税资本利得。在扣除各项法定抵扣额与非退还税收抵免后，Liam 计算出的联邦与省净应纳税额为 $0。他未收到 CRA 发出的催报通知。根据加拿大税法，Liam 是否负有提交 2026 年 T1 个人申报表的法定申报义务？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是典型的「没欠税即免报陷阱」；所得税法规定即使最终应纳税额为零，只要触发特定法定情形（如处置资本财产）就必须申报。Incorrect. This falls into the classic zero-tax-means-no-filing trap. The Income Tax Act stipulates that even if final tax payable is zero, filing is mandatory once specific statutory triggers (such as disposing of capital property) occur.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据《所得税法》(Income Tax Act, ITA)，个人若处置了资本财产 (capital property) 或实现了应税资本利得 (taxable capital gain)，即使净应纳税额 (tax payable) 为零，也属于必须强制申报的法定情形之一。Correct. Under the Income Tax Act (ITA), disposing of capital property or realizing a taxable capital gain is a mandatory filing trigger, even if the individual's net tax payable is $0.

  3. ✗ 错误。混淆了概念；$3,500 是加拿大退休金计划 (Canada Pension Plan, CPP) 的基本免供额，与资本财产处置申报无关。Incorrect. This confuses statutory concepts; $3,500 is the Canada Pension Plan (CPP) basic exemption and has no relevance to capital property dispositions.

  4. ✗ 错误。除魁北克外，各省个人所得税与资本利得申报均通过联邦 T1 申报表由 CRA 统一征管，无需且不存在单独向省里提交信息表。Incorrect. Except for Quebec, provincial personal income taxes and capital gains are administered by the CRA through the federal T1 return; there is no separate provincial information return to file.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

In 2026, Chloe earned $4,800 in net self-employment income from freelance copywriting, with no other income sources. After applying the basic personal amount, her calculated income tax liability is $0. Is Chloe legally required to file a 2026 T1 return?

2026 年，Chloe 从事自由文案写作取得净自雇收入 $4,800，且无其他收入来源。在计入基本个人免税额后，其计算出的应纳个人所得税为 $0。Chloe 是否负有提交 2026 年 T1 申报表的法定申报义务？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入「没欠税即免报陷阱」；虽然所得税为零，但自雇净收入超过 CPP 基本免供额会产生强制申报并缴纳供款的义务。Incorrect. This falls into the zero-tax-means-no-filing trap. Although income tax payable is zero, earning net self-employment income above the CPP basic exemption triggers a mandatory obligation to file and remit CPP contributions.

  2. ✗ 错误。自雇人士对 CPP 供款并非自愿选择，超过基本免供额的部分必须强制供款（仅就业保险 EI 供款为自愿加入）。Incorrect. CPP contributions are not optional for self-employed individuals; contributions on earnings above the basic exemption are mandatory (only Employment Insurance, or EI, allows voluntary participation).

  3. ✓ 正确。自雇净收入超过 2026 年 CPP 基本免供额 (basic exemption) $3,500 时，纳税人必须缴纳 CPP 供款并通过 T1 申报表申报，属于法定强制申报情形。Correct. When net self-employment earnings exceed the 2026 CPP basic exemption of $3,500, the individual is required to pay CPP contributions and report them on a T1 return, making filing mandatory under the law.

  4. ✗ 错误。超过 CPP 免供额是法定直接触发的申报义务，不需要 CRA 另行寄发催报函 (demand to file)。Incorrect. Earning net self-employment income above the CPP basic exemption directly triggers a statutory filing obligation without requiring the CRA to issue a formal demand to file.

第 2 题Q22026最低税阶税率Lowest federal tax bracket rate for 2026★陷阱：过渡期税率混淆

For the 2026 taxation year, what is the federal income tax rate applicable to an individual's taxable income in the lowest tax bracket (taxable income up to $58,523)?

对于 2026 纳税年度，个人在最低联邦税阶内（应税收入在 $58,523 及以下）适用的法定联邦所得税税率是多少？

  1. ✗ 错误。15% 是 2024 税年（旧培训材料口径）的最低税阶税率，2026 税年已降到 14%。Incorrect. The 15% rate applied to the lowest bracket in the 2024 taxation year (legacy exam materials); for the 2026 tax year, it has been reduced to 14%.

  2. ✗ 错误。14.5% 是 2025 税年中因下半年降税生效而适用的全年折算混合税率 (blended rate)，仅在 2025 单一过渡年适用。Incorrect. The 14.5% rate was a full-year blended rate resulting from the mid-year tax cut in 2025, applicable exclusively to the 2025 transition year.

  3. ✓ 正确。2026 税年联邦最低税阶 (lowest federal tax bracket) 两年降税落地，全年适用 14%（该档上限 58,523 元）。Correct. For the 2026 taxation year, the two-year phased reduction in the lowest federal tax bracket is fully implemented, applying a 14% rate for the full year (bracket threshold up to $58,523).

  4. ✗ 错误。20.5% 是联邦第二档（58,523 元以上至 117,045 元）的边际税率，不是最低税阶。Incorrect. The 20.5% rate is the federal marginal tax rate for the second bracket ($58,523 to $117,045), not the lowest bracket.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

While calculating federal tax on an individual's 2026 taxable income of $45,000, a tax preparer applies a tax rate of 14.5%. Why is this rate incorrect under 2026 tax rules?

在计算某个人 2026 年 $45,000 应税收入的联邦税时，报税员适用了 14.5% 的税率。根据 2026 税年规则，为什么这一税率是错误的？

  1. ✓ 正确。14.5% 只在 2025 过渡年出现，是上半年 15%、下半年 14% 折算出的全年混合税率；2026 税年起全年 14%。Correct. The 14.5% rate existed solely in the 2025 transition year as a blended rate (15% for the first half and 14% for the second half); starting in the 2026 tax year, the full-year rate is 14%.

  2. ✗ 错误。联邦最低税阶降至 14% 是法定结构性降税，并没有在 2025 年底过期或回滚到 15%。Incorrect. The reduction of the lowest federal bracket to 14% is a permanent structural statutory change; it did not sunset or revert to 15% at the end of 2025.

  3. ✗ 错误。第二档税阶税率为 20.5%，14.5% 并非第二档税阶税率。Incorrect. The second bracket marginal tax rate is 20.5%; 14.5% is not the second bracket rate.

  4. ✗ 错误。14.5% 是个人所得税 2025 年的过渡税率，与公司小型企业扣除无关。Incorrect. The 14.5% rate was a personal income tax transitional rate for 2025 and has no connection to the small business deduction for corporations.

第 3 题Q3自雇申报与付款双轨截止日Dual filing and payment deadlines for self-employed individuals★★陷阱：自雇延期双日混淆陷阱

Marcus operates an unincorporated web design business. For the 2026 taxation year, his return reflects a balance owing of $5,000. Marcus files his T1 return on June 10, 2027, and pays the $5,000 balance in full on the same date. What are the legal consequences regarding penalties and interest?

Marcus 经营一家未注册成公司的网页设计生意（自雇）。2026 纳税年度，其税表显示应补税款余额为 $5,000。Marcus 于 2027 年 6 月 10 日提交了 T1 申报表，并在同日全额缴清了这笔 $5,000 欠税。关于罚款与利息，法律后果是什么？

  1. ✓ 正确。自雇人士的申报截止日 (filing due date) 延至 6 月 15 日，因此 6 月 10 日申报不产生迟报罚款 (late-filing penalty)；但税款结清截止日 (balance owing due date) 仍为 4 月 30 日，因此逾期税款自 5 月 1 日起计收规定利息 (prescribed interest)。Correct. The filing due date for self-employed individuals is extended to June 15, so filing on June 10 does not incur a late-filing penalty; however, the balance owing payment due date remains April 30, meaning overdue tax accrues prescribed interest starting May 1.

  2. ✗ 错误。申报延期是法定的独立权利，并不以在 4 月 30 日前缴清税款为前提条件，不会因此产生迟报罚款。Incorrect. The extended filing deadline is an independent statutory right not conditioned upon paying the balance owing by April 30; failing to pay by April 30 does not trigger a late-filing penalty.

  3. ✗ 错误。落入「自雇延期双日混淆陷阱」；延期只针对提交申报表免除罚款，绝不免除 4 月 30 日后的欠税利息。Incorrect. This falls into the self-employed dual-deadline confusion trap. The June 15 extension applies solely to filing the return without penalties; it never waives interest on balances unpaid after April 30.

第 4 题Q4迟报罚款计算基数与费率Late-filing penalty calculation base and rates★★陷阱：迟报罚款全额基数陷阱

David 在 2026 纳税年度核定的应纳税总额为 $16,000。其雇主在源头预扣了 $12,000，尚余未缴税款余额 $4,000。David 于 2027 年 7 月 10 日提交了申报表（逾期整两个月），且未缴税。他在前三个税年中无迟报罚款记录，也未收到 CRA 催报令。David 应缴纳的迟报罚款是多少？

The statutory base is the $4,000 unpaid balance on the filing deadline, not the total annual tax payable; calculating 7% on $16,000 ($1,120) uses the incorrect base.

  2. ✗ 错误。漏了每逾期一个整月加 1%：$4,000 × 5% = $200 只算了起步的 5%，逾期两个整月还要加 2%。Incorrect. This omits the 1% addition for each complete month late: $4,000 x 5% = $200 only calculates the initial 5%, omitting the 2% for being two complete months late.

The first-time late-filing penalty is 5% of the unpaid balance at the deadline plus 1% for each complete month late (2% for two complete months), totaling 7%: $4,000 x 5% = $200, plus $4,000 x 2% = $80, giving $280.
