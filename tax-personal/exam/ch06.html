第 6 章 · 其他收入与社会福利Chapter 6 · Other Income & Social Benefits: Reporting, Offsetting, and Excluded AmountsOther income and social benefits

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 净收入计入与扣回机制题（Line 14400/14500 与 Line 25000）：考察工伤给付与社会援助「先进净收入、再扣回应税收入」的两步逻辑，重点考查对以净收入为基数的各项收入测试型福利（CCB、CGEB、BC 省级福利）的影响，以及同居配偶社会援助由高收入方申报的规则。Net income inclusion and offsetting mechanism (Line 14400/14500 and Line 25000): tests the two-step logic that workers' compensation and social assistance are first included in net income and then deducted from taxable income, especially their effect on income-tested benefits based on net income (CCB, CGEB, BC provincial benefits), and the rule that social assistance for cohabiting spouses is reported by the higher-income spouse.
  • EI 常规应税与回收挂钩计算题（Line 11900 与 Line 23500）：考察 EI 各类福利全额计入 Line 11900 应税收入，识别仅常规/延长福利（box 7 费率为 30%）在调整前净收入（Line 23400）超过 1.25×MIE（2026 税年 86,125 元）时触发 30% 回收，并坚决排除特殊福利（产假/育儿/病假）。EI regular benefits taxation and the linked recovery calculation (Line 11900 and Line 23500): tests that all EI benefits are included in full on Line 11900, that only regular/extended benefits (box 7 rate 30%) trigger a 30% recovery when net income before adjustments (Line 23400) exceeds 1.25 x MIE ($86,125 for the 2026 tax year), and firmly excludes special benefits (maternity, parental, sickness).
  • 赡养费协议日期与子女/配偶分流题（Line 12800 与 Line 22000）：给出不同年份成立或修改的离婚/分居协议，考查 1997 年 5 月 1 日分水岭——新协议子女赡养费不课税不可扣除、配偶赡养费课税且可扣除；而 1997 年前旧协议子女部分仍沿用课税与扣除旧口径（除非签 T1157 或书面修改）。Support agreement dates and the child/spouse split (Line 12800 and Line 22000): gives divorce or separation agreements made or changed in different years and tests the May 1, 1997 dividing line: for new agreements child support is neither taxable nor deductible and spousal support is taxable and deductible; for pre-1997 agreements the child support part keeps the old taxable and deductible treatment (unless Form T1157 is signed or the agreement is changed in writing).
  • 奖学金免税额与合格学生身份判定题（Line 13010）：考核全日制合格学生在大专以上教育项目中奖学金全额免税，对比非合格学生（如博士后研究员）仅享 500 元 基础免税额，以及兼读（非全日制）学生按「学费+材料费+500 元」计算免税限额的规则。Scholarship exemption and qualifying student status (Line 13010): tests that scholarships of full-time qualifying students in post-secondary programs are fully exempt, compared with non-qualifying students (such as postdoctoral fellows) who get only the $500 basic exemption, and the rule that part-time students are limited to tuition + materials + $500.
  • RESP 派款性质与退休津贴/意外之财辨析题（Line 13000、41800 与免报项目）：区分 RESP 供款本金免税、EAP 由学生报 Line 13000、AIP 由供款人报 Line 13000 且在 Line 41800 征收 20% 附加税；识别退休津贴仅 1995 年前服务的合格部分可免占额度转入 RRSP；以及彩票和人寿理赔根本不算收入无需申报。Nature of RESP payments, retiring allowances and windfalls (Line 13000, 41800 and items not reported): distinguishes RESP contributions returned tax-free, EAP reported by the student on Line 13000, and AIP reported by the subscriber on Line 13000 with a 20% additional tax on Line 41800; recognizes that only the eligible part of a retiring allowance for service before 1996 can be transferred to an RRSP without using room; and that lottery winnings and life insurance proceeds are not income and need not be reported.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 政府给的福利都不用交税Government benefits are never taxed

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把工伤给付的免税待遇或彩票的非收入性质盲目套用到 EI（就业保险）上，误以为失业金、产假津贴等属于政府福利就无需纳税。Blindly applying the tax-free treatment of workers' compensation or the non-income nature of lottery winnings to EI, believing that unemployment and maternity benefits are government benefits and so are not taxed.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 T4E 凭单金额（如失业常规福利或产假津贴），问其在 T1 表上的应税收入金额或行号归属。The stem gives a T4E slip amount (such as regular unemployment benefits or maternity benefits) and asks for the taxable income amount or the line it goes on the T1.

⚠ 工伤社保直接隐形不报Workers' compensation and social assistance left off the return

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为工伤给付（WCB）和社会援助既然最终不用交税（税负为零），就可以像彩票一样完全不写在申报表上。Believing that because workers' compensation (WCB) and social assistance end up with no tax, they can be left entirely off the return like lottery winnings.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出 T5007 box 10 或 box 11 金额，问纳税人净收入（Line 23600）是否增加，或如何列报 Line 14400/14500 与 Line 25000。The stem gives the T5007 box 10 or box 11 amount and asks whether the taxpayer's net income (Line 23600) increases, or how Line 14400/14500 and Line 25000 are reported.

⚠ EI回收与OAS回收混同Confusing the EI recovery with the OAS recovery

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 听到「福利超额要回收（clawback）」，就将 EI 回收门槛与老年金（OAS）门槛或扣回率混淆，甚至对产假/病假特殊福利也计算回收。Hearing that 'excess benefits are clawed back', candidates mix up the EI recovery threshold with the OAS threshold or rate, and even calculate a recovery on special maternity or sickness benefits.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出净收入超过 8.6 万或 9.5 万、同时领取 EI（含产假或病假）或 OAS 的情境，要求计算 Line 23500 的福利回收额。The stem gives net income above $86,000 or $95,000 together with EI (including maternity or sickness) or OAS, and asks for the benefit recovery on Line 23500.

⚠ 断章取义子女抚养费一律免税Taking child support as always tax-free out of context

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 死记硬背现行通则「子女赡养费不课税、不可扣除」，忽略了 1997 年 5 月 1 日之前的旧协议在未签署 T1157 或未修改的情况下依然维持旧税法口径。Memorizing the current general rule that child support is neither taxable nor deductible, and ignoring that a pre-May 1, 1997 agreement keeps the old tax treatment if no T1157 was signed and it was never changed.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干明确指出离婚协议或法院令成立时间为 1997 年 5 月 1 日之前（如 1995 年或 1996 年），且注明之后从未修改金额。The stem states that the divorce agreement or court order was made before May 1, 1997 (for example 1995 or 1996) and that the amount was never changed afterwards.

⚠ 全日制身份等同合格学生Full-time status equals qualifying student

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 认为只要自己全职在从事学术研究或项目（如博士后研究员），拿到 T4A box 105 的奖助金就能享受全额奖学金免税。Thinking that anyone working full time on academic research or a project (such as a postdoctoral fellow) who receives a T4A box 105 award gets the full scholarship exemption.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干中纳税人为博士后研究员（postdoctoral fellow）或就读非合格项目，收到 T4A box 105 奖学金，问免税额度或 Line 13010 申报金额。The stem has the taxpayer as a postdoctoral fellow or in a non-qualifying program who receives a T4A box 105 scholarship, and asks for the exemption or the amount reported on Line 13010.

第 1 题Q1EI常规回收门槛与特殊福利排除EI regular benefit recovery threshold and exclusion of special benefits★★陷阱：EI回收与OAS回收混同

Darren received $12,000 in Employment Insurance (EI) regular benefits during the 2026 tax year (T4E box 14 shows $12,000, box 7 rate is 30%, and box 15 contains an amount). His net income before adjustments on line 23400 is $96,125. Darren has no other deductions or additions affecting line 23500. What amount should Darren report on line 11900 as EI benefits, and what amount of EI benefit repayment must be reported on line 23500?

达伦（Darren）在 2026 税年收到了 $12,000 的就业保险（EI）常规福利（T4E box 14 显示为 $12,000，box 7 费率为 30%，box 15 有金额）。他在 Line 23400 调整前的净收入为 $96,125。达伦没有其他影响 Line 23500 的扣除或加项。达伦应在 Line 11900 申报多少 EI 福利，以及必须在 Line 23500 申报多少 EI 福利回收额？

  1. ✓ 正确。EI 常规福利全额应税，Line 11900 报 $12,000。2026 回收门槛＝1.25 × $68,900 = $86,125；超出 $96,125 − $86,125 = $10,000，× 30% = $3,000 报 Line 23500。Correct. EI regular benefits are fully taxable, so $12,000 goes on Line 11900. The 2026 recovery threshold = 1.25 x $68,900 = $86,125; the excess is $96,125 - $86,125 = $10,000, x 30% = $3,000 on Line 23500.

  2. ✗ 错误。从 $12,000 减去 $3,000 回收额报净额 $9,000，违背总额入表原则。Incorrect. Subtracting the $3,000 recovery from $12,000 and reporting the net $9,000 goes against the principle of reporting the gross amount.

  3. ✗ 错误。用了 OAS 的 15%：$10,000 × 15% = $1,500；EI 回收率是 30%。Incorrect. This uses the OAS rate of 15%: $10,000 x 15% = $1,500; the EI recovery rate is 30%.

  4. ✗ 错误。用了 2024 门槛 $79,000：($96,125 − $79,000) × 30% ≈ $5,138；2026 门槛是 $86,125。Incorrect. This uses the 2024 threshold of $79,000: ($96,125 - $79,000) x 30% = about $5,138; the 2026 threshold is $86,125.

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Nadia received $14,000 in EI maternity and parental benefits during 2026 (T4E box 14 shows $14,000, and box 7 indicates maternity/parental benefits with a rate other than 30%). Nadia returned to work later in the year, and her net income before adjustments on line 23400 reached $98,000. How should Nadia report her EI benefits and the EI repayment amount on her 2026 T1 return?

纳迪娅（Nadia）在 2026 年收到了 $14,000 的 EI 产假与育儿福利（T4E box 14 显示为 $14,000，box 7 标注为产假/育儿津贴，其费率并非 30%）。纳迪娅在当年晚些时候重返工作岗位，其在 Line 23400 调整前的净收入达到 $98,000。纳迪娅应如何在 2026 年 T1 表上申报其 EI 福利与 EI 回收额？

  1. ✗ 错误。此选项擅自将按 30% 算出的超额扣回额从收入行直接扣减，既犯了净额列报错误，又错将产假福利纳入了回收范围。Incorrect. This arbitrarily deducts the 30% excess recovery directly from the income line, making the mistake of net reporting, and wrongly brings maternity benefits into the recovery.

  2. ✗ 错误。此选项对产假津贴按 30% 计算了回收（(98,000 元 − 86,125 元) × 30% ≈ 3,563 元）。产假、育儿、病假等特殊福利的 box 7 费率不是 30%，不落入回收范围。Incorrect. This calculates a 30% recovery on maternity benefits (($98,000 - $86,125) x 30% = about $3,563). Special benefits such as maternity, parental and sickness do not have a 30% rate in box 7 and do not fall under the recovery.

  3. ✓ 正确。产假与育儿津贴是应税收入，Line 11900 全额申报 $14,000；但它们属于特殊福利，T4E box 7 费率不是 30%，不受 Line 23500 回收规则约束，即使净收入超过 86,125 元门槛，回收额仍为 $0。Correct. Maternity and parental benefits are taxable income and are reported in full as $14,000 on Line 11900; but they are special benefits, the T4E box 7 rate is not 30%, and they are not subject to the Line 23500 recovery rules, so even if net income exceeds the $86,125 threshold the recovery is $0.

  4. ✗ 错误。这落入了「政府给的福利都不用交税」的典型陷阱。EI 无论常规福利还是产假等特殊福利，一律属于应税收入。Incorrect. This falls into the classic 'government benefits are never taxed' trap. All EI, whether regular benefits or special benefits such as maternity, is taxable income.

第 2 题Q2工伤社保计入再扣回及福利影响Workers' compensation and social assistance: included then offset, and the effect on benefits★★陷阱：工伤社保直接隐形不报

In 2026, Liam sustained a workplace injury and received $15,000 in Workers' Compensation Benefits (T5007 box 10). He had no other income during the year. How do these benefits affect Liam's net income (line 23600), taxable income (line 26000), and his eligibility for income-tested social benefits?

2026 年，利亚姆（Liam）遭遇工伤，收到了 $15,000 的工伤给付（T5007 box 10）。他在当年没有其他收入。这笔给付如何影响利亚姆的净收入（Line 23600）、应税收入（Line 26000）以及他享受收入测试型社会福利的资格？

  3. ✗ 错误。每年 $2,000 的累积额度仅计算至 1995 年底，1996 年以后的年资一年额度都不再增加。Incorrect. The $2,000 per year of accumulated room is calculated only to the end of 1995, and service from 1996 on no longer adds any room.

  4. ✗ 错误。不合格退休津贴并非禁止供款，只要哈罗德自己有剩余的 RRSP 供款空间，就可以正常供款并抵扣。Incorrect. A non-eligible retiring allowance is not barred from being contributed; as long as Harold has his own remaining RRSP room, he can contribute and deduct normally.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Following the passing of her father in 2026, Vanessa received two payments: a $12,000 death benefit from her father's former employer, and a $2,500 Canada Pension Plan (CPP) death benefit. Vanessa is the sole beneficiary. How much of these receipts must Vanessa include in taxable income on line 13000 of her personal tax return?

父亲于 2026 年去世后，凡妮莎（Vanessa）收到了两笔款项：一笔是父亲生前雇主发放的 $12,000 雇员身故福利，另一笔是 $2,500 的加拿大退休金计划（CPP）身故给付。凡妮莎是唯一受益人。凡妮莎在个人报税表 Line 13000 上必须将其中多少金额计入应税收入？

  1. ✗ 错误。这落入了「身故给付全部免税」的直觉陷阱。雇主身故福利仅享有法定上限豁免，且 CPP 身故给付全额应税。Incorrect. This falls into the intuitive trap that all death benefits are tax-free. An employer death benefit gets only a statutory capped exemption, and the CPP death benefit is fully taxable.

  2. ✗ 错误。此选项误将 $10,000 免税额套用到了 CPP 身故给付上。第 2.6 节明确指出，CPP/QPP 身故给付不适用该项豁免。Incorrect. This wrongly applies the $10,000 exemption to the CPP death benefit. Section 2.6 states clearly that CPP/QPP death benefits do not qualify for this exemption.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据第 2.6 节规则，雇员身故福利（death benefit）对同一逝者的全部雇主和受益人合计享有 $10,000 的法定免税上限，雇主发放的 $12,000 扣除 $10,000 后，应税金额为 $2,000；而 CPP 身故给付（$2,500）法律明确不适用 $10,000 免税额度，属于全额应税收入。因此，两者汇总计入 Line 13000 的总应税金额为 $4,500（$2,000 + $2,500）。Correct. Under Section 2.6, an employee death benefit has a statutory exemption cap of $10,000 for all employers and beneficiaries together for the same deceased person; the $12,000 paid by the employer less $10,000 leaves $2,000 taxable; the CPP death benefit ($2,500) is expressly not eligible for the $10,000 exemption and is fully taxable income. So the total taxable amount on Line 13000 is $4,500 ($2,000 + $2,500).

  4. ✗ 错误。此选项遗漏了税法对雇员身故福利给予的 $10,000 法定免税额。Incorrect. This omits the $10,000 statutory exemption the tax rules give an employee death benefit.

答案Answer：c

第 7 题Q7工伤退还工资的三行号分流申报Three-line reporting when workers' compensation repays wages★★★陷阱：工伤社保直接隐形不报

In 2026, Kevin was injured on the job. His employer continued paying his regular salary of $50,000 throughout his recovery. WorkSafeBC approved his claim and issued a T5007 slip showing $10,000 in box 10. Pursuant to company policy, Kevin reimbursed the $10,000 to his employer. Kevin also received a T4 slip reporting $50,000 in box 14. How should Kevin properly report these transactions on his 2026 T1 return?

2026 年，凯文（Kevin）在工作中受伤。雇主在其康复休工期间按政策继续向其全额发放了 $50,000 的常规工资。WorkSafeBC 批准了他的工伤申请，并出具了一张 T5007 凭单，其 box 10 显示有 $10,000 工伤给付。依据公司政策，凯文将这笔 $10,000 退还给了雇主。凯文同时收到一张 T4 表，box 14 显示工资为 $50,000。凯文应如何在 2026 年 T1 表上合规申报这些收支事项？

  1. ✗ 错误。此选项直接用退还款与 T4 工资私下抵消，在 Line 10100 申报净额 $40,000。这属于严重错报，违背了税法分通行号的核对要求。Incorrect. This offsets the repayment against the T4 wages privately and reports a net $40,000 on Line 10100. This is a serious misreport and goes against the line-by-line matching requirement of the tax rules.

  2. ✗ 错误。此选项遗漏了退还给雇主款项在 Line 22900 的法定扣除，导致凯文为其已经退还给雇主的工资重复纳税。Incorrect. This omits the statutory deduction on Line 22900 for the amount repaid to the employer, so Kevin pays tax twice on wages he already repaid.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据第六节案例一的实务标准操作，各笔收支必须分走独立行号，严禁私自抵消：T4 表上雇主发放的 $50,000 工资如实报在 Line 10100；T5007 的 $10,000 工伤给付报在 Line 14400，同时在 Line 25000 申报 $10,000 扣除（工伤本身免税）；凯文退还给雇主的 $10,000 垫付工资，另在 Line 22900 申报其他就业扣除项。四处填报清晰完整，既不漏报也不重缴。Correct. Under the standard practice in Case 1 of Section 6, each receipt and payment must go on its own line and must never be offset privately: the $50,000 salary paid by the employer on the T4 is reported honestly on Line 10100; the $10,000 workers' compensation on the T5007 is reported on Line 14400 with a $10,000 deduction on Line 25000 (workers' compensation itself is tax-free); and the $10,000 of advanced wages Kevin repaid to the employer is separately claimed on Line 22900 as an other employment deduction. All four entries are clear and complete, with no omission and no double taxation.

  4. ✗ 错误。工伤给付绝非 Line 13000 的应税其他收入，此做法直接导致免税福利被错课所得税。Incorrect. Workers' compensation is never taxable other income on Line 13000, and this approach causes a tax-free benefit to be taxed by mistake.
