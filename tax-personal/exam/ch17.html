第 17 章 · 主居所与炒房规则Chapter 17 · Principal Residence Exemption, Change in Use Elections, and Residential Property Flipping RulesPrincipal residence and flipping

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 公式代入与免税年数计算题：考查主居所免税公式 A − (A × B / C) − D 的要素拆解，重点测试 plus one 规则的居民前提、家庭每年限一套的年份重叠取舍，以及未满足全期自住时的应税资本利得计算。Substituting into the formula and counting exempt years: tests the elements of the principal residence exemption formula A - (A x B / C) - D, in particular the residency precondition for the plus one rule, the choice of which overlapping years to designate given one property per family per year, and the taxable capital gain when the home was not a principal residence for the whole period.
  • 用途改变与选举定性题：考查自住与出租相互转换时的视同处置（s.45(1)），重点测试 45(2) 与 45(3) 选举的申报时间节点、四年上限（及 s.54.1 雇主调动例外）、以及申报折旧（CCA）对 45(3) 资格的致命破坏。Change in use and the nature of the elections: tests the deemed disposition (s.45(1)) when a home moves between personal use and rental, in particular the filing time points for the 45(2) and 45(3) elections, the four-year cap (and the s.54.1 employer relocation exception), and how claiming CCA fatally undermines eligibility for a 45(3) election.
  • 部分出租与视同处置豁免判断题：考查自住房部分出租时是否维持整套免税的 CRA 三要件（附属性、无结构改造、未计提 CCA），测试混淆折旧抵税与丧失主居所资格的取舍判断。Partial rental and the deemed disposition exemption: tests CRA's three conditions (ancillary use, no structural change, no CCA claimed) for keeping the whole home exempt when part of it is rented, and the trade-off between claiming depreciation for tax savings and losing principal residence status.
  • 联邦 365 天翻炒规则与九大生活事件题：考查连续 365 天持有期门槛、楼花合同转让的权利定性、利得全额进经营收入与亏损单边归零，以及合格搬迁等生活事件例外的判定。The federal 365-day flipping rule and the nine life events: tests the 365 consecutive day holding threshold, the characterization of an assignment of a pre-sale contract as a right, the rule that the gain is fully business income and a loss is reduced to nil, and the qualifying relocation and other life event exceptions.
  • 联邦所得税与 BC 翻炒税双轨对比题：BC 730 天递减税率、持有满 365 天才有的最高 20,000 元主要居所扣除，以及生活事件豁免必须在处置后 90 天内交表。Federal income tax versus the BC flipping tax: BC's 730-day declining rate, the primary residence deduction of up to $20,000 available only after a 365-day holding, and the requirement that the life event exemption be filed within 90 days after the disposition.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 误认自住房不报也能免税Thinking a home lived in is exempt without reporting

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 受 2016 年以前旧习惯影响，误以为只要实际自住就天然免税，忽略了 2016 年起必须在 Schedule 3 和 T2091(IND) 申报并做指定才是法定资格前提，未申报直接丧失免税资格。Out of pre-2016 habit, assuming that actually living in the home makes it exempt automatically. This overlooks that since 2016 reporting on Schedule 3 and T2091(IND) and making the designation is a legal precondition, and an unreported disposition loses the exemption outright.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现“全程自住出售但纳税人未在当年税表提及”、“咨询是否可以不填报”或考查迟报罚款金额。The question has "sold after living in it the whole time but the taxpayer did not mention it on the return", asks "whether it can be left unreported", or tests the amount of the late-filing penalty.

⚠ 出租期间计提折旧破坏 45(3) 选举Claiming CCA during the rental period breaks the 45(3) election

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 只看到当年出租收益可以用 CCA 冲减少交所得税，未意识到后续转回自住做 45(3) 选举时，只要历史上任何一年计提过 CCA，该选举即被法律视为从未做出。Seeing only that rental income for the year can be reduced by CCA to save tax, and not realizing that when the owner later moves back and makes a 45(3) election, a CCA claim in any past year makes the law treat that election as never made.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现“在出租期间申报过资本成本折旧 (CCA)”、“随后搬回自住希望做 45(3) 选举”或询问递延有效性。The question has "claimed capital cost allowance (CCA) during the rental period", "later moved back to live in it and wants to make a 45(3) election", or asks whether the deferral is valid.

⚠ 将 45(3) 选举期限简单记为卖房年Remembering the 45(3) election deadline simply as the year of sale

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 死记“45(2) 当年报、45(3) 卖房年报”口诀，漏掉了 ITA s.45(3)(a) 规定的“CRA 发出书面要求后 90 天”与“处置年申报期”取较早者的法定限制。Memorizing "45(2) is filed in the year of change, 45(3) in the year of sale" and missing the statutory limit in ITA s.45(3)(a) of the earlier of "90 days after a written CRA request" and the filing deadline for the year of disposition.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现“CRA 发出书面要求提交选举信”或考查 45(3) 最迟提交时限的具体选项。The question has "CRA issued a written request to file the election letter", or tests the specific option for the latest filing time for a 45(3) election.

⚠ 误以为楼花未交付就不算房产翻炒Thinking an undelivered pre-sale unit is not a flip

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 混淆了物理房屋的产权交割与期房买卖合同，忽略了 ITA s.12(13) 和 BC 翻炒税法明确将“取得住宅单元的权利”（assignment of a presale contract）直接纳入翻炒标的。Confusing the transfer of title to a physical home with a pre-sale contract. This overlooks that ITA s.12(13) and the BC flipping tax law expressly include "the right to acquire a housing unit" (assignment of a presale contract) as a flipping subject.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现“签下公寓预售合同（楼花）并在交房前转让合同”、“转让预售合同利得”或“未拿钥匙”。The question has "signed a pre-sale condo contract and assigned it before completion", "gain on assigning a pre-sale contract" or "never received the keys".

⚠ 将联邦自动豁免误套用于 BC 省翻炒税Applying the federal automatic exemption to the BC flipping tax

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为满足生活事件（如非自愿解雇、搬迁）即可像联邦 Schedule 3 那样自动豁免，忽略了 BC 省翻炒税规定生活事件豁免必须在处置后 90 天内单独申报才能享受，否则面临至少 $500 罚款。Assuming that meeting a life event (such as involuntary termination or relocation) gives an automatic exemption, as on federal Schedule 3. This overlooks that under the BC flipping tax a life event exemption is available only if filed separately within 90 days after the disposition, and otherwise a penalty of at least $500 applies.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现“持有不足 730 天但因生活事件卖房”、“纳税人未在 90 天内向 BC 财政厅单独申报”或考查豁免申报程序。The question has "sold after holding fewer than 730 days because of a life event", "the taxpayer did not file separately with the BC Ministry of Finance within 90 days", or tests the procedure for claiming the exemption.

第 1 题Q1主居所比例公式计算Principal residence ratio formula calculation★★陷阱：未计入加一年或误算持有年数

Elena bought a townhouse in 2017 for $400,000 and sold it in 2026 for $700,000 with no selling expenses (total gain: $300,000). She owned it for 10 tax years (2017 to 2026). Her family lived in it every year, but she designated it as her principal residence only for 2017 through 2021; for 2022 through 2026 she designated a cottage she also owned (one designation per family per year). She was a Canadian resident throughout. What is her taxable capital gain on the townhouse for 2026?

Elena 2017 年以 $400,000 买入一套联排，2026 年以 $700,000 卖出，无出售费用（利得总额 $300,000），共持有 10 个纳税年度（2017 至 2026）。全家每年都住在这里，但她只把 2017 至 2021 年指定给这套联排；2022 至 2026 年她把主居所指定给自己的另一处度假屋（每家庭每年只能指定一处）。她一直是加拿大居民。这套联排 2026 年的应税资本利得是多少？

  1. ✗ 错误。免税按年数比例给：只有指定给这套联排的年份（加一年）才进分子，指定给度假屋的年份不算，所以不能全免。Incorrect. The exemption is given in proportion to years: only the years designated to this townhouse (plus one) go in the numerator, and the years designated to the vacation home do not count, so it cannot be fully exempt.

  2. ✗ 错误。漏了「加一年」：只按 5 年算，免税 300,000 × 5/10 ＝ 150,000 元，剩 150,000 元，乘 ½ 得 75,000 元。她取得当年是居民，B 应为 5 ＋ 1 ＝ 6。Incorrect. This misses the "plus one" year: counting only 5 years gives an exemption of $300,000 x 5/10 = $150,000, leaving $150,000, and half of that is $75,000. She was a resident in the year of acquisition, so B should be 5 + 1 = 6.

  3. ✓ 正确。免税额 ＝ A × B/C ＝ 300,000 × 6/10 ＝ 180,000 元（B ＝ 5 年指定 ＋ 1，C ＝ 10）。剩余资本利得 120,000 元，按 ½ 纳入，应税资本利得 60,000 元。Correct. Exempt amount = A x B/C = $300,000 x 6/10 = $180,000 (B = 5 designated years + 1, C = 10). The remaining capital gain is $120,000, included at 1/2, so the taxable capital gain is $60,000.

  4. ✗ 错误。120,000 元是免税后的资本利得，还要乘 ½ 纳入率才是应税资本利得。Incorrect. $120,000 is the capital gain after the exemption. It must still be multiplied by the 1/2 inclusion rate to get the taxable capital gain.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

David bought a home in 2019 for $500,000 and sold it in 2026 for $900,000 with no selling expenses (total gain: $400,000). He owned it for 8 tax years (2019 to 2026). His family lived in it every year, but he designated it as his principal residence only for 2019, 2020 and 2021; for 2022 through 2026 he designated a cottage he also owned. He was a Canadian resident throughout. What is his taxable capital gain on the home for 2026?

David 2019 年以 $500,000 买入一套住宅，2026 年以 $900,000 卖出，无处置费用（利得总额 $400,000），共持有 8 个纳税年度（2019 至 2026）。全家每年都住在这里，但他只把 2019、2020、2021 年指定给这套住宅；2022 至 2026 年他把主居所指定给自己的度假屋。他一直是加拿大居民。这套住宅 2026 年的应税资本利得是多少？

  1. ✓ 正确。免税额 ＝ 400,000 × 4/8 ＝ 200,000 元（B ＝ 3 ＋ 1，C ＝ 8），剩余资本利得 200,000 元，按 ½ 纳入，应税资本利得 100,000 元。Correct. Exempt amount = $400,000 x 4/8 = $200,000 (B = 3 + 1, C = 8). The remaining capital gain is $200,000, included at 1/2, so the taxable capital gain is $100,000.

  2. ✗ 错误。漏了「加一年」：B 取 3 年时免税 150,000 元，剩 250,000 元，乘 ½ 得 125,000 元。他取得当年是居民，B 应为 4。Incorrect. This misses the "plus one" year: with B at 3 years the exemption is $150,000, leaving $250,000, and half of that is $125,000. He was a resident in the year of acquisition, so B should be 4.

  3. ✗ 错误。200,000 元是免税后的资本利得，忘了乘 ½ 纳入率。Incorrect. $200,000 is the capital gain after the exemption, and the 1/2 inclusion rate was forgotten.

  4. ✗ 错误。只有指定年数加一覆盖全部持有年数（B/C ≥ 1）时才全免，这里只指定了 3 年。Incorrect. The gain is fully exempt only when the designated years plus one cover all the years of ownership (B/C is at least 1). Here only 3 years were designated.

第 2 题Q2主居所申报与迟报罚款Principal residence reporting and the late-filing penalty★★陷阱：误认自住房不报也能免税

Marcus sold his solely-owned home in June 2024 (a year for which the T1 filing due date was April 30, 2025). He had lived in it as his sole principal residence since purchasing it. Believing that 'principal residence sales are never taxed and need not be reported,' he omitted the disposition entirely from his 2024 tax return. In August 2026 (15 complete months after the April 30, 2025 filing deadline), his accountant discovered the omission and submitted a request to CRA to accept a late designation on Form T2091(IND). What is the late-filing penalty prescribed under subsection 220(3.5) of the Income Tax Act?

Marcus 于 2024 年 6 月出售了他单独拥有的住房（该纳税年度的 T1 申报截止日为 2025 年 4 月 30 日）。自购买以来，他一直将该房屋作为唯一的主居所居住。因深信“卖自住房免税所以根本不需要报税”的民间说法，他在 2024 年税表中完全没有提及该笔处置。2026 年 8 月（即 2025 年 4 月 30 日申报截止日之后整整 15 个完整月），他的会计师发现了这一疏漏，并向 CRA 提交申请，请求接受在 T2091(IND) 表上的迟做指定。根据《所得税法》第 220(3.5) 款，法定的迟做选择罚款是多少？

  1. ✓ 正确。s.220(3.5) 的罚款是 8,000 元与「100 元 × 逾期完整月数」两者取低。逾期 15 个完整月，100 × 15 ＝ 1,500 元，低于 8,000 元。Correct. The s.220(3.5) penalty is the lesser of $8,000 and $100 times the number of complete months late. Fifteen complete months late gives $100 x 15 = $1,500, which is less than $8,000.

  2. ✗ 错误。8,000 元是封顶值，只有按月累计达到 80 个月以上才收满；本案 15 个月应取较低的 1,500 元。Incorrect. $8,000 is the cap and is reached only once the monthly accumulation reaches 80 months or more. In this case 15 months should give the lower $1,500.

  3. ✗ 错误。掉入“真自住就不罚款”的误区。2016 年税改后，主居所免税的享受以主动申报并指定为法定前置要件；忘记申报后补做指定属于税法特许程序，必须承担 s.220(3.5) 罚款。Incorrect. This falls into the misconception that a genuine home lived in is never penalized. After the 2016 reform, enjoying the principal residence exemption has the legal precondition of reporting and designating. Making a designation late after forgetting to report is a special procedure under the law and must bear the s.220(3.5) penalty.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据 ITA s.12(13)(b)(vi)，因就业或就学发生的合格搬迁（eligible relocation，距离缩短至少 40 公里）属于九项生活事件例外之一；且法条特别明确在此处不适用“新工作地点与新居须在加拿大境内”的通常限制。Grace 迁往美国西雅图就职完全符合该例外，不落入翻炒规则，其利得按资本利得处理并可主张主居所免税。Correct. Under ITA s.12(13)(b)(vi), an eligible relocation (shortening the distance by at least 40 km) for employment or study is one of the nine life event exceptions, and the law specifically says that the usual limit that the new work location and new home be in Canada does not apply here. Grace moving to Seattle, USA for work fully fits the exception, does not fall within the flipping rule, and her gain is treated as a capital gain and can claim the principal residence exemption.

  4. ✗ 错误。20% 税率是 BC 省翻炒税的规则，并非联邦所得税规则；且符合生活事件例外时并不征收惩罚税。Incorrect. The 20% rate is a BC flipping tax rule, not a federal income tax rule. And when a life event exception applies, no penalty tax is imposed.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

In January 2025, Nathan and his spouse purchased a home. In August 2025, their marriage broke down and they separated. Nathan sold the home in October 2025 (holding period: 270 days) to divide assets. At the time of the sale, Nathan and his spouse had been living separate and apart due to the breakdown of their marriage for 95 consecutive days. Does Nathan qualify for an exception from the federal flipping rule?

2025 年 1 月，Nathan 与配偶购入一套住宅。2025 年 8 月，两人婚姻破裂并开始分居。Nathan 于 2025 年 10 月出售了该房屋（持有期：270 天）以分割财产。在出售时，Nathan 与其配偶因婚姻破裂已经连续分居了 95 天。Nathan 是否符合联邦翻炒规则的生活事件例外？

  1. ✗ 错误。税法并不要求必须取得法院正式离婚判决书，只需因婚姻或同居关系破裂而分居即可。Incorrect. The law does not require a formal divorce decree issued by a court. It requires only separation because of the breakdown of the marriage or common-law relationship.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据 ITA s.12(13)(b)(iii)，因婚姻或同居伴侣关系破裂而处置房产属于生活事件例外之一，但法条设有一项客观时间要件：处置前必须已经分居至少 90 天（at least 90 days）。Nathan 夫妇已连续分居 95 天，满足了法定天数要求，因此完全符合例外条件，免受翻炒规则定性。Correct. Under ITA s.12(13)(b)(iii), a disposition because of the breakdown of a marriage or common-law partnership is one of the life event exceptions, but the law sets an objective time condition: the couple must have lived separate for at least 90 days before the disposition. Nathan and his spouse had lived apart continuously for 95 days, meeting the statutory number of days, so they fully qualify for the exception and escape flipping characterization.

  3. ✗ 错误。法定分居门槛是 90 天而非 365 天，切忌将连续持有期门槛与分居时间门槛相混淆。Incorrect. The statutory separation threshold is 90 days, not 365 days. Do not confuse the consecutive holding period threshold with the separation time threshold.

  4. ✗ 错误。税法中完全不存在将卖房款上缴 CRA 托管的荒谬要求。Incorrect. The law has no such absurd requirement of handing sale proceeds to CRA in escrow.

第 7 题Q7BC翻炒税与联邦规则差异BC flipping tax versus the federal rule★★★陷阱：将联邦自动豁免误套用于BC省

In May 2025, a homeowner purchased a single-family house in Surrey, BC, and occupied it as their primary residence. In July 2026 (holding period: 425 continuous days), the homeowner sold the property for a profit of $80,000. When preparing the 2026 tax filings, which of the following statements correctly reflects the treatment under the BC Home Flipping Tax compared to federal rules?

2025 年 5 月，一位房主在 BC 省素里购入一套独立屋并作为主要居所自住。2026 年 7 月（持有期：连续 425 天），该房主以 $80,000 的利润出售了该房产。在准备 2026 年税表时，下列哪项陈述正确反映了《BC 省住宅物业短期持有利润税法》相比于联邦所得税规则的处理要求？

  1. ✓ 正确。联邦：持有 425 天已超过 365 天，不触发联邦翻炒规则。BC：门槛 730 天，425 天仍应税（递减税率）；作为主要居所且持有满 365 天，可减最高 20,000 元的主要居所扣除。Correct. Federal: holding for 425 days exceeds 365 days, so the federal flipping rule is not triggered. BC: the threshold is 730 days, so 425 days is still taxable (at a declining rate). Because it was a primary residence held at least 365 days, the primary residence deduction of up to $20,000 can reduce the tax.

  2. ✗ 错误。混淆了管辖权与门槛。联邦翻炒规则门槛为 365 天，425 天已脱离联邦翻炒范围，联邦利得仍为资本利得；730 天是 BC 省独立法案的门槛。Incorrect. This confuses jurisdiction with threshold. The federal flipping threshold is 365 days, and 425 days is outside the federal flipping rule, so the federal gain remains a capital gain. 730 days is the threshold of the separate BC statute.

  3. ✗ 错误。把门槛完全记反了。BC 省主要居所扣除恰恰要求持有满 365 天以上才给，不足 365 天的高税率持有者反而不享有该项扣除。Incorrect. This reverses the threshold completely. The BC primary residence deduction is given only when the property is held for at least 365 days, and an owner with a shorter holding and the higher rate does not get the deduction.

  4. ✗ 错误。虽然 2024 税年考试口径定稿时 BC 翻炒税尚未开征（考试口径覆盖为零），但在 2026 现行税法下，BC 翻炒税是一部完全独立的省级税法，既不通过联邦 Schedule 3 管理，也不与联邦税合并，处置后 90 天内必须向 BC 财政厅独立交表申报。Incorrect. Although when the 2024 tax-year exam benchmark was finalized the BC flipping tax had not yet been introduced (so the exam benchmark coverage is zero), under the 2026 tax rules in force the BC flipping tax is a completely independent provincial statute. It is neither administered through federal Schedule 3 nor merged with federal tax, and it must be filed separately with the BC Ministry of Finance within 90 days after the disposition.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An employee in Victoria, BC purchased a condominium on March 1, 2025. On October 1, 2025 (holding period: 214 days), the employee was involuntarily laid off by their employer and sold the condominium at a profit of $40,000. The seller believes that because involuntary termination is a recognized life event, no tax return of any kind needs to be filed with the BC Ministry of Finance. What is the correct legal position?

BC 省维多利亚的一名雇员于 2025 年 3 月 1 日购买了一套公寓。2025 年 10 月 1 日（持有期：214 天），该雇员被雇主非自愿解雇，随后以 $40,000 的利润卖掉了该公寓。该卖家认为，既然非自愿解雇是公认的生活事件，自己就完全无需向 BC 省财政厅提交任何申报表。正确的法律事实是什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是混淆联邦与 BC 省程序要求的致命误区。联邦九项例外符合即适用，而 BC 省翻炒税的生活事件豁免属于“必须申报类”，不交表不能享受豁免。Incorrect. This is a fatal confusion of federal and BC procedural requirements. The federal nine exceptions apply once they are met, while a life event exemption under the BC flipping tax is of the "must file" kind, and without a filing the exemption cannot be enjoyed.

  2. ✗ 错误。受雇人员的非自愿解雇在 BC 省翻炒税法下同样被列为法定生活事件豁免清单（但不适用于自雇者）。Incorrect. Involuntary termination of an employee is also on the list of statutory life event exemptions under the BC flipping tax law (but does not apply to the self-employed).

  3. ✗ 错误。两套系统完全独立运作，CRA 不会替 BC 省代收或流转翻炒税申报。Incorrect. The two systems operate completely independently, and CRA does not collect or forward BC flipping tax returns on the province's behalf.

  4. ✓ 正确。根据《BC 省住宅物业短期持有利润税法》，生活事件豁免并非自动生效，纳税人必须在房产处置后 90 天内向 BC 财政厅单独提交翻炒税申报表才能取得该豁免。若未在 90 天内交表，即使事实完全符合解雇条件，也将面临至少 $500 的最低迟报罚款 (late-filing penalty)。Correct. Under the BC Residential Property (Short-Term Holding) Profit Tax Act, a life event exemption does not take effect automatically. The taxpayer must file a separate flipping tax return with the BC Ministry of Finance within 90 days after the disposition to obtain it. If the return is not filed within 90 days, even when the facts fully meet the termination condition, a late-filing penalty of at least $500 applies.
