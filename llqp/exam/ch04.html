本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 三要素拆分与良性循环推导题：给出保费缴入、账户价值变化与死亡给付方式，考察保费税（premium tax）、保险成本（COI）、净风险保额（NAAR）与累积账户（accumulating fund）之间的扣费先后顺序及因果链条。Unbundled three elements and virtuous circle deduction questions: Presenting premium deposits, account value fluctuations, and death benefit options, testing the sequential deduction order and causal chain among premium tax, cost of insurance (COI), net amount at risk (NAAR), and the accumulating fund.
  • YRT 与 LCOI 成本曲线匹配题：给出客户的持有期限（如短中期 10–15 年或终身持有）、预算及现金价值积累诉求，测试该选择逐年更新定期成本（YRT）还是平准保险成本（LCOI），以及长期只缴最低保费配合 YRT 导致保单枯竭的机制。YRT versus LCOI cost curve matching questions: Providing a client's holding horizon (such as short-to-medium term of 10 to 15 years or lifetime holding), budget, and cash value accumulation goals, testing whether to recommend yearly renewable term (YRT) or level cost of insurance (LCOI), as well as the mechanics of policy depletion caused by long-term minimum funding under YRT.
  • 平额与平额加账户价值对比题：通过对比受益人最终所得与每月扣费高低，考察净风险保额（NAAR）在不同身故给付方式下是否随账户价值增长而缩减，识别「良性循环」在何种给付方式下生效。Level versus level plus account value comparison questions: Comparing the beneficiary's net payout with monthly deduction amounts, testing whether the net amount at risk (NAAR) decreases as account value grows under different death benefit options, and identifying under which option the virtuous circle takes effect.
  • 免税测试与存量保单变更风险题：设置客户超额缴费或持 2016 年及以前签发的旧保单申请增额、转换、更换被保险人的场景，考察最高缴费上限（maximum funded）、免税测试（exemption test）后果及 2017 年 1 月 1 日代次（G1/G2/G3）降级风险。Exemption test and existing policy change risk questions: Setting scenarios where a client makes excess deposits or requests a face amount increase, conversion, or life insured change on a grandfathered policy issued on or before December 31, 2016, testing maximum funding limits, exemption test consequences, and the risk of policy generation (G1/G2/G3) demotion after January 1, 2017.
  • 累积账户六项权利与融资对比题：设置客户急需资金周转或规划退休现金流的场景，辨析部分提取、保费抵消、保单贷款（50%–90% 限额）、担保转让（collateral assignment）与杠杆化（leveraging）在税务处置、身故给付扣减及银行追偿上的差异。Accumulating fund six rights and financing comparison questions: Presenting scenarios where a client requires emergency liquidity or plans retirement cash flow, distinguishing among partial withdrawals, premium offset, policy loans (50% to 90% limit), collateral assignment, and leveraging regarding tax disposition, death benefit deductions, and lender recourse.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 期缴溢价假定陷阱Modal factor premium surcharge assumption trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生习惯了定期险和传统终身寿险月缴年化总额高于年缴（例题高约 6%），误以为万能寿险按月缴费也会被乘以按期系数（modal factor）。Accustomed to term and traditional whole life insurance where monthly annualized premiums exceed annual premiums (commonly around 6%), candidates mistakenly assume that universal life monthly payments are also multiplied by a modal factor.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干讨论万能寿险（UL）的月缴、季缴或半年缴与年缴保费总额差异，或要求计算期缴附加费。The question stem discusses the difference in annualized total premiums between monthly, quarterly, or semi-annual payments and annual payments in universal life (UL), or asks to calculate a modal surcharge.

⚠ YRT前期低价幻觉Illusion of low initial YRT pricing

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 只看到 YRT 早期扣费远低于 LCOI 且早期现金价值积累快，忽略了中后期死亡成本按当年年龄急剧攀升，长期只缴最低保费会抽干账户价值导致保单失效。Focusing only on the fact that early YRT deductions are far lower than LCOI and build cash value faster early on, candidates overlook that mortality costs climb steeply with attained age in middle and later years, so paying only minimum premiums long term will drain the account and cause the policy to lapse.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现「打算持有至终身/高龄」「选择 YRT 成本结构」以及「长期按最低缴费（minimally funded）支付」。The question stem simultaneously mentions intending to hold the policy for life or into old age, selecting a YRT cost structure, and paying only minimum funding over the long term.

⚠ NAAR与给付方式倒置Inverting NAAR and death benefit options

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为只要账户价值上涨，所有万能寿险的保险成本都会下降，忽略了「平额加账户价值」下净风险保额（NAAR）恒等于固定保额，扣费不会因账户增长而减少。Mistakenly assuming that insurance costs decrease whenever account value rises across all universal life policies, overlooking that under level plus account value, the net amount at risk (NAAR) remains constant at the fixed face amount, so deductions do not drop as the account grows.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干指定死亡给付方式为「平额加账户价值（level death benefit plus account value）」，并询问账户增值对当期 COI 扣费或 NAAR 的影响。The question stem specifies the death benefit option as level death benefit plus account value and asks about the impact of account growth on current COI charges or NAAR.

⚠ 老客户增额免核保误区Assuming face amount increases for existing clients require no underwriting

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到万能寿险具备「保额可调」的灵活性，就误以为存量保单持有人调高保额只是修改后台参数，不需要重新核保，或忽略 2017 年前旧单变更会触发代次降级。Seeing that universal life offers adjustable coverage, candidates mistakenly assume increasing the face amount for an existing policyholder is merely an administrative adjustment requiring no underwriting, or overlook that significant changes to pre-2017 policies trigger policy generation demotion.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「现有保单持有人因家庭或负债变化申请提高保额」或「对 2016 年及以前签发的保单进行重大变更」。The question stem mentions an existing policyholder applying to increase coverage due to family or debt changes, or making major changes to a policy issued on or before December 31, 2016.

⚠ 杠杆化与担保转让混淆Confusing leveraging with collateral assignment

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把「向保险公司申请保单贷款」「向银行做担保转让借款」与「保单杠杆化」混为一谈，误以为保单贷款不触发处置，或把杠杆化借来的钱当成无追偿风险的免税收入。Conflating policy loans from the insurer, collateral assignment bank loans, and policy leveraging, mistakenly believing policy loans never trigger a tax disposition, or treating borrowed funds from leveraging as risk-free tax-free income with no lender recourse.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及「动用累积账户价值获取现金流」，对比向保司借钱与向银行抵押借钱的税务后果、利息偿还方式及市场下跌时的强平风险。The question stem involves accessing accumulating fund values for cash flow, contrasting the tax treatment, interest repayment mechanics, and forced liquidation risks between insurer policy loans and third-party bank collateral assignment loans.

第 1 题Q1账户价值不足与保单失效的四类触发情形Four triggers of insufficient account value and policy lapse★★陷阱：YRT前期低价幻觉

Clara purchased a universal life policy ten years ago and chose to keep her payments at the minimally funded level while allocating her accumulating fund entirely to equity-linked index accounts. After taking a partial withdrawal last year and experiencing negative market returns this year, she receives a notice from the insurer asking for additional deposits. Why is Clara's policy now at risk of lapsing?

Clara 十年前购买了一份万能寿险，选择长期按最低缴费水平支付保费，并将累积账户全部配置于权益联动指数账户。去年她进行了一次部分提取，今年又遇上市场负回报，随后收到保险公司要求追加缴款的通知。为什么 Clara 的保单现在面临失效风险？

  1. ✓ 万能寿险（universal life insurance）存续的底线是累积账户（accumulating fund）价值必须始终盖得住每月扣除的保险成本（COI）与管理费。长期只缴最低缴费（minimally funded）、大额或部分提取账户价值、以及指数账户出现负回报，会同时触发「账户不增长」与「净风险保额（NAAR）居高不下导致扣费不降」，加速耗尽账户价值并引发失效风险。Correct. The bottom line for keeping a universal life insurance policy in force is that the accumulating fund value must always be sufficient to cover the monthly cost of insurance (COI) and administration charges. Paying only the minimum premium long term (minimally funded), taking large or partial withdrawals of account value, and experiencing negative returns in index accounts simultaneously cause the account to stagnate while the net amount at risk (NAAR) remains high—preventing deduction amounts from dropping, rapidly exhausting the account value, and triggering lapse risk.

  2. ✗ 属于范围误判陷阱：免税测试（exemption test）限制的是超额多存钱，而非提取资金；部分提取（partial withdrawal）威胁保单存续是因为削弱了账户价值和良性循环，而非违反免税测试。Falls into the 'misjudging scope' trap. The exemption test restricts depositing excess funds into a policy rather than withdrawing funds; a partial withdrawal threatens policy survival because it drains the account value and weakens the virtuous circle, not because it violates the exemption test.

  3. ✗ 同时犯了两处错误：一是只有日息账户（DIA）和保证投资账户（GIA）保底通常为 0%，指数或基金联动账户可能发生亏损；二是万能寿险没有 6% 的按期系数（modal factor）。Makes two errors simultaneously: first, only daily interest accounts (DIA) and guaranteed investment accounts (GIA) typically carry a 0% floor, whereas index-linked or fund-linked accounts can suffer investment losses; second, universal life insurance has no 6% modal factor.

  4. ✗ 混淆了设计目标与合同保证：考试口径说明最低缴费（minimally funded）虽常以维持至指定年龄（例为 100 岁）为设计目标，但绝不等于合同承诺终身有效，实际回报转负和提款后仍会因余额不足而失效。Conflates design targets with contractual guarantees. Exam benchmarks explain that while minimum funding is often designed to keep coverage in force to a specified age (such as age 100), this is not a contractual guarantee of lifetime coverage; if actual returns turn negative or withdrawals occur, the policy can still lapse due to an insufficient balance.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Victor holds a universal life policy and decides to pause his premium payments for several years while his accumulating fund is invested in mutual fund-linked accounts. During a prolonged market downturn, what combined mechanism accelerates the depletion of his policy's accumulating fund?

Victor 持有一份万能寿险，在累积账户配置于共同基金联动账户期间决定暂停缴纳保费数年。在持续的市场低迷期，哪项叠加机制会加速消耗他保单的累积账户价值？

  1. ✗ 混淆了停缴保费与退保：退保费用（surrender charge）仅在保单前 5–10 年正式退保时收取，暂停缴费（只要账户价值足够支付扣费）不会直接触发退保费。Conflates pausing premiums with a policy surrender. Surrender charges are levied only when the policy is formally surrendered in its first 5 to 10 years; pausing premium deposits (as long as the account value covers deductions) does not trigger a surrender charge.

  2. ✗ 无中生有：停缴保费不会自动改变死亡给付方式，保单只是依靠现有累积账户（accumulating fund）余额继续支付每月扣费。Fabricates an ungrounded rule. Pausing premiums does not automatically change the death benefit option; the policy simply relies on the existing accumulating fund balance to continue paying monthly charges.

  3. ✓ 当基金联动账户表现不佳甚至亏损且客户停缴保费时，会同时触发双重不利因素：累积账户（accumulating fund）自身缩水，同时因为账户价值未能压低净风险保额（net amount at risk, NAAR），每月扣除的保险成本（COI）维持在高位，两者叠加会迅速抽干账户价值。Correct. When fund-linked accounts underperform or suffer losses while the client pauses premium deposits, a dual headwind is triggered: the accumulating fund itself shrinks, and because the account value fails to reduce the net amount at risk (NAAR), monthly cost of insurance (COI) deductions remain high, rapidly draining the account value.

  4. ✗ 错记了保费税（premium tax）的征收时点：保费税仅在客户实际缴入保费时按整笔保费扣除，停缴期间没有新保费进入，不会按月从账户余额中扣保费税。Mistakes the timing of premium tax assessment. Provincial premium tax is deducted from the gross premium only when the client actually deposits a premium; during a premium pause, no new premiums enter, so no monthly premium tax is deducted from the account balance.

第 2 题Q2免税测试上限与2017年前旧单代次风险Exemption test ceiling and pre-2017 grandfathered policy generation risk★★★陷阱：老客户增额免核保误区

Hannah owns a universal life policy issued before 2017 that has accumulated significant cash value under maximum funded rules. In 2026, she asks her advisor to replace the life insured on the contract and substantially increase the coverage amount. Beyond medical underwriting, what major tax-related risk must the advisor investigate before making these changes?

Hannah 持有一份 2017 年以前签发的万能寿险，在最高缴费规则下已积累了可观的现金价值。2026 年，她要求顾问更换合同上的被保险人并大幅提高保额。除了核保之外，顾问在动手修改前必须先核查哪项重大的税务相关风险？

  1. ✓ 免税测试（exemption test）精算参数在 2017 年 1 月 1 日经历重大更新，与保单代次（G1/G2/G3 policy classification）直接挂钩。对 2016 年及以前签发的存量保单进行增加保额、转换保单类型或更换被保险人等操作，可能导致旧保单丧失既得权利而发生代次「降级」，永久失去原本更宽松的免税测试空间。Correct. Actuarial parameters for the exemption test underwent a major overhaul on January 1, 2017, directly tying to policy generation classifications (G1, G2, and G3). 一位创业者持有一份万能寿险，其累积账户价值为 $120,000，调整后成本基础（ACB）为 $40,000。她需要 $30,000 用于公司临时周转，正在比较「直接向保险公司申请保单贷款」与「用保单向银行做担保转让申请 $30,000 银行贷款」。这两种方案相比有何异同？

  1. ✗ 落入「杠杆化与担保转让混淆」陷阱：保单贷款（policy loan）本身在税法上属于处置（只是本题 $30,000 未超过 ACB $40,000 故无当期应税利得），且无论哪种贷款，只要身故时尚未偿还，都会扣减受益人最终到手的净额。Falls into the 'confusing leveraging with collateral assignment' trap. A policy loan legally constitutes a disposition under tax law (though no taxable gain arises here because the $30,000 loan does not exceed the $40,000 ACB), and regardless of loan type, any unpaid balance at death reduces the net payout to the beneficiary.

  2. ✗ 数字与法律性质均错：保单贷款（policy loan）通常可借现金价值的 50%–90%（而非 25%），且 $30,000 小于 ACB $40,000 不产生当期应税利得；担保转让（collateral assignment）只是抵押担保而非永久转让保单所有权。Erroneous in figures and legal character. Policy loans typically permit borrowing 50% to 90% of cash value (not 25%), and borrowing $30,000 against a $40,000 ACB generates no immediate taxable gain; collateral assignment serves merely as security for a loan rather than a permanent transfer of ownership.

  3. ✓ 向保司申请保单贷款（policy loan，可借现金价值的 50%–90%）在税法上属于处置，但因借款额 $30,000 未超过 ACB $40,000，不产生当期应税利得，未偿本息会在身故时从身故给付中扣除；而向银行做担保转让（collateral assignment）单纯作借款担保通常不构成处置，保单内 $120,000 现金价值继续免税增值，银行仅按尚欠余额受偿，最终留给受益人的净额与保单贷款路径可能有实质差异。Correct. Applying for a policy loan from the insurer (borrowing 50% to 90% of cash value) constitutes a disposition under tax law, but because the $30,000 loan does not exceed the $40,000 ACB, no immediate taxable gain arises, and unpaid principal and interest are deducted from the death benefit; a collateral assignment to a bank solely as loan security generally does not trigger a disposition, allowing the $120,000 cash value to compound tax-sheltered while the bank is entitled only to outstanding loan balances, resulting in substantive differences in net proceeds left to beneficiaries.

  4. ✗ 颠倒了资金来源：担保转让（collateral assignment）是由银行发放贷款，保单内的 $120,000 累积账户并不被赎回或扣除退保费，而是继续留在保单内滚动增值。Inverts the funding source. Under a collateral assignment, the bank advances the loan funds, while the $120,000 in the accumulating fund is neither surrendered nor penalized, continuing to grow inside the policy.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

After twenty years of funding her universal life policy, Maya has built a substantial accumulating fund and wishes to stop paying out-of-pocket premiums while keeping her insurance coverage in force without triggering a taxable withdrawal. Which contractual right allows her to do this, and what condition must hold?

在为万能寿险缴费二十年后，Maya 已经积累了可观的账户价值，她希望停止自掏腰包缴纳保费，同时维持保单保障有效且不触发应税提款。哪项合同权利允许她这样做，且必须满足什么条件？

  1. ✗ 混淆了部分提取、保单贷款比例与保费抵消：部分提取（partial withdrawal）视为部分退保，可能产生应税所得；50%–90% 是保单贷款（policy loan）的额度比例。Conflates partial withdrawals, policy loan limits, and premium offset. A partial withdrawal is treated as a partial surrender that may trigger taxable income; 50% to 90% represents the borrowing limit for a policy loan.

  2. ✓ 保费抵消（premium offset）是累积账户六项权利之一：当账户价值积累到一定程度后，保单持有人可以停缴保费，靠账户自身留存价值及回报覆盖未来的保险成本（COI）与管理费，前提是账户价值始终足以支付每月扣费，否则保单仍可能失效。Correct. Premium offset is one of the six rights of the accumulating fund: once account value has accumulated sufficiently, the policyholder may pause premium payments and rely on the internal cash value and investment earnings to cover future cost of insurance (COI) and admin charges, provided the account value remains sufficient to pay monthly deductions, failing which the policy may lapse.

  3. ✗ 概念张冠李戴：担保转让（collateral assignment）是用保单向第三方银行抵押借款，与停缴保费由账户自付扣费的保费抵消（premium offset）毫无关系。Misattributes concepts. A collateral assignment pledges the policy as collateral for a third-party bank loan and has nothing to do with premium offset, where the policy pays its own deductions after premiums are paused.

  4. ✗ 混淆了杠杆化（leveraging）与保费抵消：杠杆化是向银行抵押连续借款用于退休现金流，既不能免除保单内部的 COI 扣费，更不能消除投资不佳导致保单失效或银行追偿的风险。Conflates leveraging with premium offset. Leveraging involves securing ongoing bank loans against the policy for retirement cash flow; it neither eliminates internal COI deductions nor protects against the risk of policy lapse or bank recourse from weak performance.

答案Answer：b

第 7 题Q7万能寿险无按期系数（modal factor）规则Universal life absence of modal factor rules★陷阱：期缴溢价假定陷阱

A client who currently pays an annual premium into a universal life policy asks his advisor how much extra he will be charged in modal factors if he switches to monthly deposits of one-twelfth of his planned annual amount. Unlike a traditional term or whole life policy—where an exam example shows monthly payments costing roughly 6% more per year than an annual payment—how does a universal life policy treat monthly deposits?

一位目前按年向万能寿险缴纳保费的客户询问顾问：如果他改为按月存入年计划金额的十二分之一，会因为按期系数被多收多少钱。不同于传统定期险或终身寿险（考试口径某例题显示月缴年化总额比年缴高约 6%），万能寿险如何对待按月缴费？

  1. ✗ 直接掉入「期缴溢价假定陷阱」：6% 是定期险与传统终身寿险在考试口径某月缴例题中的年化溢价幅度，万能寿险（universal life insurance）根本没有按期系数（modal factor）。Falls directly into the 'modal factor premium surcharge assumption' trap. The 6% figure represents the annualized surcharge illustrative of monthly payments in term and traditional whole life exams; universal life insurance has no modal factor whatsoever.

  2. ✗ 凭空捏造数字与规则：10–15 年是考试资料中讨论 YRT 短中期持有期的年数，万能寿险无论选 YRT 还是 LCOI 均不收取按期系数（modal factor）。Fabricates rules and figures out of thin air. The 10 to 15 year timeframe relates to short-to-medium holding horizons for YRT; universal life never charges a modal factor whether YRT or LCOI is chosen.

  3. ✗ 拼凑无关数字：$40,000 是案例三中的调整后成本基础（ACB）金额，万能寿险无按期系数（modal factor）是产品本身的结构特征，不附带最低存款门槛。Cobbles together irrelevant figures. The $40,000 figure is the adjusted cost basis (ACB) amount from Case 3; the absence of a modal factor in universal life is a structural product feature, not contingent on any minimum deposit threshold.

  4. ✓ 万能寿险没有按期系数（modal factor）。因为万能寿险把保费拆分为入账后按月/按年实际扣除保险成本（COI）与管理费，客户选择月缴、季缴、半年缴在年化后的缴费总额与年缴完全相同，不会像传统捆绑产品那样产生期缴附加溢价。Correct. Universal life insurance does not have a modal factor. Because universal life unbundles premiums so that actual cost of insurance (COI) and administration charges are deducted monthly or annually after funds enter the account, choosing monthly, quarterly, or semi-annual schedules produces the exact same annualized total as an annual payment, without generating the modal surcharge seen in traditional bundled products.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An advisor is reviewing four statements about how universal life insurance operates compared with traditional bundled permanent policies. Which statement is free from common exam misconceptions?

一位顾问正在审阅四条关于万能寿险与传统捆绑型永久寿险运作差异的陈述。哪一条陈述没有落入常见的考试误区？

  1. ✗ 落入「期缴溢价假定陷阱」：万能寿险没有按期系数（modal factor），季缴年化总额与年缴完全一致。Falls into the 'modal factor premium surcharge assumption' trap. Universal life has no modal factor, so quarterly deposits annualized produce the identical total as annual payments.

  2. ✓ 准确概括了两大核心防坑要点：第一，万能寿险没有按期系数（modal factor），分期缴纳年化总额与年缴相同；第二，每期死亡风险扣费（COI）是基于净风险保额（net amount at risk, NAAR ＝ 身故给付 − 账户价值）与适用费率计算，而非简单等同于身故给付本身。Correct. Accurately synthesizes two core principles: first, universal life has no modal factor, meaning annualized fractional deposits equal annual payments; second, periodic mortality charges (COI) are calculated from the net amount at risk (NAAR = death benefit minus account value) and applicable rates, rather than the face amount alone.

  3. ✗ 落入「NAAR与给付方式倒置」陷阱：只有平额死亡给付（level death benefit）才会随账户价值增长压低净风险保额（NAAR），平额加账户价值下 NAAR 恒等于固定保额。Falls into the 'inverting NAAR and death benefit options' trap. Only under a level death benefit does account value growth shrink the net amount at risk (NAAR); under level plus account value, NAAR remains constant at the fixed face amount.

  4. ✗ 落入范围误判陷阱：免税测试（exemption test）限制的是免税多存钱的空间，失去免税资格影响的是整张保单的税务待遇，并不等于身故保障自动终止。Falls into the 'misjudging scope' trap. The exemption test limits the room for tax-sheltered deposits; losing tax-exempt status impacts the policy's tax treatment rather than automatically terminating the death benefit.
