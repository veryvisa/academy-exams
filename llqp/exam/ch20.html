① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 企业画像配资金方式题：给出雇主规模、现金流状况、理赔历史稳定性及条款自主权诉求，考你判断该选不返还式记账（non-refund accounting）、返还式记账（refund accounting）还是纯行政服务型（Administrative Services Only, ASO）。Matching employer profile to funding method: Given an employer's group size, cash flow condition, stability of claims history, and demand for plan design autonomy, test your ability to determine whether to select non-refund accounting, refund accounting, or Administrative Services Only (ASO).
  • 混合费率与信度判定题：给出团体人数（如25人分水岭）、流动率（5–10%/年为低）与历史理赔年限，考你判断信度（credibility）高低，或用信度加权经验费率（experience rating）与手册费率（manual rating）算出混合费率（blended rating）。Blended rating and credibility determination: Given group size (e.g., the 25-employee threshold), annual employee turnover rate (5-10% per year is considered low), and years of claims history, test your ability to assess credibility levels or calculate a blended rate by weighting experience rating and manual rating by credibility.
  • 双职家庭协调给付（COB）计算题：给出夫妻双方各自雇主计划的报销比例及父母生日月份，考你按生日规则（birthday rule）判断首付方，再按「较小者」原则算出次付方补差金额与家庭自付额。Dual-income family coordination of benefits (COB) calculation: Given the reimbursement percentages under both spouses' employer plans and the parents' birth months, test your ability to identify the primary payer using the birthday rule, and calculate the secondary payer's top-up amount and the out-of-pocket family cost using the lesser-of rule.
  • 团体伤残给付税务与累计缴费抵减题：给出雇主与员工共同分摊保费（如各付50%）的长期伤残（LTD）计划，考你按累计未抵减员工缴费额（而非当年保费分摊比例）扣减当年应税给付。Taxation of group disability benefits and cumulative contribution deductions: Given a long-term disability (LTD) plan where the employer and employee share premium costs (e.g., 50% each), test your ability to deduct cumulative unapplied employee contributions (rather than applying the annual premium split percentage) from the current year's taxable disability benefits.
  • EI病假福利与ESDC保费减免资格题：给出雇主短期伤残（STD）计划的给付周数（15周）与替代比例（55%），考你区分EI病假福利周数双口径（考试口径15周 vs 现行26周）以及ESDC保费减免计划的资格门槛。EI sickness benefits and ESDC premium reduction eligibility: Given an employer's short-term disability (STD) benefit duration (15 weeks) and income replacement ratio (55%), test your ability to distinguish the dual benchmarks for EI sickness benefit duration (15 weeks for exam purposes vs. 26 weeks under current law) and the eligibility thresholds for the ESDC EI Premium Reduction Program.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 保费比例当应税比例Treating premium sharing percentage as taxable benefit percentage

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到雇主与员工各付50%保费，就直觉把当年收到的伤残给付按50%算应税收入，忽略了雇主一旦参与缴费，整笔给付须减去员工历年尚未抵减的累计缴费总额。Seeing that the employer and employee each pay 50% of the premium leads to the intuitive mistake of treating 50% of the disability benefits received in the year as taxable income, overlooking that once the employer contributes, the entire benefit is taxable after deducting the employee's cumulative unapplied contributions from previous years.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现雇主与员工分摊保费百分比（如50%）以及员工历年累计未抵减缴费金额（如$3,600）。The question stem simultaneously provides an employer-employee premium split percentage (e.g., 50%) and the employee's cumulative unapplied contribution amount (e.g., $3,600).

⚠ 混合费率信度权重套反Reversing credibility weighting in blended rating

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 计算混合费率时把信度（credibility）乘到了手册费率（manual rating）上，或者误以为小团体人数少更好算所以信度更高。Multiplying credibility by the manual rate instead of the experience rate when calculating a blended rate, or mistakenly assuming smaller groups have higher credibility because fewer members are easier to calculate.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出信度百分比（如30%）及经验费率、手册费率两个数字，或询问新成立/25人以下小团体的信度评级。The question stem gives a credibility percentage (e.g., 30%) alongside both experience and manual rates, or asks for the credibility rating of a newly formed group or one with fewer than 25 employees.

⚠ 生日规则看错判据与次付漏比大小Misjudging birthday rule criteria and omitting the lesser-of comparison for secondary payer

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为谁年纪大、谁保额高或谁报销比例高谁先赔；算次付方时直接拿账单原价乘次付比例，忘了同首付未覆盖余额比大小取较小者。Mistakenly assuming that the older parent, the plan with higher coverage, or the plan with a higher reimbursement percentage pays first; or calculating the secondary payer's benefit by multiplying the original bill by the secondary percentage while forgetting to take the lesser of that amount and the unpaid balance from the primary plan.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现夫妻双方生日月份、子女医疗或牙科账单金额，以及两份团体计划不同的报销比例（如80%与100%）。The question stem provides both spouses' birth months, an eligible medical or dental bill for a dependent child, and differing reimbursement percentages under two group plans (e.g., 80% and 100%).

⚠ EI周数延长与ESDC门槛混淆Confusing extended EI duration with the ESDC reduction threshold

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 知道现行EI病假福利已延长至26周，就想当然认为雇主申请ESDC保费减免的STD计划最低周数门槛也涨到了26周，或忘了考场旧口径仍可能按15周出题。Knowing that current EI sickness benefits have been extended to 26 weeks leads to assuming that the minimum benefit duration for an employer STD plan to qualify for an ESDC premium reduction has also risen to 26 weeks, or forgetting that the exam benchmark may still test the 15-week rule.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干询问雇主STD计划申请EI保费减免（EI Premium Reduction Program）的最低给付周数要求（至少15周）或EI病假福利周数。The question stem asks for the minimum benefit duration requirement (at least 15 weeks) for an employer STD plan to qualify for the EI Premium Reduction Program, or asks about EI sickness benefit duration.

⚠ ASO误当无保险或全保无视风险Misunderstanding ASO as unmanaged self-insurance or ignoring risk volatility

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把纯行政服务型（ASO）当成雇主自己处理理赔不找第三方，或给现金流吃紧、厌恶波动的小微雇主推荐ASO而忽略其承受不了理赔波动。Treating Administrative Services Only (ASO) as an employer self-administering claims without a third party, or recommending ASO to a micro-employer with tight cash flow and risk aversion while ignoring its inability to absorb claims fluctuations.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述雇主希望省保费或自定义福利清单，同时交代其员工人数（如25人以下或40人）、现金流状况及风险承受能力。The question stem describes an employer seeking to reduce premium costs or customize its benefit formulary, while noting its headcount (e.g., fewer than 25 or 40 employees), cash flow condition, and risk tolerance.

第 1 题Q1三种资金方式的选择与风险承担Selecting funding methods and allocating claims risk★★陷阱：ASO误当无保险或全保无视风险

A software firm in Richmond has 40 employees and 5 years of stable extended health and dental claims experience. Its cash flow is healthy, and the founders want to lower long-term benefit costs and customize the drug formulary, but a sudden cluster of high-cost specialty drug claims would still strain a 40-person budget. Which funding recommendation best balances their goals and group size?

列治文一家拥有40名员工的软件公司创立5年，扩展医疗与牙科理赔经验平稳，现金流健康。创始人希望降低长期福利成本并自主决定药品清单范围，但若突然集中出现几笔高额专科用药理赔，对40人规模的预算仍会造成冲击。哪种资金方式建议最能平衡其目标与团体规模？

  1. ✗ 错误。40人规模已超过25人的联合经验池门槛，法律并未禁止40人团体采用返还式记账或ASO，一直停留在不返还式记账（non-refund accounting）无法满足雇主分享结余与逐步提高灵活性的诉求。Incorrect. A group of 40 employees already exceeds the 25-employee pooled experience threshold, and the law does not bar a 40-person group from using refund accounting or ASO. Remaining permanently in non-refund accounting fails to meet the employer's goals of sharing in surpluses and progressively increasing flexibility.

  2. ✓ 正确。40人团体虽现金流健康、理赔平稳，但规模仍偏小、波动性较大；先用返还式记账（refund accounting / retention accounting）与保险公司共担波动并在理赔优于预期时获得部分保费返还，待规模与经验进一步验证后再评估转向纯行政服务型（ASO）配止损保险，最符合三种资金方式的风险光谱逻辑。Correct. Although a 40-person group has healthy cash flow and stable claims, its size remains relatively small and subject to claims volatility. Using refund accounting (retention accounting) as a transitional step to share volatility with the insurer and receive partial premium refunds when claims are lower than expected -- before later evaluating a move to Administrative Services Only (ASO) with stop-loss insurance -- best aligns with the risk spectrum of group funding methods.

  3. ✗ 错误。ASO虽然让雇主享有全部结余，但雇主必须自行承担理赔波动风险，并非零风险（陷阱：ASO误当无保险或全保无视风险）。Incorrect. Although ASO allows the employer to retain 100% of any surplus, the employer must absorb all claims fluctuation risk itself rather than carrying zero risk (Trap: Misunderstanding ASO as unmanaged self-insurance or ignoring risk volatility).

  4. ✗ 错误。员工协助计划（EAP）提供的是心理、戒瘾、婚姻及法律财务咨询，不报销专科处方药费用。Incorrect. An Employee Assistance Plan (EAP) provides counselling for mental health, addiction, family, and legal or financial issues; it does not reimburse specialty prescription drug expenses.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A newly established bakery with fewer than 25 employees has tight monthly cash flow and zero claims history. The owner cannot afford any unexpected year-end benefit deficits. Which funding method should the group broker recommend?

一家新成立、员工少于25人的烘焙坊每月现金流紧绷，且没有任何理赔历史。店主无法承受任何意外的年终福利赤字。团体险经纪应当推荐哪种资金方式？

  1. ✓ 正确。不返还式记账（non-refund accounting / fully insured）由保险公司承担全部理赔风险，理赔劣于预期只反映在下一续保周期的费率调整上，适合现金流有限、厌恶风险、需要成本确定性的雇主。Correct. Under non-refund accounting (fully insured), the insurer assumes all claims risk and absorbs deficits, with worse-than-expected claims reflected only in subsequent renewal rate adjustments. This is ideal for employers with tight cash flow, risk aversion, and a need for budget certainty.

  2. ✗ 错误。返还式记账（refund accounting）要求雇主共担一部分理赔波动，不适合现金流吃紧的小微新团体。Incorrect. Refund accounting requires the employer to share in claims volatility and potential deficits, making it unsuitable for a small start-up with tight cash flow.

  3. ✗ 错误。ASO下雇主须自掏腰包赔付每一笔实际理赔并承担波动风险，现金流紧绷的小店主无法承受（陷阱：ASO误当无保险或全保无视风险）。Incorrect. Under ASO, the employer must directly fund every incurred claim and bear the risk of volatility, which a small business owner with tight monthly cash flow cannot absorb (Trap: Misunderstanding ASO as unmanaged self-insurance or ignoring risk volatility).

  4. ✗ 错误。没有历史理赔数据且少于25人的新团体信度（credibility）为零，必须采用手册费率并入联合经验池，且经验费率是定价方法而非资金方式。Incorrect. A new group with fewer than 25 employees and zero claims history has zero credibility and must be pooled under manual rating; furthermore, experience rating is a pricing method, not a funding method.

第 2 题Q2混合费率与信度加权计算Blended rating and credibility-weighted calculation★★陷阱：混合费率信度权重套反

A mid-sized logistics company has 4 years of complete group claims history. The insurer's actuarial department assigns the group a credibility rating of 30%. Its experience rate is $45 per employee per month, while the manual rate for the industry is $60 per employee per month. What is the company's blended rate per employee per month?

一家中型物流公司拥有4年完整的团体理赔历史。保险公司精算部门评定该团体的信度评级为30%。其经验费率为每人每月$45，同行业手册费率为每人每月$60。该公司的混合费率是每人每月多少？

  1. ✗ 错误。该团体信度（credibility）仅为30%而非100%，不能直接使用纯经验费率$45。Incorrect. The group's credibility is only 30%, not 100%, so the pure experience rate of $45 cannot be used directly.

  2. ✗ 错误。把信度权重套反了：30%是经验费率的权重，70%才是手册费率的权重（陷阱：混合费率信度权重套反）。Incorrect. This reverses the credibility weighting: 30% is the weight for the experience rate, while 70% is the weight for the manual rate (Trap: Reversing credibility weighting in blended rating).

  3. ✓ 正确。混合费率（blended rating）= 信度 × 经验费率 + (1 − 信度) × 手册费率 = 0.3 × $45 + 0.7 × $60 = $13.5 + $42 = $55.50。Correct. Blended rate = (credibility * experience rate) + ((1 - credibility) * manual rate) = (0.3 * $45) + (0.7 * $60) = $13.50 + $42.00 = $55.50.

  4. ✗ 错误。只要信度大于零，就会按信度比例对经验费率与手册费率进行加权，只有无历史数据（信度为零）时才完全退化为手册费率。Incorrect. Whenever credibility exceeds zero, experience and manual rates are weighted proportionally; the formula defaults entirely to the manual rate only when there is zero claims history (credibility = 0).

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

第 7 题Q7团体合同结构与代理人在业务链中的职责Group contract structure and broker responsibilities across the workflow★陷阱：ASO误当无保险或全保无视风险

An employer switches its group benefit plan from one insurer to another. Several employees protest that they never signed the new contract and demand that the group broker personally adjudicate and pay their pending dental claims. How do group contract law and broker duties apply in this situation?

一家雇主将其团体福利计划从一家保险公司更换到另一家。几名员工抗议称自己从未签署过新合同，并要求团体险经纪亲自审核并赔付他们待处理的牙科理赔。团体合同法与经纪人职责在此情形下如何适用？

  1. ✗ 错误。总合同（master contract）的签约双方是雇主与保险公司，无需每名员工签字；且理赔审核与支付不是经纪人的职责。Incorrect. The master contract is executed between the employer and the insurer and does not require individual signatures from every employee; furthermore, claims adjudication and payment are not broker duties.

  2. ✗ 错误。虽然员工不签主合同，但依BC省法s.96享有取得相关保单副本等法定权利（s.99另管证书，s.116管替换责任），且经纪人仍须履行存续期服务与投诉转介职责。Incorrect. Although employees do not sign the master contract, under BC Insurance Act s. 96 they have statutory rights to obtain policy copies (with s. 99 governing certificates and s. 116 governing replacement duties), and brokers must fulfill ongoing service and complaint-handling obligations.

  3. ✓ 正确。团体险总合同（master contract）由计划发起人（plan sponsor，即雇主）与保险公司签署，员工只拿福利手册（benefit booklet），因此雇主可更换承保商（但员工仍享有BC s.96取得保单副本、s.99证书及s.116替换保护等法定权利）；理赔审核与支付由保险人或第三方管理商（TPA）按约定负责，经纪人负责服务与投诉转介。Correct. The master contract is executed between the plan sponsor (the employer) and the insurer, while employees receive only a benefit booklet; the employer may change carriers (though members retain statutory rights under BC Insurance Act s. 96 for policy copies, s. 99 for certificates, and s. 116 for replacement protection). Claims adjudication and payment are handled by the insurer or third-party administrator (TPA), while the broker assists with ongoing service and complaint referral.

  4. ✗ 错误。询价请求（request for quotation, RFQ）是计划报价阶段经纪人向多家保险公司询价比较的流程，且ESDC不处理商业团体牙科理赔。Incorrect. A request for quotation (RFQ) is the process during the quoting stage where a broker solicits competing quotes from insurers; ESDC does not adjudicate private group dental claims.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

During the initial design and quoting stage of a group benefit plan, what documents and information must the group broker collect from the employer before issuing a request for quotation (RFQ) to insurers?

在团体福利计划的初步设计与报价阶段，团体险经纪在向多家保险公司发出询价请求（RFQ）之前，必须从雇主处收集哪些资料？

  1. ✗ 错误。报价阶段无需收集员工个人报税表或无关前雇主的LTD缴费记录。Incorrect. The quoting stage does not require collecting employees' personal tax returns or LTD contribution histories from unrelated prior employers.

  2. ✓ 正确。经纪人在设计计划并发出询价请求（request for quotation, RFQ）前，须收集团体构成（makeup：人数、年龄性别结构、流动率）、行业性质、员工资料表（employee data sheet：薪资、职级、家属情况），以及续保或换约时的既有计划条款与历史理赔经验。Correct. Prior to designing a plan and issuing a request for quotation (RFQ), the broker must collect group makeup details (headcount, age and gender distribution, turnover rate), industry classification, an employee data sheet (salaries, occupational classes, dependant details), and existing plan provisions along with historical claims experience if replacing a plan.

  3. ✗ 错误。员工不签署总合同（master contract），配偶生日也只在双职家庭发生子女理赔协调给付（COB）时才需核对。Incorrect. Employees do not sign the master contract, and spousal birthdays are verified only when coordinating benefits (COB) for dependent children at the time of a claim.

  4. ✗ 错误。团体寿险、STD、LTD常按薪资倍数或职级设定保额，必须依赖员工资料表（employee data sheet），且不存在所谓的固定33% ASO折扣率（陷阱：ASO误当无保险或全保无视风险）。Incorrect. Group life, STD, and LTD coverages typically base benefit schedules on salary multiples or job classifications, requiring an employee data sheet; furthermore, there is no verified standard 33% ASO discount rate (Trap: Misunderstanding ASO as unmanaged self-insurance or ignoring risk volatility).
