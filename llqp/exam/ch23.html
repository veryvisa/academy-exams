① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 客户焦虑匹配题：给出客户的退休顾虑（如害怕活得比钱久、看重本金流动性或希望保留遗产弹性），测试能否在生命年金（life annuity）、RRIF 与带保证终身提取收益（GLWB）的分离基金之间识别各自对应的风险机制。Client anxiety matching questions: Given a client's retirement concerns (such as fearing outliving their money, prioritizing capital liquidity, or wishing to preserve estate flexibility), test whether candidates can identify the corresponding risk mechanisms across life annuities, RRIFs, and segregated funds with a guaranteed lifetime withdrawal benefit (GLWB).
  • 保证期与联合末存规则辨析题：设置夫妇无需留现金遗产却被加挂保证期、或联合末存年金第一位受领人身故后受益人要求领款的场景，测试保证期（guaranteed period）对月给付额的折减效应以及双生命受领的触发顺序。Guaranteed period and joint-and-last-survivor rule discrimination questions: Setting up scenarios where a couple with no need to leave a cash estate is given a guaranteed period, or where a beneficiary claims payments immediately after the first annuitant's death under a joint-and-last-survivor annuity, test the discounting effect of a guaranteed period on monthly payments and the sequential trigger order under joint lives.
  • 即期与递延分类陷阱题：在题干中植入分批注资或一次性存入等存款方式描述，测试考生是否清楚即期年金（immediate annuity）与递延年金（deferred annuity）的唯一划分标准是给付何时开始而非注资方式。Immediate versus deferred classification trap questions: Embedding deposit methods such as periodic installments or lump-sum deposits in the question stem, test whether candidates understand that the sole criterion distinguishing an immediate annuity from a deferred annuity is when payments commence, not how deposits are funded.
  • 非注册年金税务与双口径制度题：对比规定式年金（prescribed annuity）与权责发生制年金（accrual annuity）的年度应税节奏及起付年度资格重核，或考查 Assuris 付息年金保障上限、ALDA 与 VPLA 起付年龄限制的考试口径与现行规则差异。Non-registered annuity taxation and dual-benchmark questions: Comparing the annual taxable progression of prescribed annuities versus accrual annuities and reassessing eligibility in the payout commencement year, or testing differences between exam benchmarks and current rules regarding Assuris payout annuity coverage limits and ALDA/VPLA commencement age limits.
  • 保险年金组合操作顺序题：描述客户希望用年金现金流支付永久寿险保费以锁定免税死亡给付，测试保险年金（insured annuity）必须先通过寿险核保生效、再购买单生生命年金的先后顺序。Insured annuity operational sequence questions: Describing a client who intends to use annuity cash flow to pay permanent life insurance premiums to lock in a tax-free death benefit, test the mandatory operational sequence requiring life insurance underwriting approval and issuance before purchasing a single life annuity.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 把递延等同于分期存钱Equating Deferred Annuities with Periodic Deposits Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常把递延年金误解为分期供款购买的年金，忽略了区分即期与递延的唯一标准是给付何时开始。Candidates frequently confuse a deferred annuity with an annuity funded through periodic installment deposits, overlooking that the sole criterion distinguishing immediate from deferred annuities is when payout begins.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调客户分多次注资或整笔存入，并要求判断合同属于即期年金还是递延年金。The question stem emphasizes that a client deposits funds in multiple installments or as a single lump sum, asking candidates to classify the contract as an immediate or deferred annuity.

⚠ 误以为加保证期对受领人更划算Assuming Adding a Guaranteed Period Benefits the Annuitant Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到“保证”二字就以为能提高客户本人的收入保障，忘了保证期保护的是身故后的受益人，反而会拉低受领人在世时的每期给付。Seeing the word 'guaranteed' leads candidates to believe it increases the annuitant's own retirement income, forgetting that a guaranteed period protects posthumous beneficiaries and actually reduces the payment amounts received while living.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现“不需要留遗产（子女已独立）”与“希望在世时月收入最大化”，选项却带有 10 年或 20 年保证期。The question stem mentions both 'no need to leave an estate (independent adult children)' and 'maximizing monthly income during lifetime,' yet answer choices include a 10-year or 20-year guaranteed period.

⚠ 首位受领人身故即赔付受益人Paying Beneficiaries Immediately Upon the First Annuitant's Death Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 混淆单生年金与联合末存年金的身故触发时点，误以为第一位配偶身故时受益人就能拿走保证期余额。Conflating single life annuities with joint-and-last-survivor annuities regarding death benefit triggers, mistakenly believing that a beneficiary can claim the remaining guaranteed period balance as soon as the first spouse dies.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干为联合末存年金（joint and last survivor annuity）且附带保证期，描述第一位受领人刚身故、子女作为受益人前来索赔。The scenario involves a joint-and-last-survivor annuity with a guaranteed period, describing that the first annuitant has just passed away and children as designated beneficiaries come forward to claim benefits.

⚠ 保险年金先买年金后核保寿险Purchasing the Annuity Before Underwriting the Life Insurance in an Insured Annuity Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 只记住保险年金包含终身寿险与生命年金两份合同，却忽略了若寿险拒保则已生效的即期年金无法撤销。Remembering only that an insured annuity combines permanent life insurance and a life annuity, while failing to realize that if life insurance is declined after medical underwriting, an already issued immediate annuity cannot be rescinded.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干询问搭建保险年金（insured annuity）策略的第一步，或描述客户已买好年金再去体检投保永久寿险。The question asks for the initial step in establishing an insured annuity strategy, or describes a client who bought an annuity first before undergoing medical exams for permanent life insurance.

⚠ Assuris与ALDA新旧口径混用Conflating Old and Current Benchmarks for Assuris and ALDA Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: CISRO 考试口径编写于 2022 年，其中的 Assuris 月收入保障上限（$2,000/85%）及 ALDA 提案额（$150,000）与 2026 现行标准不同。The CISRO exam curriculum was written in 2022, where the Assuris monthly payout protection limit ($2,000 / 85%) and ALDA proposed cap ($150,000) differ from the 2026 current standards.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现保险公司破产且承诺月给付 $5,000，或询问高龄递延生命年金（ALDA）的延期上限年龄与转入限额。The question stem features an insolvent insurer owing a promised monthly payout of $5,000, or inquires about the maximum deferral age and transfer limits for an Advanced Life Deferred Annuity (ALDA).

④ 要点表④ Key points

付息年金与积累年金的本质区别Essential Distinction Between Payout and Accumulation Annuitiespayout annuity vs. accumulation annuity付息年金旨在把本息按期化为收入流，而积累年金（保险 GIC）不产生收入流、仅代为持有生息，到期可转付息年金或全额无罚金退保。A payout annuity is designed to convert principal and interest into a scheduled income stream, whereas an accumulation annuity (insurance GIC) generates no income stream and merely holds deposits to earn interest, allowing conversion to a payout annuity or penalty-free full surrender at maturity.
即期年金与递延年金的划分标准Classification Standard for Immediate and Deferred Annuitiesimmediate annuity vs. deferred annuity唯一区分标准是给付何时开始：即期年金须整笔购买且在第一个付款周期（一年内）开始给付，递延年金在积累期结束后才开始给付，不存在分批注资的即期年金。The sole distinguishing criterion is when income payments begin: an immediate annuity must be purchased with a single lump sum and begin payments within the first payment interval (within one year), whereas a deferred annuity begins payments only after the accumulation phase ends; there is no such thing as an immediate annuity funded through periodic installment deposits.
寿命风险承担与保证期折价机制Longevity Risk Allocation and Guaranteed Period Discountinglife annuity, term-certain annuity & guaranteed period生命年金将寿命风险转嫁给保险公司而定期确定年金不转移寿命风险；附加保证期（如 5/10/20 年）、资本保全保证或首付前身故保证会因为增加身故赔付责任而压低每期给付额。A life annuity transfers longevity risk to the insurer's risk pool, whereas a term-certain annuity does not transfer longevity risk; adding a guaranteed period (such as 5, 10, or 20 years), a capital protection guarantee, or a return-of-premium guarantee reduces the periodic payment amount because the insurer assumes additional death benefit liabilities.
保险年金的运作结构与先后顺序Operational Structure and Purchase Sequence of an Insured Annuityinsured annuity保险年金用单生终身生命年金的给付支付永久人寿保险（如 T-100）保费并留出生活费，身故时寿险免税赔付受益人，操作上必须先通过寿险核保生效后再购买年金。An insured annuity uses payments from a single life annuity to pay premiums on a permanent life insurance policy (such as T-100) while providing disposable living income; upon death, the life insurance pays a tax-free benefit to beneficiaries. Operationally, the life insurance must be medically underwritten and issued before the annuity is purchased.
Assuris 对年金持有人的保护上限Assuris Protection Limits for Annuity HoldersAssuris protection limits付息年金月收入保护在 2022 考试口径中为 $2,000/月或 85% 取高（2026 现行为 $5,000/月或 90% 取高），积累年金在考试口径中为 $100,000 全额保障（2026 现行为 $100,000 或 90% 取高）。Monthly income protection for payout annuities under the 2022 exam benchmark is the higher of $2,000 per month or 85% of promised payments (under the 2026 current rule, the higher of $5,000 per month or 90%); accumulation annuities are fully protected up to $100,000 under the exam benchmark (under the 2026 current rule, the higher of $100,000 or 90%).
非注册年金的规定式与权责发生制计税Prescribed and Accrual Taxation for Non-Registered Annuitiesprescribed annuity vs. accrual annuity非注册规定式年金（符合 ITR s.304 条件）在给付期内保持平稳的本金与应税利息拆分，权责发生制年金在积累期按年计入应计收益，且进入给付期当年可重新核定是否符合 prescribed 资格。A non-registered prescribed annuity (meeting ITR section 304 conditions) maintains a level split between non-taxable principal return and taxable interest throughout the payout period, whereas an accrual annuity reports accrued income annually during the accumulation phase and is reassessed for prescribed eligibility in the year payouts commence.
利率环境对固定年金给付的锁定效应Lock-In Effect of Prevailing Interest Rates at Issueprevailing interest rates at issue固定给付年金的费率取决于签发当天的长期债券收益率与精算假设并按合同锁定，加息周期签发的同等本金月给付通常高于低利率时期。Payment rates for fixed annuities depend on long-term bond yields and actuarial assumptions on the date of issuance and are locked into the contract; for the same principal, monthly income issued during a rate-hike cycle is generally higher than that issued during a low-interest-rate environment.
生命年金、RRIF 与分离基金 GLWB 的取舍Trade-Offs Among Life Annuities, RRIFs, and Segregated Fund GLWBslife annuity vs. RRIF vs. GLWB生命年金解决怕活得比钱久但几乎无本金流动性，RRIF 保留投资与提取灵活性但资产耗尽即停止，分离基金 GLWB 兼顾终身按比例提取保证与部分流动性但超提会永久下调保证基数。A life annuity eliminates the risk of outliving one's money but provides virtually no principal liquidity; an RRIF preserves investment growth and withdrawal flexibility but payments cease once assets are depleted; a segregated fund GLWB combines guaranteed lifetime proportional withdrawals with partial liquidity, though excess withdrawals permanently reduce the guaranteed base.
联合末存年金的身故给付触发时点Death Benefit Trigger Sequence for Joint and Last Survivor Annuitiesjoint and last survivor annuity beneficiary trigger联合末存年金第一位受领人身故后由第二位继续作为唯一受领人领款，附带保证期的剩余金额须等第二位受领人也在保证期内身故后才轮到受益人领取。Under a joint and last survivor annuity, upon the death of the first annuitant, the second annuitant continues receiving payments as the sole annuitant; any remaining balance under an attached guaranteed period becomes payable to designated beneficiaries only if the second annuitant also dies before the guaranteed period expires.
ALDA 与 VPLA 的税务与起付年龄限制Taxation and Age Restrictions for ALDA and VPLAadvanced life deferred annuity (ALDA) & VPLAALDA 允许将合资格计划最多 25%（考试口径提案总额 $150,000，2026 限额 $180,000）延至受领人满 85 岁当年年底前起付；VPLA 设立时须至少 10 名成员并预期持续达标，考试口径起付上限为 71 岁（现行税法取满 71 岁年末与转款取得权利年末较晚者）。An ALDA permits transferring up to 25% of a qualifying registered plan (proposed lifetime cap of $150,000 under the exam benchmark; $180,000 CRA limit in 2026) with payouts deferred up to the end of the year the annuitant turns age 85; a VPLA requires at least 10 members at establishment with an ongoing expectation to maintain that count, with an exam-benchmark payout commencement deadline of age 71 (current tax rules use the later of the end of the year turning 71 or the end of the year the right is acquired).

第 1 题Q1即期与递延年金的唯一划分标准Sole classification criterion for immediate and deferred annuities★陷阱：把递延等同于分期存钱

Marcus transfers a single lump sum of $120,000 from his savings into an annuity contract today. Under the terms of the contract, the insurer will hold and credit interest on the funds for 5 years, and monthly income payments to Marcus will begin at the end of year 5. How is this annuity classified?

Marcus 今天从储蓄中取出一整笔 $120,000 资金存入一份年金合同。根据合同条款，保险公司将持有该笔资金并计息 5 年，给 Marcus 的月度收入给付将在第 5 年末开始。这份年金应如何分类？

  1. ✗ 错误，掉入“把递延等同于分期存钱”陷阱。区分即期年金（immediate annuity）与递延年金（deferred annuity）的唯一标准是给付何时开始，与存钱是一整笔还是分批无关。Incorrect. Falls into the Equating Deferred Annuities with Periodic Deposits trap: The sole criterion distinguishing an immediate annuity from a deferred annuity is when income payments begin, regardless of whether deposits are made as a lump sum or in installments.

  2. ✓ 正确。递延年金（deferred annuity）分为积累期与给付期两个阶段，积累期内存入的资金既可以是一整笔（lump sum）也可以是分批存入，只要给付从积累期结束时（本题为 5 年后）才开始，就属于递延年金。Correct. A deferred annuity consists of two distinct stages: an accumulation phase and a payout phase. Funds deposited during the accumulation phase can be either a lump sum or periodic contributions; as long as payments commence only after the accumulation phase ends (in this case, 5 years later), the contract is classified as a deferred annuity.

  3. ✗ 错误。即期年金必须在合同成立后的第一个付款周期内（一年内）开始给付；5 年后才开始给付属于积累期递延，而不是 5 年保证期。Incorrect. An immediate annuity must begin payments within the first payment interval (within one year) following contract issue; beginning payments 5 years later represents an accumulation deferral period, not a 5-year guaranteed period.

  4. ✗ 错误。递延年金完全可以用一整笔资金购买，且有 5 年积累期的非注册年金在积累期按应计规则（accrual）报税，起付当年才重新核定是否符合规定式（prescribed）条件。Incorrect. A deferred annuity can certainly be purchased with a single lump sum, and a non-registered annuity with a 5-year accumulation phase reports taxes on an accrual basis during accumulation, being reassessed for prescribed annuity status only in the year payments begin.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Elena wants her annuity income to start next month. She asks her advisor if she can set up an immediate annuity by making five annual deposits over the next 5 years while collecting monthly annuity payments right away. How should the advisor respond?

Elena 希望她的年金收入从下个月开始领取。她询问顾问，能否通过未来 5 年每年存入一笔资金的方式设立一份即期年金，同时立刻开始按月领取年金给付。顾问应如何回答？

  1. ✓ 正确。即期年金（immediate annuity）必须用一整笔资金（lump sum）购买，并在第一个付款周期（一年内）开始给付；分批存钱只会出现在递延年金的积累期，不存在分批注资的即期年金。Correct. An immediate annuity must be purchased with a single lump sum and begin payments within the first payment interval (within one year); periodic contributions occur solely during the accumulation phase of a deferred annuity, and an immediate annuity funded through installment deposits does not exist.

  2. ✗ 错误。一旦进入给付阶段（payout phase），年金合同本身已不再接受追加存款；若要追加，通常是另买一份新的年金合同。Incorrect. Once an annuity enters the payout phase, the contract no longer accepts additional deposits; adding funds generally requires purchasing a separate, new annuity contract.

  3. ✗ 错误。分批注资只发生在递延年金的积累期，无法一边分期供款一边作为同一份即期付息年金立即领款。Incorrect. Periodic contributions occur only during the accumulation phase of a deferred annuity; a client cannot make installment deposits while simultaneously receiving immediate income from the same payout annuity.

  4. ✗ 错误。保证期（guaranteed period）是身故保障条款，用于在受领人早逝时向受益人赔付剩余应付款，与保费能否分期存入毫无关系。Incorrect. A guaranteed period is a death benefit provision ensuring that remaining scheduled payments are made to a beneficiary if the annuitant dies prematurely; it has no bearing on whether premium deposits can be made in installments.

第 2 题Q2Assuris对付息年金的保障上限Assuris protection limits for payout annuities★★陷阱：Assuris与ALDA新旧口径混用

Liam holds a payout life annuity that promises him a monthly income of $5,000. If the issuing life insurance company becomes insolvent, how much of Liam's monthly benefit is protected by Assuris if the question tells you to use the pre-May 2023 figures printed in the exam syllabus?

Liam 持有一份承诺每月向他给付 $5,000 的付息生命年金。如果签发该合同的人寿保险公司破产，题目要求按考试口径印的 2023 年 5 月上调前旧标准计算，Liam 每月的年金给付有多少受 Assuris 保护？

  1. ✗ 错误。$2,000/月不是绝对封顶上限，而是与承诺给付的 85% 比较后“取高值”（$2,000/月或 85% 取高）。Incorrect. $2,000 per month is not an absolute ceiling; rather, it is compared against 85% of promised payments to determine coverage under the higher of the two ($2,000 per month or 85%, whichever is higher).

  2. ✗ 错误。90% 是 2023 年上调后的现行比例，但即便按 2026 现行标准（$5,000/月或 90% 取高），$5,000 月给付也是全额保护 $5,000 而非 $4,500。Incorrect. 90% is the current proportion following the 2023 update, but even under the 2026 current benchmark (the higher of $5,000 per month or 90%), a $5,000 monthly payment receives full 100% protection ($5,000) rather than $4,500.

  3. ✓ 正确。题目明确要求用考试口径的 2022 旧值：月收入型付息年金保护「$2,000/月或承诺给付的 85%，取高」，$5,000 × 85% = $4,250 高于 $2,000，故为 $4,250。现行（2023-05-29 起）为「$5,000/月或 90% 取高」，同一 $5,000 月给付可获全额保护；题目未标注按旧标准时用现行值。Correct. The question explicitly specifies applying the 2022 exam benchmark: monthly payout annuity protection is the higher of $2,000 per month or 85% of promised payments; since $5,000 x 85% = $4,250, which exceeds $2,000, the covered amount is $4,250. (Under current rules since May 29, 2023, the limit is the higher of $5,000 per month or 90%, which would provide full protection for this $5,000 monthly benefit).

  4. ✗ 错误。固定给付型年金的月给付额自签发之日起按合同锁定，不会在 20 年保证期结束后自动上调。Incorrect. The monthly payment for a fixed annuity is locked into the contract from the date of issue and does not automatically adjust upward after the 20-year guaranteed period expires.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

第 7 题Q7保险年金的结构与购买先后顺序Structure and purchase sequence of an insured annuity★★陷阱：保险年金先买年金后核保寿险

Walter, age 60, wants to leave a $500,000 tax-free estate to his two daughters while generating net spendable retirement income from his capital today. His advisor proposes an insured annuity strategy combining a $500,000 Term-100 life insurance policy (costing $1,000/month) and a single life annuity (yielding $1,300/month after tax). What is the mandatory execution sequence for setting up this strategy?

60 岁的 Walter 希望给两个女儿留下一笔 $500,000 的免税遗产，同时利用现有资金在今天产生可支配的退休净收入。顾问建议采用保险年金（insured annuity）策略，结合一份 $500,000 保额的 T-100 永久寿险保单（月保费 $1,000）和一份单生终身生命年金（假设税后月给付 $1,300）。实施这一策略必须遵循怎样的操作顺序？

  1. ✗ 错误，掉入“保险年金先买年金后核保寿险”陷阱。如果先买年金再申请人寿保险，一旦体检核保不通过买不了寿险，已生效的即期年金无法撤销，整套遗产结构就会彻底失败。Incorrect. Falls into the Purchasing the Annuity Before Underwriting the Life Insurance in an Insured Annuity trap: If the annuity is purchased before applying for life insurance and the client is subsequently declined during medical underwriting, the in-force immediate annuity cannot be rescinded, causing the entire estate strategy to fail completely.

  2. ✗ 错误。保险年金中的永久人寿保险（如 T-100）必须正常通过健康核保，购买年金并不会免除寿险的体检核保要求。Incorrect. Permanent life insurance in an insured annuity (such as Term-100) must undergo standard medical underwriting; purchasing an annuity does not exempt the applicant from medical underwriting requirements.

  3. ✓ 正确。保险年金（insured annuity）的购买顺序绝不能颠倒：必须先完成永久人寿保险（如 T-100）的核保并确认保单生效，然后才能动用另一笔资金购买单生终身生命年金，用年金给付（$1,300）覆盖寿险保费（$1,000）并留出生活费（$300）。Correct. The purchase sequence of an insured annuity cannot be reversed: medical underwriting for the permanent life insurance policy (such as Term-100) must be completed and the policy issued first; only then is separate capital used to purchase the single life annuity, using annuity payments ($1,300) to fund life insurance premiums ($1,000) and provide living income ($300).

  4. ✗ 错误。附加 20 年保证期的年金不是保险年金（insured annuity），不仅会压低月给付，而且如果 Walter 在 20 年后身故，女儿们将拿不到任何年金余额。Incorrect. An annuity with a 20-year guaranteed period is not an insured annuity; adding a guarantee reduces monthly income, and if Walter passes away after 20 years, his daughters would receive no remaining balance.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Once an insured annuity strategy is properly in force (with a permanent life insurance policy and a single life annuity with no guaranteed period), what happens financially when the insured annuitant dies?

一旦保险年金（insured annuity）策略按正确顺序生效（包含一份永久人寿保险和一份不带保证期的单生终身生命年金），当受保人兼年金受领人身故时，财务上会发生什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。保险年金配套的是不带保证期的单生终身生命年金，受领人身故时年金合同立即终止，不会继续向受益人付款。Incorrect. The insured annuity strategy utilizes a single life annuity with no guaranteed period; upon the annuitant's death, the annuity terminates immediately and makes no further payments to beneficiaries.

  2. ✓ 正确。在保险年金（insured annuity）结构中，本人在世时用年金给付支付寿险保费并享有剩余现金流；本人身故时，单生生命年金随之终止，寿险保费停止缴纳，而永久人寿保险的死亡给付（免税）直接支付给受益人。Correct. In an insured annuity structure, annuity payments fund the permanent life insurance premiums while providing surplus income during the client's lifetime; upon the client's death, the single life annuity terminates, premium obligations cease, and the permanent life insurance death benefit is paid tax-free directly to the beneficiaries.

  3. ✗ 错误。永久人寿保险与单生生命年金是两份独立的合同，寿险死亡给付不会扣减生前已领取的年金总额（扣减已领年金是资本保全保证的机制）。Incorrect. Permanent life insurance and the single life annuity are two distinct, independent contracts; the life insurance death benefit is never reduced by lifetime annuity payments already received (deducting cumulative payments is a feature of a capital protection guarantee).

  4. ✗ 错误。人寿保险的死亡给付在加拿大是免税支付给指定受益人的，不会按规定式年金利息征税。Incorrect. Life insurance death benefits in Canada are paid tax-free to designated beneficiaries and are not taxed as prescribed annuity interest.

答案Answer：b
