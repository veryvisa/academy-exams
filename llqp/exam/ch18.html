① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 情景缺口计算题：给定税前月薪、团体长期伤残险（Group LTD）比例、保费缴纳方与有效税率，结合政府给付直接抵消（Direct Offset），要求算出真实税后收入替代率或每月保障缺口。Scenario-based gap calculation questions: Given gross monthly earnings, group long-term disability (LTD) replacement percentage, premium payor, and effective tax rate, combined with government benefit direct offsets, calculate the true after-tax income replacement rate or the net monthly coverage gap.
  • 雇员与自雇者对比题：给出自由职业者或小企业主的收入结构（含营业开支、股息或租金）与申请就业保险特别福利（EI Special Benefits）的意向，考查可保劳动收入核定、12个月注册等待期及双风险敞口。Employee versus self-employed comparison questions: Given the income structure of a freelancer or small business owner (including business operating expenses, dividends, or rental income) and an intent to apply for Employment Insurance (EI) special benefits, evaluate insurable earned income, the 12-month registration waiting period, and dual risk exposures.
  • 财务工具与测算方法辨析题：客户面临保费预算受限或需要评估等待期自我承担能力，要求在收入法（Income Approach）与支出法（Expense Approach）、净资产表（Net Worth Statement）与现金流量表（Cash Flow Statement）之间做出正确匹配。Financial tool and calculation methodology distinction questions: When a client faces premium budget constraints or needs to evaluate self-insurance capacity during an elimination period, correctly match between the income approach and expense approach, and between the net worth statement and cash flow statement.
  • 双口径与政府福利规则题：考查就业保险病假福利（EI Sickness Benefits）考试口径（15周）与现行法规（26周）的差异、加拿大退休金计划伤残给付（CPP-D）的「严重且持久」判定及起付月份，以及注册残疾储蓄计划（RDSP）的终身供款上限与配款追回机制。Dual-standard and government benefit rule questions: Test the differences between the exam benchmark (15 weeks) and current legislation (26 weeks) for Employment Insurance (EI) sickness benefits, the severe and prolonged disability criteria and benefit start month for Canada Pension Plan disability (CPP-D), and the lifetime contribution limit and grant repayment clawback rules for the Registered Disability Savings Plan (RDSP).
  • 保障充足性四情形判定题：汇总客户现有团体险、个人险与政府给付后，对照全来源上限（All-Source Maximum，常为85%），判断属于充足、过剩、重叠超上限还是存在缺口，并选出合规的下一步行动。Coverage adequacy four-scenario determination questions: After aggregating a client's existing group coverage, individual policies, and government benefits, compare against an all-source maximum (typically 85% of pre-disability gross income) to determine whether coverage is adequate, excessive, overlapping above the cap, or deficient, and select the compliant next step.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 净收入与毛收入口径混淆Net Versus Gross Income Confusion Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 习惯性套用「伤残险给付免税」的印象，忘了雇主付保费时给付须按有效税率扣税，或直接把应税的政府给付税前金额与免税给付相加。Candidates habitually assume that disability benefits are always tax-free, forgetting that benefits become taxable at the client's effective tax rate when the employer pays the premiums, or erroneously summing taxable pre-tax government benefits directly with tax-free benefits.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现税前收入（Gross Income）、有效税率（25%）以及雇主全额支付保费（Employer-paid premiums）或应税的 CPP-D。The question stem simultaneously mentions gross income, an effective tax rate (e.g., 25%), and employer-paid premiums or taxable CPP-D benefits.

⚠ 自雇总营收当成可保收入Mistaking Gross Business Revenue for Insurable Income Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直接用自雇客户的营业总额或把持续性租金、股息直接加进可保收入基数，漏减经营开支，或以为自雇者失能后随时可申领 EI 病假福利。Candidates mistakenly use a self-employed client's gross revenue or add continuous passive rental or dividend income directly into the insurable income base without deducting business operating expenses, or assume self-employed individuals can claim EI sickness benefits immediately upon disability.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现自雇者（Self-employed）、营业总收入、经营开支、被动投资收入，或刚在 MSCA 注册未满 12 个月。The question stem mentions a self-employed individual, gross business revenue, operating expenses, passive investment income, or an MSCA registration under 12 months.

⚠ 漏算政府给付抵消与假设必批Overlooking Government Benefit Offsets and Assuming Guaranteed Approval Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把 CPP-D、EI 或工伤补偿当成可在团体险之外额外叠加的收入，或未等审批决定书就把最高月额当作既定收入写入缺口承诺。Candidates treat CPP-D, EI, or workers' compensation as additional income that can simply stack on top of group LTD, or write maximum statutory amounts into gap commitments as guaranteed income before receiving an official approval letter.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提到团体主合同含直接抵消条款（Direct Offset）、85% 全来源上限，或客户刚提交 CPP-D 申请尚未获批。The question stem mentions that the group master contract contains a direct offset clause, an 85% all-source maximum, or a newly submitted CPP-D application that has not yet been approved.

⚠ 测算尺子与财务报表错配Mismatching Calculation Methodologies and Financial Statements Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 在客户预算吃紧时固守收入法（60%）导致客户弃保，或拿净资产表去算每月保费结余、拿现金流量表去判断能否扛过等待期。Candidates rigidly insist on the income approach (60%) when the client's budget is constrained, causing the client to decline coverage, or use the net worth statement to calculate monthly premium affordability and the cash flow statement to assess whether the client can survive the elimination period.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调保费超出当前预算（Affordability constraint）、询问能否自我承担等待期，或询问每月可削减哪项开支来付保费。The question stem highlights an affordability constraint, asks whether the client can self-insure through the elimination period, or asks which monthly expenses can be trimmed to afford premiums.

⚠ 考试口径与现行政策张冠李戴Conflating Exam Benchmarks with Current Legislation Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 用现行 26 周去答明确按 2022 考试口径设问的 EI 病假周数题，或误以为团体保单归员工个人所有、离职后仍自动延续。Candidates use the current 26-week rule to answer a question that explicitly asks for the 2022 exam benchmark duration for EI sickness benefits, or mistakenly believe that group policies are owned by employees and automatically continue after resignation.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干询问标准考试口径下的 EI 病假给付周数（15 weeks vs 26 weeks）、600 小时门槛，或客户准备辞职创业仍指望原有团体 LTD。The question stem asks for the standard exam benchmark duration of EI sickness benefits (15 weeks vs. 26 weeks) or the 600-hour threshold, or involves a client resigning to start a business while expecting group LTD to continue.

第 1 题Q1自雇者可保收入与EI特别福利注册规则Self-employed insurable income and EI special benefits registration rules★★陷阱：自雇总营收当成可保收入

Julian is a self-employed landscape contractor whose financial statements show $72,000 in gross business revenue, $12,000 in operating expenses (leaving $60,000 in net business income), and $6,000 in dividends from a passive investment portfolio. Less than 12 months ago, he registered with the Canada Employment Insurance Commission via My Service Canada Account (MSCA) for Employment Insurance (EI) special benefits. If Julian is injured off the job today and cannot work, how should his agent assess his insurable earned income and his current EI sickness benefit eligibility?

Julian 是一名自雇园林承包商，其财务报表显示营业总收入为 $72,000，经营开支为 $12,000（净经营收入为 $60,000），并从被动投资组合中获得 $6,000 股息收入。不到 12 个月前，他通过加拿大服务部账户（MSCA）自愿注册了就业保险（EI）特别福利。如果 Julian 今天因非工伤意外受伤无法工作，代理人应如何评估他的可保劳动收入以及当前的 EI 病假福利资格？

  1. ✓ 正确。自雇者的可保劳动收入（Insurable earned income）按扣除经营开支后的劳动性净经营收入计算（$72,000 − $12,000 = $60,000），持续性股息列为非劳动所得（Unearned income）另行评估，不直接相加提高可保劳动收入；此外，自雇者通过 MSCA 自愿注册就业保险特别福利（EI special benefits）后，必须满 12 个月（从注册日起算）才具备申领资格，未满 12 个月无法申领。Correct. Insurable earned income for a self-employed individual is calculated based on net business income after deducting operating expenses ($72,000 - $12,000 = $60,000), while ongoing dividends are classified as unearned income and evaluated separately rather than directly added to boost insurable earned income; additionally, self-employed individuals who voluntarily register for Employment Insurance (EI) special benefits via MSCA must wait a full 12 months from the registration date to be eligible to claim benefits, and cannot claim prior to completing that 12-month period.

  2. ✗ 错误。落入「自雇总营收当成可保收入」与「忽略 12 个月注册等待期」双重陷阱：计算可保劳动收入必须先减去 $12,000 经营开支；即使上一年净自雇收入超过门槛（$9,254），注册未满 12 个月仍无权申领。Incorrect. Falls into the double trap of Mistaking Gross Business Revenue for Insurable Income and Ignoring the 12-Month Registration Waiting Period: Operating expenses of $12,000 must first be deducted to determine insurable earned income; even if the prior year's net self-employment earnings exceed the threshold ($9,254), an individual registered for less than 12 months is not entitled to claim.

  3. ✗ 错误。落入「把被动股息加进可保劳动收入」与「误解撤回规则」陷阱：被动股息在失能期间不中断，不可直接加入可保劳动收入基数；且自愿注册后仅 60 天内可无条件撤回，一旦领取过特别福利即终身不可撤回。Incorrect. Falls into the trap of Adding Passive Dividends to Insurable Earned Income and Misunderstanding Withdrawal Rules: Passive dividends continue during disability and cannot be added directly to the insurable earned income base; furthermore, an agreement can only be withdrawn unconditionally within 60 days of voluntary registration, and once special benefits have been claimed, the agreement can never be terminated.

  4. ✗ 错误。落入「误解自雇 EI 12 个月等待期起算点」陷阱：自雇者参加 EI 特别福利的 12 个月等待期是从 MSCA 协议生效（注册日）起算，而不是从企业开业日或失能发生日起算。Incorrect. Falls into the Misunderstanding the Self-Employed EI Waiting Period Start Date trap: The 12-month waiting period for self-employed EI special benefits begins on the date the MSCA agreement takes effect (registration date), not from the date the business opened or the date of disability.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Hannah is a self-employed interior designer whose past three years of tax returns average $60,000 in net business income ($5,000 per month) after deducting studio overhead, plus $6,000 in ongoing rental income from a property. She registered for EI special benefits within the past 60 days and has never filed a claim, but she now prefers to rely on an individual disability insurance policy and a business overhead expense (BOE) policy instead. Which statement accurately describes her profile?

Hannah 是一名自雇室内设计师，近三年报税表显示扣除工作室固定开支后的平均净经营收入为 $60,000（每月 $5,000），另有物业持续性租金收入 $6,000。她在过去 60 天内注册了 EI 特别福利且从未申领过福利，但现在她更倾向于改用个人伤残保险和营业费用险（BOE）。下列哪项表述准确描述了她的画像情况？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入「把租金加进可保劳动收入」与「误解 60 天撤回窗口」陷阱：持续性租金不计入可保劳动收入；且只要未领取过特别福利，在注册后 60 天内均可无条件撤回，并非签约当即终身不可撤回。Incorrect. Falls into the trap of Adding Rental Income to Insurable Earned Income and Misunderstanding the 60-Day Withdrawal Window: Ongoing rental income is not included in insurable earned income; moreover, as long as special benefits have never been claimed, registration can be unconditionally withdrawn within 60 days of registering, rather than becoming immediately permanent upon signing.

  2. ✗ 错误。落入「忽视自雇者双风险敞口」陷阱：自雇者同时面临个人收入中断与企业固定开支照旧两个敞口，往往需要个人伤残险（DI）与营业费用险（BOE）两份保单分别覆盖。Incorrect. Falls into the Overlooking Dual Self-Employed Risk Exposures trap: Self-employed individuals face dual exposures of personal income disruption and continuing business overhead expenses, which often require two separate policies: individual disability insurance (DI) and business overhead expense (BOE) insurance.

A freelance translator is self-employed and must verify income via past two to three years of T1 tax returns and financial statements rather than an employee T4 slip; furthermore, gratuitously advising an RDSP withdrawal is inappropriate and would trigger grant repayment clawbacks.

第 6 题Q6单身与双职家庭缺口测算及照护余量规则Gap calculation for single versus dual-earner households and the caregiving cushion rule★★陷阱：测算尺子与财务报表错配

When the agent advises Noah on how much disability coverage to apply for compared with Liam, what caution from the exam applies?

Liam 和 Noah 是高贵林的两名仓库经理，税前月薪均为 $5,000（按 60% 收入法测算的伤残需求均为 $3,000/月）。Liam 单身，没有其他家庭收入来源。Noah 与配偶 Maya 组成双职家庭，Maya 的稳定薪水理论上可以覆盖大部分家庭账单，使 Noah 的理论伤残缺口仅为每月 $1,000。代理人对照 Liam 为 Noah 建议投保额度时，考试口径中的哪项警示适用？

  1. ✓ 正确。单身客户 Liam 没有第二份收入托底，$3,000/月的需求几乎不能打折；双职家庭（Dual-earner household）中的 Noah 虽然因配偶收入分担使理论缺口降至 $1,000/月，但考试口径特别提醒：配偶 Maya 未来可能因为要照护失能的 Noah 而暂停工作，理论上的「配偶收入托底」届时会打折扣，因此必须在理论缺口之上留出余量，不能机械照抄 $1,000 投保。Correct. Single client Liam has no second income buffer, so his $3,000/month calculated need cannot be compromised; while dual-earner client Noah sees his theoretical gap drop to $1,000/month due to his spouse's income, the exam benchmark cautions that spouse Maya may need to reduce hours or stop working to care for Noah if he becomes disabled, diminishing this theoretical cushion. Therefore, coverage must be structured with a caregiving cushion above the bare theoretical gap rather than mechanically purchasing only $1,000.

  2. ✗ 错误。落入「机械照抄理论缺口且捏造核保上限」陷阱：保险公司并无双职配偶只能保 $1,000 的规定，且直接照抄 $1,000 不留照护余量正是考试资料指出的常见错误。Incorrect. Falls into the Mechanically Adopting Theoretical Gaps and Fabricating Underwriting Caps trap: Insurers do not restrict dual-earner spouses to $1,000 in coverage, and mechanically copying $1,000 without a caregiving cushion is precisely the mistake highlighted in exam materials.

  3. ✗ 错误。落入「捏造单身政府福利优待」陷阱：单身客户既无配偶收入托底，也不会获得双倍 EI 病假给付比例（相反，EI 家庭补充只面向有 18 岁以下子女的低收入家庭）。Incorrect. Falls into the Fabricating Government Benefit Advantages for Single Individuals trap: Single individuals have neither spousal income support nor double EI sickness benefit replacement rates (on the contrary, the EI Family Supplement is available only to low-income families with children under 18).

  4. ✗ 错误。落入「混淆劳动所得与非劳动所得」陷阱：配偶收入在客户画像中属于非劳动所得（Unearned income，分担家庭开支的因素），绝不能加进 Noah 本人的可保劳动收入基数去申请更高个人保额。Incorrect.
