① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 区分“合同写死/法律强制的保证条款”与“保险公司自定的行政规则”：题干给出免费转换次数、重置年龄上限或不可撤销受益人变更条件，要求判断哪一项属于法定或合同保证、哪一项因保险人而异。Distinguishing contractually fixed or statutory guarantees from insurer administrative rules: Scenarios present conditions such as annual free switch limits, reset age ceilings, or irrevocable beneficiary changes, requiring candidates to determine which provisions are legally binding guarantees versus insurer-discretionary rules.
  • 注册账户与非注册账户的结构性限制：给出客户希望指定候补受保人（contingent annuitant）或将分离基金合同用于银行贷款抵押的场景，测试是否掌握只有非注册合同才允许三方分离、转让及设候补受保人。Structural limitations of registered versus non-registered contracts: Presenting scenarios where a client wishes to designate a contingent annuitant or pledge a segregated fund contract as bank collateral, testing whether candidates know that only non-registered contracts permit role separation, collateral assignment, and contingent annuitants.
  • 转换（switch）与重置（reset）的叠加后果：以非注册合同的免手续费转换为切入点，同步考察资本利得税务处置（按 50% 计入率）以及触发到期重置后到期日重新顺延 10 年的连锁影响。Compounding consequences of switches and resets: Using fee-free switches in non-registered contracts as an entry point to evaluate capital gains tax dispositions (at a 50% inclusion rate) alongside the ripple effect of a 10-year maturity date extension triggered by a maturity reset.
  • 到期理赔与身故理赔的申请人资格及文件边界：在受保人失能（由 POA 代管）但尚在世、或刚发生身故的不同时点，测试此刻谁有权申领、是否需要死亡证明或主治医生证明（APS），以及部分提款对保证基数的按比例下调。Applicant eligibility and documentation boundaries for maturity versus death claims: Testing who is entitled to claim, whether a death certificate or Attending Physician's Statement (APS) is required, and proportional guarantee base reductions from partial withdrawals across scenarios where an annuitant is incapacitated under a POA during their lifetime versus deceased.
  • 年金合同的身故给付条件与反洗钱核实豁免：围绕联合末存年金（joint and last survivor annuity）首位受领人身故时的受益人权利、给付型年金的撤销限制，以及注册资金转入豁免身份识别但绝不豁免年龄证明的合规要求设题。Annuity contract death benefit terms and anti-money laundering verification exemptions: Framing questions around beneficiary rights upon the primary annuitant's death under a joint and last survivor annuity, cancellation restrictions on payout annuities, and the compliance rule that registered pension transfers exempt identity verification but never proof of age.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 免费Switch零后果陷阱The zero-consequence free switch trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到题干强调“本年度首次免费转换（free switch）”，便误以为既不产生税负也不影响合同期限，忽略了非注册转换属于税务处置且若同步触发重置会顺延到期日。Candidates see the question stem emphasize a free switch within annual limits and mistakenly assume it triggers neither tax consequences nor contract date adjustments, overlooking that non-registered switches are taxable dispositions and triggering a reset resets the 10-year maturity date.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 non-registered contract、free switch、market value 高于原投入或 switch triggering a reset 等字眼。The question stem mentions a non-registered contract, free switch, market value exceeding original deposit, or a switch triggering a reset.

⚠ 在世失能误走身故理赔陷阱Mistaking lifetime incapacity for a death claim trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到合同临近到期且持有人兼受保人丧失行为能力、同时题干点出指名受益人（beneficiary），便直接让受益人申领或要求提交死亡证明，混淆了到期理赔与身故理赔。Candidates see that a contract is near maturity while the owner-annuitant is incapacitated and a beneficiary is named, leading them to mistakenly route the claim through the beneficiary or demand a death certificate, confusing a maturity claim with a death claim.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现 contract maturing soon、incapacitated owner/annuitant、Power of Attorney (POA) 以及 named beneficiary。The question stem mentions contract maturing soon, incapacitated owner/annuitant, Power of Attorney (POA), and a named beneficiary.

⚠ 注册合同设候补受保人陷阱Naming a contingent annuitant on a registered contract trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生记住分离基金可以指定候补受保人（contingent annuitant）以避免合同因受保人身故而终止，却忽略了注册账户强制要求持有人与受保人为同一人且不可更改受保人。Candidates remember that segregated funds allow designating a contingent annuitant to keep the contract active after an annuitant dies, but overlook that registered plans legally require the owner and annuitant to be the same person and forbid altering the annuitant.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 RRSP、RRIF 或 LIRA 等注册分离基金合同，并询问能否添加 contingent annuitant 或将合同质押（collateral）。The question stem presents a registered segregated fund contract such as an RRSP, RRIF, or LIRA and asks about adding a contingent annuitant or pledging the contract as collateral.

⚠ 联合年金首位身故即赔受益人陷阱Paying the beneficiary upon the first death in a joint annuity trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到联合末存年金（joint and last survivor annuity）附带保证期且第一位受领人刚身故，便误判受益人立即获赔，忘了必须等第二位受领人成唯一受领人并随后在保证期内身故才轮到受益人。Candidates see a joint and last survivor annuity with a guarantee period where the first annuitant dies and mistakenly assume the beneficiary receives an immediate payout, forgetting that the surviving co-annuitant becomes the sole annuitant and the beneficiary only claims if the survivor dies within the guarantee period.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 joint and last survivor annuity、guarantee period，且时间点设在 the first annuitant dies while the co-annuitant is still alive。The question stem mentions a joint and last survivor annuity, a guarantee period, and specifies that the first annuitant dies while the co-annuitant is still alive.

⚠ POA全权变更受益人与免年龄证明陷阱Broad POA beneficiary changes and age proof exemption trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生以为持有财产授权书（POA）代签申请表就能随意更改受益人，或以为符合反洗钱身份识别豁免的注册年金连年龄证明（proof of age）也不用交。Candidates assume an attorney acting under a Power of Attorney (POA) who signs an application can freely change beneficiary designations, or assume that an anti-money laundering identity verification exemption on registered annuities also waives proof of age.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 attorney under a POA wants to change the beneficiary，或 registered pension transfer exempt from identity verification。The question stem mentions an attorney under a POA wanting to change the beneficiary, or a registered pension transfer exempt from identity verification.

第 1 题Q1非注册与注册合同的候补受保人与转让Contingent annuitants and collateral assignment in non-registered versus registered contracts★★陷阱：注册合同设候补受保人陷阱

Victor holds two segregated fund contracts: Contract X is held inside a registered Retirement Savings Plan (RRSP), and Contract Y is a non-registered savings contract. Victor wants to name his adult son as a contingent annuitant on both contracts and pledge both contracts as collateral for a business line of credit. Which statement accurately describes what Victor is permitted to do?

Victor 持有两份分离基金合同：合同 X 持有在注册退休储蓄计划（RRSP）内，合同 Y 是非注册储蓄合同。Victor 希望在这两份合同上都将成年儿子指定为候补受保人（contingent annuitant），并将两份合同都质押作为商业信贷额度的抵押物。下列哪项陈述准确描述了 Victor 被允许做的事？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入“注册合同设候补受保人陷阱”。虽然分离基金受省保险法约束，但注册账户（RRSP/RRIF/LIRA）强制要求持有人与受保人为同一人且不可转让、不可抵押，因此合同 X 既不能指定候补受保人（contingent annuitant），也不能用作抵押物（collateral）。Incorrect. Falls into the trap of naming a contingent annuitant on a registered contract. Although segregated funds are governed by provincial insurance legislation, registered plans (RRSP/RRIF/LIRA) legally mandate that the owner and annuitant be the same person and cannot be assigned or pledged; therefore, Contract X cannot designate a contingent annuitant or be pledged as collateral.

  2. ✓ 正确。注册与非注册合同存在结构性差异：注册合同（Contract X）强制绑定持有人＝受保人，一旦指定受保人便不能更改，没有候补受保人（contingent annuitant）选项，且不可转让（assign）或抵押（pledge）；只有非注册合同（Contract Y）才允许指定候补受保人及用作抵押物。Correct. There is a structural distinction between registered and non-registered contracts: registered contracts (Contract X) legally bind the owner and annuitant as the same person, meaning the annuitant cannot be changed once designated, carries no contingent annuitant option, and cannot be assigned or pledged; only non-registered contracts (Contract Y) permit designating a contingent annuitant and pledging as collateral.

  3. ✗ 错误。前半句落入“注册合同设候补受保人陷阱”（RRSP 合同不能设 contingent annuitant），后半句忽略了非注册分离基金合同（Contract Y）在法律上允许转让及用作贷款抵押物。Incorrect. The first half falls into the trap of naming a contingent annuitant on a registered contract (an RRSP contract cannot have a contingent annuitant), while the second half overlooks that non-registered segregated fund contracts (Contract Y) are legally permitted to be assigned and pledged as loan collateral.

  4. ✗ 错误。注册合同（RRSP）不可转让、不可用作抵押物属于法定的注册账户限制，不能通过签署合同批注（amendment）来规避。Incorrect. The prohibition against assigning or pledging registered contracts (such as RRSPs) is a statutory registered plan restriction that cannot be bypassed or waived by executing a contract amendment.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Helena owns a locked-in retirement account (LIRA) invested in a segregated fund contract, while her sister owns a non-registered segregated fund contract. Both sisters ask their advisor whether they can change the annuitant on their existing contracts to their respective children while keeping ownership themselves. What should the advisor explain?

Helena 持有一份投资于分离基金合同的锁定退休账户（LIRA），而她姐姐持有一份非注册分离基金合同。姐妹俩向代理人询问：她们能否在保留各自持有人身份的同时，将现有合同的受保人更改或增设为各自的子女？代理人应当如何解释？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入“注册合同设候补受保人陷阱”。只有非注册合同允许持有人（policy owner）与受保人（annuitant）分离及指定候补受保人（contingent annuitant）；LIRA 属于注册合同，强制要求持有人与受保人为同一人。Incorrect. Falls into the trap of naming a contingent annuitant on a registered contract. Only non-registered contracts permit role separation between the policy owner and annuitant and allow designating a contingent annuitant; a LIRA is a registered contract that legally mandates the owner and annuitant be the same person.

  2. ✗ 错误。非注册分离基金合同允许持有人与受保人分离，并可指定候补受保人（contingent annuitant）在初始受保人生前安排接替。Incorrect. Non-registered segregated fund contracts permit the policy owner and annuitant to be separate persons, and allow naming a contingent annuitant during the primary annuitant's lifetime to succeed the contract upon their death.

  3. ✗ 错误。配偶豁免书（spousal waiver）涉及的是锁定养老金身故给付或解锁规则，并不能改变注册合同“持有人＝受保人强制绑定、不可设候补受保人”的结构性限制。Incorrect. A spousal waiver relates to locked-in pension death benefit entitlement or unlocking rules, and cannot override the structural registered contract rule requiring the owner and annuitant to be the same person or allow a contingent annuitant.

  4. ✓ 正确。注册账户（含 LIRA/RRSP/RRIF）强制要求持有人与受保人是同一人，一旦指定受保人就不能更改，也没有候补受保人（contingent annuitant）选项；非注册合同则允许持有人与受保人分离并指定候补受保人。Correct. Registered plans (including LIRAs, RRSPs, and RRIFs) strictly require the owner and annuitant to be the same person, so the annuitant cannot be changed once designated and no contingent annuitant option exists; non-registered contracts, conversely, allow separating owner and annuitant and naming a contingent annuitant.

第 2 题Q2保证条款与保险公司自定行政规则区分Distinguishing guaranteed clauses from insurer-discretionary administrative rules★★陷阱：混淆法定保证与自定行政规则

While reviewing a segregated fund contract with a new client, an advisor explains which provisions are legally or contractually fixed guarantees and which are administrative rules set at the insurer's discretion.   1. ✓ 正确。受保人 Beatrice 已身故，触发身故理赔（death claim）：此时由指定受益人 Chloe（而非生前 POA 代理人 Lucas）申领，须提供令保险人满意的死亡及领取资格证明（如死亡证明或主治医生证明 APS），给付金额为市值与经此前提款下调后的身故保证两者中的较高者。Correct. The annuitant Beatrice has died, triggering a death claim: the named beneficiary Chloe (not Lucas, the lifetime attorney under a POA) must submit the claim accompanied by proof of death and entitlement satisfactory to the insurer (such as a death certificate or APS), and the payout equals the higher of the market value and the death benefit guarantee as adjusted downward for prior withdrawals.

  2. ✗ 错误。混淆了到期理赔与身故理赔：POA 是持有人在世时的代办权限，受保人身故后进入身故理赔流程，应由受益人 Chloe 提交死亡证明申领，且因市值低于调整后身故保证，应赔付调整后保证额而非较低的市值。Incorrect. Confuses a maturity claim with a death claim: a POA confers administrative authority only during the owner's lifetime; once the annuitant dies, the death claim process applies, requiring beneficiary Chloe to submit proof of death, and because market value is below the adjusted death guarantee, the higher adjusted guarantee must be paid rather than the lower market value.

  3. ✗ 错误。身故理赔必须提供死亡证明或主治医生证明（APS）等合法证明文件（只有到期理赔才不需要死亡证明），且生前部分提款已按比例下调了身故保证基数。Incorrect. A death claim strictly requires legal proof of death such as a death certificate or Attending Physician's Statement (APS) (only maturity claims do not require a death certificate), and prior lifetime withdrawals have proportionally reduced the death guarantee base.

  4. ✗ 错误。部分提款只按比例下调保证金额，不会取消有效受益人指定；只有在没有在世合格受益人时才由遗产代表（executor/administrator）申领。Incorrect. A partial withdrawal only proportionally reduces the guarantee base without voiding a valid beneficiary designation; an estate representative (executor or administrator) claims only if no living eligible beneficiary exists.

第 6 题Q6联合末存年金与保证期的受益人领取权Beneficiary claim rights under joint and last survivor annuities with guarantee periods★★陷阱：联合年金首位身故即赔受益人陷阱

Harold and his spouse, Irene, purchased a joint and last survivor annuity with a 15-year guarantee period. Harold is the primary annuitant, Irene is the co-annuitant, and their adult son, Kevin, is the named beneficiary. Six years after annuity payments began, Harold dies while Irene is still alive. Who is entitled to receive payments under the annuity contract right now?

Harold 与配偶 Irene 购买了一份附 15 年保证期的联合末存年金（joint and last survivor annuity）。Harold 为主年金受领人，Irene 为联合受领人（co-annuitant），他们的成年儿子 Kevin 为指定受益人。年金开始给付六年后，Harold 身故，此时 Irene 仍在世。此刻谁有权领取该年金合同项下的款项？

  1. ✓ 正确。联合末存年金（joint and last survivor annuity）指定年金受领人＋联合受领人（co-annuitant）；第一位受领人（Harold）身故后，第二位受领人（Irene）成为唯一受领人继续领取年金。受益人（Kevin）必须等到第二位受领人也身故、且此时仍在 15 年保证期（guarantee period）内，才有权领取剩余保证期余额。Correct. A joint and last survivor annuity designates a primary annuitant and a co-annuitant; upon the death of the first annuitant (Harold), the surviving co-annuitant (Irene) becomes the sole annuitant and continues receiving annuity payments. The beneficiary (Kevin) is entitled to receive remaining guaranteed payments only after the second annuitant dies, provided that death occurs within the 15-year guarantee period.

  2. ✗ 错误。落入“联合年金首位身故即赔受益人陷阱”。在联合末存年金中，只要联合受领人（co-annuitant）Irene 仍在世，第一位受领人身故并不会触发向受益人给付，保证期余额须待第二位受领人也在保证期内身故时才轮到受益人。Incorrect. Falls into the trap of paying the beneficiary upon the first death in a joint annuity. In a joint and last survivor annuity, as long as the co-annuitant Irene is living, the death of the first annuitant does not trigger a payout to the beneficiary; remaining guaranteed payments pass to the beneficiary only if the second annuitant also dies within the guarantee period.

  3. ✗ 错误。第一位受领人身故后，联合受领人 Irene 成为唯一受领人（sole annuitant）独自继续领取年金，受益人 Kevin 此刻没有任何分配权。Incorrect. After the first annuitant dies, the surviving co-annuitant Irene becomes the sole annuitant receiving full ongoing annuity payments, leaving beneficiary Kevin with no claim rights at this time.

  4. ✗ 错误。把联合末存年金混淆成了纯单生无保证期年金（single life annuity with no guarantee period）。联合末存年金在第一位身故后继续向在世的联合受领人给付直至其身故。Incorrect. Confuses a joint and last survivor annuity with a single life annuity having no guarantee period. A joint and last survivor annuity continues payments to the surviving co-annuitant until their death.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Compare two annuity contracts after death occurs: Contract 1 is a single life annuity with no guarantee period and no death refund option, where the annuitant dies two years after payments start. Contract 2 is a joint and last survivor annuity with a 10-year guarantee period, where both the annuitant and the co-annuitant die in Year 7. In which contract does the named beneficiary receive a death benefit?

对比两份发生身故后的年金合同：合同 1 是一份无保证期且无任何身故退款选项的纯单生年金，受领人在给付开始两年后身故；合同 2 是一份附 10 年保证期的联合末存年金，主受领人与联合受领人双双在第 7 年身故。在哪份合同中，指定受益人能获得身故给付？

  1. ✗ 错误。75% 保证属于分离基金（IVIC）的保证机制，不适用于传统给付型年金；纯单生且无保证期或退款选项的年金在受领人身故后没有身故余额。Incorrect. A 75% guarantee is a guarantee mechanism of segregated funds (IVICs) and does not apply to traditional payout annuities; a single life annuity with no guarantee period or refund option provides no death benefit once the annuitant dies.

  2. ✗ 错误。纯单生、无保证期（no guarantee period）且无退款等身故选项的终身年金通常不设受益人求偿权，受领人身故后即停止给付，无身故余额。Incorrect. A single life annuity with no guarantee period and no death refund option provides no beneficiary claim rights, terminating payments immediately upon the annuitant's death with zero death benefit.

  3. ✗ 错误。保证期（guarantee period）的长短由合同约定（如本例合同 2 为 10 年），并非5年后失效；合同 2 的两位受领人在第 7 年双双身故，仍在 10 年保证期内，受益人有权领取剩余 3 年的保证给付。Incorrect. The length of a guarantee period is set by the contract (10 years in Contract 2) rather than expiring after 5 years; because both annuitants in Contract 2 died in Year 7, within the 10-year guarantee period, the beneficiary is entitled to the remaining 3 years of guaranteed payments.

  4. ✓ 正确。纯单生、无保证期且无任何身故退款选项的年金（合同 1）在受领人身故后通常没有身故余额；而联合末存年金（合同 2）在第二位受领人也身故、且仍处于 10 年保证期内（第 7 年）时，受益人有权申领剩余保证期余额。Correct. A single life annuity with no guarantee period and no death refund option (Contract 1) yields no death benefit following the annuitant's death; in contrast, under a joint and last survivor annuity (Contract 2), when the second annuitant dies within the 10-year guarantee period (Year 7), the named beneficiary is entitled to claim the remaining balance of guaranteed payments.

第 7 题Q7POA代签申请表与变更受益人的权限边界Authority boundaries of an attorney under a POA for application signing versus beneficiary changes★★★陷阱：POA全权变更受益人与免年龄证明陷阱

Graham holds an enduring Power of Attorney for property (POA) for his elderly aunt in British Columbia. He meets with an insurance advisor to sign a new segregated fund application on his aunt's behalf and also asks to change the existing beneficiary designation on another segregated fund contract his aunt already owns from her cousin to Graham's daughter. Under the exam guidance and British Columbia's Power of Attorney Act (s. 20), which statement is correct?

Graham 在 BC 省持有其年迈姑妈的持续财产授权书（enduring POA for property）。他与保险代理人会面，准备代表姑妈代签一份新的分离基金申请表，同时要求将姑妈名下另一份既有分离基金合同上的受益人指定从姑妈的表亲变更为 Graham 的女儿。根据考试口径提示及 BC 省《Power of Attorney Act》第 20 条，下列哪项陈述是正确的？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入“POA全权变更受益人陷阱”。考试口径明确提示“能代签申请表不等于能指定或变更受益人”，且 BC《Power of Attorney Act》s.20 严格限制持续授权代理人变更既有受益人。Incorrect. Falls into the broad POA beneficiary changes trap. Exam guidance explicitly warns that authority to sign an application does not confer authority to designate or change beneficiaries, and BC Power of Attorney Act s. 20 strictly restricts an attorney under an enduring POA from altering existing beneficiary designations.

  2. ✗ 错误。申请表虽然通常要求持有人本人签字，但持有人由有效财产授权书（POA for property）代理人代表属于合法的代签例外情形，代理人可以代签申请表。Incorrect. Although applications generally require the owner's personal signature, representation by an attorney acting under a valid Power of Attorney for property constitutes a recognized legal signing exception allowing the attorney to sign.

  3. ✓ 正确。准确涵盖考试口径与 BC 现行法规则：一方面，有效 POA 代理人可以代签分离基金申请表，但是否有权代为指定受益人，考试口径明确提示须另外寻求法律意见；另一方面，根据 BC《Power of Attorney Act》s.20，持续授权代理人变更本人已有的受益人指定必须取得法院授权（court authorization），更新替换类似文书仅可按条件沿用原受益人，另建新文书的新指定仅限本人遗产。Correct. Accurately reflects both exam guidance and current BC law: on one hand, an attorney under a valid POA may sign a segregated fund application, but whether they have the authority to designate a beneficiary requires separate legal advice; on the other hand, under BC Power of Attorney Act s. 20, an attorney under an enduring POA cannot change an existing beneficiary designation without court authorization, may retain the original beneficiary only when renewing or replacing a similar instrument under qualifying conditions, and is restricted to designating the adult's estate on newly created instruments.

  4. ✗ 错误。理由信（reason-why letter）只是代理人对客户需求分析结论的书面确认，既不是核保或合同生效证明，更不能替代 BC《Power of Attorney Act》s.20 要求的法院授权（court authorization）。Incorrect. A reason-why letter is simply the advisor's written confirmation of their needs analysis findings, which is neither underwriting proof nor contract validation, and certainly cannot substitute for the court authorization mandated by BC Power of Attorney Act s. 20.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

In British Columbia, an attorney acting under an enduring Power of Attorney (POA) is moving an incapacitated client's maturing segregated fund into a replacement contract of a similar instrument and is also establishing a brand-new contract with separate cash funds. Under BC's Power of Attorney Act (s. 20), what are the rules regarding beneficiary designations on these instruments without obtaining a court order?

在 BC 省，一名依据持续授权书（enduring POA）行事的代理人正在将一名失能客户的到期分离基金转入一份替换的类似文书合同，同时用另一笔独立现金新建一份全新的合同。根据 BC 省《Power of Attorney Act》第 20 条，在未取得法院命令的情况下，关于这些文书上的受益人指定规则是什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入“看到 BC 两字就以为 POA 可任意换受益人”的陷阱。BC《Power of Attorney Act》s.20 对持续授权代理人设有严格条件，未经法院授权绝不能任意指定新家庭成员为受益人。Incorrect. Falls into the trap of assuming a POA in BC can freely alter beneficiaries. BC Power of Attorney Act s. 20 imposes strict conditions on attorneys under enduring powers of attorney, expressly barring them from naming new family members as beneficiaries without court authorization.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据 BC《Power of Attorney Act》s.20 规定：持续授权代理人在未经法院授权时，不得变更本人已有受益人指定；在更新、替换或转换类似文书（renewing, replacing, or converting a similar instrument）时，可依条件沿用本人有能力时指定的同一受益人；而在另建新文书时，新指定仅限本人遗产（estate）。Correct. Under BC Power of Attorney Act s. 20: an attorney under an enduring POA cannot alter an existing beneficiary designation without court authorization; when renewing, replacing, or converting a similar instrument, they may retain the same beneficiary designated while the adult was capable under statutory conditions; and when creating a brand-new instrument, any new designation is limited strictly to the adult's estate.

  3. ✗ 错误。POA 代理人必须为本人利益行事，未经法院授权不得把受益人改为或新指定为自己。Incorrect. An attorney under a POA must act strictly in the adult's best interests and is prohibited from changing or creating beneficiary designations in favor of themselves without court authorization.

  4. ✗ 错误。有效 POA 代理人有权代签分离基金申请表，且不会因此导致合同丧失身故保证。Incorrect. An attorney under a valid POA is legally authorized to sign segregated fund applications on the adult's behalf, and doing so does not cause the contract to lose its death benefit guarantee.
