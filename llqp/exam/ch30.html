① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 举证要件与两套时钟辨析题：给出受益人提交材料或拖延起诉的时间线，测试能否区分保护客户的付款时钟（四项充分证据齐备后 30 天内给付）与保护保险人的起诉时效时钟（证据提交后 2 年与死亡后 6 年取较早者，推定死亡宣告后另计 2 年）。Distinction questions on evidentiary requirements and the two time clocks: Presenting timelines where beneficiaries submit materials or delay legal action, testing the ability to distinguish between the client-protecting payment clock (payment within 30 days once all four items of sufficient proof are received) and the insurer-protecting limitation clock (the earlier of 2 years after submitting proof or 6 years after death, with a separate 2-year period following a declaration of presumption of death).
  • 失踪与推定死亡双口径场景题：通过登山失联或空难等案情，测试考试口径的 7 年失踪通则、有充分间接证据时无需等待的例外（对比 BC《Presumption of Death Act》s.3 无固定年限的三要件），以及失踪期间必须继续缴费以维持保单有效（in force）的核心风控。Scenario questions on the dual standards for disappearance and presumption of death: Using circumstances such as hikers missing in the mountains or aviation disasters to test the exam standard general 7-year disappearance rule, exceptions where sufficient circumstantial evidence removes the waiting period (compared with the three criteria under BC Presumption of Death Act s.3 which has no fixed time requirement), and the core risk control requirement of continuing premium payments during the disappearance to keep the policy in force.
  • 同时死亡与受益人顺位归属题：设计被保险人与主要受益人在同一事故中当场身故或相隔数日身故的场景，测试保险金适用《Insurance Act》s.83（同时死亡或先后不明时视受益人先死）且明文排除《Wills, Estates and Succession Act》五日生存规则（five-day survival rule）的去向判断。Questions on simultaneous death and beneficiary priority: Designing scenarios where the life insured and primary beneficiary die at the same time or several days apart in the same accident, testing distribution determinations under Insurance Act s.83 (presuming the beneficiary died first in simultaneous death or uncertain sequence) and the explicit exclusion of the five-day survival rule under the Wills, Estates and Succession Act.
  • 多方争抢与三把保护伞实务题：前配偶与遗嘱新受益人同时主张权利时，测试记录免责（s.77 总部收到书面文件前按原记录付款免责）、付入法院（s.82 承认付款责任但存在相争索赔人时交由法院裁断）与证据充分性宣告（s.78）的适用前提，以及代理人如实告知冲突、不预判结果的职业义务。Practical questions on competing claimants and the three safe harbours: When a former spouse and a new beneficiary named in a will assert competing claims, testing the preconditions for the record discharge rule (s.77 discharging the insurer for payments made per existing records before head office receives contrary written documents), payment into court (s.82 surrendering funds for judicial determination when admitting liability with adverse claimants), and a declaration as to sufficiency of proof (s.78), alongside the agent's professional duty to disclose conflicts objectively without predicting outcomes.
  • 拒赔抗辩与跨产品给付规则题：结合自杀条款、2 年不可抗辩期（欺诈除外）、违反公共秩序失权（不问受益人动机与是否知情）、个人 A&S 法定条件天数（30/15/90 天及误工首笔 30 天 vs 非误工 60 天）、分离基金死后计价与单人终身年金保证期，测试哪笔钱能赔、付给谁及何时付。Questions on claim denial defenses and cross-product payout rules: Combining suicide clauses, the 2-year incontestability period (except fraud), forfeiture for acts contrary to public order (regardless of beneficiary motive or knowledge), statutory condition timelines for individual A&S (30/15/90 days, and 30 days for initial loss-of-time benefits versus 60 days for non-loss-of-time), post-death segregated fund valuation, and single-life annuity guarantee periods to test which benefits are payable, to whom, and when.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 失踪七年门槛与保单失效陷阱Seven-year disappearance threshold and policy lapse trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生死记考试口径的「失踪满 7 年」，忽略了有充分间接证据（如空难）时无需等待，且在 BC《Presumption of Death Act》s.3 下并无固定年限；同时忘记若失踪期间停缴保费导致保单失效，索赔人必须证明推定死亡日落在保单有效期内。Candidates memorize the 7-year disappearance general exam rule while overlooking that sufficient circumstantial evidence (such as an air disaster) removes the waiting requirement, and that BC Presumption of Death Act s.3 sets no fixed time threshold; they also forget that if premium payments stop and cause the policy to lapse during disappearance, the claimant must prove the presumed date of death fell within the policy's in-force period.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现失踪（disappearance）、空难或灾难事故，或提到家属在失踪期间考虑停缴保费（stop paying premiums）。The question stem features a disappearance, air crash, or catastrophic accident, or mentions family members considering stopping premium payments during the absence.

⚠ 付入法院当作拒赔手段Mistaking payment into court for claim denial trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到保险公司把款项交给法院（payment into court），直觉以为是保险公司拒赔或质疑保单效力；实际上 s.82 付入法院的前提恰恰是保险人已经承认付款责任，只是面对相争索赔人（adverse claimants）无法自行裁断归属。Seeing an insurer pay funds into court leads candidates to intuitively assume the insurer is denying liability or contesting policy validity; in reality, payment into court under s.82 strictly requires that the insurer has admitted liability to pay and merely faces adverse claimants whose entitlements it cannot resolve on its own.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现两位以上索赔人各执一份指定文件争抢保险金，或询问保险人在承认责任但归属不明时的法定救济通道。The question stem describes two or more claimants presenting conflicting designation documents to claim proceeds, or asks for the statutory remedy when an insurer admits liability but entitlement remains disputed.

⚠ A&S付款时限一刀切60天Assuming a blanket 60-day A&S payment deadline trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考试口径通则笼统写个人意外与疾病险（A&S）在收到证明后 60 天内付款，导致考生忽略了 BC 法定条件将误工给付（loss of time benefit，首笔 30 天、此后每 60 天至少一次）与非误工给付（60 天）拆成两档。The general exam rule broadly states that individual Accident and Sickness (A&S) benefits are paid within 60 days of receiving proof, leading candidates to overlook that BC statutory conditions divide payments into two tiers: loss-of-time benefits (initial payment within 30 days, and at least once every 60 days thereafter) and non-loss-of-time benefits (60 days).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及个人 A&S 保单理赔，并具体区分是伤残收入/误工给付（loss of time）还是一次性非误工给付。The question stem involves an individual A&S claim and differentiates between disability income or loss-of-time benefits versus a lump-sum non-loss-of-time benefit.

⚠ 同时死亡套用五日生存规则Applying the five-day survival rule to simultaneous death trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把遗产法（WESA s.10）的五日生存规则套用到寿险保单上，误以为受益人比被保险人晚死 3 天也要视为先死；或者反过来在先后明确时仍机械套用《Insurance Act》s.83 的同时死亡推定。Candidates apply the five-day survival rule of estate law (WESA s.10) to life insurance policies, mistakenly assuming a beneficiary who survives the life insured by 3 days is deemed to have died first; alternatively, they mechanically apply the Insurance Act s.83 simultaneous death presumption even when the order of death is clearly established.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现房产/遗产与人寿保单，或写明被保险人与受益人在同一场事故中一先一后（如相隔 3 天）去世。The question stem involves both real property or estate assets and life insurance, or specifies that the life insured and beneficiary died sequentially (e.g., 3 days apart) from injuries in the same accident.

⚠ 不知情受益人免于公序失权Assuming an unknowing beneficiary avoids public order forfeiture trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为只有为了骗保而故意杀害被保险人才会丧失受益权；实际上只要受益人违法致被保险人死亡并触发违反公共秩序（contrary to public order）失权规则，不论其是否有获利动机、也不论其事先是否知道自己是受益人，均不得受领该笔保险金。Candidates mistakenly believe a beneficiary only forfeits policy rights if they killed the life insured intentionally to collect insurance proceeds; in reality, whenever a beneficiary unlawfully causes the death of the life insured triggering the rule against payments contrary to public order, they are barred from receiving proceeds regardless of profit motive or prior knowledge of being named beneficiary.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调致死受益人「事先不知道自己是保单受益人」或「并非为了保险金作案」，并询问保险人能否向其拒付。The question stem emphasizes that the beneficiary who caused the death was unaware of being named beneficiary or did not commit the crime for insurance money, asking whether the insurer can refuse payment.

第 1 题Q1四项举证门、通知义务与两套时钟Four proof-of-claim elements, notice duty, and the two time clocks★★陷阱：把索赔无时限读成随时能告或以为保司须主动通知受益人

An insured with a $500,000 life insurance policy died two years ago. Upon receiving reliable notice of death from the insured's employer, the insurer recorded the death and stopped billing premiums, but did not reach out to the named beneficiary, who was unaware of the policy. Today, the beneficiary discovers the policy and submits the death certificate, proof of the insured's age, proof of the claimant's right to receive payment, and proof of the beneficiary's name and age. Under the exam rules and the BC Insurance Act, how do the notification rules and the two time clocks apply?

一位持有 $500,000 人寿保单的被保险人于两年前去世。保险人收到被保险人雇主发来的可靠死亡通知后，登记了死亡并停止收取保费，但未联系不知情的具名受益人。今天，受益人发现了保单，并提交了死亡证明、被保险人年龄证明、索赔人受领权证明以及受益人姓名与年龄证明。根据考试口径与 BC《Insurance Act》，通知规则与两套时钟如何适用？

  1. ✗ 陷入「以为保险人有义务主动通知受益人」及「起算点混淆」陷阱：考试口径明言保险人收到死亡通知后无义务主动通知受益人（通常会联系逝者的代理人）；且 30 天付款时钟（s.73）从收到四项充分证据 [proof of claim] 之日起算，而非死亡日起算。Incorrect. Falls into the assuming an insurer is obligated to proactively notify beneficiaries and confusing start dates traps. The exam standard clearly states that an insurer has no duty to proactively notify the beneficiary upon receiving notice of death (it typically contacts the deceased's agent); furthermore, the 30-day payment clock under s.73 begins on the date all four items of sufficient proof of claim are received, rather than from the date of death.

  2. ✓ 保险人收到死亡通知后登记、停收保费、等待索赔，无主动通知受益人的义务；当 s.73 要求的四项充分证据 [proof of claim] 交齐后，保护客户的 30 天付款时钟启动；同时保护保险人的起诉时效 [limitation period]（s.76）为证据提交后 2 年与死亡后 6 年取较早者。Correct. Upon receiving notice of death, the insurer records the death, stops billing premiums, and awaits a claim, having no duty to actively notify the beneficiary; once all four items of sufficient proof of claim required by s.73 are submitted, the client-protecting 30-day payment clock begins; concurrently, the insurer-protecting limitation period under s.76 requires any legal action to be commenced within the earlier of 2 years after submitting proof or 6 years after death.

  3. ✗ 这是张冠李戴陷阱：30 天书面通知与 90 天提交证明是个人意外与疾病险（A&S）的法定条件，人寿保险无此独立法定通知与证明天数。Incorrect. Falls into the misapplying statutory provisions trap. The requirements of written notice within 30 days and proof of claim within 90 days are statutory conditions for individual Accident and Sickness (A&S) insurance; life insurance does not have these independent statutory calendar-day deadlines.

  4. ✗ 陷入「把索赔没有时限读成随时都能告」陷阱：虽然提交索赔本身无固定法定天数，但就保险金起诉保险人的诉讼时效 [limitation period] 是硬性的（交证后 2 年与死亡后 6 年取较早者，s.76）。Incorrect. Falls into the interpreting no deadline for filing a claim as an unlimited right to sue trap. While filing a claim has no fixed statutory deadline, the statutory limitation period under s.76 for suing the insurer for insurance money is strict, requiring action within the earlier of 2 years after proof is submitted or 6 years after death.

Following the death of an insured under a $300,000 term life policy, the named beneficiary promptly submits a funeral director's statement of death and her own photo identification, but has not yet provided satisfactory proof of the insured's age or proof of her right as claimant. When does the insurer's 30-day statutory payment period begin, and what governs the deadline to sue the insurer?

The insurer has 15 days, not 30 days, to provide claim forms; group A&S payment timing is governed by the group contract rather than statutory conditions, and BC individual A&S requires the initial loss-of-time payment within 30 days.

  4. ✗ 把寿险「无独立法定通知/证明天数」错套到了个人 A&S 上；且伤残理赔是持续给付、持续审核，给付期间保险人有权反复要求更新主治医生声明 [Attending physician's statement (APS)] 及医学检查。Incorrect. Erroneously applies the life insurance rule of having no independent statutory notice and proof deadlines to individual A&S; furthermore, disability claims involve ongoing payments and ongoing assessment, during which the insurer is entitled to request updated Attending Physician's Statements (APS) and medical examinations.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A claimant has been receiving ongoing disability income (loss of time) benefits under an individual A&S policy after receiving the first payment within 30 days. During the ongoing benefit period, the insurer requests an updated Attending Physician's Statement (APS) and a medical examination, and the claimant also asks her agent what the statutory payment deadline would be if she had instead been covered under an employer's group A&S contract. Which explanation is accurate?

一位索赔人在 30 天内收到首笔赔款后，正在按个人 A&S 保单领取持续的伤残收入（误工）给付。在持续给付期间，保险人要求提交更新的主治医生声明（APS）并接受医学检查；索赔人同时询问代理人，如果她受保的是雇主的团体 A&S 合同，法定付款时限会是多少。哪项解释是准确的？

  1. ✓ 伤残（误工）理赔不是一次结清，而是持续给付、持续审核，保险人在给付期间有权要求医学检查并反复更新主治医生声明 [Attending physician's statement (APS)]；团体 A&S 的付款时限依团体合同约定，法定条件针对的是个人合同。Correct. Disability (loss of time) claims are not settled in a single lump sum but involve ongoing payments and ongoing reviews, giving the insurer the right during the benefit period to require medical examinations and updated Attending Physician's Statements (APS); payment timing for group A&S is governed by the group contract, as statutory conditions apply to individual contracts.

  2. ✗ 2 年不可抗辩期（s.52）管的是投保时善意未披露能否撤销合同，并不禁止保险人在伤残给付期间持续核查伤残状态并要求更新 APS。Incorrect. The 2-year incontestability period under s.52 governs whether innocent non-disclosure at application can void the policy, and it does not prohibit an insurer from verifying ongoing disability and requesting updated APS forms during the benefit period.

  3. ✗ 团体 A&S 付款时限由团体合同约定，法律未作统一规定；15 天是个人 A&S 中保险人收到通知后提供理赔表格的期限。Incorrect. Group A&S payment timing is determined by the group policy terms rather than codified by statute; 15 days is the deadline in individual A&S for an insurer to furnish claim forms upon receiving notice.

  4. ✗ 根据 BC《Insurance Act》s.104(3)，非身故类 A&S 给付的诉讼时效是自知道或应当知道之日起 2 年（身故给付才是 2 年/6 年取较早，s.104(1)）。Incorrect. Under BC Insurance Act s.104(3), the limitation period for non-death A&S claims is 2 years from the date the claimant knew or ought to have known of the claim (the earlier of 2 years from proof or 6 years from death applies only to death claims under s.104(1)).

第 6 题Q6违反公共秩序失权与不可抗辩期及自杀Public order forfeiture, incontestability period, and suicide exclusion★★陷阱：不知情受益人免于公序失权

An insured with a $500,000 life insurance policy is killed in a criminal assault committed by his brother. The brother had no intention of profiting from the policy and did not even know that he was named as one of the two equal primary beneficiaries on the policy. Under the public order rule on the exam, how must the insurer handle the claim?

一位持有 $500,000 人寿保单的被保险人在其弟弟实施的刑事袭击中身亡。弟弟作案时并无借保单获利的意图，甚至根本不知道自己被指定为该保单两名均等份额主要受益人之一。根据考试中的公共秩序规则，保险人应如何处理该理赔？

  1. ✓ 违反公共秩序的给付 [payment contrary to public order] 规则极为严格：受益人违法致被保险人死亡触发公共政策失权时，不问其是否有获利动机、也不问其事先是否知道自己是受益人，均不得受领；但该规则只挡住这名致死受益人，合同本身并未失效，其余在世受益人或遗产仍按指定与法律受领。Correct. The rule against payments contrary to public order is exceptionally strict: when a beneficiary unlawfully causes the death of the life insured, triggering public policy forfeiture, they are barred from receiving proceeds regardless of whether they had a profit motive or knew they were named beneficiary; however, this forfeiture bars only that culpable beneficiary, while the policy contract remains valid and proceeds are distributed to the remaining innocent beneficiary or the estate.

  2. ✗ 陷入「不知情受益人免于公序失权」陷阱：考场上凡是把「他不知道自己是受益人」或「无骗保动机」当作应予给付理由的选项均为错项，因为普通法原则不允许任何人从自己的不法行为中获益。Incorrect. Falls into the assuming an unknowing beneficiary avoids public order forfeiture trap. On the exam, any option treating lack of knowledge or absence of insurance fraud motive as a justification for payment is incorrect, because public policy and common law do not permit anyone to benefit from their own criminal act.

  3. ✗ 混淆了受益人失权与合同无效：致死受益人失权只挡住该名受益人本人，保单合同本身依然有效，不能连坐剥夺其他无辜受益人或遗产的权利。Incorrect. Confuses beneficiary forfeiture with contract invalidity. Forfeiture by a beneficiary who causes the death affects only that culpable individual, whereas the policy contract itself remains valid and innocent co-beneficiaries or the estate are not disqualified.

  4. ✗ 2 年不可抗辩期 [incontestability period]（s.52）仅针对投保时的善意未披露或误述，绝不保护违反公共秩序的犯罪致死行为。Incorrect. The 2-year incontestability period under s.52 applies solely to innocent misrepresentation or non-disclosure at application and never protects against criminal acts causing death contrary to public order.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An insured bought a $300,000 life insurance policy 3 years ago. The policy later lapsed for non-payment after the 30-day grace period and was formally reinstated within the past year. Today, within 2 years of reinstatement, the insured dies by suicide. While reviewing the claim, the beneficiary's lawyer also asks how the 2-year incontestability rule treats two kinds of application errors in general: an innocent mistake about family history, and a deliberate concealment of a serious condition. Under the BC Insurance Act, how do the suicide clause and the 2-year incontestability rule apply?

被保险人于 3 年前购买了保额 $300,000 的人寿保险。后来保单在 30 天宽限期后因欠费失效，并在过去一年内正式复效。今天（复效后未满 2 年），被保险人自杀身亡。审核理赔时，受益人的律师还问到 2 年不可抗辩期一般如何对待两类投保申请错误：关于家族病史的无心疏漏，以及对重大病情的故意隐瞒。根据 BC《Insurance Act》，自杀条款与 2 年不可抗辩期规则如何适用？

  1. ✗ BC《Insurance Act》s.56 明确确认保单关于自杀不赔的约定合法且可执行；且 s.52 的 2 年不可抗辩期明文规定欺诈（fraud）不适用该保护。Incorrect. BC Insurance Act s.56 explicitly confirms that suicide exclusion clauses are lawful and enforceable; furthermore, the 2-year incontestability provision under s.52 expressly states that fraud is never protected.

  2. ✗ 保单通常约定「生效或复效后 2 年内」自杀不赔（s.56 确认合法有效），复效后未满 2 年仍在复效后的 2 年自杀限制期内。Incorrect. Policies typically stipulate that suicide within 2 years of issue or reinstatement is excluded (confirmed as valid under s.56); dying by suicide within 2 years of reinstatement falls squarely within the reinstated 2-year exclusion window.

  3. ✗ 把不可抗辩期 [incontestability period]（s.52）的保护对象彻底颠倒了：生效满 2 年后，善意未披露不再使合同可撤销，而欺诈在任何时候都可作为撤销/拒赔理由。Incorrect. Completely reverses the protections of the incontestability period under s.52: after 2 years in force, innocent non-disclosure no longer renders the contract voidable, whereas deliberate fraud remains an actionable ground for rescission or claim denial at any time.

  4. ✓ s.56 确认约定生效或复效后 2 年内自杀不赔合法有效（复效后未满 2 年自杀在除外期内）；s.52 的 2 年不可抗辩期 [incontestability period] 保护生效满 2 年后的善意误述/未披露，但欺诈（fraud）不受 2 年期保护，任何时候均可抗辩。Correct. Section 56 confirms that policy clauses excluding suicide within 2 years of issue or reinstatement are lawful and enforceable (suicide within 2 years of reinstatement is therefore excluded); the 2-year incontestability provision under s.52 protects innocent misrepresentation or non-disclosure once in force for 2 years, but deliberate fraud is never protected and may be contested at any time.

第 7 题Q7未成年受益人受领与退休金配偶优先Payment channels for minor beneficiaries and pension spouse priority★★陷阱：未成年受益人直接付给父母或混淆付入法院与BC信托通道

An insured in BC dies leaving a $250,000 life insurance policy naming his young minor nephew (under age 18) as beneficiary without appointing a trustee for the funds, and another $250,000 policy naming his 18-year-old daughter as beneficiary. How does the general exam rule describe the payment of insurance proceeds to a minor when no trustee is appointed, and how does section 88 of the BC Insurance Act specifically handle the payments to the minor nephew and the 18-year-old daughter?

BC 的一位被保险人身故，留下一份保额 $250,000 的寿险保单指定其未满 18 岁的年幼侄子为受益人（未为该笔款项指定受托人），以及另一份保额 $250,000 的保单指定其年满 18 岁的女儿为受益人。当未指定受托人时，考试口径通则如何描述向未成年人支付保险金？BC《Insurance Act》s.88 又如何具体处理向年幼侄子与 18 岁女儿的给付？

  1. ✗ 以为未成年受益人的保险金直接付给孩子的父母是典型误解：若父母未被正式指定为该笔保险金的受托人或财产监护人，保险人不能直接把钱交给父母个人。Incorrect. Assuming insurance proceeds for a minor beneficiary can be paid directly to a parent is a common misconception; unless the parent has been formally appointed as a trustee for the insurance funds or a property guardian, the insurer cannot release the money directly to the parent.

  2. ✗ 依 BC《Insurance Act》s.88(4)，年满 18 岁的受益人在领款与出具收据时即视同成年人，可直接受领并出具有效收据，更不会被保险人没收。Incorrect. Under BC Insurance Act s.88(4), a beneficiary who has reached age 18 has the same capacity to receive insurance money and discharge the insurer as an adult; proceeds are paid directly to her and are never forfeited to the insurer.

  3. ✓ 这是考试口径通则与 BC 成文法的双口径考点：考试口径提某些省可能要求付入法院以保护未成年人资金，而在 BC 走的是 s.88 信托通道——有指定受托人付给受托人，无受托人则付给公共监护与受托人办公室 [Public Guardian and Trustee]；同时 s.88(4) 明文规定年满 18 岁的受益人领款与出具收据时视同成年人，由保险人直接付给本人。（注：一句话区分考场通则「某些省付入法院」与 BC s.88「受托人或公共监护与受托人办公室及 18 岁视同成年」。）Correct. This tests the dual standard between the exam general rule and BC statutory law: exam materials note that some provinces may require payment into court to protect a minor's funds, whereas BC follows the s.88 trust mechanism—paying a designated trustee, or paying the Public Guardian and Trustee if no trustee exists; furthermore, s.88(4) explicitly provides that a beneficiary aged 18 or older is treated as an adult with full legal capacity to receive proceeds and discharge the insurer directly. (Note: Distinguish between the exam standard of payment into court in some provinces versus BC s.88 paying the trustee or Public Guardian and Trustee, and treating 18-year-olds as adults).

  4. ✗ 未成年人完全可以被指定为保单受益人，只是在满 18 岁前无法自己出具有效免责收据，需通过受托人或公共监护与受托人办公室代为受领管理。Incorrect. Minors can validly be named as policy beneficiaries; they simply lack legal capacity to give a binding discharge before reaching age 18, requiring payment to be managed through a designated trustee or the Public Guardian and Trustee.

An individual holds two contracts: a regular life insurance policy and a locked-in retirement plan funded from a pension plan. On both contracts, he designated his adult brother as beneficiary, even though he has a qualifying spouse who has not waived her statutory entitlement. When the individual dies, who has priority to receive the death benefits from the two contracts?

某人持有两份合同：一份普通人寿保险保单和一份来源于养老金计划的锁定退休资金计划。在这两份合同上，他均指定其成年弟弟为受益人，尽管他有一位未放弃法定权利的合资格配偶。当该人身故时，谁有权优先受领这两份合同的身故给付？

  1. ✗ 普通人寿保险按保单上的受益人指定给付（除非债权人凭抚养费法院令等特定情形主张），并无配偶自动推翻具名受益人的法定优先顺位。Incorrect. Proceeds under a regular life insurance policy are paid to the designated beneficiary (unless subject to specific court orders such as child or spousal support); there is no statutory rule allowing a spouse to automatically override a named beneficiary on regular life insurance.

  2. ✓ 普通人寿保险的身故给付归具名受益人（成年弟弟）；而退休金类/锁定资金 [locked-in funds] 另有法定顺序：合资格配偶优先于指定受益人，无合资格配偶才轮到指定受益人，再无则归遗产。Correct. The death benefit under the regular life insurance policy goes to the named beneficiary (the adult brother); in contrast, pension-derived locked-in funds follow a statutory priority order where a qualifying spouse ranks ahead of any designated beneficiary, passing to the named beneficiary only if there is no qualifying spouse, and reverting to the estate only if neither exists.

  3. ✗ 退休金类资金受适用养老金法约束，合资格配偶享有法定优先权，且锁定资金 [locked-in funds] 的给付方式与解锁例外须按养老金法核查，不能由普通受益人指定强行推翻。Incorrect. Pension-derived funds are governed by pension standards legislation giving a qualifying spouse statutory priority; payout methods and unlocking exceptions must comply with pension law and cannot be overridden by an ordinary beneficiary designation.

  4. ✗ 公共监护与受托人办公室 [Public Guardian and Trustee]（s.88）仅在受益人为未满 18 岁的未成年人且无指定受托人时介入，与成年弟弟或配偶顺位无关。Incorrect. The Public Guardian and Trustee under s.88 is involved only when a beneficiary is a minor under age 18 with no designated trustee, which is irrelevant to adult beneficiaries or spousal priority.
