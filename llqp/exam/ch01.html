本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 多维冲击识别题：题干给出一个家庭或企业主的完整背景（如单亲、房贷、未注册升值资产、年幼子女），要求识别身故同时触发哪几类财务冲击，重点考是否漏掉非收入方的照护者损失（loss of caregiver）或视同处置（deemed disposition）税负。Multi-dimensional impact identification questions: The question stem provides the comprehensive background of a family or business owner (such as a single parent, mortgage, non-registered appreciated assets, young children) and requires identifying which categories of financial impacts of death are triggered simultaneously, testing whether the candidate overlooks the loss of caregiver for a non-earning party or deemed disposition tax liabilities.
  • 风险管理四招匹配题：题干描述客户采取的某项行动（如戒烟、用现有存款预留丧葬费、购买保单）或询问针对死亡风险某招是否可行，考避免（risk avoidance）、减少（risk reduction）、自留（risk retention）与转移（risk transfer）的精确区分与组合使用。Four risk management strategies matching questions: The question stem describes an action taken by a client (such as quitting smoking, setting aside existing savings for funeral expenses, or purchasing an insurance policy) or asks whether a specific strategy is feasible for the risk of death, testing the precise distinction and combined application of risk avoidance, risk reduction, risk retention, and risk transfer.
  • 视同处置与税务认知题：以客户声称“加拿大没有遗产税所以身故不用交税”或企业主传承合格小型企业股份为背景，测试视同处置（deemed disposition）机制、配偶递延例外以及终身资本利得豁免（LCGE）只能部分抵消而非全免。Deemed disposition and tax awareness questions: Using scenarios where a client claims that because Canada has no estate tax, no tax is owed at death, or where a business owner transfers qualified small business corporation shares, testing the deemed disposition mechanism, the spousal rollover exception, and the rule that the Lifetime Capital Gains Exemption (LCGE) only partially offsets capital gains rather than fully eliminating tax.
  • 双口径与数据机制题：围绕生命表中的死亡概率（probability of death）与预期寿命（life expectancy）关系、CPP 遗属给付（CPP survivor's pension）与一次性身故给付（CPP death benefit）的考试口径及核定规则出题，考条件期望概念及不盲目套用最高额。Dual-benchmark and numerical mechanics questions: Centering on the relationship between probability of death and life expectancy in mortality tables, as well as the exam standard and assessment rules for the CPP survivor's pension and lump-sum CPP death benefit, testing the concept of conditional expectation and avoiding blind application of maximum amounts.
  • 实务流程与生命周期配对题：描述客户所处的人生阶段（单身、组建家庭、事业上升、临近退休）或代理人的展业步骤，考先做需求分析（needs analysis）再谈产品、以及不同阶段主导财务冲击的变化。Practice process and life cycle matching questions: Describing a client's life stage (single, starting a family, career progression, approaching retirement) or an agent's practice workflow, testing that needs analysis must precede product recommendations, as well as how primary financial impacts change across life stages.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 范畴遗漏（只算工资漏算照护）Category omission (counting lost salary while overlooking lost caregiving)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生习惯把“没有工资收入”等同于“身故没有经济损失”，或把单亲家庭的唯一收入者身故只按工资替代计算，忽略了遗属必须额外花钱雇人替代育儿与家务劳务。Candidates tend to equate having no earned income with having no economic loss upon death, or calculate the death of a single parent's sole earner strictly as salary replacement, overlooking that survivors must pay out of pocket to hire replacement childcare and domestic services.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现全职在家照顾幼童/失能亲属的配偶，或独自抚养未成年子女的单亲家长。The question stem features a stay-at-home spouse caring for young children or disabled relatives, or a single parent raising minor children alone.

⚠ 概念混淆（无遗产税等于无身故税）Concept confusion (equating no estate tax with no taxes at death)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 听到加拿大1972年取消联邦遗产税（estate tax），就误以为财产传给下一代完全不交税，忘了身故当天触发资本财产视同处置（deemed disposition）及未指定合格受益人的注册账户并入当年收入。Hearing that Canada abolished the federal estate tax in 1972, candidates incorrectly assume that wealth transfers to the next generation completely tax-free, forgetting that death triggers a deemed disposition of capital property at fair market value and includes registered accounts without a qualified beneficiary in final income.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干中客户持有升值度假屋、未注册投资组合、企业股权或大额 RRSP/RRIF，且继承人是成年子女而非配偶。The question stem features a client holding an appreciated vacation cottage, non-registered investment portfolio, corporate shares, or large RRSP/RRIF balances, with adult children rather than a spouse named as heirs.

⚠ 机制混淆（把不可规避当成不能降低或不能自留）Mechanics confusion (treating unavoidability as inability to reduce or retain risk)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 因为“活着就暴露于死亡风险”导致死亡风险不可规避（risk avoidance），考生就极端地认为遇到死亡风险只能选风险转移（risk transfer），排除了健康生活属于风险降低（risk reduction）或高净值人士对小额缺口采取风险自留（risk retention）。Because living inherently exposes someone to the risk of death, making risk avoidance impossible, candidates jump to the extreme belief that risk transfer is the only option, excluding healthy living as risk reduction or affluent individuals using risk retention for small shortfalls.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现改善生活习惯、增加安全措施，或客户拥有充裕流动资产足以覆盖小额最终开支。The question stem mentions improving lifestyle habits, adding workplace safety measures, or a client possessing ample liquid assets to cover small final expenses.

⚠ 双口径盲套（把政府给付或豁免额当成自动全额免除）Dual-benchmark blind application (assuming government benefits or exemptions automatically eliminate all liability)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 死记硬背 CPP 一次性身故给付 $2,500（或 2025 年起附加后 $5,000）及终身资本利得豁免（LCGE，考试口径 $913,630 / 2026 上限 $1,275,000），误以为所有丧葬费或企业股权税单都能被自动全额抹平。Rote memorization of the CPP lump-sum death benefit of $2,500 (or up to $5,000 under qualifying conditions from 2025 onward) and the Lifetime Capital Gains Exemption (LCGE, exam standard $913,630 / 2026 ceiling $1,275,000), incorrectly assuming that all funeral costs or corporate share tax liabilities are automatically wiped out.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提到“预计政府给付足够覆盖全部最终费用”或“因为有 LCGE 所以企业股份传承无需准备税款现金”。The question stem mentions expecting government benefits to fully cover all final expenses or assuming corporate shares can be transferred with zero cash for taxes because of the LCGE.

⚠ 性质与流程倒置（把寿险当投资或跳过需求分析直接推产品）Reversal of nature and process (treating life insurance as an investment or pitching products before needs analysis)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到客户有闲置资金或询问带现金价值的保单，就按投资收益去解释寿险存在理由，或者在确认风险转移需求后不经过事实发现面谈（fact-find）就直接挑选具体保单。Seeing that a client has idle funds or asks about cash value policies, candidates explain the purpose of life insurance in terms of investment returns, or jump straight into selecting a specific policy upon confirming a risk transfer need without conducting a fact-find.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问人寿保险的核心功能是什么，或问代理人确认客户需要转移死亡风险后的紧接下一步行动。The question stem asks for the core function of life insurance, or asks for the agent's immediate next step after confirming that the client needs to transfer the risk of death.

第 1 题Q1六类冲击并发与照护者损失识别Concurrent identification of the six financial impacts and loss of caregiver★★陷阱：范畴遗漏（只算工资漏算照护）

Marcus, age 38, is a widowed father in Coquitlam raising two children aged 4 and 6. He earns $95,000 a year as a logistics manager, carries a $420,000 mortgage, and wants to ensure $80,000 is set aside for his children's university education if he dies prematurely. When Marcus asks an agent to calculate his life insurance need based solely on replacing his $95,000 salary, which additional financial impacts of death must the agent include to avoid underestimating the shortfall?

38岁的 Marcus 是高贵林的一位丧偶单亲爸爸，独自抚养4岁和6岁的两个孩子。他担任物流经理年薪 $95,000，背负 $420,000 房贷，并希望万一自己过早身故能留出 $80,000 作为子女的大学教育金。当 Marcus 让代理人只按替代他 $95,000 年薪来测算寿险保额时，代理人必须一并纳入哪些额外的身故财务冲击才能避免低估缺口？

  1. ✓ 正确。六类身故财务冲击经常同时出现：Marcus 身故除了造成收入损失（loss of income）外，作为唯一在家照顾幼童的家长还会产生照护者损失（loss of caregiver，须付费雇人照顾孩子），外加房贷的债务清偿（debt repayment）以及为子女预留确定教育基金的遗产创造（estate creation），需求分析必须逐项核对并加总。Correct. The six categories of financial impacts of death often arise simultaneously: in addition to the loss of income caused by Marcus's death, being the sole parent caring for young children at home also creates a loss of caregiver (requiring paid help to care for the children), plus debt repayment for the mortgage and estate creation to secure the children's university education fund. Needs analysis must examine and sum each category.

  2. ✗ 掉入“范畴遗漏（只算工资漏算照护）”陷阱。单亲家长既是唯一收入来源，也是唯一在家照顾幼童的成年人，身故会同时触发收入损失与照护者损失（loss of caregiver），还漏掉了教育金对应的遗产创造。Falls into the 'category omission (counting lost salary while overlooking lost caregiving)' trap. A single parent is both the sole earner and the sole adult caring for young children at home; death simultaneously triggers loss of income and loss of caregiver, while also omitting the estate creation needed for the education fund.

  3. ✗ 掉入“范畴遗漏与债务规则误解”陷阱。遗产执行人必须先完成债务清偿（debt repayment）才能分配遗产，贷款人绝不会因借款人身故而自动免除房贷，且漏掉了照护者损失。Falls into the 'category omission and debt rule misunderstanding' trap. The estate executor must complete debt repayment before distributing the estate; lenders never automatically forgive a mortgage due to the borrower's death, and this option also omits the loss of caregiver.

  4. ✗ 掉入“概念混淆”陷阱。Marcus 是受雇物流经理而非企业主或合格农渔业合伙人，不涉及企业冲击（business impacts）与终身资本利得豁免（LCGE）。Falls into the 'concept confusion' trap. Marcus is an employed logistics manager rather than a business owner or qualified farm or fishing partner; business impacts and the Lifetime Capital Gains Exemption (LCGE) do not apply.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Elena and Sam live in Nanaimo with their two-year-old daughter and Sam's disabled brother. Elena earns $110,000 annually as a pharmacist, while Sam stays home full-time to care for both dependents. They also carry a $350,000 home mortgage. If Sam dies unexpectedly, which financial impacts of death will the household face immediately?

Elena 和 Sam 与他们两岁的女儿及 Sam 失能的哥哥住在纳奈莫。Elena 担任药剂师年薪 $110,000，Sam 全职在家照顾这两位受扶养人，家里还有 $350,000 房贷。如果 Sam 意外身故，这个家庭会立即面临哪些身故财务冲击？

  1. ✗ 掉入“范畴遗漏（只算工资漏算照护）”陷阱。把没有工资收入误判为没有经济价值，忽略了全职照护者身故带来的高额劳务替代支出。Falls into the 'category omission (counting lost salary while overlooking lost caregiving)' trap. Mistakenly equates having no earned income with having no economic value, ignoring the substantial replacement labor costs incurred when a full-time caregiver dies.

  2. ✗ 掉入“概念混淆”陷阱。受雇药剂师的工作合同不属于资本财产或企业股权，不会在配偶身故时触发企业冲击（business impacts）或视同处置。Falls into the 'concept confusion' trap. An employed pharmacist's employment contract is not capital property or corporate shares, and does not trigger business impacts or deemed disposition upon the spouse's death.

  3. ✓ 正确。Sam 作为无工资收入的全职照护者身故，直接触发照护者损失（loss of caregiver），Elena 必须额外花钱请人照顾幼女与失能亲属，同时家庭仍需面对房贷等债务清偿（debt repayment）压力。Correct. The death of Sam as a stay-at-home caregiver with no earned income directly triggers a loss of caregiver; Elena must pay out of pocket to hire replacement care for their young daughter and frail relative, while the household still faces debt repayment obligations such as the mortgage.

  4. ✗ 掉入“范畴混淆”陷阱。身故的是无薪照护方 Sam 而非收入方 Elena，核心缺口是照护者损失（loss of caregiver）而非收入损失。Falls into the 'category confusion' trap. The deceased is the unpaid caregiving spouse Sam rather than the income-earning spouse Elena; the primary shortfall is loss of caregiver rather than loss of income.

第 2 题Q2风险管理四招在死亡风险上的区分与组合Distinction and combination of the four risk management strategies for the risk of death★★陷阱：机制混淆（把不可规避当成不能降低或不能自留）

After a health scare, 42-year-old Victor quits smoking, starts exercising daily, and installs advanced safety equipment at his workshop. Overlooks the combined ceiling mechanism when a survivor age 65 or over concurrently receives their own retirement pension and survivor pension, as well as the eligibility restrictions on the additional $2,500 death benefit.

  4. ✗ 掉入“档位错配”陷阱。遗属给付的65岁分界线看的是遗属 Walter 本人的年龄（67岁），而非套用65岁以下档位。Falls into the 'bracket mismatch' trap. The age 65 threshold for the survivor's pension is determined by the surviving spouse Walter's own age (67), rather than applying the under age 65 bracket.

第 6 题Q6全生命周期需求曲线与退休阶段风险Life cycle needs curve and financial risks in retirement★陷阱：范畴遗漏（把六类冲击当成整体单一判断）

Compare two clients in Kamloops: Client X is a 33-year-old married mother of two toddlers with a new $550,000 mortgage; Client Y is a 68-year-old retired widower whose mortgage is paid off and whose children are self-supporting, but who still owns 100% of an active manufacturing company and wishes to leave $200,000 to a children's charity. How do their primary financial impacts of death differ across the life cycle?

对比坎卢普斯的两位客户：客户 X 是33岁已婚妈妈，有两个学步幼童和一笔新的 $550,000 房贷；客户 Y 是68岁退休鳏夫，房贷已还清、子女已自立，但仍持有100%一家经营中制造企业的股权，并希望留 $200,000 给儿童慈善机构。在生命周期需求曲线上，他们面临的主要身故财务冲击有何不同？

  1. ✓ 正确。对照 BC 家庭一生的寿险需求曲线：组建家庭与购房期（客户 X）收入损失（loss of income）、照护者损失（loss of caregiver）与房贷债务清偿（debt repayment）三项最急迫的冲击叠加达峰；进入退休期（客户 Y），收入损失下降，但慈善遗赠对应的遗产创造（estate creation）及未传承企业股权对应的企业冲击（business impacts）与视同处置（deemed disposition）税负成为主诉求。Correct. Based on the life cycle life insurance needs curve for a BC family: during family formation and home purchase (Client X), the three most pressing impacts—loss of income, loss of caregiver, and mortgage debt repayment—combine to reach their lifetime peak; upon entering retirement (Client Y), loss of income diminishes, but charitable bequests requiring estate creation and un-transferred corporate shares triggering business impacts and deemed disposition taxes become the primary needs.

  2. ✗ 掉入“阶段颠倒”陷阱。把年轻育儿背房贷家庭与退休企业主的核心风险完全颠倒了。Falls into the 'stage reversal' trap. Completely reverses the core risks of a young family raising children with a mortgage and a retired business owner.

  3. ✗ 掉入“范畴遗漏（把六类冲击当成整体单一判断）”陷阱。案例 C 强调，不能因为收入损失与房贷消失，就假设其余五类冲击（特别是遗产创造与股权视同处置税负）也自动消失。Falls into the 'category omission (evaluating the six impacts as a single all-or-nothing block)' trap. Case C highlights that one cannot assume the remaining categories of impact (especially estate creation and deemed disposition taxes on shares) automatically disappear simply because lost earned income and mortgage debts have ended.

  4. ✗ 掉入“概念混淆”陷阱。死亡风险敞口不是固定量，它随单身、成家、事业峰值、退休等人生阶段动态变化。Falls into the 'concept confusion' trap. Exposure to financial impacts of death is not a static amount; it evolves dynamically across life stages such as being single, starting a family, peak career, and retirement.

At age 64, Patricia is retiring next month from an engineering firm in Prince George. Her group life insurance coverage through her employer will terminate upon retirement, and she only has a limited conversion window. Meanwhile, her 29-year-old son Kevin just married, bought a townhouse with a large mortgage, and welcomed his first baby. 第 7 题Q7按龄死亡概率曲线与最佳投保时机Age-specific mortality curve and the optimal timing for coverage★陷阱：性质与流程倒置（把寿险当投资或跳过需求分析直接推产品）

Twenty-eight-year-old Owen and his wife just had their first child in Langley. Owen tells his agent, 'According to the mortality table, my probability of death this year is tiny, so I should wait until I am in my late 50s when mortality accelerates before buying life insurance.' Based on the relationship between mortality rates, risk transfer, and needs analysis in Chapter 1, what is the flaw in Owen's reasoning?

28岁的 Owen 和妻子刚在兰里迎来了第一个孩子。Owen 对代理人说：“根据生命表，我今年的死亡概率极低，所以我应该等到50多岁死亡率加速上升时再去买人寿保险。”根据第一章关于死亡率、风险转移与需求分析的逻辑，Owen 的推理错在哪里？

  1. ✗ 掉入“死亡率曲线方向颠倒”陷阱。从青壮年到中年再到老年，按龄死亡概率（probability of death）随年龄呈非线性加速上升，而非下降。Falls into the 'mortality curve direction reversal' trap. From young adulthood to middle age and into older age, the age-specific probability of death increases non-linearly with age rather than decreasing.

  2. ✗ 掉入“机制混淆”陷阱。只要活着就暴露在死亡风险下，风险规避（risk avoidance）在任何年龄都不适用于死亡风险。Falls into the 'mechanics confusion' trap. As long as someone is alive, they are exposed to the risk of death; risk avoidance does not apply to the risk of death at any age.

  3. ✓ 正确。虽然年轻阶段按龄死亡概率（probability of death）很低，但一旦发生，年轻家庭面临的收入损失（loss of income）与照护者损失（loss of caregiver）严重度（severity）极高，超出自留能力；且死亡率曲线随年龄非线性加速上升，恰恰应在年轻、死亡概率最低即保费最低时通过风险转移（risk transfer）锁定保障。Correct. Although the age-specific probability of death is very low during youth, should it occur, the severity of the loss of income and loss of caregiver for a young family is exceptionally high, exceeding personal retention capacity. Moreover, because the mortality curve accelerates non-linearly with age, transferring the risk when young—when the probability of death is lowest and premiums are cheapest—locks in coverage cost-effectively.

  4. ✗ 掉入“概念混淆”陷阱。CISRO 能力组件 c1（评估客户需求与处境，占35%权重）适用于所有年龄段客户的需求评估，与65岁无关。Falls into the 'concept confusion' trap. CISRO competency component c1 (assessing the client's needs and situation, carrying a 35 percent weight) applies to evaluating client needs across all age brackets, with no special relevance to reaching age 65.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

While studying for the LLQP Life module, a candidate notices that CISRO competency component c1 ('Assess the client's needs and situation') carries the largest exam weight at 35%, yet Chapter 1 contains no complex coverage formulas. How do Chapter 1 and Chapter 10 fit together within component c1?

在备考 LLQP 人寿保险模块时，一名考生注意到 CISRO 能力组件 c1（“评估客户需求与处境”）占考卷最大权重 35%，但第一章里却没有复杂的保额计算公式。在组件 c1 内部，第一章和第十章是如何衔接的？

  1. ✓ 正确。CISRO 能力组件（CISRO competency component）c1 占 35% 权重，其中第一章负责建立死亡风险、六类财务冲击与风险管理四招的定性框架，确认哪些风险需要转移（risk transfer）；而具体用收入替代法（income replacement approach）或资本需求法（capital needs approach）算出保额的大头集中在第十章的需求分析（needs analysis）。Correct.   2. ✗ 掉入“权重分配误解”陷阱。35% 是整个 c1 组件（第1章+第10章）的权重，且出题重点在第10章的需求分析计算，更不需要死背整张生命表。Falls into the 'weight allocation misunderstanding' trap. 35 percent is the weight for the entire c1 component (Chapter 1 plus Chapter 10), and exam testing focuses heavily on the needs analysis calculations of Chapter 10, without requiring rote memorization of the entire mortality table.

The practice workflow requires conducting a fact-finding interview and needs analysis (Chapter 10) first to quantify the shortfall, before selecting products and writing recommendations (Chapter 11); an agent must never pitch products first.

  4. ✗ 掉入“概念混淆”陷阱。团体险转换在第6章和第12章，且加拿大没有省级继承税。Falls into the 'concept confusion' trap. Group insurance conversion is covered in Chapter 6 and Chapter 12, and Canadian provinces do not levy an inheritance tax.
