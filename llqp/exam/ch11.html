第 11 章 · 提出寿险推荐Chapter 11 · Recommending an Insurance PolicyRecommending

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 产品类型与期限匹配题：给出客户的具体风险（如房贷、收入替代、身故税负、特殊需要子女终身抚养）及现金流状况，考查以风险持续期（duration of risk）为主判据选择定期险（term insurance）、可转换定期险（convertible term）还是永久险（permanent insurance）。Product type and duration matching questions: Presenting a client's specific risks (such as a mortgage, income replacement, death tax liability, or lifelong support for a special needs child) and cash flow situation, testing the selection of term insurance, convertible term insurance, or permanent insurance using duration of risk as the primary criterion.
  • 多方案成本比较与续保决策题：考查累计成本分析（cumulative cost analysis）而非仅看首年保费，或当客户在定期险期满且健康恶化（如开始吸烟）时，比较按原健康等级续保与重新核保买新单的利弊。Multi-option cost comparison and renewal decision questions: Testing cumulative cost analysis rather than merely looking at first-year premiums, or comparing the pros and cons of renewing at the original health rating versus undergoing new underwriting to purchase a new policy when an expiring term policyholder's health has deteriorated (such as starting to smoke).
  • 保单演示件（policy illustration）合规与签字意义题：考查定期险与含非保证金额永久险演示件的区别、保证值与非保证值（guaranteed vs non-guaranteed values）的分列展示、至少两套情景（at least two scenarios）的要求，以及客户签字仅代表理解演示性质而非保司承诺回报。Policy illustration compliance and signature significance questions: Testing the distinctions between term insurance illustrations and permanent insurance illustrations containing non-guaranteed amounts, the separate presentation of guaranteed versus non-guaranteed values, the requirement for at least two scenarios, and that the client's signature signifies only understanding the nature of the illustration rather than an insurer guarantee of projected returns.
  • 保单替换（policy replacement）披露与流程顺序题：考查转保（churning）与掉保（twisting）的同司/跨司区分、寿险替换声明书（LIRD）的适用范围（魁北克使用专门表格）、客户主动提出更换仍须披露，以及必须待新单生效且客户接受后才能取消旧单。Policy replacement disclosure and procedural sequence questions: Testing the intra-company versus inter-company distinction between churning and twisting, the scope of application of the Life Insurance Replacement Declaration (LIRD) (with Quebec using its own specific form), the requirement that disclosure is still mandatory even when initiated by the client, and the rule that the existing policy must only be cancelled after the new policy is in force and accepted by the client.
  • 客户拒绝或折衷接受推荐时的留痕题：考查当客户因预算限制只买部分保额或拒绝推荐时，代理人如何通过书面推荐记录（reason-why record）说明未覆盖缺口并由客户确认，而非强行施压或放弃记录。Client refusal or partial acceptance record-keeping questions: Testing how an agent uses a written reason-why record to document uncovered gaps and obtain client acknowledgement when the client purchases only partial coverage due to budget constraints or refuses the recommendation entirely, rather than applying pressure or abandoning documentation.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 目标误置（把最贵最全当默认最优）Misplaced objective (treating the most expensive and comprehensive option as the default best)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生直觉认为推荐保额最高、条款最全的永久险最能规避代理人执业责任，忽略了推荐必须匹配客户真实现金流与意愿。Candidates instinctively believe recommending permanent insurance with the highest coverage and most riders best shields the agent from liability, overlooking that recommendations must match the client's actual cash flow and intentions.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调客户目前收入有限或现金流紧张（tight cash flow），而选项出现保费昂贵的全额终身险组合。The question stem emphasizes that the client currently has limited income or tight cash flow, while options feature an expensive full whole life combination.

⚠ 漏算一步（只看首年保费或忽略原健康等级锁定）Missing a step (looking only at first-year premiums or overlooking original health rating lock-in)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生习惯直接对比第一年保费高低，或误以为买新单一定比旧单续保便宜，忽略了续保跳涨幅度及旧单续保按原发单健康等级计费的机制。Candidates habitually compare only first-year premiums or mistakenly assume buying a new policy is always cheaper than renewing an old one, overlooking the magnitude of renewal premium jumps and the mechanism where renewing an existing policy charges rates based on the original issue health rating.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现不同期限定期险组合对比，或期满客户期间健康变差、开始吸烟却要决定续保还是重买。The question stem presents a comparison of term insurance combinations with different durations, or an expiring policyholder whose health has worsened or who started smoking having to decide between renewal and new coverage.

⚠ 管辖混淆（受益人默认可撤销与魁北克替换表格）Jurisdictional confusion (revocable beneficiary default versus Quebec replacement form)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生容易把魁北克对已婚或民事结合配偶默认不可撤销的特殊规定套用到全国，或误以为魁北克不用 LIRD 就等于没有替换披露义务。Candidates easily misapply Quebec's special rule that designations of married or civil union spouses are irrevocable by default to the rest of Canada, or mistakenly assume that Quebec not using the LIRD means it has no replacement disclosure obligation.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现指定配偶为受益人未声明是否可撤销，或询问魁北克与其余省份在保单替换披露表格上的差异。The question stem mentions naming a spouse as beneficiary without specifying whether it is revocable, or asks about differences between Quebec and other provinces regarding policy replacement disclosure forms.

⚠ 同类不同规则（转保 churning 与掉保 twisting 混淆）Similar terms with different rules (confusing churning with twisting)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 两种不当替换行为的误导手法完全相同，考生若不看清新旧保单是否属于同一家保险公司就会选反。Both improper replacement practices use identical deceptive techniques; candidates will choose the opposite term if they fail to distinguish whether the existing and new policies are with the same insurer or different insurers.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述代理人诱导客户退旧单换新单时，明确提到在同一家保险人内部（same insurer）或跨不同保险人（different insurers）。The question stem describes an agent inducing a client to surrender an old policy for a new one, explicitly noting whether it occurs within the same insurer or across different insurers.

⚠ 前提误用与流程倒置（客户主动换单免披露或先退旧单）Misapplied premise and inverted process (assuming client-initiated replacements waive disclosure or cancelling the old policy prematurely)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生误以为客户自己提出换单就无需走替换披露流程，或在递交新申请时顺手帮客户停掉旧单，导致保障空窗期与两年不可抗辩期风险。Candidates mistakenly assume that when a client initiates a replacement, the disclosure process is not required, or cancel the old policy upon submitting the new application, resulting in coverage gaps and exposure to a new two-year incontestability period.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写明客户主动要求替换既有保单，或询问新单核保期间旧保单应何时取消。The question stem states that the client actively requested replacing an existing policy, or asks when the existing policy should be cancelled during underwriting of the new policy.

第 1 题Q1按风险持续期与现金流选产品Matching products by duration of risk and cash flow★陷阱：目标误置（把最贵最全当默认最优）

Elena is a single parent with a young child and a twenty-year mortgage. She is starting a new career, so her current income is limited and her long-term estate needs are still uncertain, though she expects her earnings to grow in a few years. Which life insurance recommendation best fits her situation?

Elena 是一位独自抚养幼年子女的单亲家长，背负一笔二十年期房贷。她刚起步新职业，目前收入有限且长期遗产需求尚不确定，但她预计几年后收入会增长。下列哪项寿险推荐最贴合她的处境？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据综合考量（bringing it all together）与产品选择决策树，当客户有明确的期限定期需求（如二十年房贷与抚养期）、长期需求尚不确定且目前收入有限时，优先推荐可转换定期险（convertible term insurance），把转成永久险的决定权留到未来收入更明朗时。Correct. Based on bringing it all together and the product selection decision tree, when a client has a clear term need with a fixed horizon (such as a twenty-year mortgage and child dependency period), long-term estate needs are still uncertain, and current income is limited, convertible term insurance is the preferred recommendation, preserving the option to convert to permanent coverage when future income becomes more certain.

  2. ✗ 错在「目标误置」陷阱。把保额最高、保费最贵的分红终身险当成「最安全」的默认推荐是错误的；对于目前现金流紧张的客户，高保费永久险可能导致无力持续缴费而断保，违反产品适当性。Falls into the 'misplaced objective' trap. It is an error to treat whole life insurance with the highest face amount and most expensive premiums as the 'safest' default recommendation; for a client with tight cash flow, expensive permanent insurance may lead to an inability to maintain premium payments and cause a policy lapse, violating product suitability.

  3. ✗ 错在误解了万能寿险（universal life）的缴费机制。讲义明确指出，万能寿险灵活缴费不等于可以长期不缴，必须先测试持续支付保险成本（COI）的能力，不适合目前收入有限且仅需低成本定期保障的客户。Misunderstands the premium payment mechanism of universal life insurance. Flexible premium payments do not mean a client can stop paying for years; the client's ability to consistently cover the cost of insurance (COI) must be tested, making it unsuitable for a client with limited current income who solely needs low-cost term protection.

  4. ✗ 错在「产品误配」陷阱。联合末身故（joint last-to-die）保单在最后一人身故时才赔付，用于递延至末位配偶身故的税负，完全无法解决 Elena 身故后幼年子女立即需要的房贷与生活费。Falls into the 'product mismatch' trap. A joint last-to-die policy pays only upon the death of the second insured person, designed for tax liabilities deferred until the second spouse dies, and completely fails to provide the immediate funds needed for the mortgage and child living expenses upon Elena's death.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Liam and Nora are retired spouses whose mortgage is paid off and whose children are financially independent. Their sole insurance objective is to provide cash to pay the capital gains tax on their family cottage, which will only become payable upon the death of whichever spouse dies second. They have maxed out their RRSP and TFSA room. Which policy type is most appropriate?

Liam 与 Nora 是一对已退休夫妇，房贷已还清，子女也已经济独立。他们唯一的保险目标是提供现金来支付家庭度假屋的资本利得税，而这笔税款仅在两人中后去世的那位配偶身故时才须缴纳。他们已用满 RRSP 与 TFSA 额度。哪种保单类型最合适？

  1. ✗ 错在忽视了风险持续期（duration of risk）。身故税负是终身存在的确定需求，定期险到期后若被保险人仍健在，保障将消失或面临高昂续保保费。Overlooks the duration of risk. Estate capital gains tax is a permanent, definite obligation; once term insurance expires, if the insured is still living, coverage ceases or subjects the clients to prohibitive renewal premiums.

  2. ✗ 错在用短期定期险去覆盖终身才触发的遗产税负，不仅违反按需求持续期配险种的原则，且高龄续保成本极高。Errs by using short-term coverage to address an estate tax liability triggered only upon death, which violates the principle of matching policy type to risk duration and results in exorbitant renewal costs at advanced ages.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据六步推荐流程与产品选择决策树，当配偶间资产可展期、税负递延至末位配偶身故（death of the second spouse）才触发，且需求终身存在、客户已用满 RRSP/TFSA 时，应配置联合末身故永久险（joint last-to-die permanent insurance）。Correct. According to the six-step recommendation process and product selection decision tree, when assets can roll over between spouses, taxes are deferred until the death of the second spouse, the need is lifelong, and the clients have maxed out their RRSP and TFSA room, a joint last-to-die permanent life insurance policy should be recommended.

  4. ✗ 错在误解了团体寿险（group life insurance）的特征。团体险保额通常有上限且随离职或退休而终止或受限于转换规则，不能用来解决终身遗产税负。Misunderstands the characteristics of group life insurance. Group coverage typically has maximum benefit limits, terminates upon job termination or retirement, or is subject to restrictive conversion rules, making it unsuitable for funding permanent estate tax liabilities.

第 2 题Q2累计成本比较与受益人默认规则Cumulative cost comparison and default beneficiary rules★★陷阱：管辖混淆（受益人默认可撤销与魁北克替换表格）

An agent in British Columbia is comparing a ten-year renewable term policy against a twenty-year term policy for a client who needs twenty years of coverage. The client also wants to name her married spouse as primary beneficiary and asks whether she will need her spouse's consent to change the beneficiary later. What should the agent tell her?

BC 省的一位代理人正在为需要二十年保障期的客户比较一张十年期可续保定期险与一张二十年期定期险。客户还希望指定自己的已婚配偶为主要受益人，并询问日后若想变更受益人是否需要征得配偶同意。代理人应如何告知她？

  1. ✗ 同时落入「漏算一步」与「管辖混淆」两个陷阱。比较不同期限定期险不能只看首年保费而忽略十年后的续保跳涨；且在魁北克以外的省份（如 BC 省），即使指定已婚配偶为受益人，默认也是可撤销的。Falls into both the 'missing a step' and 'jurisdictional confusion' traps. Comparing term policies of different durations cannot rely solely on first-year premiums while ignoring the renewal jump after ten years; furthermore, outside Quebec (such as in BC), naming a married spouse as beneficiary is revocable by default.

  2. ✓ 正确。在推荐的第三步与第四步中，代理人必须用累计成本分析（cumulative cost analysis）比较二十年内的总保费（考虑十年定期险续保跳涨）；同时除魁北克外，全国默认所有受益人指定均为可撤销受益人（revocable beneficiary），即使指定的是配偶，变更时无需配偶同意，除非主动声明为不可撤销。Correct. In steps three and four of the recommendation process, the agent must perform a cumulative cost analysis comparing total premiums over the full twenty years (taking into account the ten-year term renewal jump); furthermore, across Canada outside Quebec, all beneficiary designations are revocable by default, so even when naming a spouse, changes do not require spousal consent unless explicitly declared irrevocable.

  3. ✗ 错在颠倒了遗嘱认证影响（probate implication）且漏算续保成本。指定遗产（estate）为受益人恰恰会让身故金进入遗嘱认证程序并产生认证费，指定具体个人受益人才能绕过遗嘱认证。Reverses probate implications and overlooks renewal costs. Naming the estate as beneficiary causes the death benefit to fall into the probate process and incur probate fees, whereas naming a designated individual beneficiary bypasses probate.

  4. ✗ 错在「管辖混淆」陷阱，完全颠倒了 LIRD 与魁北克专表的适用范围。除魁北克使用专门表格外，其余省份统一使用 LIRD。Falls into the 'jurisdictional confusion' trap by reversing the territorial scope of the LIRD and Quebec's form. Except for Quebec which uses its own form, other provinces use the standardized LIRD.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An agent in British Columbia is handling a life insurance replacement where the policy applicant is a business owner and the life insured is a key employee. Before proceeding with the new replacement application, what documentation and signatures must the agent obtain?

BC 省的一位代理人正在办理一项人寿保险替换业务，其中投保申请人是企业主，被保险人是一名关键员工。在着手办理这项替换的新投保申请之前，代理人必须取得哪些文件与签名？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据第三节保单替换披露义务的三层落地要求，在着手属于替换的新申请之前，代理人须向申请人讲解替换声明（LIRD）并取得申请人签字，被保险人与申请人不同者也须签字，同时附上一份书面说明（讲清更换理由、新单优点、以及如重新起算 2 年不可抗辩期等可能不赔付的情形）。注：考试口径泛称多数司法辖区要求 LIRD，而在 BC 现行法下该义务由《保险合同（人寿保险替换）规则》s.3(4) 成文法明确规定。Correct. Under statutory replacement disclosure requirements, before proceeding with a replacement application, the agent must explain the replacement declaration (LIRD) and obtain signatures from the applicant, and also from the life insured when different from the applicant, while attaching a written explanation detailing the reasons for replacement, advantages of the new policy, and circumstances where the new policy might not pay (such as the new two-year incontestability period). Note: while the exam standard broadly states most jurisdictions require the LIRD, in BC this duty is explicitly codified in section 3(4) of the Insurance Contract (Life Insurance Replacement) Regulation.

  2. ✗ 错在把口头确认代替事前书面披露，并混淆了宽限期（30 或 31 天）与事前披露时点。替换声明必须在着手新申请之前讲解并签字。Errs by substituting verbal confirmation for advance written disclosure, and confuses policy grace periods (30 or 31 days) with pre-application disclosure timing. The replacement declaration must be explained and signed before proceeding with the new application.

  3. ✗ 错在漏掉了申请人的签字且免除了书面利弊说明。申请人与被保险人不同时双方都必须签字，且无论保额高低都必须附书面说明。Omits the applicant's signature and improperly waives the written explanation. Both the applicant and the life insured must sign when they are different parties, and a written comparison is mandatory regardless of coverage amounts.

  4. ✗ 错在「管辖混淆」陷阱。除魁北克使用专门表格外，包括 BC 在内的其余省份统一使用 LIRD，且 BC 有省级成文法规强制要求。Falls into the 'jurisdictional confusion' trap. Aside from Quebec which uses its own form, other provinces including BC mandate the LIRD, backed in BC by explicit provincial statutory regulation.

答案Answer：a

第 7 题Q7Reason-why记录与客户拒绝处理Reason-why records and handling client refusal★★陷阱：口头同意或客户自选免留痕误区

After completing a needs analysis showing a total insurance gap of $600,000, an agent recommends a combination of term and permanent policies. The client states that he only wants to purchase $300,000 of term insurance right now and declines the rest. Which action should the agent take to properly conclude this recommendation?

在完成需求分析并算出总保额缺口为 $600,000 后，代理人推荐了一套定期险加永久险的组合方案。客户表示自己目前只想购买 $300,000 的定期寿险，并拒绝其余建议。代理人应采取下列哪项行动来妥善完成本次推荐的收尾？

  1. ✗ 错在违背了客户拒绝推荐时的执业边界。讲义明确指出，代理人绝不能不顾客户明确表态、反复施压促成更大保额（否则可能构成不当销售）。Violates professional conduct boundaries when a client declines coverage. An agent must never pressure a client who has expressed clear preferences into purchasing larger coverage, which may constitute improper sales practices.

  2. ✗ 错在放弃留痕。如果客户拒绝后什么都不记录甚至销毁原方案记录，一旦数年后发生理赔且遗属质疑「当初为什么卖的保额不够」，代理人将没有任何证据证明当年已讲清风险缺口。Abandons required record-keeping. If an agent fails to record or discards notes regarding the original recommendation, the agent will have no evidence years later to prove that coverage gaps were fully explained if surviving family members dispute inadequate coverage.

  3. ✗ 错在混淆了替换披露与推荐留痕记录（reason-why record）的适用范围。替换披露只在换保单时强制要求，而 reason-why 记录应在每一次实质性推荐时都做，无论是否有旧保单要换。Confuses replacement disclosure with reason-why record requirements. Replacement disclosure applies specifically when replacing an existing contract, whereas a reason-why record should be created for every substantive recommendation, regardless of whether prior policies exist.

  4. ✓ 正确。根据第三节第 5、6 小节与第六节案例 C，一份完整的推荐留痕记录（reason-why record）包含客户处境摘要、考虑过的方案与取舍、最终推荐及客户回应四块内容；当客户只买 $300,000 时，代理人必须讲清未覆盖的风险缺口、将 $600,000 建议与 $300,000 最终选择记入书面记录并请客户确认已理解差异。Correct. A sound reason-why record contains four core components: client situation summary, alternatives considered and trade-offs, final recommendation matching needs, and client response; when a client purchases only $300,000, the agent must explain the uncovered risk gap, document both the $600,000 proposal and the $300,000 choice, and have the client acknowledge understanding the remaining gap.

答案Answer：d

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An agent is writing a reason-why record for a client who considered a pure term insurance option but ultimately chose a combination of term insurance and a small participating whole life policy to build cash value for retirement. Besides the summary of the client's situation and the final recommendation matched to specific needs, what other elements belong in a sound reason-why record?

一位代理人正在为客户撰写一份 reason-why 推荐留痕记录。该客户曾考虑纯定期险方案，但最终为了积累现金价值用于退休补充，选择了定期险加小额分红终身险的组合。除了「客户处境摘要」与「最终推荐及匹配的具体需求」外，一份站得住脚的 reason-why 记录还应包含哪些要素？

  1. ✗ 错在偏离了推荐留痕记录（reason-why record）的四块核心内容。它记录的是专业判断过程，不需要重抄整份合同或仅列佣金表。Deviates from the four core elements of a reason-why record. The record documents the agent's professional judgment process and does not require copying the entire policy contract or merely listing commission tables.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据第三节第 5 小节，一份能站得住脚的推荐留痕记录（reason-why record）包含四块内容：① 客户处境摘要；② 考虑过的方案与为什么没选（alternatives considered and why not chosen）；③ 最终推荐与匹配到的具体需求；④ 客户的回应（接受、部分接受或拒绝及风险说明情况）。Correct. A defensible reason-why record comprises four core elements: 1) a summary of the client's circumstances; 2) alternative options considered and why they were not selected; 3) the final recommendation matched to specific needs; and 4) the client's response (acceptance, partial acceptance, or refusal with documented risk explanation).

  3. ✗ 错在把非保证分红写成保证承诺，这不仅不是 reason-why 记录的内容，更是严重的违规误导。Misrepresents non-guaranteed dividends as guaranteed promises, which is not part of a reason-why record and constitutes severe deceptive misconduct.

  4. ✗ 错在混淆了无旧保单的新增推荐与保单替换（policy replacement）。没有既有保单被替换时不需要填替换声明书，但仍须保留 reason-why 推荐记录。Confuses a new policy recommendation with a policy replacement. When no prior policy is replaced, a replacement declaration is not required, but maintaining a written reason-why record remains mandatory.
