① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 账单分摊计算题：同时给出年度免赔额（deductible）与共保比例（co-insurance factor），测试是否严格遵循“先扣免赔额、再按比例乘余额”的顺序，以及个人免赔额与家庭免赔额（family deductible）谁先触发的连锁清算规则。Expense-sharing calculation questions: Presenting an annual deductible and a co-insurance factor simultaneously, testing whether you strictly follow the two-step sequence of subtracting the deductible first before applying the percentage to the remaining balance, as well as the sequential clearing rules governing whether the individual deductible or family deductible is satisfied first.
  • 牙科与处方药报销边界题：通过牙医实际收费高于费用标准表（fee schedule）或客户坚持使用非处方集（formulary）品牌药的场景，测试差额自付的底层原因并非保险公司违约拒赔。Dental and prescription drug reimbursement boundary questions: Presenting scenarios where a dentist charges above the fee schedule or a client insists on a brand-name drug outside the formulary, testing your understanding that paying the out-of-pocket difference arises from non-covered excess charges rather than an insurer breach or claim denial.
  • 旅行险既往病史与稳定期判定题：给出长者或慢性病患者出发前调药、复查或出现症状的天数，测试稳定期（stability period）回溯判定，以及某项既往病史拒赔是否影响同一行程中不相关意外索赔。Travel insurance pre-existing condition and stability period questions: Providing the timeline of medication adjustments, follow-up exams, or symptom emergence prior to departure for seniors or chronic condition patients, testing the retrospective determination of the stability period and confirming that denying a claim for a pre-existing condition does not void unrelated accidental injury claims on the same trip.
  • 团体福利缺口与需求分析题：列出雇主团体套餐（group package）在视力、正畸、病房等级、意外身故与伤残（AD&D）或境外旅行上的短板，测试如何保留原团体计划并用个人计划精准补缺。Group benefits gap and needs analysis questions: Identifying coverage shortfalls in an employer group package regarding vision care, orthodontics, hospital room upgrades, accidental death and dismemberment (AD&D), or out-of-country travel, testing how to retain the existing group plan while precisely filling identified gaps with individual coverage.
  • 税务处理与省际例外题：对比雇主为合资格私人健康服务计划（PHSP）缴付保费在联邦税下的非应税福利待遇，测试魁北克省税例外不可误套用于其他省份或联邦税。Tax treatment and interprovincial exception questions: Evaluating the non-taxable employment benefit treatment under federal tax law for employer-paid premiums toward a qualifying private health services plan (PHSP), testing that Quebec's provincial tax exception must not be erroneously applied to other provinces or federal taxation.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 免赔额与共保算反顺序Reversing the calculation order of deductible and co-insurance

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直接拿医疗或牙科总账单乘以共保比例，漏掉第一步先扣除尚未用完的年度免赔额，或把进入共保的余额误计入家庭免赔额累计池。Multiplying the total medical or dental bill directly by the co-insurance factor, omitting the required first step of deducting the unused annual deductible, or mistakenly crediting amounts that enter co-insurance toward the family deductible pool.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现账单总额、年度免赔额（annual deductible）和共保比例（co-insurance factor）。The question stem presents the total bill, an annual deductible, and a co-insurance factor simultaneously.

⚠ 超费用标准误当拒赔Mistaking charges exceeding the fee schedule for claim denial

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到牙科实际报销金额低于账单乘以共保比例，误以为保险公司无理拒赔，忽略了理赔上限受牙科费用标准表（fee schedule）封顶限制。Observing that actual dental reimbursement is less than the bill multiplied by the co-insurance factor and erroneously assuming an improper denial by the insurer, overlooking that claim payouts are capped by the contractual dental fee schedule.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提到牙医诊所实际收费高于省牙医协会建议或合同指定的费用标准表金额。The question stem states that the dental clinic's actual charges exceed the provincial dental association fee guide or the fee schedule specified in the contract.

⚠ 既往病史拒赔扩大化Overextending pre-existing condition exclusions to unrelated claims

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 受保人出发前稳定期（stability period）内调药导致旧疾发作被拒赔时，误以为整份旅行保单作废，连带将旅途中完全无关的意外骨折也判为拒赔。When a pre-existing condition claim is denied because a medication adjustment violated the pre-departure stability period, candidates incorrectly assume the entire travel policy is voided and mistakenly treat an unrelated accidental fracture during the trip as denied.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干先描述稳定期内调药或就诊，随后出现与该既往病史毫无关联的突发意外或新伤病索赔。The question stem describes a medication adjustment or medical visit during the stability period, followed by a sudden accident or new medical claim completely unrelated to the pre-existing condition.

⚠ 退团险换个人险补缺口Replacing group benefits with individual insurance to fill gaps

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 发现雇主团体计划缺少视力、正畸额度或旅行保障时，错误建议放弃保费低且免核保的团体计划，而非保留团险仅就缺口配置个人险。Upon identifying gaps in vision, orthodontics, or travel coverage in an employer group plan, candidates mistakenly recommend cancelling the low-cost, non-underwritten group coverage instead of retaining the group plan and purchasing individual coverage solely for the gaps.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干询问当雇主团体扩展医疗福利存在特定保障短板时，应如何通过需求分析（needs analysis）处理缺口。The question stem asks how to address coverage shortfalls through needs analysis when an employer group extended health plan lacks specific benefits.

⚠ AD&D全额赔付或跨省税混淆Assuming full AD&D payouts or confusing interprovincial tax rules

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为意外伤残一律按保额 100% 给付而忽略给付比例表（schedule of loss）分级；或将魁北克对雇主 PHSP 保费的省税应税处理误套用到 BC 省或联邦税。Mistakenly assuming that accidental dismemberment always pays 100% of the principal sum while overlooking the schedule of loss tiers, or improperly applying Quebec's provincial taxable benefit treatment for employer PHSP premiums to British Columbia or federal taxation.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现单肢、单眼、单耳等具体意外损失部位，或询问 BC 省雇主缴付合资格 PHSP 保费的税务后果。The question stem specifies accidental loss of a single limb, one eye, or one ear, or asks about the tax consequences of employer-paid PHSP premiums in British Columbia.

第 1 题Q1免赔额与共保比例联动计算Sequential calculation of deductible and co-insurance★★陷阱：免赔额与共保算反顺序

Elena, an architect in Kelowna, incurs an $800 eligible bill for routine dental maintenance. Her personal dental insurance policy has an unused annual deductible of $100 and a co-insurance factor of 80% for routine maintenance, with sufficient annual maximum remaining. How much will the insurer reimburse, and how much must Elena pay out of pocket?

基洛纳的建筑师 Elena 因常规牙科维护产生了一笔 $800 的合资格账单。她的个人牙科保单尚有 $100 的年度免赔额未用完，常规维护的共保比例为 80%，且年度报销上限充足。保险公司将报销多少，Elena 需要自付多少？

  1. ✗ 错误（陷阱：免赔额与共保算反顺序）。直接用 $800 × 80% = $640 完全漏掉了第一步先扣除 $100 年度免赔额（deductible）的要求，且受保人除免赔额外还需承担余额 20% 的共保自付部分。Incorrect. Reversing the calculation order of deductible and co-insurance. Multiplying $800 directly by 80% to get $640 completely omits the mandatory first step of deducting the $100 unused annual deductible, and fails to recognize that the insured must pay both the deductible and the 20% co-insurance share on the remaining balance.

  2. ✓ 正确。当免赔额（deductible）与共保比例（co-insurance factor）同时出现时，必须严格按“先减后乘”两步计算：先从 $800 中扣除 $100 免赔额得到余额 $700，再由保险公司按 $700 × 80% 报销 $560；Elena 自付部分为 $100 免赔额加上 $700 × 20%（$140），合计 $240。Correct. When both a deductible and a co-insurance factor apply, calculations must follow a strict two-step sequence: subtract first, then multiply. First, deduct the $100 annual deductible from the $800 bill to arrive at a $700 balance; the insurer then reimburses 80% of $700, which equals $560. Elena's out-of-pocket cost is the $100 deductible plus her 20% co-insurance share of $140 ($700 * 20%), totaling $240.

  3. ✗ 错误（陷阱：忽略共保比例分摊）。扣除 $100 免赔额（deductible）后的 $700 余额并非由保险公司全包，还必须乘以 80% 的共保比例（co-insurance factor），受保人仍需自付 $140。Incorrect. Overlooking the co-insurance cost sharing. The $700 balance remaining after subtracting the $100 deductible is not covered in full by the insurer; it must be multiplied by the 80% co-insurance factor, leaving the insured responsible for the remaining 20% ($140).

  4. ✗ 错误（陷阱：忽略成本分担机制）。扩展医疗与牙科保单通过免赔额（deductible）和共保比例（co-insurance factor）控制道德风险，不会对有免赔额与 80% 共保约定的账单全额报销 $800。Incorrect. Ignoring cost-sharing mechanisms. Extended health and dental policies use deductibles and co-insurance factors to mitigate moral hazard and will not reimburse the entire $800 bill when the contract specifies an annual deductible and an 80% co-insurance factor.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Marcus, a graphic designer in Kamloops, submits an $800 eligible claim for a dental crown (major restorative work). His policy has an unused annual deductible of $100, covers routine maintenance at 80%, and covers major restorative work at a 50% co-insurance factor. Why does the insurer apply a 50% co-insurance factor instead of 80% after deducting the $100 deductible from the $800 bill?

坎卢普斯的平面设计师 Marcus 因安装牙冠（重建类项目）提交了一笔 $800 的合资格理赔。他的保单尚有 $100 年度免赔额未用，常规维护共保比例为 80%，重建类项目共保比例为 50%。在从 $800 账单中先扣除 $100 免赔额（余 $700）后，为什么保险公司对余额适用 50% 而非 80% 的共保比例？

  1. ✗ 错误（陷阱：公费医保覆盖范围混淆）。省级医保（MSP）不覆盖常规牙科及牙冠、桥体等重建类牙科费用，剩余 50% 须由受保人自己承担。Incorrect. Confusing the scope of provincial healthcare coverage. British Columbia's Medical Services Plan (MSP) does not cover routine dental care or major restorative dental procedures like crowns and bridges; the remaining 50% must be paid out of pocket by the insured.

  2. ✗ 错误（陷阱：结算方式与共保比例混淆）。无论是否采用直接结算（direct billing），重建类项目的共保比例（co-insurance factor）均按合同约定的 50% 执行。Incorrect. Confusing the billing method with co-insurance rules. Regardless of whether direct billing is utilized at the dental clinic, the co-insurance factor for major restorative procedures is strictly governed by the contractual 50% rate.

  3. ✓ 正确。先从 $800 中扣除 $100 免赔额（deductible）余 $700 后，保险公司对牙冠、桥体这类越贵、越难预测的重建类项目（major restorative）将共保比例（co-insurance factor）从常规维护的 75%–80% 调低至 50%，其底层逻辑是抑制就医道德风险。Correct. After deducting the $100 annual deductible from $800 to leave a $700 balance, the insurer applies a lower 50% co-insurance factor rather than the 75% to 80% routine rate because crowns and bridges represent major restorative work. Setting a lower reimbursement rate on costlier, less predictable procedures is designed to curb moral hazard by increasing the insured's financial participation.

  4. ✗ 错误（陷阱：费用标准表与项目分级混淆）。重建类项目适用 50% 共保比例（co-insurance factor）是保单按项目严重度与成本所作的三级分类设计，而非因超出费用标准表（fee schedule）受到处罚。Incorrect. Confusing fee schedule limits with procedure categorization. Applying a 50% co-insurance factor to major restorative work is a structural policy feature based on procedural tier and cost, not a penalty for exceeding the provincial dental fee schedule.

第 2 题Q2牙科费用标准表与差额自付Dental fee schedules and out-of-pocket excess charges★陷阱：超费用标准误当拒赔

Tariq has a dental plan that covers 80% of routine maintenance with no deductible, based on the provincial dental association fee schedule specified in his contract. His dentist charges more than the contract's fee schedule amount for a filling, leaving Tariq with a larger out-of-pocket bill than 20% of the clinic's invoice. Tariq complains that his insurer partially denied his claim. How should the advisor explain this outcome?

Tariq 的牙科计划按合同指定的省牙医协会费用标准表报销 80% 的常规维护且无免赔额。他的牙医对一次补牙收取的费用高于合同费用标准表金额，导致 Tariq 的自付账单超过了诊所发票总额的 20%。Tariq 投诉保险公司对他进行了部分拒赔。代理人应如何解释这一结果？

  1. ✓ 正确。牙科理赔以合同采用的费用标准表（fee schedule）为计算上限；当牙医实际收费高于表内金额时，超出部分并非保险公司拒赔，而是该超额部分本身从未被承诺覆盖，保险公司已严格按表内金额支付了 80%，差额理应由受保人自付。Correct. Dental claims are capped based on the fee schedule specified in the contract. When a dentist's actual charge exceeds the fee schedule amount, the excess is not a claim denial; rather, the excess was never an eligible covered expense under the policy.   3. ✗ 错误（陷阱：雇主健康税用途混淆）。雇主健康税（Employer Health Tax, EHT）是 BC 省政府自 2019 年起向雇主征收的省级税种，与补贴商业个人健康险的视力或牙科上限毫无关系。Incorrect. Confusing the purpose of the Employer Health Tax (EHT). The Employer Health Tax is a provincial tax that the British Columbia government has levied on employers since 2019; it has nothing to do with subsidizing the vision or dental maximums of private individual health plans.

  4. ✗ 错误（陷阱：核保规则倒置）。团体计划的核心优势正是免核保或免体检额度高，而个人定制计划才通常需要经过核保（且医疗报销险核查的是健康状况，不照搬伤残收入险的收入财务核保）。Incorrect. Inverting underwriting rules. A primary benefit of group insurance is that members join without individual medical underwriting, whereas individual customized plans generally require underwriting (and for medical expense reimbursement insurance, underwriting checks health status; it does not copy the income and financial underwriting used for disability income insurance).

第 6 题Q6AD&D意外伤残分级给付表AD&D graduated schedule of loss tiers★★陷阱：AD&D全额赔付或跨省税混淆

Owen has an Accidental Death and Dismemberment (AD&D) rider attached to his policy. Under the policy's schedule of loss (which matches the standard exam illustration: 100% of the principal sum for loss of two limbs or sight in both eyes, 67% for loss of sight in one eye, 50% for loss of speech, and 33% for loss of hearing in one ear, provided the loss occurs within 365 days of the accident), Owen suffers an accident that permanently destroys the hearing in one ear 90 days after the injury. What percentage of the principal sum will the AD&D rider pay?

Owen 的保单附有意外身故与伤残（AD&D）附加险。根据保单的给付比例表（采用考试标准示例：双肢缺失或双目失明给付主保额的 100%，单眼失明给付 67%，失语给付 50%，单耳失聪给付 33%，前提是损失在意外发生后 365 天内出现），Owen 遭遇意外并在受伤后第 90 天永久丧失单耳听力。该 AD&D 附加险将按主保额的多少比例给付？

  1. ✗ 错误（陷阱：AD&D全额赔付误判）。意外身故与伤残（AD&D）的赔付方式不是“出事就赔全额”，只有身故、双肢缺失或双目失明才按 100% 保额给付，其余指定损失必须严格查给付比例表（schedule of loss）分级计算。Incorrect. Mistakenly assuming that AD&D always pays the full benefit. AD&D does not pay 100% of the principal sum for every injury; only death, loss of two limbs, or loss of sight in both eyes qualifies for 100%, while other specified losses are paid according to the graduated schedule of loss.

  2. ✗ 错误（陷阱：给付比例表档次记混）。在考试口径示例中，67%（或 75%）对应的是单肢缺失或单眼失明，而单耳失聪对应的分级比例是 33%，失语为 50%。Incorrect. Confusing the tiers on the schedule of loss. In standard exam illustrations, 67% (or 75%) corresponds to the loss of one limb or sight in one eye, speech is 50%, and loss of hearing in one ear corresponds to 33%.

  3. ✓ 正确。AD&D 附加险按给付比例表（schedule of loss）依严重度分级赔付（考试口径采用 365 天时限与该分级比例作示例，实务中不同合同可能采用 6 个月时限或不同比例，考场严格按题干给定比例答）：受伤后 90 天在 365 天时限内，单耳失聪按主保额的 33% 给付。Correct. AD&D riders pay benefits according to a graduated schedule of loss based on injury severity (the exam standard illustrates a 365-day time limit and specific percentage tiers, which candidates must follow as presented in the question stem). Because the permanent hearing loss in one ear occurred 90 days after the accident (within the 365-day limit), the rider pays 33% of the principal sum.

  4. ✗ 错误（陷阱：忽略损失发生时限窗口）。AD&D 并不要求损失在事故当天瞬间发生，只要在合同约定的时限窗口（本题给定考试示例为意外后 365 天内，90 天满足条件）内发生指定损失即可获赔。Incorrect. Ignoring the contractual time window. AD&D does not require the specified loss to occur on the exact date of the accident; coverage applies as long as the loss occurs within the contractual window (illustrated here as within 365 days, which the 90-day mark satisfies).

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Using the same AD&D schedule of loss illustration (100% of the principal sum for loss of sight in both eyes; 75% for loss of one limb; 50% for loss of speech; 33% for loss of hearing in one ear; loss must occur within 365 days of the accident), what percentage of the principal sum is payable if the insured permanently loses speech 180 days after a covered accident?

沿用同一份 AD&D 给付比例表示例（双目失明给付主保额的 100%；单肢缺失 75%；失语 50%；单耳失聪 33%；损失须在意外后 365 天内发生），若受保人在受保意外发生后第 180 天永久丧失语言能力（失语），应按主保额的多少比例给付？

  1. ✓ 正确。考试口径通过给付比例表（schedule of loss）说明 AD&D 按损失严重度分级计算（实务合同条款与时限可能不同，考场按给定示例答）：意外后 180 天未超过 365 天时限，失语对应主保额 50% 的给付比例。Correct. The exam illustrates AD&D benefits using a graduated schedule of loss based on severity. The permanent loss of speech occurred 180 days after the accident, which is within the 365-day window, and corresponds to a 50% payout of the principal sum under this schedule.

  2. ✗ 错误（陷阱：给付比例分级混淆）。75%（或 67%）在考试口径示例中用于单肢缺失或单眼失明，而失语（loss of speech）对应的比例是 50%。Incorrect. Confusing schedule of loss percentages. In exam illustrations, 75% (or 67%) applies to the loss of a single limb or sight in one eye, whereas loss of speech is assigned a 50% payout tier.

  3. ✗ 错误（陷阱：AD&D全额赔付误判）。哪怕失语是永久且完全的，在 AD&D 给付比例表（schedule of loss）中也不属于 100% 档次（100% 仅限身故、双肢缺失或双目失明）。Incorrect. Mistakenly assuming a 100% payout for complete loss of speech. Even though the loss of speech is permanent and total, it does not qualify for the 100% tier on an AD&D schedule of loss, which is reserved for death, loss of two limbs, or loss of sight in both eyes.

  4. ✗ 错误（陷阱：混淆旅行险稳定期与AD&D损失时限）。90–180 天常见于旅行险出发前稳定期（stability period），而本题明确给定考试口径示例时限为意外后 365 天内，180 天完全符合给付条件。Incorrect. Confusing travel stability periods with the AD&D loss window. Look-back periods of 90 to 180 days apply to travel insurance stability rules, whereas this AD&D provision explicitly allows 365 days from the accident date, making 180 days fully eligible.

第 7 题Q7BC省级医保覆盖边界与扩展医疗Scope of BC provincial healthcare and extended health needs★陷阱：公费医保覆盖范围混淆

Hannah, a healthy adult between the ages of 19 and 64 living in Vancouver, has no employer group benefits and relies solely on British Columbia's Medical Services Plan (MSP). This month, she visits her family physician for a medically necessary consultation, gets a routine eye exam at an optometrist's clinic, receives physiotherapy for a sprained wrist, and has a routine dental cleaning. Assuming she does not belong to any special supplementary assistance group, which of these expenses is paid by MSP?

居住在温哥华、年龄在 19 至 64 岁之间的健康成年人 Hannah 没有雇主团体福利，仅依赖 BC 省医疗服务计划（MSP）。本月她因医疗必要原因看家庭医生门诊、在验光师诊所做了常规验光、因手腕扭伤接受物理治疗，并做了一次常规洗牙。假定她不属于任何特定补助人群，上述哪项费用由 MSP 支付？

  1. ✗ 错误（陷阱：忽略年龄段验光除外）。BC 省 MSP 明确不覆盖 19 至 64 岁成年人的常规验光（routine eye exam），该年龄段常规验光需自付或由扩展医疗险（extended health insurance）报销。Incorrect. Overlooking age-based vision exclusions. British Columbia's MSP explicitly excludes routine eye examinations for healthy adults aged 19 to 64; eye exams for this age group must be paid out of pocket or reimbursed through private extended health insurance.

  2. ✓ 正确。考试口径将省级医保泛称为 provincial health care plans，落实到 BC 省现行规定，MSP 主要支付医疗必要的医生诊疗服务及极少数医院内口腔手术；常规牙科、19–64 岁常规验光、以及物理治疗等辅助医疗（paramedical services，特定受益人除外）均不由 MSP 普遍支付，这正是扩展医疗险与牙科险要接住的账单。Correct. The exam standard refers generically to provincial health care plans, which in British Columbia corresponds to MSP. MSP primarily covers medically necessary physician services and a very small number of in-hospital oral surgeries. Routine dental cleanings, routine eye exams for adults aged 19 to 64, and paramedical services like physiotherapy (unless qualifying under supplementary assistance) are excluded from general MSP coverage and represent the exact bills private extended health and dental insurance are designed to cover.

  3. ✗ 错误（陷阱：MSP保费取消误作覆盖扩围）。BC 省于 2020-01-01 取消个人 MSP 保费仅改变了筹资方式，完全不等于扩大 MSP 的服务保障目录。Incorrect. Equating premium elimination with coverage expansion. British Columbia eliminated personal MSP premiums on January 1, 2020, to change how healthcare is funded; this reform did not expand MSP's statutory scope of covered healthcare services.

  4. ✗ 错误（陷阱：辅助医疗覆盖范围混淆）。针灸、脊医、按摩、自然疗法、物理治疗（physiotherapy）等辅助服务在非特定补助人群下属于 MSP 明确不覆盖项目，这也是个人扩展医疗险五类典型保障之一。Incorrect. Confusing paramedical coverage rules. Paramedical services such as physiotherapy, chiropractic care, massage therapy, acupuncture, and naturopathy are not covered by MSP for the general population and represent a core coverage category under private extended health insurance.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

During a client meeting in Burnaby, an applicant asks why he would need private extended health insurance if Canada already has public health care and national pharmacare legislation. Which explanation by the advisor is accurate?

在本拿比的一次客户会谈中，一名投保人询问：既然加拿大已经有公共医保和联邦药物保险立法，为什么他还需要私人扩展医疗险？代理人的哪项解释是准确的？

  1. ✓ 正确。省级医保（provincial health care plans / MSP）主要覆盖医疗必要的医生与医院服务，联邦《药物保险法》（Pharmacare Act）及省级专项计划首期也仅覆盖避孕药具、糖尿病用药等列明项目；核算公共保障后，常规牙科、视力、辅助医疗、病房升级及更广泛的处方药仍存在明显缺口，这正是扩展医疗险存在的理由。Correct. Provincial health care plans (MSP in BC) cover medically necessary doctor and hospital services, while the initial phase of the federal Pharmacare Act and provincial initiatives covers only specific listed items such as contraceptives and diabetes medications. Even after factoring in public coverage, substantial gaps remain for routine dental, vision care, paramedical services, hospital room upgrades, and a wide array of prescription drugs, which is why private extended health insurance is needed.

  2. ✗ 错误（陷阱：把MSP不赔写成政府完全不管）。不能把 MSP 不赔直接等同于政府完全不管：极少数医院内的口腔/牙科手术由 MSP 覆盖，且处方药等还有专项公费计划（如 Plan NP、Fair PharmaCare）或特定人群补助，必须先核公共保障再估缺口。Incorrect. Mistaking exclusions from basic MSP for an absolute absence of government funding. Specialized public programs (such as Plan NP and Fair PharmaCare, or the rare in-hospital dental surgeries MSP covers) provide assistance for certain drugs or vulnerable groups; advisors must account for available public programs before evaluating private insurance gaps.

  3. ✗ 错误（陷阱：强制投保误解）。2020 年取消 MSP 个人保费并未使私人扩展医疗险（extended health insurance）变成法定强制险，个人是否购买完全取决于对公共保障缺口的需求分析。Incorrect. Misunderstanding insurance mandates. Eliminating personal MSP premiums in 2020 did not make private extended health insurance mandatory; purchasing private coverage remains entirely optional based on individual needs analysis.

  4. ✗ 错误（陷阱：医疗报销险与伤残收入险混淆）。补偿收入中断的是伤残收入险（disability insurance），而扩展医疗险、牙科险与旅行险解决的是“医疗账单来了谁来付”的费用报销问题，因此在核保上也不照搬伤残险的收入财务核保。Incorrect. Confusing expense reimbursement insurance with disability income insurance. Disability insurance replaces lost earned income when an illness or injury prevents working, whereas extended health, dental, and travel insurance reimburse eligible out-of-pocket medical bills.
