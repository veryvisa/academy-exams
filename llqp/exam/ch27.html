① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 六类计划匹配题：题干给出雇主诉求（如“只想在有盈利的年份出资”“希望员工也能自己供款”“不想承担精算兜底责任”），考你用“谁供款”和“谁承担投资风险”两条独立分类线选出唯一匹配的计划类型。Matching six plan types to employer objectives: Scenarios present employer needs (such as contributing only in profitable years, allowing employees to make their own contributions, or avoiding actuarial funding liabilities), testing candidates on selecting the uniquely matching plan type using the two distinct criteria of contributor identity and bearer of investment risk.
  • PA 与 RRSP 供款空间计算题：给出员工年收入及 DCPP 或 GRRSP 的劳资配比比例，考你区分哪些计划产生养老金调整额（PA）侵蚀次年额度、哪些计划直接占用当年个人 RRSP 供款空间。Calculating PA and RRSP contribution room: Given employee annual earnings and employer-employee matching contribution ratios in a DCPP or GRRSP, testing whether candidates distinguish which plans generate a Pension Adjustment (PA) reducing next year's RRSP room versus which plans directly consume current-year individual RRSP deduction limit.
  • 离职资金去向与锁定规则题：员工在跳槽或退休时处理账户余额，考你区分锁定资金（转 LIRA/LIF）与非锁定资金（转个人 RRSP/RRIF 或提现），以及考试口径“两年归属”与 BC 现行 PBSA 即时归属的双口径差异。Termination fund transfers and locking-in rules: When employees handle account balances upon job change or retirement, testing the distinction between locked-in funds (transferred to a LIRA or LIF) and unlocked funds (transferred to an individual RRSP, RRIF, or cashed out), alongside the dual-benchmark differences between the exam standard of two-year vesting and current BC PBSA immediate vesting.
  • 受托责任与实务流程题：考发起方外包管理后是否仍负监督受托责任、代理人向谁履行利益冲突披露义务，以及“发起方先申请、成员后加入”的两阶段建设计划流程。Fiduciary duty and practical operational workflows: Testing whether a plan sponsor retains supervisory fiduciary duties under the prudent person rule after outsourcing administration, to whom an agent owes conflict-of-interest disclosure duties, and the two-stage plan implementation process where the sponsor applies first before individual members enrol.
  • 税务待遇与资格红线题：考雇主向不同计划供款是否计入员工当期应税福利（GRRSP 与群组 TFSA 计入，DCPP/DPSP/PRPP 不计入），以及持股达 10% 的关联员工不得加入 DPSP 等硬性限制。Tax treatment and eligibility red lines: Testing whether employer contributions to different plans constitute current taxable benefits to employees (taxable under a GRRSP or Group TFSA; non-taxable under a DCPP, DPSP, or PRPP), alongside strict eligibility restrictions such as barring employees owning 10% or more of voting shares from participating in a DPSP.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ DPSP 缴费型陷阱The contributory DPSP trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到“利润分享激励员工”就直觉选 DPSP，忽略了 DPSP 依法只能由雇主单方出资（非缴费型），员工绝不能自己往里供款。Candidates see profit-sharing incentives for employees and instinctively select a DPSP, overlooking that a DPSP legally permits only employer contributions (it is strictly non-contributory) and employees can never make their own contributions.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现 profit sharing 与 employees also contribute / contributory plan。The question stem mentions profit sharing alongside employees also contribute or contributory plan.

⚠ PA 全覆盖错觉The universal PA coverage illusion

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为只要参加了公司的团体退休计划就会产生养老金调整额（PA），把 GRRSP、PRPP 和群组 TFSA 也算进 PA 里。Candidates mistakenly assume that participating in any employer group retirement plan generates a Pension Adjustment (PA), erroneously including GRRSPs, PRPPs, and Group TFSAs in the PA calculation.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问哪些计划会产生 Pension Adjustment (PA) 减少次年 RRSP 空间，或直接给出 GRRSP 供款问次年 PA 是多少。The question stem asks which plans generate a Pension Adjustment (PA) reducing next year's RRSP room, or provides GRRSP contribution amounts and asks for the resulting PA.

⚠ 雇主供款免税错觉The tax-free employer contribution illusion

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 默认雇主帮员工存的退休金都不算当期应税收入，忘了 GRRSP 和群组 TFSA 的雇主供款视为员工当期应税福利（额外薪资）。Candidates assume that all employer-contributed retirement funds are exempt from current taxable income, forgetting that employer contributions to a GRRSP or Group TFSA are considered current taxable benefits (additional employment income) to the employee.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问雇主配比供款（matching contribution）在当年报税时是否计入员工的 taxable income。The question stem asks whether employer matching contributions are included in the employee's taxable income for the current tax year.

⚠ 归属与锁定混同陷阱Confusing vesting with unlocking trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把“已归属（vested，权益归员工所有）”当成“不锁定（unlocked，可以马上取现金花掉）”，或把考试旧例子里的两年归属当成 BC 现行 PBSA 规则。Candidates confuse vested status (entitlement belongs to the employee) with unlocked status (funds can be immediately cashed out), or treat the exam's legacy two-year vesting example as the current BC PBSA rule.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述员工离职时已满足归属条件，问能否直接把 DCPP 余额提现出来用。The question stem describes an employee meeting vesting conditions upon termination and asks whether DCPP account balances can be directly withdrawn in cash.

⚠ CAP 与保证条款错位Misaligning CAP definitions with guarantees trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为 DBPP 也属于资本积累计划（CAP），或者误以为团体计划里的分离基金和零售版一样默认自带身故保证。Candidates assume that a DBPP qualifies as a Capital Accumulation Plan (CAP), or mistakenly believe that group segregated funds within group plans automatically include death benefit guarantees like their retail counterparts.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 Capital Accumulation Plan (CAP) 定义判断，或问团体分离基金（group segregated funds）是否默认提供 death benefit guarantee。The question stem asks to identify a Capital Accumulation Plan (CAP) definition, or asks whether group segregated funds provide an automatic death benefit guarantee.

归属期与锁定的法律区分Legal Distinction Between Vesting and Locking-InVesting vs. Locking-in归属决定员工对已赚取福利的权益归谁（BC PBSA 下即时归属，考试口径常举两年归属例子），锁定决定资金必须留作退休用途而不能自由提取现金。Vesting determines employee ownership of accrued benefits (immediate vesting under current BC PBSA, while exam questions often cite a legacy two-year vesting rule), whereas locking-in mandates that funds remain dedicated to retirement income and cannot be freely withdrawn in cash.
DPSP 关联员工禁入与供款规则DPSP Contribution Rules and Bar on Related EmployeesDeferred Profit Sharing Plan (DPSP)DPSP 仅由发起方以当年或未分配以前年度利润供款，不锁定，但持股达 10% 或以上及与雇主有关联关系的员工不得加入。A DPSP is funded solely by the sponsor out of current or retained profits and is not locked in, but employees holding 10% or more of voting shares and individuals related to the employer are strictly barred from participating.
雇主供款的当期应税福利属性Taxable Benefit Status of Employer ContributionsTaxable benefit of employer contributions雇主向 GRRSP 和群组 TFSA 的供款计入员工当期应税收入，而向 DBPP、DCPP、DPSP、PRPP 的供款不算员工当期应税收入。Employer contributions to a GRRSP and a Group TFSA are included in the employee's current taxable employment income, whereas employer contributions to a DBPP, DCPP, DPSP, and PRPP are not considered current taxable income.
养老金调整额（PA）及调整机制Pension Adjustment (PA) and Rebalancing MechanismsPension Adjustment (PA), PAR & PSPA仅 DBPP、DCPP、DPSP 产生 PA 侵蚀次年 RRSP 供款空间；中途离职多算的 PA 通过 PAR 恢复空间，追溯提高福利则产生 PSPA 压缩空间。Only DBPP, DCPP, and DPSP generate a Pension Adjustment (PA) that reduces the employee's next year's RRSP contribution room; early termination forfeitures restore room via a Pension Adjustment Reversal (PAR), while retroactive benefit upgrades create a Past Service Pension Adjustment (PSPA) that reduces available room.

第 1 题Q1管理方受托责任与审慎人原则Plan administrator fiduciary duty and the prudent person rule★★陷阱：误以为外包给保险人即可免除监督责任

A manufacturing company sponsors a Defined Contribution Pension Plan (DCPP) and serves as the legal plan administrator. To streamline operations, the company hires an insurer to manage the daily investment funds and record-keeping. If an investment option performs poorly due to a lack of oversight, how does the fiduciary duty under provincial pension legislation apply?

一家制造企业发起了一项固定供款养老金计划（DCPP）并担任法定计划管理方。为简化运营，该公司聘请一家保险人负责日常投资基金管理和记录保存。如果某个投资选项因缺乏监督而表现不佳，省级养老金立法下的受托责任如何适用？

  1. ✓ 正确。对受《养老金福利标准法》（[Pension Benefits Standards Act, PBSA]）规管的养老金，法定计划管理方（[plan administrator]）承担受托职责（[fiduciary duty]），必须遵循审慎人原则（[prudent person rule]）以成员与受益人最佳利益行事；即便将日常投资管理外包给保险人，法定管理方也不能免除监督责任。Correct. For pension plans governed by the Pension Benefits Standards Act (PBSA), the legal plan administrator holds a fiduciary duty and must adhere to the prudent person rule to act in the best interests of plan members and beneficiaries; even when outsourcing day-to-day investment management to an insurer, the legal administrator cannot be relieved of its supervisory responsibility.

  2. ✗ 错误。掉入“外包即免责”陷阱。审慎人原则约束的是法定管理方作为受托人（[fiduciary]）的身份，委托服务商管理日常运作并不会转移管理方的法定监督责任。Incorrect. Falls into the outsourcing relieves liability trap. The prudent person rule binds the legal administrator in its role as a fiduciary; delegating day-to-day operations to a service provider does not transfer the administrator's statutory supervisory responsibility.

  3. ✗ 错误。掉入“员工自选投资则雇主零责任”陷阱。即便在 DCPP 中成员自己选基金，管理方仍须在选择和监督服务商、设计投资菜单及成员沟通上履行受托责任。Incorrect. Falls into the employee investment choice eliminates employer liability trap. Even though members select their own funds in a DCPP, the administrator must still fulfill fiduciary duties in selecting and monitoring service providers, designing the investment menu, and communicating with members.

  4. ✗ 错误。团体退休计划的分销结构通常是代理人、发起方与保险人团体专员（[group specialists]）直接对接，考试口径示例未列总代理（[MGA]），且 MGA 更不是养老金法下的法定受托管理方。Incorrect. The distribution structure for group retirement plans typically connects the agent, plan sponsor, and insurer group specialists directly; the exam standard does not feature a managing general agent (MGA), and an MGA is certainly not a statutory fiduciary administrator under pension legislation.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A logistics firm acts as both plan sponsor and legal administrator for its employees' registered pension plan. The board of directors delegates asset custody and fund reporting to an insurance company. Which statement accurately describes the administrator's ongoing legal obligation?

一家物流公司同时担任员工注册养老金计划的发起方和法定管理方。董事会将资产托管和基金报告委托给一家保险公司。下列哪项陈述准确描述了管理方的持续法定义务？

  1. ✗ 错误。保险人定期向发起方提交财务报告是保险人的职责，不能因此免除法定管理方遵循审慎人原则（[prudent person rule]）的监督义务。Incorrect. While an insurer is responsible for regularly submitting financial reports to the sponsor, this does not relieve the legal administrator of its ongoing supervisory duty under the prudent person rule.

  2. ✗ 错误。无论 DBPP 还是 DCPP，受 PBSA 规管的养老金计划法定管理方均承担受托责任（[fiduciary duty]）。Incorrect. Whether a plan is a DBPP or a DCPP, the legal administrator of a pension plan governed by the PBSA owes an ongoing fiduciary duty.

  3. ✓ 正确。法定管理方（[plan administrator]）作为受托人（[fiduciary]），必须遵循审慎人原则（[prudent person rule]），以计划成员与受益人的最佳利益行事，外包日常管理不能免除其监督责任。Correct. As a fiduciary, the legal plan administrator must adhere to the prudent person rule to act in the best interests of plan members and beneficiaries; delegating day-to-day administration cannot relieve the administrator of its supervisory duty.

  4. ✗ 错误。成员（[members]）是参与计划的员工，受托监督保险人的法定义务在于计划管理方，而非员工个人。Incorrect. Members are participating employees; the statutory duty to supervise the insurer lies with the plan administrator, not individual employees.

第 2 题Q2DPSP非缴费属性与持股禁入Non-contributory nature of a DPSP and the 10% shareholding restriction★★陷阱：DPSP 缴费型陷阱

An engineering firm with fluctuating annual profits wants to set up a group plan funded out of company profits. However, the founder insists that employees must also be allowed to contribute their own money into this same plan through payroll deductions, and wants the founder's brother—who owns 10% of the company's shares—to participate. Why cannot a Deferred Profit Sharing Plan (DPSP) meet these requirements?

一家年度利润波动较大的工程公司希望设立一项由公司利润出资的团体计划。然而，创始人坚持要求允许员工通过工资扣款将自己的钱也供入同一个计划，并希望持有公司 10% 股份的创始人兄弟也能参加。为什么递延利润分享计划（DPSP）无法满足这些要求？

  1. ✗ 错误。递延利润分享计划（[DPSP]）的资金是不锁定的（[not locked in]），不适用 PBSA 的养老金锁定规则。Incorrect. Funds within a Deferred Profit Sharing Plan (DPSP) are not locked in and are not subject to the pension locking-in rules of the PBSA.

  2. ✓ 正确。递延利润分享计划（[Deferred Profit Sharing Plan, DPSP]）天生只能是非缴费型计划（[non-contributory plan]），仅允许发起方以当年或未分配以前年度利润供款，员工不得自行供款；同时，持股 ≥10% 或与雇主有关联关系的员工不得加入。Correct. A Deferred Profit Sharing Plan (DPSP) is strictly non-contributory by law, permitting contributions solely from the sponsor out of current or retained profits, and employees cannot contribute; furthermore, individuals who own 10% or more of voting shares or are related to the employer are barred from participating.

  3. ✗ 错误。DPSP 与 DCPP 之间没有互斥关系，企业完全可以保留 DCPP 作为固定供款基础，再叠加一个 DPSP 作为利润分享层。Incorrect. A DPSP and a DCPP are not mutually exclusive; an employer can maintain a DCPP as a foundational fixed-contribution plan and layer a DPSP on top as a profit-sharing incentive.

  4. ✗ 错误。雇主对 DPSP 的供款在当期不算员工应税收入（税前不计收入，直到未来提取时才全额纳税）；当期计入应税福利的是 GRRSP 和群组 TFSA。Incorrect. Employer contributions to a DPSP do not constitute current taxable income to employees (they accumulate pre-tax until fully taxed upon withdrawal); employer contributions that constitute current taxable benefits are those made to a GRRSP or Group TFSA.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A three-year-old software startup with an average employee age of 28 already offers a Defined Contribution Pension Plan (DCPP) with fixed contributions, and now wants to add a flexible layer that shares profits in good years without locking the firm into fixed obligations in lean years. Which arrangement best achieves this goal?

一家成立三年、员工平均年龄 28 岁的软件初创公司已经提供固定供款的固定供款养老金计划（DCPP），现在希望叠加一个灵活激励层，在盈利好的年份分享利润，同时不在盈利差的年份背上刚性供款负担。哪种安排最能实现这一目标？

  1. ✓ 正确。DCPP 与递延利润分享计划（[DPSP]）可以并存：DCPP 提供稳定的基础供款预期，叠加仅由雇主按利润出资（非缴费型）的 DPSP 作为浮动激励层，按计划条款可在无利润年份暂停供款（须核既有承诺）；「合资格员工」指排除持股 ≥10% 及与雇主有关联关系的员工。Correct.   1. ✗ 错误。同时踩中两个陷阱：第一，固定给付养老金计划（[DBPP]）明确排除在资本积累计划（[CAP]）之外；第二，团体分离基金默认不提供身故保证。Incorrect. Triggers two traps simultaneously: first, a Defined Benefit Pension Plan (DBPP) is explicitly excluded from being a Capital Accumulation Plan (CAP); second, group segregated funds do not offer default death benefit guarantees.

  2. ✗ 错误。判定 CAP 的标准不是“雇主是否供款”，而是“成员是否有投资选择权”；且团体分离基金默认不提供身故保证。Incorrect. The criterion for determining a CAP is whether members have investment choice, not whether the employer contributes; furthermore, group segregated funds do not provide default death benefit guarantees.

  3. ✓ 正确。判定资本积累计划（[Capital Accumulation Plan, CAP]）的核心标准是成员可在两个或以上投资选项中自主选择（[DBPP] 除外）；同时，名字里带“保证”的分离基金在团体版中默认不提供身故保证（[death benefit guarantee]）。Correct. The core criterion for a Capital Accumulation Plan (CAP) is that members can choose among two or more investment options (excluding a DBPP); additionally, despite segregated funds having guarantee features in retail markets, group segregated funds generally carry no default death benefit guarantee.

  4. ✗ 错误。只要允许成员在两个或以上投资选项中选择，DCPP、GRRSP、DPSP、PRPP 等均可构成 CAP，并非只有群组 TFSA；且团体分离基金是直接默认不含身故保证，不是靠医学核保添加。Incorrect. Any plan permitting members to choose among two or more investment options (such as a DCPP, GRRSP, DPSP, or PRPP) constitutes a CAP, not solely a Group TFSA; furthermore, group segregated funds simply omit death benefit guarantees by default rather than requiring medical underwriting to add them.
