本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 保证触发与结算金额计算题：给定初始存款、保证比例（如 75%/75% 或 75%/100%）和不同时点的市值，考察到期、身故或中途提前赎回时实际可领取的金额及保险公司补足款（top-up）。Guarantee trigger and settlement calculation questions: Given initial deposit, guarantee percentages (such as 75%/75% or 75%/100%), and market values at different points in time, test the actual payout amount and insurer top-up upon maturity, death, or early partial or full surrender.
  • 受益人指定与双重保护辨析题：通过客户希望规避遗嘱认证（probate）或防范债权人追索（creditor protection）的情景，测试非遗产受益人直接给付条件与 BC《Insurance Act》s.65(2) 家庭成员范围的区分。Beneficiary designation and dual-protection analysis questions: Using scenarios where a client wishes to bypass probate or protect against creditor claims, test the distinction between direct payment conditions for a named non-estate beneficiary and the family member class under BC Insurance Act s. 65(2).
  • 合同操作后果判断（分批注资、重置、提取与转换）：考察按存款日逐笔起算 10 年到期日、重置（reset）锁高保证基数并顺延到期日、部分提取按比例永久下调保证基数，以及基金转换（switch）触发资本利得或损失。Contract transaction consequence assessment questions (subsequent deposits, resets, withdrawals, and switches): Test deposit date-based 10-year maturity calculations, resets locking in higher guarantee bases and extending the maturity date, proportional permanent reductions of guarantee bases upon partial withdrawals, and capital gains or losses triggered by fund switches.
  • 分离基金与共同基金多维对比题：聚焦 IVIC 保险合同法律性质、省级保险监管（Insurance Council of BC / BCFSA）与证券监管（CIRO）的分界、MER 含保险成本及年龄限制（非注册 90 岁、RRSP 71 岁）。Multidimensional segregated fund versus mutual fund comparison questions: Focus on the legal nature of an IVIC insurance contract, the regulatory boundary between provincial insurance regulators (Insurance Council of BC and BCFSA) and securities regulation (CIRO), MER inclusive of insurance guarantee costs, and age limits (non-registered up to age 90, RRSP to age 71).
  • 考试口径（2022）与现行规则（2026）双口径题：考察 Assuris 破产保护上限（旧值 $60,000 或 85% vs 现行 $100,000 或 90%）、DSC 递延销售费禁令依据（BC《Financial Institutions Act》）及法院一般申请费（$208 vs $200）。Exam benchmark (2022) versus current rules (2026) dual-standard questions: Test Assuris insolvency protection limits (exam benchmark $60,000 or 85% versus current $100,000 or 90%), the legal basis for the deferred sales charge (DSC) ban (BC Financial Institutions Act), and the court probate application fee ($208 versus $200).

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 到期保证与身故保证混淆Confusing maturity guarantee with death benefit guarantee

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到 75%/100% 保证档位时容易把两个数字位置记反，或误以为身故保证也要等满 10 年才生效、单身怕亏本的客户靠身故保证保护自己。Candidates often reverse the two numbers in a 75%/100% guarantee structure, mistakenly assume that the death benefit guarantee also requires a 10-year waiting period to take effect, or believe that single clients fearing investment loss protect themselves through the death benefit guarantee.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现两个斜杠数字（如 75/100）、受保人在合同未满 10 年时身故，或询问“担心自己活着时本金亏损”应看哪项保证。The question stem displays two slash-separated figures (e.g., 75/100), the annuitant dies before the 10-year term matures, or the scenario asks which guarantee protects an investor who fears losing principal during their lifetime.

⚠ 中途赎回误算保证补足Erroneously applying guarantee top-ups to early redemptions

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把“75% 保证”当成任何时点退保的保底金额，忽略了到期保证与身故保证仅在合同到期日或受保人身故当天触发。Candidates treat the 75% guarantee as a floor value available upon surrender at any time, overlooking that maturity and death benefit guarantees trigger only on the exact maturity date or upon the annuitant's death.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现“第 6 年或第 7 年急需资金全部赎回”“到期前提前退保”且当时市值低于初始存款的 75%。The question stem mentions an urgent need for funds requiring full surrender in year 6 or 7, or early redemption before maturity while market value is below 75% of the original deposit.

⚠ 分批注资合并到期日Consolidating maturity dates for subsequent deposits

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生默认同一份分离基金合同只有一个统一到期日，误以为后续追加存款会随首笔存款在第 10 年末一起到期。Candidates assume a single segregated fund contract has only one unified maturity date, mistakenly believing that subsequent deposits mature at the end of year 10 alongside the initial deposit.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述客户在合同成立后连续数年分批追加存款（deposit date-based），询问第 2 笔或第 3 笔存款的到期时间与累计到期保证额。The question stem describes a client making subsequent deposits in successive years under a deposit date-based contract, asking for the maturity date or cumulative maturity guarantee of the second or third deposit.

⚠ 遗嘱认证与免扣押条件混同Conflating probate bypass with creditor protection requirements

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把绕开遗嘱认证（只需有效非遗产受益人）与存续期债权人免执行保护（BC s.65(2) 须为配偶、子女、孙辈或父母）合并成同一条规则，或误以为家庭成员免扣押必须同时指定为不可撤销。Candidates conflate bypassing probate (which requires only a valid named non-estate beneficiary) with lifetime creditor protection (which under BC Insurance Act s. 65(2) requires designating a spouse, child, grandchild, or parent), or assume that family designations must also be irrevocable to block creditors.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干指定朋友、兄弟姐妹或慈善机构为受益人并询问能否免遗嘱认证或防债权人，或询问配偶受益人是否必须不可撤销才能抗衡债权人。The question stem names a friend, sibling, or charity as beneficiary and asks whether the contract bypasses probate or protects against creditors, or asks if a spouse must be named irrevocably to resist creditors.

⚠ 双口径数字与监管归属错位Misaligning dual-benchmark figures with regulatory bodies

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生混用 Assuris 考试旧值（$60,000/85%）与现行新值（$100,000/90%），或误以为分离基金归 CIRO 监管、BC 禁止分离基金 DSC 的依据是《Insurance Act》。Candidates mix up older Assuris exam figures ($60,000 or 85%) with current figures ($100,000 or 90%), or mistakenly assume segregated funds are regulated by CIRO or that BC banned segregated fund DSCs under the Insurance Act.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及保险公司破产时的 Assuris 保底计算、DSC 递延销售费的监管状态与立法依据，或比较分离基金与共同基金的监管机构。The question stem involves Assuris protection calculations upon insurer insolvency, the regulatory status and legislative authority for the DSC ban, or comparisons between segregated fund and mutual fund regulators.

个人可变保险合同的法律性质与三方角色Legal nature of an Individual Variable Insurance Contract (IVIC) and three-party rolesIndividual Variable Insurance Contract (IVIC)分离基金本质是保险合同而非共同基金份额，由持有人出资决策、受保人作为到期与身故保证的锚点、受益人领取身故给付；注册账户内持有人与受保人必须为同一人。A segregated fund is legally an insurance contract rather than mutual fund units, structured around a policy owner who funds the contract and makes investment decisions, an annuitant whose survival or death anchors the maturity and death guarantees, and a beneficiary who receives death benefits; in registered accounts, the owner and annuitant must be the same person.
免遗嘱认证与债权人保护的受益人区分Distinction of beneficiary designations for probate bypass and creditor protectionprobate bypass and creditor protection指定有效非遗产受益人即可绕开遗嘱认证（BC 遗产认证费在 $25,000 以下免征，$25,000–$50,000 每千元收 $6，超 $50,000 每千元收 $14）；而存续期免债权人扣押依 BC《Insurance Act》s.65(2) 须指定受保人的配偶、子女、孙辈或父母（无需同时不可撤销）。Naming a valid non-estate beneficiary bypasses probate (BC probate fees are 0 on estates up to $25,000, $6 per $1,000 on $25,000 to $50,000, and $14 per $1,000 over $50,000); in contrast, lifetime statutory protection against creditor seizure under BC Insurance Act s. 65(2) requires designating the annuitant's spouse, child, grandchild, or parent (without needing to be irrevocable).
监管归属与销售牌照分界Regulatory jurisdiction and licensing boundariesregulatory jurisdiction and licensing分离基金属保险产品，销售代表须持人寿保险牌照并受省级保险监管机构（BC 为 Insurance Council of BC 与 BCFSA）管辖，不受监管共同基金与证券交易商的 CIRO 管辖。Segregated funds are insurance products whose sales representatives must hold a life insurance licence and are governed by provincial insurance regulators (in BC, the Insurance Council of BC and BCFSA), entirely outside the jurisdiction of CIRO, which regulates mutual funds and investment dealers.
到期保证与身故保证的触发与补足Maturity guarantee, death benefit guarantee triggers, and insurer top-upmaturity guarantee, death benefit guarantee, and top-up到期保证通常最短 10 年（常见 75% 或 100%），在到期日自动结算无需申请；身故保证从合同生效首日起适用；若触发时市值低于保证额，差额由保险公司自有资金补足（top-up），但中途赎回一律只按市值结算。Maturity guarantees generally require a minimum 10-year term (commonly 75% or 100%) and settle automatically on the maturity date without a claim; death benefit guarantees take effect on day one of the contract; if market value is below the guaranteed amount when triggered, the shortfall is topped up from the insurer's reserves, but early redemptions settle strictly at current market value.
递延销售费禁令与市场行为执法依据Deferred sales charge (DSC) ban and market conduct enforcement authoritydeferred sales charge (DSC) ban考试口径（2022）称分离基金 DSC 预期将被取消；BC 现行自 2023-06-01 起依据《Financial Institutions Act》s.244(2)(e)(ii) 禁止发行含 DSC 的新分离基金合同，旧合同可按原时间表走完。The exam benchmark (2022) states that segregated fund DSCs are expected to be banned; under current BC rules effective June 1, 2023, issuing new segregated fund contracts with DSCs is prohibited under the Financial Institutions Act s. 244(2)(e)(ii), while pre-existing contracts may run off according to their original redemption schedules.
Assuris 破产保护上限双口径Assuris insolvency protection limits under dual benchmarksAssuris protection limits保险公司破产时，分离基金保证利益在考试口径（2022）下受 $60,000 或原保证额 85%（取高，且不超过原保证额）保护；2023-05-29 起现行标准上调为 $100,000 或 90%（取高，不超过原保证额）。If a life insurer becomes insolvent, segregated fund guaranteed benefits are protected under the exam benchmark (2022) up to $60,000 or 85% of the guaranteed amount (whichever is higher, capped at the original guarantee); under current standards effective May 29, 2023, the limit was increased to $100,000 or 90% (whichever is higher, capped at the original guarantee).
分配、调整成本基础与转换的税务处理Tax treatment of allocations, adjusted cost base (ACB), and fund switchesallocation, adjusted cost base (ACB), and switch非注册分离基金每年将内部实现的利息、股息和资本利得分配给持有人（申报于 T3 税表）并相应调增调整成本基础（ACB）以避免重复征税；赎回或转换基金（switch）均视为税务处置，按赎回价与 ACB 差额计算资本利得或损失（50% 计入应税所得）。Non-registered segregated funds allocate internally realized interest, dividends, and capital gains annually to policyholders (reported on a T3 slip), which increases the adjusted cost base (ACB) to avoid double taxation; a redemption or internal fund switch is treated as a taxable disposition, triggering a capital gain or loss based on the difference between proceeds and ACB (50% included in taxable income).
犹豫期解约权与五类须确认指令Right of rescission and five client-confirmed transactionsright of rescission交付合同前须提供信息文件夹（information folder）与 Fund Facts，购买确认发出后客户通常有 2 个营业日行使解约权，退还已投入金额与当日单位价值中较低者；完成购买、转换、重置、提款、终止五类动作均须取得投资者确认。An information folder and Fund Facts must be delivered before entering into a contract; after the purchase confirmation is sent, the client generally has two business days to exercise the right of rescission, refunding the lesser of the amount invested or the current unit value; investor confirmation must be obtained for five transaction types: purchases, switches, resets, withdrawals, and terminations.
重置、部分提取与分批注资的保证计算Guarantee calculations for resets, partial withdrawals, and subsequent depositsreset, partial withdrawal, and deposit date-based maturity市场上涨时重置（reset）可将保证基数锁定至当前市值，但到期保证重置通常使到期日从重置日起再顺延 10 年；部分提取会按比例永久下调保证基数；按存款日逐笔起算的合同，每笔追加存款各自独立计算 10 年到期期。In rising markets, a reset locks in the guarantee base at the higher current market value, but a maturity guarantee reset typically resets the 10-year maturity clock from the reset date; partial withdrawals permanently reduce the guarantee base on a proportional basis; under deposit date-based contracts, each subsequent deposit runs its own independent 10-year maturity period.

第 1 题Q1免遗嘱认证与免债权人扣押的受益人条件Beneficiary conditions for probate bypass and creditor protection★★陷阱：遗嘱认证与免扣押条件混同

Victor, a business owner in British Columbia, invests $300,000 in a non-registered segregated fund contract (IVIC) where he is both the policy owner and the annuitant. He wants to know how naming either his close friend Daniel or his spouse Elena as revocable beneficiary affects probate fees and protection against his creditors during his lifetime.

Victor 是 BC 省的一名企业主，他在一份非注册分离基金合同（IVIC）中投入 $300,000，自己同时担任持有人和受保人。他想了解将挚友 Daniel 或配偶 Elena 指定为可撤销受益人，对遗嘱认证费以及他生前防范债权人追索分别有何影响。

  1. ✓ 正确。根据 BC《Insurance Act》s.65(1)，只要指定了有效且非遗产（estate）的受益人（无论是朋友 Daniel 还是配偶 Elena），身故给付均直接付给受益人而绕开遗嘱认证（probate）；但根据 s.65(2)，存续期间免于债权人执行或扣押（creditor protection）仅适用于指定受保人的配偶、子女、孙辈或父母，且不要求同时为不可撤销指定。Correct. Under BC Insurance Act s. 65(1), as long as a valid non-estate beneficiary is designated (whether friend Daniel or spouse Elena), the death benefit is paid directly to the beneficiary, bypassing probate; however, under s. 65(2), lifetime protection from creditor execution or seizure applies only when designating the annuitant's spouse, child, grandchild, or parent, without requiring the designation to be irrevocable.

  2. ✗ 错误。根据 BC《Insurance Act》s.65(2)，指定受保人的配偶、子女、孙辈或父母即可获得存续期免扣押保护，法律并未要求必须同时为不可撤销（irrevocable）指定；这是“遗嘱认证与免扣押条件混同”陷阱。Incorrect. Under BC Insurance Act s. 65(2), designating the annuitant's spouse, child, grandchild, or parent confers lifetime protection against seizure, and the law does not require the designation to be irrevocable; this falls into the trap of conflating probate bypass with creditor protection requirements.

  3. ✗ 错误。朋友 Daniel 作为非遗产受益人虽然能绕开遗嘱认证，但不属于 BC《Insurance Act》s.65(2) 规定的家庭成员范围（配偶、子女、孙辈、父母），因此不能获得法定免债权人追索保护；这是将两项保护条件混为一谈的陷阱。Incorrect. While naming friend Daniel as a non-estate beneficiary bypasses probate, he does not fall within the family member class specified in BC Insurance Act s. 65(2) (spouse, child, grandchild, or parent), so the contract receives no statutory creditor protection; this is a trap that conflates the two protection conditions.

  4. ✗ 错误。指定合格非遗产受益人使赔付款直接付给受益人，不进入遗产总值，从而绕开按《Probate Fee Act》计算的约 $3,650 分档认证费（以及在无其他须认证遗产时省去法院一般申请费，考试口径写 $208，BC 现行《Supreme Court Civil Rules》为 $200）。Incorrect. Designating a qualifying non-estate beneficiary ensures proceeds are paid directly to the beneficiary without entering the estate, thereby bypassing approximately $3,650 in tiered probate fees under the Probate Fee Act (as well as avoiding the general court application fee when there are no other probated assets, stated as $208 on the exam benchmark versus $200 under current BC Supreme Court Civil Rules).

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Grace owns a sole proprietorship in BC and holds a non-registered segregated fund contract with a current value of $300,000, which is her only asset besides a joint bank account. If she changes her revocable beneficiary from her estate to her adult daughter Chloe, what is the legal and probate cost effect?

Grace 在 BC 省经营一家个人独资企业，持有一份当前价值 $300,000 的非注册分离基金合同，除联名银行账户外这是她唯一的资产。如果她将可撤销受益人从“遗产”改为成年女儿 Chloe，会产生什么法律与遗嘱认证成本效果？

  1. ✗ 错误。女儿 Chloe 属于 BC《Insurance Act》s.65(2) 规定的家庭受益人范围（配偶、子女、孙辈、父母），即使是可撤销指定也能享有存续期债权人保护，无需改为不可撤销；这是“遗嘱认证与免扣押条件混同”陷阱。Incorrect. Daughter Chloe falls within the family beneficiary class under BC Insurance Act s. 65(2) (spouse, child, grandchild, parent), so even a revocable designation enjoys lifetime creditor protection without needing to be irrevocable; this falls into the trap of conflating probate bypass with creditor protection requirements.

  2. ✓ 正确。指定成年女儿 Chloe（受保人的子女）既满足非遗产受益人绕开遗嘱认证（probate）的条件，又符合 BC《Insurance Act》s.65(2) 的家庭成员免债权人扣押条件；$300,000 遗产的分档认证费为 $25,000×0 + $25,000×0.006 + $250,000×0.014 = $3,650，外加法院一般申请费（注：考试口径写 $208，BC 现行《Supreme Court Civil Rules》附表 1 已改为 $200，现行法院端总成本约 $3,850）。Correct. Designating adult daughter Chloe (the annuitant's child) satisfies both the non-estate beneficiary condition to bypass probate and the family member class condition under BC Insurance Act s. 65(2) for creditor protection; tiered probate fees on a $300,000 estate equal $25,000 * 0 + $25,000 * 0.006 + $250,000 * 0.014 = $3,650, plus the general court application fee (note: stated as $208 on the exam benchmark, whereas current BC Supreme Court Civil Rules Schedule 1 sets it at $200, bringing total current court costs to approximately $3,850).

Under the common deposit date-based contract structure, each subsequent deposit calculates its own 10-year guarantee period independently rather than maturing alongside the initial deposit at the end of Year 10; this is the trap of consolidating maturity dates for subsequent deposits.

  2. ✓ 正确。按存款日逐笔起算（deposit date-based）时，第 1 笔存款在第 10 年末到期（保证额 $10,000 × 75% = $7,500），第 2 年初存入的第 2 笔存款从存入日起满 10 年即在第 11 年末到期（再加 $7,500，累计到期保证额为 2 × $10,000 × 75% = $15,000），第 3 笔则在第 12 年末到期（累计 $22,500）。Correct. Under a deposit date-based structure, the first deposit matures at the end of Year 10 (guarantee $10,000 * 75% = $7,500), and the second deposit made at the start of Year 2 completes its 10 years at the end of Year 11 (adding $7,500, bringing cumulative matured guarantees to 2 * $10,000 * 75% = $15,000), while the third deposit matures at the end of Year 12 (reaching $22,500).

  3. ✗ 错误。分批追加存款不会把 75% 到期保证比例提高为 100%，且第 3 笔存款要到第 12 年末才满 10 年，因此第 11 年末的累计到期保证额是 $15,000 而非 $30,000。Incorrect. Making subsequent deposits does not increase the 75% maturity guarantee ratio to 100%, and the third deposit will not reach 10 years until the end of Year 12; thus, the cumulative matured guarantee at the end of Year 11 is $15,000 rather than $30,000.

  4. ✗ 错误。正常追加存款是按各笔存款日各自独立起算 10 年，并不会像行使到期重置（reset）那样把首笔存款的到期日也往后顺延。Incorrect. Subsequent deposits run their own independent 10-year maturity clocks from each deposit date and do not push back the maturity date of the first deposit like an exercised reset does.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Kevin holds a segregated fund contract and tells his agent he wants to withdraw part of his money in Year 4, assuming that because the contract is still six years away from its 10-year maturity date, a partial withdrawal now will have no effect on his future maturity guarantee. What must the agent explain?

Kevin 持有一份分离基金合同，他在第 4 年告诉代理人自己想取出一部分资金，并认为反正距离 10 年到期日还有 6 年，现在部分提款不会影响未来的到期保证。代理人必须向他解释什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。部分提取对原保证基数的下调是永久性的，后续新存入的资金会被视为新存款重新计算，无法恢复原存款被削减的保证基数。Incorrect. A partial withdrawal causes a permanent reduction in the original guarantee base; any funds redeposited later are treated as new deposits with new maturity clocks, incapable of restoring the reduced guarantee base.

  2. ✗ 错误。部分提取改变的是计算保证所依赖的“本金基数”，而合同约定的保证比例（如 75%）本身不变。Incorrect. A partial withdrawal alters the principal base on which the guarantee is calculated, whereas the contractual guarantee percentage (such as 75%) remains unchanged.

  3. ✓ 正确。存续期间部分提取（partial withdrawal）视同部分退保，会按比例永久性下调保证计算所依赖的本金基数；虽然 75% 的保证比例不变，但因为基数永久变小，未来的到期保证额和身故保证额都会相应下调。Correct. A partial withdrawal during the contract term is treated as a partial surrender, permanently reducing the principal base on a proportional basis; while the 75% guarantee ratio remains unchanged, the smaller base permanently lowers future dollar amounts for both maturity and death benefit guarantees.

  4. ✗ 错误。无论是口头指示还是书面指示，对保险公司均具有同等法律约束力，且提款都会按比例永久下调保证基数。Incorrect. Whether instructions are verbal or written, the transaction is legally binding and withdrawals always permanently reduce the guarantee base proportionally.

第 7 题Q7收入型附加保证与适当性流程Guaranteed lifetime income riders and suitability assessment sequence★★陷阱：到期保证与身故保证混淆

Evelyn, age 65, is retiring and fears outliving her savings if she lives past age 90 and market downturns deplete her portfolio. When her agent completes her investor profile first and then evaluates segregated fund features, which rider specifically transfers this longevity risk to the insurer by guaranteeing income for life even if the contract's market value drops to zero?

65 岁的 Evelyn 即将退休，她担心如果自己活过 90 岁且市场下跌耗尽投资组合，会出现“人活着、钱花光”的局面。当她的代理人先完成投资者画像、再评估分离基金功能时，哪一项附加保障能通过保证终身按比例提取（即使合同市值归零仍继续支付）将该长寿风险转移给保险公司？

  1. ✗ 错误。身故保证（death benefit guarantee）只在受保人身故时赔付给受益人，无法为在世的投资人提供终身源源不断的退休现金流；这是“到期保证与身故保证混淆”陷阱。Incorrect. The death benefit guarantee pays out to the beneficiary only upon the annuitant's death and cannot provide a continuous lifetime income stream to a living investor; this falls into the trap of confusing maturity guarantee with death benefit guarantee.

  2. ✗ 错误。前收费（front-end load, FEL）只是认购时扣除销售佣金的一种收费方式，既不能免除日常管理费率（MER），也不提供任何终身收入保证。Incorrect. A front-end load (FEL) is merely a sales charge deducted upon initial purchase; it neither eliminates the ongoing management expense ratio (MER) nor provides any guaranteed lifetime income.

  3. ✗ 错误。保证最低提取收益（Guaranteed Minimum Withdrawal Benefit, GMWB）保证的是在“约定期限内”每年至少提取某个金额，而将这一提取保证延伸到“终身”的是 GLWB。Incorrect. A Guaranteed Minimum Withdrawal Benefit (GMWB) guarantees a minimum annual withdrawal amount over a specified term, whereas a GLWB extends that withdrawal guarantee for life.

  4. ✓ 正确。保证终身提取收益（Guaranteed Lifetime Withdrawal Benefit, GLWB）是分离基金的收入型附加保证（通常需额外支付 rider fee），即使底层基金市值因提取和市场波动降至零，只要按约定比例提取，保险公司仍会继续支付至投资人身故，从而转移长寿风险；同时代理人先做投资者画像（investor profile）再匹配产品，符合公平对待客户原则（Fair Treatment of Customers, FTC）与适当性（suitability）顺序。Correct. A Guaranteed Lifetime Withdrawal Benefit (GLWB) rider is an income-oriented guarantee (typically requiring an additional rider fee) that continues paying scheduled withdrawals for life even if market volatility and withdrawals reduce market value to zero, transferring longevity risk to the insurer; moreover, completing the investor profile prior to recommending products complies with Fair Treatment of Customers (FTC) principles and suitability sequencing.

答案Answer：d

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An agent meets a prospective client who asks to buy a specific global equity segregated fund immediately. Under the Fair Treatment of Customers (FTC) principle and suitability rules, what is the correct sequence of steps the agent must follow, and how are underlying segregated fund investments categorized?

一位代理人会见潜在客户，客户要求立即购买某只特定的全球股权分离基金。根据公平对待客户原则（FTC）和适当性规则，代理人必须遵循的正确步骤顺序是什么？分离基金的底层投资资产是如何分类的？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据公平对待客户原则（Fair Treatment of Customers, FTC）与适当性（suitability）要求，申请环节必须先做投资者画像（investor profile，核实投资目标、风险承受能力与投资期限），第二步才是适配产品，顺序不能颠倒；按加拿大投资基金标准委员会（CIFSC）标准，底层投向分为现金、固定收益、股权、商品、其他五大类（考试口径再用约 14 类子基金作教学归类）。Correct. Under the Fair Treatment of Customers (FTC) principle and suitability requirements, the agent must complete the investor profile first (verifying investment objectives, risk tolerance, and time horizon) before matching products; under Canadian Investment Funds Standards Committee (CIFSC) standards, underlying investments are grouped into five asset classes: cash, fixed income, equity, commodities, and other (with the exam benchmark classifying them into approximately 14 sub-categories for instructional purposes).

  2. ✗ 错误。以佣金高低驱动推荐或先挑产品再倒填投资者画像，直接违反公平对待客户原则（FTC）与适当性义务。Incorrect. Recommending products based on commission levels or selecting a product first and backfilling the investor profile violates FTC principles and suitability obligations.

  3. ✗ 错误。分离基金在合同存续期间的日常市值同样可能大幅波动，中途赎回承担全部市场下跌风险，本金风险依然存在，因此绝不能省略投资者画像与适当性评估。Incorrect. Segregated fund market values fluctuate daily during the contract term, and early redemptions bear full market downside risk, meaning principal risk remains; therefore, the investor profile and suitability assessment can never be omitted.

  4. ✗ 错误。CIRO 监管的是共同基金与证券交易商，完全不管辖分离基金销售；这是“双口径数字与监管归属错位”陷阱。Incorrect. CIRO regulates mutual funds and investment dealers and has no jurisdiction over segregated fund sales; this falls into the trap of misaligning dual-benchmark figures with regulatory bodies.
