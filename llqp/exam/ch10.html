① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 情景净保额计算题：给出家庭月开支、政府遗属给付、房屋市值与按揭余额、既有团体险，考你用资本需求法（capital needs approach）算出最终建议新增保额，重点测验是否误用房价代替按揭余额、是否漏减既有保障。Scenario net coverage calculation questions: The question stem provides household monthly expenses, government survivor benefits, property market value and mortgage balance, and existing group insurance, testing your ability to calculate the recommended new insurance amount using the capital needs approach, focusing on whether you mistakenly use property market value instead of mortgage balance and whether you omit deducting existing coverage.
  • 收入替代法四步调整题：给出税后年收入、投资回报率、边际税率与通胀率，考你区分未调整、仅税后调整、仅通胀调整、税后加通胀双重调整四步的资本化价值（capitalized value）公式与结果差异。Income replacement approach four-step adjustment questions: The question stem provides annual after-tax income, investment rate of return, marginal tax rate, and inflation rate, testing your ability to distinguish the capitalized value formulas and result differences across four steps: unadjusted, after-tax adjustment only, inflation adjustment only, and dual adjustment for both taxes and inflation.
  • 资本保留法与资本耗尽法辨析题：给出有明确终止年限的抚养需求或终身配偶收入需求，考你判断何时该用资本耗尽法（capital drawdown method）、何时该用资本保留法（capital retention method），以及选错方法会导致保额如何失真。Capital retention versus capital drawdown discrimination questions: The question stem provides dependency needs with a definite end date or permanent spousal income needs, testing your ability to determine when to use the capital drawdown method versus the capital retention method, and how choosing the wrong method distorts the required coverage amount.
  • 家庭与企业复杂处境排查题：涉及再婚混合家庭的调解协议赡养费、全职照护配偶复工计划、独资与合伙及合格小型企业股份的视同处置（deemed disposition），考你六要素清单中哪些隐性负债必须计入、哪些不可变卖资产必须剔除。Complex family and business situation assessment questions: Involving mediation agreement support payments in blended families, return-to-work plans for caregiving spouses, and deemed disposition of sole proprietorships, partnerships, and qualified small business corporation (QSBC) shares, testing which hidden liabilities from the six-element checklist must be included and which non-liquid assets must be excluded.
  • 考试口径与现行规定双口径题：围绕加拿大退休金计划遗属给付（CPP survivor benefits）、终身资本利得豁免（lifetime capital gains exemption, LCGE）及团体险转换期，考你在考场按题干与考试口径作答、同时识别过度乐观代入最高额的陷阱。Exam benchmark versus current rule dual-benchmark questions: Centering on Canada Pension Plan (CPP) survivor benefits, the lifetime capital gains exemption (LCGE), and group life conversion windows, testing your ability to answer on the exam according to the question stem and exam benchmarks while identifying traps of overly optimistic assumptions using maximum amounts.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 用房价代替按揭余额Using property market value instead of mortgage balance

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到房产信息时直觉选用较大的房屋市值，忘了偿还房贷保住住房只需清偿未还本金，导致一次性资本需求被大幅高估。Candidates see real estate information and intuitively select the larger property market value, forgetting that retaining the home only requires paying off the outstanding mortgage principal balance, which drastically overestimates one-time capital needs.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现房屋市值（market value）与按揭余额（mortgage balance）两个数字。The question stem lists both property market value and mortgage balance figures.

⚠ 漏减可用既有保障Omitting deduction of available existing coverage

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生算出收入缺口资本化金额加上一次性资本需求后直接当作答案，忘了减去可赔付且用途匹配的既有团体险或个人险，或把受买卖协议锁定的企业保单误当成家庭可自由支配现金。Candidates calculate the capitalized income shortfall plus one-time capital needs and take it directly as the answer, forgetting to deduct eligible, matching existing group or individual insurance, or mistaking corporate policies locked under buy-sell agreements for discretionary family cash.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干末尾提到雇主提供的团体寿险（group life insurance）、既有个人保单或挂钩买卖协议的公司保单。The end of the question stem mentions employer-provided group life insurance, existing personal policies, or corporate policies tied to buy-sell agreements.

⚠ 不可变卖资产混入可用资产池Mixing illiquid non-saleable assets into the available asset pool

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把客户名下全部账面总资产直接从总需求中扣减，忽略了未亡配偶继续居住的自住房或家人计划保留继续经营的公司股权并不能变现救急。Candidates deduct the client's total book assets directly from total capital needs, overlooking that the family home where the surviving spouse continues to reside or corporate shares the family plans to retain and operate cannot be liquidated for cash.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现配偶将继续居住（continue living in）、家人计划保留经营（retain and continue operating）或传给子女等限制变现的措辞。The question stem includes wording restricting liquidation such as the spouse will continue living in, the family plans to retain and continue operating, or passing down to children.

⚠ 资本保留法与耗尽法混用Confusing the capital retention method with the capital drawdown method

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 对有明确终止年限的子女抚养或定期赡养义务误用本金永存的资本保留法，或在做收入替代法税后与通胀回报率调整时跳步、重复扣减。Mistakenly applying the capital retention method (which preserves principal indefinitely) to child support or fixed-term obligations with definite end dates, or skipping steps and double-deducting when adjusting after-tax and inflation returns in the income replacement approach.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干明确给出需要覆盖的具体年数（如10年、14年）或子女到达特定年龄的终点，或同时给出回报率、税率与通胀率三个百分比。The question stem specifies a definite number of years to cover (such as 10 or 14 years), a milestone child age, or provides rate of return, tax rate, and inflation rate percentages simultaneously.

⚠ 考场旧口径与现行规则混淆Confusing exam legacy benchmarks with current rules

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把政府遗属给付最高上限无条件套用于所有家庭，或混淆考试资料2022年快照数字与2026年现行标准，以及误以为所有企业或合伙权益都适用同一资本利得豁免规则。Unconditionally applying maximum government survivor benefit ceilings to all families, confusing 2022 exam snapshot figures with 2026 current standards, or mistakenly assuming all business or partnership interests qualify for the same capital gains exemption rules.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及CPP一次性身故给付、遗属与子女月给付、LCGE额度，或出现普通经营合伙（general operating partnership）与合格小型企业公司股份（QSBC shares）。The question stem involves the CPP lump-sum death benefit, monthly survivor and child benefits, or LCGE amounts, or mentions general operating partnerships versus qualified small business corporation (QSBC) shares.

资本保留法与资本耗尽法选择Choosing between capital retention and capital drawdown methodscapital retention method / capital drawdown method无明确终点的长期收入替代用资本保留法（本金永存只花利息）；有明确年限的需求用资本耗尽法（本金用尽即止，零实际净收益时等于年缺口×年数）。Long-term income replacement without a definite end date uses the capital retention method (preserving principal permanently and spending only interest); needs with a definite duration use the capital drawdown method (exhausting principal over time, which equals annual shortfall x number of years under a zero net real return assumption).

第 1 题Q1按揭余额与可用既有保障扣减Mortgage balance and deduction of available existing coverage★★陷阱：用房价代替按揭余额

Elena, age 39, is a single mother in North Vancouver raising her 8-year-old son. Her monthly take-home pay is $4,100, and if she dies, her son's monthly living expenses are projected to be $2,600 (excluding the mortgage payment, which would be paid off in full). The son would receive a monthly CPP children's benefit of $307.81 (assuming no resulting income tax), leaving a monthly income shortfall of $2,292.19, which her agent capitalizes over 10 years using the zero net real return drawdown method at approximately $275,000. Elena owns a home with a market value of $860,000 and a remaining mortgage balance of $410,000, estimates funeral expenses at $15,000, and has $100,000 of employer-provided group life insurance available to her estate. Using the capital needs approach, how much additional life insurance does Elena need?

39岁的Elena是北温哥华的一名单亲妈妈，独自抚养8岁的儿子。她的月税后实得收入为$4,100；若她身故，预计儿子的月生活开支为$2,600（已剔除拟一次性还清的按揭供款）。儿子每月可领$307.81的CPP子女给付（假定无由此产生的所得税），留下每月$2,292.19的收入缺口，代理人按零实际净收益的简化资本耗尽法覆盖10年，算出资本化金额约为$275,000。Elena名下房产市值为$860,000，按揭余额为$410,000，预计丧葬费为$15,000，且拥有雇主提供的$100,000可赔付团体寿险。按资本需求法计算，Elena还需要新增多少寿险保额？

  1. ✗ 错在落入「漏减可用既有保障」陷阱：把10年收入缺口资本化金额$275,000加上按揭余额$410,000和丧葬费$15,000得出总资本需求$700,000后，直接把总资本需求当成了最终建议保额，忘了减去既有团体寿险（existing coverage）$100,000。Incorrect. Falls into the trap of omitting deduction of available existing coverage: adding the 10-year capitalized income shortfall of $275,000 to the mortgage balance of $410,000 and funeral expenses of $15,000 yields total capital needs of $700,000, but treats total capital needs directly as the final recommended coverage, forgetting to deduct the $100,000 of existing group life insurance.

  2. ✓ 正确：按资本需求法（capital needs approach），先将收入缺口资本化金额$275,000加上一次性资本需求（按揭余额$410,000＋丧葬费$15,000＝$425,000）得出总需求$700,000，再减去可用的既有团体寿险$100,000，得出资本缺口（capital shortfall）即建议新增保额$600,000。Correct. Under the capital needs approach, first add the capitalized income shortfall of $275,000 to the one-time capital needs ($410,000 mortgage balance + $15,000 funeral expenses = $425,000) to find total capital needs of $700,000, then subtract the $100,000 of available existing group life insurance to arrive at the capital shortfall, which is the recommended new coverage of $600,000.

  3. ✗ 错在只计算了一次性资本需求（按揭余额$410,000＋丧葬费$15,000＝$425,000），既漏掉了10年收入缺口资本化金额$275,000，也未减去既有团体寿险$100,000。Incorrect. Calculates only the one-time capital needs ($410,000 mortgage balance + $15,000 funeral expenses = $425,000), omitting both the $275,000 10-year capitalized income shortfall and the deduction for $100,000 of existing group life insurance.

  4. ✗ 错在落入「用房价代替按揭余额」陷阱：直接把房屋市值$860,000当作保额需求，忽略了房贷净需求只需清偿按揭余额$410,000，且未按资本需求法（capital needs approach）合并收入缺口与扣减既有保障。Incorrect. Falls into the trap of using property market value instead of mortgage balance: uses the property's $860,000 market value directly as the coverage need, ignoring that mortgage repayment only requires clearing the $410,000 mortgage balance, and fails to incorporate the income shortfall or deduct existing coverage under the capital needs approach.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Maya, an administrative specialist in Richmond, wants to ensure her family's expenses are covered for 14 years until her child turns 18. After deducting approved CPP survivor and child benefits of $1,111.35 per month from projected post-death monthly expenses of $4,200, her monthly income shortfall is $3,088.65, which equals approximately $519,000 over 14 years under the simplified zero net real return drawdown method (compared with $2,155,000 under the capital retention method at 1.72%). She also has one-time capital needs of $20,000 for funeral costs and $8,000 to clear credit card debt, and she holds an eligible $150,000 employer group life insurance policy. What is Maya's recommended new life insurance coverage?

在列治文担任行政专员的Maya希望确保家人在未来14年（直至孩子年满18岁）的开支得到保障。从身故后预计月开支$4,200中扣除已核定的CPP遗属与子女月给付合计$1,111.35后，她的月收入缺口为$3,088.65；按零实际净收益的简化资本耗尽法覆盖14年，资本化金额约为$519,000（若按1.72%资本保留法则为$2,155,000）。她还有$20,000丧葬费和$8,000清偿信用卡债务的一次性资本需求，且名下有一份可赔付的$150,000雇主团体寿险。Maya的建议新增寿险保额是多少？

  1. ✗ 错在落入「漏减可用既有保障」及漏算一次性需求陷阱：$519,000仅是14年收入缺口的资本化金额，既未加上丧葬费$20,000和信用卡清偿$8,000，也未减去雇主提供的$150,000团体寿险（existing coverage）。Incorrect. Falls into the traps of omitting existing coverage and missing one-time capital needs: $519,000 is merely the 14-year capitalized income shortfall, which neither adds the $20,000 funeral costs and $8,000 credit card debt nor subtracts the $150,000 employer group life insurance.

  2. ✗ 错在混淆既有保障与建议新增保额：$150,000是Maya目前已有的雇主团体寿险保额，应当作为扣减项从总需求中减去，而非建议新增购买的保额。Incorrect. Confuses existing coverage with recommended new coverage: $150,000 is Maya's current employer group life insurance amount, which should be subtracted from total capital needs as a deduction rather than recommended as new coverage to purchase.

  3. ✗ 错在落入「资本保留法与耗尽法混用」陷阱：对有明确14年终止期限的子女抚养需求误用了本金永存的资本保留法（capital retention method）得出$2,155,000，且未计入一次性需求与既有团体险扣减。Incorrect. Falls into the trap of confusing capital retention with capital drawdown: mistakenly applies the capital retention method (which preserves principal indefinitely) to a 14-year child dependency need to get $2,155,000, and fails to factor in one-time capital needs or the deduction for existing group insurance.

Shares are deemed disposed of at fair market value at death, producing a capital gain of $650,000 - $10,000 = $640,000, of which 50% ($320,000) is a taxable capital gain; this can be offset against regular income tax by the capital gains deduction within the 2026 LCGE limit of $1,275,000, subject to alternative minimum tax (AMT).

答案Answer：d

第 6 题Q6身故时可用资产池的剔除规则Exclusion rules for assets available upon death★★陷阱：不可变卖资产混入可用资产池

Victor has total personal and corporate assets with a combined book and market value of $1,180,000. This total includes private corporation shares with a fair market value of $650,000 (and an adjusted cost base of $10,000) that his family intends to retain and continue operating after his death, as well as the family home in which his surviving spouse will continue living. When calculating Victor's net life insurance need under the capital needs approach, how should the $650,000 corporate shares and the family home be treated in determining 'assets available upon death'?

Victor名下个人与公司总资产合计为$1,180,000。其中包括公允市值为$650,000（调整后成本基数ACB为$10,000）、家人打算在他身故后保留并继续经营的私人公司股权，以及未亡配偶将继续居住的家庭自住房。按资本需求法计算Victor的净寿险需求时，在确定「身故时可用资产」时应如何处理这$650,000公司股权与家庭自住房？

  1. ✗ 错在落入「不可变卖资产混入可用资产池」陷阱：把账面总资产$1,180,000直接当成身故时可用资产（assets available upon death），忽略了配偶继续居住的自住房与家人保留继续经营的公司股权并不会变卖换取现金。Incorrect. Falls into the trap of mixing illiquid assets into the available asset pool: treats the total book value of $1,180,000 directly as assets available upon death, overlooking that the home where the spouse continues living and corporate shares the family retains to operate will not be liquidated for cash.

  2. ✓ 正确：身故时可用资产（assets available upon death）只计入真正愿意变卖并可用于遗产需求的资产；配偶将继续居住的自住房以及家人打算保留继续经营的$650,000公司股权均不应计入可变卖资产池，实际可用资产会远小于$1,180,000账面总值。Correct. Assets available upon death include only assets that the family is genuinely willing to liquidate for estate needs; the family home where the spouse will continue residing and the $650,000 in corporate shares retained to operate must be excluded from the liquid pool, making available assets far lower than the $1,180,000 book value.

  3. ✗ 错在混淆资本利得与可用现金：市值$650,000减去ACB $10,000得出的$640,000是用于测算视同处置（deemed disposition）税负的资本利得，绝不能把账面资本利得当成可用现金。Incorrect. Confuses capital gains with liquid cash: the $640,000 gain (market value $650,000 minus ACB $10,000) is used to calculate deemed disposition tax liabilities; paper capital gains can never be treated as liquid cash.

  4. ✗ 错在违背客户家庭保留经营的意愿：只要家人打算保留公司继续经营而非变卖，整笔$650,000公司股权都不能计入身故时可用资产（assets available upon death）池。Incorrect. Violates the family's intention to retain business operations: as long as the family plans to keep the corporation operating rather than sell it, the entire $650,000 share value cannot be included in assets available upon death.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

During a capital needs analysis, a client lists a family cottage that she wants passed intact to her children, a retirement account that her surviving spouse will continue holding for retirement, and a high-interest savings account earmarked for debt repayment at death. Which rule governs which of these assets reduce the total capital need?

在资本需求分析中，客户列出了一处希望原封不动传给子女的家族度假屋、一个未亡配偶将继续持有用于养老的退休金账户，以及一个专门留作身故清偿债务的高息储蓄账户。下列哪条规则决定了其中哪些资产可以扣减总资本需求？

  1. ✓ 正确：身故时可用资产（assets available upon death）必须严格区分总资产与可用于需求的资产——要传给子女的家族物业、配偶要继续持有的退休金账户即使账面价值再高也不计入可变卖资产池，只能扣减真正可用于清偿债务的储蓄账户。Correct. Assets available upon death must strictly distinguish total assets from assets genuinely available for estate needs: family property passed to children and retirement accounts retained by the spouse are excluded from liquid assets regardless of book value; only the savings account truly available to clear debt may be deducted.

  2. ✗ 错在落入「不可变卖资产混入可用资产池」陷阱：不仅把不可变卖的度假屋和配偶养老账户混入可用资产池，而且即便变卖资产也必须扣除债务、税费及交易成本后的净额而非毛市值。Incorrect. Falls into the trap of mixing illiquid assets into the available asset pool: mistakenly pools the retained cottage and spousal retirement account into available assets, and fails to realize that even liquidated assets must be net of debt, taxes, and transaction costs rather than gross market value.

  3. ✗ 错在完全颠倒可用资产规则：可随时动用清偿遗产债务的流动现金恰恰是身故时可用资产（assets available upon death），而保留不卖的物业与配偶继续持有的退休金账户才必须剔除。Incorrect. Completely reverses the asset availability rules: liquid cash readily available to settle debts is precisely what constitutes assets available upon death, whereas retained properties and ongoing retirement accounts must be excluded.

  4. ✗ 错在遗漏公式关键项：资本需求法的正确公式是建议保额＝总需求 − 身故时可用资产（assets available upon death）− 既有保障，可用资产与既有保障两项都要参与相减。Incorrect. Omits a vital component of the formula: the proper formula under the capital needs approach is recommended coverage = total capital needs - assets available upon death - existing coverage; both available assets and existing coverage are subtracted.

第 7 题Q7政府遗属给付考试口径与双口径区分Distinguishing government survivor benefits under exam benchmarks and dual standards★★★陷阱：考场旧口径与现行规则混淆

Owen, age 46, is married and has two young children. He has contributed to the Canada Pension Plan (CPP) throughout his career. When calculating Owen's family needs for an LLQP exam question that does not specify a newer statutory year, how should the CPP death benefit and monthly CPP survivor benefits be treated?

46岁的Owen已婚并育有两名幼子，职业生涯中一直向加拿大退休金计划（CPP）供款。在回答一道未指定新法年份的LLQP考题、计算Owen的家庭保障需求时，应当如何处理CPP一次性身故给付和CPP遗属月给付？

  1. ✗ 错在误用「考场旧口径与现行规则混淆」陷阱：即便是2025年起新增的$2,500附加额，也要求死者从未领取CPP/QPP退休或伤残给付且无合资格领取遗属养老金的配偶或同居伴侣——Owen已婚有合资格遗属，既不符合$5,000条件，更不能把月给付最高额无条件套用于所有人。Incorrect. Misapplies the dual-benchmark trap: even the $2,500 top-up introduced in 2025 requires that the deceased never received CPP/QPP retirement or disability benefits and leaves no surviving spouse or common-law partner eligible for a survivor's pension; Owen is married with an eligible survivor, so he does not qualify for $5,000, nor can maximum monthly benefits be applied unconditionally to everyone.

  2. ✗ 错在违背既有保障核对原则：CPP遗属给付（CPP survivor benefits）必须由遗属主动申请而非自动发放，且核定后的预计给付属于既有保障与身故后收入的一部分，必须在需求分析中计入。Incorrect. Violates coverage review principles: CPP survivor benefits must be formally applied for rather than paid automatically, and estimated approved benefits represent post-death income and existing coverage that must be included in the needs analysis.

  3. ✓ 正确：考场作答遵循考试口径，CPP一次性身故给付（CPP death benefit）基本额为$2,500，遗属与子女月给付按题干给出数字或考试资料快照作答，且需求分析应采用客户的预计核定给付而非无条件代入最高上限。注意考试资料对月给付使用2022年快照（65岁以下遗属$674.79、子女$264.53），而2026年现行最高月给付已调至$803.54与$307.81，且2025年起无合资格遗属等特定情形下身故给付最高可达$5,000。Correct. Exam answers follow exam benchmarks: the basic CPP lump-sum death benefit is $2,500, monthly survivor and child benefits follow the figures stated in the question stem or exam snapshot, and needs analysis uses estimated approved amounts rather than assuming the maximum ceiling. Note that while the exam uses 2022 snapshot figures ($674.79 for survivors under 65, $264.53 for children), 2026 maximums are $803.54 and $307.81, and starting in 2025 death benefits can reach up to $5,000 where there is no eligible survivor.

  4. ✗ 错在混淆工伤给付适用边界：WorkSafeBC的丧葬费用上限$11,704.51属于工伤致死给付（Workers' Compensation fatality benefits），仅限工伤或职业病致死才可赔付，不能替代一般疾病或非工伤身故的寿险需求。Incorrect. Confuses workers' compensation scope: WorkSafeBC's funeral benefit maximum of $11,704.51 is a workers' compensation fatality benefit payable only for work-related injuries or occupational diseases, and cannot substitute for CPP death benefits in non-occupational illness deaths.

Kendra is leaving her employer and wants to know how her group life insurance conversion privilege works when assessing her ongoing coverage needs in British Columbia. Which statement accurately explains the exam treatment and the actual regulatory rule in BC?

Kendra即将离职，在评估自己在BC省的持续保障需求时，她想了解团体寿险的离职转换权是如何运作的。下列哪项准确解释了考试口径与BC省的实际监管规则？
