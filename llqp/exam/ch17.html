本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 场景匹配题：给出独资、合伙或紧密控股公司面临的伤残冲击（固定开支断流、核心骨干失能、残疾合伙人退出），考你精准区分营业费用险（business overhead expense insurance, BOE）、关键人伤残险（key person disability insurance）和买卖协议伤残买断险（disability buyout insurance）的功能边界。Scenario-matching questions: Given the disability impacts faced by a sole proprietorship, partnership, or closely held corporation (disrupted fixed operating expenses, disability of a key employee, or exit of a disabled partner), test your ability to precisely distinguish the functional boundaries of business overhead expense (BOE) insurance, key person disability insurance, and buy-sell disability buyout insurance.
  • BOE可报销清单与结转计算题：给出一组企业月度账单（房租、员工薪资、业主个人薪水、商业贷款本金与利息、新设备采购）及月度封顶，考你剔除不可报销项并运用结转条款（carryover provision）计算当月实际报销额。BOE eligible expense filtering and carryover calculation questions: Given a set of monthly business expenses (rent, employee wages, owner's personal salary, commercial loan principal and interest, new equipment purchases) and a monthly benefit cap, test your ability to exclude ineligible items and apply the carryover provision to calculate the actual reimbursement for the month.
  • 税务处理正反对比题：将营业费用险（BOE）与商业贷款保护伤残险（business loan protection DI）并列，考你判断哪一种保费可抵税且给付应税、哪一种保费不可抵税但给付免税，以及两者能否就同一笔贷款重复理赔。Tax treatment contrast questions: Juxtaposing business overhead expense (BOE) insurance with business loan protection disability insurance, test your ability to determine which policy has tax-deductible premiums and taxable benefits versus non-deductible premiums and tax-free benefits, and whether both policies can claim against the same loan.
  • 伤残买断与年龄递减题：给出年满60岁后的合伙人长期完全失能场景，考你识别12至24个月长等待期、严格失能定义（regular occupation且完全不工作）以及60岁后保额每年递减20%对实际买断资金缺口的影响。Disability buyout and age reduction questions: Given a scenario involving a partner over age 60 who suffers a long-term total disability, test your ability to recognize the 12-to-24-month long waiting period, the strict disability definition (unable to perform regular occupation and not working at all), and the impact of a 20% annual benefit reduction after age 60 on the resulting buyout funding shortfall.
  • 企业附加福利与双口径辨析题：考你区分私人健康服务计划（Private Health Services Plan, PHSP）按自雇或雇员分支的扣除规则、雇员生命与健康信托（Employee Life and Health Trust, ELHT）对旧健康福利信托（Health and Welfare Trust, HWT）的制度过渡，以及考场1985年精算表数据与实务核保的区别。Corporate supplementary benefits and dual-perspective analysis questions: Test your ability to distinguish the Private Health Services Plan (PHSP) deduction rules across self-employed versus employee branches, the transition from legacy Health and Welfare Trusts (HWT) to Employee Life and Health Trusts (ELHT), and the distinction between the exam's cited 1985 actuarial table data and modern underwriting practices.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ BOE范围混淆陷阱BOE Eligible Expense Scope Confusion Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生容易把企业营业费用险（BOE）当成业主本人的收入替代工具，误将业主自己的薪水、贷款本金或新增设备资本支出算进可报销额度。Candidates often mistake business overhead expense (BOE) insurance for an owner's personal income replacement tool, erroneously including the owner's salary, loan principal repayments, or new capital equipment purchases in the reimbursable amount.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干账单中混入业主个人薪资（owner's salary）、商业贷款本金（loan principal）或新购设备（new equipment purchase）。The question stem mingles the owner's personal salary, commercial loan principal, or new equipment purchases into a list of monthly business expenses.

⚠ 税务正反颠倒陷阱Tax Treatment Reversal Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉认为所有企业伤残险税务规则一致，把营业费用险（BOE）和商业贷款保护伤残险（business loan protection DI）的保费抵扣与给付征税规则记反，或误以为同一笔贷款利息可双重报销。Candidates intuitively assume all business disability policies share identical tax rules, reversing the premium deduction and benefit taxation rules between business overhead expense (BOE) insurance and business loan protection DI, or mistakenly believing the same loan interest can be claimed twice.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现商业贷款保护险与BOE，询问保费可否抵税（tax-deductible）或理赔金是否免税（tax-free）。The question stem presents both business loan protection disability insurance and BOE, asking whether premiums are tax-deductible or benefits are tax-free.

⚠ 受益人与用途错配陷阱Beneficiary and Purpose Mismatch Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到核心员工受伤，误以为关键人伤残险（key person disability insurance）是赔给员工本人作生活费，或把关键人寿险的长期毛利贡献算法套用到短期（不超过12个月）的关键人伤残险上。Upon seeing a key employee disabled, candidates mistakenly assume key person disability insurance pays benefits directly to the employee as living expenses, or improperly apply key person life insurance's long-term gross profit contribution formula to short-term (not exceeding 12 months) key person disability coverage.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问关键人伤残险的受益人（beneficiary）是谁，或如何估算招聘与培训替代窗口期的保额。The question stem asks who the beneficiary of a key person disability policy is, or how to calculate the benefit amount needed for the replacement recruitment and training window.

⚠ 忽略60岁龄减陷阱Overlooking Post-Age-60 Reduction Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 习惯了人寿保险买断的固定保额思维，忽略伤残买断险（disability buyout insurance）在考试口径示例中60岁后每年递减20%的规则，直接按原始保额计算高龄合伙人的买断给付。Conditioned to the level benefit mindset of life insurance buyouts, candidates overlook the exam rule where disability buyout insurance benefits reduce by 20% per year after age 60, erroneously using the initial face amount to fund an older partner's buyout.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现60岁以上（如62岁）的合伙人触发买卖协议伤残买断条款，并给出初始保额。The question stem describes a partner over age 60 (such as age 62) triggering a buy-sell disability buyout provision and provides an initial policy face amount.

⚠ 失能定义与无资金空文陷阱Disability Definition and Unfunded Agreement Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误用宽松的本职职业（own occupation）定义判断买断险触发，或以为只要签好买卖协议（buy/sell agreement）无需配套保单就能自动完成股权交割。Candidates mistakenly apply the liberal own-occupation disability definition to trigger buyout coverage, or assume that merely signing a buy-sell agreement automatically guarantees share transfer liquidity without backing insurance policies.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述伤残合伙人转做兼职仍想触发买断，或两位合伙人仅签署法律协议却未安排保险资金。The question stem describes a disabled partner working part-time while seeking to trigger a buyout payout, or co-owners executing a legal agreement without arranging insurance funding.

第 1 题Q1企业三类伤残风险与产品精准匹配Precise matching of three business disability risks to corresponding products★陷阱：受益人与用途错配陷阱

Liam and Noah are equal co-owners of a commercial printing company that employs Elena, a master colour-calibration specialist whose unique skills generate 45% of the firm's contracts. Liam and Noah want insurance that will provide funds to recruit and train a temporary replacement if Elena suffers a disabling injury for eight months, and separate insurance to buy out Liam's or Noah's shares if either co-owner becomes permanently disabled for more than a year. Which combination of business disability policies addresses these two distinct exposures?

Liam和Noah是一家商业印刷公司的等额共同业主，公司雇佣了首席色彩校准专家Elena，她凭借独特技术为公司创造了45%的合同营收。Liam和Noah希望配置一份保险，以便在Elena因伤残失能八个月时提供资金招聘并培训临时替代人员；同时配置另一份保险，以便在任一共同业主永久失能超过一年时买下其所持股份。哪组企业伤残险组合能够分别覆盖这两类不同的风险敞口？

  1. ✓ 正确。针对核心员工Elena失能导致的营收下滑与招聘培训成本（loss of key employee），应由公司投保并作为受益人配置关键人伤残险（key person disability insurance）；针对共同业主长期失能需退出变现股份（inability to sell），应配置与买卖协议挂钩、等待期12–24个月的买卖协议伤残买断险（disability buyout insurance）。Correct. For the revenue decline and recruitment/training costs resulting from the disability of key employee Elena (loss of key employee), the company should own and be the beneficiary of key person disability insurance; for a co-owner's long-term disability requiring exit and share monetization (inability to sell), buy-sell disability buyout insurance tied to a buy-sell agreement with an elimination period of 12 to 24 months should be arranged.

  2. ✗ 错误（产品功能错配陷阱）。营业费用险（BOE）要求被保险人是企业业主本人而非非股东雇员，且不报销被保险人自己的收入损失；关键人伤残险给付期通常不超过12个月，用于弥补短期经营冲击，不能替代长期等待期的伤残买断险来收购股份。Incorrect. Falls into the Product Functional Mismatch trap: Business overhead expense (BOE) insurance requires the insured life to be the business owner rather than a non-shareholder employee, and it does not reimburse the insured's own lost income; furthermore, key person disability insurance has a benefit period generally not exceeding 12 months to absorb short-term operational shocks, and cannot replace disability buyout insurance with its extended elimination period to purchase shares.

  3. ✗ 错误（受益人与用途错配陷阱）。Elena只是雇员而非持股合伙人，不适用买卖协议伤残买断险；同時BOE仅按月报销房租、水电、员工工资等固定运营开支，绝不能用于股权收购。Incorrect. Falls into the Beneficiary and Purpose Mismatch trap: Elena is merely an employee rather than an equity partner, so disability buyout insurance does not apply to her; simultaneously, BOE only reimburses monthly fixed operating expenses such as rent, utilities, and staff salaries, and can never be used for share redemption or equity purchase.

  4. ✗ 错误（福利工具混淆陷阱）。私人健康服务计划（PHSP）只报销牙科、处方药等医疗费用；商业贷款保护伤残险（business loan protection DI）专款用于偿还合格商业贷款本息，均不能解决关键人替代或股权买断问题。Incorrect. Falls into the Benefit Tool Confusion trap: A Private Health Services Plan (PHSP) only reimburses health expenses such as dental and prescription drugs; business loan protection disability insurance is dedicated solely to servicing eligible commercial loan principal and interest, and neither addresses key person replacement or equity buyouts.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Hannah is the sole owner of an accounting practice with three salaried bookkeepers, and her top tax manager, Lucas (who holds 0% equity), brings in most of the complex corporate audit engagements. If Hannah wants coverage that pays the office lease and bookkeepers' salaries while she herself is disabled for four months, plus coverage that compensates the firm for lost revenue if Lucas is disabled for six months, what policies should the practice purchase?

Hannah是一家雇有三名领薪簿记员的会计师事务所的唯一业主，而她麾下不持股的首席税务经理Lucas负责带来绝大部分复杂的企业审计业务。如果Hannah希望配置一份在她本人失能四个月期间用来支付办公室房租和簿记员工资的保险，再加上一份在Lucas失能六个月期间用来补偿事务所营收损失的保险，事务所应当购买哪两份保单？

  1. ✗ 错误（产品功能错配陷阱）。Hannah是唯一业主，短期失能需付房租和员工工资属于丧失工作能力（inability to work）而非股权买断；Lucas是不持股雇员，不能作为BOE的被保险业主。Incorrect. Falls into the Product Functional Mismatch trap: Hannah is the sole owner, and needing to cover office rent and employee salaries during short-term disability represents an inability-to-work exposure rather than an equity buyout; Lucas is a non-shareholder employee and cannot be the insured business owner under a BOE policy.

  2. ✗ 错误（范围混淆陷阱）。商业贷款保护伤残险仅用于偿还合格商业贷款本息，不报销办公室房租和簿记员工资；PHSP只报销医疗账单，不补偿核心员工失能造成的营收下滑。Incorrect.   3. ✗ 错误（等待期起算日陷阱）。案例二明确指出，等待期必须从满足保单伤残定义并持续失能之日起按条款计算，不能直接用慢性病或基础疾病的确诊日起算。Incorrect. Falls into the Waiting Period Start Date trap: The waiting period must be calculated under policy terms from the date the insured satisfies the disability definition and remains continuously disabled, not retroactively from the initial diagnosis date of an underlying chronic condition.

  4. ✓ 正确。根据讲座2.4节与案例二，买卖协议伤残买断险（disability buyout insurance）在考试口径示例中60岁后保额每年递减20%。Arthur在62岁触发买断时，可用保额已经从原始$800,000打了显著折扣（按每年20%递减两年后远低于$800,000股权价值），产生买断资金缺口，这正是为什么买卖协议与保额必须定期复核。Correct. Buy-sell disability buyout insurance in exam examples reduces coverage by 20% per year after age 60. When Arthur triggers the buyout at age 62, available coverage is significantly discounted from the original $800,000 face amount (reducing by 20% per year for two years, leaving coverage far below the $800,000 buyout valuation), creating a funding shortfall that illustrates why buy-sell agreements and coverage amounts must be reviewed regularly.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Two CPA partners, Evelyn and Grace, hold disability buyout policies with a 360-day waiting period and the exam's post-age-60 reduction rule (20% per year after age 60). Evelyn is diagnosed with a progressive neurological condition at age 60, continues working full-time until she finally stops working and meets the policy's disability definition at age 61, and completes the 360-day waiting period at age 62. Meanwhile, her partner Grace asks whether Evelyn could still trigger the buyout if Evelyn worked part-time as a paid university lecturer during recovery. Which statement is correct?

两位注册会计师合伙人Evelyn和Grace持有等待期360天且含考试口径60岁后递减规则（60岁后每年递减20%）的伤残买断保单。Evelyn在60岁时确诊进行性神经系统疾病，但继续全职工作，直到61岁才彻底停止工作并满足保单的失能定义，随后在62岁完成360天等待期。与此同时，合伙人Grace询问：如果Evelyn在康复期间兼职担任有薪大学讲师，是否仍能触发买断。哪项陈述是正确的？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据案例二与2.4节，伤残买断险（disability buyout insurance）的等待期从满足保单失能定义并持续失能之日（61岁停工时）起算，绝非60岁确诊日；60岁后保额按考试口径每年递减20%打折；且其定义为“常规职业（regular occupation）且完全没有工作”，若兼职担任有薪讲师则不满足“完全没有工作”的复合门槛。Correct. The waiting period for disability buyout insurance commences from the date the insured satisfies the policy disability definition and ceases working (at age 61), never from the age-60 diagnosis date; after age 60, coverage reduces by 20% per year under exam examples; and because the definition requires being unable to perform regular occupation duties and not working at all, working part-time as a paid lecturer violates the zero-work composite threshold.

  2. ✗ 错误（等待期起算日与忽略龄减陷阱）。还在全职工作时未满足失能定义，不能从确诊日起算等待期；且60岁后保额已开始递减。Incorrect. Falls into the Waiting Period Start Date and Overlooking Reduction trap: Working full-time fails the disability definition, so the waiting period cannot start from the diagnosis date; furthermore, benefit reductions begin after age 60.

  3. ✗ 错误（失能定义级别混淆陷阱）。伤残买断险比一般个人DI的纯own occupation更严，要求“regular occupation且完全没有工作”，从事其他有薪工作不触发理赔。Incorrect. Falls into the Disability Definition Level Confusion trap: Disability buyout coverage is stricter than pure own-occupation personal DI, requiring inability to perform one's regular occupation and not working at all, so engaging in other paid work prevents the buyout payout from triggering.

  4. ✗ 错误（分期给付机制误解陷阱）。案例二指出，保单选择60期等额月付是为了避免一次性抽调大额现金，由买卖协议衔接保险金收款人与向退出方分期付款的安排，不等于另一位合伙人必须以自有资金独担五年付款。Incorrect. Falls into the Instalment Payout Mechanism Misunderstanding trap: Selecting 60 equal monthly instalments avoids draining massive lump-sum cash, using the buy-sell agreement to coordinate the recipient of insurance proceeds and instalment payouts to the exiting partner; it does not mean the surviving partner must shoulder five years of payments out of personal pocket.

第 6 题Q61985年精算表双口径与关键人持股区间Dual perspective on 1985 actuarial table data and key person shareholding ranges★陷阱：把区间当定值陷阱

An LLQP candidate is studying the disability risk statistics cited in the exam reference manual for 45-year-old business owners before age 65. According to the exam wording (based on the 1985 Commissioner's Disability Table), what are the approximate probabilities of a disability lasting 90 days or more for a single 45-year-old owner versus at least one of two 45-year-old co-owners, and what is the average duration?

一位LLQP考生正在复习考试参考资料中引用的45岁企业主在65岁前的伤残风险统计数据。按考试口径（基于1985年Commissioner's Disability Table），单一45岁业主对比两位45岁共同业主中至少一人发生持续90天以上伤残的概率分别约为多少，平均持续时间又是多久？

  1. ✗ 错误（数字记忆错误陷阱）。这组数字远低于考试口径引述的1985年精算表数据（单人约40%、双人至少一人约62.5%、平均持续3.2年）。Incorrect. Falls into the Statistics Memory Error trap: These figures are far lower than the 1985 actuarial table data cited in exam materials (approximately 40% for a single owner, approximately 62.5% for at least one of two co-owners, and an average duration of 3.2 years).

  2. ✓ 正确。考试口径引述1985年Commissioner's Disability Table（disability risk statistics）：45岁业主到65岁前发生90天以上较长期伤残的概率约为40%，两位共同业主中至少一人发生伤残的概率约为62.5%，平均持续时间为3.2年。（注：考试按此考试资料历史数字作答，但现行加拿大消费者等效统计未核实，实务中不能将这组百分比作为个案概率承诺。）Correct. Under exam conventions citing the 1985 Commissioner's Disability Table: the probability of a 45-year-old owner experiencing a disability lasting longer than 90 days before age 65 is approximately 40%, the probability for at least one of two co-owners is approximately 62.5%, and the average duration is 3.2 years. (Note: Candidates must answer using these historical exam figures, but because modern Canadian consumer equivalents are unverified, they must never be presented as individual probability commitments in practice.)

  3. ✗ 错误（单双人概率错位陷阱）。62.5%是两位共同业主中至少一人发生伤残的概率，单一业主的概率在考试口径中约为40%，平均持续时间为3.2年而非5.0年。Incorrect. Falls into the Single vs. Co-Owner Probability Inversion trap: 62.5% represents the probability that at least one of two co-owners suffers a disability, while the probability for a single owner under exam conventions is approximately 40%, with an average duration of 3.2 years rather than 5.0 years.

  4. ✗ 错误（混淆龄减比例与伤残概率陷阱）。20%是伤残买断险在60岁后每年的保额递减比例示例，而非45岁业主的伤残发生概率。Incorrect. Falls into the Confusing Age Reduction with Disability Probability trap: 20% represents the annual coverage reduction percentage after age 60 in buyout insurance examples, not the disability probability for a 45-year-old business owner.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Two 45-year-old business partners ask their advisor how likely it is that at least one of them will suffer a disability lasting 90 days or longer before age 65, and how long such a disability lasts on average according to the LLQP exam reference material. Which figures reflect the exam's cited 1985 table?

两位45岁的商业合伙人询问顾问，根据LLQP考试参考资料，他们在65岁前至少有一人发生持续90天或以上伤残的可能性有多大，且此类伤残平均持续多久。哪组数字反映了考试引用的1985年统计表？

  2. ✗ 错误（数字混淆陷阱）。50%和1年（12个月）分别是关键人伤残险持股上限区间高端与最长给付期，不是1985年表的双人伤残概率与持续期。Incorrect.   3. ✗ 错误（数字混淆陷阱）。20%是买断险60岁后年递减率，60个月是现行买断险分期月付期数，均非1985年表统计值。Incorrect.   4. ✓ 正确。根据考试口径引用的1985年精算表（1985 Commissioner's Disability Table），两位45岁共同业主在65岁前至少一人发生90天以上伤残的概率约为62.5%（单一业主约为40%），平均持续时间为3.2年。（注：考场按此考试资料数字作答，实务柜台仅用其定性结论而不用作现行个案概率承诺。）Correct. According to the 1985 Commissioner's Disability Table cited in exam materials, the probability that at least one of two 45-year-old co-owners suffers a disability lasting over 90 days before age 65 is approximately 62.5% (approximately 40% for a single owner), with an average duration of 3.2 years. (Note: Candidates must answer using these exam figures, while practitioners use only the qualitative conclusion rather than making individual probability commitments.)

第 7 题Q7拼团式个人险、PHSP与ELHT综合辨析Comprehensive analysis of grouped individual plans, PHSPs, and ELHTs★★陷阱：BOE范围混淆陷阱

A growing software firm with 18 employees wants to provide disability and health benefits specifically for its four senior vice-presidents without purchasing a traditional group insurance policy, and also wants to understand the difference between a grouped individual disability plan, a Private Health Services Plan (PHSP), and an Employee Life and Health Trust (ELHT). Which statement accurately describes these arrangements?

一家拥有18名员工的成长型软件公司希望专门为其四位高级副总裁提供伤残与健康福利，而不购买传统的团体保险保单；同时希望厘清“拼团式”个人伤残险计划、私人健康服务计划（PHSP）以及雇员生命与健康信托（ELHT）之间的区别。哪项陈述准确描述了这些安排？

  1. ✓ 正确。根据2.5节，“拼团式”个人险计划（grouped disability/CI plan）是指雇主替某一特定类别（defined class，如全体高级副总裁，而非任意挑选个别人）的多名员工统一购买个人DI或CI保单；而私人健康服务计划（Private Health Services Plan, PHSP）报销的是牙科、视力、处方药等合资格医疗支出，既不是收入替代也不是企业营业费用。Correct. A grouped disability/CI plan refers to an employer purchasing individual DI or CI policies for multiple employees belonging to a defined class (such as all senior vice-presidents, rather than arbitrarily picking individual favourites); meanwhile, a Private Health Services Plan (PHSP) reimburses eligible medical expenses such as dental, vision, and prescription drugs, functioning neither as income replacement nor as business overhead.

  2. ✗ 错误（违反按类别承保规则陷阱）。“拼团式”个人险计划（grouped disability/CI plan）要求雇主针对“某一类别”（class of employees）的员工统一采购，不能任意挑选个别人。Incorrect. Falls into the Violating Defined Class Underwriting Rules trap: A grouped disability/CI plan requires the employer to purchase coverage for an objective class of employees, and cannot be used to arbitrarily pick and choose specific individuals.

  3. ✗ 错误（混淆PHSP与BOE陷阱）。报销办公室租金和水电费的是营业费用险（BOE），PHSP仅报销医疗开支，两者不能互相替代。Incorrect. Falls into the Confusing PHSP with BOE trap: Reimbursing office lease payments and utility bills is the role of business overhead expense (BOE) insurance; a PHSP only reimburses health expenses, and the two cannot substitute for each other.

  4. ✗ 错误（夸大HWT过渡后果陷阱）。CRA自2021年底停止适用HWT旧行政规则，未转换的信托一般按普通信托规则纳税，并非被政府没收或宣布违法强制解散。Incorrect. Falls into the Exaggerating HWT Transition Consequences trap: The CRA discontinued legacy administrative rules for Health and Welfare Trusts (HWT) after 2021; unconverted trusts are generally taxed under ordinary trust rules, rather than being seized by the government or declared illegal and forcibly dissolved.

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An engineering firm is reviewing its employee benefit structures: it wants to offer individual critical illness and disability policies to its entire class of licensed project managers, maintain a Private Health Services Plan (PHSP) for dental and prescription drug bills, and check the tax status of an Employee Life and Health Trust (ELHT). Which statement is correct under the lecture's rules?

一家工程公司正在检视其员工福利结构：公司希望向“全体持牌项目经理”这一类别的员工统一提供个人重疾与伤残保单，保留一份用于报销牙科与处方药账单的私人健康服务计划（PHSP），并核查雇员生命与健康信托（ELHT）的税务地位。根据讲座规则，哪项陈述是正确的？

Falls into the Confusing PHSP with DI/BOE Functions trap: A Private Health Services Plan (PHSP) reimburses only routine medical expenses; it cannot replace personal DI to pay salaries, nor can it replace BOE to reimburse loan interest.

  2. ✗ 错误（混淆自雇特定扣除分支与全行业统一供款上限陷阱）。$1,500成人/$750未满18岁是联邦《所得税法》第20.01条下特定自雇分支按期间折算的扣除限制（deduction limit），不是所有PHSP的统一供款上限（contribution cap）。Incorrect. Falls into the Confusing Self-Employed Deduction Limits with Universal Contribution Caps trap: The $1,500 per adult and $750 per child under age 18 limits represent period-prorated deduction limits under Income Tax Act s.20.01 for specific self-employed categories, not a universal contribution cap across all PHSPs.

  3. ✓ 正确。雇主为某一类别员工统一购买个人DI/CI保单属于“拼团式”个人险计划（grouped disability/CI plan）；私人健康服务计划（PHSP）用于报销省医疗保险未覆盖的牙科、视力、处方药等医疗支出；雇员生命与健康信托（ELHT）适用联邦《所得税法》第144.1条（ITA s.144.1），承接了2021年底终止旧行政待遇的健康福利信托（HWT）过渡。Correct. An employer purchasing individual DI/CI policies for a defined class of employees constitutes a grouped disability/CI plan; a Private Health Services Plan (PHSP) reimburses medical expenses outside provincial coverage such as dental, vision, and prescription drugs; and Employee Life and Health Trusts (ELHT) fall under Income Tax Act s.144.1, accommodating the transition from Health and Welfare Trusts (HWT) whose legacy administrative regime ended after 2021.

  4. ✗ 错误（混淆拼团式个人险与传统团体险陷阱）。“拼团式”个人险计划（grouped disability/CI plan）采购的是多份独立的个人DI/CI保单，而非保险公司的传统团体险总保单（group policy）。Incorrect. Falls into the Confusing Grouped Individual Plans with Traditional Group Policies trap: A grouped disability/CI plan procures multiple independent individual DI/CI policies rather than an insurer's traditional group master policy.
