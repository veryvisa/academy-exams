本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 预算受限时的保费调整杠杆题：给出客户觉得保费过高的情境，考延长等待期、缩短给付期、降低保额、改换续保性质四个杠杆各自牺牲什么保障，以及为什么改换为可撤销续保代价最大。Premium adjustment levers under budget constraints questions: Given a scenario where a client finds premiums unaffordable, test what coverage is sacrificed under each of the four levers (extending the elimination period, shortening the benefit period, reducing the benefit amount, or changing the renewability tier), and why switching to a cancellable policy carries the highest cost.
  • 法定条款时限辨析题：在理赔场景中混排 30 天书面通知、90 天提交理赔证明、考试口径 6 个月（BC 现行法 1 年）迟延豁免上限，测试能否区分通知期与交证期以及客观无法按期报案时的救济规则。Statutory condition time limits discrimination questions: In a claims scenario mixing the 30-day written notice, 90-day proof of claim, and exam-benchmark 6-month (BC current statute 1-year) late notice relief limit, test whether candidates can distinguish notice from proof deadlines and apply relief rules when timely reporting is objectively impossible.
  • 续保等级对比题：给出不可撤销、保证续保、可撤销或有条件续保合同，测试保司在服务期内能否单方终止合同、能否针对个人或按同一费率分类整批上调保费，以及终止通知的法定天数（15 天挂号信或 5 天亲送）。Renewability tiers comparison questions: Given non-cancellable, guaranteed renewable, cancellable, or conditionally renewable contracts, test whether an insurer can unilaterally terminate the policy during the service period, whether it can adjust premiums individually or en masse by rate class, and the statutory notice period for termination (15 days by registered mail or 5 days personal delivery).
  • 跨险种规则防混题：在保单交付未付首期保费、保单失效复效、职业变更至高风险工种、不可撤销受益人指定等场景中，测试能否避开人寿险规则干扰，按 A&S 合同的法定条款（如比例折算给付、已交付即约束保司）作答。Cross-product line boundary questions: In scenarios involving delivery without initial premium payment, lapse and reinstatement, change of occupation to a higher-risk role, or irrevocable beneficiary designation, test whether candidates can avoid conflation with life insurance rules and correctly apply accident and sickness statutory conditions (such as pro-rata benefit adjustments and the binding nature of delivery upon the insurer).
  • 理赔给付节奏与团体转换实务题：考收到理赔证明后误工首笔给付（30 天内）与持续给付（至少每 60 天一次）的法定时限，以及离职时团体 A&S 转个人险须核对具体计划文件而非盲目套用寿险惯例。Claims payment schedule and group conversion practical questions: Test statutory deadlines for the initial loss-of-income payment (within 30 days) and ongoing payments (at least every 60 days) after receiving proof of claim, and the requirement to verify specific plan documents when converting group A&S to individual coverage upon employment termination rather than blindly applying group life conventions.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 理赔三时限张冠李戴Confusing the Three Claim Time Limits Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常把 30 天书面通知期、90 天提交理赔证明期混为一谈，或误以为超过 30 天通知期就无条件丧失理赔权，忽略迟延豁免条款及双口径差异（考试口径最长 6 个月 vs BC 法条 1 年）。Candidates frequently conflate the 30-day written notice of claim with the 90-day proof of claim deadline, or assume that missing the 30-day notice window results in automatic claim forfeiture, ignoring late notice relief provisions and dual benchmark differences (exam benchmark of up to 6 months versus BC statutory limit of 1 year).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现事故或伤残发生后的天数（如第 45 天）、住院无法理事、提交收据或医疗证明的期限。The question stem mentions days elapsed following an accident or disability (e.g., day 45), hospitalization preventing management of affairs, or deadlines for submitting receipts or medical proof.

⚠ 保证续保等同不可撤销Equating Guaranteed Renewable with Non-Cancellable Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到“保证续保（guaranteed renewable）”就误以为保费永远锁定不变，忽略保司虽不能单方终止或对个人单独涨价，但仍可按同一费率分类整批调整保费。Candidates see guaranteed renewable and assume premiums are locked indefinitely, overlooking that while the insurer cannot unilaterally cancel the contract or single out an individual for an increase, it retains the right to adjust premiums en masse for an entire rate class.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问保司在服务期内能否调整保费、能否拒绝续保，或对比 non-cancellable 与 guaranteed renewable 的区别。The question stem asks whether the insurer can adjust premiums or refuse renewal during the service period, or contrasts non-cancellable with guaranteed renewable provisions.

⚠ 职业变更高风险即拒赔Assuming Claim Denial for High-Risk Change of Occupation Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉认为投保后换了高危职业且未及时通知保司属于违约，出险会被直接拒赔或合同作废，不知道法定条款规定按原保费在新职业费率下能买到的保额比例折算。Candidates instinctively assume that switching to a high-risk occupation without notifying the insurer constitutes a breach leading to outright claim denial or policy voidance, unaware that statutory conditions require a pro-rata benefit reduction based on the coverage the original premium would have purchased under the new rate classification.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述被保险人投保后转入更高风险职业（change of occupation）并在新职业工作或生活中出险。The question stem describes an insured switching to a higher-risk occupation (change of occupation) after policy issuance and suffering a loss during work or everyday life.

⚠ 跨险种套用寿险生效与复效规则Transposing Life Insurance Issue and Reinstatement Rules Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把人寿险“交付+付首期保费+可保性无变化”才生效的规则，或人寿险法定复效窗口（30 天/2 年）直接搬到个人 A&S 合同上，忽略 A&S 已交付保单即使首期保费未付也约束保司，且 A&S 复效条件由合同约定。Candidates mistakenly transpose life insurance rules (requiring delivery, initial premium payment, and unchanged insurability for policy formation, or uniform statutory reinstatement windows of 30 days or 2 years) onto individual accident and sickness contracts, overlooking that a delivered A&S policy binds the insurer even if the initial premium is unpaid, and that A&S reinstatement conditions are governed by contract terms.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 A&S 保单已交付但首期保费未付、交付前健康变化，或询问 A&S 保单断缴后的复效期限。The question stem presents an A&S policy delivered without initial premium payment, a change in health prior to delivery, or asks for the reinstatement deadline following policy lapse.

⚠ 理赔须等结案才首付或盲套转换天数Expecting Deferred First Claim Payment or Blindly Applying Group Conversion Days Trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为长期伤残理赔要等伤残结束或调查彻底完结才付第一笔钱（实际交证后 30 天内首付、此后至少每 60 天续付），或在团体 A&S 转个人险时盲目背诵单一转换天数而不看具体计划条款。Candidates incorrectly believe that long-term disability claims require the claim investigation to be completely closed or disability to end before the first payment is made (whereas statute requires initial payment within 30 days of proof and ongoing payments at least every 60 days), or blindly memorize a single conversion window without verifying specific group plan provisions.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及长期伤残给付的付款节奏（payment of claims），或员工离职申请将团体 A&S 转为个人保单的窗口期。The question stem involves the claims payment schedule for long-term disability, or an employee applying to convert group A&S coverage to an individual policy upon termination.

第 1 题Q1理赔通知与交证时限及迟报豁免Notice and proof of claim time limits and late filing relief★★陷阱：理赔三时限张冠李戴

Helena, a freelance graphic designer, was severely injured in an accident and remained hospitalized and unable to manage her affairs until day 45, when she immediately sent written notice of claim to her individual disability insurer. Based on the standard exam rules for statutory conditions, how is her claim affected?

自由平面设计师 Helena 在一次意外中受重伤，住院期间无法处理个人事务，直到第 45 天恢复能力后立即向个人伤残险保司发送了书面理赔通知。根据法定条款的考试标准口径，她的索赔会受到什么影响？

  1. ✓ 正确。依 s.101 Condition 5，理赔通知（notice of claim [理赔通知]）法定时限为 30 天，理赔交证（proof of claim [理赔交证]）为 90 天；若因客观原因在 30 天内报案不合理可行且之后尽快补办，适用迟延豁免（late notice relief [迟延豁免]），不会直接丧失理赔权。注意双口径差异：考试口径写迟报上限“最长不超 6 个月”，而 BC 现行 s.101 Condition 5 法条规定最迟不超过 1 年，第 45 天在两个口径下均未超限。Correct. Under section 101 Condition 5, the statutory deadline for notice of claim is 30 days and for proof of claim is 90 days; if reporting within 30 days is not reasonably possible due to objective circumstances and notice is provided as soon as reasonably possible thereafter, late notice relief applies and the claim is not invalidated. Note the dual benchmark distinction: the exam benchmark caps late relief at no more than 6 months, whereas current BC statutory Condition 5 sets the ceiling at no later than 1 year; day 45 falls safely within both standards.

  2. ✗ 落入“理赔三时限张冠李戴”陷阱，误把 30 天理赔通知（notice of claim [理赔通知]）当成过期即失权的绝对红线，忽略了 s.101 Condition 5 的迟延豁免（late notice relief [迟延豁免]）条款。Incorrect. Falls into the Confusing the Three Claim Time Limits trap: Mistakenly treats the 30-day notice of claim deadline as an absolute bar causing automatic forfeiture, overlooking the late notice relief provisions of section 101 Condition 5.

  3. ✗ 落入“理赔三时限张冠李戴”陷阱：将 30 天理赔通知期与 90 天理赔交证（proof of claim [理赔交证]）期限混为一谈，且两项时限均从索赔产生日起算，不能叠加计算。Incorrect. Falls into the Confusing the Three Claim Time Limits trap: Conflates the 30-day notice of claim window with the 90-day proof of claim period; both statutory deadlines run concurrently from the date the claim arose and cannot be stacked sequentially.

  4. ✗ 张冠李戴：按比例折算给付是职业变更条款（change of occupation clause [职业变更条款]，Condition 3）针对转入高风险职业的处理机制，与迟延报案毫无关系。Incorrect. Misattributes rules: A pro-rata benefit reduction is the remedy under the change of occupation clause (Condition 3) when an insured switches to a higher-risk occupation, and has no application to late notice of claim.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Marcus developed a disabling illness and submitted written notice of claim to his individual disability insurer on day 15. By what statutory deadline from the date the claim arose must he submit written proof of claim, and what is the exam rule if submitting on time is not reasonably possible?

Marcus 患上致残疾病，并在第 15 天向其个人伤残险保司提交了书面理赔通知。自索赔产生之日起，他必须在哪个法定期限内提交书面理赔证明？如果按期提交在客观上不合理可行，考试规则怎么规定？

  1. ✗ 落入“理赔三时限张冠李戴”陷阱：30 天是理赔通知（notice of claim [理赔通知]）时限，不是理赔交证（proof of claim [理赔交证]）时限，且并非逾期即绝对无效。Incorrect. Falls into the Confusing the Three Claim Time Limits trap: 30 days is the deadline for notice of claim rather than proof of claim, and missing the deadline does not cause absolute forfeiture when relief applies.

  2. ✗ 数字混淆：60 天是持续伤残给付的续付间隔或非误工保险金的付款时限（payment of claims [理赔付款期限]），而非提交理赔证明的 90 天期限。Incorrect. Conflates numbers: 60 days represents the ongoing claims payout frequency or non-income benefit payment window (payment of claims), not the 90-day proof of claim filing deadline.

  3. ✓ 正确。依 s.101 Condition 5(1)(b)，书面理赔交证（proof of claim [理赔交证]）须在索赔发生后 90 天内提交（与 30 天通知期均从索赔产生日起算）；若客观上不合理可行且尽快补办，适用迟延豁免（late notice relief [迟延豁免]）。考试口径写上限最长不超 6 个月，而 BC 现行法条为最迟不超过 1 年。Correct. Under section 101 Condition 5(1)(b), written proof of claim must be submitted within 90 days from the date the claim arose (running concurrently with the 30-day notice period); if doing so on time is not reasonably possible and proof is furnished as soon as reasonably possible, late notice relief applies. The exam benchmark caps this relief at up to 6 months, whereas current BC legislation provides up to 1 year.

  4. ✗ 混淆了保司义务与客户义务：15 天是保司收到通知后提供交证表格的期限（Condition 6）或挂号信终止合同的通知期（Condition 4），不是客户交证的期限。Incorrect. Conflates insurer obligations with insured duties: 15 days is the insurer's deadline to supply claim forms after receiving notice (Condition 6) or the registered mail notice window for policy termination (Condition 4), not the insured's proof deadline.

第 2 题Q2职业变更条款的比例折算机制Change of occupation clause and pro-rata benefit reduction★★陷阱：职业变更高风险即拒赔

When Victor purchased his individual disability insurance policy, he worked as an office administrator. Two years later, he switched to a higher-risk occupation as a commercial roofer without notifying the insurer, and he was subsequently injured on the job. Elena 漏缴了一笔续期保费，且在 30 天法定宽限期届满后，她的个人伤残保单失效。她询问代理人，按法律规定她必须在多长期限内将意外与疾病保单复效。正确的规则是什么？

  1. ✗ 落入“跨险种套用寿险生效与复效规则”陷阱。s.57 的两年法定复效权属于人寿险条款，A&S Part 4 并未规定与之相同的普遍法定复效窗口。Incorrect. Falls into the Transposing Life Insurance Issue and Reinstatement Rules trap: The 2-year statutory reinstatement right under section 57 is a life insurance rule; Part 4 of the Insurance Act provides no equivalent universal statutory reinstatement window for A&S.

  2. ✗ 数字混淆：90 天是理赔交证（proof of claim [理赔交证]）的法定时限，与保单失效后的复效（reinstatement [A&S 复效]）无关。Incorrect. Conflates numbers: 90 days is the statutory deadline for submitting proof of claim, having no relation to policy reinstatement following a premium lapse.

  3. ✓ 正确。A&S Part 4 没有像寿险 s.57 那样规定统一的法定复效窗口，具体恢复条件看合同约定（考场若问复效期限，必须依据题干给定的合同条款）；但 s.113 规定复效（reinstatement [A&S 复效]）时的披露与不可抗辩期（incontestability period [不可抗辩期]，2 年，自复效日起算）仍受法律约束。Correct. Part 4 contains no uniform statutory reinstatement window like section 57 in life insurance; reinstatement terms depend entirely on the contract provisions. However, section 113 mandates that disclosures upon reinstatement and the two-year incontestability clock (running afresh from reinstatement) remain strictly governed by statute.

  4. ✗ 错误。宽限期（grace period [宽限期]，30 天，s.106）过后保单虽然失效，但只要符合合同约定的复效条件仍可恢复，法律并未禁止 A&S 保单复效。Incorrect. While coverage lapses after the 30-day grace period expires (s.106), the policy may still be reinstated if contractual conditions are met; statute does not prohibit reinstatement.

答案Answer：c

第 7 题Q7残疾定义与给付期等待期取舍Definition of disability trade-offs with elimination and benefit periods★陷阱：跨险种套用寿险生效与复效规则

Dr. Aris, a specialized hand surgeon, is comparing individual disability insurance options with a 90-day elimination period and a 5-year benefit period. Why would Dr. Aris pay a higher premium to select an own-occupation definition of disability rather than an any-occupation definition, and how does the 90-day elimination period affect his 5-year benefit period?

专科手部外科医生 Aris 正在对比具有 90 天等待期和 5 年给付期的个人伤残保险方案。为什么 Aris 医生愿意支付更高的保费来选择“本职定义”而非“任何职业定义”的残疾条款？此外，90 天的等待期会如何影响他的 5 年给付期？

  1. ✗ 双重错误：把本职定义（own occupation）与任何职业定义（any occupation）说反了，且等待期（elimination period [等待期]）与给付期（benefit period [给付期]）互不冲抵，不会从 5 年给付期中扣减 90 天。Incorrect. Dual error: Reverses the meanings of own-occupation and any-occupation definitions, and mistakenly assumes the 90-day elimination period is deducted from the 5-year benefit period; elimination and benefit periods do not offset each other.

  2. ✓ 正确。本职定义（own occupation）下，只要被保险人不能履行本职业的主要职责即算残疾，即便转行做其他工作（且收入可能更高）仍可持续领取给付，适合职业转换成本极高的专业人士；同时，等待期（elimination period [等待期]）与给付期（benefit period [给付期]）互不冲抵，90 天等待期结束后仍可享有完整的最长 5 年给付期。Correct. Under an own-occupation definition, the insured qualifies for total disability benefits if unable to perform the essential duties of their regular surgical specialty, even if working in another occupation (potentially earning equal or higher income); additionally, the elimination period and benefit period do not offset, ensuring the full 5-year benefit period remains available after the 90-day waiting period ends.

  3. ✗ 无中生有：残疾定义的宽严不会豁免 s.112 的两年不可抗辩期（incontestability period [不可抗辩期]），且 90 天等待期更不会把 5 年给付期变成保至 65 岁。Incorrect. Fabricates rules: Selecting an own-occupation definition does not waive the two-year statutory incontestability period under section 112, nor does a 90-day elimination period convert a 5-year benefit period into coverage to age 65.

  4. ✗ 完全相反：本职定义（own occupation）是最贵的选择而非最便宜的选择；同时 90 天等待期（elimination period [等待期]）不能替代 s.101 Condition 5 规定的 30 天理赔通知（notice of claim [理赔通知]）时限。Incorrect. Completely backwards: Own-occupation is the most expensive definition, not the cheapest, and a 90-day elimination period does not replace or modify the 30-day statutory notice of claim deadline under Condition 5.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Kenji is a salaried employee who already has a group short-term disability (STD) plan through his employer that covers the first 120 days of disability. How can his advisor use this existing coverage to structure an individual long-term disability policy more affordably?

Kenji 是一名领薪雇员，已经通过雇主拥有一份覆盖伤残前 120 天的团体短期伤残（STD）计划。他的代理人如何利用这份现有保障，以更实惠的保费为他设计个人长期伤残保单？

  1. ✓ 正确。等待期（elimination period [等待期]）越长保费越省；已有团体 STD 垫底的客户可以选择与 STD 给付期衔接的长等待期（如 120 天）来显著降低个人 DI 保费，而自雇者没有团体计划垫底则需靠现金流硬扛等待期。Correct. A longer elimination period significantly reduces policy premiums; because Kenji has employer-provided group STD covering the first 120 days, his advisor can align the individual policy with a 120-day elimination period to lower costs without creating an unhedged income gap.

  2. ✗ 违背推荐取舍原则：短等待期（如 30 天）保费昂贵，既然已有团体 STD 垫底，选短等待期不仅浪费保费预算，还会造成保障重叠。Incorrect. Contradicts sound recommendation trade-offs: A short 30-day elimination period is costly, wastes premium budget, and produces redundant coverage alongside the employer's existing STD plan.

  3. ✗ 错误。团体 STD 只管短期，客户恰恰需要长期给付期（benefit period [给付期]，如至 65 岁）来覆盖长期伤残风险；且改为可撤销（cancellable [可撤销 / 有条件续保]）会让保司随时能收回保障。Incorrect. Group STD only covers short-term illness, meaning the client specifically requires a long benefit period (e.g., to age 65) for catastrophic disability protection; furthermore, switching to cancellable allows the insurer to cancel coverage unilaterally.

  4. ✗ 违反推荐六步中的步骤⑤：无论客户是否有团体保障，代理人在推荐个人 A&S 保单时都必须完成书面留痕记录（reason-why record [推荐留痕记录]），记录推荐理由并由客户签字确认。Incorrect. Violates step 5 of the six recommendation steps: Regardless of existing group coverage, an advisor recommending an individual A&S policy must always document a written reason-why recommendation record confirmed by the client.
