第 2 章 · 定期寿险Chapter 2 · Term Life InsuranceTerm Life

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 合同权利辨析题：给出被保险人健康恶化或保单即将到期的情境，测试能否区分续保权（renewability，到期免核保继续买定期险）与转换权（convertibility，在约定年龄上限前免核保换成永久险）这两项各自独立的合同权利。Contractual rights distinction questions: Presenting scenarios where the life insured's health deteriorates or the policy is nearing expiry, testing whether candidates can distinguish between renewability (continuing term coverage at expiry without proof of insurability) and convertibility (exchanging for permanent coverage before a specified maximum age without proof of insurability) as two completely separate contractual rights.
  • 转换法律后果题：给出持有多年的可转换定期险在行使转换权后的理赔或撤单争议，测试转换属于原合同权利延续而非退旧买新——免交可保性证明（proof of insurability），且不可抗辩期（incontestability period）与自杀除外条款（suicide exclusion clause）的两年时钟沿用原定期险签发日。Legal consequences of conversion questions: Presenting claim or policy cancellation disputes after exercising conversion on a convertible term policy held for several years, testing that conversion represents the continuation of existing contractual rights rather than cancelling and reissuing—requiring no proof of insurability, and with the two-year clocks for the incontestability period and suicide exclusion clause running continuously from the original term policy issue date.
  • 转换与续保定价比较题：对比现龄转换（attained-age conversion）与原龄转换（original-age conversion），或对比保证费率续保（renewable with guaranteed rates）与重新核保续保（re-entry term），测试是否理解原龄转换需补缴保费差额及可能利息、重新核保续保对投保人属于单向保护。Conversion and renewal pricing comparison questions: Comparing attained-age conversion with original-age conversion, or comparing renewability with guaranteed rates with re-entry term, testing whether candidates understand that original-age conversion requires paying the difference in premiums plus possible interest, and that re-entry term provides one-way protection for the policyholder.
  • 保额形态与负债周期匹配题：结合剩余按揭年限、子女抚养期或共同借款人场景，测试平额（level）、递减（decreasing）、递增（increasing）定期险及联合首身故（joint first-to-die）的保额、保费特征，以及贷款人按揭寿险与个人定期险在受益人和可携带性上的差异。Coverage structure and debt liability matching questions: Combining remaining mortgage terms, child dependency periods, or co-borrower scenarios, testing the coverage and premium characteristics of level, decreasing, and increasing term insurance, as well as joint first-to-die coverage, along with the differences between lender mortgage insurance and individual term policies regarding beneficiaries and portability.
  • 保单权利归属与双口径边界题：测试离婚或家庭变动下联合首身故保单的变更权归属（取决于投保人 policyholder 身份及不可撤销受益人、质押或法院命令限制），以及考试口径固定期限档位（1/5/10/20 年）与现行按年定制、合同具体转换截止年龄的区分。Policy rights ownership and dual-benchmark boundary questions: Testing policy change rights ownership for joint first-to-die policies upon divorce or family changes (determined by policyholder status and subject to irrevocable beneficiary, collateral assignment, or court order restrictions), as well as distinguishing between exam-standard fixed terms (1, 5, 10, 20 years) and modern annually customized terms, along with specific contractual maximum conversion ages.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 混淆续保权与转换权Confusing renewability with convertibility

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 两项权利都强调免核保（without proof of insurability），考生容易把它们当成同一项权利的两种叫法，忽略了续保是期满继续持有定期险、转换是在年龄上限前换成永久险，且各自有独立的年龄上限。Both rights emphasize being exercised without proof of insurability, so candidates easily mistake them as two names for the same right, overlooking that renewal means continuing term coverage upon expiry, whereas conversion means exchanging for permanent coverage before a maximum age, and each possesses an independent maximum age limit.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 at expiry / continue temporary coverage 指向续保权（renewability）；出现 permanent coverage / whole life / Term 100 before a maximum age 指向转换权（convertibility）。The question stem mentions 'at expiry' or 'continue temporary coverage' pointing to renewability; mentions 'permanent coverage', 'whole life', or 'Term 100 before a maximum age' pointing to convertibility.

⚠ 误认转换会重置两年时钟Assuming conversion resets the two-year clocks

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 转换后会签发一份永久险合同，考生直觉以为有了新保单就会重新起算两年的不可抗辩期（incontestability period）和自杀除外条款（suicide exclusion clause），或者误以为健康异常时转换需先等检查结果。Because a permanent insurance contract is issued upon conversion, candidates intuitively assume a new policy resets the two-year incontestability period and suicide exclusion clause, or mistakenly assume that when medical test abnormalities arise, one must wait for final test results before converting.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出原定期险已持有满三年或五年、或还在两年窗口内，询问转换后保险公司能否以转换时未告知体检异常拒赔，或询问自杀除外期从何时起算。The question stem notes that the original term policy has been in force for three or five years, or remains within the two-year window, and asks whether the insurer can deny a claim for undisclosed medical abnormalities at conversion, or when the suicide exclusion period starts.

⚠ 盲信原龄转换必然更划算Blindly assuming original-age conversion is always more cost-effective

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到原龄转换（original-age conversion）按最初投保时的年轻年龄计算永久险保费，就误以为总成本一定低于现龄转换（attained-age conversion），忽略了原龄可转换定期险前期保费更高且转换时须一次性补缴差额及可能利息。Seeing that original-age conversion calculates permanent insurance premiums based on the younger age at initial policy issue, candidates incorrectly assume the total outlay must be lower than attained-age conversion, ignoring that original-age convertible term policies charge higher initial premiums and require a lump-sum payment of premium differences plus possible interest at conversion.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干比较 attained-age conversion 与 original-age / retroactive conversion，选项出现 always results in lower total outlay 或 requires no lump-sum adjustment。The question stem compares attained-age conversion with original-age or retroactive conversion, and options include 'always results in lower total outlay' or 'requires no lump-sum adjustment'.

⚠ 误认递减定期险保费随保额递减Assuming decreasing term premiums decrease alongside coverage

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 递减定期险（decreasing term）的身故给付（death benefit）随时间下降，考生顺着字面意思误以为保费也会逐年下降，或把贷款机构的按揭寿险与个人定期险在受益人和可携带性上混为一谈。Because the death benefit of decreasing term insurance declines over time, candidates take the phrasing literally and assume premiums also decrease annually, or conflate lender mortgage insurance with individual term policies regarding beneficiaries and portability.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 decreasing term policy covering a mortgage 或对比 lender mortgage insurance 与 individual term policy，询问保费走势、受益人归属或保单控制权。The question stem features a decreasing term policy covering a mortgage or compares lender mortgage insurance with an individual term policy, asking about premium trends, beneficiary rights, or policy ownership.

⚠ 混淆被保险人身份与投保人控制权Confusing life insured status with policyholder control

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 在联合首身故（joint first-to-die）或共同房贷场景中，考生误以为只要是共同被保险人（life insured）或已经离婚，双方就自动平分保单变更权，忽略了只有投保人（policyholder）才能在无不可撤销受益人、质押或法院命令限制时行使变更权。In joint first-to-die or joint mortgage scenarios, candidates mistakenly believe that being a joint life insured or getting divorced automatically gives both individuals equal policy alteration rights, overlooking that only the policyholder can exercise change rights absent irrevocable beneficiary, collateral assignment, or court order restrictions.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 joint first-to-die policy 与 divorce / separation / changing the beneficiary，且指出保单最初由其中一方单独作为 policyholder 投保。The question stem features a joint first-to-die policy alongside divorce, separation, or changing the beneficiary, and indicates the policy was originally taken out by one party alone as policyholder.

第 1 题Q1转换权免核保及两年时钟沿用原签发日Guaranteed conversion without evidence and continuation of the two-year clocks from original issue date★★陷阱：误认转换会重置两年时钟

Five years ago, Elena purchased a 10-year convertible term life insurance policy. Last month, she exercised her conversion privilege to convert the policy into a permanent life insurance contract without providing proof of insurability. How do the two-year incontestability period and the two-year suicide exclusion clause apply to Elena's permanent policy?

五年前，Elena 购买了一份 10 年期可转换定期寿险保单。上个月，她行使转换权将该保单转换为一份永久寿险合同，且未提供可保性证明。两年不可抗辩期与两年自杀除外条款在 Elena 的永久险保单上如何适用？

  1. ✓ 正确。行使转换权（convertibility）不是退旧买新，而是原合同权利的延续：不仅免交可保性证明（proof of insurability），而且不可抗辩期（incontestability period）与自杀除外条款（suicide exclusion clause）的两年时钟均沿用原定期险合同的签发日期起算，满五年后早已届满。Correct. Exercising convertibility is not canceling and reissuing, but the continuation of existing contractual rights: not only is proof of insurability waived, but the two-year statutory clocks for both the incontestability period and the suicide exclusion clause continue running from the original term policy issue date, and after five years they have long since expired.

  2. ✗ 误触「误认转换会重置两年时钟」陷阱。不可抗辩期与自杀除外条款的起算时间同步沿用原定期险签发日，只要保额与合同属正常转换，自杀除外期不会在转换日重新起算两年。Falls into the 'assuming conversion resets the two-year clocks' trap. The commencement of both the incontestability period and the suicide exclusion clause continues from the original term policy issue date; as long as the face amount is a standard conversion, the suicide exclusion clause does not restart for two years on the conversion date.

  3. ✗ 误触「误认转换会重置两年时钟」陷阱，把转换权（convertibility）当成了「退保后重新投保」。若重新起算两年时钟，就剥夺了可转换定期险的核心合同权利。Falls into the 'assuming conversion resets the two-year clocks' trap, treating convertibility as surrendering and reapplying for new coverage. Restarting the two-year clocks would strip convertible term insurance of its core contractual benefit.

  4. ✗ 违反转换权免核保原则。转换权的核心就是无需提交任何新的可保性证明（proof of insurability）即可延续原签发日起算的两年时钟。Violates the principle that convertibility requires no proof of insurability. The core essence of convertibility is continuing the two-year clocks running from the original issue date without submitting any new proof of insurability.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

One year after purchasing a 10-year convertible term life policy, Victor exercises his conversion privilege to switch to a permanent policy. How much time remains in the two-year incontestability and suicide exclusion periods under Victor's permanent contract?

在购买一份 10 年期可转换定期寿险一年后，Victor 行使转换权将其换成一份永久险保单。在 Victor 的永久险合同下，两年的不可抗辩期与自杀除外期还剩多少时间？

  1. ✗ 误触「误认转换会重置两年时钟」陷阱。即使原定期险还在两年窗口内，转换后也不会把已经走过的一年清零重算。Falls into the 'assuming conversion resets the two-year clocks' trap. Even though the original term policy is still within the two-year window, conversion does not erase the one year already elapsed and reset the clock to zero.

  2. ✗ 误解时钟延续规则。转换沿用原定期险签发日计算两年期限，原保单刚满一年，剩余的一年会带入新合同继续计算，而非直接豁免。Misunderstands the clock continuation rule. Conversion calculates the two-year period from the original term policy issue date; since the original policy has been in force for one year, the remaining one year carries over into the permanent contract rather than being immediately waived.

  3. ✓ 正确。行使转换权（convertibility）后，不可抗辩期（incontestability period）与自杀除外条款（suicide exclusion clause）沿用原定期险签发日起算；原定期险已过一年，剩余的一年带入永久险继续计算。Correct. Upon exercising convertibility, both the incontestability period and the suicide exclusion clause continue running from the original term policy issue date; since one year has passed on the term policy, the remaining one year carries over into the permanent policy to complete the two-year requirement.

  4. ✗ 误触「误认转换会重置两年时钟」陷阱。不可抗辩期与自杀除外条款均沿用原合同签发日，不会单独重置不可抗辩期。Falls into the 'assuming conversion resets the two-year clocks' trap. Both the incontestability period and the suicide exclusion clause run from the original contract issue date, and the incontestability period does not restart separately.

第 2 题Q2现龄转换与原龄转换的定价与补差机制Pricing and retroactive adjustment mechanics of attained-age versus original-age conversion★★★陷阱：盲信原龄转换必然更划算

Julian is comparing two convertible term policies: Policy X uses an attained-age conversion method, while Policy Y permits an original-age (retroactive) conversion. If Julian converts five years after issue, how do the financial mechanics of Policy Y compare with Policy X?

Julian 正在比较两份可转换定期寿险：保单 X 采用现龄转换算法，而保单 Y 允许原龄（追溯）转换。如果 Julian 在签发五年后行使转换权，保单 Y 的财务机制与保单 X 相比如何？

  1. ✗ 把现龄转换（attained-age conversion）的机制安在了原龄转换上。按转换当时年龄计费且无需一次性补缴差额的是保单 X（现龄转换）。Applies the mechanics of attained-age conversion to original-age conversion. Pricing premiums at the age at conversion without requiring a lump-sum retroactive payment is the feature of Policy X (attained-age conversion).

Joint first-to-die covers two lives under a single benefit amount and pays out once upon the first death; the insurer is exposed to the risk of paying only one claim, which represents actual risk savings inherent to joint coverage rather than a simple marketing discount.

  2. ✗ 混淆了联合首身故（joint first-to-die）与联合末身故（joint last-to-die）。最后一人身故才赔付的是常用于覆盖递延身故税负的联合末身故保单。Confuses joint first-to-die with joint last-to-die insurance. Payouts occurring only upon the death of the second insured characterize joint last-to-die policies, commonly used to cover deferred death taxes.

  3. ✗ 虚构限制。联合首身故保费较低的底层原因是只赔付一次身故给付（death benefit），而非一律禁止续保或转换条款。Fabricates a non-existent restriction. The fundamental reason for lower premiums on joint first-to-die policies is the single death benefit payout, not any blanket prohibition on renewability or convertibility.

  4. ✗ 曲解「首身故」含义。联合首身故（joint first-to-die）在任一被保险人于期限内最先身故时即触发理赔，无需两人同时身故。Distorts the definition of first-to-die. Joint first-to-die triggers a claim upon the first death of either insured during the term; it does not require both insureds to die in the same event.

第 7 题Q7需求时间窗口测量与期限及保额形态选择Measuring the need time window and selecting appropriate term lengths and coverage structures★陷阱：误认递减定期险保费随保额递减

An agent is completing a needs analysis for a client who wants temporary coverage for two distinct needs: a 20-year income replacement period while her children are growing up, and a liability that is expected to grow with inflation over time. How do standard term insurance death benefit modalities address these needs?

一名代理人正在为客户做需求分析，客户有两项明确的临时保障需求：子女成长期间 20 年的收入替代期，以及一项预计随时间因通胀而扩大的责任缺口。标准的定期寿险身故给付形态如何对应这些需求？

  1. ✗ 把平额（level）与递减（decreasing）的适用场景彻底颠倒。按揭余额逐年下降适合用递减定期险省保费，而抚养期收入替代或遗产规划等不随时间递减的需求适合用平额定期险。Completely reverses the appropriate use cases for level and decreasing term insurance. A declining mortgage balance suits decreasing term to save premium costs, whereas child dependency income replacement and estate planning represent non-declining needs best covered by level term.

  2. ✓ 正确。平额定期险（level term）保额始终不变，适合子女抚养费等恒定需求；递增定期险（increasing term）用于覆盖随通胀或收入增长扩大的缺口，但考试口径与加拿大市场实务中独立递增定期险较少见，通常改用附加险（rider）或分批追加平额保单来实现。Correct. Level term maintains a constant death benefit, matching constant child dependency needs; increasing term addresses shortfalls expanding with inflation or income growth, though under exam conventions and Canadian market practice, standalone increasing term policies are rare and typically implemented via riders or layered level policies.

  3. ✗ 与加拿大市场事实相反。平额定期险（level term）才是最常见的形态，独立递增定期险（increasing term）在加拿大市场并不常见，且保额递增不会伴随保费递减。Contradicts Canadian market realities. Level term is the most common form, whereas standalone increasing term contracts are rare in Canada, and increasing coverage is never accompanied by decreasing premiums.

  4. ✗ 违背定期寿险的基本定位。定期寿险（term life insurance）的核心就是覆盖一段已知或可估算的时间窗口（考试常见档位含 1/5/10/20 年，现行市场还可按年定制），无需为临时窗口强制购买终身寿险。Violates the fundamental purpose of term life insurance. Term insurance is designed specifically to cover known or estimable time windows (with exam bands including 1, 5, 10, or 20 years and modern practice offering custom annual terms), without mandating permanent whole life for temporary needs.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Before recommending a specific term length such as 10 years, 20 years, or a customized 17-year duration, what is the agent's primary step during the needs analysis stage?

在建议 10 年、20 年或按年定制的 17 年等具体定期寿险期限之前，代理人在需求分析阶段的首要步骤是什么？

Term life insurance covers a truncated window of mortality risk; the agent's first step in needs analysis is to measure the known or estimable time window of risk (such as remaining mortgage amortization or years until children become independent), and only then match standard bands or custom terms (answering exam questions using given bands, while measuring exact liability duration in practice).
