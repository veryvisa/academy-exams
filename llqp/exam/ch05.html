第 5 章 · 附加条款Chapter 5 · Riders and Supplementary BenefitsRiders

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 生前给付对身故金的影响计算与辨析：给出客户的保单面额及绝症给付（TI）或重疾给付（DD）比例上限（如 50% 或最高 $50,000），考查加速身故金作为「预支」会等额扣减最终身故金，并与独立重疾险（CI）的额外独立赔付作对比。Calculating and distinguishing the impact of living benefits on the death benefit: Given a client's policy face amount and an advance limit for a terminal illness (TI) or dread disease (DD) benefit (such as 50% or up to $50,000), test that an accelerated death benefit acts as an advance that reduces the ultimate death benefit dollar-for-dollar, and contrast this with the separate, additional payout of a stand-alone critical illness (CI) insurance policy.
  • 投保人与被保险人分离时的豁免条款选择：设计父母为未成年子女投保、或配偶一方为另一方投保并交费的场景，测试考生能否区分普通完全伤残豁免（只保被保险人伤残）与父母/代付人豁免（保投保人身故或伤残）。Selecting waiver of premium benefits when the policyholder and life insured are different: In scenarios where parents purchase coverage for minor children or one spouse purchases coverage and pays premiums for the other, test whether the candidate can distinguish between a standard waiver of premium for total disability (which covers only disability of the life insured) and a parent or payor waiver benefit (which covers the policyholder's death or total disability).
  • 阶段性需求与长期需求的组合配置：给出客户既有终身税务需求又有 10 年期债务的场景，考查用终身寿险主险加定期险附加险（节省单次核保与 $50–$100 保单年费）的逻辑，以及主险退保或失效时依附型附加险与已缴清增额（PUA）的法律后果。Structuring combinations of temporary and permanent needs: Given a scenario where a client has both lifelong tax liabilities and a 10-year debt obligation, test the rationale of combining a whole life base policy with a term insurance rider (saving duplicate underwriting and a $50 to $100 annual policy fee), as well as the legal consequences for dependent riders and paid-up additions (PUA) if the base policy lapses or is surrendered.
  • 逆选择保护机制的反向应用：围绕健康恶化或实质不可保的客户（如患病满一定年龄的子女或年轻投保人），考查子女附加险转换权（到期前免核保转永久险、最高放大 5 倍）与保证可保利益（GIB，固定周年日免核保按当时年龄加保）的行权规则。Application of anti-selection protection mechanisms: Focus on clients with deteriorating health or who have become effectively uninsurable (such as an impaired child reaching a certain age or young policyholders), testing exercise rules for the child rider conversion privilege (converting to permanent insurance before expiry without medical evidence, up to a 5-fold increase) and guaranteed insurability benefit (GIB, exercising on fixed policy anniversaries without evidence of insurability at attained-age rates).
  • 意外身故附加险（AD）的理赔边界与需求分析误区：通过客户死于疾病、自杀或在事故发生 1 年限期后身故的案例，或者试图用 AD 双倍赔付凑齐家庭核心保额缺口的做法，考查除外条款与「遗属所需资金不因死因改变」的原则。Claim boundaries and needs analysis pitfalls of the accidental death (AD) rider: Through cases where a client dies of illness, suicide, or beyond the 1-year post-accident limit, or attempts to use double indemnity under an AD rider to fill a core family coverage gap, test policy exclusions and the principle that survivors' financial needs do not change based on the cause of death.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 附加险当独立保单（类别错配）Treating Riders as Standalone Policies (Category Mismatch)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为附加险（rider）尤其是带有「已缴清」字样的已缴清增额（paid-up additions, PUA）在主险失效或整单退保后仍能独立存续。Mistakenly assuming that riders, particularly paid-up additions (PUA) labelled as paid-up, can continue to exist independently after the base policy lapses or is surrendered in full.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现主险失效（lapse）、到期（expire）或整单退保（surrender），问名下附加险或增额保障能否继续保留。The question stem mentions base policy lapse, expiry, or full surrender, and asks whether attached riders or supplemental paid-up coverage can be maintained.

⚠ 预支与另付混淆（产品混淆）Confusing Advances with Additional Payouts (Product Confusion)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到重疾给付（dread disease, DD）或绝症给付（terminal illness, TI）就以为是在身故金之外额外多赔一笔，忽略了加速身故金（accelerated death benefit）本质是提前支取身故金。Assuming that a dread disease (DD) benefit or terminal illness (TI) benefit provides an additional payout on top of the death benefit, overlooking that an accelerated death benefit is fundamentally an advance of the ultimate death benefit.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问领取生前给付后，受益人最终能拿到的身故金（death benefit）如何变化，或对比附加重疾给付（DD）与独立重疾险（stand-alone CI）的赔付后果。The question stem asks how the final death benefit received by beneficiaries changes after living benefits are paid, or compares an attached dread disease (DD) benefit with stand-alone critical illness (CI) insurance.

⚠ 豁免对象张冠李戴（条款错配）Mismatched Waiver Insured Parties (Provision Mismatch)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 投保人（policyholder/payor）与被保险人（life insured）不是同一人时，习惯性选择普通完全伤残豁免（waiver of premium for total disability），忘了它只针对被保险人本人的伤残。When the policyholder or payor is not the life insured, habitually selecting a standard waiver of premium for total disability, forgetting that it applies solely to the disability of the life insured.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现父母为未成年子女投保、或夫妻一方为另一方投保并负责缴费，问付款人身故或完全伤残时如何确保保单不失效。The question stem presents parents insuring minor children or one spouse insuring the other and paying premiums, asking how to keep the policy in force if the payor dies or becomes totally disabled.

⚠ GIB把免核保当免费或随时加保（术语与双口径误读）Misinterpreting GIB as Free or Anytime Coverage (Terminology and Dual-Standard Misreading)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把保证可保利益（guaranteed insurability benefit, GIB）的「保证」误解为锁定原投保年龄费率，或把实务中特定合同的人生事件触发当成考场通用规则，忽略了考场默认按固定周年日行权且按新年龄计费。Misinterpreting guaranteed in the guaranteed insurability benefit (GIB) as locking in the original issue-age premium rate, or treating practical life-event triggers from specific contracts as general exam rules, overlooking that the exam standard exercises on fixed policy anniversaries and charges premiums at the attained age.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问行使 GIB 加保时的保费定价依据、是否需要健康证明（evidence of insurability），或在考场问标准 GIB 何时可以行权。The question stem asks about premium pricing basis when exercising GIB, whether evidence of insurability is required, or when standard GIB option dates occur on the exam.

⚠ 靠意外双倍赔付凑基础保额（范围夸大与以偏概全）Relying on Accidental Double Indemnity for Core Coverage (Scope Inflation and Generalization)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看中意外身故附加险（accidental death, AD）保费低、能双倍赔付（double indemnity），就用它填补家庭常规身故保额缺口，忽略疾病身故与除外条款不赔。Attracted by low premiums and double indemnity under an accidental death (AD) rider, attempting to fill a family's core death benefit shortfall with it, ignoring that death from illness and excluded causes are not covered.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出客户的固定家庭负债或遗属生活费缺口，提议用低成本的 AD 附加险把总赔付凑够目标金额。The question stem gives a client's fixed family debt or survivor living expense shortfall and proposes using low-cost AD riders to bring total payout up to the target amount.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
附加险与补充保障的分类逻辑Classification of Riders and Supplementary Benefitsriders and supplementary benefits考试口径将身故后追加给付归为附加险（riders），将生前触发的预支、另付或保费豁免归为补充保障（supplementary benefits），但实务中须按具体合同触发条款核对。Under exam conventions, additional benefits paid after death are categorized as riders, while living triggers such as advances, separate payouts, or premium waivers are classified as supplementary benefits; however, in practice each contract's specific trigger provisions must be verified.
捆绑定价优势与依附性限制Bundled Pricing Advantages and Dependency Limitationsterm insurance rider / paid-up additions (PUA) rider附加险因共用一次核保与单份保单年费（通常 $50–$100）而比独立保单便宜，但依附主险存续，主险失效或整单退保时附加险同时终止、PUA 现金价值一并结算。Riders are less expensive than stand-alone policies because they share a single underwriting assessment and one annual policy fee (typically $50 to $100); however, they are dependent on the base policy, meaning riders terminate and PUA cash values are settled together upon base policy lapse or full surrender.
意外身故与意外伤残的理赔边界Claim Boundaries for Accidental Death and Dismembermentaccidental death (AD) rider / accidental dismemberment benefitAD 仅在死因属于意外且发生在事故后限期内（常见 1 年）时额外赔付（常见 2 倍身故金），除外自杀、自伤、战争与犯罪；AD&D 另按损失表在生前赔付肢体或功能损失。An AD rider pays an additional benefit (typically double indemnity) only if death is accidental and occurs within a stipulated timeframe following the accident (commonly 1 year), excluding suicide, self-inflicted injury, war, and criminal acts; AD&D additionally pays living benefits for loss of limbs or function according to a schedule of losses.
加速身故金的预支扣减机制Advance and Deduction Mechanism of Accelerated Death Benefitsaccelerated death benefit: terminal illness (TI) and dread disease (DD)绝症给付（TI，常见上限为面额 25%–75% 或举例 50% 上限 $50,000）与重疾给付（DD，需存活满常见 30 天等待期）均属提前预支，会等额减少最终身故金，而独立重疾险（CI）不扣减寿险身故金。Terminal illness (TI, commonly capped at 25% to 75% of face value or 50% up to $50,000) and dread disease (DD, requiring survival past a typical 30-day waiting period) are advances that reduce the ultimate death benefit dollar-for-dollar, whereas stand-alone critical illness (CI) insurance does not deduct from the life insurance death benefit.
子女附加险的自动覆盖与免核保转换Automatic Coverage and Conversion Privilege of Child Ridersfamily/child coverage rider and converting child coverage rider子女附加险按固定单位计费（不随子女人数加费），新生儿满一定天数（常见 15 天，出生后头 2 周内身故不赔）自动覆盖至 21 或 25 岁，到期前可免核保转为最高达原保额 5 倍的个人永久险。A child rider charges a flat premium per unit regardless of the number of children, automatically covering newborns once they reach a specified age (commonly 15 days, with no benefit if death occurs within the first 2 weeks) up to age 21 or 25, and can be converted before expiry without medical evidence into an individual permanent policy for up to 5 times the rider face amount.
保证可保利益的行权触发与定价Exercise Triggers and Premium Pricing for Guaranteed Insurability Benefitguaranteed insurability benefit (GIB)考试口径下 GIB 在固定保单周年日（如第 5、10 周年日）行权且受单次与累计限额约束（部分现行合同新增毕业、结婚、生子、收养后 31 天内行权），免核保但保费按加保时的实际年龄计算。Under exam conventions, GIB is exercised on fixed policy anniversaries (such as the 5th or 10th anniversary) subject to single and cumulative limits (some modern contracts also allow exercise within 31 days of graduation, marriage, birth, or adoption), requiring no medical evidence but pricing new coverage at the insured's attained age.
重疾定义基准与公司间差异Critical Illness Benchmark Definitions and Inter-Company DifferencesCLHIA CI Benchmark Definitions / Big 4考试口径以「Big 4」（心脏病发、中风、冠状动脉搭桥、危及生命的癌症）概括重疾门槛（部分保司保 25 种以上），行业虽有 CLHIA 2024 Final 基准定义但各保司自愿采用，须逐份合同核对。The exam summarizes common critical illness coverage thresholds through the Big 4 (heart attack, stroke, coronary artery bypass surgery, and life-threatening cancer, while some insurers cover 25 or more conditions); although the industry has the CLHIA 2024 Final benchmark definitions, their adoption by insurers is voluntary and wording must be verified in each specific contract.
基础缺口覆盖原则与第三人核保Core Shortfall Coverage Principle and Third-Party Underwritingvalue of coverage and underwriting of riders家庭核心保额缺口必须由基础保额全额覆盖而不能靠附加险凑数；若在保单上附加覆盖配偶或其他人的定期险附加险，该被保险人必须单独接受核保。A family's core coverage shortfall must be fully covered by the base insurance policy rather than supplemented with riders; if a term insurance rider is attached to cover a spouse or another third party, that individual must undergo separate underwriting.
完全伤残豁免与父母代付人豁免的区分Distinguishing Total Disability Waiver from Parent or Payor Waiverwaiver of premium for total disability vs. parent/payor waiver benefit普通完全伤残豁免在被保险人伤残满等待期后豁免保费且保单分红与现金价值照常累积；当投保人非被保险人时，须配置父母/代付人豁免（代付人亦需核保）才能在代付人身故或伤残时豁免保费。A standard waiver of premium for total disability waives premiums after the life insured satisfies the waiting period while dividends and cash values continue to accumulate; when the policyholder is not the life insured, a parent or payor waiver benefit (requiring underwriting of the payor) must be added to waive premiums upon the payor's death or total disability.

第 1 题Q1加速身故金扣减身故金与独立重疾对比Accelerated death benefit deduction versus stand-alone critical illness★★陷阱：预支与另付混淆（产品混淆）

Victor and Elena each own a $50,000 life insurance policy. Victor's policy includes an accelerated dread disease (DD) benefit with a 50% advance limit ($25,000) and a 30-day survival waiting period. Elena did not add any riders to her life policy, but she owns a separate $25,000 stand-alone critical illness (CI) insurance policy. Both Victor and Elena suffer a severe stroke covered under their respective contracts, survive for more than 30 days, and each receives a $25,000 lump-sum payout. If both pass away later while their policies are in force, how much death benefit will each of their beneficiaries receive?

Victor 和 Elena 各自持有一份保额为 $50,000 的寿险保单。Victor 的保单包含一项加速身故金下的重疾给付（DD）补充保障，预支上限为面额的 50%（即 $25,000），存活等待期为 30 天。Elena 没有在她的寿险保单上添加任何附加险，但她另有一份保额 $25,000 的独立重疾险（CI）保单。Victor 和 Elena 均突发各自合同承保的严重中风，存活超过 30 天，且各自获得了一笔 $25,000 的一次性给付。如果两人日后在保单有效期内身故，他们各自的受益人将获得多少身故金？

  1. ✗ 错在「预支与另付混淆（产品混淆）」陷阱。把 Victor 的加速身故金·重疾给付（accelerated death benefit: dread disease, DD）当成了额外赔付，忽略了 DD 本质上是从最终身故金中提前预支（advance），必须从 $50,000 中扣减已领取的 $25,000。Incorrect. Falls into the Confusing Advances with Additional Payouts (Product Confusion) trap: It treats Victor's accelerated death benefit: dread disease (DD) as an additional payout, overlooking that DD is fundamentally an advance from the ultimate death benefit and must be deducted from the $50,000 face amount by the $25,000 received.

  2. ✓ 正确。加速身故金下的重疾给付（dread disease, DD benefit）属于生前预支机制，Victor 领取的 $25,000 会等额减少最终身故金（$50,000 - $25,000 = $25,000）；而 Elena 持有的是独立重疾险（stand-alone critical illness, CI insurance），按独立合同赔付，绝不预支或扣减其寿险保单的 $50,000 身故金。Correct. A dread disease (DD) benefit under an accelerated death benefit operates as a living advance mechanism, so the $25,000 Victor received reduces the ultimate death benefit dollar-for-dollar ($50,000 - $25,000 = $25,000); in contrast, Elena holds stand-alone critical illness (CI) insurance, which pays under an independent contract and never advances or reduces the $50,000 death benefit of her life insurance policy.

  3. ✗ 错在「预支与另付混淆（产品混淆）」陷阱。误以为独立重疾险（stand-alone CI insurance）也会像加速身故金（accelerated death benefit）一样扣减寿险身故金；实际上独立 CI 保单与寿险保单是两份平行合同，互不扣减。Incorrect. Falls into the Confusing Advances with Additional Payouts (Product Confusion) trap: It mistakenly assumes that stand-alone CI insurance reduces the life insurance death benefit like an accelerated death benefit; in reality, a stand-alone CI policy and a life insurance policy are parallel contracts that do not reduce each other.

  4. ✗ 错在扣减金额计算错误（limitations and exclusions）。Victor 按 50% 比例预支了 $25,000，身故金只等额扣减已预支的 $25,000（剩余 $25,000），而不会扣减至 $10,000。Incorrect. Calculation error in the deducted amount: Victor advanced $25,000 under the 50% limit, so the death benefit is reduced only dollar-for-dollar by the $25,000 advance (leaving $25,000), rather than being reduced to $10,000.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Arthur owns a $50,000 whole life insurance policy that includes a terminal illness (TI) accelerated death benefit allowing an advance of 50% of the face amount ($25,000). After Arthur's physician certifies that his life expectancy falls within the policy's stipulated terminal period, Arthur applies for and receives the $25,000 TI benefit. When Arthur passes away later, what happens to the remaining death benefit?

Arthur 持有一份面额为 $50,000 的终身寿险保单，其中包含一项绝症给付（TI）加速身故金条款，允许预支面额的 50%（即 $25,000）。在医生证明 Arthur 的预期寿命已进入保单规定的绝症期限后，Arthur 申请并领取了 $25,000 绝症给付。当 Arthur 去世时，剩余的身故金会如何处理？

  1. ✗ 错在「预支与另付混淆（产品混淆）」陷阱。绝症给付（terminal illness, TI benefit）属于加速身故金（accelerated death benefit），机制是把将来才兑现的身故金提前搬到现在用，而不是额外多给一笔。Incorrect. Falls into the Confusing Advances with Additional Payouts (Product Confusion) trap: A terminal illness (TI) benefit is an accelerated death benefit, which functions by advancing a portion of the future death benefit for use now rather than paying an extra sum in addition to the face amount.

  2. ✗ 错在误以为领取部分预支金会导致整张保单作废。绝症给付（TI benefit）只预支了 50%（$25,000），保单剩余的身故保额依然有效并留待身故时赔付。Incorrect. Mistakenly assumes that receiving a partial advance voids the entire policy: The terminal illness (TI) benefit advanced only 50% ($25,000), leaving the remaining death benefit in force to be paid upon the insured's death.

  3. ✗ 错在编造行政罚金规则。绝症给付（TI benefit）按实际预支的 $25,000 从 $50,000 面额中等额扣减，不会额外扣除所谓 30% 罚金。Incorrect. Fabricates an administrative penalty rule: The terminal illness (TI) benefit is deducted dollar-for-dollar from the $50,000 face amount by the $25,000 actually advanced, without any fictitious 30% penalty.

  4. ✓ 正确。绝症给付（terminal illness, TI benefit）是加速身故金（accelerated death benefit）的一种，付给投保人本人的 $25,000 属于预支款项，被保险人身故时最终赔付给受益人的身故金须等额扣减至 $25,000。Correct. A terminal illness (TI) benefit is a form of accelerated death benefit where the $25,000 paid to the policyholder represents an advance; upon the life insured's death, the final death benefit payable to the beneficiary is reduced dollar-for-dollar to $25,000.

Falls into the Treating Riders as Standalone Policies (Category Mismatch) trap: A term insurance rider trades independence for a lower bundled price and depends strictly on the base policy; if the whole life base policy is cancelled or surrendered in year 5, the term rider terminates simultaneously.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Five years ago, Brenda bought a whole life base policy to cover final expenses and attached a 10-year term insurance rider to cover a business debt. This year, Brenda decides to let the whole life base policy lapse because she finds the permanent premiums too high, assuming she can keep paying only the lower premium for the remaining 5 years of the term insurance rider. Is Brenda's assumption correct?

五年前，Brenda 购买了一份终身寿险主险来覆盖身后最终费用，并附加了一份 10 年期定期险附加险来覆盖一笔生意债务。今年，Brenda 觉得永久险保费太高，决定让终身寿险主险失效，并以为自己可以在剩下的 5 年里只继续缴纳较低的定期险附加险保费。Brenda 的想法正确吗？

  1. ✗ 错在「附加险当独立保单（类别错配）」陷阱。虽然定期险附加险（term insurance rider）有自己的 10 年期限，但它不是独立保单，不能脱离主险单独存在。Incorrect. Falls into the Treating Riders as Standalone Policies (Category Mismatch) trap: Although a term insurance rider has its own 10-year term, it is not an independent policy and cannot survive on its own apart from the base policy.

  2. ✗ 错在「附加险当独立保单（类别错配）」陷阱。附加险的依附性是合同结构决定的，主险失效后无法靠补交 $50–$100 的保单年费（policy fee）让附加险单独存续。Incorrect. Falls into the Treating Riders as Standalone Policies (Category Mismatch) trap: A rider's dependency is dictated by contract structure; once the base policy lapses, the rider cannot be maintained independently by paying the $50 to $100 annual policy fee.

  3. ✓ 正确。定期险附加险（term insurance rider）的核心限制就是依附性（dependency）：附加险用失去独立性换取捆绑低价，一旦主险因未交费而失效（lapse）或被退保（surrender），挂在主险上的定期险附加险会同时失效。Correct. The primary constraint of a term insurance rider is dependency: riders trade independence for bundled savings, so if the base policy lapses for non-payment or is surrendered, attached term riders terminate immediately.

  4. ✗ 错在捏造转换规则。定期险附加险（term insurance rider）不能转换为什么意外身故附加险（AD rider）来规避主险失效的后果。Incorrect. Fabricates conversion rules: A term insurance rider cannot be converted into an accidental death (AD) rider to circumvent the consequences of a base policy lapse.

第 6 题Q6意外身故附加险理赔条件与需求分析Accidental death rider claim conditions and needs analysis principles★★陷阱：靠意外双倍赔付凑基础保额（范围夸大与以偏概全）

A comprehensive needs analysis shows that Gordon requires $1,000,000 of life insurance to support his family if he dies. To save money on premiums, Gordon proposes buying a $300,000 base term policy and adding an accidental death (AD) double indemnity rider, arguing that an unexpected death is his main concern. Two years later, Gordon dies from a sudden heart attack. Why was Gordon's planning strategy flawed, and how much will his beneficiary receive if his policy had a $300,000 base and an AD rider?

一份全面的需求分析显示，Gordon 需要 $1,000,000 的寿险保额才能在他去世时保障家人的生活。为了节省保费，Gordon 提议只购买一份 $300,000 的基础定期保单，并附加一份意外身故（AD）双倍赔付附加险，理由是他最担心的是突发死亡。两年后，Gordon 因突发心脏病去世。为什么 Gordon 的规划策略存在缺陷？如果他的保单是 $300,000 主险加 AD 附加险，他的受益人将获得多少赔付？

  1. ✓ 正确。需求分析的核心原则（value of coverage）是：遗属实际需要的钱不因被保险人死于意外还是疾病而改变，保额缺口应由基础保额全额覆盖，绝不能靠意外身故附加险（accidental death, AD rider）去凑数；同时心脏病发属于疾病而非意外，不触发 AD 双倍赔付（double indemnity），受益人只能拿到 $300,000 基础身故金。Correct. A core principle of needs analysis (value of coverage) is that survivors' capital needs do not change depending on whether death is caused by an accident or an illness, so the coverage gap must be fully covered by the base policy rather than supplemented with an accidental death (AD) rider; furthermore, a heart attack is an illness rather than an accident, so double indemnity is not triggered and the beneficiary receives only the $300,000 base death benefit.

  2. ✗ 错在「范围夸大」陷阱。意外身故附加险（accidental death, AD rider）严格区分外来的意外事故与内在疾病；突发心脏病属于疾病身故，绝不会触发 2 倍身故金的双倍赔付（double indemnity）。Incorrect. Falls into the Scope Inflation trap: An accidental death (AD) rider strictly distinguishes external accidental causes from internal illnesses; a sudden heart attack is a death from illness and never triggers double indemnity.

  3. ✗ 错在记反了意外身故（AD rider）的时间限制条件。AD 附加险要求死亡必须是意外所致，且发生在事故后的限定时间内（常见为 1 年内，within 1 year），而不是超过 1 年。Incorrect. Reverses the time limitation for an accidental death (AD) rider: The rider requires that death be caused by an accident and occur within a specified timeframe following the accident (commonly within 1 year), not after 1 year.

  4. ✗ 错在混淆险种边界。意外身故附加险（AD rider）与重疾给付（dread disease, DD benefit）是完全不同的条款，AD 不会因为死因属于「Big 4」重疾就自动变成重疾给付。Incorrect. Confuses coverage boundaries: An accidental death (AD) rider and a dread disease (DD) benefit are entirely separate provisions; the AD rider does not transform into a critical illness payout simply because the cause of death is a Big 4 illness.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Julian owns a $25,000 life insurance policy with an accidental death (AD) rider that provides double indemnity (2 times the face amount) if death results from an accident within 1 year of the event. Julian is injured in a car crash while fleeing the scene of a bank robbery he committed, and he dies from his injuries 10 days later. How much will the insurer pay under the AD rider, and why?

Julian 持有一份 $25,000 的寿险保单，并附带一份意外身故（AD）附加险，约定若在事故发生后 1 年内因意外导致身故可获双倍赔付（2 倍保额）。Julian 在实施银行抢劫后驾车逃逸途中发生车祸受伤，并于 10 天后伤重不治身亡。保险公司在 AD 附加险项下会赔付多少，为什么？

  1. ✗ 错在忽略了意外身故附加险（accidental death, AD rider）的合同除外条款（limitations and exclusions）。虽然死亡发生在 1 年限期内，但因犯罪行为导致的事故属于明确除外情形。Incorrect. Overlooks contractual exclusions under the accidental death (AD) rider: Although death occurred within the 1-year limit, accidents resulting from criminal acts are explicitly excluded.

  2. ✗ 错在编造按比例扣减规则。一旦触发除外责任（exclusions），意外身故附加险（AD rider）直接拒赔，不存在部分折算赔付。Incorrect. Fabricates a proportional reduction rule: Once an exclusion is triggered, the accidental death (AD) rider denies the claim entirely; no partial or prorated payout exists.

  3. ✗ 错在把子女附加险的新生儿 15 天起保等待期生搬硬套到成人车祸理赔上，混淆了不同附加条款的规则。Incorrect. Misapplies the 15-day newborn waiting period from child riders to an adult vehicular collision claim, confusing provisions between completely different riders.

  4. ✓ 正确。意外身故附加险（accidental death, AD rider）在合同订立时就将自杀、自伤、战争以及犯罪行为（criminal acts）列为明确除外情形（limitations and exclusions）；Julian 在实施犯罪逃逸时发生车祸身亡，即使在 1 年限期内，AD 附加险也不予赔付。Correct. Contracts for accidental death (AD) riders explicitly list suicide, self-inflicted injury, war, and criminal acts as exclusions; because Julian died in a vehicle crash while fleeing a crime, the AD rider pays no benefit even though death occurred within the 1-year timeframe.

答案Answer：d

第 7 题Q7第三人附加险单独核保与收费方式区分Separate underwriting for third-party riders and identifying cost structures★★陷阱：误以为买主险就自动免核保保配偶及豁免必免费

When Simon applies for a whole life insurance policy on his own life, he wants to attach a 10-year term insurance rider covering his wife, Rachel, as well as a waiver of premium for total disability benefit. Simon assumes that because he is already undergoing medical underwriting for the base policy, Rachel will be covered automatically without underwriting, and that the waiver of premium is always included free of charge. How should the advisor correct Simon?

当 Simon 为自己申请一份终身寿险保单时，他希望在保单上附加一份覆盖妻子 Rachel 的 10 年期定期险附加险，以及一项完全伤残豁免保费条款。Simon 以为既然自己已经为主险接受了医学核保，Rachel 就会自动获得保障而无需核保，并且以为保费豁免条款总是免费包含在内的。顾问应当如何纠正 Simon？

  1. ✗ 错在忽略了第三人核保规则。当定期险附加险（term insurance rider）覆盖的是主险被保险人以外的其他人（如配偶 Rachel）时，该被保险人必须单独接受核保。Incorrect. Overlooks third-party underwriting rules: When a term insurance rider covers someone other than the base life insured (such as spouse Rachel), that individual must undergo separate underwriting.

  2. ✗ 错在「定价误解（cost of coverage）」陷阱。完全伤残豁免保费（waiver of premium for total disability）未必免费，在许多合同中它是需要另外付费选购的附约。Incorrect. Falls into the Cost of Coverage Misunderstanding trap: A waiver of premium for total disability is not necessarily free; in many contracts, it is an optional rider requiring an additional premium.

  3. ✓ 正确。核保阶段（underwriting）的规则是：覆盖被保险人本人的定期险附加险通常沿用主险核保结果，但覆盖配偶或其他人的定期险附加险（term insurance rider）必须对该「其他人（Rachel）」单独核保；同时不能凭名字判断收费方式（cost of coverage），保费豁免可能是另行收费的附约，报价时须逐项核对。Correct. Underwriting rules dictate that while a term rider on the primary insured typically shares base policy underwriting, a term rider covering a spouse or third party (Rachel) requires separate underwriting on that individual; additionally, pricing cannot be deduced from feature names alone, as waiver of premium may carry an extra premium and each cost must be verified on the quote.

  4. ✗ 错在捏造投保禁令。定期险附加险（term insurance rider）或家庭附加险可以覆盖配偶，前提是配偶本人按合同要求单独通过核保。Incorrect. Fabricates an underwriting prohibition: A term insurance rider or family rider can cover a spouse, provided that the spouse separately satisfies contract underwriting requirements.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

While reviewing two life insurance quotes, Paula notices that Policy X includes a terminal illness (TI) benefit at no extra itemized fee, whereas Policy Y lists a waiver of premium benefit with a separate monthly charge. Paula asks her advisor whether all supplementary benefits are supposed to be free simply because they are called "benefits" rather than "riders". What is the correct explanation?

在比较两份寿险报价单时，Paula 注意到保单 X 包含一项不额外单列收费的绝症给付（TI），而保单 Y 则将保费豁免列为一项单独按月收费的条款。Paula 询问顾问，是不是所有被称为「补充保障（benefits）」而不是「附加险（riders）」的条款都应该是免费的。正确的解释是什么？

  1. ✗ 错在「定价误解（cost of coverage）」陷阱。不能按「附加险（rider）」或「补充保障（supplementary benefit）」的名称来判断收费方式。Incorrect. Falls into the Cost of Coverage Misunderstanding trap: One cannot assume pricing structure based solely on whether an item is labelled a rider or a supplementary benefit.

  2. ✓ 正确。不能凭「rider / supplementary benefit」的名称判断收费方式（cost of coverage）：某些绝症给付（TI benefit）内建在基础合同里（成本已计入主险保费），而保费豁免（waiver of premium）常作为另外付费选购的附约；顾问报价时应将基础保费、每项附约费用及内建权益逐一对应说明。Correct. Cost of coverage cannot be judged by labels like rider or supplementary benefit: some terminal illness (TI) benefits are built into the base contract (with costs absorbed into the base premium), while waiver of premium often requires an additional optional charge; advisors must itemize base premiums, optional rider fees, and built-in features separately on quotes.

  3. ✗ 错在「定价误解」陷阱。保费豁免（waiver of premium）在多数公开产品指南中属于可选付费保障，并非一律免费。Incorrect. Falls into the Cost of Coverage Misunderstanding trap: Waiver of premium is an optional, fee-bearing benefit in most published product guides, rather than universally free.

  4. ✗ 错在混淆了保单年费（annual policy fee，$50–$100）与内建保障成本。内建免费附加保障的成本已经包含在主险保费中，不会按每个内建条款重复收取保单管理年费。Incorrect. Confuses the annual policy fee ($50 to $100) with built-in coverage costs: Costs for built-in benefits are already included in the base premium, and insurers do not charge duplicate annual policy fees for each built-in provision.
