本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 场景匹配题（财务目标 × 四类风险）：给出一个家庭或个人的突发事件（如早逝、短期骨折、癌症确诊、年老需要护理），考你识别这属于哪一类风险、冲击哪一个财务目标（财富积累、退休收入或日常需求），并区分一般死亡风险（寿险）与意外身故责任（AD&D）。Scenario-matching questions (financial goals x four risk categories): Given an unexpected event for a family or individual (such as premature death, a short-term fracture, a cancer diagnosis, or needing care in old age), tests identifying which risk category it belongs to, which financial goal it impacts (wealth accumulation, retirement income, or daily family needs), and distinguishing general mortality risk (life insurance) from accidental death coverage (AD&D).
  • 需求分类与产品对号题（四大需求）：给出客户想要保护的对象（月度现金流、日常储蓄、已积累资产/遗产、企业固定开支），考你区分保收入（DI）、保储蓄（扩展医疗/牙科/旅行险）、保资产（CI/LTC）与保企业（BOE/关键人险/买卖协议伤残险）。Needs classification and product-matching questions (four core needs): Given what the client wants to protect (monthly cash flow, routine savings, accumulated assets or estate, or business overhead expenses), tests distinguishing protecting income (DI), protecting savings (extended health, dental, and travel insurance), protecting assets (CI and LTC), and protecting business (BOE, key person insurance, or buy-sell disability insurance).
  • 多来源给付先后与协调题（先付人逻辑）：给出工伤或非工伤、短期或长期伤残场景，考你判断政府项目（工伤补偿、EI 病假福利、CPP-D、省级医保）与团体/个人商业保险谁先赔、谁补缺口，以及为什么不能把各来源给付简单相加。Multi-source benefit order and coordination questions (first payor rule): Given a work-related or non-work-related, short-term or long-term disability scenario, tests determining whether government programs (workers' compensation, EI sickness benefits, CPP-D, provincial health coverage) or group/individual private insurance pay first, which fills the gap, and why multiple benefit sources cannot simply be added together.
  • 企业风险与管理策略辨析题：给出中小企业主面临的经营困境或健康冲击，考你区分系统性风险（市场/经济波动，A&S 不保）与非系统性风险（业主健康等企业特有风险，A&S 可保），以及风险管理四招（避免、减少、自留、转移）的应用。Business risk and management strategy distinction questions: Given operational challenges or health shocks faced by small and medium-sized business owners, tests distinguishing systematic risk (market and economic fluctuations, not covered by A&S) from unsystematic risk (business-specific risks such as owner health, coverable by A&S), and applying the four risk management techniques (avoidance, reduction, retention, and transfer).
  • 考试口径与双口径数字题：针对 EI 病假福利周数（考试口径 15 周 vs 现行 26 周）、工伤补偿替代比例（考试口径 80–90% 区间）及 CPP-D 判定标准（严重且持久），考你在考场按考试口径作答并识别时间轴缺口。Exam standard and dual-benchmark numerical questions: Focusing on EI sickness benefit weeks (exam standard of 15 weeks vs. current 26 weeks), workers' compensation wage-loss replacement ratio (exam standard of 80% to 90%), and CPP-D qualification criteria (severe and prolonged), tests answering according to the exam standard in the exam room and identifying timeline gaps.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 伤残与重疾判定混淆Confusing disability and critical illness claim triggers

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常把“确诊大病”等同于“不能工作”，忽略伤残收入保险（DI）看的是工作能力是否丧失，而重疾险（CI）看的是是否符合合同清单内的疾病诊断定义，两套标准互相独立。Candidates often equate being diagnosed with a critical illness with being unable to work, overlooking that disability income insurance (DI) assesses the loss of working capacity, while critical illness insurance (CI) requires meeting the diagnosis definition of a covered condition in the contract schedule, and the two claim criteria are completely independent.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现客户被诊断出某种严重疾病但仍能远程或兼职工作，或因背痛/骨折暂时无法工作但未达重疾列表标准。The question stem describes a client diagnosed with a severe illness who can still work remotely or part-time, or a client temporarily unable to work due to back pain or a fracture without meeting any covered critical illness definition.

⚠ 多来源给付简单相加Simply adding multiple benefit sources together

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到客户同时有政府福利（EI / CPP-D / 工伤补偿）、团体险和个人保单时，直接把几笔金额加总当成到手收入，漏看先付人顺序、全来源上限（all-source maximum）与抵消条款。When seeing that a client has government benefits (EI, CPP-D, or workers' compensation), group insurance, and individual policies simultaneously, candidates directly add the amounts together as take-home income, overlooking first payor order, all-source maximum limits, and offset clauses.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干列出两项或以上收入替代来源（如同时提及工伤与 EI、或团体 LTD 与 CPP-D），问客户实际能拿到多少或谁先赔。The question stem lists two or more income replacement sources (such as mentioning both workers' compensation and EI, or group LTD and CPP-D), asking how much the client actually receives or which pays first.

⚠ 保储蓄与保资产错位Confusing protecting savings with protecting assets

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 两者都在防止“钱被花掉”，考生容易把应对日常药费/牙科账单的扩展医疗险（保储蓄）与应对大额一次性或长期照护支出的 CI / LTC（保资产/遗产）混为一谈。Because both prevent money from being drained, candidates easily confuse extended health insurance for routine prescription drug and dental bills (protecting savings) with CI or LTC for large lump-sum or long-term care expenses (protecting assets and estates).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干对比“日常非医保覆盖的处方药/理疗开销”与“需要变卖退休投资或房产来支付的重疾康复/长期护理费用”。The question stem compares routine non-government-covered prescription drug and physiotherapy expenses with needing to liquidate retirement investments or real estate to pay for critical illness recovery or long-term care costs.

⚠ 企业固定开支套用个人重疾Using personal critical illness insurance for business overhead

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为业主买了一份个人重疾险（CI）拿到一次性给付，就等于解决了企业停摆期间的房租和员工工资，忽略家庭生活康复支出与企业持续固定开支混用会导致两边都不够。Candidates assume that an owner buying a personal critical illness (CI) policy with a lump-sum payout solves business lease and employee wage obligations during a shutdown, overlooking that blending family living and recovery costs with ongoing business overhead leaves neither adequately funded.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干主角是小企业主或自雇人士，既担心家庭房贷生活费，又担心自己生病或伤残期间店面租金和员工薪水无法支付。The question stem features a small business owner or self-employed person who worries about both home mortgage living costs and paying commercial rent and employee wages during an illness or disability.

⚠ EI 病假周数双口径错位Dual-benchmark confusion on EI sickness benefit weeks

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 实务中联邦已将 EI 病假福利延长至 26 周，但 LLQP 考试口径仍沿用 15 周旧值，考生若在考场默认按 26 周计算短期收入中断时间轴，会选错衔接长期伤残险的等待期或缺口周数。In practice the federal government extended EI sickness benefits to 26 weeks, but the LLQP exam standard still uses the 15-week rule; if candidates default to 26 weeks on the exam when calculating short-term income interruption timelines, they will select the wrong waiting period or gap weeks before long-term disability kicks in.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及非工伤短期失能，询问 EI 病假福利可覆盖的最长周数，或计算雇员在团体 LTD 启动前的政府给付周期。The question stem involves a non-work-related short-term disability, asking for the maximum weeks covered by EI sickness benefits, or calculating the government benefit duration before group LTD starts.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
三大财务目标与四类风险矩阵Financial goals and four risk categories matrixfinancial goals and four risk categories (mortality, disability, illness, longevity)个人与家庭追求财富积累、退休收入和家庭日常需求三大目标，会受死亡、伤残、疾病、长寿四类风险冲击；一般早逝主要由寿险承担，A&S 的 AD&D 仅保意外身故。Individuals and families pursue three primary financial goals—wealth accumulation, retirement income, and daily family needs—which are threatened by four risk categories: mortality, disability, illness, and longevity; premature death from any cause is primarily covered by life insurance, while AD&D in A&S covers only accidental death.
长寿风险的本质Nature of longevity risklongevity risk长寿风险指活得比资产和储蓄能支撑的时间更久，是四类风险中唯一看似“好事”但会稀释退休积累、侵蚀遗产或转嫁护理成本的财务风险。Longevity risk refers to outliving accumulated assets and savings; it is the only one among the four risk categories that appears positive on the surface, but financially threatens retirement accumulation, erodes the estate, or shifts long-term care costs onto family members.
企业系统性与非系统性风险Systematic and unsystematic business riskssystematic risk vs. unsystematic risk (business risk)利率、行业周期等系统性风险不属于 A&S 承保范围；业主或关键员工健康受损属于非系统性（企业特有）风险，可通过自留（self-funding）与投保组合应对。Systematic risks, such as interest rate fluctuations and industry economic cycles, fall outside the scope of A&S insurance; health impairment of an owner or key employee is an unsystematic (business-specific) risk that can be managed through a combination of risk retention (self-funding) and insurance coverage.
EI 病假福利双口径规则Dual-benchmark rules for EI sickness benefitsEmployment Insurance (EI) sickness benefitsEI 病假福利按收入 55% 替代，考试口径最长给付 15 周（1 周等待期后，旧上限 $638/周），而 2026 现行政策为最长 26 周（上限 $729/周），考试作答须服从考试口径与题干。EI sickness benefits replace 55% of insurable earnings; the exam standard provides a maximum benefit period of 15 weeks (after a one-week waiting period, with an earlier maximum of $638 per week), whereas the 2026 current rule provides up to 26 weeks (maximum $729 per week); candidates must follow the exam standard and specific question stems on the exam.
CPP 伤残给付判定与起付CPP disability benefit qualification and commencementCPP disability benefit (CPP-D)申领 CPP-D 须未满 65 岁、满足缴费年限（通常近 6 年满 4 年，或满 25 年缴费时近 6 年满 3 年），且伤残须“严重且持久”（severe and prolonged），通常在认定伤残开始四个月后起付。Qualifying for CPP-D requires being under age 65, meeting contribution criteria (generally 4 of the last 6 years, or 3 of the last 6 years with at least 25 years of contributions), and having a disability that is severe and prolonged; benefits typically begin four months after the month ESDC determines the severe and prolonged disability started.
工伤补偿与先付人逻辑Workers' compensation and first payor ruleWorkers' Compensation and first payor rule多来源给付先看触发原因：工伤或职业病优先归工伤补偿（考试口径替代净收入 80–90%，BC 现行 WorkSafeBC 约 90%），通常不再走 EI；各来源须分别核对全来源上限与抵消条款。When coordinating multiple benefit sources, determine the triggering cause first: workplace injuries or occupational diseases are covered primarily by workers' compensation (exam standard replaces 80% to 90% of net earnings; BC WorkSafeBC pays about 90%), and the claimant usually does not receive EI; each source must be separately reviewed for all-source maximum limits and offset clauses.
风险管理四招Four risk management techniquesrisk avoidance, reduction, retention, and transfer风险管理包含避免（不参与高风险活动）、减少（健康管理/安全培训）、自留（应急储备/拉长等待期）与转移（购买 A&S 保险），评估需求时应先审视前三招再确定转移额度。Risk management comprises avoidance (refraining from high-risk activities), reduction (health management and safety training), retention (emergency savings and lengthening waiting periods), and transfer (purchasing A&S insurance); advisors should evaluate the first three techniques before determining the amount of risk to transfer.
A&S 四大需求与产品映射Four A&S need categories and product mappingprotecting income, savings, assets, and businessA&S 四大需求分别为：保收入（个人/团体 DI）、保储蓄（扩展医疗/牙科/旅行险）、保资产（重疾险 CI 与长期护理险 LTC）、保企业（营运费用险 BOE、关键人险、买卖协议伤残险）。The four A&S need categories are: protecting income (individual and group DI), protecting savings (extended health, dental, and travel insurance), protecting assets (critical illness insurance CI and long-term care insurance LTC), and protecting business (business overhead expense BOE, key person insurance, and buy-sell disability insurance).
伤残定义四级光谱Four-level spectrum of disability definitionsown occupation, regular occupation, any occupation, CPP total disability衡量伤残是否达到“不能工作”的标准按宽松到严格分为四级：本职工作（own occupation）→ 常规职业（regular occupation）→ 任何职业（any occupation）→ CPP 完全伤残，越靠后获赔门槛越高。The standards defining an inability to work range from most liberal to most restrictive across four levels: own occupation -> regular occupation -> any occupation -> CPP total disability; the further along the spectrum, the higher the threshold required to qualify for benefits.
自雇人士与平台劳动者的政府保障边界Government benefit boundaries for self-employed and platform workersself-employed EI special benefits and platform workers coverage自雇者默认不参加 EI 特别福利，须主动注册且通常满 12 个月方可申领；但 BC 自 2024-09-03 起将网约车及配送平台劳动者纳入 WorkSafeBC 工伤制度。Self-employed individuals are excluded by default from EI special benefits and must register voluntarily and usually wait 12 months before claiming; however, British Columbia has included app-based ride-hail and delivery platform workers under the WorkSafeBC workers' compensation system effective September 3, 2024.

第 1 题Q1财务目标与长寿风险识别Identifying financial goals and longevity risk★陷阱：长寿被当成不算风险

Arthur and Beatrice, a married couple approaching age 65, are reviewing their financial plan with their advisor. They have accumulated a retirement portfolio designed to support their expected retirement years, but longevity runs in both of their families and Beatrice's elderly mother is currently living in a private care facility. If Arthur and Beatrice live much longer than expected and exhaust their savings while requiring ongoing assistance with daily living, which risk category and primary financial goal are directly threatened?

即将年满 65 岁的 Arthur 和 Beatrice 夫妇正在与顾问审视他们的理财计划。他们积累的退休投资组合预计能支撑预期退休年限的生活，但双方家族都有长寿基因，Beatrice 年迈的母亲目前住在私人护理机构。如果 Arthur 和 Beatrice 的实际寿命远超预期，在需要持续日常照护的同时耗尽积蓄，这直接威胁到哪一类风险和哪一个核心财务目标？

  1. ✗ 错误（陷阱：混淆早逝风险与长寿风险）。死亡风险（mortality risk）指早逝导致遗属失去收入来源，而题干描述的是活得超过资产可支撑年限。Incorrect. Falls into the trap of confusing premature mortality risk with longevity risk. Mortality risk refers to premature death depriving dependents of income, whereas the question stem describes outliving the lifespan that assets can support.

  2. ✓ 正确。长寿风险（longevity risk）的定义正是“活得比资产/储蓄能支撑的时间更久”，会导致退休资金在预期寿命前耗尽（威胁退休收入 retirement income），且长期护理费用会侵蚀已积累资产或转嫁给下一代。Correct. Longevity risk is defined as outliving accumulated assets and savings, which causes retirement funds to run out before life expectancy (threatening retirement income), while ongoing long-term care costs erode accumulated wealth or pass the financial burden onto the next generation.

  3. ✗ 错误（陷阱：混淆个人长寿风险与企业系统性风险）。系统性风险（systematic risk）指利率或经济周期等企业外部市场风险，与个人退休资产因长寿耗尽无关。Incorrect. Confuses personal longevity risk with business systematic risk. Systematic risk refers to external market risks such as interest rates or economic cycles, which are unrelated to exhausting personal retirement assets by living a very long life.

  4. ✗ 错误（陷阱：混淆短期伤残与退休长寿风险）。短期伤残风险针对的是工作期间的劳动收入中断，而非退休后活得比储蓄支撑期更久。Incorrect. Confuses short-term disability with retirement longevity risk. Short-term disability risk addresses interruption of employment earnings during working years, not living longer than accumulated savings can support in retirement.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Evelyn, an adult under age 65, tells her insurance advisor that she is only worried about dying too soon or becoming disabled before age 65. She believes that living a very long life past her life expectancy is purely a blessing and poses no financial risk to her retirement plan or the estate she hopes to leave her children. How should the advisor correct Evelyn's understanding?

未满 65 岁的 Evelyn 告诉保险顾问，她只担心自己过早去世或在 65 岁前伤残。她认为活得远超预期寿命纯粹是福气，不会对她的退休计划或希望留给子女的遗产构成任何财务风险。顾问应如何纠正 Evelyn 的理解？

  1. ✗ 错误（陷阱：把长寿当成不算风险）。长寿是四类核心风险之一，需求分析必须涵盖退休后的长寿与护理风险。Incorrect. Falls into the trap of treating longevity as non-existent risk. Longevity is one of the four core risk categories, and a comprehensive needs analysis must address post-retirement longevity and long-term care risks.

  2. ✗ 错误（陷阱：概念混用）。活得久属于长寿风险（longevity risk），而非早逝风险（mortality risk），也不能靠意外身故保险解决。Incorrect. Conflates distinct risk concepts. Living a long life is longevity risk rather than mortality risk, and it cannot be solved with accidental death insurance.

  3. ✓ 正确。长寿风险（longevity risk）是四类风险中唯一表面看似“好事”的一类，但活得比资产能支撑的时间更久会耗尽退休收入，且年迈时的长期护理支出会侵蚀原本留给下一代的资产。Correct. Longevity risk is the only one among the four risk categories that appears positive on the surface, but outliving the lifespan that assets can support exhausts retirement income, and eldercare expenses in later life erode assets intended for the next generation.

  4. ✗ 错误（陷阱：胡乱套用政府短期福利）。EI 病假福利仅针对符合条件的就业人员短期疾病收入中断，不负责补贴年老时耗尽退休储蓄的人员。Incorrect. Inappropriately applies a short-term government benefit. EI sickness benefits only replace short-term earnings interruption due to illness for qualifying workers; they do not subsidize individuals who exhaust retirement savings in old age.

答案Answer：c

第 2 题Q2企业系统性与非系统性风险Distinguishing systematic and unsystematic business risks★★陷阱：系统性与非系统性混淆

Logan owns a custom cabinetry manufacturing company with salaried employees. Over the past year, he has become concerned about two potential threats: (1) a province-wide housing market slowdown and rising interest rates that could sharply reduce customer orders, and (2) the possibility that he suffers a severe stroke, leaving the business unable to pay its monthly factory lease and staff wages while he recovers. How are these two threats classified in an A&S business needs analysis?

一名顾问正在向一位外科专科医生解释为什么不同的伤残收入保单会有不同的保费费率和理赔门槛。顾问引用了贯穿 LLQP 课程的伤残定义四级光谱。按从最宽松（索赔人最容易获赔）到最严格（获赔门槛最高）排序，这条光谱的正确顺序是什么？

  1. ✗ 错误（陷阱：将最严格到最宽松的顺序完全写反）。CPP 完全伤残（严重且持久）门槛最高，本职工作（own occupation）门槛最宽松，顺序颠倒。Incorrect. Completely reverses the spectrum from most restrictive to most liberal. CPP total disability (severe and prolonged) has the highest threshold, while own occupation is the most liberal, so this order is inverted.

  2. ✓ 正确。衡量“不能工作”的四级定义光谱从最宽松到最严格依次为：本职工作（own occupation）→ 常规职业（regular occupation）→ 任何职业（any occupation）→ CPP 完全伤残（CPP total disability），光谱越靠后，获赔门槛越高。Correct. The four-level spectrum defining inability to work ranks from most liberal to most restrictive as follows: own occupation -> regular occupation -> any occupation -> CPP total disability; the further along the spectrum, the higher the qualification threshold.

  3. ✗ 错误（陷阱：打乱中间与两端等级）。本职工作（own occupation）比常规职业（regular occupation）更宽松，且 CPP 完全伤残比一般商业保单的 any occupation 更严格。Incorrect. Scrambles the middle and end tiers of the spectrum. Own occupation is more liberal than regular occupation, and CPP total disability is substantially more restrictive than the commercial any occupation definition.

  4. ✗ 错误（陷阱：混淆商业保单三级定义的宽严顺序）。在商业伤残险中，any occupation（不能从事任何合适工作）比 own occupation（不能从事本职工作）严格得多。Incorrect. Confuses the relative stringency of commercial disability definitions. In commercial disability policies, any occupation (unable to work in any suitable job) is significantly more restrictive than own occupation (unable to perform the duties of one's specific occupation).

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Dr. Lin is an orthopedic surgeon whose hands are injured in an accident. She can no longer perform surgery, but she is medically able to work full-time as a university medical lecturer or hospital administrator. Under which disability definition on the four-level spectrum would Dr. Lin have the LOWEST threshold (greatest likelihood) to qualify for disability benefits despite her ability to teach?

Lin 医生是一名骨科外科医生，在一次事故中双手受伤。她不能再做手术，但医学上可以全职担任大学医学讲师或医院行政人员。在四级光谱的哪一种伤残定义下，尽管 Lin 医生能够教书，她获赔的门槛最低（最容易获得伤残给付）？

  1. ✗ 错误（陷阱：选了光谱中最严格的一端）。CPP 完全伤残要求“严重且持久”，经常不能从事任何实质有收益的工作，既然她能全职教书或做行政，极难满足 CPP-D。Incorrect. Selects the most restrictive end of the spectrum. CPP total disability requires a severe and prolonged disability preventing the claimant from regularly pursuing any substantially gainful occupation; because she can work full-time teaching or managing hospital administration, she would not qualify for CPP-D.

  2. ✗ 错误（陷阱：混淆 any occupation 与 own occupation）。在任何职业（any occupation）定义下，只要她能从事教育或资历相符的其他工作（如医学院讲师），就可能不获赔。Incorrect. Confuses any occupation with own occupation. Under an any occupation definition, as long as she is medically able to perform other commensurate work suited by education and training (such as teaching at a medical school), she would not qualify for benefits.

  3. ✗ 错误（陷阱：混入非伤残收入定义）。扩展医疗险（extended health insurance）报销医疗账单，不属于伤残收入险的四级工作能力定义光谱。Incorrect. Introduces a non-disability definition. Extended health care reimburses eligible medical expenses and does not belong to the four-level disability spectrum measuring working capacity.

  4. ✓ 正确。在四级定义光谱（own occupation → regular occupation → any occupation → CPP total disability）中，本职工作（own occupation）位于最宽松的一端，只看被保险人能否继续从事其特定本职（外科手术），获赔门槛最低。Correct. Across the four-level spectrum (own occupation -> regular occupation -> any occupation -> CPP total disability), own occupation sits at the most liberal end, requiring only that the insured cannot perform the essential duties of her specific occupation (orthopedic surgery), representing the lowest threshold to qualify for benefits.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

第 7 题Q7企业开支与个人重疾分账Separating business overhead expenses from personal critical illness insurance★★★陷阱：企业固定开支套用个人重疾

Victor owns a thriving dental clinic with ongoing monthly fixed overhead expenses (staff salaries, equipment leases, and commercial rent) and also has ongoing monthly family living expenses at home. He tells his advisor that he plans to buy a single personal Critical Illness (CI) policy and skip Business Overhead Expense (BOE) insurance, reasoning that a CI lump sum has no spending restrictions and can easily cover both his clinic and his family if anything happens to his health. What is the primary flaw in Victor's plan?

Victor 拥有一家生意兴隆的牙科诊所，每月有持续的固定营运开支（员工工资、设备租赁和商业房租），同时家里每月还有持续的家庭生活开支。他告诉顾问，他打算只买一份个人重疾险（CI）保单，不买企业营运费用险（BOE），理由是 CI 一次性给付没有用途限制，万一健康出问题可以轻松同时覆盖诊所和家庭。Victor 计划的主要漏洞是什么？

  1. ✗ 错误（陷阱：编造法律用途禁令）。若个人重疾险（CI）合同没有用途限制，被保险人拿到一次性给付后确实可以把部分款项用于企业房租与工资，不能说只有 BOE 才能把钱用于企业。Incorrect. Fabricates a statutory spending restriction. When a personal critical illness (CI) contract contains no restrictions on fund usage, the insured can indeed spend part of the payout on commercial rent and wages; using proceeds for business purposes is not legally prohibited.

  2. ✗ 错误（陷阱：误以为政府项目保企业开支）。没有任何政府项目直接覆盖企业的房租、员工工资等固定开支，必须靠商业保险或自留解决。Incorrect. Mistakenly assumes government programs pay business overhead. No government program covers commercial rent or employee salaries; business overhead must be addressed through commercial insurance or self-funding.

  3. ✓ 正确。把个人重疾险当成保企业的唯一方案有两个核心问题：① 触发条件不同——导致无法工作的伤残未必符合重疾险（CI）清单内的疾病定义，而企业营运费用险（BOE）按伤残触发并按月报销合资格固定开支；② 即使触发 CI，同一笔定额给付既要应付家庭生活与康复，又要填补诊所持续的房租与工资等固定开支，可能不足以同时覆盖两边。顾问应将家庭现金流与企业固定开支分列评估。Correct. Relying on personal critical illness insurance as the sole business solution has two fundamental flaws: first, the claim triggers differ—a disabling injury or illness preventing work may not meet the strict definitions of covered conditions in a CI contract, whereas Business Overhead Expense (BOE) is triggered by disability and reimburses eligible overhead monthly; second, even if CI is triggered, a single lump sum forced to cover both family living and recovery costs as well as ongoing clinic rent and payroll will likely leave both underfunded. Advisors must assess personal cash flow and business overhead separately.

  4. ✗ 错误（陷阱：混淆 BOE 与 CI 的给付方式）。BOE 是在业主伤残期间按合同实际报销合资格企业固定开支（有独立等待期、月限额及最长给付期），而非确诊重疾时一次性定额给付。Incorrect. Confuses the benefit structure of BOE with CI. BOE reimburses eligible business overhead expenses incurred during the owner's disability under policy terms (with a distinct waiting period, monthly maximum, and benefit duration), rather than paying a lump sum upon critical illness diagnosis.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Camille owns a small bakery in Richmond. She recently suffered a covered critical illness and received a lump-sum payout from her personal Critical Illness (CI) policy. Because the policy has no restriction on how she uses the money, she is using it to pay the bakery's rent and bakers' wages while also paying her household mortgage and medical recovery costs. When reviewing this case as a lesson for other small business owners, how should an advisor compare personal CI with Business Overhead Expense (BOE) insurance?

Camille 在列治文拥有一家小型烘焙店。她最近患上受保的重大疾病，并从个人重疾险（CI）保单中获得了一笔一次性给付。由于保单对资金用途没有限制，她用这笔钱支付烘焙店的房租和面包师工资，同时支付家里的房贷和医疗康复费用。在将此案例作为其他小企业主的借鉴时，顾问应如何比较个人 CI 与企业营运费用险（BOE）？

  1. ✓ 正确。正如讲解页案例 C（列治文小餐馆老板 Mireille）所示，无用途限制的 CI 给付虽可用于支付店租与工资，但同一笔钱兼顾家庭生活康复与企业固定开支容易捉襟见肘；BOE 专门按合同报销合资格企业固定开支（含独立等待期、月限额及最长给付期），推荐时必须把家庭现金流和企业固定开支分账评估。Correct. As demonstrated in Case Study C, unrestricted CI payouts can legally be used to pay commercial rent and staff wages, but using one fixed lump sum for both household recovery living costs and ongoing business overhead easily leads to shortfalls; BOE specifically reimburses eligible business fixed expenses monthly under defined terms (including an elimination period, monthly maximum, and benefit period), so advisors must assess family cash flow and business overhead separately.

  2. ✗ 错误（陷阱：虚构保单失效规定）。个人 CI 一次性给付通常无用途限制，用于企业不会导致保单追溯失效，真正的问题在于保额够不够分摊以及触发条件是否匹配。Incorrect. Fabricates a policy voidance penalty. Personal CI lump sums generally carry no restrictions on use, and spending proceeds on business expenses does not void the policy retroactively; the real risk is benefit exhaustion and mismatched claim triggers.

  3. ✗ 错误（陷阱：把个人生活开支塞进 BOE）。BOE 仅报销合资格的企业固定营运费用（如店租、员工工资），不报销业主个人的家庭房贷和买菜开销。Incorrect. Inappropriately includes personal living expenses under BOE. BOE reimburses only eligible business operating overhead (such as commercial rent and non-owner staff wages), never personal household mortgages or grocery bills.

  4. ✗ 错误（陷阱：忽略企业保单权属核查）。若采用企业持有的重疾安排，也必须严格核对保单所有人（policyowner）及收款人（beneficiary）设置。Incorrect. Overlooks ownership and beneficiary verification for corporate-owned insurance. If a corporate-owned critical illness structure is implemented, the advisor must strictly verify policyowner and beneficiary designations.
