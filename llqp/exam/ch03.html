① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 分红机制与红利选项辨析题：给出客户健康恶化但想增加终身保额、或误以为分红是合同承诺的固定回报的情景，考查参与分红（participating, par）的董事会自由裁量属性及购买增额已缴清保险（paid-up additions, PUA）免核保的核心优势。Dividend mechanics and dividend option distinction questions: Presenting scenarios where a client's health deteriorates but they wish to increase lifetime coverage, or mistakenly believe dividends are contractually promised fixed returns, testing the board discretion nature of participating (par) policies and the core advantage of purchasing paid-up additions (PUA) without medical underwriting.
  • 现金退保价值与保单贷款计算题：给出保单现金退保价值（cash surrender value, CSV）、最高贷款比例（考试口径常见例为 90%）及未偿还贷款本息，考查受让人或受益人在退保或被保险人身故时实际能拿到的净额。Cash surrender value and policy loan calculation questions: Providing a policy's cash surrender value (CSV), the maximum loan percentage (the standard exam benchmark commonly cites 90%), and outstanding loan principal and interest, testing the net amount an assignee or beneficiary actually receives upon surrender or the death of the life insured.
  • 停缴保费与不丧失利益四选一情景题：描述投保人停止缴费后的不同诉求（如不想再缴费但想终身保留小额保障，或想在有限期内保住原始保额），考查现金退保、自动垫缴保费（automatic premium loan, APL）、减额已缴清保险（reduced paid-up insurance）与展期定期保险（extended term insurance）的精准匹配。Premium cessation and four non-forfeiture option scenario questions: Describing different policyholder objectives after stopping premium payments (such as wanting to permanently stop paying while maintaining a reduced lifetime benefit, or wanting to keep the full original face amount for a limited period), testing the precise matching among cash surrender, automatic premium loan (APL), reduced paid-up insurance, and extended term insurance.
  • T-100 与终身寿险的产品定位对比题：给出遗产税覆盖、确定金额慈善捐赠或需要生前应急提现的客户案例，考查 T-100（Term-100）平准保险成本（level cost of insurance, LCOI）、通常无现金退保价值及失效后无不丧失利益缓冲的特征。Product positioning comparison questions between Term-100 and whole life insurance: Presenting client cases involving estate tax coverage, fixed-amount charitable donations, or emergency living cash access needs, testing Term-100 (T-100) features including level cost of insurance (LCOI), the typical absence of cash surrender value, and the lack of non-forfeiture benefit buffers upon lapse.
  • 保费抵消（premium offset）与双栏披露合规题：还原利率下行导致预计停缴年限顺延的纠纷场景，考查消失保费（vanishing premium）的非保证本质以及保单演示件（illustration）与年度对账单并列展示保证栏与非保证栏的合规要求。Premium offset and dual-column disclosure compliance questions: Reconstructing dispute scenarios where falling interest rates push back projected premium-stop years, testing the non-guaranteed nature of vanishing premiums and the compliance requirement that policy illustrations and annual statements display guaranteed and non-guaranteed columns side by side.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 混淆合同参与权与分红金额Confusing contractual participation rights with dividend amounts

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到分红保单（participating policy）写进合同，就误以为每年分红金额或分红率（dividend scale interest rate）也是合同保证的固定回报。Seeing that a participating policy is written into the contract, candidates mistakenly assume that annual dividend amounts or the dividend scale interest rate are also contractually guaranteed fixed returns.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干或选项出现 guaranteed dividend、fixed annual return 或将分红率等同于累积生息账户存款利率的表述。The question stem or options include terms like guaranteed dividend, fixed annual return, or equate the dividend scale interest rate with a deposit rate on an accumulate-at-interest account.

⚠ 减额缴清与展期定期张冠李戴Mixing up reduced paid-up insurance and extended term insurance

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 两者都用现金退保价值（CSV）作趸缴保费且都不再缴费，考生容易搞混哪一个是保住原保额、哪一个是保住终身期限。Because both options use the cash surrender value (CSV) as a single premium and eliminate future premium payments, candidates easily confuse which one preserves the original face amount and which one preserves lifetime duration.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调客户停缴后希望 maintain the original face amount（选展期定期）还是 maintain permanent lifetime coverage（选减额已缴清）。The question stem emphasizes whether the client stopping premiums wishes to maintain the original face amount (extended term) or maintain permanent lifetime coverage (reduced paid-up).

⚠ 把 PUA 当成需核保的附加险Treating paid-up additions as an underwritten rider

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到保额增加就顺着直觉以为必须提交可保性证明（evidence of insurability）或另行支付附加险（rider）保费。Seeing the face amount increase, candidates intuitively assume the client must provide evidence of insurability or pay additional rider premiums out of pocket.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提到被保险人健康恶化（health has deteriorated）但仍想利用保单红利增加永久保额。The question stem mentions that the life insured's health has deteriorated, yet they still wish to use policy dividends to increase permanent coverage.

⚠ T-100 失效套用终身寿险缓冲Applying whole life lapse buffers to Term-100

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 因为 T-100 属于永久险且采用平准保费，考生误以为它停缴后也能触发自动垫缴保费（APL）或减额缴清等不丧失利益选项。Because Term-100 is classified as permanent insurance and uses level premiums, candidates mistakenly assume that stopping premiums can trigger non-forfeiture options like automatic premium loans (APL) or reduced paid-up insurance.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干明确保单为 Term-100（T-100）且投保人超过宽限期未缴保费，询问能否用保单价值垫缴或转为缴清保单。The question stem identifies the policy as Term-100 (T-100) and states the policyholder missed the premium beyond the grace period, asking if policy value can advance the premium or convert to paid-up coverage.

⚠ 保费抵消演示当成合同承诺Treating premium offset illustrations as contractual promises

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 被早期销售演示中某一年后无需再交保费的预测误导，以为达到演示年数后保费义务自动在法律上免除。Misled by early sales illustrations projecting that no further premiums would be required after a certain year, candidates believe premium obligations are legally extinguished once the illustrated year is reached.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 premium offset 或 vanishing premium，且提及宏观利率与分红率下调后保司要求继续缴费。The question stem mentions premium offset or vanishing premium, noting that following declines in market interest rates and dividend scales, the insurer requires ongoing premium payments.

保单准备金与平准保费机制Policy reserves and level premium mechanicspolicy reserve终身寿险通过早期多收保费建立保单准备金并连本带息滚存，用于补贴老年飙升的死亡成本，从而维持终身保费固定不变。Whole life insurance builds a policy reserve by collecting more premium than needed in early years, compounding with interest to subsidize surging mortality costs in old age, thereby keeping lifetime premiums level and fixed.
分红保单的权利与裁量边界Rights and discretionary boundaries of participating policiesparticipating (par) policy参与盈余分配是写进合同的权利，但每年是否分红及分多少完全由保险公司董事会自行裁定，并不保证；同等保障下不分红（non-par）保费通常更低。Participating in surplus distributions is a contractual right, but whether a dividend is declared and its amount are at the sole discretion of the insurer's board of directors and are not guaranteed; for the same coverage, non-participating premiums are typically lower.
限期缴终身寿险的期限拆解Structure of limited payment whole life insurancelimited payment whole life限期缴（如 10 年缴或 20 年缴）仅缩短缴费期以消除长寿缴费风险，保障期仍然是终身，代价是单期保费显著高于终身缴。Limited-payment policies (such as 10-pay or 20-pay) shorten the premium-paying period to eliminate the risk of outliving one's ability to pay, while coverage remains for life, at the cost of significantly higher periodic premiums than life-pay policies.
增额已缴清保险（PUA）机制Paid-up additions (PUA) mechanicspaid-up additions (PUA)将当年宣布的红利作为趸缴保费购买无需核保的小额已缴清永久险，永久增加总保额和未来现金价值，考试口径称超过 90% 的分红保单采用此选项。Using declared annual dividends as single premiums to purchase small amounts of paid-up permanent insurance without medical underwriting permanently increases both the total death benefit and future cash values; the exam benchmark states that over 90% of participating policies select this option.
分红率与累积生息利率的区别Distinction between dividend scale interest rate and accumulate-at-interest deposit ratedividend scale interest rate分红率只是精算师计算红利表的内部假设参数，红利宣布后若选择留存累积生息（accumulate at interest），适用的是保司另定的存款利率，通常显著低于分红率。The dividend scale interest rate is merely an internal actuarial assumption used to calculate dividend scales; if declared dividends are left to accumulate at interest, a separate deposit rate set by the insurer applies, which is typically significantly lower than the dividend scale rate.
现金退保价值与保单贷款上限Cash surrender value and policy loan limitscash surrender value (CSV) & policy loan早期由于退保费用（surrender charge）扣减，CSV 常显示为 $0；CSV 累积后可申请保单贷款（考试口径常见上限为 CSV 的 90%），未还本息会同时从退保金和身故给付中扣除。Early cash surrender value (CSV) often reflects $0 due to surrender charges; once accumulated, policyholders can borrow against it (the exam benchmark commonly cites 90% of CSV as the maximum loan limit), and unpaid principal plus interest are deducted from both surrender proceeds and death benefits.
不丧失利益四选项的适用区分Distinguishing the four non-forfeiture benefit optionsnon-forfeiture benefits有足够 CSV 时，APL 自动垫缴保费防失效；退保直接领走 CSV；减额已缴清用 CSV 买保额缩水的终身险；展期定期用 CSV 按原保额买一段有限期限的定期险。When sufficient CSV exists, an APL automatically advances premiums to prevent policy lapse; surrendering pays out the net CSV directly; reduced paid-up insurance uses CSV to buy reduced lifetime coverage; extended term insurance uses CSV to buy term coverage for the full original face amount for a limited duration.

第 1 题Q1分红保单权利与自由裁量权Participating policy contractual rights and board discretion★陷阱：混淆合同参与权与分红金额

Nadia is reviewing a participating whole life policy proposal and asks her advisor whether the annual policy dividends shown in the current column of the illustration are legally guaranteed once the contract is signed. How should the advisor explain the legal nature of policy dividends?

Nadia 正在审阅一份分红终身寿险建议书，询问顾问：一旦签署合同，演示件当前栏（current column）中显示的年度保单红利在法律上是否受到保证。顾问应如何解释保单红利的法律性质？

  1. ✓ 正确。分红保单（participating policy, par）持有人享有写进合同的盈余分配参与权，但每年分不分、分多少，完全由保险公司董事会自行裁定（at the sole discretion of the Board of Directors），属于非保证利益。Correct. Holders of participating (par) policies enjoy a contractual right to participate in surplus distributions, but whether a dividend is declared and how much is paid each year are determined at the sole discretion of the insurer's board of directors, representing a non-guaranteed benefit.

  2. ✗ 掉入「混淆合同参与权与分红金额」陷阱。将写进合同的参与权误等同于每年必发红利或保证最低分红率（dividend scale interest rate），事实上红利在极端情况下甚至可能阶段性归零。Falls into the 'confusing contractual participation rights with dividend amounts' trap. This mistakenly equates the contractual right of participation with a mandatory annual dividend or a guaranteed minimum dividend scale interest rate; in reality, dividends can drop to zero under extreme conditions.

  3. ✗ 掉入「亏损分摊误解」陷阱。无论分红（par）还是不分红（non-par）保单，持有人都不承担保司亏损，已经写进合同的保证保额和保证现金价值表绝不会因公司经营不善而缩水。Falls into the 'misconception of loss sharing' trap. Neither participating nor non-participating policyholders bear the insurer's corporate losses, and guaranteed death benefits and guaranteed cash surrender value tables written into the contract never diminish due to poor company performance.

  4. ✗ 掉入「股东分红混淆」陷阱。保单红利（policy dividend）源于分红账户精算盈余分配，与公司股东分红（corporate shareholder dividend）在法律性质和税务处理上完全不同。Falls into the 'confusing policy dividends with shareholder dividends' trap. Policy dividends stem from the actuarial distribution of surplus in the participating account and are completely different in legal character and tax treatment from corporate shareholder dividends.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Victor owns a participating whole life policy. This year, the insurer's board of directors announced a lower dividend scale due to softer long-term investment returns. Victor claims the insurer breached his contract because his annual dividend dropped below the initial illustration. Which statement is correct?

Victor 持有一份分红终身寿险。今年，由于长期投资回报走弱，保司董事会宣布下调红利表（dividend scale）。Victor 声称保司违约，因为他的年度红利低于最初的销售演示。下列哪项陈述是正确的？

  1. ✗ 掉入「保费抵消演示当成合同承诺」陷阱。保单演示件（illustration）中的非保证栏（non-guaranteed column）仅是基于当年分红率假设的预测，不构成合同保证承诺。Falls into the 'treating premium offset illustrations as contractual promises' trap. The non-guaranteed column in a policy illustration is merely a projection based on the current dividend scale interest rate assumption, not a contractually guaranteed promise.

  2. ✗ 掉入「保证与非保证混同」陷阱。保证现金退保价值表（guaranteed CSV table）在签发时已固定写进合同，保司无义务因非保证红利下调而上调保证现金价值。Falls into the 'conflating guaranteed and non-guaranteed values' trap. The guaranteed cash surrender value table is fixed in the contract at issue, and the insurer has no obligation to raise guaranteed cash values to offset a reduction in non-guaranteed dividends.

  3. ✓ 正确。分红保单（participating policy）的红利金额属于董事会年度自由裁量且并不保证，非保证演示未兑现不自动等于违约，同时合同写明的保证保额与保证现金价值丝毫不受影响。Correct. Dividend amounts under a participating policy are subject to the board's annual discretion and are not guaranteed; failing to match non-guaranteed illustrations does not constitute a breach of contract, while the contractually guaranteed death benefit and cash surrender values remain entirely intact.

  4. ✗ 掉入「虚构退费权利」陷阱。红利表（dividend scale）随死亡率、费用和投资经验变动而调整是分红险的正常机制，投保人只能按现金退保价值（CSV）退保，无权要求全额退还历史保费。Falls into the 'fabricating premium refund rights' trap. Adjusting the dividend scale to reflect changes in mortality, expense, and investment experience is a normal mechanism of participating insurance; the policyholder can only surrender for the cash surrender value (CSV) and has no legal right to demand a full refund of past premiums.

第 2 题Q2增额已缴清保险（PUA）免核保Paid-up additions (PUA) purchased without medical underwriting★★陷阱：把 PUA 当成需核保的附加险

Liam, age 46, bought a $400,000 participating whole life policy ten years ago. Last year he was diagnosed with a serious chronic illness that makes him uninsurable for new coverage. He wants to use his annual policy dividends to permanently increase his death benefit and cash value without undergoing medical underwriting. Which dividend option should he use?

46 岁的 Liam 十年前买了一份 $400,000 的分红终身寿险。去年他被诊断出患有严重慢性疾病，无法再通过核保购买新保险。他希望利用每年的保单红利永久增加身故保额和现金价值，且无需进行医学核保。他应该使用哪种红利选项？

  1. ✗ 掉入「红利选项机制混淆」陷阱。抵扣保费（premium reduction / offset）只是用当年红利冲抵下一期应缴保费，完全不会增加保单的身故保额。Falls into the 'confusing dividend option mechanics' trap. Premium reduction merely applies declared dividends to offset upcoming premium dues and does not increase the policy's death benefit whatsoever.

  2. ✓ 正确。购买增额已缴清保险（paid-up additions, PUA）将当年宣布的红利作为趸缴保费，买下一小笔已缴清永久险，永久增加总保额与未来现金价值，且完全免核保（无需提供可保性证明）；按考试口径陈述，超过 90% 的分红保单采用此选项（注：该比例属考试口径历史陈述，实务中无公开统计佐证）。Correct. Purchasing paid-up additions (PUA) applies declared dividends as single premiums to purchase small parcels of paid-up permanent coverage, permanently increasing both total death benefit and future cash values without medical underwriting (no evidence of insurability required); according to the exam benchmark, over 90% of participating policies choose this option (note: this percentage is an exam narrative statement without public empirical validation in current practice).

  3. ✗ 掉入「把 PUA 当成需核保的附加险」与「利率误用」双重陷阱。累积生息（accumulate at interest）只是把红利留在保司账户按另定的存款利率计息，随时可提取，既不适用分红率，也不会变成永久保险。Falls into the dual trap of 'treating paid-up additions as an underwritten rider' and 'rate misapplication'. Leaving dividends to accumulate at interest merely deposits them in an insurer account earning a separate deposit interest rate, available for withdrawal at any time; it neither earns the dividend scale rate nor converts into permanent coverage.

  4. ✗ 掉入「把 PUA 当成需核保的附加险」陷阱。单独购买附加险（rider）或新保单使用的是投保人额外支付的资金且通常需要核保，而 PUA 直接用红利免核保购入已缴清永久险。Falls into the 'treating paid-up additions as an underwritten rider' trap. Purchasing a separate rider or new policy requires additional out-of-pocket funds and usually requires medical underwriting, whereas PUA automatically purchases paid-up permanent insurance directly with dividends without underwriting.

答案Answer：b

42 岁的 Sonia 正在不分红终身寿险和 T-100 保单之间做选择以覆盖一项永久遗产需求，但她同时希望如果 60 多岁时企业现金流吃紧，能够灵活申请保单贷款或选择减额已缴清保险。为什么 T-100 不适合 Sonia？

  1. ✗ 掉入「把 T-100 当 YRT」陷阱。T-100 采用平准保险成本（level cost of insurance）定价，保费从签发起固定不变直到 100 岁交清，不会逐年上涨。Falls into the 'treating Term-100 as yearly renewable term' trap. Term-100 uses level cost of insurance pricing with premiums fixed from issue until fully paid up at age 100, never increasing annually.

  2. ✓ 正确。T-100 是刻意不做储蓄功能的永久险，早期多收的保费仅用于支撑终身平准费率，不对投保人开放现金退保价值（CSV）、保单贷款（policy loan）或减额已缴清等不丧失利益选项。Correct. Term-100 is a permanent policy intentionally designed without savings features; early premium overpayments exist solely to support level lifetime rates and do not provide the policyholder with cash surrender values (CSV), policy loans, or non-forfeiture options like reduced paid-up insurance.

  3. ✗ 掉入「把 T-100 当定期续保」陷阱。T-100 属于永久险类别，不到期、不需要续保核保。Falls into the 'treating Term-100 as renewable term insurance' trap. Term-100 is classified as permanent insurance; it does not expire and requires no renewal underwriting.

  4. ✗ 掉入「案例场景绝对化」陷阱。慈善捐赠和资本利得税覆盖只是 T-100 的典型用途，其受益人指定并不限于慈善机构。Falls into the 'treating typical case scenarios as absolute rules' trap. Charitable giving and capital gains tax liabilities are merely typical uses for Term-100; designating beneficiaries is not legally restricted to charitable institutions.

第 7 题Q7T-100 停缴失效与无现金价值后果Term-100 lapse upon premium cessation and zero cash value consequences★★★陷阱：T-100 失效套用终身寿险缓冲

Keith has held a standard Term-100 (T-100) policy for 14 years. While travelling abroad for three months, he misses his premium due date and fails to pay before the 30-day grace period expires. According to standard exam rules for a T-100 policy with no cash surrender value, what is the immediate consequence once the grace period ends?

Keith 持有一份标准 T-100 保单已有 14 年。在出国旅行三个月期间，他错过了保费到期日，且未能在 30 天宽限期届满前补缴。根据无现金退保价值 T-100 保单的标准考试规则，宽限期结束后立即发生的后果是什么？

  1. ✗ 掉入「T-100 失效套用终身寿险缓冲」陷阱。虽然 T-100 内部存在精算准备金以维持平准保费，但对投保人不开放现金退保价值（CSV），因此无法触发自动垫缴保费（APL）。Falls into the 'applying whole life lapse buffers to Term-100' trap. Although Term-100 maintains internal actuarial reserves to support level premiums, it makes no cash surrender value (CSV) accessible to the policyholder, preventing an automatic premium loan (APL) from triggering.

  2. ✗ 掉入「T-100 失效套用终身寿险缓冲」陷阱。没有现金退保价值（CSV），就不可能凭空购买减额已缴清保险（reduced paid-up insurance）或展期定期保险。Falls into the 'applying whole life lapse buffers to Term-100' trap. Without cash surrender value (CSV), it is impossible to purchase reduced paid-up insurance or extended term insurance.

  3. ✓ 正确。按考试口径，通常无现金退保价值（CSV）的 T-100 无法靠 APL 垫缴或转为缴清保障，超过宽限期即失效归零，部分合同可在约 2 年内申请复效（注：考试口径仅笼统提及部分合同约 2 年内可重新激活，而现行 BC《保险法》s.57 规定普通个人寿险宽限期满失效后再有 30 天生存补缴复效期及 2 年附条件法定复效权）。Correct. According to the exam benchmark, a standard Term-100 policy without cash surrender value (CSV) cannot rely on APL advances or convert to paid-up coverage, lapsing outright to zero once the grace period ends, though some contracts permit applying for reinstatement within roughly two years (note: while the exam benchmark broadly mentions reactivation within approximately two years, under current BC Insurance Act s. 57, standard individual life policies have a further 30-day window to reinstate by paying the overdue premium while the insured is alive, plus a two-year conditional statutory reinstatement right following lapse).

  4. ✗ 掉入「虚构保费退还」陷阱。T-100 没有不丧失利益（non-forfeiture benefit）和现金退保价值，失效后保司不退还过往已缴保费。Falls into the 'fabricating premium refund rights' trap. Term-100 has no non-forfeiture benefits or cash surrender value, and upon policy lapse the insurer does not refund any previously paid premiums.

Why does a traditional whole life policy have three layers of protection against accidental lapse after the 30-day grace period (APL, reduced paid-up, extended term), whereas a standard Term-100 (T-100) policy lapses outright once the grace period expires?

为什么传统终身寿险在 30 天宽限期过后还有三道防失效或保底缓冲（APL、减额已缴清、展期定期），而标准 T-100 保单一旦超过宽限期就会直接失效？

  1. ✓ 正确。传统终身寿险的不丧失利益（non-forfeiture benefits）和自动垫缴保费（APL）全部以可动用的现金退保价值（CSV）为资金来源；T-100 通常不建立 CSV，因此宽限期满未缴费即失效（注：考试口径强调 T-100 无 CSV 保底直接终止，BC 实务中普通个人寿险仍须核查《保险法》s.57 的法定复效权）。Correct. Non-forfeiture benefits and automatic premium loans (APL) in traditional whole life rely entirely on accessible cash surrender value (CSV) as their funding source; because Term-100 typically accumulates no CSV, it lapses outright once the grace period expires (note: while the exam benchmark stresses that Term-100 terminates directly without CSV buffers, BC practice still requires reviewing statutory reinstatement rights under Insurance Act s. 57).

  2. ✗ 掉入「产品分类混淆」陷阱。监管与行业将 T-100 归入永久险（permanent insurance）类别，因为它不到期且无需续保核保，它缺的是 CSV 而非永久险身份。Falls into the 'confusing product categories' trap. Standard individual life insurance policies (whether whole life or Term-100) provide a 30-day grace period (not counting the due date) for unpaid renewal premiums (28 days for industrial life).

  4. ✗ 掉入「法定权利放弃误解」陷阱。投保 T-100 放弃的是现金退保价值（CSV）与不丧失利益这层储蓄弹性，并未放弃法定复效权。Falls into the 'assuming statutory rights are waived' trap. Purchasing Term-100 forfeits cash surrender values and non-forfeiture savings flexibility, but does not waive statutory reinstatement rights.
