① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 时间价值与通胀算例题：给出本金、名义回报率（nominal return）、通胀率与年限，考复利（compounding）终值计算、72 法则（Rule of 72）估算翻倍时间，或区分名义回报与实际回报（real return）的正负。Time value of money and inflation calculation questions: Given principal, nominal return, inflation rate, and time horizon, testing compounding future value calculations, estimating doubling time using the Rule of 72, or distinguishing between positive and negative real returns versus nominal returns.
  • 注册账户规则与税务后果辨析题：设定客户供款、提款或超额情景，测试 RRSP、TFSA、RESP、RDSP、RRIF 及 FHSA 的供款上限、抵税属性、提款课税规则、每月 1% 超额惩罚税与 TFSA 次年恢复空间规则。Registered account rules and tax consequence questions: Setting client contribution, withdrawal, or over-contribution scenarios to test RRSP, TFSA, RESP, RDSP, RRIF, and FHSA contribution limits, tax deductibility, withdrawal taxation rules, the 1% per month over-contribution penalty tax, and TFSA room restoration the following year.
  • 投资者保护与监管归属匹配题：给出客户持有的具体投资工具（GIC、股票、共同基金、分离基金、年金），考察发生机构破产时由 CDIC、CIPF 还是 Assuris 兜底，以及分离基金归省级保险监管而非证券自律组织监管。Investor protection and regulatory jurisdiction matching questions: Given specific investment vehicles held by a client (GICs, stocks, mutual funds, segregated funds, annuities), testing whether CDIC, CIPF, or Assuris provides protection in the event of institutional insolvency, and verifying that segregated funds fall under provincial insurance regulation rather than securities self-regulatory organizations.
  • 双口径与政策演进识别题：针对考试资料成文（2022 年）与现行制度的差异（如 IIROC/MFDA 合并为 CIRO、CIPF 与 IPC 合并、新增 FHSA 与 CPP2），按题干问法区分考试口径答案与现行实务规则。Dual benchmark and regulatory evolution questions: Addressing differences between the exam reference manual (written in 2022) and current rules (such as IIROC and MFDA merging into CIRO, CIPF and IPC merging, and the introduction of the FHSA and CPP2), distinguishing between exam benchmark answers and current practical rules based on how the question stem is phrased.
  • KYC 评估与四维选品情景题：结合客户的投资时间跨度、风险承受意愿（risk tolerance）与风险承受能力（risk capacity），从风险、收益、流动性（liquidity）与课税四个维度判断某类投资工具或账户安排是否匹配。KYC assessment and four-dimensional product suitability scenario questions: Combining a client's investment time horizon, risk tolerance, and risk capacity to evaluate whether an investment vehicle or account structure matches across four dimensions: risk, return, liquidity, and taxation.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ TFSA 当年回存超额陷阱TFSA same-year re-contribution over-contribution trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为 TFSA 取出免税就等于随时取出后可以在同一年内把额度原样存回。Mistakenly assuming that tax-free TFSA withdrawals allow a client to re-contribute the withdrawn amount within the same calendar year at any time.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现客户在当年已用满 TFSA 额度后提款，并计划在同一年底前把资金重新存回 TFSA。The question stem features a client who has already maximized their TFSA contribution room for the year, withdraws funds, and plans to deposit funds back into the TFSA before the end of the same year.

⚠ RRSP 供款基数与上限遗漏陷阱RRSP contribution base year and dollar limit omission trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直接用客户当年（而非上一年）收入乘以 18%，或算出 18% 后忘记与年度金额上限（Dollar Limit）取较小值。Directly multiplying the client's current-year (instead of previous-year) earned income by 18%, or calculating 18% but forgetting to take the lesser of that amount and the annual dollar limit.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出客户上一年与当年的收入，或给出极高收入要求计算当年新增 RRSP 供款空间。The question stem provides both previous-year and current-year income, or gives a very high income and asks for new RRSP contribution room generated for the current year.

⚠ 分离基金监管与保护错配陷阱Segregated fund regulation and protection mismatch trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 因为分离基金底层投资像共同基金，就误选证券业监管机构（CIRO/IIROC/MFDA）或证券保护基金（CIPF），或把破产保护当成市场亏损兜底。Because segregated fund underlying investments resemble mutual funds, mistakenly choosing securities regulators (CIRO/IIROC/MFDA) or securities protection funds (CIPF), or confusing insolvency protection with a guarantee against market losses.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问分离基金销售受哪个机构监管、破产由谁保护，或问市场大跌 20% 时保护基金赔多少。The question stem asks which regulator oversees segregated fund sales, which entity protects against insolvency, or asks how much a protection fund pays during a 20% market decline.

⚠ 名义回报掩盖负实际回报陷阱Nominal return masking negative real return trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 只看账面正收益率，忽略当通胀率高于名义回报率时，实际购买力反而缩水（实际回报为负）。Looking only at a positive nominal return on paper while ignoring that when inflation exceeds the nominal rate, actual purchasing power declines (real return is negative).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出产品名义回报率与当年通胀率，且通胀率接近或高于名义回报率，问客户的实际购买力变化。The question stem provides both a product's nominal return and the annual inflation rate, where inflation is close to or higher than the return, asking how the client's actual purchasing power changes.

⚠ 净值高等于风险承受力高陷阱High net worth equating to high risk capacity trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到客户净资产数字大、负债低，就跳过现金流和客观财务承受力评估，直接推断其可配置高波动产品或无需保障。Observing a large net worth figure and low debt, then skipping cash flow and objective financial capacity evaluations to improperly conclude that the client can hold high-volatility products or requires no insurance protection.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调客户净值表（net worth statement）资产丰厚但全部存放在 GIC 或储蓄账户，问代理人在评估阶段能得出什么结论。The question stem emphasizes that the client's net worth statement shows substantial assets held entirely in GICs or savings accounts, asking what conclusion the agent can draw during the assessment phase.

复利与单利差距及 72 法则Difference between compound and simple interest and the Rule of 72compounding / Rule of 72复利将利息滚入本金再生息（FV = PV × (1 + r)^n），随期限拉长加速放大；用 72 除以年化回报率整数百分比可估算本金翻倍年数（6%–10% 区间最准）。Compounding reinvests earned interest back into principal to earn additional interest (FV = PV * (1 + r)^n), accelerating exponentially over longer horizons; dividing 72 by the annual return percentage estimates the years required for principal to double (most accurate between 6% and 10%).

TFSA 提款免税与次年额度恢复TFSA tax-free withdrawals and next-year room restorationTax-Free Savings Account (TFSA)TFSA 供款税后、提款免税且不影响 OAS/GIS 等福利（2026 年度限额 $7,000）；提取的额度须等到次年 1 月 1 日才恢复，当年无剩余额度时存回会触发超额部分每月 1% 惩罚税。TFSA contributions are after-tax, withdrawals are tax-free and do not affect income-tested benefits like OAS or GIS ($7,000 annual limit for 2026); withdrawn amounts are not restored until January 1 of the following calendar year, and re-contributing without available room triggers a 1% per month penalty tax on the excess amount.
FHSA 双重税务优惠与限额FHSA dual tax advantages and contribution limitsFirst Home Savings Account (FHSA)2023 年上线的 FHSA 兼具供款税前抵税与合规首次购房提款免税双重优惠，年度参与额度 $8,000、终身上限 $40,000、单年结转上限 $8,000（考试口径因 2022 年成文未收录）。Introduced in 2023, the FHSA combines tax-deductible contributions with tax-free withdrawals for a qualifying first home purchase, with an $8,000 annual participation limit, $40,000 lifetime limit, and up to $8,000 in unused room carryforward per year (unmentioned in the exam manual due to its 2022 publication date).

第 1 题Q1复利终值与单利差额计算Calculating compound interest future value and simple interest difference★★陷阱：单利与复利混淆或误用72法则近似

Elena invests a lump sum of $10,000 at a nominal annual return of 6% for 20 years, making no additional contributions or withdrawals. Compared with an account that pays only simple interest on the original principal at the same 6% rate for 20 years, approximately how much more will Elena accumulate through annual compounding?

Elena 一次性投资 $10,000，年化名义回报率为 6%，投资期 20 年，期间不追加也不提取。与同样按 6% 年利率仅对原始本金计单利的账户相比，每年复利（annual compounding）大约能让 Elena 多累积多少金额？

  1. ✓ 正确。复利（compounding）终值 FV = $10,000 × (1.06)^20 ≈ $32,071，单利（simple interest）终值 = $10,000 × (1 + 6% × 20) = $22,000，两者差额约为 $10,071，完全来自"利息生利息"。Correct. Compound interest future value FV = $10,000 * (1.06)^20 ≈ $32,071, whereas simple interest future value = $10,000 * (1 + 6% * 20) = $22,000. The difference is approximately $10,071, arising entirely from interest earning interest.

  2. ✗ $12,000 是单利在 20 年内产生的利息总额（$10,000 × 6% × 20），不是复利比单利多出的差额。Incorrect. The $12,000 figure is the total interest generated by simple interest over 20 years ($10,000 * 6% * 20), not the excess accumulated by compounding over simple interest.

  3. ✗ $22,000 是单利账户在 20 年后的本息合计终值，误把单利本息总额当成了复利与单利的差额。Incorrect. The $22,000 figure is the total future value (principal plus interest) of the simple interest account after 20 years, mistakenly confusing the total simple interest balance with the difference between compounding and simple interest.

  4. ✗ $32,071 是复利账户在 20 年后的本息合计终值（FV），未减去单利账户的终值 $22,000。Incorrect. The $32,071 figure is the total future value (FV) of the compounding account after 20 years, without subtracting the simple interest account future value of $22,000.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Victor places $10,000 into a long-term savings vehicle earning 6% compounded annually for 20 years with no subsequent deposits or withdrawals. What will be the approximate total future value of Victor's investment at the end of the 20-year period?

Victor 将 $10,000 存入一项长期储蓄工具，按年化 6% 复利计息 20 年，期间无后续存款或提款。20 年期满时，Victor 这笔投资的终值（future value）总额大约是多少？

  1. ✗ 这是按单利公式 $10,000 × (1 + 6% × 20) = $22,000 算出的结果，漏算了利息滚入本金再生息的复利效应。Incorrect. This is calculated using the simple interest formula $10,000 * (1 + 6% * 20) = $22,000, omitting the compounding effect of interest reinvested into principal to generate further earnings.

  2. ✗ $10,071 是 20 年复利终值减去单利终值后的净差额，不是期末账户的总终值（FV）。Incorrect. The $10,071 figure is the net difference between the 20-year compound future value and the simple interest future value, not the total future value (FV) of the account at maturity.

  3. ✓ 正确。根据复利终值公式（future value, FV = PV × (1 + r)^n），$10,000 × (1.06)^20 ≈ $10,000 × 3.207 ≈ $32,071。Correct. According to the compound interest future value formula (FV = PV * (1 + r)^n), $10,000 * (1.06)^20 ≈ $10,000 * 3.207 ≈ $32,071.

  4. ✗ 这是误用 72 法则（72 ÷ 6 = 12 年翻倍至 $20,000）后直接按比例粗略外推 24 年翻四倍的结果，精确复利金额题必须代入 FV = PV × (1 + r)^n 计算。Incorrect. This results from misapplying the Rule of 72 (72 / 6 = 12 years to double to $20,000) and roughly extrapolating a fourfold increase over 24 years, whereas exact compound future value calculations require using FV = PV * (1 + r)^n.

第 2 题Q2TFSA次年恢复额度与超额罚则TFSA room restoration the following year and over-contribution penalties★★陷阱：TFSA 当年回存超额陷阱

Nadia has already maximized her lifetime Tax-Free Savings Account (TFSA) contribution room by March of this year. In June, she withdraws $5,000 from her TFSA to pay for car repairs. In October of the same year, she receives a bonus and wants to re-contribute the $5,000 back into her TFSA immediately. What is the tax consequence if she makes this deposit in October?

Nadia 在今年 3 月已经用满了她所有的免税储蓄账户（TFSA）可用供款额度。6 月她从 TFSA 提取了 $5,000 用于修车。同年 10 月她收到一笔奖金，想立刻把这 $5,000 重新存回 TFSA。如果她在 10 月存入这笔钱，会产生什么税务后果？

  1. ✗ 掉入 TFSA 当年回存超额陷阱；TFSA 提取的额度不会在当年立刻恢复，必须等到次年 1 月 1 日才重新计入可用空间。Incorrect. Falls into the TFSA same-year re-contribution over-contribution trap; withdrawn TFSA room does not restore immediately in the same year and is only added back to available room on January 1 of the following calendar year.

  2. ✓ 正确。由于 Nadia 当年已无剩余免税储蓄账户（Tax-Free Savings Account, TFSA）额度，而提款空间要到次年 1 月 1 日才恢复，同年存回 $5,000 构成超额供款，按《所得税法》对超额部分每月征收 1% 惩罚税。Correct. Because Nadia has no remaining Tax-Free Savings Account (TFSA) contribution room in the current year and the withdrawn room is not restored until January 1 of the following year, re-contributing $5,000 in the same calendar year constitutes an over-contribution subject to a 1% per month penalty tax under the Income Tax Act.

  3. ✗ 张冠李戴：$2,000 终身超额缓冲额度是注册退休储蓄计划（RRSP）的规则，TFSA 没有任何免罚缓冲额度。Incorrect. Confuses different account rules: the $2,000 lifetime over-contribution buffer applies to Registered Retirement Savings Plans (RRSPs), whereas TFSAs provide no penalty-free over-contribution cushion.

  4. ✗ TFSA 的合规提款本身免税，超额供款触发的是对超额部分每月 1% 的惩罚税，不会让此前的提款变成应税收入。Incorrect. Qualifying TFSA withdrawals remain tax-free; an over-contribution triggers a 1% per month penalty tax on the excess amount, but does not retroactively make previous withdrawals taxable.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Liam had zero remaining contribution room in his TFSA after making his annual deposit in January. In August, he withdrew $7,000 from his TFSA for a family trip. What is the earliest date Liam can re-contribute that $7,000 withdrawn amount without triggering a monthly over-contribution penalty?
