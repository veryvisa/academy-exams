① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 当事人身份与行权资格辨析题：给出投保人、被保险人、不可撤销受益人、雇主或持续授权书代理人等角色，考查谁有权变更受益人、申请保单贷款或变更保障额度。Distinction questions on party status and authority: Presenting roles such as policyholder, life insured, irrevocable beneficiary, employer, or attorney under an Enduring Power of Attorney, testing who has the authority to change beneficiaries, apply for policy loans, or modify coverage limits.
  • 合同成立与生效节点判断：给出递交申请、缴纳首期保费、核保加费（反要约）、健康状况变化及交付保单等多个时间点，考查合同何时真正成立生效。Determining contract formation and effective dates: Presenting multiple milestones such as submitting the application, paying the initial premium, underwriting ratings (counter-offers), changes in health status, and policy delivery, testing when the contract actually takes legal effect.
  • 时间轴与法定条款机制推演：将误报年龄、善意误述、故意隐瞒（欺诈）、自杀、宽限期身故及复效放到2年时间轴上，判断保险公司应当拒赔、退费还是调整保额。Timeline deduction on statutory provision mechanics: Placing misstatement of age, innocent misrepresentation, intentional non-disclosure (fraud), suicide, death during the grace period, and policy reinstatement onto a two-year timeline, testing whether the insurer must deny the claim, refund premiums, or adjust the face amount.
  • 受益人指定与债权人保护情景题：结合离婚协议、未送达总部的变更表、同时死亡或将受益人指定为遗产，判断保险金归属及是否享有免于债权人扣押的保护。Scenario questions on beneficiary designations and creditor protection: Integrating separation agreements, change forms not delivered to head office, simultaneous death, or designating the estate as beneficiary, testing the entitlement to insurance proceeds and eligibility for statutory exemption from seizure by creditors.
  • 全国考试口径与BC省成文法双口径对比：针对保单质押是否须不可撤销受益人同意、冷静期依据、替换披露及持续授权书变更受益人等考点，考查全国统一考试口径与BC现行法的差异。Comparative questions on national exam standards versus BC statutory law: Testing distinctions between national uniform exam standards and current BC statutory law regarding whether collateral assignment requires irrevocable beneficiary consent, the legal basis of the rescission period, policy replacement disclosure, and beneficiary changes under an Enduring Power of Attorney.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 误把单一节点当生效日Mistaking a single milestone for the effective date trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生容易把签申请书、核保通过或单纯把保单交到客户手上直接当成合同生效，忽略了首期保费缴纳、可保性未变以及加费反要约须经客户重新接受等必要条件。Candidates tend to mistake signing the application, underwriting approval, or physical policy delivery as the effective date, overlooking essential preconditions such as initial premium payment, unchanged insurability, and customer acceptance of any rated counter-offer.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干罗列申请日、核保日、交付日、补缴保费日，或提到核保后保单条件发生改变（加费、除外、降额）但客户尚未签字确认。The question stem lists application, approval, delivery, and premium payment dates, or notes that underwriting modified policy terms (ratings, exclusions, reduced face amount) without signed client acceptance.

⚠ 混淆年龄误报与健康误述Confusing misstatement of age with health misrepresentation trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 年龄和吸烟/病史都填在申请书上，考生容易把两者都当成按比例调保额，或者都当成2年内可撤单，忘了年龄是定价变量而吸烟/健康是核保实质性事实。Both age and smoking or medical history appear on the application, leading candidates to treat both as subject to proportional benefit adjustments or both as voidable within two years, forgetting that age is a pricing variable while health and smoking are material facts.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现出生年份写错与隐瞒吸烟或健康状况，询问保险公司在理赔调查发现后的处理方式。The question stem combines an erroneous birth year with non-disclosure of smoking or health conditions, asking how the insurer will handle the claim upon discovery during investigation.

⚠ 误认不可抗辩期保护欺诈Assuming the incontestability period protects fraud trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生记住了生效满2年进入不可抗辩期，看到保单已满2年或3年就直接选正常理赔，忽略了欺诈永远不受不可抗辩期保护。Candidates remember that policies become incontestable after two years and automatically select full claim payment for policies in force for two or three years, overlooking that fraudulent misrepresentation is never protected by the incontestability clause.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干一边强调保单已生效满2年以上，一边使用明知故犯、故意隐瞒（deliberately omit）等指向主观欺诈的措辞。The question stem emphasizes that the policy has been in force for over two years while using phrases indicating subjective fraud such as knowingly, deliberately omitted, or intentional concealment.

⚠ 误以为离婚或交代理人即改受益人Assuming divorce or handing forms to an agent changes the beneficiary trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉认为离婚协议写了放弃权益就能自动解除不可撤销受益人，或以为把变更表交给代理人就算完成变更，忽略了送达保司总部及不可撤销受益人书面同意的法定要求。Candidates intuitively assume a divorce agreement waiving policy rights automatically revokes an irrevocable beneficiary, or that handing a change form to an agent completes the change, ignoring statutory requirements for head office delivery and written irrevocable consent.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现离婚协议放弃保单权益、变更表留在代理人抽屉里未寄达保险公司总部，随后被保险人身故。The question stem features a divorce agreement waiving policy rights or a change form left in an agent's drawer without reaching the insurer's head office, followed by the death of the life insured.

⚠ 忽视复效后限制期重新起算Overlooking that reinstatement restarts exclusionary periods trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到保单最初是6年前买的，就以为早已过了2年不可抗辩期和自杀限制期，忘了断缴失效后办理复效会让这两个2年期限从复效日起重新起算。Candidates see that a policy was originally purchased six years ago and assume the two-year incontestability and suicide periods have long expired, forgetting that reinstating a lapsed policy restarts both two-year periods from the reinstatement date.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述一份持有多年的老保单因欠费超过宽限期而失效，复效后不足2年内发生自杀或被发现复效申请存在重大误述。The question stem describes an older policy held for years that lapsed after missing the grace period, followed by suicide or discovery of material misrepresentation within two years of reinstatement.

④ 要点表④ Key points

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
完整合同原则Entire Contract PrincipleEntire contract申请书、保单、附件及事后书面批注共同构成完整合同（BC s.41、s.42），代理人的任何口头承诺均不在合同内。The application, policy, attached documents, and subsequent written endorsements together constitute the entire contract (BC Insurance Act ss.41, 42); verbal promises made by an agent are excluded from the contract.
保险利益与同意Insurable Interest and Written ConsentInsurable interest合同生效时原则上须有可保利益，但团体保险或成年被保险人书面同意的合同不因缺乏可保利益而无效（BC s.45），未满16岁可由父母同意。An insurable interest must generally exist when a contract takes effect, but group insurance or contracts where an adult life insured consents in writing are not void for lack of insurable interest (BC Insurance Act s.45); minors under 16 can be consented for by a parent.
合同生效与反要约Contract Formation and Counter-OffersCounter-offer默认须满足保单交付、缴纳首期保费且申请至交付期间可保性未变（BC s.48）；若核保改变条件（如加费、除外）则构成反要约，须客户重新接受方成立。By default, coverage requires policy delivery, initial premium payment, and unchanged insurability between application and delivery (BC Insurance Act s.48); if underwriting modifies terms (ratings or exclusions), it constitutes a counter-offer requiring client acceptance before taking effect.
不可抗辩期与欺诈例外Incontestability Period and Fraud ExceptionIncontestability period保单在被保险人在世期间生效满2年后，善意误述不再使合同可撤销，但欺诈（故意隐瞒）永远不受2年不可抗辩期保护（BC s.51、s.52）。After a policy has been in force during the lifetime of the insured for two years, innocent misrepresentation no longer renders the contract voidable, but fraud (intentional non-disclosure) is never protected by the two-year incontestability period (BC Insurance Act ss.51, 52).
误报年龄与误报吸烟对比Misstatement of Age versus Misrepresentation of SmokingMisstatement of age误报年龄通常不撤单，而是按同样保费在真实年龄下本可购得的保额调整给付（BC s.54）；误报吸烟属于核保实质性事实，走误述撤单与不可抗辩轨道。Misstating age generally does not void the contract, but adjusts the death benefit to what the premiums paid would have purchased at the true age (BC Insurance Act s.54); misrepresenting smoking status is a material fact subject to policy rescission and the incontestability rules.
宽限期与复效重算Grace Period and Reinstatement TimelinesGrace period & Reinstatement保费逾期享有至少30天宽限期（工业险28天），期内身故照赔并扣未缴保费（BC s.50）；失效后2年内复效须补齐欠费利息并提供可保性证明，且不可抗辩期与自杀限制期均从复效日重新起算（BC s.56、s.57）。Overdue premiums enjoy a minimum 30-day grace period (28 days for industrial insurance), during which death claims are paid minus overdue premiums (BC Insurance Act s.50); reinstatement within two years of lapse requires paying overdue premiums with interest and providing evidence of insurability, restarting both the incontestability and suicide periods from the reinstatement date (BC Insurance Act ss.56, 57).
不可撤销受益人Irrevocable Beneficiary DesignationsIrrevocable beneficiary须在被保险人在世期间以书面指定送达保险公司总部方生效（BC s.60(1)），生效后变更受益人、退保或保单贷款均须其书面同意，且离婚不会自动解除该指定。An irrevocable designation takes effect only when filed in writing with the insurer's head office during the life insured's lifetime (BC Insurance Act s.60(1)); subsequent beneficiary changes, surrenders, or policy loans require the irrevocable beneficiary's written consent, and divorce does not automatically revoke the designation.
免于债权人追偿的两层保护Two-Tier Exemption from Creditor SeizureExemption from seizure指定受益人可使身故保险金不入遗产并免于投保人债权人追偿（BC s.65(1)）；指定配偶、子女、孙辈或父母（保护类受益人）还可使保单在投保人在世期间免于扣押（BC s.65(2)）。Naming any beneficiary keeps death proceeds outside the estate and exempt from the policyholder's creditors (BC Insurance Act s.65(1)); naming a spouse, child, grandchild, or parent (preferred family class) also exempts the policy from seizure during the policyholder's lifetime (BC Insurance Act s.65(2)).
保险人付款免责与同时死亡Insurer Payment Discharge and Simultaneous DeathPayment discharge & Simultaneous death保险公司在其主要办事处收到变更文件前按现有记录付款即可免责（BC s.77）；被保险人与受益人同时死亡且顺序不明时，推定受益人先于被保险人死亡（BC s.83）。An insurer is fully discharged by paying according to its records before receiving contrary documentation at its principal office (BC Insurance Act s.77); when the life insured and beneficiary die simultaneously and the order of death is uncertain, the beneficiary is presumed to have died first (BC Insurance Act s.83).
绝对转让与担保转让Absolute Assignment versus Collateral AssignmentAbsolute assignment & Collateral assignment绝对转让转移保单全部所有权并可能触发税务处置；担保转让仅将保单作为贷款抵押，不触发税务处置，身故时未偿贷款余额优先受偿（BC s.66）。An absolute assignment transfers all policy ownership rights and may trigger a taxable policy disposition; a collateral assignment merely pledges the policy as loan security without triggering a disposition, granting the lender priority repayment for the unpaid loan balance at death (BC Insurance Act s.66).

第 1 题Q1合同生效条件与核保反要约Conditions for policy taking effect and underwriting counter-offers★陷阱：误把单一节点当生效日

Noah applied for a $500,000 term life insurance policy without paying the initial premium at the time of application. Due to a hobby risk, the insurer issued the policy with an exclusion rider and an increased premium. The agent received the policy from the insurer on Monday and handed it to Noah on Wednesday, when Noah paid the first premium and confirmed his health had not changed, but Noah did not sign the acceptance form for the modified terms until Friday. When did the contract take effect?

Noah 申请了一份 $500,000 的定期寿险保单，申请时未缴纳首期保费。由于一项业余爱好风险，保险公司签发保单时附加了除外责任批注并上调了保费。代理人周一收到保单，周三将保单交给 Noah，当天 Noah 缴纳了首期保费并确认健康状况未变，但直到周五才签署修改条件接受确认书。合同何时生效？

  1. ✗ 错误。陷阱：误把单一节点当生效日。虽然无条件按原申请条件签发时交给代理人可视为交付（s.48(2)），但本案中保险公司更改了承保条件（加费并加除外），且首期保费尚未缴纳、客户尚未接受反要约，周一合同并未成立。Incorrect. Falls into the mistaking a single milestone for the effective date trap. Although unconditional delivery of a policy as applied for to an authorized agent constitutes delivery under s.48(2), the insurer here modified coverage terms (adding a rating and an exclusion rider), the initial premium had not been paid, and the counter-offer had not been accepted; therefore, no contract was formed on Monday.

  2. ✓ 正确。核保后签发的保单若与原申请不一致（加费、除外、降额），在法律上构成反要约（counter-offer），不再是原要约的承诺；必须等客户重新接受（周五签署接受确认书），结合已交付、已付首期保费且可保性未变，合同才正式成立生效。Correct. When an issued policy differs from the original application (rated premium, exclusion rider, or reduced face amount), it legally constitutes a counter-offer rather than an acceptance of the applicant's offer; the contract only takes legal effect once the client accepts the counter-offer (signing the acceptance on Friday), combined with delivery, payment of the initial premium, and unchanged insurability.

  3. ✗ 错误。陷阱：少走一步。如果保单是按原申请条件签发的，周三完成交付、缴费且可保性未变即可生效（s.48）；但因为保司修改了条件，该保单属于反要约，周三客户尚未签署接受确认，合同尚未成立。Incorrect. Falls into the stopping one step short trap. Had the policy been issued as applied for, completing delivery and premium payment on Wednesday with unchanged insurability would have brought coverage into effect under s.48; however, because the insurer modified the terms, the policy was a counter-offer that Noah had not yet formally accepted on Wednesday, leaving the contract unformed.

  4. ✗ 错误。申请书只是要约（offer），且申请时未随附首期保费，也没有临时保险（interim insurance），事后缴费与接受反要约不会让保障自动追溯到申请日生效。Incorrect. An application is merely an offer; because no initial premium accompanied the application and no interim insurance was in place, subsequent premium payment and acceptance of a counter-offer do not retroactively backdate coverage to the application date.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Grace applied for a $750,000 life insurance policy and did not pay the first premium with the application. The insurer approved the application as applied for and sent the policy to the agent. Between the application date and the delivery appointment, Grace was advised by her doctor to undergo a biopsy for a newly discovered nodule. At delivery, she offered to pay the first premium. How does this health change affect the taking effect of the policy?

Grace 申请了一份 $750,000 的寿险保单，申请时未缴首期保费。保险公司按原申请条件核保通过并将保单寄给代理人。在申请日至交付面谈期间，Grace 因新查出结节被医生建议做活检。交付时她表示愿意缴纳首期保费。这一健康变化对保单生效有何影响？

  1. ✗ 错误。陷阱：误把核保通过或缴费当成唯一条件。未随申请预缴保费时，生效必须同时满足交付、缴付首期保费以及申请至交付期间可保性（insurability）未发生变化（s.48）。Incorrect. Falls into the mistaking underwriting approval or payment as the sole condition trap. When no premium accompanies the application, taking effect strictly requires policy delivery, initial premium payment, and unchanged insurability between application and delivery under s.48.

  2. ✗ 错误。保险公司不会自动附加2年除外使保单直接生效；当可保性发生变化时，代理人不能直接完成交付，必须退回由保险公司重新核保。Incorrect. An insurer does not automatically append a two-year exclusion rider to make coverage take effect; when insurability changes, the agent cannot complete delivery and must return the policy for underwriting reassessment.

  3. ✗ 错误。交付前确认健康状况未变（通常签署 statement of good health）是代理人的法定义务而非礼貌；明知可保性已变仍违规交付是高频追责点。Incorrect. Confirming unchanged health prior to delivery (typically by obtaining a signed statement of good health) is a legal and regulatory requirement rather than a courtesy; knowingly delivering a policy when insurability has changed exposes the agent to regulatory discipline and liability.

  4. ✓ 正确。根据合同生效的四个法定条件（s.48），从申请到交付并缴纳首期保费期间，客户的可保性（insurability）必须未发生变化；查出结节并被建议活检改变了可保性，在保险公司重新核保评估前保单不能按原件交付生效。Correct. Under the statutory conditions for taking effect in s.48, the applicant's insurability must remain unchanged from the application date until delivery and payment of the initial premium; discovering a nodule and being advised to undergo a biopsy represents a change in insurability, preventing the policy from taking effect as issued until reassessed by underwriting.

答案Answer：d

第 2 题Q2误报年龄与误述吸烟的双轨后果Dual-track consequences of misstated age versus smoking misrepresentation★★陷阱：混淆年龄误报与健康误述

Lucas purchased a $500,000 term life insurance policy. On the application, he accidentally stated his birth year as 3 years younger than his actual age (which was still within the policy's insurable age limit), and he also checked 'non-smoker' even though he smoked cigars every week. Lucas died of heart disease within the first 2 years of the policy. How will the insurer handle these two inaccuracies discovered during the claim investigation?

Lucas 购买了一份 $500,000 的定期寿险保单。在申请书上，他无意中把出生年份写小了3岁（其真实年龄仍在保单承保年龄上限之内），同时尽管他每周都抽雪茄，却在吸烟栏勾选了「非吸烟」。Lucas 在保单生效后不足2年内因心脏病身故。保险公司在理赔调查中发现这两项不实后会如何处理？

  1. ✗ 错误。陷阱：混淆年龄误报与健康误述，误把两项合并成「都按比例调整」。只有误报年龄（misstatement of age）才按真实年龄调整保额；吸烟状态会影响承保决定本身，不适用比例调保额。Incorrect. Falls into the confusing misstatement of age with health misrepresentation trap, mistakenly assuming both items are adjusted proportionally. Only misstatement of age triggers an adjusted face amount based on true age; smoking status affects the underwriting decision itself and is not subject to proportional adjustment.

According to the standard LLQP exam rule, what must happen for Claire to pledge the policy and give the bank priority for the unpaid loan balance upon her death?

Claire 持有一份 $500,000 的终身寿险保单，其母亲已被正式指定为不可撤销受益人。Claire 想为自己的小生意向银行贷款 $180,000，银行要求以该保单作担保转让。根据 LLQP 考试口径通则，Claire 要质押保单并让银行在自己身故时优先受偿未偿贷款余额，必须满足什么条件？

  1. ✗ 错误。无论在考试口径还是实务中，未经不可撤销受益人同意，银行都不可能排在不可撤销受益人之前优先受偿身故保险金。Incorrect. Under both the national exam standard and practical insurance rules, a bank cannot obtain first priority over an irrevocable beneficiary to death proceeds without that beneficiary's consent.

  2. ✗ 错误。银行贷款要求的是担保转让（collateral assignment）而非绝对转让（absolute assignment），且投保人更不能通过单方绝对转让来消灭不可撤销受益人的权利。Incorrect. A bank loan requires a collateral assignment rather than an absolute assignment, and a policyholder cannot extinguish an irrevocable beneficiary's rights through a unilateral absolute assignment.

  3. ✓ 正确。按全国考试口径通则，保单存在不可撤销受益人（irrevocable beneficiary）时，办理担保转让（collateral assignment）须取得该不可撤销受益人的书面同意并送达保司，身故时银行方可优先受偿未偿贷款余额（如尚欠 $120,000 则先付银行 $120,000，余下 $380,000 归母亲）。（注：BC 现行法 Regulation s.9.1(4)(e) 与 s.66(3) 允许不经同意作出转让本身，但未获同意时银行权益排在不可撤销受益人之后，因此要让银行优先受偿仍须母亲书面同意。）Correct. Under the national exam standard, when a policy has an irrevocable beneficiary, executing a collateral assignment requires the irrevocable beneficiary's written consent filed with the insurer for the bank to have first priority over unpaid loan balances at death (e.g., paying $120,000 to the bank first and the remaining $380,000 to the mother). (Note: Current BC Regulation s.9.1(4)(e) and s.66(3) permit executing the assignment without consent, but without consent the bank's interest ranks behind the irrevocable beneficiary; thus granting the bank priority still requires the mother's written consent).

  4. ✗ 错误。不可撤销指定并非永久禁止保单质押，只要不可撤销受益人签署书面同意并送达保险公司，即可正常办理担保转让。Incorrect. An irrevocable designation does not permanently bar pledging a policy as loan collateral; as long as the irrevocable beneficiary signs a written consent delivered to the insurer, a collateral assignment can proceed.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Following up on a valid collateral assignment where the irrevocable beneficiary gave written consent for a $180,000 bank loan against a $500,000 life insurance policy, the policyholder dies when the remaining loan balance is $120,000. How will the insurer distribute the $500,000 death benefit?

承接一笔有效的担保转让：在一份 $500,000 的寿险保单上，不可撤销受益人已书面同意为 $180,000 的银行贷款提供质押担保。后来投保人在尚欠贷款余额 $120,000 时身故。保险公司将如何分配这 $500,000 身故保险金？

  1. ✗ 错误。把担保转让（collateral assignment）混同于绝对转让（absolute assignment）；担保转让中银行只享有在未偿贷款余额范围内优先受偿的有限权益。Incorrect. Confuses a collateral assignment with an absolute assignment; under a collateral assignment, the lender holds only a limited security interest with priority up to the unpaid loan balance.

  2. ✗ 错误。银行在身故时优先取回的是「身故时未偿还的贷款余额」（$120,000），而不是最初放款时的本金总额（$180,000）。Incorrect. The bank's priority claim at death is limited to the unpaid loan balance at death ($120,000), rather than the original loan principal borrowed ($180,000).

  3. ✗ 错误。由于不可撤销受益人已经签署了书面同意并办妥担保转让，银行的担保权益优先于受益人受偿。Incorrect. Because the irrevocable beneficiary provided written consent and the collateral assignment was properly filed, the bank's security interest has priority over the beneficiary's claim.

  4. ✓ 正确。在经不可撤销受益人书面同意的担保转让（collateral assignment）中，被保险人身故时，保险公司先向贷款银行支付未偿贷款余额 $120,000，剩余的 $380,000 归受益人领取。Correct. Under a collateral assignment executed with the irrevocable beneficiary's written consent, when the life insured dies, the insurer first pays the outstanding loan balance of $120,000 to the bank, and distributes the remaining $380,000 to the designated beneficiary.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

第 7 题Q7免于债权人追偿与指定遗产后果Exemption from creditor seizure and consequences of naming the estate★★★陷阱：指定遗产却以为保护还在

Arthur owns a $500,000 life insurance policy on his own life and has significant personal business debts. He tells his agent that he wants to change his beneficiary designation from his wife to 'my estate' so that the insurance proceeds will be distributed together with his other assets under his will. What is the legal consequence if Arthur names his estate as beneficiary?

Arthur 持有一份以自己为被保险人的 $500,000 寿险保单，同时背负着大笔个人生意债务。他告诉代理人，自己想把受益人从妻子改为「我的遗产（estate）」，以便让保险金与其他资产一起按遗嘱分配。如果 Arthur 将受益人指定为自己的遗产，会产生什么法律后果？

  1. ✓ 正确。指定配偶（属于配偶、子女、孙辈、父母组成的保护类受益人，preferred / family class beneficiary，s.65(2)）原本享有在世保单免于扣押及身故保险金免于债权人追偿（s.65(1)）的两层保护；一旦改为「我的遗产（estate）」，等同于自愿放弃这两层豁免，身故保险金将流入遗产走遗嘱认证程序、计缴认证费，并完全暴露在 Arthur 债权人的追偿之下。Correct. Naming a spouse (who belongs to the preferred family class of spouse, child, grandchild, or parent under s.65(2)) originally provided two tiers of protection: lifetime policy exemption from seizure and death benefit exemption from the policyholder's creditors under s.65(1); changing the beneficiary to "my estate" forfeits both exemptions, routing the death benefit into probate where it incurs probate fees and becomes fully exposed to Arthur's creditors.

  2. ✗ 错误。陷阱七：指定「遗产」却以为保护还在。只要保险金的受益人写成 estate 而非直接在保险合同或书面声明中指定具体受益人，钱一旦进入遗产就会先暴露给遗产债权人，遗嘱最后分给谁无法挽回保险法的债权人豁免。Incorrect. Falls into the assuming protection remains after naming the estate trap. Whenever proceeds are made payable to an estate rather than designated directly to a beneficiary in the contract or declaration, the money enters the estate and is exposed to estate creditors; testamentary distribution under a will cannot restore Insurance Act creditor immunity.

  3. ✗ 错误。法律并不禁止投保人将受益人改为自己的遗产（或撤回原指定使保险金归入遗产），但这会导致丧失债权人豁免并产生遗嘱认证成本，代理人有义务事先讲清这三大代价。Incorrect. The law does not prohibit a policyholder from naming their own estate (or revoking an existing designation so proceeds fall to the estate); however, doing so forfeits creditor protection and triggers probate costs, consequences an agent must explain beforehand.

  4. ✗ 错误。只有指定了具体受益人（而非 estate）时，保险金才能绕开遗产和遗嘱认证（probate）；指定给 estate 的保险金必须进入遗产并纳入遗嘱认证资产计费。Incorrect. Only naming a designated beneficiary outside the estate allows death proceeds to bypass probate; proceeds payable to an estate must pass through probate administration and are included in calculating probate fees.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A corporation purchases a $750,000 life insurance policy on the life of its key shareholder, naming the corporation itself (the policyholder) as the recipient of the death benefit. Meanwhile, the shareholder owns a personal $500,000 policy on his own life naming his daughter as revocable beneficiary. If both the corporation and the shareholder face creditor claims while the shareholder is alive, which policy enjoys lifetime protection from seizure by the policyholder's creditors?

某公司以核心股东为被保险人购买了一份 $750,000 的寿险保单，并指定公司自己（即投保人）领取身故保险金。同时，该股东个人持有一份以自己为被保险人的 $500,000 保单，指定女儿为可撤销受益人。如果公司与股东在股东在世期间均面临债权人追讨，哪份保单在投保人在世期间享有免于债权人扣押的保护？

  1. ✗ 错误。投保人给自己指定自己（如公司作为投保人指定自己收款），在法律定义上根本不构成「受益人（beneficiary）」，不享有受益人债权人豁免；且在世期间的保单扣押豁免仅限指定保护类（家庭类）受益人。Incorrect. A policyholder naming itself to receive proceeds (such as a corporation as policyholder collecting proceeds) does not qualify as a beneficiary under statutory definitions, receiving no beneficiary creditor protection; furthermore, lifetime exemption from seizure strictly requires designating a preferred family class beneficiary.

  2. ✓ 正确。女儿属于被保险人的保护类（家庭类）受益人（preferred / family class beneficiary：配偶、子女、孙辈、父母，s.65(2)），可使保单在投保人在世期间免于债权人执行与扣押（前提是非为逃债而临时恶意指定）；而公司保单中公司以投保人（policyholder）身份收款，法定定义下的 beneficiary 不包括投保人本人，因此不享有免于债权人追偿的保护。Correct. A daughter belongs to the preferred family class of beneficiaries (spouse, child, grandchild, parent under s.65(2)), which exempts the policy from execution and seizure by the policyholder's creditors during his lifetime (assuming no fraudulent conveyance); in contrast, under the corporate policy, the corporation collects as policyholder rather than a statutory beneficiary, enjoying no creditor immunity under s.65.

  3. ✗ 错误。恰恰相反，商业保单中投保人指定自己收款属于以投保人身份而非受益人身份领款，完全不享有 s.65 的债权人豁免。Incorrect. On the contrary, when a corporate policyholder receives proceeds, it collects as policyholder rather than as a statutory beneficiary, enjoying zero creditor exemption under s.65.

  4. ✗ 错误。在世期间免于扣押的保护（s.65(2)）既适用于不可撤销受益人，也适用于可撤销的保护类（家庭类：配偶、子女、孙辈、父母）受益人。Incorrect. Lifetime exemption from seizure under s.65(2) applies both to irrevocable beneficiaries and to revocable designations within the preferred family class (spouse, child, grandchild, parent).

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b
