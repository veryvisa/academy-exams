① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 生前处置与ACB算题：题干给出累计保费、纯保险净成本（NCPI）与现金退保价值（CSV）或保单贷款金额，要求计算调整后成本基础（ACB）、应税保单利得（policy gain）或新增应纳税额，测试保单利得100%计入应税收入而非按资本利得50%计入。Lifetime disposition and ACB calculation questions: Presenting cumulative premiums, net cost of pure insurance (NCPI), and cash surrender value (CSV) or policy loan amounts, requiring candidates to calculate the adjusted cost basis (ACB), taxable policy gain, or resulting tax payable, testing that policy gains are 100% included in taxable income rather than included at the 50% capital gains rate.
  • 处置动作辨析题：题干对比全额退保、保单贷款、担保转让（collateral assignment）、绝对转让（absolute assignment）或变为非免税单，测试哪一动作触发处置（disposition）或视同处置（deemed disposition），以及贷款本金是否超ACB与贷款利息能否抵税的独立判断。Disposition action distinction questions: Comparing full surrenders, policy loans, collateral assignments, absolute assignments, or conversions into non-exempt policies, testing which action triggers a disposition or deemed disposition, alongside independent determinations of whether loan principal exceeds ACB and whether loan interest is tax-deductible.
  • 家庭内部转让与展期题：题干描述夫妻间生前转让保单或父母无偿将保单转给子女，测试配偶展期（spousal rollover）的默认适用与主动选择退出（opt out）利用亏损的策略，以及子女展期必须直接过户给子女本人而非转入信托的法定边界。Intra-family transfer and rollover questions: Describing inter vivos policy transfers between spouses or parents transferring policies to children for no consideration, testing the default application of the spousal rollover, the strategy to elect out of the rollover to utilize business losses, and the statutory requirement that child rollovers must be transferred directly to the child rather than into a trust.
  • 企业持有寿险与资本红利账户（CDA）题：题干给出私人公司作为受益人收到身故给付及保单当时的ACB，测试全额赔款免税进公司、但仅有「身故给付减ACB」的差额计入CDA并须按时提交T2054选择申报才可免税分配给股东。Corporate-owned life insurance and capital dividend account (CDA) questions: Presenting private corporations receiving death benefits as beneficiaries alongside the policy's ACB, testing that the full death benefit is received tax-free by the corporation, but only the excess of the death benefit over ACB credits the CDA and requires a timely filed Form T2054 election to be distributed tax-free to shareholders.
  • 遗产规划、慈善捐赠与双口径参数题：题干结合遗产均衡（estate equalization）、慈善保单转让或遗嘱受益人指定、BC省遗嘱认证费（probate fee）及终身资本利得豁免（LCGE），测试生前转让与身后捐赠的收据差异、具名受益人绕开遗嘱认证，以及考试口径（2022）与现行法规口径的区分。Estate planning, charitable giving, and dual-benchmark parameter questions: Combining estate equalization, charitable policy transfers or beneficiary designations, BC probate fees, and the lifetime capital gains exemption (LCGE), testing the receipt differences between inter vivos transfers and testamentary gifts, named beneficiaries bypassing probate, and the distinction between 2022 exam syllabus figures and current tax rules.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 保单利得误套资本利得50%计入率Applying the 50% capital gains inclusion rate to policy gains

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到退保或贷款产生「利得（gain）」，下意识套用资本利得（capital gain）50%的计入率，忽略了寿险保单利得（policy gain）须100%计入当年应税收入。Seeing a gain arise from a surrender or policy loan, candidates instinctively apply the 50% capital gains inclusion rate, overlooking that life insurance policy gains must be 100% included in taxable income for the year.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现退保（surrender）、保单贷款（policy loan）或转让并要求计算应税收入（taxable income）或应纳税额。The question stem mentions a surrender, policy loan, or policy transfer and asks for the taxable income or tax payable.

⚠ 保单贷款误判为免税或混淆利息抵扣Assuming policy loans are entirely tax-free or confusing interest deductibility

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生误以为「借自己保单里的钱不用交税」，忽略了保单贷款在税法上属于处置，超过ACB的部分即产生应税利得；或把贷款本金是否超ACB与贷款利息能否抵税混为一谈。Mistakenly believing that borrowing against one's own policy is never taxable, overlooking that a policy loan constitutes a disposition under tax law where any amount exceeding ACB generates a taxable policy gain, or conflating whether loan principal exceeds ACB with whether loan interest is deductible.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现保单持有人申请policy loan，或同时提及借款金额、ACB与借款用途（个人消费vs赚取应税投资收入）。The question stem involves a policyholder taking a policy loan, or mentions loan amount, ACB, and borrowed fund usage (personal spending versus earning taxable investment income).

⚠ 担保转让与绝对转让混同Confusing collateral assignment with absolute assignment

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到向银行借款并把保单交给银行，误以为发生所有权转让从而触发处置税负，未区分仅作抵押的担保转让（collateral assignment）与改变所有权的绝对转让（absolute assignment）。Seeing a policy pledged to a bank for a loan, candidates assume an ownership transfer occurred triggering a disposition and tax liability, failing to distinguish between a collateral assignment as security and an absolute assignment transferring ownership.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现assign the policy as collateral security for a bank loan（将保单作为银行贷款的担保物）。The question stem mentions assigning the policy as collateral security for a bank loan.

⚠ CDA免税额误算为全额身故给付Crediting the full death benefit to the CDA without deducting ACB

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生知道公司收到身故给付免税，便误以为全额赔款都能計入资本红利账户（CDA）免税分给股东，漏减了保单的ACB，或忽略了T2054选择申报手续。Knowing corporate receipt of a death benefit is tax-free, candidates mistakenly assume the entire payout can credit the capital dividend account (CDA) for tax-free distribution to shareholders, omitting the deduction of the policy's ACB or overlooking the mandatory Form T2054 election.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出公司持有保单的身故给付金额及当时的非零ACB，问可免税分配给股东的资本红利（capital dividend）或计入CDA的金额。The question stem provides a corporate-owned policy's death benefit and a non-zero ACB, asking for the CDA credit or tax-free capital dividend amount.

⚠ 配偶/子女展期条件与默认方向颠倒Reversing spousal or child rollover conditions and default directions

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生误以为配偶生前转让需要主动申请才能展期，或误以为把保单转入以子女为受益人的信托也能享受子女直系展期（rollover）。Assuming an inter vivos spousal transfer requires an election to obtain a rollover, or mistakenly assuming that transferring a policy into a trust for a child qualifies for the statutory intergenerational child rollover.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述生前将保单转让给配偶（且转让人当年有营业亏损）或通过信托（trust）转让给未成年子女。The question stem describes transferring a policy to a spouse during lifetime (especially when the transferor has business losses) or transferring a policy to minor children through a trust.

第 1 题Q1全额退保的ACB与应税保单利得计算ACB calculation and taxable policy gain on full surrender★★陷阱：保单利得误套资本利得50%计入率

Clara, a resident of Vancouver, purchased an exempt participating whole life policy in 2006 (a G2 policy). She has never taken a policy loan or cash dividend. According to the insurer's records, her cumulative premiums paid total $50,000, the cumulative net cost of pure insurance (NCPI) is $16,000, and the current cash surrender value (CSV) is $64,000. If Clara is in a 30% marginal tax bracket and fully surrenders the policy today, what is her approximate tax payable on this transaction (ignoring AMT and other credits)?

居住在温哥华的Clara于2006年购买了一张免税分红终身寿险保单（G2保单），从未申请过保单贷款或领取现金红利。根据保险公司记录，她累计缴纳保费$50,000，累计纯保险净成本（NCPI）为$16,000，当前现金退保价值（CSV）为$64,000。若Clara当年的边际税率为30%，并在今天全额退保，忽略替代最低税（AMT）及其他抵免，这笔交易大约需缴纳多少税款？

  1. ✓ 正确。首先计算调整后成本基础（adjusted cost basis, ACB）＝累计保费$50,000 − 累计纯保险净成本（NCPI）$16,000 ＝ $34,000；其次计算保单利得（policy gain）＝ 处置所得（CSV $64,000）− ACB（$34,000）＝ $30,000；保单利得100%计入应税收入，按30%边际税率估算应纳税额为$30,000 × 30% ＝ $9,000。Correct. First calculate the adjusted cost basis (ACB): cumulative premiums ($50,000) minus cumulative net cost of pure insurance (NCPI of $16,000) equals $34,000. Next, calculate the policy gain: proceeds of disposition (CSV of $64,000) minus ACB ($34,000) equals $30,000. Policy gains are 100% included in taxable income, so at Clara's 30% marginal tax rate, tax payable is $30,000 x 30% = $9,000.

  2. ✗ 错误。落入了「保单利得误套资本利得50%计入率」的头号陷阱；寿险退保产生的是保单利得（policy gain），必须100%计入应税收入，不适用资本利得（capital gain）50%计入率。Falls into the 'applying the 50% capital gains inclusion rate to policy gains' trap. Life insurance surrenders generate policy gains, which must be 100% included in taxable income and do not qualify for the 50% capital gains inclusion rate.

  3. ✗ 错误。误把已缴保费（$50,000）直接当成ACB，漏减了累计纯保险净成本（NCPI $16,000），导致少算应税保单利得。Falls into the 'mistaking cumulative premiums for ACB' trap. Erroneously treats cumulative premiums of $50,000 as the ACB without deducting cumulative NCPI of $16,000, thereby understating the taxable policy gain.

  4. ✗ 错误。混淆了身故给付（death benefit）与生前退保处置（disposition）；免税保单（exempt policy）仅在持有期间免于按年计提增值，全额退保时超出ACB的部分必须纳税。Confuses death benefits with a lifetime surrender disposition. An exempt policy is only exempt from annual accrual taxation during policy accumulation; surrendering the policy for cash requires tax to be paid on the excess of proceeds over ACB.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Owen owns an exempt whole life policy acquired in 2008 with no prior loans or dividends. He has paid $32,000 in cumulative premiums, the insurer's cumulative NCPI is $9,000, and the current CSV is $38,000. If Owen is in a 40% marginal tax bracket and surrenders the entire policy today, what is his approximate tax liability from the surrender?

Owen持有一张2008年购入的免税终身寿险保单，无过往贷款或红利领取。他累计缴纳保费$32,000，保险公司累计NCPI为$9,000，当前CSV为$38,000。若Owen处于40%边际税率档并在今天全额退保，因退保产生的大约税负是多少？

  1. ✗ 错误。未从累计保费$32,000中扣除累计纯保险净成本（NCPI $9,000），误将ACB算作$32,000。Fails to deduct cumulative NCPI ($9,000) from cumulative premiums ($32,000), mistakenly calculating the ACB as $32,000.

  2. ✗ 错误。误将保单利得（policy gain）当作资本利得按50%计入应税收入；保单利得必须100%计入。Falls into the 'applying the 50% capital gains inclusion rate to policy gains' trap. Mistakenly includes the $15,000 policy gain at 50% as a capital gain; policy gains must be 100% included in taxable income.

  3. ✓ 正确。ACB ＝ $32,000 − $9,000 ＝ $23,000；保单利得（policy gain）＝ $38,000 − $23,000 ＝ $15,000，100%计入应税收入，按40%边际税率计算税款为$15,000 × 40% ＝ $6,000。Correct. The ACB is $32,000 - $9,000 = $23,000. The policy gain is $38,000 - $23,000 = $15,000. Policy gains are 100% included in taxable income, so at Owen's 40% marginal tax rate, tax payable is $15,000 x 40% = $6,000.

  4. ✗ 错误。既没有扣除ACB（$23,000），又错误套用了50%的资本利得计入率。Simultaneously fails to deduct the ACB ($23,000) and incorrectly applies a 50% capital gains inclusion rate to the entire cash surrender value.

Since other estate assets already exceed the $50,000 threshold, the entire $300,000 attracts the rate of $14 per $1,000 (which is identical between the exam syllabus and 2026 current rules), calculating as ($300,000 / $1,000) x $14 = $4,200.

  3. ✗ 错误。每$1,000收$6的费率仅适用于遗产估值在$25,000至$50,000之间的区段；超过$50,000的部分适用每$1,000收$14。The $6 per $1,000 rate applies strictly to the estate value tier between $25,000 and $50,000; values exceeding $50,000 are subject to $14 per $1,000.

  4. ✗ 错误。混淆了遗嘱认证费（probate fee）与所得税（income tax）：普通免税寿险的身故给付本金即使进入遗产，在所得税上仍不计入所得，只是丧失了遗嘱认证费豁免。Confuses probate fees with income taxes. The death benefit principal of an ordinary exempt policy remains non-taxable for income tax purposes even if paid into the estate, but simply loses the exemption from probate fees.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Before passing away in Maple Ridge, BC, the same senior updated his $300,000 ordinary life insurance policy to name his niece directly as the revocable beneficiary. How are the $300,000 death benefit proceeds treated at his death?

在BC省枫树岭去世前，这位长者更新了其$300,000普通寿险保单，将自己的侄女直接指定为可撤销具名受益人。他身故时这$300,000身故给付如何处理？

  1. ✓ 正确。明确指定具名受益人（named beneficiary，哪怕是侄女）后，身故给付（death benefit）通常在遗产之外直接支付给有效受益人，既绕开BC省遗嘱认证程序免交遗嘱认证费（probate fee），本金也不计入受益人的应税所得。Correct. Specifying a valid named beneficiary (even a niece) allows the death benefit to be paid outside the estate directly to the beneficiary, bypassing BC probate and avoiding probate fees, while the principal is received tax-free.

  2. ✗ 错误。绕开遗嘱认证程序只需要有有效的具名受益人（如案例三中的侄女），并不要求受益人必须是不可撤销或必须是配偶/子女。Bypassing the probate process requires only a valid named beneficiary; there is no statutory requirement that the beneficiary designation be irrevocable or restricted to a spouse or child.

  3. ✗ 错误。普通免税寿险的身故给付（death benefit）本金对任何具名受益人均免征所得税，不因受益人是侄女而非配偶就征税。The death benefit principal of an exempt life policy is non-taxable to any named beneficiary, and naming a niece rather than a spouse does not make 50% of the payout taxable.

  4. ✗ 错误。具名受益人直接向保险公司申请理赔，无需向法院申请遗嘱认证，因此既不交按比例的认证费，也不交法院遗嘱认证提交费（考试口径印$208，2026现行法为$200）。A named beneficiary claims proceeds directly from the insurer without applying to court for probate, paying neither the proportional probate fee nor the flat court filing fee ($208 in the exam syllabus versus $200 currently).

第 7 题Q7LCGE合格资产与考试双口径数字LCGE qualifying assets and exam syllabus versus current figures★★陷阱：误把普通经营合伙权益当成LCGE合格资产或混用年度数字

Martin is planning his estate and wants to know how the lifetime capital gains exemption (LCGE) applies to his business interests. Based on the LLQP exam syllabus rules and the 2022 syllabus figure, which statement accurately describes the eligibility and exam-syllabus limit for the LCGE?

Martin正在做遗产规划，想了解终身资本利得豁免（LCGE）如何适用于他的商业权益。根据LLQP考试口径规则及2022年考试口径数字，以下哪项准确描述了LCGE的资产资格与考试口径上限？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入了第七节第7条的资产资格陷阱：普通经营合伙权益（general partnership interest）通常不属于LCGE豁免范围，只有合格家族农场或渔业合伙权益才有例外。Falls into the asset eligibility trap: general commercial partnership interests do not qualify for the LCGE, with exceptions restricted strictly to qualified family farm or fishing partnerships.

  2. ✗ 错误。终身资本利得豁免（LCGE）仅用于抵消处置合格小型企业公司股份（QSBC shares）或合格农渔业财产产生的资本利得，绝不适用于寿险保单利得（policy gain）。The lifetime capital gains exemption (LCGE) applies solely to capital gains on qualified small business corporation (QSBC) shares or qualified farm and fishing property, and can never shelter life insurance policy gains.

  3. ✓ 正确。依考试口径（2022年），合格小型企业公司股份（QSBC shares）的终身资本利得豁免（lifetime capital gains exemption, LCGE）上限为$913,630，且普通经营合伙权益通常不适用（注：2026年现行法下LCGE上限已调升至$1,275,000，对应50%计入率的扣除上限为$637,500，考试按题干给定口径作答）。Correct. Under the 2022 exam syllabus, the lifetime capital gains exemption (LCGE) limit for qualified small business corporation (QSBC) shares is $913,630, and general partnership interests do not qualify (note: under 2026 current rules, the LCGE limit is $1,275,000, providing a cumulative deduction of $637,500 at a 50% inclusion rate; exam candidates must follow the syllabus figures provided in the question stem).

  4. ✗ 错误。并非任何加拿大私人公司（CCPC）股份都合格，必须核实资产、持有期及使用等法定条件，不能仅凭「公司」二字就认定适用LCGE。Shares of any Canadian private corporation do not automatically qualify for the LCGE without verifying statutory holding period and active business asset tests.
