① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 情景定性题：给一段代理人或未持牌助理的日常动作（如代打电话约访、口头承诺核保必过），考你该行为属于哪一层法律管辖、是否构成越权或无牌招揽。Scenario characterization questions: Presenting daily actions of an agent or unlicensed assistant (such as phoning prospects to book appointments or verbally guaranteeing underwriting approval), testing which level of law governs the conduct and whether it constitutes ultra vires actions or unlicensed solicitation.
  • 年龄与行为能力辨析题：混搭15岁、16岁、18岁、19岁与100岁高龄申请人，考你能否区分法定行为能力（Legal capacity）、自行投保最低年龄与受益人直接领款年龄。Age and legal capacity distinction questions: Combining 15-, 16-, 18-, 19-, and 100-year-old applicants, testing whether candidates can distinguish legal capacity, the minimum statutory age to contract for insurance on one's own life, and the age required for a beneficiary to receive proceeds directly.
  • 文书权限与流程题：家属持普通授权书（Power of Attorney）或持续授权书（Enduring Power of Attorney）复印件要求变更受益人，考你四步实务流程、法院授权门槛及双口径差异。Legal documentation authority and procedural workflow questions: Presenting a family member holding an ordinary Power of Attorney or a photocopy of an Enduring Power of Attorney demanding a beneficiary change, testing the four-step practical workflow, court authorization thresholds, and dual-benchmark distinctions.
  • 跨部门法冲突题：分居协议承诺保留前配偶为受益人但未办不可撤销指定（Irrevocable beneficiary designation），考你家庭法协议与保险法免责付款规则（s.77）谁在保单层面优先。Inter-statutory conflict questions: When a separation agreement promises to maintain an ex-spouse as beneficiary but no irrevocable beneficiary designation was filed, testing whether family law agreements or Insurance Act discharge of insurer rules (s.77) take priority at the policy level.
  • 外围合规与公共保障筛查题：结合大额现金分拆交费、自雇客户申请就业保险（EI）病假给付或省内客户档案管理，考你PCMLTFA的24小时合并规则、PIPA适用边界及政府保障缺口。Perimeter compliance and public benefit screening questions: Combining split large cash premium payments, a self-employed client applying for Employment Insurance (EI) sickness benefits, or provincial client file management, testing the PCMLTFA 24-hour aggregation rule, PIPA jurisdictional boundaries, and public safety net gaps.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 省管保险等于联邦无关Equating provincial insurance regulation with total federal irrelevance trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生记住了宪法把保险合同划归省管，就误以为寿险业务完全不受联邦法律约束，忽略了税法、反洗钱、隐私、电销与基因不歧视等联邦外围立法。Candidates recall that the Constitution assigns insurance contracts to provincial jurisdiction and mistakenly assume life insurance is entirely free from federal law, overlooking federal perimeter statutes including taxation, anti-money laundering, privacy, telemarketing, and genetic non-discrimination.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干或选项出现「寿险业务仅受省级立法管辖」或询问大额现金报告、基因检测禁令、跨省信息流适用哪一层法律。The question stem or options state that life insurance is governed solely by provincial legislation, or ask which level of law governs large cash reporting, genetic testing bans, or interprovincial data flows.

⚠ 未持牌助理仅约见面不违规Assuming an unlicensed assistant only booking appointments commits no violation trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉认为只要不谈具体保费、不签投保单，让行政助理打电话给潜在客户约时间或转递申请表只算行政辅助，不算销售。Candidates intuitively assume that as long as an administrative assistant does not discuss specific premium numbers or sign the application, calling prospective clients to book appointments or forwarding application forms is merely administrative assistance rather than selling.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现未持牌雇员（unlicensed assistant）致电潜在客户约访、接收或转递申请、收取保费或制作寿险演示表。The question stem describes an unlicensed assistant telephoning prospects to book appointments, receiving or forwarding applications, collecting premiums, or preparing life insurance illustrations.

⚠ 高龄老人推定无缔约能力Presuming elderly individuals lack contracting capacity trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把高龄（如100岁）等同于丧失法定行为能力，或误以为年纪大就必须让成年子女代签、以及把BC省19岁成年年龄当成自行投保最低年龄。Candidates equate advanced age (such as age 100) with a lack of legal capacity, mistakenly believe that older clients must have adult children co-sign, or confuse British Columbia's age of majority of 19 with the minimum age to purchase insurance on one's own life.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调申请人年满100岁但神志清醒，或出现16岁、17岁未成年人为自己投保、18岁受益人领款等年龄数字。The question stem emphasizes that an applicant is 100 years old but mentally sharp, or presents ages such as a 16- or 17-year-old purchasing their own policy or an 18-year-old beneficiary collecting proceeds.

⚠ 持授权书即可直接改受益人Assuming holding a Power of Attorney permits changing beneficiaries directly trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 混淆普通授权书与持续授权书在丧失能力后的效力，或看到考试口径提及BC省例外就误以为受托代理人凭持续授权书复印件即可任意更换既有受益人。Candidates confuse the legal effect of an ordinary Power of Attorney versus an Enduring Power of Attorney after incapacity, or notice the exam curriculum mentions a BC exception and mistakenly believe an attorney can freely replace existing beneficiaries using a photocopy of an Enduring PoA.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现授权人已因认知障碍失能、家属持普通授权书或持续授权书复印件要求将保单受益人改成自己。The question stem describes a donor who is incapacitated by cognitive impairment, with a family member presenting an ordinary PoA or a photocopy of an Enduring PoA demanding to change the policy beneficiary to themselves.

⚠ 分居协议自动锁死保单受益人Assuming a separation agreement automatically locks policy beneficiaries trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为婚姻分居协议里写明维持前配偶为受益人就自动冻结了保单变更权，分不清合同对人的约束与不可撤销受益人指定对保单的约束。Candidates assume that a marital separation agreement stipulating to maintain an ex-spouse as beneficiary automatically freezes the policyowner's right to change designations, confusing an inter-party personal contract with an irrevocable beneficiary designation binding the insurer at the policy level.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述被保险人签有分居协议承诺不改受益人，但未向保险公司登记不可撤销指定，随后在线或书面改成了新配偶并身故。The question stem describes an insured who signed a separation agreement promising not to change the beneficiary, but never registered an irrevocable designation with the insurer, subsequently changing the beneficiary online or in writing to a new spouse before dying.

代理关系与未持牌禁区Agency Relationship and Prohibited Unlicensed ActivitiesPrincipal / Agent / Unlicensed staff寿险代理人代表委托人（保险公司）且权限受书面合同限制；代理行内凡涉及招揽、接收或转递申请、收保费、做演示表的雇员均须持牌。A life insurance agent represents the principal (the insurer) with authority circumscribed by the written agency contract; agency employees who solicit insurance, receive or forward applications, collect premiums, or prepare policy illustrations must be licensed.
法定行为能力与年龄界限Legal Capacity and Age BoundariesLegal capacity / Age of majority行为能力取决于缔约时能否理解条款与协议约束力，无最高年龄上限（100岁仍推定有能力）；BC成年年龄为19岁，但满16岁即可为自己投保。Legal capacity depends on whether an individual understands policy terms and the binding nature of the contract at execution, with no upper age limit (a 100-year-old is presumed capable); the BC age of majority is 19, but individuals aged 16 or older can contract for insurance on their own lives.
可保利益与书面同意例外Insurable Interest and the Written Consent ExceptionInsurable interestBC《Insurance Act》s.45(1)规定生效时缺乏可保利益的合同无效，但s.45(2)明确豁免团体保险及被保险人本人书面同意（未满16岁由父母同意）的情形。BC Insurance Act s.45(1) provides that a contract is void if insurable interest is lacking when it takes effect, but s.45(2) explicitly exempts group insurance and cases where the insured consents in writing (or parental consent if under age 16).
BC两部核心法的分工与监管机构Division Between BC Core Statutes and Regulatory BodiesInsurance Act / Financial Institutions Act / BCFSA《Insurance Act》管保险合同条款，《Financial Institutions Act》管人与机构行为；BC现行保险公司监管机构为BCFSA（取代旧称FICOM），代理人发牌机构为Insurance Council of BC。The Insurance Act governs contract terms, while the Financial Institutions Act governs market conduct and institutions; BC insurers are regulated by BCFSA (which replaced FICOM), and life agents are licensed by the Insurance Council of BC.
普通授权书与持续授权书及受益人限制Ordinary PoA versus Enduring PoA and Beneficiary RestrictionsPower of Attorney (PoA) / Enduring Power of Attorney普通授权书在授权人丧失心智能力时终止，持续授权书须明写失能后继续有效；BC《Power of Attorney Act》s.20规定变更本人已有受益人指定须取得法院授权。An ordinary Power of Attorney terminates upon the donor's mental incapacity, whereas an Enduring Power of Attorney must explicitly state it continues after incapacity; BC Power of Attorney Act s.20 mandates court authorization to change the donor's existing beneficiary designations.
公共保障机制（CPP / OAS / GIS / EI）Public Benefit Programs (CPP / OAS / GIS / EI)CPP / OAS / GIS / Employment Insurance (EI)CPP为劳资等额强制缴费型；OAS为非缴费型且应税，GIS为低收入OAS领取者的免税补充；EI病假最长26周按收入55%计（每周上限$729），自雇者不符合常规EI资格。The CPP is a mandatory contributory plan with equal employer-employee contributions; OAS is non-contributory and taxable, while GIS is a tax-free supplement for low-income OAS recipients; EI sickness benefits pay 55% of earnings up to 26 weeks (2026 weekly maximum $729), and self-employed individuals do not qualify for regular EI.
隐私法分工与反洗钱个人报告义务Privacy Law Division and Personal AML Reporting DutiesPIPEDA / PIPA / PCMLTFA / FINTRACBC省内私营商业活动适用实质相似的省法PIPA，跨省跨境或联邦企业适用PIPEDA；反洗钱报告义务主体是代理人本人，大额现金交易门槛$10,000含连续24小时合并规则。Intra-provincial private-sector commercial activity in BC is governed by the substantially similar provincial PIPA, while cross-border data flows or federal works fall under PIPEDA; under the PCMLTFA, reporting duties rest personally upon the agent, with a $10,000 large cash threshold subject to a 24-hour aggregation rule.
基因不歧视法与人权法边界Genetic Non-Discrimination and Human Rights BoundariesGenetic Non-Discrimination Act (GNDA) / Bona fide groundsGNDA禁止以接受基因检测或披露检测结果作为缔结或维持保险合同的前提；人权法下须对照具体受保护理由与法条核实善意且合理的依据（bona fide grounds）。The Genetic Non-Discrimination Act (GNDA) prohibits requiring genetic tests or disclosing test results as a condition of entering or maintaining an insurance contract; human rights legislation permits distinctions only where justified by bona fide and reasonable underwriting grounds.
受益人变更、免责付款与分居协议Beneficiary Revocation, Discharge of Insurer, and Separation AgreementsIrrevocable beneficiary / Discharge of insurer未登记并送达不可撤销指定（s.60）时，后指定优先于先指定（s.61），保险人在主要办事处收到影响权利的书面文件前依现有记录付款即可免责（s.77）。Unless an irrevocable designation is recorded and delivered (s.60), a subsequent designation overrides an earlier one (s.61); an insurer is fully discharged (s.77) upon paying the recorded beneficiary before receiving written notice of competing claims at its principal office.

第 1 题Q1未持牌人员禁区与营销合规Prohibited unlicensed activities and marketing compliance★★陷阱：未持牌助理仅约见面不违规

Marcus, a licensed life insurance agent in British Columbia, hires an unlicensed administrative assistant to help grow his practice. Marcus instructs the assistant to call prospective clients from a purchased lead list to schedule insurance review appointments, prepare life insurance policy illustrations before meetings, and forward signed applications to the managing general agent (MGA). Which assessment of these duties is correct?

BC省持牌寿险代理人Marcus雇了一名未持牌行政助理协助拓展业务。Marcus指示该助理按购买的潜在客户名单致电约时间面谈保险、在会前制作寿险保单演示表，并将签妥的投保申请转递给寿险总代理（MGA）。关于这些工作职责，下列哪项评估是正确的？

  1. ✗ 需要持牌的行为清单（Licensing requirements）并不限于签单或给建议；招揽（Soliciting）、制作寿险演示表（Preparing illustrations）和转递申请（Forwarding applications）均明确要求持牌（未持牌助理仅约见面不违规陷阱）。Incorrect. Falls into the assuming an unlicensed assistant only booking appointments commits no violation trap. Statutory licensing requirements are not limited to signing applications or giving advice; soliciting insurance, preparing life insurance illustrations, and forwarding applications all legally require an insurance license.

  2. ✓ 正确。代理行里凡涉及招揽保险（含致电潜在客户约谈保险）、接收或转递申请及保单、收取保费、制作寿险演示表的雇员都必须持牌；同时直接致电潜在客户还须遵守全国勿扰电话名单（National DNCL）规则。Correct. Agency employees who solicit insurance (including telephoning prospects to book insurance discussions), receive or forward applications and policies, collect premiums, or prepare life insurance policy illustrations must be licensed; furthermore, unsolicited calls to prospective clients must comply with National Do Not Call List (DNCL) rules.

  3. ✗ 未持牌助理替代理人打电话给潜在客户约时间谈保险在性质上就属于无牌招揽（Unlicensed solicitation），转递申请同样在必须持牌的清单内。Incorrect. An unlicensed assistant telephoning prospective clients to schedule insurance appointments constitutes unlicensed solicitation, and forwarding applications is likewise an activity that strictly requires a license.

  4. ✗ 改用群发商业电邮不仅不能绕开无牌招揽禁令，还会触发加拿大反垃圾邮件法（CASL）关于商业电子讯息同意与退订机制的强制要求。Incorrect. Switching to bulk commercial emails does not circumvent unlicensed solicitation prohibitions and additionally triggers mandatory consent and unsubscribe mechanism requirements under Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An incorporated insurance agency in British Columbia employs an unlicensed receptionist. When walk-in clients visit the office, the receptionist collects their initial premium cheques, helps them fill out and submit policy applications, and generates basic life insurance illustrations while the licensed agent is away. Why does this arrangement violate regulatory rules?

BC省一家注册成立的保险代理行雇有一名未持牌前台。当持牌代理人外出时，该前台接待上门客户，代为收取首期保费支票、协助接收并递交投保申请，并生成基础寿险演示表。为什么这一安排违反监管规则？

  1. ✗ 代理行（Agency）可以雇佣纯行政人员，违规点在于让未持牌雇员从事了法定必须持牌的保险业务动作，而非禁止雇佣行政员工。Incorrect. An insurance agency is permitted to employ purely administrative personnel; the violation arises from assigning unlicensed staff activities that legally require a license, rather than a blanket ban on hiring administrative staff.

  2. ✗ 独立代理人（Independent agent）与专属代理人（Captive agent）只要持牌并在代理合同授权范围内，均可收取保费和制作演示表。Incorrect. Both independent agents and captive agents are permitted to collect premiums and prepare illustrations, provided they are properly licensed and authorized under their agency contracts.

  3. ✓ 正确。考试口径明确规定：代理行内凡是在招揽保险、接收投保申请、洽谈或促成保险、转递申请或保单、收取保费、制作寿险演示表的雇员，都必须持牌。Correct. The exam standard specifies that agency employees who solicit insurance, receive applications, negotiate or effect insurance, forward applications or policies, collect premiums, or prepare life insurance illustrations must hold an insurance license.

  4. ✗ 两名高管或董事签字是公司作为投保人申请保单时的常见签字权要求，与代理行前台无牌收保费无关；且管代理人持牌的是《Financial Institutions Act》而非《Insurance Act》。Incorrect. Having two corporate officers sign is a standard signing authority requirement when a corporation applies for a policy, which is irrelevant to collecting premium cheques; moreover, agent licensing is governed by the Financial Institutions Act rather than the Insurance Act.

第 2 题Q2法定行为能力与投保年龄Legal capacity and statutory insurance purchase ages★★陷阱：高龄老人推定无缔约能力

A life insurance agent in British Columbia meets two prospective applicants on the same day: Arthur, a mentally sharp 100-year-old who wants to sign an annuity contract on his own, and Chloe, a 17-year-old student who understands policy terms and wants to apply for life insurance on her own life. How does the law of legal capacity apply to Arthur and Chloe?

BC省一名寿险代理人在同一天接待了两位潜在投保人：一位是头脑清醒、想自己签署年金合同的100岁老人Arthur；另一位是理解保单条款、想为自己生命投保寿险的17岁学生Chloe。法定行为能力（Legal capacity）规则如何适用于Arthur和Chloe？

  1. ✓ 正确。法律没有设定推定丧失同意能力的最高年龄，100岁老人只要能理解条款并明白自己在缔结有约束力的协议，仍被推定具有法定行为能力（Legal capacity）；而在BC省，虽成年年龄为19岁，但依《Insurance Act》s.72，年满16岁者即有能力为自己投保。Correct. The law sets no maximum age at which capacity is presumed lost, so a 100-year-old who understands policy terms and the binding nature of the contract is presumed to possess legal capacity; furthermore, under BC Insurance Act s.72, anyone aged 16 or older has the legal capacity to contract for insurance on their own life, even though BC's age of majority is 19.

  2. ✗ 法律无能力上限年龄，代理人不能因客户高龄就默认要求家属签字；且BC省成年年龄（Age of majority）是19岁而非16岁（高龄老人推定无缔约能力陷阱）。Incorrect. Falls into the presuming elderly individuals lack contracting capacity trap. There is no statutory upper age limit for capacity, and an agent cannot arbitrarily demand family co-signatures based solely on age; additionally, the age of majority in British Columbia is 19, not 16.

  3. ✗ 在BC省，由父母基于可保利益以孩子名义代为投保适用于15岁及以下；年满16岁（含17岁）已具备为自己订立保险合同的法定能力（s.72）。Incorrect. Having parents apply on a minor's behalf based on insurable interest applies to children aged 15 and under; individuals aged 16 or older (including 17-year-olds) possess statutory capacity to contract for life insurance on their own lives under s.72.

  4. ✗ Arthur神志清醒具备行为能力，Chloe已满16岁具备自行投保能力，两人均无需代表协议（Representation agreement）或公共监护人介入。Incorrect. Arthur is mentally sharp and retains legal capacity, while Chloe is 16 or older and has statutory contracting capacity; neither requires a representation agreement or intervention by the Public Guardian and Trustee.

  4. ✗ 虽然宪法将保险合同法划归省管，但反洗钱（PCMLTFA）与跨省/联邦隐私（PIPEDA）等外围法律明确由联邦深度介入并直接约束代理人（省管保险等于联邦无关陷阱）。Incorrect. Falls into the equating provincial insurance regulation with total federal irrelevance trap. While the Constitution allocates insurance contracts to provincial jurisdiction, federal perimeter legislation—including anti-money laundering (PCMLTFA) and cross-border privacy (PIPEDA)—directly regulates life insurance agents.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A life insurance agent in British Columbia suspects that a client is using a non-exempt life insurance product to launder illicit funds, and also transfers personal client data across provincial borders to an affiliate in another province. Who bears the statutory responsibility for reporting the suspicious transaction to FINTRAC, and which privacy law applies to the cross-border data flow?

BC省一名寿险代理人怀疑客户正利用一份非豁免寿险产品清洗非法资金，同时代理人还将客户个人信息跨省传输给外省的关联机构。谁承担向FINTRAC报告可疑交易的法定责任？跨省数据传输适用哪一部隐私法？

  1. ✗ 反洗钱报告义务直接压在代理人本人身上，并非只由公司合规官承担；且《Representation Agreement Act》管个人照护与日常财务代表协议，不管隐私。Incorrect. Anti-money laundering reporting obligations rest directly on individual agents rather than solely on an insurer's chief compliance officer; additionally, the Representation Agreement Act governs personal care and financial representation, not data privacy.

  2. ✗ 可疑交易报告（Suspicious transaction report）不以达到某个固定金额为前提（大额现金门槛也是$10,000而非CPP上限$74,600）；s.42管保单必备条款不管隐私。Incorrect. A suspicious transaction report (STR) has no minimum monetary threshold (and the large cash threshold is $10,000, not the CPP ceiling of $74,600); moreover, BC Insurance Act s.42 prescribes required policy provisions, not privacy rules.

  3. ✗ 反洗钱向FINTRAC报告的主体是代理人本人而非客户向BCFSA自首（10个营业日是CASL/CRTC退订期限）；且跨省/跨境信息流受联邦PIPEDA管辖。Incorrect. The legal duty to report to FINTRAC falls upon the agent rather than the client self-reporting to BCFSA (the 10-business-day window relates to CASL unsubscribe requests); furthermore, cross-border personal data transfers fall under federal PIPEDA.

  4. ✓ 正确。PCMLTFA将报告可疑交易与涉恐财产的责任直接压在代理人本人身上，未报告面临严厉个人处罚乃至监禁；而在隐私法下，虽然BC省内商业活动走省法PIPA，但个人信息跨省或跨境流动仍受联邦PIPEDA管辖。Correct. The PCMLTFA places the statutory obligation to report suspicious transactions and terrorist property directly upon the individual agent, with non-compliance carrying severe penalties including imprisonment; while intra-provincial business in BC falls under provincial PIPA, personal data crossing provincial or national borders is governed by federal PIPEDA.

第 7 题Q7公共保障层机制与自雇限制Public benefit program mechanics and self-employed limitations★★陷阱：省管保险等于联邦无关

A life insurance agent in British Columbia is conducting a disability and retirement needs analysis for Sophia, an unincorporated self-employed graphic designer who has never opted into any special federal program, and for her 65-year-old retired father, who collects both the Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS) and Old Age Security (OAS). Which statement accurately describes how government benefit programs apply to Sophia and her father?

BC省一名寿险代理人正在为未注册自雇平面设计师Sophia（从未参加任何联邦特别自愿计划）及其65岁退休父亲（正在领取老年保障金OAS与保证收入补充金GIS）做伤残与退休需求分析。下列哪项准确描述了政府公共保障计划如何适用于Sophia和她的父亲？

  1. ✗ 自雇者（Self-employed）不符合常规就业保险（EI）资格；且OAS与GIS均由政府一般税收（General tax revenues）供款而非雇主缴费，其中OAS应税、只有GIS免税。Incorrect. Self-employed individuals are ineligible for regular Employment Insurance (EI) sickness benefits; furthermore, both OAS and GIS are funded from federal general tax revenues rather than payroll deductions, and OAS is taxable while only the GIS is tax-free.

  2. ✗ 工伤补偿（Workers' compensation）是由雇主强制缴款、保障工人的省级无过错制度，而CPP是强制缴费型且应税。Incorrect. Workers' compensation is an employer-funded provincial no-fault system protecting eligible workers, whereas the CPP is a mandatory contributory program whose retirement benefits are taxable.

  3. ✓ 正确。就业保险（Employment Insurance, EI）要求具备最低可保工作小时数，自雇者不符合常规EI资格（这在做伤残险需求分析时至关重要）；而老年保障金（OAS）是非缴费型、由政府税收供款且对领取人应税，保证收入补充金（GIS）则是发给低收入OAS领取者的免税（Non-taxable）补充金。Correct. Regular Employment Insurance (EI) requires insurable employment hours, meaning self-employed individuals do not qualify for regular EI (making individual disability coverage essential); Old Age Security (OAS) is a non-contributory pension funded by general tax revenues and is taxable, whereas the Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS) is a tax-free supplement for low-income seniors.

  4. ✗ BC省医保保费已于2020年1月1日取消（此前家庭每年最多$1,800），且省医保覆盖医疗必需的医生服务，不覆盖常规处方药、常规牙科及19至64岁常规验光。Incorrect. BC Medical Services Plan (MSP) individual premiums were eliminated on January 1, 2020 (previously up to $1,800 annually per family); additionally, MSP covers medically required physician services, not routine prescription drugs, routine dental care, or eye exams for adults aged 19 to 64.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

In British Columbia, a salaried employee earning $90,000 per year asks his insurance agent how the Canada Pension Plan (CPP), provincial workers' compensation, and private group health plans coordinate when he faces an injury or medical expense. Which explanation is accurate?

在BC省，一名年薪$90,000的受薪雇员向保险代理人询问：当他面临工伤或医疗费用支出时，加拿大退休金计划（CPP）、省工伤补偿（Workers' compensation）以及私营团体健康险计划是如何运作与协调的？下列哪项解释是准确的？

  1. ✓ 正确。工伤补偿（Workers' compensation）是省管、无过错、雇主强制缴款的制度，代价是工人放弃起诉雇主的权利（BC 工资损失给付大致为计算所得净收入的 90%）；给付协调（Coordination of benefits）确保夫妻各有团体计划时同一笔费用先由一方赔付、另一方只补余额，不重复赔付。Correct. Workers' compensation is an employer-funded, no-fault provincial program under which injured workers surrender their right of action against employers (in BC wage-loss benefits equal approximately 90% of net earnings); coordination of benefits ensures that when spouses each have group plans, claims are adjudicated so the combined payout covers up to 100% of eligible expenses without duplicate reimbursement.

  2. ✗ CPP是由雇员与雇主等额缴费供款的强制缴费型计划而非一般税收供款；且工伤补偿的核心交换条件就是工人放弃起诉雇主的权利。Incorrect. The CPP is a contributory social insurance program funded equally by employee and employer payroll contributions rather than general tax revenues; furthermore, the foundational historic compromise of workers' compensation is that workers surrender the right to sue employers for workplace injuries.

  3. ✗ 考试口径专门澄清车险「无过错」（No-fault）不等于不判定谁造成事故，而是被保险人向自己的保险公司索赔医疗与收入替代给付；且给付协调禁止同一笔牙科账单双倍赔付。Incorrect. The exam standard clarifies that auto insurance 'no-fault' does not mean fault is never determined, but rather that injured parties claim medical and income benefits from their own insurer; moreover, coordination of benefits rules strictly prohibit double recovery on a single dental expense.

  4. ✗ 最长26周、按收入55%计（每周上限$729）是就业保险（EI）病假给付；CPP伤残给付要求符合缴费条件且伤残「严重且长期」（Severe and prolonged，2026年月度最高$1,741.20）。Incorrect. A benefit paying 55% of weekly earnings up to 26 weeks (2026 weekly maximum $729) describes Employment Insurance (EI) sickness benefits; CPP disability benefits require meeting contribution thresholds and having a severe and prolonged disability (2026 monthly maximum $1,741.20).
