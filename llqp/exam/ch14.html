本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 残疾定义与续保等级判定题：给出投保人失能后的复工或转岗情况，考查四种残疾定义（own occupation、regular occupation、any occupation、total disability according to CPP）下是否继续赔付，或考查三种续保等级（non-cancellable、guaranteed renewable、cancellable）下保险公司能否单方涨价、改条款或撤单。Disability definition and renewability classification questions: Given an insured's return to work or job transfer after disability, tests whether benefits continue under the four disability definitions (own occupation, regular occupation, any occupation, and CPP total disability), or tests whether the insurer can unilaterally raise premiums, alter policy terms, or cancel coverage under the three renewability provisions (non-cancellable, guaranteed renewable, and cancellable).
  • 全来源上限与多方抵消计算题：给出失能前税前月收入、团体长期伤残（group LTD）、CPP 伤残给付（CPP-D）以及个人伤残险金额，考查按主合同全来源上限（all-source maximum，常见值 85%）计算团体险被削减后的实际给付额。All-source maximum and multi-benefit offset calculation questions: Given pre-disability gross monthly income, group long-term disability (group LTD), CPP disability benefits (CPP-D), and individual disability insurance amounts, tests calculating the net reduced group benefit under the master contract's all-source maximum (typically 85%).
  • 等待期与给付期独立封顶算数题：同时给出等待期（waiting period）、给付期（benefit period）和实际失能持续月数，考查等待期与给付期互不冲抵、先用给付期上限封顶的理赔月数计算。Waiting period and benefit period independent cap calculation questions: Given the waiting period, benefit period, and actual months of continuous disability, tests calculating payable claim months where the waiting period and benefit period do not offset each other and the benefit period cap applies first.
  • 复发条款时间窗口辨析题：给出首次领款月数、复工月数及同因再次失能情况，考查在复发窗口（recurring disability clause，常见值 6 个月）内免重计等待期但合并扣减剩余给付期，或超出窗口后重走等待期并重置给付期。Recurring disability clause time-window distinction questions: Given the initial benefit months, months returned to work, and subsequent relapse from the same cause, tests distinguishing whether relapses within the recurring disability window (typically 6 months) waive the waiting period while combining against the remaining benefit period, or whether relapses outside the window require a fresh waiting period and reset the benefit period.
  • 考试口径与现行政策双口径题：考查联邦就业保险病假福利（EI sickness benefits）最长给付周数（考试口径 15 周 vs 现行 26 周）及团体转个人险转换权（conversion privilege）免核保机制。Exam standard and current policy dual-benchmark questions: Tests the maximum benefit weeks for federal Employment Insurance (EI) sickness benefits (exam standard of 15 weeks vs. current 26 weeks) and the medical underwriting waiver under group-to-individual conversion privileges.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 团体与个人本职定义同名不同义Confusing group and individual own occupation definitions

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到 own occupation 就套用个人险定义，误以为兼职其他工作不影响给付；实际上考试口径下团体险的 own occupation 等同于个人险的 regular occupation，接受雇主安排的匹配岗位或自愿复工即不能同时全额兼得给付。Candidates see own occupation and assume the individual policy definition applies, mistakenly believing that taking another job does not affect benefits; in fact, under the exam standard, a group plan's own occupation operates like an individual regular occupation clause, meaning that accepting a suitable reassignment or returning to work prevents collecting full unreduced disability benefits concurrently.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现 group plan / group LTD 与 own occupation，且提到转岗或从事其他有报酬工作。The question stem mentions both group plan / group LTD and own occupation, and describes a job transfer or engaging in other gainful work.

⚠ 等待期与给付期互相冲抵Offsetting waiting period and benefit period against each other

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生误把等待期从给付期上限里扣除，或者把等待期加到给付期上限上，忘了等待期只是起赔前的间隔、给付期是独立的最长领款月数封顶。Candidates mistakenly deduct the waiting period from the maximum benefit period or add the waiting period to the benefit period cap, overlooking that the waiting period is merely the pre-claim elimination interval while the benefit period is an independent cap on the maximum payable months.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出等待期（如 90 或 120 天）、给付期（如 2 年或 5 年）和总失能时长三个时间数字。The question stem provides three time figures simultaneously: the waiting period (such as 90 or 120 days), the benefit period (such as 2 or 5 years), and total disability duration.

⚠ 复发条款只记免等待不记并上限Remembering waived waiting period but forgetting combined benefit cap under recurring disability

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生只记住复发窗口内同因再次失能不用重新计算等待期，却忽略了两段失能视为同一次残疾、必须共用同一个给付期上限，导致多算可领月数。Candidates remember that a relapse from the same cause within the recurring window waives a new waiting period, but overlook that both disability periods are treated as a single claim sharing the same benefit period cap, leading to overcounting payable months.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现同一原因再次失能（same cause），并明确给出两次失能之间复工的具体月数（如 4 个月或 7 个月）。The question stem features a relapse from the same cause and specifies the exact number of months returned to work between disabilities (such as 4 months or 7 months).

⚠ 全来源上限漏看主合同或错把政府给付当额外收入Overlooking master contract terms in all-source maximum or treating government benefits as unreduced extra income

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生以为获批 CPP-D 后总收入会直接增加，或武断认为个人保单绝对不计入 85% 全来源上限池子，忽略了直接来源会削减团体给付且个人险是否计入须看团体主合同约定。Candidates assume that receiving CPP-D simply increases total income, or assume that individual policies are never included in the 85% all-source maximum pool, overlooking that direct offsets reduce group payouts and that individual policy inclusion depends on group master contract provisions.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出失能前税前收入、团体伤残险比例、CPP-D 金额及 all-source maximum（如 85%）。The question stem provides pre-disability gross income, group disability benefit percentage, CPP-D amount, and the all-source maximum (such as 85%).

⚠ 自雇者可保收入漏减经营支出或混入被动收入Failing to deduct operating expenses or including passive income in self-employed insurable earnings

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生直接用自雇者的营业总收入（gross revenue）乘以替代比例，或者把失能后仍持续取得的租金、股息等非劳动收入加进可保劳动收入基数。Candidates directly multiply self-employed gross revenue by the replacement ratio, or improperly add ongoing passive income (such as rent or dividends that continue during disability) into the insurable earned income baseline.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干主人公为自雇人士（self-employed），并同时列出营业总收入、经营费用以及租金或投资收益。The question stem features a self-employed individual and lists gross business revenue, operating expenses, and ongoing rental or investment income.

第 1 题Q1个人与团体本职定义差异及复工影响Differences between individual and group own occupation definitions and impact of returning to work★★陷阱：团体与个人本职定义同名不同义

Dr. Marcus, an orthopedic surgeon, is covered by both an individual disability insurance policy with an own occupation definition and a hospital group long-term disability plan that also uses an own occupation definition. After a hand tremor permanently prevents him from performing surgery, he takes a full-time medical school teaching position earning $95,000 a year. Ignoring any all-source maximum offset, how do the two policies treat his disability benefits while he teaches?

骨科外科医生 Marcus 医生同时受一份采用本职定义（own occupation）的个人伤残保险保单和一份同样写着本职定义的医院团体长期伤残计划保障。在一次手部震颤永久阻碍他进行外科手术后，他接受了一份年薪 $95,000 的医学院全职教学职位。在不考虑全来源上限抵消的情况下，这两份保单在他任教期间会如何处理他的伤残给付？

  1. ✓ 个人险的本职定义（own occupation）是四级光谱最宽的一级，只要不能履行原职业全部主要职责即算全残，转做其他工作甚至赚得更多，给付照发不减；而考试口径明确指出，团体伤残险合同里的 own occupation 实际效果等同于个人险的常规职业定义（regular occupation），一旦选择从事其他有报酬工作，给付会被削减或终止。Correct. The individual own occupation definition is the most liberal on the four-level spectrum: as long as the insured cannot perform all essential duties of their specific occupation, they qualify for total disability, and taking another job or earning more does not reduce benefits. In contrast, the exam standard explicitly states that an own occupation clause in a group disability contract functions like an individual regular occupation definition, meaning benefits are reduced or terminated if the insured engages in other gainful employment.

  2. ✗ 误把团体险的 own occupation 当成个人险的 own occupation；团体险的 own occupation 在实际效果上等同于 regular occupation，复工从事他业不能与全额给付兼得（团体与个人本职定义同名不同义陷阱）。Incorrect. Mistakenly equates group own occupation with individual own occupation. A group own occupation clause operates in practice like a regular occupation clause, meaning returning to work in another occupation cannot be combined with full disability benefits.

  3. ✗ 只要能从事教育、训练、经验相匹配的工作就停付是任何职业定义（any occupation）的判据，而非个人险 own occupation 的判据（混淆 own occupation 与 any occupation 陷阱）。Incorrect. Terminating benefits because the insured can perform work matching education, training, and experience is the test for an any occupation definition, not an individual own occupation definition.

  4. ✗ 把个人险与团体险对本职定义的处理完全颠倒了：个人险 own occupation 下转行任教不减给付，反而是团体险会因复工而减停给付（颠倒个人险与团体险定义规则陷阱）。Incorrect. Completely inverts how individual and group policies treat own occupation: under individual own occupation, teaching does not reduce benefits, whereas group coverage reduces or terminates benefits upon returning to work.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Clara, a commercial airline pilot, loses her medical flight certification due to a permanent inner-ear disorder. She has a group long-term disability policy with an own occupation definition. Two months into her claim, the airline offers her a full-time ground-based flight simulator instructor position that matches her background, and she accepts it. Under the exam rules for group own occupation definitions, what happens to her group disability benefit?

商业航空公司飞行员 Clara 因永久性内耳疾病失去了飞行医疗合格证。她拥有一份采用本职定义（own occupation）的团体长期伤残保单。理赔两个月后，航空公司向她提供了一份与她背景相匹配的全职地面飞行模拟器教员职位，她接受了该职位。根据考试口径关于团体本职定义的规则，她的团体伤残给付会怎样？

  1. ✗ 只有在个人险的本职定义（own occupation）下才能边从事其他工作边全额领款；团体险的 own occupation 等同于常规职业定义（regular occupation），不能在接下匹配岗位的同时全额兼得给付（团体与个人本职定义同名不同义陷阱）。Incorrect. Only an individual own occupation definition allows collecting full benefits while working in another occupation. Group own occupation functions like regular occupation, precluding full benefits once a suitable reassignment is accepted.

  2. ✗ 内部转岗属于复工处理范畴，不会触发离职转换权，更不会自动转为不可撤销保单（虚构自动转保机制陷阱）。Incorrect. An internal job reassignment is a return-to-work scenario that does not trigger a termination conversion privilege, nor does it automatically convert coverage into a non-cancellable policy.

  3. ✓ 考试口径规定团体伤残险中的本职定义（own occupation）实际效果等同于个人险的常规职业定义（regular occupation）：若公司安排了匹配岗位或被保险人自愿复工，只能二选一或被减停给付，不能既领新岗位全薪又拿全额伤残给付。Correct. The exam standard specifies that an own occupation clause in group disability insurance operates like an individual regular occupation clause: if the employer offers a suitable reassignment or the insured voluntarily resumes work, benefits are reduced or stopped, preventing the employee from drawing a full salary and full disability benefits simultaneously.

  4. ✗ 伤残险合同不存在复工后法定翻倍给付的规则，复工只会按常规职业定义或康复条款减停给付（虚构法定奖励规则陷阱）。Incorrect. Disability policies contain no statutory rule doubling benefits upon returning to work; returning to work reduces or terminates benefits under regular occupation or rehabilitation provisions.

第 2 题Q2自雇者可保收入核算与非劳动收入处理Self-employed insurable earnings calculation and treatment of unearned passive income★★陷阱：自雇者可保收入漏减经营支出或混入被动收入

Liam is a self-employed graphic designer applying for an individual disability income policy. His tax return shows $140,000 in gross business revenue, $50,000 in deductible business operating expenses, and $18,000 in ongoing rental income from a residential duplex that a property manager runs and that will continue regardless of whether Liam can work. If the insurer's maximum insurable limit is 60% of eligible pre-tax earned income, what is the maximum annual disability benefit Liam can be underwritten for based on his earned income?

自雇平面设计师 Liam 正在申请一份个人伤残收入保单。他的报税表显示营业总收入为 $140,000，可扣除的经营支出为 $50,000，此外还有一套由物业经理打理的双拼住宅带来的 $18,000 持续租金收入（无论 Liam 能否工作都会继续取得）。如果保险公司的最高可承保限额为合格税前劳动收入的 60%，按他的劳动收入计算，Liam 最高可获核保的年伤残给付额是多少？

Under the recurring disability clause, a relapse from the same cause within the 6-month window (here, 4 months of work) is treated as a continuation of the same disability. The benefit is waiving a new 90-day waiting period so payments resume immediately; the trade-off is combining both periods under the original 24-month cap, leaving only 10 payable months after deducting the 14 months already used.

答案Answer：d

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Suppose Grace (same policy: benefits start after 90 days, run for up to 24 months, and relapses use a 6-month window) collected 14 months of benefits during her first disability, returned to full-time work for 7 months, and then suffered a relapse from the same chronic condition that lasted another 18 months. How will the insurer treat this second disability?

假设 Grace（同一份保单：90 天等待期、24 个月给付期、6 个月复发窗口）在第一次失能期间领取了 14 个月给付，全职复工满 7 个月后，因同一种慢性病复发并再次失能 18 个月。保险公司将如何处理这第二次失能？

  1. ✗ Grace 已经全职复工满 7 个月，超出了 6 个月的复发窗口，不再视为同一次残疾（忽略复工时长已超出复发窗口陷阱）。Incorrect. Grace returned to full-time work for 7 months, exceeding the 6-month recurring window, so the relapse is no longer treated as the same disability.

  2. ✓ 因为她复工满 7 个月已超出 6 个月复发条款（recurring disability clause）窗口，此次同因复发被视为全新的一次残疾：需要重新经历 90 天等待期，但给付期上限也重置为全新的 24 个月。Correct. Because returning to work for 7 months exceeds the 6-month recurring disability window, this relapse is treated as a brand new disability: it requires satisfying a fresh 90-day waiting period, but resets the maximum benefit period to a full 24 months.

  3. ✗ 在伤残险复发规则中，免等待期与重置给付期上限不可能同时发生：窗口内免等待但并上限，窗口外重等但重置上限（虚构两头占便宜规则陷阱）。Incorrect. In disability insurance, waiving the waiting period and resetting the benefit period never happen together: relapses inside the window waive the waiting period but combine caps; relapses outside require a waiting period but reset the cap.

  4. ✗ 复发窗口届满只是意味着第二次失能按新一次残疾重计等待期与给付期，绝不是拒赔同因疾病（误把超出复发窗口当成拒赔理由陷阱）。Incorrect. Expiration of the recurring window simply means the second disability is adjudicated as a new claim with a new waiting period and benefit period, not that the claim is denied.

答案Answer：b

第 7 题Q7保证承保与续保等级区分及65岁转换规则Distinguishing guaranteed issue from renewability provisions and age-65 conversion rules★★陷阱：混淆核保概念guaranteed issue与续保等级

Arthur, a 64-year-old corporate lawyer, holds an individual non-cancellable disability insurance policy that expires at age 65. Meanwhile, his younger associate, Ben, recently enrolled in a professional association disability plan advertised as guaranteed issue, which also states in the contract that the policy is cancellable. Which statement accurately describes their coverage rights?

64 岁的公司法律师 Arthur 持有一份将在 65 岁到期的个人不可撤销（non-cancellable）伤残保险保单。与此同时，他的年轻助理 Ben 最近加入了一项宣传为保证承保（guaranteed issue）的专业协会伤残计划，而该合同中写明保单属于可撤销（cancellable）等级。哪项陈述准确描述了他们的保障权利？

  1. ✗ 既忽略了 non-can 保单在 65 岁时免医核转换为 guaranteed renewable 的安排，又把核保概念 guaranteed issue 当成了不可撤单的续保承诺（混淆 guaranteed issue 与续保等级陷阱）。Incorrect. Overlooks the standard medical-free conversion of non-cancellable policies to guaranteed renewable at age 65, while confusing the underwriting concept of guaranteed issue with a non-cancellable renewal guarantee.

  2. ✓ 考试口径规定不可撤销保单（non-cancellable policy）持有人到 65 岁时，通常可免医疗核保转换为保证续保（guaranteed renewable）合同（给付期缩短到一两年、保费按转换时年龄重定，通常延续到 72 或 75 岁）；而保证承保（guaranteed issue）只是入场时的核保概念，与续保等级是两个维度，一份 guaranteed issue 保单完全可以是可撤销（cancellable）并由保司单方终止的。Correct. Under the exam standard, a non-cancellable policyowner reaching age 65 can typically convert to a guaranteed renewable policy without medical evidence (with benefits shortened to 1 or 2 years and rates based on attained age, often continuing to age 72 or 75). In contrast, guaranteed issue is an entry underwriting method completely distinct from renewability provisions; a guaranteed issue plan can be cancellable and terminated unilaterally by the insurer.

  3. ✗ 65 岁转换后变为 guaranteed renewable，保费按转换时年龄重新核定且给付期缩短到一两年，并非按原锁死费率延续 non-can；且 guaranteed issue 不锁死保费（虚构 65 岁后原费率锁死陷阱）。Incorrect. Converting at age 65 transitions coverage to guaranteed renewable with rates based on attained age and benefit periods reduced to 1 or 2 years, rather than locking in original rates; furthermore, guaranteed issue does not freeze premiums.

  4. ✗ non-can 保单持有人在 65 岁转换为 guaranteed renewable 合同的权利是无需医疗核保的（忽略 65 岁免医核转换规则陷阱）。Incorrect. The right of a non-cancellable policyowner to convert to a guaranteed renewable contract at age 65 does not require medical underwriting.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Dr. Evelyn is comparing two disability insurance offers: Plan X is an individual non-cancellable policy to age 65, and Plan Y is an association plan marketed as guaranteed issue with a cancellable renewal clause. Which statement correctly distinguishes the underwriting and renewability features of these two plans?

Evelyn 医生正在比较两份伤残保险报价：计划 X 是保至 65 岁的个人不可撤销（non-cancellable）保单，计划 Y 是宣传为保证承保（guaranteed issue）且附带可撤销（cancellable）续保条款的协会计划。哪项陈述正确区分了这两个计划的核保与续保特征？

  1. ✗ 保证承保（guaranteed issue）只是核保概念（符合资格即可承保），不是续保等级，不能防止保司在 cancellable 条款下调费或撤单（混淆 guaranteed issue 与 non-cancellable 陷阱）。Incorrect. Guaranteed issue is an underwriting standard (acceptance without individual medical evidence) rather than a renewability provision, and does not prevent the insurer from adjusting rates or cancelling coverage under a cancellable clause.

  2. ✗ 在 65 岁前允许按整类人群调保费的是 guaranteed renewable 而非 non-cancellable（Plan X 保费锁死到 65 岁），且 Plan Y 作为 cancellable 保单根本不锁死保费（颠倒续保等级权限陷阱）。Incorrect. Raising premiums class-wide prior to age 65 is permitted under guaranteed renewable, not non-cancellable (Plan X locks premiums to age 65), and Plan Y as a cancellable policy does not lock premiums.

  3. ✓ 不可撤销保单（non-cancellable policy）在 65 岁前锁死保费与保障且不可撤单，65 岁时通常可免医疗核保转为保证续保（guaranteed renewable）保单至 72 或 75 岁；而保证承保（guaranteed issue）仅指投保时符合计划资格保证承保，因其续保等级为可撤销（cancellable），保险公司仍可单方终止合同。Correct. A non-cancellable policy locks in premiums and terms to age 65 with no cancellation, and typically allows medical-free conversion to guaranteed renewable up to age 72 or 75. Meanwhile, guaranteed issue applies only to initial enrollment; because its renewability provision is cancellable, the insurer retains the right to terminate the contract.

  4. ✗ non-cancellable 保单持有人在 65 岁时通常可以无需医疗核保转换为 guaranteed renewable 合同继续保障至 72 或 75 岁（忽略 65 岁免医核转换权陷阱）。Incorrect. Non-cancellable policyowners can typically convert to a guaranteed renewable policy at age 65 without medical evidence to maintain coverage to age 72 or 75.
