本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 保单变更分类题：给出客户变更请求（如加保额、改吸烟状态、改受益人、保单转让），考查是否触发重新核保（underwriting）或税务处置。Policy change classification questions: Given client change requests (such as increasing the face amount, changing smoker status, changing beneficiaries, or policy assignments), tests whether each change triggers medical underwriting or tax disposition rules.
  • 欠费与复效时间轴题：给出逾期天数与被保险人生存或健康状态，考查 30 天宽限期（grace period）、宽限期末后 30 天免核保复效与 2 年有条件复效（reinstatement）的边界。Premium default and reinstatement timeline questions: Given the number of overdue days and the living or health status of the life insured, tests the boundaries between the 30-day grace period, the 30-day post-grace period reinstatement window without medical underwriting, and the 2-year conditional reinstatement window.
  • 受益人指定与保护题：结合婚姻变动、口头通知、不可撤销受益人（irrevocable beneficiary）或债权人追偿，考查书面声明（declaration）形式要求、生前存档及免债范围。Beneficiary designation and protection questions: In scenarios involving marital changes, oral notifications, irrevocable beneficiaries, or creditor recovery, tests the written declaration formal requirements, lifetime filing rules, and the scope of creditor protection.
  • 保单替换与合规流程题：围绕同公司转保（churning）或跨公司掉保（twisting），考查寿险替换声明书（LIRD）签署时点、利弊披露及新单生效前保留旧单的原则。Policy replacement and compliance process questions: Centered on internal churning versus external twisting, tests the timing for signing the Life Insurance Replacement Declaration (LIRD), mandatory disclosure of advantages and disadvantages, and the principle of keeping the existing policy in force until the new policy takes effect.
  • 理赔核定与特殊推定题：围绕年龄误报（misstatement of age）比例调整、同时死亡推定（simultaneous death presumption）、付入法院（payment into court）及失踪 7 年推定死亡（presumption of death）的考试口径与 BC 法条差异。Claim adjudication and statutory presumption questions: Centered on proportional death benefit adjustments for misstatement of age, the simultaneous death presumption, payment into court, and the dual-benchmark difference between the 7-year exam standard and BC statutory provisions for presumption of death.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 双30天连续保障陷阱The dual 30-day continuous coverage trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生误把 30 天宽限期与宽限期末后 30 天短窗口复效拼成 60 天连续有效保障，忽略失效后必须付款时被保险人仍生存才能免核保复效。Candidates mistakenly combine the 30-day grace period and the 30-day post-grace reinstatement window into 60 days of continuous coverage, overlooking that once the policy lapses, the life insured must still be alive when payment is tendered to qualify for reinstatement without evidence of insurability.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现宽限期届满后第 N 天被保险人身故，家属试图补缴保费要求理赔。The question stem describes the life insured dying N days after the grace period expires, and family members attempt to pay overdue premiums and demand a claim payment.

⚠ 口头或自动改受益人陷阱The oral or automatic beneficiary change trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生以为离婚会自动撤销前配偶受益人资格，或以为打电话告知代理人就算完成受益人变更，忽略必须有书面声明且保司收件前依记录付款免责。Candidates assume that divorce automatically revokes a former spouse's beneficiary status, or that telephoning an agent completes a beneficiary change, overlooking that a written declaration is mandatory and that the insurer is legally discharged by paying the beneficiary on record before receiving written notice.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现离婚再婚未改保单、口头嘱咐代理人改受益人、或未送达书面变更文件即身故。The question stem features a divorce or remarriage without updating policy documents, verbal instructions given to an agent to change the beneficiary, or the life insured dying before written documents are delivered.

⚠ 年龄误报拒赔或反算陷阱The age misstatement claim denial or inverted ratio trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把非欺诈年龄误报当成欺诈直接拒赔，或在按比例调整保额时把实缴保费与应缴保费的分子分母颠倒。Candidates treat an unintentional age misstatement as fraudulent misrepresentation leading to total claim denial, or invert the numerator and denominator of actual premium paid versus correct premium payable when calculating the proportional adjustment.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现理赔时发现投保年龄填错（非欺诈），给出原保额、实缴保费与真实年龄应缴保费。The question stem reveals an unintentional age error during claim settlement, providing the original face amount, actual premium paid, and correct premium payable for the true age.

⚠ 两年复效无条件恢复陷阱The 2-year unconditional reinstatement trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生以为只要在失效后 2 年内申请复效，就像前 30 天一样只需补缴保费，忽略超过短窗口后必须提供令保司满意的健康与可保性证据。Candidates assume that applying for reinstatement within 2 years of lapse merely requires paying overdue premiums like the initial 30-day window, forgetting that after the short window expires, the insured must provide evidence of insurability and good health satisfactory to the insurer.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现保单失效超过 30 天但未满 2 年（如失效后 55 天），客户要求直接补费恢复保单。The question stem presents a policy that has been lapsed for more than 30 days but less than 2 years (such as 55 days post-lapse), where the client requests immediate policy reinstatement simply by paying overdue premiums.

⚠ 考试口径与BC成文法混淆陷阱The exam standard versus BC statutory rule confusion trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生在失踪推定死亡（考试口径 7 年 vs BC 无固定年限三要件）或授权书改受益人（考试口径提 BC 例外 vs 实务改既有指定须法院授权）上混淆考场标准答案与本地法条。Candidates confuse the standard exam answers with local provincial statutes regarding presumption of death (the 7-year exam-standard rule vs. BC's three statutory conditions without a fixed time threshold) or changing beneficiaries via a power of attorney (exam standard highlighting BC exceptions vs. actual practice requiring court authorization to alter existing designations).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及失踪多年申请推定死亡，或持财产授权书（POA）要求变更既有保单受益人。The question stem involves applying for a declaration of presumption of death after years of disappearance, or an attorney under an enduring power of attorney (POA) attempting to alter an existing policy beneficiary designation.

第 1 题Q1保单变更是否需重新核保与税务判断Determining underwriting requirements and tax implications for policy changes★陷阱：混淆行政变更、核保变更与保单转让性质

Liam owns an individual life insurance policy and contacts his agent to request several changes over the course of the year. Which of the following policy changes requires Liam to provide evidence of insurability for underwriting approval?

Liam 持有一份个人人寿保单，并在一年内联系代理人提出多项变更申请。下列哪一项保单变更需要 Liam 提供可保性证据以供核保批准？

  1. ✓ 正确。将吸烟状态从吸烟改为非吸烟直接影响死亡率假设与费率等级，属于核保变更（underwriting change），必须提供声明或检测等证据由保司重新核定。Correct. Changing smoking status from smoker to non-smoker directly affects mortality assumptions and premium rate classifications; it is an underwriting change that requires evidence such as a non-smoking declaration or cotinine testing for insurer approval.

  2. ✗ 错误。保单抵押担保转让（collateral assignment）不需要重新核保，且被所得税法 s.148 的处置定义排除，不触发保单收益税务处置。Incorrect. A collateral assignment of a policy for a bank loan does not require medical underwriting and is expressly excluded from the definition of a disposition under section 148 of the Income Tax Act, triggering no policy gain tax consequences.

  3. ✗ 错误。减少保额降低了保司的风险敞口，属于通常不需要健康核保的行政变更（administrative change），按合同最低保额与保司手续办理即可。Incorrect. Reducing the face amount decreases the insurer's net amount at risk; it is an administrative change that typically requires no evidence of insurability, provided it meets minimum policy limits and company administrative rules.

  4. ✗ 错误。以书面声明更改可撤销受益人（revocable beneficiary）属于行政变更，不增加被保险人死亡风险，无需重新核保。Incorrect. Changing a revocable beneficiary by written declaration is an administrative change that does not increase mortality risk and requires no medical underwriting.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Nora owns a permanent life insurance policy and wants to transfer all ownership rights permanently to her adult daughter, while also removing an accidental death rider she no longer needs. How does the insurer treat these two requests with respect to underwriting and taxation?

Nora 持有一份终身人寿保单，希望将全部保单所有权永久转让给成年女儿，同时删除一项不再需要的意外身故附加险。保险公司在核保和税务上如何处理这两项请求？

  1. ✗ 错误。无论是保单所有权转让（assignment）还是删除附加险，都不会增加保司的承保风险，均不需要医学核保。Incorrect. Neither an ownership assignment nor removing a rider increases the insurer's mortality risk, so neither requires medical underwriting.

  2. ✗ 错误。把两者的性质完全颠倒了：删除附加险是纯行政变更，而永久转让保单所有权属于绝对转让，通常构成税务上的权益处置。Incorrect. This inverts the true nature of both actions: removing a rider is a purely administrative change, while permanently transferring policy ownership is an absolute assignment that generally constitutes a taxable disposition.

  3. ✓ 正确。删除附加险和保单所有权转让均无需重新核保；但永久转让所有权属于绝对转让（absolute assignment），通常构成所得税法下的权益处置，须结合调整后成本基础（ACB）与展期规则做税务判断。Correct. Neither removing a rider nor assigning policy ownership requires medical underwriting. However, permanently transferring ownership is an absolute assignment that generally constitutes a disposition under the Income Tax Act, requiring a tax analysis based on the policy's adjusted cost basis (ACB) and rollover rules.

  4. ✗ 错误。只有单纯抵押担保转让（collateral assignment）才被 s.148 排除在处置定义之外，绝对转让不享受该排除。Incorrect. Only a pure collateral assignment is excluded from the definition of a disposition under section 148; an absolute assignment does not qualify for this statutory tax exclusion.

第 2 题Q2宽限期与两档复效窗口的边界条件Boundary conditions between the grace period and the two-tier reinstatement windows★★陷阱：双30天连续保障陷阱 / 两年复效无条件恢复陷阱

Marcus missed a renewal premium on his individual term life policy. After the 30-day grace period ended without payment and the policy lapsed, Marcus waited another 55 days before contacting his insurer to reinstate the policy. Under BC Insurance Act s.57, what is required for Marcus to reinstate his policy at this point?

Marcus 错过了一份个人定期寿险的续期保费。在 30 天宽限期届满仍未缴费导致保单失效后，Marcus 又过了 55 天才联系保险公司申请复效。根据 BC《Insurance Act》s.57，此时 Marcus 必须满足什么条件才能使保单复效？

  1. ✗ 错误。掉入「两年复效无条件恢复陷阱」；免健康证据补费复效仅限宽限期末后另 30 天短窗口内，失效后第 55 天已错过短窗口。Incorrect. Falls into the 2-year unconditional reinstatement trap. Reinstatement by paying overdue premiums without evidence of insurability is only available during the 30-day post-grace short window; 55 days post-lapse falls outside this window.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据 BC s.57，错过宽限期末后 30 天短窗口但仍在失效后 2 年长窗口内申请复效（reinstatement），必须付清全部欠费及其他合同债务与法定上限内利息，并提供令保司满意的健康及可保性证据；条件齐全时保司必须恢复。Correct. Under BC Insurance Act section 57, when applying for reinstatement after the 30-day post-grace window but within the 2-year long window, the policyowner must pay all overdue premiums and other indebtedness with interest (subject to statutory caps) and provide evidence of good health and insurability satisfactory to the insurer; when all statutory conditions are met, the insurer is obligated to reinstate the policy.

  1. ✗ 错误。2 年是不可抗辩期、复效长窗口或收到推定死亡宣告后的诉讼时效（s.76），不是考试口径的失踪推定死亡年限。Incorrect. Two years is the incontestability period, the long reinstatement window, or the limitation period following a declaration of presumption of death under section 76, not the exam-standard threshold for disappearance.

  2. ✗ 错误。5 年是 BC s.54 超出最高可保年龄且被保险人在世时撤销保单的期限，与失踪推定死亡无关。Incorrect. Five years is the period under BC section 54 during which an insurer may rescind a contract if the true age exceeds insurable limits while the insured is living, having no relation to presumption of death.

  3. ✗ 错误。6 年是 BC s.76 死亡保险金自死亡之日起算的最终诉讼时效上限，不是失踪等待年限。Incorrect. Six years is the absolute long-stop limitation period for life insurance death claims running from the date of death under BC section 76, not the waiting period for disappearance.

  4. ✓ 正确。考场标准答案按考试口径通则答失踪满 7 年可申请推定死亡（presumption of death）；但需注意 BC 现行《Presumption of Death Act》s.3 只要求无音讯、无理由相信在世、有合理依据推定已死亡三要件，并无 7 年法定等待期。Correct. The standard exam answer follows the general exam-standard rule of 7 years of unexplained absence before applying for a declaration of presumption of death; note that under section 3 of BC's Presumption of Death Act, only three conditions (no contact, no reason to believe alive, reasonable grounds of death) are required, with no fixed 7-year statutory waiting period.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A husband (the policyowner and life insured) and his wife (the sole primary beneficiary) die together in a car crash in BC, and it cannot be determined who died first; the policy has no contingent beneficiary or contrary declaration. Meanwhile, two distant relatives file competing claims with the insurer demanding the proceeds. Under BC Insurance Act s.82 and s.83, which rules apply?

在 BC 省，丈夫（投保人兼被保险人）与妻子（唯一主要受益人）在同一场车祸中遇难，无法确定谁先死亡；保单未设条件受益人或相反声明。与此同时，两名远房亲属向保司提出互相冲突的索赔。根据 BC《Insurance Act》s.82 与 s.83，适用什么规则？

  1. ✗ 错误。把同时死亡推定（simultaneous death presumption）的先后顺序说反了：若被保险人先死，保险金会流入已故受益人遗产，这正是 s.83 默认规则所要避免的。Incorrect. Inverts the statutory order of the simultaneous death presumption; if the insured were presumed to die first, proceeds would flow into the deceased beneficiary's estate, which is precisely what section 83's default rule avoids.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据 BC s.83 同时死亡推定（simultaneous death presumption），先后不明时默认受益人先于被保险人死亡，保险金按受益人不在世处理（归投保人或其遗产代表人）；根据 s.82，面对多方竞争索赔人，保司可申请付入法院（payment into court）以免责。Correct. Under BC Insurance Act section 83 (simultaneous death presumption), when sequence of death is uncertain, the beneficiary is presumed to have predeceased the life insured, so proceeds are distributed as if the beneficiary were not living (passing to the policyowner or their estate); under section 82, faced with competing claimants, the insurer may apply to pay the proceeds into court to secure a full discharge.

  3. ✗ 错误。7 年是考试口径的失踪推定死亡年限，车祸同时死亡已有明确死亡事实，无需等待 7 年。Incorrect. Seven years is the exam standard period for presumption of death in missing person cases; a fatal car crash establishes the fact of death immediately without any 7-year wait.

  4. ✗ 错误。s.83 不会在双方遗产之间平分保险金，且 s.82 明文允许保司在多方争议时申请付入法院（payment into court）。Incorrect. Section 83 does not divide proceeds equally between estates, and section 82 explicitly allows the insurer to apply for payment into court when claims are disputed.
