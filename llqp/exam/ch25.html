本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 画像到产品的决策树匹配题：给出客户的目标、时间跨度、风险承受力与账户类型，考察能否在给付型年金（payout annuity）、个人分离基金（individual segregated fund）、积累型年金（accumulation annuity）与团体分离基金（group segregated fund）之间做出合规选择，核心测试产品结构特征与客户真实处境的匹配度。Decision tree matching questions from profile to product: Given a client's objectives, time horizon, risk tolerance, and account type, scenarios test whether candidates can make compliant choices among payout annuities, individual segregated funds, accumulation annuities, and group segregated funds, focusing on matching product structural characteristics to the client's actual situation.
  • 申请表角色与权限边界题：通过客户想把年轻配偶设为受保人、配偶 RRSP 出资方想指定受益人、或代理人替客户填表等场景，测试注册账户（registered account）与非注册账户（non-registered account）在合同持有人（contract owner）、受保人（annuitant）及受益人（beneficiary）上的法定限制。Application form roles and authority boundary questions: Through scenarios where a client wants to name a younger spouse as the annuitant, a contributing spouse to a spousal RRSP wants to designate the beneficiary, or an agent completes the form on behalf of a client, scenarios test the statutory restrictions in registered accounts versus non-registered accounts regarding the contract owner, annuitant, and beneficiary.
  • 特殊签署与代理权限题：设置客户年迈患病或不在国内、由持有财产授权书（Power of Attorney for property）的人代签申请表的场景，测试代签申请表例外与指定或变更受益人权限的严格区分，以及考试口径与 BC 现行法律的差异。Special execution and representative authority questions: Scenarios involve an elderly, ill, or abroad client whose application form is signed by an attorney acting under a Power of Attorney (POA) for property, testing the strict distinction between the exception allowing signing the application and the authority to designate or change beneficiaries, as well as the difference between exam standard rules and current British Columbia statutory law.
  • 披露三件套与撤回权计算题：结合信息文件夹（information folder）、基金情况说明书（Fund Facts）与合同文本的交付节点，考察犹豫期撤回权（right of rescission）的 2 个营业日时限、视为收悉（deemed receipt）的 5 个营业日起算规则，以及撤回时按投入金额与次一估值日价值孰低退还的计算逻辑。Three disclosure documents and rescission calculation questions: Combining the delivery milestones of the information folder, Fund Facts, and the original contract, scenarios test the two-business-day time limit for the right of rescission, the five-business-day deemed receipt triggering rule, and the calculation logic of refunding the lesser of the amount invested or the value on the next valuation date upon rescission.
  • 推荐理由信与双口径合规题：通过咖啡厅口头推荐、凭过往三年业绩推荐或询问销售费（sales charge）与年度成本披露的场景，测试推荐理由信（reason-why letter）的强制性五要素、同类比同类（like compared to like）原则，以及 DSC 禁令与总成本报告（TCR）的考场双口径。Reason-why letter and dual-standard compliance questions: Through scenarios involving verbal recommendations in a café, recommendations based solely on three-year past performance, or inquiries about sales charges and annual cost disclosure, scenarios test the five mandatory elements of a reason-why letter, the 'like' compared to 'like' principle, and the dual exam versus current regulatory standards for the DSC ban and Total Cost Reporting (TCR).

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 代填等于代决陷阱Agent completion equated to agent decision trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到代理人负责把信息记录到申请表上，就误以为代理人可以自行决定或替客户补充表格内容，混淆了操作表格与提供信息内容的边界。Candidates see that the agent is responsible for recording information on the application form and mistakenly assume the agent may unilaterally decide or supplement form contents on the client's behalf, confusing the operational duty of recording the form with the client's responsibility for providing factual information.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现代理人帮客户填写分离基金申请表（application form）、客户未亲自提供某项信息或存在语言理解障碍仍继续推进签字。The question stem features an agent completing a segregated fund application form for a client, where the client has not personally provided certain information or where language and comprehension barriers exist, yet the agent proceeds toward signature.

⚠ 注册账户分离角色陷阱Registered account role splitting trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 客户常希望用更年轻的家人作受保人以延长保证期，或误以为配偶 RRSP 的出资人拥有合同控制权，考生容易顺着客户的主观意愿选错。Clients often wish to name a younger family member as the annuitant to extend the guarantee period, or mistakenly assume that the contributor to a spousal RRSP holds contract control; candidates easily make incorrect choices by deferring to the client's subjective wishes.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及注册账户（registered account）或配偶注册退休储蓄计划（spousal RRSP），且客户试图让持有人（owner）与受保人（annuitant）分离，或由出资方指定受益人。The question stem involves a registered account or a spousal Registered Retirement Savings Plan (spousal RRSP), and the client attempts to separate the contract owner from the annuitant, or have the contributing spouse designate the beneficiary.

⚠ 授权书越权变更受益人陷阱Power of Attorney exceeding authority to alter beneficiaries trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生记住财产授权书（POA for property）在特定情况下可代签申请表，就误以为受托人可直接凭该授权书替客户指定或变更既有受益人。Candidates remember that an attorney acting under a Power of Attorney (POA) for property may sign the application form under specific circumstances, and mistakenly assume the attorney can directly designate or alter existing beneficiaries for the client under that document.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现持有人因病、行为能力受限或不在国内由财产授权书（POA）持有人代签申请表，以及要求指定或修改受益人（beneficiary）。The question stem simultaneously features an owner who is ill, incapacitated, or abroad having an application form signed by an attorney under a Power of Attorney (POA), alongside a request to designate or change the beneficiary.

⚠ 口头同意替代推荐理由信陷阱Verbal assent substituting for reason-why letter trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 题干强调客户与代理人关系熟稔、当场口头认可并签字，或某只基金过去三年表现优异，诱导考生认为推荐理由信（reason-why letter）可以省略。The question stem emphasizes that the client and agent are close acquaintances, that the client verbally agreed and signed on the spot, or that a specific fund performed exceptionally well over the past three years, tempting candidates to believe a reason-why letter can be omitted.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现非正式场合沟通、仅凭历史业绩（past performance）推荐、客户口头同意即签字而未留存书面推荐理由信。The question stem features informal communications, recommendations based solely on past performance, or a client verbally agreeing and signing without a written reason-why letter being documented and retained.

⚠ 产品名称与双口径混淆陷阱Product naming and dual-standard confusion trap

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到团体分离基金就默认带身故保证，或在双口径题中分不清考试口径（FEL 与 DSC 并存、MER 披露）与现行规则（BC 禁新单 DSC、TCR 单列保证成本）。Candidates assume group segregated funds automatically carry death benefit guarantees simply from the name, or fail to distinguish between the exam standard (coexistence of FEL and DSC, MER disclosure) and current rules (BC ban on new DSC contracts, separate TCR guarantee cost disclosure).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现团体分离基金（group segregated fund）要求身故本金保障，或询问考试口径下的销售费种类、撤回权（right of rescission）计算及时限。The question stem features a group segregated fund where the client requests death benefit principal protection, or asks about exam standard sales charge types or right of rescission calculations and deadlines.

第 1 题Q1推荐决策树与团体分离基金无身故保证Recommendation decision tree and lack of death benefit guarantee in group segregated funds★★陷阱：产品名称与双口径混淆陷阱

A client participates in an employer's group retirement plan where her savings are held in a group segregated fund. She tells her advisor that her primary objective is to ensure her principal is guaranteed upon death for her heirs, and she assumes her current plan already does this because it has the words 'segregated fund' in its name. How should the advisor guide her based on the recommendation decision tree?

一位客户参加了雇主的团体退休计划，其储蓄持有一只团体分离基金。她告诉代理人，她的首要目标是确保身故时本金得到保障并留给继承人，且她认为现有计划名字里有“分离基金”字样就已经具备这项保障。根据推荐决策树，代理人应如何引导她？

  1. ✗ 团体分离基金（group segregated fund）默认不提供身故保证（death benefit guarantee），不能因为名字带“分离基金”就默认它具备个人分离基金的 75% 或 100% 保证结构（产品名称与双口径混淆陷阱）。Incorrect. Group segregated funds carry no death benefit guarantee by default; one cannot assume that because a product has 'segregated fund' in its name it possesses the 75% or 100% guarantee structure of individual segregated funds (product naming and dual-standard confusion trap).

  2. ✓ 符合推荐决策树规则：团体分离基金（group segregated fund）默认不提供身故保证；若客户的首要诉求是确保身故本金安全，团体分离基金本身不满足，需另行评估个人分离基金（individual segregated fund）或人寿保险。Correct. In accordance with the recommendation decision tree rules, group segregated funds provide no death benefit guarantee by default; if the client's primary objective is principal protection upon death, the group segregated fund cannot satisfy it, requiring an evaluation of an individual segregated fund or life insurance.

  3. ✗ 首套房储蓄账户（FHSA）是针对短期买房等用途核对开户与提款资格的税务容器，并非提供 10 年到期或身故本金保证的保险产品（产品名称与双口径混淆陷阱）。Incorrect. A First Home Savings Account (FHSA) is a tax container for which eligibility to open and withdraw is verified for short-term home purchase goals, not an insurance product providing a 10-year maturity or death benefit principal guarantee (product naming and dual-standard confusion trap).

  4. ✗ 候补受保人（contingent annuitant）仅适用于非注册合同（non-registered contract），注册退休计划不可指定候补受保人，且该角色也不创造团体分离基金原本没有的身故本金保证（注册账户分离角色陷阱）。Incorrect. A contingent annuitant can only be named on non-registered contracts; registered retirement plans cannot designate a contingent annuitant, and this role does not create a death benefit principal guarantee that a group segregated fund does not already possess (registered account role splitting trap).

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An investor has non-registered savings that she plans to use within two years to purchase a home. An advisor considers recommending an individual segregated fund because of its maturity and death benefit guarantees. Why does this recommendation conflict with the recommendation decision tree?

一位投资者有一笔非注册储蓄，计划在两年内用于买房。代理人考虑到期与身故保证而打算推荐个人分离基金。为什么这一推荐与推荐决策树相冲突？

  1. ✗ 非注册账户（non-registered account）完全可以持有个人分离基金并选择 75/75、75/100 或 100/100 担保档位，问题出在时间跨度而非账户限制（注册账户分离角色陷阱）。Incorrect. Non-registered accounts can fully hold individual segregated funds with a choice of 75/75, 75/100, or 100/100 guarantee tiers; the conflict stems from the time horizon rather than account restrictions (registered account role splitting trap).

  2. ✗ 个人分离基金（individual segregated fund）无论在注册还是非注册账户中均具备到期与身故担保结构，并非必须在 RRSP 内才有效（产品名称与双口径混淆陷阱）。Incorrect. Individual segregated funds provide maturity and death benefit guarantee structures whether held in registered or non-registered accounts, and do not require being inside an RRSP to be effective (product naming and dual-standard confusion trap).

  3. ✗ 给付型年金（payout annuity）适用于已退休或临近退休且需要稳定终身或定期收入的客户，不适用于两年内要提取本金买房的短期资金（产品名称与双口径混淆陷阱）。Incorrect. Payout annuities are designed for clients who are retired or nearing retirement and require stable lifetime or term income, not for short-term funds where principal will be withdrawn within two years to buy a home (product naming and dual-standard confusion trap).

  4. ✓ 时间跨度（time horizon）须按资金的真实用途判断：个人分离基金最短 10 年到期，与客户“两年内要用钱买房”直接冲突；合规做法是先比较首套房储蓄账户（FHSA）等税务容器的资格，再配置适合短期用款的现金或短期投资。Correct. The time horizon must be determined by the actual intended use of the funds: individual segregated funds have a minimum 10-year maturity, which directly conflicts with the client needing the money to buy a home within two years; compliant practice is to first evaluate eligibility for tax containers such as an FHSA and then allocate to cash or short-term investments suited for short-term needs.

答案Answer：d

第 2 题Q2注册账户与配偶RRSP的持有人限制Ownership restrictions in registered accounts and spousal RRSPs★★陷阱：注册账户分离角色陷阱

A husband holds funds in his individual Registered Retirement Savings Plan (RRSP) and wants to open a segregated fund contract. Because his wife is younger, he asks the advisor to name himself as the contract owner and his wife as the annuitant so the contract guarantees will last longer. If that is not possible, he proposes contributing to a spousal RRSP where he remains the contract owner and designates the beneficiary. How must the advisor respond?

  1. ✓ 第一，分离基金申请表（application form）的信息内容必须由投资者本人提供，代理人只负责记录与解释，不能代客户做决定或编造信息；第二，比较基金须遵守同类比同类（"like" compared to "like"）纪律，跨类型比较必须向客户说明理由。Correct. First, the factual content of the segregated fund application form must be provided by the investor, with the advisor only recording and explaining information rather than deciding or fabricating details on the client's behalf; second, fund comparisons must follow the 'like' compared to 'like' discipline, and any cross-category comparison requires explaining the rationale to the client.

  2. ✗ 71 岁强制转换与 85 岁高龄延期年金（ALDA）起付限制是注册退休账户的法定转换规则，与代理人在申请表上代编信息及跨类别不当比较无关（产品名称与双口径混淆陷阱）。Incorrect. Mandatory conversion at age 71 and Advanced Life Deferred Annuity (ALDA) commencement limits at age 85 are statutory conversion rules for registered retirement accounts, unrelated to an advisor fabricating application information or conducting inappropriate cross-category comparisons (product naming and dual-standard confusion trap).

  3. ✗ 到期与身故保证由保险公司依个人可变保险合同（IVIC）提供，代理人并不私人担保本金；违规后果是触发合规调查与监管纪律处分（口头同意替代推荐理由信陷阱）。Incorrect. Maturity and death benefit guarantees are provided by the insurance company under an Individual Variable Insurance Contract (IVIC); the advisor does not personally guarantee principal, and non-compliance triggers compliance investigations and regulatory disciplinary proceedings (verbal assent substituting for reason-why letter trap).

  4. ✗ 代理人负责把投资者提供的信息记录到电子或纸质申请表上是合规的本职工作，错在“自己决定填什么内容”而非“代为操作表格”（代填等于代决陷阱）。Incorrect. Recording information provided by the investor onto an electronic or paper application form is a compliant operational duty; the misconduct lies in unilaterally deciding what content to enter rather than in physically handling the form (agent completion equated to agent decision trap).

Throughout the segregated fund recommendation process—whether the advisor presents a single recommendation, a primary option with an alternative, or multiple options side by side—seven overarching regulatory principles apply from product design to after-sales service. Under what framework are these seven requirements grouped?

在分离基金推荐全过程中——无论代理人给出单一方案、“主推方案+备选方案”还是并列多个方案——从产品设计到售后服务的七条核心监管要求全程适用。这七条要求归属于哪个原则框架？

  1. ✗ 总成本报告（Total Cost Reporting, TCR）是 2026-01-01 生效的年度费用与保证成本披露规则，并非涵盖设计、宣传、冲突规避与投诉处理七条要求的总体原则框架（产品名称与双口径混淆陷阱）。Incorrect. Total Cost Reporting (TCR) is an annual fee and guarantee cost disclosure rule effective January 1, 2026, not an overarching principles framework covering product design, promotion, conflict management, and complaint resolution across seven areas (product naming and dual-standard confusion trap).

  2. ✗ 配偶注册退休储蓄计划（spousal RRSP）规定出资方无所有权且不可转让，与贯穿全流程的七条监管要求无关（注册账户分离角色陷阱）。Incorrect. Spousal RRSP rules establish that the contributing spouse has no ownership rights and cannot transfer the contract, which is unrelated to the seven overarching lifecycle regulatory requirements (registered account role splitting trap).

  3. ✗ 视为收悉（deemed receipt）的 5 个营业日规则仅用于计算撤回权（right of rescission）起算点，不是全流程七条监管要求的总框架（产品名称与双口径混淆陷阱）。Incorrect. The five-business-day deemed receipt rule is strictly used to determine the start of the right of rescission period, not as an overarching framework of seven regulatory expectations across the full customer lifecycle (product naming and dual-standard confusion trap).

  4. ✓ 公平对待客户原则（Fair Treatment of Customers, FTC）的七条要求全程适用：产品设计与销售以客户利益优先、宣传材料准确清晰、存续期内持续提供有助决策的信息、避免利益冲突、最小化不适当销售风险、确保建议相关、公平及时处理投诉。Correct. The seven expectations of the Fair Treatment of Customers (FTC) principles apply across the entire customer lifecycle: prioritizing client interests in product design and marketing, ensuring promotional materials are clear and not misleading, providing timely information throughout the contract life, managing conflicts of interest, minimizing unsuitable sales risks, ensuring relevant advice, and handling complaints fairly and promptly.

第 7 题Q7销售费与年度成本披露的考场双口径Dual exam and current regulatory standards for sales charges and annual cost disclosure★★★陷阱：产品名称与双口径混淆陷阱

A candidate taking the LLQP exam encounters a question asking which sales charge options are described in the exam for segregated funds, and also wants to know how the exam wording compares to current rules in British Columbia. According to the exam, how are segregated fund sales charges described?

一名参加 LLQP 考试的考生遇到一道题，询问考试口径中描述了哪些分离基金销售费选项，同时他也想理清考试资料表述与 BC 省现行规则的差异。按考试口径，分离基金的销售费是如何表述的？

  1. ✗ 顾问追偿式销售费（advisor chargeback）是 2023 年 DSC 禁令后市场保留的非递延式佣金安排，考试口径完全没有出现过这个名词（产品名称与双口径混淆陷阱）。Incorrect. The advisor chargeback sales charge is a non-deferred commission structure retained in the market following the 2023 DSC ban; this term does not appear anywhere in the exam standard (product naming and dual-standard confusion trap).

  2. ✗ 2022 年考试口径成文时禁令尚未生效；且即使在 2023-06-01 新单禁令生效后，旧合同的 DSC 赎回时间表仍按各自购买日及原表正常走完，不能设统一清零年（产品名称与双口径混淆陷阱）。Incorrect. When the 2022 exam standard was written, the ban was not yet in effect; furthermore, even after the ban on new contracts took effect on June 1, 2023, existing contracts continue their DSC redemption schedules based on their respective purchase dates and original schedules rather than having a unified reset date (product naming and dual-standard confusion trap).

  3. ✓ 考试口径（2022 成文）将前收费（front-end load, FEL）与递延销售费（deferred sales charge, DSC）并列，并写明 DSC“预期会被淘汰”。注意考试口径与 BC 现行法规不同：BC 已自 2023-06-01 起依《金融机构法》（Financial Institutions Act）s.244(2)(e)(ii) 正式禁止新分离基金合同含 DSC，考场问考试资料列出的种类时仍须涵盖 FEL 与 DSC。Correct. The exam standard lists front-end load (FEL) and deferred sales charge (DSC) options side by side, noting that DSC is expected to be phased out. Note that the exam standard differs from current BC regulations: BC officially prohibited DSC on new segregated fund contracts under Financial Institutions Act s.244(2)(e)(ii) effective June 1, 2023, but exam questions about the sales charge types the exam materials list must still cover both FEL and DSC.

  4. ✗ 《Power of Attorney Act》s.20 规范的是持续授权代理人变更既有受益人指定须取得法院授权，与分离基金销售费（sales charge）毫无关系（授权书越权变更受益人陷阱）。Incorrect. Section 20 of the Power of Attorney Act governs the requirement for an attorney under an enduring power of attorney to obtain court authorization before altering an existing beneficiary designation, which is entirely unrelated to segregated fund sales charges (Power of Attorney exceeding authority to alter beneficiaries trap).

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An investor in British Columbia holds an existing segregated fund contract purchased before June 1, 2023 under a deferred sales charge (DSC) schedule, and now wants to open a brand-new segregated fund contract in 2026. How do the current rules in British Columbia apply to her existing DSC units and her new contract?

一位 BC 省投资者持有一份在 2023 年 6 月 1 日之前按递延销售费（DSC）时间表购买的既有分离基金合同，现在想于 2026 年开立一份全新的分离基金合同。BC 省现行规则如何适用于她的既有 DSC 单位与新合同？

  1. ✗ BC 自 2023-06-01 起已依《金融机构法》（Financial Institutions Act）s.244(2)(e)(ii) 正式禁止新分离基金合同含递延销售费（DSC），且监管机构已将《分离基金整合指引》采纳为对 BC 持牌人的执业期望，不可向新合同推荐 DSC（产品名称与双口径混淆陷阱）。Incorrect.   2. ✓ 2023-06-01 起 BC 依《金融机构法》（Financial Institutions Act）s.244(2)(e)(ii) 正式禁止新分离基金合同含递延销售费（DSC），新单只能比较前收费（FEL）、无负荷（no-load）或顾问追偿（advisor chargeback）等安排；旧合同的 DSC 赎回时间表可正常走完，依各批单位购买日及原表核实。考试口径（2022）仅称 DSC 预期被淘汰，本题考察 2026 年现行柜台规则。Correct.   3. ✗ 2026-01-01 生效的是总成本报告（Total Cost Reporting, TCR）年度披露新规，旧合同的 DSC 赎回排期依购买日及原表核实，不能设统一清零年（产品名称与双口径混淆陷阱）。Incorrect.   4. ✗ BC 新单 DSC 禁令的执法依据是管市场行为的《金融机构法》（Financial Institutions Act）s.244(2)(e)(ii），不是管保单条款的《保险法》（Insurance Act），也不要求在 71 岁把旧 DSC 单位强制转为给付型年金（产品名称与双口径混淆陷阱）。Incorrect. The statutory basis for BC's ban on new DSC contracts is Financial Institutions Act s.244(2)(e)(ii) governing market conduct, not the Insurance Act governing policy terms, and it does not mandate converting existing DSC units into a payout annuity at age 71 (product naming and dual-standard confusion trap).
