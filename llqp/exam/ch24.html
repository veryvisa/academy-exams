本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 意愿与能力冲突题：题干给出一位主观上渴望高收益或自称不怕亏损、但客观财务状况脆弱（如唯一收入来源、短期内有刚性支出或仅靠政府养老金生活）的客户，测试考生能否识别风险承受意愿（risk tolerance）与风险承受能力（risk capacity）的独立性，并以较低者作为推荐的硬约束上限。Willingness versus capacity conflict questions: Scenarios present a client who subjectively desires high returns or claims to tolerate steep losses, but has a fragile objective financial position (such as being the sole household earner, facing nondiscretionary short-term expenses, or relying solely on government pensions), testing whether candidates can recognize the independence of risk tolerance and risk capacity and apply the lower of the two as the binding upper constraint for recommendations.
  • 时间跨度与多目标拆解题：用客户的实际年龄（如二十多岁的年轻客户或接近七十岁的退休客户）作为干扰项，结合短期用款目标（如三年内使用）或长期跨代目标（如二十年后的孙辈教育），测试时间跨度（time horizon）必须按资金的具体目标而非客户年龄来判定，且同一客户的不同资金目标须分别建立画像。Time horizon and multi-goal separation questions: Scenarios use the client's chronological age (such as a client in their twenties or a retiree near age seventy) as a distractor, combined with short-term expenditure goals (such as funds needed within three years) or long-term intergenerational goals (such as grandchildren's education in twenty years), testing whether candidates determine the time horizon based on the specific goal of the funds rather than the client's age, and build separate investor profiles for different goals of the same client.
  • 财务报表与负债性质判读题：给出客户的净资产表（net worth statement）、现金流量表（cash flow statement）及对账单细节，测试反向按揭（reverse mortgage）属于侵蚀遗产的负债而非资产、良性负债（good debt）仍须核实按期偿还能力、以及单月合理大额支出与持续性透支的区别。Financial statements and debt classification questions: Scenarios provide details from a client's net worth statement, cash flow statement, and account statements, testing whether candidates identify that a reverse mortgage is an estate-eroding liability rather than an asset, that good debt still requires verifying ongoing repayment ability, and how to distinguish an explainable single-month large expenditure from ongoing structural deficit.
  • 持续适当性与留痕合规题：设定客户多年前完成过投资者画像（investor profile），期间发生离婚、失业或提前退休等重大变化但未主动联系代理人，或代理人受高佣金诱导推荐产品，测试适当性原则（suitability principle）的持续复核义务、推荐理由信（reason-why letter）的免责证据作用，以及适当性与公平对待客户原则（FTC）的层级区分。Ongoing suitability and compliance documentation questions: Scenarios involve a client who completed an investor profile years earlier and experienced major life events such as divorce, job loss, or early retirement without contacting the agent, or an agent tempted by higher commissions, testing the ongoing review obligation under the suitability principle, the evidential protection of a reason-why letter, and the hierarchical distinction between suitability and Fair Treatment of Customers (FTC).
  • 政策规则与法律边界题：结合财产授权书（Power of Attorney, POA）代管人在分离基金（IVIC）上的受益人变更限制、CPP 起缴额与供款天花板、以及 RRSP/TFSA/FHSA 等注册账户的核验文件（如评税通知书 NOA），测试考试口径规则与 BC 现行法条（如《Power of Attorney Act》s.20）及 2026 现行数字的把握。Policy rules and legal boundary questions: Scenarios combine limitations on attorney authority under a Power of Attorney (POA) for property to change segregated fund (IVIC) beneficiary designations, CPP basic exemption and contribution ceilings, and verification documents for registered accounts such as RRSP, TFSA, and FHSA (like the Notice of Assessment, NOA), testing candidates' grasp of exam syllabus rules versus current BC statutory provisions (such as Power of Attorney Act s.20) and 2026 figures.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 顺从客户主观偏好Deferring to client subjective preference

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为尊重客户自主选择就是合规，把客户自称的风险感知（risk perception）或高风险承受意愿当成免责挡箭牌，忽略了客观风险承受能力（risk capacity）是不可逾越的硬约束。Candidates mistakenly assume that honoring client autonomy equates to compliance, treating the client's self-assessed risk perception or high risk tolerance as a liability shield while overlooking that objective risk capacity is an insurmountable hard constraint.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现客户强烈要求购买高收益股票型基金、声称“能承受大跌”，但同时交代其应急资金不足、属于家庭唯一收入来源或仅靠养老金维持生活。The question stem shows a client insisting on high-yield equity funds and claiming comfort with sharp market drops, while simultaneously revealing an inadequate emergency fund, sole breadwinner status, or reliance solely on pensions for living expenses.

⚠ 以年龄代替时间跨度Substituting age for time horizon

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 习惯性地认为“年轻人＝投资期长＝应配激进产品”或“老年人＝投资期短＝只能配保守产品”，忘记时间跨度（time horizon）取决于这笔钱何时被用于具体目标。Candidates reflexively assume that a young client always has a long time horizon requiring aggressive products or an older client always has a short horizon requiring conservative products, forgetting that time horizon depends on when the specific funds will be spent for a defined goal.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干突出强调客户的年龄（如 28 岁或 68 岁），但紧接着说明该笔资金用于近期确定开支（如两年内办婚礼、三年后交学费）或远期传承目标（如 20 年后孙辈教育）。The question stem emphasizes the client's chronological age (such as 28 or 68), but immediately clarifies that the funds are allocated for a fixed near-term expenditure (such as a wedding in two years or tuition in three years) or a distant legacy goal (such as grandchildren's education in twenty years).

⚠ 持仓结构外推财务结论Extrapolating financial conclusions from portfolio holdings

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到客户净资产较高或全部持仓都在担保投资证（GIC）里，就跳跃推导出“客户客观财务承受能力低”、“退休一定无忧”或“不再需要人寿保险”，用单一线索替代完整 KYC 六要素核实。Candidates see a high net worth or a portfolio held entirely in GICs and leap to premature conclusions such as low financial capacity, guaranteed comfortable retirement, or no need for life insurance, substituting a single clue for full verification across all six KYC elements.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干只给出一张净资产表或保守型持仓明细，未提供收入、支出、年龄或现有保障等完整信息，选项却出现“财务承受能力必定低下”或“无需保险保障”等绝对化结论。The question stem provides only a net worth statement or conservative holding breakdown without complete information on income, expenses, age, or existing coverage, while options offer definitive conclusions like financial capacity is definitely low or life insurance is unnecessary.

⚠ 把画像当成一次性开户手续Treating the profile as a one-time onboarding formality

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 认为开户时填妥问卷并签字就已经完成了了解你的客户（KYC）与适当性（suitability）义务，把客户事后的“沉默”当成对原配置的“默许”。Candidates assume that completing a risk questionnaire and signing forms at account opening permanently satisfies KYC and suitability duties, misinterpreting the client's subsequent silence as implied consent to maintain the existing allocation.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提及客户几年前做过画像评估，此后经历了退休、离婚、继承遗产或失业等重大生活事件，且期间未主动联系代理人更新资料。The question stem notes that the client completed a profile several years ago and subsequently experienced a major life event such as retirement, divorce, an inheritance, or unemployment, without reaching out to update their records.

⚠ 夸大财产授权书（POA）权限Exaggerating Power of Attorney authority

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为持有财产授权书（POA）的代理人既然能买卖和转换投资，就自然有权随意变更人寿保单或分离基金合同（IVIC）的受益人指定，或误以为考试口径提到的“BC 例外”允许代理人无条件改名。Candidates believe that because an attorney acting under a Power of Attorney (POA) for property can buy, sell, or switch investments, they automatically possess the authority to alter beneficiary designations on life insurance or segregated fund (IVIC) contracts, or misunderstand the BC exception mentioned in the exam syllabus as an unconditional right to change designations.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现客户因病失能，其授权代理人（attorney）出示 POA 要求将分离基金或保单的既有受益人直接改为自己或第三人，且未提及法院授权。The question stem features an incapacitated client whose attorney under a POA presents the document and requests changing the existing segregated fund or insurance beneficiary to themselves or a third party without court authorization.

负债分类与反向按揭的财务归属Debt Classification and Accounting Treatment of Reverse MortgagesGood debt, Bad debt & Reverse mortgage良性负债（按揭、投资贷款）与不良负债（消费型信用卡债）均须以能否按期偿还为评估关键；反向按揭（reverse mortgage）在房主身故或卖房时须偿还并侵蚀遗产，KYC 时必须计入负债而非资产。Both good debt (mortgages, investment loans) and bad debt (consumer credit card balances) must be assessed on whether they can be serviced on schedule; a reverse mortgage must be repaid upon the homeowner's death or home sale and erodes estate equity, meaning it must be recorded as a liability rather than an asset during KYC.
财产授权书（POA）变更受益人的权限边界Authority Boundaries of a Power of Attorney (POA) for Property Regarding BeneficiariesPower of Attorney (POA) for property财产授权代理人（attorney）可代管、转换或出售投资，但通常无权变更人寿保单或分离基金（IVIC）的受益人；BC《Power of Attorney Act》s.20 规定变更既有指定须经法院授权，更新或替换类似文书可依条件沿用原受益人，另建新文书的新指定仅限本人遗产。An attorney under a Power of Attorney for property can manage, switch, or sell investments, but generally lacks authority to alter beneficiary designations on life insurance or segregated fund (IVIC) contracts; BC Power of Attorney Act s.20 mandates court authorization to change an existing designation, permits retaining the same beneficiary when renewing or replacing an instrument under specified conditions, and restricts new designations under fresh instruments to the grantor's estate.
适当性原则与公平对待客户原则的层级关系Hierarchical Relationship Between the Suitability Principle and Fair Treatment of CustomersSuitability principle vs. Fair Treatment of Customers (FTC)适当性原则（suitability principle）专门管单次推荐中投资特征与客户目标、约束及风险画像的持续匹配（且不得受佣金或追踪费影响），而公平对待客户原则（FTC）是贯穿销售全流程 7 项要求的上位监管原则。The suitability principle specifically governs ongoing alignment between investment features and the client's goals, constraints, and risk profile in an individual recommendation (uninfluenced by commissions or trailer fees), whereas Fair Treatment of Customers (FTC) serves as the overarching regulatory doctrine spanning seven obligations across the entire client lifecycle.

第 1 题Q1风险承受意愿与能力孰低约束Risk tolerance versus risk capacity and the lower-limit constraint★★陷阱：顺从客户主观偏好

An individual is the sole income earner for his household, holds emergency savings covering only 3 months of expenses, and must pay for his child's university tuition in 3 years. During the KYC interview, he insists on putting all of his savings into a high-risk equity segregated fund, stating that he can easily handle a 20% market drop if it means higher long-term returns. How should the agent proceed?

某客户是家庭唯一的收入来源，名下应急储蓄仅够维持 3 个月开支，且 3 年后必须支付子女的大学学费。在 KYC 面谈中，他坚持要把全部储蓄投入高风险股票型分离基金，并声称为了高回报完全能承受 20% 的市场下跌。代理人应当如何处理？

  1. ✗ 错误。客户“想要”的产品不等于客户“适合”的产品，客户常高估自己的风险承受意愿（risk tolerance）；主观意愿不能凌驾于代理人的适当性原则（suitability principle）判断之上（顺从客户主观偏好陷阱）。Incorrect. What a client wants is not necessarily what is suitable for the client, and clients frequently overestimate their own risk tolerance; subjective willingness cannot override the agent's professional judgment under the suitability principle (deferring to client subjective preference trap).

  2. ✓ 正确。风险承受意愿（risk tolerance，主观心理）与风险承受能力（risk capacity，客观财务）必须分开评估，并采用二者中较低的水平作为推荐硬约束；由于他是唯一收入来源、应急金仅够 3 个月且 3 年后有确定学费支出，客观承受能力偏低，且短期教育目标与长期目标必须分开建立投资者画像（investor profile）并完整留痕。Correct. Risk tolerance (subjective psychological willingness) and risk capacity (objective financial ability) must be evaluated separately, with the lower of the two serving as the binding constraint for recommendations; because he is the sole household earner, holds emergency savings for only 3 months, and faces definite tuition payments in 3 years, his objective capacity is low, and short-term educational goals must be profiled separately from long-term wealth goals with full documentation.

  3. ✗ 错误。分离基金（segregated fund）的到期保证只在合同到期日或受保人身故当天生效，3 年后取款时尚未到达通常的到期保证日，中途市值若下跌 20% 客户仍需自行承担本金损失。Incorrect. Segregated fund maturity guarantees apply only on the contract maturity date or upon the annuitant's death; withdrawing funds after 3 years falls well short of the standard maturity date, meaning the client would fully bear any 20% interim market drop in principal.

  4. ✗ 错误。代理人必须如实记录客户较高的风险承受意愿（risk tolerance）与较低的风险承受能力（risk capacity），并在推荐理由信（reason-why letter）中说明为何受较低能力限制，不能篡改访谈记录来迎合推荐结果。Incorrect. The agent must faithfully record both the client's high risk tolerance and low risk capacity, and explain in the reason-why letter why advice is constrained by the lower capacity, rather than altering interview notes to fit the proposed product.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A retired widower relies solely on CPP, OAS, and a small pension to cover his monthly living costs, and his entire liquid savings consist of $180,000 in maturing GICs that he may need to draw on at any time. After attending a seminar, he asks his agent to transfer the full $180,000 into a 100% global equity segregated fund because he wants higher returns and believes the contract guarantee makes it just as safe as a GIC. What should the agent do?

  3. ✗ 错误。尽管反向按揭在房主居住期间通常无需每月还款，但它连同累积利息在卖房或身故时必须从房产价值中偿还，属于净资产表（net worth statement）上必须列明的负债，不可遗漏。Incorrect. Although a reverse mortgage generally requires no regular monthly payments while the owner lives in the home, the principal plus compounded interest must be repaid from sale proceeds or the estate, making it an essential liability that must appear on the net worth statement.

  4. ✗ 错误。消费型信用卡债属于消耗财力的不良负债（bad debt），而养老金调整（Pension Adjustment, PA）是雇主养老金计划对次年 RRSP 供款额度的扣减项，与反向按揭毫无关系。Incorrect. Consumer credit card balances represent bad debt that drains wealth, while a Pension Adjustment (PA) reflects employer pension accruals reducing subsequent-year RRSP room and has no connection to reverse mortgages.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

During a financial review, a client asks his agent which documents should be used to accurately verify his available RRSP deduction limit and his remaining TFSA contribution room for 2026, and whether his home equity line of credit (HELOC) and mortgage can be left off the risk review since they are 'good debt.' What is the correct explanation?

在财务审阅中，一位客户询问代理人应当使用哪些文件来准确核实他 2026 年的可用 RRSP 扣除限额与剩余 TFSA 供款空间，以及他的房屋净值信贷额度（HELOC）和按揭既然属于“良性负债”，是否可以从风险审阅中剔除。正确的解释是什么？

  1. ✓ 正确。个税评税通知书（Notice of Assessment, NOA）可用于核对个人 RRSP 扣除限额（并体现养老金调整 PA 等影响）；而免税储蓄账户（TFSA）因 CRA 账户不实时更新，必须用客户在全部机构的实际供款与提款记录计算；此外，按揭与 HELOC 即使算良性负债（good debt），也必须核实能否按期偿还，不能直接忽略。Correct. The Notice of Assessment (NOA) verifies an individual's RRSP deduction limit (reflecting factors like the Pension Adjustment); because CRA online accounts do not update TFSAs in real time, available room must be verified through actual contribution and withdrawal records across all institutions; additionally, even if mortgages and HELOCs are considered good debt, scheduled repayment ability must be verified rather than ignored.

  2. ✗ 错误。$109,000 是 2009 年已满 18 岁且历年均符合居民资格者的累计限额总额，不等于人人都现有的可用余额；且把按揭当成“没问题的良性负债”直接忽略是典型的审阅疏漏。Incorrect. $109,000 is the cumulative lifetime room for an individual who turned 18 by 2009 and remained a resident each year, not an assumed available balance for everyone; moreover, ignoring a mortgage simply because it is considered good debt is a classic review oversight.

  3. ✗ 错误。RRSP 扣除限额应核对 CRA 发出的评税通知书（NOA）而非水电账单；已动用的 HELOC 余额属于净资产表（net worth statement）上的负债而非流动资产。Incorrect. RRSP deduction limits must be verified against CRA Notices of Assessment (NOA) rather than utility bills; drawn HELOC balances represent liabilities on the net worth statement, not liquid assets.

  4. ✗ 错误。CRA 账户的 TFSA 记录并不实时更新当月交易，必须核对全部金融机构的实际存取记录；且反向按揭（reverse mortgage）属于负债而非免税收入。Incorrect. CRA online account records for TFSAs do not track current-year transactions in real time, requiring verification of actual deposits and withdrawals across all institutions; furthermore, reverse mortgages are liabilities, not tax-free income.

第 6 题Q6重大人生变化触发画像复核Material life changes triggering mandatory profile review★★陷阱：把画像当成一次性开户手续

Five years ago, a 50-year-old employed client completed a thorough investor profile and was placed into a medium-risk balanced segregated fund. Eighteen months ago, she went through a divorce that cut her net worth in half and took early retirement, changing her income structure, but she never contacted her agent to update her file. When a sharp market correction occurs today, how is the agent's obligation assessed?

五年前，一位 50 岁的在职客户完成了一份完整的投资者画像，并配置了中等风险的平衡型分离基金。18 个月前，她经历了离婚（个人净资产缩水一半）并提前退休（收入结构改变），但她从未主动联系代理人更新档案。当今天市场发生剧烈回调时，应如何评估代理人的义务？

  1. ✓ 正确。了解你的客户（KYC）与适当性原则（suitability principle）是持续性义务而非一次性开户手续；客户发生离婚、退休等重大人生变化时，时间跨度与风险承受能力（risk capacity）均已改变，代理人不能把“客户沉默”当成“默许维持现状”，必须建立定期复核提醒机制并做好复核记录留痕（把画像当成一次性开户手续陷阱）。Correct. KYC and the suitability principle represent ongoing obligations rather than a one-time onboarding formality; when a client experiences major life changes such as divorce or retirement, their time horizon and risk capacity change, so the agent cannot treat silence as implied consent and must implement periodic reviews with documented records (treating the profile as a one-time onboarding formality trap).

  2. ✗ 错误。投资者画像（investor profile）绝非签单当天做一次就永久有效，在合同存续期间必须定期更新并在重大变化时重新评估（把画像当成一次性开户手续陷阱）。Incorrect. An investor profile is never permanently valid once completed at contract signing; it must be updated periodically and reassessed whenever major changes occur (treating the profile as a one-time onboarding formality trap).

  3. ✗ 错误。无论是产品特征本身发生变化，还是客户人生发生重大变化（退休、离婚、继承遗产、失业），都会触发重新评估适当性（suitability）的持续义务。Incorrect. Both changes in product features and material life events in a client's life (retirement, divorce, inheritance, unemployment) trigger the ongoing regulatory obligation to reassess suitability.

  4. ✗ 错误。即便原本配置的是平衡型分离基金，当客户因离婚导致净资产大幅缩水、因提前退休导致时间跨度缩短且收入结构改变时，原有的中等风险配置可能已超出其当下的风险承受能力（risk capacity），必须重新复核。Incorrect. Even if the original holding is a balanced segregated fund, when divorce significantly reduces net worth and early retirement shortens the horizon while altering cash flow, the moderate-risk allocation may exceed current risk capacity, necessitating a formal review.

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Eighteen months after setting up a 75/100 balanced segregated fund for her retirement goal (originally planned for 13 years away) and a GIC for her child's tuition, a teacher takes early retirement and begins drawing her employer pension. Her agent conducts a formal review. Which elements must the agent include in the review documentation to serve as proper compliance evidence?

在为一位教师的退休目标（原定 13 年后）配置了 75/100 平衡型分离基金、并为子女学费配置了 GIC 的 18 个月后，该客户提前退休并开始领取雇主养老金。她的代理人对此进行了正式复核。代理人的复核记录必须包含哪些要素才能作为合格的合规留痕证据？

  1. ✗ 错误。仅打印佣金演示表无法证明已重新评估客户需求与风险画像，不能构成适当性（suitability）复核的合规证据。Incorrect. Merely printing a commission illustration fails to prove that client needs and risk profiles were reassessed, and cannot serve as compliant evidence of a suitability review.

  2. ✗ 错误。教育费部分的目标未变不代表退休储蓄部分不受影响；提前退休直接缩短了退休资金的时间跨度（time horizon）并改变了家庭收入结构，必须专门复核退休配置。Incorrect. Leaving the education goal intact does not mean retirement savings are unaffected; early retirement directly shortens the time horizon for retirement assets and alters income structure, requiring a dedicated review of the retirement allocation.

  3. ✗ 错误。不留书面记录会让代理人在日后面对投诉或省级监管机构（Insurance Council of BC / BCFSA）问询时失去关键的免责证据，适当性复核必须完整书面留痕。Incorrect. Failing to keep written records deprives the agent of crucial defensive evidence if challenged by complaints or provincial regulatory inquiries from the Insurance Council of BC or BCFSA; suitability reviews require comprehensive written documentation.

  4. ✓ 正确。一份能作为代理人免责证据的重大变化复核记录（reason-why / review record），应完整包含：触发事件与日期（提前退休）、复核项（重新评估收入替代来源、时间跨度是否缩短、原定 13 年配置是否仍适配）、复核结论（如教育费不变、退休部分建议下调权益比重）以及客户签字确认日期。Correct. A material change review record (reason-why or review record) serving as defensive evidence for an agent must include: the triggering event and date (early retirement), items reviewed (re-evaluating income replacement, shortened time horizon, and whether the original 13-year allocation remains suitable), review conclusions (such as leaving education intact while recommending a lower equity weighting for retirement), and the date of client acknowledgment.

第 7 题Q7适当性原则与公平对待客户区别Distinction between the suitability principle and fair treatment of customers★★陷阱：混淆适当性原则与FTC层级

Two segregated fund products both meet a client's general time horizon, but Fund X pays a significantly higher sales commission and trailing fee to the agent while carrying higher volatility than the client's investor profile allows. Which regulatory principle specifically governs whether this single product recommendation matches this specific client's profile, and how must commission be treated?

两款分离基金产品都符合某客户的大致时间跨度，但基金 X 向代理人支付的销售佣金和追踪费明显更高，同时其波动率超出了该客户投资者画像所允许的范围。哪一项监管原则专门管辖“这一次产品推荐是否匹配这一位具体客户的画像”？代理人在判断时应如何对待佣金？

  1. ✗ 错误。公平对待客户原则（Fair Treatment of Customers, FTC）是覆盖从产品设计到理赔处理全流程 7 项要求的上位监管原则，而专门管“这一个产品配不配这一个客户”单次推荐匹配度的是其下位的适当性原则（suitability principle）；且事后披露绝不能让受高佣金驱动的不适当推荐变得合规。Incorrect. Fair Treatment of Customers (FTC) is an overarching regulatory doctrine covering seven obligations across the full lifecycle from product design to claims settlement, whereas the specific alignment of whether this product fits this client in a single recommendation is governed by the subsidiary suitability principle; furthermore, post-sale disclosure can never validate an unsuitable recommendation driven by high commissions.

  2. ✗ 错误。了解你的客户（KYC）是收集客户身份、财务、需求与风险信息的程序，本身不直接产出推荐结论；且高追踪费或高佣金绝不等于产品更安全或更适合客户。Incorrect. KYC is the process of collecting client identity, financial, objective, and risk data, and does not itself produce a product recommendation; furthermore, higher trailer fees or commissions never indicate that a product is safer or more suitable.

  3. ✓ 正确。题目问“哪个原则专门管这一次的产品匹配”时，应答适当性原则（suitability principle）而非笼统答公平对待客户原则（FTC，适当性只是 FTC 众多要求中的一项）；同时考试口径明确强调，代理人比对投资特征时，佣金或追踪费不得影响判断，并在推荐理由信（reason-why letter）中单独列明佣金结构未影响配置选择。Correct. When asked which principle specifically governs product matching for a single recommendation, the answer is the suitability principle rather than the broad FTC doctrine (suitability is just one obligation under FTC); moreover, the exam syllabus explicitly emphasizes that commissions or trailer fees must not influence product comparisons, and the reason-why letter should document that compensation structure did not bias the recommendation.

  4. ✗ 错误。养老金调整（Pension Adjustment, PA）是计算次年 RRSP 供款空间的税务概念，与产品适当性匹配毫无关系；且佣金高低在任何情况下都不得影响适当性判断。Incorrect. The Pension Adjustment (PA) is a tax metric reducing subsequent-year RRSP room and has no relevance to product suitability matching; furthermore, commission levels must never influence suitability assessments under any circumstances.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A compliance officer is training new licensing candidates on the relationship between Know Your Client (KYC), the suitability principle, the reason-why letter, and Fair Treatment of Customers (FTC). Which statement accurately describes how these four concepts fit together?

一位合规官正在为新考牌学员培训了解你的客户（KYC）、适当性原则、推荐理由信与公平对待客户原则（FTC）四者之间的关系。下列哪项陈述准确描述了这四个概念的逻辑关系？

  1. ✗ 错误。适当性原则（suitability principle）与公平对待客户原则（FTC）并非同一层级，二者均贯穿客户关系全周期而非仅限开户当天；且推荐理由信（reason-why letter）是给客户的书面披露，必须与客户讨论并存档。Incorrect. The suitability principle and Fair Treatment of Customers (FTC) exist at different regulatory tiers, and both operate across the entire client relationship rather than only at account opening; moreover, the reason-why letter is a written disclosure that must be discussed with the client and archived.

  2. ✓ 正确。公平对待客户原则（Fair Treatment of Customers, FTC）是贯穿销售全流程（从产品设计到理赔处理共 7 条）的上位监管原则；了解你的客户（KYC）汇总形成投资者画像（investor profile），适当性原则（suitability principle）是把画像转化为适配产品推荐的那座桥（也是 FTC 的其中一项要求），而推荐理由信（reason-why letter）则是将推荐逻辑向客户书面披露并留存归档的关键合规证据。Correct. Fair Treatment of Customers (FTC) is an overarching regulatory principle spanning seven obligations across the entire sales cycle from product design to claims handling; KYC compiles information into an investor profile, the suitability principle bridges that profile to a tailored product recommendation (functioning as one core requirement of FTC), and the reason-why letter serves as critical compliance evidence documenting the recommendation rationale for the client and agency files.

  3. ✗ 错误。KYC 收集的信息本身不产出结论，必须通过适当性原则（suitability principle）持续比对投资特征与客户画像才能形成推荐；且风险感知（risk perception）或问卷不能替代完整的适当性判断。Incorrect. Information gathered through KYC does not independently produce recommendations; the agent must apply the suitability principle to continuously compare product features against the client profile, and risk perception or questionnaires cannot substitute for full suitability analysis.

  4. ✗ 错误。这里把适当性原则（suitability principle）与公平对待客户原则（FTC）的上下位关系完全颠倒了：覆盖产品设计到理赔处理全流程的是 FTC，专门管产品推荐是否匹配客户画像的是适当性原则。Incorrect. This completely reverses the hierarchical relationship between the suitability principle and Fair Treatment of Customers (FTC): FTC is the overarching doctrine spanning product design to claims settlement, whereas suitability specifically governs whether product recommendations match the client's profile.
