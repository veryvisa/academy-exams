① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 保费与身故金税务判定题：给出雇主与员工各自的保费分摊比例及年薪倍数保额，考查哪一部分计入当年应税福利（taxable benefit）、哪一部分不可抵扣，以及身故保险金本金是否免税，专门测试是否与团体伤残险记反。Premium and death benefit tax determination questions: Presenting employer and employee premium split percentages and salary-multiple coverage amounts, testing which portion constitutes a taxable benefit in the current year, which portion is not deductible, and whether the death benefit principal is tax-free, specifically checking whether candidates confuse the rules with group disability insurance.
  • 离职或计划终止转换权（conversion privilege）计算与条件题：结合员工年龄（65岁前后）、原保额与非魁省 $200,000 免核保上限、31天申请及缴清首期保费窗口，考查员工在离职时能免健康证明转换多少个人寿险保额。Conversion privilege calculation and condition questions upon termination or plan end: Combining employee age (before versus at age 65), original coverage, the non-Quebec $200,000 non-medical conversion cap, and the 31-day window to apply and pay the first premium, testing how much individual life coverage an employee can convert without evidence of insurability upon leaving employment.
  • 三方合同关系与法定查阅权场景题：通过雇主调整或终止某一会籍分级（membership class）、更换承保人或员工索取条款发生争议，测试总合同（master contract）与福利手册（benefit booklet）的区别及 BC《Insurance Act》下的主保单取阅权。Three-party contractual relationship and statutory inspection right scenario questions: Presenting employer adjustments or terminations of a membership class, insurer replacements, or disputes arising from an employee requesting policy terms, testing the distinction between the master contract and benefit booklet as well as the right to inspect and copy the master policy under the BC Insurance Act.
  • 入职资格与逆选择（adverse selection）控制机制题：设定新员工尚在试用期（probationary period）、生效日未满足在职要求（actively-at-work requirement）或错过 60 天限定窗口才申请自选追加保额（optional additional coverage），判断保障是否生效及是否需要核保。Eligibility and adverse selection control mechanism questions: Setting scenarios where a new employee is within the probationary period, fails to satisfy the actively-at-work requirement on the effective date, or misses the 60-day limited window before applying for optional additional coverage, testing whether coverage takes effect and whether medical underwriting is required.
  • 附属保障读表与概念辨析题：将家属寿险（dependant life coverage）统一家庭费率、遗属月给付（survivor income benefits）的终止条件（65岁/再婚/身故）以及家属 AD&D 的百分比（50% vs 40%/15%）放在家庭场景中考查。Ancillary coverage schedule reading and concept distinction questions: Placing flat family rates for dependant life coverage, termination conditions for monthly survivor income benefits (age 65, remarriage, or death), and dependant AD&D schedule percentages (50% versus 40% / 15%) into family scenarios for testing.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 跨险种税务方向记反Inverting tax treatment across insurance lines

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把团体伤残险『雇主出保费则给付应税』的规则套用到团体寿险上，误以为雇主付保费会导致寿险身故金应税，或误以为员工全额自付能让身故金『更免税』。Applying the group disability rule (benefits are taxable if the employer pays premiums) to group life insurance, mistakenly believing that employer-paid premiums make the life insurance death benefit taxable, or mistakenly assuming that employee 100% self-funding makes the death benefit even more tax-exempt.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现雇主分摊团体寿险保费比例（如 70%/30% 或全额支付）并询问员工当年 T4 应税福利或受益人身故金税务后果。The question stem presents an employer group life premium contribution ratio (such as 70% / 30% or 100% employer-paid) and asks about the employee's current-year T4 taxable benefit or the beneficiary's death benefit tax consequences.

⚠ 转换上限当成固定保额或漏看缴付条件Treating the conversion cap as a fixed coverage amount or missing payment conditions

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误把非魁省 $200,000 免核保转换上限当成离职后自动获得的保额，或以为 31 天内只要口头决定、递交表格即可，忽略必须在窗口内缴清首期保费且受 65 岁年龄边界限制。Mistaking the non-Quebec $200,000 non-medical conversion cap for an automatic coverage amount granted upon termination, or assuming that a verbal decision or merely submitting an application form within 31 days is sufficient, overlooking that the first premium must be paid in full within the window and is subject to the age 65 boundary.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出员工离职日期、原保额（高于或低于 $200,000）及年龄，询问免健康证明转换（conversion privilege）的额度或截止动作。The question stem provides an employee's termination date, original coverage amount (above or below $200,000), and age, asking for the conversion privilege maximum amount without evidence of insurability or the required completion actions by the deadline.

⚠ 主保单查阅权与总合同边界混淆Confusing master policy inspection rights with master contract boundaries

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 受考试口径对魁省规则描述的误导，误以为 BC 团体成员只能看福利手册（benefit booklet）而无权查阅主保单，或以为会员是总合同（master contract）的签字投保人。Misled by exam references contrasting Quebec rules, candidates mistakenly believe that BC group plan members can only view the benefit booklet with no right to inspect the master policy, or assume that group members are signatory policyholders to the master contract.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现福利手册与主保单条款不一致、理赔争议或雇主变更会籍类别，询问谁持有主保单及成员能否向保险人索取主保单副本。The question stem presents inconsistencies between the benefit booklet and master contract terms, claim disputes, or an employer modifying membership classes, asking who holds the master contract and whether a member can request a copy of the master policy from the insurer.

⚠ 自选追加保额与在职要求过度推定Over-presuming non-underwritten status for optional coverage and actively-at-work requirements

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为只要是团体寿险就永远免核保，忽略自选追加保额（optional additional coverage）逾越限定窗口（如 60 天示例）须核保，或忽略试用期满后还须按合同满足在职要求（actively-at-work requirement）。Assuming that all group life insurance is perpetually exempt from medical underwriting, overlooking that optional additional coverage requires medical evidence once past the limited enrolment window (60 days in the exam example), or overlooking that after completing the probationary period, the actively-at-work requirement must still be met under the contract.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现新员工入职满 3 个月当天未上班，或员工在入职数年后因健康变化想加购自选保额。The question stem involves a new employee being absent from work on the day their 3-month probationary period ends, or an employee wanting to purchase optional coverage years after joining due to health changes.

⚠ 家属险计费与遗属给付概念混淆Confusing dependant life rating with survivor income benefit concepts

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 凭直觉认为孩子越多家属寿险（dependant life coverage）保费越贵，或把按月发放的遗属收入给付（survivor income benefits）当成一次性身故金的一部分，或算错家属 AD&D 比例。Relying on intuition that dependant life coverage premiums increase with each additional child, treating monthly survivor income benefits as part of the lump-sum death benefit, or miscalculating dependant AD&D schedule percentages.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及多子女家庭购买统一家庭费率家属险、配偶再婚对遗属月给付的影响，或有/无子女时配偶 AD&D 百分比（50% 与 40%/15%）切换。The question stem involves a multi-child family enrolling in flat family rate dependant life coverage, the effect of spousal remarriage on monthly survivor income benefits, or switching spousal AD&D percentages between scenarios with and without children (50% versus 40% / 15%).

第 1 题Q1保费与身故金税务规则Tax rules for premiums and death benefits★★陷阱：跨险种税务方向记反

Hannah is a senior analyst at an engineering firm in Vancouver, BC, earning $110,000 per year. Under her employer's group plan, senior analysts receive basic group life coverage of 2 times annual salary. The annual premium for Hannah's basic group life coverage is $1,200, paid 50% by the employer and 50% by Hannah. Which statement correctly describes the income tax treatment of the premiums and the death benefit?

Hannah 是 BC 省温哥华一家工程公司的高级分析师，年薪 $110,000。根据雇主的团体计划，高级分析师享有 2 倍年薪的团体基础寿险保额。Hannah 团体基础寿险的年度总保费为 $1,200，由雇主和 Hannah 各付 50%。下列哪项正确描述了保费与身故保险金的所得税处理？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入『跨险种税务方向记反』陷阱：把团体周期性收入替代计划（WLRP）在给付端收税的规则误套到团体寿险上，且错误地按保费比例切分寿险身故金。Falls into the 'inverting tax treatment across insurance lines' trap. Incorrectly applies the group wage loss replacement plan (WLRP) rule of taxing benefits at payout to group life insurance, and erroneously splits the life insurance death benefit based on premium contribution proportions.

  2. ✓ 正确。在团体寿险中，雇主支付的保费（$600）计入员工当年应税收入，构成应税福利（taxable benefit）；员工自付的保费（$600）不可抵扣也不产生应税福利；寿险身故保险金本金（2 倍年薪即 $220,000）不论保费谁付，通常全额免所得税。Correct. Under group life insurance, the $600 premium paid by the employer is included in the employee's taxable income for the year as a taxable benefit; the $600 paid by the employee is not deductible and generates no taxable benefit; and the life insurance death benefit principal (2 times annual salary, or $220,000) is generally exempt from income tax in full regardless of who paid the premiums.

  3. ✗ 错误。落入『误以为员工自付保费可抵税』陷阱：员工自付的那部分团体寿险保费不可在个人报税时抵扣（not deductible）。Falls into the 'assuming employee contributions are tax-deductible' trap. The portion of group life insurance premiums paid by the employee cannot be deducted on personal income tax returns.

  4. ✗ 错误。落入『员工全额自付才免税』陷阱：团体寿险身故保险金本金原本就免所得税，与谁付保费无关；员工全额自付消除的是保费端的应税福利（taxable benefit），而不是改变身故金本身的免税属性。Falls into the 'assuming only 100% employee-funded plans yield tax-free death benefits' trap. Group life insurance death benefit principal is inherently exempt from income tax regardless of who pays the premiums; paying 100% of the premium simply eliminates the taxable benefit at the premium payment stage rather than altering the tax-free status of the death benefit itself.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Lucas works for a software company in Kelowna, BC where the group life insurance plan requires employees to pay 100% of the basic life premiums ($540 per year for $150,000 of coverage), while the employer pays 100% of the group disability income plan premiums. How are Lucas's group life premiums and group life death benefit treated for tax purposes?

Lucas 在 BC 省基洛纳一家软件公司工作，该公司的团体寿险计划要求员工 100% 自付基础寿险保费（$150,000 保额每年保费 $540），而雇主 100% 支付团体伤残收入计划的保费。Lucas 的团体寿险保费和团体寿险身故金在税务上如何处理？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入『员工全额自付即可抵税』陷阱：员工自付的团体寿险保费不可抵扣，仅是不产生应税福利（taxable benefit）。Falls into the 'assuming 100% employee payment confers tax deductibility' trap. Employee-paid group life insurance premiums are not tax-deductible; paying them directly merely prevents a taxable benefit from arising.

  2. ✗ 错误。落入『跨险种税务混淆』陷阱：员工自己付的保费不会产生应税福利，雇主为伤残计划付保费也绝不会使团体寿险身故金变成应税。Falls into the 'cross-product tax confusion' trap. Premiums paid by the employee do not create a taxable benefit, and the employer funding a separate disability plan in no way makes the group life insurance death benefit taxable.

  3. ✓ 正确。员工全额自付团体寿险保费时，该保费不可抵扣且不产生应税福利（taxable benefit）；寿险身故保险金本金（$150,000）无论由雇主还是员工付保费，通常均全额免所得税。Correct. When an employee pays 100% of group life insurance premiums, the premium is not deductible and creates no taxable benefit; the life insurance death benefit principal ($150,000) is generally exempt from income tax in full whether paid by the employer or the employee.

  4. ✗ 错误。落入『按比例切分寿险身故金』陷阱：寿险身故保险金本金通常全额免税，不会按福利包分摊比例切分征税。Falls into the 'proportional death benefit taxation' trap. The life insurance death benefit principal is generally fully tax-free and is never apportioned or taxed based on overall benefit package cost-sharing ratios.

第 2 题Q2离职免核保转换上限与窗口Non-medical conversion limit and window upon termination★★陷阱：转换上限当成固定保额或漏看缴付条件

Tariq, age 44, is laid off from his accounting job in Burnaby, BC on October 1, which is also the date his $150,000 basic group life coverage terminates. Since developing a heart condition last year, Tariq is uninsurable on the individual market. Under the standard non-Quebec conversion rules tested on the exam, what are Tariq's rights?

  3. ✗ 错误。遗属收入给付（survivor income benefits）是团体寿险下的附加给付，并非仅限意外身故才赔付。Survivor income benefits are an ancillary benefit under group life insurance and are payable upon death from illness or injury, not restricted solely to accidental death.

  4. ✗ 错误。配偶的遗属收入给付在满 65 岁、再婚或身故时即停止，不会在再婚后继续终身发放；15%（$22,500）是每名子女的 AD&D 比例而非配偶比例。Spousal survivor income benefits terminate upon reaching age 65, remarriage, or death, rather than continuing for life after remarriage; additionally, 15% ($22,500) represents the benefit percentage per eligible child, not the spouse.

答案Answer：a

第 6 题Q665岁退休转换与高龄保额递减Conversion at age 65 retirement and late-age coverage reductions★★★陷阱：转换上限当成固定保额或漏看缴付条件

Arthur, a quality supervisor at a BC plant with $240,000 of basic group life coverage, is planning to retire on his 65th birthday next month. Under his employer's group schedule of benefits, retirees who stay in a retiree class see their coverage reduce by 50% at age 65 and terminate completely at age 70. Arthur asks his advisor whether he can simply convert the full $240,000 on his 65th birthday without medical evidence or whether he should retire a week early solely to lock in a conversion. How should the advisor handle this situation?

Arthur 是 BC 省一家工厂的品控主管，享有 $240,000 的团体基础寿险保额，计划在下个月 65 岁生日当天退休。根据雇主团体保额表，留在退休人员分级的会员其保额在 65 岁时减半，并在 70 岁时完全终止。Arthur 询问顾问，他是否可以在 65 岁生日当天免健康证明直接转换全部 $240,000 保额，或者他是否应该仅仅为了锁定转换权而提前一周退休。顾问应如何处理这一情况？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入『把单一保险须知的 65 岁限制当成全行业统一禁令并盲目建议提前退休』陷阱：退休日涉及养老金和雇佣福利，不能只靠一个保险转换规则就要求客户提前退休。Falls into the 'treating one insurer's age 65 guideline as an industry-wide ban and prematurely urging early retirement' trap. A retirement date affects pensions and employment benefits, so an advisor must never advise a client to retire early based solely on an insurance conversion rule.

  2. ✗ 错误。同时落入『忽略 $200,000 转换上限与 $40,000 缺口』、『忽略 65 岁当天边界差异』以及『忽略高龄保额到 70 岁完全终止』三个陷阱。Simultaneously falls into three traps: overlooking the $200,000 conversion cap and the resulting $40,000 coverage gap, ignoring contract boundary differences on the 65th birthday, and ignoring the complete termination of retiree coverage at age 70.

  3. ✗ 错误。落入『把团体定期寿险保额递减当成现金价值给付』陷阱：团体基础定期寿险的高龄阶梯递减（schedule of benefits reduction）只是保额下调，不产生任何现金退保价值给付。Falls into the 'treating group term life coverage reductions as cash value payouts' trap. Age-banded reductions under a group term schedule of benefits represent downward coverage adjustments and generate no cash surrender value or living benefit.

  4. ✓ 正确。考试口径写转换权适用于『65 岁前/时』，而现行部分保险人须知排除保障在 65 岁生日当天或之后终止者；顾问应在退休前取得书面合同条款核实最后工作日、退休日与保障终止日的关系，并列比较退休递减延续（至 70 岁终止）、$200,000 免核保转换上限（注意原保额 $240,000 存在 $40,000 缺口）及其他符合条件的产品，且绝不单凭保险上限要求客户提前退休。Correct. The exam material states that the conversion privilege applies 'before or at age 65,' whereas some insurer guidelines exclude coverage terminating on or after the 65th birthday; the advisor should obtain written contract provisions before retirement to verify the interaction among the final working day, retirement date, and coverage termination date, while comparing retiree continuation (terminating at age 70), the $200,000 non-medical conversion cap (noting the $40,000 gap from his $240,000 coverage), and alternative eligible products, without urging early retirement based solely on insurance rules.

答案Answer：d

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Evelyn is reviewing her employer's group life insurance schedule of benefits in BC. She currently has $220,000 of basic coverage at age 64. The schedule states that active coverage reduces by 50% on a member's 65th birthday and reduces to zero at age 70. Regarding the exam conversion rule ('before or at age 65' up to $200,000) and real-world contract verification, which statement is accurate?

Evelyn 正在审阅其 BC 省雇主的团体寿险保额表。她目前 64 岁，享有 $220,000 的基础保额。保额表规定，保障在会员 65 岁生日时减少 50%，并在 70 岁时归零。关于考试口径转换规则（『65 岁前/时』最高 $200,000）以及实务合同核对，下列哪项陈述是准确的？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入『忽略高龄保额递减与终止规则』陷阱：退休或高龄会员的团体保额几乎都会递减，并通常在特定年龄（如考试口径举例的 70 岁）完全终止。Falls into the 'ignoring late-age coverage reduction and termination rules' trap. Group life coverage for retired or senior members almost always reduces in stages and typically terminates completely at a specified age, such as age 70 in the exam examples.

  2. ✓ 正确。双口径对比：考场若未给特殊合同细节，按考试口径『65 岁前/时』及最高 $200,000 免核保转换上限（conversion privilege）作答；而实务中部分公司须知排除 65 岁生日当天或之后终止者，且 $220,000 原保额超过 $200,000 上限的部分（$20,000 缺口）需另行核对安排。Correct. Dual-benchmark distinction: in exam scenarios without specific contract variations, apply the exam standard of 'before or at age 65' up to the $200,000 non-medical conversion cap; in practice, some insurer guidelines exclude terminations on or after the 65th birthday, and any original coverage exceeding $200,000 (a $20,000 gap on $220,000) requires separate planning.

  3. ✗ 错误。高龄保额阶梯递减（schedule of benefits reduction）是保障额度的下调，团体定期寿险没有现金价值或递减现金给付。Age-banded coverage reductions under a schedule of benefits merely decrease the death benefit; group term life insurance carries no cash value or cash surrender payout.

  4. ✗ 错误。非魁省免健康证明转换上限为 $200,000 或原保额中较小者，并不会对 60 岁以上会员取消 $200,000 上限。The non-Quebec non-medical conversion cap is the lesser of $200,000 or the prior group coverage, and this $200,000 ceiling is never waived for members over age 60.

第 7 题Q7计划终止保费豁免与债权人险Disability waiver of premium on plan termination and creditor insurance★★★陷阱：主保单查阅权与总合同边界混淆

A BC logistics company decides to replace its group life insurer on July 1 and simultaneously eliminates the membership class for seasonal drivers. Mateo, a full-time warehouse worker, is currently off work on an approved disability waiver of premium under the old group policy. Meanwhile, the company controller asks about the exam cooling-off rule for group creditor insurance (CLHIA Guideline G7) versus individual life insurance (Guideline G10). Which statement is correct?

BC 省一家物流公司决定于 7 月 1 日更换其团体寿险承保公司，并同时取消季节性司机这一会籍分级。全职仓库工人 Mateo 目前正根据原团体保单处于已获批的伤残保费豁免期内。与此同时，公司财务主管询问考试口径中关于团体债权人保险（CLHIA Guideline G7）与个人寿险（Guideline G10）冷静期规则的区别。下列哪项陈述是正确的？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入『假定计划变动将已有伤残豁免一笔注销』与『把资格丧失当成保额降低』两个陷阱：已处于伤残保费豁免（waiver of premium）的会员即使雇主终止或更换合同，其豁免与保障仍须持续或核对责任归属；而被取消会籍分级的季节性司机在新计划下是直接失去参保资格，而非保留 50% 保额。Falls into both the 'assuming plan changes wipe out existing premium waivers' and 'confusing loss of eligibility with benefit reduction' traps. For a member on an approved disability waiver of premium, coverage and premium waiver continue or are apportioned between contracts despite employer changes; seasonal drivers whose membership class was eliminated lose plan eligibility entirely rather than retaining 50% coverage.

  2. ✗ 错误。落入『团体债权人保险当事人与受益方向记反』陷阱：在团体债权人保险（group creditor insurance）中，金融机构为投保人，借款人为会员，身故金即未偿债务余额并用于清偿贷款。Falls into the 'reversing creditor insurance parties and beneficiary directions' trap. Under group creditor insurance, the lending financial institution is the master policyholder, the borrower is the group member, and the death benefit equals the outstanding loan balance and is applied to pay off the debt.

  3. ✓ 正确。对于已有伤残保费豁免（waiver of premium）的会员，即使雇主更换承保公司或终止合同，其豁免与保障仍须持续并核对新旧约责任归属；被取消会籍分级（membership class）的成员直接失去新计划资格，应取得通知并比较转换或延续路径；考试口径引用 CLHIA Guideline G7 第 6(d) 项写收到证明后 20 天全额退款（不同于个人寿险 G10 的 10 天冷静期），而现行公开页仅确认 G7 适用范围且法条优先，实务办理须核对当前团体合同。Correct. For members with an approved disability waiver of premium, coverage and waiver rights must continue across insurer replacements or terminations, with liability allocated between contracts; members whose class is eliminated lose eligibility under the new plan and must receive notice to evaluate conversion or continuation options; the exam cites CLHIA Guideline G7 section 6(d) providing a 20-day full refund window after receipt of certificate (distinct from the 10-day cooling-off period under individual life Guideline G10), while current public sources confirm only G7's scope and statutory priority, requiring verification against the active group contract.

  4. ✗ 错误。考试口径内部对转换窗口天数写的是 31 天（两处）与 30 天（一处），并非 45 天；且更换承保公司的新约（replacement contract）旨在确保合资格在职会员不出现保障空窗。The exam cites conversion window durations of 31 days (in two places) and 30 days (in one place), never 45 days; furthermore, replacement contracts when switching insurers are designed to ensure continuous coverage for active eligible members without coverage gaps.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Paul takes out a business loan through a lending institution in BC and enrols in the lender's group creditor life insurance plan. At the same time, his sister Rosa is on an approved disability waiver of premium under her employer's group life plan when her employer terminates the master contract. According to the lecture's rules on group creditor insurance and waiver of premium on plan termination, which statement is accurate?

Paul 通过 BC 省一家贷款机构办理了一笔商业贷款，并加入了该贷款机构的团体债权人寿险计划。与此同时，他的姐姐 Rosa 在雇主终止团体寿险总合同时，正处于已获批的伤残保费豁免期内。根据讲义关于团体债权人保险及计划终止时保费豁免的规则，下列哪项陈述是准确的？

  1. ✓ 正确。团体债权人保险（group creditor insurance）以金融机构为投保人、借款人为会员，身故金即未偿债务余额；考试口径引用 CLHIA Guideline G7 写收到证明后 20 天全额退款（现行公开页仅确认适用范围，实务须核对当前团体合同）；而处于伤残保费豁免（waiver of premium）的会员即使雇主终止合同，豁免与保障仍须持续。Correct. Under group creditor insurance, the financial institution is the policyholder and the borrower is the group member, with the death benefit equaling the outstanding loan balance; the exam cites CLHIA Guideline G7 providing a 20-day refund window upon receipt of the certificate (current public sources confirm scope while actual practice requires verifying the active contract); and a member on an approved disability waiver of premium retains continuous coverage and waiver protection even if the employer terminates the master contract.

  2. ✗ 错误。团体债权人保险的投保人是金融机构而非借款人 Paul；且已获批伤残保费豁免（waiver of premium）的会员不会因雇主终止主保单而立即失去保障。The policyholder of group creditor insurance is the lending institution rather than Paul; additionally, a member on an approved disability waiver of premium does not lose coverage immediately upon termination of the employer's master policy.

  3. ✗ 错误。团体债权人保险的身故金等于未偿债务余额（随贷款偿还而递减）；且 10 天冷静期是个人寿险的 Guideline G10，不要与债权人团体险的 G7 混用。Group creditor life death benefits equal the outstanding loan balance (declining as the loan is paid down) rather than remaining level; and the 10-day cooling-off period belongs to individual life insurance under Guideline G10, which must not be confused with creditor group insurance under Guideline G7.

  4. ✗ 错误。伤残保费豁免（waiver of premium）无需会员在计划终止时补缴以往豁免的保费，且其保障与豁免责任仍须依条款持续。A disability waiver of premium never requires the member to repay previously waived premiums when the plan terminates, and coverage and waiver liabilities must continue under the governing terms.
