① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 场景辨析题：给出多股东或合伙人企业的背景（如人数变化、年龄或健康差距大），考查应选择交叉持有（cross-purchase）、公司赎回（share redemption）还是混合法（hybrid method），以及谁是投保人、受益人和买方。Scenario distinction questions: Presenting multi-shareholder or partnership business backgrounds (such as changes in participant numbers, or substantial age and health disparities), testing whether to select a cross-purchase, corporate share redemption, or hybrid method, as well as identifying the policyowner, beneficiary, and purchasing party.
  • 税务与成本基础机制题：对比交叉持有与公司赎回对幸存股东调整后成本基础（ACB）和未来资本利得的影响，測試是否识破“持股比例结果相同代表税务后果等价”的误区。Taxation and cost basis mechanism questions: Comparing the impact of cross-purchase versus corporate share redemption on the surviving shareholders' adjusted cost basis (ACB) and future capital gains, testing whether candidates avoid the trap of assuming that identical shareholding percentages mean equivalent tax consequences.
  • CDA 与公司保单资金流向题：考查公司收到免税身故给付后，哪部分能计入资本红利账户（CDA）并免税分配给股东，以及定期险或永久险的调整后成本基础（ACB）对 CDA 进账额的扣减作用。CDA and corporate policy fund flow questions: Testing which portion of a tax-free life insurance death benefit received by a corporation can credit the capital dividend account (CDA) for tax-free distribution to shareholders, and how the adjusted cost basis (ACB) of term or permanent life insurance reduces the CDA credit.
  • 企业风险冲击与保额估算题：结合随时可催收贷款（demand loan）抽贷危机、关键人离世带来的技能损失（loss of skills）或视同处置资本利得税，考查关键人保险的三种保额估算思路与担保转让（collateral assignment）保费扣除条件。Business risk impact and coverage valuation questions: Combining demand loan recall crises, loss of skills upon a key person's death, or capital gains tax upon deemed disposition, testing the three valuation methods for key person insurance and the statutory conditions for deducting premiums under a collateral assignment.
  • 企业组织形式与双口径规则题：对比独资企业（sole proprietorship）、合伙企业（partnership）与公司（corporation）在业主身故时的资产处置与责任差异，以及终身资本利得豁免（LCGE）的适用资格与考试口径计算。Business structure and dual-benchmark rule questions: Comparing sole proprietorships, partnerships, and corporations regarding asset disposition and liability exposure upon the owner's death, alongside eligibility requirements and exam-benchmark calculations for the lifetime capital gains exemption (LCGE).

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 持股比例相同等于机制与税务相同Assuming identical ownership percentages mean identical mechanisms and tax results

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 交叉持有与公司赎回在股东身故后都能让幸存股东的持股比例上升至 100%，考生容易忽略买方主体不同，且公司赎回下幸存股东拿不到成本基础提升（step-up in basis）。Both cross-purchase and share redemption agreements result in the surviving shareholder owning 100% of the equity, tempting candidates to overlook that the purchasing party differs and that the surviving shareholder receives no cost basis step-up under corporate redemption.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现两名或多名股东讨论日后出售公司时的资本利得税，或询问总股数是否发生变化、谁是买方。The question stem features two or more shareholders discussing capital gains taxes on a future sale of the business, or asks whether total shares outstanding change and who the purchasing party is.

⚠ 免税身故金全额进 CDACrediting the full tax-free death benefit to the CDA

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 公司作为受益人收到的寿险身故给付确实全额免税进公司账户，考生常误以为这笔钱可以一分不少地全部计入资本红利账户（CDA）免税发给股东，忽略了必须扣除保单的调整后成本基础（ACB）。Because life insurance death benefits received by a corporate beneficiary enter the corporate bank account entirely tax-free, candidates frequently assume the entire amount can credit the capital dividend account (CDA) for tax-free payout to shareholders, forgetting to deduct the policy's adjusted cost basis (ACB).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出公司持有保单的身故给付金额与非零的调整后成本基础（ACB），问可免税派发的资本红利增加额。The question stem provides the death benefit of a corporate-owned policy alongside a non-zero ACB and asks for the tax-free capital dividend increase.

⚠ 交叉互保保额相等即负担公平Assuming equal coverage under criss-cross insurance creates equal premium burdens

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 股东之间互相为对方购买相同面额的保单看似对等，但保费按被保险人的年龄与健康状况定价，年轻健康的股东替年长体弱股东买单时保费负担会远高于对方。Shareholders buying equal face amounts on each other appear to share costs equally, but premiums reflect the life insured's age and health, meaning a younger, healthier shareholder buying coverage on an older, higher-risk partner bears a substantially higher premium burden.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干特意交代股东之间存在明显的年龄差距、吸烟习惯差异或健康状况不对等，并采用交叉互保（criss-cross insurance）。The question stem notes significant disparities in age, smoking status, or health conditions between shareholders using criss-cross life insurance.

⚠ 随时可催收贷款到期前安全Believing a demand loan is safe before maturity

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生习惯性地把商业贷款当成只要按时付息就不会被抽贷的固定期限贷款，忽略了随时可催收贷款（demand loan）即使借款人未违约，贷方也可按约满足通知条件后要求全额还款。Candidates mistakenly treat commercial loans as fixed-term debts that cannot be called as long as interest payments are current, overlooking that a lender holding a demand loan may require full repayment upon proper notice even in the absence of a default.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提到企业依赖核心人物经营或担保，且账上有一笔商业随时可催收贷款（demand loan），询问核心人物身故后的流动性风险。The question stem mentions a business relying on a core founder or guarantor with a commercial demand loan on its balance sheet, asking about liquidity risks upon that person's death.

⚠ 买卖协议只要定好买家和价格就完备Believing a buy-sell agreement is complete once buyer and valuation are set

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到买卖协议已经写明强制出售、固定价格公式和接盘人就以为万无一失，忽略了“保证资金（guaranteed funding）”才是避免协议沦为一纸空文的最关键条件。Seeing an agreement specifying mandatory sale, a clear valuation formula, and designated purchasers, candidates assume the arrangement is fully protected, overlooking that guaranteed funding is the most essential requirement to prevent the agreement from failing.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述股东已由律师起草协议约定好作价方式和互相收购义务，但尚未配置任何资金工具或寿险保单。The question stem describes shareholders executing a drafted agreement with clear pricing and buyout obligations, but without securing funding vehicles or life insurance policies.

企业身故三大可量化财务冲击Three quantifiable financial impacts of business deathloss of skills / creditor demands / capital gains tax关键人或业主身故对企业的可量化冲击主要为技能损失空窗期成本、随时可催收贷款（demand loan）被要求还款的流动性危机，以及依据 ITA s.70(5) 死亡前按公允市值视同处置引发的资本利得税。The quantifiable impacts of a key person or owner's death on a business primarily include interim costs from loss of skills, liquidity crises caused by lender demands on demand loans, and capital gains taxes triggered by the deemed disposition of capital property at fair market value immediately prior to death under ITA s.70(5).
企业组织形式与独立 ACB 差异Business structures and separate ACB distinctionssole proprietorship / partnership / corporation独资企业与业主为同一法律实体且企业本身无独立调整后成本基础（ACB）；合伙权益与公司股权均有独立 ACB 可买卖继承，且公司为独立法人、股东原则上不对公司债务负责（个人担保或董事法定连带责任除外）。A sole proprietorship is legally identical to its owner and possesses no separate adjusted cost basis (ACB); both partnership interests and corporate shares have a separate ACB that can be bought, sold, or inherited, and a corporation is an independent legal entity whose shareholders are generally not liable for corporate debts (absent personal guarantees or statutory director liabilities).
企业场景下的可保利益原则Insurable interest principle in business contextsinsurable interest核心判断标准是投保人是否会因被保险人身故遭受实际经济损失（涵盖企业对关键员工、债权人对债务人、合伙人或股东之间），保额须与预期经济损失合理对应并经财务核保。The core test of insurable interest is whether the policyowner will suffer a genuine financial loss upon the death of the life insured (including a business insuring key employees, creditors insuring debtors, and partners or shareholders insuring each other); coverage must reasonably correspond to the anticipated financial loss and satisfy financial underwriting.
终身资本利得豁免（LCGE）双口径与资格Lifetime capital gains exemption (LCGE) dual benchmarks and eligibilitylifetime capital gains exemption (LCGE)考试口径 2022 年合格 CCPC 股份豁免额为 $913,630（农渔业财产 $1,000,000），2026 现行上限为 $1,275,000（按 50% 计入率对应扣除上限 $637,500）；普通经营合伙权益通常不适用，但符合定义的家族农渔业合伙权益可能适用。Under the 2022 exam standard, the lifetime capital gains exemption (LCGE) limit for qualified small business corporation (QSBC) shares is $913,630 ($1,000,000 for qualified farm and fishing property), whereas the 2026 current limit is $1,275,000 (providing a cumulative deduction of $637,500 at the 50% inclusion rate); ordinary commercial partnership interests are ineligible, but qualifying family farm or fishing partnerships may qualify.
关键人保额三种估算方法Three key person insurance valuation methodskey person insurance valuation行业常用收入/薪酬倍数法、利润贡献法（毛利润贡献份额乘以替代期）、替代与债务保障法（猎头培训双薪成本加相关随时可催收贷款余额）互相印证，并非只取简单平均数。The industry commonly cross-references the income/salary multiple method, the profit contribution method (gross profit contribution share multiplied by replacement years), and the replacement and debt protection method (headhunter fees, training dual-salary costs, and outstanding balances of tied demand loans), rather than taking a simple mathematical average.
买卖协议完备的四个必要条件Four essential conditions of an enforceable buy-sell agreementguaranteed buyer / guaranteed value / mandatory sale / guaranteed funding一份能真正落地的买卖协议必须同时具备保证有买家、保证价格、强制出售和保证资金四项条件，其中保证资金（通常由寿险提供）是避免协议沦为空文的最关键一环。A fully workable buy-sell agreement must simultaneously satisfy four conditions: guaranteed buyer, guaranteed value, mandatory sale, and guaranteed funding; guaranteed funding (typically provided by life insurance) is the most critical element to prevent the agreement from becoming unenforceable.
交叉持有与公司赎回的核心差异Core distinctions between cross-purchase and share redemptioncross-purchase agreement vs. share redemption (entity purchase)交叉持有由股东互保并直接买股，总股数不变且幸存股东获得新股份的成本基础提升（step-up in basis），但多人时保单数量按排列组合膨胀且年龄健康差异会导致保费不均；公司赎回由公司持保并注销股份，总股数减少、每人一张保单易管理，但幸存股东无成本基础提升。Under a cross-purchase agreement, shareholders insure each other and purchase shares directly, keeping total shares constant and giving the survivor a cost basis step-up on purchased shares, but multiple owners cause policy counts to multiply and health/age disparities create unequal premium burdens; under share redemption, the corporation owns policies and cancels redeemed shares, reducing total share count and requiring only one policy per shareholder, but providing no cost basis step-up to survivors.
混合法（本票法）的双重优势Dual advantages of the hybrid method using promissory noteshybrid method (promissory note)公司统一持有保单并收取免税身故金记入 CDA，再向幸存股东派发免税资本红利，由幸存股东用该资金（常配合本票先行买股）偿还向遗产开出的本票，兼顾保单数量精简与幸存股东成本基础提升。Under the hybrid method, the corporation owns the policies and receives the tax-free death benefit to credit its CDA, then distributes a tax-free capital dividend to surviving shareholders, who use those funds to repay the promissory notes issued to the estate for purchasing the deceased shareholder's shares; this achieves both a streamlined policy count and a cost basis step-up for the survivors.
资本红利账户（CDA）计入规则与申报Capital dividend account (CDA) credit rules and reportingcapital dividend account (CDA) / Form T2054符合条件的私人公司收到免税寿险身故给付后，只有超出保单调整后成本基础（ACB）的部分能增加 CDA 余额，并通过提交 T2054 表（ITA s.83(2) 资本红利选择表）向加拿大税务居民股东免税分配。When a private corporation receives a tax-free life insurance death benefit, only the excess over the policy's adjusted cost basis (ACB) credits the capital dividend account (CDA), which can then be distributed tax-free to Canadian-resident shareholders by filing Form T2054 under ITA s.83(2).
保单担保转让与贷款保费扣除Collateral assignment of policies and loan premium deductibilitycollateral assignment企业将保单抵押给受限金融机构作贷款担保属于担保转让而非绝对转让，不触发处置；若贷款利息可扣且满足 ITA s.20(1)(e.2)，可在当年保费与纯保费成本（NCPI）较小者中按合理对应期间欠款比例扣除。Pledging a policy to a restricted financial institution as loan collateral constitutes a collateral assignment rather than an absolute transfer and does not trigger a disposition; where loan interest is deductible and ITA s.20(1)(e.2) requirements are met, a tax deduction is allowed for the portion of the lesser of annual premium and net cost of pure insurance (NCPI) that reasonably corresponds to the outstanding loan balance.

第 1 题Q1随时可催收贷款与担保转让扣除Demand loans and premium deductibility under collateral assignment★★陷阱：随时可催收贷款到期前安全

Apex Precision Ltd. relies heavily on its founder and chief metallurgist, Dr. Lin. The company carries an $800,000 commercial demand loan with a chartered bank, and the bank requires Apex to assign a $1,000,000 key person permanent life insurance policy on Dr. Lin's life as collateral. The loan interest is deductible for business purposes. Which statement correctly describes the lender's rights and the tax treatment of this arrangement?

Apex 精密制造有限公司高度依赖其创始人兼首席冶金学家 Lin 博士。该公司在一家特许银行有一笔 $800,000 的商业随时可催收贷款，银行要求 Apex 将一份保额 $1,000,000、以 Lin 博士为被保险人的关键人永久寿险保单转让作为抵押担保。该贷款利息符合商业扣除条件。下列哪项准确描述了贷方权利及该安排的税务处理？

  1. ✓ 正确。随时可催收贷款（demand loan）无须等到违约或固定到期日，贷方可依贷款条款及适用通知条件要求还款；将保单作担保转让（collateral assignment）给受限金融机构且满足 ITA s.20(1)(e.2) 条件时，可在当年保费与纯保费成本（net cost of pure insurance, NCPI）较小者中，按合理对应期间欠款（$800,000 / $1,000,000）的部分予以扣除。Correct. A demand loan does not require a borrower default or a fixed maturity date; the lender may demand repayment under the loan terms and applicable notice rules. When a policy is assigned as collateral security to a restricted financial institution and satisfies ITA s.20(1)(e.2), the business may deduct the portion of the lesser of the annual premium and the net cost of pure insurance (NCPI) that reasonably corresponds to the debt outstanding ($800,000 / $1,000,000).

  2. ✗ 错误。误中“随时可催收贷款到期前安全”陷阱：随时可催收贷款（demand loan）即使未违约也可按约及通知条件催收；此外，向银行作担保转让（collateral assignment）并非绝对转让，不触发保单处置。Falls into the 'believing a demand loan is safe before maturity' trap. A demand loan can be called under its terms upon proper notice even without default. Furthermore, assigning a policy to a bank as collateral is a collateral assignment rather than an absolute transfer and does not trigger a policy disposition.

  3. ✗ 错误。催收权仍须依贷款条款及适用通知条件执行；且保费扣除须取当年保费与 NCPI 的较小者并按贷款与保额的合理比例折算，不能仅凭有贷款就扣除全部保费。Demand rights must still be exercised under the contractual terms and applicable notice conditions. Moreover, premium deductions are capped at the lesser of annual premium and NCPI prorated to the loan balance, rather than allowing a 100% deduction simply because a business loan exists.

  4. ✗ 错误。扣除基数是当年保费与 NCPI 较小者（不能只取 NCPI），且比例是合理对应期间欠款占保额的比例，绝不能倒写成保额除以贷款额。The deduction base is the lesser of the annual premium and NCPI (not NCPI alone), and the deductible fraction represents the debt outstanding relative to the policy face amount, rather than inverting the ratio to face amount over loan balance.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Cedar Ridge Millwork Ltd. has a $450,000 commercial demand loan from a restricted financial institution to finance factory equipment. As a condition of the loan, the lender required the company to collaterally assign an existing $900,000 key person life insurance policy on its operations director. How does this collateral assignment affect the company's liquidity risk and tax deductions?

Cedar Ridge 木业有限公司从一家受限金融机构获得了一笔 $450,000 的商业随时可催收贷款用于购置工厂设备。作为贷款条件，贷方要求公司将其运营总监名下一份现有的 $900,000 关键人寿险保单进行担保转让。这项担保转让对公司的流动性风险和税务扣除有何影响？

  1. ✗ 错误。担保转让（collateral assignment）不是绝对转让，不触发处置；且随时可催收贷款（demand loan）不受设备折旧年限束缚。A collateral assignment is not an absolute assignment and does not trigger a disposition; furthermore, a commercial demand loan is not bound by equipment depreciation schedules.

  2. ✗ 错误。符合豁免条件（exempt）的保单内现金价值增长继续递延纳税，并非作为费用扣除项目。Cash surrender value growth inside an exempt life insurance policy continues to accumulate tax-deferred, rather than serving as a deductible expense item.

  3. ✓ 正确。随时可催收贷款（demand loan）在关键人身故时可能被贷方按约催收从而引发流动性危机；满足 ITA s.20(1)(e.2) 的担保转让（collateral assignment）可在保费与 NCPI 较小者中扣除合理对应贷款额（$450,000 / $900,000）的部分。Correct. A demand loan may be called by the lender under its terms upon a key person's death, triggering a corporate liquidity crisis. Under ITA s.20(1)(e.2), a collateral assignment allows the company to deduct the portion of the lesser of the premium and NCPI that reasonably corresponds to the $450,000 loan ($450,000 / $900,000).

  4. ✗ 错误。不能因为存在贷款就扣除全部保费，必须在保费与 NCPI 较小者中按贷款占保额的合理对应比例计算扣除。The company cannot deduct the entire annual premium simply because a loan exists; it must calculate the deduction from the lesser of premium and NCPI prorated to the loan balance relative to coverage.

  1. ✗ 错误。虽然企业对关键员工有可保利益（insurable interest），但保额不能凭空定得很高，必须与企业实际可能遭受的经济损失存在合理对应关系。Although a business has an insurable interest in a key employee, the face amount cannot be set arbitrarily high; it must reasonably correspond to the actual financial loss the firm would suffer.

  2. ✗ 错误。考试口径没有法定公式要求只取中间数或平均数，且核保员一定会要求提交财务报表等资料。The exam standard contains no statutory formula mandating a simple mathematical average, and underwriters will invariably require supporting financial statements.

  3. ✓ 正确。三种估算方法（收入倍数法、利润贡献法、替代与债务保障法）互相印证，代理人应解释各估计覆盖的损失、时间范围及可能重叠，选择有财务依据的申请额度；保司财务核保会要求提交财务资料佐证保额与預期经济损失的合理对应关系，凭空报出远超实际损失的保额会被打回或大幅调降。Correct. The three valuation methods (income multiple, profit contribution, and replacement/debt protection) cross-reference one another. The agent must explain the underlying losses and timeframes, selecting a financially justified amount; underwriters require financial verification and will decline or reduce coverage that vastly exceeds realistic economic loss.

  4. ✗ 错误。分成保单安排（split-dollar arrangement）是企业与员工共享面额与现金价值权益的福利机制，不能用来规避财务核保对保额合理性的审查。A split-dollar arrangement is an executive benefit mechanism dividing policy rights between employer and employee; it cannot be used to bypass financial underwriting scrutiny.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An agent is helping a construction company estimate the key person insurance amount needed for its lead project manager. The agent calculates the recruiter headhunting fee, the double-salary cost during the new hire's training period, and the outstanding balance of a commercial demand loan tied directly to the manager's personal guarantee. Which valuation approach is the agent applying, and who will own and be the beneficiary of this key person policy?

一名代理人正在协助一家建筑公司估算其首席项目经理所需的关键人保险保额。代理人将猎头招聘费、新人培训期间的双薪成本，以及与该经理个人担保直接挂钩的商业随时可催收贷款余额直接加总。代理人运用的是哪种估算思路？这份关键人保单应由谁持有并作为受益人？

  1. ✗ 错误。加总猎头培训成本与相关贷款余额属于替代与债务保障法，而非利润贡献法；且关键人保单由企业持有。Summing headhunter fees, training costs, and tied loan balances represents the replacement and debt protection method, not the profit contribution method; furthermore, key person policies are owned by the business.

  2. ✓ 正确。将招聘猎头费、新人培训期双薪成本以及个人担保的随时可催收贷款余额直接加总，属于替代与债务保障法；关键人保险（key person insurance）由企业持有并作受益人，免税身故金直接进入企业账户以弥补损失。Correct. Summing recruiter headhunting fees, training overlap salary costs, and personally guaranteed demand loan balances represents the replacement and debt protection method. Key person life insurance is owned by and payable to the corporation, providing tax-free proceeds to absorb these specific financial shocks.

  3. ✗ 错误。这不是按年薪倍数计算的收入倍数法，且由配偶持有并受益的是个人家庭寿险，而非企业关键人保险。This is not the income multiple method based on annual salary multiples; furthermore, coverage owned by and payable to a spouse is personal life insurance, not corporate key person insurance.

  4. ✗ 错误。分成保单（split-dollar）是福利安排而非关键人保额估算方法，关键人保险也不由其他普通员工交叉持有。Split-dollar is an executive benefit arrangement rather than a coverage valuation method, and key person insurance is not owned across a group of ordinary employees.

答案Answer：b

第 7 题Q7分成保单安排与小企业税率差Split-dollar policy arrangements and small business tax differentials★★陷阱：分成保单保费分摊与小企业税率误区

An engineering firm wants to provide its vice-president of operations with permanent life insurance protection as an executive benefit without bearing the entire cost of the policy. At the same time, the firm's owner is comparing the corporate small business tax rate against personal marginal tax rates for retaining earnings inside the corporation. Which statement accurately describes a split-dollar arrangement and the small business tax rate rules in BC?

一家工程公司希望为其运营副总裁提供一份永久寿险保障作为高管福利，但不想全额承担保单成本。与此同时，该公司业主正在对比公司小企业税率与个人边际税率在公司内部留存收益上的差异。下列哪项准确描述了分成保单安排（split-dollar arrangement）以及 BC 省小企业税率规则？

  1. ✓ 正确。分成保单安排（split-dollar arrangement）由企业与员工分享同一张永久险的不同权益（面额部分与账户价值部分），按公允市场价值分摊保费（依 CRA 行政观点）；符合小企业扣除（small business deduction）条件的 CCPC 首 $500,000 主动经营收入，2026 现行联邦税率 9% 加 BC 省税率 2% 合计为 11%（注：跨模块考试资料例题曾用约 15% 作教学示范，BC 现行符合条件的小企业合并税率为 11%）。Correct. Under a split-dollar arrangement, the corporation and employee share distinct interests in one permanent policy (death benefit vs. cash value) and split premiums based on fair market value under CRA administrative guidelines. For a qualifying BC CCPC, the small business tax rate on the first $500,000 of active business income is 11% (federal 9% plus BC 2%).

  2. ✗ 错误。分成保单是共享同一张永久险保单的不同权益（而非两张独立定期险），且保费必须按各部分权益的公允市场价值合理分摊，否则会引发税务福利问题。Split-dollar involves sharing rights in a single permanent policy rather than issuing two separate term policies, and premiums must be divided at fair market value to avoid taxable employee benefit issues.

  3. ✗ 错误。公司当期小企业税率较低只是经济动因之一，比较个人和公司持有寿险时还须计入个人提款税、业务限额、保单 ACB 及后续分配成本，不能单凭当期公司税率低就断定永久节税。A lower corporate small business tax rate is only one consideration; determining whether corporate ownership saves tax overall requires factoring in personal withdrawal taxes, business limits, ACB tracking, and CDA distribution costs.

  4. ✗ 错误。11%（联邦 9% + BC 2%）仅适用于符合小企业扣除条件的 CCPC 主动经营收入（一般业务限额 $500,000），并不适用于无限额的被动投资收入。The 11% combined small business rate applies strictly to active business income of an eligible CCPC up to the $500,000 business limit, not to unlimited passive investment income.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Horizon Tech Inc. sets up a split-dollar permanent life insurance arrangement with its chief software architect. How are the policy rights and premium payments structured under this type of arrangement?

Horizon 科技有限公司与其首席软件架构师建立了一份分成保单（split-dollar）永久寿险安排。在这种安排下，保单权益和保费支付是如何构建的？

  1. ✗ 错误。如果员工拥有全部权益而公司全额付保费，那属于应税员工福利而非分成保单（split-dollar）的公允价值分摊安排。If the employee holds 100% of the policy rights while the company pays the entire premium, the arrangement constitutes a taxable employee benefit rather than a bona fide split-dollar arrangement.

  2. ✓ 正确。分成保单安排（split-dollar arrangement）的核心机制是企业与核心员工分享同一张保单的不同权益（面额部分与账户价值部分分属不同权益人），并按公允市场价值分摊保费。Correct. The core mechanism of a split-dollar arrangement is that the corporation and key employee share distinct rights in a single permanent policy (such as dividing face amount and cash surrender value) and split the premium payments according to the fair market value of their respective interests.

  3. ✗ 错误。资本红利账户（CDA）是私人公司的税务账户，个人员工并不存在个人 CDA。The capital dividend account (CDA) is a corporate tax account belonging exclusively to private corporations; an individual employee has no personal CDA.

  4. ✗ 错误。分成保单安排并无专门的成文税法固定比例条款，而是依据 CRA 行政观点按各自所获权益的公允市场价值分摊保费。The Income Tax Act contains no statutory provision establishing a rigid 50/50 split; under CRA administrative policy, premiums must be allocated based on the fair market value of the respective interests.
