本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 生活方式与保费影响辨析题：给出申请人的吸烟状态、偶尔饮酒或高风险极限运动等背景，考查哪些因素只决定基础费率表分类（如吸烟人与非吸烟人标准费率），哪些因素才会真正触发加费或除外条款。Lifestyle and premium impact discrimination questions: The question stem provides an applicant's background such as smoking status, occasional alcohol use, or high-risk extreme sports, testing which factors merely determine standard rate classification (such as smoker versus non-smoker standard rates) and which factors actually trigger a rating or an exclusion clause.
  • 核保信息源与权限边界题：围绕代理人实地核保、医疗信息局（MIB）编码记录、主治医生报告（APS）及机动车记录（MVR）设问，考查谁承担费用、代理人能否替核保员定级，以及MIB记录能否单独作为直接拒保依据。Underwriting information sources and authority boundaries questions: Centering on agent field underwriting, Medical Information Bureau (MIB) coded records, Attending Physician's Statements (APS), and Motor Vehicle Records (MVR), testing who pays the costs, whether an agent can assign risk classifications on behalf of underwriters, and whether an MIB record alone can serve as direct grounds for decline.
  • 临时保险协议（TIA）与保单交付生效题：在递交申请到保单交付的空窗期设置健康变化或问卷答「是」的情景，测试TIA自动拒签条件、代理人签发裁量权，以及保单交付时「可保性无变化」的生效前提。Temporary Insurance Agreement (TIA) and policy delivery validity questions: Setting scenarios of health changes or answering 'yes' on questionnaires during the gap between application submission and policy delivery, testing automatic TIA disqualification conditions, agent issuance discretion, and the precondition that insurability must remain unchanged upon policy delivery.
  • 不实陈述与两年不可抗辩期题：对比申请人在投保单上的无意错误、故意欺诈性虚报与年龄误报，考查合同在被保险人生存期间有效满两年后的法律保护边界，以及欺诈为何不受不可抗辩期保护。Misrepresentation and two-year incontestability questions: Comparing innocent mistakes, deliberate fraudulent misrepresentations, and misstatements of age on the application, testing the legal boundaries of protection once the contract has been in force for two years during the lifetime of the insured, and why fraud is never protected by incontestability.
  • 双口径与承保通道对比题：考查加速核保与简化承保在是否保留风险分级上的核心差异，以及家族病史问询（合法）与索要基因检测结果（《基因非歧视法》禁止）之间的合规边界。Dual benchmarks and underwriting channel comparison questions: Testing the core difference between accelerated underwriting and simplified issue regarding whether risk classification tiers are retained, as well as the compliance boundary between asking about family medical history (permitted) and demanding genetic test results (prohibited by the Genetic Non-Discrimination Act).

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 混淆基础费率分类与评级加费Confusing standard rate classification with premium ratings

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到吸烟会影响保费，就误以为吸烟者的保单属于「加费保单（rated policy）」，忽略了吸烟人与非吸烟人本身是两套独立的标准基础费率表。Candidates see that smoking increases premiums and incorrectly assume that a smoker's policy is a rated policy, overlooking that smokers and non-smokers are simply two separate standard rate tables.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干列举吸烟、偶尔小酌与频繁高风险运动，问哪一项会导致保单被加费（rated）或附加除外条款。The question stem lists smoking, occasional social drinking, and frequent high-risk sports, asking which item will cause the policy to be rated or issued with an exclusion clause.

⚠ 误认MIB报告可直接作为拒保依据Assuming an MIB report can serve directly as grounds for decline

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生知道核保员会查询医疗信息局（MIB）数据库防欺诈，便顺着直觉选「保司凭MIB不良编码直接拒保」，漏掉了必须先独立核实事实的硬性规则。Knowing underwriters check the Medical Information Bureau (MIB) database to prevent fraud, candidates follow intuition and select direct decline based on adverse MIB codes, missing the strict rule requiring independent verification of facts first.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 选项中出现「仅凭MIB记录直接拒保或加费（solely based on the MIB report）」等绝对化表述。Options contain absolute statements such as declining or rating solely based on the MIB report.

⚠ 认为交了首期保费TIA就必然生效Believing paying the first premium guarantees TIA coverage

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把「提交完整申请并缴纳首期保费」当成临时保险协议（TIA）生效的充分条件，忽略了专用健康问卷任一答「是」即自动拒签及代理人的观察裁量权。Candidates treat submitting a complete application and paying the first premium as sufficient conditions for a Temporary Insurance Agreement (TIA), overlooking the strict rule that answering 'yes' to any TIA health question automatically disqualifies coverage, as well as the agent's observational discretion.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提到申请人已付首月保费，但在TIA健康问卷有一项答「是」，或代理人观察到申请人持续剧烈咳嗽等异常。The question stem mentions an applicant paying the first month's premium but answering 'yes' to an item on the TIA health questions, or the agent observing abnormalities like persistent severe coughing.

⚠ 把可保利益当成理赔时的持续要求Treating insurable interest as an ongoing requirement at claim time

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生误以为投保人与被保险人离婚或解除雇佣关系后，原有的寿险合同会因为失去可保利益而自动失效。Candidates mistakenly assume that when a policyowner and the life insured divorce or end an employment relationship, the original life insurance contract becomes void due to the loss of insurable interest.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「多年前投保时为夫妻或商业伙伴，现已离婚或离职，问原保单是否有效或能否追加新保单」。The question stem features a scenario where a couple or business partners bought insurance years ago and are now divorced or separated, asking whether the existing policy remains valid or if a new policy can be added.

⚠ 混淆无意错误与欺诈的2年不可抗辩规则Confusing innocent mistakes with fraud under the two-year incontestability rule

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生只记住了「2年不可抗辩期」，看到保单生效满2年就一律选保司必须理赔，或者反过来把无意漏报也当成随时可撤单。Candidates only remember the two-year incontestability period and choose mandatory claim payment whenever a policy has been in force for two years, or conversely treat innocent omissions as voidable at any time.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干明确区分申请人是「不知情/无意漏报（mistake）」还是「故意隐瞒病史或吸烟史（fraud）」，并给出保单已在被保险人生存期间满2年的时间节点。The question stem clearly distinguishes whether the applicant made an innocent mistake or committed deliberate fraud concealing medical or smoking history, providing a timeline where the policy has been in force for two years during the insured's lifetime.

第 1 题Q1基础费率分类与加费触发因素区分Distinction between standard rate classification and rating triggers★陷阱：混淆基础费率分类与评级加费

Marcus, age 38, is applying for a 20-year term life insurance policy. During the application interview, he discloses that he smokes half a pack of cigarettes daily, drinks a glass of beer on weekends, works as a high school history teacher, and participates in heli-skiing in remote backcountry mountains every winter. Which of these disclosures is most likely to result in his policy being issued as a rated policy or with an exclusion clause?

38岁的Marcus正在申请一份20年期定期寿险保单。在投保面谈中，他披露自己每天抽半包烟、周末喝一杯啤酒、职业是高中历史老师，并且每年冬天都会去偏远的后山区域参加直升机滑雪。这些披露中哪一项最可能导致他的保单被签发为加费保单（rated policy）或附加除外条款（exclusion clause）？

  1. ✗ 错误。典型陷阱：混淆基础费率分类与评级加费。吸烟状态决定申请人适用「吸烟人」这档独立的标准基础费率表（standard rate classification），并不等于保单本身是一张加费保单（rated policy）。Incorrect. Classic trap: confusing standard rate classification with premium ratings. Smoker status determines that the applicant falls under the independent smoker standard rate classification; it does not mean the policy itself is a rated policy.

  2. ✓ 正确。频繁的高风险爱好（hazardous avocation，如后山滑雪、滑翔伞、赛车）超出了常规基础费率分类框架，代表额外的死亡率风险，会将申请人推向加费（rated）或特定风险除外条款（exclusion clause）。Correct. Frequent participation in a hazardous avocation (such as backcountry skiing, paragliding, or motor racing) falls outside the standard rate classification framework and represents additional mortality risk, leading to a rated policy or a specific exclusion clause.

  3. ✗ 错误。只要不构成酗酒滥用或伴随其他健康问题，偶尔饮酒（occasional social drinking）按标准风险处理，不会单独触发加费。Incorrect. As long as it does not constitute alcohol abuse or accompany other health issues, occasional social drinking is treated as a standard risk and will not trigger a rating on its own.

  4. ✗ 错误。高中教师属于低风险常规职业，不会触发职业加费；年龄则只决定适用哪一档标准基础费率。Incorrect. A high school teacher is a low-risk standard occupation that does not trigger an occupational rating; age merely determines which standard rate table applies.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Four applicants are applying for standard term life insurance policies. Applicant 1 is a 45-year-old daily cigarette smoker with an office job; Applicant 2 is a 30-year-old non-smoker who drinks wine at family dinners; Applicant 3 is a 50-year-old non-smoker with a clean driving record; Applicant 4 is a 29-year-old non-smoker who accumulated four serious speeding and reckless driving convictions in the past two years. Which applicant is most likely to receive a rated policy?

四名申请人正在申请标准定期寿险保单。申请人1是45岁的坐班族，每天吸烟；申请人2是30岁非吸烟者，仅在家庭聚餐时喝红酒；申请人3是50岁非吸烟者，驾驶记录良好；申请人4是29岁非吸烟者，过去两年内累计有四次严重超速及鲁莽驾驶违规记录。哪一位申请人最可能收到加费保单（rated policy）？

  1. ✗ 错误。吸烟状态（smoker status）直接对应独立的吸烟人基础费率表，不属于在基础费率之上另行叠加的加费保单（rated policy）。Incorrect. Smoker status directly corresponds to an independent smoker standard rate table, rather than an extra rating superimposed on standard rates.

  2. ✗ 错误。家庭聚餐偶尔小酌不构成酒精滥用，不会触发加费（rated policy）。Incorrect. Occasional social drinking at family dinners does not constitute alcohol abuse and will not trigger a rated policy.

  3. ✗ 错误。年龄影响的是基础费率表定价，50岁健康非吸烟者依然适用标准费率（standard rate），而非加费档。Incorrect. Age affects pricing on the standard rate tables; a healthy 50-year-old non-smoker still qualifies for standard rates rather than a rated category.

  4. ✓ 正确。机动车记录（Motor Vehicle Record, MVR）反映3至10年的驾驶史，多次严重驾驶违规超出了常规标准费率框架，会导致保单被评级加费（rated）。Correct. A Motor Vehicle Record (MVR) reflects 3 to 10 years of driving history; multiple serious driving convictions exceed the standard rate framework and lead to a rated policy.

答案Answer：d

第 2 题Q2临时保险协议（TIA）生效与拒签条件Temporary Insurance Agreement (TIA) validity and disqualification criteria★★陷阱：认为交了首期保费TIA就必然生效

Hannah completes a life insurance application for $300,000 and gives her agent a cheque for the first month's premium so she can obtain immediate coverage under a Temporary Insurance Agreement (TIA). While reviewing the separate TIA health questions, Hannah answers 'yes' to a question asking whether she has been advised to undergo diagnostic cardiac testing within the past year. What must happen regarding the TIA?

Hannah完成了一份$300,000的寿险投保申请，并向代理人交付了首月保费支票，以便通过临时保险协议（TIA）获得即时保障。在回答单独的TIA健康问卷时，Hannah对「过去一年内是否曾被建议接受心脏诊断检查」这一问题回答了「是」。关于这份TIA应当如何处理？

  1. ✓ 正确。临时保险协议（Temporary Insurance Agreement, TIA / conditional receipt）要求单独填写专用健康问卷，只要其中任何一项回答「是」，即自动拒绝签发TIA，即使申请人已缴纳首期保费。Correct. A Temporary Insurance Agreement (TIA / conditional receipt) requires completing a separate health questionnaire; answering 'yes' to any question automatically disqualifies the applicant from receiving a TIA, even if the initial premium has been paid.

  2. ✗ 错误。典型陷阱：把「提交完整申请并缴纳至少一个月保费」当成TIA生效的充分条件，忽略了TIA健康问卷全部答「否」这一硬性门槛。Incorrect. Classic trap: treating submitting a complete application and paying at least one month of premium as sufficient conditions for a TIA, ignoring the strict requirement that all TIA health questions must be answered 'no'.

  3. ✗ 错误。TIA不存在「问卷答是就减半承保」的规则，任一健康问题答「是」即自动不予签发临时保障。Incorrect. There is no rule in a TIA granting half coverage when answering 'yes'; answering 'yes' to any health question automatically prevents temporary coverage from being issued.

  4. ✗ 错误。代理人的裁量权是当心存疑虑时主动拒绝签发TIA，绝无权力在申请人对TIA健康问题答「是」时越权强行签发。Incorrect. An agent's discretion permits refusing to issue a TIA when having doubts; the agent has no authority to override a 'yes' answer on TIA health questions and issue temporary coverage.

  2. ✗ 错误。典型陷阱：把可保利益（insurable interest）误解为必须持续存在到理赔时；实际上只要签发时存在夫妻可保利益，事后离婚不会令原合同失效。Incorrect. Classic trap: misunderstanding insurable interest as a continuous requirement up to the claim date; because spousal insurable interest existed at issue, a subsequent divorce does not void the existing contract.

  3. ✗ 错误。追加购买新保单是一次全新的投保行为，必须重新审核签发时的可保利益（或书面同意）、财务与健康条件。Incorrect. Purchasing an additional policy is a brand-new transaction requiring re-evaluation of insurable interest (or written consent), financial justification, and underwriting insurability at the time of the new application.

  4. ✗ 错误。原保单已正式生效，不需要也不可能通过每年重签临时保险协议（TIA）来维持效力。Incorrect. The existing policy is already fully in force and does not require nor permit annual renewal of a Temporary Insurance Agreement (TIA) to maintain its validity.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Six years ago, a software firm purchased a $1,000,000 key person life insurance policy on its lead architect, Martin, with the firm as policyowner and beneficiary. Last month, Martin resigned from the firm to retire completely. If the firm continues paying the premiums on the existing policy, what happens to the validity of that policy?

六年前，一家软件公司为其首席架构师Martin购买了一份$1,000,000的关键人物寿险保单，公司为保单所有人兼受益人。上个月，Martin从该公司辞职并彻底退休。如果该公司继续按时缴纳现有保单的保费，该保单的效力会如何？

  1. ✗ 错误。可保利益（insurable interest）只需在保单签发时存在，雇佣关系事后终止不会导致已生效的保单自动失效。Incorrect. Insurable interest is required only at policy issue; terminating the employment relationship does not cause an existing policy to become void.

  2. ✓ 正确。可保利益（insurable interest）只需在保单签发时（at the time of policy issue）存在，此后雇佣关系或婚姻关系的变化不溯及既往，已生效合同继续有效。Correct. Insurable interest is required only at the time of policy issue; subsequent changes in employment or marital relationships do not have retroactive effect, and the contract remains valid and in force.

  3. ✗ 错误。保单所有权不会因被保险人离职而自动转移，也不会自动变成简化承保（simplified issue）保单。Incorrect. Policy ownership does not automatically transfer when an employee resigns, nor does the contract convert into a simplified issue policy.

  4. ✗ 错误。被保险人6年后正常离职退休既不构成投保时的不实陈述，也不赋予保司事后撤单的权利。Incorrect. The employee retiring after six years does not constitute misrepresentation at application, nor does it grant the insurer the right to void the policy.
