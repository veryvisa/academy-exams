第 15 章 · 重疾与长期护理Chapter 15 · Critical Illness & Long-Term Care InsuranceCritical Illness & LTC

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 情景定性题：给一段临床突发症状或初步诊断（如短暂性脑缺血发作或早期病变），考你能否识别理赔取决于合同条款的严格定义，而非日常病名。Scenario qualification questions: Presenting acute clinical symptoms or an initial diagnosis (such as a transient ischemic attack or an early-stage lesion), testing whether you can recognize that claim eligibility depends strictly on contractual definitions rather than everyday disease names.
  • 时间闸门辨析题：在投保生效后不久确诊、或在确诊后短期内身故的场景中，测试合格期（qualification period）与存活期（survival period）的起算时点与法律后果。Time-gate discrimination questions: In scenarios where a diagnosis occurs shortly after policy inception or death occurs shortly after diagnosis, testing the starting points and legal consequences of the qualification period versus the survival period.
  • 给付计算与顺延题：给出重疾险的部分给付比例（如35%），或长期护理险的每日给付上限、实际每日开销与总保障池，考你算实际赔付额或资金池可用天数的顺延。Benefit calculation and carry-forward questions: Providing a partial benefit percentage for critical illness (such as 35%), or a daily maximum benefit, actual daily expenses, and total benefit pool for long-term care, testing your ability to calculate the actual claim payout or the extended days available from the carried-forward benefit pool.
  • 二次索赔与合同存续题：客户曾获全额重疾理赔或早期部分给付，数年后再患第二种重疾，考你原合同是否已终止、以及二次理赔附加险（second event rider）的适用条件。Second-event claim and policy continuation questions: A client previously received a full critical illness payout or an early partial benefit and suffers a second critical illness years later, testing whether the original policy terminated and the applicable conditions of a second event rider.
  • 双口径与产品机制题：考核长期护理险的六项日常生活活动能力（ADLs）或认知障碍触发判据、免赔期（0–90天考试口径）及居家报销与机构定额给付的通则。Dual-standard and product mechanics questions: Testing the two trigger criteria for long-term care insurance—the six activities of daily living (ADLs) or cognitive impairment—the elimination period (0 to 90 days under the exam rule), and standard rules for home care reimbursement versus facility care indemnity.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 合格期与存活期混淆Confusing qualification period with survival period

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 两者常见天数都是30天或90天，容易把保单刚生效时的防逆选择等待期与确诊后的存活要求记反，或误以为存活期内身故仍可赔付重疾险。Both commonly use 30 or 90 days, making it easy to reverse the post-issue anti-selection waiting period with the post-diagnosis survival requirement, or incorrectly assume that dying during the survival period still pays a critical illness benefit.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现政策刚生效/复效后不久查出症状，或出现确诊后第N天不幸身故的时间线。The question stem presents symptoms discovered shortly after policy issue or reinstatement, or outlines a timeline where the insured dies on day N following diagnosis.

⚠ 凭日常病名直接判赔Determining claims based solely on colloquial disease names

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到俗称小中风或某种癌症就默认符合四大核心条件（Big 4），忽略了合同对症状持续时间、永久性神经缺损或除外责任的精确定义。Candidates see colloquial terms like mini-stroke or a type of cancer and assume they satisfy the Big 4 conditions, overlooking strict contractual requirements regarding symptom duration, permanent neurological deficits, or specific policy exclusions.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调症状在短时间内完全消退、影像无永久损伤，或专门写出医生的具体临床诊断缩写。The question stem highlights symptoms resolving completely within a short time, imaging showing no permanent impairment, or specifies the physician's precise clinical diagnostic abbreviation.

⚠ 重疾险一概全额或终身持续Assuming critical illness always pays in full or continues indefinitely

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为所有受保病种都按100%保额赔付，或误以为全额理赔结案后保单仍像医疗险一样可以继续报销下一次大病。Candidates mistakenly assume that all covered conditions pay 100% of the face amount, or that after a full payout the policy continues like medical insurance to cover subsequent illnesses.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干明确提到某项条件按固定百分比（如35%）给付，或提到客户几年前已领取过100%全额重疾保险金且未加购附加险。The question stem specifies a condition paying a fixed percentage (such as 35%), or notes that the client received a 100% full critical illness payout years earlier without purchasing a rider.

⚠ 漏算LTC未用完日额的顺延Overlooking the carry-forward of unused daily LTC benefits

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直接把合同名义天数当作绝对上限，忽略了报销型长期护理险在每日实际花费低于每日上限时，差额仍留在总保障池中可延长给付天数。Candidates treat the nominal number of days as a hard ceiling, failing to recognize that under a reimbursement LTC policy, when actual daily expenses are below the daily maximum, the unused balance remains in the aggregate benefit pool to extend coverage days.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出每日给付上限、名义天数（或总保障上限）以及低于每日上限的实际每日照护账单。The question stem provides the daily maximum benefit, nominal days (or total maximum benefit pool), and an actual daily care invoice lower than the daily maximum.

⚠ 把现行市场细节当成考场通则Treating current market details as general exam rules

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把实际市场中部分产品的365/730天等待期、公费按收入收费或自愿行业基准当作全行业强制统一规则，偏离考试口径的基础设计模型。Candidates confuse specific market product features like 365 or 730-day waiting periods, public income-tested facility fees, or voluntary industry guidelines with mandatory industry-wide rules, deviating from the core exam framework.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干询问长期护理险典型免赔期设计区间、居家与机构给付模式分类，或询问不同保司的重疾定义是否强制统一。The question stem asks about the typical LTC elimination period range, the general classification of home versus facility care models, or whether critical illness definitions are legally mandated across insurers.

要点Point考卷用词Exam term规则 / 数字Rule / number
资产保护定位Asset protection purposecritical illness (CI) / long-term care (LTC) insurance重疾险与长期护理险保护的是被吃掉的资产而非替代工作收入，重疾险通常一次性定额给付且不限制款项用途。Critical illness (CI) and long-term care (LTC) insurance protect depleted assets rather than replacing lost earned income; CI typically pays a lump-sum benefit with no restrictions on how the funds are used.
合格期与存活期区分Distinction between qualification period and survival periodqualification period / survival (waiting) period合格期自保单生效或复效起算（常见30/90天）以防逆选择；存活期自确诊或手术后起算（常见30/90天），死于存活期结束前则不发生CI给付，活过存活期后、核准前身故仍照付。The qualification period runs from policy issue or reinstatement (commonly 30 or 90 days) to prevent anti-selection; the survival period runs from diagnosis or surgery (commonly 30 or 90 days), where dying before completing the survival period yields no CI benefit, but dying after satisfying the survival period and before claim approval still pays the benefit.
四大核心病种与分级The Big 4 core conditions and product tiers"Big 4" conditions考试口径的四大核心重疾为心脏病发作、中风、癌症、冠状动脉搭桥手术，市场产品常见3/4项基础款、10项款与20项以上综合款。Under exam rules, the Big 4 core critical conditions are heart attack, stroke, cancer, and coronary artery bypass surgery; market products commonly range from basic 3- or 4-condition plans to 10-condition plans and comprehensive plans covering 20 or more conditions.
行业重疾定义基准Industry critical illness benchmark definitionsCLHIA CI Benchmark Definitions行业重疾定义基准经历了2013、2018与2024三版演进，但各保险公司是否采用及采用哪一版完全自愿，理赔必须逐条核对具体合同原文。The CLHIA CI Benchmark Definitions have evolved through 2013, 2018, and 2024 versions, but adoption by insurance companies is entirely voluntary, meaning claim adjudication requires verifying the exact wording in the specific policy contract.
LTC触发双判据Dual benefit triggers for LTCactivities of daily living (ADLs) / cognitive impairment test长期护理险理赔通常要求六项日常生活活动能力（穿衣、洗澡、如厕、移位、进食、控制大小便）中至少2项不能独立完成，或满足需要持续监督的认知障碍判据。Long-term care insurance claims typically require the inability to perform at least 2 of the 6 activities of daily living (ADLs: dressing, bathing, toileting, transferring, eating, and continence) independently, or satisfying the cognitive impairment test requiring continual supervision.
LTC免赔期双口径Dual standard for LTC elimination periodselimination period (LTC)考试口径将长期护理险免赔期描述为0–90天可选（越短保费越高），而现行在售个人产品的等待期可达365或730天且须先满足保障生效日门槛。The exam describes the long-term care elimination period as an optional 0 to 90 days (shorter periods mean higher premiums), whereas individual products sold today can have waiting periods of 365 or 730 days and require satisfying a coverage effective date threshold first.
BC照护费用双轨制Dual framework for BC care costslong-term care (LTC) insurance考试口径仅列出未补贴私营机构月费旧区间（$995–$3,500），而2026现行BC公费机构按税后收入80%核定月费（标准最低$1,507.70、最高$4,142.60）。The exam only lists an outdated monthly fee range for unsubsidized private facilities ($995 to $3,500), whereas 2026 BC publicly subsidized facilities assess monthly fees at 80% of after-tax income (standard minimum $1,507.70, maximum $4,142.60).
CI全额结案与二次理赔Full CI claim termination and second event ridersecond event rider / partial benefit传统重疾险在支付100%全额理赔后合同终止，若未加购二次理赔附加险则后续重疾不再获赔；但部分给付（如按35%比例或早期疾病给付）未必导致主保障终止。A traditional critical illness policy terminates upon paying a 100% full claim, and subsequent conditions are not covered without a second event rider; however, partial benefits (such as a 35% payout or early-stage condition payout) do not necessarily terminate the base coverage.
LTC给付模式与余额顺延LTC payment models and balance carry-forwardindemnity model / reimbursement model / daily maximum benefit考试口径通则将居家照护归为凭单据报销、机构照护归为定额给付；按总资金池扣减的报销型合同在每日实际花费低于日上限时，未用完额度可顺延延长给付天数。General exam rules categorize home care under an expense reimbursement model and facility care under an indemnity model; in reimbursement policies deducting from an aggregate pool, when daily actual expenses are below the daily cap, the unused balance carries forward to extend the benefit period.
抗通胀与保费返还附加险Inflation protection and return of premium riderscost-of-living adjustment (COLA) / return of premium (ROP) riderLTC可通过COLA附加险（考试口径示例按年2%–3%上调上限）抵御通胀且理赔期自动豁免保费；CI可通过ROP附加险在身故、退保或满期无理赔时退还部分或全部保费。LTC policies can offset inflation through a COLA rider (with exam examples showing a 2% to 3% annual cap increase) and automatically waive premiums during claim periods; CI policies can refund partial or full premiums upon death, surrender, or maturity without claims via an ROP rider.

第 1 题Q1合格期与存活期及身故给付规则Qualification period, survival period, and death benefit rules★★陷阱：合格期与存活期混淆

Victor, age 49, purchased a $150,000 critical illness (CI) insurance policy with a 30-day qualification period and a 30-day survival period. Six months after the policy took effect, Victor suffered a severe stroke that fully satisfied the contract's medical definition. He survived for 34 days following the diagnosis, submitted his claim forms, and then passed away from complications two days before the insurer finished processing the payout. Who is entitled to the $150,000 CI benefit?

49 岁的 Victor 购买了一份保额 $150,000 的重大疾病（CI）保险保单，带有 30 天合格期和 30 天存活期。保单生效六个月后，Victor 突发严重中风，完全符合合同的医学定义。他在确诊后存活了 34 天，提交了理赔表格，但在保险公司完成赔付审批前两天因并发症去世。谁有权获得这 $150,000 的 CI 保险金？

  1. ✗ 混淆了「存活期结束前身故」与「存活期结束后、核准前身故」（合格期与存活期混淆陷阱）。只要被保险人活过了 30 天存活期（survival period），给付权利就已经确立，不会因审批期间身故而作废。Incorrect. Confuses dying before the survival period ends with dying after completing the survival period but prior to claim approval (qualification period vs. survival period trap). Once the insured survives the 30-day survival period, the entitlement to the benefit is established and is not voided if death occurs during administrative processing.

  2. ✓ 正确。保单已生效六个月（跨过了合格期 qualification period），且 Victor 在确诊后存活了 34 天（已满 30 天存活期 survival period）。根据制度设计，被保险人在存活期结束之后、理赔核准之前身故，重疾保险金仍照付给其遗产或受益人。Correct. The policy had been in force for six months (satisfying the qualification period), and Victor survived for 34 days following diagnosis (exceeding the 30-day survival period). Under policy design rules, when the insured dies after completing the survival period but before the claim is finalized, the critical illness benefit is still payable to the estate or named beneficiary.

  3. ✗ 凭病情严重程度自行篡改合同存活期天数（主观臆断陷阱）。存活期按实际疾病条款确认（题干已明确为 30 天），不能因病情严重自行把 30 天改为 90 天。Incorrect. Arbitrarily altering the contractual survival period based on clinical severity (subjective assumption trap). The survival period is determined strictly by the actual policy terms (specified as 30 days in the scenario) and cannot be extended to 90 days simply because the insured was in intensive care.

  4. ✗ 把合格期的起算点记成确诊日（合格期与存活期混淆陷阱）。合格期（qualification period）自保单生效或复效后起算以防逆选择，确诊后起算的是存活期（survival period）。Incorrect. Mistaking the date of diagnosis as the starting point of the qualification period (qualification period vs. survival period trap). The qualification period runs from policy issue or reinstatement to prevent anti-selection, whereas the period starting upon diagnosis is the survival period.

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Clara purchased a $200,000 critical illness (CI) policy with a 30-day qualification period and a 30-day survival period. Eight months after policy issue, she was diagnosed with a covered heart attack. Tragically, Clara passed away 19 days after the diagnosis. She did not purchase a return of premium on death (ROPD) rider. How will the insurer handle the $200,000 CI benefit claim?

Clara 购买了一份保额 $200,000 的重大疾病（CI）保单，带有 30 天合格期和 30 天存活期。保单签发八个月后，她被诊断出患有受保的心脏病发作。不幸的是，Clara 在确诊 19 天后去世。她没有加购身故保费返还（ROPD）附加险。保险公司将如何处理这 $200,000 的 CI 理赔申请？

  1. ✗ 只看合格期却漏看了存活期（合格期与存活期混淆陷阱）。虽然确诊发生在 30 天合格期（qualification period）之后，但确诊后还必须活满 30 天存活期（survival period）才能触发给付。Incorrect. Focusing solely on the qualification period while ignoring the survival period (qualification period vs. survival period trap). Although the heart attack was diagnosed well after the 30-day qualification period expired, the insured must also survive for the full 30-day survival period following diagnosis to trigger the benefit.

  2. ✗ 虚构了按存活天数比例折算的规则（规则虚构陷阱）。存活期是全有或全无的门槛条件，未活满 30 天不会按天数比例赔付。Incorrect. Inventing a rule that pro-rates benefits based on days survived (fabricated rule trap). The survival period is an all-or-nothing condition; failing to complete the required 30 days results in no pro-rated payout.

  3. ✗ 混淆了重疾险与人寿险的立意（险种混淆陷阱）。重疾险的立意是给活下来的人重建生活，死在存活期结束前不会变成人寿保险金。Incorrect. Confusing the core purposes of critical illness insurance and life insurance (line of insurance confusion trap). Critical illness insurance is designed to provide funds for a living insured to rebuild their life; dying before the survival period ends does not convert the coverage into life insurance death benefits.

  4. ✓ 正确。如果被保险人死在存活期（survival period）结束之前（19 天 < 30 天），则视为未满足存活期条件，这笔 $200,000 的重疾险给付根本不会发生（且未买身故退保费附加险）。Correct. If the insured dies before completing the required survival period (19 days < 30 days), the condition is not satisfied and no critical illness benefit of $200,000 is payable (and no return of premium on death rider was purchased).

答案Answer：d

第 2 题Q2LTC免赔期与给付模式双口径Dual standards for LTC elimination periods and benefit models★★陷阱：把现行市场细节当成考场通则

Hannah is preparing for her licensing exam and reviewing how long-term care (LTC) insurance policies are structured in the exam. According to the standard exam rules, what is the typical elimination period range for an LTC policy, and how are home care versus facility care benefits generally categorized?

Hannah 正在备考牌照考试，复习考试课程中长期护理（LTC）保险保单的结构设计。根据标准考试口径，LTC 保单典型的免赔期（等待期）区间是多少？居家照护与机构照护的给付模式通常如何分类？

  1. ✓ 正确。按考试口径，LTC 免赔期（elimination period）的设计区间为 0–90 天可选（越短保费越高），给付形态通则将居家照护归为报销模式（reimbursement model）、机构照护归为定额给付模式（indemnity model）。注意：已核实的某款现行个人 LTC 产品等待期为 365 或 730 天、且居家与机构均按收入型定额给付，实务须逐份核合同，但考场按考试口径作答。Correct. Under standard exam rules, the typical LTC elimination period range is 0 to 90 days (shorter periods result in higher premiums), and benefit models generally categorize home care under an expense reimbursement model and facility care under an indemnity model. Note: a verified current individual LTC product features waiting periods of 365 or 730 days with income-style indemnity for both settings; practical work requires verifying each contract, but exam questions follow the standard 0 to 90 day exam model.

  2. ✗ 将现行个别产品的等待期与错误给付模式混作考场通则（把现行市场细节当成考场通则陷阱）。365/730 天及 65 岁生效门槛是现行特定在售产品的参数，而非考试口径的 0–90 天通则。Incorrect. Conflating specific current product waiting periods and incorrect payout models with general exam rules (treating market details as exam rules trap). The 365 or 730-day waiting periods and age 65 effective threshold are parameters of a specific current market product, not the standard 0 to 90-day exam framework.

  3. ✗ 把重疾险的双时间闸门和一次性给付套用到长期护理险上（跨险种混淆陷阱）。合格期、存活期与一次性定额给付属于重大疾病保险（CI），而非长期护理保险（LTC）。Incorrect. Applying critical illness time gates and lump-sum payouts to long-term care insurance (cross-product confusion trap). Qualification periods, survival periods, and one-time lump-sum settlements belong to critical illness insurance, not long-term care insurance.

The CLHIA explicitly states that adoption of its benchmark definitions by member companies is entirely voluntary, not a statutory mandate.

  2. ✗ 误以为四大核心病种的定义在全行业完全统一（以偏概全陷阱）。即便同属 Big 4（如心脏病发作），不同保司或不同时期签发的合同在具体指标和措辞上仍可能不同，必须逐条核对合同。Incorrect. Assuming that Big 4 definitions are completely identical across all insurers (overgeneralization trap). Even for Big 4 conditions like heart attack, specific diagnostic criteria and contractual wording can vary between carriers and policy vintages, requiring verification of each specific contract.

  3. ✓ 正确。CLHIA 重疾定义基准（CLHIA CI Benchmark Definitions，现行 2024 Final 版，此前有 2018 与 2013 版）由各保险公司自愿采用（voluntary），并非强制统一标准；因此不能假设不同保司的「心脏病发作」定义完全相同，必须逐条核对具体合同原文。Correct. The CLHIA CI Benchmark Definitions (current 2024 Final version, preceded by 2018 and 2013 versions) are adopted voluntarily by insurers rather than being mandatory statutory standards; therefore, an advisor cannot assume that heart attack definitions are identical across carriers, and must verify the exact wording in the client's policy contract.

  4. ✗ 张冠李戴了 CLHIA 基准的适用险种（术语归属陷阱）。CLHIA CI Benchmark Definitions 专门针对重大疾病保险（critical illness insurance）制定，而非团体伤残险。Incorrect. Misattributing CLHIA benchmarks to an unrelated insurance product (terminology misattribution trap). The CLHIA CI Benchmark Definitions were developed specifically for critical illness insurance, not group disability insurance.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An advisor tells a client, "Every basic critical illness policy on the market must cover all four of the Big 4 conditions, and every insurer's contract uses the exact same definition threshold." Why is the advisor's statement inaccurate?

一位顾问告诉客户：「市场上每一份基础重大疾病保单都必须涵盖全部四个『四大核心病种（Big 4）』，而且每家保险公司的合同都使用完全相同的定义门槛。」为什么这位顾问的说法是不准确的？

  1. ✗ 混淆了 Big 4 与日常生活活动能力 ADLs（术语混淆陷阱）。Big 4 是重疾险的四大常见核心病种（心脏病发作、中风、癌症、冠状动脉搭桥手术），ADL 则是长期护理险的六项生活自理能力。Incorrect. Confusing the Big 4 with activities of daily living (terminology confusion trap). The Big 4 refers to the four core critical illness conditions (heart attack, stroke, cancer, and coronary artery bypass surgery), whereas ADLs are the six self-care activities used in long-term care insurance.

  2. ✓ 正确。「Big 4 conditions」只是考试口径用于认识常见重疾的组合，并非每份保单的法定最低覆盖要求（现售基础款可能只保 3 项或更少，甚至单保癌症或心脏）；同时 CLHIA 定义基准属自愿采用，各合同定义原文需逐一核对。Correct. The Big 4 conditions represent the core illness combination used in the exam to teach common critical illnesses rather than a statutory minimum coverage mandate (basic market plans may cover only 3 conditions or even a single condition like cancer or heart disease); furthermore, CLHIA benchmark definitions are voluntary, meaning exact contractual definitions vary by insurer.

  3. ✗ 违背了重疾险产品分档的事实（市场结构陷阱）。考试口径与现行市场均并存 3/4 项基础款、10 项款与 20 项以上综合款（comprehensive 20+）。Incorrect. Contradicting the established market tiers of critical illness insurance (market structure trap). Both exam standards and current market reality offer basic 3- or 4-condition plans, 10-condition plans, and comprehensive 20+ condition policies.

  4. ✗ 误以为临床医生可以改写保单定义（理赔权限陷阱）。医生负责做出医学诊断并提供检查证据，但是否符合理赔标准严格取决于保险合同预先写明的定义条款。Incorrect. Assuming that attending physicians determine policy definitions (claim authority trap). Physicians provide medical diagnoses and clinical evidence, but whether a condition qualifies for benefits is governed strictly by the pre-established contractual definitions in the insurance policy.

第 7 题Q7LTC日限额与未用余额顺延计算LTC daily maximum benefits and carry-forward calculation of unused balances★★★陷阱：漏算LTC未用完日额的顺延

Owen holds a reimbursement-style long-term care (LTC) policy with a daily maximum benefit of $150 and a nominal benefit period of 365 days, creating a total maximum benefit pool of $54,750 ($150 × 365). After satisfying the elimination period, Owen receives eligible home care that costs exactly $100 per day. Under the exam's benefit pool carry-forward rule, how much is reimbursed per day and how many days can Owen's total benefit pool last at this spending rate?

Owen 持有一份报销型长期护理（LTC）保单，每日给付上限为 $150，名义给付期为 365 天，形成总保障额上限为 $54,750（$150 × 365）的资金池。在满足免赔期后，Owen 接受合资格的居家照护，每天实际花费恰好为 $100。根据考试口径的资金池顺延规则，每天报销多少金额？按此花费速度，Owen 的总保障资金池可以持续多少天？

  1. ✗ 混淆了报销模式与定额给付模式（模式混淆陷阱）。Owen 的保单在居家阶段采用报销模式（reimbursement model），按合资格实际支出（$100/天）且不超过日上限报销，不会超额发放 $150。Incorrect. Confusing an expense reimbursement model with a fixed indemnity model (model confusion trap). Owen's policy uses a reimbursement model for home care, paying eligible actual daily expenses ($100 per day) up to the daily cap, rather than paying an unspent $150 daily indemnity.

  2. ✗ 掉入「漏算 LTC 未用完日额的顺延」陷阱。在按报销额扣减总资金池的考试模型中，每天未用完的 $50 差额不会作废，而是留在总保障额中顺延至后续天数使用。Incorrect. Falling into the trap of overlooking the carry-forward of unused daily LTC benefits. Under the exam model where reimbursements are deducted from an aggregate benefit pool, the unused $50 daily balance is not forfeited, but remains in the pool to extend coverage to subsequent days.

  3. ✗ 把实际账单当成了自付额并用差额报销（差额误算陷阱）。每日花费 $100 低于 $150 的每日给付上限（daily maximum benefit），因此 $100 可获全额报销，而非只报销差额 $50。Incorrect. Treating the actual invoice as a deductible and reimbursing only the difference (differential miscalculation trap). Because the actual daily cost of $100 is below the $150 daily maximum benefit, the full $100 is reimbursed, rather than paying the $50 difference.

  4. ✓ 正确。在报销型（reimbursement model）且受总保障额上限约束的考试模型中，当每日实际合资格花费（$100）低于每日给付上限（daily maximum benefit, $150）时，每天按实际花费报销 $100，未用满的 $50 顺延保留在 $54,750 的总保障池中，因此可用天数延长至 $54,750 ÷ $100 = 547.5 天。Correct. Under the exam's reimbursement model governed by an aggregate maximum benefit pool, when eligible actual daily expenses ($100) are below the daily maximum benefit ($150), the insurer reimburses $100 per day, and the unused $50 daily balance carries forward within the $54,750 total pool, extending coverage up to $54,750 / $100 = 547.5 days.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Vera has a long-term care (LTC) policy with a daily maximum benefit of $200 and a total maximum benefit pool of $146,000 (equivalent to 730 days at the maximum rate). After completing her elimination period, she incurs $160 per day in eligible home care expenses. Under the exam's carry-forward rule for unused daily benefits, how many days of care will her $146,000 total benefit pool fund if her daily cost remains $160?

Vera 持有一份长期护理（LTC）保单，每日给付上限为 $200，总保障额上限资金池为 $146,000（相当于按最高日额给付 730 天）。在完成免赔期后，她每天产生 $160 的合资格居家照护费用。根据考试口径关于未用完日限额的顺延规则，如果她的每日费用保持在 $160，她的 $146,000 总保障资金池可以支持多少天的照护？

  1. ✗ 掉入「漏算 LTC 未用完日额的顺延」陷阱。直接套用名义天数 730 天，忽略了每日少花的 $40 会留在总保障池中延长给付期。Incorrect. Falling into the trap of overlooking the carry-forward of unused daily LTC benefits. Simply applying the nominal 730-day period ignores the fact that spending $40 less each day keeps funds within the total pool to extend the benefit period.

  2. ✓ 正确。由于每日实际合资格开销（$160）低于每日给付上限（daily maximum benefit, $200），未用完的额度可顺延至后续天数使用，直至总保障额 $146,000 用尽，因此总可用天数为 $146,000 ÷ $160 = 912.5 天。Correct. Because eligible actual daily expenses ($160) are below the daily maximum benefit ($200), the unused balance carries forward to fund subsequent days until the total pool of $146,000 is exhausted, yielding a total benefit period of $146,000 / $160 = 912.5 days.

  3. ✗ 误用日限额与实际账单的差额（$40）做除数（差额误算陷阱）。保险公司每天报销的是实际花费 $160，应以 $146,000 除以 $160 而非除以 $40。Incorrect. Erroneously using the $40 difference between the daily cap and the invoice as the divisor (differential miscalculation trap). The insurer reimburses the actual daily expense of $160; therefore, $146,000 must be divided by $160, not by $40.

  4. ✗ 捏造了居家照护扣减总保额 20% 的规则（规则虚构陷阱）。每日花费仅为日上限的 80%（$160/$200）会让资金池使用时间变长（除以 0.8），而不是把总天数乘以 0.8。Incorrect. Fabricating a rule that home care reduces the total benefit pool by 20% (fabricated rule trap). Spending only 80% of the daily maximum ($160 / $200) allows the benefit pool to last longer (dividing days by 0.8), rather than multiplying the nominal days by 0.8.
