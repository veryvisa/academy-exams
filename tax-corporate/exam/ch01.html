本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 场景定性题：给出一段工作安排（谁定工时、谁备工具、谁担亏损、是否嵌入日常运营），考你用四因素测试判定是雇员还是自雇，或识破用公司外壳规避雇佣关系的个人服务业务（PSB）。Scenario classification questions: you are given a working arrangement (who sets the hours, who supplies the tools, who bears the loss, whether the worker is embedded in daily operations) and must use the four-factor test to decide employee or self-employed, or spot a personal services business (PSB) that uses a corporate shell to avoid an employment relationship.
  • 表格分流与会计方法题：给出具体职业（如IT顾问、建筑师 vs 网约车司机）或行业（农渔业、佣金代理 vs 普通服务），考你填 T2125 的 Part 3A 还是 Part 3B、期末在制品（WIP）怎么入账、能不能选现金制及如何改回权责发生制。Form routing and accounting method questions: given a specific occupation (such as an IT consultant, an architect vs. a ride-share driver) or industry (farming and fishing, commission agents vs. ordinary services), you decide whether to complete Part 3A or Part 3B of T2125, how year-end work in progress (WIP) is recognized, whether the cash method may be chosen and how to switch back to the accrual method.
  • 费用扣除闸门与计算题：把探索期开办费、资本性改良、超额不合理开支、餐饮娱乐费混在一起，考你用 s.18(1)(a) 目的测试、s.18(1)(b) 资本排除、s.67 合理性测试和 s.67.1 五折规则剔除不可扣部分。Expense deduction gate and calculation questions: exploratory start-up costs, capital improvements, excessive or unreasonable amounts and meals and entertainment are mixed together, and you must strip out the non-deductible parts using the s.18(1)(a) purpose test, the s.18(1)(b) capital exclusion, the s.67 reasonableness test and the s.67.1 50% rule.
  • CPP双份分拆与EI自愿参保题：给出自雇净利润（如 $52,000），考你扣除 $3,500 基本免税额后按 11.9% 算基础 CPP，并在 Schedule 8 上分拆到 Line 31000（抵免）与 Line 22200（扣除），以及自雇 EI 的 12 个月等待期、60 天退出窗口和无雇主 1.4 倍份额。Double CPP split and voluntary EI questions: given net self-employed income (such as $52,000), you deduct the $3,500 basic exemption and apply 11.9% to get base CPP, then split it on Schedule 8 between Line 31000 (credit) and Line 22200 (deduction); also self-employed EI: the 12-month waiting period, the 60-day opt-out window and no 1.4 employer multiple.
  • 时间节点与亏损结转题：考你区分自雇 6 月 15 日申报延期与 4 月 30 日缴税计息日、$3,000 净欠税分期预缴门槛，以及非资本亏损先抵当年其他收入再回抵 3 年、结转 20 年的顺序。Deadline and loss carryover questions: you distinguish the June 15 self-employed filing extension from the April 30 date when payment is due and interest starts, the $3,000 net-owing instalment threshold, and the order for non-capital losses: first against other income of the year, then carried back 3 years or forward 20 years.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 合同写承包就算自雇A contract that says contractor means self-employed

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉以为双方自愿签了独立承包商协议、不代扣税款就能锁定自雇身份，忽略了 CRA 只看经济实质。Intuition says that if both sides voluntarily signed an independent contractor agreement and no tax is withheld, self-employed status is locked in. This ignores that the CRA looks only at economic substance.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调 signed an independent contractor agreement，但同时出现固定排班、对方提供设备、按固定时薪结算且无亏损风险。The stem stresses that the worker signed an independent contractor agreement, but also shows fixed shifts, equipment supplied by the other party, and a fixed hourly rate with no risk of loss.

⚠ 6月15日才算欠税晚交Treating June 15 as the payment deadline

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 记住自雇报税截止日延到 6 月 15 日，就顺手把缴税截止日也当成 6 月 15 日。You remember that the self-employed filing deadline is extended to June 15 and assume the payment deadline is June 15 too.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时问 filing deadline 和 balance-due date，或问 5 月份交表并付清欠税有没有利息（interest）和迟报罚款（late-filing penalty）。The stem asks about both the filing deadline and the balance-due date, or asks whether filing in May and paying the balance then triggers interest and a late-filing penalty.

⚠ 开业前考察费事后补扣Deducting pre-business investigation costs after the fact

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为只要生意后来真的开起来了，前期去考察市场、买参考书的花费都是为了生意，理应能扣。You assume that once the business actually opens, the earlier costs of researching the market and buying reference books were all for the business and should be deductible.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干把支出按时间拆成两段：一段在 deciding whether to start a business 阶段，另一段在签订首份合同或租下场地之后。The stem splits spending by time into two periods: one while deciding whether to start a business, the other after the first contract is signed or premises are rented.

⚠ 专业人士年底排除WIPProfessionals excluding year-end WIP

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 被旧考试资料或 2017 年前的历史规则误导，以为会计师、律师、医生还能选按开票入账（billed-basis）把未开票在制品排除在外。You are misled by old exam material or the pre-2017 rules and think accountants, lawyers and doctors can still choose billed-basis accounting to leave unbilled work in progress out of income.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 designated professional（会计师、律师、医生等）在年底有 unbilled work-in-progress (WIP)，问当年如何确认收入。The stem has a designated professional (accountant, lawyer, doctor, etc.) with unbilled work-in-progress (WIP) at year end and asks how income is recognized for the year.

⚠ CPP全额当扣除或EI交2.4倍Whole CPP as a deduction, or EI at 2.4 times

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 知道自雇交双份 CPP，就以为两份都走扣除或都走抵免；或者把自雇交双份的逻辑套到自愿 EI 上，误算雇主 1.4 倍。You know the self-employed pay both halves of CPP and assume both halves go through the deduction or both through the credit; or you apply the pay-both-halves logic to voluntary EI and wrongly add the 1.4 employer multiple.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问 Schedule 8 算出的自雇 CPP 在 T1 哪两行申报，或问自愿加入 EI special benefits 的保费率与雇员有何不同。The stem asks on which two lines of the T1 the self-employed CPP calculated on Schedule 8 is reported, or how the premium rate for voluntary EI special benefits differs from an employee's.

Budget 2026 (announced 2026-11-05) proposes aligning the filing and payment deadlines at May 15 from the 2027 tax year and raising the instalment threshold to $7,500 from 2028, but this is not yet law; for the 2026 tax year the rules remain June 15 filing, April 30 payment and the $3,000 threshold.

It cannot be mixed with a net capital loss.

Under current rules all WIP is included in income for the year at the lower of cost and fair market value.

第 1 题Q1四因素测试判定雇员与自雇Four-factor test for employee vs. self-employed status★★陷阱：合同写承包就算自雇

Marcus signs an agreement titled 'Independent Contractor Agreement' with a logistics firm. The firm sets his daily shift from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., provides the delivery van and pays for all fuel and repairs, prohibits him from hiring helpers or working for other clients, and pays him a fixed hourly wage regardless of delivery volume. If the CRA reviews this arrangement, what is the most likely outcome?

Marcus 与一家物流公司签了一份题为「独立承包商协议」的合同。公司规定他每天早上 8 点到下午 4 点排班，提供送货厢式车并承担全部油费与维修费，禁止他雇助手或为其他客户工作，且不论送货量多少都按固定时薪付酬。如果 CRA 审查这段关系，最可能的结果是什么？

  1. ✓ 正确。CRA 按四因素测试（four-factor test / Wiebe Door test：控制、工具、利润机会与亏损风险、整合程度）看经济实质而非合同标题；一旦重新定性为雇佣关系，付款方须补缴未代扣的 CPP、EI 与所得税并承担罚款与利息。Correct. The CRA applies the four-factor test (Wiebe Door test: control, tools, chance of profit and risk of loss, integration) to economic substance, not to the contract title. Once the relationship is recharacterized as employment, the payer must pay the unwithheld CPP, EI and income tax and bears penalties and interest.

  2. ✗ 错误。掉入「合同写承包就算自雇」的陷阱：CRA 不看合同怎么写或双方怎么称呼，只看四因素反映的经济实质。Wrong. This falls into the trap "a contract that says contractor means self-employed": the CRA does not look at how the contract is worded or what the parties call it, only at the economic substance shown by the four factors.

  3. ✗ 错误。在这套判定下身份不会按税种拆开优待；四项因素全部指向雇员（公司控制工时、提供工具、承担风险、高度整合），整体都会被定性为雇佣。Wrong. Under this test status is not split by tax type for favorable treatment; all four factors point to employee (the firm controls hours, supplies tools, bears the risk, and the worker is fully integrated), so the whole relationship is classified as employment.

  4. ✗ 错误。判错身份的代价不对称且主要落在付款方（雇主）头上，付款方要补缴未代扣代缴的 CPP、EI 与所得税及罚息，而非让雇员去补雇主份额。Wrong. The cost of misclassification is asymmetric and falls mainly on the payer (the employer), who must pay the unremitted CPP, EI and income tax plus penalties and interest; the employee is not made to pay the employer share.

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Elena provides home daycare services in her own residence. She sets her own operating hours and fee schedule, purchases all toys and food at her own expense, bears the financial loss if enrollment drops, and serves six different families independently. How will the CRA most likely classify Elena under RC4110?

Elena 在自己家里提供家庭日托服务。她自行决定作息时间与收费标准，自费购买所有玩具和餐饮食材，若招不满孩子则自己承担亏损风险，并独立为六个不同家庭提供服务。根据 RC4110 指南，CRA 最可能如何认定 Elena 的身份？

  1. ✗ 错误。不能套用职业名称一刀切；CRA 明确指出在自己家里提供日托、自行决定收费与作息、自担场地耗材成本的人通常属于自雇。Wrong. You cannot apply a blanket rule by job title; the CRA states that a person who provides daycare in her own home, sets her own fees and hours and bears her own premises and supply costs is generally self-employed.

  2. ✗ 错误。个人服务业务（personal service business）针对的是借公司外壳提供实质雇佣服务的人，Elena 是以个人身份独立自雇经营。Wrong. A personal services business targets people who provide what is in substance employment services through a corporate shell; Elena runs her business independently as an individual.

  3. ✓ 正确。对照四因素测试（four-factor test），Elena 自主掌握控制权、自备场地与耗材工具、自担盈亏风险且独立于单一付款方，属于典型的自雇者（self-employed）。Correct. Under the four-factor test, Elena controls her own work, supplies her own premises and supplies, bears the risk of profit and loss, and is independent of any single payer, so she is a classic self-employed person.

  4. ✗ 错误。自雇还是雇佣取决于四因素的经济实质，不需要也不靠双方签署放弃代扣义务的书面声明来决定。Wrong. Self-employed vs. employee is decided by the economic substance of the four factors; it does not depend on, and is not determined by, a written statement in which the parties waive withholding obligations.

第 2 题Q2权责发生制默认与现金制例外Accrual method by default and cash method exceptions★★陷阱：任意小生意都可选现金制

Clara operates a sole proprietorship providing residential landscaping services (she is not a farmer, fisher, or self-employed commission sales agent). In December 2026, she completes a $4,000 landscaping job and invoices the client, who pays her on January 18, 2027. She also receives a $600 repair bill in December 2026 for her lawn equipment and pays it in January 2027. How must Clara report these items for the 2026 tax year?

Clara 经营一家独资园林绿化服务部（她不是农民、渔民或按佣金计酬的销售代理人）。2026 年 12 月，她完成了一单 $4,000 的绿化工程并向客户开具账单，客户于 2027 年 1 月 18 日付款。她还在 2026 年 12 月收到一张 $600 的草坪设备维修账单，并于 2027 年 1 月付款。Clara 在 2026 税年必须如何申报这两项收支？

  1. ✗ 错误。现金制（cash method）只开放给农民、渔民以及按佣金计酬的销售代理人三类人，普通服务业独资经营者无权选择现金制。Wrong. The cash method is open only to farmers, fishers and sales agents paid on commission; an ordinary service sole proprietor has no right to choose it.

  2. ✓ 正确。绝大多数自雇者默认必须使用权责发生制（accrual method）：收入在赚得的 2026 财年入账，支出在归属的 2026 财年扣除，与 2027 年实际收付款的时间点无关。Correct. Most self-employed people must use the accrual method by default: revenue is recorded in the 2026 fiscal year in which it is earned and expenses are deducted in the fiscal year to which they belong, regardless of when cash actually moves in 2027.

  3. ✗ 错误。权责发生制对收入和支出是一致适用的，2026 年已经发生并归属当年的 $600 维修支出在 2026 年即可扣除，不必等付现。Wrong. The accrual method applies consistently to revenue and expenses; a $600 repair that was incurred and belongs to 2026 is deductible in 2026 without waiting for payment.

  4. ✗ 错误。净资本亏损可回抵 3 年、无限期结转（不会在 3 年后过期），它不能用的原因是抵扣对象仅限于资本利得。Wrong. A net capital loss can be carried back 3 years and carried forward indefinitely (it does not expire after 3 years); the reason it cannot be used here is that it can offset only capital gains.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

第 7 题Q7专业收入Part 3B与期末WIP规则Professional income Part 3B and year-end WIP rules★★★陷阱：专业人士年底排除WIP

Dr. Lin is a self-employed dentist in Vancouver. At her December 31, 2026 fiscal year-end, she has completed stage-one dental procedures for several patients that have not yet been billed; the cost of her time and materials for this work-in-progress (WIP) is $9,000, and its fair market value is $14,000. Her bookkeeper finds a 2016 tax return showing a section 34 election to exclude WIP. How must Dr. Lin report her income and WIP on her 2026 Form T2125?

Lin 医生是温哥华一名自雇牙医。在 2026 年 12 月 31 日财年末，她已为数名患者完成第一阶段牙科治疗但尚未开票；这批在制品（WIP）的时间与材料成本为 $9,000，公允市价为 $14,000。她的记账员翻出一份 2016 年的旧报税单，上面显示曾用第 34 条选举排除 WIP。Lin 医生在 2026 年 Form T2125 上必须如何申报其收入与 WIP？

  1. ✗ 错误。掉入「专业人士年底排除WIP」的历史陷阱：原允许六类指定专业人士按开票入账（billed-basis accounting）排除 WIP 的 ITA s.34 已于 2017 年被整条废除。Wrong. This falls into the historical trap "professionals excluding year-end WIP": ITA s.34, which let six types of designated professionals use billed-basis accounting to exclude WIP, was repealed outright in 2017.

  2. ✓ 正确。牙医属于专业收入（professional income），填 T2125 Part 3B；原 s.34 排除选举已于 2017 年废除且五年过渡期已在 2022 税年前结束，现行规则下所有专业人士必须在期末将在制品（work-in-progress, WIP）按成本与公允市价孰低（$9,000）计入当年收入。Correct. A dentist has professional income and completes T2125 Part 3B; the former s.34 exclusion election was repealed in 2017 and the five-year transition ended before the 2022 tax year, so under current rules all professionals must include work in progress (WIP) at year end in the year's income at the lower of cost and fair market value ($9,000).

  3. ✗ 错误。牙医属于蕴含专门知识的专业人士，应填 Part 3B 而非 Part 3A，且期末 WIP 按成本与公允市价孰低（$9,000）计入，而非强制按更高的公允市价 $14,000。Wrong. A dentist is a professional whose work rests on specialized knowledge and should use Part 3B, not Part 3A, and year-end WIP is included at the lower of cost and fair market value ($9,000), not at the higher fair market value of $14,000.

  4. ✗ 错误。每年带回 20% 的五年过渡期早在 2022 税年之前就已经全部走完，2026 税年期末 WIP 必须全额按成本与公允市价孰低计入当年收入。Wrong. The five-year transition, which brought back 20% each year, ended well before the 2022 tax year; for the 2026 tax year year-end WIP must be included in full in the year's income at the lower of cost and fair market value.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Tariq earns income from two separate unincorporated activities in 2026: he works as an independent IT architecture consultant (a profession involving specialized knowledge) and he also runs an online retail store selling computer accessories with year-end merchandise inventory. How must Tariq complete Form T2125 for 2026?

Tariq 在 2026 年从两项独立的未注册公司活动中取得收入：他担任独立 IT 架构顾问（一项蕴含专门知识的职业），同时还经营一家销售电脑配件且期末有商品存货的网店。Tariq 在 2026 年必须如何填报 Form T2125？

  1. ✗ 错误。专业收入（professional income）的区分标准是「是否属于蕴含专门知识、区别于单纯技能的职业」，法院明确将建筑师、工程师、IT 顾问包含在内，且同时有营业和专业两类收入必须分开填两张 T2125。Wrong. The test for professional income is whether the occupation rests on specialized knowledge as distinct from mere skill; the courts expressly include architects, engineers and IT consultants, and a person with both business and professional income must complete two separate T2125s.

  2. ✗ 错误。同时有营业收入与专业收入的人必须分别填两张 T2125，网店商品属于期末存货（Part 3A），不是专业服务在制品（WIP）。Wrong. A person with both business income and professional income must file two separate T2125s, and online-shop goods are year-end inventory (Part 3A), not the work in progress (WIP) of a professional service.

  3. ✓ 正确。同时有营业收入（business income, Part 3A，核算期末存货成本）和专业收入（professional income, Part 3B，包括 IT 顾问，按成本与公允市价孰低核算期末 WIP）的人，必须分别填报两张 Form T2125，且网店还需填写 Part 2 网络收入信息。Correct. A person with both business income (Part 3A, which accounts for year-end inventory cost) and professional income (Part 3B, which includes IT consultants and accounts for year-end WIP at the lower of cost and fair market value) must file two separate Forms T2125, and the online shop must also complete the Part 2 online income information.

  4. ✗ 错误。独立自雇的 IT 顾问属于专业收入，填报在另一张 T2125 的 Part 3B（最终落到 T1 Line 13700），不是雇佣收入。Wrong. An independent self-employed IT consultant has professional income, reported on Part 3B of the other T2125 (and ending up on T1 Line 13700), not employment income.
