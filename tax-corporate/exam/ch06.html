第 6 章 · T2 与小企业扣除Chapter 6 · T2 Return & Small Business DeductionT2 basics and the small business deduction

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 资格定性题：给出股东居住地、上市公司持股或董事会实际控制人情况，判断公司是否属于加拿大受控私人公司（CCPC），以及失去 CCPC 身份后是否触发视同年结（deemed year-end）和失去四项核心税务优惠。Eligibility classification questions: given the shareholders' residence, a public company's shareholding, or who actually controls the board, you decide whether the company is a Canadian-controlled private corporation (CCPC), and whether losing CCPC status triggers a deemed year-end and the loss of four core tax benefits.
  • 小企业扣除（SBD）与合并税率计算题：给出经营活动收入（ABI）、应税收入、经营限额（business limit）与递减后限额四个数字，要求按「四数取最小 × 19%」算联邦 SBD，并结合卑诗省 2% 小企业税率与 12% 一般税率算合并应缴税额。Small business deduction (SBD) and combined rate calculation questions: given active business income (ABI), taxable income, the business limit and the reduced business limit, you compute the federal SBD as "the least of the four numbers x 19%" and, with BC's 2% small business rate and 12% general rate, the combined tax payable.
  • 两条限额递减（business limit reduction）辨析与计算题：同时给出上一税年联营组应税资本（taxable capital employed in Canada）与调整后合计投资收入（AAII），考查「两条递减取大者而非相加」的规则，以及 2022 年 4 月 7 日前开始税年的旧规则（$15,000,000 归零）与 2026 现行规则（$50,000,000 归零）的差异。Two business limit reductions: distinction and calculation questions: given both the associated group's taxable capital employed in Canada and adjusted aggregate investment income (AAII) for the prior tax year, you apply the rule that "the greater of the two reductions applies, not their sum", and the difference between the old rule for tax years beginning before April 7, 2022 (zero at $15,000,000) and the current 2026 rule (zero at $50,000,000).
  • 联营公司（associated corporations）判定与限额分摊题：设置夫妻、兄弟等亲属分别控股两家公司的场景，考查「25% 交叉持股红线」是否触发联营、Schedule 23 合计不超过 100% 的分摊规则，以及短税年（short tax year）按天数除以 365 缩减经营限额。Associated corporations determination and limit allocation questions: scenarios in which spouses or brothers hold two companies separately test whether the "25% cross-ownership line" triggers association, the Schedule 23 rule that allocations total no more than 100%, and the short tax year reduction of the business limit by days over 365.
  • T2 四个时点与合规罚则题：给出公司年结日、应税收入及过往合规记录，考查申报截止日（年结后 6 个月）、欠税截止日（年结后 2 个月或符合条件 CCPC 的 3 个月）、分期预缴免除门槛（$3,000）及「一个月差一天」的首期预缴日，外加按未缴税款计算的迟报罚。T2 four dates and compliance penalty questions: given the company's year end, taxable income and past compliance record, you apply the filing deadline (6 months after year end), the balance-due date (2 months after year end, or 3 months for a qualifying CCPC), the $3,000 instalment exemption threshold and the "one month less a day" first instalment date, plus the late-filing penalty computed on unpaid tax.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 把 $500,000 经营限额当成免税额Treating the $500,000 business limit as a tax-free allowance

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 学习者常把经营限额（business limit）误解为个人基本免税额那样的零税率区间，以为前 $500,000 利润一分钱税都不用交，忽略了限额内仍须缴纳 9% 联邦税与 2% 卑诗省税（合计 11%）。Learners often misread the business limit as a zero-rate band like the personal basic exemption, thinking the first $500,000 of profit bears no tax at all, overlooking that within the limit 9% federal tax and 2% BC tax (11% in total) must still be paid.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干或选项出现「tax-free」「exempt from tax on the first $500,000」或声称收入低于 $500,000 时应缴所得税为零。The stem or options contain "tax-free" or "exempt from tax on the first $500,000", or claim that tax payable is zero when income is below $500,000.

⚠ 两条限额递减直接相加或错用当年数据Adding the two limit reductions or using current-year data

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到公司既有超 $10,000,000 的应税资本、又有超 $50,000 的被动投资收入时，习惯性把两项递减额加总扣除，或误用本税年而非上一税年的数字计算。When the company has both taxable capital over $10,000,000 and passive investment income over $50,000, the habit is to add the two reductions together, or to use the current tax year's numbers rather than the prior tax year's.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出上一税年与本税年的应税资本及被动收入（AAII）两组数据，询问递减后的经营限额。The stem gives two sets of data, taxable capital and passive income (AAII) for both the prior tax year and the current tax year, and asks for the reduced business limit.

⚠ 把报税截止日（6 个月）当成缴税截止日（2 或 3 个月）Treating the filing deadline (6 months) as the payment deadline (2 or 3 months)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 受个人税 T1 报税与缴税同日（4 月 30 日）的思维定式影响，误以为 T2 在年结后 6 个月申报时才需要交清税款，导致迟缴 3 至 4 个月并产生不可抵扣的 7% 欠税利息。Under the habit of personal T1 filing and payment falling on the same day (April 30), candidates wrongly think the tax is due only when the T2 is filed 6 months after year end, so payment is 3 to 4 months late and non-deductible 7% arrears interest accrues.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出年结日（fiscal year-end），分别询问 balance-due day（欠税截止日）与 filing deadline（申报截止日）。The stem gives the fiscal year-end and asks separately for the balance-due day and the filing deadline.

⚠ 见到配偶或兄弟各开一家公司就自动判为联营Automatically deeming spouses or brothers who each own a company to be associated

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 只记住了「亲属关系会影响联营」，却漏掉了《所得税法》要求其中一人必须在两家公司里都持有某一类非指定股份至少 25%（cross-ownership test）才会触发联营。Candidates remember only that "a family relationship affects association", but miss that the Income Tax Act requires one of them to hold at least 25% of some class of non-specified shares in both companies (the cross-ownership test) before association is triggered.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现配偶或兄弟姐妹各自 100% 持有一家公司，然后给出零交叉持股或恰好达到 25% 交叉持股的股权结构。The stem has a spouse or siblings each owning 100% of one company, then gives a share structure with zero cross-ownership or exactly 25% cross-ownership.

⚠ 短税年漏摊经营限额或误摊税率Missing the short tax year allocation of the business limit or wrongly prorating the rate

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 新公司首年注册或变更年结日形成不足 51 周的短税年时，容易直接按完整的 $500,000 限额算 SBD，或者误把 9% 的税率按天数打折。When a new company's first year, or a change of year end, produces a short tax year of fewer than 51 weeks, candidates easily compute SBD with the full $500,000 limit, or wrongly prorate the 9% rate by days.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现年中注册日期（如 3 月 1 日注册、12 月 31 日年结）或因控制权变更触发视同年结的短税年天数。The stem has a mid-year incorporation date (such as incorporated March 1 with a December 31 year end) or the number of days in a short tax year caused by a deemed year-end on a change of control.

第 1 题Q1CCPC身份判定与丧失身份后果CCPC status determination and the consequences of losing it★★陷阱：忽视事实控制或控制权变更触发视同年结

Design Co. is a private corporation incorporated in British Columbia with a regular December 31 year-end. Until June 30, 2026, 100% of its shares were owned by Ms. Lin, a Canadian resident. On July 1, 2026, Ms. Lin sold 55% of the voting shares to an investor who resides in Singapore. Which statement accurately describes the tax consequences for Design Co. in 2026?

Design Co. 是一家在卑诗省注册的私人公司，常规年结日为 12 月 31 日。在 2026 年 6 月 30 日之前，其 100% 股份由加拿大居民林女士持有。2026 年 7 月 1 日，林女士将 55% 的表决权股份出售给一位居住在新加坡的投资人。下列哪项准确描述了 Design Co. 在 2026 年的税务后果？

  1. ✗ 错误。小企业扣除（SBD）要求公司在整个税年内都是加拿大受控私人公司（CCPC）；一旦在年中被非居民取得控制权，不能把全年合并当作 CCPC 处理。Wrong. The small business deduction (SBD) requires the company to be a Canadian-controlled private corporation (CCPC) throughout the tax year; once a non-resident acquires control mid-year, the whole year cannot be treated as CCPC.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据《所得税法》s.125(7)，被一个或多个非居民控制的公司不再是 CCPC；控制权被取得或 CCPC 身份改变会触发视同年结（deemed year-end，ITA s.249(4)、s.249(3.1)），交割前的短税年仍可按天数比例享 11% 合并低税率（联邦 9% ＋ BC 2%），交割后的税年起失去 CCPC 资格，全部按 27% 合并一般税率（联邦 15% ＋ BC 12%）计税，且需多报一份 T2。Correct. Under s.125(7) of the Income Tax Act, a company controlled by one or more non-residents is no longer a CCPC; the acquisition of control or a change in CCPC status triggers a deemed year-end (ITA s.249(4), s.249(3.1)); the short tax year before closing can still enjoy the 11% combined low rate (federal 9% + BC 2%) prorated by days, from the tax year after closing the CCPC status is lost and everything is taxed at the 27% combined general rate (federal 15% + BC 12%), and an additional T2 must be filed.

  3. ✗ 错误。公司是独立纳税人，不会按股东持股比例把公司利润拆成两档税率；非居民持有 55% 表决权股份已构成法律上的控制（de jure control），踩中 CCPC 三条否定条件之一，整家公司失去 CCPC 身份。Wrong. A company is a separate taxpayer and its profit is not split into two rate bands by the shareholders' proportions; a non-resident holding 55% of the voting shares already amounts to legal control (de jure control), meeting one of the three negative CCPC conditions, and the whole company loses CCPC status.

  4. ✗ 错误。失去 CCPC 身份不会追溯剥夺交割前那段期间的 CCPC 待遇，而是通过视同年结把一年切成两个税年：前一段短税年仍享按天数摊分的 SBD，后一段才按一般税率计税。Wrong. Losing CCPC status does not retroactively strip CCPC treatment from the period before closing; the deemed year-end cuts the year into two tax years: the earlier short tax year still enjoys the SBD prorated by days, and only the later one is taxed at the general rate.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Tech Co. is a Canadian private corporation whose voting shares are held 60% by two Canadian residents and 40% by a public corporation listed on a designated stock exchange. However, under a unanimous agreement and debt covenant, the public corporation has the legal and practical power to appoint a majority of the board of directors. Does Tech Co. qualify as a CCPC for 2026?

Tech Co. 是一家加拿大私人公司，其表决权股份由两名加拿大居民持有 60%，一家在指定证券交易所上市的公众公司持有 40%。但根据一致协议和债务契约，该公众公司拥有任命董事会多数席位的法律与实际权力。Tech Co. 在 2026 年是否符合 CCPC 资格？

  1. ✗ 错误。CCPC 定义中的「控制」不仅看表决权多数的法律控制（de jure control），还包括事实上的控制（de facto control）；不能只看 60% 的表面持股比例。Wrong. "Control" in the CCPC definition looks not only at legal control by a voting majority (de jure control) but also at de facto control; you cannot look only at the face shareholding of 60%.

  2. ✗ 错误。虽然公司自身股份未上市，但 CCPC 的三条否定条件还包括「不得被公众公司控制」；踩中三条否定中的任何一条即丧失 CCPC 资格。Wrong. Although the company's own shares are not listed, the three negative CCPC conditions also include "not controlled by a public corporation"; meeting any one of the three negative conditions loses CCPC status.

  3. ✗ 错误。CCPC 是一个非黑即白的开关，不存在按公众公司持股比例打折享受小企业扣除（SBD）的规则。Wrong. CCPC is an on-off switch; there is no rule prorating the small business deduction (SBD) by a public corporation's shareholding.

第 6 题Q6迟报罚基数与重犯升级机制Late-filing penalty base and the repeat offender escalation★★★陷阱：把迟报罚基数当成税款总额而非未缴税款

Corp Y had $40,000 of total 2025 tax, paid $10,000 before the filing deadline (leaving $30,000 unpaid on June 30, 2026), and is a first-time late filer. What are the late-filing penalties for Corp X and Corp Y?

两家卑诗省公司的年结日均为 2025 年 12 月 31 日（申报截止日为 2026 年 6 月 30 日），且都在 2026 年 11 月 2 日迟交 T2 报表（恰好迟报满 4 个整月）。X 公司 2025 年税款总额为 $40,000，但在申报截止日前已通过分期预缴足额交清 $40,000（2026 年 6 月 30 日未缴税款为 $0）。Y 公司 2025 年税款总额也是 $40,000，截止日前已交 $10,000（2026 年 6 月 30 日未缴税款为 $30,000），且属于首次迟报。X 公司和 Y 公司的迟报罚款分别是多少？

  2. ✗ 错误。17% 是迟报满 12 个月的首犯封顶比例（5% ＋ 1% × 12），而本题只迟报了 4 个整月（5% ＋ 1% × 4 ＝ 9%）；X 公司截止日不欠税，迟报罚为 $0，$1,000 是应电子申报 T2 而未电子申报的罚款，与迟报罚无关。Wrong. 17% is the cap for a first-time offender late for a full 12 months (5% + 1% x 12), whereas here the filing was only 4 full months late (5% + 1% x 4 = 9%); Company X owes no tax on the deadline, so its late-filing penalty is $0, and $1,000 is the penalty for failing to file a T2 electronically when required, unrelated to the late-filing penalty.

  3. ✗ 错误。Y 公司是首次迟报（first-time late filer），适用 5% 加每月 1%（共 9%），只有收到 CRA 催报要求且前三年内曾被罚过的重犯才适用 10% 加每月 2%（10% ＋ 2% × 4 ＝ 18%）。Wrong. Company Y is a first-time late filer, to which 5% plus 1% per month (9% in total) applies; only a repeat offender who received a CRA demand to file and had been penalized in the preceding three years gets 10% plus 2% per month (10% + 2% x 4 = 18%).

  4. ✓ 正确。根据 ITA s.162(1)，迟交 T2 的首犯罚款基数是「申报截止日当日的未缴税款（unpaid tax）」，比例为 5% 加每满一个整月 1%（最多 12 个月，封顶 17%）。X 公司在截止日不欠税，故迟报罚为 $0（但迟报仍会推迟评税并留下不良记录）；Y 公司未缴税款为 $30,000，迟报 4 个整月按 5% ＋ 4 × 1% ＝ 9% 计算，罚款为 $30,000 × 9% ＝ $2,700。Correct. Under ITA s.162(1), the base of a first-time late T2 penalty is the "unpaid tax" on the filing deadline, at 5% plus 1% for each full month (up to 12 months, capped at 17%). Company X owes no tax on the deadline, so its late-filing penalty is $0 (although late filing still delays assessment and leaves a poor record); Company Y's unpaid tax is $30,000, 4 full months late at 5% + 4 x 1% = 9%, so the penalty is $30,000 x 9% = $2,700.

Why does the lecture describe the Small Business Deduction (SBD) as a tax deferral rather than a permanent tax reduction under Canada's corporate-personal tax integration system?
