① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 身份分流与税务后果题：给出老板或家人从公司拿钱、用车或收礼的场景，考察是以雇员身份（ITA s.6(1)(a)，上 T4、公司可扣除）还是以股东身份（ITA s.15(1)，上 T4A code 028、公司不可扣除）计税，以及家人高薪超出合理范围被 s.67 砍除公司扣除但个人照交税的双重惩罚。Capacity-split and tax consequence question: an owner or family member takes money, uses a car or receives a gift from the corporation; tests whether it is taxed as an employee (ITA s.6(1)(a), on a T4, deductible by the corporation) or as a shareholder (ITA s.15(1), on a T4A code 028, not deductible by the corporation), and the double penalty where a family member's high salary above a reasonable amount loses the corporate deduction under s.67 while the individual still pays tax.
  • 2026 源头扣缴与雇主总成本计算题：给出年薪（如超过 $85,000 的高薪），要求算清 CPP（上限 $74,600、免税额 $3,500、费率 5.95%）、CPP2（区间 $74,600–$85,000、费率 4%）、EI（上限 $68,900、雇员 1.63%、雇主 1.4 倍）的雇员扣款或雇主配比成本，或考察「EI 费率下降但最高保费因上限提高而上涨」的判断。2026 source deduction and total employer cost calculation question: given an annual salary (for example above $85,000), compute the employee deductions or employer matching cost for CPP (ceiling $74,600, exemption $3,500, rate 5.95%), CPP2 (range $74,600-$85,000, rate 4%) and EI (ceiling $68,900, employee 1.63%, employer 1.4 times), or judge the statement 'the EI rate fell but the maximum premium rose because the ceiling rose'.
  • 公司车辆双层福利与减免判据题：给出购车成本或租金、全年总里程与业务里程，先考减免待命费的两个并列条件（业务用途过半且个人里程不超过每 30 天 1,667 公里／全年 20,004 公里），再考运营费用福利在固定费率法（34¢/km）与选择性算法（待命费的一半）之间的择优，以及车辆福利只扣 CPP 和所得税、不扣 EI 的细节。Company car two-layer benefit and relief question: given the purchase cost or lease payment, total kilometres for the year and business kilometres, first tests the two parallel conditions for reducing the standby charge (business use over half and personal kilometres not over 1,667 km per 30 days / 20,004 km a year), then the choice for the operating expense benefit between the fixed rate method (34 cents/km) and the optional method (half of the standby charge), and the detail that a car benefit attracts CPP and income tax but not EI.
  • 行政政策排除条款与时间死线题：重点测试 $500 非现金礼品免税政策对非公平交易对象（股东及亲属）完全失效、计提奖金必须在税年结束后 180 天内支付（ITA s.78(4)）、低息贷款在年结后 30 天内付息及购房贷款五年利率封顶（ITA s.80.4）、运营费用在年结后 45 天内报销等硬性死线。Administrative policy exclusions and time-deadline question: focuses on the $500 non-cash gift policy not applying at all to non-arm's length persons (shareholders and relatives), the requirement that an accrued bonus be paid within 180 days after the tax year-end (ITA s.78(4)), interest on a low-interest loan paid within 30 days after year-end and the five-year rate cap for a home-purchase loan (ITA s.80.4), and the 45-day deadline for reimbursing operating expenses.
  • 薪资合规档次与 BC 雇主健康税题：考察按两年前月均预扣额（AMWA）划分四档汇款人、季度汇款人完美合规记录对销售税的联动、联营公司合并计算 AMWA 与共享 BC 雇主健康税（EHT）$1,000,000 免税额，以及 $1,000,000 至 $1,500,000 之间 5.85% 缺口费率与超过 $1,500,000 按全额 1.95% 计税的计算。Payroll compliance tiers and BC Employer Health Tax question: tests the four remitter categories based on average monthly withholding amount (AMWA) from two years earlier, the link between a quarterly remitter's perfect compliance record and sales tax, combining AMWA of associated corporations and sharing the BC Employer Health Tax (EHT) $1,000,000 exemption, and the calculation of the 5.85% notch rate between $1,000,000 and $1,500,000 and 1.95% on the full amount above $1,500,000.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 股东亲属套用 $500 礼品免税Applying the $500 gift exemption to a shareholder's relative

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生记住 CRA 非现金礼品每年 $500 免税的行政政策，却忽略该政策明文排除非公平交易雇员（股东本人、配偶、子女等亲属），导致对老板或老板家属收到的节日礼物错误扣减 $500。Candidates remember the CRA administrative policy that non-cash gifts up to $500 a year are tax-free but overlook that the policy expressly excludes non-arm's length employees (the shareholder, spouse, children and other relatives), and so wrongly deduct $500 from holiday gifts received by the owner or the owner's family.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 sole shareholder、controlling shareholder、spouse 或 child of the owner 收到 non-cash gift 或 award。The question has a sole shareholder, controlling shareholder, spouse or child of the owner receiving a non-cash gift or award.

⚠ 减免待命费只看业务比例过半Reducing the standby charge by looking only at business use over half

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到业务里程占总里程过半（如总里程 60,000 公里、业务 36,000 公里占 60%），就立即按比例折算减免待命费，忘了第二个并列条件是个人里程绝对值不能超过每 30 天 1,667 公里（全年 20,004 公里）。Seeing business kilometres over half of the total (for example 36,000 of 60,000 km, 60%) and at once prorating the reduced standby charge, forgetting that the second parallel condition is that personal kilometres cannot exceed 1,667 km per 30 days (20,004 km a year) in absolute terms.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出很高的全年总里程（如 45,000 公里以上），业务占比过半，但减出来的个人里程超过 20,004 公里。The question gives a high annual total (for example 45,000 km or more), business use is over half, but the personal kilometres left over exceed 20,004.

⚠ 混淆里程津贴与运营费用福利Confusing the mileage allowance with the operating expense benefit

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把雇员开私家车办公事拿的免税里程津贴（2026 年前 5,000 公里 73¢/km、其后 67¢/km）与雇员开公司车办私事产生的运营费用福利固定费率（34¢/km）混为一谈，用错费率或搞反方向。Mixing the tax-free mileage allowance for an employee driving a personal vehicle on business (for 2026, 73 cents/km for the first 5,000 km and 67 cents/km after) with the fixed-rate operating expense benefit (34 cents/km) for an employee driving a company car for personal use, using the wrong rate or the wrong direction.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 先看车辆登记在谁名下：company-provided vehicle 找 34¢/km（或待命费一半）；employee-owned vehicle driven for business 找 73¢/67¢。First look at whose name the vehicle is registered in: for a company-provided vehicle look for 34 cents/km (or half of the standby charge); for an employee-owned vehicle driven for business look for 73/67 cents.

⚠ 只看 EI 费率下调就断定保费减少或忽略雇主 1.4 倍Concluding from the lower EI rate that the premium falls, or ignoring the employer's 1.4 multiple

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到 2026 年联邦 EI 费率从 1.66% 降到 1.63%，就以为最高保费下降，忽略了可保收入上限（MIE）升至 $68,900 导致最高保费反而上升；或者在算雇主成本时把 EI 也当成与 CPP 一样的 1:1 配比，漏乘 1.4 倍。Seeing the 2026 federal EI rate fall from 1.66% to 1.63% and assuming the maximum premium falls, ignoring that the maximum insurable earnings (MIE) rose to $68,900 so the maximum premium actually rises; or, in computing the employer cost, treating EI as a 1:1 match like CPP and missing the 1.4 multiple.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干要求比较不同年份最高 EI 保费，或要求计算 employer portion of payroll deductions。The question asks to compare the maximum EI premium of different years, or to compute the employer portion of payroll deductions.

⚠ 联营公司拆薪资躲避加速汇款或 BC 雇主健康税Splitting payroll across associated corporations to avoid accelerated remittance or BC EHT

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为名下两家独立注册的公司可以各自按自己的月均预扣额（AMWA）定汇款档次、各自享受 BC 雇主健康税 $1,000,000 免税额，忽略了联营公司必须合并计算 AMWA 且必须共享同一个 $1,000,000 免税额。Thinking two separately registered corporations can each set their own remitter category by their own average monthly withholding amount (AMWA) and each claim the $1,000,000 BC Employer Health Tax exemption, ignoring that associated corporations must combine AMWA and must share a single $1,000,000 exemption.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 associated corporations、two corporations controlled by the same shareholder，并分别列出各自的薪酬或 AMWA。The question refers to associated corporations, or two corporations controlled by the same shareholder, and lists their payroll or AMWA separately.

第 1 题Q1雇员福利与股东福利的身份分流与报表Splitting employee and shareholder benefits by capacity, and the slips★★陷阱：混淆雇员福利（s.6，T4，可扣）与股东福利（s.15(1)，T4A，不可扣）

Marco is the sole shareholder and president of a plumbing corporation. During 2026, the corporation paid $18,000 directly to a contractor to renovate the kitchen in Marco's personal residence and recorded the payment as a corporate repair expense. This benefit was not part of any employment compensation package offered to employees. How is this $18,000 treated for tax purposes?

Marco 是一家水暖公司的唯一股东兼总裁。2026 年期间，公司直接向承包商支付了 $18,000 用于翻修 Marco 私人住宅的厨房，并在账上记为公司维修费用。这项好处不属于提供给雇员的任何薪酬方案。这 $18,000 在税务上应如何处理？

  1. ✓ 正确。Marco 是以股东身份（因为公司是他的）而非雇员身份获得私人住宅装修，走股东福利（shareholder benefit, ITA s.15(1)）路径：由于该支出不是为赚取经营收入而发生（过不了 s.18(1)(a)），公司不得扣除这 $18,000，而 Marco 个人仍须全额计入收入，并报在 T4A 表的 code 028（其他收入）栏。Correct. Marco receives the private home renovation as a shareholder (because the corporation is his), not as an employee, so it follows the shareholder benefit (ITA s.15(1)) path: because the expense was not incurred to earn business income (it fails s.18(1)(a)), the corporation cannot deduct the $18,000, while Marco must still include the full $18,000 in income, reported on a T4A slip in code 028 (other income).

  2. ✗ 错误。这是「同时是雇员和股东却一律按雇员报」的典型陷阱。如果 Marco 不是股东，公司根本不会替他装修私人厨房，因此事实判据属于股东福利（s.15(1)）而非受雇福利（taxable employment benefit, s.6(1)(a)），公司拿不到扣除。Wrong. This is the classic trap of treating a person who is both employee and shareholder always as an employee. If Marco were not a shareholder, the corporation would never renovate his private kitchen, so the factual test points to a shareholder benefit (s.15(1)) rather than a taxable employment benefit (s.6(1)(a)), and the corporation gets no deduction.

  3. ✗ 错误。公司直接替股东支付私人装修并记入公司费用，属于直接授予股东福利（s.15(1)），本金 $18,000 全额计入收入并报 T4A code 028；只有股东贷款产生的推定利息福利（deemed interest benefit, s.80.4）才报 code 117。Wrong. The corporation directly paying a shareholder's private renovation and recording it as a corporate expense is a benefit conferred directly on a shareholder (s.15(1)); the full $18,000 principal is included in income and reported on a T4A code 028; only the deemed interest benefit (s.80.4) from a shareholder loan is reported under code 117.

  4. ✗ 错误。公司能否扣除与个人是否计入收入是两道各自独立的闸；走上 s.15(1) 股东福利路径的后果正是公司不得扣除、个人照样全额计税，两级税负直接叠加，没有税收整合（integration）。Wrong. Whether the corporation can deduct and whether the individual includes the amount are two independent gates; going down the s.15(1) shareholder benefit path means precisely that the corporation cannot deduct and the individual is still taxed on the full amount, so the two levels of tax stack with no integration.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据 ITA s.78(4) 未付薪酬规则（unpaid remuneration rule），2026-12-31 年结后第 180 天为 2027 年 6 月 29 日，7 月 15 日才付导致公司 2026 年扣除被取消，改到实际支付的 2027 年才能扣除；根据 CRA 礼品与奖励政策（gifts and awards policy），公平交易店员 $500 以内的非现金礼品免税，但股东及亲属被明文排除，Chen 的 $450 全额应税。Correct. Under the ITA s.78(4) unpaid remuneration rule, the 180th day after the 2026-12-31 year-end is June 29, 2027; payment on July 15 cancels the corporation's 2026 deduction and moves it to 2027, the year of actual payment. Under the CRA gifts and awards policy, a non-cash gift of up to $500 to an arm's length clerk is tax-free, but shareholders and relatives are expressly excluded, so Chen's $450 is fully taxable.

  3. ✗ 错误。ITA s.78(4) 的死线是精确的「税年结束后第 180 天」（对 12 月 31 日年结而言是次年 6 月 29 日），不是 6 个月加 30 天；此外公平交易店员收到的 $450 非现金礼篮在 $500 额度内免税。Wrong. The deadline in ITA s.78(4) is exactly 'the 180th day after the end of the tax year' (June 29 of the next year for a December 31 year-end), not six months plus 30 days; also, the $450 non-cash gift basket received by an arm's length clerk is tax-free within the $500 limit.

  4. ✗ 错误。错过 180 天死线的后果是计提年度（2026）不得扣除，改为在实际支付的那一年（2027）扣除，并非永久不得扣除；且股东 Chen 不适用 $500 免税额。Wrong. The consequence of missing the 180-day deadline is that the deduction is denied in the year of accrual (2026) and moves to the year of actual payment (2027); it is not permanently non-deductible; also, the shareholder Chen does not get the $500 exemption.

It also gives a $300 non-cash holiday gift to the sole shareholder's spouse who works part-time as a bookkeeper. Which items are taxable benefits in 2026?

For an arm's length employee, the annual $500 non-cash gift limit and the $500 non-cash long-service award limit every five years do not use each other up, so the $400 and $450 are both tax-free, but cash and near-cash ($200) are always a taxable benefit; for the shareholder's spouse, a non-arm's length employee is expressly excluded from the $500 policy, so the $300 is fully taxable.

第 6 题Q6源头扣缴汇款四档与迟缴未扣罚则Four remitter tiers and penalties for late or missed deduction★★陷阱：忽略联营公司合并 AMWA 或销售税迟报导致季度汇款资格丢失

Company X had an average monthly withholding amount (AMWA) of $2,400 two calendar years ago and $2,600 last year. It has paid all payroll deductions on time for the past 12 months, but it filed its quarterly GST/HST return three weeks late four months ago. Separately, Company Y is associated with Company Z; two calendar years ago, Company Y had an AMWA of $4,000 and Company Z had an AMWA of $23,000. Which statement correctly identifies their remitter categories?

X 公司两个日历年前的月均预扣额（AMWA）为 $2,400，去年为 $2,600。过去 12 个月它按时缴纳了所有薪资扣款，但四个月前它的季度 GST/HST 申报迟交了三周。另外，Y 公司与 Z 公司联营；两个日历年前，Y 公司的 AMWA 为 $4,000，Z 公司的 AMWA 为 $23,000。哪项陈述正确认定了它们的汇款人档次？

  1. ✗ 错误。两边都踩了坑：第一，季度汇款人（quarterly remitter）要求的完美合规记录（perfect compliance history）涵盖过去 12 个月的薪资扣缴、GST/HST 缴纳以及 T4 和 GST/HST 按时申报，GST/HST 迟报一次即丧失按季汇款资格；第二，联营公司（associated corporations）的 AMWA 必须合并计算（$4,000 + $23,000 = $27,000 ≥ $25,000），Y 和 Z 都变成加速汇款人。Wrong. It falls into traps on both sides: first, the perfect compliance history required of a quarterly remitter covers the last 12 months of payroll remittances, GST/HST payments, and on-time filing of T4s and GST/HST returns, so one late GST/HST filing loses quarterly status; second, the AMWA of associated corporations must be combined ($4,000 + $23,000 = $27,000, at least $25,000), so both Y and Z become accelerated remitters.

  2. ✗ 错误。混淆了未扣缴罚（failure to deduct penalty，一律 10%）与迟缴罚（late remittance penalty，阶梯罚：1–3 天 3%、4–5 天 5%、6–7 天 7%、超过 7 天 10%）。迟缴 5 天适用 5% 的阶梯罚率，而非 10%。Wrong. This confuses the failure to deduct penalty (always 10%) with the late remittance penalty (a tiered penalty: 3% for 1-3 days, 5% for 4-5 days, 7% for 6-7 days, 10% for more than 7 days). Five days late falls under the 5% tier, not 10%.

  3. ✗ 错误。X 公司因 GST/HST 迟报已失去季度资格；而 Y 与 Z 合并后的 AMWA 为 $27,000，属于加速门槛一（$25,000–$99,999.99，每月两次），达到 $100,000 及以上才是加速门槛二（Threshold 2）。Wrong. Company X has already lost quarterly status because of its late GST/HST filing; and the combined AMWA of Y and Z is $27,000, which makes them Threshold 1 accelerated remitters ($25,000-$99,999.99, twice a month); Threshold 2 begins only at $100,000 or more.

  4. ✓ 正确。季度汇款人除了要求前一年或前两年 AMWA 低于 $3,000 外，还要求过去 12 个月具备完美合规记录（包括薪资扣缴、GST/HST 缴纳以及 T4 与销售税按时申报），X 公司迟报 GST/HST 导致失去季度资格，只能按普通汇款人（次月 15 日）缴；同时联营公司的两年前 AMWA 必须合并计算，Y 与 Z 合计 $27,000 落入 $25,000–$99,999.99 区间，因此组内所有公司（含仅 $4,000 的 Y 公司）均成为加速门槛一（Threshold 1 accelerated remitter）汇款人。Correct. Besides requiring an AMWA below $3,000 in the previous one or two years, a quarterly remitter must have a perfect compliance history for the last 12 months (including payroll remittances, GST/HST payments and on-time filing of T4s and sales tax returns); Company X's late GST/HST filing loses it quarterly status and it can only remit as a regular remitter (by the 15th of the next month). Also, the AMWA of associated corporations from two years earlier must be combined, so Y and Z together at $27,000 fall in the $25,000-$99,999.99 band, and every company in the group (including Y with only $4,000) becomes a Threshold 1 accelerated remitter.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An employer deducted $10,500 in CPP, EI, and income tax from its employees' paycheques in May 2026 (its first offence and not due to gross negligence), but remitted the $10,500 to the CRA six days after the due date. What is the federal late remittance penalty rule for this delay?

一家雇主在 2026 年 5 月从雇员薪水中扣下了 $10,500 的 CPP、EI 和所得税（属首次违规且非严重疏忽），但在到期日之后第 6 天才将这 $10,500 上缴给 CRA。针对这次延误的联邦迟缴罚则是什么？

  1. ✓ 正确。扣了迟交适用四级阶梯迟缴罚（late remittance penalty）：迟交 1–3 天罚 3%、4–5 天罚 5%、6–7 天罚 7%、超过 7 天或未交罚 10%；在非明知或严重疏忽的一般情形下，只对超过 $500 的那一部分计罚。迟交 6 天对应 7%。Correct. Late remittance attracts the four-tier late remittance penalty: 3% for 1-3 days late, 5% for 4-5 days, 7% for 6-7 days, and 10% for more than 7 days or not remitted; in ordinary cases that are not knowing or grossly negligent, the penalty applies only to the part above $500. Six days late corresponds to 7%.

  2. ✗ 错误。10% 是未扣缴罚（failure to deduct penalty）或迟缴超过 7 天／根本未交的最高档罚率，迟交 6 天适用 7%。Wrong. 10% is the failure to deduct penalty, or the top tier for remitting more than 7 days late or not at all; six days late attracts 7%.

  3. ✗ 错误。3% 仅适用于迟交 1–3 天（或加速门槛二雇主按时缴但未经金融机构缴纳的情形），6–7 天为 7%。Wrong. 3% applies only to remittances 1-3 days late (or where a Threshold 2 accelerated employer pays on time but not through a financial institution); 6-7 days is 7%.

  4. ✗ 错误。20% 罚率仅在同一日历年内第二次违规、且属于明知或严重疏忽（knowingly or under circumstances amounting to gross negligence）时才触发。Wrong. The 20% penalty is triggered only on a second violation in the same calendar year that is knowing or in circumstances amounting to gross negligence.

第 7 题Q7BC雇主健康税缺口费率与联营共享BC EHT notch rate and sharing by associated corporations★★★陷阱：联营公司拆薪资躲避加速汇款或 BC 雇主健康税

Westcoast Design Ltd. is a regular (non-charitable) employer in British Columbia with no associated corporations. For the 2026 calendar year, its total BC remuneration is $1,300,000. What is its 2026 BC Employer Health Tax (EHT), and how would the EHT change if its sole shareholder also controlled a second BC corporation with $400,000 of 2026 BC remuneration?

Westcoast Design Ltd. 是卑诗省的一家一般（非慈善）雇主，没有联营公司。2026 日历年其 BC 薪酬总额为 $1,300,000。它 2026 年的 BC 雇主健康税（EHT）是多少？如果其唯一股东同时控制另一家 2026 年 BC 薪酬为 $400,000 的 BC 公司，EHT 会如何变化？

  1. ✗ 错误。第一，薪酬在 $1,000,000.01 至 $1,500,000 之间适用 5.85% 的缺口费率（notch rate），不是 1.95%；第二，联营雇主（associated employers）必须共享 $1,000,000 免税额，且合计薪酬一旦超过 $1,500,000，全组都没有免税额。Wrong. First, remuneration between $1,000,000.01 and $1,500,000 is subject to the 5.85% notch rate, not 1.95%; second, associated employers must share the $1,000,000 exemption, and once combined remuneration exceeds $1,500,000, the whole group has no exemption.

  2. ✗ 错误。单家公司薪酬 $1,300,000 未超过 $1,500,000，仍享有 $1,000,000 免税额，按 5.85% × ($1,300,000 − $1,000,000) = $17,550 计算；而加上第二家公司的 $400,000 后，联营组合计薪酬达 $1,700,000（超过 $1,500,000），全组免税额归零，不能再共享 $1,000,000 免税额。Wrong. A single corporation with $1,300,000 does not exceed $1,500,000 and still has the $1,000,000 exemption, so tax is 5.85% x ($1,300,000 - $1,000,000) = $17,550; and with the second corporation's $400,000 added, the associated group's combined remuneration is $1,700,000 (above $1,500,000), the exemption of the whole group goes to zero and cannot be shared as a $1,000,000 exemption.

  3. ✓ 正确。单家公司 BC 薪酬 $1,300,000 落在 $1,000,000.01–$1,500,000 区间，按缺口费率（notch rate）计算：5.85% × ($1,300,000 − $1,000,000) = $17,550；当与第二家公司联营时，联营组合计薪酬为 $1,300,000 + $400,000 = $1,700,000，超过 $1,500,000 门槛，根据 BC 雇主健康税（Employer Health Tax, EHT）规则，联营组全组都没有免税额，各自按全额薪酬的 1.95% 缴税。Correct. A single corporation's BC remuneration of $1,300,000 falls in the $1,000,000.01-$1,500,000 band and is computed at the notch rate: 5.85% x ($1,300,000 - $1,000,000) = $17,550; when associated with the second corporation, the group's combined remuneration is $1,300,000 + $400,000 = $1,700,000, above the $1,500,000 threshold, and under the BC Employer Health Tax (EHT) rules the whole associated group has no exemption, each paying 1.95% on its full remuneration.

  4. ✗ 错误。这是「拆公司发薪避税」的常见误区：BC EHT 规定联营组合计薪酬超过 $1,500,000 的，全组都没有免税额，看的是联营组合计数而非单家公司是否超 $1,500,000。Wrong. This is the common misconception of 'splitting payroll across corporations to avoid tax': BC EHT provides that where the associated group's combined remuneration exceeds $1,500,000, the whole group has no exemption, looking at the group's combined total and not at whether a single corporation exceeds $1,500,000.
