本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 题型一：Schedule 1 从财报税后净利润（代码 9999 / 金额 A）到税务净收入与应税收入的综合计算题，重点考账面折旧、所得税费用、餐饮娱乐 50%、俱乐部会费全额加回及慈善捐赠「先加回、后扣除」的两步顺序。Type 1: a combined calculation question on Schedule 1 from the after-tax net income on the financial statements (code 9999 / Amount A) to net income for tax purposes and taxable income, focusing on book depreciation, income tax expense, the 50% limit on meals and entertainment, full add-back of club dues and the two-step order for charitable donations (add back first, deduct afterwards).
  • 题型二：GIFI 申报与校验规则辨析题，考三条校验等式、七个必填代码（金额为零也必须填 0）、代码粒度要求（不许只报小计和合计）、免交传统财报的唯一例外（承保保险公司）以及 T1178 简表的一百万门槛。Type 2: a question distinguishing GIFI filing and validity rules, testing the three validity check equations, the seven mandatory codes (zero amounts must still be entered as 0), the granularity requirement (no reporting of subtotals and totals only), the only exception to not filing traditional financial statements (insurance companies involved in underwriting) and the $1,000,000 thresholds for the T1178 short form.
  • 题型三：不可扣费用与三道法律闸门（s.18(1)(a) 目的检验、s.67 合理性检验、s.18(1)(b) 资本性支出、s.18(1)(e) 准备、s.18(1)(l) 俱乐部会费、s.67.7 不合规短租开支）的定性判断题，测试哪一项全额加回、哪一项只能扣一半、哪一项可扣 80%。Type 3: a characterization question on non-deductible expenses and the statutory gates (the s.18(1)(a) purpose test, the s.67 reasonableness test, s.18(1)(b) capital outlays, s.18(1)(e) reserves, s.18(1)(l) club dues, and s.67.7 non-compliant short-term rental expenses), testing which item is added back in full, which is deductible only by half and which by 80%.
  • 题型四：2026 税年最新参数与旧考试口径对比题，重点考资本利得纳入率（提案取消后仍为 1/2）、再加速投资激励财产（RIIP）与即时费用化退坡年份（44/46/50 类至 2027 年前可用 vs 53/43.1 类 2029 年后退坡）以及 10.1 类乘用车上限（条例文本 $38,000 vs 已宣布 $39,000）。Type 4: a comparison of the latest 2026 tax year parameters with the old exam basis, focusing on the capital gains inclusion rate (still 1/2 after the proposal was cancelled), the reaccelerated investment incentive property (RIIP) and the phase-down years of immediate expensing (Classes 44/46/50 usable before 2027 versus Classes 53/43.1 phasing down after 2029), and the Class 10.1 passenger vehicle ceiling ($38,000 in the regulation text versus $39,000 announced).
  • 题型五：附表衔接与实务流程题，考 Schedule 6、8、13、73、141 与 Schedule 1 的回填方向（如合伙企业分来的资本利得进 Schedule 6 而非 Schedule 1 第 129 行、财报准备走 Schedule 1 第 414/126 行而非 Schedule 13）以及零活动公司的 T2/GIFI 申报义务。Type 5: a schedule linking and practical process question, testing the direction of entries between Schedules 6, 8, 13, 73 and 141 and Schedule 1 (for example, a capital gain allocated by a partnership goes to Schedule 6, not to Schedule 1 line 129; accounting reserves go through Schedule 1 lines 414/126, not Schedule 13) and the T2 / GIFI filing obligation of an inactive corporation.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 俱乐部会费误按餐饮娱乐扣 50%Deducting only 50% of club dues as meals and entertainment

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生习惯把所有招待客户的花费都归入餐饮娱乐（s.67.1）加回一半，忽略了 s.18(1)(l) 对高尔夫球场、游艇、营地、旅舍及餐饮康乐体育俱乐部会费是 100% 完全禁止扣除。Candidates are used to putting all spending on entertaining clients into meals and entertainment (s.67.1) and adding back half, overlooking that s.18(1)(l) forbids deduction of dues for golf courses, yachts, camps, lodges and dining, recreational or sporting clubs completely (100%).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 golf club dues、yacht、lodge、camp 或 recreational club membership fees 时，立即全额加回，一分钱不扣。When the question mentions golf club dues, a yacht, a lodge, a camp or recreational club membership fees, add the amount back in full, with nothing deducted.

⚠ 慈善捐赠在 Schedule 1 漏加回或重复扣Missing the add-back of a charitable donation on Schedule 1, or deducting it twice

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉认为合格慈善捐赠可以抵税，于是在 Schedule 1 不加回甚至再扣一次，忘记了捐赠不属于计算税务净收入（net income for tax purposes）的扣除项，必须在 Schedule 1 先加回，再到 T2 主表计算应税收入（taxable income）时扣除。Intuition says a qualifying charitable donation reduces tax, so the donation is not added back on Schedule 1, or is even deducted again, forgetting that a donation is not a deduction in computing net income for tax purposes and must be added back on Schedule 1 first and then deducted on the main T2 form in computing taxable income.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干问 Schedule 1 的税务净收入（net income for tax purposes）还是最终的应税收入（taxable income），并同时给出账面已扣的 charitable donations。The question asks whether Schedule 1's net income for tax purposes or the final taxable income is wanted, and also gives charitable donations already deducted in the books.

⚠ 误用已取消的三分之二资本利得纳入率或旧版 AII 日期Using the cancelled two-thirds capital gains inclusion rate or the old AII dates

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 2024–2025 年间的旧模板和旧考试资料按 2/3 资本利得纳入率及 2018 版加速投资激励（AII，2024 年前 50% 加成、2028 年前可用）编写，考生未更新到 2026 税年现行 T2 指南口径。Old templates and old exam material from 2024-2025 were written for a two-thirds capital gains inclusion rate and the 2018 Accelerated Investment Incentive (AII, 50% net addition before 2024, usable before 2028), and candidates have not updated to the current 2026 tax year T2 guide.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及 2026 税年公司处置资本财产（Schedule 6）或 2024 年之后新购置可折旧资产（RIIP / 44、46、50、53、43.1 类即时费用化）的可用年份。The question involves a corporation's disposition of capital property in the 2026 tax year (Schedule 6) or the years in which depreciable assets newly acquired after 2024 can be used (RIIP / immediate expensing for Classes 44, 46, 50, 53 and 43.1).

⚠ GIFI 必填代码留空、只报小计或零活动不报Leaving GIFI mandatory codes blank, reporting only subtotals, or not filing for an inactive corporation

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为没有发生业务或某项金额为零就可以留空不填，或者把财报 40 行明细合并成几个小计代码填报，导致无法通过电子申报校验。Wrongly thinking that if there was no business or an amount is zero it can be left blank, or condensing the 40 lines of the financial statements into a few subtotal codes, so that the return cannot pass electronic validity checks.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 inactive corporation、zero balance、summary/subtotal only 或询问 GIFI 校验必填代码（2599、3499、3620、8299、9368、9999 等）的填报方式。The question has an inactive corporation, a zero balance, summary or subtotals only, or asks how to report the GIFI validity check codes (2599, 3499, 3620, 8299, 9368, 9999 and others).

⚠ 附表回填错位：账面准备进表 13、合伙利得进第 129 行Misplaced schedule entries: accounting reserves on Schedule 13 and partnership gains on line 129

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到「准备（reserves）」就想填 Schedule 13，看到「合伙企业分配（partnership allocation）」就想填 Schedule 1 第 129 行，没分清会计准备与税法准备、合伙经营收入与合伙资本利得的通道差异。Seeing 'reserves' and thinking of Schedule 13, or seeing 'partnership allocation' and thinking of Schedule 1 line 129, without distinguishing accounting reserves from tax reserves, or partnership business income from partnership capital gains.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提到 accounting bad debt/warranty reserve（应走 Schedule 1 第 414/126 行）或 partnership allocated taxable capital gains（应走公司自己的 Schedule 6）。The question mentions an accounting bad debt or warranty reserve (which goes through Schedule 1 lines 414/126) or taxable capital gains allocated by a partnership (which go through the corporation's own Schedule 6).

第 1 题Q1税会调整与应税收入综合计算Combined calculation of tax and accounting adjustments and taxable income★★★陷阱：俱乐部会费误按餐饮娱乐扣 50%

An engineering firm with a December 31, 2026 year-end reports net profit after tax of $180,000 on its financial statements (GIFI code 9999). In arriving at that profit, the bookkeeper deducted all of the following expenses: book depreciation of $42,000; client meals and entertainment of $16,000; annual golf club membership dues for a director of $9,500; an increase in the accounting allowance for doubtful accounts of $8,000; charitable donations to registered charities of $5,000; and book income tax expense of $38,000. Schedule 8 capital cost allowance (CCA) for the year is $55,000. What are the firm's net income for tax purposes (Schedule 1 result) and taxable income for 2026?

一家年结日为 2026 年 12 月 31 日的工程公司，其财务报表上的税后净利润（GIFI 代码 9999）为 $180,000。在计算该账面利润时，记账员已经扣除了以下全部项目：账面折旧 $42,000；客户餐饮与娱乐费 $16,000；一位董事的高尔夫俱乐部年费 $9,500；会计坏账准备增加额 $8,000；向注册慈善机构的慈善捐赠 $5,000；以及账面所得税费用 $38,000。当年按 Schedule 8 算出的资本成本折让（CCA）为 $55,000。该公司 2026 年的税务净收入（Schedule 1 结果）和应税收入分别是多少？

  1. ✗ 落入「慈善捐赠在 Schedule 1 漏加回」陷阱：算税务净收入（net income for tax purposes）时忘记把账上已扣的 $5,000 慈善捐赠加回（少加回 $5,000 得出 $230,500），随后在算应税收入（taxable income）时又扣了一次 $5,000 得出 $225,500，等于把同一笔捐赠扣了两次。Falls into the trap 'Missing the add-back of a charitable donation on Schedule 1': in computing net income for tax purposes, it forgets to add back the $5,000 charitable donation already deducted in the books (adding back $5,000 too little gives $230,500), and then deducts the $5,000 again in computing taxable income to get $225,500, which deducts the same donation twice.
