本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 定性对比题：给出业主经理的家庭结构与退休储蓄需求，考查工资与分红在公司税前扣除、注册退休储蓄计划（RRSP）额度、加拿大退休金计划（CPP）记录及托儿费扣除资格上的本质差异。Qualitative comparison question: given an owner-manager's family structure and retirement saving needs, tests the essential differences between salary and dividends in corporate deductibility, registered retirement savings plan (RRSP) room, Canada Pension Plan (CPP) records and eligibility for the child care expense deduction.
  • 整合与毛额化计算题：给出加拿大控管私人公司（CCPC）享小企业扣除后的税后利润，要求计算非合资格股息的15%毛额化应税股息金额、联邦9/13股息税收抵免以及卑诗省1.96%省级抵免基数。Integration and gross-up calculation question: given the after-tax profit of a Canadian-controlled private corporation (CCPC) that claimed the small business deduction, compute the taxable amount of a non-eligible dividend with the 15% gross-up, the federal 9/13 dividend tax credit and the BC 1.96% provincial credit base.
  • 家庭分拆收入（TOSI）判定题：设计配偶或成年子女从家族公司领分红的场景，考查合资格股份（年满25岁、双10%持股、非专业公司且服务收入低于90%）、实质参与经营（每周至少20小时）及业主年满65岁三条排除线。Tax on split income (TOSI) question: a spouse or adult child receives dividends from the family corporation; tests the three exclusion lines: excluded shares (age 25 or over, double 10% holding, not a professional corporation, and service income under 90%), substantial involvement in the business (at least 20 hours a week), and an owner aged 65 or over.
  • 股东贷款与公司车福利题：考查股东贷款在借款年公司年结后一年内还清免计s.15(2)本金、但仍须按规定利率计算s.80.4推定利息福利的双轨规则，以及公司车公务用途过半才可打折待命费的条件。Shareholder loan and company car question: tests the two parallel rules for a shareholder loan (repaying within one year after the end of the corporation's tax year in which the loan was made avoids including the principal under s.15(2), but the s.80.4 deemed interest benefit at the prescribed rate still applies), and the condition that business use of a company car must exceed half before the standby charge can be reduced.
  • 合规滑单与卑诗省雇主健康税（EHT）实务题：考查T4、T5与T4A的申报边界、资本股息账户（CDA）T2054 表须不晚于分红成为应付之时提交的时限、超额合资格股息20%罚税，以及卑诗省EHT的$1,000,000免征门槛。Compliance slips and BC Employer Health Tax (EHT) question: tests the boundary between T4, T5 and T4A reporting, the timing rule that Form T2054 for the capital dividend account (CDA) must be filed no later than the time the dividend becomes payable, the 20% tax on excessive eligible dividends, and the BC EHT exemption threshold of $1,000,000.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 股息计入赚取收入陷阱Treating dividends as earned income

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生常把个人现金总收入等同于税法上的赚取收入（earned income），误以为领高额分红也能累积RRSP供款额度或抵扣托儿费。Candidates equate total personal cash income with earned income for tax purposes, wrongly believing that a large dividend can also build RRSP room or support a child care deduction.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现业主经理只领分红不发工资，却计划新增RRSP额度、累积CPP养老金或申报托儿费扣除。The owner-manager takes only dividends and no salary, yet plans to add RRSP room, build a CPP pension or claim a child care deduction.

⚠ TOSI年龄与工时错配陷阱Mismatching the age and hours tests for TOSI

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 容易把合资格股份排除（需年满25岁、非服务及非专业公司）与实质参与经营排除（年满18岁、每周工作满20小时）的条件张冠李戴。Mixing up the conditions of the excluded shares exclusion (age 25 or over, not a services or professional corporation) with those of the excluded business exclusion (age 18 or over, at least 20 hours a week).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现18至24岁成年子女、律师或会计师等专业公司、咨询服务公司，或挂名不干活的配偶领取分红。The question involves an adult child aged 18 to 24, a professional corporation such as a law or accounting firm, a consulting services corporation, or a spouse who does no work but takes dividends.

⚠ 股东贷款免本金即免利息陷阱Assuming no principal inclusion means no interest benefit on a shareholder loan

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 记住了借款年公司年结后一年内还清可免除s.15(2)本金并入所得，却忽略了s.80.4推定利息福利是并行独立的另一条规则。Remembering that repaying within one year after the corporation's year-end avoids including the s.15(2) principal, but overlooking that the s.80.4 deemed interest benefit is a separate rule that runs in parallel.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调股东从公司零息或低息借款并在规定的一年内按期还清本金，询问当年是否完全没有应税福利。The question stresses that a shareholder borrowed from the corporation at zero or low interest and repaid the principal on time within the permitted year, and asks whether there is no taxable benefit at all for the year.

⚠ 控股股东照常缴EI陷阱Paying EI as usual for a controlling shareholder

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把普通员工强制参保雇佣保险（EI）的常识直接套用到控股股东身上，忽略了持股超40%属于不可保雇佣。Applying the ordinary rule that employees must be covered by Employment Insurance (EI) to a controlling shareholder, ignoring that a holding of more than 40% is uninsurable employment.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干说明业主经理持有公司超过40%的有表决权股份并给自己发放工资，询问公司需代扣代缴哪些法定供款。The owner-manager holds more than 40% of the voting shares and pays himself a salary, and the question asks which statutory contributions the corporation must withhold and remit.

⚠ 滑单混报与超额合资格股息陷阱Mixing up slips, and excessive eligible dividends

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为只要是给股东的好处都能上T5享股息抵免，或以为公司随时可以决议发放合资格股息而不管一般税率收入池（GRIP）余额。Believing that any benefit to a shareholder can go on a T5 and earn the dividend credit, or that the corporation may declare eligible dividends at any time regardless of the general rate income pool (GRIP) balance.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提到公司利润全部按小企业税率纳税却派发合资格股息，或将股东私人福利填报在T5滑单上。All of the corporation's profit was taxed at the small business rate but it paid eligible dividends, or a private shareholder benefit was reported on a T5 slip.

第 1 题Q1赚取收入与三项法定资格Earned income and the three statutory qualifications★陷阱：股息计入赚取收入陷阱

Marcus is the sole owner-manager of a British Columbia retail CCPC. Since incorporating three years ago, he has compensated himself exclusively with $100,000 of non-eligible dividends each year and $0 in salary. He has no other income sources. Which statement accurately describes his tax and benefit position for 2026?

Marcus 是一家卑诗省零售类 CCPC 的唯一业主经理。自三年前成立公司以来，他每年全部以 $100,000 非合资格股息的形式给自己发薪酬，工资为 $0，且无其他收入来源。关于他 2026 年的税务与福利状况，下列哪项表述准确？

  1. ✗ 落入股息计入赚取收入陷阱。根据《所得税法》（ITA）s.146(1)，注册退休储蓄计划（RRSP）的赚取收入（earned income）只包含雇佣所得、自营所得及不动产净租金等，股息不在清单内，因此不会产生 $18,000 的 RRSP 额度。Falls into the 'Treating dividends as earned income' trap. Under ITA s.146(1), earned income for the registered retirement savings plan (RRSP) includes only employment income, self-employment income and net rental income from real property, among others; dividends are not on the list, so no $18,000 of RRSP room is generated.

  2. ✓ 正确。股息既不产生加拿大退休金计划（Canada Pension Plan, CPP）可保收入，也被排除在 ITA s.146(1) 的 RRSP 赚取收入（earned income）和 ITA s.63(3) 的托儿费扣除赚取收入之外，只靠分红维生会让这三项资格全部归零。Correct. Dividends neither create Canada Pension Plan (CPP) pensionable earnings nor count as earned income for RRSP room under ITA s.146(1) or for the child care deduction under ITA s.63(3); living on dividends alone reduces all three qualifications to zero.

  3. ✗ 落入股息计入赚取收入陷阱。托儿费扣除额度虽按赚取收入（earned income）的三分之二封顶（ITA s.63(3)），但该定义只认雇佣与自营所得，不含股息或毛额化股息。Falls into the 'Treating dividends as earned income' trap. The child care deduction is indeed capped at two-thirds of earned income (ITA s.63(3)), but that definition recognizes only employment and self-employment income and does not include dividends or grossed-up dividends.

  4. ✗ 混淆了工资与分红在公司层面的扣除规则。工资可以作为经营支出在算税前扣除，而分红是用公司交完税后的留存利润分配的，在公司层面不可扣除。Confuses the deduction rules for salary and dividends at the corporate level. Salary can be deducted before tax as an operating expense, while a dividend is paid out of after-tax retained profit and is not deductible at the corporate level.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Sonia owns 100% of a BC graphic design CCPC. In 2026, her corporation has $150,000 of pre-tax profit eligible for the small business deduction. She wants to create exactly $12,600 of new RRSP contribution room for 2027 and qualify to deduct her toddler's daycare fees as the lower-income spouse. What must she do?

Sonia 100% 持有一家卑诗省平面设计 CCPC。2026 年公司有 $150,000 符合小企业扣除条件的税前利润。她希望为 2027 年创造恰好 $12,600 的新增 RRSP 供款额度，并作为低收入一方具备扣除幼儿托儿费的资格。她必须怎么做？

  1. ✗ 落入股息计入赚取收入陷阱。非合资格股息（non-eligible dividend）不属于赚取收入（earned income），发 $70,000 分红产生的 RRSP 额度和托儿费扣除基数均为 $0。Falls into the 'Treating dividends as earned income' trap. A non-eligible dividend is not earned income, so paying a $70,000 dividend generates $0 of RRSP room and a $0 child care deduction base.

  2. ✗ 落入股息计入赚取收入陷阱。无论股息是否经过 15% 毛额化（dividend gross-up），股息都不计入 ITA s.146(1) 或 s.63(3) 的赚取收入。Falls into the 'Treating dividends as earned income' trap. Whether or not the dividend is grossed up by 15%, dividends are never included in earned income under ITA s.146(1) or s.63(3).

  3. ✗ 混淆了公司留存利润与个人 RRSP 额度生成机制。个人 RRSP 额度必须凭个人上一年 18% 的赚取收入（earned income）产生，不能用公司留存利润直接生成。Confuses retained corporate profit with how personal RRSP room is generated. Personal RRSP room must come from 18% of the individual's prior-year earned income; it cannot be created directly from profit retained in the corporation.

  4. ✓ 正确。发放 $70,000 工资属于雇佣所得（赚取收入［earned income］），既可在下一年产生 18% × $70,000 = $12,600 的 RRSP 供款额度，也能提供托儿费扣除所需的赚取收入基数及 CPP 记录。Correct. Paying $70,000 of salary is employment income (earned income), which generates RRSP room of 18% x $70,000 = $12,600 for the following year, and also provides the earned income base needed for the child care deduction and a CPP record.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：d

第 2 题Q2股息毛额化与税收抵免计算Dividend gross-up and tax credit calculation★★陷阱：滑单混报与超额合资格股息陷阱

A British Columbia CCPC earns $100 of active business income in 2026 that fully qualifies for the federal and BC small business deduction (combined 11% corporate tax rate). It distributes the entire $89 after-tax profit as a non-eligible dividend to its sole shareholder. How is this dividend treated on the shareholder's 2026 personal tax return?

一家卑诗省 CCPC 在 2026 年赚取 $100 积极营业收入，全额享受联邦与卑诗省小企业扣除（合计 11% 公司税率）。公司将税后剩下的 $89 利润全部作为非合资格股息分给唯一股东。这笔股息在股东 2026 年个人税表上如何处理？

  1. ✗ 错用了合资格股息的参数。38% 毛额化、联邦 6/11 抵免和 BC 省 12% 抵免适用于一般税率利润派发的合资格股息（eligible dividend），而非享小企业扣除后的非合资格股息。Uses the parameters of an eligible dividend. 第 6 题Q6公司车待命费与股东福利滑单Company car standby charge and shareholder benefit slips★★★陷阱：滑单混报与超额合资格股息陷阱

A BC corporation purchases an automobile for $50,000 (including taxes) and makes it available to its sole shareholder for all 12 months of 2026. The shareholder drives the car 18,000 km in total during the year, of which 7,000 km is for business and 11,000 km is personal. How is the standby charge calculated, and if the benefit is provided strictly by virtue of shareholding, which slip is used?

一家卑诗省公司以 $50,000（含税）买断一辆汽车，并在 2026 年全年 12 个月提供给唯一股东使用。股东全年共行驶 18,000 公里，其中公务 7,000 公里、私用 11,000 公里。汽车待命费（standby charge）如何计算？若该福利纯因股东身份提供，应使用哪张滑单申报？

  1. ✗ 同时犯了打折条件错误与滑单混报错误。公务里程 7,000 / 18,000 未超过 50%，不能享受按私用公里打折；且股东福利不能报在 T5 上当股息。Makes both an error in the conditions for the reduction and a slip-mixing error. Business use of 7,000 of 18,000 km is not over 50%, so the reduction by personal kilometres is not available, and a shareholder benefit cannot be reported on a T5 as a dividend.

  2. ✗ 混淆了买断车与租赁车的公式及申报表。2/3 是租赁车乘月租的系数，买断车应按每月 2% 乘原始成本；且因股东身份给的福利应报进 T4A 而非 T4。Confuses the formulas for an owned and a leased car and the reporting form. 2/3 is the factor for the monthly lease payment on a leased car; an owned car uses 2% of the original cost per month; and a benefit given because of shareholder status goes on a T4A, not a T4.

  3. ✓ 正确。买断车待命费（standby charge）公式为每月 2% × 原始成本 × 可用月数（ITA s.6(1)(e)/(2)）；只有公务用途超过 50% 时才能按私用公里 ÷（1,667 × 12）打折，本题公务仅 7,000/18,000（低于 50%），按全额 $12,000 算；因股东身份取得的应税福利（taxable benefit）应报在 T4A 上（计入 T1 第 13000 行），不享股息抵免。Correct. The standby charge for an owned car is 2% per month x original cost x months available (ITA s.6(1)(e)/(2)); it can be reduced by personal km / (1,667 x 12) only when business use exceeds 50%, and here business use is only 7,000/18,000 (below 50%), so the full $12,000 applies; a taxable benefit received because of shareholder status goes on a T4A (included on line 13000 of the T1) and does not earn the dividend credit.

  4. ✗ 落入滑单混报陷阱。股东福利报进 T4A 后属于普通所得（报在 T1 第 13000 行），不是股息，不能享受联邦或 BC 省的股息税收抵免（DTC）。Falls into the slip-mixing trap. A shareholder benefit reported on a T4A is ordinary income (on line 13000 of the T1), not a dividend, and does not earn the federal or BC dividend tax credit (DTC).

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A corporation leases a vehicle for $900 per month (including taxes) and makes it available to its shareholder-employee for all 12 months of 2026. He drives 24,000 km in total during the year, including 18,000 km for business and 6,000 km for personal use, and keeps no mileage logbook, estimating the split from memory. How is his standby charge determined?

一家公司以每月 $900（含税）租赁一辆汽车，在 2026 年全年 12 个月提供给股东兼雇员使用。他全年共行驶 24,000 公里，其中公务 18,000 公里、私用 6,000 公里，但未记行车日志，仅凭感觉估算比例。他的汽车待命费如何确定？

  1. ✗ 忽略了行车日志的证据要求。待命费（standby charge）只有在公务用途超过 50% 且备有私用与公务公里记录（行车日志）时才能打折，凭感觉估算无日志只能按全额计算。Ignores the evidence requirement of a mileage log. The standby charge can be reduced only if business use exceeds 50% and a record of personal and business kilometres (a logbook) is kept; an estimate without a log can only be charged in full.

  2. ✗ 混淆了买断车与租赁车的计算公式。每月 2% 适用于买断车的原始成本，租赁车应按 2/3 × 月租计算。Confuses the formulas for an owned and a leased car. The 2% per month applies to the original cost of an owned car, whereas a leased car is charged at 2/3 x the monthly lease payment.

  3. ✗ 误解了待命费减免机制。即便公务过半且有日志，也只是按私用公里 ÷（1,667 × 可用月数）比例打折，只要有私用就不会直接免除为 $0，更何况本题没有行车日志。Misunderstands how the standby charge is relieved. Even with more than half business use and a log, the charge is only reduced in proportion to personal km / (1,667 x months available); as long as there is personal use it never goes straight to $0, and in this case there is no logbook at all.

  4. ✓ 正确。租赁车待命费（standby charge）按 2/3 × 月租 × 可用月数计算（ITA s.6(1)(e)/(2)）；待命费只有在公务用途超过 50% 且有里程日志记录时才能按私用公里 ÷（1,667 × 可用月数）打折，不记行车日志凭感觉估只能按全额 $7,200 计算。Correct. The standby charge for a leased car is 2/3 x monthly lease payment x months available (ITA s.6(1)(e)/(2)); the charge can be reduced by personal km / (1,667 x months available) only when business use exceeds 50% and mileage records exist, so without a logbook an estimate gives only the full $7,200.

答案Answer：d

第 7 题Q7家庭雇员s.67与合规截止日Family employee salary, s.67 and compliance deadlines★★陷阱：TOSI年龄与工时错配陷阱

Gavin owns 100% of a BC software CCPC. In 2026, the corporation pays his 19-year-old daughter $65,000 in salary for updating the company website for two hours a month — a task that an arm's-length freelancer would perform for $3,000 a year. What is the tax consequence of this salary payment, and when are payroll remittances normally due?

Gavin 100% 持有一家卑诗省软件 CCPC。2026 年公司向他 19 岁的女儿支付 $65,000 工资，工作内容是每月花两小时更新公司网站——同等工作找非关联自由职业者每年只需 $3,000。这笔工资支出有何税务后果？常规按月汇缴源头扣缴的截止日是哪天？

  1. ✓ 正确。付给家庭成员的报酬必须通过 ITA s.67 合理性测试（reasonableness test），超出付给陌生人做同等工作公允市价（$3,000）的不合理部分（$62,000）公司不得扣除，但个人已收款项仍可能被追征导致双重不利；此外常规雇主的源头扣缴（payroll source deductions）须在每月 15 日前汇缴。Correct. Pay to a family member must pass the s.67 reasonableness test; the unreasonable portion ($62,000) above the fair market amount an unrelated person would be paid for the same work ($3,000) is not deductible by the corporation, although the individual may still be assessed on what was received, a double disadvantage; also, regular employer payroll source deductions must be remitted by the 15th of the following month.

  2. ✗ 混淆了工资与分红的反避税规则。分割收入税（TOSI）及每周 20 小时排除线管辖的是分红等分拆收入，而支付给家庭成员的雇佣工资受 s.67 合理性测试与 business purpose 测试规制。Confuses the anti-avoidance rules for salary and dividends. Tax on split income (TOSI) and the 20-hour-a-week exclusion apply to split income such as dividends, whereas salary paid to a family member is governed by the s.67 reasonableness test and the business purpose test.

  3. ✗ 误以为按时申报 T4 就能绕过实质审查。虽然 T4 滑单确须在次年 2 月最后一天前申报，但徒有其名或远超公允市价的家庭成员工资仍会被 CRA 按 s.67 打回扣除。Wrongly assumes that filing the T4 on time bypasses substantive review. A T4 must indeed be filed by the last day of February of the following year, but a salary to a family member that is nominal or far above fair market value can still be denied by the CRA under s.67.

  4. ✗ 错配了罚税条款。20% 的 Part III.1 罚税专门针对公司超一般税率收入池（GRIP）余额错发合资格股息的行为，不适用于超额工资。Mismatches the penalty provision. The 20% Part III.1 penalty tax is aimed specifically at a corporation paying eligible dividends in excess of its general rate income pool (GRIP) balance and does not apply to excess salary.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

An owner-managed CCPC in BC hires the owner's spouse as the full-time office and purchasing manager, paying her $45,000 in 2026. Comparable arm's-length managers in the local market earn between $42,000 and $48,000. Which statement correctly describes the deductibility and filing requirements for her remuneration?

一家卑诗省业主经理 CCPC 雇佣业主配偶担任全职办公室兼采购经理，2026 年支付工资 $45,000。当地市场上非关联同类岗位经理的年薪在 $42,000 至 $48,000 之间。下列哪项准确描述了该笔薪酬的扣除资格与申报要求？

  1. ✗ 混淆了工资合理性测试与 TOSI 满 65 岁分红排除。真实付出劳动、按公允市价领工资的配偶一直合法可扣，无需等业主满 65 岁。Confuses the salary reasonableness test with the TOSI age-65 dividend exclusion. A spouse who really works and is paid fair market salary is always deductible, with no need to wait until the owner is 65.

  2. ✓ 正确。配偶真实提供服务且 $45,000 工资处于同类岗位市场公允区间（$42,000–$48,000）内，满足 business purpose 测试与 s.67 合理性测试（reasonableness test），公司可全额税前扣除；T4 滑单须于次年 2 月最后一天前完成发放与申报。Correct. The spouse actually provides services and the $45,000 salary is within the fair market range for the same kind of job ($42,000-$48,000), so it passes the business purpose test and the s.67 reasonableness test and the corporation can deduct it in full; the T4 must be issued and filed by the last day of February of the following year.

  3. ✗ 违反了滑单互斥原则。T4 与 T5 是两张完全独立的表，同一笔钱绝对不能同时上两张单。Violates the rule that slips are mutually exclusive. A T4 and a T5 are two entirely separate slips, and the same money can never be reported on both.

  4. ✗ 混淆了工资申报与资本股息账户（CDA）申报。T2054 当天申报要求仅适用于用 CDA 派发免税资本股息，发工资走的是 RP 薪资账户按月汇缴与次年 2 月底报 T4。Confuses salary reporting with the capital dividend account (CDA) filing. The same-day T2054 filing requirement applies only when a tax-free capital dividend is paid from the CDA; salary goes through the RP payroll account with monthly remittances and the T4 by the end of February.
