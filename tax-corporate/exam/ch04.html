① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 情景定性题：给出网约车、纯送餐、混合接单或短租的业务组合，要求判断适用的所得税申报表（Form T2125 还是 Form T776）、主营业务 NAICS 代码，或首元即须登记 GST/HST 还是适用 $30,000 小供应商门槛，真正考的是按「服务性质与附加服务」而非「是否使用平台」分类。Scenario classification questions: given a combination of ridesharing, pure food delivery, mixed gigs or short-term rentals, you decide which income tax form applies (Form T2125 or Form T776), the main business NAICS code, or whether GST/HST registration is required from the first dollar or the $30,000 small supplier threshold applies; what is really tested is classification by "the nature of the service and any additional services", not by "whether a platform is used".
  • 门槛触发与生效日判定题：给出某日历季度突增的大额配送合同或连续四季度滚动收入数据，要求指出失去小供应商身份的确切时点及哪一笔收入开始计征 GST/HST，真正考的是单季触发（越线当笔立即生效）与四季累计触发（超标季末次月月底失效）两条路径的差异。Threshold trigger and effective date questions: given a large delivery contract that appears in one calendar quarter or rolling income over four consecutive quarters, you identify the exact point at which small supplier status is lost and which income starts to bear GST/HST; what is really tested is the difference between the single-quarter trigger (effective at once on the transaction that crosses the line) and the four-quarter cumulative trigger (effective at the end of the month after the quarter in which the line is crossed).
  • 平台申报规则（DPI）辨析题：设计低单量零工卖家或未提供税号的场景，测试是否误用「30 单且 $2,800」卖货排除线，以及不提供税号的 $500 罚款打在谁身上，真正考的是《所得税法》Part XX 的覆盖范围与信息对账逻辑。Platform reporting rules (DPI) questions: scenarios with a low-volume gig seller or one who gives no tax number test whether you wrongly apply the "30 transactions and $2,800" goods exclusion, and on whom the $500 penalty for not providing a tax number falls; what is really tested is the coverage of Part XX of the Income Tax Act and the logic of reconciling information.
  • CPP 双层供款与分期预缴计算题：给出自雇净收入（如 $50,000）及前两年欠税记录，要求计算一般层供款并拆分 line 22200 扣除与 line 31000 抵免，或判断当年是否触发按季分期预缴，真正考的是自雇现金流管理与双重 $3,000 门槛的「且」字关系。Two-tier CPP and instalment calculation questions: given net self-employed income (such as $50,000) and the prior two years' balance owing, you compute the base-tier contribution and split it between the line 22200 deduction and the line 31000 credit, or decide whether quarterly instalments are triggered for the year; what is really tested is self-employed cash flow management and the "and" relationship between the two $3,000 thresholds.
  • 车辆 CCA 与省级跨界综合题：结合车辆取得当年的网约车使用比例（是否超过 50%）或 BC 省 TNS 牌照合规支出、WorkSafeBC 保障，要求判断多数报税实务下的 CCA 类别（Class 10 30% 与 Class 16 40% 的区分）及费用可否在 T2125 扣除，真正考的是税法定义与劳工法规边界。Vehicle CCA and provincial interface questions: combining the ridesharing use percentage in the year the vehicle is acquired (whether over 50%) or BC TNS licence compliance costs and WorkSafeBC coverage, you decide the CCA class in most filing practice (Class 10 at 30% vs. Class 16 at 40%) and whether the costs are deductible on T2125; what is really tested is the boundary between tax-law definitions and labour regulation.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 网约车误套小供应商豁免Applying the small supplier exemption to ridesharing

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 学习者把纯送餐适用的 $30,000 小供应商门槛推广到所有平台零工，忽略了商业性载客网约车自 2017 年 7 月 1 日起已并入出租车业务定义，从第一元收入起就必须登记并收取 GST/HST，且一旦登记会连带同一账户下的送餐收入一起收税。Learners extend the $30,000 small supplier threshold that applies to pure food delivery to every platform gig, overlooking that commercial ridesharing passenger transport has been part of the taxi business definition since July 1, 2017, must register and collect GST/HST from the first dollar, and that once registered, delivery income in the same account bears tax along with it.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 commercial ridesharing、passenger transport、或者「网约车载客兼做送餐」且年收入低于 $30,000。The stem has commercial ridesharing, passenger transport, or "ridesharing passengers plus food delivery" with annual income under $30,000.

⚠ 只记四季累计、漏看单季越线Remembering only the four-quarter total and missing a single-quarter breach

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 多数人只背了「过去四个日历季度累计不超过 $30,000」，没注意在单一日历季度内越过 $30,000 时没有任何缓冲期，必须从使累计额跨过 $30,000 的那一笔供应起立即计收 GST/HST。Most people memorize only "cumulative total over the past four calendar quarters not exceeding $30,000" and miss that crossing $30,000 within a single calendar quarter has no grace period at all; GST/HST must be collected from the very supply that takes the total over $30,000.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调某单一季度（如 Q3）因接下团体配送合同或临时大单导致当季收入突破 $30,000。The stem stresses that in a single quarter (such as Q3), taking on a group delivery contract or a temporary large order pushes the quarter's income past $30,000.

⚠ 服务类卖家误套卖货排除线Applying the goods exclusion to service sellers

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到 DPI 规则里有「少于 30 单且不超过 $2,800 可排除报告」的数字就直接使用，忘了该豁免仅适用于销售货物（sale of goods），网约车、送餐与不动产短租等个人或租赁服务哪怕只接几单也属于可报告卖家。Seeing the DPI figure "fewer than 30 transactions and not over $2,800 may be excluded from reporting" and applying it directly, forgetting that the exemption applies only to the sale of goods; personal or rental services such as ridesharing, food delivery and short-term rental of real property are reportable sellers even with just a few transactions.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述兼职网约车司机、送餐员或短租房东全年单量少于 30 单、收入仅数百元，问平台是否向 CRA 申报。The stem describes a part-time rideshare driver, delivery worker or short-term rental host with fewer than 30 transactions in the year and income of only a few hundred dollars, and asks whether the platform reports to the CRA.

⚠ CPP 供款全额当成所得扣除Treating the whole CPP contribution as an income deduction

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 既然自雇者要交双倍 CPP，学习者容易直觉认为整笔供款都能从总收入中扣除，忽略了一般层基础供款必须拆成两半：雇主等价部分进 line 22200 扣除，雇员等价部分只能进 line 31000 按最低税率算不可退还抵免（仅增强段及 CPP2 全额扣除）。Since the self-employed pay double CPP, learners instinctively think the whole contribution can be deducted from total income, overlooking that the base-tier contribution must be split in two: the employer-equivalent half goes to the line 22200 deduction, while the employee-equivalent half goes only to the line 31000 non-refundable credit at the lowest rate (only the enhanced portion and CPP2 are deducted in full).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干要求计算自雇 CPP 在 T1 上的税务处理、line 22200 扣除额或 line 31000 抵免额。The stem asks for the tax treatment of self-employed CPP on the T1, the line 22200 deduction amount, or the line 31000 credit amount.

⚠ 首年欠税超标就急着算分期预缴Rushing to instalments because first-year owing is large

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 看到当年净欠税远超 $3,000 就认为当年必须按季分期预缴，忘了触发分期预缴需要「当年净欠税超 $3,000」且「前两个税年至少有一年也超 $3,000」双重条件同时满足。Seeing current-year net owing far above $3,000 and concluding that quarterly instalments are required, forgetting that instalments need both "current-year net owing over $3,000" and "at least one of the prior two tax years also over $3,000".

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干说明纳税人今年刚从受雇转为全职零工，前两年均有充足工资代扣、净欠税低于 $3,000。The stem says the taxpayer just moved from employment to full-time gig work this year, and in both prior years had ample withholding on wages and net owing under $3,000.

第 1 题Q1网约车与送餐的GST/HST登记门槛GST/HST registration threshold for ridesharing vs. food delivery★陷阱：网约车误套小供应商豁免

In 2026, Leo earned $9,500 from passenger ridesharing and $12,000 from restaurant food delivery through digital apps in British Columbia. He has no other business income and his total gross revenue of $21,500 is well below $30,000. Which statement correctly describes Leo's GST/HST registration and collection obligations?

2026 年，里奥在 BC 省通过数字应用从事网约车载客取得 $9,500 收入，从事餐厅送餐取得 $12,000 收入。他没有其他经营收入，全年总收入 $21,500 远低于 $30,000。关于里奥的 GST/HST 登记与收税义务，下列哪项表述正确？

  1. ✗ 错误。这是「网约车误套小供应商豁免」陷阱：商业性网约车载客属于出租车业务（taxi business），不适用 $30,000 小供应商（small supplier）豁免。Wrong. This is the trap "applying the small supplier exemption to ridesharing": commercial ridesharing passenger transport is a taxi business and the $30,000 small supplier exemption does not apply.

  2. ✓ 正确。根据《消费税法》s.123(1) 对出租车业务（taxi business）的定义，商业性网约车载客无小供应商门槛（small supplier threshold），须从第一元收入起登记并收取 GST/HST；且网约车与送餐都做时，一旦登记，同一账户下的送餐收入必须跟着一起收税，不能分开处理。Correct. Under the definition of taxi business in s.123(1) of the Excise Tax Act, commercial ridesharing passenger transport has no small supplier threshold and must register and collect GST/HST from the first dollar; and when a person does both ridesharing and delivery, once registered, the delivery income in the same account must bear tax along with it and cannot be handled separately.

  3. ✗ 错误。这是混合接单常见的「拆账豁免」误区：GST/HST 登记针对的是纳税人主体而非单一应用，一旦因网约车登记为注册人，名下所有应税商业活动（含送餐收入）都必须一并收税。Wrong. This is the common mixed-gig misconception of a "split-account exemption": GST/HST registration attaches to the taxpayer and not to a single app, and once registered because of ridesharing, all taxable commercial activities in that name (including delivery income) must collect tax together.

  4. ✗ 错误。混淆了数字平台申报规则（DPI）中卖货排除线的 $2,800 与小供应商四季累计触发时点；网约车从第一元收入起就必须登记。Wrong. This confuses the $2,800 goods exclusion line in the digital platform reporting rules (DPI) with the four-quarter small supplier trigger point; ridesharing must register from the first dollar.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

In 2026, Clara worked exclusively as an independent food delivery courier in the first half of the year, earning $18,000 without registering for GST/HST. On September 1, 2026, she began accepting passenger ridesharing trips and earned $1,400 from ridesharing and $4,000 from food delivery during the rest of the year.   3. ✗ 错误。前两个税年的条件是「至少有一年」超过 $3,000（2024 或 2025），并非要求前两年全都超过 $3,000。Wrong. The condition for the prior two tax years is "at least one" over $3,000 (2024 or 2025), not that both of the prior two years exceeded $3,000.

  4. ✗ 错误。混淆了加美规则：主动寄出 Form INNS1 提醒信、按信缴纳免利息是加拿大的机制；纳税人自行按安全港规则计算并主动缴纳、不靠税务局提醒是美国的机制。Wrong. This confuses the Canadian and US rules: the CRA proactively mailing a Form INNS1 reminder letter, with payment per the letter free of interest, is a Canadian mechanism; the taxpayer computing and paying on their own under the safe harbour rules without reminders from the tax authority is the US mechanism.

第 6 题Q6网约车与送餐车辆的CCA归类与报税实务CCA classification and filing practice for rideshare and delivery vehicles★★★陷阱：混淆乘用车排除条件与实务CCA类别
