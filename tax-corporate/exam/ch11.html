第 11 章 · 非营利与免税Chapter 11 · Tax-Exempt & Non-Profit Organizations (T2, T1044 & T3)Non-profits and tax-exempt status

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 身份辨析题：给出一个社区社团、共管公寓管理公司或同乡会的章程与实际运作情况，要求判断其是否符合 ITA s.149(1)(l) 的四个要件、能否开具捐款收据，以及与注册慈善机构（registered charity）在 T2 申报义务上的相反待遇。Status identification question: gives the by-laws and actual operation of a community society, a condominium management corporation or a hometown association, and asks whether it meets the four requirements of ITA s.149(1)(l), whether it can issue donation receipts, and how its T2 filing obligation is the opposite of that of a registered charity.
  • T1044 触发条件判定题：列出组织的会费、政府拨款、利息、租金及连续两期的期末资产账面价值，测试考生能否只加总四项被动收入与 $10,000 比较、用「上一期末」总资产与 $200,000 比较，或识别「曾经报过即永久要报」的棘轮规则（ratchet rule）。T1044 trigger question: lists the organization's membership fees, government grants, interest, rent and the year-end book values of its assets for two consecutive periods, and tests whether the candidate can add up only the four passive income items against $10,000, compare total assets at the end of the prior period against $200,000, or recognize the ratchet rule ('once required, always required').
  • 会所视同信托分流题：针对高尔夫球会、游艇会或会员制餐会，考核 s.149(5) 视同生前信托（deemed inter vivos trust）下哪些所得继续免税（会费与餐饮收入）、哪些所得必须改报 T3 课税（财产收入及未用于提供餐饮/娱乐/体育服务的财产资本利得）。Club deemed trust split question: for a golf club, yacht club or membership dining club, tests which income stays exempt under the s.149(5) deemed inter vivos trust (membership fees and dining income) and which must be reported and taxed on a T3 (property income and capital gains on property not used to provide dining, recreational or sporting services).
  • 罚款与程序实务题：对比 T1044 按天计罚（$25/日，最低 $100、封顶 $2,500）与零税额公司迟报 T2 按未付税款比例计罚的差异，以及迟报后「先交表止损、同时随信申请豁免」的实务顺序与 6 年留档期。Penalties and procedure question: compares the T1044 daily penalty ($25 a day, minimum $100, maximum $2,500) with the penalty for a zero-tax corporation filing the T2 late, which is based on a proportion of unpaid tax, and the practical order after being late of 'file first to stop the penalty and request relief with a cover letter', and the 6-year record retention period.
  • 跨税种 BC 雇主健康税计算题：对比一般雇主（$1,000,000 免税额、5.85% 缺口费率）与非营利雇主（$1,500,000 免税额、2.925% 缺口费率、按排他使用的合格地点各享一份免税额）的应缴税额。Cross-tax BC Employer Health Tax calculation question: compares tax payable by a general employer ($1,000,000 exemption, 5.85% notch rate) with a non-profit employer ($1,500,000 exemption, 2.925% notch rate, one exemption for each qualifying location it uses exclusively).

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 零税免报 T2 陷阱Skipping the T2 because there is no tax

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生误以为既然非营利组织免征企业所得税、应纳税额为零，就无需提交 T2 公司所得税申报表，或把注册慈善机构免报 T2 的规则套用到非营利组织上。Candidates wrongly think that because a non-profit organization pays no corporate income tax and tax payable is zero, it need not file a T2 corporation income tax return, or they apply the rule that a registered charity need not file a T2 to a non-profit organization.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 incorporated non-profit organization、condominium corporation 或 zero tax payable，且询问是否需要每年提交 T2。The question has an incorporated non-profit organization, a condominium corporation or zero tax payable and asks whether a T2 must be filed every year.

⚠ T1044 收入混算与当期资产陷阱Mixing income for T1044 and using current-period assets

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 计算 $10,000 门槛时把会费、募捐或政府拨款一起算进去，或用「本期期末」而非「上一会计期末」的总资产去对比 $200,000 门槛。In computing the $10,000 threshold, adding in membership fees, fundraising or government grants, or comparing total assets at the end of the 'current period' instead of the 'prior fiscal period' with the $200,000 threshold.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出会员费/拨款与利息/租金，或同时列出上一期末与本期期末两个资产数字。The question lists both membership fees or grants and interest or rent, or lists the asset figures for both the end of the prior period and the end of the current period.

⚠ 棘轮可逆陷阱Assuming the ratchet can be reversed

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为某年被动收入降到 $10,000 以下且资产降到 $200,000 以下后，当年就可以停止申报 T1044。Thinking that once passive income falls below $10,000 and assets below $200,000 in some year, the organization can stop filing the T1044 that year.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干提到该组织在往年曾因资产或被动收入达标提交过 T1044，而本年度资产与收入均大幅萎缩。The question says the organization filed a T1044 in earlier years because its assets or passive income met the thresholds, while this year its assets and income have shrunk sharply.

⚠ 会所全部免税或全部课税陷阱Treating a club as entirely exempt or entirely taxed

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 面对餐饮、娱乐或体育俱乐部时，要么以为既然是 s.149(1)(l) 组织就全部免税，要么以为触发 s.149(5) 后连会费和用于球场服务设施的土地利得都要交税。Faced with a dining, recreational or sporting club, either assuming everything is tax-free because it is a s.149(1)(l) organization, or assuming that once s.149(5) is triggered even membership fees and gains on land used for the club's service facilities are taxed.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 dining, recreational, or sporting facilities、club 投资账户利息股息，或出售闲置土地与服务用停车场的对比。The question mentions dining, recreational, or sporting facilities, interest and dividends in a club's investment account, or a comparison between selling idle land and a parking lot used for services.

⚠ BC 雇主健康税单限额与共享场地陷阱One BC Employer Health Tax exemption per entity, and shared premises

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 按所得税直觉以为一个非营利法人只能享有一份 $1,000,000 或 $1,500,000 免税额，或误以为每天借用几小时的共享社区中心体育馆也算独立合格地点。By income tax intuition, thinking a non-profit corporation can have only one $1,000,000 or $1,500,000 exemption, or thinking that a shared community centre gymnasium borrowed for a few hours a day is also an independent qualifying location.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及 BC Employer Health Tax (EHT)、非营利雇主拥有多个办公场所、同一栋楼不同楼层，或分时段借用场馆。The question involves BC Employer Health Tax (EHT), a non-profit employer with several offices, different floors of the same building, or borrowing a venue for set time slots.

第 1 题Q1非营利组织四要件与禁开收据The four requirements for a non-profit organization and the ban on receipts★陷阱：零税免报 T2 陷阱

A newly formed neighbourhood heritage society is incorporated as a non-share-capital corporation and operates exclusively for civic improvement under ITA s.149(1)(l). It is not a registered charity. A local business owner offers a donation and asks for an official tax receipt, while the society's treasurer believes the society never needs to register with the CRA or file any tax return because it owes no tax. Which statement is correct?

一家新成立的社区历史文化社团注册为无股本非营利公司，并按 ITA s.149(1)(l) 专为公民改善目的运营，不属于注册慈善机构。一位本地企业主表示愿意捐款并索要正式抵税收据，而社团财务主管认为社团既然不欠税，就既不需要向 CRA 注册，也永远不用交任何申报表。下列哪项说法正确？

  1. ✓ 非营利组织（non-profit organization, NPO）在 s.149(1)(l) 下无需向联邦或省级注册即可享受免税，但不能就捐款或会费开具税务抵扣收据；同时，作为居民公司，即使应纳税额为零，每个税年仍必须提交 T2 公司所得税申报表（T2 return）。A non-profit organization (NPO) under s.149(1)(l) need not register federally or provincially to be tax-exempt, but it cannot issue tax-deductible receipts for donations or membership fees; also, as a resident corporation, it must file a T2 corporation income tax return every tax year even if tax payable is zero.

  2. ✗ 这混淆了非营利组织与注册慈善机构（registered charity）：只有按 s.149(1)(f) 免税的注册慈善机构才需要向 CRA 申请注册并获得开具捐赠收据的权力。This confuses a non-profit organization with a registered charity: only a registered charity exempt under s.149(1)(f) must apply to the CRA for registration and obtain the power to issue donation receipts.

  3. ✗ 这掉进了「零税免报 T2 陷阱」：全年都是注册慈善机构的公司确实免报 T2，但作为公司的非营利组织并不在 T2 免报例外名单中，哪怕零税也必须每年报 T2 或 T2 简表（T2 Short Return）。This falls into the 'Skipping the T2 because there is no tax' trap: a corporation that is a registered charity throughout the year is indeed not required to file a T2, but a non-profit organization that is a corporation is not on the list of exceptions to T2 filing and must file a T2 or a T2 Short Return every year, even with zero tax.

  4. ✗ s.149(1)(l) 看的是「组织并运营」（exclusively organized and operated）两个动词，是逐年的事实判断而非永久保证，且非营利组织对捐款和会费均不能开具税务抵扣收据。Section 149(1)(l) looks at the two verbs 'organized and operated' (exclusively organized and operated), which is a year-by-year factual test, not a permanent guarantee; and a non-profit organization cannot issue tax-deductible receipts for either donations or membership dues.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Compare an incorporated registered charity under ITA s.149(1)(f) with an incorporated non-profit organization (NPO) under ITA s.149(1)(l), assuming neither has any tax payable for the year. How do their rules differ regarding official donation receipts and annual T2 returns?

对比一家符合 ITA s.149(1)(f) 的公司制注册慈善机构与一家符合 ITA s.149(1)(l) 的公司制非营利组织（假设两者当年应纳税额均为零）。它们在开具正式捐赠收据和年度 T2 申报义务上有何不同？

  1. ✗ 非营利组织（non-profit organization, NPO）既不能开具捐赠收据，也不能因为零税而免报 T2。A non-profit organization (NPO) can neither issue donation receipts nor skip the T2 just because tax is zero.

  2. ✗ 注册慈善机构（registered charity）必须经 CRA 注册才能取得身份，且全年均为注册慈善机构的公司免报 T2。A registered charity must be registered with the CRA to obtain its status, and a corporation that is a registered charity throughout the year need not file a T2.

  3. ✓ 注册慈善机构（registered charity）接受 CRA 注册审查后按 s.149(1)(f) 免税、可开具捐赠收据且免报 T2；而作为公司的非营利组织无需注册、不可开收据，但每个税年结束后 6 个月内仍必须申报 T2（或 T2 简表）。Correct. A registered charity, after CRA registration review, is exempt under s.149(1)(f), can issue donation receipts and need not file a T2; an incorporated non-profit organization need not register and cannot issue receipts, but must still file a T2 (or T2 Short Return) within 6 months after the end of each tax year.

  4. ✗ 把两者的待遇完全颠倒了：注册慈善机构既免报 T2 也免报 Form T1044，而非营利公司必须报 T2 且满足 s.149(12) 条件时还要报 T1044。This reverses the treatment of the two completely: a registered charity is exempt from both the T2 and Form T1044, whereas a non-profit corporation must file a T2 and must also file a T1044 when the s.149(12) conditions are met.

第 2 题Q2T1044 四项被动收入门槛The T1044 four-item passive income threshold★★陷阱：T1044 收入混算与当期资产陷阱

An incorporated community sports association exempt under ITA s.149(1)(l) has never previously been required to file Form T1044. Its total assets had a GAAP book value of $150,000 at the end of the prior fiscal period and $198,000 at the end of the current fiscal period. During the current fiscal period, it received $610,000 in membership dues and government grants, $7,000 in rental income, and $3,500 in bank interest. Must it file Form T1044 for the current fiscal period?

一家按 ITA s.149(1)(l) 免税的社区体育协会公司此前从未触发过 T1044 申报义务。其上一会计期末按 GAAP 计算的总资产账面价值为 $150,000，本会计期末为 $198,000。在本会计期内，该协会收到会费与政府拨款共 $610,000、租金收入 $7,000 以及银行利息 $3,500。它本会计期是否必须申报 T1044？

  1. ✗ s.149(12) 的被动收入门槛看的是应税股息、利息、租金、特许权使用费四项的「合计」（total），而不是单看某一项是否超过 $10,000。The passive income threshold in s.149(12) looks at the 'total' of the four items of taxable dividends, interest, rent and royalties, not at whether any single item exceeds $10,000.

一家按 ITA s.149(1)(l) 免税的社区服务机构，2026 日历年的 BC 薪酬总额为 $2,600,000，平均分布在列治文和温哥华两处各自独立租用且排他使用的办公楼（每处薪酬各 $1,300,000）。它 2026 年应缴的 BC 雇主健康税（EHT）是多少？如果这两个部门改为设在同一栋楼的不同楼层，应缴税额又会是多少？

  1. ✓ 在 BC 雇主健康税（Employer Health Tax, EHT）下，非营利雇主的每个排他使用的合格地点（qualifying location）各享一份 $1,500,000 免税额，两处独立办公楼各发薪 $1,300,000 均未超限，应缴为 $0；若同一法人的两个内部部门在同一栋楼的不同楼层，则合算为一个合格地点，仅享一份 $1,500,000 免税额，落在 $1,500,000.01–$4,500,000 缺口区间（notch rate amount），按 2.925% ×（$2,600,000 − $1,500,000）＝ $32,175 计税。Under BC Employer Health Tax (EHT), a non-profit employer gets a $1,500,000 exemption for each qualifying location it uses exclusively; two separate office buildings each paying $1,300,000 do not exceed the limit, so tax is $0; if two internal departments of the same legal entity are on different floors of the same building, they count as one qualifying location with a single $1,500,000 exemption, falling in the $1,500,000.01-$4,500,000 notch rate band, taxed at 2.925% x ($2,600,000 - $1,500,000) = $32,175.

  2. ✗ $50,700（1.95% × $2,600,000）是一般雇主超过 $1,500,000 时的算法；非营利雇主不仅按每个合格地点各享 $1,500,000 免税额，而且单地点要超过 $4,500,000 才按全额计税。$50,700 (1.95% x $2,600,000) is the method for a general employer above $1,500,000; a non-profit employer not only gets a $1,500,000 exemption for each qualifying location, but a single location must exceed $4,500,000 before the full amount is taxed.

  3. ✗ 这掉进了「BC 雇主健康税单限额陷阱」：与联邦所得税一个纳税人一份限额不同，BC 非营利雇主在省内有多个排他使用的合格地点时，每个合格地点各享一份 $1,500,000 免税额。This falls into the 'one BC Employer Health Tax exemption per entity' trap: unlike federal income tax, where a taxpayer gets one limit, a BC non-profit employer with several exclusively used qualifying locations in the province gets a $1,500,000 exemption for each qualifying location.

  4. ✗ 根据 BC 省财政厅口径，同一法人在同一栋楼里的各个内部部门只算一个合格地点（qualifying location），只能合用一份 $1,500,000 免税额。According to the BC Ministry of Finance, the internal departments of the same legal entity in the same building count as only one qualifying location and can share only one $1,500,000 exemption.

How do the BC Employer Health Tax (EHT) rules for a charitable or non-profit employer compare with the rules for a general for-profit employer?

慈善或非营利雇主适用的 BC 雇主健康税（EHT）规则与一般营利雇主的规则相比有何不同？

  1. ✗ 非营利雇主的免税额提高到了 $1,500,000（而非 $1,000,000），且其缺口费率是 2.925%（即 5.85% 的一半），不是 1.95%。The exemption of a non-profit employer is raised to $1,500,000 (not $1,000,000), and its notch rate is 2.925% (half of 5.85%), not 1.95%.

  2. ✓ BC 给慈善与非营利雇主的三重优待（Employer Health Tax, EHT）包括：每个合格地点免税额从 $1,000,000 提至 $1,500,000；缺口区间拉长至 $1,500,000.01–$4,500,000 且缺口费率减半为 2.925%（一般雇主为 5.85%）；与非营利雇主联营的其他雇主无需与它共享免税额。Correct. BC's three preferential treatments of charitable and non-profit employers (Employer Health Tax, EHT) are: the exemption for each qualifying location is raised from $1,000,000 to $1,500,000; the notch band is extended to $1,500,000.01-$4,500,000 and the notch rate is halved to 2.925% (5.85% for general employers); and other employers associated with the non-profit employer need not share the exemption with it.

  3. ✗ 非营利雇主并非无上限免征 BC EHT：单合格地点薪酬在 $1,500,000.01–$4,500,000 之间按 2.925% 计征，超过 $4,500,000 则按全额计税。A non-profit employer is not exempt from BC EHT without limit: payroll of a single qualifying location between $1,500,000.01 and $4,500,000 is taxed at 2.925%, and above $4,500,000 the full amount is taxed.

  4. ✗ $4,500,000 是缺口区间（notch rate amount）的上限而非免税额，且联营雇主不必与非营利雇主共享免税额。$4,500,000 is the upper limit of the notch rate band, not an exemption, and associated employers need not share the exemption with a non-profit employer.

第 7 题Q7年度身份四问与账面价值口径The four annual status questions and the book value basis★★陷阱：T1044 收入混算与当期资产陷阱

At the end of its fiscal period, a non-profit association is performing its annual four-step compliance review to check its ITA s.149(1)(l) status and Form T1044 requirement. When testing the $200,000 asset threshold and the $10,000 passive income threshold under s.149(12), which accounting bases must it use?

在会计期结束时，一家非营利协会正在进行年度四步合规自检，以核实其 ITA s.149(1)(l) 身份及 T1044 申报义务。在测试 s.149(12) 下的 $200,000 资产门槛与 $10,000 被动收入门槛时，该协会必须采用什么会计计量口径？

  1. ✗ 两项口径均错误：资产门槛看的是「上一会计期末」按公认会计原则计算的「账面价值」（而非本期公允价值），被动收入包含「收到或应收」（而非仅实收现金）。Both bases are wrong: the asset threshold looks at the 'book value' as at the end of the 'prior fiscal period' under generally accepted accounting principles (not the fair value of the current period), and passive income includes amounts 'received or receivable' (not only cash actually received).

  2. ✓ 根据 s.149(12) 及 T4117 指南，资产门槛看的是上一会计期末按公认会计原则确定的账面价值（book value under GAAP）是否超过 $200,000；被动收入门槛看的是本会计期内收到或应收（received or receivable，即权责发生制应收也算）的应税股息、利息、租金、特许权使用费四项合计是否超过 $10,000。Under s.149(12) and Guide T4117, the asset threshold looks at whether the book value under generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) at the end of the prior fiscal period exceeds $200,000; the passive income threshold looks at whether the total of the four items of taxable dividends, interest, rents and royalties received or receivable in the current fiscal period (that is, accrual receivables also count) exceeds $10,000.

  3. ✗ 资产看的是全部总资产的 GAAP 账面价值而非仅不动产评估重置价值，且会费等非被动收入不计入 $10,000 门槛。The asset test uses the GAAP book value of total assets, not just the appraised replacement value of real estate, and membership fees and other non-passive income are not counted in the $10,000 threshold.

  4. ✗ 法条看的是上一会计期末时点的总资产账面价值以及本期收到或应收的四项被动收入总额，不采用月均余额或扣减管理费后的净额。The statute looks at the book value of total assets at the end of the prior fiscal period and the total of the four passive income items received or receivable in the current period; they do not use average monthly balances or the net after deducting management fees.

Why must an organization claiming exemption under ITA s.149(1)(l) review its operations at the end of every fiscal period rather than relying on its initial articles of incorporation?

为什么主张按 ITA s.149(1)(l) 免税的组织必须在每个会计期结束时复核其实际运营情况，而不能仅凭最初的公司章程高枕无忧？

  1. ✗ 非营利组织（non-profit organization, NPO）本来就不需要向 CRA 注册，也没有非营利注册号需要每年续期。A non-profit organization (NPO) does not need to register with the CRA in the first place, so there is no non-profit registration number to renew every year.

  2. ✗ s.149(12) 的 $10,000 与 $200,000 门槛自 1993 年以来从未调整，没有指数化机制。The $10,000 and $200,000 thresholds in s.149(12) have not been adjusted since 1993 and there is no indexation mechanism.

  3. ✓ s.149(1)(l) 要求组织「专为」非营利目的「组织并运营」（exclusively organized and operated），这是逐年的事实判定而非一次性证书；每个会计期结束时都须自检章程目的、实际经营是否偏离、是否有任何形式让成员个人受益（personal benefit of any member）以及是否仍非慈善机构。Correct. Section 149(1)(l) requires the organization to be 'exclusively organized and operated' for a non-profit purpose, which is a year-by-year factual determination and not a one-time certificate; at the end of each fiscal period it must self-check its constating purpose, whether actual operation has departed from it, whether there is any form of personal benefit to any member and whether it is still not a charity.

  4. ✗ 6 个月是申报期限，公司的会计期最长可达 53 周、其他主体最长 12 个月；只有主要目的为提供餐饮、娱乐或体育设施的会所才会触发 s.149(5) 视同生前信托。6 months is the filing deadline; a corporation's fiscal period can be up to 53 weeks and for other entities up to 12 months; only a club whose main purpose is to provide dining, recreational or sporting facilities triggers the s.149(5) deemed inter vivos trust.
