第 3 章 · GST/HST 注册与快速法Chapter 3 · GST/HST: Registration, ITCs and the Quick MethodGST/HST registration and the Quick Method

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 情景跨线题：给出某自雇者各季度或名下多门生意的营业额，要求判断失去小供应商（small supplier）身份的具体日期、首笔必须收税的交易以及完成注册的29天期限。Scenario threshold-crossing questions: given a self-employed person's quarterly sales or several businesses held in one name, you decide the exact date the small supplier status is lost, the first transaction on which tax must be collected, and the 29-day deadline to complete registration.
  • 三类供应与进项辨析题：混合应税、零税率（zero-rated supply）与豁免供应（exempt supply），考查哪类业务允许注册并全额抵扣进项税额抵免（input tax credit, ITC）。Three kinds of supply and input tax questions: taxable, zero-rated supply and exempt supply are mixed together, testing which kind of business may register and fully claim input tax credits (ITCs).
  • 快速法与常规法对比计算题：给出营业额与采购成本，要求分别计算快速法（quick method）与常规法下的GST34表第101行、第103行与第109行净税，以及对T2125所得税表应税收入的影响。Quick method vs. regular method calculation questions: given sales and purchase costs, you compute net tax on lines 101, 103 and 109 of Form GST34 under the quick method and the regular method, and the effect on taxable income on the T2125 income tax form.
  • 卑诗省双轨与网约车特例题：考查卑诗省5% GST与7% PST的进项拆算，以及出租车与商业网约车司机首日强制注册、车费含税倒算（5/105）和无GST项目的剔除。BC dual-system and ride-share special rule questions: splitting input tax between BC's 5% GST and 7% PST, and the mandatory registration from day one for taxi and commercial ride-share drivers, fares treated as tax-included (back-calculated at 5/105), and removing items that carry no GST.
  • 合规期限、凭证三档与新政题：考查12月31日财年个体户“4月30日缴款、6月15日申报”的双期限、100元/500元发票信息要求，以及新斯科舍省14% HST和首次购房者GST退税。Compliance deadline, three-tier documentation and new policy questions: the two deadlines for a sole proprietor with a December 31 year end (pay by April 30, file by June 15), the invoice information required at $100 and $500, plus Nova Scotia's 14% HST and the first-time home buyers' GST rebate.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 单季跨线误套缓冲期Applying the grace period to a single-quarter breach

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生只记住“连续四季累计超30,000元有次月末缓冲期”，忽略了单个日历季度内超线必须从跨线那一笔交易起立即收税、毫无缓冲期。Candidates remember only that "exceeding $30,000 over four consecutive quarters gives a grace period to the end of the following month", and overlook that a breach within a single calendar quarter requires collecting tax immediately from the transaction that crosses the line, with no grace period.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现单个日历季度（single calendar quarter）收入突破$30,000，或问跨线当笔交易是否要收GST/HST。The stem has revenue exceeding $30,000 within a single calendar quarter, or asks whether the transaction that crosses the line must collect GST/HST.

⚠ 多门生意分开算门槛Testing the threshold separately for each business

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 误以为$30,000门槛按每个独立店铺或业务单独计算，忘了必须将个人名下全部应税生意（含零税率）及关联人（associates）合并计算。Candidates wrongly think the $30,000 threshold is tested separately for each store or business, forgetting that all taxable businesses held in the individual's name (including zero-rated ones) and those of associates must be combined.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述同一人兼营两份自雇业务（如设计加摄影）或与关联人共同经营，单项均未满$30,000但合计超线。The stem describes the same person running two self-employed businesses (such as design plus photography) or running a business together with an associate, each under $30,000 but over the line in total.

⚠ 快速法第101行填不含税额Entering the amount before tax on line 101 under the quick method

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 习惯了常规法在第101行填不含税收入，在快速法下也用不含税营业额去乘汇款率（remittance rate），或者忘记扣减前$30,000的1%抵免（最高$300）。Used to the regular method where line 101 holds sales before tax, candidates multiply the sales before tax by the remittance rate under the quick method too, or forget to subtract the 1% credit on the first $30,000 (maximum $300).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干指明已选举快速法（quick method），要求计算GST34第101行、第103行或第109行净税金额。The stem says the quick method has been elected and asks for net tax on line 101, line 103 or line 109 of GST34.

⚠ 卑诗省PST与免税项混入ITCLetting BC PST and exempt items into the ITC

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 在卑诗省把发票上5% GST和7% PST合计12%全额填入第106行抵扣，或将不含税的保险费、银行车贷利息按5/105倒算进项税额抵免。In BC, candidates enter the combined 12% (5% GST plus 7% PST) on the invoice in full on line 106, or back-calculate input tax credits at 5/105 on insurance premiums and bank car loan interest, which carry no tax.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干背景在卑诗省（BC），采购发票含7% PST，或费用清单里夹杂车辆保险（insurance）与贷款利息（interest）。The stem is set in BC, a purchase invoice includes 7% PST, or the expense list mixes in vehicle insurance and loan interest.

⚠ 个体户6月15日才缴税A sole proprietor paying tax only on June 15

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 混淆了申报截止日与缴款截止日，以为12月31日财年结束的自雇个体户可以等到6月15日交表时才付款，导致5月1日起被计收不可抵扣的利息。Candidates confuse the filing deadline with the payment deadline and think a self-employed sole proprietor with a December 31 year end can wait until June 15, when the return is filed, to pay, so that non-deductible interest runs from May 1.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现个体经营者（sole proprietor）、12月31日财年结束（December 31 fiscal year-end），询问何时缴清GST/HST欠款才不计息。The stem has a sole proprietor with a December 31 fiscal year-end and asks when the GST/HST balance must be paid to avoid interest.

第 1 题Q1三类供应与进项抵免分野Three kinds of supply and the ITC divide★陷阱：混淆零税率与豁免供应的ITC资格

Elena operates two activities in Vancouver: she exports custom software solely to foreign clients ($85,000 in 2026) and rents out a residential condo on a long-term lease ($28,000 in 2026). How do the GST/HST registration and input tax credit (ITC) rules apply to her two activities?

Elena在温哥华经营两项业务：专门向境外客户出口定制软件（2026年收入$85,000），以及长租一套住宅公寓（2026年租金$28,000）。GST/HST注册与进项税额抵免（ITC）规则如何适用于她的这两项业务？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入“不向客户收税就是豁免供应”的陷阱：软件出口属于零税率供应（zero-rated supply），虽然税率为0%，但仍属应税供应，可注册并全额退回进项税。Wrong. This falls into the trap "no tax charged to the customer means exempt supply": software exports are zero-rated supplies; although the rate is 0%, they are still taxable supplies, may be registered, and the input tax is refunded in full.

  2. ✗ 错误。住宅长租属于豁免供应（exempt supply），既不计入$30,000注册门槛，也不能抵扣与之相关的任何进项税额抵免（ITC）。Wrong. Long-term residential rental is an exempt supply; it neither counts toward the $30,000 registration threshold nor allows any input tax credit (ITC) related to it.

  3. ✓ 正确。出口属于零税率供应（zero-rated supply），本质仍是应税供应（taxable supply），计入$30,000全球应税供应门槛且进项税额抵免（input tax credit, ITC）照常全额抵扣；住宅长租属于豁免供应（exempt supply），不在税网内，不计入门槛且进项一分都不能抵。Correct. Exports are zero-rated supplies, which are still taxable supplies in substance, count toward the $30,000 worldwide taxable supply threshold, and allow input tax credits (ITCs) in full as usual; long-term residential rental is an exempt supply, outside the tax net, does not count toward the threshold and allows no input tax credit at all.

  4. ✗ 错误。把零税率供应与豁免供应的定义完全颠倒了：出口是零税率供应（可抵ITC），住宅长租是豁免供应（不可抵ITC）。Wrong. This completely reverses the definitions of zero-rated and exempt supplies: exports are zero-rated supplies (ITC allowed) and long-term residential rental is an exempt supply (no ITC).

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Marcus runs a neighbourhood pharmacy in Victoria that sells prescription drugs and basic groceries, along with a licensed daycare centre in the adjacent unit. Which statement correctly describes his eligibility to claim input tax credits (ITCs)?

Marcus在维多利亚经营一家销售处方药和基本食品杂货的社区药房，并在隔壁单元经营一家持牌托儿中心。下列哪项准确描述了他申报进项税额抵免（ITC）的资格？

  1. ✓ 正确。处方药与基本食品杂货属于零税率供应（zero-rated supply），对客户的税率为 0，但上游进项税额抵免（ITC）可全额退回；托儿服务属于豁免供应（exempt supply），不能抵扣任何进项税。Correct. Prescription drugs and basic groceries are zero-rated supplies; the rate to the customer is 0, but upstream input tax credits (ITCs) are refunded in full; child care services are exempt supplies and no input tax can be claimed.

  2. ✗ 错误。混淆了零税率与豁免：虽然柜台都不收税，但处方药和基本食品是零税率应税供应，注册后可全额退回进项税。Wrong. This confuses zero-rated with exempt: although no tax is charged at the counter in either case, prescription drugs and basic groceries are zero-rated taxable supplies, and once registered the input tax is fully refunded.

  3. ✗ 错误。将两类供应颠倒了：托儿服务是豁免供应（不可抵ITC），处方药和基本食品是零税率供应（可抵ITC）。Wrong. This reverses the two kinds of supply: child care is an exempt supply (no ITC), while prescription drugs and basic groceries are zero-rated supplies (ITC allowed).

  4. ✗ 错误。即便因为药房业务成为登记人（registrant），专门用于豁免供应（托儿服务）的采购依然不得抵扣任何ITC。Wrong. Even if the pharmacy business makes the person a registrant, purchases used exclusively for exempt supplies (child care) still cannot claim any ITC.

第 2 题Q2小供应商3万门槛与合并计算The $30,000 small supplier threshold and combined calculation★★陷阱：单季跨线误套缓冲期

Kevin is an unregistered sole proprietor in Burnaby who does freelance copywriting and weekend event photography. In Q1 and Q2 of 2026, his combined revenues were $6,000 per quarter. In Q3 (July to September 2026), copywriting brings in $19,000 and photography brings in $15,000, with the invoice that pushed his Q3 combined total past $30,000 issued on September 12, 2026. When does Kevin cease to be a small supplier, and what must he do?

Kevin是本拿比一名未注册的个体经营者，兼做自由文案撰稿与周末活动摄影。2026年第1和第2季度，他两项业务合计收入均为每季$6,000。在第3季度（2026年7月至9月），文案收入$19,000、摄影收入$15,000，其中使第3季度合计突破$30,000的那张发票开具于2026年9月12日。Kevin何时失去小供应商身份？他必须怎么做？

  1. ✗ 错误。落入“多门生意分开算门槛”陷阱：$30,000门槛按人（连同关联人）计算，个体经营者名下所有应税生意必须合并加总（$19,000 + $15,000 = $34,000）。Wrong. This falls into the trap "testing the threshold separately for each business": the $30,000 threshold is measured per person (together with associates), and all taxable businesses held by the sole proprietor must be added together ($19,000 + $15,000 = $34,000).

  2. ✓ 正确。Kevin在第3季度单个日历季度内合并应税供应达$34,000，触发“单季超过$30,000”路径：毫无缓冲期，从9月12日跨线那一笔交易本身起就失去小供应商（small supplier）身份并必须收税，且须在生效日后29天内完成注册。Correct. Kevin's combined taxable supplies reached $34,000 within a single calendar quarter in Q3, triggering the "over $30,000 in a single quarter" path: there is no grace period; he loses small supplier status from the very transaction on September 12 that crossed the line and must collect tax, and must complete registration within 29 days after the effective date.

  1. ✗ 错误。自雇提供出租车与商业网约车（commercial ridesharing）客运服务是全国统一的例外，不适用$30,000门槛，必须从第一天起注册。Wrong. Self-employed taxi and commercial ridesharing passenger services are a nationwide exception, not subject to the $30,000 threshold, and must register from day one.

  2. ✗ 错误。两处违规：一是网约车车费视为含税，应用$21,000 × 5/105 = $1,000倒算销项而非外加5%；二是保险费与车贷利息属于金融服务（豁免供应），本身不含GST，永远不能拆出ITC。Wrong. Two violations: first, ride-share fares are treated as tax-included, so collected tax should be back-calculated as $21,000 x 5/105 = $1,000, not 5% added on top; second, insurance premiums and car loan interest are financial services (exempt supplies), carry no GST themselves, and can never be split out as an ITC.

  3. ✓ 正确。网约车司机必须从第一天起注册；车费视为含税，销项税 = $21,000 × 5/105 = $1,000；可抵进项税额抵免（ITC）= 燃油$3,150 × 5/105（$150）+ 平台服务费GST（$52.50）= $202.50；保险与利息不含GST，不可抵扣ITC。Correct. A ride-share driver must register from day one; fares are treated as tax-included, so tax collected = $21,000 x 5/105 = $1,000; claimable input tax credits (ITCs) = $3,150 of fuel x 5/105 ($150) + GST on the platform service fee ($52.50) = $202.50; insurance and interest carry no GST and earn no ITC.

  4. ✗ 错误。“30笔且$2,800”仅适用于数字平台上卖货的卖家信息报送豁免（且客运服务根本无此豁免），与GST注册无关；此外平台向司机收取的服务费上的GST是完全可抵的进项。Wrong. "30 transactions and $2,800" applies only to the exemption from seller information reporting for sellers of goods on digital platforms (and passenger transportation services have no such exemption at all) and has nothing to do with GST registration; moreover, the GST on the service fee the platform charges the driver is a fully claimable input.

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A registered graphic designer in Victoria, BC buys a computer workstation for her studio for $3,000, paying $150 in federal GST (5%) and $210 in BC PST (7%). She also works part-time delivering restaurant meals by bicycle ($9,000 annual income, her only other activity, with total combined revenues of $26,000 before voluntary registration). How should the $150 GST and $210 PST be treated, and did the meal delivery alone require day-one registration?

卑诗省维多利亚一名已注册的平面设计师为工作室购置一台$3,000的电脑工作站，支付了$150联邦GST（5%）与$210卑诗省PST（7%）。她还兼职骑车送餐（年收入$9,000，自愿注册前两项业务合计$26,000）。这$150 GST和$210 PST应如何处理？仅做送餐服务是否需要从第一天起强制注册？

  1. ✗ 错误。卑诗省7% PST（$210）不是增值税，绝不能进第106行作ITC；且送餐、代购、跑腿不属于“客运服务”，仍适用一般$30,000门槛。Wrong. BC's 7% PST ($210) is not a value-added tax and can never go on line 106 as an ITC; and food delivery, errands and shopping on behalf of customers are not "passenger transportation services" and the general $30,000 threshold still applies.

  2. ✓ 正确。卑诗省发票上12%的税只有5% GST（$150）可填入GST34第106行抵扣进项税额抵免（ITC），7% PST（$210）计入资产成本在所得税上折旧扣除；送餐服务不属于应税客运，不触发首日强制注册规则。Correct. Of the 12% tax on a BC invoice, only the 5% GST ($150) can be entered on GST34 line 106 as an input tax credit (ITC), and the 7% PST ($210) is added to the asset cost and depreciated for income tax; food delivery is not taxable passenger transportation and does not trigger the day-one mandatory registration rule.

  3. ✗ 错误。无论在常规法还是快速法下，用于应税活动的资本资产（capital property）上的5%联邦GST均可合规抵扣ITC。Wrong. Whether under the regular method or the quick method, the 5% federal GST on capital property used in taxable activities may properly be claimed as an ITC.

  4. ✗ 错误。将GST与PST的处理完全颠倒了：可抵ITC的是5% GST（$150），计入成本的是7% PST（$210），且送餐属于应税服务而非豁免供应。Wrong. This completely reverses the treatment of GST and PST: the 5% GST ($150) is claimable as an ITC, the 7% PST ($210) is added to cost, and food delivery is a taxable service, not an exempt supply.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

第 7 题Q7个体户年报与缴款双期限The sole proprietor's annual filing and payment deadlines★陷阱：个体户6月15日才缴税

David is a self-employed architect (sole proprietor) in Vancouver who files his GST/HST return annually and has a December 31, 2026 fiscal year-end. He owes $4,800 in net GST for 2026. What are the statutory deadlines for David to pay the $4,800 net GST and to file his 2026 Form GST34 without incurring interest or late-filing penalties?

David是温哥华一名自雇建筑师（个体经营者），按年申报GST/HST，财年结束日为2026年12月31日。他2026年欠缴净GST $4,800。为避免产生利息或迟报罚款，David缴纳$4,800税款与提交2026年GST34报表的法定期限分别是哪一天？

  1. ✗ 错误。“财年结束后三个月（3月31日）同时申报并缴款”适用于有限公司或非12月31日财年结束的登记人；12月31日财年结束且有经营收入的个体经营者适用特殊的4月30日/6月15日规则。Wrong. "Filing and paying together three months after the fiscal year end (March 31)" applies to corporations or registrants whose fiscal year does not end on December 31; a sole proprietor with business income and a December 31 year end is under the special April 30 / June 15 rule.

  2. ✗ 错误。落入“个体户6月15日才缴税”陷阱：6月15日只是报表提交截止日，税款必须在4月30日前缴清，否则从5月1日起按日计收不可抵扣的利息。Wrong. This falls into the trap "a sole proprietor paying tax only on June 15": June 15 is only the deadline for filing the return; the tax must be paid by April 30, or non-deductible interest runs daily from May 1.

  3. ✓ 正确。同时满足“个体经营者（sole proprietor）、财年12月31日结束、当年有经营收入”三个条件时，GST/HST税款缴付截止日为次年4月30日（April 30），而GST34报表申报截止日为次年6月15日（June 15）。Correct. When all three conditions are met (a sole proprietor, fiscal year ending December 31, business income in the year), the GST/HST payment deadline is April 30 of the following year, and the GST34 filing deadline is June 15 of the following year.

  4. ✗ 错误。把缴款日与申报日颠倒了：政府要求先在4月30日把税款交齐，报表可以宽限到6月15日提交。Wrong. This reverses the payment date and the filing date: the government requires the tax to be paid in full by April 30, while the return may be filed as late as June 15.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A small design corporation in Richmond, BC has a December 31, 2026 fiscal year-end and $420,000 in annual taxable supplies. It files its GST/HST return annually. When are the corporation's 2026 GST34 return and net tax payment due?

卑诗省列治文一家小型设计有限公司，财年结束日为2026年12月31日，年应税供应额为$420,000，按年申报GST/HST。该公司的2026年GST34报表与净税款何时到期？

  1. ✓ 正确。“4月30日缴款、6月15日申报”仅适用于12月31日财年结束且有经营收入的个体经营者（sole proprietor）；有限公司（corporation）缺少“个体经营者”这一条件，退回“财年结束后3个月”的通则，即申报与缴款均在次年3月31日到期。Correct. "Pay by April 30, file by June 15" applies only to a sole proprietor with a December 31 year end and business income; a corporation lacks the condition of being a sole proprietor and falls back to the general rule of 3 months after the fiscal year end, so both filing and payment are due March 31 of the following year.

  2. ✗ 错误。这是个体经营者的专属期限，有限公司不适用4月30日与6月15日规则。Wrong. This is a deadline specific to sole proprietors; the April 30 and June 15 rule does not apply to a corporation.

  3. ✗ 错误。“报告期结束后1个月”是月报和季报的期限；年报的通则是财年结束后3个月（3月31日）。Wrong. "One month after the end of the reporting period" is the deadline for monthly and quarterly filers; the general rule for annual filers is 3 months after the fiscal year end (March 31).

  4. ✗ 错误。混淆了T2企业所得税申报期（财年结束后6个月）与GST/HST年报申报期（财年结束后3个月）。Wrong. This confuses the T2 corporate income tax filing period (6 months after the fiscal year end) with the GST/HST annual filing period (3 months after the fiscal year end).
