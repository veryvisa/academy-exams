本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 时间轴与死线跨年推算题：给出借款日与公司不同的财年结束日（如 12 月 31 日或 1 月 31 日），考查 ITA s.15(2.6)『放款公司税年结束后一年内』的还款死线，以及 s.80.4 与 s.15(2) 哪一条适用。Timeline and cross-year deadline question: gives a loan date and a corporate year-end that differs from the calendar year (for example December 31 or January 31), and tests the ITA s.15(2.6) repayment deadline of 'one year after the end of the lending corporation's tax year' and which of s.80.4 and s.15(2) applies.
  • s.85 选举金额上下限与票据（boot）计算题：给出转让资产的公平市值、税基与非股份对价（boot），考查零税负转让的选举金额填报、boot 超过税基时的当期应税收益，以及 s.85(2.1) 对新发股份实缴资本（PUC）的压减。Section 85 elected amount limits and boot calculation question: gives the fair market value and tax basis of transferred assets and the non-share consideration (boot), and tests how to fill in the elected amount for a tax-free transfer, the immediate taxable gain when boot exceeds the tax basis, and the reduction of the new shares' paid-up capital (PUC) under s.85(2.1).
  • ABIL 三道闸门与双向冲减综合题：结合小企业公司（SBC）过去 12 个月资产达标测试、s.50(1) 年末视同处置选举、2026 税年 ½ 纳入率，以及历年终身资本利得豁免（LCGE）对营业投资损失的两倍冲减规则出题。Combined question on the three gates for an ABIL and the two-way offset: tests the small business corporation (SBC) asset test over the past 12 months, the s.50(1) year-end deemed disposition election, the 2026 inclusion rate of 1/2, and the rule that capital gains deductions claimed in earlier years under the lifetime capital gains exemption (LCGE) reduce a business investment loss at twice the amount.
  • 公司清算股东三步计税题：同时给出清算分配现金、股份类别的 PUC、股东个人的调整成本基础（ACB）与资本股息账户（CDA）余额，考查先算 s.84(2) 视同股息、再切出 s.83(2) 免税资本股息、最后从处置对价剔除视同股息计算资本损益的先后次序。Three-step shareholder tax on a wind-up: gives the cash distributed on liquidation, the PUC of the share class, the shareholder's personal adjusted cost base (ACB) and the capital dividend account (CDA) balance, and tests the order of first computing the s.84(2) deemed dividend, then carving out the s.83(2) tax-free capital dividend, and finally computing the capital gain or loss after removing the deemed dividend from the proceeds.
  • 法定程序、表号与先后顺序题：考查清算解散的七步法定顺序（特别是先拿 TX19 清税证明书再分配财产，否则触发 s.159(2)(3) 法定代表人个人连带责任），以及 T2057 截止日、迟报罚金两档取小、联邦与卑诗省解散决议门槛差异。Statutory procedure, form numbers and sequence question: tests the seven statutory steps of a wind-up and dissolution (especially obtaining the TX19 clearance certificate before distributing property, failing which the legal representative has personal liability under s.159(2)(3)), the T2057 deadline, the lesser-of-two-tiers late-filing penalty, and the difference between the federal and BC dissolution resolution thresholds.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 用借款日加一年算还款死线Counting the repayment deadline as one year from the loan date

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉认为『一年内还清』是从拿到钱那天往后推 365 天，忽略了条文写的是『放款公司那个税年结束之后再往后一年』，或被年底还清年初再借的表面流水迷惑。Intuition says 'repaid within a year' runs 365 days from the day the money was received, ignoring that the statute counts one more year after the end of the lending corporation's tax year, or being misled by apparent cash flows such as repaying at year-end and borrowing again early in the next year.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现具体放款日期与非日历年的公司财年结束日（如 January 31 year-end），或出现年末还款、次年年初再借的连续动作。The question gives both a specific loan date and a corporate year-end that is not the calendar year (such as a January 31 year-end), or describes a repayment at year-end followed by a new loan early next year.

⚠ s.85 拿超额票据仍以为零税负Assuming a s.85 transfer is tax-free even with excess boot

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 只记得 s.85 可以把选举金额填成税基实现零税递延，忘了选举金额的法定下限是所收非股份对价（boot）的公平市值，也忘了新股 PUC 会被压到『选举金额减 boot』。Remembering only that s.85 lets the elected amount be set at tax basis for a tax deferral, but forgetting that the statutory floor of the elected amount is the fair market value of the non-share consideration (boot), and that the PUC of the new shares is reduced to the elected amount minus boot.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现 promissory note、cash 或 debt assumption（即 boot）金额大于所转让资产的税基（ACB/UCC），或询问转让后新发行股份的 PUC。The amount of a promissory note, cash or assumed debt (boot) is greater than the tax basis (ACB/UCC) of the transferred assets, or the question asks for the PUC of the shares issued after the transfer.

⚠ ABIL 误用 2/3 纳入率或漏算豁免冲减Using a 2/3 inclusion rate for ABIL or forgetting the exemption offset

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 受旧版考试资料未生效提案影响，误按 66.67% 计算 ABIL；或忽略以前年度申报过的 s.110.6 资本利得扣除要按两倍冲减本年营业投资损失（BIL），以及 ABIL 仅能结转 10 年的限制。Misled by old exam material based on a proposal that never took effect, the candidate computes the ABIL at 66.67%; or ignores that a s.110.6 capital gains deduction claimed in earlier years reduces this year's business investment loss (BIL) at twice the amount, and that an ABIL carries forward only 10 years.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干计算 2026 税年的 ABIL 金额、提及纳税人往年曾使用过终身资本利得豁免（LCGE / capital gains deduction），或问第 10 年之后未用完的 ABIL 去向。The question computes the ABIL for the 2026 tax year, mentions that the taxpayer used the lifetime capital gains exemption (LCGE / capital gains deduction) in an earlier year, or asks what happens to an ABIL not used after year 10.

⚠ 清算时拿个人 ACB 算视同股息Using the personal ACB to compute the deemed dividend on a wind-up

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把公司层面的实缴资本（PUC）与股东溢价买股形成的个人调整成本基础（ACB）混为一谈，直接用分配款减 ACB 算收益，导致视同股息与资本损失全错。Confusing the corporation-level paid-up capital (PUC) with the shareholder's personal adjusted cost base (ACB) from buying shares at a premium, then subtracting the ACB from the distribution to compute the gain, which gets both the deemed dividend and the capital loss wrong.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时给出 shares' paid-up capital (PUC) 和 shareholder's adjusted cost base (ACB) 两个不同数值（如从前合伙人手中溢价收购股份）。The question gives two different numbers for the shares' paid-up capital (PUC) and the shareholder's adjusted cost base (ACB) (for example from buying shares at a premium from a former partner).

⚠ 先分财产后办清税证明或注销后才选 CDADistributing property before the clearance certificate, or making the CDA election after dissolution

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为把公司账上现金分给股东只是内部转账，分完钱再去申请清税证明书（TX19），或者先把公司注销才想起要作 s.83(2) 资本股息选举。Thinking that giving the corporation's cash to shareholders is just an internal transfer and applying for the clearance certificate (TX19) afterwards, or dissolving the corporation first and only then remembering the s.83(2) capital dividend election.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述董事或清算人在取得 clearance certificate (TX19) 之前向股东分配剩余财产，或询问 CDA 选举与公司注销（dissolution）的先后顺序。The question describes directors or a liquidator distributing remaining property to shareholders before obtaining the clearance certificate (TX19), or asks about the order of the CDA election and the dissolution.

第 1 题Q1股东贷款一年例外死线与雇员例外One-year loan exception deadline and the employee exception★★陷阱：用借款日加一年算还款死线

Ms. Gao owns 100% of the shares of Cedar Design Inc., which has a December 31 taxation year-end. On March 1, 2026, she withdraws an $80,000 interest-free loan from the corporation to renovate her principal residence. It is not part of a series of loans and repayments. By what date must she repay the loan so that the $80,000 principal is not included in her 2026 income under ITA s.15(2)?

高女士持有雪松设计公司 100% 的股份，该公司税年结束日为 12 月 31 日。2026 年 3 月 1 日，她从公司支取一笔 $80,000 的无息借款用于装修自住房，且不属于一系列借与还。她最迟必须在哪一天之前还清这笔钱，才能使 $80,000 本金不按 ITA s.15(2) 计入她 2026 年的收入？

  1. ✗ 落入了『用借款日加一年算还款死线』的陷阱；ITA s.15(2.6) 的一年起算点是放款公司该税年结束之日（2026-12-31），而不是放款当天（2026-03-01）。Falls into the 'Counting the repayment deadline as one year from the loan date' trap; the starting point of the one year in ITA s.15(2.6) is the end of the lending corporation's tax year (2026-12-31), not the day of the loan (2026-03-01).

  2. ✓ 首先，s.15(2.4) 的雇员股东购房例外（employee-shareholder exception）仅限『取得（购买）』供本人居住的住宅，装修不在其中，且她持股 100% 属指定雇员（specified employee）；因此只能走 s.15(2.6) 的一年内还清例外，死线为放款公司税年结束日（2026-12-31）之后一年，即 2027-12-31。First, the employee-shareholder home purchase exception in s.15(2.4) covers only 'acquiring (buying)' a dwelling for the person's own use, and renovation is not included; she also holds 100% of the shares and is a specified employee; so only the one-year repayment exception in s.15(2.6) is available, and the deadline is one year after the end of the lending corporation's tax year (2026-12-31), that is, 2027-12-31.

  3. ✗ 混淆了本金还款死线与利息支付死线；次年 1 月 30 日（日历年结束后 30 天内）是支付实际利息以抵减 s.80.4 视同利息利益（deemed interest benefit）的截止日。Confuses the deadline for repaying principal with the deadline for paying interest; January 30 of the next year (within 30 days after the end of the calendar year) is the deadline for paying actual interest to reduce the s.80.4 deemed interest benefit.

  4. ✗ 误读了 s.15(2.4) 住宅例外的适用范围；条文写的是『购买』供本人居住的住宅，借钱装修现有自住房不能适用该条例外。Misreads the scope of the s.15(2.4) residence exception; the provision says 'buying' a home for the person's own use, and borrowing to renovate an existing home cannot use that exception.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Mr. Deng is the sole shareholder of Pacific Supply Ltd., which has a January 31 taxation year-end. On February 1, 2026, he borrows $100,000 from the corporation for personal use (not part of a series of loans and repayments). What is the latest date he can repay the principal to qualify for the one-year repayment exception under ITA s.15(2.6)?

邓先生是太平洋供应公司的唯一股东，该公司税年结束日为 1 月 31 日。2026 年 2 月 1 日，他因个人用途从公司借款 $100,000（不属于一系列借与还）。为符合 ITA s.15(2.6) 的一年还款例外，他最迟可在哪一天归还本金？

  4. ✗ 把公司清算分配当成了向第三方卖股票；公司清算必须先按 s.84(2) 将超过 PUC 的部分定性为视同股息，再将视同股息从处置对价中剔除。Treats the wind-up distribution as a sale of shares to a third party; on a wind-up, the part above PUC must first be characterized as a deemed dividend under s.84(2), and the deemed dividend is then removed from the proceeds of disposition.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

During its 2026 wind-up, a corporation has an actual CDA balance of $40,000, but the director mistakenly files an s.83(2) capital dividend election for $50,000. What is the tax consequence under ITA s.184, and what relief is available?

在 2026 年清算过程中，一家公司的实际 CDA 余额为 $40,000，但董事误按 $50,000 递交了 s.83(2) 资本股息选举。按 ITA s.184 会产生什么税务后果，又有什么救济途径？

  1. ✗ 超额选举不会使 CDA 真实余额内的 $40,000 免税部分自动作废；惩罚针对的是超出的 $10,000 部分。An excessive election does not automatically void the $40,000 tax-free part that is within the real CDA balance; the penalty targets only the $10,000 excess.

  2. ✓ 根据 ITA s.184，资本股息（capital dividend）选举金额超过资本股息账户（CDA）余额的部分（$10,000），公司要缴 3/5 的第三部分税（Part III tax）；救济是在超额评税通知书发出后 90 天内再作一次选举，把超额部分改按应税股息处理。Correct. Under ITA s.184, the part of a capital dividend election that exceeds the capital dividend account (CDA) balance ($10,000) bears 3/5 Part III tax for the corporation; the relief is to make another election within 90 days after the notice of assessment for the excess, treating the excess as a taxable dividend.

  3. ✗ 混淆了不同条文的罚则；每月 $100、封顶 $8,000 是 s.85(8) 资产转让选举或 s.220(3.2) 迟报选举的罚金，不是 s.184 超额资本股息的第三部分税。Confuses the penalties of different provisions; $100 a month capped at $8,000 is the penalty for s.85 transfer elections or late-filed elections under s.220(3.2), not the Part III tax on an excessive capital dividend under s.184.

  4. ✗ CDA 超额选举与终身资本利得豁免（LCGE）没有自动抵扣关系，超额部分触发的是公司层面的 3/5 第三部分税。There is no automatic offset between an excessive CDA election and the lifetime capital gains exemption (LCGE); the excess triggers 3/5 Part III tax at the corporate level.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

第 6 题Q6清算解散法定顺序与清税证明书责任Statutory order of wind-up and dissolution and clearance certificate liability★★陷阱：先分财产后办清税证明或注销后才选 CDA

Mr. Fang is the sole director and liquidator of a corporation winding up in 2026 with $300,000 in cash. Eager to close the books, he distributes the $300,000 to the shareholders first and plans to file the final T2 return and Form TX19 next month. What is the legal consequence under ITA s.159(2) and (3)?

方先生是一家在 2026 年清算的公司的唯一董事兼清算人，公司账上有 $300,000 现金。为尽快结账，他先把 $300,000 分给了股东，打算下个月再去报最后一张 T2 和递交 TX19 表。按 ITA s.159(2)(3) 会产生什么法律后果？

  1. ✓ 根据 ITA s.159(2)(3)，法定代表人（legal representative）在向股东分配公司财产之前，必须先凭 TX19 取得清税证明书（clearance certificate）；未取得证书就先分钱的，在已分配财产价值（$300,000）的范围内对公司欠缴的税款、利息与罚金承担个人连带责任。Correct. Under ITA s.159(2)(3), before distributing the corporation's property to shareholders, the legal representative must first obtain the clearance certificate with TX19; one who distributes without a certificate is personally and jointly liable for the corporation's unpaid tax, interest and penalties up to the value of the property distributed ($300,000).

  2. ✗ RC145 只是最后一步用来关闭营业号下各程序账户的申请表，不能免除未取得 TX19 清税证明书就先行分配财产所触发的 s.159(3) 个人责任。RC145 is only the application form in the last step for closing the program accounts under the business number, and it cannot remove the s.159(3) personal liability triggered by distributing property without first obtaining the TX19 clearance certificate.

  3. ✗ $8,000 是迟报选举的封顶罚金；s.159(3) 的个人责任上限是『已分配财产的价值』（本例高达 $300,000），而非 $8,000。$8,000 is the maximum penalty for a late election; the cap of personal liability under s.159(3) is 'the value of the property distributed' (as high as $300,000 here), not $8,000.

  4. ✗ 税法并不宣告转账在公司法上无效，而是直接依据 s.159(3) 让先分钱的法定代表人自己掏腰包赔付公司欠税。The tax law does not declare the transfer void under corporate law; instead s.159(3) makes the legal representative who distributed first personally liable to pay the corporation's tax.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：a

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Which sequence correctly orders the mandatory steps for winding up and dissolving a corporation and closing its CRA accounts?

下列哪项正确排列了公司清算解散及关闭 CRA 账户的法定步骤顺序？

  1. ✗ 把最致命的两步全做反了：不能在拿到 TX19 清税证明书之前分钱，且公司一旦注销就不存在了，CDA 选举必须在注销前完成。Reverses the two most fatal steps: money cannot be distributed before the TX19 clearance certificate is obtained, and once the corporation is dissolved it no longer exists, so the CDA election must be completed before dissolution.

  2. ✗ 顺序颠倒：申请 TX19 清税证明书必须附上『最终 T2 的评税通知书』，因此必须先报最终 T2 并拿到评税通知书才能申请 TX19，而 RC145 关户排在最后一步。The order is reversed: the TX19 application must attach the final T2 notice of assessment, so the final T2 must be filed and its notice of assessment received before applying for TX19, and the RC145 closing of accounts comes last.

  3. ✗ 在还没申报最终 T2 并取得最终 T2 评税通知书（含 Schedule 100 资产分配信息）之前，无法完成 TX19 清税证明书的申请。Before the final T2 is filed and its notice of assessment (including Schedule 100 asset distribution information) is obtained, the TX19 clearance certificate application cannot be completed.

  4. ✓ 正确的七步法定顺序是：①股东决议 → ②变现资产清偿债务 → ③报最终 T2（第 078 行勾『是』）→ ④凭最终 T2 评税通知书等三份材料交 TX19 申请清税证明书（clearance certificate）→ ⑤收到清税证明书 → ⑥向股东分配剩余财产并在注销前完成 s.83(2) CDA 选举 → ⑦用 RC145 关闭营业号程序账户。The correct seven statutory steps are: (1) shareholder resolution, (2) realize assets and pay debts, (3) file the final T2 (answer 'Yes' on line 078), (4) apply for the TX19 clearance certificate with three documents including the final T2 notice of assessment, (5) receive the clearance certificate, (6) distribute the remaining property to shareholders and complete the s.83(2) CDA election before dissolution, (7) close the program accounts under the business number with RC145.

第 7 题Q7s.50(1)视同处置选举年份与实物分配s.50(1) deemed disposition election year and distributions in kind★★陷阱：先分财产后办清税证明或注销后才选 CDA

A corporation ceased operations in 2023 and by the end of 2024 met all four conditions under ITA s.50(1)(b) (insolvent, ceased business, shares had nil fair market value, and reasonably expected to be dissolved without resuming business). The shareholder only remembers to claim an ABIL when preparing his 2026 T1 return. How must this be handled?

一家公司在 2023 年停业，到 2024 年末同时满足了 ITA s.50(1)(b) 的四个条件（资不抵债、停止经营、股份公平市值为零、且可合理预期将解散而不会重新开业）。股东直到准备 2026 年 T1 报税表时才想起要申报 ABIL。应当如何处理？

  1. ✗ 忽略了 s.50(1) 触发年份的唯一性；四个条件在某一个年末同时成立的那一年（2024 年）就是唯一可以正常作 s.50(1) 选举的年份，不能随便挪到 2026 年。Overlooks that the year in which s.50(1) is triggered is unique; the one year in which the four conditions are all met at year-end (2024) is the only year in which the s.50(1) election can be made in the normal way, and it cannot be moved to 2026 at will.

  2. ✗ 虽然错过了 2024 当年的正常申报，但税法在 s.220(3.2) 下留有税年结束后十个日历年内的迟报选举救济窗口，并非永久丧失。Although he missed the normal filing for 2024, the tax law leaves a late-election relief window under s.220(3.2) of ten calendar years after the end of the tax year, so the loss is not permanent.

  3. ✓ 第 50(1) 条选举（subsection 50(1) election）只能在四个条件于年末同时成立的那一年（2024 年）作出；既然该年已过去，只能按 s.220(3.2) 走迟报选举（窗口为税年结束后十个日历年，罚金为 $8,000 与 $100 × 完整月数取小）。A subsection 50(1) election can be made only in the year (2024) in which the four conditions all hold at year-end; since that year has passed, a late election under s.220(3.2) is the only way (the window is ten calendar years after the tax year-end, and the penalty is the lesser of $8,000 and $100 x full months).

  4. ✗ 根据 s.39(1)(c)，向关联方（如配偶）转让股份不能触发营业投资损失（BIL）。Under s.39(1)(c), a transfer of shares to a related person (such as a spouse) cannot trigger a business investment loss (BIL).

答案Answer：c

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Instead of selling its delivery van and computers for cash before winding up under ITA s.88(2), a corporation transfers those capital assets directly to its sole shareholder on the wind-up. How does s.88(2) treat this distribution at the corporate level?

一家公司在按 ITA s.88(2) 清算前没有把送货车和电脑卖成现金，而是直接在清算时将这些资本资产过户给唯一股东。s.88(2) 在公司层面如何处理这次分配？

  1. ✓ 根据 ITA s.88(2)，清算（winding-up）时分给股东的每一项财产均按公平市值视同处置，公司当年要认列相应的收入回收、折旧回收与资本利得，把车和电脑直接过户给股东并非零税事件。Correct. Under ITA s.88(2), on a winding-up each property distributed to shareholders is deemed disposed of at its fair market value, and the corporation recognizes the resulting income inclusions, recapture of depreciation and capital gains in the year; transferring the van and computers directly to the shareholder is not a tax-free event.

  2. ✗ 普通小公司向个人股东清算适用的是 s.88(2) 而非免税结转；财产按公平市值视同处置，不存在自动按未折旧资本成本（UCC）零税过户的待遇。A wind-up of an ordinary small corporation into individual shareholders falls under s.88(2), not a tax-free rollover; the property is deemed disposed of at fair market value, and there is no automatic transfer at undepreciated capital cost (UCC) with no tax.

  3. ✗ 清算分配是股东身份下的分配，公司在 s.88(2) 下按市值视同处置资产，股东则按 s.84(2) 与资本损益规则计税，不是雇佣福利。A liquidation distribution is made to shareholders in their capacity as shareholders; the corporation is deemed to dispose of the assets at market value under s.88(2) and the shareholder is taxed under s.84(2) and the capital gain and loss rules; it is not an employment benefit.

  4. ✗ 违反了『公司与股东是两个独立纳税主体』的底层逻辑；财产在清算离开公司那一刻即按公平市值视同处置。Violates the underlying logic that the corporation and the shareholder are two separate taxpayers; the property is deemed disposed of at fair market value the moment it leaves the corporation on liquidation.
