本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 资格门槛辨析题：给出自雇者在家办公与外出执业的混合场景，考查家庭办公室（business-use-of-home expenses）两道替代性门槛（主要营业地或专用且经常持续当面会客），测试考生是否被「在家工作时间长」或「经常视频会议」带偏。Eligibility threshold questions: a self-employed person works partly at home and partly out of an outside practice, and you are tested on the two alternative thresholds for business-use-of-home expenses (principal place of business, or exclusive use and regular, continuous use for meeting clients in person), to see whether you are misled by "many hours worked at home" or "frequent video meetings".
  • 自雇与雇员规则对比题：列出水电暖、物业税（property tax）、房屋保险、按揭利息（mortgage interest）与按揭本金，测试谁能扣按揭利息、谁永远不能扣按揭本金或资本性装修。Self-employed vs. employee comparison questions: utilities, property tax, home insurance, mortgage interest and mortgage principal are listed, testing who can deduct mortgage interest and which items (mortgage principal, capital renovations) can never be deducted.
  • 扣除顺序与净收入限制题：给出扣除家庭办公室支出前的经营净收入与分摊后的家庭办公室支出总额，考查 T2125 先算车辆与普通费用及 CCA、最后算家庭办公室且不得制造或扩大经营亏损的规则，以及超出部分向后结转的条件。Deduction order and net income limit questions: you are given business net income before the home office deduction and the total of allocated home office expenses, testing the rule that T2125 first computes vehicle and ordinary expenses and CCA, the home office last, with no creating or increasing a business loss, and the conditions for carrying the excess forward.
  • 车辆分摊、记录与多重税费综合题：结合基准年抽样法（base year method）的 3 个月与 10% 浮动条件、Class 10.1 资本成本上限（$39,000）及处置规则、联邦精选豪华品税（Select Luxury Items Tax）与 BC 省车辆 PST 分档税率，测试多层规则互不替代的计算逻辑。Combined questions on vehicle allocation, records and multiple taxes: the base year method with its 3 months and 10% variance condition, the Class 10.1 capital cost cap ($39,000) and disposition rules, the federal Select Luxury Items Tax and BC's tiered vehicle PST rates, testing the logic that layers of rules do not replace one another.
  • CCA 分类与 2026 首年折旧新政题：考查 $500 上下工具的类别分界（Class 8 vs Class 12）、Class 53 截止新增并转入 Class 43、加速投资激励（AII）恢复 1.5 倍加成、Classes 44/46/50 首年全扣，以及制造加工建筑 100% 即时费用化仍停留在 Bill C-31 未生效的立法状态区分。CCA classes and the new 2026 first-year depreciation rules: the $500 tool threshold between classes (Class 8 vs. Class 12), the closing of Class 53 to new additions with a move to Class 43, the Accelerated Investment Incentive (AII) restoring the 1.5 times enhancement, Classes 44/46/50 full first-year write-off, and the legislative status distinction that 100% immediate expensing for manufacturing and processing buildings is still in Bill C-31 and not in force.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 工时与视频会客混淆陷阱Mixing up hours worked with video client meetings

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生误以为「每周在家工作很多小时」或「经常在专用房间与客户开视频/电话会议」就符合家庭办公室资格，忽略了 CRA 要求必须是主要营业地（principal place of business）或专门用于当面（面对面）接待客户。Candidates wrongly think that "working many hours a week at home" or "often holding video or phone meetings with clients in a dedicated room" qualifies for the home office, overlooking that the CRA requires either the principal place of business or space used exclusively for meeting clients in person (face to face).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「另有租用工作室/门店/诊所」且在家仅处理账务、回复订单或通过电话/视频会议联系客户。The stem says there is "another rented studio, shop or clinic" and that at home the person only handles accounts, answers orders or contacts clients by phone or video.

⚠ 雇员与自雇清单串位陷阱Swapping the employee and self-employed lists

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生要么把雇员不能扣按揭利息（mortgage interest）的限制套到自雇者头上导致少扣，要么误以为按揭本金（mortgage principal）或疫情期雇员专属的临时统一费率法（按天数定额扣）自雇者也能用。Candidates either apply the employee restriction on deducting mortgage interest to the self-employed and deduct too little, or wrongly think that mortgage principal, or the temporary flat rate method (a per-day amount) that was available to employees only during the pandemic, can also be used by the self-employed.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现 mortgage interest、mortgage principal、property tax 或按在家工作天数定额申报的表述。The stem mentions mortgage interest, mortgage principal, property tax, or a flat amount based on days worked at home.

⚠ 家庭办公室制造亏损陷阱Creating a loss with the home office

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生直接用分摊后的家庭办公室支出全额冲减净收入算出负数经营亏损，忘了 s.18(12) 规定家庭办公室支出最多只能将经营净收入扣到 $0，超出部分只能结转下年对着同一业务使用。Candidates subtract the full allocated home office expenses from net income and arrive at a negative business loss, forgetting that s.18(12) says home office expenses can reduce business net income only to $0, and the excess can only be carried forward to the next year against the same business.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出的扣除家庭办公室支出前净收入低于分摊后的家庭办公室支出金额。The stem gives net income before the home office deduction that is lower than the allocated home office expense amount.

⚠ 豪车上限与处置混同陷阱Confusing the luxury car cap with disposition rules

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把超过法定上限的乘用车放进普通 Class 10 按实际买价提折旧，或误以为 Class 10.1 在处置当年一分钱折旧都不能提、或还要算追加折旧（recapture）与期末亏损（terminal loss）。Candidates put a passenger vehicle above the statutory cap into ordinary Class 10 and depreciate it at its actual purchase price, or wrongly think that Class 10.1 allows not a cent of depreciation in the year of disposition, or that recapture and terminal loss still have to be calculated.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现高价乘用车（如 $132,000）、Class 10.1、资本成本上限 $39,000 或处置上年年末仍在 Class 10.1 中的车辆。The stem has a high-priced passenger vehicle (such as $132,000), Class 10.1, the $39,000 capital cost cap, or a vehicle still in Class 10.1 at the end of the year before disposition.

⚠ 新政已宣布与已生效混淆陷阱Confusing announced with in force

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把全部投资激励一概当成「2026 已生效」或「已全部废止」，分不清 C-15 已恢复的 AII/Classes 44/46/50 首年全扣、2025 年底截止新增的 Class 53，以及仍在 Bill C-31 中、尚未立法的制造加工建筑 100% 即时费用化，或误以为车辆豪华税已随飞机船舶一起终止。Candidates treat all the investment incentives as "in force for 2026" or "all repealed", and cannot tell apart the AII and Classes 44/46/50 first-year write-off restored by C-15, Class 53 closed to new additions at the end of 2025, and the 100% immediate expensing for manufacturing and processing buildings that is still in Bill C-31 and not yet law; or they wrongly think the vehicle luxury tax ended along with the aircraft and boat tax.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干涉及 2026 年购置制造加工机器设备、制造加工建筑（Bill C-31）、电脑硬件（Class 50）或超过 $100,000 的乘用车豪华税。The stem involves manufacturing and processing machinery acquired in 2026, manufacturing and processing buildings (Bill C-31), computer hardware (Class 50), or the luxury tax on a passenger vehicle over $100,000.

第 1 题Q1家庭办公室两道替代门槛与当面会客Two alternative home office thresholds and meeting clients in person★★陷阱：工时与视频会客混淆陷阱

Maya is a self-employed Speech therapist who rents a treatment room at a commercial clinic where she conducts all in-person patient sessions. Each evening, she spends six to eight hours in a spare room at home updating patient charts, billing, and holding video consultations with parents. Can Maya deduct business-use-of-home expenses on Form T2125?

Maya 是一名自雇语言治疗师，在一家商业诊所租用治疗室进行所有面对面患者诊疗。每天晚上，她在家中的备用房间花六到八小时更新病历、处理账单并与家长进行视频咨询。Maya 能否在 T2125 表上申报家庭办公室支出？

  1. ✓ 根据 s.18(12) 及 Income Tax Folio S4-F2-C2，家庭办公室支出（business-use-of-home expenses）须满足两道替代门槛之一：要么是主要营业地（principal place of business），要么专门用于赚取营业收入并经常持续用于当面（面对面）接待客户。Maya 的主要营业地是商业诊所，且 CRA 明确电话或视频会议不算当面会客，因此两条门槛均不满足。Under s.18(12) and Income Tax Folio S4-F2-C2, business-use-of-home expenses must meet one of two alternative thresholds: the space is either the principal place of business, or it is used exclusively to earn business income and regularly and continuously to meet clients in person (face to face). Maya's principal place of business is the commercial clinic, and the CRA is clear that phone or video meetings do not count as meeting in person, so neither threshold is met.

  2. ✗ 这落入了「工时与视频会客混淆陷阱」：「花了很多时间在家工作」从来不是法定资格判据，只要另有主要营业地且未在家当面接待客户，耗时再长也不能扣。This falls into the trap "mixing up hours worked with video client meetings": spending a lot of time working at home has never been the statutory test; if there is another principal place of business and clients are not met in person at home, no amount of time makes the expenses deductible.

  3. ✗ 这落入了「工时与视频会客混淆陷阱」：CRA 对「见客户（meet clients）」沿用最传统的面对面含义，电话或视频会议不算数。This falls into the trap "mixing up hours worked with video client meetings": the CRA gives "meet clients" its most traditional face-to-face meaning, and phone or video meetings do not count.

  4. ✗ 这落入了「雇员与自雇清单串位陷阱」：疫情期按天数定额扣的临时统一费率法（2020–2022）从一开始就只对雇员开放且早已作废，自雇者从未适用过该方法。This falls into the trap "swapping the employee and self-employed lists": the temporary flat rate method based on days worked (2020-2022) during the pandemic was open only to employees from the start and has long been discontinued; the self-employed never qualified for it.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Liam is a self-employed business coach who has no outside office and performs all his research, preparation, and client coaching from a dedicated home study using video calls. He never meets clients face to face at home. Is Liam eligible to claim business-use-of-home expenses?

Liam 是一名自雇商业教练，没有外部办公室，所有研究、备课与客户辅导均在家中一间专用书房通过视频通话完成，从不在家面对面见客户。Liam 是否有资格申报家庭办公室支出？

  1. ✗ 这落入了把两条替代门槛当成必须同时满足的陷阱：虽然视频会议不满足第二条「当面会客」门槛，但只要家是主要营业地，就不需要靠第二条门槛过关。This falls into the trap of treating the two alternative thresholds as both required: although video meetings do not satisfy the second threshold of meeting clients in person, as long as the home is the principal place of business there is no need to pass the second threshold.

  2. ✗ s.18(12) 的两条门槛是替代关系（满足其一即可），并不要求同时满足两条。The two thresholds of s.18(12) are alternatives (meeting either one is enough); both are not required at once.

  3. ✓ 家庭办公室的两条门槛互为替代：由于 Liam 没有其他营业场所，家中的书房就是其主要营业地（principal place of business），直接满足第一条门槛，无需再满足第二条当面会客要求。The two home office thresholds are alternatives: since Liam has no other place of business, the study at home is his principal place of business, which satisfies the first threshold directly with no need to also meet the second in-person requirement.

  4. ✗ 这落入了「雇员与自雇清单串位陷阱」：自雇者满足门槛后不仅可以扣水电暖，还能按比例扣物业税、房屋保险以及按揭利息。This falls into the trap "swapping the employee and self-employed lists": once a self-employed person meets the threshold, he can deduct not only utilities but also, proportionally, property tax, home insurance and mortgage interest.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

A BC business owner is comparing the provincial PST tiers on passenger vehicles purchased from a GST-registered dealer in 2026. Which statement correctly describes the BC PST rate thresholds and the federal luxury tax scope in 2026?

一名 BC 省企业主正在对比 2026 年从 GST 注册经销商处购买乘用车的省级 PST 分档税率。哪项陈述正确描述了 2026 年的 BC PST 税率分界及联邦豪华税适用范围？

  1. ✗ GST 注册商出售普通乘用车低于 $55,000 为 7%（私人交易才有 12% 等税率），而 $150,000 起为 20%（$57,000 至不足 $125,000 才是 10%）。A GST registrant selling an ordinary passenger vehicle charges 7% below $55,000 (private transactions carry rates such as 12%), and 20% from $150,000 (the 10% tier is $57,000 to under $125,000).

  2. ✗ ZEV 的前三个高档起点提高到了 $75,000、$76,000、$77,000；且飞机与船舶的联邦豪华税已在 2025-11-04 之后由 C-15 终止。For a ZEV the starting points of the first three high tiers are raised to $75,000, $76,000 and $77,000; and the federal luxury tax on aircraft and boats was ended by C-15 after 2025-11-04.

  3. ✓ GST 注册商出售普通乘用车：低于 $55,000 为 7%，$55,000 至不足 $56,000 为 8%，$56,000 至不足 $57,000 为 9%，$57,000 至不足 $125,000 为 10%，$125,000 至不足 $150,000 为 15%，$150,000 起为 20%（ZEV 前三个起点改为 $75,000、$76,000、$77,000）；同时联邦豪华税在飞机与船舶部分终止后，对超 $100,000 的车辆依然适用。A GST registrant selling an ordinary passenger vehicle: 7% below $55,000, 8% from $55,000 to under $56,000, 9% from $56,000 to under $57,000, 10% from $57,000 to under $125,000, 15% from $125,000 to under $150,000, and 20% from $150,000 (for a ZEV the first three starting points become $75,000, $76,000 and $77,000); and after the aircraft and boat part of the federal luxury tax ended, it still applies to vehicles over $100,000.

  4. ✗ 车辆豪华税是「总价的 10%」与「超出 $100,000 部分的 20%」两者取较小值，绝不是按全部车价的 20% 征收。The vehicle luxury tax is the lesser of "10% of the total price" and "20% of the amount over $100,000"; it is never 20% of the whole vehicle price.

第 7 题Q72026加速投资激励与即时费用化三态2026 Accelerated Investment Incentive and the three states of immediate expensing★★★陷阱：新政已宣布与已生效混淆陷阱

In 2026, a proprietor acquires three new capital assets that become available for use before 2027: general computer hardware (Class 50), manufacturing machinery, and a new manufacturing building. Given the 2026 legislative status (Bill C-15 enacted, Bill C-31 not yet enacted), how do the first-year CCA rules apply?

2026 年，一名业主购置了三项在 2027 年前可供使用的新资本资产：一般计算机硬件（Class 50）、制造加工机器设备，以及一栋新建制造加工建筑。按 2026 年的立法状态（Bill C-15 已御准、Bill C-31 尚未立法），首年 CCA 规则如何适用？

  1. ✗ 这落入了「新政已宣布与已生效混淆陷阱」：制造加工建筑 100% 即时费用化仍在 Bill C-31 中、尚未立法；且 Class 53 已于 2025 年底截止新增。This falls into the trap "confusing announced with in force": 100% immediate expensing for manufacturing and processing buildings is still in Bill C-31 and not yet law; and Class 53 closed to new additions at the end of 2025.

  2. ✗ Bill C-15 已于 2026-03-26 御准，逆转了加速投资激励（AII）的退坡并恢复首年增强扣除，同时落实了 Classes 44/46/50 的首年全扣。Bill C-15 received royal assent on 2026-03-26, reversing the phase-out of the Accelerated Investment Incentive (AII) and restoring the enhanced first-year deduction, and it also enacted the full first-year write-off for Classes 44/46/50.

  3. ✓ 必须把 2026 年的三态分清：① 2024-04-16 起取得且 2027 前可用的 Classes 44/46/50 生产性资产首年全扣已由 Bill C-15 御准生效；② Class 53 到 2025 年底截止新增，2026 年新购制造机器设备归入 Class 43（普通年率 30%），但它若在 2025 年取得本会归 Class 53，作为 RIIP 首年可 100% 扣除，2029 年后才可供使用的开始退坡；③ 制造加工建筑 100% 即时费用化仍在 Bill C-31 中、尚未立法，属于「已宣布、非现行规则」，2026 申报须按现行规则处理。You must keep the three states of 2026 apart: (1) the full first-year write-off for Classes 44/46/50 productive assets acquired from 2024-04-16 and available for use before 2027 is in force through the royal assent of Bill C-15; (2) Class 53 closed to new additions at the end of 2025, and newly purchased manufacturing machinery in 2026 goes into Class 43 (ordinary 30% rate), but if it would have gone in Class 53 had it been acquired in 2025, it can be deducted 100% in the first year as RIIP, with phase-out starting only for property available for use after 2029; (3) 100% immediate expensing for manufacturing and processing buildings is still in Bill C-31 and not yet law, which is "announced, not a current rule", and 2026 filing must follow the current rules.

  4. ✗ 这把两个法案的内容颠倒了：Classes 44/46/50 生产性资产首年全扣已在 Bill C-15 中御准生效，而制造加工建筑 100% 即时费用化才在未御准的 Bill C-31 里。This reverses the content of the two bills: the full first-year write-off for Classes 44/46/50 productive assets was enacted in Bill C-15, which received royal assent, while 100% immediate expensing for manufacturing and processing buildings is in Bill C-31, which has not.

A small manufacturer buys new processing equipment in 2026 and wants to know why she cannot put it into Class 53 or simply multiply by a flat 30% half-year rule without checking acquisition dates. Which explanation is accurate for the 2026 tax year?

  2. ✗ Class 53 已于 2025 年底截止新增；且 CCA 是否申报可自行选择（不申报或申报低于上限均合法）。Class 53 closed to new additions at the end of 2025; and whether to claim CCA is the taxpayer's choice (not claiming, or claiming less than the maximum, is lawful).
