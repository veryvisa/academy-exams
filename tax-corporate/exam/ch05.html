第 5 章 · 要不要注册公司Chapter 5 · Sole Proprietor or IncorporateWhether to incorporate

本章掌握度：Chapter mastery: 未测

① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 概念辨析题：给你一位自雇者把当年全部利润以工资或股息取回家花掉的场景，考你能否区分税务递延（tax deferral）与真正省税——只有留在公司里不分配的利润才享受11%低税率带来的递延价值，全部花掉时由于税收整合原理（principle of integration）加合规成本，注册反而倒贴。Concept distinction questions: a self-employed person takes home all the year's profit as salary or dividends and spends it, and you must tell tax deferral from genuine tax savings; only profit left undistributed in the company enjoys the deferral value of the 11% low rate, and when it is all spent, the principle of integration plus compliance costs mean that incorporating actually loses money.
  • 税率与门槛计算题：对比2026税年BC省个人最高边际税率53.5%与CCPC小企业综合税率11%（差额42.5个百分点）、一般公司综合税率27%，以及被动收入递减（passive income grind，$50,000起、$150,000归零）和应税资本递减（$10M至$50M）对$500,000业务限额（business limit）的影响。Rate and threshold calculation questions: compare the 2026 tax-year BC top personal marginal rate of 53.5% with the CCPC small business combined rate of 11% (a gap of 42.5 percentage points) and the general corporate combined rate of 27%, and the effect on the $500,000 business limit of the passive income grind (starting at $50,000, reaching zero at $150,000) and the taxable capital grind ($10M to $50M).
  • 个人服务业务（PSB）判定与税率题：描述一位只服务单一客户、像挂名雇员的独立顾问注册公司后的税务后果，考你PSB综合税率45%的构成（联邦基本28%无一般税率减免＋PSB附加税5%＋BC一般税率12%）、费用扣除受限规则，以及「全年雇有超过5名全职员工」或「为关联公司提供服务」两条法定豁免。Personal services business (PSB) determination and rate questions: describing the tax consequences after an independent consultant who serves only one client, like a disguised employee, incorporates; you must know the composition of the PSB combined rate of 45% (federal base 28% with no general rate reduction + PSB additional tax 5% + BC general rate 12%), the restricted expense deductions, and the two statutory exemptions: "more than five full-time employees throughout the year" or "providing services to an associated corporation".
  • 公司合规与董事连带责任情景题：考你T2申报截止日（年结后6个月）与欠税到期日（年结后2个月，符合条件的CCPC为3个月）的时间差、无经营也须申报T2的规则，以及董事对未缴代扣税款和GST/HST的个人连带责任（卸任后2年追责期与勤勉尽责抗辩）。Corporate compliance and director joint liability scenario questions: the gap between the T2 filing deadline (6 months after year end) and the balance-due date (2 months after year end, 3 months for a qualifying CCPC), the rule that a T2 must be filed even with no activity, and directors' personal joint liability for unremitted source deductions and GST/HST (a 2-year limitation period after leaving office and the due diligence defence).
  • 跨辖区注册成本与长期规划对比题：对比联邦注册（$200网上注册费＋BC省外注册$380）与BC省内直接注册（$380）的费用差异，以及出售合格小企业公司股份（QSBCS）适用2026税年$1,275,000终身资本利得豁免（LCGE）与独资出售资产无法豁免的根本区别。Cross-jurisdiction registration cost and long-term planning comparison questions: compare the cost difference between federal incorporation ($200 online fee + BC extra-provincial registration $380) and direct BC incorporation ($380), and the fundamental difference that the sale of qualified small business corporation shares (QSBCS) can use the 2026 tax-year lifetime capital gains exemption (LCGE) of $1,275,000, while the sale of a sole proprietorship's assets cannot be sheltered.

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ 混淆「税务递延」与「永久免税」Confusing tax deferral with permanent tax exemption

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生看到CCPC小企业综合税率只有11%，就误以为只要注册公司不管怎么花钱都能省税，忽略了利润一旦取出到个人手上还要按整合机制补交个人税，且每年多出T2会计费和年报费。Candidates see that the CCPC small business combined rate is only 11% and wrongly think that incorporating saves tax however the money is spent, overlooking that once profit is taken out to the individual, personal tax is topped up through integration, and every year adds extra T2 accounting and annual report fees.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「全部取出用于家庭日常开销」「年底公司账上不留存利润」或「年利润较低（如$58,000）且全部花完」。The stem has "all taken out for everyday family spending", "no profit left on the company's books at year end" or "low annual profit (such as $58,000) that is all spent".

⚠ 把「单一客户」绝对等同于个人服务业务（PSB）Treating "single client" as absolutely equal to a personal services business (PSB)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生记住单一客户会被定性为PSB并按45%征税，却漏看《所得税法》明文规定的两条豁免：全年雇有超过5名全职员工（more than five full-time employees），或服务对象是关联公司（associated corporation）。Candidates remember that a single client gets the arrangement classified as a PSB taxed at 45%, but miss the two exemptions written in the Income Tax Act: more than five full-time employees throughout the year, or the services being provided to an associated corporation.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干虽然写「全年只服务一家客户」，但同时提到「公司全职雇有6名（或超过5名）程序员/员工」或「客户为关联公司」。The stem says "serves only one client all year" but also mentions that "the company employs 6 (or more than 5) full-time programmers/employees throughout the year" or that "the client is an associated corporation".

⚠ 误以为独资出售资产也能用终身资本利得豁免（LCGE）Thinking a sole proprietor selling assets can also use the lifetime capital gains exemption (LCGE)

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生记得2026税年有$1,275,000的终身资本利得豁免额（对应扣除上限$637,500），但没注意该豁免只针对处置合格小企业公司股份（QSBCS），独资经营直接卖设备、存货或商誉根本不能用。Candidates remember the $1,275,000 lifetime capital gains exemption for the 2026 tax year (with a corresponding deduction limit of $637,500), but miss that it applies only to dispositions of qualified small business corporation shares (QSBCS); a sole proprietor directly selling equipment, inventory or goodwill cannot use it at all.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干写的是「sole proprietor selling business assets / goodwill（个体户出售生意资产或商誉）」而非「selling shares of a QSBCS（出售合格小企业公司股份）」。The stem says "sole proprietor selling business assets / goodwill" rather than "selling shares of a QSBCS".

⚠ 误以为有限责任公司能隔绝所有税务个人追责Thinking a limited liability company shields against all personal tax liability

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生把公司法上的商业债务有限责任套用到全部税款上，不知道《所得税法》s.227.1规定公司拖欠工资代扣税款（payroll source deductions）或未缴GST/HST时，在任董事须承担个人连带责任。Candidates apply the limited liability of corporate law for business debts to all taxes, not knowing that s.227.1 of the Income Tax Act makes the directors in office personally jointly liable when the company defaults on payroll source deductions or unremitted GST/HST.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现「拖欠代扣所得税或GST/HST」「挂名董事配偶不参与日常管理」或「卸任董事未满2年」。The stem has "unpaid withholding income tax or GST/HST", "a nominal director spouse who takes no part in daily management", or "a director who resigned less than 2 years ago".

⚠ 忽略仅在BC一省经营时联邦注册须叠加「省外注册费」Overlooking that federal incorporation with business only in BC needs an added extra-provincial fee

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 考生只看到联邦网上注册费$200比BC省内注册费$380便宜、年报费$12比BC省$43.39便宜，忘了联邦公司在BC省实际经营还必须额外办理BC省外注册（extra-provincial registration）再交$380。Candidates see only that the federal online fee of $200 is cheaper than the BC fee of $380 and the annual return of $12 cheaper than BC's $43.39, forgetting that a federal company actually operating in BC must also complete BC extra-provincial registration and pay $380.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干强调「仅在BC省一个城市经营本地业务（如温哥华餐馆或素里水电维修）」并问哪种注册方式首年注册规费更低。The stem stresses "operating a local business in just one city in BC (such as a Vancouver restaurant or Surrey plumbing and electrical repair)" and asks which registration route has the lower first-year registration fees.

第 1 题Q1递延本质与利润全取出时的成本倒贴The nature of deferral and the loss when all profit is taken out★★陷阱：混淆「税务递延」与「永久免税」

Liam and Chloe run a home-renovation business in Surrey, BC that earns $58,000 in net profit in 2026. They spend every dollar of profit each year on their mortgage and family groceries, leaving nothing retained in the business. A friend tells them to incorporate immediately to get the 11% small business tax rate. Based on 2026 rules, what is the true tax and cost outcome if they incorporate?

Liam和Chloe在BC省素里经营一家家居维修生意，2026年净利润为$58,000。他们每年把每一分利润都花在房贷和家庭日常开销上，生意账上分文不留。朋友建议他们立刻注册公司以享受11%的小企业税率。根据2026年规则，如果他们注册公司，真实的税务与成本结果是什么？

  1. ✗ 跌入「混淆税务递延与永久免税」陷阱。11%的小企业综合税率只对留在公司里不分配的利润产生递延（tax deferral）；一旦利润全部取出花掉，个人层面还要补税，省税效果基本消失。This falls into the trap "confusing tax deferral with permanent tax exemption". The 11% small business combined rate produces tax deferral only on profit left in the company undistributed; once all profit is taken out and spent, personal tax must be topped up and the tax saving essentially disappears.

  2. ✓ 正确。当利润全部取出用于个人开销时，税收整合原理（principle of integration）使总税负与自雇相差无几，递延价值趋近于零，但每年却要多付BC省年度维持费$43.39及高昂的T2专业报税费，净支出反而增加。Correct. When profit is all taken out for personal spending, the principle of integration makes the total tax close to that of self-employment and the deferral value approaches zero, but every year there is an extra BC annual maintenance fee of $43.39 and substantial T2 professional filing fees, so net spending actually increases.

  3. ✗ 个人服务业务（PSB）取决于服务关系是否实质等同于雇员（incorporated employee），与公司净利润是否低于$100,000无关。A personal services business (PSB) depends on whether the service relationship is in substance the same as that of an employee (incorporated employee), and has nothing to do with whether the company's net profit is under $100,000.

  4. ✗ 所有加拿大居民公司无论当年应税所得是否为零，每个税年都必须提交T2申报表（T2 Corporation Income Tax Return）。Every resident Canadian corporation must file a T2 Corporation Income Tax Return for every tax year, whether or not taxable income is zero for the year.

看答案与每个选项的解析Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong

答案Answer：b

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

Nora is a self-employed graphic designer in Burnaby, BC earning $55,000 net profit in 2026. She withdraws 100% of her earnings every month to pay rent and living expenses. Why does incorporating her business in 2026 fail to reduce her overall annual cost?

Nora是BC省本拿比的一名自雇平面设计师，2026年净利润为$55,000。她每个月将100%的收入取出来支付房租和生活费。为什么在2026年注册公司无法降低她的年度总成本？

  1. ✗ $500,000是联邦与BC省小企业扣除的业务限额上限（business limit），不是最低起征门槛。$500,000 is the upper limit of the business limit for the federal and BC small business deduction, not a minimum starting threshold.

  2. ✗ 股息税收抵免（DTC）没有$100,000公司利润的最低门槛限制。The dividend tax credit (DTC) has no minimum threshold of $100,000 of corporate profit.

  3. ✗ 5%附加税是《所得税法》s.123.5针对个人服务业务（PSB）公司留存利润征收的税项，并非针对正常提取利润的附加税。The 5% additional tax is the tax under s.123.5 of the Income Tax Act on the retained profit of a personal services business (PSB) company, and is not a surtax on ordinary profit withdrawals.

  4. ✓ 正确。税务递延（tax deferral）只在利润留在公司账上不分配时才成立；把钱全部取出来花掉时个人层面的税照交，再加上固定的公司年报费和T2会计费，总体反而倒贴。Correct. Tax deferral holds only when profit stays on the company's books undistributed; when all the money is taken out and spent, personal tax is still paid, and together with the fixed corporate annual report fee and T2 accounting fees, the overall result is actually a net loss.

第 7 题Q7被动收入递减与分拆收入税三道防线Three guardrails: passive income grind and tax on split income★★★陷阱：误以为小企业$500,000低税率限额无条件固定不变

A profitable BC CCPC has accumulated a large investment portfolio inside the corporation and also wants to pay dividends to the founder's adult family members who do not work in the business. Under the anti-avoidance rules governing corporations, what happens when the corporation's passive investment income exceeds $50,000 and reaches $150,000?

一家盈利丰厚的BC省CCPC在公司内部积累了庞大的投资组合，同时还想向不参与公司经营的创始人成年家庭成员发放股息。在管辖公司的防滥用规则下，当公司的被动投资收入超过$50,000并达到$150,000时会发生什么？

  1. ✓ 正确。政府围绕小企业低税率设了三道防线：一是被动收入递减（passive income grind），被动投资收入从$50,000起逐步削减$500,000业务限额，到$150,000归零；二是向未实际参与经营的家庭成员发股息会触发分拆收入税（tax on split income, TOSI）按最高档课税；三是个人服务业务（PSB）规则。Correct. The government has set three guardrails around the low small business rate: first, the passive income grind, under which passive investment income above $50,000 progressively reduces the $500,000 business limit, reaching zero at $150,000; second, dividends paid to family members who do not actually take part in the business trigger the tax on split income (TOSI) at the top rate; third, the personal services business (PSB) rules.

  2. ✗ $10M至$50M是应税资本递减（taxable capital grind）的区间，而被动收入递减从$50,000起算、$150,000归零。$10M to $50M is the range of the taxable capital grind, whereas the passive income grind runs from $50,000, reaching zero at $150,000.

  3. ✗ 被动收入超过$150,000只会把$500,000小企业扣除限额削减为零（改按27%一般公司综合税率交税），不会将公司定性为45%税率的个人服务业务（PSB）。Passive income over $150,000 only cuts the $500,000 small business deduction limit to zero (so the income is taxed at the 27% general corporate combined rate); it does not turn the company into a personal services business (PSB) at 45%.

  4. ✗ 向未实质参与经营的家庭成员发放股息会触发分拆收入税（TOSI），按个人最高档税率课税，绝不会按11%课税。Dividends paid to family members who do not substantially take part in the business trigger the tax on split income (TOSI), taxed at the top personal rate, and never at 11%.
