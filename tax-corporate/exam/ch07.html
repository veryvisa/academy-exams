① 这一章怎么考① How this chapter is tested

  • 计算题：给定上一年调整后合计投资收入（AAII）与本年经营利润，计算本年 $500,000 经营限额的削减额（每超 $50,000 一元削 $5）及因此多交的联邦与卑诗省公司税。Calculation question: given last year's adjusted aggregate investment income (AAII) and this year's operating profit, compute the reduction of this year's $500,000 business limit ($5 cut for every $1 over $50,000) and the extra federal and BC corporate tax that results.
  • 概念辨析题：区分合计投资收入（AII，算可退还税，填 Schedule 7 第 1 部分）与调整后合计投资收入（AAII，算限额递减，填 Schedule 7 第 2 部分）的四项差异，特别是处置经营性资产的资本利得与非联结公司股息。Concept question: distinguish aggregate investment income (AII, used for refundable tax, Schedule 7 Part 1) from adjusted aggregate investment income (AAII, used for the business limit grind, Schedule 7 Part 2) on four differences, especially the capital gain on disposing of an active asset and dividends from non-connected corporations.
  • 税率与账户流向题：考查 CCPC 投资收入联邦 38⅔%（含 10⅔% 附加可退还税）与 30⅔% 可退还部分的拆分，以及 Part IV 税（38⅓%）在联结公司与非联结公司之间的判据及进 ERDTOH 还是 NERDTOH。Rates and account-flow question: the split of the federal 38⅔% rate on CCPC investment income (including the 10⅔% additional refundable tax) and the 30⅔% refundable portion; the test for Part IV tax (38⅓%) between connected and non-connected corporations; and whether the amount goes to ERDTOH or NERDTOH.
  • 股息派发与退税顺序题：测试双池 RDTOH 的掏池顺序（非合资格股息先掏 NERDTOH、掏空再动 ERDTOH；合资格股息只掏 ERDTOH）、38⅓% 退税率以及三年报税时效。Dividend payment and refund-order question: the order in which the two RDTOH pools are drawn (non-eligible dividends draw NERDTOH first and ERDTOH only after it is empty; eligible dividends draw ERDTOH only), the 38⅓% refund rate, and the three-year filing limit.
  • 合规与惩罚税情景题：考查资本股息账户（CDA）超额派发的 60% Part III 税与股东连带责任、合资格股息超额指定的 20%/30% Part III.1 税，以及 2026 现行规则下 substantive CCPC 的适用与 $10M–$50M 应税资本递减区间（旧培训材料写 $10M–$15M）。Compliance and penalty-tax scenario: the 60% Part III tax on capital dividends paid above the capital dividend account (CDA) and the shareholders' joint liability; the 20% / 30% Part III.1 tax on excessive eligible dividend designations; and, under the 2026 rules in force, the substantive CCPC and the $10M-$50M taxable capital grind range (old training material says $10M-$15M).

② 高频陷阱② Traps that cost marks

⚠ AII与AAII口径混用Mixing up the AII and AAII bases

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 两者都在 Schedule 7 上计算且名称相似，考生常把处置经营性资产的资本利得算进 AAII，或把非联结公司股息算进 AII。Both are computed on Schedule 7 and the names look alike, so candidates put the capital gain on an active asset into AAII, or put dividends from a non-connected corporation into AII.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干同时出现卖出自用厂房（active asset）或收到上市股票股息（non-connected dividend），并问明年经营限额递减或当年可退还税。The question mentions selling an owner-used factory (active asset) or receiving dividends on listed shares (non-connected dividend), and asks about next year's business limit reduction or this year's refundable tax.

⚠ 被动收入递减错算当年或全额乘5Applying the passive income grind to the wrong year, or multiplying the full AAII by 5

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 直觉以为当年赚投资收入当年就罚，或直接拿 AAII 全额乘以 5，忽略了 $50,000 免削门槛和跨年滞后机制。Intuition says investment income earned this year is penalized this year, or that the full AAII is multiplied by 5. That ignores the $50,000 free threshold and the one-year lag.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出 2025 年与 2026 年两年的投资收入，或给出的 AAII 介于 $50,000 到 $150,000 之间。The question gives investment income for both 2025 and 2026, or gives an AAII between $50,000 and $150,000.

⚠ 联结与联营判据张冠李戴Using the associated test where the connected test applies, or the reverse

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把小企业扣除用的联营（associated，看控制权与亲属 25% 持股）当成 Part IV 税用的联结（connected，看控制或双 10% 门槛）。Treating 'associated' (control and 25% relative shareholdings, used for the small business deduction) as if it were 'connected' (control, or the double 10% test, used for Part IV tax).

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干描述一家公司持有另一家公司 15% 或 20% 的表决权股份及市值，问收到的股息是否按 38⅓% 全额交 Part IV 税。One corporation holds 15% or 20% of the voting shares and market value of another, and the question asks whether the dividend received bears the full 38⅓% Part IV tax.

⚠ 双池RDTOH退税方向搞反Getting the direction of the dual-pool RDTOH refund backwards

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 以为派合资格股息比非合资格股息更高级，可以通吃两个池子，实际上合资格股息只能掏 ERDTOH，非合资格股息才能先掏 NERDTOH 再掏 ERDTOH。Thinking an eligible dividend is a 'better' dividend that can draw from both pools. In fact an eligible dividend draws ERDTOH only, while a non-eligible dividend draws NERDTOH first and then ERDTOH.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干给出公司账上只有 NERDTOH（来自利息或租金）却派发了合资格股息（eligible dividend），问能拿回多少股息退税。The corporation's only balance is NERDTOH (from interest or rent) but it pays an eligible dividend, and the question asks how much dividend refund it gets.

⚠ 资本股息超额与迟报后果混淆Confusing the capital dividend excess with the consequences of late filing

为什么会错：Why people miss it: 把 T2054 迟报的轻微罚款（年化 1% 或每月 $500/12 取小）与派超 CDA 余额的 60% 惩罚性 Part III 税混为一谈，或以为年末才算 CDA 余额。Mixing up the mild late-filing penalty on the T2054 election (the lesser of 1% a year or $500/12 a month) with the 60% punitive Part III tax on paying more than the CDA balance, or assuming the CDA balance is measured at year-end.

题干信号：Signal in the stem: 题干出现资本股息（capital dividend）金额超过派息当日 CDA 余额，或同一年晚些时候发生资本亏损。The capital dividend exceeds the CDA balance on the payment date, or a capital loss arises later in the same year.

第 1 题Q1AII与AAII的四项口径调整The four basis adjustments between AII and AAII★★陷阱：AII与AAII口径混用

In its 2026 tax year, Pinecrest Manufacturing Ltd., a Canadian-controlled private corporation (CCPC), reports the following passive and capital items: (1) $30,000 of Canadian term-deposit interest; (2) a $100,000 capital gain ($50,000 taxable capital gain) from selling a factory building used actively in its manufacturing business until the sale; and (3) $25,000 of eligible portfolio dividends from Canadian public corporations (deductible under s.112). What are Pinecrest's aggregate investment income (AII) and adjusted aggregate investment income (AAII) for 2026?

在 2026 税年，加拿大受控私人公司（CCPC）Pinecrest Manufacturing Ltd. 报告了以下被动与资本项目：(1) 加拿大定期存款利息 $30,000；(2) 出售一处直到出售前一直用于其制造业务的厂房，实现资本利得 $100,000（应税资本利得 $50,000）；(3) 来自加拿大上市公司的合资格组合股息 $25,000（可按 s.112 扣除）。Pinecrest 在 2026 税年的合计投资收入（AII）与调整后合计投资收入（AAII）分别是多少？

  1. ✓ 正确。合计投资收入（aggregate investment income, AII）包含应税资本利得净额（$50,000）加利息（$30,000），剔除按 s.112 扣除的股息，合计 $80,000；调整后合计投资收入（adjusted aggregate investment income, AAII）在 AII 基础上剔除处置经营性资产（active asset）的应税资本利得（−$50,000），并加回非联结公司（non-connected corporation）股息（＋$25,000），得出 $30,000 ＋ $25,000 ＝ $55,000。Correct. Aggregate investment income (AII) includes net taxable capital gains ($50,000) plus interest ($30,000) and excludes dividends deducted under s.112, for a total of $80,000. Adjusted aggregate investment income (AAII) starts from AII, removes the taxable capital gain on disposing of an active asset (-$50,000) and adds back dividends from a non-connected corporation (+$25,000), giving $30,000 + $25,000 = $55,000.

  2. ✗ 错误。掉入了『AII与AAII口径混用』陷阱，将 Schedule 7 第 1 部分的 AII（$80,000）与第 2 部分的 AAII（$55,000）两个数值完全颠倒。Wrong. This falls into the 'Mixing up the AII and AAII bases' trap and swaps the two numbers: AII in Schedule 7 Part 1 ($80,000) and AAII in Part 2 ($55,000).

  3. ✗ 错误。计算 AAII 时只记住了加回非联结公司的组合股息（$25,000），却忘了剔除处置自用经营性资产（active asset）产生的 $50,000 应税资本利得（ITA s.125(7)）。Wrong. In computing AAII it only adds back the $25,000 of portfolio dividends from a non-connected corporation and forgets to remove the $50,000 taxable capital gain on disposing of an owner-used active asset (ITA s.125(7)).

  4. ✗ 错误。计算 AII 时误把按 s.112 扣除的 $25,000 上市公司股息也加了进去；该股息归 Part IV 税管辖，不计入 Part I 的 AII（ITA s.129(4)）。Wrong. In computing AII it wrongly adds the $25,000 of listed-company dividends deducted under s.112. Those dividends fall under Part IV tax and are not part of Part I AII (ITA s.129(4)).

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

In 2026, Oakridge Logistics Inc., a CCPC, earns $40,000 of net rental income from a warehouse leased to an unrelated tenant, realizes an $80,000 capital gain ($40,000 taxable capital gain) from selling delivery trucks used exclusively in its active freight business, and receives $15,000 of taxable dividends from public Canadian corporations. What are Oakridge's AII and AAII for 2026?

2026 税年，一家 CCPC（Oakridge Logistics Inc.）从出租给非关联租户的仓库赚取净租金收入 $40,000，出售专门用于其积极货运业务的送货卡车实现资本利得 $80,000（应税资本利得 $40,000），并收到加拿大上市公司的应税股息 $15,000。Oakridge 在 2026 税年的 AII 和 AAII 分别是多少？

  1. ✗ 错误。掉入了『AII与AAII口径混用』陷阱，把两个口径的计算规则反过来套用，导致结果颠倒。Wrong. This falls into the 'Mixing up the AII and AAII bases' trap: it applies the two bases the wrong way round, so the results are swapped.

  2. ✗ 错误。计算 AAII 时加回了 $15,000 非联结公司股息，但漏了剔除出售货运卡车（经营性资产 active asset）的 $40,000 应税资本利得。Wrong. In computing AAII it adds back the $15,000 of non-connected dividends but fails to remove the $40,000 taxable capital gain on selling the freight trucks (active assets).

  3. ✓ 正确。合计投资收入（AII）＝ 净租金 $40,000 ＋ 应税资本利得 $40,000 ＝ $80,000（不含按 s.112 扣除的股息）；调整后合计投资收入（AAII）剔除处置经营性资产（active asset）的 $40,000 应税资本利得，并加回非联结公司（non-connected）股息 $15,000，即 $40,000 ＋ $15,000 ＝ $55,000。Correct. Aggregate investment income (AII) = net rent $40,000 + taxable capital gain $40,000 = $80,000 (dividends deducted under s.112 are excluded). Adjusted aggregate investment income (AAII) removes the $40,000 taxable capital gain on disposing of an active asset and adds back the $15,000 of non-connected dividends: $40,000 + $15,000 = $55,000.

  4. ✗ 错误。AII 必须包含所有应税资本利得（无论是否属于经营性资产），而 AAII 必须加回非联结公司的组合股息 $15,000。Wrong. AII must include all taxable capital gains (whether or not the asset was an active asset), and AAII must add back the $15,000 of portfolio dividends from non-connected corporations.

第 2 题Q2被动收入递减跨年计算与省税联动Cross-year passive income grind calculation and the BC tax link★★陷阱：被动收入递减错算当年或全额乘5

Westbrook Design Ltd. is a CCPC operating solely in British Columbia with a December 31 year-end, no associated corporations, and taxable capital well below $10,000,000. In 2025, Westbrook had adjusted aggregate investment income (AAII) of $80,000 and active business income of $500,000. In 2026, Westbrook has AAII of $20,000 and active business income of $500,000. 2026 年 3 月 15 日，私人公司 Silver Birch Ltd. 通过 Schedule 89 向 CRA 核实其资本股息账户（CDA）余额为 $80,000。2026 年 3 月 20 日，该公司提交 T2054 表格并向唯一股东派发了 $80,000 资本股息。2026 年 10 月（同一税年内），Silver Birch 出售上市股票并实现了 $60,000 资本亏损（不可抵扣的一半为 $30,000）。2026 年 3 月 20 日那笔资本股息的 Part III 税后果是什么？

  1. ✗ 错误。掉入了『年末才算 CDA 余额』的陷阱：年末计算的是 GRIP，而 CDA 余额是累计到『股息成为应付或实际支付那一刻』计算的。Wrong. This falls into the trap of measuring the CDA balance at year-end: it is GRIP that is computed at year-end, whereas the CDA balance accumulates up to the moment the dividend becomes payable or is paid.

  2. ✓ 正确。资本股息账户（capital dividend account, CDA）是从公司最后一次成为私人公司起累计到『派息那一刻』的通知账户，而非年末结算账户。既然 2026 年 3 月 20 日派息前一刻 CDA 确有 $80,000 余额，该次资本股息选择完全有效；同年 10 月后来才发生的资本亏损只会冲减以后的 CDA 可派余额，不会追溯导致 3 月的派息超额。Correct. The capital dividend account (CDA) is a notional account accumulated from the time the corporation last became a private corporation up to the moment the dividend is paid, not a year-end account. Since the CDA did have an $80,000 balance immediately before the March 20, 2026 payment, that capital dividend election is fully valid; a capital loss arising later, in October of the same year, reduces the CDA available for later dividends and does not retroactively make the March payment an excess.

  3. ✗ 错误。因为 3 月派息时并未超额，根本不产生 Part III 税，无需走 s.184(3) 的补救选择。Wrong. Because the March payment was not an excess, there is no Part III tax and no need for the s.184(3) remedial election.

  4. ✗ 错误。资本亏损不可扣的一半只冲减由资本利得形成的那部分 CDA（减至零为止），不冲减寿险赔款形成的额度，且不会触发 20% 罚款。Wrong. The non-allowable half of a capital loss reduces only the CDA component built from capital gains (down to zero), not the component from life insurance proceeds, and it does not trigger a 20% penalty.

Which of the following statements accurately describes the rules governing the general rate income pool (GRIP) and eligible dividend designations for a Canadian-controlled private corporation (CCPC) in 2026?

下列哪项表述准确描述了 2026 税年加拿大受控私人公司（CCPC）关于一般税率收入池（GRIP）及合资格股息指定的规则？

  1. ✓ 正确。CCPC 的一般税率收入池（general rate income pool, GRIP，Schedule 53）在税年末按 0.72 的一般税率因子（general rate factor，ITA s.89(1)）计算；公司可在年中先派合资格股息（须在付款时或之前书面通知股东），但若年末回看时已派总额超过年末 GRIP，超额部分须缴 20% 的 Part III.1 税，若属人为拉高 GRIP 则罚 30%（ITA s.185.1(1)）。Correct. A CCPC's general rate income pool (GRIP, Schedule 53) is computed at the end of the tax year using the 0.72 general rate factor (ITA s.89(1)). A corporation may pay eligible dividends earlier in the year (with written notice to shareholders at or before payment), but if at the year-end look-back the total paid exceeds year-end GRIP, the excess bears 20% Part III.1 tax, or 30% if GRIP was artificially inflated (ITA s.185.1(1)).

  2. ✗ 错误。低税率收入池（low rate income pool, LRIP，Schedule 54）是非 CCPC 才有的阻挡池；CCPC 只有 GRIP，没有 LRIP。Wrong. The low rate income pool (LRIP, Schedule 54) is a blocking pool for non-CCPCs only; a CCPC has GRIP and no LRIP.

  3. ✗ 错误。要求在每次派息前一刻核实余额、超额罚 60% Part III 税的是资本股息账户（CDA），而 GRIP 是在年末计算的，超额罚 20% 或 30%（Part III.1 税）。Wrong. The account that must be checked immediately before each payment, with 60% Part III tax on the excess, is the capital dividend account (CDA); GRIP is computed at year-end and the excess is taxed at 20% or 30% (Part III.1 tax).

  4. ✗ 错误。根据 ITA s.89(14)，合资格股息必须在付款当时或之前书面通知股东（董事会纪要记一行即可）；迟报指定可在三年内补作，但也要通知股东，不存在「永不通知、只在 T2 上报」就成立的合资格股息。Wrong. Under ITA s.89(14), an eligible dividend must be notified to shareholders in writing at or before payment (one line in the board minutes will do); a late designation can be made within three years but must still be notified to shareholders; there is no eligible dividend that is valid by 'never notifying and merely reporting it on the T2'.

换个情境再练一次（同一考点的变式题）Try a variant: same point, new situation

In June 2026, Pacific Timber Ltd., a CCPC with a December 31 year-end, pays a $100,000 dividend and records a resolution in its board minutes notifying the shareholder that the entire dividend is an eligible dividend. At that time, its GRIP balance from 2025 was $0, as it expected high-rate income in the second half of 2026. 2026 年 6 月，年结日为 12 月 31 日的 CCPC（Pacific Timber Ltd.）派发了 $100,000 股息，并在董事会纪要中记录决议，书面通知股东该股息全额为合资格股息。当时其承接自 2025 年的 GRIP 余额为 $0，因为公司预计 2026 年下半年会有按一般税率纳税的收入。由于市场下滑，其 2026 年末计算的 GRIP 最终只有 $40,000（无滥用避税安排）。后果是什么？

  2. ✓ 正确。年末回看时已派合资格股息（$100,000）超出年末 GRIP（$40,000）的 $60,000 构成超额合资格股息指定（excessive eligible dividend designation，Schedule 55），适用 20% 的 Part III.1 税（$12,000）；因为不涉及人为拉高 GRIP（30% 档），该 20% 罚税可经股东同意将超额部分改按普通（非合资格）股息处理来化解（ITA s.185.1）。Correct. At the year-end look-back, the eligible dividends paid ($100,000) exceed year-end GRIP ($40,000) by $60,000, which is an excessive eligible dividend designation (Schedule 55) bearing 20% Part III.1 tax ($12,000). Because no artificial inflation of GRIP is involved (the 30% tier), the 20% tax can be avoided if the shareholder consents to treat the excess as an ordinary (non-eligible) dividend (ITA s.185.1).

  3. ✗ 错误。年中先派、等年末算 GRIP 是税法允许的正常时点机制，不属于人为拉高 GRIP 的安排，不适用不可豁免的 30% 惩罚档。Wrong. Paying early and settling GRIP at year-end is a normal timing mechanism the Act allows. It is not an arrangement to inflate GRIP artificially, so the non-waivable 30% tier does not apply.

  4. ✗ 错误。60% 的 Part III 税和 T2054 表格针对的是资本股息账户（CDA），与 GRIP 和合资格股息无关。Wrong. The 60% Part III tax and Form T2054 relate to the capital dividend account (CDA), not to GRIP or eligible dividends.

第 7 题Q7两条递减取大者与现行应税资本区间The greater of the two grinds and the current taxable capital range★★★陷阱：两条递减相加或套用旧区间

Applying the 2026 rules, by how much is Kootenay's $500,000 small business deduction business limit reduced?

独立 CCPC（Kootenay Precision Ltd.）上一税年在加拿大运用的应税资本为 $12,000,000，上一历年内结束的税年的调整后合计投资收入（AAII）为 $70,000。按 2026 税年现行规则，Kootenay 的 $500,000 小企业扣除经营限额被削减多少？

The two grinds are not added; the reduction is the greater of the two (ITA s.125(5.1)). Taxable capital grind = $500,000 x ($12,000,000 - $10,000,000) / ($50,000,000 - $10,000,000) = $25,000; passive income grind = ($70,000 - $50,000) x 5 = $100,000; $25,000 + $100,000 = $125,000 is the mistaken sum.
